## What makes a case catastrophic for damages purposes

For damages planning, a case is catastrophic when the injury permanently
changes both what a person can do for work and what their care will cost for
the rest of their life. Spinal cord injury, moderate to severe brain injury,
amputation, severe burns, blindness, and birth injury are the recurring
examples. The defining features are permanence, lifetime horizon, and the
interaction of two loss streams: earning capacity that is reduced or
extinguished, and future medical and support needs that continue for decades.
Either stream alone can dominate the verdict or settlement value, and both
depend on expert analysis that will be examined closely by the other side.

## The three workstreams

The vocational evaluation establishes what work, if any, remains within the
person's residual capacity, and what it pays, using records review, interview,
testing where indicated, transferable skills analysis, and labor market
research. The life care plan projects the medical care, therapies, attendant
care, equipment, supplies, and environmental needs across the person's
lifetime, each item traced to a clinical foundation and priced from disclosed
sources. The economic analysis converts the earning capacity difference and
the plan's costs into present value using worklife expectancy, life
expectancy, growth, and discount assumptions that are stated and supported.

At Purinton Analytics the first two workstreams are performed by the firm's
principal. The economic analysis is performed and signed by an independent
economist partner coordinated by the firm and selected for the venue and case
needs.

## Why coordination matters

When the three analyses are retained separately and never reconciled,
predictable failures appear at deposition. The economist values a loss the
vocational evaluation does not support. The life care plan and the household
services calculation pay for the same care twice. The disciplines assume
different life expectancies without explanation. Each expert is then
cross-examined on the seams between the reports rather than on the merits of
their own work.

Coordination closes the seams while preserving the signatures. The team works
from one records base, adopts consistent foundational assumptions with each
source identified, and passes outputs downstream as stated inputs: earning
capacity findings and plan costs into the economic analysis. Each report
remains separately authored, signed, and defended. The result is a damages
presentation whose pieces fit, offered by experts who each stay inside their
own discipline.

## When to engage the team

<ul class="checklist">
  <li>Permanent restrictions are documented or clearly anticipated, and the person's return to comparable work is doubtful</li>
  <li>Future medical, attendant care, or equipment needs will continue beyond the near term</li>
  <li>The plaintiff is a minor or had not yet established a work history</li>
  <li>Claimed damages will require present value testimony to present or to challenge</li>
  <li>The matter is a defense engagement requiring discipline-by-discipline review of an opposing presentation</li>
</ul>

Engage early. Records collection is the longest step, the clinical picture
takes time to stabilize, and pediatric matters add developmental
documentation. A team retained early can also shape discovery: one combined
records request, issued at the start, serves all three disciplines.

## Sequencing the work

The sequence follows the dependency structure of the analysis. First, the
conflict check clears and the combined records request issues. Second, the
vocational evaluation and the life care plan proceed in parallel once
restrictions and treating recommendations are documented. Third, their
outputs pass to the independent economist partner as stated inputs. Fourth,
the economist completes and signs the present value analysis, with time
reserved before the disclosure deadline for counsel's review and for the
depositions that follow. The deadline worksheet in this resource library maps
the same sequence onto a scheduling order.

## What the referral looks like

One conflict check covers the anticipated team: submit the case caption or
parties, the venue, the deadlines, and a brief non-confidential summary
through the referral page. After clearance, a scope call defines which
disciplines the case needs now and which may be added, a single records
request issues, and a secure upload link is provided for the file. Fee
schedules and CVs for the principal and the proposed economist partner are
provided before retention. Send no medical records or confidential material
before the conflict check clears.

## Plaintiff and defense engagements

Plaintiff counsel typically engage the team to build the affirmative damages
case. Defense counsel engage the same disciplines to test one: reviewing the
opposing vocational evaluation, life care plan, and economic report each
against its own foundation, data, methodology, and arithmetic, and providing
affirmative alternative analyses where counsel wants them. The firm accepts
both engagements under the same methodology, and the coordinated structure
serves both purposes.
