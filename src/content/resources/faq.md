## How referrals work

Every engagement follows the same four steps. First, counsel submits a
conflict check with the case caption or party names; the firm clears conflicts
and confirms availability before any substantive discussion. Second, the scope
is defined and a tailored records request issues, with a secure upload link
for the file. Third, the evaluation or review is completed and a written
report is delivered by the agreed date, tracked against the disclosure
calendar from the start. Fourth, the expert is available for deposition,
pretrial consultation, mediation support, and trial testimony.

The answers below cover the questions counsel most often ask about that
process. They describe the firm's general practices; the engagement terms
provided at retention govern any specific matter.

## Standards behind the answers

Two reference points sit behind several of these answers. Reports are prepared
for scrutiny under Federal Rule of Evidence 702 and its state analogues, which
require reliable methods reliably applied. And the firm's vocational work sits
within the credentialing and ethics frameworks of its certifying bodies,
including the American Board of Vocational Experts. Neither point is a
guarantee of any outcome; both shape how the work is documented.
