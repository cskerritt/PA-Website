Moreno Valley belongs to the Riverside County side of the
Riverside-San Bernardino-Ontario metro, and that county assignment shapes
where its cases are heard and which local market its residents work in.
Purinton Analytics accepts plaintiff and defense engagements arising in
Moreno Valley, delivering
[vocational evaluations](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care plans](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) of opposing
expert work, all prepared to a standard that assumes the reader is opposing
counsel with a red pen.

## Courts serving Moreno Valley

State court engagements for Moreno Valley matters are accepted in the
Superior Court of Riverside County. Federal work is accepted in the United
States District Court for the Central District of California, and the
practice appears as well in the United States District Court for the
Northern District of California, the United States District Court for the
Eastern District of California, and the United States District Court for the
Southern District of California when a matter is venued there. The forum
never changes the file's architecture: documented conflict check, written
scope, tailored records request, and a report that shows its work at every
step.

## Labor market position within the metro

For a Moreno Valley evaluee, the relevant employment market is the one
reachable from home, and the firm measures it with Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the
Riverside-San Bernardino-Ontario metropolitan area and for California
statewide, joined by state workforce agency labor market information and
direct labor market research when the referral questions demand more
granularity. No wage or employment figure enters a report without a cited
source, because an opinion that cannot be traced cannot be defended. The
[metro page](/locations/california/riverside/) describes how the practice
covers the region's market as a whole.

## Evaluation logistics, stated plainly

Counsel will not find a staffed local office in Moreno Valley, and none is
claimed; candor about presence is part of the same habit that keeps the
reports honest. Engagements typically open remotely, and much of the work,
from records analysis to structured vocational interviews to standardized
testing under publisher-approved remote protocols, is completed without
anyone traveling at all. When an in-person evaluation is warranted, it is
held in the evaluee's own community. When hearings, depositions, or trial
require the expert in the room, travel is planned from the Kansas City home
office well ahead of the setting, and remote appearance is offered wherever
the court and the parties permit. Scheduling assumptions are confirmed with
counsel in writing at intake, so that testing windows, interview dates,
and report delivery all trace back to the operative case management order
instead of to guesswork.
