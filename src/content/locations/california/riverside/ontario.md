Ontario is one of the named cities in the Riverside-San Bernardino-Ontario
metropolitan title, a large San Bernardino County city substantial enough
that the region is partly defined by it. Litigation arising there draws on
the same practice that serves the rest of the metro: Purinton Analytics
provides [vocational expert work](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning components proceed under a
single engagement structure, for counsel on either side of the caption.

## County and courthouse context

Ontario sits in San Bernardino County, so its state court matters are
accepted in the Superior Court of San Bernardino County. On the federal
side, the practice accepts engagements in the United States District Court
for the Central District of California and across the state's other
districts: the United States District Court for the Northern District of
California, the United States District Court for the Eastern District of
California, and the United States District Court for the Southern District
of California. Whatever the venue, the opinion is built the same way,
beginning with a documented conflict check and a defined written scope, so
that the record supporting each conclusion is open to inspection by both
sides.

## Ontario's place in the metro labor market

Because Ontario anchors part of a metropolitan employment area, vocational
findings for its residents are grounded in Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates for the
Riverside-San Bernardino-Ontario metro and for California statewide, with
state workforce agency labor market information and targeted employer
research added when the referral questions call for it. The premise is
simple: an earning capacity opinion should reflect the jobs genuinely open
to this evaluee in this market. Life care plans and
[medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/) apply the
same locality rule to care pricing. Regional context for the whole
employment area is set out on the
[metro page](/locations/california/riverside/).

## By-appointment service in Ontario

No street address is published for Ontario because none exists; the firm
serves the city by appointment and says so without hedging. In practice,
engagements begin remotely, with records review, structured interviews, and
standardized testing conducted under publisher protocols that permit remote
administration. Where the referral requires an in-person evaluation, it
takes place in the evaluee's own community. Deposition and trial attendance
is coordinated with counsel early, with travel from the Kansas City home
office scheduled against the court's dates, and remote testimony remains
available whenever the court and the parties agree to it. The evaluee's treating providers and
counsel's paralegal team receive clear instructions for what the records
request covers, which shortens the collection cycle and keeps the analysis
from stalling while documents trickle in.

## Opening an engagement

Retention follows the sequence used throughout the
[California practice](/locations/california/) and the firm's
[nationwide coverage](/locations/nationwide/): conflict check, engagement
confirmation, tailored records request, analysis. Counsel can
[start the conflict check](/refer-a-case/) by submitting the caption and
party names, together with any disclosure deadlines or trial settings
already on the calendar, and the work is then scheduled backward from those
dates rather than forward from the expert's convenience.
