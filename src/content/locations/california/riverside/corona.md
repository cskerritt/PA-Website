In a metropolitan area that spans a county line, where a person lives and
where that person can realistically work are separate questions, and an
earning capacity opinion that ignores the difference invites impeachment.
For Corona, a large Riverside County city within the
Riverside-San Bernardino-Ontario metro, Purinton Analytics frames the
vocational question around the employment genuinely accessible to the
evaluee, and it does so for plaintiff and defense counsel through
[vocational expert evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis under the same engagement structure as the vocational and
care-planning work.

## Riverside County venue context

Corona's state court matters are accepted in the Superior Court of
Riverside County. Federal matters are accepted in the United States
District Court for the Central District of California, and the practice
serves the rest of the state's federal bench as needed: the United States
District Court for the Northern District of California, the United States
District Court for the Eastern District of California, and the United
States District Court for the Southern District of California. The forum
changes nothing about the method, which begins in every case with a
documented conflict check, a written scope, and a records request drawn
from the pleadings rather than from a form.

## A commuting market, measured honestly

The metro's labor market does not stop at municipal or county boundaries,
so the analysis for a Corona evaluee uses Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates at the level where the data
actually lives: the Riverside-San Bernardino-Ontario metropolitan area and
California statewide, refined with state workforce agency labor market
information and direct labor market research when the referral calls for
it. The report states which market was measured and why, with each source
cited so the choice itself can be examined. A fuller account of the
regional market appears on the
[metro overview page](/locations/california/riverside/).

## Meeting the evaluee where they live

Purinton Analytics has no Corona office and says so plainly, because
claims about presence should meet the same standard as claims in a report.
The engagement is structured accordingly: records analysis, structured
vocational interviews, and standardized testing proceed remotely under
publisher protocols that support it, and when the referral questions
require an in-person evaluation, the appointment is set in the evaluee's
own community. Travel for depositions and trial is scheduled from the
Kansas City home office and locked to the court's dates early, with remote
testimony available where the court and the parties consent. Because the
evaluation happens on the evaluee's ground, observations about commuting
tolerance, transportation access, and daily functioning enter the record
as findings rather than assumptions.
