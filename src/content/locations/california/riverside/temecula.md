A life care plan is only as credible as its pricing, and pricing is local.
When catastrophic injury litigation involves a Temecula resident, the
future care a plan describes will be purchased in and around Temecula, so
Purinton Analytics costs it to that community rather than to a national
average. That locality principle runs through the firm's
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/),
[medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/), and
[vocational expert](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) work for
plaintiff and defense counsel alike, and it is documented in the report so
either side can check the arithmetic behind every line.

## The venues that hear Temecula matters

Temecula's state court litigation is accepted in the Superior Court of
Riverside County, the trial bench for this Riverside County city. Federal
matters are accepted in the United States District Court for the Central
District of California, and the practice appears as well in the United
States District Court for the Northern District of California, the United
States District Court for the Eastern District of California, and the
United States District Court for the Southern District of California when
a case is venued in those courts. Forum aside, every engagement opens the
same way: documented conflict check, written scope, and a records request
matched to the claims.

## Labor market context for Temecula

On the vocational side, opinions about a Temecula evaluee's earning
capacity are built from Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment
and wage estimates for the Riverside-San Bernardino-Ontario metropolitan
area and for California statewide, reinforced with state workforce agency
labor market information and direct labor market research when the
referral questions warrant it. Where the vocational findings feed a
damages model, the practice offers coordinated engagements in which
independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis
while the vocational and care-planning work continues under one
engagement structure. The regional market as a whole is addressed on the
[metro page](/locations/california/riverside/).

## Care planning logistics for local evaluees

Temecula appointments are arranged case by case; there is no local office
behind them, and the firm states that outright rather than leaving it to
inference. The working pattern is remote where remote is sound: records
review, structured vocational interviews, and standardized testing under
publisher protocols that support remote administration. In-person
evaluation, when the referral requires it, happens in the evaluee's own
community, which is also where care resources are surveyed for the plan
itself. Deposition and trial attendance is scheduled with travel from the
Kansas City home office, fixed to the court's dates, with remote
testimony available where the court and the parties agree. Vendors,
facilities, and provider types named in a plan are the kinds actually
available to this evaluee, and the plan says where each cost figure came
from so a reviewing expert can audit it.
