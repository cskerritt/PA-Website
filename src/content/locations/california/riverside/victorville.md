Loss of earning capacity is a claim about a particular person in a
particular labor market, and Purinton Analytics treats it as something to
be measured rather than assumed. For a Victorville evaluee, measurement
means structured vocational interviews, standardized testing chosen for
the questions the case actually presents, and a transferable skills
analysis that maps documented work history onto the occupations a
limitation still permits. Plaintiff and defense counsel retain the firm
for [vocational assessment](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care plans](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[medical cost projection work](/services/medical-cost-projection/), and
every deliverable identifies the instruments used, the norms applied, and
the reasoning that connects results to conclusions.

## The market data behind the findings

Test results alone do not establish a loss; they have to be read against
the labor market the evaluee actually faces. For Victorville, a sizable
city within the Riverside-San Bernardino-Ontario metropolitan area, that
market is documented through Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational
employment and wage estimates for the metro and for California statewide,
supplemented by state workforce agency labor market information and by
direct labor market research when the referral questions justify evidence
gathered closer to the ground. The report states which market definition
was applied and why, and it cites each source, so a reviewing expert can
rerun the analysis rather than argue with a summary of it. How the
practice approaches the broader employment region anchored by Riverside
is set out in its [Riverside metro coverage](/locations/california/riverside/).

## Appointments, testing conditions, and travel

Victorville is served from the firm's Kansas City home office, by
appointment; there is no local office, and the firm states that plainly
instead of implying a presence it does not have. Standardized instruments
are given only under the conditions their publishers sanction, and where
those conditions permit remote administration, testing and structured
interviews are completed without anyone traveling. Records analysis is
remote in nearly every engagement. When the referral requires an
in-person evaluation, the meeting happens in the community where the
evaluee actually lives, and that community likewise anchors the pricing
of any life care plan or cost projection. Deposition and trial attendance
is planned with counsel as soon as settings are known, with travel booked
from Kansas City against the court's dates; where the bench and the
parties are willing, testimony is delivered remotely instead.
