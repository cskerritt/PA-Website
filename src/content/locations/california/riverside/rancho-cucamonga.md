## Where and how evaluations happen

The practice keeps no office in Rancho Cucamonga and does not pretend
otherwise. What counsel gets instead is a process engineered to need none:
remote records review, structured vocational interviews, and standardized
testing under publisher protocols that support remote administration,
followed by in-person evaluation in the evaluee's own community when the
referral warrants it. For depositions and trial, attendance is arranged
with travel from the Kansas City home office, booked against the settings
counsel identifies at intake, and remote appearance is used where the court
and the parties permit, which often removes a scheduling fight entirely. If a deadline moves, the
work plan moves with it in writing, so counsel always knows what will be
ready when, and no draft ages silently in a drawer while the calendar
tightens.
