A damages opinion is only as strong as the labor market evidence beneath it,
and the labor market that matters for a Visalia evaluee is the one in Tulare
County, not a national abstraction. Purinton Analytics builds its work for
the Visalia metro on that premise, offering plaintiff and defense counsel
[vocational expert evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) of
earning capacity and employability,
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) in catastrophic injury
matters, and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Venues served from Visalia referrals

State court engagements are accepted in the Superior Court in Tulare County,
the trial court serving Visalia and the surrounding communities. Federal
engagements are accepted throughout the state's district courts: the United
States District Court for the Northern District of California, the United
States District Court for the Eastern District of California, the United
States District Court for the Central District of California, and the United
States District Court for the Southern District of California. The choice of
forum affects deadlines and disclosure practice, never the substance of the
analysis. Counsel with matters pending elsewhere in the state can find the
firm's full coverage on the
[California locations page](/locations/california/).

## Appointments, not an office

The firm has no staffed office in Visalia and publishes no street address
for this location. Saying so directly is deliberate: the same discipline
that requires a cited source for every wage finding requires candor about
where the firm actually sits. Counsel meetings and evaluee sessions are
scheduled by appointment. Records review, structured interviews, and
standardized testing generally proceed remotely under publisher protocols
where remote administration is supported, and when an in-person evaluation
or a Tulare County deposition or trial date requires presence, travel is
scheduled from the Kansas City home office in coordination with the
litigation calendar.

## The research behind a Tulare County opinion

Vocational findings rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational
employment and wage estimates for the Visalia metropolitan area and for
California statewide, supplemented by state workforce agency labor market
information and direct labor market research where the referral questions
warrant it. Life care plans and medical cost projections are priced to the
evaluee's own market using provider-specific inquiries and published
regional fee data. The report cites its sources at each step, because the
firm assumes its most careful reader will be the lawyer on the other side of
the caption. That is the same report standard the firm applies to
[national work](/locations/nationwide/), delivered without pretending to a
local storefront that does not exist.

## From conflict check to signed report

Engagements open with clearance rather than commitment. Counsel should
[request a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) with the case caption and party
names, noting any imminent disclosure, deposition, or trial dates. Once
conflicts clear, the firm issues a defined scope and a records request
tailored to the claims, and the analysis proceeds on a schedule set with
counsel, whether the opinion is ultimately used in negotiation, at
mediation, or on the stand.
