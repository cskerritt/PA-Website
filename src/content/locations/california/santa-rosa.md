Most of the work in a vocational or life care planning engagement is records
work, interview work, and analysis, and none of it requires the expert to
live down the street. That is how Purinton Analytics serves counsel in the
Santa Rosa-Petaluma metro: remote-first where the work allows it, in person
when the case requires it. Attorneys on either side of the caption retain
the firm for
[earning capacity and employability evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) in catastrophic injury
cases, and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Sonoma County and the federal districts

Matters are accepted in the Superior Court in Sonoma County, the state trial
court for Santa Rosa, Petaluma, and the rest of the county. In federal
practice, the firm accepts engagements across California's district courts:
the United States District Court for the Northern District of California,
the United States District Court for the Eastern District of California, the
United States District Court for the Central District of California, and the
United States District Court for the Southern District of California. The
firm's coverage of other venues in the state is collected on the
[California locations page](/locations/california/).

## What remote-first means in practice

An engagement usually opens with records review and a structured interview
conducted remotely, followed by standardized testing administered under
publisher protocols where remote administration is supported. The firm keeps
no staffed office in Santa Rosa and publishes no street address for this
location; meetings are by appointment, and that is stated plainly because
honesty about presence is part of the discipline counsel are retaining. When
the referral questions require an in-person evaluation, or when a deposition
or trial in Sonoma County requires attendance, travel is scheduled from the
Kansas City home office around the dates that matter. The evaluee sees the
same interview structure, and counsel sees the same report standard, in
either mode.

## Data behind the opinions

Vocational conclusions for Sonoma County evaluees rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Santa Rosa
metropolitan area and for California statewide, supported by state workforce
agency labor market information and direct labor market research where the
questions warrant it. Life care plans and medical cost projections are
priced to the evaluee's own market through provider-specific inquiries and
published regional fee data. When a care plan draws on several provider
types, each item carries its own pricing basis, so the projection can be
audited item by item rather than accepted on trust. Sources are cited so opposing counsel can
retrace every step, which is the same standard the firm carries into
[engagements nationwide](/locations/nationwide/).

## Requesting a conflict check

New matters start with clearance. Use the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) to submit the caption or party names,
and include any disclosure, deposition, or trial dates already on calendar.
After conflicts clear, the firm confirms scope and schedule in writing, and
the engagement proceeds under a defined records protocol from the first
request to the final signed report.
