## Reading Roseville's labor market honestly

Because Roseville functions as a hub of employment within the metro, the
question in an earning capacity dispute is often not whether work exists
but which work is realistically available to this evaluee with these
restrictions. That analysis draws on Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates for the Sacramento
metropolitan area and for California statewide, state workforce agency
labor market information, and, where warranted, direct contact with
employers. Each source is cited in the report so the opposing expert can
check the same data and the trier of fact can see where any disagreement
actually lies. Where testing informs the employability finding, the
instruments, their publishers, and the administration conditions are all
named, because an opinion that hides its inputs invites the
cross-examination it deserves.
