## Placer County, and the courts that follow from it

Rocklin belongs to Placer County, so its state court litigation is
ordinarily venued in the Superior Court of Placer County rather than in
the courts of the metro's other counties, a detail worth settling before
any schedule is drawn. Federal matters from the area proceed most often
in the United States District Court for the Eastern District of
California, and the practice accepts engagements throughout the state's
federal courts, among them the United States District Court for the
Northern District of California. In every forum the opinion is written to
survive the other side's closest reading.

## Placing a Rocklin worker in the regional economy

Rocklin is a mid-sized Placer County city whose residents work across the
Sacramento-Roseville-Folsom, CA metro, so employability and wage findings
are grounded in Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and
wage estimates for the Sacramento metropolitan area and for California
statewide, with state workforce agency labor market information layered
in. When the dispute turns on whether identified jobs are real for this
evaluee, direct labor market research settles the question, and the
report shows the work: sources named, contacts logged, reasoning stated.
Rebuttal assignments apply the same tests in reverse, asking whether the
opposing report's sources exist, say what the report claims they say, and
support the conclusions drawn from them.

## Appointments, evaluations, and travel

The firm operates no staffed Rocklin office and publishes no street
address for this location. That is stated plainly because the practice
treats candor about presence as part of its method. Engagements open
remotely with records review and interviews; standardized instruments
are given under publisher protocols where remote administration is
supported; in-person evaluation, when warranted, is held in the
evaluee's community. Travel for evaluations and testimony originates at
the Kansas City home office, timed to deposition and trial settings, with
remote testimony available where the court and parties consent.
