Elk Grove has grown into one of the largest cities in the
Sacramento-Roseville-Folsom, CA metro, and its injury, employment, and
disability dockets raise the full range of questions a
[vocational expert](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) is retained to
answer: what work an injured person can still perform, what that work
actually pays, and how a [life care plan](/services/life-care-planning/)
should account for the cost of future care. Purinton Analytics accepts
these referrals from plaintiff and defense counsel alike. For cases that
also require economic loss calculations, the firm offers
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Sacramento County venue, state and federal

Elk Grove sits in Sacramento County, so state court matters involving Elk
Grove parties are ordinarily heard in the Superior Court of Sacramento
County. Federal engagements are accepted throughout California, most often
for this area in the United States District Court for the Eastern District
of California, and also in the United States District Court for the
Northern District of California, the United States District Court for the
Central District of California, and the United States District Court for
the Southern District of California. The forum never changes the product:
a written opinion resting on a documented record, prepared with the
expectation that opposing counsel will examine every source.

## A large suburb inside a regional labor market

An Elk Grove resident's earning capacity is rarely confined to Elk Grove
itself; the relevant market is the broader metro economy anchored by
Sacramento. Vocational findings therefore rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Sacramento
metropolitan area and for California statewide, supplemented by state
workforce agency labor market information and direct employer research
where the referral questions warrant it. Transferable skills analysis and
labor market surveys are documented so the path from data to opinion can
be retraced in deposition, whichever side conducts it.

## Evaluating an Elk Grove resident

The firm maintains no staffed office in Elk Grove and publishes no street
address for this location; meetings are by appointment. Most engagements
begin remotely, with records review, vocational interviews, and
standardized testing administered under publisher protocols that support
remote use. When an in-person evaluation is appropriate, it is conducted
in the evaluee's own community, and when deposition or trial attendance is
required, travel is arranged from the Kansas City home office with lead
time built around the court's calendar. Remote testimony is supported
where the court and the parties permit it.
