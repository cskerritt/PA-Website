For an injured person living in Citrus Heights, the evaluation itself is
the part of litigation that touches them directly, and Purinton Analytics
structures that experience with the same care it gives the written
opinion. The vocational interview happens on the evaluee's schedule, the
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) assessment considers
the care resources of the community where the evaluee actually lives, and
the resulting [medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/)
is priced to that community rather than to a national average. Plaintiff
and defense counsel receive the same process, because the evaluee's
experience should not depend on which side made the call.

## Sacramento County jurisdiction

Citrus Heights matters belong to Sacramento County, and its state court
litigation is ordinarily venued in the Superior Court of Sacramento
County. When a Citrus Heights case is federal, it most commonly proceeds
in the United States District Court for the Eastern District of
California; the practice also takes California matters in the United
States District Court for the Northern District of California and the
remaining federal districts of the state. Whichever courthouse controls,
the deliverable is a written opinion whose sources are cited well enough
for the other side to test them.

## A suburb's workers, a metro's labor market

Citrus Heights is a sizable suburb inside the
Sacramento-Roseville-Folsom, CA metro, and its residents draw their
employment prospects from across that regional economy. Vocational
findings for a Citrus Heights evaluee therefore rely on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Sacramento
metropolitan area and for California statewide, alongside state workforce
agency labor market information. Where a referral question turns on
whether specific work is genuinely open to a person with specific
restrictions, direct labor market research supplements the published
data, and every contact is documented. That documentation habit is not
decorative: it lets counsel decide, before deposition, exactly how much
weight the opinion will bear under questioning.

## Meetings by appointment, honestly framed

The firm has no staffed office in Citrus Heights and does not publish a
street address for this location, because claiming a local presence that
does not exist would sit poorly beside reports that trade on accuracy.
Records review, interviews, and standardized testing under publisher
protocols supporting remote administration all begin without travel. When
in-person evaluation, deposition, or trial attendance is needed, the trip
is planned from the Kansas City home office with the litigation calendar
in view, and remote testimony remains an option where the court and
parties permit.
