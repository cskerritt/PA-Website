## Yolo County courts and their federal counterpart

State court matters for Davis parties ordinarily proceed in the Superior
Court of Yolo County. On the federal side, cases from this part of the
Sacramento-Roseville-Folsom, CA metro are usually heard in the United
States District Court for the Eastern District of California, though the
practice takes matters throughout the state, including the United States
District Court for the Northern District of California, the United States
District Court for the Central District of California, and the United
States District Court for the Southern District of California. The
opinion's structure and sourcing are held to the same standard wherever
the case is filed.

## The labor market around a Davis evaluee

Davis is a mid-sized Yolo County city whose residents participate in the
wider metro employment market. Vocational conclusions accordingly rest on
Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for
the Sacramento metropolitan area and for California statewide, joined
with state workforce agency labor market information and, where a
question demands it, direct labor market research. The aim is a finding
that either side could audit: which occupations fit the evaluee's
capacities, what those occupations pay in this region, and how that
squares with the evaluee's pre-injury trajectory. Life care planning for
Davis evaluees follows the same audit-ready construction, with future
care needs itemized against the evaluee's diagnoses and priced to the
community where the care will actually be delivered.
