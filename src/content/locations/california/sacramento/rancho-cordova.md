## Measuring work in a commuting economy

Because Rancho Cordova workers move through a metro-wide employment
market, vocational conclusions here lean on Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates for the Sacramento
metropolitan area and for California statewide, along with state
workforce agency labor market information. Transferable skills analysis
identifies what an evaluee's work history actually qualifies them to do;
labor market research then tests whether that work exists in practice for
someone with the evaluee's restrictions. Both steps are documented, so
cross-examination finds a method, not an impression. When the earning
capacity finding feeds an economic loss calculation, the handoff to the
independent economist partner is documented as well, keeping the chain
from data to damages figure unbroken and reviewable.
