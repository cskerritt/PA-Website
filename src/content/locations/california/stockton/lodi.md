Lodi shares its name with the metropolitan area itself: the federal
statistical designation is Stockton-Lodi, CA, which means the labor market
data that grounds a vocational opinion here carries Lodi in its very title.
Purinton Analytics serves counsel litigating Lodi matters, for plaintiff
and defense alike, with
[vocational expert evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) of
earning capacity, [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) in
catastrophic claims, and
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) of opposing
damages work. Where the case needs an economic loss calculation, the firm
offers
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

The county is San Joaquin County, and the trial court is the Superior Court
of San Joaquin County, where injury, employment, and probate-adjacent
damages disputes arising in Lodi are ordinarily filed. Engagements are also
accepted in the federal trial courts, including the United States District
Court for the Northern District of California, the United States District
Court for the Eastern District of California, the United States District
Court for the Central District of California, and the United States
District Court for the Southern District of California. Counsel with
matters elsewhere in the state can review coverage on the
[California locations page](/locations/california/).

## Labor market context for a Lodi evaluee

Because the metro designation embraces Lodi directly, Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Stockton
metropolitan area speak to the market a Lodi evaluee faces with unusual
fit. The firm pairs those estimates with California statewide figures,
state workforce agency labor market information, and, where a referral
question turns on an actual hiring practice, direct labor market research.
Life care plans and medical cost projections are priced through
provider-specific inquiry in the evaluee's own market, and every source is
cited in the report itself so that opposing counsel can check the work
rather than take it on faith. The regional framework behind this approach
is described on the [Stockton metro page](/locations/california/stockton/).

## Presence in Lodi, described accurately

The firm's only staffed office is its Kansas City home office. It keeps no
storefront in Lodi and publishes no local street address, a fact stated
here without hedging because an expert practice should be as precise about
itself as it is about an evaluee's earnings history. Meetings and
evaluations in Lodi happen by appointment. Remote conferencing and
publisher-approved remote test administration handle much of the work;
travel from Kansas City is scheduled when in-person evaluation, deposition
testimony, or trial attendance in San Joaquin County requires it, with the
litigation calendar driving the timing. Counsel referring matters from
outside the region will find the same structure applied to
[engagements nationwide](/locations/nationwide/).

## The conflict check comes before everything

No Lodi engagement opens without clearance. Send the caption or party names
through the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/), together with any
disclosure, deposition, or trial dates already scheduled. Once conflicts

delivers a written opinion built to be examined by whichever side did not
retain it.
