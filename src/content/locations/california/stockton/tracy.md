## The labor market a Tracy evaluee faces

Tracy is a substantial city, and its residents work across a wide span of
occupations and employers rather than in any single dominant setting. That
is why the firm does not reduce a Tracy evaluee to a generic statewide
profile. Vocational findings rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational
employment and wage estimates for the Stockton metropolitan area and for
California statewide, on state workforce agency labor market information,
and on direct labor market research where the referral questions warrant it.
Transferable skills analysis then connects the evaluee's actual work history
to occupations that exist in that documented market, so the resulting
opinion can withstand the cross-examination it should expect.

## How the evaluation is conducted

The firm maintains no staffed office in Tracy and publishes no street
address here, and it says so plainly because candor about presence belongs
to the same discipline that governs its reports. Counsel conferences,
evaluee interviews, and standardized testing are handled by appointment.
Much of the work proceeds remotely under publisher protocols where remote
administration is supported, which often serves a Tracy evaluee best. When
the referral questions call for in-person evaluation, or when a deposition
or trial requires presence in San Joaquin County, travel is scheduled from
the Kansas City home office with lead time that protects the litigation
calendar. The same procedures govern
[engagements nationwide](/locations/nationwide/), so counsel in Tracy
receive the identical process a referring attorney anywhere else receives.
