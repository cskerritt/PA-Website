Most of the work in a San Marcos vocational or life care planning engagement
happens in the file long before anyone meets. Medical records, employment and
education histories, deposition transcripts, and prior expert reports shape
the questions an evaluation must answer, and Purinton Analytics front-loads
that review in every San Marcos matter it accepts, for plaintiff or defense.
The visible products, whether a
[vocational expert](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) report, a
[life care plan](/services/life-care-planning/), or a
[medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/), are only as
strong as that foundation, and that order of operations is what keeps the
eventual opinion inside the evidence.

## The county and its courts

San Marcos belongs to San Diego County and to the San Diego-Chula
Vista-Carlsbad, CA metropolitan area. State-court matters are venued in the
Superior Court of San Diego County; federal matters in the United States
District Court for the Southern District of California. The record-first
approach serves both forums, because a report assembled from documented
evidence transfers cleanly between them and holds its shape under
cross-examination. Coverage across the rest of the state is described on the
[California practice page](/locations/california/).

## Labor market evidence for San Marcos evaluees

Once the record defines the vocational questions, the market analysis
answers them with published data: Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational
employment and wage estimates for the San Diego-Chula Vista-Carlsbad
metropolitan area and for California statewide, state workforce agency labor
market information, and direct labor market research when a specific
placement or accommodation question requires it. San Marcos residents compete
in a regional market, and the analysis treats them that way, characterizing
scale and opportunity qualitatively with a source cited for each claim. Where
the record shows restrictions, transferable skills are mapped only to
occupations the published estimates document for the region.

## Remote-first, in person when it matters

The sequence is deliberate: records review and structured interviews proceed
remotely; standardized testing follows under publisher protocols, remotely
where the instrument supports it; in-person evaluation is reserved for the
matters that genuinely need it and is then held in the evaluee's own
community. The firm keeps no office in San Marcos, publishes no local
address, and meets by appointment, with travel, when required for evaluation,
deposition, or trial, scheduled from the Kansas City home office. Engagements
that call for economic quantification add independent economist partners, who
perform and sign the economic analysis within a
[coordinated damages engagement](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/).
