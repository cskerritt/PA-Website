El Cajon's workforce does not stay in El Cajon. Like most cities in the San
Diego-Chula Vista-Carlsbad, CA metropolitan area, it sends workers across the
region every day, which means the earning capacity of an injured El Cajon
resident is a regional question from the outset. Commuting patterns matter to
damages analysis because they define the geography of comparable employment,
and a report that ignores them will misstate capacity in whichever direction
serves nobody's credibility. Purinton Analytics builds its
[vocational expert](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) opinions for El
Cajon matters on that premise, pairing them where needed with
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) and
[medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/), for retention
by either side of the caption.

El Cajon is part of San Diego County. Its civil disputes are filed

## Measuring a commuter's labor market

Because El Cajon workers participate in the metropolitan economy, vocational
findings rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage
estimates for the San Diego-Chula Vista-Carlsbad metropolitan area and for
California statewide, alongside state workforce agency labor market
information. Where a claim turns on access to a particular occupation, direct
employer research supplements the published estimates. The analysis asks what
work the evaluee can still perform, what that work pays in this region, and
how the gap between pre-injury and post-injury capacity should be described
to the finder of fact, always in qualitative terms tied to cited data.

## Practical arrangements for evaluees and counsel

The firm maintains no El Cajon office and publishes no address here; meetings
occur by appointment. An engagement usually opens with remote records review
and interviews, followed by standardized testing under publisher protocols
where remote administration is supported. In-person evaluation, when
warranted, is conducted in the community where the evaluee actually lives,
and travel for evaluations, depositions, and trial is scheduled from the
Kansas City home office. When the damages picture requires an economist,
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
are structured so that independent economist partners perform and sign the
economic analysis while vocational and care-planning work continues under the
same engagement.
