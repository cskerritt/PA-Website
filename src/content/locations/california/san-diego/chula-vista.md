## A metro-wide labor market

An earning capacity opinion for a Chula Vista evaluee cannot stop at the city
limits. Residents work throughout the region, and the relevant market for
employability and wage-loss analysis is the metropolitan one. Vocational
findings therefore rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment
and wage estimates for the San Diego-Chula Vista-Carlsbad metropolitan area
and for California statewide, supplemented by state workforce agency labor
market information and by direct employer contact when the referral questions
call for it. Where a claimant's history spans several occupations,
transferable skills analysis maps prior work demands onto the occupations the
regional data actually supports. Market scale is described qualitatively and
sourced precisely, so that every characterization in the report can be traced
back to published data.
