Catastrophic injury cases involving Encinitas residents tend to arrive with
the same pair of questions: what will lifelong care actually require, and
what happens to the injured person's working life. The answers have to
survive scrutiny from the other side of the caption, so both are built from
the record rather than from advocacy. Purinton Analytics addresses them in a
coordinated way, preparing [life care plans](/services/life-care-planning/)
that document future care needs with a source behind each recommendation,
[medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/) priced to the
evaluee's own market, and
[vocational assessments](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) of residual
earning capacity, for retention by plaintiff or defense counsel in an
Encinitas matter.

## San Diego County venue

Encinitas is a smaller city in San Diego County, part of the San Diego-Chula
Vista-Carlsbad, CA metropolitan area. Its civil cases proceed in the Superior
Court of San Diego County and, where federal jurisdiction exists, in the
United States District Court for the Southern District of California.
Care-planning and vocational opinions are prepared to the same evidentiary
standard in either court: recommendations tied to the medical record, costs
tied to identifiable sources, and testimony that tracks the written plan. The
firm's [California page](/locations/california/) covers venues across the
state for counsel who need them.

## Work capacity in a regional economy

Even in a care-focused case, the vocational side matters, because residual
earning capacity offsets or frames the loss. For Encinitas evaluees that
capacity is measured against the metropolitan labor market using Bureau of
Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the San
Diego-Chula Vista-Carlsbad metropolitan area and for California statewide,
supported by state workforce agency labor market information. The market is
described qualitatively, with citations, and the interaction between care
schedules and work capacity is addressed rather than ignored, since a plan
that assumes both round-the-clock care and an unimpaired work life has not
been thought through.

## Evaluation and care planning logistics

Life care planning for an Encinitas resident starts from the record and from
structured interviews with the evaluee and, where appropriate, family members
and treating providers; much of that proceeds remotely. In-person assessment,
when the referral requires it, is conducted in the evaluee's own community,
which for a catastrophically injured person is not a courtesy but a
necessity. The firm has no Encinitas office and publishes no local address;
meetings are by appointment, with any required travel scheduled from the
Kansas City home office. Where the damages model needs an economist,
independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis through
a [coordinated damages engagement](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/).
