A meaningful share of expert work in La Mesa matters is not the opening
report in the case but the response to one. Counsel arrive with an opposing
expert's vocational evaluation or life care plan and need to know whether its
methods hold up. Purinton Analytics performs that
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) work for La Mesa
cases with the same neutrality it brings to affirmative engagements: the
review tests methodology against professional standards, and if the opposing
report is sound, counsel are told exactly that, in writing, with the basis
for each conclusion spelled out. Affirmative
[vocational expert](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) and
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) engagements are available
on the same terms, for either party.

## Testing market assumptions

Many opposing reports stand or fall on their labor market foundations.
Review of a La Mesa matter checks those foundations against Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the San Diego-Chula
Vista-Carlsbad metropolitan area and for California statewide, and against
state workforce agency labor market information, asking whether the cited
occupations appear in the regional data and whether the wage assumptions
match the published estimates. The firm's own affirmative opinions are built
from those same sources, described qualitatively and cited fully, because the
test applied to others is the test the firm expects to meet.
