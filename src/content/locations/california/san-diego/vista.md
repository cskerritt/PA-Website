## The regional market behind a Vista opinion

Vocational conclusions for Vista evaluees are grounded in Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the San Diego-Chula
Vista-Carlsbad metropolitan area and for California statewide, with state
workforce agency labor market information and direct employer inquiry added
when a question demands it. The report describes market scale and opportunity
in qualitative terms, and each characterization carries a citation an
opposing expert can verify. Transferable skills analysis links the evaluee's
documented history to occupations the regional data shows to exist, not to
hypothetical positions, because hypothetical placements are exactly what
cross-examination is built to expose.
