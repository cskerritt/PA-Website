[vocational expert evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),

in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic

## Venue footprint in San Diego County

The metro's civil caseload runs through the Superior Court of San Diego
County, where the firm accepts matters for either side of the caption.
Federal engagements are accepted throughout California, including the
United States District Court for the Southern District of California
together with the United States District Court for the Central District of
California, the United States District Court for the Northern District of
California, and the United States District Court for the Eastern District
of California. Because the methodology is documented and consistent, an
opinion prepared for a Superior Court matter holds up under the standards
applied in the federal forums as well.

## How remote-first evaluation actually works

Records review, structured vocational interviews, and standardized testing
under publisher protocols where remote administration is supported can all
proceed without travel, which keeps schedules predictable and costs
proportionate. San Diego meetings are by appointment; the firm keeps no
staffed office in the metro and publishes no street address here, a fact
stated plainly because honesty about presence belongs in the same category
as honesty about data. When an in-person evaluation is warranted, or when a
deposition or trial requires attendance, travel is scheduled from the
Kansas City home office around the dates counsel identify at intake.

## Grounding opinions in the San Diego labor market

Vocational findings for San Diego County evaluees rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the San Diego
metropolitan area and for California statewide, supplemented by state
workforce agency labor market information and direct labor market research
where the referral questions call for it. Life care plans and
[medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/) are priced
to the evaluee's own market, not to a national average, and every source
appears in the report so that opposing counsel can trace each element of
the opinion back to its origin. Where the referral requires it,
transferable skills analysis and targeted labor market surveys are
performed and documented with the same traceability as the published
sources.

Once the check clears, counsel receive a written engagement confirmation
and a working schedule keyed to the court's calendar, whether the matter is
pending in San Diego County or in a federal courtroom elsewhere in the
