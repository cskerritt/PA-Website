Purinton Analytics provides vocational expert and life care planning services
to attorneys litigating in the Oxnard-Thousand Oaks-Ventura metro area.
Referrals from plaintiff and defense counsel alike typically involve
[earning capacity and employability evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/)
in personal injury and employment matters,
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) in catastrophic injury
cases, and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. Every matter begins with a documented conflict check
and a defined scope, so the written opinion rests on a record either side of
the caption can examine.

## Venue footprint in Ventura County

Engagements are accepted for matters venued in the Superior Court in Ventura
County, the state trial court serving Oxnard, Thousand Oaks, Ventura, and the
surrounding communities. For cases proceeding in federal court, the firm
accepts work throughout California's federal trial courts: the United States
District Court for the Northern District of California, the United States
District Court for the Eastern District of California, the United States
District Court for the Central District of California, and the United States
District Court for the Southern District of California. Venue does not change
the method. The analysis is built the same way regardless of forum, and
counsel with matters elsewhere in the state can start from the
[California locations page](/locations/california/).

## Meetings by appointment

There is no staffed Purinton Analytics office in Oxnard, and the firm
publishes no street address for this location, because accuracy about
presence is part of the same discipline that governs its reports. Meetings
with counsel and evaluees are scheduled by appointment. Most engagements
begin remotely, with records review, structured interviews, and standardized
testing administered under publisher protocols where remote administration
is supported. When the referral questions call for in-person evaluation, or
when deposition and trial calendars require presence, travel is arranged
from the Kansas City home office and planned around the case schedule rather
than the other way around.

## Labor market research for Ventura County evaluees

Vocational findings for evaluees in the metro rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Oxnard
metropolitan area and for California statewide, supplemented by state
workforce agency labor market information and direct labor market research
where the referral questions warrant it. Life care plans and medical cost
projections are priced to the evaluee's own market using provider-specific
inquiries and published fee data for the region, not national averages
applied by default. Opposing counsel should be able to trace every data
source in the report back to its origin, and the firm writes with that
reader in mind. The same standards apply to
[national engagements](/locations/nationwide/), so a Ventura County report
reads the same as one prepared for any other venue.

## Starting an engagement

Retention begins with a conflict check, not a sales call. Submit the caption
or party names through the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and note
any imminent disclosure, deposition, or trial dates so scheduling can be
confirmed alongside clearance. Once conflicts are cleared, counsel receive a
defined scope, a records request tailored to the claims, and a single point
of contact for the life of the engagement, whether the matter resolves
before disclosure or proceeds through testimony.
