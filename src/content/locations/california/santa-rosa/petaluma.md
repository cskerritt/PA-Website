Petaluma is written into the region's own title: the metropolitan area is named Santa Rosa-Petaluma, and that shared billing reflects the city's standing as a substantial employment center in its own right rather than a bedroom community of the principal city. For counsel, that standing matters, because an earning capacity opinion about a Petaluma plaintiff or defendant cannot be a generic metro opinion with the city's name substituted in. Purinton Analytics provides [vocational expert evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/), [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement structure. Plaintiff and defense referrals are handled with the same method and the same skepticism.

## Sonoma County venue, from filing to disclosure

State court matters arising in Petaluma proceed in the Superior Court of Sonoma County, and its case management orders supply the deadlines the expert work is built around. Federal engagements are accepted throughout the state, including matters in the United States District Court for the Northern District of California, where scheduling orders and expert disclosure requirements play the same organizing role. Venue context for the wider region appears on the [Santa Rosa metro page](/locations/california/santa-rosa/), and matters filed elsewhere in the state are covered through the firm's [California practice](/locations/california/).

## Reading the Petaluma labor market honestly

Because Petaluma is a named anchor of the metropolitan area, its residents' employment prospects are assessed against the metro market as a whole, not against city limits. Vocational findings rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Santa Rosa-Petaluma metropolitan area and for California statewide, supplemented by state workforce agency labor market information and, where the referral questions warrant it, direct research with employers. Every source is identified in the report by name and coverage, so that opposing counsel can trace each finding to the series it came from. Transferable skills analysis follows the same rule: the occupations considered are those the record supports, matched against demand in the metro market, and the report explains why each occupation was included or set aside.

## How the evaluation gets done

The firm keeps no office in Petaluma and does not pretend otherwise; meetings are by appointment, and candor about presence is treated as part of the same discipline that governs the opinions. Records review, structured vocational interviews, and standardized testing under publisher protocols proceed remotely in most engagements, which shortens the interval between retention and a working analysis. When in-person evaluation is called for, it is conducted in the evaluee's own community, and [medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/) price future care against sources the report cites. Deposition and trial appearances are scheduled as travel from the Kansas City home office.

## Opening the file

An engagement begins when the conflict check clears, so the caption and party names are the useful starting materials. Counsel can [request a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) and note any disclosure, deposition, or trial dates already on calendar. Petaluma matters then follow the same sequence applied across the firm's [national practice](/locations/nationwide/): a defined scope, a records request tailored to the claims, and a written opinion prepared on the assumption that the other side will read it as closely as the retaining side does.
