What counsel in a Sebastopol matter ultimately buys from an expert is a document: a report that will be produced in discovery, marked at deposition, and possibly read aloud at trial. Purinton Analytics treats the deliverable as the point of the engagement, not a formality at the end of it. Each [vocational expert report](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) and [life care plan](/services/life-care-planning/) is built from a defined scope and a tailored records request, states its methodology in full, and cites every data source it depends on. The same document standard supports [rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) of opposing expert work, and it is offered to plaintiff and defense retention alike, since a report worth signing reads the same no matter who commissioned it.

## Jurisdiction over Sebastopol matters

Sebastopol's state court litigation belongs to the Superior Court of Sonoma County, whose orders determine when the report must exist and in what form. Federally, the firm accepts California engagements including matters in the United States District Court for the Northern District of California. The wider region's court and labor market structure is described on the [Santa Rosa metro page](/locations/california/santa-rosa/); engagements arising elsewhere in the state fall under the firm's [California coverage](/locations/california/).

## Labor market sourcing, named on the page

Behind every employability conclusion for a Sebastopol evaluee sits a documented data trail: Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Santa Rosa-Petaluma metropolitan area and for California statewide, state workforce agency labor market information, and, where a contested placement question warrants it, direct labor market research recorded in the working file. The report identifies which series supports which finding and where each one's coverage stops, because a source that cannot be checked is not evidence, it is decoration. The working file is indexed to match, so a production request for the materials behind the report can be met promptly and without surprises.

## Meeting the evaluee where they are

Sebastopol has no Purinton Analytics office, and the firm considers saying so a feature of the practice rather than an admission: reports about facts should come from a firm that is factual about itself. Meetings run by appointment. The remote-first sequence puts records analysis, structured interviews, and standardized testing under publisher protocols at the front of the calendar, and when in-person assessment is warranted it takes place in the evaluee's own community, with [medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/) priced to that community from cited sources. For [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/), independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement structure. When testimony requires presence, travel runs from the Kansas City home office.

From there a Sebastopol engagement follows the same documented sequence the firm applies to its [national practice](/locations/nationwide/), ending in a deliverable both sides can interrogate line by line.
