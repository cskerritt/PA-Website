## Labor market evidence without shortcuts

A Healdsburg evaluee's employability is measured against the regional market that a realistic job search would cover, and the measurement has to survive cross-examination. That is why the sourcing is conservative: Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Santa Rosa-Petaluma metropolitan area and for California statewide, state workforce agency labor market information, and, when a specific placement question demands it, direct contact with employers documented in the file. The report states what each source covers and what it does not, which is faster to write honestly than to defend later if written loosely.
