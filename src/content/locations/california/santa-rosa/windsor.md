Windsor is a Sonoma County town, and Sonoma County's trial courts hear its state law disputes. ## Windsor workers in a regional market

When a Windsor resident's earning capacity is at issue, the relevant market is regional, since work opportunities cross town boundaries freely. The firm's opinions draw on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Santa Rosa-Petaluma metropolitan area and for California statewide, on state workforce agency labor market information, and on direct labor market research where the questions call for verification against actual employers. Numbers never appear in the report without a named source behind them, and the methodology section explains what each series measures and where its coverage ends. Where restrictions limit an evaluee to accommodated or reduced-schedule work, the analysis says so directly and traces the effect on placement prospects through those same named sources, rather than folding the judgment into an unexplained adjustment.

## By-appointment logistics for Windsor evaluees

Purinton Analytics maintains no Windsor office and publishes no local address, because claiming a storefront that does not exist would sit poorly with a practice whose product is accuracy. What counsel gets instead is a by-appointment model: intake, records analysis, structured interviewing, and standardized testing conducted remotely under controlled conditions, with in-person evaluation arranged in the evaluee's own community when the referral requires it. In [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/), independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement structure. Appearance obligations, from deposition through trial, are met by travel scheduled from the Kansas City home office.

## Requesting a conflict check

A Windsor engagement opens with clearance, not with conclusions. Counsel should [request a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) with the case caption and parties, and include the trial setting or expert disclosure deadline if the court has issued one. After clearance, the matter proceeds under the same engagement discipline the firm applies to its [national caseload](/locations/nationwide/): defined scope, a records request matched to the claims, and a written opinion drafted to withstand the reader who wants it to fail.
