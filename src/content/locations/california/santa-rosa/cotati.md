## Grounding opinions in metro labor data

A defensible employability opinion for a Cotati evaluee names its evidence. The practice relies on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Santa Rosa-Petaluma metropolitan area and for California statewide, on state workforce agency labor market information, and on direct employer research when the referral questions require confirmation that published categories match hiring reality. Cross-examination tends to concentrate on gaps between data and conclusion, so the report closes those gaps in writing: what each series covers, why it applies to this evaluee, and where professional judgment enters and on what basis. Where the record will not support a conclusion, the report says that too, because an acknowledged limit holds up better under questioning than a claim stretched past its evidence.
