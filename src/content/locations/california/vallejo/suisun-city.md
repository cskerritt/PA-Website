District Court for the Eastern District of California or the United States
District Court for the Northern District of California, depending on where
the action is pending, and the firm's reporting format satisfies the
disclosure expectations of state and federal practice alike. Any
venue-specific registration or qualification requirement is identified and

## A small city inside a regional employment market

Suisun City is a small city whose workers participate in the wider Vallejo
metropolitan labor market. That regional reality shapes the analysis: the
firm evaluates what work the evaluee can obtain and hold across the market
actually open to them, using Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational
employment and wage estimates for the Vallejo metropolitan area and for
California statewide, state workforce agency labor market information, and
targeted employer research where the referral calls for it. Every
employability finding names its source. Nothing in the opinion asks the
reader to take the expert's word for anything.

## How the evaluation itself works

Remote-first is the default. Interviews, records conferences, and much of
the standardized testing can be completed by video, which spares a Suisun
City evaluee unnecessary travel and keeps the schedule inside counsel's
deadlines. Where the referral questions require face-to-face assessment,
the firm evaluates in the evaluee's own community, and the examiner travels
from the Kansas City home office, the firm's only staffed location. There
is no Suisun City office and no local address to publish; what counsel get
instead is a defined scope, a predictable timeline, and a report written to
withstand the scrutiny of the lawyer on the other side. When the damages
case also needs economic loss modeling, the firm arranges
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.
