## Venue for Rio Vista disputes

State court matters from Rio Vista are heard in the Superior Court in
Solano County across the injury, employment, probate, and family dockets
where earning capacity and future care questions surface. Cases in
federal court proceed in the United States District Court for the Eastern
District of California or the United States District Court for the
Northern District of California, depending on where the action is
pending. The firm accepts referrals from plaintiff and defense counsel
alike, and any venue-specific registration or qualification requirement
is identified and confirmed during the conflict check and intake
process.

## The labor market question, asked precisely

The analytical work for a Rio Vista evaluee begins by defining the market
the evaluee can actually access given restrictions, skills, work history,
and the realities of a small town within the Vallejo metropolitan area.
Findings draw on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and
wage estimates for the Vallejo metropolitan area and for California
statewide, on state workforce agency labor market information, and on
direct labor market research where a referral turns on specific
occupations or employers. Where the honest answer is that suitable work
requires commuting toward the metro's employment centers, the report says
so and traces the consequence for employability; where it is not, the
report says that instead. Care-plan pricing follows the evaluee's own
community and the services genuinely available to it.

## Reaching a Rio Vista evaluee

Distance is managed, not hidden. The firm's only staffed office is the
Kansas City home office; Rio Vista is served by appointment, with
interviews, records work, and preparation sessions conducted by video and
in-person evaluation carried out in the evaluee's own community when the
referral requires it. Travel for evaluations and for deposition or trial
testimony is planned from Kansas City around the dates confirmed at
intake, and matters needing economic loss analysis can proceed as
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.
