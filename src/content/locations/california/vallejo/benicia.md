Expert deadlines decide as much in Benicia litigation as expert opinions
do. A vocational or care-planning report that lands after the disclosure
cutoff, or that leaves no room for a rebuttal round, costs counsel leverage
no matter how sound its reasoning. Purinton Analytics therefore treats the
disclosure calendar as part of the engagement itself: dates are collected
at intake, the work plan is mapped to them in writing, and counsel in this
small Solano County city know from the outset when the
[vocational expert witness](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) report,
the [life care plan](/services/life-care-planning/), or the
[rebuttal review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) will be ready.

## Solano County venue, state and federal

Benicia earning capacity and future care disputes belong to the Superior
Court in Solano County when filed in state court. A federally filed or
removed matter proceeds in the United States District Court for the Eastern
District of California or the United States District Court for the Northern
District of California, depending on where the action is pending; the
firm's opinions are drafted to meet the written-report expectations of
federal practice and the disclosure customs of state practice without
reworking. Plaintiff and defense referrals move through the same intake,
the same conflict screening, and the same reporting standard, which is
what allows an opinion to be useful to whichever side commissioned it.

## Reading the labor market around Benicia

Benicia is a small city within the Vallejo metropolitan area, and its
residents work across that regional market. Employability and earning
capacity findings are grounded in Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational
employment and wage estimates for the Vallejo metropolitan area and for
California statewide, in state workforce agency labor market information,
and in direct labor market inquiry when a referral turns on a specific
occupation or employer practice. The analysis is qualitative where the
data are qualitative and cited wherever a fact is asserted, so that a
deposition about sources becomes a confirmation exercise rather than an
ambush.

## Appointments, not an office

There is no Purinton Analytics office in Benicia, and the firm does not
suggest otherwise. Work is by appointment: video for scoping calls,
records conferences, testimony preparation, and much of the evaluation
protocol, and in-person sessions in the evaluee's own community when the
referral questions require them. Travel for evaluations, depositions, and
trial appearances is scheduled from the Kansas City home office and is
booked against the same calendar the engagement was scoped to, so a
deposition set on short notice does not catch the file unprepared. Where
counsel need only a targeted review of an opposing expert's report, the
same calendar discipline applies to the rebuttal timeline, with the
review scoped to the disclosures already served.
