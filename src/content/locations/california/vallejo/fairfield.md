## Solano County courts and the federal alternative

Fairfield civil matters are heard in the Superior Court in Solano County,

earning capacity disputes. The firm's reports follow the same structure
regardless of department or case type: a documented conflict check, a defined
scope, and a source cited for every conclusion, so the written opinion rests
on a record either lawyer can test. When a Fairfield case is removed or filed
