A Vacaville evaluee rarely works only in Vacaville. Residents of this large
Solano County city commute across the Vallejo metropolitan area and beyond
it, which means the first analytical question in a Vacaville earning
capacity dispute is not what jobs exist inside the city limits but what
labor market the evaluee can realistically reach after the injury or event
at issue. Purinton Analytics builds its
[vocational expert witness](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) opinions
around that question, and it accepts Vacaville referrals from plaintiff and
defense counsel on identical terms.

personal injury, employment, or dissolution. Federal filings proceed in the
United States District Court for the Eastern District of California or the
United States District Court for the Northern District of California,
according to where the action is pending. Forum never changes the method:

## Measuring a commuting market honestly

Because Vacaville workers move throughout the region, the firm's analysis
does not stop at a single set of local statistics. Employability and wage
findings draw on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and
wage estimates covering the Vallejo metropolitan area and California as a
whole, supplemented by state workforce agency labor market information and,
where the referral requires it, direct contact with employers in the
occupations at issue. The resulting report states which commuting radius
the evaluee's restrictions, transferable skills, and history support, and
it shows the sourcing behind that judgment line by line. A
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) engagement follows the
parallel rule: future care is priced in the community where the evaluee
actually lives and receives services.
