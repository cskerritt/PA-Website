Purinton Analytics has no office in Dixon and does not pretend to. That
admission opens this page because it is the honest version of what every
expert practice should tell a small Solano County town: the value offered
here is not a storefront but a method. Dixon counsel retaining the firm for
[vocational expert witness](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) work,
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), or
[medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/) get a
documented conflict check, a defined scope, a records request tailored to
the claims, and a written opinion built to be read by the opposing side,
whichever side that happens to be.

## County and courthouse context for Dixon matters

Civil disputes arising in Dixon are heard in the Superior Court in Solano
County, and administrative claims involving work capacity follow their own
forums with the same underlying vocational questions. Federal matters
proceed in the United States District Court for the Eastern District of
California or the United States District Court for the Northern District
of California, depending on where the action is pending, and the firm
supports engagements throughout California's federal districts, including
the United States District Court for the Central District of California
and the United States District Court for the Southern District of
California when a client's litigation reaches those courts. Method,
sourcing, and report format stay constant across all of them.

## A small town, measured against a real market

Dixon is a small town at the edge of the Vallejo metropolitan area's
economy, and a credible opinion has to respect that scale rather than
paper over it. The firm's employability and earning capacity analyses use
Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for
the Vallejo metropolitan area and for California statewide, together with
state workforce agency labor market information and direct employer
research when the referral demands it. The report says, with citations,
what work is realistically available to this evaluee from this community,
which is a different and more defensible statement than a metro-wide
generalization.

## Serving Dixon from a distance, deliberately

By-appointment service is a design choice, not a limitation. Scoping
calls, records conferences, testing where protocols permit, and
deposition preparation all run by video, which keeps costs proportionate
for a small-town case. In-person vocational evaluation, when the referral
requires it, happens in the evaluee's own community; presence at
depositions, hearings, and trial is arranged by travel from the Kansas
City home office, planned around the dates counsel identify at intake.
For matters that need economic loss analysis alongside the vocational and
care-planning work, the firm structures
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.
