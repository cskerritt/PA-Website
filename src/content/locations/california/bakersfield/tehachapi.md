## Care Planning for a Tehachapi Evaluee

A defensible life care plan is a sourcing exercise before it is a document. Each recommended item traces to the record or to a named clinical foundation; each cost traces to a database, a published schedule, or a documented local quote; and the plan distinguishes what is medically probable from what is merely possible. Because a care plan is frequently the largest single component of claimed damages, it draws the sharpest scrutiny, and the firm writes every plan for a Tehachapi case as though the opposing expert's critique were already on the desk. The same rigor applies in reverse when the firm is retained to review a plan served by the other side.
