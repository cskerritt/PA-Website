## Small Community, Full Methodology

What a small community changes is not the method but the fieldwork. Employment alternatives for a McFarland evaluee must be documented rather than presumed, which is why the firm's analyses combine Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Bakersfield-Delano, CA metropolitan area and for California statewide with state workforce agency labor market information and, where the questions are specific, direct labor market research. A report never asserts that suitable work exists nearby without showing the basis for saying so, and it is equally careful in the other direction: an opinion that no work exists gets the same evidentiary support. Both sides of the caption benefit from that symmetry.
