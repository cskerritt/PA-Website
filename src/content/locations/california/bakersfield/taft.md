Not every Taft engagement begins at the start of a case. Many begin when the other side's expert report arrives and counsel needs to know, quickly and candidly, whether it holds up: whether the vocational conclusions follow from the testing, whether the life care plan's items are clinically founded, whether the claimed labor market actually exists as described. Purinton Analytics performs that [rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) work for Taft matters with the same neutrality it brings to affirmative opinions, and it provides those affirmative opinions too, through [vocational expert witness](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) and [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) engagements for either side, as part of a [practice that covers the country](/locations/nationwide/).

## Reviewing the Opposing Report

A useful rebuttal is not a louder opinion; it is an audit. The firm's reviews for Taft cases test the opposing expert's methodology against its own stated sources, check whether instruments were used as designed, and separate legitimate professional disagreement from demonstrable error. When the opposing report is sound, counsel is told so directly, because a review that only ever finds fault is worth nothing at deposition. That candor is the same trait that makes the firm's affirmative reports durable when the roles are reversed.

## Venue: The Superior Court and the Federal Districts

Taft's civil matters in state court are heard in the Superior Court, Kern County's trial court. Federal cases involving Taft parties proceed in California's district courts, among them the United States District Court for the Eastern District of California and the United States District Court for the Northern District of California. Reports are drafted to satisfy the disclosure requirements of the forum, and intake confirms any venue-specific qualification point before the engagement is scoped, never after.

## The Labor Market a Taft Evaluee Faces

Employment and earning conclusions about a Taft evaluee are documented from the regional market outward. The firm draws on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Bakersfield-Delano, CA metropolitan area and for California statewide, on state workforce agency labor market information, and on direct employer research where a placement or wage question needs first-hand confirmation. In a community of Taft's modest scale, the difference between a documented market and an assumed one can be the difference between an opinion that stands and one that is excluded, and the firm's reports are built accordingly.
