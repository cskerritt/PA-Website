Reports for California City matters are written on the assumption that the most attentive reader will be the lawyer on the other side. That assumption governs everything: opinions are stated with their bases, records are listed so nothing appears to have been overlooked, and limitations are acknowledged before a deposition forces the acknowledgment. Purinton Analytics offers that standard of work to attorneys litigating California City cases from either chair, through [vocational expert witness](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) engagements, [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and [expert testimony and litigation consulting](/services/expert-testimony-litigation-consulting/), as one venue among many in a [nationwide practice](/locations/nationwide/).

## Built for Cross-Examination

A vocational or care-planning opinion earns its keep at deposition. The firm's California City reports are therefore constructed so that every step can be walked backward: from conclusion to analysis, from analysis to data, from data to source. Instruments are administered under documented conditions, interview content is recorded in the file, and no draft language is traded with retaining counsel for advocacy purposes. Retaining attorneys sometimes find this discipline strict; opposing attorneys find it difficult to attack, which is the point.

## Kern County and Federal Venue

California City's state-court litigation proceeds in the Superior Court serving Kern County. Matters in federal court are venued among California's districts, which include the United States District Court for the Eastern District of California and the United States District Court for the Southern District of California, and the practice supports counsel in any of them, appearing for deposition and trial in person or remotely as the court permits. Any registration or qualification question particular to a venue is resolved during intake, before it can become an issue in the case.

## The Labor Market Behind the Opinion

For a California City evaluee, employment and earning conclusions rest on data for the markets the evaluee can genuinely reach, examined at both the metropolitan and statewide levels. The firm works from Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Bakersfield-Delano, CA metropolitan area and for California, from state workforce agency labor market information, and from direct employer inquiry where a referral question requires primary evidence.
