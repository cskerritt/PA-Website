## How a Ridgecrest Evaluee Is Evaluated

The default is remote-first. Records review, structured vocational interviews conducted by video, standardized instruments administered under appropriate conditions, and written reporting all proceed without anyone leaving town. That matters for evaluees whose injuries, work schedules, or caregiving obligations make travel a burden. When the referral questions require an in-person evaluation, the firm comes to the evaluee's community rather than summoning the evaluee to an office; travel is arranged from the Kansas City home office and scheduled around depositions and disclosure dates. The firm keeps no staffed office in Ridgecrest and publishes no street address here, and it says so plainly, because candor about logistics is part of the credibility of the final report.

## Venue: Kern County and the Federal Courts

Ridgecrest sits within Kern County, and state-court matters are heard in the Superior Court. Federal matters follow their own venue rules; among the districts in which California cases proceed are the United States District Court for the Eastern District of California and the United States District Court for the Southern District of California, and the firm prepares its opinions to the same documentary standard whichever court will receive them. Opinions are written to be examined: methodology stated, sources cited, and limits acknowledged, so that cross-examination tests the analysis rather than uncovers it.

## Labor Market Analysis for Ridgecrest Cases

Earning capacity conclusions for a Ridgecrest evaluee rest on published data for the markets that evaluee can actually access. The firm uses Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates covering the Bakersfield-Delano, CA metropolitan area and California statewide, together with state workforce agency labor market information, and it supplements those sources with direct labor market research when a specific occupation or employer question needs a specific answer. The analysis is qualitative about the community and rigorous about the data: no figure appears in a report without a source that opposing counsel can check.
