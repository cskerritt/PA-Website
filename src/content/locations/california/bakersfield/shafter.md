A matter filed in the Superior Court serving Kern County moves under California procedure and state disclosure practice; a matter removed to or filed in federal court moves under federal expert rules, with a written report whose completeness is tested at deposition. Purinton Analytics prepares its Shafter work for either path from the outset, offering [vocational expert witness](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) services, [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and [expert testimony and litigation consulting](/services/expert-testimony-litigation-consulting/) to counsel on both sides of the caption, within the [nationwide practice](/locations/nationwide/).

## State and Federal Venues for Shafter Litigation

Shafter's state-court matters are heard in the Superior Court in Kern County. On the federal side, California litigation is distributed among district courts that include the United States District Court for the Eastern District of California and the United States District Court for the Central District of California, and the practice appears in whichever forum the case occupies. Any venue-specific registration or qualification requirement is identified and confirmed during the conflict check and intake process, so that a procedural detail never surfaces for the first time at deposition. The opinion itself is venue-proof by design: methodology stated, records enumerated, sources cited.

## Reading the Labor Market from Shafter

An earning capacity opinion about a Shafter evaluee has to describe the market that evaluee can actually enter, and for most Shafter residents that market is regional. The analysis is built on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Bakersfield-Delano, CA metropolitan area and for California as a whole, alongside state workforce agency labor market information and, when a referral question is narrow enough to demand it, direct contact with employers. The written report presents the data with citations; these pages describe the market only in qualitative terms, which is the appropriate register for a landing page and the wrong one for sworn testimony.

## Opening a Shafter File

The engagement sequence is deliberately plain. Counsel for either party sends the case caption and party names through the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/), flagging disclosure, deposition, or trial dates that constrain the schedule. After clearance, the firm issues a defined scope and a records request tailored to the claims. Where the case also needs economic analysis, [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) place that work with independent economist partners, who perform and sign the economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning components proceed under one engagement structure.
