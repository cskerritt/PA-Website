## Vocational analysis alongside the care plan

Many Antioch cases pair the care-planning question with an earning-capacity question, and the firm handles both under one roof through its [vocational expert witness](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) practice. Employability and wage-loss findings draw on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the San Francisco-Oakland-Fremont, CA metropolitan area and for California statewide, together with state workforce agency labor market information. The metro offers one of the broadest labor markets in the country, but breadth in the aggregate is not access for the individual: the analysis examines what work is genuinely within reach for this evaluee given restrictions, skills, and circumstances, and says so explicitly.
