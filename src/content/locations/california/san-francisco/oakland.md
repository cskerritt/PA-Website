The first analytical decision in an earning-capacity opinion is which labor market to measure, and for an Oakland evaluee Purinton Analytics makes that decision on the record rather than by default. The firm serves counsel on Oakland matters with [vocational expert witness](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) opinions and [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) prepared to a single standard: the report names the market it measures, explains why that market fits the evaluee's documented restrictions and work history, and lets opposing counsel trace each conclusion back to the record that supports it. Plaintiff and defense engagements are accepted on the same terms, and the same methodology applies regardless of which side issues the retention letter.

## Reading the Oakland labor market

Oakland is a major city inside one of the largest labor markets in the country, and that scale is exactly why the market-definition question cannot be waved away: the larger the market, the more room a careless opinion has to define it in whichever way suits the party paying for it. Earning-capacity opinions for Oakland evaluees rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the San Francisco-Oakland-Fremont, CA metropolitan area and for California statewide, supplemented by state workforce agency labor market information and direct employer research where the referral questions warrant it. The written opinion then does the narrowing work openly: it addresses both the occupations the evaluee can still perform and whether those occupations actually exist in the market the report has defined, rather than assuming placement in the abstract or borrowing whichever geography flatters the retaining side.
