Not every Berkeley engagement begins with a blank page; many begin with the other side's expert report already on the table. Purinton Analytics is retained for both kinds of work. When the assignment is an original opinion, the firm's [vocational expert](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) reports name their methodology, cite their data sources, and separate assumptions from findings. When the assignment is [rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/), the firm applies the same scrutiny to another expert's report that it invites on its own: whether the stated method was actually followed, whether the cited data supports the conclusions drawn, and whether the opinion stays within the author's discipline. Plaintiff and defense counsel retain the firm for either task on identical terms.

## Where Berkeley cases are heard

Berkeley sits in Alameda County, and its state-court civil matters proceed in the Superior Court of Alameda County. Cases resting on federal jurisdiction are typically heard in the United States District Court for the Northern District of California. Because a defensible opinion must hold up under the evidentiary standards of either forum, the firm's reports are built on a documented record from the outset, and any venue-specific registration or qualification requirement is identified and confirmed during the conflict check and intake process. Statewide coverage is outlined on the [California locations page](/locations/california/).

## Data behind the vocational opinion

Vocational and earning-capacity findings for Berkeley evaluees rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates from the OEWS program for the San Francisco-Oakland-Fremont, CA metropolitan area and for California statewide, joined with state workforce agency labor market information and, where the referral questions justify it, direct labor market research. Berkeley is a substantial city inside one of the nation's largest metropolitan labor markets, and the analysis treats that scale as something to be examined, not assumed: an opinion about what work an evaluee can obtain must account for the evaluee's actual restrictions, credentials, and competitive position, and the report shows that accounting. The same tests are applied in reverse when the firm reviews an opposing report that has skipped them.

## Life care planning and coordinated damages

For catastrophic injury matters the firm prepares [life care plans](/services/life-care-planning/) that price future care from documented clinical foundations, and it structures [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement structure. Each professional signs only within their own discipline, which keeps the resulting opinions clean on cross-examination.
