## Transferable skills in a diversified market

The core vocational question in a Fremont case is usually transferability: which of the evaluee's demonstrated skills carry into work that remains within restrictions. Answering it responsibly requires market data, not intuition. The firm grounds its findings in Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates from the OEWS program for the San Francisco-Oakland-Fremont, CA metropolitan area and for California as a whole, alongside state workforce agency labor market information. Because the metro labor market is unusually broad, the analysis is explicit about which occupations are realistic for the specific evaluee rather than merely present somewhere in the region.
