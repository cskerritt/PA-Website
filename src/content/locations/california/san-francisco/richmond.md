Counsel evaluating an expert for a Richmond case usually wants to know one thing early: whether the opinions shift depending on who signs the retainer. At Purinton Analytics they do not. The firm is retained by plaintiff and defense counsel in comparable measure, and its Richmond work product, whether a [vocational expert](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) report or a [life care plan](/services/life-care-planning/), is built from the same records-first process either way. An opinion that would change with the client is not an opinion the firm will write, and that constraint is the practice's principal asset when the report reaches cross-examination.

## What balance looks like in the analysis

Even-handedness is a method, not a slogan. In a Richmond earning-capacity analysis it means the report tests the hypotheses of both sides: whether residual capacity is being overstated and whether it is being understated, against the same data. Those data are the Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the San Francisco-Oakland-Fremont, CA metropolitan area and for California statewide, supplemented by state workforce agency labor market information and direct market research when the referral warrants it. Richmond sits within one of the largest metropolitan labor markets in the country, and the report addresses what that scale realistically means for the specific evaluee rather than letting the size of the market argue either side's case by itself.
