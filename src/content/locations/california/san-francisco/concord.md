Expert work in a Concord case is governed by the calendar before anything else: the disclosure deadline, the discovery cutoff, the deposition window, the trial setting. Purinton Analytics builds every Concord engagement backward from those dates. At intake the firm asks for the operative scheduling order, commits in writing to a delivery date that leaves counsel room to review the report before disclosure, and structures the [vocational expert](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) or [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) work so that no opinion is rushed into the record. The same calendar discipline applies whether the retention comes from the plaintiff or the defense.

## Contra Costa County courts

Concord is a large city in Contra Costa County. State-court matters arising here proceed in the Superior Court of Contra Costa County, while federal claims and diversity cases are generally heard in the United States District Court for the Northern District of California. Reports are prepared with both forums in mind, stating the referral question, the records reviewed, and the basis for each opinion in a form that survives scrutiny in either system. Coverage for venues elsewhere in the state is described on the [California locations page](/locations/california/).

## Scheduling the work itself

Records review and analysis proceed remotely, which keeps the timeline short even when the deadline is close. If the referral questions require an in-person vocational evaluation, the interview is set in Concord or wherever the evaluee lives, and testimony dates are protected early: deposition and trial appearances are scheduled as travel from the Kansas City home office as soon as the dates firm up. The firm has no staffed office in Concord and publishes no street address for this location, a fact stated plainly because accurate representations about presence belong to the same discipline as accurate opinions.

## Labor market analysis for Concord evaluees

Earning-capacity and employability findings for Concord evaluees are grounded in Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the San Francisco-Oakland-Fremont, CA metropolitan area and for California statewide, along with state workforce agency labor market information. The metro is among the largest labor markets in the country, and the analysis is careful about what that means for a specific evaluee: opinions address the occupations genuinely open given restrictions, skills, and the realities of the market a Concord resident can actually reach, not a hypothetical worker anywhere in the region. Broader metro context appears on the [San Francisco metro page](/locations/california/san-francisco/).

## When damages need more than one discipline

Cases that pair vocational loss with economic projections are handled through [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement structure. This keeps the disclosure calendar manageable for counsel, since one coordinated schedule governs the related opinions.
