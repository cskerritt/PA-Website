## What an evaluation involves

A typical engagement opens with a documented conflict check and a defined referral question, followed by a records request tailored to the claims. The evaluee interview covers work history, education, transferable skills, and the functional picture established by the medical record. Testing, when indicated, is selected for the referral question rather than administered by habit. The finished report states its methodology plainly enough that opposing counsel can probe every step, which is the standard the firm considers non-negotiable for both plaintiff and defense work.
