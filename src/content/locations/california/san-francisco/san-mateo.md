Damages cases out of San Mateo often arrive with more than one expert question attached: what work the injured person can still do, what future care will be required, and what the combined loss is worth. Purinton Analytics is structured for exactly that shape of case. Through [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/), independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement structure, giving San Mateo counsel a single coordination point without any expert straying outside their own discipline. Plaintiff and defense retention are welcome on identical terms.

## San Mateo County courts

The city of San Mateo shares its name with its county, and civil matters arising here proceed in the Superior Court of San Mateo County. Cases invoking federal jurisdiction are generally heard in the United States District Court for the Northern District of California. Reports are drafted to satisfy the record and methodology expectations of either forum, and coverage of the state's other venues is collected on the [California locations page](/locations/california/).

## The vocational and care-planning components

The [vocational expert witness](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) opinion addresses employability and earning capacity from documented work history, transferable skills, and the functional limits established in the medical record. The [life care plan](/services/life-care-planning/), where the injury requires one, translates clinical recommendations into itemized future care with a traceable source for every element. In a coordinated engagement these components are sequenced so the economist partners receive settled inputs, which shortens the overall timeline and keeps the opinions consistent with one another at deposition.

## Labor market grounding

San Mateo is a substantial city within one of the largest metropolitan labor markets in the country, and vocational conclusions here are anchored accordingly: Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates from the OEWS program for the San Francisco-Oakland-Fremont, CA metropolitan area and for California statewide, state workforce agency labor market information, and direct labor market inquiry where the referral questions call for it. The report ties market evidence to the individual evaluee's restrictions and credentials instead of resting on the general prosperity of the region.
