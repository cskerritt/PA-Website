## Venue and county context

Carpinteria is part of Santa Barbara County, and its state-court matters are
heard in the Superior Court. The firm supports counsel there in injury,
employment, family, and disability litigation, and it accepts federal matters
statewide, including in the United States District Court for the Central
District of California and the United States District Court for the Southern
District of California. Disclosure practice differs between state and federal
forums, which is precisely why intake asks for the operative dates before
anything else: the work plan for a Superior Court matter with a distant trial
date looks different from a federal case with staged expert reports, and both
are planned backward from the deadline in writing.

## Labor market evidence for a Carpinteria case

Carpinteria is a small city within the larger
[Santa Maria-Santa Barbara metro](/locations/california/santa-maria/), and
sound earning capacity analysis reflects that scale honestly. The employment
base within the town itself is limited, so opinions about what a Carpinteria
evaluee can earn are grounded in Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational
employment and wage estimates for the metropolitan area and for California
statewide, along with state workforce agency labor market information. Where
the dispute turns on the availability of particular work for a particular set
of restrictions, direct labor market research is added and documented. The
report distinguishes clearly between published estimates and firm-conducted
research, so opposing counsel can see exactly where each figure in the
analysis came from.
