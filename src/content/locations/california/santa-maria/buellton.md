What counsel in a Buellton case usually needs is not a single opinion but a
coherent set of them: what work the injured person can still perform, what
their future care will require, and what the whole loss is worth. Purinton
Analytics structures its Buellton engagements around that full arc. The firm
provides [vocational evaluation and expert testimony](/services/vocational-expert-witness/)
and [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) directly, and through
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
it brings in independent economist partners who perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under a single
engagement structure. Plaintiff and defense referrals are welcome on
identical terms, which is easier to say than to practice; the proof is in
reports written so either side could have commissioned them.

## Courts serving Buellton litigants

Buellton is part of Santa Barbara County, and its state-court disputes are
heard in the Superior Court. The firm's opinions support injury,
wrongful-death, employment, family, and disability matters there. On the
federal side, engagements are accepted in each of California's district
courts: the United States District Court for the Central District of
California, the United States District Court for the Northern District of
California, the United States District Court for the Eastern District of
California, and the United States District Court for the Southern District of
California. Forum drives the disclosure calendar and testimony format, so
both are captured at intake and built into the work plan before the analysis
begins.

## Labor market research at metro scale

Buellton is a small city, and pretending its municipal boundaries contain a
complete labor market would hand opposing counsel an easy point. Vocational
findings for a Buellton evaluee are therefore anchored to the
[Santa Maria-Santa Barbara metropolitan area](/locations/california/santa-maria/),
using Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates
for the metro and for California statewide, alongside state workforce agency
labor market information. Direct labor market research, documented so it can
be replicated, is added when the referral turns on the availability of
particular work for a person with particular restrictions. Scale is treated
qualitatively and honestly: the report explains why the metro is the relevant
market and what that means for this evaluee's realistic options.

## Logistics for counsel and evaluee

No Purinton Analytics office exists in Buellton, and the firm publishes no
address here; stating that plainly costs nothing and protects credibility.
Work is by appointment and remote-first: secure records exchange, remote
interviews, and remote review conferences with counsel. In-person evaluation
sessions, when the questions require them, are held in the evaluee's own
community, and all travel, including deposition and trial appearances, is
scheduled from the Kansas City home office. That same home office runs the
firm's [national practice](/locations/nationwide/), so a Buellton referral
gets big-docket infrastructure without pretending to a local storefront.
