Where does a Goleta worker actually earn a living after an injury changes what
they can do? That is the question a vocational expert is retained to answer,
and answering it credibly for Goleta means treating the town as part of a
wider commuting market rather than a closed economy. Goleta belongs to the
[Santa Maria-Santa Barbara, CA metropolitan area](/locations/california/santa-maria/),
and residual earning capacity for a Goleta evaluee is assessed against the
occupations, employers, and wage structures of that metro as a whole. Purinton
Analytics performs this work for plaintiff and defense counsel alike, with the
same methodology regardless of which side issues the retainer.

## A commuting economy

Because Goleta functions inside a larger regional employment market, the
vocational analysis starts with Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational
employment and wage estimates for the Santa Maria-Santa Barbara metropolitan
area and for California statewide, then narrows to what the individual evaluee
can access given restrictions, transferable skills, education, and realistic
commuting tolerance. State workforce agency labor market information and,
where warranted, direct contact with employers refine the picture. The
distinction matters on cross-examination: an opinion that quietly assumes
work available somewhere in California is vulnerable, while an opinion that
maps the evaluee's actual reachable market is defensible. The written report
shows that mapping step rather than asserting its conclusion.

the interviewing and records work, in-person sessions are held in the
