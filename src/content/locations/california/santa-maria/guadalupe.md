the forum question has an answer before the analysis begins. Purinton
Analytics organizes its Guadalupe work around the court that will eventually
test the opinion: the disclosure calendar, the deliverable format, and the
deposition logistics are identified at intake, and the analysis is planned
backward from them. The firm prepares

defense counsel on identical terms, and every engagement follows the same
documented sequence: conflict check first, defined scope second, a records

opposing side.

## Venue for Guadalupe matters

Guadalupe sits in Santa Barbara County, and its state-court litigation
proceeds in the Superior Court. The firm's vocational and care-planning
evidence supports the claim types where such testimony carries weight there:
personal injury and wrongful death, employment disputes, marital dissolution
where support turns on earning capacity, and disability litigation. Federal
engagements are accepted throughout California as well, including the United
States District Court for the Central District of California and the United
States District Court for the Eastern District of California. The forum
shapes the schedule and the shape of the deliverable, never the analytical
standard, so the same methodology appears in every report regardless of where
the case is heard.

## Guadalupe within the metro labor market

Guadalupe is a small city, and it belongs to the
[Santa Maria-Santa Barbara metropolitan area](/locations/california/santa-maria/),
the market whose principal city is Santa Maria. For vocational purposes,
metro membership is the fact that matters: the Bureau of Labor Statistics
publishes its occupational employment and wage estimates at the level of the
metropolitan area and the state, and the firm grounds earning capacity
findings for a Guadalupe evaluee in those estimates, together with state
workforce agency labor market information. Where the referral turns on
whether particular work is actually available to a person with particular
restrictions, documented direct labor market research supplements the
published data. The report states its market definition openly, distinguishes
published estimates from firm-conducted research, and keeps every figure
traceable to a source an opposing expert can check.

## How a Guadalupe evaluation proceeds

The firm keeps no office in Guadalupe and publishes no address here; meetings
are by appointment, and plain statements about presence belong to the same
discipline that governs the findings. Most of the engagement proceeds
remotely, from secure records exchange through much of the interviewing.
When the referral questions call for an in-person evaluation, the session is
conducted in the evaluee's own community rather than at a distant office, and
travel for evaluation sessions, depositions, and trial testimony is scheduled
from the Kansas City home office, the same base that supports the firm's
[nationwide practice](/locations/nationwide/). When counsel needs the
complete damages picture, the matter can proceed as a
[coordinated damages engagement](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/),
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under a single
engagement structure.
