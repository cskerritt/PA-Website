An injured Lompoc resident should not have to organize their recovery around
an expert's convenience. That practical concern, where the evaluee will be
interviewed, tested, and observed, is often the retaining attorney's early
question, so this page answers it plainly: Purinton Analytics evaluates Lompoc
evaluees remotely where the referral permits, in the evaluee's own community
when an in-person assessment is warranted, and never by requiring a trip to a
distant office as a condition of the engagement. Both plaintiff and defense
retention are accepted, and each begins with a conflict check, a defined
scope, and a records request tailored to the claims.

## The county and courts serving Lompoc

Lompoc sits in Santa Barbara County, and its state-court matters are heard in
the Superior Court. Vocational expert and life care planning evidence from the
firm appears in the case types that dominate that docket: injury and
wrongful-death claims, employment disputes, dissolution matters where earning
capacity is contested, and long-term disability litigation. For federal work,
the practice accepts appointments across California's district courts,
including the United States District Court for the Central District of
California and the United States District Court for the Northern District of
California. Whatever the forum, the report is written to be handed to the
other side: sources cited, assumptions stated, and limits of the data
acknowledged.

## Lompoc's place in the metro labor market

Lompoc is a mid-sized city within the
[Santa Maria-Santa Barbara metro](/locations/california/santa-maria/), and an
earning capacity opinion for a Lompoc evaluee must reflect the employment
actually available to someone living there, not simply the strongest market in
the region. The firm grounds its vocational findings in Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the metropolitan
area and for California as a whole, together with state workforce agency labor
market information. Where the case turns on whether specific work exists for a
person with specific restrictions, direct labor market research and employer
contact supplement the published estimates, and the methodology for that
research is documented so it can be replicated and cross-examined.

## How a Lompoc evaluation proceeds

The firm keeps no office in Lompoc and claims none. Intake, records review,
and much of the clinical interviewing proceed by secure remote connection.
When the questions require observation or standardized testing best done in
person, the evaluation is scheduled in the evaluee's community by appointment,
and travel for that session, as for deposition and trial testimony, is
arranged from the Kansas City home office. Counsel who need a damages figure
as well as a vocational opinion can structure the referral as a
[coordinated damages assessment](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/),
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis
while the [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) and vocational
work proceed under the same engagement. The same intake path serves counsel
with matters far beyond this metro, since the practice operates
[nationwide](/locations/nationwide/).
