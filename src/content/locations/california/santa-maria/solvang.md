A remote-first expert practice exists precisely for places like Solvang.
Counsel in a small town should not have to choose between a local generalist
and a distant specialist who treats the file as an afterthought; the structure
of Purinton Analytics is designed to remove that tradeoff. Records move
through secure channels, interviews and much of the assessment happen over
reliable remote connections, and the parts of the work that genuinely require
presence, an in-person evaluation, a deposition, a trial appearance, are
scheduled deliberately rather than promised loosely. Retention is open to
plaintiff and defense counsel on equal terms, and every Solvang engagement
follows the same sequence: conflict check, defined scope, tailored records
request, written opinion.

## Where Solvang cases are heard

Solvang sits in Santa Barbara County, and its state-court matters proceed in
the Superior Court. The firm's vocational and care-planning evidence appears
in injury, employment, family, and disability litigation in that court, and
federal engagements are taken throughout the state, including the United
States District Court for the Central District of California and the United
States District Court for the Northern District of California. The firm's
reports are venue-aware in a specific sense: the analysis itself does not bend
to the forum, but the schedule, the disclosure format, and the deposition
logistics are planned around the rules of the court actually hearing the
case.

## Grounding opinions in the regional labor market

For an evaluee from a town of Solvang's size, the honest unit of vocational
analysis is the surrounding region, and the firm says so in its reports rather
than leaving the assumption implicit. Earning capacity findings rest on
Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the
[Santa Maria-Santa Barbara metropolitan area](/locations/california/santa-maria/)
and for California statewide, supported by state workforce agency labor market
information. When a case requires proof about specific employment for a
specific set of restrictions, documented direct labor market research is
added. The result is an opinion about what this evaluee can earn in the
market actually open to them, stated with sources an opposing expert can
check.

## Remote-first, in-person when it matters

There is no Purinton Analytics office in Solvang, and the firm will not
suggest otherwise with a borrowed address; meetings are by appointment only.
Remote work covers intake, records analysis, and most interviewing. When the
referral questions call for an in-person evaluation, the session takes place
in the evaluee's own community, and travel, for that session and for
testimony, is arranged from the Kansas City home office, the hub of a practice
that serves counsel [nationwide](/locations/nationwide/). Cases needing a
complete damages presentation can proceed as
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/),
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis
while the [vocational](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) and care-planning
work advances under the same retention.
