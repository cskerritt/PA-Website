Opinions offered in Santa Barbara courtrooms are read closely, and they should
be. Judges and opposing experts in the Superior Court of Santa Barbara County
can tell the difference between a vocational evaluation and an advocacy piece,
so Purinton Analytics writes every Santa Barbara report as if the sharpest
reader in the room will be the retaining attorney's adversary. The firm serves
plaintiff and defense counsel here with
[vocational expert](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) and
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) work built on a documented
methodology, transparent sources, and conclusions that stay within the record.
Santa Barbara is among the larger cities of the
[Santa Maria-Santa Barbara metro](/locations/california/santa-maria/), and its
cases receive the same engagement structure the practice applies
[nationwide](/locations/nationwide/).

## Santa Barbara County venue and courts

State-court matters arising in Santa Barbara proceed in the Superior Court,
which hears Santa Barbara County cases across the range of claims where
vocational and care-planning evidence matters: personal injury, employment,
family law support disputes, and workers' compensation issues that spill into
civil litigation. Federal engagements are accepted throughout California,
including the United States District Court for the Central District of
California, the United States District Court for the Northern District of
California, the United States District Court for the Eastern District of
California, and the United States District Court for the Southern District of
California. Because either side of the caption may retain the firm, the file
that supports each opinion is assembled so that it can be produced, examined,
and defended without embarrassment.

## Reading the Santa Barbara labor market

A vocational opinion about a Santa Barbara evaluee has to describe the labor
market that person can actually reach, not a generic statewide abstraction.
Findings rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage
estimates for the Santa Maria-Santa Barbara metropolitan area and for
California statewide, supplemented by state workforce agency labor market
information and direct employer research where the referral questions warrant
it. Transferable skills analysis, earning capacity opinions, and labor market
surveys are framed at the metro level and then tested against the evaluee's
specific restrictions, education, and work history, so the resulting numbers
in the economic analysis rest on a defensible vocational foundation.

## Evaluation logistics

There is no staffed Purinton Analytics office in Santa Barbara, and the firm
does not publish a street address here, because candor about presence belongs
in the same category as candor about findings. Meetings are by appointment.
Much of the work, including records review, diagnostic interviewing, and
standardized testing where appropriate, can proceed remotely. When the
referral calls for an in-person evaluation, the evaluee is seen in their own
community rather than being asked to travel, and when deposition or trial
requires presence, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office with
the calendar agreed in advance. Counsel who need economic damages quantified
alongside the vocational work can use
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/),
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis
while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under a single engagement
structure.
