The city of Tulare shares its name with the county around it, and in
litigation that overlap is more than a curiosity: the caption, the
courthouse, and the labor market that decides what an injured worker can
still earn all answer to the same word. Purinton Analytics serves plaintiff
and defense counsel in Tulare matters with
[vocational expert evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) of
employability and earning capacity,
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) in catastrophic injury
and medical negligence cases, and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Where a Tulare case is heard

State court engagements proceed in the Superior Court in Tulare County, the
trial court with jurisdiction over claims arising in the city. Federal
engagements are accepted across California's district courts, including the
United States District Court for the Eastern District of California, and the
substance of the analysis does not change with the forum; only the
disclosure practice and the calendar do. Counsel handling matters beyond the
county line will find the firm's statewide coverage described on the
[California locations page](/locations/california/).

## Earning capacity in the Tulare labor market

Tulare is one of the larger communities in the Visalia metropolitan area,
and a Tulare evaluee's residual earning capacity is measured against that
regional market rather than against a national abstraction. Vocational
findings rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage
estimates for the Visalia metro area and for California statewide,
supplemented by state workforce agency labor market information and direct
labor market research when the referral questions call for it. Transferable
skills are tested against work that actually exists within the evaluee's
commuting range, not against job titles in the abstract, and each finding
carries a citation, because the firm writes every report on the assumption
that its most careful reader will be the lawyer on the other side. The
regional practice, and the venue detail behind it, is set out on the
[Visalia metro page](/locations/california/visalia/).

## How a Tulare evaluee is evaluated

The firm keeps no office in Tulare and lists no address here; accuracy about
presence is part of the same discipline that governs the citations in its
reports. Counsel meetings are held by appointment. Records review,
structured interviews, and standardized testing generally proceed remotely
under publisher protocols where remote administration is supported, which
spares an injured evaluee unnecessary travel and keeps the schedule inside
counsel's deadlines. When the referral requires an in-person evaluation, or
when a deposition or trial date in Tulare County requires presence, travel
is arranged from the Kansas City home office around the litigation calendar
rather than the other way around.

## Opening a Tulare engagement

Every matter begins with clearance, not commitment. Counsel should
[request a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) with the case caption and party
names, noting any imminent disclosure, deposition, or trial dates. Once
conflicts clear, the firm issues a defined scope and a records request
tailored to the claims, and the work proceeds on a schedule set with
counsel. The report standard applied in Tulare is the standard applied to
the firm's [nationwide practice](/locations/nationwide/): documented
methods, cited sources, and opinions written to withstand cross-examination
from either side of the caption.
