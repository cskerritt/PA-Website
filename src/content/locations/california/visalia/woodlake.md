## Small community, regional evidence

Woodlake sits within the Visalia metropolitan area, and the honest way to
evaluate a Woodlake worker is against the regional market that metro
represents. The firm's vocational conclusions draw on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Visalia metro
area and for California statewide, on state workforce agency labor market
information, and on direct inquiry with employers when a question cannot be
resolved from published estimates. Where the record supports only a
qualitative statement, the report says so and stops there; overstating
precision is how experts get impeached. The same restraint applies to care
pricing, which is tied to the evaluee's own market rather than to a generic
national schedule. How this regional research program
operates across the metro is set out on the
[Visalia metro page](/locations/california/visalia/).

## Reaching an expert from Woodlake

No Purinton Analytics office exists in Woodlake, and the firm will not
pretend otherwise with a borrowed address; by-appointment service, stated
plainly, is the arrangement. The working reality is that distance rarely
matters: records review, structured interviews, and standardized testing
proceed remotely under publisher protocols where remote administration is
supported, so a Woodlake evaluee participates fully without leaving their
community. Where the referral requires an evaluation conducted in person, or
where counsel needs live testimony at a Tulare County deposition or trial,
the expert travels from the Kansas City home office on dates coordinated
with the court's schedule. Counsel is told at intake which parts of the
engagement will run remotely and which will not, so the logistics never
become a surprise in discovery.

party names, together with any disclosure, deposition, or trial dates
already entered, so both conflicts and calendar feasibility are resolved up
front. The firm then defines the scope, issues a records request matched to
the claims, and produces a signed report held to the standard of its
[nationwide practice](/locations/nationwide/): documented methodology,
cited sources, and prose composed for the most skeptical reader on the
other side of the caption.
