## The market a Farmersville worker actually faces

Farmersville is a small city within the Visalia metropolitan area, and the
employment questions in its cases are regional questions: what work exists
across the metro, which of it the evaluee can still perform, and what it
pays. The firm answers them with Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational
employment and wage estimates for the Visalia metro area and for California
statewide, with state workforce agency labor market information, and with
direct employer inquiry where published data leaves a referral question
open. Scale is described honestly and qualitatively; no figure appears in a
report without a source behind it, and no occupation is listed without
asking whether it is genuinely open to this evaluee in this market. The
metro practice as a whole is presented on the
[Visalia metro page](/locations/california/visalia/).

## Appointments, remote work, and travel

Purinton Analytics maintains no staffed location in Farmersville and prints
no address for one, because honesty about presence belongs in the same
column as honesty about data. Engagements here run by appointment. The
substantial share of the work, records review, structured interviews, and
standardized testing under publisher protocols where remote administration
is supported, proceeds without anyone traveling at all. Where an in-person
evaluation is warranted or a Tulare County deposition or trial date demands
attendance, the trip is made from the Kansas City home office, planned with
counsel so the litigation calendar holds.
