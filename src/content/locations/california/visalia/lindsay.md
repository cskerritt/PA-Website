In a community the size of Lindsay, the jobs a person held before an injury
and the jobs available afterward may not sit inside the same town limits,
and a credible earning capacity opinion has to account for that commuting
reality rather than pretend the town is a sealed economy. That question,
what work remains reachable and what it pays, is the center of the firm's
Lindsay practice: [vocational evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/)
of employability and residual earning capacity,
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) where injuries are
catastrophic, and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Lindsay's courts

Civil claims arising in Lindsay belong to the Superior Court in Tulare
County in the state system. In the federal system, the firm accepts
engagements in each of California's district courts, including the United
States District Court for the Eastern District of California; the forum
determines procedure and timing, never the rigor of the analysis. Counsel
whose docket reaches other parts of the state can review the firm's full
footprint on the [California locations page](/locations/california/).

## A small town inside a regional market

Lindsay is a small community, and small communities punish sloppy vocational
work: a report that lists occupations without asking whether such jobs exist
within a reasonable commute reads as fiction to anyone who lives there.
Findings for Lindsay evaluees therefore rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates for the Visalia metropolitan area
and for California statewide, joined with state workforce agency labor
market information and, where the referral warrants, direct labor market
research into actual openings and actual requirements. The written opinion
states what the regional market offers, what the evaluee can access within
it, and where the evidence for each statement comes from. The metro-level
picture is drawn on the [Visalia metro page](/locations/california/visalia/).

## Meeting the evaluee where they are

The firm has no office in Lindsay and does not suggest otherwise; every
location it serves outside Kansas City is served by appointment, and saying
so plainly is part of the credibility it sells. Interviews and standardized
testing are conducted remotely wherever publisher protocols support remote
administration, an approach that suits evaluees whose injuries make travel a
burden. When the case calls for an in-person evaluation in the evaluee's own
community, or when testimony is required at a Tulare County deposition or
trial, the appearance is scheduled through travel from the Kansas City home
office, arranged around counsel's dates.

## Beginning a Lindsay engagement

The door into the practice is the conflict check. Counsel should
[send the caption or the party names](/refer-a-case/) along with any
disclosure, deposition, or trial dates already set, and the firm will
confirm clearance and feasibility before scope is discussed. From there the
engagement follows the pattern used across the firm's
[national practice](/locations/nationwide/): a defined scope, a records
request tailored to the claims, and a signed report whose method and sources
are laid out for examination by either side of the caption.
