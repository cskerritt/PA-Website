Plaintiff and defense counsel bring different questions to a vocational
expert. One side needs to know what an injury has truly taken from a
client's working life; the other needs to know whether a claimed loss holds
up against the evidence. In an Exeter case, Purinton Analytics answers
either question with the same method, because an opinion that changes with
the retaining party is not an opinion worth signing. The practice offers
[vocational evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) of
employability and earning capacity,
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) for catastrophic
injuries, and [rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) of
opposing expert work, each grounded in disclosed sources.

## The forums for an Exeter dispute

State claims arising in Exeter are heard in the Superior Court in Tulare
County. For federal matters, engagements are accepted in California's
district courts across the state, including the United States District
Court for the Eastern District of California; procedure varies by forum,
method does not. The firm's coverage of courts elsewhere in California is
laid out on the [California locations page](/locations/california/).

## What the regional data says about Exeter work

Exeter is a small town, and the labor market relevant to an Exeter evaluee
extends across the Visalia metropolitan area that surrounds it. Earning
capacity findings are therefore built from Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates for that metro area and for
California statewide, from state workforce agency labor market information,
and from direct labor market research when a referral question turns on
whether particular work is genuinely available. The analysis names its
sources at every step and keeps its characterizations qualitative and
verifiable, which is what allows the same report to serve a plaintiff
reader and a defense reader without flinching. The regional foundation of
this work is described on the
[Visalia metro page](/locations/california/visalia/).

## Evaluation without an Exeter office

The firm has no office on Exeter's streets and claims none; its only staffed
office is the Kansas City home office, and every other location on this site
is served by appointment. In practice, most engagements move remotely:
records analysis, structured interviews, and standardized testing under
publisher protocols where remote administration is supported. That serves
injured evaluees, who travel less, and serves counsel, whose deadlines are
easier to hold. Remote administration is used only where the test publisher
supports it; where a protocol requires in-person conditions, the report says
which instrument was used and under what conditions, so the record is clean
on both points. When the referral questions require an in-person evaluation,
or when a deposition or trial in Tulare County calls for the expert's
presence, travel from Kansas City is scheduled against the case calendar.

## Engaging the firm

Because the practice stands available to both sides, no substantive
conversation precedes clearance. Counsel should
[request a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) with the caption or party names
and note any disclosure, deposition, or trial dates already fixed. On
clearance the firm defines scope, issues a records request tailored to the
claims, and proceeds to a signed report. Exeter matters get the identical
standard the firm carries into its
[national work](/locations/nationwide/): transparent method, cited
evidence, and conclusions prepared for the scrutiny of whichever side did
not retain the firm.
