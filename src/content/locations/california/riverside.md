Counsel who try cases in the Riverside-San Bernardino-Ontario metro often
carry dockets that cross the county line, and they need an expert practice
that treats Riverside County and San Bernardino County as a single service
area rather than separate assignments. Purinton Analytics accepts plaintiff
and defense engagements throughout the metro, providing
[vocational expert evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Courts served in the metro

State court matters are accepted in the Superior Court of Riverside County
and the Superior Court of San Bernardino County. Federal engagements are
accepted across California's district courts, including the United States
District Court for the Central District of California, the United States
District Court for the Northern District of California, the United States
District Court for the Eastern District of California, and the United States
District Court for the Southern District of California. The same written
methodology applies in every forum: the defined scope, the tailored records
request, and each analytic step are documented in the report itself,
whichever court will receive the opinion.

## Appointment-based service, stated plainly

There is no staffed Purinton Analytics office in Riverside, San Bernardino,
or Ontario, and the firm publishes no street address for this location.
Meetings happen by appointment because that is how the work actually
proceeds: records review, structured vocational interviews, and standardized
testing typically begin remotely, under publisher protocols where remote
administration is supported. When an in-person evaluation is warranted, or
when deposition and trial dates require attendance, travel is scheduled from
the Kansas City home office and coordinated with counsel well before the
dates at issue. Remote deposition testimony is supported where the court
and the parties permit it, which often narrows scheduling disputes before
they begin, and counsel receive a single point of contact from intake
through testimony.

## Labor market data for the metro

Vocational opinions for evaluees in the region rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Riverside
metropolitan area and for California statewide, together with state
workforce agency labor market information and direct labor market research
where the referral questions call for it. The metro-area estimates are used
because a capacity opinion should describe the market where the evaluee
actually lives and works, and
[medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/) and life care
plans are priced to that same community, with each source cited so the
analysis can be tested by the other side.

## Starting an engagement in Riverside or San Bernardino

Every matter begins with a documented conflict check and a defined scope,
the same sequence the firm applies across its
[California practice](/locations/california/) and its
[national coverage](/locations/nationwide/). To confirm availability,
[request a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) with the case caption and the
parties, and identify any disclosure deadlines, hearing dates, or
settlement conferences already on calendar. Once cleared, counsel receive a
written engagement confirmation and a tailored records request, and the
analysis proceeds on a schedule built around the court's deadlines rather
than the expert's convenience.
