Ceres shares its county, and for practical purposes its courthouse, with
Modesto itself. Personal injury, employment, and
family law matters involving Ceres parties are filed in the Superior Court of
Stanislaus County, which means counsel here work under the same local rules,
the same judges, and the same disclosure expectations as their colleagues in
the principal city. Purinton Analytics accepts retention from Ceres attorneys
on both plaintiff and defense matters, providing
[vocational expert testimony](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and coordination of
forensic economic damages analyses signed by independent economist partners.

## The courthouse and the record

Because Stanislaus County venue is a near certainty for a Ceres state court
case, the firm's work product is shaped for that court from intake forward:
a documented conflict check, a scope letter that names the referral
questions, and a records request tailored to the claims. Where a Ceres
matter proceeds federally, it is heard in the United States District Court
for the Eastern District of California, and the written report is prepared to
federal disclosure standards without needing to be rebuilt for the different
forum. Counsel comparing venues across the state can start with the
[California overview](/locations/california/).

## Reading the labor market from Ceres

A Ceres evaluee participates in the broader Modesto regional economy, and the
vocational analysis treats that economy as the relevant market rather than
the town line. The firm grounds its findings in Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates for the Modesto metropolitan area
and for California as a whole, along with state workforce agency labor market
information. Transferable skills analysis asks what the evaluee did before
injury, what capacities survive it, and which occupations in this regional
market actually match, described qualitatively and cited to source so the
opinion withstands cross-examination from either direction.

## Appointments, not an office

The firm maintains its home office in Kansas City and keeps no staffed
location in Ceres or elsewhere in the [Modesto metro](/locations/california/modesto/);
it publishes no local street address because accuracy about presence is part
of the same discipline that governs its reports. Most phases of an engagement
proceed remotely: records analysis, attorney conferences, and structured
evaluee interviews by video where appropriate. In-person evaluations,
depositions, and trial testimony are handled through scheduled travel from
Kansas City, arranged around the court's calendar and disclosed to counsel in
advance.

## When damages need more than a vocational opinion

Catastrophic cases often need layered opinions: future care needs, the cost
of that care, and the economic value of lost earning capacity. The firm
prepares [medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/) and
full life care plans, and it offers
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
where independent economist partners perform and sign the economic loss
analysis while the vocational and care-planning components proceed in
parallel. The structure mirrors the firm's
[national practice](/locations/nationwide/), so a Ceres referral gets the
same architecture a complex out-of-state matter would.
