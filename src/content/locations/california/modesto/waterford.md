Either party in a Waterford case can retain Purinton Analytics, and that
fact shapes everything the firm writes. An expert whose opinions shift with
the retaining side is a liability to whoever hires him; an expert whose
methodology produces the same answer no matter who asks the question is an
asset to the record itself. Waterford counsel, whether pressing a claim or
defending one, engage the firm for
[vocational evaluation and testimony](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[peer review of opposing expert work](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/), and
they receive the same analysis they would have received sitting at the other
table.

## Waterford's place in the court system

Waterford is a Stanislaus County town, and disputes arising here are heard
in the Superior Court of Stanislaus County when filed in state court. When a
matter proceeds federally, the forum is the United States District Court for
the Eastern District of California. The firm's reports carry the full
statement of opinions, bases, and data that federal disclosure practice
requires, and it holds state court work to that same standard, which spares
counsel the scramble that follows removal. Surrounding coverage is set out
on the [Modesto metro page](/locations/california/modesto/), and statewide
engagement details on the [California page](/locations/california/).

## Earning capacity in the regional market

A Waterford evaluee's post-injury employability is measured against the
regional economy in which the evaluee actually participates. The firm's
sources are consistent from case to case: Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates for the Modesto metropolitan area
and for California statewide, state workforce agency labor market
information, and direct labor market inquiry where the referral questions
warrant it. Because the sourcing is the same for plaintiff and defense
retentions, the resulting opinions can be compared line by line against an
opposing expert's work, which is often exactly what the case requires.

## By-appointment service, stated plainly

Purinton Analytics has no Waterford office, no local storefront, and no
published address in the town, and it declines to manufacture the
appearance of one. The practice operates from its Kansas City home office.
Records move through secure channels, conferences happen by video, and
structured interviews proceed remotely where appropriate. In-person
evaluations take place in the evaluee's community, and testimony is given in
person, with travel from Kansas City scheduled against the deposition or
trial calendar and communicated to counsel in advance.

## Coordinating the full damages picture

Cases that turn on lost earnings or lifetime care costs usually need more
than a vocational opinion. The firm structures
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis, the care plan and
[medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/) are prepared
within their own disciplines, and the assumptions underlying each component
reconcile. The model is identical to the one the firm runs
[across the country](/locations/nationwide/); Waterford matters simply plug
into it.
