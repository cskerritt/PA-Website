When the evaluee lives in Turlock, the evaluation should reflect Turlock. A
vocational interview conducted without regard for where a person actually
lives and looks for work produces a report that opposing counsel can pick
apart, which is why Purinton Analytics builds each Turlock engagement around
the evaluee's own circumstances: the person's work history, the restrictions
documented in the record, and the employment that is realistically reachable
from where that person lives. Counsel on either side of a caption can retain
the firm for [vocational evaluation and testimony](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and coordinated economic
damages work throughout the surrounding [Modesto metro area](/locations/california/modesto/).

## Stanislaus County venue context

Turlock matters in state court proceed in the Superior Court of Stanislaus
County, and the firm prepares its reports and disclosures to that court's
practice. Federal matters arising in this part of California are heard in the
United States District Court for the Eastern District of California, and the
firm's written opinions are prepared to the disclosure standard federal
practice demands: a complete statement of opinions, the bases for them, and
the data considered, so that neither side is surprised at deposition.
Engagements from elsewhere in the state are described on the
[California locations page](/locations/california/).

## Turlock in the metro labor market

Vocational opinions for a Turlock evaluee rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates for the Modesto metropolitan area
and for California statewide, supplemented by state workforce agency labor
market information and direct employer research where the referral questions
warrant it. The analysis is qualitative and sourced: what occupations exist
in the regional economy, what they demand physically and cognitively, and
whether the evaluee's residual capacity meets those demands. The firm does
not stretch a national average over a local question, and it says so plainly
in the report when local data limits the analysis.

## How a Turlock evaluation is scheduled

There is no Purinton Analytics office in Turlock, and the firm does not
pretend otherwise. Meetings are by appointment. Records review, counsel
conferences, and many evaluee interviews proceed remotely, which keeps
schedules moving between hearing dates. When the referral calls for an
in-person evaluation, the firm travels from its Kansas City home office and
conducts the evaluation in the evaluee's community rather than requiring an
injured person to travel to the expert. Deposition and trial appearances are
scheduled the same way, with travel planned around the court's calendar.

## Life care planning and coordinated damages

For catastrophic injury matters, the firm prepares
[life care plans](/services/life-care-planning/) and
[medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/) grounded in the
medical record and treating-provider input. Where the case requires an
economic loss opinion, the firm offers
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. The same coordination is available to counsel
[anywhere in the country](/locations/nationwide/), because the methodology
does not change with the venue.
