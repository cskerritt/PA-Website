Where a Patterson resident works and where a Patterson resident lives are
often different places, and a credible vocational opinion has to account for
that. Earning capacity is measured against the jobs an evaluee can actually
reach and sustain, so Purinton Analytics maps each Patterson analysis to the
evaluee's real commuting range across the regional economy rather than
treating the town limits as the labor market. That commuter-aware approach
runs through the firm's
[vocational expert work](/services/vocational-expert-witness/), its
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and the
[coordinated damages assessments](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
it structures for counsel on either side of the caption.

## County, courts, and forum

Patterson litigation in state court is filed in the Superior Court of
Stanislaus County, and the firm prepares its disclosures with that court's
schedule and practice in mind. Federal matters from this part of the state
proceed in the United States District Court for the Eastern District of
California. In either forum the firm's report reads the same way: opinions
stated plainly, methodology shown, sources cited, and limitations
acknowledged, because a report written for the retaining side alone rarely
survives contact with the other one. The firm's regional footprint is laid
out on the [Modesto metro page](/locations/california/modesto/) and the
[California state page](/locations/california/).

## Measuring a commuter's labor market

For a Patterson evaluee the relevant question is which occupations exist
within a reasonable commute, what they require, and whether the evaluee's
residual functional capacity meets those requirements after injury. The firm
answers it with Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage
estimates for the Modesto metropolitan area and for California statewide,
state workforce agency labor market information, and direct employer contact
when the referral questions call for it. Pre-injury earnings history,
transferable skills, and documented restrictions frame the comparison; the
conclusions are qualitative, sourced, and written to be tested at
deposition.

## Logistics for Patterson counsel and evaluees

The firm's only office is its Kansas City home office. It maintains no

## When the case needs an economic loss opinion

Lost earning capacity and future care costs frequently require an
economist's signature. The firm does not blur that line: independent
economist partners perform and sign the forensic economic analysis, while
Purinton Analytics coordinates the engagement so the vocational foundation,
the [medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/), and the
economic model rest on consistent assumptions. Counsel receive opinions that
fit together without any expert opining outside a defensible scope.
