A Newman referral reaches the firm as a set of questions before it becomes
a case file. Can the evaluee return to the former occupation, and if not,
to what work? What has the injury done to earning capacity? What care will
the coming years require, and what should that care cost? Purinton
Analytics is retained by Newman counsel on both the plaintiff and the
defense side to answer those questions in writing, through
[vocational expert evaluations](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/), and each
answer takes the form the question deserves: a stated opinion, its bases,
and the evidence behind both.

## The courts where the answers must hold

An answer is only useful if it survives the forum it was written for. For
a Newman matter filed in state court, that forum is the Superior Court of
Stanislaus County, the trial court of the county where the town sits. A
case that belongs in federal court goes before the United States District
Court for the Eastern District of California, whose practice expects a
comprehensive written report. The firm writes to that fuller standard in
every engagement, whatever the initial forum, so the answers counsel
receive never have to be rebuilt after removal or refiling. Coverage of
the surrounding communities appears on the
[Modesto metro page](/locations/california/modesto/), and the firm's
statewide practice on the [California page](/locations/california/).

## Answering the earning capacity question

The earning capacity question starts with the evaluee, not the map: the
work history in the record, the skills that history built, and the
restrictions treating providers have documented. Those facts are then set
against the occupations of the regional economy as described in Bureau of
Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the
Modesto metropolitan area and for California statewide, supplemented by
state workforce agency labor market information and, for questions the
published data cannot settle, direct inquiry with employers in the
regional market. The conclusion is qualitative, and every step toward it
is cited, because an answer counsel cannot trace to a source is an answer
opposing counsel will not let stand.

## Reaching Newman from the Kansas City home office

None of these answers requires a storefront, and the firm keeps none in
Newman; its single office is the Kansas City home office, and Newman
engagements are served from it by appointment. The documentary bulk of an
engagement moves wherever the documents do: secure exchange puts the
record on the analyst's desk, attorney conferences happen by video on
short scheduling notice, and a structured interview can often proceed the
same way when the clinical circumstances permit it. Presence is reserved
for the moments that genuinely need it. An in-person evaluation is
conducted where the evaluee lives, and depositions and trial appearances
are met through travel planned from Kansas City and confirmed with counsel
against the court's dates.

## The question that belongs to an economist

One question on the list belongs to a different discipline. The economic
value of lost earnings, or of a lifetime of care, is an economist's
opinion, and the firm treats it that way. It offers
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. Coordination means the economist's inputs match the
vocational and care-planning findings they build on, and that no signer
answers a question outside that signer's own field. Counsel retain the
firm on the same terms [across the country](/locations/nationwide/),
because this division of labor is a feature of the method, not of the
venue.
