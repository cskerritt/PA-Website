The test of an expert report in an Oakdale case is not how it reads to the
lawyer who commissioned it but how it holds up in the hands of the lawyer
who did not. Purinton Analytics writes to that standard. Every opinion the
firm issues for an Oakdale matter, whether a
[vocational assessment](/services/vocational-expert-witness/), a
[life care plan](/services/life-care-planning/), or a
[rebuttal of another expert's work](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/), is
built on a documented methodology, cited data, and stated limitations, so
that cross-examination confirms the discipline rather than exposing its
absence. Plaintiff firms and defense firms retain the practice in roughly
the manner you would expect of a methodology that favors neither.

## Venue for Oakdale disputes

Oakdale lies in Stanislaus County, and its state court matters are heard in
the Superior Court of Stanislaus County. Cases proceeding in federal court
from this region go to the United States District Court for the Eastern
District of California. The distinction matters chiefly for disclosure
practice: the federal forum requires a comprehensive written report, and the
firm prepares one for every engagement regardless of forum, which means an
Oakdale state court matter that later removes or refiles does not need its
expert work redone. Regional and statewide coverage are described on the
[Modesto metro page](/locations/california/modesto/) and the
[California page](/locations/california/).

## The labor market behind the opinion

An Oakdale evaluee's employment prospects are analyzed against the regional
economy that surrounds the town. The firm draws on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Modesto
metropolitan area and for California statewide, state workforce agency labor
market information, and targeted employer research when the referral
questions require it. The analysis names its sources and characterizes the
market qualitatively; it does not decorate the report with statistics that
cannot be traced, because untraceable figures are the softest target in any
deposition.

## Presence, honestly described

There is no Purinton Analytics office in Oakdale. The firm works from its
Kansas City home office and serves Oakdale by appointment, a fact stated
here plainly because accuracy about presence belongs to the same habit of
mind as accuracy about data. Records review and counsel conferences proceed
remotely; evaluations are conducted in person in the evaluee's community
when the referral requires it, with travel scheduled from Kansas City; and
deposition or trial appearances are calendared with counsel well before the
dates arrive.

## Coordinated damages without blurred roles

Where an Oakdale case requires an economic loss opinion, the firm arranges
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. Each professional signs only within that
professional's discipline. The arrangement is the same the firm offers
[across the country](/locations/nationwide/), and it exists precisely so
that no expert in the chain can be impeached for opining beyond scope.
