Referrals from the Sacramento-Roseville-Folsom metro tend to arrive in a few
recognizable shapes: an earning capacity dispute in an injury or employment
case that calls for [vocational expert evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
a catastrophic injury requiring a [life care plan](/services/life-care-planning/)
that will be examined line by line, or a damages case suited to
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. Purinton Analytics accepts each of these from
plaintiff and defense counsel alike, applying the same written methodology
to both sides of the caption.

## Courts across the metro's counties

State court engagements are accepted in the Superior Court of Sacramento
County, the Superior Court of Placer County, the Superior Court of El
Dorado County, and the Superior Court of Yolo County. Federal matters are
accepted throughout California, including the United States District Court
for the Eastern District of California as well as the United States
District Court for the Northern District of California, the United States
District Court for the Central District of California, and the United
States District Court for the Southern District of California. Counsel with
matters spread across these forums work with a single expert practice and a
single report standard.

## Presence, honestly described

There is no staffed Purinton Analytics office in Sacramento, Roseville, or
Folsom, and no street address is published for this location. Meetings are
by appointment because the work is structured that way: records review,
vocational interviews, and standardized testing under publisher protocols
where remote administration is supported all typically begin remotely. When
the referral questions call for an in-person evaluation, or when deposition
and trial dates require attendance, travel is scheduled from the Kansas
City home office with enough lead time to protect the litigation calendar.
Remote deposition testimony is supported where the court and the parties
permit it, which keeps scheduling workable when calendars compress.

## Starting a Sacramento engagement

Every engagement in the firm's [California practice](/locations/california/)
begins the way it does [nationwide](/locations/nationwide/): with a
documented conflict check, a defined scope, and a records request tailored
to the claims, so the written opinion rests on a record both sides can
examine. To confirm availability for a Sacramento metro matter,
[request a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) with the caption and party
names, and identify any disclosure deadlines or trial settings already on
calendar. Counsel then receive a written engagement confirmation and a
schedule built around the court's dates.
