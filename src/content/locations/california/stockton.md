Counsel in the Stockton-Lodi metro litigating injury and employment claims
in the Superior Court of San Joaquin County can engage Purinton Analytics
for the damages side of the case. The firm supports those matters, for
plaintiff and defense counsel alike, with
[vocational expert evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) of
earning capacity and employability,
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) for catastrophic injury
claims, and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Where the firm accepts matters

Beyond the Superior Court in San Joaquin County, engagements are accepted in
California's federal trial courts: the United States District Court for the
Northern District of California, the United States District Court for the
Eastern District of California, the United States District Court for the
Central District of California, and the United States District Court for the
Southern District of California. Forum shapes deadlines and disclosure
practice, and the firm calendars to them, but it does not change the
analysis itself. Counsel handling related matters in other parts of the
state will find the full list of covered venues on the
[California locations page](/locations/california/).

## Appointment-based presence, honestly described

The firm keeps no staffed office in Stockton and publishes no street address
for this location. That is a deliberate choice, stated plainly because
accuracy about offices belongs to the same discipline that governs the
reports themselves. Counsel meetings, evaluee interviews, and standardized
testing are scheduled by appointment, and much of the work proceeds remotely
under publisher protocols where remote administration is supported. When an
in-person evaluation is warranted, or when a deposition or trial date
requires presence in San Joaquin County, travel is scheduled from the Kansas
City home office with enough lead time to protect the litigation calendar.

## Grounding opinions in the local labor market

A vocational opinion about a Stockton evaluee should reflect the labor
market that evaluee actually faces. Findings rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Stockton
metropolitan area and for California statewide, together with state
workforce agency labor market information and direct labor market research
where the referral questions call for it. Life care plans and medical cost
projections are priced to the evaluee's own market through provider-specific
inquiries and published regional fee data. Every source is cited so that the
opinion can be tested in cross-examination, which is the standard the firm
applies to [engagements nationwide](/locations/nationwide/) as well as to
work close to home.

## Opening a San Joaquin County engagement

The first step is always clearance. Counsel should
[request a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) with the case caption and party
names, and flag any disclosure, deposition, or trial dates that are already
set. After conflicts clear, the firm issues a defined scope and a records
request tailored to the claims, so that the resulting opinion, whether it
supports a demand, a defense, or a mediation position, rests on a record
either side can examine.
