Every report Purinton Analytics writes for the Santa Maria-Santa Barbara
metro is written on the assumption that opposing counsel will read it line
by line, and that assumption is the firm's real credential. It shapes the
[vocational expert work](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) on earning
capacity and employability, the
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) prepared in catastrophic
injury cases, and the
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. Because the standard is scrutiny rather than
advocacy, the firm takes retentions from plaintiff and defense counsel on
the same terms.

## Santa Barbara County venue coverage

Engagements are accepted for matters in the Superior Court in Santa Barbara
County, the state trial court serving Santa Maria, Santa Barbara, and the
county's other communities. Federal matters are accepted throughout
California's district courts: the United States District Court for the
Northern District of California, the United States District Court for the
Eastern District of California, the United States District Court for the
Central District of California, and the United States District Court for the
Southern District of California. The firm's coverage across the rest of the
state is set out on the [California locations page](/locations/california/).

## No storefront, and no pretense of one

There is no staffed Purinton Analytics office in Santa Maria, and the firm
publishes no street address for this location. Counsel and evaluee meetings
are by appointment. Most engagements begin remotely, through records review,
structured interviews, and standardized testing administered under publisher
protocols where remote administration is supported. When the referral
questions call for an in-person evaluation, or when a deposition or trial in
Santa Barbara County requires attendance, travel is scheduled from the
Kansas City home office with the litigation calendar driving the dates.
Candor about presence costs nothing and protects the record; an expert who
overstates an office is easier to impeach on everything else.

## Wage and care-cost research for the metro

Vocational findings for evaluees in the region rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Santa Maria
metropolitan area and for California statewide, supplemented by state
workforce agency labor market information and direct labor market research
where warranted. Life care plans and medical cost projections are priced to
the evaluee's own market through provider-specific inquiries and published
regional fee data rather than borrowed national figures. Where local pricing
and statewide data diverge, the report says so and explains which basis the
projection uses. Each source appears
in the report where it is used, the same way it does in the firm's
[national engagements](/locations/nationwide/), so that either side of the
caption can check the work.

## Requesting availability

Availability for a Santa Barbara County matter is confirmed through a
conflict check. Submit the caption or party names through the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and note any imminent disclosure,
deposition, or trial dates. Once conflicts clear, counsel receive a defined
scope and a records request tailored to the claims, and the engagement
proceeds under the same documented protocol whether it ends in settlement or
testimony.
