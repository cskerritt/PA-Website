A vocational opinion has a fixed anatomy: a records review, a structured interview, a transferable skills analysis, labor market evidence, and a conclusion that follows from everything before it or does not deserve to be written. Purinton Analytics applies that anatomy to Santa Paula matters exactly as it does everywhere else, for plaintiff counsel and defense counsel alike. What changes from case to case is the record, never the method, and that constancy is the point: an expert whose approach bends with the retaining party is an expert with a short career in front of a jury.

## Labor Market Evidence for Santa Paula Evaluees

For a Santa Paula resident, employability and earning capacity are assessed against the broader regional market, not the town alone. The evidentiary base is Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Oxnard-Thousand Oaks-Ventura metropolitan area and for California statewide, joined with state workforce agency labor market information and, where a disputed occupation warrants it, direct labor market research. In a smaller community, the gap between what regional statistics show and what an individual evaluee can actually obtain deserves explicit treatment, and the reports give it that treatment rather than leaving the inference to argument.

## Conducting the Evaluation

Santa Paula engagements are served by appointment from the firm's Kansas City home office; there is no local office and no published Santa Paula address, and the practice says so directly because overstating presence would be a strange habit for a witness whose value is accuracy. Interviews proceed by secure video where the record permits. When the referral questions require in-person work, including [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) evaluations and [medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/) assessments involving the treatment record, the evaluation is conducted in the evaluee's own community. Deposition and trial appearances are scheduled through travel from Kansas City, keyed to the dates counsel supplies.

When a matter also needs economic loss quantification, the firm arranges [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning components move forward under one engagement structure, preserving both efficiency and each discipline's independent authorship.
