When catastrophic injury reshapes a life in a small town like Ojai, the central expert question is often not employment at all but care: what this person will need, for how long, delivered by whom, and at what point each need begins. [Life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) is where Purinton Analytics starts many of its Ojai engagements, building future-care plans from the medical record, treating-provider input, and evaluation of the evaluee's actual circumstances, with [medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/) work available where the dispute is narrower. Vocational earning capacity analysis joins the engagement when the claims require it, and both sides of the caption refer this work in roughly equal measure across the practice.

## A Small Community, A Regional Market

Where earning capacity is at issue for an Ojai evaluee, the analysis reaches beyond the town to the regional market, grounded in Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Oxnard-Thousand Oaks-Ventura metropolitan area and for California statewide, supplemented by state workforce agency labor market information and direct labor market research where warranted. In a community of Ojai's size, the report must confront the practical question of access: whether the occupations the data identify are realistically reachable for this evaluee, or exist mainly on paper. Care-planning opinions face a parallel question about the local availability of services, and the plans document how each recommended service will actually be obtained.

## Evaluation and Appearance Logistics

Ojai is served by appointment. The firm has no office or published address in the city; its single home office is in Kansas City, and it treats plain statements about presence as part of the same accuracy it owes the court. Records-based work and interviews proceed by secure video where appropriate. In-person evaluation, frequent in care-planning matters where the home environment informs the plan, is conducted in the evaluee's own community. Deposition and trial appearances are met through travel scheduled from the Kansas City home office against the case calendar.

Matters requiring economic quantification of lost earnings or care costs can proceed as [coordinated damages assessments](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/), in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis while the care-planning and vocational opinions continue under the same engagement, each carrying its author's own signature.
