## The Regional Market, Measured Honestly

Vocational findings for Moorpark evaluees are built from Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Oxnard-Thousand Oaks-Ventura metropolitan area and for California as a whole, augmented by state workforce agency labor market information and by direct labor market research when a contested occupation demands it. Because the city is small relative to the metro around it, the transferable skills analysis pays particular attention to commuting-range employment and to whether a given occupation is actually available to the evaluee, not merely present somewhere in the regional statistics. That distinction, between existence and availability, is often where these cases are won or lost.
