## Evaluation Logistics for Simi Valley Evaluees

Meetings are by appointment. The firm keeps no staffed office in Simi Valley and publishes no local street address, because accuracy about presence is part of the same discipline that governs its reports. Vocational interviews and [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) assessments are conducted by secure video when the record supports it, or in the evaluee's own community when in-person evaluation is warranted by the referral questions. When deposition or trial dates require presence, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office and coordinated around the court's calendar rather than the firm's.

Where lost earnings must be reduced to present value, the firm offers [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement structure. Counsel receives an integrated set of opinions without the scheduling friction of retaining each discipline separately, and each expert signs only the work within that expert's own qualifications.
