Who retains a vocational expert says a great deal about the expert. In Thousand Oaks matters, Purinton Analytics is retained by plaintiff firms testing what an injured client can still earn, by defense counsel probing whether a claimed loss withstands scrutiny, and by both sides in matters where a neutral [rebuttal or peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) of an existing report is what the case actually needs. The methodology does not change with the caption. Each Thousand Oaks engagement starts with a documented conflict check and a written scope, and it ends with an opinion the retaining lawyer can hand to the other side without flinching.

## Labor Market Context for Thousand Oaks Evaluees

Thousand Oaks is one of the metro's larger cities, and an earning capacity analysis for a resident evaluee has to reflect the full market a worker there can realistically reach, not a snapshot of a single town. The firm grounds its findings in Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates covering the Oxnard-Thousand Oaks-Ventura metropolitan area and the state as a whole, together with California workforce agency data and, where the questions call for it, direct research into specific occupations. The result is a transferable skills and placeability analysis whose geographic and occupational assumptions are stated on the face of the report.

## How Evaluations Are Conducted

There is no Thousand Oaks storefront and no published local address; the practice states this plainly because candor about presence is a credibility issue, and credibility is the product being sold. Evaluations are by appointment: secure video where the referral permits, or conducted in the evaluee's own community when the questions require observation in person. [Life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) work, including collaboration with treating providers, follows the same pattern. Testimony obligations are met through travel arranged from the Kansas City home office, planned against the disclosure and trial calendar counsel provides at intake.

When damages require an economic loss calculation, the firm coordinates [combined damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis, keeping the vocational and care-planning opinions and the economic opinion within a single engagement while preserving each expert's independent signature.

## Requesting a Conflict Check

The first step in any Thousand Oaks referral, plaintiff or defense, is the same: send the caption or the party names through the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/), along with any dates already on the court's calendar. The firm responds with a cleared conflict check, a proposed scope, and a records request, so that the opinion that eventually reaches opposing counsel rests on a record both sides can examine. That is the standard for every matter the practice accepts, in Thousand Oaks and [across the country](/locations/nationwide/).
