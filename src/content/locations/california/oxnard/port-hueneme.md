## What By-Appointment Service Means in Practice

For record review, interviews, and most [vocational expert](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) work, secure video conferencing lets the engagement proceed without delay. When the questions call for in-person assessment, particularly in [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) matters where home settings and daily routines matter to the plan, the evaluation is conducted in the evaluee's own community. Depositions and trial testimony are handled through travel scheduled from the Kansas City home office, arranged around the court's dates. Nothing about the model depends on geography except the calendar, which is why the same firm serves matters [across the country](/locations/nationwide/).

Cases needing an economic damages calculation alongside the vocational and care-planning opinions can be structured as [coordinated damages assessments](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/): independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis, and all disciplines proceed under a single engagement with separate signatures.
