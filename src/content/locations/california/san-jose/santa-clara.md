The city of Santa Clara shares its name with the county that surrounds it,
and for counsel the overlap is more than trivia. Pleadings, venue
statements, and expert disclosures in a Santa Clara case all pass through
institutions named for the county, and an expert report that blurs the city
into the county reads as carelessness before the substance is ever reached.
Purinton Analytics prepares [vocational expert](/services/vocational-expert-witness/)
and [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) opinions for Santa
Clara litigation with the precision that distinction demands, and it accepts
referrals from plaintiff and defense counsel on identical terms.

## Where Santa Clara cases are heard

State court matters arising in the city proceed in the Superior Court of
Santa Clara County. Federal referrals are accepted anywhere in the state,
including the United States District Court for the Northern District of
California and the United States District Court for the Eastern District of
California, as well as the state's other federal district courts. Because
methodology does not change with the forum, the same documented approach
serves either setting, and any venue-specific registration or qualification
requirement is identified and confirmed during the conflict check and intake
process. Engagements elsewhere in the state are described on the
[California locations page](/locations/california/).

## Reading the labor market around Santa Clara

Vocational findings for a Santa Clara evaluee are grounded in Bureau of
Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates covering the San
Jose-Sunnyvale-Santa Clara metropolitan area and California as a whole,
alongside state workforce agency labor market information. Where the
referral turns on a specific occupation or a disputed placement claim,
direct labor market research supplements the published estimates. The aim is
never a number pulled from a table; it is a defensible chain from the
evaluee's documented work history, through transferable skills, to the
occupations and earnings the market actually offers.

## How the work gets done

Purinton Analytics keeps its home office in Kansas City and no other. There
is no staffed Santa Clara location and no local street address, and this
page will not invent either. What counsel gets instead is a remote-first
practice: records analysis, interviews by secure video where appropriate,
and report production that runs to the deadlines set at intake. In-person
evaluations are held in the evaluee's own community when the referral
questions require them, and travel for depositions or trial is planned from
the home office well ahead of the dates that matter. The broader
[San Jose metro practice](/locations/california/san-jose/) and the
[nationwide practice](/locations/nationwide/) operate the same way.

## Beginning an engagement

When damages need a fuller frame than vocational findings alone, counsel can
retain [coordinated damages assessment](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/),
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under a single
engagement structure. Whatever the shape of the referral, it starts the same
way: send the caption or party names through the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/), note the court, and identify any
disclosure or testimony dates already set. A defined scope and a documented
record follow from there.
