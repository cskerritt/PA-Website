The San Jose-Sunnyvale-Santa Clara metro crosses a county line to reach
Hollister. Unlike its sibling communities in the metro, Hollister sits in
San Benito County rather than Santa Clara County, and that distinction
controls where its cases are filed, which clerk's office holds the docket,
and which courthouse an expert must be prepared to appear in. Purinton
Analytics accounts for that geography from the outset of every Hollister
referral, whether the retaining counsel represents the plaintiff or the
defense, and whether the need is a
[vocational expert opinion](/services/vocational-expert-witness/), a
[life care plan](/services/life-care-planning/), or both.

## San Benito County venue, statewide federal reach

State court matters arising in Hollister are accepted in the Superior Court
of San Benito County. Federal engagements are accepted anywhere in
California, including the United States District Court for the Northern
District of California, the United States District Court for the Eastern
District of California, the United States District Court for the Central
District of California, and the United States District Court for the
Southern District of California. Any venue-specific registration or
qualification requirement is identified and confirmed during the conflict
check and intake process, before the analysis starts rather than after it
is challenged, so venue is never an afterthought bolted onto a finished
opinion.

## A distinct county inside a shared labor market

Hollister's evaluees are analyzed against the labor market they actually
occupy: the city belongs statistically to the San Jose-Sunnyvale-Santa
Clara metropolitan area, so Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational
employment and wage estimates for that metro and for California statewide
anchor the wage and employment evidence, refined with state workforce
agency labor market information. But belonging to a metro on paper is not
the same as working across it in fact, so where an evaluee's restrictions
or circumstances confine the realistic search area, direct labor market
research establishes what is actually attainable, and the written opinion
keeps the distinction visible. The metro-wide practice is described on the
[San Jose metro page](/locations/california/san-jose/).

## Serving Hollister from the Kansas City home office

The firm has no Hollister office and claims none; its single office is the
Kansas City home office, and all San Benito County work is performed by
appointment from there. Records review, analysis, and drafting proceed
remotely on the schedule committed at intake. Interviews run by secure
video when the referral questions permit, in-person evaluation is convened
in the evaluee's own community when they do not, and travel for deposition
or trial testimony is planned from Kansas City against the case calendar.
California engagements beyond this county are indexed on the
[state locations page](/locations/california/), and the full geographic
scope on the [nationwide page](/locations/nationwide/).

## Beginning a Hollister referral

Send the caption or party names through the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/), identify the court, and note the
governing dates. After conflicts clear, scope and fees are fixed in
writing. Matters needing economic loss analysis can be structured as
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/),
with independent economist partners performing and signing the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.
