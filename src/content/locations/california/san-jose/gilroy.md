answer rather than an assumption. Gilroy is counted within the San
Jose-Sunnyvale-Santa Clara metro for statistical purposes, but a vocational
opinion still has to establish, not presume, that metro-wide data fairly
describes the jobs a particular Gilroy resident can obtain and hold.
Purinton Analytics treats that threshold question as part of the analysis
itself, in [vocational expert engagements](/services/vocational-expert-witness/)
accepted from plaintiff and defense counsel on the same terms.

## Fitting metro data to a Gilroy evaluee

The analytical sequence runs from the person outward. An evaluee's education,
work history, transferable skills, and documented restrictions define what
should be searched; Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and
wage estimates for the San Jose-Sunnyvale-Santa Clara metropolitan area and
for California statewide, plus state workforce agency labor market
information, describe what the region offers; and direct labor market
research tests whether those offerings are genuinely open to this evaluee
from this community. When the honest answer is that an occupation exists in
the metro but not realistically within the evaluee's reach, the report says
exactly that, with the supporting inquiry documented so the conclusion can
be verified rather than merely trusted.
