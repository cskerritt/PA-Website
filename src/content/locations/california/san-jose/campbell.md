until a disclosure deadline looms to retain a vocational expert inherit

engagement the other way around, committing evaluation dates, report

## By-appointment service, honestly described

Campbell has no Purinton Analytics office, and this page will not pretend
otherwise. The firm operates from its Kansas City home office and serves
Campbell by appointment: records handled electronically, interviews by
secure video where the referral questions allow, and in-person evaluation
arranged in the evaluee's own community when the analysis requires it.
Deposition and trial travel is scheduled from the home office against the
dates committed at intake, which is precisely why those dates are gathered
before the engagement begins. The same commitments hold across the
[nationwide practice](/locations/nationwide/).

## Securing dates with a conflict check

Because calendar control is the point, the referral process starts early
and simply: submit the caption or party names through the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) with the venue and every deadline
already set, from expert disclosure through trial. Counsel can pair the
vocational and care-planning work with
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/),
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis under a single engagement structure, or add
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) when an
opposing report needs a documented, methodical examination. Intake responds
with a written confirmation of scope, fee structure, and the delivery dates
the engagement will honor. Nothing about that exchange obligates counsel to
proceed; it simply means that when the retention decision is made, the
groundwork is already laid and the calendar already protected. For matters
that later expand, whether toward additional evaluees or additional claims,
the engagement structure flexes without renegotiating the basics, because
the basics were documented at the start.
