Sunnyvale is written into the formal name of its own metropolitan area, the
San Jose-Sunnyvale-Santa Clara, CA metro, and litigation arising there tends
to reflect that stature: serious injury claims, employment disputes, and
family law matters in which earning capacity sits at the contested center of
the case. Purinton Analytics serves counsel on either side of the caption in
Sunnyvale with [vocational expert evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. The regional practice as a whole is described on the
[San Jose metro page](/locations/california/san-jose/).

## Venues for Sunnyvale matters

Sunnyvale sits in Santa Clara County, and state court matters are accepted
in the Superior Court of Santa Clara County. Federal engagements are
accepted throughout the state, including the United States District Court
for the Northern District of California, the United States District Court
for the Eastern District of California, the United States District Court for
the Central District of California, and the United States District Court for
the Southern District of California. Reports are drafted so that the same
methodology reads cleanly in either forum: a defined scope, documented
sources, and opinions that stay within the record. Coverage across the rest
of the state is outlined on the [California locations page](/locations/california/).

## Sunnyvale's place in the regional labor market

A Sunnyvale evaluee competes in a labor market that reaches well past the
city limits, and a credible vocational opinion has to account for that
reach. Findings rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment
and wage estimates from the OEWS program for the San Jose-Sunnyvale-Santa
Clara metropolitan area and for California statewide, supplemented by state
workforce agency labor market information and direct labor market research
where the referral questions warrant it. No figure appears in a report
without a cited source, and no occupational match is offered without the
reasoning behind it, because the report is written on the assumption that
opposing counsel will test both.

## Evaluating a Sunnyvale evaluee

Most phases of a Sunnyvale engagement proceed remotely. Records review,
transferable skills analysis, labor market research, and report drafting
move on the schedule fixed at intake, with secure video interviews when the
referral permits them. When the questions call for an in-person interview or
assessment, the evaluation is arranged in the evaluee's own community, and
testimony travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office. The firm
maintains no Sunnyvale office and publishes no local address, because
accuracy about presence belongs to the same discipline that governs the
reports. [Rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) of
opposing expert work is available under the same standards, and the national
scope of the practice appears on the [nationwide page](/locations/nationwide/).

## Requesting a conflict check

Engagements begin with a conflict check rather than a sales conversation.
Submit the caption or the party names through the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/), identify the venue, and flag any
disclosure, deposition, or trial date already on the calendar. Intake
confirms scope, fee structure, and any venue-specific qualification
requirement before substantive work begins, so that counsel for plaintiff or
defense know exactly what the engagement covers and when the written opinion
will arrive.
