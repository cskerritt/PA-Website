A Palo Alto case can put the work question and the care question on the
table at once: what employment the evaluee can still perform, and what care
the evaluee will need in the years ahead. Purinton Analytics answers both
within the same practice, pairing
[vocational expert evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/)
with [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) so that the
earning capacity analysis and the future care plan rest on the same
records, the same interview, and the same documented method rather than on
separately retained experts who never compared notes. Plaintiff and defense counsel retain
the firm on identical terms, and each discipline's opinion is written to
stand on its own should the case later narrow to a single question.

## Santa Clara County venue and the federal courts

Palo Alto sits in Santa Clara County, and state court engagements proceed
in the Superior Court of Santa Clara County. Federal referrals are accepted
throughout California, including the United States District Court for the
Northern District of California and the United States District Court for
the Eastern District of California, together with the state's other federal
district courts. The forum determines the disclosure format and calendar,
both of which are captured at intake; it does not change the analysis
itself. Coverage for the surrounding region appears on the
[San Jose metro page](/locations/california/san-jose/), and statewide
coverage on the [California locations page](/locations/california/).

## A shared evidentiary base for work and care questions

Both disciplines draw on the same regional evidence. Vocational findings
for a Palo Alto evaluee rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational
employment and wage estimates for the San Jose-Sunnyvale-Santa Clara
metropolitan area and for California statewide, alongside state workforce
agency labor market information, with direct labor market research added
when a contested occupation requires it. The life care plan is built with
the same discipline: sources cited, assumptions stated, and the reasoning
from record to recommendation left visible for either side of the caption
to examine. Where published occupational categories fit an evaluee's
documented history imperfectly, the report says so and explains the
judgment applied, rather than letting an unstated assumption carry the
conclusion. When the analyses inform each other, as they often do when
capacity limits shape both employability and care needs, the connection is
made in writing where it can be tested.

## Serving Palo Alto evaluees by appointment

The firm keeps its home office in Kansas City and no office in Palo Alto;
no local address is published for this location because none exists.
Records analysis, transferable skills work, care-plan research, and
drafting proceed remotely on the schedule set at intake. Interviews run by
secure video where the referral questions permit, and when an in-person
evaluation is warranted it is arranged in the evaluee's own community
rather than requiring travel from the person being evaluated. Appearances
for deposition or trial testimony are planned from the Kansas City home
office against the dates counsel identifies, and the same by-appointment
model carries the firm's [nationwide practice](/locations/nationwide/).

## Referring a Palo Alto matter

Whether the referral raises the vocational question, the care question, or
both, it begins the same way: submit the caption or party names through the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) along with the venue and any
disclosure, deposition, or trial dates already fixed. When the damages
picture also requires economic loss analysis, the matter can proceed as a
[coordinated damages engagement](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. Counsel holding an opposing expert's report can also
request [rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) under
the same documented standards, and intake confirms scope, fees, and any
venue-specific qualification requirement in writing before substantive work
begins.
