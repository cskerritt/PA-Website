An evaluation should come to the evaluee, not the other way around. When a
Morgan Hill resident needs a vocational or care-planning evaluation for
litigation, Purinton Analytics arranges the in-person portions in the
evaluee's own community and conducts the rest remotely, so that an injured
or displaced person in this smaller Santa Clara County city is never asked
to organize their recovery around an expert's convenience.
[Vocational expert evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) and
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) are both available on
these terms, to plaintiff and defense counsel equally.

## The county and courts behind Morgan Hill cases

Morgan Hill litigation in state court is accepted in the Superior Court of
Santa Clara County. Federally, referrals are accepted throughout
California, including the United States District Court for the Northern
District of California and the United States District Court for the Eastern
District of California, alongside the state's other districts. Court
requirements shape the calendar, not the analysis: disclosure deadlines are
logged at intake, report delivery is committed against them, and any
venue-specific qualification requirement is identified and confirmed
during the conflict check and intake process. The rest of the metro's
coverage appears on the [San Jose metro page](/locations/california/san-jose/).

## Situating Morgan Hill in the metro's labor market

A Morgan Hill evaluee's employment prospects are assessed against the San
Jose-Sunnyvale-Santa Clara metropolitan area using Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates, with California
statewide estimates and state workforce agency labor market information
providing the broader frame. The analysis then narrows to the evaluee:
documented restrictions, commuting realities, and transferable skills
determine how much of that regional market is honestly available. Scale
matters in this work; a smaller community inside a large metro is neither
the metro core nor an isolated town, and the report reflects that rather
than flattening it.

## Practical logistics, stated plainly

Morgan Hill meetings happen by appointment, not at a local storefront,
because the firm has no storefront there; its only office is the Kansas
City home office, and no local address is published for this or any other
served location. Records exchange, interviews where appropriate, and draft
review all run remotely on a schedule fixed at intake. When testimony
requires presence, travel from Kansas City is planned around counsel's
deposition and trial dates rather than squeezed in after them. The same
practices govern work statewide, described on the
[California page](/locations/california/), and across the
[nationwide practice](/locations/nationwide/). Counsel receive status as
the work progresses, not silence followed by a surprise, and evaluees
receive scheduling that respects medical appointments and recovery
demands already on their calendar.

## How to refer a Morgan Hill matter

Referrals open with the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/): provide the
caption or party names, the venue, and every date already fixed. Counsel
whose damages theory needs an economist can structure the matter as a
[coordinated damages engagement](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/),
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while vocational and care-planning work continues under a single
engagement structure. Opposing expert reports can be evaluated through
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) under the same
documented standards.
