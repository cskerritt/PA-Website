What can this person still earn? Mountain View litigation puts that question
to experts in injury, employment, and family law matters, and the answer is
only as strong as the method behind it. Purinton Analytics answers it the
same way in every case: a documented records review, a transferable skills
analysis tied to the evaluee's actual work history, labor market research
with cited sources, and a written
[vocational expert opinion](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) that shows
its reasoning step by step. Where future care is also in dispute,
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) proceeds under the same
standards, for plaintiff and defense referrals alike.

## The labor market a Mountain View evaluee faces

A responsible opinion about a Mountain View evaluee starts from published
data and says so. Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and
wage estimates for the San Jose-Sunnyvale-Santa Clara metropolitan area and
for California statewide anchor the analysis, with state workforce agency
labor market information layered in and direct employer contact undertaken
when a specific placement question demands it. Qualitative judgment enters
the analysis openly, in writing, where opposing counsel can see it and probe
it, rather than hiding inside an unexplained conclusion. That transparency is
deliberate: an assumption disclosed can be argued; an assumption concealed
gets discovered, usually at the worst possible moment of a deposition.

## Logistics for counsel and evaluees

Counsel will not find a Mountain View office listed here, because none
exists; the firm works from its Kansas City home office and says so plainly.
In practice that costs a Mountain View case nothing. Records move
electronically, interviews run by secure video when the referral permits,
and when an in-person evaluation is warranted it is scheduled in the
community where the evaluee lives. Deposition and trial appearances are
planned from the home office around dates counsel already holds, and
availability is committed at intake rather than promised loosely. The same
model supports the firm's [nationwide practice](/locations/nationwide/).

## Starting with a conflict check

Some referrals arrive as a fresh damages question; others arrive as an
opposing report that needs testing, which is the province of
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/). Either way, the
door in is the same. Provide the caption or party names through the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/), describe the referral questions, and
list the dates that govern the case. Scope, fees, and delivery are confirmed
in writing before the analysis begins, and for matters pairing vocational
work with economic loss,
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
place the economic analysis with independent economist partners who perform
and sign it themselves.
