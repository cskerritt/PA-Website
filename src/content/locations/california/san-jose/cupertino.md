board a plane. The opinion lives in the file: medical and employment
records, testing and interview notes, labor market sources, and the written
analysis connecting them. Purinton Analytics builds Cupertino engagements

[vocational expert evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/) through a
remote-first process that plaintiff and defense counsel retain on equal
footing, with in-person steps added only where the referral genuinely needs
them.

## Labor market evidence for Cupertino matters

Vocational conclusions about a Cupertino evaluee draw on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the San
Jose-Sunnyvale-Santa Clara metropolitan area and for California statewide,
supported by state workforce agency labor market information and, where a
disputed question warrants, direct labor market research. The data is
treated as evidence, which means it gets cited, dated, and bounded: the
report identifies which estimates were used, what they cover, and where
their limits fall, so that cross-examination confirms the method instead of
exposing it. Transferable skills analysis receives the same treatment: the
work history is taken from records and interview, the skills are named, and
the occupational matches are justified against published requirements
rather than asserted from experience alone. An opinion assembled this way
can be handed to either side of the caption without embarrassment.

## What by-appointment service means here

Cupertino work is handled without a local office, and the firm says so
rather than implying otherwise; its home office is in Kansas City, and
meetings anywhere else happen by appointment. For the evaluee, that means
interviews by secure video where appropriate, or an in-person evaluation
arranged in the community where the evaluee actually lives. For counsel, it
means records move electronically, drafts arrive on the dates committed at
intake, and deposition or trial appearances are scheduled from the Kansas
City home office with enough lead time to be dependable. The model scales
from a single Cupertino referral to the firm's full
[nationwide practice](/locations/nationwide/).

## Submitting a Cupertino referral

The engagement door is the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/): caption or
party names, venue, referral questions, and the dates already on calendar.
Once conflicts clear, scope and fees are confirmed in writing. Counsel
needing an opposing report examined can add
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/), and matters
requiring economic loss analysis can proceed as
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/),
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work moves under one
engagement structure.
