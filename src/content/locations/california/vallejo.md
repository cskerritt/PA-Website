Damages cases rarely turn on a single expert discipline, and coordinating
the experts involved is often where counsel lose time. For attorneys in the Vallejo metro,
Purinton Analytics structures that coordination from the outset:
[vocational evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) of earning
capacity and employability,
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) for catastrophic injury
claims, and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. Plaintiff and defense referrals move through the same
intake, the same conflict check, and the same reporting standard, so the
finished opinions hold together instead of arriving as disconnected exhibits.

## Solano County and California's federal courts

State court matters are accepted in the Superior Court in Solano County, the
trial court serving Vallejo and the surrounding communities. In federal
practice, engagements are accepted across the state's district courts: the
United States District Court for the Northern District of California, the
United States District Court for the Eastern District of California, the
United States District Court for the Central District of California, and the
United States District Court for the Southern District of California. For
related matters venued elsewhere in the state, the
[California locations page](/locations/california/) lists the firm's other
covered metros.

## Presence in Vallejo, stated accurately

The firm maintains no staffed office in Vallejo and publishes no street
address for this location. Meetings with counsel and evaluees are by
appointment, and the firm says so plainly because an expert who is careful
about small facts is more credible on large ones. Engagements typically
begin remotely, with records review, structured interviews, and
standardized testing under publisher protocols where remote administration
is supported. When in-person evaluation is warranted, or when deposition and
trial dates in Solano County require attendance, travel is scheduled from
the Kansas City home office and confirmed with counsel early enough to
protect the calendar. Counsel receive the same interview structure and the
same reporting format in either mode, so nothing about the opinion depends
on where the work happened to be performed.

## Labor market evidence for Solano County evaluees

Vocational conclusions rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational
employment and wage estimates for the Vallejo metropolitan area and for
California statewide, together with state workforce agency labor market
information and direct labor market research where warranted. Life care
plans and medical cost projections are priced to the evaluee's own market
through provider-specific inquiries and published regional fee data. Because
the economic analysis in a coordinated engagement is performed and signed by
independent economist partners, the wage, employability, and care-cost
foundations are documented so that each opinion can stand on its own under
cross-examination, in Solano County or in
[any other venue the firm serves](/locations/nationwide/).

## How to begin

The engagement starts with clearance. Submit the caption or party names
through the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and identify any
disclosure, deposition, or trial dates already set. Once conflicts are
cleared, counsel receive a written scope covering each coordinated
component, a records request tailored to the claims, and a schedule matched
to the case, from first records review through signed reports and, where
needed, testimony.
