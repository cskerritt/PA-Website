A vocational or life care planning opinion is only as strong as the record
behind it, and that is the premise on which Purinton Analytics serves the
Bakersfield-Delano metro. Every engagement produces a written analysis in
which the methodology is stated, the sources are cited, and the reasoning
can be traced step by step, whether the referral is for
[vocational expert evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), or
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. Plaintiff and defense counsel receive identical
rigor.

## Kern County venues, state and federal

State court matters are accepted in the Superior Court of Kern County,
which carries the metro's civil caseload. Federal engagements are accepted
throughout California, including the United States District Court for the
Eastern District of California, and equally the United States District
Court for the Northern District of California, the United States District
Court for the Central District of California, and the United States
District Court for the Southern District of California when a related
matter is venued there. A report prepared for Kern County litigation is
written to the same standard the firm applies in any federal forum.

## Appointment-based presence in Bakersfield

The firm keeps no staffed office in Bakersfield or Delano and publishes no
street address for this location. That is stated plainly because honesty
about presence and honesty about data come from the same habit. Meetings
are by appointment, and most engagements begin remotely with records
review, structured vocational interviews, and standardized testing under
publisher protocols where remote administration is supported. When the
referral questions call for in-person evaluation, or when deposition and
trial dates require attendance, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City
home office and confirmed with counsel before deadlines tighten.
Scheduling commitments are made in writing, and counsel work with the same
engagement team from intake through testimony.

## The Kern County labor market in the analysis

Vocational findings for Kern County evaluees rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Bakersfield
metropolitan area and for California statewide, supplemented by state
workforce agency labor market information and direct labor market research
where the referral warrants it. Employment conditions in this metro are not
interchangeable with those elsewhere in the state, and an earning capacity
opinion that borrows another market's data invites impeachment. Life care
plans and [medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/)
are priced to the evaluee's own community, with each source identified in
the report.

## Starting a Bakersfield engagement

Engagements follow the same intake sequence used across the firm's
[California practice](/locations/california/) and its
[national coverage](/locations/nationwide/): a documented conflict check, a
defined scope, and a records request tailored to the claims. To confirm
availability for a Kern County matter,
[request a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) with the case caption and
parties, noting any disclosure, deposition, or trial dates already set.
Once the check clears, counsel receive a written engagement confirmation
and a schedule aligned to the court's calendar rather than to the expert's
convenience.
