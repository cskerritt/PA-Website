Every opinion Purinton Analytics issues in a Selma matter is written for the reader who wants it to fail: opposing counsel preparing cross-examination. That standard shapes the methodology before it shapes the prose, and it applies equally to [vocational expert evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) in injury and employment cases, to [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) in catastrophic ones, and to [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement structure. Because the firm is retained by plaintiffs' counsel in some matters and defense counsel in others, the discipline is not a posture; it is the product.

Forum shapes procedure but never the analytical standard: the same written opinion must hold up in either courthouse. Regional context for the surrounding communities is collected on the [Fresno metro page](/locations/california/fresno/), and the statewide [California practice](/locations/california/) page describes the rest.

## The labor market behind a Selma opinion

A conclusion about what a Selma resident can earn after injury has to name its evidence. The firm draws on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Fresno metropolitan area and for California statewide, on state workforce agency labor market information, and on direct labor market research when the referral questions require contact with the market itself. The report walks the reader from source to inference in order, because an opinion that cannot show its path from data to conclusion invites the cross-examination it should have foreclosed. Assumptions are labeled as assumptions and kept separate from findings, so the reader is never asked to accept an inference on faith.

## Appointments, evaluations, and travel

The firm has no Selma office and says so plainly; by-appointment service, honestly described, is what counsel are retaining. Records analysis, structured vocational interviews, and standardized testing under publisher protocols proceed remotely where remote administration is supported. Where an in-person evaluation is warranted, it is conducted in the evaluee's own community, and life care plans and [medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/) price care against that community's providers with each source cited. Deposition and trial attendance is met by travel from the Kansas City home office, scheduled with counsel around the operative dates, and remote testimony is available where the court and the parties allow it. Preparation for any appearance includes a candid review of the report's own vulnerabilities, because the questions worth anticipating are the ones counsel opposite will actually ask.

## Requesting a conflict check

Engagement begins the way it does across the firm's [national coverage](/locations/nationwide/): with a documented conflict check before any substantive discussion of the merits. Counsel should [submit the caption and party names](/refer-a-case/) and note any disclosure, deposition, or trial dates already fixed. Once the check clears, the firm confirms scope in writing and issues a records request tailored to the claims, so the opinion that eventually reaches the other side rests on a record both parties can examine.
