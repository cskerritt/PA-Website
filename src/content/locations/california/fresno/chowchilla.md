## Reading Chowchilla's labor market

A small Madera County community is still part of a substantial regional economy, and an employability opinion must respect both facts at once. The firm grounds its findings in Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Fresno metropolitan area and for California statewide, supplemented by state workforce agency labor market information and by direct labor market research when the referral questions turn on what specific employers actually require.
