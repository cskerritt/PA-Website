## Earning capacity in the surrounding market

Where residual work capacity exists, it is measured against the market a Kingsburg evaluee can actually reach. The firm relies on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Fresno metropolitan area and for California statewide, on state workforce agency labor market information, and on direct labor market research when the referral questions warrant employer-level detail. Where the injuries foreclose work altogether, the report says so and shows why, rather than padding a plan with token placements; where capacity remains, the data defines it. Either finding is documented to withstand the other side's reading, and family caregiving already in place is recorded rather than assumed away, since the analysis must reflect the household as it actually exists.
