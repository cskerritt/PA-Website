## Kerman inside the Fresno labor market

Kerman is a small community whose residents participate in a far larger regional economy, and the analysis reflects that scale honestly. Employability and earning capacity findings rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Fresno metropolitan area and for California statewide, on state workforce agency labor market information, and on direct labor market research when a referral question turns on what a particular class of employers actually hires for. The report distinguishes what each series measures from what it merely suggests, identifies the geography each covers, and refuses to let a statewide figure stand in for a local one or the reverse. That transparency is deliberate: an opinion whose data can be pulled and re-run by the opposing expert is an opinion that survives. The report also notes when each series was collected and published, so dated material is never passed off as current.

## Conducting the evaluation

Kerman appointments are arranged case by case; the firm neither staffs nor advertises a local office, and it puts that fact in writing rather than imply presence it does not have. Early-stage work proceeds remotely: records analysis, structured vocational interviews, and standardized testing under publisher protocols where remote administration is supported. An in-person evaluation, when warranted, is conducted in the evaluee's own community, and [medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/) and life care plans price future care against that community's providers with each source cited. Travel for depositions and trial testimony is scheduled from the Kansas City home office once counsel confirm the dates.
