## Parlier's county and court

Parlier is located in Fresno County, and state court matters arising there proceed in the Superior Court of Fresno County. Federal engagements are accepted anywhere in California, including in the United States District Court for the Eastern District of California, whether the assignment is an affirmative opinion or a review of someone else's. The [Fresno metro page](/locations/california/fresno/) collects the regional venue detail, and the firm's [California practice](/locations/california/) page covers the remainder of the state.

## Grounding a Parlier opinion in the labor market

Affirmative and rebuttal work draw on the same well: Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Fresno metropolitan area and for California statewide, state workforce agency labor market information, and direct labor market research where the referral questions demand it. In an affirmative report those sources carry the findings; in a rebuttal they become the measuring stick, because the fastest way to test an opposing opinion about a Parlier evaluee is to check whether its data covers the geography and occupations it claims to. Either way, the analysis names its sources so the reader can repeat it. A rebuttal engagement concludes with a written review memorandum or a disclosed report, as counsel elect, and its criticisms are confined to what the record supports.

## Logistics for a Parlier evaluee

The firm does not keep an office in Parlier and does not pretend to; service is by appointment. Records review, structured vocational interviews, and standardized testing under publisher protocols are handled remotely where remote administration is supported, and an in-person evaluation, when the questions call for it, takes place in the evaluee's own community. [Medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/) and life care plans are priced to that community, sources cited. When depositions or trial require the expert in the room, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office around the dates counsel provide. Review-only assignments often require no evaluee contact at all, which shortens the timeline considerably.

## First step for a Parlier matter

Whether the assignment is a new evaluation or a review of an opposing disclosure, intake follows the sequence used across the firm's [national coverage](/locations/nationwide/): conflict check, scope, tailored records request. Counsel should [submit a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) with the caption and party names, attach or describe any report to be reviewed, and note the operative disclosure, deposition, and trial dates. A written engagement confirmation and schedule follow once the check clears. Where both an affirmative opinion and a review are needed, the scopes are defined separately so each deliverable stays clean.
