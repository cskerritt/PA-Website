## How the evaluation happens

The firm maintains no storefront in Sanger and lists no street address here; what counsel get instead is an evaluation brought to the evaluee. Records review, structured vocational interviews, and standardized testing under publisher protocols proceed remotely where remote administration is supported, which spares an injured person an unnecessary trip. Where the questions require an in-person evaluation, it is scheduled in the evaluee's community, and [medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/) and life care plans are priced against that community's actual providers and costs, each source cited. Depositions and trial appearances are met through travel from the Kansas City home office, planned with counsel around the settings that matter, and remote testimony is likewise supported where the court and the parties permit it, which keeps a hearing from becoming another demand on the household.
