Counsel selecting an expert for a matter in Fresno County or Madera County
are ultimately selecting a written opinion: the report that will be
disclosed, deposed on, and examined by the other side. Purinton Analytics
builds that report the same way in the Fresno metro as in every market it
serves nationally, offering
[vocational expert evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) in
injury and employment matters,
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) in catastrophic cases,
and [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure, available to plaintiff and defense counsel alike.

## Venue footprint for the Fresno metro

Engagements are accepted for matters in the Superior Court of Fresno County
and the Superior Court of Madera County. Federal matters are accepted
throughout the state, in the United States District Court for the Eastern
District of California, the United States District Court for the Northern
District of California, the United States District Court for the Central
District of California, and the United States District Court for the
Southern District of California. Whatever the forum, retaining counsel on
either side of the caption receive an opinion built to be examined by the
other.

## Serving Fresno by appointment

Fresno meetings are by appointment. The firm has no staffed office in the
metro and publishes no street address for this location, and it says so
directly because accuracy about presence is part of the same discipline
that governs its reports. Most engagements begin remotely: records review,
structured vocational interviews, and standardized testing under publisher
protocols where remote administration is supported. When the referral
questions require an in-person evaluation, or when deposition or trial
attendance is needed, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office
and coordinated with counsel around the dates that matter. Remote
testimony is supported where the court and the parties permit it, an
option that often simplifies scheduling for regional counsel.

## Labor market research for Fresno County evaluees

Vocational findings for evaluees in Fresno County and Madera County rest on
Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for
the Fresno metropolitan area and for California statewide, together with
state workforce agency labor market information and direct labor market
research where warranted. An opinion is only as testable as its sourcing,
so the report documents each series relied on and states what it does and
does not show before any conclusion is drawn from it. Life care plans and
[medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/) are priced
to the evaluee's own community, with every source cited.

## Starting a Fresno engagement

The firm's [California practice](/locations/california/) uses the intake
sequence applied across its [national coverage](/locations/nationwide/): a
documented conflict check, a defined scope, and a records request tailored
to the claims, so the written opinion rests on a record both sides can
examine. To begin, [request a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) with the case
caption and parties, and identify any disclosure deadlines, depositions, or
trial settings already scheduled. Counsel then receive a written engagement
confirmation and a working schedule keyed to the court's calendar.
