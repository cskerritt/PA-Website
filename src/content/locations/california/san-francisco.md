Plaintiff and defense counsel in the San Francisco-Oakland-Fremont metro
rarely agree on what a damages case is worth, but they ask the same things
of a vocational and care-planning expert: a stated methodology, sources
that can be checked, and conclusions that follow from the record rather
than from the side that sent the referral. Purinton Analytics is built
around that common ground, accepting retentions from either side of the
caption on identical terms and providing
[vocational expert evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## A multi-county venue footprint

Engagements are accepted for matters in the Superior Court of San Francisco
County, the Superior Court of Alameda County, the Superior Court of Contra
Costa County, the Superior Court of San Mateo County, and the Superior Court
of Marin County. Federal matters are accepted throughout the state,
including the United States District Court for the Northern District of
California along with the United States District Court for the Eastern
District of California, the United States District Court for the Central
District of California, and the United States District Court for the
Southern District of California. Wherever the matter sits, both sides of the
caption receive the same report structure and the same documented
methodology.

## Meetings by appointment only

The firm maintains no staffed office in San Francisco, Oakland, or Fremont
and publishes no street address for this location; candor about presence is
part of the discipline counsel are retaining. Most engagements begin
remotely with records review, structured interviews, and standardized
testing under publisher protocols where remote administration is supported.
When the referral calls for in-person evaluation, or when deposition and
trial calendars require attendance, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City
home office and confirmed against the case deadlines counsel provide at
intake, so that presence is planned rather than improvised. Counsel work
with the same expert and the same engagement team from intake through
trial, regardless of the county in which the matter is pending.

## Labor market research for the metro

Vocational findings for evaluees in the metro rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the San Francisco
metropolitan area and for California statewide, along with state workforce
agency labor market information and direct labor market research where the
questions warrant it. The report identifies the geographic area each
estimate covers, so the reader can verify that a finding rests on data for
the market actually at issue rather than take that on faith. Life care
plans and
[medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/) are priced to
the evaluee's own community, with every source cited for examination by
opposing counsel.

## Starting a San Francisco engagement

The intake sequence is the one used across the firm's
[California practice](/locations/california/) and its
[national footprint](/locations/nationwide/): documented conflict check,
defined scope, tailored records request. Submit the caption or party names
through the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and flag any imminent
disclosure, deposition, or trial dates. After clearance, counsel receive a
written engagement confirmation and a schedule aligned to the court's
calendar, whether the matter is pending in San Francisco County, in Alameda
County or Contra Costa County, or in federal court.
