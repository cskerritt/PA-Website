the way every Purinton Analytics engagement begins: with a documented

claims, so that the written opinion rests on a record both sides can

## Stanislaus County venues

State court matters are accepted in the Superior Court in Stanislaus County,
the trial court serving Modesto and the surrounding communities. Federal
matters are accepted throughout the state's district courts: the United
States District Court for the Northern District of California, the United
States District Court for the Eastern District of California, the United
States District Court for the Central District of California, and the United
States District Court for the Southern District of California. Counsel whose
dockets extend beyond the metro can review the firm's other covered venues
on the [California locations page](/locations/california/).

## How work gets done at a distance

Modesto meetings are by appointment. The firm maintains no staffed office in
the city and publishes no street address for this location, a fact stated
here rather than obscured, because candor about presence is part of the
credibility an expert brings to the stand. Records review, structured
interviews, and standardized testing typically proceed remotely, under
publisher protocols where remote administration is supported. When the
referral requires an in-person evaluation, or when deposition and trial
dates call for presence in Stanislaus County, travel is scheduled from the
Kansas City home office and coordinated with counsel well ahead of the
appearance.

## Labor market and cost foundations

Opinions about a Modesto evaluee are grounded in Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates for the Modesto metropolitan area
and for California statewide, alongside state workforce agency labor market
information and direct labor market research where warranted. Life care
plans and medical cost projections are priced to the evaluee's own market
using provider-specific inquiries and published fee data for the region.
Where a plan spans provider types, the pricing basis for each item is stated
so the projection can be audited line by line. None of this is unique to
Modesto. It is the same evidentiary foundation the
firm builds for [matters across the country](/locations/nationwide/), which
is why a report prepared for a Stanislaus County jury withstands the same
scrutiny as one prepared for any other forum.

## Beginning the engagement

Availability is confirmed through clearance, not negotiation. Submit the
caption or party names through the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/),
and identify any imminent disclosure, deposition, or trial dates. Once
conflicts are cleared, counsel receive a written scope, a tailored records
request, and a realistic schedule, and the work proceeds on that footing
whether the matter settles early or is tried to verdict.
