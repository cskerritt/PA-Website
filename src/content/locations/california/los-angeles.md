Few regions generate a broader range of disputed earning capacity and care
questions than the Los Angeles-Long Beach-Anaheim metro, one of the largest
labor markets in the country. Purinton Analytics supports plaintiff and
defense counsel throughout the region with
[vocational expert evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) in
injury and employment matters,
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) in catastrophic injury
cases, and [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Venue footprint across the metro

Engagements are accepted for matters venued in the Superior Court of Los
Angeles County and the Superior Court of Orange County, the state trial
courts that carry the metro's civil caseload. Federal matters are accepted
throughout California, including the United States District Court for the
Central District of California, the United States District Court for the
Northern District of California, the United States District Court for the
Eastern District of California, and the United States District Court for the
Southern District of California. Retaining counsel on either side of the
caption receive the same report structure, the same disclosure practice, and
the same methodology regardless of forum.

## By appointment, without a local storefront

Los Angeles meetings are by appointment. The firm keeps no staffed office in
the metro and publishes no street address for this location, because
accuracy about presence is part of the same discipline that governs its
reports. Most engagements begin remotely, with records review, structured
interviews, and standardized testing administered under publisher protocols
where remote administration is supported. When the referral questions call
for in-person evaluation, or when deposition and trial calendars require
presence, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office and planned
around the case deadlines counsel identify at intake.

## Labor market research for Los Angeles evaluees

Vocational findings for evaluees from Los Angeles, Long Beach, Anaheim, and
the surrounding communities rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational
employment and wage estimates for the Los Angeles metropolitan area and for
California statewide, supplemented by state workforce agency labor market
information and direct employer research where warranted. Life care plans
and [medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/) are
priced to the evaluee's own market rather than to national averages, so that
the written opinion reflects the costs a Southern California resident would
actually face. Every source is cited in the report so that opposing counsel
can trace each figure to its origin.

## Starting a Los Angeles engagement

The firm's [California practice](/locations/california/) runs on the same
intake sequence used [nationwide](/locations/nationwide/): a documented
conflict check, a defined scope, and a records request tailored to the
claims, so that the written opinion rests on a record both sides can
examine. Submit the caption or party names through the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and note any imminent disclosure,
deposition, or trial dates. Once the conflict check clears, counsel receive
a written engagement confirmation, a records request, and a schedule aligned
to the court's deadlines, whether the matter sits in Los Angeles County,
Orange County, or a federal courtroom elsewhere in the state.
