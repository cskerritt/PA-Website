## The courts that hear Garden Grove cases

Garden Grove lies in Orange County, and its state-court civil matters are
ordinarily venued in the Superior Court of that county. Federal litigation
proceeds in the United States District Court for the Central District of
California; engagements in the state's other federal districts are
accepted on identical terms. The venue is recorded at intake together with
the operative complaint and any scheduling order, because scope in a
Garden Grove matter is set against real dates, not hypothetical ones, and
an honest assessment of feasibility is part of the intake conversation.
The uniform process continues past intake: the same records checklist is
built from the pleadings, the same review sequence moves from medical
evidence to functional capacity to vocational analysis, and the same
internal review precedes every signed report, whichever party retained it.

## Serving Garden Grove evaluees

Garden Grove meetings occur by appointment; the firm has no staffed office
in the city and publishes no address there, and says so plainly because
accurate statements about presence are part of the same habit as accurate
statements about damages. Records review, interviews, and supported
standardized testing proceed remotely first, under publisher protocols
wherever remote administration is sanctioned. When in-person evaluation is
warranted, it is conducted in the evaluee's own community rather than
forcing the evaluee to travel, and trips for evaluations or testimony are
scheduled from the Kansas City home office with lead time measured against
the disclosure calendar. The report notes how each component was
administered, so the process the engagement promised at intake is the
process the record shows at the end.
