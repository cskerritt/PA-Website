Where can this person actually work? For a Santa Clarita evaluee that
question is rarely answered inside city limits, because a large city that
sits within the Los Angeles-Long Beach-Anaheim, CA metropolitan area gives
its residents an employment radius far wider than the city itself.
Purinton Analytics frames every Santa Clarita
[earning capacity evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) around
that commuting reality, defining the labor market the evaluee can
realistically reach and defending that definition in the report. The firm
serves plaintiff and defense counsel from the city with vocational
opinions, [life care plans](/services/life-care-planning/) in catastrophic
cases, and [medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/)
where future care is defined but a comprehensive plan is not required.

## Venue in Los Angeles County

Santa Clarita is part of Los Angeles County, so its state-court civil
matters are ordinarily heard in the Superior Court of that county. Federal
cases proceed in the United States District Court for the Central District
of California, with the state's remaining federal districts covered under
the same national engagement model. Intake for a Santa Clarita referral
confirms the venue and the calendar first, because disclosure and
discovery schedules, not geography, are what usually constrain the work.
The conflict check is documented before any substantive material is
reviewed, scope is stated in writing, and the records request is drafted
against the operative pleadings rather than a generic checklist, so the
file beneath the opinion is a file both sides can inspect.

## Defining the relevant labor market

A commuting-market analysis stands or falls on its sources and on the
order of its steps. The evaluation moves from the medical record to
residual functional capacity, from work history to transferable skills,
and only then to the occupations those skills realistically support within
the market the evaluee can reach. Findings for Santa Clarita evaluees rest
on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates
for the Los Angeles-Long Beach-Anaheim, CA metropolitan area and for
California statewide, alongside state workforce agency labor market
information and direct employer inquiry where a referral question demands
it. The report states the geographic frame it uses and explains why that
frame fits the evaluee's circumstances, so opposing counsel can challenge
the frame itself rather than guess at it. No wage conclusion is drawn
until restrictions, transferable skills, and residence have been weighed
together, and each conclusion cites the table or inquiry that supports it.

## Logistics for a Santa Clarita evaluee

The firm meets Santa Clarita evaluees by appointment and keeps no staffed
office or published address in the city. Remote methods carry most of the
early work: electronic records review, structured interviews conducted by
video, and standardized testing administered under the publisher's
remote-administration protocols where those exist. When an instrument
requires supervised in-person administration, or when the referral
questions call for direct observation, the evaluation is conducted in the
evaluee's own community rather than asking an injured person to travel.
Appearances for deposition and trial are arranged through travel from the
Kansas City home office, coordinated early against counsel's dates so that
expert availability never becomes the pressure point in the discovery
calendar. The report discloses how each component of the evaluation was
administered, remotely or in person, so the method itself is on the
record.
