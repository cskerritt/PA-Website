Long Beach is prominent enough to appear in the formal name of its own
metropolitan area, the Los Angeles-Long Beach-Anaheim, CA region, and the
litigation that arises in a city of that scale calls for damages testimony
prepared with unusual care. Purinton Analytics serves plaintiff and defense
counsel in Long Beach matters with
[earning capacity and employability evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) in catastrophic injury
cases, and [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. Every Long Beach file is held to the publication
standard of the firm's [national practice](/locations/nationwide/): written
to be read closely by the lawyer on the other side.

## Los Angeles County venue and courts

Long Beach sits in Los Angeles County, and civil actions arising in the city
are ordinarily filed in the Superior Court there. Matters that belong in
federal court proceed in the United States District Court for the Central
District of California, and the firm also accepts California engagements
venued in the state's other federal districts. Venue changes little about
the work itself. A Long Beach engagement opens with a documented conflict
check and a defined written scope, and the records request is tailored to
the pleadings so that the eventual opinion rests on materials both sides can
inspect and challenge.

## A major city inside a broader labor market

Long Beach is a major city in its own right, yet its residents work within
one of the broadest labor markets in the country. Vocational findings for
Long Beach evaluees therefore rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates for the Los Angeles-Long
Beach-Anaheim, CA metropolitan area and for California statewide,
supplemented by state workforce agency labor market information and direct
employer contact where the referral questions warrant it. Transferable
skills analysis considers the occupations realistically open to the evaluee
across the region rather than inside city limits alone, and the report
states that geographic frame explicitly so it can be tested on
cross-examination rather than assumed.

## Evaluating a Long Beach resident

Meetings in Long Beach are by appointment. The firm keeps no staffed office
in the city and publishes no local street address, because candor about
presence belongs to the same discipline that governs the reports. Most
engagements begin remotely: records review, structured interviews by video,
and standardized testing administered under publisher protocols where remote
administration is supported. When the referral calls for in-person
evaluation, the assessment is scheduled in the evaluee's own community, and
when deposition or trial requires attendance, travel is arranged from the
Kansas City home office around counsel's calendar rather than the other way
around.
