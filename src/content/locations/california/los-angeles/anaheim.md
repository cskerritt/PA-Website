## Orange County courts serving Anaheim

Anaheim lies in Orange County, and civil matters arising in the city are
ordinarily heard in the Superior Court there rather than in the courts of
the metro's principal city of Los Angeles. Federal-question and diversity
cases proceed in the United States District Court for the Central District
of California, and engagements venued in California's other federal
district courts are accepted on the same terms. The distinction between
county venues matters most for scheduling and disclosure practice, so
intake for an Anaheim referral records the court, the assigned dates, and
the operative pleadings before any scope is set.

## The labor market around Anaheim

Anaheim shares its name with the Los Angeles-Long Beach-Anaheim, CA
metropolitan area, and its workforce participates in a regional labor
market of remarkable breadth. Employability and wage findings for Anaheim
evaluees draw on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and
wage estimates published for that metropolitan area and for California as a
whole, together with state workforce agency data and targeted labor market
research when a specific occupation or employer question drives the case.
Findings never stand alone in the report: each conclusion is tied to an
identified source so opposing counsel can pull the same tables and check
the reasoning line by line.
