The Superior Court in Orange County anchors most of the civil litigation
that reaches Purinton Analytics from Santa Ana, and the firm builds its
Santa Ana engagements around the rhythms of that court: pleadings first,
then a defined scope, then a records request broad enough to support an
opinion and narrow enough to defend. Plaintiff and defense counsel retain
the firm for [vocational expert](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) work
on employability and earning capacity,
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) in serious injury
cases, and [forensic economic damages](/services/forensic-economic-damages/)
engagements in which independent economist partners perform and sign the
economic analysis coordinated by the firm.

## County and federal venues

Santa Ana matters in state court proceed in the Superior Court of Orange
County. Cases filed in or removed to federal court are heard in the United
States District Court for the Central District of California, and the firm
accepts engagements across the state's other federal district courts as
well. None of this changes the method. Whether the caption reads
plaintiff-side or defense-side, the analysis follows the same sequence, and
the resulting report is written so that the opposing expert, not just
retaining counsel, can follow every step from source to conclusion.

## Reading the Santa Ana labor market

Santa Ana is a major city within the Los Angeles-Long Beach-Anaheim, CA
metropolitan area, and its residents compete in a labor market that
stretches across county lines. Employability and wage conclusions for
Santa Ana evaluees are grounded in Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational
employment and wage estimates for that metropolitan area and for
California statewide, along with state workforce agency labor market
information. Where a case turns on a particular occupation or industry,
direct labor market research supplements the published series, and the
report identifies which findings rest on which source so the foundation can
be examined rather than guessed at.

## Meeting evaluees where they are

Santa Ana work is handled by appointment. The firm has no staffed office in
the city and publishes no street address for it, a deliberate choice:
claims about presence should be as accurate as claims about damages.
Evaluations typically open remotely with records review and structured
interviews, standardized testing follows publisher protocols for remote
administration where supported, and in-person assessment, when the
referral requires it, takes place in the evaluee's own community with
travel scheduled from the Kansas City home office. Deposition and trial
appearances are calendared the same way, with dates confirmed early.
