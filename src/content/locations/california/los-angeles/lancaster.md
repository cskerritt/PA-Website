Labor market evidence is only as good as the match between the data and
the evaluee, and that match takes deliberate work in a Lancaster case.
Purinton Analytics builds vocational findings for Lancaster evaluees from
Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for
the Los Angeles-Long Beach-Anaheim, CA metropolitan area and for
California statewide, read together with state workforce agency labor
market information and, where warranted, direct labor market research into
the occupations actually at issue. On that foundation the firm delivers
[earning capacity and employability opinions](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care plans](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[forensic economic damages](/services/forensic-economic-damages/)
engagements in which independent economist partners perform and sign the
economic analysis while the firm's vocational and care-planning work
proceeds in parallel.

## Los Angeles County venue for Lancaster matters

Lancaster's state-court civil litigation is ordinarily heard in the
Superior Court of Los Angeles County, and its federal matters proceed in
the United States District Court for the Central District of California;
the firm also takes California engagements venued in the state's other
federal districts. Intake confirms the court, the calendar, and the
pleadings before any analysis begins, and each engagement opens with a
documented conflict check and a scope statement both sides could read
without objection. The records request that follows is built from the
claims and defenses actually pleaded, because the data-to-evaluee match
that anchors the analysis starts with a complete picture of the evaluee:
medical restrictions, education, work history, and earnings records all
arrive before any occupational matching begins.

## A distinct corner of a large metro

Lancaster belongs to the Los Angeles-Long Beach-Anaheim, CA metropolitan
area, yet a careful analysis never assumes that metro-wide conditions
describe every community within it. For a Lancaster evaluee the report
addresses which occupations are realistically available to that person
given residence, restrictions, and transferable skills, and it states the
geographic frame it applies together with the reasons that frame fits.
Where the published metropolitan series from the Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage program needs local grounding,
direct employer and occupational inquiry supplies it, and the report
distinguishes findings drawn from published tables from findings drawn
from that inquiry. Every conclusion traces to an identified source that
opposing counsel can retrieve and test, which is the whole point of
matching data to the evaluee rather than the other way around.

## Evaluations for Lancaster residents

The firm serves Lancaster by appointment, with no staffed office and no
published address in the city. Most engagements start remotely: records
arrive electronically, interviews run by video, and standardized
instruments are administered according to publisher protocols when remote
use is supported; instruments without a sanctioned remote protocol wait
for the in-person session rather than being improvised. When an in-person
evaluation is called for, it is held in the evaluee's own community rather
than requiring the evaluee to travel, and appearances for deposition or
trial are arranged through travel from the Kansas City home office with
dates confirmed early against the disclosure calendar. The report records
how each component was administered so the method is as reviewable as the
findings.
