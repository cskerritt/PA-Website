Most vocational evaluations of Irvine residents begin without anyone
boarding a plane. Records arrive electronically, the clinical interview is
conducted by video, and standardized instruments are administered under
publisher protocols wherever remote administration is validated, so an
[employability and earning capacity evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/)
is often well underway before any in-person meeting is scheduled. Purinton
Analytics serves plaintiff and defense counsel in Irvine matters on that
remote-first model, with [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/)
for catastrophic injuries and
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) of opposing
expert work rounding out the engagements most often referred from the city.

## Where Irvine cases are heard

Irvine is an Orange County city, and its civil litigation is ordinarily
venued in the Superior Court for that county. When a matter belongs in
federal court it proceeds in the United States District Court for the
Central District of California, and the firm likewise accepts California
cases pending in the state's other federal districts. Wherever the case
sits, the same working rule applies: the opinion is only as strong as the
record beneath it, so the engagement starts with a conflict check, a
written scope, and a records request matched to the claims and defenses
actually pleaded rather than to a generic checklist.

## Irvine within the regional economy

Irvine is a large city inside the Los Angeles-Long Beach-Anaheim, CA
metropolitan area, and an Irvine evaluee's realistic employment options
extend well beyond the city itself. Vocational findings therefore rest on
Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for
the metropolitan area and for California statewide, joined by state
workforce agency labor market information and, where the questions require
it, direct research into specific occupations. The report describes the
labor market frame it applies and cites every source it relies on, so that
counsel on both sides can trace each finding back to a published table.

## In-person work, by appointment

When remote methods are not enough, in-person evaluation is arranged in the
evaluee's own community, and testimony is given wherever the court
requires. The firm keeps no staffed office in Irvine and publishes no local
address; presence in the city is by appointment, with travel scheduled from
the Kansas City home office. Counsel receive a consistent point of contact
and a consistent report standard whether the work was performed remotely,
in Irvine, or through a mix of both, and the report itself discloses how
each component of the evaluation was administered.
