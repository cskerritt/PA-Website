## By-appointment logistics from the home office

The firm keeps no staffed office in San Jose, Sunnyvale, or Santa Clara and
publishes no street address for this location; that candor is deliberate,
because a practice that is precise about its data should be precise about
its presence. Engagements usually begin remotely with records review,
structured interviews, and standardized testing under publisher protocols
where remote administration is supported. When in-person evaluation is
warranted or testimony requires attendance, travel is scheduled from the
Kansas City home office against the dates counsel identify, with
confirmation in writing before the deadline pressure begins.

## Reading the San Jose labor market

Vocational findings for evaluees in the metro rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the San Jose
metropolitan area and for California statewide, supplemented by state
workforce agency labor market information and direct labor market research
where the referral questions call for it. When the published series leave a
referral question open, direct labor market research fills the gap, and the
report describes how that research was conducted so it can be probed in
deposition. Life care plans and
[medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/) are priced
to the evaluee's own community, and every source is cited in the report for
the other side to examine.

Intake follows the firm's uniform sequence, applied across its
