The questions Wisconsin counsel bring to a vocational expert are concrete:
what work an injured or displaced person can still perform, what that work
pays in the evaluee's own labor market, and what a lifetime of care will
require when an injury is catastrophic. Purinton Analytics answers those
questions for plaintiff and defense counsel across Wisconsin with written
opinions built for cross-examination, whether the matter proceeds in a
Circuit Court or in federal court, and regardless of which side of the
caption made the referral.

## Working with the firm from Wisconsin

Wisconsin engagements are remote-first by design. Records arrive
electronically, vocational interviews are conducted by structured protocol,
and standardized instruments are administered remotely wherever the
publisher's protocols allow it, which keeps schedules predictable for
counsel on both sides. In-person meetings in Wisconsin are by appointment;
the firm keeps no staffed office in the state and publishes no Wisconsin
street address, a plain statement of fact that reflects the same discipline
the reports themselves follow. When an evaluation should happen in the
evaluee's community, or when deposition and trial dates require presence,
travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office under the same
procedures the firm applies to its
[national practice](/locations/nationwide/).

Vocational findings rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational
employment and wage estimates for the relevant Wisconsin metropolitan areas
and for the state as a whole, along with state workforce agency labor market
information and direct labor market research where the referral questions
warrant it. For matters that also require economic analysis, coordinated
damages engagements are available: independent economist partners perform
and sign the economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning work
proceeds within the same engagement structure.

## Qualification in Wisconsin courts

Each forum applies its own rules of evidence to expert qualification, and
the firm treats that scrutiny as a design constraint rather than an
afterthought: methods are documented, sources are cited, and disclosure
materials are kept ready for production in the form the venue expects. Any
venue-specific registration or qualification requirement is identified and
confirmed during the conflict check and intake process.

## Opening a Wisconsin engagement

Every Wisconsin matter starts the same way: with a conflict check. Provide
the caption or party names through the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and note any disclosure, deposition,
or trial deadlines that bear on timing. Plaintiff and defense referrals are
reviewed under the same standard, and counsel hear back on availability once
the conflict review is done.
