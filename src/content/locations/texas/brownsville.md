The referral questions that reach Purinton Analytics from the
Brownsville-Harlingen, TX metropolitan area tend to be direct: what work can
this person still perform, what will that work pay, and what will lifelong
care actually cost. Answering them draws on
[vocational expert witness](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) evaluation
of employability and earning capacity,
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) in catastrophic injury
matters, and [medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/)
where the dispute is narrower. When a matter also requires economic damages,
the firm offers
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under a single
engagement structure. Plaintiff and defense counsel are served on identical
terms and to an identical report standard.

District Court for the Southern District of Texas, the United States

Court for the Western District of Texas. The working method does not change

the caption can examine line by line. Counsel with matters elsewhere in the
state will find statewide coverage described on the firm's
[Texas locations page](/locations/texas/).

## Meetings by appointment in Brownsville

Brownsville meetings are by appointment. The firm keeps no staffed office in
the metro and publishes no local street address, because accuracy about
presence is part of the same discipline that governs its reports. Most
engagements begin remotely: records review, structured evaluee interviews by
secure video, and standardized testing under publisher protocols where
remote administration is supported. When the assignment calls for in-person
evaluation, or when deposition and trial calendars require attendance,
travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office and planned around the
litigation dates counsel identify at intake. Counsel receive the same point
of contact and the same reporting conventions whether the work is performed
remotely or on site.

## Labor market research grounded in local data

Vocational findings for evaluees in the Brownsville-Harlingen area rest on
Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for
the metro and for Texas statewide, supplemented by state workforce agency
labor market information and direct employer contact where the transferable
skills analysis warrants it. Opinions about what an evaluee can earn are
tied to the labor market the evaluee actually lives in, not to national
composites. Life care plans and medical cost projections follow the same
principle: recommended care is priced to local availability through
provider-specific inquiries and published fee data, so the resulting figures
can withstand cross-examination about where the numbers came from and
whether the care can actually be obtained there.
