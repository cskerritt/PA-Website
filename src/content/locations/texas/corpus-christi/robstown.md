Expert work in a Robstown case is governed by the calendar as much as by the record: disclosure deadlines, deposition windows, and trial settings arrive whether or not the opinion is ready. Purinton Analytics scopes every Robstown engagement against those dates from the outset, so that the [vocational analysis](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) or [life care plan](/services/life-care-planning/) is complete, supported, and producible when the schedule demands it rather than supplemented after the fact. Plaintiff and defense referrals are accepted on the same footing, and a rushed retention is treated as a reason for tighter scoping, never for thinner support.

## Nueces County Courts and the Federal Bench

Robstown lies in Nueces County, and engagements are accepted for matters before the District Courts that carry the county's civil docket. Federal assignments proceed in the United States District Court for the Southern District of Texas, with the state's other federal districts served on the same basis; the [Texas locations page](/locations/texas/) lays out the statewide venue picture. During the conflict check and intake process, any venue-specific qualification or registration requirement is identified and confirmed, and the disclosure schedule is captured alongside it, because an opinion that misses its deadline serves neither party.

## Robstown in the Regional Employment Picture

An earning-capacity opinion for a Robstown evaluee looks past the town itself to the commuting market of the Corpus Christi metropolitan area, which the [metro page](/locations/texas/corpus-christi/) addresses in detail. The analysis rests on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the metro area and for Texas statewide, together with state workforce agency labor market information and direct labor market research where the referral justifies it. Prose findings stay qualitative; the numbers live in the report's tables with their sources cited, so opposing counsel can verify rather than merely dispute them.

## Seeing the Evaluee Where They Live

The practice maintains no Robstown office and claims none. Engagements run by appointment: records and correspondence move electronically, conferences and many interviews are held by secure video, and in-person evaluation takes place in the evaluee's own community when the questions presented require direct observation of function. Where deposition or trial testimony calls for presence, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office. This is the same remote-first structure through which the practice serves counsel [nationwide](/locations/nationwide/), and it is described plainly here because honesty about logistics is part of the work product.

## Referring the Case

When the file needs a damages number and not only a care plan, [coordinated damages assessment](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) engages independent economist partners who perform and sign the economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning opinions develop under a shared engagement structure. A standalone [medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/) fits narrower assignments. To refer a Robstown matter, send the caption and party names through the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and state the operative deadlines up front: disclosure date, discovery cutoff, and any trial setting, so the engagement can be built backward from the dates that cannot move.
