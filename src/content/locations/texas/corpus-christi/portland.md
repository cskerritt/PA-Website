Many people who live in Portland earn their living elsewhere in the Corpus Christi metropolitan area, and a vocational opinion that stops at the town limits will misstate what work is actually available to them. Purinton Analytics builds its [vocational expert analysis](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) around the commuting market an injured Portland resident genuinely occupies, and pairs it with [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) when the injury carries lifelong care needs. Counsel on either side of the caption retain the practice on identical terms, and each report is drafted so that the opposing expert can trace every conclusion to its source.

## San Patricio County Venue and the Courts That Follow

Portland sits in San Patricio County, and engagements are accepted for civil matters in the District Courts that hear the county's docket. When a Portland case is filed federally, the practice appears in the United States District Court for the Southern District of Texas and accepts assignments in the state's other federal districts on the same basis; the [Texas locations page](/locations/texas/) describes that venue landscape in full. Any registration or qualification requirement particular to a venue is identified and confirmed during the conflict check and intake process, before scoping begins, so neither side encounters a late surprise about the expert's standing to testify.

## Reading Portland Within the Metro Labor Market

For earning-capacity purposes, a Portland evaluee is analyzed within the wider Corpus Christi labor market, which is treated in depth on the [Corpus Christi metro page](/locations/texas/corpus-christi/). The analysis draws on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the metropolitan area and for Texas statewide, supplemented by state workforce agency labor market information and, where the referral warrants it, direct employer research. Findings stay qualitative in prose and quantitative only in the tables of the report itself, with each figure keyed to a cited source that either party can pull and check.

## Appointments, Not an Office

The practice keeps no office in Portland and publishes no local address, because accuracy about presence belongs to the same discipline that governs the reports. Work proceeds by appointment: records move through secure electronic channels, conferences and many interviews run by video, and in-person evaluation is conducted in the evaluee's own community when the referral questions require it. Deposition and trial appearances are scheduled as travel from the Kansas City home office. The identical model serves referring counsel [nationwide](/locations/nationwide/), which means a Portland file receives the same process a case in any other market would.

## Starting a Portland Engagement

When the damages picture requires an economic loss calculation alongside the vocational and care-planning work, [coordinated damages assessment](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) brings in independent economist partners who perform and sign the economic analysis while the remaining opinions develop under a shared engagement structure. A [medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/) is available when the assignment is narrower than a full plan. To begin, submit the caption and party names through the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/), note any pending disclosure or deposition dates, and describe the injury and treatment posture so scope can be defined at the outset rather than renegotiated midstream.
