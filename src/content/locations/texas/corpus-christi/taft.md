The real test of an expert opinion in a Taft case comes in a deposition chair or a witness box, not on the page. Purinton Analytics writes every [vocational opinion](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) and [life care plan](/services/life-care-planning/) for that moment: conclusions stated no more broadly than the record supports, methods disclosed rather than implied, and limitations acknowledged in the report before opposing counsel can frame them as concessions. Taft engagements are accepted from plaintiff and defense counsel on identical terms, because an opinion built to withstand cross-examination serves whichever side happens to have retained it.

## Venue for a Taft Matter

Taft is part of San Patricio County, and its civil cases proceed in the District Courts responsible for the county's docket. Federal matters are accepted in the United States District Court for the Southern District of Texas, and the practice serves the state's other federal districts on the same basis, as mapped on the [Texas state page](/locations/texas/). Any expert qualification or registration requirement specific to the venue is identified and confirmed during the conflict check and intake process, keeping foundational challenges off the table before testimony is ever scheduled and sparing counsel a motion fight that careful intake would have prevented.

## The Labor Market Around Taft

An honest earning-capacity analysis for a Taft evaluee reaches beyond the town into the Corpus Christi metropolitan commuting market, examined fully on the [metro page](/locations/texas/corpus-christi/). The opinion is grounded in Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the metro area and for Texas statewide, in state workforce agency labor market information, and in direct labor market research when the file requires it. On the stand, that sourcing matters: each occupational match and wage conclusion can be walked back to a public document, which converts what might be an argument about credibility into a demonstration of method.

## Practical Logistics for Counsel and Evaluee

The practice keeps no office in Taft, and it will not blur that fact with vague language about local presence. Service is by appointment: records and drafts move through secure electronic channels, conferences and many interviews are conducted by video, and when the referral questions require in-person assessment, the evaluation takes place in the evaluee's own community. Appearances for deposition and trial are scheduled as travel from the Kansas City home office, using the same engagement model the practice applies for counsel [across the country](/locations/nationwide/).

Beyond authorship, Taft counsel retain the practice for [rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) of opposing reports and for [medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/) when the assignment is narrower than a complete plan. To refer a Taft matter, [request a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) with the caption and parties, and flag the deposition, disclosure, or trial dates the opinion must be ready to meet when the calendar turns.
