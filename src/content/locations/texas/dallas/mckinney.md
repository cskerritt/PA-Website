Purinton Analytics serves McKinney by design rather than by proximity. The
practice operates nationally from its Kansas City home office, and its
methodology was built so that distance changes almost nothing about the
work: the records travel electronically, the interviews and testing travel
to the evaluee, and the expert travels only when testimony or an in-person
assessment genuinely requires it. For plaintiff and defense counsel in
McKinney, that structure delivers
[vocational expert evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[litigation consulting and testimony](/services/expert-testimony-litigation-consulting/)
without the compromises of a part-time local presence.

## Why the structure holds up

A remote-first practice succeeds or fails on documentation, so
documentation is the constant. Every McKinney engagement carries a
documented conflict check, a written scope, and a records request tailored
to the claims before analysis begins. Standardized instruments are
administered under publisher protocols validated for remote use;
where the referral questions call for in-person evaluation instead, the
assessment is conducted in the evaluee's own community. Nothing about the
approach is improvised for distance, which is precisely what allows the
resulting opinion to read the same whether the courthouse is nearby or
across the country.

## McKinney venue in state and federal court

State-court engagements proceed in the District Courts of Collin County,
where McKinney matters are filed. In federal practice, the firm accepts
engagements in the United States District Court for the Eastern District of
Texas and in the United States District Court for the Northern District of
Texas, and it appears with the same method in the United States District
Court for the Southern District of Texas and the United States District
Court for the Western District of Texas when a case is venued in those
districts.

## Labor market findings for a McKinney evaluee

McKinney is a sizable suburban city within the Dallas-Fort Worth-Arlington
metropolitan economy, and vocational conclusions about its residents are
grounded in Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage
estimates for that metropolitan area and for Texas statewide. State
workforce agency labor market information and direct labor market research
supply local confirmation when the referral questions require it, and life
care plans price services to the evaluee's community with sources cited
throughout. The [metro page](/locations/texas/dallas/) explains how this
town-level work fits the regional practice.

## Testimony and travel

When deposition or trial calls for the expert in person, travel is arranged
from the Kansas City home office against dates fixed with counsel, and
remote testimony is supported wherever the court and the parties permit it.
No McKinney office exists, no local address is published, and the firm
treats that candor as part of its credibility rather than a limitation to
obscure.
