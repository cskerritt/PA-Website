Sometimes the engagement begins not with an injured person but with a
document: the other side's expert report, already served, already shaping
the case. Denton counsel bring Purinton Analytics those reports for
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/), an audit of the
opposing opinion's sources, calculations, and reasoning conducted to the
same standard the firm applies to its own work. Just as often the retention
is affirmative, and the firm prepares
[vocational expert evaluations](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) and
[life care plans](/services/life-care-planning/) for Denton evaluees from
the ground up. Plaintiff and defense counsel use both services, and the
analytical standard does not move with the client.

## What a rebuttal review examines

A credible critique is specific. The review traces whether the opposing
report's labor market sources actually say what the report claims, whether
its care recommendations carry clinical foundation, whether its pricing is
tied to the evaluee's community or to convenience, and whether its
conclusions follow from its own stated method. The product is a written
opinion counsel can use in deposition preparation or serve as a rebuttal
disclosure, and it names what the reviewed report gets right as readily as
what it gets wrong, because a critique that concedes nothing persuades
nobody.

## The Denton evaluee's labor market

Denton is a mid-sized city within the Dallas-Fort Worth-Arlington
metropolitan economy, and earning capacity conclusions for its residents
are grounded in Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage
estimates for that metropolitan area and for Texas statewide, sharpened by
state workforce agency labor market information and direct labor market
research when the referral questions turn on local conditions. The
[Dallas-Fort Worth metro page](/locations/texas/dallas/) sets out the
regional practice this analysis draws on.

## Appointments, not an address

The firm publishes no Denton address because it keeps none. Records review
and analysis are remote; interviews and standardized testing are
administered under publisher protocols that support remote use; in-person
evaluations, where the referral requires them, are conducted in the
evaluee's own community. Travel for deposition or trial testimony is
arranged from the Kansas City home office once dates are fixed with
counsel.
