Counsel with a Grand Prairie case tend to arrive with versions of the same
questions. What can this person earn now, compared with before the injury?
What will the required care cost over a lifetime, and on what evidence? Is
the opposing expert's report as solid as it looks? Purinton Analytics
answers each with a distinct, written work product:
[vocational expert evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) for
earning capacity, [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) and
[medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/) for future
care, and [rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) when
the assignment is to test someone else's opinion rather than form a fresh
one. The practice is open to plaintiff and defense counsel alike, and each
answer is written so that the retaining side cannot be inferred from the
analysis.

## Matching the question to the method

An earning capacity question triggers the full vocational sequence:
records synthesis, structured interview, standardized testing, and labor
market analysis. A future-care question adds clinical foundation
requirements and community-level pricing. A rebuttal assignment reverses
the lens, auditing an existing report's sources, math, and logic against
the same standards the firm applies to itself. Defining which question the
engagement answers, and putting that definition in writing, is the intake
step that keeps the eventual opinion inside its competence and out of
avoidable cross-examination trouble.

## Dallas County courts and the federal districts

Grand Prairie matters in state court are accepted in the District Courts of
Dallas County. Federal engagements proceed in the United States District
Court for the Northern District of Texas, and the firm is equally available
in the United States District Court for the Eastern District of Texas, the
United States District Court for the Southern District of Texas, and the
United States District Court for the Western District of Texas when a case
sits in one of those districts. Any registration or qualification
requirement particular to a venue is identified and confirmed during the
conflict check and intake process.

## Evidence for the labor market opinion

For a Grand Prairie evaluee, vocational findings rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Dallas-Fort
Worth-Arlington metropolitan area and for Texas statewide, with state
workforce agency labor market information and direct labor market research
brought in when local confirmation matters to the referral questions. The
[metro page](/locations/texas/dallas/) describes the regional labor market
practice in more depth.

## Serving the evaluee where they are

The firm keeps no Grand Prairie office; engagements are by appointment and
remote wherever the method permits. Interviews and testing follow publisher
protocols designed for remote administration, in-person evaluations happen
in the evaluee's community when the referral requires them, and expert
travel for deposition or trial is scheduled from the Kansas City home
office.
