Expert opinions are due when the scheduling order says they are due, and a
retention that arrives late in the calendar constrains what any honest
expert can responsibly do. Purinton Analytics therefore organizes every
Garland engagement around the disclosure calendar from the outset: designation
deadlines, report dates, and deposition windows are gathered at intake and
the analysis is planned backward from them.
[Vocational expert evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/) are available
to plaintiff and defense counsel on Garland matters, on timelines stated in
writing before the work begins.

## The calendar drives the scope

A full evaluation, a records-based opinion, and a
[rebuttal review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) of an opposing expert's
report each carry different time demands, and the honest response to a
compressed schedule is a scope the deadline can actually support. That
conversation happens at intake, not after the designation date has passed.
When the calendar allows the complete method, the complete method is what
the engagement gets: structured interview, standardized testing, records
synthesis, labor market analysis, and a written opinion that discloses each
step.

## Where Garland cases are heard

Garland sits in Dallas County, and state-court engagements proceed in the
District Courts of Dallas County. In federal practice, the firm accepts
Garland-related engagements in the United States District Court for the
Northern District of Texas and stands equally ready in the United States
District Court for the Eastern District of Texas, the United States District
Court for the Southern District of Texas, and the United States District
Court for the Western District of Texas when a matter is venued elsewhere in
the state.

## Labor market evidence for a Garland evaluee

Vocational findings are grounded in Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational
employment and wage estimates for the Dallas-Fort Worth-Arlington
metropolitan area and for Texas statewide, augmented by state workforce
agency labor market information and by direct labor market research when a
referral question turns on local hiring conditions. Garland is a
substantial city within a very large metropolitan economy, and the opinion
says explicitly how that context bears on this evaluee. Care pricing in life
care plans follows the same rule: costed to the evaluee's community, each
source cited. The broader regional practice is described on the
[metro page](/locations/texas/dallas/).

## Evaluation without a local office

The firm maintains no Garland office and frames its presence honestly:
service is by appointment, remote wherever the method permits, in the
evaluee's community when the referral requires an in-person assessment, and
supported by travel from the Kansas City home office for depositions and
trial. Remote testimony is accommodated when the court and parties allow it.
