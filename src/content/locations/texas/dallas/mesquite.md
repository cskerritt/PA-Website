A Mesquite case file does not tell an expert which side sent it, and the
finished report should not tell the reader either. That is the working test
Purinton Analytics applies to its own opinions: if the conclusions section
were removed, the method and sources should look the same whether plaintiff
or defense counsel signed the retention letter. On that footing the firm
provides [vocational expert evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/) for Mesquite
litigation, together with
[testimony and litigation consulting](/services/expert-testimony-litigation-consulting/)
when the opinion needs defending in deposition or at trial.

## Why even-handedness is a method, not a slogan

Neutrality shows up in specific habits. The records request is tailored to
the claims rather than to a desired answer. Findings that cut against the
retaining side are reported, not buried. Assumptions are labeled as
assumptions and separated from evidence. Sources are cited so the opposing
reader can pull them and check. Those habits make the opinion useful to
whichever side retained it, precisely because the other side has a harder
time dismissing it.

## Courts hearing Mesquite matters

State-court engagements for Mesquite proceed in the District Courts of
Dallas County. On the federal side, the firm takes engagements in the
United States District Court for the Northern District of Texas, and it is
just as available in the United States District Court for the Eastern
District of Texas, the United States District Court for the Southern
District of Texas, and the United States District Court for the Western
District of Texas when the case is venued elsewhere in Texas. Method,
sourcing, and disclosure practice remain constant across every forum.

## Placing a Mesquite evaluee in the labor market

Mesquite is a substantial suburb within the Dallas-Fort Worth-Arlington
metropolitan economy, and the vocational analysis for a Mesquite resident
draws on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage
estimates for that metropolitan area and for Texas statewide. State
workforce agency labor market information adds detail, and direct labor
market research is used when a referral question depends on current local
hiring. Life care plans price recommended care to the evaluee's own
community, with every source disclosed in the plan. The
[metro page](/locations/texas/dallas/) covers the regional practice behind
this work.

## Meeting the evaluee, honestly framed

There is no Mesquite office and none is implied; service is by appointment.
Most of the work is remote, including interviews and standardized testing
run under publisher protocols that permit remote administration, and
in-person evaluations, when required, take place in the evaluee's
community. For deposition and trial appearances, the expert travels from
the Kansas City home office on dates coordinated with counsel, and remote
testimony is supported where the court and parties allow.
