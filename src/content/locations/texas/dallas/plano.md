## Collin County venue, stated precisely

State-court engagements for Plano proceed in the District Courts of Collin
County. On the federal side, the firm accepts engagements in the United
States District Court for the Eastern District of Texas, in the United
States District Court for the Northern District of Texas, and just as
readily in the United States District Court for the Southern District of
Texas and the United States District Court for the Western District of Texas
when a matter is filed there. Getting the venue right is not clerical
housekeeping; it fixes the calendar that the entire analysis must serve, and
any venue-specific qualification question is identified during the conflict
check and intake process.

## The market a Plano evaluee actually faces

Plano is a large suburban city inside the Dallas-Fort Worth-Arlington
metropolitan economy, and an earning capacity opinion must describe the
employment its residents can realistically obtain rather than an average for
the metro as a whole. Vocational findings therefore rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Dallas-Fort
Worth-Arlington metropolitan area and for Texas statewide, refined with state
workforce agency labor market information and direct labor market research
when the referral questions demand local confirmation. Life care plans apply
the same evidentiary standard, pricing care to the evaluee's own community
and citing each source. The [metro page](/locations/texas/dallas/) shows how
Plano work connects to the wider regional practice.

## By appointment, not by storefront

There is no Plano office and no local address to publish; accuracy about
presence is part of the same discipline that governs the reports. The work is
remote-first: records analysis, structured interviews, and standardized
testing under remote-capable publisher protocols are completed without asking
the evaluee to travel. In-person evaluation, when the referral requires it,
takes place in the evaluee's community, and appearances for deposition or
trial are scheduled as travel from the Kansas City home office once dates are
set. Remote testimony is supported when the court and the parties permit it,
which often lets the calendar breathe without sacrificing the record.

## Opening the file

An engagement opens the same way in Plano as it does anywhere in the firm's
[Texas practice](/locations/texas/) or its
[nationwide work](/locations/nationwide/): with a documented conflict check
and nothing before it. [Send the caption and parties](/refer-a-case/)
through the conflict check form and identify any expert designation or
report deadlines the scheduling order has already fixed. Clearing the check
produces a written engagement confirmation, a defined scope, and a records
request tailored to the claims, after which the analysis is scheduled
backward from the court's dates.
