An injured worker or catastrophically injured person in Arlington should not
have to organize a recovery around an expert's travel schedule. Purinton
Analytics structures its practice the other way around: the analysis,
interviews, and testing are arranged so that the work reaches the Arlington
evaluee where they live. Plaintiff and defense counsel retain the firm for
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/),
[vocational expert evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/), and
[expert testimony](/services/expert-testimony-litigation-consulting/), and
the deliverable in every engagement is a written opinion that either side of
the caption could probe line by line.

## Tarrant County courts and the federal forums

Arlington sits in Tarrant County, and state-court engagements are accepted in
the District Courts of Tarrant County across injury, employment, and family
law dockets. In federal practice the firm takes engagements in the United
States District Court for the Northern District of Texas as readily as in the
United States District Court for the Eastern District of Texas, the United
States District Court for the Southern District of Texas, or the United
States District Court for the Western District of Texas, wherever the matter
happens to be venued. The forum sets the calendar; the method stays the same.

## Situating Arlington in the metro's labor market

A large city inside a much larger metropolitan economy raises a question
every earning capacity opinion must answer: which market does this evaluee
actually face? The firm answers it with Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates for the Dallas-Fort
Worth-Arlington metropolitan area and for Texas statewide, checked against
state workforce agency labor market information and, where warranted, direct
labor market research. Care costs follow the same discipline, priced to the
evaluee's community with every source disclosed. How this local work fits the
broader regional practice is laid out on the
[metro page](/locations/texas/dallas/).

## What evaluee-centered logistics look like

Records review happens remotely, on a defined scope set at intake. Structured
vocational interviews are scheduled around the evaluee's medical
appointments, not the reverse, and standardized instruments are administered
under protocols their publishers have approved for remote administration.
When the referral questions require an in-person assessment, the evaluation
is conducted in the evaluee's own community, and where a home or facility
visit is part of the care-planning method, it is planned into the engagement
timeline from the start. Travel for depositions and trial comes from the
Kansas City home office; there is no Arlington office, and the firm says so
plainly rather than implying a local storefront it does not keep.
