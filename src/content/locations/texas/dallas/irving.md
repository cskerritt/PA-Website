In a metropolitan economy the size of Dallas-Fort Worth-Arlington, where a
person lives and where that person works are rarely the same place, and an
earning capacity opinion for an Irving evaluee has to take that commuting
reality seriously. A market defined too narrowly understates opportunity; a
market defined too broadly assumes jobs the evaluee cannot practically hold.
Purinton Analytics draws that boundary explicitly and defends it in writing,
in [vocational expert evaluations](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care plans](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[forensic economic damages work](/services/forensic-economic-damages/)
coordinated for plaintiff and defense counsel alike.

## Dallas County and federal venues

Irving matters in state court are accepted in the District Courts of Dallas
County. Federal engagements are accepted in the United States District Court
for the Northern District of Texas, with the same practice available in the
United States District Court for the Eastern District of Texas, the United
States District Court for the Southern District of Texas, and the United
States District Court for the Western District of Texas whenever a case sits
in those districts. Forum changes the calendar and the disclosure rhythm; it
never changes the method.

## Defining the commuting market

The starting evidence is the Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational
employment and wage estimates for the Dallas-Fort Worth-Arlington
metropolitan area and for Texas statewide, because those estimates describe
the regional economy an Irving resident can actually reach. From there the
analysis narrows: state workforce agency labor market information, the
evaluee's documented work history and restrictions, and direct labor market
research where the referral questions require confirmation that specific
work exists on specific terms. The opinion states its market definition
openly so that opposing counsel can test it rather than guess at it. The
regional context for this approach is described on the
[Dallas-Fort Worth metro page](/locations/texas/dallas/).

## Logistics for the Irving evaluee

No Irving office exists, and the firm does not imply one. The practice is
remote-first by design: records analysis proceeds off-site, and interviews
and standardized testing are administered under publisher protocols suited
to remote use, which spares most evaluees any travel at all. Where an
in-person evaluation is required by the referral questions, it is performed
in the evaluee's own community. Expert travel for deposition and trial is
arranged from the Kansas City home office, booked against the dates counsel
and the court have fixed.

offers
