A vocational report earns nothing from its letterhead; it earns credibility,
or fails to, in the space between its conclusions and its sources. That is
the standard Purinton Analytics applies to every Frisco engagement, whether
retained by plaintiff or defense counsel. Each
[vocational expert opinion](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care plan](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/) is written so
that a reader who disagrees with the conclusion can still trace exactly how
it was reached, which records it rests on, and which published data stand
behind each finding.

## What the method requires

The sequence is fixed regardless of who retains the firm: a documented
conflict check, a scope defined in writing, a records request tailored to
the claims, a structured interview and standardized testing where the
referral calls for evaluation, and a report that separates findings from
assumptions and labels both. An opinion assembled this way does not need to
be defended into shape after the fact, because the record supporting it
exists before the first draft is written and every step of the analysis can
be produced on request. When a source will not bear the weight of a
conclusion, the conclusion is narrowed until it will, and the report says
so plainly.

## Grounding the labor market analysis

Frisco is a large suburban city within the Dallas-Fort
Worth-Arlington metropolitan economy, and the vocational analysis for a
Frisco evaluee is anchored in Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational
employment and wage estimates for that metropolitan area and for Texas
statewide. State workforce agency labor market information and direct labor
market research refine the picture when a referral question depends on
current, local hiring conditions. In care planning, costs are priced to the
evaluee's community with each source cited in the plan itself, so the
opposing reader can pull the same source and check the figure. The
[Dallas-Fort Worth metro page](/locations/texas/dallas/) situates this local
work within the regional practice.

## Presence, stated accurately

The firm publishes no Frisco address because it has none; claiming otherwise

remote, testing follows publisher protocols that support remote
administration, and in-person evaluation, where required, happens in the
evaluee's own community. For depositions and trial, the expert travels from
the Kansas City home office on dates coordinated with counsel.

## From conflict check to signed report

Engagements across the firm's [Texas work](/locations/texas/) and its
[national practice](/locations/nationwide/) open through the same door:
[a conflict check request](/refer-a-case/) carrying the caption and party
names, plus any disclosure, deposition, or trial dates already ordered. When
the check clears, counsel receives the written scope and records request,
and the analysis proceeds on the court's schedule toward a report both
sides can examine.
