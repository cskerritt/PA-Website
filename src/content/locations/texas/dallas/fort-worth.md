Fort Worth is written into the name of its own metropolitan area, Dallas-Fort
Worth-Arlington, and a vocational or care-planning opinion for a Fort Worth
matter should treat the city as the major employment center it is rather than
an appendage of Dallas. Purinton Analytics serves plaintiff and defense
counsel in Fort Worth cases through
[vocational expert evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/). Each
engagement produces a written opinion that explains its own method, because
the reader the firm writes for is not the retaining lawyer but the one across
the aisle.

## Venue: Tarrant County and the federal districts

Fort Worth litigation in state court proceeds in the District Courts of
Tarrant County, and engagements are accepted there wherever an earning
capacity, care-cost, or employability question arises. Federal engagements
are accepted in the United States District Court for the Northern District of
Texas, and the practice is equally available in the United States District
Court for the Eastern District of Texas, the United States District Court for
the Southern District of Texas, and the United States District Court for the
Western District of Texas when a case is filed elsewhere in the state. The
spine of the work does not change with the forum: documented conflict check,
defined scope, records request tailored to the claims, and a report that
shows its sources.

## A labor market of its own

Earning capacity findings for a Fort Worth evaluee rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Dallas-Fort
Worth-Arlington metropolitan area and for Texas statewide, supplemented by
state workforce agency labor market information and direct labor market
research when a referral question turns on what work actually exists within
the evaluee's reach. Fort Worth anchors one of the largest and most varied
labor markets in the country, which cuts in different directions in different
cases; the analysis states which way it cuts here and cites what it relied
on. The [Dallas-Fort Worth metro page](/locations/texas/dallas/) describes
the regional practice this local work belongs to.

## How the evaluation reaches the evaluee

The firm keeps no office in Fort Worth and does not pretend otherwise.
Records analysis is remote by default, and structured vocational interviews
and standardized testing are administered under publisher protocols that
support remote use, so most Fort Worth evaluees are assessed without leaving
home. When an in-person evaluation is genuinely warranted, it is conducted in
the evaluee's own community. When deposition or trial requires the expert at
counsel table or on the stand, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home
office against dates fixed with counsel well in advance.

## Starting a Fort Worth engagement

Every matter begins with a documented conflict check, the same gate that
governs the firm's [Texas practice](/locations/texas/) and its
[nationwide engagements](/locations/nationwide/). Counsel can
[request a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) with the caption and party names,
flag any disclosure, deposition, or trial dates already on the calendar, and
expect a defined scope and a tailored records request once the check clears.
Where the damages picture calls for economic modeling as well, the firm
offers
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.
