Not every Corpus Christi referral asks Purinton Analytics to write a
report of its own; many ask it to test someone else's. Alongside
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) of opposing
vocational and life care opinions, counsel in the Corpus Christi metro
retain the firm for
[earning capacity and employability evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
for [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) in catastrophic
injury cases, and for
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. Because the practice serves plaintiff and defense in
roughly equal spirit, its rebuttal work applies the same methodological
tests it expects its own reports to pass.

## Where these matters are venued

The metro's state court engagements are venued in the District Courts of
Nueces County, San Patricio County, and Aransas County. Federal matters in
Texas proceed in the United States District Court for the Southern District
of Texas, the United States District Court for the Northern District of
Texas, the United States District Court for the Eastern District of Texas,
and the United States District Court for the Western District of Texas.
Any venue-specific registration or qualification requirement is identified
and confirmed during the conflict check and intake process. Counsel
handling matters elsewhere in the state will find the firm's coverage
described on the [Texas locations page](/locations/texas/).

## By-appointment logistics

Corpus Christi meetings are by appointment. There is no staffed local
office and no published street address here, and the firm says so rather
than trading on an implied presence, because accuracy about offices is part
of the same discipline that governs its reports. Engagements ordinarily
begin remotely with a documented conflict check, a defined scope, and a
records request tailored to the claims, followed by structured interviews
and standardized testing under publisher protocols where remote
administration is supported. When in-person evaluation is warranted, the
evaluee can be seen in their own community, and travel for evaluations,
depositions, and trial testimony is scheduled from the Kansas City home
office against the court's calendar.

## Labor market research for Nueces County evaluees

Vocational findings for evaluees in the metro rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Corpus
Christi metropolitan area and for Texas statewide, on state workforce
agency labor market information, and on direct labor market research where
the referral questions warrant it. The same evidentiary posture governs
rebuttal work: when the firm critiques an opposing opinion, it checks that
opinion's occupational and wage claims against the published data for the
evaluee's actual region. Life care plans and medical cost projections are
priced to the evaluee's own market through provider-specific inquiries and
published fee data, not through national figures assumed to apply locally.

## Engaging the firm

Plaintiff and defense counsel begin the same way. Submit the caption or
party names through the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/), describe
the questions presented, and note any imminent disclosure, deposition, or
trial dates. Once conflicts clear, scope and scheduling are confirmed in
writing, and the matter proceeds under the same report standard the firm
applies across its [national practice](/locations/nationwide/).
