Scheduling is usually the opening question from El Paso counsel, so it is
worth answering up front: Purinton Analytics serves the El Paso metropolitan
statistical area by appointment, performs most engagement work remotely,
and schedules travel from its Kansas City home office when evaluations,
depositions, or trial dates require someone in the room. Within that
structure, plaintiff and defense counsel retain the firm for
[earning capacity and employability evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
for [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) in catastrophic
injury matters, and for
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Counties and courts

The metro comprises El Paso County and Hudspeth County, and state court
engagements are venued in the District Courts of those counties. Federal
matters in Texas proceed in the United States District Court for the
Western District of Texas, the United States District Court for the
Northern District of Texas, the United States District Court for the
Southern District of Texas, and the United States District Court for the
Eastern District of Texas. The firm's reports are written to the same
disclosure standard in each of those forums, and any
venue-specific registration or qualification requirement is identified and
confirmed during the conflict check and intake process. The firm's work in
the rest of the state is described on the
[Texas locations page](/locations/texas/).

## Presence by appointment, travel by schedule

There is no staffed El Paso office and no published street address for this
location; the firm considers accuracy about presence part of the same
discipline that governs its opinions. What counsel get instead is a
practice built for distance. Records review, structured interviews, and
standardized testing under publisher protocols that support remote
administration proceed without travel at all. When the referral questions
call for in-person evaluation, the evaluee can be seen in their own
community, and testimony appearances are coordinated with counsel well
ahead of the setting so that court dates, not logistics, control the
calendar.

## El Paso labor market research

Vocational findings for evaluees in the metro rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the El Paso
metropolitan area and for Texas statewide, on state workforce agency labor
market information, and on direct labor market research where the case
warrants it. That local grounding matters because employability is not an
abstraction: an opinion about what work an evaluee can obtain must describe
the occupations actually present where the evaluee lives. Life care plans
and medical cost projections apply the same rule to future care, priced to
the evaluee's own market through provider-specific inquiries and published
fee data for the region.

Whether retained by plaintiff or defense, counsel start with the same step:
a documented conflict check, followed by a defined scope and a records
request tailored to the claims. Send the caption or party names through the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and identify any disclosure,
deposition, or trial dates that bear on scheduling. El Paso matters are
handled within the same [national practice](/locations/nationwide/) that
serves every other venue, with the identical report standard and the same
point of contact from intake through testimony.
