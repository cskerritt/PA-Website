A vocational or life care planning opinion disclosed in a Port Neches case
will be read most carefully by the lawyer who did not commission it, and
every report this practice signs is written for that reader. That single
assumption shapes the method: opinions stated with their bases, data cited
to sources the other side can pull, and no conclusion resting on an
adjective. Counsel for plaintiff and defendant alike retain Purinton
Analytics here for
[vocational expert evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/) in injury
and employment litigation.

## Courts serving Port Neches

Port Neches is a Jefferson County community, and state-court engagements
proceed in the District Court serving that county. Federal work is
accepted across the state's districts as filings require: the United
States District Court for the Eastern District of Texas, the United States
District Court for the Southern District of Texas, the United States
District Court for the Northern District of Texas, and the United States
District Court for the Western District of Texas. Any venue-specific
registration or qualification requirement is identified and confirmed
during the conflict check and intake process. Because the report is built
to the fuller federal disclosure standard in every case, moving between
forums changes the caption page, not the rigor.

## Testing employability against the regional economy

For a Port Neches evaluee the honest unit of analysis is the surrounding
regional labor market. Findings rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates for the Beaumont-Port Arthur,
TX metropolitan area and for Texas statewide, corroborated by state
workforce agency labor market information and, where a placement question
is genuinely contested, direct labor market research. The report walks the
reader from restrictions to transferable skills to identifiable
occupations, so that a cross-examiner finds a chain of sourced steps
rather than a leap. Statewide practice details appear on the
[Texas locations page](/locations/texas/).

## Appointments, travel, and testimony

The firm keeps no storefront in Port Neches and publishes no local street
address; candor about presence is part of what makes the reports credible.
Work proceeds remotely wherever remote work serves the case: records
analysis, conferences, many interviews. When in-person evaluation or
courtroom presence is required, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City
home office and evaluations are held in the evaluee's own community.
Neighboring engagements across the region are collected on the
[Beaumont-Port Arthur metro page](/locations/texas/beaumont/), and the
practice accepts referrals [nationwide](/locations/nationwide/) on
identical terms.

## Keeping the economics independent

When an economic loss figure is needed, the firm offers
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under a
single engagement structure. The separation is deliberate: each opinion
carries the signature of the professional qualified to give it.
