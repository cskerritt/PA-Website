In a Bridge City case, the same expert methodology should produce the same
answer no matter which party pays the invoice. That is the premise of this
practice: Purinton Analytics is retained in Bridge City matters by
plaintiff counsel and defense counsel on equal terms, and its
[vocational evaluations](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care plans](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[rebuttal and peer review work](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) follow
the same written methodology regardless of who makes the referral. An opinion
that changes with the client is not an opinion; it is advocacy, and it
does not survive cross-examination.

## Orange County venue and the federal map

Bridge City lies in Orange County, and state-court engagements proceed in
the District Court serving that county. Federally, the practice follows
the filing: the United States District Court for the Eastern District of
Texas, the United States District Court for the Northern District of
Texas, the United States District Court for the Southern District of
Texas, and the United States District Court for the Western District of
Texas are each available forums as venue requires. Any venue-specific
registration or qualification requirement is identified and confirmed
during the conflict check and intake process. Counsel weighing engagements
elsewhere in the state will find the
[Texas locations page](/locations/texas/) a useful map of the practice.

## The same market, examined from either chair

Whether the question is posed by the party claiming lost earning capacity
or the party disputing it, the evidence is the same: Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the
Beaumont-Port Arthur, TX metropolitan area and for Texas statewide, state
workforce agency labor market information, and direct labor market
research where the referral demands it. Bridge City evaluees compete in
that regional market, and the report locates them in it with sourced,
qualitative findings that either side can verify. Retaining counsel gets
candor about weak points before the other side finds them.

## By-appointment service in Bridge City

There is no Bridge City office and the firm never implies one; meetings
are by appointment, and travel, when needed, runs from the Kansas City
home office. Records review and most conferences are remote. In-person
evaluations are conducted in the evaluee's own community, and deposition
or trial appearances are scheduled around the court's calendar. The
surrounding region's engagements are described on the
[Beaumont-Port Arthur metro page](/locations/texas/beaumont/), and the
identical structure is offered to counsel
[across the country](/locations/nationwide/).

## Economic analysis with clean signatures

Cases requiring an economic loss opinion proceed as
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. Neither role borrows the other's credentials, which
is exactly how an opposing expert will check it.

## Opening a Bridge City matter

Conflicts are checked before scope is discussed. Send the caption or party
names through the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) with the referral
questions and any set disclosure or trial dates, and the firm will return
a written scope and a records request built to the claims. The terms do
not shift with the side of the caption, because the methodology never
does.
