A working life in Groves rarely stays inside the city limits. Residents
participate in the commuting market of the wider Beaumont-Port Arthur, TX
region, which means the central vocational question in litigation, what
work this person can actually obtain and hold, must be answered at the
scale of that regional market rather than a single town. Purinton
Analytics approaches Groves referrals with exactly that frame, offering
plaintiff and defense counsel
[vocational expert evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[expert testimony and litigation consulting](/services/expert-testimony-litigation-consulting/)
built to be examined by the side that did not pay for them.

## Where Groves cases are heard

Groves sits in Jefferson County. State-court engagements proceed in the
District Court serving that county, and the report is prepared with the
court's disclosure practice in view. In federal litigation the practice
appears as venue requires, whether in the United States District Court for
the Eastern District of Texas, the United States District Court for the
Northern District of Texas, the United States District Court for the
Southern District of Texas, or the United States District Court for the
Western District of Texas. Any venue-specific registration or qualification
requirement is identified and confirmed during the conflict check and
intake process, and statewide context is collected on the
[Texas locations page](/locations/texas/).

## Earning capacity at commuting-market scale

Because the relevant market is regional, vocational findings for a Groves
evaluee rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and
wage estimates for the Beaumont-Port Arthur, TX metropolitan area and for
Texas statewide, checked against state workforce agency labor market
information. Where the dispute narrows to whether specific jobs are open
to a specific person with specific restrictions, direct labor market
research supplements the published series. The report treats commuting
range, transferable skills, and residual functional capacity as questions
of evidence, each tied to a source, never as assumptions.

## Practical logistics for Groves counsel

The firm maintains no Groves office; meetings are by appointment, and no
street address is published for this location because accuracy about
presence is part of the product. Most records analysis and counsel
conferences are remote. In-person evaluation, when the referral requires
it, is conducted in the evaluee's community with travel scheduled from the
Kansas City home office, and deposition or trial appearances are planned
the same way, coordinated with retaining counsel well ahead of the court's
dates so testimony never becomes a scramble. Related engagements around the region are described on the
[Beaumont-Port Arthur metro page](/locations/texas/beaumont/); the
practice serves counsel [across the country](/locations/nationwide/) under
the same structure.

## When the case needs an economist

Economic loss opinions are not signed by the vocational expert. The firm
offers [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under a
single engagement structure, keeping roles distinct and disclosures clean.
