Purinton Analytics has no office in West Orange, and this page will not
pretend otherwise. The firm works from a single home office in Kansas City
and serves West Orange, a small Orange County community, by appointment.
That candor is not a disclaimer; it is a preview of the reports
themselves, which claim only what the record supports. What counsel gets
instead of a storefront is a disciplined national practice:
[vocational expert evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) available to
plaintiff and defense counsel in equal measure.

## Venue for West Orange matters

State-court cases from West Orange proceed in the District Court serving
Orange County, and each report is prepared with that court's disclosure
practice in mind. Federal engagements follow the filing to its district,
whether the United States District Court for the Eastern District of
Texas, the United States District Court for the Southern District of
Texas, the United States District Court for the Northern District of
Texas, or the United States District Court for the Western District of
Texas. Any venue-specific registration or qualification requirement is
identified and confirmed during the conflict check and intake process.
The broader state practice is laid out on the
[Texas locations page](/locations/texas/).

## A small town inside a regional economy

West Orange is small; the labor market its residents actually work in is
not. Vocational findings therefore rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates for the Beaumont-Port Arthur,
TX metropolitan area and for Texas statewide, read with state workforce
agency labor market information and confirmed by direct labor market
research when a specific placement question is disputed. The report is
explicit about which conclusions come from published series and which from
original research, so the analysis can be checked rather than merely
believed.

## What by-appointment service looks like

Nearly all of an engagement runs without regard to distance: records
review, indexing, counsel conferences, and many structured interviews are
handled remotely, which keeps a West Orange engagement moving at the same
pace as one across town from the home office. When the referral questions
require an in-person evaluation, the firm comes to the evaluee's community
rather than the reverse, with travel scheduled from the Kansas City home
office; the same planning covers depositions and trial appearances. Counsel with matters spread across
neighboring communities can orient from the
[Beaumont-Port Arthur metro page](/locations/texas/beaumont/), and the
firm's [national practice](/locations/nationwide/) runs on the identical
model.

## Economic opinions from economist partners

Where an economic loss opinion is required, the firm arranges
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under a
single engagement structure. No signature is borrowed and no opinion is
issued outside its author's qualifications.

## Referring a West Orange case

The engagement starts with the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/): caption or party names, referral
questions, and any dates the court has already fixed. A written scope and
a records request tailored to the claims follow once conflicts clear,
whichever party brings the referral.
