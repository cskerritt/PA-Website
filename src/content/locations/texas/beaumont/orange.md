The city of Orange shares its name with Orange County, and for counsel the
county is where an engagement here starts: the court, the docket, and the
disclosure schedule shape what a usable expert report looks like before any
interview is scheduled. Purinton Analytics accepts Orange referrals from
either side of the caption for
[vocational expert evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/), and it
prepares each written opinion on the assumption that its most attentive
reader will be the lawyer who opposes it.

## The Orange County courthouse and the federal forums

State-court matters from Orange proceed in the District Court serving
Orange County, and reports are drafted to that court's practice. When a
case is filed federally, the practice appears as the venue requires in the
United States District Court for the Eastern District of Texas, the United
States District Court for the Northern District of Texas, the United States
District Court for the Southern District of Texas, and the United States
District Court for the Western District of Texas. Any venue-specific
registration or qualification requirement is identified and confirmed
during the conflict check and intake process, not discovered after
disclosure. Counsel with matters elsewhere in the state can start from the
[Texas locations page](/locations/texas/).

## Reading the labor market from Orange

An Orange evaluee competes for work across the Beaumont-Port Arthur, TX
regional economy, so vocational findings draw on Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates for that metropolitan area and
for Texas statewide, cross-checked against state workforce agency labor
market information. Direct labor market research supplements the published
series when a referral question turns on whether particular work is
realistically open to a particular person. Nothing in the analysis is
asserted by adjective: each occupational conclusion is tied to a source the
other side can pull and test.

## How the firm serves Orange

There is no Purinton Analytics office in Orange, and the firm says so
plainly rather than implying a storefront it does not keep. Service is by
appointment and remote-first: records analysis, conferences with counsel,
and many evaluee interviews are handled without travel, which keeps work
moving between hearing dates. Where the referral questions call for
in-person evaluation, or where deposition and trial calendars require
presence, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office and the
evaluation is conducted in the evaluee's community. Engagements that span
the region are described on the
[Beaumont-Port Arthur metro page](/locations/texas/beaumont/); the same
approach serves counsel [nationwide](/locations/nationwide/).

## Coordinated damages under a single engagement

Where the case needs an economic loss opinion alongside the vocational and
care-planning work, the firm offers
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and life care planning work proceeds under a
single engagement structure. Coordination reduces seams between experts
without blurring who signs what.
