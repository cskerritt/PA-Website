Port Arthur lends its name to the metropolitan area it anchors alongside
Beaumont, and litigation arising here draws on the full breadth of that
regional economy. Plaintiff and defense counsel with Port Arthur matters
retain Purinton Analytics for
[vocational expert evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) of
employability and earning capacity,
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) in catastrophic injury
cases, and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. The discipline is identical to the practice's work
[across Texas](/locations/texas/): a documented conflict check, a defined
scope, and a written opinion that withstands the reader who did not
commission it.

## Jefferson County venue context

Port Arthur sits in Jefferson County, and state-court engagements proceed
in the District Court serving that county. Federal engagements are accepted
wherever the case is filed, including the United States District Court for
the Eastern District of Texas, the United States District Court for the
Southern District of Texas, the United States District Court for the
Northern District of Texas, and the United States District Court for the
Western District of Texas; the operative district, along with any
venue-specific registration or qualification requirement, is identified and
confirmed during the conflict check and intake process. Whatever the forum,
the report carries a complete statement of opinions, the bases for them,
and the records considered, so neither side meets a surprise at deposition.

## A namesake city inside a regional market

Because Port Arthur workers participate in the labor market of the whole
Beaumont-Port Arthur, TX region, vocational findings rest on Bureau of
Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for that
metropolitan area and for Texas statewide, supplemented by state workforce
agency labor market information and by direct labor market research where
the referral questions warrant it. The analysis stays qualitative and
sourced: which occupations exist in the regional economy, what they demand,
and whether the evaluee's documented residual capacity meets those demands.
Where published data thins out, the report says so rather than papering
over the gap.

## Evaluations, meetings, and appearances

The firm keeps no office in Port Arthur and publishes no local address,
because accuracy about presence belongs to the same discipline that governs
its reports. Records review, counsel conferences, and many structured
interviews proceed remotely. When the referral calls for an in-person
evaluation, or when deposition and trial dates require presence, travel is
scheduled from the Kansas City home office and the evaluation takes place
in the evaluee's own community. Counsel coordinating related matters
elsewhere in the region will find the wider
[Beaumont-Port Arthur metro page](/locations/texas/beaumont/) useful, and
the same engagement structure extends to counsel
[anywhere in the country](/locations/nationwide/).

## Starting a Port Arthur engagement

Every file begins with conflicts. Submit the caption or party names through
the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/), state the referral questions,
and note any disclosure, deposition, or trial dates already on the docket.
The firm responds with a defined scope and a records request tailored to
the claims actually pleaded, whichever side of the caption makes the
referral.
