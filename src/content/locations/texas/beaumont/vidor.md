travel. Records arrive, are indexed, and are read closely; counsel
conferences run by video; the evaluee interview is scheduled in whatever

[vocational expert evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),

the United States District Court for the Eastern District of Texas, the
United States District Court for the Southern District of Texas, the
United States District Court for the Northern District of Texas, and the
United States District Court for the Western District of Texas. Any

## Situating a Vidor evaluee in the regional market

Vocational conclusions for a Vidor evaluee are grounded in the labor
market of the surrounding Beaumont-Port Arthur, TX region. The evidence
base is the Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage
estimates for that metropolitan area and for Texas statewide, read
together with state workforce agency labor market information; direct
labor market research is added when a contested question turns on real
openings rather than published categories. Findings stay qualitative and
cited, tracing restrictions through transferable skills to occupations the
evaluee can realistically reach. Where the data is thin, the report acknowledges the limit, because
an acknowledged limit is defensible and a hidden one is not.

## What remote-first means in practice

It does not mean the firm never appears. It means no step waits on a
flight: analysis, drafting, and conferences proceed continuously, and
travel from the Kansas City home office is scheduled when an in-person
evaluation, a deposition, or trial testimony requires presence. Vidor
evaluations are conducted in the evaluee's own community, not in a distant
office, and the firm keeps no Vidor address and claims none. The wider
regional practice is described on the
[Beaumont-Port Arthur metro page](/locations/texas/beaumont/), and the
same workflow serves counsel [throughout the country](/locations/nationwide/).

## Adding the economic layer

Where the referral includes economic loss, the firm structures
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under a
single engagement structure. Coordination is administrative; authorship
and signature stay with the qualified professional.
