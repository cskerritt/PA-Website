## The commuting market, measured qualitatively

The labor market description in a Weslaco report is built from Bureau of
Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates covering the
McAllen metropolitan area, which is the market a Weslaco evaluee commutes
within, together with the corresponding Texas statewide estimates for
context. State workforce agency labor market information and direct labor
market research supplement those sources when the referral questions call
for confirmation that specific occupations are actually hiring locally.
The opinion never assumes that a job existing somewhere in Texas is
available to this evaluee; availability is demonstrated, occupation by
occupation, from the sources the report cites.

## How a Weslaco evaluee is evaluated

The firm maintains its single home office in Kansas City and keeps no
premises in Weslaco; it publishes no local address because it has none,
and it considers that candor part of the report standard. Evaluation
proceeds remotely wherever the method allows: records review, structured
vocational interviews, and standardized testing under publisher protocols
that support remote administration. When in-person assessment is
warranted, the evaluee is seen in or near their own community, so the
evaluation reflects the conditions the evaluee actually lives and works
in. Travel for evaluations, depositions, and trial appearances is
scheduled from the Kansas City home office, and remote testimony is
available where the court and parties permit. Life care plans and medical
cost projections price future care to the evaluee's own regional market
using provider-specific inquiries and published fee data.
