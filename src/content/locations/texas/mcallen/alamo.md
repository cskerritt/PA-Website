An expert opinion in an Alamo case is only as strong as the record beneath
it, so Purinton Analytics begins every Alamo engagement with the file, not
the conclusion. The records request is tailored to the claims at intake,
the medical, employment, and educational documents are read before any
opinion takes shape, and the finished report walks its reader from source
to finding in order. That reader is assumed to be opposing counsel.
Plaintiff and defense attorneys retain the firm on these terms for
[vocational expert](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) opinions,
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) of the other
side's expert work.

## Documenting the labor market, not asserting it

The labor market section of an Alamo report holds itself to the same
documentary standard as the rest of the opinion. Findings on
employability and earning capacity rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates for the McAllen metropolitan
area and for Texas statewide, corroborated by state workforce agency labor
market information and, where warranted, direct labor market research. No
occupation appears in the report without a cited basis showing it is
within the evaluee's capacities and present in the market the evaluee can
reach. Care-related costs follow the same rule: life care plans and
medical cost projections are priced from provider-specific inquiries and
published fee data for the evaluee's own region, each figure traceable to
its source.

## Logistics for the Alamo evaluee

The firm's only staffed office is its Kansas City home office; it holds no
premises in Alamo and publishes no local address. The engagement is built
so that this rarely matters. Document work proceeds remotely. Structured
interviews and standardized testing are administered under publisher
protocols that support remote administration, and where the referral
questions require an in-person evaluation, the evaluee is seen in their
own community. Deposition and trial travel is scheduled from the Kansas
City home office against the court's calendar, with remote testimony
supported where the court and the parties permit. When the damages model
requires economic analysis, the firm coordinates
[a combined damages engagement](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
work while the vocational and care-planning analyses proceed in parallel.
