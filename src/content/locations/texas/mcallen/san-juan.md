Retain Purinton Analytics for a San Juan plaintiff and the method is
identical to the one a defense retention would receive: the same
instruments, the same sources, the same report architecture, the same
willingness to say what the evidence does not support. That symmetry is
the practice's core credential, and it is why San Juan referrals arrive
from both directions in injury, employment, and disability litigation.
Engagements typically involve
[vocational expert](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) opinions on
employability and earning capacity,
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) where injuries are
catastrophic, and [rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/)
when the assignment is to test another expert's work against its own cited
sources.

## San Juan's courts

State court litigation from San Juan is venued in the District Courts of
Hidalgo County. In federal court, the firm accepts engagements across the
districts serving Texas, among them the United States District Court for
the Southern District of Texas and the United States District Court for
the Western District of Texas, and it confirms the operative disclosure
requirements for the assigned forum during intake. Any venue-specific
registration or qualification requirement is identified and confirmed
during the conflict check and intake process. San Juan's venue position
within the broader McAllen-Edinburg-Mission, TX area is laid out on the
[metro page](/locations/texas/mcallen/).

## One labor market standard, applied locally

Because the same method serves either side, the labor market analysis for
a San Juan evaluee cannot lean toward anyone's preferred conclusion. It
begins with Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage
estimates for the McAllen metropolitan area and for Texas statewide, adds
state workforce agency labor market information, and extends to direct
labor market research when the referral questions require evidence about
specific local hiring. Every occupational finding traces to a cited
source. The same evenhandedness governs care planning: recommended future
care must follow from the medical record and treating-provider input, and
its pricing must come from the evaluee's own regional market, documented
so either party can check it.

## Appointments, not an office

There is no San Juan office and no published local address; the firm
works from its Kansas City home office and meets San Juan counsel and
evaluees by appointment. Most of the engagement is conducted remotely,
through records analysis, structured interviews, and standardized testing
under publisher protocols that support remote administration. In-person
evaluation, when warranted, happens in the evaluee's community. For
depositions and trial testimony, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City
home office against the court's dates, and remote testimony is supported
where the court and the parties allow it. Where economic damages
calculations are needed, the firm coordinates
[damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) in which
independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis
alongside the vocational and care-planning work.
