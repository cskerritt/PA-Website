throughout the districts serving Texas, including the United States

forum's disclosure rules confirmed during intake. Any venue-specific
registration or qualification requirement is identified and confirmed

## A small community inside a regional market

Alton is a smaller community whose residents work within the wider McAllen
metropolitan labor market, and the vocational analysis reflects that
reality. Employability and earning capacity findings draw on Bureau of
Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the
McAllen metropolitan area and for Texas statewide, on state workforce
agency labor market information, and on direct labor market research when
the referral questions require it. The analysis stays qualitative and
sourced: the report describes the character of the market an Alton evaluee
can reach and demonstrates, with citations, that each identified
occupation is real hiring rather than a statistical artifact.

## What an appointment actually involves

The absence of a local office changes nothing about the depth of the work.
Records review and analysis are performed remotely from the Kansas City
home office. Structured vocational interviews and standardized testing are
administered under publisher protocols that support remote administration,
so an Alton evaluee is commonly assessed from home. When the referral
requires an in-person evaluation, the appointment is set in the evaluee's
own community, and when deposition or trial requires the expert's
presence, travel is scheduled from Kansas City around the dates the court
has fixed. Remote testimony is supported where the court and the parties
allow. Life care plans price future care to the evaluee's regional market
through provider-specific inquiries and published fee data, with each
source cited in the report.
