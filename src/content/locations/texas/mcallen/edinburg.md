Edinburg carries its own name in the McAllen-Edinburg-Mission, TX title, and
litigation arising there deserves expert work addressed to the city itself
rather than borrowed wholesale from the metro. Purinton Analytics accepts
Edinburg referrals from counsel on either side of the caption for
[vocational expert](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) opinions on
employability and earning capacity, for
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) in catastrophic injury
matters, and for [rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/)
of opposing expert reports. Each engagement produces a written opinion whose
method is disclosed step by step, because the intended reader is not only
the retaining attorney but the lawyer across the table.

District of Texas and the state's other federal districts when a matter
sits elsewhere; the disclosure requirements of the specific forum are

## Edinburg's place in the metro labor market

An employability opinion for an Edinburg evaluee must describe the
employment reality that evaluee can actually reach, not a national
abstraction. Vocational findings rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates for the McAllen metropolitan
area and for Texas statewide, supplemented by state workforce agency labor
market information and by direct labor market research where the referral
questions warrant it. The point of that layering is auditability: when the
report identifies occupations an evaluee can perform, opposing counsel can
trace every one of them to a cited source describing hiring in the market
an Edinburg evaluee can reach rather than an assumption imported from
somewhere else.

## Seeing an Edinburg evaluee

The firm keeps no staffed office in Edinburg and publishes no street
address for this location; accuracy about offices is part of the same
discipline that governs the reports. Engagements ordinarily begin remotely,
with records review, structured vocational interviews, and standardized
testing administered under publisher protocols where remote administration
is supported. When the referral questions call for in-person evaluation,
the evaluee is seen in the Edinburg community, and travel for evaluations,
depositions, and trial testimony is scheduled from the Kansas City home
office around the court's dates. Remote testimony is supported wherever the
court and the parties permit it. Where the damages picture requires
economic analysis, the firm arranges
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.
