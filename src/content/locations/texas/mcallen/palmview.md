## County and courthouse context

Palmview matters filed in state court are venued in the District Courts
of Hidalgo County. Federal engagements are accepted throughout the
districts serving Texas, including the United States District Court for
the Southern District of Texas, and where a related matter sits in the
United States District Court for the Western District of Texas or another
Texas district, the practice is equally available there; the assigned
forum's disclosure requirements are confirmed at intake. Any
venue-specific registration or qualification requirement is identified
and confirmed during the conflict check and intake process. Palmview's
shared county footprint with the McAllen-Edinburg-Mission, TX area is
described on the [metro page](/locations/texas/mcallen/).

## A small town, analyzed inside its metro

Palmview is a smaller community whose labor market is the McAllen
metropolitan area around it, and the vocational analysis treats it that
way. Employability and earning capacity findings are grounded in Bureau
of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the
McAllen metropolitan area and for Texas statewide, in state workforce
agency labor market information, and in direct labor market research
where the referral questions call for local confirmation. The national
scope of the practice cuts in the evaluee's favor here: the same
methodology applied across many markets makes it obvious when an
occupation genuinely exists in this one, and the report says so with
citations rather than generalities.

## How the work reaches Palmview

There is no Palmview office, and the firm publishes no address here; it
serves the community by appointment from its Kansas City home office and
regards that plain statement as part of its credibility. The engagement
is remote-first: records analysis, structured vocational interviews, and
standardized testing under publisher protocols that support remote
administration. When the referral questions require an in-person
evaluation, the evaluee is seen in their own community. Travel for
evaluations, depositions, and trial testimony is scheduled from the
Kansas City home office and planned around the court's calendar, and
remote testimony is supported where the court and parties permit. Where
economic loss must be quantified, independent economist partners perform
and sign that analysis within a
[coordinated damages engagement](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
managed by the firm.
