How does a Kansas City practice evaluate someone who lives in Mission? That
is usually the first question counsel asks, and it has a concrete answer:
the work travels to the evaluee. Records review and analysis are remote by
default. Structured vocational interviews and standardized testing proceed
under publisher protocols that support remote administration, which means a
Mission evaluee is often assessed without leaving home, and when the
referral genuinely requires an in-person evaluation, it takes place in the
evaluee's own community rather than in a distant office. Purinton Analytics
structures its [vocational expert](/services/vocational-expert-witness/)
and [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) engagements this
way for plaintiff and defense counsel alike, so distance imposes no
discount on rigor.

## Courts serving Mission

Mission litigation in state court proceeds in the District Courts of
Hidalgo County. In the federal system, engagements are accepted in the
United States District Court for the Southern District of Texas, in the
United States District Court for the Northern District of Texas, and in
the state's remaining federal districts when a matter is venued there,
with the forum's disclosure requirements confirmed during intake. Any
venue-specific registration or qualification requirement is identified and
confirmed during the conflict check and intake process. Mission shares its
county, and therefore its state court venue, with the rest of the
McAllen-Edinburg-Mission, TX area; the
[metro page](/locations/texas/mcallen/) sets out that shared footprint.

## The market a Mission evaluee actually faces

Earning capacity is a local question. A Mission resident's realistic
employment options are described using Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates for the McAllen metropolitan
area and for Texas statewide, checked against state workforce agency labor
market information and, where the referral questions require it, direct
labor market research with employers in the region. Life care plans and
[medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/) follow the
same principle: future care is priced to the evaluee's own market through
provider-specific inquiries and published fee data, never inferred from a
national average. The report cites each source so that either side can
verify the figure trail.

## What by-appointment service means here

There is no Purinton Analytics storefront in Mission and no street address
published for this location, and the firm says so plainly because a
practice built on accurate reports should not maintain an inaccurate map.
What counsel gets instead is a defined logistics plan set at intake:
remote work wherever the method supports it, in-community evaluation when
the questions call for presence, and travel for depositions and trial
testimony scheduled from the Kansas City home office against dates fixed
with counsel in advance. Remote testimony is supported wherever the court
and the parties allow it, which keeps scheduling flexible without
sacrificing availability for live appearance.

## Opening a Mission engagement

Whether the referral comes from the plaintiff's side or the defense, the
gate is identical: no engagement before a documented conflict check.
Counsel can [request a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) with the case
caption and parties, flag the deadlines already on the docket, and expect
a written engagement confirmation and a tailored records request once the
check clears. Matters elsewhere in the state are described on the
[Texas locations page](/locations/texas/), and the firm's
[nationwide practice](/locations/nationwide/) applies the same process and
report standard in every forum it serves.
