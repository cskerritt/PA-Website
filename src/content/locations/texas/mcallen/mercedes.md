The cost of future care for a Mercedes evaluee is set by the providers who
will actually deliver that care, and a life care plan that prices it from
national averages misstates the damages for both sides. Purinton
Analytics builds its Mercedes
[life care plans](/services/life-care-planning/) and
[medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/) the other
way around: recommended care follows from the medical record and
treating-provider input, and pricing comes from provider-specific
inquiries and published fee data for the evaluee's own region, cited item
by item. Plaintiff and defense counsel also retain the firm for
[vocational expert](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) opinions when
the question is what work remains open to an injured Mercedes resident.

## Care planning that names its sources

A life care plan is a forecast, and forecasts earn credibility through
their sourcing. Each category of recommended care in a Mercedes plan
traces to the clinical record; each price traces to a provider inquiry or
published fee schedule for this region; and the narrative explains how
the pieces fit, so that opposing counsel can audit the plan line by line
rather than take its totals on trust. Where a damages presentation also
requires economic analysis, the firm arranges
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the care-planning and vocational work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## The vocational side of a Mercedes file

When earning capacity is in dispute, the analysis describes the market a
Mercedes evaluee can realistically reach, grounded in Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the McAllen
metropolitan area and for Texas statewide, in state workforce agency
labor market information, and in direct labor market research where the
referral questions call for it. The treatment is strictly qualitative and
strictly sourced, so vocational findings and care costs arrive in one
consistent evidentiary style.
