What counsel in the Houston-Pasadena-The Woodlands metro receive from
Purinton Analytics is a written opinion built to survive cross-examination:
a report that states its methods, cites its data, and reads the same
whether the retaining party is plaintiff or defense. That deliverable
anchors [vocational expert](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) work in
injury and employment litigation,
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) in catastrophic injury
matters, and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Where Houston matters are heard

The firm accepts matters venued in the District Courts of Harris County and
in the District Courts of the surrounding counties of the metro, including
Fort Bend County, Montgomery County, Brazoria County, Galveston County,
Chambers County, Liberty County, Waller County, Austin County, and
San Jacinto County. Federal engagements in Texas proceed in the United States
District Court for the Southern District of Texas, the United States
District Court for the Northern District of Texas, the United States
District Court for the Eastern District of Texas, and the United States
District Court for the Western District of Texas, with the disclosure
posture of each forum confirmed at intake. Matters arising in other parts
of the state are covered through the firm's
[Texas practice](/locations/texas/).

## Serving the metro without a storefront

Houston meetings are by appointment. The firm keeps no staffed office in
the metro and publishes no local street address, and it says so plainly
because candor about presence belongs in the same category as candor about
methodology. Engagements ordinarily open remotely: a documented conflict
check, a defined scope, a records request tailored to the claims, then
structured interviews and standardized testing under publisher protocols
where remote administration is supported. When the questions presented call
for an in-person evaluation, or when deposition and trial settings require
attendance, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office and
confirmed against the court's calendar.

## Grounding findings in Houston labor market data

Employability and earning capacity opinions for evaluees in the metro rest
on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates
for the Houston metropolitan area and for Texas statewide, together with
state workforce agency labor market information and direct labor market
research where warranted. The firm treats labor market evidence as
something to be documented, not assumed: occupations identified in a
transferable skills analysis are checked against the data for the region
the evaluee actually lives and works in. Life care plans and medical cost
projections follow the same logic, priced to the evaluee's own market
through provider-specific inquiries and published fee data rather than
national averages applied by default.

## Requesting a conflict check

Every Houston engagement, for either side of the caption, begins with a
documented conflict check and a defined scope of work. To begin, submit the
caption or party names through the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and identify any disclosure
deadlines, deposition settings, or trial dates that bear on scheduling. The
same intake process, report standard, and point of contact apply to every
matter the firm accepts, in the metro and across its
[national practice](/locations/nationwide/).
