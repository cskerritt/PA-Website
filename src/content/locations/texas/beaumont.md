Matters from the Beaumont-Port Arthur, TX metropolitan area reach Purinton
Analytics from the District Courts of Jefferson County, Hardin County, and
Orange County, and from the federal bench alike, and the firm answers them
with the same services it provides nationwide:
[vocational expert witness](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) analysis
of employability and earning capacity,
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) for catastrophic
injuries, and
[expert testimony and litigation consulting](/services/expert-testimony-litigation-consulting/)
when counsel need the analysis defended at deposition or trial. The firm
accepts retention from either side of the caption, and every report is
written so that the side that did not retain the firm can still follow its
reasoning.

## Courts served across the metro

Engagements are accepted for matters venued in the District Court of any of
the metro's counties, Jefferson, Hardin, and Orange, exactly as captioned.
Federal matters proceed in whichever district holds the case: the United
States District Court for the Eastern District of Texas, the United States
District Court for the Southern District of Texas, the United States
District Court for the Northern District of Texas, or the United States
District Court for the Western District of Texas. Where a matter also
requires economic damages, the firm offers
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under a single
engagement structure. The rest of the state is covered through the firm's
[Texas locations page](/locations/texas/).

## No storefront, and no pretense of a local office

The firm keeps no staffed office in Beaumont or Port Arthur and publishes no
street address for this location. Meetings are by appointment, and most
engagements never require a local office at all: records are transmitted
securely, evaluee interviews proceed by structured video conference, and
standardized testing is administered under publisher protocols where remote
administration is supported. When the referral questions call for in-person
evaluation, or when a deposition or trial date requires presence, travel is
arranged from the Kansas City home office and coordinated with the case
calendar counsel provide at intake. Honesty about presence is deliberate:
the same standard that forbids invented facts in a report forbids an
invented office on a website.

## Labor market evidence for local evaluees

Employability and earning capacity opinions for evaluees in the
Beaumont-Port Arthur area are grounded in Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates for the metro and for Texas
statewide, alongside state workforce agency labor market information and
direct labor market research where the transferable skills picture calls for
it. The firm does not substitute national averages for local evidence. Life
care plans and medical cost projections apply the same rule to care pricing:
recommended services are costed to the evaluee's own market through
provider-specific inquiries and published fee data, so opposing counsel can
trace each figure to a verifiable source rather than to an assumption.

## Opening a Beaumont-Port Arthur engagement

Every engagement begins with clearance, not commitment. Send the caption or
the party names through the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/), and flag
any disclosure deadline, deposition, or trial setting that bears on
scheduling. Counsel managing dockets that extend beyond the metro can
review the firm's [national coverage](/locations/nationwide/) to see how a
single engagement structure carries across venues.
