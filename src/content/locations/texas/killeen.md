Catastrophic injury cases drive many of the referrals Purinton Analytics
receives from the Killeen-Temple metro, and they usually arrive with the
same pair of questions: what will lifelong care cost, and what work remains
open to the injured person. The firm answers both, for plaintiff and
defense counsel, through [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/)
and [vocational expert](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) evaluation,
and adds
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. Employment matters from the metro follow the same
intake path and the same report standard.

## Venues across the Killeen-Temple metro

Engagements are accepted for matters in the District Courts of Bell County,
Coryell County, and Lampasas County. In federal court, Texas matters
proceed in the United States District Court for the Western District of
Texas, the United States District Court for the Eastern District of Texas,
the United States District Court for the Northern District of Texas, and
the United States District Court for the Southern District of Texas, with
the forum's disclosure requirements confirmed at intake. Any venue-specific
registration or qualification requirement is identified and confirmed
during the conflict check and intake process, and counsel with matters
elsewhere in the state can consult the
[Texas locations page](/locations/texas/).

## How the firm appears locally

Killeen and Temple meetings are by appointment. The firm maintains no
staffed office in the metro and publishes no street address for this
location, a fact it states plainly because a practice whose reports must
withstand cross-examination should not blur the facts about itself. Most
work proceeds remotely: records review, structured vocational interviews,
and standardized testing administered under publisher protocols where
remote administration is supported. When the referral questions require an
in-person evaluation, the evaluee is seen in their own community, and
travel for evaluations, depositions, and trial is scheduled from the
Kansas City home office around the dates the court sets.

## Labor market data for Bell County matters

Employability and earning capacity findings for evaluees in the metro rest
on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates
for the Killeen metropolitan area and for Texas statewide, on state
workforce agency labor market information, and on direct labor market
research where warranted. The firm verifies that occupations identified in
a transferable skills analysis are actually represented in the evaluee's
region before opining that they constitute an employment prospect. On the
care side, life care plans and medical cost projections are priced to the
evaluee's own market through provider-specific inquiries and published fee
data, so that the projected cost of care reflects the community where the
care will actually be delivered.

## Next steps for counsel

An engagement from either side of the caption starts with a documented
conflict check, a defined scope, and a records request tailored to the
claims. Submit the caption or party names through the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and note any imminent disclosure,
deposition, or trial dates so availability can be confirmed at intake. The
metro is served within the firm's broader
[national practice](/locations/nationwide/), which brings the same
evaluator, process, and report standard to every venue it covers.
