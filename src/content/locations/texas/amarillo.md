Purinton Analytics is a national practice, and the Amarillo, TX metropolitan
area is served the way every distant venue is served: through a structure
deliberately built so that distance from the Kansas City home office never
dilutes the work. Counsel in the region retain the firm for
[vocational expert witness](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) evaluation
of employability and earning capacity,
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) in catastrophic injury
matters, and [rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) of
opposing experts' opinions. Plaintiff and defense engagements are accepted
on equal terms, and each begins the same way regardless of venue: a
documented conflict check, a defined scope, and a records request tailored
to the claims.

## The metro's courts and counties

Engagements are accepted for matters venued in the District Courts of Potter
County, Randall County, Armstrong County, Carson County, and Oldham County,
the counties that compose the Amarillo Metropolitan Statistical Area.
Federal matters are handled in whichever court holds the case: the United
States District Court for the Northern District of Texas, the United States
District Court for the Western District of Texas, the United States District
Court for the Southern District of Texas, or the United States District
Court for the Eastern District of Texas. Any venue-specific registration or
qualification requirement is identified and confirmed during the conflict
check and intake process, and matters elsewhere in the state are covered
through the firm's [Texas locations page](/locations/texas/).

## Travel planned around the docket

There is no staffed Purinton Analytics office in Amarillo, and no street
address is published for this location; meetings are by appointment. Most of
an engagement proceeds remotely, through secure records transfer, structured
evaluee interviews by video, and standardized testing under publisher
protocols where remote administration is supported. When the referral
questions require in-person evaluation, or when a deposition or trial
setting requires attendance, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home
office and planned around the case deadlines counsel identify at intake, so
appearance dates are protected without inventing a local presence the firm
does not have.

## Amarillo labor market data, not national averages

Employability and earning capacity opinions for Amarillo area evaluees rest
on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for
the Amarillo metropolitan area and for Texas statewide, on state workforce
agency labor market information, and on direct labor market research where
the transferable skills analysis warrants it. Life care plans and medical
cost projections are priced to the evaluee's own community through
provider-specific inquiries and published fee data. Where the case also
requires an economic loss calculation, the firm offers
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under a single
engagement structure, keeping the disciplines aligned without merging them.

## Engaging the firm for an Amarillo matter

Start with the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/): the caption or party
names, plus any imminent disclosure, deposition, or trial dates that should
shape scheduling. Once clearance is confirmed, scope and fee terms follow in
writing. The firm's [national coverage](/locations/nationwide/) page
explains how the same remote-first structure and the same report standard
apply from this metro to any other venue counsel's docket reaches.
