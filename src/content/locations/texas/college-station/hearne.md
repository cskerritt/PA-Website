## Situating Hearne in the Metro Economy

Because Hearne is a small town within a larger metropolitan labor market, the vocational analysis has to be explicit about geography. Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the College Station-Bryan metropolitan area and for Texas statewide supply the occupational backbone, state workforce agency labor market information adds the local grain, and direct labor market research fills gaps the published data cannot. Findings on transferable skills, occupational access, and residual capacity are expressed qualitatively and sourced line by line, so the opinion can be tested on the record it cites. ## Meeting Counsel and Evaluees Where They Are

Hearne engagements run by appointment, and the firm states plainly that it keeps no local office there. Records review, intake conferences, and pre-deposition preparation proceed remotely over secure channels; evaluations are conducted remotely where the protocols support it and otherwise scheduled in the evaluee's community. Court appearances and depositions that require the expert present are met with travel from the Kansas City home office, planned against the case calendar rather than promised loosely. This is the same operating model the practice applies to matters [nationwide](/locations/nationwide/), and it is why a Robertson County file is never treated as an afterthought.
