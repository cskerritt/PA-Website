## A small town, a metro-sized labor market

San Elizario is a small community, and small communities pose a specific
methodological trap: an expert who treats the town as its own labor market
will understate or overstate an evaluee's options depending on which side
retained the analysis. The defensible unit is the El Paso metropolitan
labor market the evaluee can realistically access. Vocational findings
therefore rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and
wage estimates for the El Paso metropolitan area and for Texas statewide,
on state workforce agency labor market information, and on direct labor
market research where warranted. Where the referral pairs vocational loss
with economic loss, the firm provides
[coordinated damages assessment](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/),
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. The metro picture is drawn in fuller detail on the
[El Paso metro page](/locations/texas/el-paso/).
