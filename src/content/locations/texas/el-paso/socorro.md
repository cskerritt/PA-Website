An earning capacity opinion for a Socorro resident is only as good as its
definition of the relevant labor market. Socorro is one of the larger
suburban communities in the El Paso metropolitan area, and its workers do
not stop at the city limits; the jobs an injured evaluee could realistically
hold are spread across the region, and an expert who analyzes the town in
isolation produces a number no cross-examiner should let stand. Purinton
Analytics builds its Socorro engagements on the regional market as it
actually functions, offering
[vocational expert opinions](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) on
employability and earning capacity,
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) for catastrophic injury,
and [rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) of opposing
reports, for counsel on either side of the caption.

## Courts serving Socorro matters

State-court damages cases arising in Socorro proceed in the District Court
sitting in El Paso County, and the scheduling orders those courts issue
govern when expert designations and reports come due. For federal filings,
the firm accepts matters throughout the state's federal judicial districts,
including the United States District Court for the Western District of
Texas. Venue determines calendar, and calendar determines feasibility, so
both are confirmed at intake before any commitment is made. Statewide
coverage is described on the [Texas locations page](/locations/texas/).

## The regional market behind the opinion

Vocational findings for a Socorro evaluee rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the El Paso
metropolitan area and for Texas statewide, supplemented by state workforce
agency labor market information and by direct employer research when the
referral questions call for it. The analysis keeps separate a pair of
questions that are often blurred: what work exists in the regional economy,
and what work this particular evaluee can secure and sustain given medical
restrictions, transferable skills, education, and work history. Where a
matter pairs vocational loss with economic damages, the firm offers
[coordinated damages assessment](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/),
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. Context for the surrounding region appears on the
[El Paso metro page](/locations/texas/el-paso/).

## Meeting a Socorro evaluee

The firm keeps no office in Socorro and publishes no local address, because
it has none, and it says so plainly rather than implying a presence that
does not exist. Work is by appointment. Records review, structured
interview, and standardized testing generally proceed remotely, under
publisher protocols where remote administration is supported, which spares
an injured evaluee unnecessary travel and keeps the workplan moving. When
the referral questions require an in-person evaluation, or when a
deposition or trial in El Paso County requires the expert present, travel
is arranged from the Kansas City home office with lead time built around
the litigation dates.

## Opening a Socorro engagement

Every engagement begins with a documented conflict check, a defined scope,
and a records request tailored to the claims, so the eventual written
