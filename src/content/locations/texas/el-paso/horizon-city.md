For a Horizon City evaluee recovering from serious injury, the evaluation
itself should not become another burden. Purinton Analytics structures its
Horizon City work around the person being evaluated: records are gathered
and reviewed before any appointment is set, the structured interview and
standardized testing are scheduled at the evaluee's convenience, and remote
administration is used wherever test publishers support it. That
evaluee-centered logistics model serves whichever side retained the firm,
plaintiff or defense, and it underpins
[vocational expert opinions](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) on
employability and earning capacity,
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) for lifelong care
needs, and [medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/)
where future treatment must be priced.

## Where the evaluation leads: El Paso County courts

The evaluation exists to support an opinion that will be tested in a
courtroom. For Horizon City matters filed in state court, that courtroom is
the District Court sitting in El Paso County, whose docket and scheduling
orders fix the dates the expert work must meet. Federal matters are
accepted across the state's federal judicial districts, among them the
United States District Court for the Western District of Texas. The firm's
broader coverage of the state is set out on the
[Texas locations page](/locations/texas/).

## Placing Horizon City in the metro economy

Horizon City is a suburban community within the El Paso metropolitan
area, and the employment its residents can reach extends well beyond the
town itself. Earning capacity analysis therefore proceeds at the
level of the metro market, using Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational
employment and wage estimates for the El Paso metropolitan area and for
Texas statewide, together with state workforce agency labor market
information and targeted employer contact where a referral question
demands it. The written report keeps the chain of inference visible: from
documented restrictions, to transferable skills, to occupations that exist
regionally, to the subset this evaluee can actually obtain. Regional
context for the metro as a whole appears on the
[El Paso metro page](/locations/texas/el-paso/).

## How appointments actually work

There is no Purinton Analytics office in Horizon City, no storefront, and
no published street address, because accuracy about presence is part of
the same discipline that governs the reports. What counsel gets instead is
a by-appointment practice: most phases of the engagement proceed remotely,
which shortens timelines and reduces cost, and when an in-person
evaluation is warranted, or when deposition or trial testimony requires
the expert in El Paso County, travel is planned from the Kansas City home
office far enough ahead to protect the dates that matter.

## Before anything else, the conflict check

No records are reviewed and no opinions are formed until conflicts clear.
Counsel with a Horizon City matter should
[submit the caption or party names](/refer-a-case/) through the conflict
check form and note any imminent disclosure, deposition, or trial dates,
so the firm can say honestly at the outset whether the calendar is
workable. From clearance forward, the engagement follows the same defined
scope and documented method the firm brings to
[cases nationwide](/locations/nationwide/), producing a report written for
the most skeptical reader in the room: the lawyer on the other side.
