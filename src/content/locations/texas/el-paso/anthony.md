The disclosure calendar is the quiet adversary in every Anthony damages
case. Retain an expert late, and the choice narrows to a rushed report or
a missed deadline; retain early, and the same expert can build an opinion
with the depth a serious claim or defense deserves. Purinton Analytics
asks about dates before anything else in an Anthony engagement, then
commits to a workplan that honors them, whether the assignment is a
[vocational expert opinion](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) on
employability and earning capacity, a
[life care plan](/services/life-care-planning/) for lifelong medical and
support needs, or
[expert testimony and litigation consulting](/services/expert-testimony-litigation-consulting/)
as trial approaches. The firm takes these assignments from both plaintiff
and defense lawyers, and the reports read the same either way.

## The courts that set those dates

For an Anthony matter in state court, scheduling authority belongs to the
District Court sitting in El Paso County, and its orders establish when
expert designations and reports fall due. Federal engagements are accepted
throughout the state's federal judicial districts, among them the United
States District Court for the Western District of Texas. Because state and federal
systems run on different disclosure conventions, the firm identifies the
controlling forum and its operative dates during intake, before scope is
set. The rest of the state's coverage is described on the
[Texas locations page](/locations/texas/).

## What the market data must show

Anthony is a small town within the El Paso metropolitan area, so the
earning capacity question is never what work exists inside the town line;
it is what work an evaluee with documented restrictions can reach and hold
in the regional economy. That analysis draws on Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates for the El Paso metropolitan
area and for Texas statewide, on state workforce agency labor market
information, and on employer-level research when a referral question turns
on it. Each step from restriction to occupation to attainable employment
is written out and sourced, because an opinion that hides its reasoning
invites the cross-examination it deserves. The metro-level analysis is
discussed further on the [El Paso metro page](/locations/texas/el-paso/).

## Appointments without an office

There is no Anthony office to visit; the firm maintains none and publishes
no address for this location, and it prefers to say so rather than let a
map listing imply otherwise. Evaluations proceed by appointment, with
remote administration used wherever test publishers support it, so that
records review, interview, and testing rarely require an injured evaluee
to travel at all. When an in-person evaluation is warranted, or when
deposition or trial dates in El Paso County call for the expert's
presence, travel is arranged from the Kansas City home office with the
courtroom schedule as the fixed point.

## Put the dates on the table first

An Anthony engagement opens the same way every engagement does: with a
documented conflict check, a defined scope, and a records request tailored
to the claims. Counsel should
[submit the caption or party names](/refer-a-case/) and list every
disclosure, deposition, and trial date already set, so feasibility is
addressed candidly at the outset. Cleared matters then receive the
methodology the firm brings to its
[national practice](/locations/nationwide/): documented sources, explicit
reasoning, and an opinion prepared for the scrutiny of opposing counsel.
