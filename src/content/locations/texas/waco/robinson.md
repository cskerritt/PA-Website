The scheduling order usually decides whether a damages expert helps or hurts a Robinson case. Retained early, a vocational expert can shape discovery, flag the records that matter, and deliver a report with time to spare before the disclosure deadline; retained late, the same expert is racing the calendar. Purinton Analytics builds its Robinson engagements around that reality, mapping report, deposition, and supplementation dates at intake so that [vocational opinions](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) and [life care plans](/services/life-care-planning/) arrive when the litigation needs them. Plaintiff and defense referrals receive the same calendar discipline and the same methodology.

## Where Robinson Cases Are Heard

Robinson sits in McLennan County, whose civil disputes proceed in the District Court that serves the county. Federal filings are accepted across the state, from the United States District Court for the Western District of Texas to the Northern, Southern, and Eastern Districts, each on the same engagement terms; the [Texas locations page](/locations/texas/) describes the full landscape. Venue-specific qualification requirements are surfaced during the conflict check and resolved before scoping, never after.

## Labor Market Evidence for a Robinson Evaluee

Robinson belongs to the commuting market of the Waco metropolitan area, which the practice treats comprehensively on its [Waco metro page](/locations/texas/waco/). Vocational findings for an evaluee here rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates covering the metro area and Texas as a whole, together with state workforce agency labor market information and targeted employer contact where the questions warrant it. The written report keeps its prose qualitative and confines figures to sourced exhibits, because an opinion that cannot be traced to its data does not survive cross-examination and should not. If discovery later produces new medical restrictions, the analysis is revisited against the same sources and supplemented on the record rather than defended by improvisation.

## Appointment Logistics

The practice maintains no office in Robinson and publishes no local address, because there is none to publish. What counsel gets instead is a remote-first workflow: encrypted records transfer, video conferences for most attorney and clinician communication, and in-person evaluation conducted in the evaluee's own community when the assignment requires direct observation of function. When deposition or trial requires presence in the courtroom, travel is arranged from the Kansas City home office. The identical structure supports engagements [across the country](/locations/nationwide/), which is why a Robinson file is handled no differently than a metropolitan file anywhere else.

## Requesting a Conflict Check

Some Robinson referrals need the full damages architecture: in [coordinated damages assessment](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/), independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning opinions develop under a shared engagement structure, so the pieces fit without duplicating each other. Others need only a focused product, such as a [rebuttal review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) of an opposing expert's plan. Either way the door in is the same: [request a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) with the caption and parties, and include the operative scheduling order or the key dates from it, so the engagement can be built backward from the deadlines that govern it.
