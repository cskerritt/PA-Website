What can this person still earn? For a wage earner from Bellmead whose working body has been changed by injury, that question sits at the center of the damages case, and it deserves a better answer than a guess dressed up as an opinion. Purinton Analytics answers it through structured [vocational assessment](/services/vocational-expert-witness/): a review of the medical restrictions as documented, an inventory of the evaluee's skills and work history, and a disciplined comparison against the occupations genuinely open to them. Both sides of the caption retain the practice for this work, and the method does not bend to the retaining party.

## McLennan County and Federal Venues

Bellmead is a McLennan County community, and its civil cases are tried in the county's District Court. In federal court the practice serves the whole of Texas, appearing in the United States District Court for the Western District of Texas as readily as in the Northern, Southern, or Eastern Districts; statewide coverage is detailed on the [Texas locations page](/locations/texas/). Whatever qualification or registration a particular venue demands is identified at intake and confirmed before any opinion work begins.

## Measuring Work in the Waco Area

An earning capacity opinion is only as good as the labor market evidence beneath it. For a Bellmead evaluee that market is the Waco metropolitan area, covered in depth on the [metro page](/locations/texas/waco/), and the evidence comes from Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the metro and for Texas statewide, from state workforce agency labor market information, and from direct labor market research when a referral turns on the availability of specific work. Loss is expressed as the difference between the pre-injury and post-injury occupational pictures, each documented, so that the conclusion can be tested rather than merely asserted. Where the evaluee has already returned to lighter duties, the analysis examines the durability and trajectory of that placement instead of treating any current job as the end of the inquiry.

## Seeing the Evaluee in Bellmead

The practice keeps no Bellmead office and claims none. Engagements run by appointment: records arrive electronically, most conferences happen over secure video, and in-person evaluation, when the referral requires it, is conducted in the evaluee's home or community, where functional limits show themselves in ordinary tasks. Travel for evaluations, depositions, and trial is scheduled from the Kansas City home office, the same arrangement that supports the firm's [nationwide practice](/locations/nationwide/). Where future medical needs are also in dispute, [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) or a narrower [medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/) can be joined to the vocational work without duplicating it.

## Referring the Case

When counsel needs the lost earnings finding converted into economic damages, the practice offers [coordinated damages assessment](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/), in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning opinions proceed under a shared engagement structure. The entry point is the same regardless of scope: [submit the caption or party names for a conflict check](/refer-a-case/), summarize the injury and the employment history at issue, and flag any dates that constrain the work ahead.
