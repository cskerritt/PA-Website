## Bosque County Courts

Clifton lies in Bosque County, and civil actions arising there are heard in the District Court that serves the county. Federal work is accepted throughout the state, whether venued in the United States District Court for the Western District of Texas or in the Northern, Southern, or Eastern Districts; the [Texas locations page](/locations/texas/) lays out the statewide court structure. Any venue-particular registration or qualification obligation is identified in the intake and conflict-check process and confirmed before the scope of work is finalized.

## Grounding Opinions in Regional Labor Data

A Clifton evaluee's employment prospects are analyzed against the commuting region anchored by the Waco metropolitan area, which the practice covers on its [metro page](/locations/texas/waco/). The evidentiary base is consistent from case to case: Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the metro area and for Texas as a whole, state workforce agency labor market information, and direct labor market research when the contested issue is the real availability of particular jobs. Because the town is small and the region's economy is not, the report is explicit about which market it is measuring and why, a transparency that holds up when the methodology is challenged. The same record discipline governs the care-planning side: every recommended service traces to a clinician's order, a published standard of care, or a documented need, and the plan identifies which.

## How Distance Is Handled

There is no Purinton Analytics office in Clifton, and none is claimed; candor about presence is part of the product. In practice, distance rarely touches the quality of the work. Records arrive electronically, attorney conferences and many interviews run over secure video, and where the referral requires hands-on functional evaluation, the assessment takes place in the evaluee's own home or community rather than in a rented conference room. Appearances for deposition and trial are planned as travel from the Kansas City home office, on the same logistical footing the practice uses for engagements [throughout the country](/locations/nationwide/).
