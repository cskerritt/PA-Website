## Reading the Labor Market for a Marlin Evaluee

The vocational questions in a Marlin file are answered against the commuting market of the Waco metropolitan area, which the practice covers in depth on its [Waco metro page](/locations/texas/waco/). Findings about occupational access and earnings rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for that metropolitan area and for Texas statewide, supplemented by state workforce agency labor market information and by direct labor market research when the contested issue is whether particular work is genuinely available to this evaluee with these documented restrictions. The written report keeps its narrative qualitative and ties every conclusion to cited exhibits, so that an opposing expert or cross-examiner can retrace each step from source to opinion.

## Serving Marlin by Appointment

Purinton Analytics maintains no office in Marlin and publishes no local address, because candor about presence belongs to the same discipline that governs the reports. Engagements run by appointment. Records move through secure electronic transfer, attorney conferences and many interviews proceed over video, and when the referral calls for in-person evaluation, the assessment is conducted in the evaluee's home or community, where functional capacity shows itself in ordinary tasks rather than in a conference room. Travel for evaluations, depositions, and trial testimony is scheduled from the Kansas City home office, the same base that supports the practice's [national caseload](/locations/nationwide/), and appearance dates are confirmed with counsel early so the calendar never becomes the surprise in the case.
