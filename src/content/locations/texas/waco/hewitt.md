An injured person living in Hewitt should not have to organize a recovery around an expert's office hours in a distant city. Purinton Analytics brings the assessment to the case instead: [vocational evaluations](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) and [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) interviews are arranged where the evaluee actually lives and functions, and the resulting opinions are written to withstand examination from either side of the caption. Counsel for plaintiffs and counsel for defendants retain the practice on the same terms, and the work product does not change with the client.

## The McLennan County Venue

Hewitt lies in McLennan County, and civil matters arising there are heard in the District Court serving the county. When a Hewitt case is filed federally, the practice appears throughout Texas, including in the United States District Court for the Western District of Texas, with the state's remaining federal districts served on identical terms; the [Texas locations page](/locations/texas/) sets out the statewide venue picture. Any registration or qualification requirement particular to a venue is identified during intake and confirmed before the engagement is scoped, so neither side discovers a procedural surprise on the eve of disclosure.

## Hewitt Workers in the Waco Commuting Market

A vocational opinion about a Hewitt evaluee must reckon with the commuting market that surrounds the town, which is the Waco metropolitan area addressed in full on the [Waco metro page](/locations/texas/waco/). The analysis draws on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for that metro area and for Texas statewide, supplemented by state workforce agency labor market information and by direct labor market research when the referral calls for it. Transferable skills are analyzed against occupations that actually exist within the evaluee's reach, not against a national abstraction, and every source is cited so opposing counsel can retrace the path from data to conclusion.

## Evaluations Where the Evaluee Lives

There is no Purinton Analytics office in Hewitt, and the practice does not pretend otherwise. Records move electronically, conferences and many clinical interviews proceed over secure video, and when the referral requires an in-person functional assessment it takes place in the evaluee's home or community, where daily function can be observed honestly rather than reconstructed from memory in a conference room. Travel for evaluations, depositions, and trial testimony is scheduled from the Kansas City home office, the same base from which the firm serves counsel [nationwide](/locations/nationwide/). Where the assignment is narrower than a full plan, [medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/) can be scoped to the disputed items alone.

## Beginning a Hewitt Engagement

When the damages picture requires a present-value figure, the practice offers [coordinated damages assessment](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/), in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under a shared engagement structure. Every file, whether plaintiff or defense, begins with a documented conflict check and a defined scope. To start, [submit a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) with the caption or party names, describe the injury in general terms, and note any disclosure or deposition dates already on the calendar so scheduling can be confirmed at the outset.
