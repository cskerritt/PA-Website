The Dallas-Fort Worth-Arlington metropolitan statistical area spreads
litigation across a county footprint that includes Hunt County,
Kaufman County, Parker County, and Wise County alongside the metro's core
counties, and Purinton Analytics serves plaintiff and defense counsel
throughout it.
Referrals from the metro typically involve
[earning capacity and employability evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/)
in injury and employment matters,
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) in catastrophic injury
cases, and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Venue footprint in Dallas-Fort Worth

Engagements are accepted for matters in the District Courts of Dallas County
and Tarrant County and in the District Courts of the surrounding counties of
the metro, including Collin County, Denton County, Ellis County,
Johnson County, and Rockwall County. In the federal system, Texas matters
proceed in
the United States District Court for the Northern District of Texas, the
United States District Court for the Eastern District of Texas, the United
States District Court for the Southern District of Texas, and the United
States District Court for the Western District of Texas, and the firm
prepares every report to the same disclosure standard wherever the filing or
removal decision places a case. Counsel with matters elsewhere in the state
will find the broader practice described on the
[Texas locations page](/locations/texas/).

## Meetings by appointment across the metro

There is no staffed Purinton Analytics office in Dallas or Fort Worth, and
the firm publishes no street address for this location, because accuracy
about presence is part of the same discipline that governs its reports.
Meetings, evaluations, and testimony appearances in the metro are arranged
by appointment. Most engagements begin remotely, with records review,
structured interviews, and standardized testing administered under publisher
protocols that support remote use. When an in-person evaluation is
warranted, or when deposition and trial calendars require presence, travel
is scheduled from the Kansas City home office and planned around the court's
dates rather than the firm's.

## Labor market research for North Texas claims

Vocational findings for evaluees in the metro rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Dallas-Fort
Worth-Arlington metropolitan area and for Texas statewide, supplemented by
state workforce agency labor market information and direct employer
research where the referral questions warrant it. Because the metro
functions as a major regional employment center, transferable skills
analysis frequently turns on which occupations are actually present in the
local economy, and the firm documents that presence rather than assuming
it. Life care plans and medical cost projections are priced to the
evaluee's own market using provider-specific inquiries and published fee
data for the region.

## Starting an engagement

Whether the caption names a plaintiff or a defendant, the engagement begins
the same way: a documented conflict check, a defined scope, and a records
request tailored to the claims. Submit the caption or party names through
the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and note any imminent disclosure,
deposition, or trial dates so scheduling can be confirmed at intake. Counsel
weighing a matter outside the metro can review the firm's
[national coverage](/locations/nationwide/), which applies the same report
standard and the same engagement process to every venue the firm serves.
