An earning capacity opinion is only as good as the labor market it is
anchored to, and for the Lubbock, TX metropolitan area that anchoring is the
starting point of every engagement Purinton Analytics accepts. The metro's
counties, Lubbock, Cochran, Crosby, Garza, Hockley, and Lynn, form a wide
catchment in which an evaluee's realistic employment options can differ
sharply from what a statewide summary would suggest. The firm's
[vocational expert witness](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) work,
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/) services are
all built to reflect that local reality, for plaintiff and defense counsel
alike, in the same written form either side could hand to the other.

## Grounding opinions in the Lubbock labor market

Vocational findings for Lubbock area evaluees rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Lubbock
metropolitan area and for Texas statewide, on state workforce agency labor
market information, and on direct labor market research where the
transferable skills analysis warrants employer-level verification. Life care
plans and cost projections are priced the same way: to the evaluee's own
community, using provider-specific inquiries and published fee data, so that
every figure in the plan traces to a source counsel can test. Where the
damages picture also requires an economist, the firm offers
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under a single
engagement structure.

## Venues across the metro's counties

Engagements are accepted for matters venued in the District Court of
Lubbock County and the District Courts of Cochran County and the other
surrounding metro counties, Crosby, Garza, Hockley, and Lynn, as captioned. Federal matters are
handled in whichever forum holds the case: the United States District Court
for the Northern District of Texas, the United States District Court for the
Western District of Texas, the United States District Court for the Eastern
District of Texas, or the United States District Court for the Southern
District of Texas. Any venue-specific registration or qualification
requirement is identified and confirmed during the conflict check and intake
process. Coverage for the rest of the state is described on the firm's
[Texas locations page](/locations/texas/).

## By appointment, on the schedule counsel set

Lubbock meetings are by appointment. The firm has no staffed office in the
metro and publishes no street address for this location, a choice made for
the same reason its reports avoid unsupported assertions. Engagements
typically begin remotely, with secure records transfer, structured evaluee
interviews by video, and standardized testing under publisher protocols
where remote administration is supported. When in-person evaluation is the
right method, or when deposition and trial dates require attendance, travel
is scheduled from the Kansas City home office around the dates counsel
supply at intake, and the report standard does not vary with the location of
the work.
