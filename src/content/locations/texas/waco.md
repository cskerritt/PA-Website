Every report Purinton Analytics writes for the Waco, TX metropolitan area is
drafted on the assumption that opposing counsel will read it closely, and
that assumption is the practice's core discipline rather than a slogan.
Plaintiff and defense retentions proceed on the same methodology, the same
fee structure, and the same willingness to say what the record does not
support. For Waco counsel that discipline is applied through
[vocational expert witness](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) evaluation
of employability and earning capacity,
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) in catastrophic injury
matters, and [rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/)
when the assignment is to test another expert's work rather than to build an
affirmative opinion.

## Where Waco matters are venued

The metro comprises McLennan County, Bosque County, and Falls County, and
engagements are accepted for matters venued in the District Court of each.
Federal cases proceed in whichever forum holds them: the United States
District Court for the Western District of Texas, the United States District
Court for the Northern District of Texas, the United States District Court
for the Eastern District of Texas, or the United States District Court for
the Southern District of Texas. Any venue-specific registration or
qualification requirement is identified and confirmed during the conflict
check and intake process, and counsel litigating elsewhere in the state can
consult the firm's [Texas locations page](/locations/texas/) for statewide
coverage.

## Presence in Waco, stated plainly

There is no staffed Purinton Analytics office in Waco, and the firm
publishes no street address for this location. Meetings happen by
appointment, and the bulk of most engagements happens at a distance: secure
records exchange, structured evaluee interviews by video, and standardized
testing under publisher protocols where remote administration is supported.
When an in-person evaluation better serves the referral questions, or when a
deposition or trial setting requires attendance, travel is scheduled from
the Kansas City home office and built around the case calendar counsel
provide. Stating this plainly matters, because a firm that would blur the
facts about its own offices invites the inference that it would blur other
facts too.

## Labor market and cost foundations

Employability and earning capacity findings for Waco area evaluees are
grounded in Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage
estimates for the Waco metropolitan area and for Texas statewide, in state
workforce agency labor market information, and in direct labor market
research where warranted. Care recommendations in life care plans and
medical cost projections are priced to the evaluee's own market with
provider-specific inquiries and published fee data. When a full damages
presentation is needed, the firm offers
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under a single
engagement structure, keeping the analyses methodologically aligned without
blurring the line between disciplines.
