Every San Antonio engagement begins the way Purinton Analytics begins its
work everywhere: with a documented conflict check, a defined scope, and a
records request tailored to the claims, so that the written opinion rests
on a record both sides can examine. From that foundation, counsel in the
San Antonio-New Braunfels metro retain the firm for
[earning capacity and employability evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
for [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) in catastrophic
injury matters, and for
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Courts and counties served

The metro's venue footprint centers on the District Courts of Bexar County
and extends to the District Courts of the surrounding counties, including
Comal County, Guadalupe County, Kendall County, Medina County,
Bandera County, Atascosa County, and Wilson County. In federal court, Texas
matters
proceed in the United States District Court for the Western District of
Texas, the United States District Court for the Southern District of Texas,
the United States District Court for the Northern District of Texas, and
the United States District Court for the Eastern District of Texas. Any
venue-specific registration or qualification requirement is identified and
confirmed during the conflict check and intake process, and matters from
other regions of the state are addressed on the
[Texas locations page](/locations/texas/).

## By-appointment presence in San Antonio

The firm maintains no staffed office in San Antonio and publishes no street
address for this location; meetings, evaluations, and testimony appearances
are arranged by appointment. That candor is deliberate. A practice that
holds its reports to a standard suitable for opposing counsel applies the
same standard to how it describes itself. In practice, most engagements
proceed remotely through records review, structured interviews, and
standardized testing under publisher protocols that support remote
administration. Where the referral questions require an in-person
evaluation, or where deposition and trial settings require presence, travel
is scheduled from the Kansas City home office around the dates the court
sets.

## Labor market evidence for Bexar County matters

Vocational opinions for evaluees in the metro are grounded in Bureau of
Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the San
Antonio metropolitan area and for Texas statewide, in state workforce
agency labor market information, and in direct labor market research where
the case warrants it. When a transferable skills analysis identifies
candidate occupations, the firm verifies that those occupations exist in
the evaluee's actual labor market rather than relying on national
assumptions. Life care plans and medical cost projections are priced to the
evaluee's own community through provider-specific inquiries and published
fee data for the region, since the cost of future care is a local question
even when the litigation is not.

## How an engagement begins

Plaintiff and defense referrals follow an identical path. Submit the
caption or party names through the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/), describe the questions the case
presents, and flag any imminent disclosure, deposition, or trial dates.
Once conflicts clear, scope and scheduling are confirmed in writing. The
process mirrors the firm's [national practice](/locations/nationwide/), so
counsel who later bring a matter from another venue will find nothing about
the engagement changed but the caption.
