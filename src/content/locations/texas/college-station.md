Catastrophic injury litigation rarely needs just a single expert, and
counsel in the College Station-Bryan, TX metropolitan area often come to
Purinton Analytics precisely because the firm can keep several strands of a
damages case aligned. Through
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/),
independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis while
the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under a single engagement
structure, so the earning capacity findings, the
[life care plan](/services/life-care-planning/), and the economic loss
calculation rest on consistent assumptions instead of colliding at
deposition. Counsel who need a narrower retention engage the firm for
[vocational expert witness](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) work or
[medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/) alone, on the
same terms for plaintiff and defense.

## Venues in and around Brazos County

Engagements are accepted for matters venued in the District Courts of
Brazos County, Burleson County, and Robertson County, the counties of the
College Station-Bryan Metropolitan Statistical Area. Federal matters proceed
in whichever forum holds the case: the United States District Court for the
Southern District of Texas, the United States District Court for the Western
District of Texas, the United States District Court for the Eastern District
of Texas, or the United States District Court for the Northern District of
Texas. Any venue-specific registration or qualification requirement is
identified and confirmed during the conflict check and intake process, and
the firm's [Texas locations page](/locations/texas/) describes coverage for
the rest of the state.

## Appointment-based presence, honestly described

Purinton Analytics maintains no staffed office in College Station or Bryan
and publishes no street address for this location. Meetings are arranged by
appointment, and most stages of an engagement are handled remotely: secure
records exchange, structured evaluee interviews by video, and standardized
testing under publisher protocols where remote administration is supported.
Where the referral questions require in-person evaluation, or where
deposition and trial settings require attendance, travel is scheduled from
the Kansas City home office around the dates counsel provide at intake. The
firm treats accuracy about its own footprint as part of the same standard
that governs its expert opinions, which is why no local office is implied
where none exists.

## Labor market and care cost research

Employability and earning capacity findings for evaluees in the College
Station-Bryan area are built on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational
employment and wage estimates for the metro and for Texas statewide, on
state workforce agency labor market information, and on direct labor market
research where the transferable skills analysis calls for employer-level
confirmation. Care recommendations in life care plans and medical cost
projections are priced to the evaluee's own market using provider-specific
inquiries and published fee data, so each cost line can be traced to its
source when the plan is examined by the other side of the caption.
