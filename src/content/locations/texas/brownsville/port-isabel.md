Purinton Analytics has no office in Port Isabel. That sentence leads this page deliberately, because an expert practice that would shade the truth about its own footprint invites the question of what else it shades. What counsel with a Port Isabel matter gets instead of a storefront is a defined process: a documented conflict check, a scope agreed in writing, [vocational evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) and [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) conducted remotely or in the evaluee's own community as the referral requires, and a written opinion that plaintiff and defense counsel can each hold to the same standard.

## Earning Capacity Beyond the Town Limits

For a Port Isabel evaluee, the vocational question is what employment the surrounding labor market realistically offers given documented restrictions, and the answer is grounded in sources rather than local impressions. The practice relies on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Brownsville-Harlingen metropolitan area and for Texas statewide, on state workforce agency labor market information, and on direct labor market research when the referral questions call for it. Findings on occupational access and transferable skills remain qualitative and traceable, and where the record leaves a question open, the report says so plainly rather than papering over the gap with an assumption. The [Brownsville metro page](/locations/texas/brownsville/) describes the regional market these analyses draw upon.

## Appointments, Not Addresses

Everything in-person is scheduled, and nothing is pretended. Counsel conferences run by video or by arranged visit. Evaluee interviews happen by secure video when appropriate; when standardized instruments or hands-on assessment require presence, the evaluator comes to the evaluee's community, with travel planned from the Kansas City home office. The same planning covers depositions, hearings, and trial testimony, coordinated against the case calendar at the time the engagement is scoped so appearance logistics never become a late surprise, and counsel always knows in advance which commitments will be met in person and which by remote means.
