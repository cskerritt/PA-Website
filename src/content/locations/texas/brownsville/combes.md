Municipal boundaries carry almost no weight in a competent earning capacity analysis. For an evaluee living in Combes, a small community within the Brownsville-Harlingen metropolitan area, the vocational question is what employment the commuting market as a whole offers a person with documented restrictions, not what exists inside one town's limits. Purinton Analytics frames every Combes referral that way from the outset, whether the engagement is a [vocational evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/), a [life care plan](/services/life-care-planning/), or both, and whether the retaining counsel represents the plaintiff or the defense.

## How a Commuting Market Is Actually Analyzed

Treating the region as the market imposes an evidentiary burden the analysis must meet. The practice meets it with Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Brownsville-Harlingen metropolitan area and for Texas statewide, with state workforce agency labor market information, and with direct labor market research when the referral questions demand ground-level confirmation. Occupational access, transferable skills, and the contrast between pre-injury and post-injury capacity are described qualitatively, each finding resting on a stated source, and the report explains why the commuting market, rather than the town alone, is the correct frame, so the framing itself survives cross-examination. What the regional market looks like from its center is addressed on the [Brownsville metro page](/locations/texas/brownsville/).

## Serving an Evaluee Where the Evaluee Lives

Because the analysis reaches across the region, the evaluation itself does not have to. Interviews with a Combes evaluee proceed by secure video when the referral permits, scheduled around treatment and family obligations; when standardized testing or hands-on assessment is warranted, the evaluator travels to the evaluee's own community rather than summoning the evaluee to an office. The firm has no office in Combes and claims none anywhere it does not exist. In-person commitments, including depositions and trial testimony, are met through travel from the Kansas City home office, planned against the case calendar.
