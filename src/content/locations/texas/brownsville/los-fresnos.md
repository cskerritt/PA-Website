## How the Evaluation Actually Unfolds

Records arrive electronically under a request tailored to the claims. The clinical interview and work history review are scheduled at times chosen around treatment, caregiving, and employment, not around an office lobby. Instruments that must be administered in person are flagged at intake, and when they are required the evaluator travels, with trips planned from the Kansas City home office. The firm maintains no Los Fresnos office and publishes no local address, a matter of accuracy rather than apology: candor about presence is part of the same standard that governs every citation in the report.
