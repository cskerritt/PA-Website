Harlingen shares its name with the metropolitan area it helps anchor, and that fact shapes how Purinton Analytics approaches referrals from the city. A vocational or life care planning question arising here is a question about a regional employment center in its own right, not about a satellite of somewhere else. The practice brings the same disciplined method to Harlingen that it applies nationally: [vocational evaluations](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) grounded in documented sources, [life care plans](/services/life-care-planning/) built from the medical record, and written opinions prepared on the assumption that opposing counsel will read every line. Plaintiff and defense referrals are accepted on identical terms, and the analysis does not change with the side of the caption.

## A Regional Employment Center in the Analysis

Because Harlingen functions as an employment hub within its region, earning capacity questions here often run in both directions: what work the city itself offers an injured resident, and what the wider commuting market adds. The foundation is qualitative and sourced. Vocational findings rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Brownsville-Harlingen metropolitan area and for Texas statewide, supplemented by state workforce agency labor market information and direct labor market research where the referral questions warrant it. Scale is described carefully and every conclusion about occupational access and transferable skills is tied to a cited source, so the opinion can withstand cross-examination. The market context for the region as a whole appears on the [Brownsville metro page](/locations/texas/brownsville/).

## Meetings by Appointment, Honesty About Offices

The firm keeps no staffed office in Harlingen and publishes no local street address, because accuracy about presence belongs to the same discipline that governs the reports. Counsel conferences happen by video or by scheduled visit. Evaluee interviews proceed remotely when the referral permits, and in the evaluee's own community when hands-on assessment or standardized testing is warranted, so that the person being evaluated is not asked to organize recovery around an expert's calendar. When deposition, hearing, or trial dates require the evaluator in the courtroom, travel is arranged from the Kansas City home office and planned against the case schedule.
