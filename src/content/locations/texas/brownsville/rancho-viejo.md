Serious injury litigation rarely raises one expert question at a time. A Rancho Viejo matter may need a [vocational opinion](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) on lost earning capacity, a [life care plan](/services/life-care-planning/) projecting future care, and an economic analysis that translates both into present terms, and counsel who retain those pieces separately often inherit experts whose assumptions quietly contradict one another. Purinton Analytics exists to prevent that. The practice coordinates the disciplines under one engagement structure so that the opinions reaching a courtroom, for plaintiff or defense, share consistent foundations.

## One Labor Market Foundation Across the Disciplines

Consistency starts with sources. The vocational analysis for a Rancho Viejo evaluee, a resident of a small community assessed against the region's commuting market, rests on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Brownsville-Harlingen metropolitan area and for Texas statewide, on state workforce agency labor market information, and on direct labor market research where the referral questions warrant it. Those same documented findings feed the care plan's assumptions about function and the economists' inputs, so no expert in the engagement is building on a fact another has contradicted. ## Coordination Without a Local Office

None of this requires a storefront, and the firm does not maintain one in Rancho Viejo or publish a local address. Evaluee interviews proceed by secure video when the referral permits and in the evaluee's own community when in-person assessment is warranted. Counsel conferences, joint scheduling across the disciplines, and draft review all run by appointment, so counsel deals with a single point of coordination rather than chasing separate calendars. Appearances for deposition or trial are met through travel from the Kansas City home office, planned when testimony dates are set rather than negotiated under deadline pressure.
