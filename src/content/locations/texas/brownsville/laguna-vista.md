Referrals from Laguna Vista can arrive from either direction: a plaintiff firm proving what an injury took from a client's working life, or a defense team examining whether a claimed loss survives scrutiny. Purinton Analytics runs one method for both. The [vocational evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) asks the same questions, the [life care plan](/services/life-care-planning/) applies the same clinical standards, and the report reads the same whichever side signs the engagement letter, because an opinion that shifts with its audience is not an opinion a court should hear.

## Cameron County, State and Federal Forums

Laguna Vista is part of Cameron County, and civil engagements are accepted for the District Court that serves the county. On the federal side, the practice accepts appointments across the federal districts of Texas, among them the United States District Court for the Southern District of Texas. Whether a particular forum imposes registration or qualification requirements is a question resolved during the conflict check and intake process, not left for motion practice to expose. The practice's coverage of courts throughout the state is collected on the [Texas locations page](/locations/texas/).

## The Same Market, Whoever Asks

The labor market does not care who retained the expert, and the analysis reflects that. Earning capacity for a Laguna Vista evaluee is measured against the commuting market of the surrounding region using Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Brownsville-Harlingen metropolitan area and for Texas statewide, state workforce agency labor market information, and direct labor market research where the referral questions require it. A small community's residents are evaluated against the employment actually reachable from it, described qualitatively and cited throughout. The regional market is discussed at greater depth on the [Brownsville metro page](/locations/texas/brownsville/).

## Logistics That Do Not Depend on an Office

The firm keeps no office in Laguna Vista and does not publish a local address, and neither side of a caption is disadvantaged by that. Counsel meetings occur by appointment, usually by video. Most evaluee interviews are completed over an encrypted video connection. Where the referral calls for testing or observation that cannot be done remotely, the evaluator makes the trip and conducts that portion of the work where the evaluee lives, at hours that accommodate medical care and employment. Which parts of the assessment demand presence is settled while the engagement is scoped, so the evaluation follows a plan fixed at the outset rather than one improvised as obstacles appear. Appearances for deposition, hearing, or trial are handled through travel from the Kansas City home office, arranged when dates are set so testimony is never hostage to logistics.

## Retaining the Practice

Where a matter needs economic modeling in addition to vocational and care-planning work, the firm offers [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis under one engagement structure, keeping the disciplines consistent with one another. Counsel in any state can engage the practice the same way, as the [nationwide page](/locations/nationwide/) sets out. For a Laguna Vista matter, the first step is the same for every client: [submit a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) with the caption and parties so that clearance, scope, and schedule can be confirmed before any analysis begins.
