## The Harrison County venue and the federal bench

Engagements are accepted for matters in the District Court of Harrison County and, on the federal side, in each of the federal districts of Texas on identical terms: the United States District Court for the Eastern District of Texas, the United States District Court for the Northern District of Texas, the United States District Court for the Southern District of Texas, and the United States District Court for the Western District of Texas. Which district holds a particular Marshall case is a matter for counsel and the docket; the firm's obligations are the same in all of them, and the methodology does not change with the forum. Each engagement opens with a documented conflict check, a defined scope, and a records request tailored to the claims, so the resulting report can support direct examination and withstand cross-examination in equal measure. Counsel who already hold an opposing expert's report can retain the firm for [rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) on the same disciplined footing. Any venue-specific registration or qualification requirement is identified and confirmed during the conflict check and intake process.

## Meeting and evaluating in Marshall

There is no Purinton Analytics office in Marshall, and the firm does not pretend otherwise. Meetings are by appointment; most of the work proceeds remotely through secure document exchange and video conference. In-person vocational or life care planning evaluations are arranged in the evaluee's own community when the referral calls for them, which spares an injured person a difficult trip and lets the evaluator see circumstances as they are. When deposition or trial testimony requires presence in Harrison County, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office and planned around the court's calendar rather than the firm's convenience.
