An evaluation should come to the evaluee, not the other way around. For a Henderson resident whose earning capacity or future care needs are at issue, Purinton Analytics arranges [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) and [vocational expert witness](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) evaluations in the evaluee's own community, or by secure remote means when health, distance, or scheduling makes that the sounder path. Counsel on either side of a Rusk County case get the same thing either way: a defined scope, a documented conflict check at the outset, and a written opinion that holds up when the other side reads it.

## What a Henderson evaluation involves

The process is deliberately unhurried. Records are requested against the claims actually pleaded, reviewed before any interview, and gaps are flagged early so counsel can cure them before deadlines close in. The clinical interview and any functional or vocational testing are scheduled around the evaluee's treatment calendar, and collateral interviews with family or caregivers are added when the referral questions require them. For a catastrophic injury, the [life care plan](/services/life-care-planning/) is built item by item from the medical record and treating-provider input; a [medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/) is available when the assignment calls for a narrower costing exercise instead. None of this depends on the evaluee traveling to a distant office, because the firm keeps no office in Henderson at all and does not suggest otherwise.
