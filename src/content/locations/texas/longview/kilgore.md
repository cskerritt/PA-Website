Because Kilgore sits within the Longview, TX metropolitan area for statistical purposes, the wage and employment data most relevant to a Kilgore evaluee describe the metro economy as a whole rather than the town in isolation, and a careful expert says so plainly instead of implying town-level precision the data cannot support. Purinton Analytics builds its [vocational expert witness](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) opinions for Kilgore matters on that foundation, applied to the specific evaluee's skills, restrictions, and work history, and pairs them with [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) when future care is in dispute.

## Reading the Longview area data honestly

Vocational findings for Kilgore evaluees draw on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates covering the Longview metropolitan area and Texas as a whole, together with state workforce agency labor market information and, where the questions justify it, direct contact with employers about actual openings and requirements. Scale matters to interpretation: a regional employment center offers a different range of transferable-skill placements than a rural crossroads, and the analysis says which comparison is being made and why. Nothing in the report asks the reader to take the expert's word for anything; the sources are cited so that both parties can pull them.
