## The Commuting Market and How It Is Measured

Because a Wolfforth evaluee's realistic job search spans the metro, earning capacity opinions are grounded in Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Lubbock metropolitan area and for Texas statewide, together with state workforce agency labor market information. Where the referral questions justify it, direct labor market research tests whether specific occupations are actually hiring within commuting range. The report never assumes that a suburban address limits or expands opportunity; it documents the market as the data shows it, in terms that hold up whether the reader is retaining counsel or the examiner across the table.
