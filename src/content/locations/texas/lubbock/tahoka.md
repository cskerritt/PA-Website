Rural venues have long been underserved by the expert witness market, and Lynn County counsel know the pattern: the qualified evaluators cluster in major cities, and a Tahoka case gets either a long wait or a report written at arm's length from the evaluee's actual circumstances. Purinton Analytics built a remote-first national practice specifically so that geography stops deciding who gets rigorous expert work. The firm provides [vocational expert witness](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) services and [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) for injury, employment, and disability matters involving Tahoka parties, retained by plaintiff or defense counsel on equal terms.

## Remote-First, Not Remote-Only

The model is straightforward. Records analysis, counsel conferences, and the structured vocational interview proceed by secure video or telephone, which means a Tahoka engagement starts as quickly as an engagement in any city. When the referral questions require hands-on evaluation, or when a life care plan calls for assessing the evaluee's home environment, that work is performed in the evaluee's own community in Lynn County; the evaluee is not summoned to a distant office. Deposition and trial appearances are scheduled from the Kansas City home office, and the firm is candid that it keeps no staffed location in Tahoka or anywhere else in the metro, because a practice that testifies about facts for a living should not blur its own.

## Measuring a Rural Labor Market Fairly

Earning capacity in a Lynn County case cannot be assessed as if the evaluee lived in the principal city, and it cannot be written off because they do not. The analysis draws on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Lubbock metropolitan area and for Texas statewide, on state workforce agency labor market information, and on direct labor market research where the referral questions demand evidence about specific, reachable employment. The opinion states the character of the market in qualitative terms and lets the cited data carry the quantitative weight, an allocation that holds up under cross-examination from either direction.
