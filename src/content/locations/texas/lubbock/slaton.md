## How the Evaluation Comes to the Evaluee

The default engagement is remote-first. Medical, employment, and educational records are reviewed before anyone is interviewed, the vocational interview itself is conducted by video or telephone at a scheduled appointment, and standardized instruments are administered in formats validated for remote use where appropriate. When the referral questions require an in-person evaluation, it takes place in the evaluee's own community rather than in a distant office, and any home assessment supporting a life care plan is likewise done where the evaluee actually lives. Travel, when the case calls for it, is arranged from the Kansas City home office. The firm maintains no Slaton office and lists no local address, a fact stated here plainly because the reports make the same commitment to stating things plainly.
