Expert deadlines drive expert quality, and nowhere is that clearer than in a case from a small town like Post, where counsel may wait to retain until the disclosure calendar forces the question. Purinton Analytics builds its engagements backward from those dates: designation, report disclosure, deposition, trial. Counsel handling injury, employment, or disability litigation involving Post parties can retain the firm for [vocational expert witness](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) work and [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), on either side of the caption, with the schedule addressed in writing before the work begins.

## Working the Calendar, Not Against It

Intake starts by mapping the scheduling order. The conflict check form asks for imminent disclosure, deposition, and trial dates precisely so that the scope proposal can say, candidly, what is achievable before each of them. Records requests go out early because records custodians, not experts, are usually the slowest link in the chain. Interview and evaluation dates are set against the disclosure deadline with room for the evaluee's treatment schedule, and draft-free report practices mean the written opinion that goes out under the deadline is the same disciplined document it would have been with unlimited time. When a matter arrives too late to do the work properly, the firm says so at intake rather than disclosing a thin report.

## Labor Market Analysis for a Garza County Evaluee

A vocational opinion for a Post evaluee weighs the employment available in a small town against the wider market of the Lubbock metropolitan area, and it does so with sources that can be produced in discovery: Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Lubbock metro area and for Texas statewide, state workforce agency labor market information, and direct labor market research where the referral questions call for it. Scale is described qualitatively and honestly; the report does not inflate a rural market or dismiss it, because either move invites impeachment.
