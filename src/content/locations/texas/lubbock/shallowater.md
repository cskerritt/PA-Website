## What Method Looks Like on the Page

An opinion for a Shallowater case begins with a documented conflict check and a defined scope, followed by a records request tailored to the claims. The vocational analysis moves from the evaluee's actual work history and transferable skills to the labor market evidence, and every step is cited so the reasoning can be retraced without the author in the room. Life care plans follow the same architecture: each recommended item of future care is tied to the medical record or to a treating or consulting source, priced from identifiable data, and presented so that cross-examination tests the evidence rather than exposes a gap. Nothing in the report depends on being taken on faith.

Shallowater is a small community within the Lubbock, TX metropolitan area, and its residents' employment prospects are evaluated against that regional market rather than against the town alone.
