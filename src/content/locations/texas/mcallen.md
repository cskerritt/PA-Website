Hidalgo County is the whole of the McAllen-Edinburg-Mission metropolitan
statistical area, which makes venue analysis here unusually direct: a
matter arising in McAllen, Edinburg, or Mission is a Hidalgo County matter,
and Purinton Analytics accepts those referrals from plaintiff and defense
counsel alike. Engagements from the metro typically call for
[vocational expert](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) opinions on
employability and earning capacity,
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) in catastrophic injury
cases, or
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Hidalgo County venue footprint

State court engagements in the metro are venued in the District Courts of
Hidalgo County. In the federal system, Texas matters proceed in the United
States District Court for the Southern District of Texas, the United States
District Court for the Western District of Texas, the United States
District Court for the Northern District of Texas, and the United States
District Court for the Eastern District of Texas, and the firm confirms the
disclosure requirements of the specific forum at intake. Any venue-specific
registration or qualification requirement is identified and confirmed
during the conflict check and intake process. Matters from other Texas
venues are described on the [Texas locations page](/locations/texas/).

## Appointments in the McAllen metro

The firm keeps no staffed office in McAllen, Edinburg, or Mission and
publishes no street address for this location. Saying so directly is a
matter of policy: a report meant to be read by opposing counsel should not
sit on a website that implies offices that do not exist. Meetings and
evaluations in the metro are arranged by appointment. Engagements
ordinarily begin remotely, through records review, structured interviews,
and standardized testing under publisher protocols where remote
administration is supported, and evaluees can be seen in their own
community when the referral questions call for in-person evaluation. Travel
for evaluations, depositions, and trial testimony is scheduled from the
Kansas City home office and planned around the court's dates.

## Labor market grounding for the region

A credible employability opinion for a Hidalgo County evaluee has to
describe the labor market that evaluee actually faces. Vocational findings
for the metro rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment
and wage estimates for the McAllen metropolitan area and for Texas
statewide, on state workforce agency labor market information, and on
direct labor market research where warranted, so that identified
occupations reflect local hiring rather than national assumption. Life care
plans and medical cost projections are priced to the evaluee's own market
using provider-specific inquiries and published fee data for the region,
because the cost of future care in this metro is established locally, not
inferred from elsewhere.
