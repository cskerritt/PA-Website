Kendall County is its own venue. A Boerne case tried at home is decided
under a different roof and by a different jury pool than a case tried in
the metro's principal city, and counsel who practice there know the
difference matters. What should not differ is the quality of the expert
work, and Purinton Analytics applies the same methodology in a Kendall
County matter that it applies anywhere in its
[national practice](/locations/nationwide/): a
[vocational evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) that shows
its sources, a [life care plan](/services/life-care-planning/) whose every
line item is cited, and an opinion drafted for scrutiny by plaintiff and
defense counsel in equal measure.

## Courts serving Boerne matters

The District Court in Kendall County hears the state court side of the
Boerne docket. When a matter is federal, the firm accepts retention in the
United States District Court for the Western District of Texas, the United
States District Court for the Northern District of Texas, the United
States District Court for the Eastern District of Texas, and the United
States District Court for the Southern District of Texas, as venue
dictates. Intake confirms any forum-specific qualification question at the
outset, and the engagement letter fixes scope before the records arrive.

## A small county inside a large labor market

Although Kendall County is a smaller venue, a Boerne evaluee's employment
prospects are appraised against the San Antonio-New Braunfels metropolitan
labor market as a whole. Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational
employment and wage estimates for the metropolitan area and for Texas
statewide anchor the vocational findings, state workforce agency labor
market information refines them, and direct labor market research is
undertaken when the referral questions require evidence the published
series cannot supply. If the case proceeds to a damages calculation, the
firm arranges
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work stays under the same
engagement structure.

## Presence, stated honestly

The firm's only office is its home office in Kansas City. It keeps no
Boerne location and advertises none, on the view that an expert whose
opinions must be exact should be exact about where it sits. Boerne
engagements therefore run by appointment: interviews and records analysis
remotely as a rule, standardized testing under publisher protocols, and
in-person assessment carried out in the evaluee's own community whenever
the referral calls for it. Travel for depositions and trial is scheduled
from the Kansas City home office to meet the court's calendar, with remote
appearance used where the parties and the court permit.

## Beginning a Kendall County engagement

Counsel weighing a referral can review the regional practice on the
[San Antonio-New Braunfels metro page](/locations/texas/san-antonio/) and
the statewide practice on the [Texas page](/locations/texas/), but the
opening step is always the same:
[request a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) with the caption and the party
names, and identify any disclosure deadlines or trial settings that are
already fixed. Once conflicts clear, a written scope and tailored records
request follow, and the work proceeds on the schedule the Kendall County
court, or the federal court, has set.
