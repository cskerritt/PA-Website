A small town does not mean a small analysis. Selma is a compact Bexar
County community, but a damages opinion about a Selma resident must be
built on the same evidentiary base as an opinion about anyone in the
metropolitan area, because the labor market that determines earning
capacity does not stop at municipal boundaries. Purinton Analytics treats
the town's size as a fact to account for, not a reason to thin the file:
its [vocational expert evaluations](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care plans](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[rebuttal and peer review work](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) for
Selma matters are documented to the identical standard the firm applies
across its [national practice](/locations/nationwide/), for plaintiff and
defense counsel equally.

## Metro-scale data for a small-town evaluee

Published labor data describes markets, not towns, which is why the
vocational analysis for a Selma evaluee draws on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the San
Antonio-New Braunfels metropolitan area and for Texas statewide. State
workforce agency labor market information sharpens the picture, and
direct labor market research supplies what the published estimates
cannot, such as evidence about specific placement prospects when the
referral questions turn on them. Every source is named in the report, so
the analysis can be tested rather than taken on faith.

## Bexar County and federal venues

Selma's state court matters proceed in the District Court in Bexar
County. Federal engagements are accepted according to venue in the United
States District Court for the Western District of Texas, the United
States District Court for the Southern District of Texas, the United
States District Court for the Northern District of Texas, and the United
States District Court for the Eastern District of Texas. Whichever court
controls, the engagement opens with a documented conflict check and a
scope confirmed in writing, and any forum-specific qualification item is
identified during intake.

## Working with the firm at a distance

Purinton Analytics has no Selma office and claims none; its only office
is the home office in Kansas City, and stating that plainly is part of the same
habit of accuracy the reports rely on. Engagements proceed by
appointment. Interviews and records analysis usually run remotely,
standardized instruments are administered under their publishers'
protocols, and in-person evaluation is arranged in the evaluee's own
community when the referral needs it. Travel for depositions and trial is
scheduled from the Kansas City home office to match the court's
settings, and remote appearance is used when the parties and the court
allow.

## Economic damages, properly signed

When counsel needs the vocational and care findings extended into an
economic damages opinion, the firm coordinates
[damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) in which
independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis
while the vocational and care-planning work remains under the same
engagement structure.
