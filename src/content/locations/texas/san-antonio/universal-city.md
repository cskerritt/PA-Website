When counsel retains an expert for a Universal City client, the practical
questions come fast: where will the evaluation take place, how will the
records move, and what happens if the evaluee cannot easily travel?
Purinton Analytics answers them at intake rather than mid-engagement. For
this Bexar County community, evaluations are structured around the
evaluee: remote interviews and records analysis where the referral
permits, standardized testing administered under the publishers' own
protocols, and in-person assessment brought to the evaluee's community
when the questions require it. The underlying services,
[vocational expert evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/), are
available to plaintiff and defense counsel on the same terms.

## No local office, by design

The firm operates from a home office in Kansas City and nowhere else, and
it declines to suggest a Universal City presence it does not have.
Meetings happen by appointment. When deposition or trial requires the
expert in the courtroom, travel is planned from the Kansas City home
office around the settings the court has ordered, and remote testimony is
offered wherever the court and the parties will accept it. The logistics
are documented in the file like everything else, so no question about how
the evaluation was conducted goes unanswered at deposition.

## Courts hearing Universal City matters

State court cases from Universal City proceed in the District Court in
Bexar County. On the federal side, the practice accepts engagements in the
United States District Court for the Eastern District of Texas, the United
States District Court for the Western District of Texas, the United States
District Court for the Southern District of Texas, and the United States
District Court for the Northern District of Texas, wherever the case is
properly venued. Forum-specific qualification requirements are confirmed
during the conflict check and intake, before designation deadlines make
them urgent.

## Labor market evidence for a Bexar County evaluee

Universal City workers participate in the metropolitan economy, so
vocational conclusions are referenced to Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates for the San Antonio-New
Braunfels metropolitan area and for Texas statewide, supported by state
workforce agency labor market information and, when the referral questions
demand it, direct labor market research. Care-related costs in a
[life care plan](/services/life-care-planning/) are priced to the
evaluee's community with the source of each figure cited. When the file
needs a full damages presentation, the firm coordinates
[damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) in which
independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis
while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under the same
engagement structure.
