In a Schertz case, the scheduling order is the terrain the expert work is
built on. A designation deadline fixes the date by which opinions must
exist, disclosure requirements fix what must accompany them, and when an
opposing report arrives, the response window fixes how long counsel has to
answer it. Purinton Analytics structures its Schertz engagements around
that calendar. Intake fixes the
operative dates before substantive work starts, and each deliverable,
whether a [vocational expert report](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
a [life care plan](/services/life-care-planning/), or a
[rebuttal review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/), is scoped honestly
against the time actually available. Plaintiff and defense counsel are
served on identical terms, and neither is promised a shortcut the record
would later expose.

## Guadalupe County courts and federal forums

Schertz matters filed in state court proceed in the District Court serving
Guadalupe County, and the deposition and trial settings of that court
anchor the engagement timeline. For federal cases, retention is accepted in
the United States District Court for the Western District of Texas, the
United States District Court for the Eastern District of Texas, the United
States District Court for the Northern District of Texas, and the United
States District Court for the Southern District of Texas, according to
where the matter is filed. Forum-specific qualification questions are
resolved during the conflict check and intake, never left for opposing
counsel to raise.

## The employment picture for a Schertz worker

Because a Schertz resident's job prospects extend well past the town line,
vocational conclusions are grounded in Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates for the San Antonio-New
Braunfels metropolitan area and for Texas as a whole, with state workforce
agency labor market information layered in and direct labor market research
conducted when the referral requires it. When lost earnings must be reduced
to a damages figure, the firm arranges
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work continues under the
same engagement structure, so the file speaks with a consistent set of
assumptions.

## Appointments, not an address

The firm publishes no Schertz address because it keeps none; every meeting
is by appointment, and saying so plainly is part of the credibility the
reports depend on. Interviews and records analysis typically run remotely
on a schedule set with counsel, standardized instruments are administered
under their publishers' protocols, and in-person evaluation, when the
questions demand it, is arranged in the community where the evaluee
actually lives. Testimony is calendared the same way, with travel from the
Kansas City home office planned around the dates the court controls and
remote appearance offered wherever the parties and the bench will accept
it.
