Sometimes the referral arrives with another expert's report already in the
file, and the immediate assignment is not to build an opinion but to test
someone else's. Purinton Analytics accepts that posture in Fair Oaks
Ranch matters through
[rebuttal and peer review engagements](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/):
auditing an opposing vocational report or life care plan against its own
stated methodology, checking whether the cited data says what the report
claims, and giving counsel a candid written assessment either way. The
same rigor runs the other direction when the firm authors the affirmative
[vocational evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) or
[life care plan](/services/life-care-planning/) itself, and retention is
open to plaintiff and defense counsel on the same footing.

## Candor as methodology

A reviewer who overstates flaws is as useless to counsel as an author who
hides them, so review findings distinguish between disagreements of
judgment and demonstrable methodological error, and they say plainly when
an opposing report is sound. That habit of candor extends to the firm's
own posture: it has no Fair Oaks Ranch office, publishes no local
address, and works entirely by appointment from its Kansas City home
office, because an expert who audits the accuracy of others must be
accurate about itself.

## Venue for Fair Oaks Ranch disputes

Fair Oaks Ranch is a Bexar County community, and its state court cases
are heard in the District Court there. Federally, the firm accepts
engagements as venue directs in the United States District Court for the
Northern District of Texas, the United States District Court for the
Eastern District of Texas, the United States District Court for the
Southern District of Texas, and the United States District Court for the
Western District of Texas. Whether the assignment is affirmative or
rebuttal, it opens with a documented conflict check and a written scope,
and any qualification requirement particular to the forum is resolved
during intake.

## The data behind the opinions

Affirmative and rebuttal work alike depend on knowing the regional labor
market cold. For a Fair Oaks Ranch evaluee, that means Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the San
Antonio-New Braunfels metropolitan area and for Texas statewide, state
workforce agency labor market information, and direct labor market
research when the referral questions call for evidence beyond the
published series. If the case requires a full damages presentation, the
firm coordinates
[damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) in which
independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis
while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under the same
engagement structure.

## Evaluation and testimony logistics

When the assignment includes evaluating a Fair Oaks Ranch resident,
interviews and records analysis are ordinarily remote, testing follows
publisher protocols, and in-person assessment takes place in the
evaluee's own community when needed. Deposition and trial travel is
planned from the Kansas City home office around the court's dates, with
remote testimony wherever the court and the parties agree to it.
