The record comes before the opinion. In a Cibolo matter, Purinton
Analytics does not start from a conclusion about earning capacity or
future care and then gather support for it; the work starts with the file
itself, the medical records, the employment and education history, and a
structured interview that tests what the documents appear to show. Only on
that foundation does a
[vocational assessment](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) take shape,
paired with [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) and
[medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/) when the
injuries call for future care, and the engagement is accepted from
whichever side of the caption in a Guadalupe County case makes the call.

## Data before conclusions

Vocational findings for a Cibolo evaluee are built on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the San
Antonio-New Braunfels metropolitan area and for Texas statewide, checked
against state workforce agency labor market information, and extended by
direct labor market research when the referral questions justify it.
Published estimates describe the metropolitan market rather than any
single town, so the analysis states the market definition it applies and
the reason for choosing it. Transferable
skills, residual capacity, and placeability are addressed in terms an
opposing expert can replicate, because a conclusion that cannot be
replicated should not be offered.

## Where Cibolo cases are heard

State court matters proceed in the District Court that serves Guadalupe
County. Federal matters are accepted wherever venue lies, in the United
States District Court for the Western District of Texas, the United States
District Court for the Southern District of Texas, the United States
District Court for the Eastern District of Texas, and the United States
District Court for the Northern District of Texas. Court-specific
qualification or registration requirements are surfaced during intake so
they never become an issue after designation.

## Evaluations without a storefront

Purinton Analytics keeps no office in Cibolo and lists no local address,
and that candor is intentional: a firm whose reports depend on precise
sourcing should not blur the facts of its own footprint. Engagements run by
appointment. Interviews and records work are generally remote, testing is
conducted under the publishers' protocols, and when the referral calls for
an in-person evaluation the appointment is set in the evaluee's own
community. When depositions or trial require presence, travel is arranged
from the Kansas City home office to meet the court's dates, and remote
appearance is used when the court and counsel prefer it.

## The forensic economics handoff

Counsel often need the vocational findings carried through to a damages
number. For that, the firm coordinates
[damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) in which
independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis
while the vocational and care-planning work continues under the same
engagement structure, keeping assumptions aligned from the wage data to
the final calculation.
