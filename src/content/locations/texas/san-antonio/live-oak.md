In catastrophic injury litigation, the care plan often carries more of the
damages case than the wage loss does, and it draws the sharper scrutiny.
For Live Oak matters, Purinton Analytics builds
[life care plans](/services/life-care-planning/) item by item, each
treatment, therapy, piece of equipment, and attendant care hour tied to
the medical record and priced through
[medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/) methods that
cite their sources, so that counsel on either side of a Bexar County case
can trace every figure to its origin. The
[vocational analysis](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) that usually
accompanies the plan is held to the same sourcing standard, and the firm
takes plaintiff and defense retention without preference.

## Care costs priced to the evaluee's community

A projection is only useful if it reflects what care actually costs where
the evaluee lives, so Live Oak plans are priced to the evaluee's own
community rather than to a national average, with the basis for each
input disclosed in the plan itself. When the plan and the earnings
analysis must be carried into a present-value damages opinion, the firm
arranges [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work continues under the
same engagement structure.

## Vocational grounding in the metro market

Earning capacity questions for a Live Oak evaluee are answered against
the regional market: Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment
and wage estimates for the San Antonio-New Braunfels metropolitan area
and for Texas statewide, state workforce agency labor market information,
and direct labor market research where the referral questions require
more than the published series offer. The result is a set of vocational
findings an opposing expert can check step by step, which is precisely
the point.

## Venues for Live Oak litigation

Live Oak sits in Bexar County, and its state court matters are heard in
the District Court there. Federal retention follows the case to the
United States District Court for the Southern District of Texas, the
United States District Court for the Northern District of Texas, the
United States District Court for the Western District of Texas, or the
United States District Court for the Eastern District of Texas, whichever
holds venue. Registration or qualification particulars for a given forum
are settled during intake, not discovered later.

## Appointments and travel

There is no Live Oak office; the firm works from its Kansas City home
office and says so without hedging. Evaluations are by appointment, with
interviews and records work handled remotely in the ordinary case,
testing run under publisher protocols, and in-person assessment
conducted in the evaluee's community when the referral requires it.
Deposition and trial appearances are scheduled from the Kansas City home
office against the court's dates, with remote testimony where the court
and counsel agree.
