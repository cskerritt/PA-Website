## Meeting the evaluee

There is no Purinton Analytics office in New Braunfels, and the firm does
not pretend otherwise; accuracy about presence belongs to the same
discipline that governs citations. Work is by appointment. Records review
and structured interviews are ordinarily handled remotely, standardized
testing follows publisher protocols, and when the referral calls for
in-person evaluation it takes place in the evaluee's own community rather
than in a borrowed conference room. For depositions and trial, travel is
scheduled from the Kansas City home office against the dates the court has
set, and remote testimony is used where the court and the parties agree it
serves the case.
