An expert report earns its keep under questioning, and every Converse
opinion Purinton Analytics writes is drafted with the deposition transcript
in mind. Whichever side retains the firm in a Bexar County matter, the
opposing side will eventually hold the report, compare its stated
methodology to its conclusions, and probe for the seam between them. The
practice removes the seam in advance: defined scope, cited sources,
reasoning laid out step by step. That standard applies across the Converse
service set, from [vocational expert work](/services/vocational-expert-witness/)
and [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) to
[expert testimony and litigation consulting](/services/expert-testimony-litigation-consulting/)
support for counsel preparing to examine someone else's expert.

## Bexar County venue and the federal bench

Converse sits in Bexar County, and its state court matters are heard in
the District Court there. Federal engagements follow the case: the
practice accepts appointments in the United States District Court for the
Northern District of Texas, the United States District Court for the
Western District of Texas, the United States District Court for the
Southern District of Texas, and the United States District Court for the
Eastern District of Texas. Every retention starts from the same checklist
regardless of forum, with the conflict check documented and the scope
reduced to writing before analysis begins, so the file's chronology itself
is clean if it is ever examined.

## Grounding opinions in the metro labor market

A Converse evaluee competes for work in the labor market of the San
Antonio-New Braunfels metropolitan area, and the vocational analysis says
so explicitly. Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage
estimates for that metropolitan area and for Texas statewide supply the
foundation, state workforce agency labor market information adds depth,
and direct labor market research fills gaps the published data cannot
reach. Where lost earning capacity must become a calculated damages
opinion, the firm offers
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work runs under the same
engagement structure.

## How the evaluation happens

No Converse office exists, and the practice says so rather than implying
otherwise, because a report that survives cross-examination cannot sit on
a website that would not. Everything is by appointment: remote interviews
and records analysis in the ordinary course, standardized testing under
publisher protocols, and in-person evaluation in the evaluee's own
community when the referral requires it. Deposition and trial travel is
planned from the Kansas City home office against the settings the court
has ordered, and remote testimony is available where the parties and the
bench agree.
