Most of the work in a vocational or life care planning engagement happens
long before anyone meets in a conference room, and that is how Purinton
Analytics serves the Longview, TX metropolitan area: records review,
interviewing, testing, and analysis proceed remotely under a defined scope,
with in-person work reserved for the moments that genuinely require it. The
services counsel retain here are the same offered nationally:
[vocational expert witness](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) analysis
of employability and earning capacity,
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) for catastrophic
injuries, and
[expert testimony and litigation consulting](/services/expert-testimony-litigation-consulting/)
through deposition and trial. Plaintiff and defense counsel receive the same
methodology and the same report standard, sentence for sentence.

## How remote-first works in practice

An engagement typically opens with secure records transfer and a records
request tailored to the claims, followed by structured evaluee interviews by
video and standardized testing under publisher protocols where remote
administration is supported. When the referral questions call for in-person
evaluation, or when a deposition or trial setting requires attendance,
travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office and coordinated with
the deadlines counsel identify at intake. The firm keeps no staffed office
in Longview and publishes no street address for this location; meetings are
by appointment, and the absence of a storefront is disclosed rather than
disguised, because candor about small facts is the best evidence of candor
about large ones.

## Venue coverage across the metro

Engagements are accepted for matters venued in the District Courts of Gregg
County, Harrison County, Rusk County, and Upshur County, the counties that
make up the Longview Metropolitan Statistical Area. Federal matters are
handled in whichever court holds the case: the United States District Court
for the Eastern District of Texas, the United States District Court for the
Northern District of Texas, the United States District Court for the
Southern District of Texas, or the United States District Court for the
Western District of Texas. Any venue-specific registration or qualification
requirement is identified and confirmed during the conflict check and intake
process. Statewide coverage is set out on the firm's
[Texas locations page](/locations/texas/).

## Local data behind every opinion

Employability and earning capacity opinions for Longview area evaluees rest
on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for
the metro and for Texas statewide, on state workforce agency labor market
information, and on direct labor market research where the analysis warrants
it. Life care plans and medical cost projections are priced to the evaluee's
own community through provider-specific inquiries and published fee data
rather than national composites. When counsel need the economic loss
calculation as well, the firm offers
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under a single
engagement structure.
