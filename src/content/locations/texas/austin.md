Most of the work in an Austin engagement happens long before anyone books a
flight. Records review, structured vocational interviews, standardized
testing under publisher protocols that support remote administration, and
labor market research all proceed remotely, which is how Purinton Analytics
serves the Austin-Round Rock-San Marcos metro on plaintiff and defense
referrals alike. The service mix from this metro runs from
[vocational expert](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) opinions on
employability and earning capacity to
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) in catastrophic injury
cases and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Venue coverage for the Austin metro

Engagements are accepted for matters in the District Courts of
Travis County and Williamson County and in the District Courts of
Hays County,
Bastrop County, and Caldwell County. On the federal side, Texas matters
proceed in the United States District Court for the Western District of
Texas, the United States District Court for the Northern District of Texas,
the United States District Court for the Eastern District of Texas, and the
United States District Court for the Southern District of Texas. Reports
carry the same disclosure discipline in every forum, and any venue-specific
registration or qualification requirement is identified and confirmed
during the conflict check and intake process. Counsel with matters beyond
the metro can consult the [Texas locations page](/locations/texas/).

## Remote-first, in person when it matters

There is no staffed Austin office and no published street address here,
and the firm states that plainly rather than implying a local presence it
does not keep. Meetings in the metro are by appointment. When the referral
questions call for an in-person evaluation, or when deposition and trial
settings require attendance, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home
office and coordinated with counsel around the court's calendar. The result
for retaining counsel is a consistent engagement: the same evaluator, the
same report standard, and the same point of contact whether the work is
performed remotely or in the evaluee's community.

## Labor market research for Austin evaluees

Employability and earning capacity findings for evaluees in the metro rest
on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates
for the Austin metropolitan area and for Texas statewide, on state
workforce agency labor market information, and on direct labor market
research where warranted. The firm documents which occupations are actually
hiring in the evaluee's region instead of assuming that national patterns
hold locally. Life care plans and medical cost projections apply the same
principle to future care, priced to the evaluee's own market through
provider-specific inquiries and published fee data rather than through
averages that may not describe the region at all.

## Beginning with a conflict check

An Austin engagement opens the way every engagement opens, whichever side
of the caption is calling: a documented conflict check, then a defined
scope and a records request tailored to the claims. Send the caption or
party names through the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and note any
disclosure deadlines, deposition settings, or trial dates so availability
can be confirmed at intake. The firm's
[national coverage](/locations/nationwide/) describes how the identical
process serves venues well past the metro's edge.
