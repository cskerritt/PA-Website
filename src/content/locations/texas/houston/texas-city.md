Not every referral asks for a first opinion. In a Texas City case,
counsel sometimes already holds the opposing expert's report and needs
to know, before anything else is decided, whether that report can bear
weight. Purinton Analytics is retained for precisely that inquiry
through [rebuttal or peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/): the
served report is audited against its own citations, its methods are
checked against the way the underlying instruments and data are meant to
be used, and the resulting critique is confined to what the audit
actually supports. The firm takes that assignment from plaintiff and
defense counsel on equal footing, and it holds its affirmative work,
[vocational expert evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) and
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) for evaluees in and
around Texas City, to the identical standard.

## Venue for the challenge

Where a critique will matter is decided by the caption. Galveston County
is Texas City's county, and its District Court is where the firm accepts
the city's state-court engagements. A federal filing changes the forum
without changing the assignment: appointments are accepted in the
United States District Court for the Southern District of Texas, the
United States District Court for the Northern District of Texas, the
United States District Court for the Eastern District of Texas, and the
United States District Court for the Western District of Texas, with
the controlling venue identified during the conflict check and intake. A review
destined for any of those benches is drafted in the knowledge that it
may be quoted back to its author, so it stays inside the record and
claims nothing the record cannot carry.

## The measuring stick

An opposing report cannot be judged without a benchmark, and the
benchmark is public data. Vocational conclusions about a Texas City
evaluee, whether the firm's own or an opposing expert's under review,
are measured against the Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational
employment and wage estimates for the Houston-Pasadena-The Woodlands, TX
metropolitan area and for Texas statewide, read together with state
workforce agency labor market information; direct labor market research
is added when a disputed placement question outruns the published
series. Care planning is held to the matching test. A plan the firm
writes prices each item in the evaluee's community with its source
shown, and a plan the firm reviews is asked to demonstrate the same
thing. Where a served opinion clears that bar, the review acknowledges
it without hedging, because criticism spent on sound conclusions
squanders the credibility the weak ones deserve.

## Assessment logistics in Texas City

Purinton Analytics has no Texas City office and does not suggest one;
the practice is staffed from its Kansas City home office and serves this
community by appointment. Little of the work requires travel. Records
arrive and are analyzed remotely, the structured vocational interview is
conducted by scheduled appointment, and standardized instruments are
administered under the remote protocols their publishers sanction. An
in-person evaluation, when the referral genuinely requires one, is held
in the evaluee's own community rather than in a distant office, and
testimony dates are met by travel from Kansas City once counsel confirms
them. Regional context for this local work appears on the
[Houston metro page](/locations/texas/houston/).

Whether the assignment is a review of a served report or an affirmative
