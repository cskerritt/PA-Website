## Harris County venue context

Pasadena sits in Harris County, and state-court engagements are accepted
in the District Court there. Federal matters from this part of Texas most
often proceed in the United States District Court for the Southern
District of Texas, and the practice appears just as readily in the United
States District Court for the Northern District of Texas, the United
States District Court for the Eastern District of Texas, and the United
States District Court for the Western District of Texas when a case is
venued elsewhere in the state. The courthouse changes nothing about the
method: a documented conflict check, a defined scope, a records request
tailored to the claims, and an opinion that shows its work.

## The labor market a Pasadena evaluee actually faces

An earning capacity conclusion is only as good as the market it
describes. For Pasadena evaluees, the firm builds that description from
occupational employment and wage estimates published by the Bureau of
Labor Statistics under its OEWS program, covering the
Houston-Pasadena-The Woodlands metropolitan area and Texas statewide,
supplemented by state workforce agency labor market information and by
direct labor market research when a referral question turns on local
confirmation. The same sourcing discipline carries into care pricing:
life care plans are costed to the evaluee's own community, with every
source cited so the opposing expert can retrace the path. How this local
work fits the broader regional practice is described on the
[Houston metro page](/locations/texas/houston/).

## Evaluation logistics, by appointment

There is no staffed Purinton Analytics office in Pasadena, and the firm
does not pretend otherwise; accuracy about presence is part of the same
discipline that governs its reports. Records analysis is remote by
default. Structured interviews and standardized testing follow publisher
protocols that support remote administration, so most Pasadena evaluees
are assessed without leaving home. When the referral genuinely calls for
an in-person evaluation, it takes place in the evaluee's community, and
when deposition or trial requires the expert in the room, travel is
scheduled from the Kansas City home office against dates confirmed with
counsel well in advance.

## Requesting a conflict check

Every engagement, whether it arises here, elsewhere in the firm's
[Texas practice](/locations/texas/), or within its
[nationwide work](/locations/nationwide/), begins the same way: with a
documented conflict check before any substantive discussion of the case.
Counsel should [submit the caption and party names](/refer-a-case/) along
with any disclosure, deposition, or trial dates already on the calendar.
Once the check clears, the firm issues a written engagement confirmation
and a tailored records request, and the analysis is scheduled backward
from the court's deadlines.
