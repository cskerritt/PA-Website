Strip away the pleadings and most Galveston damages disputes reduce to a
short list of factual questions: the work still open to the injured
person, the pay that work commands in the markets available to them, and
the cost of the care their condition will require. Purinton Analytics
exists to answer those questions in writing for whichever side retains
it. Counsel engage the firm for
[vocational expert evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) and
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and, when the
damages model needs an economist, for
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## State and federal forums

Federal appointments are accepted across all of the districts of the
state: the United States District Court for the Southern District of
Texas, the United States District Court for the Northern District of
Texas, the United States District Court for the Eastern District of
Texas, and the United States District Court for the Western District of
Texas are each available forums, and the controlling venue for a given
caption is confirmed during the conflict check and intake. In state court, Galveston matters are
accepted in the District Court of Galveston County, the county in which
the city lies. Forum selection never alters the working method: the
conflict check is documented first, the scope is fixed in writing, and
the records request is drawn against the claims as pleaded, so the
finished opinion can be traced from source to conclusion by either side
of the caption.

## Sourcing the answers

Each of the core questions has its own evidentiary base. What work
remains open is answered from the evaluee's documented capacities read
against occupational requirements. What that work pays is answered from
the Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage
estimates for the Houston-Pasadena-The Woodlands, TX metropolitan area
and for Texas statewide, supplemented by state workforce agency labor
market information and, where genuine local availability is contested,
by direct labor market research. What future care will cost is answered
by pricing each planned item among providers in the evaluee's own
community, with the source behind every entry cited. The answers are
only as persuasive as their sourcing, which is why the report lays out
its sources before it states its conclusions.

## The path from referral to report

A Galveston engagement typically runs its full course without the
evaluee leaving home. After clearance, records are gathered and analyzed
remotely; the structured vocational interview follows by appointment;
standardized testing is administered under the remote protocols the test
publishers themselves sanction. The firm is direct about why this
structure exists: it maintains no Galveston office and publishes no
local address, its staffed presence begins and ends at the Kansas City
home office, and this community is served by appointment instead. Where
the referral questions truly require an in-person evaluation, the firm
conducts it in the evaluee's community, and when deposition or trial
calls for the expert in the room, travel proceeds from Kansas City on
the dates counsel fixes. How Galveston engagements sit within the wider
regional practice is covered on the
[Houston metro page](/locations/texas/houston/).

## First step: the conflict check

The rule that opens the firm's [Texas practice](/locations/texas/) and
its [nationwide docket](/locations/nationwide/) opens Galveston matters
too: no substantive discussion until a conflict check is documented.
[Request a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) with the caption and the
party names, withhold confidential records at that stage, and list any
disclosure, deposition, or trial dates the court has ordered. Clearance
brings written confirmation of the engagement, a records request matched
to the pleadings, and an analysis sequenced to the calendar the court
has already set.
