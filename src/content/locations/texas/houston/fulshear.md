## County and courthouse

Fulshear lies in Fort Bend County, and state-court engagements are
accepted in the District Court that serves it. Federal cases arising
here are usually venued in the United States District Court for the
Southern District of Texas, and the practice extends without friction to
the United States District Court for the Northern District of Texas, the
United States District Court for the Eastern District of Texas, and the
United States District Court for the Western District of Texas.
Venue-specific qualification requirements, where they exist, are
identified and confirmed during the conflict check and intake process.

## A smaller city inside a major labor market

Fulshear itself is a smaller community, but its residents work within
one of the country's larger metropolitan labor markets, and a credible
earning capacity opinion has to hold both facts at once. The firm's
vocational analysis draws on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational
employment and wage estimates for the Houston-Pasadena-The Woodlands
metropolitan area and for Texas statewide, together with state workforce
agency labor market information; direct labor market research is
deployed when the question is whether particular work is truly open to
this evaluee. A transferable skills review then connects those series to
the evaluee's own education and employment record, keeping the
occupational base personal rather than merely statistical. Life care
plans are costed in the evaluee's own community, with each item cited.

## Evaluation without the commute

Remote-first structure matters most in places like Fulshear, where
requiring an injured evaluee to travel for assessment would add burden
without adding rigor. Records review is fully remote; structured
vocational interviews and standardized testing are administered under
remote protocols the publishers support, so the evaluee typically
participates from home. When an in-person evaluation is genuinely
indicated, the firm comes to the evaluee's community. Deposition and
trial presence is arranged from the Kansas City home office on the
schedule counsel sets, remote appearance can be used where the court
consents, and the
[Houston metro page](/locations/texas/houston/) shows how Fulshear fits
the wider regional docket.
