## Montgomery County and the federal bench

Conroe matters in state court are accepted in the District Court in
Montgomery County. When a case proceeds federally, the United States
District Court for the Southern District of Texas is the most common
setting for disputes arising in this part of the state, and the firm is
likewise available in the United States District Court for the Northern
District of Texas, the United States District Court for the Eastern
District of Texas, and the United States District Court for the Western
District of Texas. Any venue-specific registration or qualification
requirement is identified and confirmed during the conflict check and
intake process, so it never surfaces as a surprise near a deadline.

## Labor market evidence for Conroe matters

Vocational conclusions for a Conroe evaluee rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates covering the
Houston-Pasadena-The Woodlands metropolitan area and the Texas statewide
series, read together with state workforce agency labor market
information. Direct labor market research is added when a disputed issue
requires confirmation beyond the published tables. None of this is
rushed even under a tight schedule; compressing the timeline changes the
sequencing of the work, never the sourcing standard, because a report
assembled from uncited shortcuts does not survive cross-examination.
When a rebuttal deadline follows an initial disclosure, the sequencing
simply runs in reverse: the opposing report is audited against its own
cited sources before any responsive opinion is drafted.
