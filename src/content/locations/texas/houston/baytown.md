The starting point for a Baytown engagement is a person, not a
courthouse. Somewhere behind the caption is an evaluee whose capacity to
work, and whose future care needs, the court will be asked to weigh, and
the quality of that assessment depends on how carefully the evaluation
is brought to them. Purinton Analytics structures its Baytown work
around that person: records gathered against the claims, a
structured interview and standardized testing administered on the
evaluee's schedule, and findings reported in a form both sides of the
caption can interrogate. The services counsel draw on most often are
[vocational expert evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[forensic economic damages](/services/forensic-economic-damages/), the
last performed and signed by independent economist partners whom the
firm coordinates.

## Where Baytown cases are heard

Baytown is part of Harris County, and state-court referrals are accepted
in the District Court there. Federally, matters from this corner of the
metro generally sit in the United States District Court for the Southern
District of Texas; the firm also takes engagements in the United States
District Court for the Northern District of Texas, the United States
District Court for the Eastern District of Texas, and the United States
District Court for the Western District of Texas when venue places a
case there. Nothing in the analysis bends to the forum; the same
documented conflict check, defined scope, and cited sourcing govern in
each.

## Labor market research for Baytown evaluees

To describe what work a Baytown resident can realistically secure, the
firm looks to the Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and
wage estimates for the Houston-Pasadena-The Woodlands metropolitan area,
read with the Texas statewide estimates and state workforce agency labor
market information. When the dispute turns on the availability of
particular jobs for a particular person, direct labor market research
closes the gap between published tables and the evaluee's actual
options. Care items in a life care plan are priced within the evaluee's
community and cited so opposing review can follow every step. Where
capacity before injury and capacity after it are both in dispute, the
analysis keeps those findings and the wage evidence distinct, so the
fact-finder can see precisely where the parties differ and why.

## Presence, honestly stated

There is no Baytown office; the firm operates from a single Kansas City
home office and serves everywhere else by appointment. Remote-first
methods carry most of the load, including publisher-approved remote
protocols for testing, so an evaluee is usually assessed at home.
In-person evaluation, when warranted, happens in the evaluee's
community, and travel for deposition or trial is planned from Kansas
City around the dates counsel provides. Regional context for these
engagements sits on the [metro page](/locations/texas/houston/).
