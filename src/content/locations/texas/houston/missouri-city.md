the claim as pleaded. In Missouri City matters, Purinton Analytics

## Fort Bend County courthouse context

Missouri City belongs to Fort Bend County, and its state-court cases are
accepted in the District Court serving the county. In federal practice,
the United States District Court for the Southern District of Texas is
the customary forum for cases arising here, and the firm appears as
readily in the United States District Court for the Northern District of
Texas, the United States District Court for the Eastern District of
Texas, and the United States District Court for the Western District of
Texas. Any registration or qualification particular to a venue is
identified during the conflict check and intake process rather than

## Reading the metro labor market from Missouri City

The employment options open to a Missouri City evaluee extend across the
Houston-Pasadena-The Woodlands metropolitan area, and the analysis
treats them that way. Vocational findings rest on the Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for that
metropolitan area and for Texas statewide, checked against state
workforce agency labor market information, with direct labor market
research reserved for questions the published series cannot settle. In
life care planning, costs come from the evaluee's own community and
carry citations, because a plan that cannot be verified is a plan that
cannot be defended. The report also distinguishes what the data can
establish from what it cannot, a line opposing counsel will look for
and should find already drawn.

## Remote-first, by appointment

The firm holds no office in Missouri City and claims none. Its
remote-first structure means records analysis begins on receipt,
structured interviews and standardized testing proceed under remote
protocols the test publishers support, and the evaluee ordinarily
participates from home. Where an in-person evaluation is genuinely
necessary, it is arranged in the evaluee's community. Testimony travel
is scheduled from the Kansas City home office once dates are fixed, with
remote appearance available whenever the court and parties agree. That
same structure keeps costs predictable for whichever party bears them. The
regional frame for this work appears on the
