Every opinion Purinton Analytics issues for a Sugar Land matter is
written on the assumption that the most careful reader will be the
lawyer on the other side. That assumption disciplines everything: the
scope is defined before work begins, each conclusion traces to a cited
source, and the methodology is laid out so it can be checked rather than
argued about. Counsel for plaintiffs and counsel for defendants retain
the firm on identical terms for
[vocational expert evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/), and the
report reads the same no matter who signed the engagement letter.

## Fort Bend County and the federal districts

Sugar Land lies in Fort Bend County, where state-court engagements are
accepted in the District Court. Cases that proceed in federal court from
this area are typically before the United States District Court for the
Southern District of Texas, with the firm equally at home in the United
States District Court for the Northern District of Texas, the United
States District Court for the Eastern District of Texas, and the United
States District Court for the Western District of Texas. Where forensic
economic damages are part of the referral, the firm offers
[coordinated engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) in
which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis alongside the vocational and care-planning work.

## Grounding the opinion in the regional market

A Sugar Land evaluee competes for work across the
Houston-Pasadena-The Woodlands metropolitan area, so the firm anchors
its vocational findings in the Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational
employment and wage estimates for that area and for Texas statewide,
tested against state workforce agency labor market information and,
when a contested question demands it, direct labor market research.
Between those published estimates and the conclusion sits a transferable
skills analysis built from the evaluee's documented work history,
education, and residual capacities, which keeps the opinion tethered to
this case rather than to a statistical composite.
Life care plan costs are drawn from providers in the evaluee's own
community and cited item by item. Qualitative honesty matters here as
much as sourcing: the report describes the market as the data shows it,
not as either side might wish it.

## Appointments, not an office

The firm keeps no office in Sugar Land and publishes no local address,
because claiming a presence it does not have would undercut the
credibility the reports depend on. Work is remote-first: records
analysis, structured vocational interviews, and standardized testing
under publisher-supported remote protocols all proceed without
travel. An in-person evaluation, where the referral requires one, is
conducted in the evaluee's community. When testimony requires the
expert's physical presence, travel is arranged from the Kansas City
home office against confirmed dates, and the
[Houston metro page](/locations/texas/houston/) outlines the regional
logistics that make short-notice settings manageable.
