## Bell County venue, state and federal

Harker Heights sits in Bell County, and state court litigation arising
there proceeds in the District Court sitting in Bell County. Federal
matters are venued separately among the districts of Texas, including the
United States District Court for the Western District of Texas and the
United States District Court for the Southern District of Texas, and
intake confirms which forum governs before the engagement calendar is
drawn. Forum carries particular weight in review work: the served report
is measured against the disclosure standard of the court that will receive
it, and the responsive opinion must itself be disclosed on the same
calendar, so the applicable rules are settled before any critique is
drafted rather than discovered after it.

## The data an opinion must answer to

Whether the firm is writing an original opinion or reviewing someone
else's, the measuring stick is the same. Employability and earning
capacity findings for Harker Heights evaluees rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the
Killeen-Temple metropolitan area and for Texas statewide, on state
workforce agency labor market information, and on direct labor market
research when a referral question turns on whether specific work is
genuinely available to the evaluee. Harker Heights residents earn across
the surrounding metro, whose regional picture is set out on the
[Killeen-Temple metro page](/locations/texas/killeen/). A served opinion
that cannot show how it defined the evaluee's commutable market, or that
leans on conclusions the published estimates do not support, is identified
as such in the review, with the gap documented on the page rather than
merely asserted.

## Appointments without pretense

The firm keeps no staffed location in Harker Heights and publishes no
local street address, and it says so plainly because candor about presence
belongs to the same discipline as candor about data. Engagements run by
appointment: records move by secure transfer, conferences and
pre-deposition preparation are held remotely, and evaluation proceeds
remotely whenever the protocols behind the referral questions allow. When
in-person evaluation is warranted, it is conducted in the evaluee's own
community, and when a deposition or trial setting requires the expert
present, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office against
dates committed at retention. The model is the same one that serves the
firm's broader [Texas practice](/locations/texas/) and its
[national work](/locations/nationwide/).

## Requesting review or retention

Whether counsel needs an original opinion or a critique of an opinion
already served, the path in is identical. Submit the caption or party
names through the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and identify any
disclosure, deposition, or trial dates the court has fixed. A documented
conflicts answer comes back before confidential records are exchanged, the
scope is then defined in writing, and the work is calendared to the
forum's rules, so the finished product, opinion or rebuttal, arrives
tested rather than hurried.
