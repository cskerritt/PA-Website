## Small town, metro-scale evidence

Nolanville is a small Bell County community whose working residents
participate in the labor market of the surrounding Killeen-Temple
metropolitan area. That distinction matters methodologically. Rather than
pretending town-level data exists where it does not, the analysis grounds
employability and earning capacity findings in Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates for the Killeen-Temple area and
for Texas statewide, corroborated by state workforce agency labor market
information and, where a referral question requires it, direct contact with
employers in the evaluee's commutable range. The
[Killeen-Temple metro page](/locations/texas/killeen/) carries the regional
overview; the Nolanville report shows its work at the level the case
demands, stating what the data can support and, just as carefully, what it
cannot, so the opinion never outruns its foundation.

## Where a Nolanville case is tried

Because Nolanville lies in Bell County, state court matters proceed in the
District Court sitting in Bell County. Federal cases are placed under the
federal venue rules among the districts of Texas, including the United
States District Court for the Western District of Texas and the United
States District Court for the Southern District of Texas, and the correct
forum is nailed down at intake. Forum dictates the shape of the expert
file: disclosure timing, the required contents of the written report, and
deposition practice all differ between systems, and a report drafted to
the wrong standard hands the other side an argument it did not have to
earn.

No staffed office exists in Nolanville, and none is claimed. Engagements
