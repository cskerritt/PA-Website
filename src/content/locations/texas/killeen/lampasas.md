Lampasas County anchors its own corner of the Killeen-Temple, TX
metropolitan area, and litigation arising in the city of Lampasas carries
the character of that county rather than of the metro's principal city.
Counsel handling injury, disability, and employment matters there retain
Purinton Analytics for
[vocational evaluation and testimony](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[coordinated damages assessment](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/),
the last structured so that independent economist partners perform and sign
the economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning components
proceed within a single engagement. The firm works for whichever side
retains it first, on the same written standard either way.

## Venue in Lampasas County

State court matters from Lampasas are tried in the District Court serving
Lampasas County. Federal litigation is venued under the federal map of
Texas, and intake confirms whether a case belongs in the United States
District Court for the Western District of Texas, the United States
District Court for the Northern District of Texas, or another district
before the expert calendar is drawn. County and forum are more than
letterhead details for an expert: they determine which disclosure rules
shape the report, which deposition practice applies, and how far in advance
appearance dates must be secured. The engagement letter for a Lampasas
matter reflects those answers from the start.

## The county's workers and the region's market

An earning capacity opinion for a Lampasas evaluee has to respect the fact
that county residents may look toward the wider region for work while
remaining rooted in a smaller local economy. The analysis draws on Bureau
of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the
Killeen-Temple metropolitan area and for Texas statewide, on state
workforce agency labor market information, and on direct employer research
where a referral question demands it. Regional context lives on the
[Killeen-Temple metro page](/locations/texas/killeen/); the Lampasas report
defines the evaluee's commutable market in plain terms and explains the
reasoning, because a definition that cannot be explained cannot be
defended at deposition. Where transferable skills are at issue, the
analysis documents the evaluee's work history against the occupational
requirements in the published data, so the pathway from past work to
residual capacity is visible on the page.

## What by-appointment service means here

The firm's only home office is in Kansas City; there is no Lampasas
storefront and no local address to publish, and the practice says so
rather than implying otherwise. Day-to-day work proceeds at a distance
through secure records exchange, remote conferences, and remote evaluation
where testing protocols permit. In-person evaluation, when the referral
calls for it, takes place in the evaluee's own community. Travel for
depositions and trial settings is planned from Kansas City with dates
committed at retention, the same operating model the firm applies across
[Texas](/locations/texas/) and its
[national practice](/locations/nationwide/).

## The first step for Lampasas counsel

Retention begins with a documented conflict check. Provide the caption or
the party names through the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/), together
with any disclosure, deposition, or trial dates already on the docket. A
