Temple appears by name in the designation of the Killeen-Temple, TX
metropolitan area, and that naming reflects something real: litigation
arising in Temple involves evaluees whose working lives are rooted in a
city with an employment base of its own. An opinion about what such an
evaluee can earn should name that base and work from it, not from a
generic regional label. Purinton Analytics serves Temple counsel with
[vocational evaluation and expert testimony](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. Referrals from plaintiff and defense counsel are
handled on identical terms, as they are throughout the firm's
[Texas practice](/locations/texas/).

## Courts hearing Temple and Bell County matters

Temple sits in Bell County, and significant civil matters arising there are
tried in the District Court sitting in Bell County. Federal cases proceed
separately: Texas is divided among several federal districts, including the
United States District Court for the Western District of Texas and the
United States District Court for the Northern District of Texas, and the
operative forum is confirmed during intake rather than assumed. The
distinction is not academic. Disclosure obligations, expert report
requirements, and deposition practice differ between the state district
courts and the federal bench, so each Temple engagement is calendared
against the scheduling order that actually governs the case, and appearance
dates are committed in writing before the work begins.

## A labor market read on its own terms

Employability and earning capacity findings for Temple evaluees rest on
Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for
the Killeen-Temple metropolitan area and for Texas statewide, supplemented
by state workforce agency labor market information and direct employer
research where the referral questions warrant it. The wider regional context
is described on the [Killeen-Temple metro page](/locations/texas/killeen/);
what matters at the town level is that the report states plainly how the
evaluee's commutable labor market was defined and why, so that the
foundation of every opinion can be examined by either side of the caption
without guesswork.

## Meetings by appointment, work built for distance

Temple meetings are held by appointment. The firm keeps no staffed office in
Temple and publishes no street address for this location, because accuracy
about presence belongs to the same discipline that governs its written
opinions. Counsel work with a practice designed for distance: secure records
transfer, remote conferences for scheduling and pre-deposition preparation,
and remote evaluation where the referral questions and testing protocols
permit. When an in-person evaluation is appropriate, it is arranged in the
evaluee's own community, and when deposition or trial settings require
presence, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office against the
deadlines counsel identify at intake. The same structure supports the
firm's [national practice](/locations/nationwide/), so a Temple matter that
later involves out-of-state parties or venues does not require a change of
expert.

## Begin with a conflict check

The first step in any Temple engagement is a documented conflict check,
followed by a defined scope and a records request tailored to the claims.
Submit the caption or party names through the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and note any imminent disclosure,
