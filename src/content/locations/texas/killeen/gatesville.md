Gatesville badly or not at all. Purinton Analytics was built the other way

[medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/) wherever the

## Remote-first, with a plan for presence

Most of a Gatesville engagement needs no travel at all. Records arrive by
secure transfer, attorney conferences and pre-deposition preparation run by
remote conference, and evaluation is conducted remotely whenever the
protocols supporting the referral questions permit. Where an in-person
evaluation is warranted, it is held in the evaluee's own community, so a
claimant with functional limitations is not asked to make a long drive to
prove them. When deposition or trial requires the expert physically
present, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office and the
appearance dates are committed against the court's calendar at the time of
retention, not negotiated later. There is no staffed Gatesville location
and no published local address; the firm states that plainly because
honesty about presence is part of what counsel are buying.

## Coryell County and the federal districts

Gatesville matters filed in state court proceed in the District Court
serving Coryell County. Federal matters follow federal venue rules, and the
engagement's forum is confirmed during intake against the Texas districts,
among them the United States District Court for the Western District of
Texas and the United States District Court for the Eastern District of
Texas. Getting the forum right at the start is more than housekeeping,
because the disclosure sequence, the required contents of the written
report, and the deposition practice all follow from it, and the work plan
for the opinion is drafted to match.

## Labor market evidence for a smaller community

A smaller community demands more care from the vocational analyst, not
less, because thin local data tempts experts toward assumptions. Findings
for Gatesville evaluees rely on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational
employment and wage estimates for the Killeen-Temple metropolitan area and
for Texas statewide, on state workforce agency labor market information,
and on direct labor market research when the question is whether particular
work is genuinely available within the evaluee's commutable range. The
[Killeen-Temple metro page](/locations/texas/killeen/) frames the regional
economy; the Gatesville report says explicitly how the evaluee's market was
drawn and why that drawing is defensible, in language a jury can follow and
opposing counsel can test.
