## Evaluation logistics, planned before retention

Counsel are told at intake exactly how the evaluation will be conducted and
why. Where the referral questions can be answered through remote protocols,
the evaluation proceeds by secure video with records reviewed in advance,
which spares an injured evaluee an unnecessary trip. Where in-person
assessment is warranted, the appointment is arranged near where the evaluee
lives rather than at a distant office, and travel for the evaluator is
scheduled from the Kansas City home office. The firm has no staffed
location in Morgan's Point Resort and does not publish an address
suggesting it does; the logistics above are what by-appointment service
actually means, stated without decoration.

## How to refer a Morgan's Point Resort matter

Referral starts with conflicts, not records. Submit the caption or party
names through the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and identify any
dates the court has already set. After a documented conflicts clearance,
the scope is agreed in writing and a records request tailored to the
claims issues, so the evaluation can be scheduled with the care the
evaluee's situation calls for. Counsel who flag mobility or scheduling
constraints at referral give the engagement its best chance of finishing
early rather than on the courthouse steps.
