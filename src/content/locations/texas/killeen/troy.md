Every Troy engagement follows the same sequence, and the sequence is the
point: a documented conflict check, a scope defined in writing, a records
request tailored to the claims, and only then an opinion. That order of
operations is what lets a report from Purinton Analytics stand in front of
opposing counsel without apology, whether the retention is for
[vocational expert testimony](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), or a
[coordinated damages engagement](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis alongside the vocational and care-planning work. Troy counsel on
either side of a caption are served on identical terms.

## What the sequence produces

The conflict check comes first because nothing else is trustworthy without
it. The written scope follows because an expert who drifts beyond the
referral questions creates exposure for the retaining party. The tailored
records request comes next because an opinion is only as strong as the
record beneath it, and a boilerplate request either misses what matters or
buries it. By the time a Troy report is signed, each conclusion in it can
be traced to a source that was disclosed, and the file is organized for the
deposition that may follow. This is the same standard the firm applies
across its [Texas practice](/locations/texas/) and its
[work nationwide](/locations/nationwide/).

## The courts that hear Troy matters

Troy is a Bell County community, and state court litigation arising there
proceeds in the District Court sitting in Bell County. For federal matters,
the venue question is resolved at intake among the districts of Texas,
including the United States District Court for the Northern District of
Texas and the United States District Court for the Western District of
Texas. The answer controls the disclosure sequence and the form of the
written report, so it is settled before the engagement calendar is drawn
rather than discovered midstream.

## Labor market foundations for a small community

Troy is a small town whose residents work within the broader Killeen-Temple
metropolitan labor market, and the vocational analysis is honest about
that. Employability and earning capacity findings draw on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the
Killeen-Temple area and for Texas statewide, on state workforce agency
labor market information, and on direct labor market research when a
referral question turns on the availability of specific work within the
evaluee's reach. The [Killeen-Temple metro page](/locations/texas/killeen/)
sets out the regional context that the town-level report then applies to
the individual evaluee, qualitatively and with its reasoning on display.

## Meetings, evaluations, and appearances

There is no Purinton Analytics office in Troy; meetings are by appointment
and the firm says so plainly. The working engagement runs on secure records
transfer, remote conferences, and remote evaluation where protocols
permit, with in-person evaluation conducted in the evaluee's own community
when the referral requires it. Deposition and trial appearances are
traveled from the Kansas City home office on dates committed at retention.

## Submit the conflict check

To put the sequence in motion, send the caption or party names through the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and flag any disclosure, deposition,
or trial dates already ordered. Counsel receive a conflicts answer before
any confidential material changes hands.
