## The disclosure calendar comes first

At intake, counsel are asked for the operative scheduling order, or for the
anticipated deadlines if none has issued. That is not a formality. Expert
disclosure sequences, report requirements, and deposition practice differ
between Texas state courts and the federal bench, and the work plan for a
Belton matter is written to the rules that actually govern it. Belton lies
in Bell County, where state court litigation proceeds in the District Court
sitting in Bell County; federal matters are venued according to the map of
Texas districts, which includes the United States District Court for the
Western District of Texas and the United States District Court for the
Northern District of Texas. Confirming the forum before the engagement
letter is signed keeps the calendar honest.
