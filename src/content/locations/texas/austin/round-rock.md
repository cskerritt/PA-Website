Round Rock sits in Williamson County, and engagements are accepted for matters pending in the District Courts that hear civil cases there. Federal engagements are accepted as well, whether the case is filed in the United States District Court for the Western District of Texas, the United States District Court for the Northern District of Texas, the United States District Court for the Southern District of Texas, or the United States District Court for the Eastern District of Texas. ## How Round Rock Fits the Metro Labor Market

A Round Rock evaluee is assessed against the labor market that person actually faces, which for most residents means the commuting range of the Austin metropolitan area rather than city limits. Vocational findings rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the metro area and for Texas statewide, supplemented by state workforce agency labor market information and direct labor market research where the referral questions warrant it. The analysis stays qualitative until the record supports specifics: transferable skills, residual functional capacity, access to suitable occupations, and the gap between pre-injury and post-injury earning capacity are each documented from source data both sides can examine.

The firm keeps no office in Round Rock and publishes no local address, because candor about presence belongs to the same discipline that governs the reports.
