A report that cannot be defended from the witness chair is not worth serving, so testimony is where planning for a Lakeway engagement begins. Purinton Analytics offers [expert testimony and litigation consulting](/services/expert-testimony-litigation-consulting/) alongside its core [vocational](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) and [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) work, and every Lakeway opinion is prepared with the deposition transcript in mind: definitions the witness can state cleanly, sources the witness has actually read, and conclusions the witness reached rather than adopted. Plaintiff and defense counsel receive the same preparation standard.

## Testimony Logistics for Lakeway Counsel

Deposition and trial appearances are scheduled early against the case calendar, with travel arranged from the Kansas City home office; remote deposition testimony is supported where the parties and the court permit it. Pre-testimony work includes a review of the full expert file, the opposing reports, and the deposition exhibits, so the answers given under oath match the report served in discovery. Counsel receive the file organized for examination, not a box of paper to decode the week before trial.

## Travis County and the Federal Bench

Lakeway sits in Travis County, and the practice accepts engagements in the District Courts hearing civil cases there. Federal engagements are accepted throughout the state, including in the United States District Court for the Western District of Texas; the [Texas page](/locations/texas/) presents the complete statewide venue picture. Registration or qualification requirements particular to any venue are identified and confirmed during the conflict check and intake process, ahead of designation deadlines.

## Situating a Lakeway Evaluee in the Regional Market

A Lakeway resident's occupational outlook is analyzed within the commuting market of the Austin metropolitan area, which the [Austin metro page](/locations/texas/austin/) examines in detail. The report's labor market foundation is qualitative and cited: Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the metro area and for Texas statewide, state workforce agency labor market information, and direct labor market research when the referral questions call for it. Transferable skills, occupational access, and the comparison of pre-injury and post-injury capacity are each carried from evidence to conclusion in steps a cross-examiner can follow and a jury can understand.
