Sometimes the expert file in a Manor case is already complete when counsel calls: the other side has served a life care plan or a vocational report, and the question is whether it holds up. Purinton Analytics takes those engagements as readily as affirmative retentions, offering [rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) that examines an opposing opinion's sources, methods, and internal consistency with the same rigor the firm applies to its own [vocational expert](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) and [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) work. A rebuttal that overstates its criticisms is as vulnerable as the report it attacks, so the review says only what the record supports.

## Rebuttal Posture and Affirmative Work

In rebuttal posture, the deliverable identifies where an opposing plan departs from the clinical record, where a vocational conclusion skips an analytical step, and where a stated source does not say what the report claims. In affirmative posture, the firm produces the original opinions, structured so the eventual reviewing expert finds nothing to work with. Either way, a Manor engagement opens with a documented conflict check, a defined scope, and a records request tailored to the claims, and the opinions are available to plaintiff and defense counsel alike.

## Manor Evaluees and the Metro Market

Manor is a small suburb, and its residents' earning capacity is properly measured against the commuting market of the Austin metropolitan area rather than the town itself; the [Austin metro page](/locations/texas/austin/) develops that market fully. Vocational findings draw on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the metro area and for Texas statewide, on state workforce agency labor market information, and on direct labor market research where the referral questions require more. Market definition is a frequent point of attack in cross-examination, which is why the report states its market explicitly and defends the choice.

## Practical Arrangements

The firm keeps no office in Manor and does not dress up coverage as presence.
