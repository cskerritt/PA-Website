Two competing accounts of one person's working future: that is the shape a contested San Marcos injury or employment case tends to take. One side argues that the evaluee's earning capacity is largely gone; the other argues that it largely remains. Purinton Analytics exists to replace that argument with evidence. The firm prepares [vocational expert witness](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) opinions and [life care plans](/services/life-care-planning/) for San Marcos litigation, and it takes the retention from plaintiffs and defendants alike, because a forecast of future earnings earns its place in the record only by surviving the side that did not commission it.

## Hays County Courts and Federal Venue

San Marcos matters proceed in the District Courts that hear Hays County's civil docket, and state-court engagements are accepted there for injury, employment, and disability disputes. In federal court, engagements are accepted across Texas, whether the case is filed in the United States District Court for the Western District of Texas, the United States District Court for the Northern District of Texas, the United States District Court for the Southern District of Texas, or the United States District Court for the Eastern District of Texas. ## From Restrictions to a Documented Forecast

A statement about the future is credible only when every step toward it can be checked in the present. The analysis for a San Marcos evaluee therefore moves in documented stages: medical restrictions establish residual functional capacity, residual capacity defines occupational access, and occupational access supports the comparison of pre-injury and post-injury earning capacity. The market behind each stage is the commuting market of the Austin metropolitan area, which the [Austin metro page](/locations/texas/austin/) treats in depth, and the sources are named in the report itself: Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the metro area and for Texas statewide, state workforce agency labor market information, and direct labor market research where the referral calls for it. The prose stays qualitative throughout, and when placement or accommodation questions form part of the referral, the report answers them directly rather than leaving counsel to infer a position from silence.
