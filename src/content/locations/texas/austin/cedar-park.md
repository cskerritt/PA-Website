Every vocational opinion written for a Cedar Park case is drafted as though the opposing expert has already been retained, because in serious litigation that is usually true. Purinton Analytics prepares [vocational expert](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) reports and [life care plans](/services/life-care-planning/) for Cedar Park matters on the assumption that each finding will be tested at deposition, so the methodology, the sources, and the reasoning are laid out in the report itself rather than reconstructed under cross-examination. That standard holds regardless of which side issues the retention.

## Methodology That Survives Scrutiny

The sequence is the same in every Cedar Park engagement: a documented conflict check, a defined scope, and a records request tailored to the claims. Vocational conclusions follow a transparent chain from medical restrictions to residual capacity to occupational access, and life care plans tie every recommended service to a clinical source. Where the record is thin, the report says so instead of papering over the gap. Counsel receive drafts early enough to probe them before disclosure, and [rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) engagements apply the same lens to the opposing expert's work.
