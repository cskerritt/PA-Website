For an injured worker or a disabled claimant living in Leander, the practical question is rarely where the expert sits; it is whether the evaluation can happen without upending recovery, work schedules, or family logistics. Purinton Analytics answers that question with a remote-first structure. [Vocational evaluations](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) and [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) interviews for Leander evaluees are conducted by secure video whenever the referral permits, and in the evaluee's own community when hands-on assessment is warranted, so the person at the center of the case is never asked to travel to an expert's office that a suburb was never going to have anyway.

## What Remote-First Means in Practice

Records arrive electronically under a request tailored to the claims. The clinical interview, work history review, and functional discussion proceed by video at times that respect medical appointments and caregiving duties. Standardized instruments that require in-person administration are flagged at intake rather than discovered late, and when they are needed, the evaluation is scheduled in Leander itself or nearby, with the evaluator traveling from the Kansas City home office. Nothing about the format softens the product: the written opinion documents its sources so that plaintiff and defense counsel alike can trace every finding.
