A Georgetown engagement rarely begins with a blank calendar. By the time counsel contacts Purinton Analytics, the court has usually entered a scheduling order, expert designation and disclosure dates are on the docket, and a mediation may already be set. The practice is organized around that fact. [Vocational expert](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) opinions and [life care plans](/services/life-care-planning/) for Georgetown litigation are sequenced backward from the dates the court has fixed, so the report is served on time and still able to withstand examination. Plaintiff and defense counsel retain the firm on identical terms, and the [Austin metro overview](/locations/texas/austin/) describes the wider regional practice.

Working backward changes the shape of the work, not its rigor. The records request goes out at the start of the engagement, tailored to the claims, so the written opinion rests on documents rather than on assumptions patched in near the deadline. Draft findings reach counsel with enough lead time to inform mediation posture or settlement valuation, and the expert file is complete before the designation date instead of assembled after it.

The [Texas state page](/locations/texas/) sets out the full statewide venue picture. ## Measuring Earning Capacity Against the Austin Metro Market

A Georgetown resident's earning capacity is measured against the commuting labor market of the Austin metropolitan area, not against a single town's job listings. The vocational analysis draws on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Austin-Round Rock-San Marcos, TX metropolitan area and for Texas statewide, on state workforce agency labor market information, and on direct employer research when the referral requires it. Conclusions about transferable skills, occupational access, and post-injury capacity are stated qualitatively in prose and tied to source data that opposing counsel can check line by line.

## By Appointment in Georgetown

There is no Purinton Analytics office in Georgetown, and the firm will not manufacture the appearance of one with a rented address; meetings here are by appointment. The work itself travels well.
