A catastrophic injury case out of Kyle turns on a question that outlasts the verdict: what will this person's care actually require, year after year, and what will living with the injury do to their working life? Purinton Analytics answers with [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) built item by item from the clinical record, paired with [vocational analysis](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) of what work remains possible. Plaintiff and defense counsel in Kyle matters retain the practice on equal footing, and the plans read the same way regardless of who pays the invoice.

## Building a Life Care Plan That Holds Together

Each recommended service in a Kyle life care plan traces to a treating or evaluating clinician, a published standard of care, or a documented need, and the plan says which. Where future care depends on contested medical questions, the plan flags the dependency instead of resolving it silently. [Medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/) are available when the assignment is narrower than a full plan, and [rebuttal review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) is available when the assignment is the other side's plan. The vocational companion analysis addresses residual capacity and occupational access so the damages picture is complete rather than duplicative.

## Hays County Courts and Beyond

Kyle sits in Hays County, and engagements are accepted in the District Courts that hear the county's civil docket. Federal matters are accepted throughout Texas, including in the United States District Court for the Western District of Texas, with the state's other federal districts served on the same basis; the [Texas locations page](/locations/texas/) maps the full venue landscape. Venue-specific registration or qualification requirements are identified and confirmed during the conflict check and intake process.

## Work and Wages in the Surrounding Metro

For vocational purposes, a Kyle evaluee is situated in the commuting market of the Austin metropolitan area, treated fully on the [Austin metro page](/locations/texas/austin/). The analysis relies on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the metro area and for Texas statewide, alongside state workforce agency labor market information and, where the referral warrants, direct labor market research. Findings remain qualitative in prose and are documented so that either party can trace them to their sources.

## How a Kyle Evaluee Is Seen

No Purinton Analytics office exists in Kyle, and this page will not suggest otherwise. The practice runs by appointment: records and conferences move electronically, many interviews proceed by secure video, and in-person evaluations are conducted in the evaluee's own community, often in the home when the injury makes that the honest setting for assessing daily function. Travel, including for deposition and trial, is scheduled from the Kansas City home office. The same approach serves referring counsel [across the country](/locations/nationwide/).

## Referring a Kyle Case

Where lost earnings and care costs must become a damages number, [coordinated damages assessment](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) adds independent economist partners who perform and sign the economic analysis while the care-planning and vocational opinions develop under a shared engagement structure. To refer a Kyle matter, [complete a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) with the caption and party names, and describe the injury and current treatment posture so scoping can begin promptly.
