## Randall County Venue and the Courts Behind It

Engagements are accepted for matters pending in the District Court serving Randall County, where most Canyon civil litigation of the kind that calls for a vocational or care-planning expert will be filed. Federal appointments are accepted as well: the practice works throughout Texas, including matters in the United States District Court for the Northern District of Texas and in the Southern, Eastern, and Western Districts of the state. The [Texas state page](/locations/texas/) sets out that statewide venue picture in full. Where a particular court imposes its own registration or qualification requirement, that requirement is identified and confirmed during the conflict check and intake process, before any engagement letter is signed and before any deadline is promised.

## Measuring a Canyon Evaluee Against the Amarillo Market

A Canyon resident does not compete for work within the city limits alone; earning capacity is measured against the commuting labor market anchored by Amarillo. Vocational findings for a Canyon evaluee rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Amarillo metropolitan area and for Texas statewide, on state workforce agency labor market information, and on direct labor market research where the referral questions warrant it. Transferable skills, occupational access, and post-injury capacity are analyzed the same way for a plaintiff referral as for a defense referral, and every conclusion is written so that the opposing expert can trace it back to its source data.

## Evaluation Logistics for a Canyon Case

The firm keeps no staffed office in Canyon and publishes no local street address, because accuracy about presence belongs to the same discipline that governs the reports themselves. Meetings are by appointment. Records review, counsel conferences, and many evaluee interviews proceed remotely on a scheduled basis, which keeps timelines short and costs proportionate. When the referral questions require an in-person evaluation, the evaluation is conducted in the evaluee's own community rather than requiring an injured person to travel, with the trip scheduled from the Kansas City home office. Deposition and trial appearances are planned the same way, around the dates the court has actually set. The identical structure serves counsel [across the country](/locations/nationwide/), so a Canyon referral is handled with the same file conventions as a matter from any other venue.
