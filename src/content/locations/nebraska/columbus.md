An expert report offered in a Columbus courtroom will be read most
carefully by the lawyer who did not commission it, and Purinton Analytics
writes for that reader. The firm serves plaintiff and defense counsel in
the Columbus micropolitan area with opinions built to survive
cross-examination:
[earning capacity and employability evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/)
in injury and employment matters,
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) in catastrophic injury
cases, and [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Where the firm appears for Columbus counsel

The Columbus micropolitan area comprises Platte County and Colfax County.
The firm accepts matters venued in the District Courts of both counties and
in the United States District Court for the District of Nebraska, and it
brings the same protocol to each: a documented conflict check before any
substantive discussion, a defined scope, and a records request tailored to
the claims. Because retention can come from either side of the caption, the
file is assembled so that both sides could examine it, which is the
sturdiest position an expert opinion can occupy. Counsel with matters
beyond these counties will find the firm's statewide court coverage on the
[Nebraska overview](/locations/nebraska/).

## A by-appointment practice, described accurately

Purinton Analytics keeps no staffed office in Columbus and publishes no
street address for this location. The firm says so directly because an
expert whose website invents an office invites questions about what else
has been invented. Columbus area engagements run by appointment: records
are exchanged and reviewed through secure channels, vocational interviews
are conducted by secure video or in person in the evaluee's own community
when the referral questions call for it, and conferences with counsel fit
the litigation calendar rather than office hours. When a deposition,
hearing, or trial requires the expert in the room, travel is scheduled from
the Kansas City home office with lead time planned around counsel's
deadlines.

## Labor market foundations for Columbus area opinions

Employability, mitigation, and earning capacity findings for Columbus area
evaluees are grounded in Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment
and wage estimates for the area and for Nebraska statewide, in state
workforce agency labor market information, and in direct labor market
research when a question depends on what regional employers actually hire
for. The report cites its sources, describes the local market in the
market's own terms rather than through a metropolitan template, and frames
each conclusion no more broadly than the data support. The same evidentiary
standard carries into the vocational foundation of any life care plan
prepared for an evaluee in the area.

Columbus matters are handled inside the same national engagement structure
described on the [nationwide practice](/locations/nationwide/) page, so
intake, disclosure practice, and scheduling work the same here as in any
venue the firm serves. To begin a local or national engagement,
[request a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) with the case caption and
parties, and note any imminent disclosure, deposition, or trial dates. Once
the conflict check clears, the firm confirms scope and fee structure and
identifies the records needed to open the file for matters in Platte
