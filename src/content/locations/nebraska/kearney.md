Distance is usually the first question counsel in Kearney ask a national
expert practice, so it is worth answering first: the work does not require
the expert to live nearby, and it never has. Purinton Analytics serves
plaintiff and defense counsel in the Kearney micropolitan area through a
remote-first practice built for exactly this situation, providing
[earning capacity and employability evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/)
in injury and employment matters,
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) in catastrophic injury
cases, and [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Courts and counties served

The Kearney micropolitan area consists of Buffalo County and Kearney
County. The firm accepts matters venued in the District Courts of both
counties and in the United States District Court for the District of
Nebraska, and it applies the identical protocol in each forum: a documented
conflict check before substantive discussion, a defined scope, and a
records request tailored to the claims, so that the opinion rests on a
record both sides of the caption can examine. The
[Nebraska overview](/locations/nebraska/) describes the firm's coverage of
the state's remaining trial and federal courts for counsel whose dockets
extend beyond the area.

## What remote-first actually means here

The firm keeps no staffed office in Kearney and publishes no street address
for this location, because claiming a local presence that does not exist
would sit poorly beside reports that trade on accuracy. Instead, the
engagement is structured so that geography stops mattering. Records are
exchanged and reviewed through secure channels. Vocational interviews are
conducted by secure video, or in person in the evaluee's own community when
the referral questions call for face-to-face evaluation, which spares an
injured evaluee the burden of traveling to an expert's city. Conferences
with counsel are held by appointment at whatever hour the trial calendar
demands. When a deposition or trial requires the expert physically present,
travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office with lead time built
around counsel's deadlines.

## Labor market data for the Kearney area

Vocational conclusions for Kearney area evaluees are grounded in Bureau of
Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the area
and for Nebraska statewide, in state workforce agency labor market
information, and in direct labor market research when a placement,
mitigation, or transferable-skills question turns on what regional
employers actually hire for. The report cites each source so that opposing
counsel can trace every finding to its data, and it describes the local
market qualitatively rather than forcing it into a big-city frame: the
question is always what work exists for this evaluee, with these
restrictions, in this labor market.

## Starting a Kearney engagement

Kearney matters run on the same national structure described on the
[nationwide practice](/locations/nationwide/) page, so counsel get
consistent intake, disclosure practice, and scheduling regardless of venue.
To begin a local or national engagement,
[request a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) with the case caption and
parties, and flag any imminent disclosure, deposition, or trial dates. Once
the conflict check clears, the firm confirms scope and fee structure and
identifies the records needed to open the file, putting a defined
engagement in place for matters in Buffalo County, Kearney County, or
federal court.
