Waverly's working residents largely earn their livings on the strength of
the Lincoln labor market: careers built in town, careers built in the
principal city, and work histories that move between the two. That commuting
reality is where any honest earning-capacity analysis for a Waverly evaluee
has to start, because the question is never what jobs exist inside the town
limits but what work the evaluee can actually reach and hold. Purinton
Analytics brings that frame to
[vocational expert](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) and
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) engagements for both
plaintiff and defense counsel, applying the documented methodology set out
on the firm's [nationwide page](/locations/nationwide/).

## Lancaster County courts and the federal alternative

Waverly lies in Lancaster County, and state-court claims arising there are
generally tried in the District Court sitting in Lancaster County. The
federal forum Nebraska offers is the United States District Court for the
District of Nebraska; the firm takes no position on where a particular
caption belongs, and the controlling venue is identified during the conflict
check and intake process. What matters for the expert file is that state and
federal disclosure practice diverge, so the operative deadlines are
confirmed at intake and every draft is written with the governing standard
in view. The firm's broader work across the metro appears on the
[Lincoln metro page](/locations/nebraska/lincoln/), and its statewide
practice on the [Nebraska page](/locations/nebraska/).

## Measuring the market a Waverly evaluee actually uses

The vocational opinion is anchored in Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates for the Lincoln metropolitan area
and for Nebraska statewide, together with state workforce agency labor
market information and, where the questions call for it, direct contact with
employers. From that base the analysis tests transferable skills against
occupations within the evaluee's demonstrated commuting pattern and states,
in writing, the reasons an alternative occupation is or is not realistically
available. Counsel who need the vocational and care findings carried into a
damages calculation can retain a
[coordinated damages engagement](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure, keeping the expert chain clean for disclosure.

## Appointments in the evaluee's community

The firm maintains no Waverly office and lists no street address here;
candor about where the firm actually sits is part of the credibility it
sells. Records review, strategy calls, and report conferences run remotely
by default. In-person vocational or life care planning evaluations, when
warranted, are held by appointment in professional space in or near the
evaluee's own community, and testimony obligations are met through travel
planned from the Kansas City home office. Counsel learn at intake exactly
what the calendar will bear, not after a scheduling order makes the answer
costly.

## Starting a Waverly engagement

The first step is the same for either side of the caption: a documented
conflict check before any substantive discussion. Send the caption or party
names through the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) along with a short
statement of the referral questions and any dates already fixed for
disclosure, deposition, or trial. A scope proposal and fee terms follow, and
the resulting opinion answers to the record rather than to the retaining
party.
