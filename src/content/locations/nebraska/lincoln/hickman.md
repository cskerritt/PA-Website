## Where Hickman claims are heard

Hickman is a Lancaster County community, and state-court injury, employment,
and disability claims arising there are generally heard in the District
Court sitting in Lancaster County. Nebraska's federal trial court is the
United States District Court for the District of Nebraska; which forum
governs a particular caption is a question for counsel, resolved and
confirmed during the conflict check and intake process. The choice matters
to the expert because the state and federal systems impose different disclosure and report
requirements, so intake fixes the operative rules before analysis begins.
Metro-wide engagements are addressed on the
[Lincoln metro page](/locations/nebraska/lincoln/), and the firm's full
Nebraska practice on the [Nebraska page](/locations/nebraska/).

## The labor market behind a Hickman opinion

For a Hickman evaluee, employability and earning capacity are assessed
against the employment base the evaluee can genuinely reach, which for most
residents means the Lincoln metropolitan labor market as well as work closer
to home. The analysis draws on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational
employment and wage estimates for the Lincoln metropolitan area and for
Nebraska statewide, on state workforce agency labor market information, and
on direct labor market research where the referral requires it. Every
occupational alternative named in the report comes with the reasoning that
supports or excludes it. Where the case also needs the loss reduced to
economic terms, counsel may structure the referral as a
[coordinated damages engagement](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Evaluating close to home

Purinton Analytics keeps no office in Hickman and publishes no street
address for this page, because overstating presence would sit poorly beside
reports that trade on precision. The working relationship is remote-first:
records arrive electronically, conferences happen by video or phone, and
drafts are discussed without anyone traveling. When an in-person vocational
or life care planning evaluation serves the case, it is scheduled by
appointment in appropriate professional space in the evaluee's own
community, sparing an injured evaluee a long trip, and courtroom or
deposition appearances are handled through travel arranged from the Kansas
City home office.

## Opening the file

Before any merits discussion, the firm runs a documented conflict check.
Provide the caption or party names through the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/), state the referral questions briefly,
and identify any disclosure, deposition, or trial dates the scheduling order
has already set. Counsel receive a defined scope and fee terms in response,
and the analysis that follows is the same whichever party signs the
engagement letter.
