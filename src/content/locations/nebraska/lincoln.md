Counsel who try cases in the District Court of Lancaster County and in the
United States District Court for the District of Nebraska retain experts
whose files must hold up in both forums, and Purinton Analytics builds its
Lincoln work to that standard. The firm prepares vocational expert and life
care planning opinions for plaintiff and defense counsel across the Lincoln
metropolitan area. Engagements from the area typically involve
[earning capacity and employability evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/)
in injury and employment litigation,
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) in catastrophic injury
matters, and [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Courts served in the Lincoln area

The Lincoln metropolitan area comprises Lancaster County and Seward County,
and the firm accepts matters venued in the District Courts of both, as well
as in the United States District Court for the District of Nebraska. State
and federal matters follow the same protocol: a documented conflict check
before any substantive discussion, a defined scope, and a records request
tailored to the claims, so the resulting opinion rests on a record both
sides can examine. Counsel handling matters beyond these counties can find
the statewide picture on the [Nebraska overview](/locations/nebraska/),
which maps the firm's coverage of the state's trial and federal courts.

## By-appointment presence, stated plainly

The firm maintains no staffed office in Lincoln and publishes no street
address for this location. That is stated plainly because honesty about
presence is part of the same discipline that governs the reports
themselves. In practice, the absence of a storefront changes little about
how a Lincoln engagement runs: records arrive and are reviewed through
secure channels, vocational interviews are conducted by secure video or
scheduled in person in the evaluee's community, and conferences with
counsel are held by appointment. When a deposition, evidentiary hearing, or
trial requires the expert in the room, travel is scheduled from the Kansas
City home office with enough lead time to protect counsel's calendar.

## Grounding opinions in the Lincoln labor market

Employability and earning capacity conclusions for Lincoln area evaluees
are grounded in Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage
estimates for the Lincoln metropolitan area and for Nebraska statewide,
supplemented by state workforce agency labor market information and by
direct labor market research when the referral questions call for it. The
report cites its sources so that every finding can be traced to the data
behind it, and the analysis is qualitative where the data are qualitative:
no figure appears in an opinion unless the underlying source supports it.
The same standard applies whether the question is post-injury
employability, mitigation of damages, or the vocational foundation of a
life care plan.

## Starting a Lincoln engagement

Lincoln matters are accepted within the same structure the firm applies to
its [nationwide practice](/locations/nationwide/), which keeps scheduling
and disclosure practice consistent no matter the venue. To begin a local or
national engagement, [request a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) with the
case caption and parties, and note any imminent disclosure, deposition, or
trial dates. Once the conflict check clears, the firm confirms scope and
fee structure and identifies the records needed to open the file, so that
work begins on a defined footing rather than an open-ended one.
