Purinton Analytics provides [vocational expert](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) and [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) opinions for counsel litigating those questions, accepting retention from plaintiff and defense alike and writing every report on the assumption that opposing counsel will study it line by line.

## Bellevue and the Omaha Commuting Market

Earning capacity for a Bellevue evaluee is measured against the occupations realistically open to that person, not against a hypothetical worker. Vocational findings rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Omaha metropolitan area and for Nebraska statewide, on state workforce agency labor market information, and on direct labor market research when the referral questions call for it. The analysis addresses transferable skills, occupational access, and placeability for the evaluee's specific education and work history, and it stays qualitative about the community itself, describing Bellevue's place in the wider commuting market without leaning on unverifiable local claims. Where economic damages must be quantified, [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) allow independent economist partners to perform and sign the economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement structure.
