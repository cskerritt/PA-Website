Every earning capacity opinion about a Wahoo evaluee reduces to the same working questions: which skills the person's actual work history transfers, which occupations remain open within the documented restrictions, and whether real employers in the relevant market would hire this person on the record as it stands. Purinton Analytics builds its [vocational expert](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) and [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) opinions around those questions rather than around advocacy, accepting plaintiff and defense retention on identical terms and writing every report as if opposing counsel will read it with a highlighter.

## Where a Wahoo Opinion Will Be Tested

Those questions get answered under oath in a particular courtroom. For a Wahoo dispute of general jurisdiction, that courtroom is the District Court, sitting as Saunders County's trial court. A claim that supports federal jurisdiction may instead be tested in the United States District Court for the District of Nebraska, the federal forum available in the state; which venue governs a particular case is settled during the conflict check and intake. The report is drafted to a disclosure standard that holds in either system, so the answer changes the caption but not the method, and the [Nebraska locations page](/locations/nebraska/) collects the rest of the state's venues for comparison.

## Transferable Skills and the Market Around Wahoo

The analysis follows the evaluee's real occupational reach rather than assuming it. Some Wahoo work histories stay close to home in Saunders County; others run through the wider metropolitan economy anchored by Omaha; the opinion documents which is true for this evaluee and why. The quantitative base is Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Omaha metropolitan area and for Nebraska statewide, joined by state workforce agency labor market information and direct labor market research where the referral questions justify it. Placeability conclusions are stated for the specific person, community scale stays a qualitative observation, and nothing is asserted that a careful cross-examination could not confirm. When economic loss must be quantified, [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) assign the calculation to independent economist partners, who perform and sign the economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning work continues under one engagement structure.

## The Conflict Check Comes First

The conflict check comes before any substantive discussion of the case, so the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) is the first step for counsel on either side. It needs only the parties and a short account of the dispute, along with any disclosure, deposition, or trial dates the court has already entered, because the delivery commitment is built around them. After clearance the firm defines scope in writing and issues a tailored records request. Opinions written by others are welcome as assignments in their own right through [rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/), and counsel whose matters sit outside Nebraska will find the same practice described on the [nationwide page](/locations/nationwide/).
