## How Blair Earning Capacity Is Measured

An earning capacity analysis for a Blair evaluee starts from facts held in tension: the community is small, and it sits within the Omaha metropolitan area. Local work histories are taken as they are, and the wider metropolitan labor market is weighed only where it genuinely bears on the evaluee's realistic options. Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Omaha metropolitan area and for Nebraska statewide supply the quantitative foundation, with state workforce agency labor market information and direct labor market research added when the referral questions call for the extra step. Findings address transferable skills, occupational access, and placeability for the specific person, and community scale is described qualitatively rather than dressed up as a statistic. When damages must be computed, [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) route that work to independent economist partners, who perform and sign the economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement structure.
