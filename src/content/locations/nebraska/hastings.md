Catastrophic injury cases in smaller markets raise a hard question: what
will a lifetime of care actually require for a person who lives here, not
in a major city? Purinton Analytics answers that question for plaintiff and
defense counsel in the Hastings micropolitan area through
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) grounded in the
evaluee's own community, alongside
[earning capacity and employability evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/)
in injury and employment matters and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Venue coverage for the Hastings area

The Hastings micropolitan area takes in Adams County, Clay County, and
Webster County. The firm accepts matters venued in the District Courts of
each county and in the United States District Court for the District of
Nebraska, applying the same protocol in every forum: a documented conflict
check before substantive discussion, a defined scope, and a records
request tailored to the claims, so that the opinion rests on a record
either side of the caption can test. For matters arising elsewhere in the
state, the [Nebraska overview](/locations/nebraska/) sets out the firm's
full court coverage.

## Care planning and evaluation without a local office

The firm keeps no staffed office in Hastings and publishes no street
address for this location; stating that plainly is part of the same
discipline that governs its reports. A life care plan, though, is built
around the evaluee rather than around an office. Medical and vocational
records are collected and reviewed through secure channels, treating
providers are contacted wherever they practice, and the evaluation itself
is conducted by secure video or in person in the evaluee's own community
when the referral questions call for face-to-face assessment. Conferences
with counsel are held by appointment, and when deposition or trial dates
require the expert's presence, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City
home office around counsel's deadlines.

## The labor market behind the numbers counsel will argue

Every vocational finding in a Hastings area matter, whether it stands alone
or supports a care plan, is grounded in Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates for the area and for Nebraska
statewide, in state workforce agency labor market information, and in
direct labor market research when a question turns on regional hiring
practice. The report cites each source so opposing counsel can trace every
conclusion, and it treats the local market on its own terms: employability
after injury in Adams County, Clay County, or Webster County is assessed
against the work that exists there, not against an assumption imported from
a larger metro.

## Starting a Hastings engagement

Hastings matters run inside the same structure that governs the firm's
[nationwide practice](/locations/nationwide/), which keeps intake,
disclosure, and scheduling consistent across every venue it serves. To
begin, submit the caption or party names through the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and note any imminent disclosure,
deposition, or trial dates. Once the conflict check clears, the firm
confirms scope and fee structure and identifies the records needed to open
the file, so a catastrophic injury case or employment matter in the
Hastings area starts with a defined plan rather than an open-ended
retention.
