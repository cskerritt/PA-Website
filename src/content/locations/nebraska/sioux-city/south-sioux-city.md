For counsel, that geography is procedural before it is anything else: a claim involving a South Sioux City party may proceed under Nebraska law in a Nebraska forum even though the working life at the center of the dispute plays out across the line in Sioux City, Iowa. Purinton Analytics is retained for [vocational expert](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) and [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) work at exactly that junction, in plaintiff and defense engagements alike, and every opinion is written to withstand the scrutiny of the lawyer on the other side of the caption.

## Dakota County Venue and the Nebraska Forum

Civil actions arising in South Sioux City are heard in the District Court, the trial court of general jurisdiction for Dakota County matters. Where a claim carries federal jurisdiction, the United States District Court for the District of Nebraska is available as a forum; the controlling venue for any particular matter is identified during the conflict check and intake, not assumed from the map. Reports, disclosures, and testimony are prepared to the same standard in either system, so counsel does not inherit a different work product because the case landed in state rather than federal court. ## A State-Line Position in the Sioux City Labor Market

Earning capacity for a South Sioux City evaluee is measured against the market where that person actually competes for work, and here that market ignores the border: the commuting area spans Iowa, Nebraska, and South Dakota, anchored by Sioux City itself. Vocational findings therefore rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Sioux City metropolitan area alongside the Nebraska statewide estimates, with state workforce agency labor market information and direct employer research added where the referral questions warrant it. Transferable skills, occupational access, and placeability are assessed for the specific evaluee's history rather than for a generic worker, and the report says plainly which data series supports each finding. When the claim requires economic damages, the firm offers [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement structure.
