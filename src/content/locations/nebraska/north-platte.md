## Serving Litigation in the North Platte Area

The venue comes before the opinion. A case filed in the District Court of Lincoln County reads differently to a jury than the same facts would elsewhere, and a credible expert report has to be grounded in the labor market the evaluee actually faces. That is the starting point for the firm's work in the North Platte micropolitan area, where counsel on either side of the caption retain [vocational expert witness](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) analysis, [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and [expert testimony and litigation consulting](/services/expert-testimony-litigation-consulting/) for injury, employment, and family law matters. The [Nebraska practice page](/locations/nebraska/) describes the statewide framework these engagements sit within.

## Courts and Counties Covered

The North Platte micropolitan area comprises Lincoln County and Logan County. The firm accepts engagements for matters in the District Court in each county and in the United States District Court for the District of Nebraska. Scope is defined in writing at intake: whether the assignment is an employability evaluation, an earning capacity opinion, a life care plan, or a review of an opposing expert's report, the engagement letter states the questions to be answered before any records are reviewed. Where economic loss calculations are needed alongside the vocational work, [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) place the economic analysis with independent economist partners, who perform and sign that analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under the same engagement structure.

## Meetings by Appointment, Not a Local Office

There is no staffed Purinton Analytics office in North Platte, and the firm publishes no street address for this location because it does not maintain a physical presence here. What counsel gets instead is a remote-first practice built for exactly this situation: secure video conferencing for evaluee interviews and attorney conferences, encrypted transfer for medical and employment records, and by-appointment in-person availability when an evaluation, deposition, or trial requires it. Travel to Lincoln County or Logan County is scheduled from the Kansas City home office and coordinated with counsel around court deadlines, so the absence of a storefront never becomes an absence of the expert.

## Labor Market Evidence Behind Each Opinion

Vocational findings for North Platte area evaluees are anchored in Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates, using the OEWS program's coverage of Nebraska statewide and of the state's nonmetropolitan regions, together with state workforce agency labor market information. Where published series run thin for a specific occupation, the firm conducts direct labor market research and says so in the report, identifying employers contacted and responses received rather than substituting assumption for evidence. Transferability of skills, placeability, and the gap between pre-injury and post-injury earning capacity are each addressed with sources cited, so that cross-examination confirms the method instead of exposing it.

## Beginning an Engagement

An engagement starts with a conflict check, not a sales call. Counsel with a matter in Lincoln County or Logan County can [submit the caption or party names through the conflict check form](/refer-a-case/), noting any disclosure deadlines, depositions, or trial settings already on the calendar. After conflicts clear, the firm confirms scope and schedule in writing and issues a tailored records request. The same intake process serves the practice's [nationwide docket](/locations/nationwide/), and [rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) work follows it as well, so a North Platte referral moves on a familiar and documented track from the outset.
