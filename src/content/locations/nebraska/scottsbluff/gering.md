## Gering's position in the Scottsbluff area labor market

Earning-capacity analysis for a Gering evaluee cannot borrow assumptions from a large urban market. The relevant market is the compact regional economy anchored by Scottsbluff as the principal city, together with the work available in the surrounding county and the practical limits of commuting from a smaller community. Vocational findings rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the applicable Nebraska areas and for Nebraska statewide, state workforce agency labor market information, and direct labor market research where published estimates run thin for a market of this size.
