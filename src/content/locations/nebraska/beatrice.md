## No Storefront, Full Availability

The firm keeps no staffed office in Beatrice and publishes no street address for this location. That is stated plainly because honesty about presence is part of the same discipline that governs the reports. What counsel in Gage County receives instead is a remote-first engagement: secure video interviews with evaluees, encrypted handling of medical and employment records, and attorney conferences scheduled around the litigation calendar. When an in-person evaluation is warranted, or when a deposition or trial date requires the expert in the courtroom, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office and confirmed with counsel well ahead of the appearance. Distance changes the logistics; it does not change the depth of the file review or the availability of the expert.

## Grounding Opinions in the Local Labor Market

An earning capacity opinion for a Beatrice area evaluee has to reflect the employment realities of the market that evaluee will actually re-enter. Vocational findings therefore rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates, drawing on the OEWS program's coverage of Nebraska statewide and of the state's nonmetropolitan regions, together with state workforce agency labor market information. Direct labor market research supplements the published series where the referral questions warrant it, and the report discloses the method: which sources were consulted, what they showed, and where the data required qualitative judgment. Transferable skills analysis and placeability findings are documented step by step, so cross-examination retraces the reasoning rather than uncovering gaps in it.

## Submitting a Matter

Counsel with a matter in Gage County can [submit the caption or party names through the conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and note any imminent disclosure, deposition, or trial dates. After conflicts clear, the firm confirms scope, fees, and schedule in writing and issues its records request, the same sequence that governs the [nationwide practice](/locations/nationwide/). Early referral gives the analysis room to develop before deadlines compress it, but late-arriving matters are evaluated candidly at intake: if the calendar allows sound work, the engagement proceeds, and if it does not, counsel is told so directly.
