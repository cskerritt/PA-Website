## What the Firm Does for Lexington Cases

The work itself is the best introduction. For matters arising in the Lexington micropolitan area, the firm prepares [vocational expert witness](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) opinions on employability, transferable skills, and earning capacity; [life care plans](/services/life-care-planning/) that itemize future care needs with sourced costs; and [medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/) for cases that need future-care pricing without a full plan. ## Appointment-Based Availability in Lexington

Candor about logistics is part of the practice. The firm maintains no staffed office in Lexington and lists no street address for this location, because it has none to list. Engagements run remote-first: evaluee interviews proceed by secure video or telephone, records move through encrypted channels, and conferences with counsel are scheduled around litigation deadlines rather than office hours. When the referral questions call for in-person evaluation, or when a deposition or trial in Dawson County or Gosper County requires presence, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office and confirmed with counsel in advance. The structure keeps costs proportionate to the case without thinning the analysis.

## Labor Market Foundations

Opinions about what an evaluee in the Lexington area can earn must rest on data for the market that evaluee actually faces. Vocational findings therefore draw on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates, through the OEWS program's coverage of Nebraska statewide and the state's nonmetropolitan regions, alongside state workforce agency labor market information. Where the published series do not reach a specific occupation or setting, direct labor market research fills the gap, and the report identifies what was done and what was found. The treatment stays qualitative where the data warrant caution; no figure appears in a report without a source that opposing counsel can pull and check.

## Starting with a Conflict Check

The path into an engagement is deliberately uniform, in Lexington as across the [national practice](/locations/nationwide/). Counsel [request a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) with the case caption or party names and flag any imminent disclosure, deposition, or trial dates. When conflicts are cleared, the firm confirms the scope, the fee structure, and the delivery schedule in writing, then issues a records request tailored to the claims. That documented sequence, repeated the same way in every matter, is what allows a report prepared for a Dawson County courtroom to hold up when the other side reads it closely.
