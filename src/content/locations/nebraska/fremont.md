## Dodge County venues, state and federal

The Fremont micropolitan area is coextensive with Dodge County, which keeps
the venue picture simple: the firm accepts matters in the District Court of
Dodge County and in the United States District Court for the District of
Nebraska. Simplicity of venue does not relax the protocol. Every engagement
opens with a documented conflict check, a defined scope, and a records
request tailored to the claims, so that the written opinion rests on a
record either side of the caption can examine, in whichever forum the case
proceeds. Counsel whose dockets reach beyond Dodge County will find the
firm's statewide court coverage on the
[Nebraska overview](/locations/nebraska/).

## Honest logistics for Fremont counsel

There is no staffed Purinton Analytics office in Fremont, and the firm
publishes no street address for this location. Accuracy about presence is
part of the same discipline that governs the opinions, and it costs counsel
nothing in practice. Records are exchanged and reviewed through secure
channels; vocational interviews are conducted by secure video, or in person
in the evaluee's own community when the referral questions call for
face-to-face evaluation, so an injured evaluee is never asked to travel for
the expert's convenience. Conferences with counsel are held by appointment
around the litigation calendar, and when deposition or trial dates require
the expert's presence, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office
with lead time matched to counsel's deadlines.

## Assessing employability in the Fremont labor market

Vocational conclusions for Fremont area evaluees rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the area and for
Nebraska statewide, on state workforce agency labor market information, and
on direct labor market research when a placement, mitigation, or
transferable-skills question turns on what area employers actually hire
for. The analysis asks what work exists for this evaluee, with these
restrictions, in this market, and the report cites every source so that
opposing counsel can follow each step from record to conclusion. The same
sourcing standard supports the vocational foundation of any life care plan
prepared for an evaluee in the area.
