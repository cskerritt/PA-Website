Every Norfolk engagement begins the same way engagements begin everywhere
else in the practice: with a documented conflict check, a defined scope,
and a records request tailored to the claims. Purinton Analytics extends
that discipline to plaintiff and defense counsel throughout the Norfolk
micropolitan area, offering
[earning capacity and employability evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/)
in injury and employment matters,
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) in catastrophic injury
cases, and [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## The Norfolk area venue footprint

The micropolitan area covers Madison County, Pierce County, and Stanton
County, and the firm accepts matters venued in the District Courts of all
of them, along with matters in the United States District Court for the
District of Nebraska. The forum never changes the method. Whether an
opinion is headed for a county courtroom or federal court, it is built on
the same record-first foundation, written so that either side of the
caption can trace each conclusion to its source. Counsel whose matters
arise elsewhere in the state can consult the
[Nebraska overview](/locations/nebraska/) for the firm's statewide court
coverage.

## Appointments, not an office

The firm has no staffed office in Norfolk and publishes no street address
for this location. That candor is deliberate: a practice that will not
overstate its own presence is less likely to overstate an evaluee's
employability or a care plan's necessity. Norfolk area work is handled by
appointment. Records move through secure channels and are reviewed
remotely. Vocational interviews are conducted by secure video, or in person
in the evaluee's own community when the referral questions require
face-to-face evaluation. Conferences with counsel are scheduled around the
litigation calendar, and when deposition or trial dates require the
expert's presence, travel is arranged from the Kansas City home office with
the lead time counsel's deadlines demand.

## Reading the Norfolk area labor market

An employability or mitigation opinion for a Norfolk area evaluee has to
describe the labor market the evaluee actually lives in. Vocational
findings rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and
wage estimates for the area and for Nebraska statewide, on state workforce
agency labor market information, and on direct labor market research when a
question turns on regional hiring practice. Sources are cited in the report
itself, findings are framed no more broadly than the data support, and the
same standards govern the vocational foundation of a life care plan as
govern a stand-alone earning capacity opinion. The point is not volume of
citation but traceability: opposing counsel should be able to check every
step.

## Starting a Norfolk engagement

Norfolk matters are accepted within the same structure the firm applies
across its [nationwide practice](/locations/nationwide/), which keeps
intake, disclosure, and scheduling uniform from venue to venue. To begin,
submit the caption or party names through the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and note any imminent disclosure,
deposition, or trial dates. Once the conflict check clears, the firm
confirms scope and fee structure and identifies the records needed to open
the file, so that a matter in Madison County, Pierce County, or Stanton
County starts on the same defined footing as any case in the practice.
