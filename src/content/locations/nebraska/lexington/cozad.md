## Cozad in the Lexington Area Labor Market

A residual earning capacity opinion for a Cozad evaluee has to reflect the work that actually exists within a reasonable commuting range of a small Dawson County community, not the employment landscape of a major city. Vocational findings rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Lexington, NE area and for Nebraska statewide, on state workforce agency labor market information, and on direct labor market research where the published estimates are too coarse for a rural market. Transferable skills analysis considers the occupations realistically available to the evaluee, and the report says plainly when a finding depends on relocation or retraining assumptions rather than burying that judgment in a table.
