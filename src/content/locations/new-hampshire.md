The measure of an expert report is whether it holds up for the reader who
wants it to fail. Purinton Analytics writes for that reader in every New
Hampshire matter it accepts, which is why the practice draws referrals from
plaintiff and defense counsel alike. The work spans
[earning capacity and employability evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/)
in injury and employment litigation,
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) in catastrophic injury
cases, and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## New Hampshire venues

Engagements are accepted for matters in the Superior Court across New
Hampshire and in the United States District Court for the District of New
Hampshire. Community pages describe the practice's work in
Manchester and Nashua,
Lebanon and Claremont,
Concord,
Keene, and
Laconia. A New Hampshire matter venued
elsewhere is served on the same terms; where the case sits determines
scheduling, not the standard applied to the analysis.

## How the practice reaches New Hampshire

New Hampshire meetings are by appointment. The firm has no staffed office in
the state and publishes no New Hampshire street address, and it states that
plainly because a practice built on documented accuracy cannot afford
imprecision about its own footprint. Engagements generally begin remotely:
records analysis, structured vocational interviews by video, and
standardized testing under publisher protocols where remote administration
is supported. When the referral questions require evaluation in the
evaluee's own community, or when deposition and trial calendars require the
expert's presence, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office and
coordinated with counsel around the court's dates. Counsel receive the same
report format, the same file documentation, and the same point of contact
whether the work is performed remotely or on site.

Vocational findings for New Hampshire evaluees rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for New Hampshire
statewide and for its metropolitan areas, on state workforce agency labor
market information, and on direct labor market research where the referral
questions warrant it. Life care plans and future medical cost projections
are priced to the evaluee's own market, because the cost of care in a New
Hampshire community is established by inquiry, not assumed from national
tables.

## Qualification and disclosure

Credentials in vocational evaluation and life care planning are issued by
national certifying bodies, and admissibility of expert opinion in a New
Hampshire courtroom is decided by the forum under its own rules of evidence.
Any venue-specific registration or qualification requirement is identified
and confirmed during the conflict check and intake process, before
substantive work begins, so the engagement proceeds without a qualification
question hanging over the disclosure schedule.

## Beginning a New Hampshire engagement

Start by submitting the caption or party names through the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/), and identify any disclosure,
deposition, or trial dates that should shape the work plan. Once conflicts
clear, counsel receive a defined scope, a fee schedule, and a records
request tailored to the claims. For related matters in other states, the
firm's [national coverage](/locations/nationwide/) page describes the same
intake and report standard applied nationwide.
