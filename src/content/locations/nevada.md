A Nevada case can put the same damages issues before a jury in Las Vegas, in
Reno, or in a rural district courtroom, and the expert analysis should not
vary with the venue. Purinton Analytics serves plaintiff and defense counsel
throughout Nevada under a uniform methodology and report standard, providing
[earning capacity and employability evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) in catastrophic injury
cases, and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. Because the analysis is built to be examined by the
opposing side, it serves plaintiff and defense referrals equally.

## Serving Nevada from a national practice

Meetings in Nevada are by appointment. The firm keeps no staffed office
anywhere in the state and publishes no Nevada street address, a fact stated
here directly because honesty about presence is part of the discipline that
opposing counsel will test in the report itself. Most engagements proceed
remotely from the outset: records analysis, structured vocational
interviews by video, and standardized testing under publisher protocols
where remote administration is supported. When the referral questions
require an in-person evaluation, or when deposition and trial dates require
the expert in the room, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office
and confirmed against the court's calendar at the start of the engagement
rather than at the end.

Vocational findings for Nevada evaluees rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates for Nevada statewide and for its
metropolitan areas, on state workforce agency labor market information, and
on direct labor market research where the referral questions call for it.
Life care plans and future medical cost projections are priced to the
evaluee's own market, since care costs in Nevada's communities are a matter
for evidence rather than assumption.

## Qualification for Nevada testimony

Professional credentials in vocational evaluation and life care planning are
issued by national certifying bodies, and each Nevada forum applies its own
rules of evidence when it decides what expert opinion the finder of fact
will hear. Any venue-specific registration or qualification requirement is
identified and confirmed during the conflict check and intake process, so
the question is resolved before it can complicate a disclosure deadline.
