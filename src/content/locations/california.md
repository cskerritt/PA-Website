An earning capacity opinion in California has to be local to the venue that
will decide the case and local to the labor market where the evaluee actually
lives and works. Purinton Analytics builds both kinds of locality
into every California engagement, preparing vocational expert and life care
planning opinions for plaintiff and defense counsel with findings grounded in
the evaluee's own metropolitan area and written to withstand review by the
other side of the caption.

## California venues served

Engagements are accepted for matters in the Superior Courts that serve
California's counties and in each of the state's federal districts: the
United States District Court for the Northern District of California, the
United States District Court for the Eastern District of California, the
United States District Court for the Central District of California, and the
United States District Court for the Southern District of California.
Workers' compensation and administrative matters proceed under the same
engagement structure as civil litigation.

## How the firm serves California

The firm keeps no staffed office in California and publishes no street
address for this location; meetings are by appointment, and candor about
presence is part of the same discipline that governs the reports. Most
California engagements begin remotely, because records review, clinical
interviews, and many standardized vocational instruments can be administered
by secure video under publisher protocols. When the referral questions call
for in-person evaluation, or when deposition and trial dates require
presence, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office, and the
written report identifies how each component of the evaluation was conducted.

Because California's metropolitan areas are distinct labor markets, the data
is chosen to match the evaluee, not the state as a whole. Vocational findings
for California evaluees rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational
employment and wage estimates for the relevant metropolitan area and for
California statewide, on state workforce agency labor market information, and
on direct labor market research where warranted. Where economic damages
testimony is required, the firm offers
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. The [nationwide coverage page](/locations/nationwide/)
sets out the national practice in full, from evaluation protocols to
deposition and trial availability.

## Qualification and disclosure

Admissibility of expert testimony is governed by the forum's rules of
evidence, and disclosure obligations differ between California's state and
federal courts, so each engagement is scoped to its forum at intake. Expert
disclosure deadlines, deposition dates, and trial settings are confirmed
before work begins and tracked through delivery. Any venue-specific
registration or qualification requirement is identified and confirmed during
the conflict check and intake process.

## Starting a California engagement

To begin a California matter, [request a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) with
the case caption and parties, and note any imminent disclosure, deposition,
or trial dates. The firm responds with availability, CVs, and fee
information; a records checklist tailored to the claims follows once the
conflict check clears.
