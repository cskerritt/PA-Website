Every report Purinton Analytics writes for a Bennington matter is drafted
on the assumption that opposing counsel will read it closely, and that
assumption shapes the work more than any local consideration does. The
practice serves plaintiff and defense counsel in the Bennington area with
[vocational evaluation and expert testimony](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/),
[medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/), and
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) of opposing
expert opinions, applying the same methodology in a Bennington County
courtroom that it applies anywhere in the country.

## The venues this page covers

Engagements are accepted for matters pending in the Superior Court in
Bennington County and in the United States District Court for the District
of Vermont. Forum does not alter the sequence: a documented conflict check,
a defined scope, and a records request tailored to the claims come before
any opinion is formed, so that the written conclusions rest on a record
either side of the caption can examine. The [Vermont page](/locations/vermont/)
describes the firm's coverage of the state's other courts, and matters
venued outside Vermont proceed through the
[nationwide practice](/locations/nationwide/) on identical terms.

## No local office, and why that is stated here

The firm's home office is in Kansas City, and it maintains no staffed
office in Bennington. No street address is published for this location, no
storefront exists, and the practice does not imply otherwise, because a
firm that would stretch the truth about its offices invites the same
question about its opinions. Bennington meetings are by appointment. Most
of the engagement runs remotely: secure videoconference for vocational
interviews and counsel conferences, electronic records exchange, and
report delivery timed to disclosure deadlines. When the referral questions
call for in-person evaluation, or when a deposition or trial appearance is
set in Bennington County, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home
office and coordinated with counsel far enough ahead to protect the
court's calendar.

## Labor market data behind the opinions

Vocational findings for Bennington-area evaluees rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the relevant
area and for Vermont statewide, together with state workforce agency labor
market information and direct labor market research where the referral
warrants it. Conclusions about employability and earning capacity are tied
to published occupational data for the labor market the evaluee can
actually reach, never to impressions of the local economy, and the report
cites its sources so the analysis can be traced and challenged. When
economic damages are part of the referral, the firm coordinates
[damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) in which
independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis while
the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement
structure.

## Starting a Bennington County engagement

The intake path is deliberately unglamorous: conflicts before anything
else. Counsel
should [request a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) with the case caption and
party names, noting any imminent disclosure, deposition, or trial dates
that bear on scheduling. Once conflicts clear, the firm issues a defined
scope, a fee agreement, and a records request tailored to the claims, and
the engagement proceeds with the same discipline described throughout this
page, from initial records review to testimony.
