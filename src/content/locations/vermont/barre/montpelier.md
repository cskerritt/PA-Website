## Washington County Venues

Montpelier sits in Washington County, and matters arising there are
ordinarily litigated in the Superior Court. Federal claims involving a
Montpelier party may proceed in the United States District Court for the
District of Vermont, with the controlling venue for any particular case
confirmed during the conflict check and intake. Reports are prepared to
the same standard for either forum and for either side of the caption:
methodology stated, sources cited, and conclusions limited to what the
record supports, so the opinion holds its shape under cross-examination.
Where a referral also requires economic damages, the firm offers
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Evaluating a Montpelier Evaluee

The firm keeps no staffed office in Montpelier and publishes no local
street address, because accuracy about presence is part of the same
discipline that governs its reports. What counsel gets instead is a
delivery model that does not depend on proximity. Records review,
counsel conferences, and most interviews proceed remotely on a secure
platform, which suits evaluees for whom travel is itself a limitation.
When the referral questions call for in-person evaluation, the assessment
is scheduled in the evaluee's own community, in appropriate professional
space arranged for the appointment. Deposition and trial appearances are
planned the same way: travel is arranged from the Kansas City home
office and coordinated against the case calendar, with remote testimony
available where the court and the parties permit it. Any venue-specific
registration or qualification requirement is identified and confirmed
during the conflict check and intake process.
