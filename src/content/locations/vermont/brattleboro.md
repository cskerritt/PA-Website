For an evaluee who lives in or near Brattleboro, the practical question is
often logistical: how does a vocational evaluation or life care planning
assessment actually happen without a local expert office? Purinton
Analytics answers that question by design. The practice is remote-first,
and it serves plaintiff and defense counsel in the Brattleboro area with
[vocational evaluation and expert testimony](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) of opposing
expert work, with interviews conducted by secure videoconference or, where
warranted, in the evaluee's own community.

## Windham County and federal venues

Engagements are accepted for matters pending in the Superior Court in
Windham County and in the United States District Court for the District of
Vermont. Whether the matter is a state-court injury claim or a federal
employment or disability dispute, the file is built the same way: a
documented conflict check, a defined scope, and a records request tailored
to the claims, so that the written opinion rests on a record both sides can
test. Counsel with matters elsewhere in the state can consult the
[Vermont page](/locations/vermont/) for statewide coverage, and referrals
outside Vermont are handled through the
[nationwide practice](/locations/nationwide/).

## What by-appointment service means in Brattleboro

The firm's only home office is in Kansas City. It keeps no staffed office
in Brattleboro, publishes no street address for this location, and does not
describe itself as local, because candor about presence belongs in the same
category as candor about data. Counsel get something more useful than a
storefront: records exchange and conferences that run electronically,
vocational interviews scheduled around the evaluee's circumstances rather
than an office's hours, and written work product delivered against
disclosure deadlines. When the referral questions call for in-person
evaluation, or when deposition and trial dates require presence in Windham
County, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office and confirmed
with counsel in advance, so the litigation calendar is never hostage to
geography.

## Reading the labor market around Brattleboro

Vocational opinions for Brattleboro-area evaluees are grounded in Bureau of
Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the
relevant area and for Vermont statewide, supplemented by state workforce
agency labor market information and direct labor market research where the
referral warrants it. The analysis asks what work exists in the labor
market the evaluee can realistically access, what it pays according to
published estimates, and how the evaluee's residual capacity matches those
occupations, and it cites every source so the path from data to conclusion
stays visible. When future care is at issue, the firm coordinates
[damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) in which
independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis while
the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement
structure.

## Referring a Brattleboro matter

Conflicts are cleared before anything else in every engagement. Submit the
caption or party names
through the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and note any imminent
disclosure, deposition, or trial dates so scheduling can be resolved at the
outset. After conflicts clear, counsel receive a defined scope, a fee
agreement, and a records request tailored to the claims, and the matter
moves forward on the same terms that govern the firm's work in every other
venue.
