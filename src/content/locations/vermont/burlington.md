Counsel litigating injury, disability, and employment matters in the
Burlington-South Burlington metropolitan area retain Purinton Analytics for
[vocational evaluation and expert testimony](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. The practice accepts referrals from plaintiff and
defense counsel alike, and every report is written to withstand the reading
it will get from the other side of the caption.

## Venue footprint in the Burlington metro

Engagements are accepted for matters pending in the Superior Court in
Chittenden County, Franklin County, and Grand Isle County, and in the
United States District Court for the District of Vermont. The court does not
change the method: the same documented conflict check, defined scope, and
records request precede every opinion, whether the matter is a personal
injury claim in state court or an employment dispute in the federal
district. Counsel with matters elsewhere in the state can review the
firm's statewide coverage on the [Vermont page](/locations/vermont/), and
matters beyond Vermont are handled through the
[nationwide practice](/locations/nationwide/).

## Meetings by appointment, not a local storefront

Purinton Analytics maintains its home office in Kansas City and keeps no
staffed office in Burlington, South Burlington, or anywhere else in the
metro. No street address is published for this location, because accuracy
about presence is part of the same discipline that governs the reports
themselves. Most engagement work proceeds remotely: records review,
counsel conferences, and vocational interviews are conducted by secure
videoconference, and many evaluations are completed without any travel at
all. When an in-person evaluation is warranted, or when deposition and
trial dates require presence, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City
home office and planned around the court's calendar. Counsel receive a
clear account of logistics and timing at intake rather than an implied
local presence that does not exist.

## Labor market research for Burlington-area evaluees

Vocational opinions for evaluees in the metro rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the
Burlington-South Burlington metropolitan area and for Vermont statewide,
supplemented by state workforce agency labor market information and direct
labor market research where the referral questions warrant it. Earning
capacity, employability, and placeability findings are tied to the labor
market the evaluee actually faces, and every source is cited in the report
so that opposing counsel can trace each conclusion to its data. Where a
[medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/) or full life
care plan is part of the referral, future care items are sourced and priced
with the same documentation standard.

## Starting a Burlington engagement

Every matter begins with a conflict check. Submit the caption or
party names through the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and note any
imminent disclosure, deposition, or trial dates so scheduling can be
addressed at the outset. Once conflicts clear, counsel receive a defined
scope, a fee agreement, and a records request tailored to the claims, and
the engagement proceeds on the same terms whether it is a new matter in
Chittenden County or part of a longstanding relationship with a firm
across the country.
