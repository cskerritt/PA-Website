## Franklin County venue and the federal alternative

The Superior Court sitting in Franklin County is the ordinary destination
for a St. Albans claim brought under state law. For a matter that belongs
in federal court, Vermont litigation is conducted in the United States
District Court for the District of Vermont. The firm presumes neither; the
operative venue is confirmed during the conflict check and intake process,
and because that confirmation fixes the disclosure schedule the report must
satisfy, it happens before scope is set rather than after. Nothing in that
sequence depends on which side sends the referral; the same order governs a
plaintiff retention and a defense retention alike. Counsel with matters
elsewhere in the Burlington region will find the
[Burlington metro page](/locations/vermont/burlington/) relevant, and
statewide coverage is described on the
[Vermont page](/locations/vermont/).

## Labor market evidence for a Franklin County work history

A St. Albans evaluee may hold a work history rooted in the local economy,
employment elsewhere in the wider Burlington region, or some mix of the
two, and the vocational analysis has to honor whichever pattern the record
shows.
Opinions rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and
wage estimates for the Burlington area and for Vermont statewide, joined
with state workforce agency labor market information and direct labor
market research where the referral questions warrant it. The report states
its occupational conclusions qualitatively and shows its reasoning, because
it is written for the reader most motivated to find its flaws. When
quantified damages are required, counsel may add a
[coordinated damages engagement](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Appointments and travel

Purinton Analytics keeps no office in St. Albans, publishes no street
address for this location, and will not imply a local presence it does not
have; that honesty is of a piece with how the reports are written. The
bulk of the work, records review, conferences with counsel, and report
drafting, is performed remotely. Should the referral questions require an
in-person vocational or life care planning evaluation, the appointment is
set near the evaluee's residence, in surroundings suited to a confidential
evaluation, and appearances for deposition or trial are met through travel
planned from the Kansas City home office. Scheduling constraints are
surfaced at intake, not discovered at the deadline.
