South Burlington is not a bedroom community in the ordinary sense. It shares
the metro's name with Burlington itself, and its residents divide their
working lives between employers inside the city and employers across the
municipal line, so the labor market that matters in litigation is the
commuting market the evaluee actually participates in rather than either
city alone. Purinton Analytics provides
[vocational expert](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) and
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) services to plaintiff
and defense counsel in South Burlington matters, applying the same
documented methodology described on the firm's
[nationwide page](/locations/nationwide/).

## Chittenden County venue, state and federal

South Burlington lies in Chittenden County, and injury, disability, and
employment claims arising there are generally heard in the Superior Court
sitting in Chittenden County. On the federal side, the United States
District Court for the District of Vermont is the forum the state offers;
which court controls a particular caption is a question for counsel, and the
controlling venue is confirmed during the conflict check and intake process.
Because expert disclosure practice differs between state and federal
proceedings, the applicable schedule is established at intake and every
report is drafted against it. Coverage of the wider metro appears on the
[Burlington metro page](/locations/vermont/burlington/), and statewide work
is described on the [Vermont page](/locations/vermont/).

## Measuring the commuting market, not the town line

Vocational findings for South Burlington evaluees rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Burlington
area and for Vermont statewide, supplemented by state workforce agency labor
market information and direct labor market research where the referral
questions call for it. Transferable skills are mapped to occupations
realistically available within the evaluee's demonstrated commuting range,
and the written opinion explains why each alternative is or is not a genuine
option, in terms opposing counsel can test. When the damages presentation
requires economic quantification, counsel may add a
[coordinated damages engagement](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## No local office, and candor about that

The firm maintains its home office in Kansas City and keeps no staffed
office in South Burlington; no street address is published for this
location, because accuracy about presence is part of the same discipline
that governs the reports themselves. Records review, counsel conferences,
and draft discussions proceed remotely as a rule. When the referral
questions warrant an in-person vocational or life care planning evaluation,
it is scheduled in the evaluee's own community in appropriate professional
space, and deposition or trial appearances are met through travel planned
from the Kansas City home office. Counsel learn at intake what the calendar
can absorb before deadlines make the answer expensive.

## Starting a South Burlington engagement

Every engagement begins the same way for either side of the caption: a
documented conflict check, a defined scope, and a records request tailored
to the claims, so the written opinion rests on a record both sides can
examine. Submit the caption or party names through the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/), summarize the referral questions, and
note any disclosure, deposition, or trial dates already on the schedule. The
firm responds with a scope proposal and fee terms, and the resulting opinion
follows the record wherever it leads.
