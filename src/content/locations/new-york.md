New York's trial court of general jurisdiction carries a name that confuses
outsiders and no one who practices there: the Supreme Court. Purinton
Analytics accepts vocational expert and life care planning engagements for
Supreme Court matters across the state and for federal matters in the
United States District Court for the Northern District of New York, the
United States District Court for the Southern District of New York, the
United States District Court for the Eastern District of New York, and the
United States District Court for the Western District of New York. The work
is available to counsel on either side of the caption, and every opinion is
written on the assumption that opposing counsel will read it closely.

## How the firm serves New York

The firm maintains no staffed office in New York and publishes no street
address there; meetings are by appointment, and plain honesty about
presence is part of the product. Engagements typically begin remotely with
records review, structured interviews, and standardized testing under
publisher protocols where remote administration is supported. When
in-person evaluation is warranted, or when deposition and trial dates
require presence, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office and
coordinated with counsel's calendar. Vocational findings for New York
evaluees rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and
wage estimates for New York statewide and for the relevant metropolitan
areas, state workforce agency labor market information, and direct labor
market research where warranted. Life care plans for New York residents are
priced to the evaluee's own market rather than to a national average, and
coordinated damages engagements pair that work with independent economist
partners who perform and sign the economic analysis under one engagement
structure. Counsel receive one report standard and one point of contact
regardless of where in the state the work is performed.

## Qualification and disclosure in New York matters

Certifications held within the practice are issued by national
credentialing bodies rather than by any single state. Qualification to
testify in a New York matter is determined by the forum under its own rules
of evidence, whether the case sits in Supreme Court or in one of the
federal districts. Any venue-specific registration or qualification
requirement is identified and confirmed during the conflict check and
intake process, and disclosure materials are prepared in the form the venue
requires. Prior testimony lists and fee schedules are supplied so that both
sides of the caption can evaluate the expert on a complete record.

## Starting a New York engagement

The first step is always the same: a documented conflict check and a
defined scope. To begin, [request a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) with
the case caption and parties, and flag any imminent disclosure, deposition,
or trial dates. After the conflict check clears, a written scope and fee
agreement is issued before any records are reviewed. For matters that cross
state lines, the firm's
[national coverage](/locations/nationwide/) page describes how the same
report standard travels with the case.
