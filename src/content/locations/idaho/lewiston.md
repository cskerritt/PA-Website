The Lewiston metropolitan area sits astride a state line, taking in Nez
Perce County, Idaho, and Asotin County, Washington, and that geography
shapes how damages cases arise here: counsel on either side of the border
may need an expert whose methods hold up in either state's courts. Purinton
Analytics serves plaintiff and defense counsel throughout the metro with
[vocational expert witness services](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. Because the credentials behind the work are issued by
national certifying bodies rather than any single state, the analysis does
not change when the caption does.

## Venues on both sides of the state line

On the Idaho side, engagements are accepted for matters in the District
Court serving Nez Perce County and in the United States District Court for
the District of Idaho. Matters arising in Asotin County, Washington, are
evaluated under the same engagement structure and the same report standard,
with any venue-specific qualification or registration requirement identified
and confirmed during the conflict check and intake process. Counsel with
Idaho matters beyond the metro can start from the
[Idaho locations page](/locations/idaho/), which describes statewide
coverage.

## Appointment-based presence, stated plainly

Lewiston meetings are by appointment. The firm keeps no staffed office in
the metro and publishes no street address for this location, because
accuracy about presence is part of the discipline the rest of the work is
held to. Engagements typically begin remotely with records review,
structured interviews, and standardized testing under publisher protocols
where remote administration is supported. When in-person evaluation is
warranted, or when deposition and trial dates on either side of the state
line require attendance, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home
office and coordinated with counsel's calendar. The point of contact and
the report standard stay constant throughout.

## Labor market research across a border metro

Vocational findings for evaluees in the metro rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Lewiston area,
which span the state line, together with statewide data for the evaluee's
own state, state workforce agency labor market information, and direct labor
market research where warranted. A cross-border market rewards care:
opinions are anchored to where the evaluee actually lives and can reasonably
work, not to whichever state's averages are more convenient. Life care plans
and [medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/) are
priced to the evaluee's own market through provider-specific inquiries and
published fee data, and the same sources support
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) of opposing
reports.

## Starting a Lewiston metro engagement

Whichever state the matter is filed in, the path to retention is the same:
[request a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) with the case caption and
parties, and note any imminent disclosure, deposition, or trial dates. Any
venue-specific qualification requirement is identified during that intake
process. Counsel weighing the reach of the practice beyond this region can
review the firm's [nationwide coverage](/locations/nationwide/), which
explains how the same remote-first model serves courts across the country.
