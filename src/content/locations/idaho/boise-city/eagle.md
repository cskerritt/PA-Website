The evaluee's work history, not the town line, sets the frame for an Eagle
case. Many Eagle residents built their careers across the wider Boise City,
ID metropolitan area, and an earning capacity opinion for such an evaluee
has to reconstruct that pre-injury trajectory honestly before it can say
anything defensible about the post-injury market. Purinton Analytics
approaches [earning capacity and employability evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/)
for Eagle matters from the individual outward: education, licensure, actual
earnings, transferable skills, and only then the occupations realistically
open within commuting range. The same individualized discipline carries
into [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) for catastrophic
injuries and into
[forensic economic damages](/services/forensic-economic-damages/), where
independent economist partners coordinated by the firm perform and sign the
economic analysis themselves.

## Courts and venue for Eagle matters

Eagle lies in Ada County, so state law claims proceed in the District Court
serving Ada County. The United States District Court for the District of
Idaho stands as the federal forum available when a claim carries a federal
basis, and the venue controlling any particular matter is identified and
confirmed during the conflict check and intake, along with any
qualification requirement specific to that forum. Reports are prepared to
the same evidentiary standard for either courthouse and for either side of
the caption, because a document written only to persuade the retaining
party rarely survives the other side's expert.

## What the metro market means for an Eagle evaluee

Eagle is a small city inside a large metropolitan economy, which cuts both
ways in a vocational analysis. The local employment base alone understates
what an Eagle worker can reach; the metro-wide statistics alone can
overstate it if restrictions limit commuting or scheduling. The firm's
findings rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and
wage estimates for the Boise City metropolitan area and for Idaho
statewide, supplemented by state workforce agency labor market information
and by direct labor market contact where the referral questions justify it,
and the written opinion states expressly how the evaluee's restrictions
were mapped against that data.

## Meeting, evaluating, and testifying in Eagle cases

There is no staffed Purinton Analytics office in Eagle and no published
local address; the firm treats accuracy about its own presence as part of
its credibility. Engagements typically run remotely from intake through
report delivery, with interviews by secure video where appropriate. When an
in-person functional or vocational assessment serves the case, the firm
travels from its Kansas City home office and conducts the evaluation in the
evaluee's own community, and it appears for deposition and trial in Ada
County on the same by-appointment basis. The surrounding market and sibling
