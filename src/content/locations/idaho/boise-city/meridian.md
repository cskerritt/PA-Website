Defining the labor market is the decision that shapes everything downstream
in a Meridian case. Meridian is a major city in its own right within the
Boise City, ID metropolitan area, and a Meridian evaluee's realistic
employment options run in both directions: into the jobs based in Meridian
itself and outward across the commuting reach of the surrounding metro.
Purinton Analytics frames its
[earning capacity and employability evaluations](/services/vocational-expert-witness/)
for Meridian matters around that dual geography, so the transferable skills
analysis describes where this worker could actually be employed rather than
defaulting to a statewide abstraction. Counsel for plaintiffs and defendants
alike also retain the firm for
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) in catastrophic injury
cases and for
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Ada County venue and the courts that hear Meridian matters

Meridian civil disputes proceed in the District Court serving Ada County,
Idaho's trial court for the county. Where a claim arises under federal law
or diversity jurisdiction, the United States District Court for the District
of Idaho is the available federal forum; which court controls a particular
matter is confirmed during the conflict check and intake, together with any
venue-specific qualification requirement. The firm's work product does not
change with the forum. Each report discloses its methodology, cites its
sources, and is written so that opposing counsel can trace every conclusion
back to the record.

## Reading Meridian within the metro economy

A city of Meridian's scale generates substantial employment of its own, yet
its residents still participate in a wider regional market anchored by
Boise City. Vocational findings for Meridian evaluees therefore rest on
Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for
the Boise City metropolitan area and for Idaho statewide, supplemented by
state workforce agency labor market information and, where the referral
questions warrant it, direct employer contact and job market research. The
report explains which data describe the metro and which describe the state,
and why each source fits this evaluee's occupation and restrictions.

## How Meridian engagements are staffed

There is no Purinton Analytics office in Meridian, and the firm does not
publish a local address, because candor about presence belongs to the same
discipline that governs its opinions. Records review, interviews by secure
video, and report drafting proceed remotely for most engagements. When the
referral requires an in-person vocational assessment, or when deposition
and trial dates require testimony in Ada County, travel is scheduled from
the Kansas City home office and the evaluation is conducted in the
evaluee's own community. Broader regional coverage is described on the
[Boise City metro page](/locations/idaho/boise-city/) and the
[Idaho page](/locations/idaho/).
