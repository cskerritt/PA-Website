Counsel calling about a Nampa matter usually already know where the case
will be tried: the District Court serving Canyon County for state claims,
or the United States District Court for the District of Idaho when a

confirmed during the conflict check and intake. Purinton Analytics prepares
its Nampa work with that trial setting in mind from the outset. An
[earning capacity and employability evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/)
written for a Canyon County jury has to survive cross-examination by
whichever side did not retain the firm, and the reports are drafted to that
standard whether the engagement comes from the plaintiff or the defense.

## Nampa as an employment center, not a bedroom community

Nampa is a major city within the Boise City, ID metropolitan area with a
working economy of its own, and treating it as a mere satellite of the
principal city misstates the market a Nampa evaluee actually faces.
Vocational conclusions for Nampa residents draw on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Boise City
metropolitan area and for Idaho statewide, on state workforce agency labor
market information, and on direct labor market research where the referral
questions call for it. The written opinion identifies which occupations are
realistically available to this evaluee given restrictions, transferable
skills, and the commuting range between Nampa and the rest of the metro,
and it says plainly which data source supports each step.

## Care planning and coordinated damages for Canyon County cases

Catastrophic injury matters in Canyon County frequently need more than a
vocational opinion. The firm prepares
[life care plans](/services/life-care-planning/) grounded in the treating
record and priced from cited sources, and offers
[medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/) where the
question is narrower. When counsel want damages presented as an integrated
package, [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
bring in independent economist partners who perform and sign the economic
analysis themselves, keeping the economist's opinion independent while the
vocational and care-planning work moves on the same schedule.
