How the evaluation will actually happen is the practical question counsel
ask first about a Kuna referral, and the answer shapes the schedule. For
most Kuna engagements, Purinton Analytics runs the workup remotely: medical
and employment records arrive electronically, the vocational interview is
conducted by secure video, and the draft report moves on the litigation
calendar without anyone boarding a plane. When the referral questions call
for an in-person assessment, the firm travels from its Kansas City home
office and evaluates the evaluee in Kuna itself, in the community where
that person lives and looks for work, because observations made in the
evaluee's own setting carry more weight than observations made in a
borrowed conference room. Both plaintiff and defense counsel use this model
for [vocational expert evaluations](/services/vocational-expert-witness/)
and [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) alike.

## Kuna's county and courts

Kuna is an Ada County community, and civil matters arising there are heard
in the District Court serving Ada County. For claims with a federal
dimension, the United States District Court for the District of Idaho is
the available federal forum in the state; the venue that governs a
particular case is identified and confirmed during the conflict check and
intake process, together with any venue-specific qualification
requirement. Nothing about the firm's written work changes between forums,
and nothing about it changes with the identity of the retaining party.

## Kuna's place in the metro labor market

Kuna is a small city within the Boise City, ID metropolitan area, and its
residents' realistic employment options extend across the metro's commuting
range rather than stopping at the town's edge. Vocational
conclusions for Kuna evaluees are therefore grounded in Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Boise City
metropolitan area and for Idaho statewide, in state workforce agency labor
market information, and in targeted labor market research when the referral
requires it. The report identifies which occupations remain open to the
evaluee after restrictions are applied and shows the data behind each
placement conclusion, so the opinion can be tested rather than merely
asserted.

## Damages beyond the vocational question

Where a Kuna case involves catastrophic injury, counsel often need the
vocational opinion, the care plan, and the economic loss analysis to fit
together. Through
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/),
independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis while
the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement
structure, which keeps each expert's opinion independently defensible. The
firm also accepts narrower assignments, including
[medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/) when the
dispute is confined to future care pricing.

The firm keeps no office in Kuna and publishes no address there; meetings
are by appointment, and honesty about that is part of the product. To
begin, submit the caption or party names through the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and note any imminent disclosure,
deposition, or trial dates. Engagements across the surrounding cities are
described on the [Boise City metro page](/locations/idaho/boise-city/),
Idaho-wide coverage on the [Idaho page](/locations/idaho/), and the
