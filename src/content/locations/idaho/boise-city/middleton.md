A vocational report earns its keep in a Middleton case by what it shows,
not what it claims. Every Purinton Analytics opinion written for a
Middleton matter follows the same documented arc: a conflict check before
any substantive discussion, a defined scope in writing, a records request
tailored to the claims, an interview and testing protocol suited to the
evaluee, and a report that lays out its methodology and sources so that
opposing counsel can follow every step. That construction is deliberate.
Whether the engagement is an
[earning capacity evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) for the
plaintiff or an employability analysis for the defense, the document is
built to be examined, and it reads the same way no matter who paid for it.

## Canyon County venue and the federal alternative

Middleton is a Canyon County community, and state law claims arising there
proceed in the District Court serving Canyon County. Where federal
jurisdiction exists, the United States District Court for the District of
Idaho is the forum available to Idaho litigants; the controlling venue for
any specific matter, and any expert qualification requirement that venue
imposes, is identified and confirmed during the conflict check and intake
process. The report's structure does not bend to the forum, only its
caption does.

## Situating a Middleton evaluee in the regional market

Middleton is a small town within the Boise City, ID metropolitan area, and
its residents typically work across a commuting territory considerably
wider than the town itself. The firm's vocational findings for Middleton
evaluees rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and
wage estimates for the Boise City metropolitan area and for Idaho
statewide, joined with state workforce agency labor market information and
direct labor market research when the referral questions warrant that
depth. The written opinion specifies which conclusions the metro data
support, which require the statewide series, and how the evaluee's
restrictions narrow the occupations either can justify.

## Life care planning documented to the same standard

In catastrophic injury litigation, the firm's
[life care plans](/services/life-care-planning/) apply the discipline
described above to future care: each item in the plan traces to the
treating record or to a cited clinical source, and pricing traces to
identified data. When counsel want economic loss quantified alongside the
plan, [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
place that analysis with independent economist partners who perform and
sign the economic opinion themselves, preserving the independence of each
expert while the work shares an engagement structure.
