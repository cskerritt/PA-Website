A Garden City referral can arrive in either posture: as a request for an
original opinion, or as a request to test one already served by the other
side. Purinton Analytics accepts Garden City engagements from plaintiff
and defense counsel in each: affirmative
[vocational expert evaluations](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) that
build an earning capacity opinion from the record up, and
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) assignments
that examine an opposing expert's data selection, methodology, and
reasoning against the standards the field itself publishes. Because the
firm works both sides of the caption, its critiques and its affirmative
opinions are written to the identical standard, which is precisely what
makes each credible.

## Garden City's courts and county

Garden City is an Ada County municipality within the Boise City, ID
metropolitan area. Its civil matters are tried in the District Court
serving Ada County, and the United States District Court for the District
of Idaho is the federal forum available for claims that arise under
federal jurisdiction. Which venue controls, and whether that venue imposes
any registration or qualification requirement on a testifying expert, is
identified and confirmed during the conflict check and intake process
rather than assumed.

## The labor market questions a Garden City case raises

Garden City is a small city situated within a large metropolitan economy,
so the analytical question is rarely whether jobs exist somewhere in the
region; it is which of them this evaluee can actually obtain and hold.
The firm grounds Garden City findings in Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates for the Boise City metropolitan
area and for Idaho statewide, in state workforce agency labor market
information, and in direct labor market inquiry where the referral calls
for it. In rebuttal work the same sources become the measuring stick: an
opposing opinion that skipped them, cherry-picked among them, or applied
them to the wrong geography gets documented, not merely disparaged. A
rebuttal that cannot show its own work is no better than the report it
attacks, so the firm's critiques carry the same citations its affirmative
opinions do.

## Care planning and the coordinated damages option

For catastrophic injuries, the firm prepares
[life care plans](/services/life-care-planning/) tied to the treating
record, and through
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
brings in independent economist partners who perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. Counsel who need only a targeted piece, such as a
review of an opposing life care plan, can scope the engagement that
narrowly.
