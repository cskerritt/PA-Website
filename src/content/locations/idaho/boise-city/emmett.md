Gem County is where an Emmett case starts, and the county line matters more
here than it does for the metro's inner suburbs. Emmett belongs to the
Boise City, ID metropolitan area, but it sits in its own county, with its
own courthouse serving a community whose working life blends local
employment with commuting toward the metro core. Purinton Analytics writes
vocational opinions for Emmett matters with both facts in view: the case
will likely be tried before a Gem County jury, while the evaluee's
realistic labor market reaches beyond Gem County into the wider metro.

## The courts serving Emmett

State court matters from Emmett proceed in the District Court serving Gem
County. For claims within federal jurisdiction, the United States District
Court for the District of Idaho is the federal forum available in the
state; the venue controlling a particular case, together with any
venue-specific qualification requirement for a testifying expert, is
identified and confirmed during the conflict check and intake process. The
firm prepares the same disclosed-methodology work product for either
setting, on the premise that a report defensible before a rural jury and a
federal judge alike is the only kind worth serving.

## An outlying county in a metro economy

Emmett is a small town, and its inclusion in the Boise City, ID
metropolitan area reflects commuting reality rather than civic identity.
That distinction does analytical work: a placeability opinion for an Emmett
evaluee must weigh the jobs available locally in Gem County against those
reachable across the metro, and must account for what the evaluee's
restrictions do to that commuting calculus. Vocational findings rest on
Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for
the Boise City metropolitan area and for Idaho statewide, on state
workforce agency labor market information, and on direct labor market
research where the referral questions call for it, with the report stating
which geography each data source describes.

## Serving Emmett evaluees by appointment

The firm maintains no Emmett office and publishes no local address, and it
says so plainly because overstating presence would undercut the discipline
the reports depend on. Records review, secure video interviews, and report
drafting proceed remotely for most engagements. When an in-person
evaluation is warranted, the firm travels from its Kansas City home office
and conducts the assessment in the evaluee's own community, where the
functional observations mean the most; deposition and trial appearances in
Gem County are scheduled the same way.
