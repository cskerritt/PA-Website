Scope is the first decision in a Homedale referral. Not every case needs
every service the firm offers, so counsel calling about a Homedale matter
are asked early what the disputed question actually is: earning capacity,
employability, future care, or all of them together. Purinton Analytics
then builds the engagement to that answer. Some Homedale files call for a
complete [vocational expert evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/)
with interview, testing, and a full written opinion; some need a
[life care plan](/services/life-care-planning/) for catastrophic injury;
and some are best served by
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. A narrowly defined assignment is not a lesser one. It
keeps the opinion inside the expert's mandate, which is where plaintiff and
defense counsel alike need it to stay.

## The courts that will read a Homedale opinion

Homedale sits in Owyhee County, and state law claims arising there proceed
in the District Court serving Owyhee County. Where a claim supports federal
jurisdiction, the United States District Court for the District of Idaho is
the federal forum available to Idaho litigants. The venue that controls a
specific matter, together with any registration or qualification
requirement that venue imposes on a testifying expert, is identified and
confirmed during the conflict check and intake process. The scope decision
made at intake matters here too: a report confined to the question actually
in dispute gives the court a cleaner document and gives opposing counsel
less to object to, and the firm writes to that standard for either
courthouse and either side of the caption.

## Placing a Homedale evaluee in the labor market

Homedale is a small rural community whose inclusion in the Boise City, ID
metropolitan area connects its residents to a regional employment base far
larger than the town itself supports. Vocational findings for Homedale
evaluees rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and
wage estimates for the Boise City metropolitan area and for Idaho
statewide, on state workforce agency labor market information, and on
direct labor market research where the referral questions warrant that
depth. What the analysis must resolve is individual: which occupations
remain open to this evaluee given restrictions, transferable skills, and a
realistic commuting range between Homedale and the rest of the metro. The
written opinion ties each conclusion to the source that supports it, so the
reasoning can be examined rather than taken on faith.

## By-appointment service in Owyhee County

The firm keeps no office in Homedale and publishes no Owyhee County
address. What counsel get instead is a by-appointment practice that travels
from the Kansas City home office when the work requires presence and runs
remotely when it does not. Records move electronically, interviews proceed
by secure video where the case allows, and when the referral calls for an
in-person assessment, the evaluation takes place in the evaluee's own
community. Deposition and trial testimony in Owyhee County are scheduled
the same way, with dates confirmed against the litigation calendar counsel
provide at intake.
