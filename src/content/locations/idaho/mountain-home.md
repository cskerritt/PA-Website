## Elmore County courts

The Mountain Home micropolitan area consists of Elmore County, and the firm
accepts matters pending in the District Court serving the county and in the
United States District Court for the District of Idaho. Engagements arrive
at every stage: pre-suit consultation on whether a vocational or care claim
will hold together, retained evaluation and disclosure, rebuttal of an
opposing expert's report, and deposition or trial testimony. Because the
methodology does not change with the forum, counsel can move between state
and federal court without the analysis needing to be rebuilt, and the
opinion discloses its sources the same way in either setting.

## No local office, said in plain terms

The firm has no staffed office in Mountain Home and publishes no street
address for this location. Meetings are by appointment, and the practice
states that openly because accuracy about presence belongs to the same
discipline that produces the reports. Most work proceeds remotely: records
review, structured interviews, and standardized testing under publisher
protocols where remote administration is supported. When the referral
questions require evaluating the evaluee in person, or when deposition and
trial dates require appearance, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City
home office. What counsel in Elmore County receive is what counsel receive
everywhere the firm works: the same report standard and the same point of
contact from intake through testimony.

## Labor market research for Elmore County evaluees

Vocational findings for Mountain Home evaluees are grounded in Bureau of
Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for Idaho
statewide and for the area that includes Elmore County, in state workforce
agency labor market information, and in direct employer contact where the
referral questions warrant it. Life care plans and
[medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/) price each
recommended service to the evaluee's own community using provider-specific
inquiries and published fee data, and where local availability is limited,
the plan documents the realistic source of care rather than assuming
services exist nearby. Each figure in the report traces to a stated source
that opposing counsel can check.
