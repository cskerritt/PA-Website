For a case that begins in Jerome, the caption tends to read Jerome County
before it reads anything else. The town gives the Twin Falls metropolitan
area a venue of its own, and Purinton Analytics treats that fact as the
starting point for engagement planning rather than a footnote. The firm
provides
[vocational expert witness services](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/),
[medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/), and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. Plaintiff and defense counsel receive the same
methods, the same documentation, and the same willingness to defend either
under cross-examination.

## Jerome County and the courts that serve it

Jerome matters proceed in the District Court serving Jerome County, and
engagements are accepted there on the same terms as anywhere else in the
practice: a documented conflict check, a defined scope, and a records
request tailored to the claims. Idaho's federal forum, the United States
District Court for the District of Idaho, is available for matters that
belong there; which court controls a given case is identified during the
conflict check and intake rather than assumed from geography. State and
federal work is documented to the same standard, with methods disclosed
and sources cited so that either side of the caption can trace a
conclusion back to the record. Counsel whose matters sit elsewhere in the
metro can begin from the
[Twin Falls metro page](/locations/idaho/twin-falls/), and the
[Idaho locations page](/locations/idaho/) describes coverage across the
rest of the state.

## Jerome's place in the metro labor market

Vocational analysis for a Jerome evaluee does not stop at the county line.
Workers in the town participate in the wider Twin Falls labor market, and
a transferable-skills analysis has to reflect the employment a claimant
can actually reach, not just the employers listed under the town's own
name. Findings rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment
and wage estimates for the Twin Falls metropolitan area and for Idaho
statewide, on state workforce agency labor market information, and on
direct labor market research where the published estimates need testing
against the facts of the case. Scale is described qualitatively and method
precisely, so that the labor market section of a report reads the same way
to retaining and opposing counsel: as documentation, not advocacy.

## Evaluations without a storefront

Purinton Analytics keeps no staffed office in Jerome and publishes no
street address for the town, because accuracy about presence belongs to
the same discipline that governs the reports. Meetings are by appointment.
Records review, counsel conferences, and much of the interview work
proceed remotely, which keeps costs proportionate for a smaller venue.
When the referral questions call for in-person evaluation, the evaluation
is scheduled in the evaluee's own community rather than asking an injured
person to travel to the expert; when a deposition or trial date requires
presence, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office. The
[nationwide practice page](/locations/nationwide/) describes how the same
model serves venues of every size.

## Starting a Jerome engagement

Every engagement opens the same way: conflict check, scope, records.
Submit the caption or party names through the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/), note any disclosure, deposition, or
trial dates already on the calendar, and state the referral questions as
precisely as the pleadings allow. Intake confirms the controlling venue,
any registration or qualification requirement it carries, and the records
needed before an opinion is committed to writing.
