Kimberly's labor market is best understood through its commuters. The town
sits inside Twin Falls County, and its residents work across the Twin
Falls metropolitan area, which means a vocational opinion about a Kimberly
claimant that looks only at the town itself would miss most of the
employment actually open to them. Purinton Analytics builds its Kimberly
engagements around that reality, providing
[vocational expert witness services](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/),
[medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/), and
[forensic economic damages](/services/forensic-economic-damages/) analyses
that are performed and signed by independent economist partners the firm
coordinates, for counsel on either side of the caption.

## Venue for Kimberly matters

A Kimberly case is a Twin Falls County case, heard in the District Court
serving that county. The United States District Court for the District of
Idaho stands as the state's federal forum for matters that belong in
federal court, and the controlling venue for any particular dispute is
confirmed during the conflict check and intake rather than presumed.
Whatever the forum, the working file is built the same way: methods
disclosed, sources cited, assumptions stated, so the report can be
examined line by line by whoever sits across the aisle. The
[Twin Falls metro page](/locations/idaho/twin-falls/) covers the
county-level footprint in more detail, and the
[Idaho locations page](/locations/idaho/) maps the practice across the
state.

## The metro market a Kimberly worker can reach

Transferable-skills analysis for a Kimberly evaluee starts from the jobs
within realistic reach, which for most residents means the metro as a
whole rather than the town alone. The analysis draws on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Twin Falls
area and for the state of Idaho, supplemented by state workforce agency
labor market information and by direct labor market research when a
referral question turns on whether specific work is genuinely available.
Scale stays qualitative in the write-up; what gets stated precisely is the
method, so the conclusion survives the scrutiny it will meet in
deposition.

## How and where evaluations happen

There is no Purinton Analytics office in Kimberly, staffed or otherwise,
and the firm will not imply one by publishing an address it does not keep.
Engagement work is remote-first: records analysis, conferences with
counsel, and many structured interviews are handled without travel. Where
the referral questions require an in-person evaluation, it is conducted in
the evaluee's community, by appointment and arranged around treatment
schedules; where testimony requires presence, travel runs from the Kansas
City home office. The
[nationwide practice page](/locations/nationwide/) explains the model as
it applies to every venue the firm serves.

## The path in for counsel

Referrals begin with a documented conflict check and a defined scope. Send
the caption or party names through the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/), flag any imminent disclosure,
deposition, or trial dates, and describe the claimant's work history in
whatever detail is already available. Intake settles the controlling
venue, any registration or qualification requirement, and the records
request before any opinion work begins, so both sides of the file know
exactly what the engagement covers and what it deliberately does not.
