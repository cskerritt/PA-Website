travel to an expert's office, and counsel should not have to discount a
small-town case because the logistics look expensive. Purinton Analytics
structures its Buhl engagements so that neither happens: assessment work
comes to the evaluee, and everything that does not require presence is
handled remotely. On that footing the firm offers
[vocational expert witness services](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),

## Where a Buhl case is heard

Buhl sits in Twin Falls County, and matters arising there proceed in the
District Court serving the county. For cases with a federal character, the
United States District Court for the District of Idaho is the available
federal forum; the venue that actually controls is identified during the
conflict check and intake, never assumed. The report itself does not
change with the courthouse: the same disclosure of methods, the same cited
sources, and the same documentation standard apply whether the matter is
state or federal. Details of the county-level practice appear on the
[Twin Falls metro page](/locations/idaho/twin-falls/), and the
[Idaho locations page](/locations/idaho/) sets out how the firm serves the
rest of the state.

## The labor market beyond the town line

A vocational opinion about a Buhl evaluee has to be honest about what a
small town's economy is and is not. Employability and placeability are
assessed against the Twin Falls metropolitan labor market the evaluee can
realistically access, using Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational
employment and wage estimates covering the Twin Falls area and Idaho
statewide, state workforce agency labor market information, and direct
labor market research when the question is whether particular jobs exist
within reach of a particular claimant. The report characterizes scale in
words rather than figures and shows its method in full, because a
conclusion that cannot be traced is a conclusion that will not survive
cross-examination.

## Assessment in the evaluee's community

The firm maintains no office in Buhl and does not pretend otherwise;
meetings are by appointment, and honesty about presence is part of the
product. In practice, most of an engagement never requires anyone to
travel: records review, conferences with counsel, and structured
interviews proceed remotely. When an in-person evaluation is warranted, it
is scheduled in the evaluee's own community, at times that work around
treatment and employment. When a deposition or trial calls for the expert
in the room, travel is arranged from the Kansas City home office. The
[nationwide practice page](/locations/nationwide/) describes this model at
full scale.

## Opening a Buhl matter

The path in is deliberately plain: submit the caption or party names
through the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/), identify the referral
questions, and note any dates already fixed for disclosure, deposition, or
trial. Intake confirms the controlling venue and any registration or
qualification requirement, defines the scope in writing, and issues a
records request tailored to the claims before any opinion is drafted.
Plaintiff and defense referrals move through the identical process, and
the resulting file is built to be read by whichever side did not send it.
