Distance from major litigation centers should not decide which expert a
Bonner County case gets. Purinton Analytics serves counsel in the Sandpoint
micropolitan area on a remote-first model: the analysis comes first,
performed through records review and remote evaluation, and travel is added
only when the referral questions or the court's calendar actually require
it. The practice serves plaintiff and defense counsel with
[vocational expert witness work](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/),
[medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/), and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Bonner County venue and process

Engagements are accepted for matters in the District Court serving Bonner
County and in the United States District Court for the District of Idaho.
Every file opens the same way regardless of venue: a documented conflict
check, a defined scope, and a records request tailored to the claims, so
that the eventual written opinion rests on a record either side of the
caption can examine. Disclosure deadlines are addressed at intake, so
counsel know before retention whether the timeline can be met. The rest of
the state is covered under identical standards, as described on the
[Idaho locations page](/locations/idaho/).

## No storefront, and no pretense of one

The firm has no staffed office in Sandpoint and publishes no street address
for this location. That is stated outright because accuracy about presence
is part of the same discipline that keeps unsupported claims out of the
reports. What counsel gets instead is a practice built to work at distance:
records review, structured interviews, and standardized testing under
publisher protocols where remote administration is supported, with the
evaluee assessed in or near their own community whenever in-person work is
warranted. When deposition or trial dates require presence in Bonner County,
travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office and planned around the
court's calendar.

## Evidence about the local labor market

Vocational findings for evaluees in the area rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates and on Idaho statewide
data, along with state workforce agency labor market information and direct
labor market research where the referral questions call for it. In a
smaller market, that direct research carries real weight: published
estimates are tested against the employers and openings actually within the
evaluee's reach. Life care plans for area residents are priced to the
evaluee's own market through provider-specific inquiries and published fee
data for the region, and the same sources drive
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) when the
assignment is to evaluate an opposing expert's report rather than to write
an affirmative one.

## Engaging the firm for a Sandpoint case

Qualification to testify in an Idaho matter is determined by the forum under
its rules of evidence, and any venue-specific registration or qualification
requirement is identified and confirmed during the conflict check and intake
process. To find out whether the firm can take the case,
[request a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) with the case caption and
parties, and note any imminent disclosure, deposition, or trial dates.
Counsel who want to see how the same remote-first model performs in venues
across the country can review the firm's
[nationwide coverage](/locations/nationwide/).
