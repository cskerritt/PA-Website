A damages report is only as strong as the record beneath it, and that
premise shapes every Twin Falls engagement Purinton Analytics accepts. Work
for the metro begins with a documented conflict check, a defined scope, and
a records request tailored to the claims, so that the written opinion can
withstand the closest reading opposing counsel can give it. On that
foundation the firm provides
[vocational expert witness services](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/),
[medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/), and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure, for plaintiff and defense counsel alike.

## Venues in Twin Falls and Jerome counties

The metro's footprint covers Twin Falls County and Jerome County.
Engagements are accepted for matters in the District Courts serving those
counties and in the United States District Court for the District of Idaho.
State and federal matters are handled to an identical documentation
standard, with methods disclosed and sources cited so that either side of
the caption can trace each conclusion back to the record. Counsel with
matters in other Idaho venues can begin with the
[Idaho locations page](/locations/idaho/), which describes how the same
practice covers the rest of the state.

## Presence in the metro, described accurately

Twin Falls meetings are by appointment. The firm has no staffed office in
the metro and publishes no street address for this location; the same
discipline that keeps fabricated facts out of reports keeps implied offices
off this page. In practice, most engagements proceed remotely from the
start: records review, structured interviews, and standardized testing under
publisher protocols where remote administration is supported. When an
in-person evaluation is warranted, or when deposition and trial dates
require presence, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office and
built around the litigation calendar. Counsel work with a direct point of
contact from intake through testimony.

## Grounding opinions in the local labor market

Vocational findings for evaluees in the metro rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Twin Falls
area and for Idaho statewide, supplemented by state workforce agency labor
market information and direct labor market research where the referral
questions warrant it. Earning capacity conclusions are anchored to the
evaluee's actual market, not to national abstractions, and life care plan
pricing for area residents draws on provider-specific inquiries and
published fee data for the region. When the assignment is defensive, the
same data supports [rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/)
of opposing reports, with agreement noted where the opposing work is sound.

## Starting a Twin Falls engagement

Qualification to testify in an Idaho matter is determined by the forum under
its rules of evidence, and any venue-specific registration or qualification
requirement is identified and confirmed during the conflict check and intake
process. To put a case in front of the firm,
[request a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) with the caption or party names,
and note any imminent disclosure, deposition, or trial dates. A broader view
of the practice, including how engagements run in venues far from Idaho, is
available on the [nationwide coverage page](/locations/nationwide/).
