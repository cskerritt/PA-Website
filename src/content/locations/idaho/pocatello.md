Most Pocatello engagements begin at a desk, not at a courthouse: records
arrive, a scope is defined, and the evaluation is under way before anyone
books travel. Purinton Analytics runs a remote-first practice, which means
counsel in the Pocatello metropolitan area can retain
[vocational expert](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) and
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) services without waiting
on an expert's travel schedule to start the work. Structured interviews and
standardized testing proceed under publisher protocols where remote
administration is supported, and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
let independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis
while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement
structure. Plaintiff and defense referrals are handled with the same
methodology and the same written product.

## The Bannock County venue picture

Engagements are accepted for matters in the District Court serving Bannock
County and in the United States District Court for the District of Idaho.
Each file is built for scrutiny from either side of the caption: a
documented conflict check, a defined scope, a records request tailored to
the claims, and a written opinion whose sources can be traced and checked.
Counsel with matters venued elsewhere in the state will find the same
standards described on the [Idaho locations page](/locations/idaho/).

## What by-appointment service means here

The firm keeps no staffed office in Pocatello and publishes no street
address for this location. That candor is deliberate: accuracy about
presence is part of the same discipline that governs the reports themselves.
When the referral questions call for in-person evaluation, when an evaluee
is best assessed in person, or when deposition and trial dates require
attendance in Bannock County, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home
office and coordinated with counsel and the court. Between appearances, the
work continues remotely, and counsel keep a direct point of contact from
intake through testimony.

## Labor market data behind each opinion

Vocational findings for evaluees in the metro rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Pocatello area
and for Idaho statewide, together with state workforce agency labor market
information and direct labor market research where warranted. Employability
and earning capacity opinions are tied to the evaluee's actual market
conditions rather than to national generalizations. Life care plans and
[medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/) for area
residents are priced through provider-specific inquiries and published fee
data for the region, and when an opposing expert has already filed,
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) applies the same
data discipline to their work.

## Bringing a Pocatello matter to the firm

Qualification to testify in an Idaho matter is determined by the forum under
its rules of evidence, and any venue-specific registration or qualification
requirement is identified and confirmed during the conflict check and intake
process. To begin,
[request a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) with the case caption and
parties, and note any imminent disclosure, deposition, or trial dates so
scheduling can be resolved early. For the fuller picture of how the same
remote-first model serves courts across the country, see the firm's
[nationwide coverage](/locations/nationwide/).
