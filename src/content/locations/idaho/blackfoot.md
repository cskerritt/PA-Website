## By-appointment service, stated plainly

Purinton Analytics keeps no staffed office in Blackfoot and publishes no
street address for this location, because candor about presence is part of
the same discipline that governs the reports. Meetings are by appointment.
Most engagements begin remotely, with records review, structured interviews,
and standardized testing administered under publisher protocols where remote
administration is supported. When the referral questions are better answered
through in-person evaluation, or when deposition and trial dates require
attendance, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office. Counsel
work with the same point of contact and receive the same report standard
regardless of where any given task is performed.

## Labor market research for the Blackfoot area

Vocational findings for Blackfoot evaluees rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates covering Idaho
statewide and the area that includes Bingham County, on state workforce
agency labor market information, and on direct employer contact where the
referral questions warrant it. Life care plans and
[medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/) are priced to
the evaluee's own community rather than to national averages, using
provider-specific inquiries and published fee data, because the cost of care
in a smaller market can differ materially from what a generic database would
suggest. Where a needed service is not available locally, the plan documents
the realistic source of care and the travel that obtaining it requires.
