A vocational opinion prepared for a Ketchum case should read as if opposing
counsel drafted the standards it will be judged by. Purinton Analytics writes
to that standard on every referral from the town:
[vocational expert witness](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) work and
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) both begin with a
documented conflict check, a defined scope, and a records request tailored to
the claims, so the written opinion rests on a record both sides can examine.
The practice accepts engagements from either side of the caption, and the
methodology does not change with the client. Ketchum matters are handled
within the firm's national, remote-first model, described on the
[nationwide practice page](/locations/nationwide/), with in-person work
arranged by appointment.

## Blaine County courts and the venue question

Ketchum sits in Blaine County, and state-court claims arising in the town are
ordinarily heard in the District Court sitting in Blaine County. When a matter
proceeds federally, the United States District Court for the District of Idaho
is the available federal forum. Which court will actually control a given
caption is not something the firm presumes; the controlling venue, along with
any venue-specific registration or qualification requirement, is identified
and confirmed during the conflict check and intake process. Engagements that
span the wider market around the principal city are described on the
[Hailey metro page](/locations/idaho/hailey/), and statewide coverage is
outlined on the [Idaho locations page](/locations/idaho/).

## Earning capacity measured against the reachable market

Ketchum is a small town within the Hailey metro area, and questions of
employability and earning capacity for a Ketchum evaluee have to be answered
against the labor market actually reachable from the town rather than against
a generic national picture. Vocational findings rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Hailey
metropolitan area and for Idaho statewide, on state workforce agency labor
market information, and on direct labor market research where warranted.
Scale is described qualitatively, assumptions are stated in the report, and
conclusions are confined to what the data and the medical record will bear.
When the loss must also be expressed in economic terms, the firm offers
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under the same
engagement structure.

## By-appointment presence, honestly described

The firm keeps no office in Ketchum and publishes no street address for this
location, because accuracy about presence belongs to the same discipline that
governs the reports. Most phases of an engagement, records review, counsel
conferences, and draft discussion among them, are handled remotely with
secure document transfer. When the referral questions call for an in-person
evaluation, the appointment is arranged in the evaluee's own community in
suitable professional space, and travel for evaluations, depositions, and
trial testimony is scheduled from the Kansas City home office against the
deadlines counsel supplies at intake.

## Begin with a conflict check

Every Ketchum engagement starts the way engagements start everywhere in the
practice. Submit the caption or party names through the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and note any imminent disclosure,
deposition, or trial dates. Counsel receive a defined scope proposal and fee
terms once the check clears, and the opinion that follows is written to
withstand examination by whichever side did not send the referral.
