Bellevue residents generally earn their livings across the wider Hailey metro
area, and that commuting reality is where a sound vocational analysis of a
Bellevue case has to begin. An opinion about what an injured person can still
earn means little if it ignores the market that person actually works in, so
Purinton Analytics grounds its
[vocational expert witness](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) and
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) work for Bellevue matters
in the labor market surrounding the town, not in abstractions. Plaintiff and
defense referrals move through an identical process, because a report built
for advocacy rather than for scrutiny fails whichever side commissioned it.

## The county, the courthouse, and the available forums

Bellevue lies in Blaine County. State-court litigation from the town
ordinarily proceeds in the District Court sitting in Blaine County, while the
United States District Court for the District of Idaho stands available for
federal matters. The firm names the forums without presuming which will
control a particular caption; that answer, together with any venue-specific
qualification requirement, is settled during the conflict check and intake.

[Hailey metro page](/locations/idaho/hailey/), the

## Reading a small town's labor market honestly

Bellevue is a small community, and small-market vocational work invites a
specific temptation: substituting impressions for data. The firm resists that
temptation by method. Employability and earning-capacity findings rest on
Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the
Hailey metropolitan area and for Idaho statewide, supplemented by state
workforce agency labor market information and by direct labor market research
where the referral questions warrant it. The report describes market scale
qualitatively, discloses its assumptions, and ties each conclusion to the
vocational and medical record. Where the case also requires the loss to be
quantified in economic terms, the firm arranges
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work continues within the
same engagement.

## Evaluations that come to the evaluee

A Bellevue evaluee is not asked to organize travel to a distant office. The
firm maintains no office in Bellevue and lists no street address for this
location; meetings are by appointment, and when an in-person evaluation is
called for, it is conducted in the evaluee's own community in suitable
professional space arranged in advance with counsel. Records exchange,
conferences, and draft review run remotely over secure channels. When
depositions or trial testimony require physical presence, travel is planned
from the Kansas City home office with the disclosure calendar in hand, so
that expert deadlines, discovery cutoffs, and trial settings drive the
schedule rather than collide with it.

## Start the conflict check

The intake sequence is deliberately plain: conflict check before any
substantive discussion, then a defined scope. Send the caption or party names
through the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/), identify the referral
questions, and flag any deadlines already set by the court. A scope proposal
