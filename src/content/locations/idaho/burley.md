The Burley micropolitan area spans Cassia County and Minidoka County, and
referrals from either county tend to raise the questions this practice was
built to answer: what work an injured or displaced person can still perform,
what that work realistically pays, and what future care will cost over a
lifetime. Purinton Analytics answers them for plaintiff and defense counsel
alike, through
[vocational expert witness](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) services,
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Courts serving Cassia County and Minidoka County

The firm accepts matters pending in the District Court serving Cassia County
and Minidoka County and in the United States District Court for the District
of Idaho. Because the same methodology and report format apply in every
forum, an opinion prepared for a state court matter needs no rebuilding if
the case is removed, and a federal disclosure translates cleanly when a
related state action follows. Counsel whose matters sit elsewhere in the
state will find the broader venue picture on the firm's
[Idaho page](/locations/idaho/), which describes how the practice covers
district courts statewide from a remote-first footing.

## Appointment-only presence in Burley

There is no Purinton Analytics office in Burley, staffed or otherwise, and
the firm publishes no street address for this location. That is a deliberate
statement rather than an omission: accuracy about presence belongs to the
same discipline that governs every opinion the firm signs. Meetings happen
by appointment. Records review, structured interviews, and standardized
testing under publisher protocols proceed remotely in most engagements, and
when an in-person evaluation, deposition, or trial appearance is required,
travel is arranged from the Kansas City home office with enough lead time to
protect the case schedule.

## Labor market evidence for the Burley area

Employability and earning capacity conclusions for evaluees in Cassia County
and Minidoka County are grounded in Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational
employment and wage estimates for Idaho statewide and for the area covering
these counties, supplemented by state workforce agency labor market
information and direct labor market research where the referral questions
call for it. Care-related costs in life care plans and
[medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/) are developed
from provider-specific inquiries and published fee data tied to the
evaluee's own community, so that the projected cost of care reflects where
the evaluee actually lives rather than a national composite.

## Beginning a Burley engagement

Every engagement starts with a
[conflict check request](/refer-a-case/): send the caption or the party
names, identify the venue, and note whether an expert disclosure deadline,
deposition, or trial date is already set, since scheduling drives how
quickly the records request goes out. Early referrals leave room for a
thorough evaluation; late ones are told candidly what can and cannot be done
before the deadline. Attorneys comparing options for matters outside Idaho can
review the firm's [national coverage](/locations/nationwide/) to see the
same engagement structure applied across the country, with the same
insistence that the written opinion withstand the scrutiny of whichever side
did not retain it.
