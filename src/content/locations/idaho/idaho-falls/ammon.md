An Ammon evaluee rarely works only in Ammon. The town is a sizeable suburb
within the Idaho Falls metropolitan area, and its residents participate in a
commuting market centered on the principal city, which means the questions
counsel bring to Purinton Analytics are metro questions before they are town
questions: what work this person can still perform, where that work actually
exists within commuting range, and how the loss should be measured when
restrictions close off parts of that market. The firm answers those
questions for plaintiff counsel and defense counsel on identical terms,
most often through [vocational expert opinions](/services/vocational-expert-witness/)
on employability and earning capacity,
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) in catastrophic-injury
matters, and [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Bonneville County venue and the courts

Ammon sits in Bonneville County, and civil matters arising there proceed in
the District Court. For claims that belong in federal court, the United
States District Court for the District of Idaho is the forum available in
the state; which court controls a given matter, and any registration or
qualification requirement that follows from it, is identified and confirmed
during the conflict check and intake. Because the same report may be
examined in either setting, every opinion is written to withstand the
scrutiny of the venue it ends up in: a methodology section a qualified
opposing expert could replicate, sources cited precisely enough to be
retrieved, and conclusions that stay within the disclosed scope. The
regional venue picture is covered on the
[Idaho Falls metro page](/locations/idaho/idaho-falls/), and statewide
practice on the [Idaho page](/locations/idaho/).

## Ammon in the Idaho Falls labor market

A suburb the size of Ammon does not have a labor market of its own; it has
a position inside a regional one. Vocational findings for Ammon evaluees
therefore rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and
wage estimates for the Idaho Falls, ID metropolitan area and for Idaho
statewide, supplemented by state workforce agency labor market information
and by direct employer research when the referral questions call for it.
The report is explicit about which geography each finding describes and why
that geography is the right one for this evaluee, so that neither side can
recast the opinion as resting on data from somewhere the evaluee could not
plausibly work.

## Evaluation logistics for Ammon evaluees

Purinton Analytics keeps no office in Ammon and publishes no local address,
because accuracy about presence belongs to the same discipline that governs
the reports themselves. Engagements run remote-first: records review,
counsel conferences, and most vocational interviews are conducted by secure
video, which keeps schedules short and costs predictable. When the referral
questions require in-person assessment, or when deposition and trial dates
require the expert in the courtroom, travel is scheduled from the Kansas
City home office and the evaluation is conducted in the evaluee's own
community, where daily functioning can be observed in its real context
rather than reconstructed from records alone.
