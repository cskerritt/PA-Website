When counsel calls about an evaluee who lives in Iona, the opening
practical question is usually logistical: how does a rigorous vocational evaluation
happen for someone in a small Bonneville County community without forcing
that person to travel or settling for a thinner record? Purinton Analytics
has built its process around exactly that problem. Secure video handles the
interview and conference work that never needed a conference room, records
move through protected channels, and when hands-on assessment is warranted
the evaluation comes to the evaluee rather than the other way around.
Plaintiff and defense counsel retain the firm on the same terms for
[vocational expert opinions](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) on
employability and earning capacity,
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) in catastrophic cases,
and [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
where independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis
alongside the vocational and care-planning work, all under one engagement
structure.

## The county and courts behind an Iona case

The forum question in an Iona matter starts at the county level. Bonneville
County's District Court hears the civil docket for disputes arising in the
town, and claims with a federal basis have the United States District Court
for the District of Idaho as the available federal forum in the state;
which venue controls a specific matter, and any registration or
qualification requirement that comes with it, is identified and confirmed
during the conflict check and intake. The firm treats forum as a fact to
be established, not assumed, and writes every report to a standard
indifferent to it: transparent methods, citations an opposing expert can
pull, and opinions confined to what was disclosed. Counsel comparing
venues across the region can start with the
[Idaho Falls metro page](/locations/idaho/idaho-falls/) or the statewide
[Idaho page](/locations/idaho/).

## Labor market evidence for a small community

The honest labor market for an Iona evaluee is regional, reached from a
small town near the principal city of Idaho Falls, and the analysis has to
say so explicitly instead of pretending the town is its own economy.
Findings rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and
wage estimates for the Idaho Falls, ID metropolitan area and for Idaho
statewide, on state workforce agency labor market information, and on
direct employer research when the referral questions require it. The
report walks through the bridge between data and person: which occupations
remain open given the documented restrictions, what access this particular
evaluee has to them from this community, and what the record supports about
earning capacity before and after the injury.

## What an Iona evaluation looks like in practice

The firm maintains no office in Iona and no staffed location anywhere in
the metro; meetings happen by appointment, and no street address is
published because none exists to publish. Most of the engagement is
conducted remotely by design, which shortens timelines and keeps the file
clean. Where the questions demand in-person work, or where deposition and
trial calendars require the expert present, travel is scheduled from the
Kansas City home office, and the assessment itself is conducted in the
evaluee's own community, where routines, supports, and barriers can be
seen rather than inferred.

## Starting the conflict check

The fastest way to learn whether the firm can take an Iona matter is also
the simplest: [request a conflict check](/refer-a-case/). Nothing beyond
the caption or the parties is needed at that stage, and screening on that
limited information protects counsel as much as the firm, since no
substantive facts change hands until conflicts are cleared against every
party named in the case. Once clearance issues, scheduling is drafted
backward from whatever the court has already fixed, so a looming
disclosure deadline, mediation, or trial date belongs in the very first
message. The same front door serves engagements well beyond Idaho; the
[nationwide page](/locations/nationwide/) describes that broader practice,
and an Iona file receives no less of the discipline that governs it.
