A Rigby case file is a Jefferson County case file, and that distinction
shapes how Purinton Analytics approaches the work. The town belongs to the
Idaho Falls metropolitan area for economic purposes, but its state-court
litigation is anchored in its own county, and an expert who blurs that line
invites cross-examination on it. The firm keeps the two frames separate
from intake forward: venue and disclosure obligations are analyzed where
the case is actually pending, while the economic evidence is drawn from
the regional market where a Rigby resident actually looks for work.
Retentions come from both sides of the caption, typically for
[vocational expert opinions](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) on
employability and earning capacity,
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) where injuries are
catastrophic, and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis and the vocational and care-planning components proceed under one
engagement structure.

## Jefferson County courts and the forums beyond

Civil matters arising in Rigby proceed in the District Court serving
Jefferson County. Where a claim supports federal jurisdiction, the United
States District Court for the District of Idaho is the federal forum
available in the state; the controlling venue for any particular matter,
together with any registration or qualification requirement attached to
it, is identified and confirmed during the conflict check and intake. The
firm's reports are drafted so the choice of forum changes nothing about
their quality: the same documented methodology, the same retrievable
citations, the same refusal to opine beyond the disclosed scope. Counsel
working across the region will find the broader court landscape on the
[Idaho Falls metro page](/locations/idaho/idaho-falls/) and the statewide
summary on the [Idaho page](/locations/idaho/).

## Reading the labor market from Rigby

Rigby is a small town whose workers are tied to the larger regional
economy around the principal city of Idaho Falls, and a credible earning
capacity opinion has to respect both halves of that fact. The firm grounds
its findings in Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and
wage estimates for the Idaho Falls, ID metropolitan area and for Idaho
statewide, in state workforce agency labor market information, and in
direct labor market research where the referral questions warrant it. Each
report then does the interpretive work the data cannot do alone,
explaining what realistic access this evaluee, with these restrictions and
this work history, has to the jobs the estimates describe.

## How the firm works a Rigby file

There is no Purinton Analytics office in Rigby or anywhere else in
Jefferson County, and the firm says so plainly rather than implying a
storefront it does not keep. Meetings are by appointment. The default
process is remote: secure video for vocational interviews, counsel
conferences, and records discussions, with documents exchanged through
protected channels. When in-person evaluation is warranted, or when a
deposition or trial requires physical presence, travel is arranged from
the Kansas City home office, and evaluations take place in the evaluee's
own community so that function is assessed where daily life actually
happens.
