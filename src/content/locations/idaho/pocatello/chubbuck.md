## Evaluating a Chubbuck evaluee

There is no Purinton Analytics office in Chubbuck or anywhere else in Idaho,
and the firm does not pretend otherwise. Interviews and testing are conducted
remotely by secure video when the referral questions permit, which keeps costs
down and scheduling simple for counsel and evaluee alike. When the matter
calls for in-person evaluation, or when deposition or trial testimony requires
presence, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office and the
evaluation is conducted in the evaluee's own community, where their actual
commuting range and daily functioning can be assessed honestly. Records
review, transferable skills analysis, and report drafting proceed on the same
methodology regardless of format, and the report states which format was used
and why.
