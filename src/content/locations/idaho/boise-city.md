Purinton Analytics accepts vocational expert and life care planning
engagements throughout the Boise City metropolitan area, whose venue
footprint covers Ada, Boise, Canyon, Gem, and Owyhee counties. Referrals
from the metro typically involve
[earning capacity and employability evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/)
in injury and employment matters,
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) and
[medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/) in catastrophic
cases, and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. Plaintiff and defense counsel receive the same
methodology, the same disclosure practices, and the same written product.

## Venue footprint across the metro

Engagements are accepted for matters in the District Court serving Ada
County and in the District Courts of Boise, Canyon, Gem, and Owyhee
counties, as well as in the United States District Court for the District of
Idaho. The working file is built the same way regardless of forum: a
documented conflict check, a defined scope, and a records request tailored
to the claims, so the written opinion rests on a record either side of the
caption can examine. Counsel with matters elsewhere in the state can start
from the [Idaho locations page](/locations/idaho/), which describes
statewide coverage under the same standards.

## Meetings by appointment, with honesty about presence

Boise City meetings are by appointment. The firm keeps no staffed office in
the metro and publishes no local street address, because accuracy about
offices is part of the same discipline that governs its reports. Most
engagements begin remotely with records review, structured interviews, and
standardized testing under publisher protocols where remote administration
is supported. When the referral questions call for in-person evaluation, or
when deposition and trial calendars require presence, travel is scheduled
from the Kansas City home office and planned around the court's dates.
Counsel receive a consistent report standard and a direct point of contact
wherever the work is performed, and scheduling is confirmed early enough
that expert availability never becomes a case-management problem.

## Labor market research grounded in published data

Vocational findings for evaluees in the metro rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Boise City
metropolitan area and for Idaho statewide, supplemented by state workforce
agency labor market information and direct employer research where the
referral questions warrant it. Life care plans and medical cost projections
for Idaho residents are priced to the evaluee's own market rather than to
national averages, using provider-specific inquiries and published fee data
for the region. Where a plan is challenged, the same sources support
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) of opposing
reports, with findings stated only as far as the record supports them.

## Starting a Boise City engagement

Qualification to testify in an Idaho matter is determined by the forum under
its rules of evidence, and any venue-specific registration or qualification
requirement is identified and confirmed during the conflict check and intake
process. To explore availability for a matter venued in the metro, submit
the caption or party names through the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and note any imminent disclosure,
deposition, or trial dates. Counsel weighing whether a local or national
retention fits the case can also review the firm's
[nationwide coverage](/locations/nationwide/), which explains how the same
remote-first practice serves venues across the country.
