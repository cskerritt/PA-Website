Post Falls has grown into one of the larger communities in the Coeur d'Alene
metro area, and its workforce moves accordingly: some residents build careers
close to home, others commute toward Coeur d'Alene, and many hold work
histories that span the metro's employers over time. An earning-capacity
dispute involving a Post Falls worker therefore turns on the commuting market
the evaluee actually participates in, not on the town in isolation. Purinton
Analytics offers [vocational expert](/services/vocational-expert-witness/)
and [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) services to plaintiff
and defense counsel litigating Post Falls matters, on the same documented
methodology described on the firm's
[nationwide page](/locations/nationwide/).

## Venue context: Kootenai County and the federal forum

Post Falls lies in Kootenai County, and state-court injury, employment, and
disability claims arising there are generally heard in the District Court
sitting in Kootenai County. On the federal side, the United States District
Court for the District of Idaho is the trial forum the state offers; whether
a particular caption belongs in state or federal court is a question the firm
leaves to counsel, with the controlling venue confirmed during the conflict
check and intake process. Expert disclosure practice differs between those
forums, so the applicable schedule is established at intake and the report is
drafted against it. Work across the wider metro is described on the
[Coeur d'Alene metro page](/locations/idaho/coeur-dalene/), and statewide
coverage on the [Idaho page](/locations/idaho/).

## Reading the Post Falls commuting market

Vocational opinions for Post Falls evaluees rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Coeur d'Alene
metropolitan area and for Idaho statewide, together with state workforce
agency labor market information and direct employer research where the
referral questions call for it. The analysis stays qualitative where the
record demands caution: transferable skills are mapped to occupations
realistically available within the evaluee's demonstrated commuting range,
and the written report explains why each alternative is or is not a genuine
option. When the damages presentation requires economic quantification,
counsel may add a
[coordinated damages engagement](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Appointments, not a storefront

The firm keeps no office in Post Falls and publishes no street address for
this location, because candor about presence belongs to the same discipline
that governs the opinions themselves. Records review, counsel conferences,
and draft-report discussion are handled remotely as a rule. Where an
in-person vocational or life care planning evaluation is warranted, it is
scheduled in the evaluee's own community in appropriate professional space,
and testimony dates are met through travel planned from the Kansas City home
office. Counsel are told at intake what the calendar can and cannot absorb,
before deadlines make the answer expensive.

## Beginning a Post Falls engagement

Every engagement opens the same way, for either side of the caption: a
documented conflict check, a defined scope, and a records request tailored to
the claims. Send the caption or party names through the

note any disclosure, deposition, or trial dates already set. The firm replies
