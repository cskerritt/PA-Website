An injured worker living in Rathdrum should not have to organize a long trip
to be evaluated, and counsel should not have to guess whether a national
expert practice can actually reach a small Idaho town. Purinton Analytics
answers both concerns the same way it does everywhere: most of the work
happens remotely, and when the referral calls for an in-person
[vocational evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) or
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) assessment, the
appointment is arranged in the evaluee's own community, in suitable
professional space, on a schedule set with counsel in advance. Plaintiff and
defense referrals are handled with an identical process and an identical
standard of care.

## Where Rathdrum cases are heard

Rathdrum is part of Kootenai County, so state-court claims from the town are
ordinarily litigated in the District Court sitting in Kootenai County.
Federal matters have the United States District Court for the District of
Idaho available to them; the firm does not presume which forum will control a
particular caption, and the answer, along with any venue-specific
qualification requirement, is settled during the conflict check and intake
process. Metro-wide engagements are described on the
[Coeur d'Alene metro page](/locations/idaho/coeur-dalene/), statewide work on
the [Idaho page](/locations/idaho/), and the firm's methodology as a whole on
the [nationwide practice page](/locations/nationwide/).

## A small town inside a metro labor market

Rathdrum is a small community whose residents typically earn their livings
across the broader Coeur d'Alene metro area, and the vocational analysis has
to reflect that reality. Employability and earning-capacity opinions are
grounded in Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage
estimates for the Coeur d'Alene metropolitan area and for Idaho statewide,
along with state workforce agency labor market information and direct
research where warranted. The report describes scale and opportunity
qualitatively, states its assumptions, and confines every conclusion to what
the data and the medical record will bear. If the case also needs the loss
expressed in economic terms, a
[coordinated damages engagement](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
brings in independent economist partners who perform and sign that analysis
while the vocational and care-planning work continues under the same
engagement.

## Logistics built around the evaluee and the calendar

The firm maintains no Rathdrum office and lists no street address for this
location; saying so plainly is part of the discipline the reports themselves
follow. Records exchange, counsel conferences, and draft review are conducted
remotely with secure transfer. Travel for evaluations, depositions, and trial
testimony is planned from the Kansas City home office, and it is planned
against the case calendar: expert disclosure deadlines, discovery cutoffs,
and trial settings are collected at intake so that the report is complete
when the schedule demands it, not after.

## Start with the conflict check

Before any substantive discussion, the firm runs a documented conflict check.
Submit the caption or party names through the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/), describe the referral questions, and
identify any dates already on the disclosure calendar. Counsel receive a
defined scope proposal and fee terms in response. Whichever side of the
caption makes the referral, the resulting opinion is written to be examined
by the other one.
