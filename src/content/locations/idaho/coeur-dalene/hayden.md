For counsel with a Hayden case, the practical starting point is the
courthouse. Hayden sits in Kootenai County, and the District Court sitting in
that county is where most injury, disability, and employment matters from the
town are tried. The judges and juries there will read whatever a vocational
or life care planning expert writes, which is why Purinton Analytics prepares
every Hayden report as if opposing counsel were its first reader: a defined
referral question, a documented records basis, and conclusions the record can
actually support. Both
[vocational expert witness](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) and
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) engagements are accepted
from plaintiff and defense counsel alike.

## Forums available for Hayden captions

Beyond the District Court in Kootenai County, the United States District
Court for the District of Idaho stands as the federal forum within the state,
and the firm's reports are built to serve either setting. Which court
controls a given caption, and what expert qualification or disclosure
requirements attach to it, are identified and confirmed during the conflict
check and intake process rather than assumed. Counsel handling matters
elsewhere in the metro will find the firm's regional work described on the
[Coeur d'Alene metro page](/locations/idaho/coeur-dalene/); the
[Idaho state page](/locations/idaho/) covers engagements across the rest of
the state, and the [national practice page](/locations/nationwide/) explains
how the same methodology travels to any venue.

## Labor market evidence with the metro in view

Hayden is a smaller community within a metro labor market, and honest
vocational analysis treats it that way. Earning-capacity and employability
opinions draw on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage
estimates covering the Coeur d'Alene metropolitan area and Idaho statewide,
supplemented by state workforce agency data and targeted labor market
research when the referral requires it. No figure appears in a report that
the underlying data cannot carry, and the transferable skills analysis
addresses the occupations a Hayden evaluee could realistically reach, not a
hypothetical worker in a hypothetical market. Where lost earnings must be
reduced to economic terms, counsel can retain a
[coordinated damages assessment](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis alongside the vocational and care-planning work.

## Meeting counsel and evaluees by appointment

There is no Purinton Analytics office in Hayden, and the firm publishes no
street address here; that candor is deliberate. The bulk of any engagement,
from intake through records analysis and report drafting, proceeds remotely
with secure document exchange. In-person evaluations, when the referral
questions justify them, are arranged in the evaluee's own community so that
an injured Hayden resident is examined close to home. Deposition and trial
appearances are scheduled from the Kansas City home office, and dates are
committed only after the calendar has been checked against existing
obligations.

## The conflict check comes first

Retention begins with a conflict check, not a sales call. Provide the caption
or party names through the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/), outline the
referral questions in general terms, and flag any disclosure deadline or
testimony date already fixed. The firm responds with a proposed scope and fee
terms. Plaintiff and defense counsel receive the same process and the same
kind of opinion: one that stands on the record and says no more than the
record allows.
