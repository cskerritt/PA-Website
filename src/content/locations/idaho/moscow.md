Every matter Purinton Analytics accepts out of Moscow begins with the same
sequence: a documented conflict check, a scope of work agreed in writing,
and a records request built around the actual claims, so that the eventual
opinion rests on a record either side of the caption can examine. From that
foundation the firm provides
[vocational expert witness](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) services
in injury and employment litigation,
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) where catastrophic
injury puts future care at issue, and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Latah County venue footprint

The Moscow micropolitan area consists of Latah County, and engagements are
accepted for matters in the District Court serving the county and in the
United States District Court for the District of Idaho. The firm also takes
consulting-only assignments in the area, including
[expert testimony and litigation consulting](/services/expert-testimony-litigation-consulting/)
support for counsel testing a damages theory before committing to
disclosure. Whatever the posture, plaintiff and defense referrals are
handled under identical terms, and the analysis reads the same whichever
party paid for it. The firm's [Idaho page](/locations/idaho/) describes the
statewide venue coverage that surrounds this local footprint.

## Remote-first, by appointment

Moscow meetings are by appointment. The firm maintains no staffed office
here and publishes no street address for this location, a choice made
because honesty about presence is part of the same discipline that shapes
the reports themselves. In practice, most of the work never requires a local
address: records analysis, structured interviews, and standardized testing
under publisher protocols are conducted remotely wherever remote
administration is supported. When the assignment calls for an in-person
evaluation, or when a deposition or trial appearance is set, travel is
scheduled from the Kansas City home office and coordinated around the
court's calendar rather than the firm's convenience. Counsel keep the same
point of contact from intake through testimony, wherever each stage of the
work happens to be performed.

## Grounding findings in the Moscow labor market

Employability and earning capacity opinions for Latah County evaluees draw
on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for
Idaho statewide and for the area that includes Latah County, together with
state workforce agency labor market information and direct employer research
where warranted. Because published estimates describe a market in the
aggregate, the firm treats them as a starting point and documents any
evaluee-specific adjustment expressly, so opposing counsel can trace each
figure in the report to a stated source. Care costs in life care plans and
[medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/) are developed
the same way, priced to the evaluee's own community through
provider-specific inquiries and published fee data.

## Requesting a conflict check

Counsel with a Moscow or Latah County matter can start by
[submitting the caption or party names](/refer-a-case/) for a conflict
check, along with the venue and any dates already fixed for disclosure,
deposition, or trial. Where a case connects to litigation in other states,
the firm's [nationwide practice](/locations/nationwide/) shows how the same
methodology and the same report standard travel with the engagement across
jurisdictions.
