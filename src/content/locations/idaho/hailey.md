Catastrophic injury puts future care at the center of damages, and those
questions do not become simpler in smaller communities, where the nearest
qualified provider may not be nearby at all. Purinton Analytics serves
counsel in Hailey and across Blaine County, Camas County, and Lincoln County
with [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) and
[medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/) built around
where the evaluee actually lives, alongside
[vocational expert](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) evaluation of
employability and earning capacity for both plaintiff and defense
retentions. When lost earnings must be reduced to a damages figure, the firm
offers [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Venue coverage across the Hailey area

The Hailey micropolitan area takes in Blaine County, Camas County, and
Lincoln County, and the firm accepts matters pending in the District Court
serving those counties as well as in the United States District Court for
the District of Idaho. Assignments range from full evaluation and testimony
to targeted [rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) of
an opposing expert's life care plan or vocational report. In each posture
the deliverable is the same: a written opinion with its sources stated,
prepared so that the attorney who did not retain the firm can still follow
every step of the reasoning.

## Presence in Hailey, described accurately

The firm keeps no staffed office in Hailey and publishes no street address
for this location. Meetings are by appointment, and stating that plainly is
part of the same accuracy the reports themselves are held to. Most
engagements proceed remotely from the outset, through records review,
structured interviews, and standardized testing under publisher protocols
where remote administration is supported. When the referral questions
require an in-person evaluation in the evaluee's community, or when
deposition and trial dates demand attendance, travel is scheduled from the
Kansas City home office and planned against the litigation calendar.

## Labor market and care cost research

Vocational conclusions for evaluees in Blaine County, Camas County, and
Lincoln County rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment
and wage estimates for Idaho statewide and for the area covering these
counties, on state workforce agency labor market information, and on direct
labor market research where warranted. Care costs receive the same
treatment: rather than importing national averages, life care plans and
medical cost projections price each recommended service through
provider-specific inquiries and published fee data for the evaluee's own
market, and where a service is not available locally, the plan says so and
accounts for the realistic alternative. Counsel on either side can test each
entry against the cited source.
