St. Anthony is a small Fremont County community within the Rexburg, ID metropolitan area, and the injury, employment, and disability cases that arise there turn on the same damages questions litigated in far larger venues: what work an injured person can still perform, what that work pays, and what future care will cost. Purinton Analytics provides [vocational expert witness](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) and [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) services for St. Anthony matters, accepting engagements from plaintiff and defense counsel alike. Every file opens the same way: a documented conflict check, a defined scope, and a records request tailored to the claims, so the written opinion rests on a record either side of the caption can examine.

## Fremont County and the courts that hear St. Anthony cases

Civil matters arising in St. Anthony are heard in the District Court serving Fremont County. For cases that belong in federal court, Idaho's federal forum is the United States District Court for the District of Idaho; the controlling venue for any particular matter, along with any venue-specific registration or qualification requirement, is identified and confirmed during the conflict check and intake process. The work product does not change with the forum. Reports are written to survive deposition and cross-examination, with methodology stated, sources cited, and conclusions confined to what the record supports, whether the reader is a Fremont County jury or a federal judge ruling on admissibility.

## A small community inside the Rexburg commuting market

Earning capacity in St. Anthony cannot be assessed as if the town were an island. Its residents participate in a labor market organized around Rexburg, the metro's principal city, and a credible vocational opinion has to weigh what employment actually exists within a reasonable commute rather than what a national database abstractly lists. Vocational findings for a St. Anthony evaluee rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Rexburg area and for Idaho statewide, state workforce agency labor market information, and direct labor market research where published estimates run thin for a community of this size. Transferable skills analysis is then matched against that real market, not against a hypothetical metropolitan one. Regional venue context appears on the [Rexburg metro page](/locations/idaho/rexburg/), and the statewide practice is described on the [Idaho page](/locations/idaho/).

## Evaluating an evaluee who lives in St. Anthony

The practice is remote-first, which serves outlying communities well: records review, vocational interviews, and counsel conferences proceed by secure video without asking an injured person to travel. When the referral questions call for in-person evaluation, or when deposition and trial dates require presence, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office and the evaluation is conducted in the evaluee's own community. The firm keeps no staffed office in St. Anthony or anywhere in Idaho and publishes no street address for this location, because accuracy about presence is part of the same discipline that governs its reports. Where a matter requires a full damages picture, counsel may structure [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement structure.
