Smaller markets deserve the same analytical rigor as major metros, and the
Rexburg micropolitan area is a case in point. A damages question arising in
Madison County or Fremont County turns on the same issues as anywhere else:
what work the evaluee can still perform, what that work pays in the market
where the evaluee actually lives, and what future care will cost. Purinton
Analytics answers those questions for plaintiff and defense counsel through
[vocational expert witness work](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/),
[medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/), and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Courts for Madison and Fremont county matters

Engagements are accepted for matters in the District Courts serving Madison
County and Fremont County and in the United States District Court for the
District of Idaho. The venue's size never relaxes the process: every
engagement opens with a documented conflict check, a defined scope, and a
records request tailored to the claims, and every report is written so that
either side of the caption can audit its reasoning. Coverage for the rest of
the state, including its larger metros, is described on the
[Idaho locations page](/locations/idaho/).

## By appointment, without a local storefront

The firm maintains no staffed office in Rexburg and publishes no street
address for this location, and it says so plainly because honesty about
presence belongs to the same discipline as honesty in reports. Counsel get a
remote-first practice instead: records review, structured interviews, and
standardized testing under publisher protocols where remote administration
is supported, all of which proceed without regard to distance. When the
referral questions call for in-person evaluation, or when deposition and
trial dates require attendance, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City
home office and planned around the court's calendar. The report standard
does not vary with the size of the venue.

## Researching a smaller labor market honestly

Vocational findings for evaluees in the area rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates and on Idaho statewide
data, together with state workforce agency labor market information and
direct labor market research where the referral questions warrant it. In a
smaller market, direct research matters more, not less: published estimates
are checked against what employers in the evaluee's actual commuting range
are hiring for and paying. Life care plan pricing for area residents draws
on provider-specific inquiries and published fee data for the region, and
the same discipline supports
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) when an opposing
report prices care or employment to markets where the evaluee does not live.

## Referring a Rexburg area case

Qualification to testify in an Idaho matter is determined by the forum under
its rules of evidence, and any venue-specific registration or qualification
requirement is identified and confirmed during the conflict check and intake
process. To begin an engagement,
[request a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) with the case caption and
parties, and note any imminent disclosure, deposition, or trial dates.
Counsel can also review the firm's
[nationwide coverage](/locations/nationwide/) to see how the same standards
apply from the smallest venues to the largest.
