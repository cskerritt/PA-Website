Kentucky divides its federal trial work between an Eastern and a Western District, and its state-court damages claims proceed in Circuit Court, so the first question in many referrals is simply where the matter sits. For Purinton Analytics the answer changes the disclosure calendar, not the work: the vocational assessment and the life care plan are built the same way in every Kentucky venue, from a documented record, under a defined scope, in a report written to be examined by the other side.

## Where Kentucky engagements are accepted

The practice accepts matters pending in the Circuit Courts of Kentucky and in the United States District Court for the Eastern District of Kentucky and the United States District Court for the Western District of Kentucky. Dedicated pages describe service to the Louisville and Lexington-Fayette areas and to Bowling Green, Corbin, Richmond-Berea, Elizabethtown, Owensboro, Paducah, Pikeville, Frankfort, Somerset, Henderson, Danville, Glasgow, and Mount Sterling. A matter outside those areas is still a Kentucky matter the firm can serve; the metro pages exist to explain local logistics, not to draw a boundary around coverage.

## How the practice serves Kentucky

Most Kentucky work proceeds remotely. Records are reviewed, vocational interviews are conducted by video, and reports and pre-deposition conferences are completed on the schedule the litigation sets, without travel driving the timeline. Kentucky meetings are by appointment: the firm keeps no staffed office in the state and publishes no Kentucky street address, and it says so plainly because candor about presence belongs in the same category as candor about methodology. When an in-person evaluation is warranted, or when a deposition or trial requires attendance, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office. Vocational findings for Kentucky evaluees rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the relevant metropolitan areas and for Kentucky statewide, state workforce agency labor market information, and direct labor market research where warranted. Where the referral calls for a complete damages presentation, independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement structure. Referrals come from plaintiff and defense counsel alike, and the reports are written so that either side of the caption can test them.

## Qualification and disclosure

Kentucky counsel should not have to brief the firm on local expert practice. Any venue-specific registration or qualification requirement is identified and confirmed during the conflict check and intake process, and the firm supplies disclosure materials, prior testimony history, and fee schedules in the form the venue expects. Intake also fixes the scope of the referral questions and the records that must be gathered before an opinion can responsibly issue, so the engagement is defined before the analysis begins.

## Starting a Kentucky engagement

Kentucky matters enter the same intake path as the rest of the [national practice](/locations/nationwide/). To begin, [request a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) with the case caption and parties, and flag any disclosure, deposition, or trial dates that are already set. When the conflict check clears, scope and fee terms are confirmed in writing and a tailored records request follows, so both counsel and expert know exactly what the engagement covers from the first day.
