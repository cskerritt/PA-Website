When the evaluee lives in Kauai County, the evaluation is built around the
evaluee, not around the expert's office. Purinton Analytics serves the
Kapaa, HI micropolitan area on exactly that principle: records analysis and
interviews proceed remotely, testing follows publisher protocols that
support remote administration, and in-person work is scheduled where the
referral questions require it. Plaintiff and defense counsel retain the
firm on the same terms for
[vocational expert](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) evaluations,
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[expert testimony and litigation consulting](/services/expert-testimony-litigation-consulting/),
with [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
available where independent economist partners perform and sign the
economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds
under one engagement structure.

## Where Kauai County cases are heard

The firm accepts matters venued in the Circuit Court serving Kauai County
and in the United States District Court for the District of Hawaii.
Whichever court holds the case, the engagement opens with a documented
conflict check, a defined scope, and a records request tailored to the
claims, so the resulting opinion stands on a record both sides can examine
and test. Counsel handling related matters elsewhere in the state can find
the firm's full coverage on the [Hawaii page](/locations/hawaii/).

## No storefront, and no pretense of one

The firm does not keep a staffed office in Kapaa and publishes no street
address for this location. Meetings are by appointment, and the absence of
a local storefront is stated plainly because accuracy about presence
belongs to the same discipline as accuracy in a report. What counsel can
rely on instead is a consistent process: responsive scheduling, remote
evaluation wherever the methodology supports it, and travel from the Kansas
City home office when an in-person evaluation, deposition, or trial
appearance is needed. Referrals made early in the life of a case give that
travel calendar the most room and keep expert disclosures unhurried.

## Reading the labor market an evaluee actually faces

An earning capacity opinion for a Kauai County evaluee has to reflect the
employment landscape that evaluee actually confronts. Vocational findings
therefore rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and
wage estimates for the area and for Hawaii statewide, state workforce
agency labor market information, and direct labor market research where the
referral questions warrant it. Care-related opinions follow the same logic:
life care plans and [medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/)
are priced to the evaluee's own market using provider-specific inquiries
and published fee data for the region, because local availability and local
pricing of care, not a national composite, determine what a plan will
actually cost to fund.

## Beginning a Kapaa engagement

Kauai County matters move through the same intake the firm applies to every
venue it serves, summarized on the [nationwide page](/locations/nationwide/).
Before any substantive discussion of the case, conflicts are checked and
confirmed. To start that process, submit the caption or party names through
the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and note any imminent disclosure,
deposition, or trial dates. Once conflicts clear, scope and fee terms are
set out in an engagement letter, the records request goes out, and the
evaluation format is matched to the referral questions and the court's
schedule.
