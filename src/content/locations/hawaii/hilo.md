A practice built for distance serves Hilo well. Purinton Analytics performs
most of its work remotely by design, which means counsel in the Hilo-Kailua,
HI micropolitan area receive the same
[vocational expert](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) and
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) services, on the same
report standard, as counsel in the firm's home market. Plaintiff and defense
referrals are accepted on equal terms, and where a matter requires economic
damages work, the firm arranges
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Courts serving Hawaii County matters

The firm accepts engagements for matters venued in the Circuit Court
serving Hawaii County and in the United States District Court for the
District of Hawaii. Method does not vary by forum. Every engagement opens
with a documented conflict check, a defined scope, and a records request
tailored to the claims, so that the written opinion rests on a record both
sides can examine. Where a case involves parties or treatment providers
beyond the county, the [Hawaii state page](/locations/hawaii/) describes
how the firm handles matters that reach across the rest of the state.

## Remote by default, present when it counts

There is no staffed Purinton Analytics office in Hilo, and the firm
publishes no street address for this location. Saying so plainly is part of
the same discipline that governs its reports. What counsel gets instead is
a workflow that does not depend on geography: records analysis, structured
interviews by videoconference, and standardized testing under publisher
protocols where remote administration is supported. When the referral
questions require an in-person evaluation, or when a deposition or trial
date requires attendance, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home
office. Because that travel takes planning, counsel are encouraged to refer
early rather than close to a disclosure deadline.

## Grounding opinions in the local labor market

Distance never excuses generic labor market analysis. Vocational findings
for Hawaii County evaluees rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational
employment and wage estimates for the area and for Hawaii statewide, state
workforce agency labor market information, and direct labor market research
where warranted. A transferable skills conclusion that ignores what
employment actually looks like in the evaluee's community would not survive
cross-examination, and it should not. Life care plans and
[medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/) for Hawaii
County residents are likewise priced to the evaluee's own market, using
provider-specific inquiries and published fee data rather than national
averages that may not reflect local availability of care.

## Referring a Hilo matter

Counsel weighing an expert for a Hawaii County case can review the firm's
approach on the [nationwide page](/locations/nationwide/), which describes
the same intake, evaluation, and reporting sequence applied here. The
opening step never varies: a conflict check before any substantive discussion
of the case. Submit the caption or party names through the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/), note any imminent disclosure,
deposition, or trial dates, and the firm will confirm conflicts, define
scope, and document fee terms in an engagement letter before work begins.
