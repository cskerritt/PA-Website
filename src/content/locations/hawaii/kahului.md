Counsel with a matter venued in Maui County or Kalawao County can retain
Purinton Analytics for the vocational and care-planning side of damages
without regard to which party issues the retention. The firm works for
plaintiff and defense alike across the Kahului-Wailuku, HI metropolitan
area, providing [vocational expert witness](/services/vocational-expert-witness/)
evaluations of earning capacity and employability,
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) in catastrophic injury
matters, and [rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) of
opposing expert work. Where the damages model requires an economist, the
firm offers [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## State and federal venues for the metro

Engagements are accepted for matters in the Circuit Court serving Maui
County and Kalawao County and in the United States District Court for the
District of Hawaii. Every engagement begins the way it does everywhere else
in the practice: with a documented conflict check, a defined scope, and a
records request tailored to the claims, so that the written opinion rests
on a record either side of the caption can examine. The firm's statewide
coverage, including its other Hawaii service areas, is described on the
[Hawaii page](/locations/hawaii/).

## How meetings and evaluations happen here

Kahului meetings are by appointment. The firm maintains no staffed office
in the metro and publishes no street address for this location; candor
about presence is part of the same standard that governs its reports. In
practice, most of an engagement happens without travel at all. Records
review, structured vocational interviews by videoconference, and
standardized testing under publisher protocols with supported remote
administration carry the bulk of the work. Where the referral questions
call for an in-person evaluation, or where deposition and trial calendars
require attendance, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office.
Early referral gives that scheduling room to work and keeps the evaluation
timeline comfortably ahead of disclosure deadlines.

## Labor market evidence for Maui County evaluees

Opinions about what an evaluee can earn are only as strong as the labor
market evidence beneath them. Vocational findings for evaluees in the metro
rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage
estimates for the Kahului-Wailuku area and for Hawaii statewide, state
workforce agency labor market information, and direct labor market research
where the questions warrant it. [Medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/)
and life care plans are priced to the evaluee's own market through
provider-specific inquiries and published fee data for the region, because
the availability and cost of care where the evaluee actually lives is the
fact that matters, not a national average.

## Opening a Kahului-Wailuku engagement

The intake sequence for a Maui County matter is identical to the sequence
the firm applies everywhere, described in more detail on the
[nationwide page](/locations/nationwide/). Conflicts come before
conversation: submit the caption or party names through the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and flag any imminent disclosure,
deposition, or trial dates. Once conflicts clear, scope and fee terms are
documented in an engagement letter, records are requested, and the
evaluation is scheduled in whichever format the referral questions and the
court's calendar require.
