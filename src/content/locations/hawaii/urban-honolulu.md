Purinton Analytics provides vocational expert and life care planning support
to plaintiff and defense counsel litigating in the Urban Honolulu, HI
metropolitan area. Referrals from Honolulu commonly involve
[earning capacity and employability evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/)
in injury and employment matters,
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) and
[medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/) in catastrophic
injury cases, and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. Whichever side of the caption sends the referral, the
work follows the same sequence: a documented conflict check, a defined
scope, and a records request tailored to the claims.

## Venue footprint in Honolulu County

Engagements are accepted for matters venued in the Circuit Court serving
Honolulu County and in the United States District Court for the District of
Hawaii. The forum does not change the method. Opinions are written to be
examined, so the file behind each report is organized for disclosure from
the start: sources identified, testing documented, and assumptions stated
in the report itself rather than left for cross-examination to surface.
Counsel with matters elsewhere in the state will find the broader coverage
described on the firm's [Hawaii page](/locations/hawaii/).

## By-appointment meetings, without a local storefront

Honolulu meetings are by appointment. The firm keeps no staffed office in
the metro and publishes no street address for this location, because
accuracy about presence is part of the same discipline that governs its
reports. Most engagements proceed remotely from the outset: records review,
structured vocational interviews by videoconference, and standardized
testing under publisher protocols where remote administration is supported.
When the referral questions call for in-person evaluation, or when
deposition and trial dates require attendance, travel is scheduled from the
Kansas City home office and planned around the court's calendar. Counsel
receive the same report standard and the same point of contact regardless
of where the work is performed.

## Labor market research for Honolulu evaluees

Vocational findings for evaluees in the metro rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Urban
Honolulu metropolitan area and for Hawaii statewide, supplemented by state
workforce agency labor market information and direct labor market research
where the referral questions warrant it. Employability and placeability
conclusions are grounded in the evaluee's own labor market rather than in
national generalizations, and the sources for each conclusion are cited so
that opposing counsel can trace every step. Life care plans and medical
cost projections are priced to the evaluee's own market as well, using
provider-specific inquiries and published fee data for the region rather
than national averages.

## Starting a Honolulu engagement

The practice is built to serve distant venues without diluting its
standards, and Honolulu matters receive the same intake rigor as those
described on the [nationwide services page](/locations/nationwide/).
Scheduling depth matters in a venue where travel must be arranged in
advance, so early referral protects both the evaluation window and the
disclosure deadline. To begin, submit the caption or party names through
the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and note any imminent disclosure,
deposition, or trial dates. Conflicts are confirmed before any substantive
discussion of the case, and scope and fee terms are documented in an
engagement letter before work begins.
