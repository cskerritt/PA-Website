schedule around an expert's office hours in a distant city, and counsel
should not have to accept a thinner evaluation because the evaluee lives
where they live. Purinton Analytics resolves that tension with a
remote-first practice that brings
[vocational expert witness](/services/vocational-expert-witness/)
evaluation, [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)

## Evaluation where the evaluee is

Alexandria meetings are by appointment; the firm keeps no staffed office in
the city and publishes no street address for this location, because it
describes its footprint as carefully as it describes an evaluee's work
history. Most evaluations proceed without anyone traveling: structured
records review, vocational interviews by secure video at times that fit the
evaluee's circumstances, and standardized testing under publisher protocols
where remote administration is supported. When the referral questions call
for an in-person evaluation, the firm comes to the evaluee's community
rather than the reverse, with travel scheduled from the Kansas City home
office; the same travel practice covers depositions and trial appearances
when the calendar requires the expert in the courtroom.

## The labor market behind the opinion

What an Alexandria evaluee can earn depends on the labor market around
them, so the firm's vocational findings are grounded in Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for Minnesota
statewide and for the regional area that includes Douglas County, in state
workforce agency labor market information, and in direct labor market
research where warranted. Each source is cited where it is used, keeping
the analysis open to inspection by opposing counsel. Life care plans and
medical cost projections for Douglas County residents are priced to the
evaluee's own market using provider-specific inquiries and published fee
data for the region, not to national averages that may not reflect what
care actually costs locally.
