A Hermantown evaluee competes for work in the labor market of the
[Duluth metro](/locations/minnesota/duluth/) as a whole, and that single
economic fact drives most of the vocational analysis in a Hermantown case.
Post-injury employability, transferable skills, and residual earning
capacity all have to be measured against the jobs the metro actually
offers, not against assumptions. Purinton Analytics performs that
measurement for plaintiff and defense counsel through
[vocational expert](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) engagements,
[life care plans](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) of opposing
expert work.

## The metro market in the written opinion

Because Duluth is the principal city of the metropolitan area that
includes Hermantown, the employment base counsel cares about is regional.
The firm's findings rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational
employment and wage estimates for the Duluth metropolitan area and for
Minnesota statewide, together with state workforce agency labor market
information and, where the referral questions call for it, direct labor
market research. Those sources are cited in the report and characterized
qualitatively; the opinion never leans on a statistic it cannot defend at
deposition. Where a treating provider's restrictions and the demands of
available work diverge, the report addresses that gap directly instead of
leaving it for cross-examination to expose.

## Where a Hermantown case is filed

Hermantown is part of St. Louis County, and civil litigation arising there
ordinarily proceeds in the District Court serving St. Louis County. For
federal matters, the forum available statewide is the United States
District Court for the District of Minnesota. Which court controls a
specific dispute, and what registration or qualification steps that forum
requires of a testifying expert, are confirmed during the conflict check
and intake, so counsel never has to retrofit an opinion to an unexpected
venue.

## Scheduling evaluation and testimony

The practice runs from a single home office in Kansas City; there is no
Hermantown office, no local storefront, and no published street address
for this location, because the firm treats accuracy about its own presence
as part of its credibility. Interviews and conferences are usually held by
secure video. When the assignment justifies an in-person evaluation, it is
scheduled in the evaluee's community at a mutually workable time, and when
a deposition or trial requires the expert's attendance, travel is arranged
from Kansas City in the same manner the firm's
[nationwide practice](/locations/nationwide/) handles appearances in any
by-appointment venue. Written status updates keep counsel informed as
evaluation and drafting proceed.

## From referral to report

The path into an engagement is deliberately uneventful: a conflict check,
a defined scope, a records request tailored to the claims, then the
evaluation and the written opinion, delivered with time for counsel's
review before disclosure. When a case also needs economic loss
quantified, the firm arranges
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. Refer a Hermantown matter through the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) with the caption or party names and
any looming dates; the [Minnesota overview](/locations/minnesota/) shows
how this page fits the statewide practice.
