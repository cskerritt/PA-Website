Plaintiff and defense counsel come to a vocational expert for different
reasons, and an Eveleth case can present either one. A plaintiff's lawyer
may need residual earning capacity and future care needs established on a
defensible record; a defense lawyer may need an opposing expert's
assumptions tested against the same standards. Purinton Analytics accepts
both kinds of Eveleth assignments on identical terms:
[vocational evaluations](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) and
[life care plans](/services/life-care-planning/) built from documented
methodology, and [rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/)
that critiques method rather than advocacy.

## One standard, whichever side retains

The firm's answer to the retention question is symmetry. The same records
request, the same interview protocol, the same published methods, and the
same sourcing discipline apply whether the engagement letter comes from
the plaintiff's side or the defense's, which is why a report prepared for
one side reads as though it expected the other side's scrutiny. In
practice it did: every opinion is drafted on the assumption that the
cross-examining lawyer will have read it more carefully than anyone else.
Engagement terms do not vary by side either, a fact the firm is content to
see explored at deposition.

## Where Eveleth disputes are heard

Eveleth is situated in St. Louis County, and civil litigation arising in
town ordinarily proceeds in the District Court serving St. Louis County.
The federal forum available statewide is the United States District Court
for the District of Minnesota. The controlling venue for any specific
matter, together with whatever expert registration or qualification
requirements that forum imposes, is identified and confirmed during the
conflict check and intake.

## Employability measured against the metro

An Eveleth evaluee's realistic alternatives are assessed within the
commuting market of the [Duluth area](/locations/minnesota/duluth/), the
metropolitan region whose principal city is Duluth. Findings rest on
Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for
the Duluth metropolitan area and for Minnesota statewide, alongside state
workforce agency labor market information and, when the referral questions
call for it, direct labor market research. The report describes that
market in qualitative terms and ties each characterization to a cited
source, because an unsourced adjective is a gift to the other side.

## Logistics for evaluees and counsel

There is no Eveleth office; the firm works from its Kansas City home
office and serves every location in its
[nationwide practice](/locations/nationwide/) by appointment. Interviews
and conferences run over secure video in the ordinary course, in-person
evaluations are arranged in the evaluee's community when the assignment
requires them, and travel for depositions and trial testimony is scheduled
from Kansas City around the court's dates.

## Retaining the firm

Referrals from either side begin identically: a documented conflict check,
then scope, records, evaluation, and report. Where the case requires
economic damages quantified, the firm arranges
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. Send the caption or party names through the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) with any pressing dates; the
[Minnesota overview](/locations/minnesota/) supplies the statewide
picture.
