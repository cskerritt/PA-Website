A Proctor engagement starts with paperwork rather than promises. Before
any opinion is discussed, Purinton Analytics runs a documented conflict
check, agrees a written scope with counsel, and issues a records request
matched to the claims, so that everything that follows, from the
[vocational evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) to the
[life care plan](/services/life-care-planning/) to any
[testimony](/services/expert-testimony-litigation-consulting/), rests on a
record both sides of the caption can inspect. That sequence is the same
for a plaintiff referral and a defense referral, which is the point.

## Why the process is the product

Litigation experts are judged on process as much as conclusions, and the
firm treats its intake sequence as the foundation of every later answer at
deposition. Scope defines which questions the opinion will and will not
address. The records request defines what the opinion knows. The interview
and any testing define what the evaluee adds to the file. By the time a
Proctor report is disclosed, its boundaries and sources are already on
paper, which leaves cross-examination with methodology to probe rather
than surprises to spring. Amendments to scope, when a case evolves, are
documented the same way the original scope was.

## Venue and qualification for Proctor matters

Proctor lies in St. Louis County, and civil actions arising in the town
are ordinarily brought in the District Court serving St. Louis County. The
federal forum available across the state is the United States District
Court for the District of Minnesota. The intake process, not habit,
determines which forum controls a given case and what registration or
qualification requirements apply to the testifying expert there; those
questions are resolved during the conflict check and intake.

## The commuting market around a Proctor evaluee

Employability for a Proctor evaluee is measured against the
[Duluth metro](/locations/minnesota/duluth/) commuting market, anchored by
the principal city of Duluth, because that is the market in which
alternative work would actually be sought. The firm relies on Bureau of
Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Duluth
metropolitan area and for Minnesota statewide, supplemented by state
workforce agency labor market information and by direct labor market
research where a referral question requires it, and it keeps every market
characterization qualitative and sourced.

## Appointments in Proctor, home office in Kansas City

The firm has no Proctor office and claims none; its staffed location is
the Kansas City home office, from which its entire
[national practice](/locations/nationwide/) operates by appointment.
Secure video covers most interviews and conferences. In-person evaluation,
when the assignment calls for it, is arranged in the evaluee's own
community, and travel for depositions or trial is scheduled from Kansas
City around the dates the court has set.
