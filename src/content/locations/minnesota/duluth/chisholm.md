Methodology does not shrink to fit a small community. The earning capacity
and future care questions raised by a Chisholm injury case get the same
analytical treatment Purinton Analytics applies in the country's largest
venues: a defined scope, a complete record, standardized vocational and
care-planning methods, and a written opinion built to withstand
examination by whichever side did not retain the firm. Chisholm counsel,
plaintiff or defense, can draw on
[vocational expert witness](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) work,
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/) prepared on
exactly those terms.

## The record before the opinion

Every Chisholm engagement is record-driven. Medical files, employment and
wage documentation, educational history, and prior testimony are gathered
under a records request tailored to the claims, and the evaluee interview
is conducted against that documentation rather than around it. The
resulting report shows its work: sources are cited, methods are named, and
the reasoning from record to conclusion is laid out so opposing counsel
can probe each step. That transparency is the firm's answer to the
skepticism any expert opinion should expect. Gaps in the record are
flagged to counsel early, while there is still time to cure them.

## St. Louis County courts and the federal forum

Chisholm is located in St. Louis County, and civil matters arising in town
are ordinarily heard in the District Court serving St. Louis County.
Statewide, the federal forum is the United States District Court for the
District of Minnesota. The firm treats venue as an intake question, not an
assumption: the controlling court for a given dispute, and any
registration or qualification requirement it imposes on a testifying
expert, are confirmed during the conflict check and intake.

## Chisholm within the Duluth labor market

Alternative employment for a Chisholm evaluee is assessed against the
commuting market of the [Duluth metro area](/locations/minnesota/duluth/),
anchored by the principal city of Duluth, rather than against the town in
isolation. Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage
estimates for the Duluth metropolitan area and for Minnesota statewide
supply the foundation, with state workforce agency labor market
information and direct labor market research layered in when the referral
questions require it. The opinion characterizes opportunity qualitatively
and leaves quantification of economic loss to the appropriate discipline.

## Meetings by appointment

The firm's staffed presence is its Kansas City home office and nothing
else; it neither keeps nor claims an office in Chisholm. Secure video
carries most interviews and counsel conferences, in-person evaluation is
arranged in the evaluee's community when an assignment warrants it, and
travel for depositions and trials is scheduled from Kansas City, as it is
across the firm's [national practice](/locations/nationwide/).
