Distance is a logistical fact, not an analytical one. The case file for an
Ely matter travels as easily as any other: medical records, wage
documentation, deposition transcripts, and imaging all move electronically
to the reviewing expert, and the quality of the resulting opinion depends
on the discipline applied to that file, not on where the expert happens to
sit. Purinton Analytics serves Ely counsel on that premise, delivering
[vocational expert](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) analyses,
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/) to
plaintiff and defense alike.

## A remote-first practice that still shows up

Most of the work in an Ely engagement is remote by design: record review
at the Kansas City home office, evaluee interviews over secure video,
counsel conferences at whatever hour the litigation calendar allows. The
technology is tested with the evaluee ahead of time, so a scheduled
interview is never lost to a connection that fails. What
remote-first does not mean is absent. When an assignment requires hands-on
evaluation, the appointment is set in the evaluee's own community, and
when a deposition or trial requires the expert at the table, attendance is
scheduled with travel from Kansas City, exactly as the firm's
[nationwide footprint](/locations/nationwide/) is served everywhere else.
The firm maintains no office in Ely and says so without hedging; it
regards plain statements about presence as a preview of how it writes
reports.

## Courts available to an Ely case

Ely belongs to St. Louis County, so a civil action arising in town is
ordinarily filed in the District Court serving St. Louis County. For
matters that proceed federally, the district available statewide is the
United States District Court for the District of Minnesota. Venue for a
particular dispute, and any qualification requirement the chosen forum
attaches to a testifying expert, are confirmed during the conflict check
and intake rather than assumed.

## Situating Ely in the regional labor market

For employability and earning capacity purposes, an Ely evaluee is
analyzed within the labor market of the surrounding
[Duluth metropolitan area](/locations/minnesota/duluth/), whose principal
city is Duluth. The firm's sources are the Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates for the Duluth metropolitan
area and for Minnesota statewide, state workforce agency labor market
information, and direct labor market research when the referral questions
justify it. Where the local picture and the metro picture differ, the
report says so in qualitative terms and lets the cited data carry the
weight.

## Engaging the firm from Ely

A referral opens with a documented conflict check and a defined scope, and
the records request follows immediately, because remote practice makes
early access to the file even more valuable. If economic quantification is
part of the damages theory, the firm arranges
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. Counsel can begin by submitting the caption or party
names through the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/), flagging any
disclosure, deposition, or trial dates; the statewide practice is
described on the [Minnesota page](/locations/minnesota/).
