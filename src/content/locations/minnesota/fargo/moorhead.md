Moorhead's legal geography is the place to start. The town anchors the
Minnesota side of a metropolitan area whose principal city, Fargo, sits
across the state line in North Dakota, so an evaluee can live in Moorhead,
work in another state, and litigate in either. The forum question and the
labor market question do not always point the same direction here, and
Purinton Analytics is structured for that split. The firm provides
[vocational expert opinions](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure, and it accepts referrals from plaintiff and defense
counsel alike.

## Clay County venue and the Minnesota federal forum

Moorhead is a Clay County city, and state-court matters arising here are
heard in the District Court serving Clay County. When a Minnesota case
proceeds federally, the available forum is the United States District Court
for the District of Minnesota; which court controls a particular caption,
and any registration or qualification requirement that forum imposes, is
identified and confirmed during the conflict check and intake process.
Because the metro reaches across the state line, some Moorhead evaluees are
parties in North Dakota courts instead, and the written opinion is prepared
to the same standard for every forum, so a venue fight does not become an
expert problem.

## Reading a labor market that crosses a state line

Earning capacity for a Moorhead resident cannot be measured against
Minnesota alone, because the commuting market includes the principal city
and its surrounding employers. Vocational findings therefore rest on Bureau
of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the
Fargo metropolitan area and for Minnesota statewide, supplemented by state
workforce agency labor market information and by direct labor market
research when the disputed occupations require it. The report then
addresses access in plain terms: which occupations in this cross-border
market the evaluee can realistically obtain and hold given the documented
restrictions, work history, and transferable skills, with the reasoning
shown rather than assumed.

## By-appointment service, stated plainly

The firm keeps no staffed office in Moorhead and publishes no street
address for this location, because accuracy about presence belongs to the
same discipline that governs the reports. Counsel conferences are by
appointment, most often by video. Records review, structured interviews,
and standardized testing proceed remotely under publisher protocols where
remote administration is supported; when the referral questions call for
in-person evaluation, or when deposition and trial dates require presence,
travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office. Coverage of the wider
metro is described on the [Fargo page](/locations/north-dakota/fargo/), and
the [nationwide page](/locations/nationwide/) shows the same model applied
across the country.
