## The Mankato venue footprint

Engagements are accepted for matters in the District Court sitting in Blue
Earth County and Nicollet County, and in the United States District Court
for the District of Minnesota. Depositions and trial testimony are provided
in person or by remote appearance where the court permits. Referrals that
arise elsewhere in southern Minnesota or beyond the region are handled
through the same intake process, and the
[Minnesota locations page](/locations/minnesota/) sets out the firm's
statewide availability.

## What counsel should expect logistically

Mankato meetings are by appointment. The firm has no staffed office in the
metro and publishes no street address for this location; stating that
plainly is part of the same candor the reports are built on. The working
relationship is structured instead around the case calendar: conflict
checks answered promptly, evaluation dates set against disclosure deadlines
at intake, and evaluation sessions held in the evaluee's community, at
counsel's offices, or by secure video under publisher protocols. When the
referral questions call for in-person evaluation, or when deposition and
trial dates require presence, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home
office.

## Measuring the Mankato labor market

An earning capacity opinion for a Mankato evaluee has to reflect the
occupational structure of this region, not a generic statewide average and
not a larger metro's wage profile. Vocational findings rest on Bureau of
Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Mankato
metropolitan area and for Minnesota statewide, state workforce agency labor
market information, and direct labor market research, including employer
contact and job posting analysis, when the referral questions require
evidence about actual hiring in the communities where the evaluee lives and
works. The report cites every source it relies on, describes the market in
qualitative terms, and confines its conclusions to what the record
supports, so the same document serves direct examination and survives
cross.
