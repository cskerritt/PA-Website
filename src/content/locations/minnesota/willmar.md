Plaintiff firms and defense firms bring Purinton Analytics the same
questions in different postures: what can this person still do, what will
that work pay, and what will the injury cost over a lifetime of care. The
firm answers those questions for Willmar, MN counsel on either side of the
caption, through [earning capacity and employability evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. Because the method does not change with the client,
the opinion does not change with the caption.

## Venues for Willmar matters

The Willmar micropolitan area consists of Kandiyohi County. Engagements are
accepted for matters venued in the District Court there and in the United
States District Court for the District of Minnesota. Opinions are prepared
with the disclosure requirements of the chosen forum in mind, and drafts
are never split into a plaintiff version and a defense version. Counsel
whose dockets reach beyond Kandiyohi County can consult the
[Minnesota locations page](/locations/minnesota/) for the firm's statewide
coverage; the conflict check process is the same wherever in the state the
matter sits.

## By-appointment presence, stated plainly

The firm has no staffed office in Willmar and publishes no street address
for this location. Willmar meetings are by appointment, and most
engagements are conducted remotely from intake forward: structured records
review, vocational interviews by secure video, and standardized testing
under publisher protocols where remote administration is supported. When
the referral questions require an in-person evaluation, or when a
deposition or trial date requires the expert's presence, travel is
scheduled from the Kansas City home office and planned around the dates
counsel cannot move. Whatever the format, counsel receive a consistent
report standard and a single point of contact from conflict check through
testimony.

## Willmar labor market and cost foundations

An opinion about a Kandiyohi County evaluee's employability has to describe
the labor market that evaluee actually faces. The firm's vocational
findings rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and
wage estimates for Minnesota statewide and for the regional area that
includes Kandiyohi County, on state workforce agency labor market
information, and on direct labor market research where the questions
warrant it. Sources are cited in the report so that the analysis can be
checked line by line. Life care plans and medical cost projections for
area residents are priced to the evaluee's own market using
provider-specific inquiries and published fee data, since the local cost of
care can sit above or below national figures depending on the service
category.

## Retaining the firm for a Willmar case

Every engagement, for either side, begins the same way: with conflicts. To
determine availability, [request a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) with the
case caption and parties, and note any disclosure, deposition, or trial
dates that constrain scheduling. Any venue-specific registration or
qualification requirement is identified and confirmed during the conflict
check and intake process. Counsel comparing the firm's approach across
jurisdictions can review the [nationwide coverage page](/locations/nationwide/),
which describes the remote-first model that makes a Willmar engagement
practical without a local storefront.
