Expert deadlines decide more Pine Island cases than expert opinions do. A well-supported vocational or care-planning report that arrives after the disclosure window closes helps no one, which is why Purinton Analytics organizes every Pine Island engagement around the case calendar from the first call. The firm provides [vocational expert](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) and [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) services to counsel on both sides of the caption, and it treats candor about schedule the same way it treats candor about the evidence: if the remaining time will not support careful work, counsel hear that at intake, not at the deadline.

## Working to the disclosure calendar

Intake for a Pine Island matter begins by mapping the dates that govern it: expert disclosure, any independent evaluation window, rebuttal periods, and deposition and trial settings. Scope is then defined so the report can be complete and defensible within that frame. Records requests go out early because medical, employment, and educational records drive both the vocational analysis and any care plan, and slow-arriving records are the most common threat to an expert schedule. When rebuttal work is the assignment, [rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) engagements follow the same calendar-first approach on compressed timelines.
