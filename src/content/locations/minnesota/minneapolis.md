The Minneapolis-St. Paul-Bloomington metro is one of the largest labor
markets in the region, and its litigation reflects that breadth: personal injury, medical malpractice, employment, and
disability matters that turn on what an injured person can still do and
earn. Purinton Analytics serves plaintiff and defense counsel throughout the
metro with [vocational evaluation and expert testimony](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[rebuttal or peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) of opposing
expert reports. Catastrophic cases frequently add
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Venue footprint across the metro

Engagements are accepted for matters in the District Court venues serving
Anoka County, Carver County, Chisago County, Dakota County, Hennepin
County, Isanti County, Le Sueur County, Mille Lacs County, Ramsey County,
Scott County, Sherburne County, Washington County, and Wright County. Federal matters proceed in the United States District Court
for the District of Minnesota. Because the metro reaches across the state
line, referrals arising in Pierce County, Wisconsin, and St. Croix County,
Wisconsin, are accepted on the same terms, and matters elsewhere in the
state are covered through the [Minnesota locations page](/locations/minnesota/).

## Meetings by appointment, not a storefront

Minneapolis meetings are by appointment. The firm maintains no staffed
office in the metro and publishes no street address for this location,
because accuracy about presence belongs to the same discipline that governs
its reports. What counsel can count on instead is a defined process:
conflict checks answered promptly, evaluation dates set against the
disclosure calendar at intake, and depositions and trial testimony provided
in person or by remote appearance where the court permits. When the referral
calls for in-person evaluation or courtroom presence, travel is scheduled
from the Kansas City home office.

## Labor market research at metro scale

A market this large and occupationally diverse cuts both ways in
litigation: broader post-injury placement options for some evaluees,
sharper wage stratification for others. The analysis measures rather than
assumes. Employability and earning capacity findings rest on Bureau of
Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the
Minneapolis-St. Paul-Bloomington metropolitan area and for Minnesota
statewide, state workforce agency labor market information, and direct
labor market research, including employer contact and job posting analysis,
when the referral questions require evidence about actual hiring in a
particular corridor of the metro. The report presents those findings
qualitatively, cites each source, and separates what the data supports
from what it cannot, so the opinion holds up under cross-examination
whichever side commissioned it.

## Starting a Minneapolis engagement

Every engagement in the metro begins the same way the practice begins
everywhere: a documented conflict check, a defined scope, and a records
request tailored to the claims, so the written opinion rests on a record
both sides can examine. Submit the caption or party names through the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and note any imminent disclosure,
deposition, or trial dates. CVs and fee information follow promptly, and
the [national coverage page](/locations/nationwide/) describes remote
evaluation logistics, travel, and the full geographic reach of the
practice.
