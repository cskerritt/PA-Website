## Rice County venue and the courts that serve it

Northfield is a Rice County community, and state-court matters arising here
proceed in the District Court serving Rice County. When a claim runs through
the federal system instead, Minnesota's federal trial forum is the United
States District Court for the District of Minnesota; the controlling venue
for a particular matter, together with any registration or qualification
requirement that venue carries, is identified and confirmed during the
conflict check and intake process. The opinion itself is not rewritten for
the forum. Sources are cited, methods are stated, and the reasoning from
records to conclusions is set out so the report holds up wherever the
caption lands.

## Reading earning capacity from Northfield

A responsible earning capacity analysis for a Northfield evaluee starts with
data that actually exists at a defensible level. Vocational findings rest on
Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for
the Faribault-Northfield area and for Minnesota statewide, supplemented by
state workforce agency labor market information and by direct labor market
research when the occupations in dispute call for it. The question the
report then answers is concrete: given this work history, this education,
and these restrictions, which occupations can this person realistically
obtain and hold from Northfield, and what does that access mean for the
damages model. Where the answer depends on judgment, the judgment is
explained, not asserted.
