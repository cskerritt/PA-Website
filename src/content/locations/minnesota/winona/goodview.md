## Earning capacity in a small labor market

A Goodview evaluee's employment prospects are shaped by the labor market of the Winona, MN area, where the principal city of Winona anchors most of the hiring within practical commuting reach. Assessing residual earning capacity in a market this size requires more care, not less: the range of employers is narrower than in a major metropolitan area, and an opinion that assumes big-city occupational variety will not survive cross-examination. Vocational findings therefore rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the relevant area and for Minnesota statewide, supplemented by state workforce agency labor market information and direct labor market research where the referral questions warrant it. Every source is cited in the report so opposing counsel can trace each conclusion to its data.
