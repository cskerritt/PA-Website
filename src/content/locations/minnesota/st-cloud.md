Every St. Cloud engagement starts with process, not promises: a documented
conflict check, a scope defined in writing, and a records request tailored
to the claims before any opinion work begins. Purinton Analytics brings
that structure to plaintiff and defense referrals across the St. Cloud
metro, offering
[vocational evaluation and expert testimony](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/),
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) of opposing
expert reports, and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Where St. Cloud matters are heard

Engagements are accepted for matters in the District Court sitting in
Stearns County and Benton County, and in the United States District Court
for the District of Minnesota. Depositions and trial testimony are provided
in person or by remote appearance where the court permits. When a referral
reaches beyond the metro, the same intake process applies statewide, as
described on the [Minnesota locations page](/locations/minnesota/).

## By appointment, and only by appointment

St. Cloud meetings are by appointment. The firm maintains no staffed office
in the metro and publishes no street address for this location, and it says
so plainly because accuracy about presence is part of the discipline
counsel is hiring. In exchange, the engagement runs on a firm calendar:
conflict checks are answered promptly, evaluation dates are set against the
disclosure schedule at intake, and evaluation sessions are held in the
evaluee's community, at counsel's offices, or by secure video under
publisher protocols. When in-person evaluation or courtroom presence is
required, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office and
coordinated with the case calendar rather than left to chance.

## Labor market analysis for the St. Cloud region

Employability and earning capacity opinions for St. Cloud area evaluees
are grounded in the market where those evaluees actually live and seek
work. The analysis rests on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational
employment and wage estimates for the St. Cloud metropolitan area and for
Minnesota statewide, state workforce agency labor market information, and
direct labor market research, including employer contact and job posting
analysis, when the referral questions require evidence about actual hiring
in the region. The report describes the market qualitatively, cites its
sources, and separates what the data shows from what it cannot show, so
that the opinion holds up under cross-examination from either side. Where
transferable skills analysis is part of the referral, the same sources
anchor the match between the evaluee's residual capacities and the
occupations actually present in the regional economy.

## Starting a St. Cloud engagement

St. Cloud referrals most often arise in personal injury, employment,
disability, and wrongful death matters, and the firm accepts work from
either side of the caption. Because the practice begins with intake rather
than argument, the first step is simple:
[request a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) with the case caption and party
names, and note any imminent disclosure, deposition, or trial dates. CVs
and fee information follow promptly. The
[national coverage page](/locations/nationwide/) explains remote evaluation
logistics, travel scheduling, and the broader geographic reach of the
practice.
