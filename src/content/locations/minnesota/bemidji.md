Distance from a major litigation market does not lower the standard a
defensible expert opinion has to meet, and it should not limit which experts
counsel can retain. Purinton Analytics built its practice on that premise,
and it serves the Bemidji, MN area the same way it serves the rest of the
country: [vocational expert witness](/services/vocational-expert-witness/)
work and [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) for plaintiff
and defense counsel alike, plus
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Courts serving Bemidji and Beltrami County

The Bemidji micropolitan area consists of Beltrami County, and the firm
accepts matters venued in the District Court there as well as in the United
States District Court for the District of Minnesota. Because a practice
concentrated in Bemidji often carries files from surrounding venues as
well, a conflict check can address related matters together; the
[Minnesota locations page](/locations/minnesota/) sets out the firm's
coverage across the state.

## Remote-first by design, present when it matters

The firm keeps no office in Bemidji and lists no street address for this
location. That is a deliberate choice, not an omission: the practice is
built to deliver its work wherever the case is, and it describes its
presence honestly. Records review, structured vocational interviews over
secure video, and standardized testing under publisher protocols carry most
engagements from intake through report without travel. When an in-person
evaluation is warranted, or when a deposition or trial requires the expert
before the court, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office and
planned around the dates that cannot move. Counsel work with a single point
of contact throughout, and the report reads the same whether the underlying
work was remote, in person, or a mix of formats.

## Labor market evidence for Bemidji evaluees

Employability and earning capacity conclusions for evaluees in the Bemidji
area are grounded in Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and
wage estimates for Minnesota statewide and for the regional area that
includes Beltrami County, in state workforce agency labor market
information, and in direct labor market research when the referral
questions call for it. The report shows its sources and its reasoning so
that the opinion can be tested rather than taken on faith. Life care plans
and medical cost projections for Beltrami County residents are priced to
the evaluee's own market through provider-specific inquiries and published
fee data, not to a national average that may not describe local care
availability.

qualification requirement is identified and confirmed during the conflict
check and intake process. Counsel who want the fuller picture of how a
remote-first national practice maintains a uniform standard can review the

matter.
