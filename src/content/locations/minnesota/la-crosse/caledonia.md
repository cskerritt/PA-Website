## Venue context in Houston County

State-court matters arising in Caledonia proceed in the District Court
serving Houston County, where injury, employment, and family law disputes
each raise their own vocational and care-planning questions. Claims with a
federal basis, or between diverse parties, may instead proceed in the United
States District Court for the District of Minnesota. Which forum controls a
given case is confirmed during the conflict check and intake rather than
assumed from geography, and the answer drives the engagement plan:
retained-expert disclosure deadlines, rebuttal windows, and deposition
availability all flow from the operative scheduling order. The firm
calendars the report against those dates from the first conversation, so
counsel is not requesting extensions late in discovery. The statewide court
structure and additional Minnesota venues are described on the
[Minnesota locations page](/locations/minnesota/).

## Choosing the labor market frame

Caledonia is a small community within the
[La Crosse and Onalaska metro](/locations/wisconsin/la-crosse/), and the
first analytical decision in an earning-capacity opinion for a Caledonia
evaluee is which labor market frame fairly represents that person's
realistic options. The firm treats the choice as a finding to be justified,
not a default to be applied: every report states the geographic frame it
used and explains why that frame, and not another, fits the evaluee's
circumstances. The underlying data come from Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates for the La Crosse and Onalaska
metropolitan area and for Minnesota statewide, from state workforce agency
labor market information, and from direct labor market research when the
referral questions call for it. Where lost earnings must ultimately be
expressed in dollars for trial, counsel can pair the vocational work with
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Evaluation logistics for a Caledonia evaluee

There is no staffed Purinton Analytics location in Caledonia, and the firm
says so plainly, because candor about presence belongs to the same
discipline that governs its conclusions. Service is by appointment. Most
engagements open remotely, with records review, a structured vocational
interview over secure video, and standardized instruments administered under
protocols designed for remote use. When the referral questions require an
in-person evaluation, or when a deposition or trial date calls for
attendance, travel is arranged from the Kansas City home office and the
evaluee is seen in their own community, so functional and vocational
findings rest on the setting in which the person actually lives and works.
The same procedures govern the firm's
[nationwide practice](/locations/nationwide/).
