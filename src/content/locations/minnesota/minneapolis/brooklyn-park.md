Every earning capacity question in a Brooklyn Park case begins with a work
history. Before any data series is consulted and before venue is even
discussed, the analysis has to establish what the evaluee has actually done
for a living, what skills that history demonstrates, and which of those
skills survive the restrictions the medical record imposes. Purinton
Analytics builds its Brooklyn Park opinions from that foundation for
plaintiff and defense counsel alike, offering
[vocational expert opinions](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Venue for a Brooklyn Park filing

Brooklyn Park lies in Hennepin County, and state-court matters arising
here proceed in the District Court serving Hennepin County. Minnesota's
federal trial court is the United States District Court for the District
of Minnesota. Which forum controls a particular claim is counsel's call,
and the operative venue, together with any venue-specific registration or
qualification requirement, is confirmed during the conflict check and
intake. The work history at the center of the opinion does not change with
the courtroom, and neither does the file that documents it.

## Skills, restrictions, and the metro market

A documented work history only matters when it is measured against a real
market, and for a Brooklyn Park evaluee that market is the
Minneapolis-St. Paul-Bloomington metropolitan area. Transferable skills
analysis maps the evaluee's demonstrated history to the occupations
genuinely open under the medical restrictions of record, and the
availability and pay of those occupations are established through Bureau
of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the
metropolitan area and for Minnesota statewide, state workforce agency
labor market information, and direct labor market research when the
referral questions require evidence beyond the published series. Every
finding carries its source, so a disputed conclusion can be traced rather
than argued in the abstract. The regional practice is described on the
[Minneapolis metro page](/locations/minnesota/minneapolis/).

## Evaluation logistics for a Brooklyn Park evaluee

There is no Purinton Analytics office in Brooklyn Park, and the firm does
not publish an address suggesting otherwise; meetings are by appointment.
The work history itself is usually established at distance. Records are
read in full before anyone speaks with the evaluee, the interview proceeds
by video under a documented protocol, and standardized instruments are
administered under their publishers' protocols, remotely where the
publisher supports remote administration, with the testing conditions
recorded in the file. When the referral questions call for presence, the
evaluation is conducted in the evaluee's own community, and travel for
deposition or trial testimony is scheduled from the Kansas City home
office. The same model serves engagements in every state, as the
[nationwide page](/locations/nationwide/) describes.
