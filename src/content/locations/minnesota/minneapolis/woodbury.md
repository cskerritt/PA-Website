The deliverable in a Woodbury engagement is a written report, and
Purinton Analytics plans the engagement around that document from the
outset. Everything upstream, the records request, the structured
interview, the choice of data series, exists so that the finished report
states its methods plainly, cites a source for each finding, and reads
the same whether the person holding it retained the firm or opposes it.
Woodbury referrals are accepted from plaintiff and defense counsel alike,
for [vocational expert opinions](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## What the report contains

A vocational report for a Woodbury matter sets out the evaluee's
documented work history, the restrictions the medical record supports,
the occupations that remain open under those restrictions, and the wages
those occupations command in the relevant market, with each conclusion
tied to a named source. A life care plan does the same for future care:
needs grounded in the medical record, items and services stated with
their basis, and costs traceable to cited data rather than assertion.
Where the record does not support a conclusion, the report says so,
because a candid limitation reads better in litigation than a stretched
one.

## Washington County venue, state and federal

Woodbury sits in Washington County, and state-court matters arising here
are heard in the District Court serving Washington County. Minnesota's
federal trial court is the United States District Court for the District
of Minnesota; which forum controls a given claim is counsel's
determination, and the operative venue, along with any venue-specific
registration or qualification requirement, is confirmed during the
conflict check and intake. Because the report is written to be examined
rather than merely filed, it holds in either courtroom without revision.

## How the underlying work proceeds

The firm keeps no premises in Woodbury and publishes no local address;
service is by appointment, and candor about presence is part of the
product. Most engagements run at distance. The full record is read before
anyone interviews the evaluee, and the interview itself proceeds by video
against a documented protocol. Where a standardized instrument's
publisher permits remote administration, testing is done remotely and the
testing conditions are logged in the file alongside everything else. When
the questions require presence, the in-person evaluation is held in the
evaluee's own community, and travel for deposition or trial testimony is
scheduled from the Kansas City home office. The identical structure
serves referrals across the country, per the
[nationwide page](/locations/nationwide/).
