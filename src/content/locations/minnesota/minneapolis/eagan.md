Not every expert assignment in an Eagan case asks for a fresh opinion.
Often the report that matters most is the one the other side has already
served, and the question for counsel is whether its methods, sources, and
conclusions withstand scrutiny. Purinton Analytics takes affirmative and
responsive assignments on equal footing and accepts Eagan referrals from
plaintiff and defense counsel alike, providing
[vocational expert opinions](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Rebuttal held to the same standard

A [rebuttal engagement](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) examines an
opposing expert's report the way the firm expects its own to be examined:
are the data sources named and current, do the stated methods actually
produce the stated conclusions, does the work history support the
transferable skills claimed, and does the care plan trace to the medical
record? The review applies the same standard the firm accepts against its
own work, no harsher and no gentler, and it reports what it finds either
way. Where the opposing analysis is sound, saying so is part of the
service, because counsel is better served by an honest assessment than by
a reflexive attack.

## Dakota County venue and the federal forum

Eagan sits in Dakota County; state-court matters arising here are heard
in the District Court serving Dakota County. Minnesota's federal trial
court is the United States District Court for the District of Minnesota,
and the controlling venue for a specific claim, together with any
venue-specific registration or qualification requirement, is identified
with counsel during the conflict check and intake. Affirmative or
responsive, the work product is indifferent to the forum: one documented
file, one methodology, either courtroom.

## Measuring the market for an Eagan evaluee

When the assignment is affirmative, employment prospects for an Eagan
evaluee are measured across the Minneapolis-St. Paul-Bloomington
metropolitan area, where residents actually compete for work. Findings
rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage
estimates for the metropolitan area and for Minnesota statewide, joined
by state workforce agency labor market information and direct labor
market research where the referral questions warrant it. When the
assignment is responsive, the same sources become the yardstick against
which the opposing report's data choices are tested. The regional
practice appears in full on the
[Minneapolis metro page](/locations/minnesota/minneapolis/).

## How service is delivered here

No office is kept in Eagan, and no address is published for this
location; meetings occur by appointment. The default engagement is
remote, with records exchange, structured video interview, and
standardized testing under publisher protocols where remote
administration is supported and documented. When in-person evaluation
serves the questions better, it is conducted in the evaluee's own
community, and travel for deposition or trial testimony is scheduled
from the Kansas City home office. The same delivery model supports
matters in every state, as described on the
[nationwide page](/locations/nationwide/).
