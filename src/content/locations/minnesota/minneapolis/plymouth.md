The commute defines the vocational question in Plymouth. Residents here
hold jobs across the Minneapolis-St. Paul-Bloomington metropolitan area,
and employers in Plymouth draw workers from well beyond it, so when an
injury or termination interrupts a career, the relevant market is the
metro, not the municipality. Purinton Analytics analyzes that market for
plaintiff and defense counsel, producing
[vocational expert opinions](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) on
employability and earning capacity,
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) where injuries are
catastrophic, and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## A commuter's labor market, measured properly

Because Plymouth's workforce is metro-wide by habit, vocational findings
for a Plymouth evaluee are grounded in Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates for the
Minneapolis-St. Paul-Bloomington metropolitan area and for Minnesota
statewide, with state workforce agency labor market information and
direct labor market research added where the referral questions justify
it. The transferable skills analysis asks which occupations remain open
given the restrictions documented in the medical record and which of
those occupations are realistically within reach across the metro; the report
then cites the source behind every wage and employment finding it makes.
The regional practice as a whole is set out on the
[Minneapolis metro page](/locations/minnesota/minneapolis/).

## Venue for Plymouth matters

Plymouth lies in Hennepin County, and state-court cases arising here
proceed in the District Court serving Hennepin County. Federal claims in
Minnesota are heard in the United States District Court for the District
of Minnesota. Which forum controls a specific matter is determined by
counsel, and the firm confirms the operative venue, along with any
venue-specific registration or qualification requirement, during the
conflict check and intake. Nothing in the analysis is forum-dependent;
the same file supports state motion practice, federal disclosure, and
cross-examination in either courtroom.

## Meetings by appointment, work by design

No staffed Plymouth location exists, and the firm does not pretend
otherwise: honesty about offices is a small, visible instance of the
discipline counsel is actually buying. The engagement is structured so
that geography rarely matters. Records move through a defined transfer
channel, interviews follow a structured protocol by video, and
standardized testing proceeds under publisher protocols where remote
administration is supported, with conditions noted in the file. When the
questions require an in-person evaluation, it is conducted where the
evaluee lives; when testimony requires presence, travel is scheduled from
the Kansas City home office. Counsel with matters elsewhere in the
country will find the identical structure on the
[nationwide page](/locations/nationwide/).

## From referral to defined scope

An engagement exists only after a documented conflict check clears and
the scope is reduced to writing, so the practical first step is simple:
[request a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) with the case caption and
parties, and identify any disclosure, deposition, or trial dates that
will drive the schedule. Include a note on where the evaluee resides;
logistics are settled at intake, not improvised later. The firm's
coverage across the rest of the state is described on the
[Minnesota page](/locations/minnesota/).
