An expert opinion is only as good as its worst moment under
cross-examination, and every Maple Grove engagement at Purinton Analytics
is built with that moment in mind. The question is never merely what the
evaluee can earn or what care will cost, but whether each step from
record to conclusion survives a hostile reading. Counsel for plaintiffs
and counsel for defendants retain the firm on the same terms, for
[vocational expert opinions](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## A file built to be examined

Method discipline is concrete, not rhetorical. The record is reviewed
before the interview so questions test the documents rather than repeat
them. Standardized instruments are administered under publisher
protocols, with the setting and conditions logged. Every wage,
employment, and cost finding carries a citation to its source, and the
working file is maintained so that production in discovery holds no
surprises. When the assignment is rebuttal rather than an affirmative
opinion, the same standard is applied to the opposing report, no more
and no less harshly than the firm applies it to its own work.

## Where Maple Grove cases are tried

Maple Grove lies in Hennepin County, and state-court matters arising
here proceed in the District Court serving Hennepin County. Federal
litigation in Minnesota is heard in the United States District Court for
the District of Minnesota; which forum controls a particular claim is
counsel's determination, and the operative venue, along with any
venue-specific registration or qualification requirement, is confirmed
during the conflict check and intake.

## The employment market behind the earning capacity question

Maple Grove residents work across the
Minneapolis-St. Paul-Bloomington metropolitan area, so an honest earning
capacity analysis measures the metro, not the suburb alone. Findings
rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage
estimates for the metropolitan area and for Minnesota statewide,
supported by state workforce agency labor market information and by
direct labor market research when the referral questions require
evidence beyond the published series. Transferable skills analysis
connects the evaluee's documented history to occupations genuinely open
under the medical restrictions of record. The metro-level practice is
described on the
[Minneapolis metro page](/locations/minnesota/minneapolis/).

## Logistics without pretense

The firm operates no Maple Grove office and prints no address for one,
because a practice that documents everything else should not be vague
about where it actually sits. Engagements proceed remotely in the main:
secure records exchange, structured video interviews, and remote testing
where instruments permit it. In-person evaluation occurs in the
evaluee's own community when the questions demand presence, and travel
for deposition or trial is arranged from the Kansas City home office.
The same operating model covers referrals from every state, as the
[nationwide page](/locations/nationwide/) explains.
