Expert engagements are governed by calendars before they are governed by
anything else. In a Lakeville case, the disclosure deadline, the
deposition window, and the trial setting fix what is possible, and a
vocational or life care planning opinion commissioned too late arrives
compromised no matter how good the methodology. Purinton Analytics
builds Lakeville engagements backward from those dates, offering
plaintiff and defense counsel
[vocational expert opinions](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Sequencing the work against the case schedule

Intake fixes the timeline: which dates are already ordered, which
records exist and which must still be requested, and when the evaluee
can sit for interview and testing. From there the engagement runs in a
documented sequence, records review, structured interview, standardized
testing, labor market analysis, and a written report issued with enough
margin for counsel to use it, so that the opinion is complete before the
disclosure it supports. Rebuttal timelines get the same treatment when
the assignment is responsive rather than affirmative.

## Dakota County and the federal alternative

Lakeville sits in Dakota County, and state-court matters arising here
are heard in the District Court serving Dakota County. Minnesota's
federal trial forum is the United States District Court for the District
of Minnesota; the controlling venue for a given claim is a question for
counsel, confirmed along with any venue-specific registration or
qualification requirement during the conflict check and intake. The
report does not care which courtroom it ends up in: sources are cited,
methods are stated, and the file is kept so that either forum's scrutiny
finds the same disciplined record.
