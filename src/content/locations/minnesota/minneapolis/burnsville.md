because counsel deciding whether to retain an expert deserves the

## When remote is not enough

Some referral questions require presence: an evaluation best conducted
face to face, a functional context that video cannot capture, or simply
a trial date. In those cases the in-person evaluation is held in the
evaluee's own community rather than in any office of the firm's, and
travel for depositions and trial testimony is scheduled from the Kansas
City home office. The firm neither rents nor staffs space in Burnsville
and publishes no address here; the by-appointment model is stated
openly because a report that demands precision from others should
describe its own operations precisely.

## The courts a Burnsville matter may see

Burnsville lies in Dakota County, and state-court claims arising here
are tried in the District Court serving Dakota County. Federal claims
in Minnesota proceed in the United States District Court for the
District of Minnesota. Deciding which forum controls is counsel's
province; confirming the operative venue, and any venue-specific
registration or qualification requirement, is part of the firm's
conflict check and intake. The analysis is prepared to the same
standard either way, so removal or remand changes nothing in the file.
