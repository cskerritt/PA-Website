St. Paul is not a suburb of anywhere. It is a principal city of the metro
whose formal name it shares, and litigation arising here carries the full
range of vocational and care-planning questions that any major city
produces: lost earning capacity after injury, employability after
termination, and lifetime care needs after catastrophic harm. Purinton
Analytics addresses those questions for counsel on either side of the
caption, providing
[vocational expert opinions](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Ramsey County venue and the federal forum

St. Paul sits in Ramsey County, and state-court matters arising here are
heard in the District Court serving Ramsey County. Minnesota's federal
forum is the United States District Court for the District of Minnesota;
whether a particular claim belongs in state or federal court is counsel's
venue call, and the firm confirms the operative forum, along with any
venue-specific registration or qualification requirement, during the
conflict check and intake. The written opinion is built to hold in either
setting, because the methodology, the source citations, and the file
documentation do not change when the caption does.

## Reading the labor market from a principal city

A St. Paul evaluee competes for work across an employment market anchored
by both principal cities, not within municipal boundaries. Earning
capacity findings therefore rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates for the
Minneapolis-St. Paul-Bloomington metropolitan area and for Minnesota
statewide, together with state workforce agency labor market information
and direct labor market research when the referral questions call for it.
Transferable skills analysis maps the evaluee's demonstrated work history
against occupations realistically reachable from St. Paul, and each wage
and employment source relied on appears in the report so that the finding
can be traced, checked, and cross-examined. The broader regional practice
is described on the [Minneapolis metro page](/locations/minnesota/minneapolis/).

## Appointments, not an address

The firm maintains no staffed office in St. Paul and lists no street
address for this location, because accuracy about presence belongs to the
same discipline that governs the reports themselves. What counsel gets
instead is a practice built to work at distance without losing rigor:
records review and structured interviews conducted remotely, standardized
testing administered under publisher protocols where remote administration
is supported, and in-person evaluation in the evaluee's own community when
the questions require it. Deposition and trial appearances are scheduled
from the Kansas City home office and confirmed against the case calendar
at intake. The same approach serves engagements throughout the country, as
the [nationwide page](/locations/nationwide/) explains.

## Opening a St. Paul engagement

Every file starts with a documented conflict check and a defined scope,
before any opinion work begins. Send the caption or party names through
the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/), flag any disclosure,
deposition, or trial date already on the calendar, and describe the
questions the case actually presents, whether they concern employability,
earning capacity, future care, or all of them together. Coverage across
the rest of the state, including the other district courts and the
firm's Minnesota locations, is laid out on the
[Minnesota page](/locations/minnesota/).
