Sartell is one of the larger residential communities in the St. Cloud
metropolitan area, and its workers move through the wider metro rather than
inside city limits. That commuting pattern is where a serious earning
capacity analysis has to start. When counsel on either side of the caption
retains Purinton Analytics for a Sartell matter, the question is never what
jobs exist on a national printout; it is what work a person with this
medical record, this history, and these restrictions can obtain and sustain
in the labor market that actually surrounds them. The firm answers that
question through [vocational expert opinions](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Stearns County venue and the federal forum

Sartell matters proceed in the District Court serving Stearns County when
they are tried in the state system. Minnesota's federal forum is the
United States District Court for the District of Minnesota, and the
controlling venue for any specific case, along with any registration or
qualification requirement that venue carries, is identified and confirmed
during the conflict check and intake process. The report is drafted so
that it holds up in either system: a defined scope, a documented records
basis, and opinions expressed with stated methods that opposing counsel
can test line by line.

## Earning capacity in a commuting market

Because Sartell residents work across the metro, vocational findings are
grounded in Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage
estimates for the St. Cloud metropolitan area and for Minnesota statewide,
supplemented by state workforce agency labor market information and by
direct labor market research when the occupations in dispute call for it.
The analysis stays qualitative about the market and specific about the
person: which occupational paths remain open, which are foreclosed by
documented restrictions, and what the transferable skills evidence
actually supports. Those judgments are explained in the written opinion,
not assumed.

## Meeting and evaluating in Sartell

The firm keeps no office in Sartell and publishes no local address,
because a practice that certifies the accuracy of its opinions owes the
same accuracy about its own footprint. Counsel conferences are held by
appointment, most often by video. Records review, structured interviews,
and standardized testing proceed remotely under publisher protocols where
remote administration is supported; when the referral questions require
an in-person evaluation, the evaluation is scheduled in the evaluee's own
community, with travel arranged from the Kansas City home office. The
same by-appointment model covers depositions and trial testimony, and it
