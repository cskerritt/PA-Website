Purinton Analytics has no office in St. Augusta. That admission opens
this page deliberately, because expert directories are full of
letterhead addresses that turn out to be mail drops, and a witness whose
marketing shades the truth invites the same suspicion of his reports.
What St. Augusta counsel actually gets is a by-appointment practice that
serves the community without pretending to occupy it:
[vocational expert opinions](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure, available to plaintiff and defense referrals on
identical terms.

## Venue and county context

St. Augusta lies in Stearns County. Its state-court matters are heard in
the District Court serving Stearns County, and federal matters proceed
in the United States District Court for the District of Minnesota.
Determining which forum controls a given case, and satisfying any
qualification or registration requirement that forum imposes, happens
during the conflict check and intake process, before analysis begins.
Reports are drafted once, to the standard of the stricter reader, so the
opinion travels intact between systems.

## The metro labor market seen from St. Augusta

St. Augusta is a small community whose workers look to the surrounding
St. Cloud region for employment, and a credible earning capacity opinion
has to be built at that regional scale. The firm relies on Bureau of
Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the
St. Cloud metropolitan area and for Minnesota statewide, on state
workforce agency labor market information, and on direct labor market
research where the disputed occupations call for employer-level
confirmation. Against that market backdrop, the analysis turns
individual: documented restrictions, demonstrated skills, and the
occupations this evaluee can realistically secure and keep, each
conclusion tied to a cited source in the written report. When the
evidence points away from a tidy answer, the report follows the
evidence, because credibility spent on advocacy is never recovered on
the stand.

## Scheduling evaluations and testimony

Counsel conferences are held by appointment, usually by video. For the
evaluee, records review, structured interviews, and standardized testing
proceed remotely under publisher protocols where remote administration
is supported; an in-person evaluation, when the referral questions
require it, is scheduled in the evaluee's own community, sparing an
injured person the burden of traveling to be examined. Travel for
evaluations, depositions, and trial appearances is arranged from the
Kansas City home office with dates fixed at intake, so counsel never learns of a
scheduling problem after the disclosure deadline has passed. The same service
structure is described for the whole region on the
[St. Cloud page](/locations/minnesota/st-cloud/) and for the national
practice on the [nationwide page](/locations/nationwide/).
