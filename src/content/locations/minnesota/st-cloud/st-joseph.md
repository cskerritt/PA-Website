trial setting that will not move. Purinton Analytics builds its intake
around those dates. At the conflict check stage counsel is asked for the

## County and courthouse: venue for St. Joseph matters

St. Joseph is a Stearns County city. Its state-court cases are heard in
the District Court serving Stearns County, and matters proceeding
federally go to the United States District Court for the District of
Minnesota. The venue that controls a specific dispute, and any
qualification or registration requirement attached to it, is identified
and confirmed with referring counsel during the conflict check and
intake process. Because either bench may ultimately read the report, it
is written for the more demanding audience from the start.

## The labor market question in St. Joseph

An earning capacity opinion for a St. Joseph evaluee cannot stop at the
city limits; residents here participate in the broader St. Cloud labor
market. The firm's vocational findings therefore rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the St. Cloud
metropolitan area and for Minnesota statewide, on state workforce agency
labor market information, and on direct labor market research where the
occupations in dispute justify it. The market description stays
qualitative; the individual analysis is where precision lives, tracing
documented restrictions and transferable skills to occupations the
evaluee can realistically obtain and hold. Each step of that reasoning
is sourced in the report itself, so opposing counsel can check the
chain rather than speculate about it.

## How the evaluation happens

There is no staffed Purinton Analytics office in St. Joseph and no
published local address, a fact the firm states rather than obscures.
Counsel conferences run by appointment, typically by video. Interviews
and standardized testing proceed remotely under publisher protocols
where remote administration is supported, and when the referral
questions call for an in-person evaluation, it takes place in the
evaluee's own community, with travel arranged from the Kansas City home
office. Deposition and trial dates are locked in during intake. The
same model is laid out metro-wide on the
[St. Cloud page](/locations/minnesota/st-cloud/) and nationally on the
[nationwide page](/locations/nationwide/).
