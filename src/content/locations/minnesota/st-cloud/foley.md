Damages cases from Foley rarely need just a vocational opinion; they
usually need vocational, medical-cost, and economic evidence that fit
together without contradicting each other. Purinton Analytics is
organized around that fit. Counsel can retain
[vocational expert opinions](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) or
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) alone, or bring the
strands together through
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure, so the loss figures presented at trial rest on a
consistent factual spine. Referrals are welcome from the plaintiff bar
and the defense bar alike.

## Benton County courts

Foley is a Benton County city, and its state-court litigation proceeds
in the District Court serving Benton County. Cases that proceed
federally are heard in the United States District Court for the
District of Minnesota. Confirming the controlling venue for a specific
matter, and any registration or qualification requirement that attaches
to it, is part of the conflict check and intake process. Because the
report is built to disclosure standards from the first draft, it does
not need rewriting if the forum changes.

## Qualitative grounding in the regional labor market

Foley is a small community whose workers are part of the broader
St. Cloud regional economy, so the earning capacity analysis is
anchored regionally. The firm draws on Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates for the St. Cloud
metropolitan area and for Minnesota statewide, on state workforce
agency labor market information, and on direct labor market research
when the occupations at issue call for direct employer verification.
Market scale stays qualitative in the report; the precision is spent on
the evaluee, whose restrictions, skills, and realistic occupational
options are traced through cited evidence rather than asserted. If a
coordinated engagement is in place, those vocational findings feed the
economist partners' loss calculations directly, keeping the damages
presentation internally consistent from wage base to bottom line.

## Evaluation and testimony logistics

Purinton Analytics maintains no office and lists no address in Foley,
and it will say so anywhere the question arises, because accurate
self-description is the cheapest credibility a witness owns. Meetings
with counsel are by appointment, generally by video. Structured
interviews and standardized testing are administered remotely under
publisher protocols where remote administration is supported; when the
referral questions require an in-person evaluation, it is held in the
evaluee's own community. Travel for evaluations, depositions, and trial
testimony is scheduled from the Kansas City home office, with the
relevant court dates confirmed at intake so the calendar holds from
retention through testimony. The regional
service model appears on the
[St. Cloud page](/locations/minnesota/st-cloud/), and the
[nationwide page](/locations/nationwide/) presents the national
structure.
