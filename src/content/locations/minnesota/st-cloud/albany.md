Process is the product. An Albany engagement with Purinton Analytics
begins with a documented conflict check, moves to a written scope tied
to the referral questions, and only then reaches the records, the
evaluee, and the analysis, so every step of the file can be reconstructed
under oath. Counsel on either side of an Albany matter can retain the
firm for [vocational expert opinions](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. The discipline is the same in a small-town file as
in a metropolitan one, because opposing counsel will read the report
either way.

## Stearns County venue, state and federal

Albany sits in Stearns County, and its state-court cases are tried in
the District Court serving Stearns County. Matters proceeding federally
go to the United States District Court for the District of Minnesota.
The controlling venue for a particular dispute, along with any
venue-specific registration or qualification requirement, is identified
and confirmed during the conflict check and intake process. Disclosure
materials are prepared to satisfy whichever forum ultimately governs,
and the intake record preserves how that determination was made.

## Labor market analysis for a small community

Albany is a small community, and the firm says so in its reports rather
than dressing the town up as an employment hub; residents participate
in the wider St. Cloud regional labor market, and that is the market
the analysis measures. Employability and earning capacity findings rest
on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates
for the St. Cloud metropolitan area and for Minnesota statewide, on
state workforce agency labor market information, and on direct labor
market research where contested occupations require employer-level
checking. The opinion then narrows from market to person: what the
documented restrictions permit, what the work history demonstrates, and
which occupational doors the evidence shows open or shut. The sourcing
travels with each conclusion, so a reviewing expert retained by the
other side can reproduce the analysis step by step, which is precisely
the point.

## Reaching an Albany evaluee

The firm keeps no Albany office and publishes no address here; service
is by appointment, and candor about that is part of the brand. Records
review, structured interviews, and standardized testing are conducted
remotely under publisher protocols where remote administration is
supported. When the referral questions require an in-person evaluation,
the expert travels to the evaluee's community rather than the reverse,
and when deposition or trial testimony requires courtroom presence,
travel is arranged from the Kansas City home office on dates confirmed
during intake. The regional logistics are described on the
[St. Cloud page](/locations/minnesota/st-cloud/); the
[nationwide page](/locations/nationwide/) shows the practice at full
scale.
