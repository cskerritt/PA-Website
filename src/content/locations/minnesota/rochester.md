Catastrophic injury and medical malpractice litigation turns on questions
that outlast the trial date: what care an injured person will need across a
lifetime, and what work remains open to them. For Rochester counsel,
Purinton Analytics answers both questions under a defensible methodology,
pairing [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) and
[medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/) with
[vocational evaluation and expert testimony](/services/vocational-expert-witness/).
Where the damages model requires present-value economic analysis, the firm
offers [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. Referrals are accepted from plaintiff and defense
counsel on equal terms.

## Venues in and around Olmsted County

Engagements are accepted for matters in the District Court sitting in
Olmsted County, Dodge County, Fillmore County, and Wabasha County, and in
the United States District Court for the District of Minnesota. Depositions
and trial testimony are provided in person or by remote appearance where
the court permits. Referrals that arise outside the Rochester metro are
covered through the same intake process, and the
[Minnesota locations page](/locations/minnesota/) describes the firm's
statewide availability.

## Presence, stated accurately

Rochester meetings are by appointment. The firm has no staffed office here
and publishes no street address for this location, because a practice that
asks juries to trust its numbers should be equally exact about its own
footprint. What counsel receives instead is a structured engagement:
conflict checks answered promptly, scope defined in writing, evaluation
sessions held in the evaluee's community, at counsel's offices, or by
secure video under publisher protocols, and travel scheduled from the
Kansas City home office when in-person evaluation or courtroom presence is
required.

## Grounding opinions in the regional labor market

Rochester's labor market has its own occupational structure, and earning
capacity opinions must reflect it rather than borrow from a larger metro.
Vocational findings for Rochester area evaluees rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Rochester
metropolitan area and for Minnesota statewide, state workforce agency labor
market information, and direct labor market research, including employer
contact and job posting analysis, when the referral questions require
evidence about actual hiring in the communities at issue. Life care plans
are costed from sources appropriate to the region where care will actually
be delivered, and every source is cited in the report so that the opinion
can be tested on cross-examination rather than taken on faith.

## Starting a Rochester engagement

Rochester referrals most often involve medical malpractice, personal
injury, wrongful death, employment, and disability claims, and the firm
works for either side of the caption. Every engagement begins with a
documented conflict check, a defined scope, and a records request tailored
to the claims, so the written opinion rests on a record both sides can
examine. Submit the caption or party names through the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and flag any imminent disclosure,
deposition, or trial dates. CVs and fee schedules follow promptly, and the
[national coverage page](/locations/nationwide/) explains remote evaluation
logistics and the practice's full geographic reach.
