When an injury or employment dispute in Red Wing turns on what work an
evaluee can still perform and what that capacity is worth, counsel on
either side of the caption need findings that will hold up under
cross-examination, not advocacy dressed as analysis. Purinton Analytics
serves that need for the Red Wing, MN area with
[earning capacity and employability evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) in catastrophic injury
matters, and [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Where Red Wing matters are venued

The Red Wing micropolitan area consists of Goodhue County. Engagements are
accepted for matters in the District Court there and in the United States
District Court for the District of Minnesota, and the firm's opinions are
written to the same standard whichever forum will hear them. Counsel whose
practice extends beyond Goodhue County can review the firm's statewide
availability on the [Minnesota locations page](/locations/minnesota/); a
single conflict check can cover related matters in more than a single venue.

## How the firm appears in Red Wing

There is no staffed Purinton Analytics office in Red Wing, and the firm
publishes no street address for this location, because it will not imply a
presence it does not keep. Meetings are by appointment. The working model is
remote-first: records analysis, structured interviews conducted by secure
video, and standardized vocational testing under publisher protocols where
remote administration is supported. Where the referral questions require
in-person evaluation, or where deposition and trial calendars require the
expert in the room, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office
with enough lead time to protect the court's dates. The report standard and
the point of contact do not change with the format of the work.

## Grounding opinions in the local labor market

A Red Wing evaluee's employability is a question about a real labor market,
so the analysis starts with Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational
employment and wage estimates for Minnesota statewide and for the regional
area that includes Goodhue County, supplemented by state workforce agency
labor market information and direct employer research where warranted. Every
figure that reaches the report carries a citation, and the path from source
to conclusion is written out for examination. Life care plans and medical
cost projections for Goodhue County residents are priced to the evaluee's
own market using provider-specific inquiries and published fee data, since
local care costs can differ from national averages in either direction.
