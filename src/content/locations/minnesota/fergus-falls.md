## Venue coverage for Otter Tail County

Engagements are accepted for matters in the District Court sitting in
Otter Tail County and in the United States District Court for the District
of Minnesota. Depositions and trial testimony are provided in person or by
remote appearance where the court permits, and evaluation sessions are
held in the evaluee's community, at counsel's offices, or by secure video.
Matters arising elsewhere in the state are handled through the same
intake, as the [Minnesota locations page](/locations/minnesota/) describes.

## What remote-first means in practice

Fergus Falls meetings are by appointment, and the firm publishes no street
address for this location because it maintains no staffed office here.
Remote-first does not mean testimony-averse: when the referral questions
call for in-person evaluation, or when deposition and trial dates require
presence in the courtroom, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home
office and coordinated with the case calendar set at intake. The point of
the remote-first structure is that counsel in a smaller market gets the
same responsiveness, the same report quality, and the same disclosure-date
discipline as counsel in the largest metros, without paying for logistics
the case does not need.

## Earning capacity in an outstate market

An earning capacity opinion for an evaluee in Otter Tail County must
reflect the occupational mix of an outstate market, where placement
options concentrate differently than they do in a metropolitan core.
Vocational findings draw on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational
employment and wage estimates for the relevant area and for Minnesota
statewide, supplemented by state workforce agency labor market information
and by direct labor market research, including employer contact and job
posting analysis, when the referral questions require evidence about
actual hiring in the communities where the evaluee lives and can
realistically work. The
report presents those findings qualitatively and cites every source, so
either side can test the opinion against the record.
