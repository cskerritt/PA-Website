Counsel in Austin, MN retain Purinton Analytics for a defined set of
services rather than a general promise of expertise:
[vocational expert witness](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) evaluation
of employability and earning capacity in injury and employment matters,
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) where injuries will
require care for years to come, and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. Each service is available to plaintiff and defense
counsel on identical terms, and each produces a written opinion prepared
for examination by the other side.

## The Mower County venue

The Austin micropolitan area consists of Mower County, and the firm accepts
matters venued in the District Court there as well as in the United States
District Court for the District of Minnesota. The venue is confirmed at
intake so that scheduling, disclosure practice, and any qualification
questions are addressed before the work begins. Where a firm's caseload spans
additional Minnesota venues, the [Minnesota locations page](/locations/minnesota/)
describes statewide coverage, and related matters can be raised together in
a single conflict check rather than piecemeal.

## What by-appointment service means in Austin

There is no Purinton Analytics office in Austin, no local storefront, and
no published street address for this location; the firm states that plainly
because honesty about presence belongs to the same discipline that governs
its opinions. Meetings are scheduled by appointment. The bulk of each
engagement proceeds remotely: records analysis, structured interviews over
secure video, and standardized vocational testing under publisher protocols
where remote administration is supported. When the referral questions call
for in-person evaluation, or when deposition and trial dates require the
expert before the court, travel is arranged from the Kansas City home
office with the case calendar in hand. The format of the work changes with
the needs of the case; the report standard does not.

## Evidence about the Austin labor market

Employability conclusions for a Mower County evaluee are anchored in Bureau
of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for
Minnesota statewide and for the regional area that includes Mower County,
in state workforce agency labor market information, and in direct labor
market research where the referral questions warrant it. The report
identifies every source so the path from data to opinion stays visible.
Life care plans and medical cost projections for residents of the area are
priced to the evaluee's own market through provider-specific inquiries and
published fee data for the region, because a projection priced to a
national average can misstate local care costs in either direction.

## How an Austin engagement starts

The intake sequence is deliberately unglamorous: conflicts cleared, scope
defined, records requested. To set it in motion,
[request a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) with the case caption and the
parties, and identify any imminent disclosure, deposition, or trial dates.
Any venue-specific registration or qualification requirement is identified
and confirmed during the conflict check and intake process. The
[nationwide coverage page](/locations/nationwide/) shows how the same
sequence and the same reporting standard govern the firm's work across the
country, so counsel know an Austin referral receives nothing less.
