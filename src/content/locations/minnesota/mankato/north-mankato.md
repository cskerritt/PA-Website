The first question in a North Mankato retention is often jurisdictional
rather than vocational. The city lies in Nicollet County even though daily
working life is oriented toward Mankato, the metro's principal city, so
counsel should never assume that venue follows the commute. Purinton
Analytics is built for exactly that kind of care. Whether the firm is
retained by the plaintiff or by the defense, the engagement produces
[vocational expert opinions](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), or
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure, and every opinion is written to withstand the reader
who is paid to disagree with it.

## Nicollet County venue and the federal forum

State-court matters arising in North Mankato are heard in the District
Court serving Nicollet County, and the report is scoped to that setting
from the outset: a documented conflict check, a defined referral question,
and a records request tailored to the claims. Minnesota's federal forum
is the United States District Court for the District of Minnesota. Which
court controls a particular case, and whether that venue imposes any
registration or qualification requirement on a testifying expert, is
identified and confirmed during the conflict check and intake process
rather than assumed from geography.

## Reading the Mankato area labor market

A North Mankato evaluee competes for work across the metro, not within a
single municipality, so the vocational analysis is anchored in Bureau of
Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the
Mankato metropolitan area and for Minnesota statewide, along with state
workforce agency labor market information and direct labor market
research where the disputed occupations warrant it. All of it stays
qualitative about the market and concrete about the individual: the
occupations that remain realistic given documented restrictions, the
transferable skills the record actually supports, and the gap, if any,
between pre-injury and post-injury earning capacity. Each conclusion is
tied to its source so opposing counsel can trace the reasoning.

## How a North Mankato evaluation proceeds

There is no Purinton Analytics office in North Mankato, and the firm
will not pretend otherwise; stating its footprint plainly is part of the
same discipline that governs its reports. Conferences with counsel are
held by appointment, usually by video. Interviews and standardized
testing are administered remotely when publisher protocols support
remote administration, and when the referral calls for an in-person
evaluation, it takes place in the evaluee's own community, with travel
scheduled from the Kansas City home office. Depositions and trial
appearances are handled on the same by-appointment basis described on
the [Mankato metro page](/locations/minnesota/mankato/) and, for the
practice at large, on the [nationwide page](/locations/nationwide/).
