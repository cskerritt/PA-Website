Eagle Lake is a small community, and small communities are where careless
vocational work does the most damage. An evaluee who lives in a town this
size almost certainly earns a living somewhere in the wider Mankato area,
so an opinion that treats the town itself as the labor market will
understate opportunity, while an opinion built on national averages will
ignore the market the person actually faces. Purinton Analytics starts
from the commuting reality and works outward, whether the client is
plaintiff or defense counsel, delivering
[vocational expert opinions](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Blue Earth County and the federal courts

Before the market is analyzed, the forum is settled. Eagle Lake lies in
Blue Earth County, and a state-court action arising there is heard in
the District Court serving that county. When a matter proceeds
federally, Minnesota's forum is the United States District Court for
the District of Minnesota; the controlling venue for a given case, and
any registration or qualification requirement that venue imposes on a
testifying expert, is confirmed during the conflict check and intake
process rather than assumed. The report itself is venue-ready either
way, with scope, records basis, and methodology stated on the page
where opposing counsel can examine them.

## A commuter's labor market, measured honestly

With venue fixed, the vocational baseline for an Eagle Lake evaluee is
the Mankato metropolitan labor market, described through Bureau of
Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the
metro area and for Minnesota statewide, supplemented by state workforce
agency labor market information and by direct labor market research
when the occupations in dispute justify it. The written opinion keeps
the market description qualitative and puts its precision where
precision belongs: in the match between the evaluee's documented
restrictions, education, and work history and the occupations that
remain open. Transferable skills are demonstrated from the record, not
asserted, and the same rigor applies whichever party retained the firm.

## What retention looks like for a local evaluee

No storefront serves Eagle Lake, and the firm does not manufacture the
appearance of one; its only staffed office is the Kansas City home
office, and everything else is by appointment. In practice that means
counsel conferences by video, records review conducted remotely,
interviews and standardized testing administered under publisher
protocols that support remote use, and, when the referral questions
require presence, an in-person evaluation held in the evaluee's own
community with travel scheduled from Kansas City. Deposition and trial
appearances follow the same model. The
[Mankato metro page](/locations/minnesota/mankato/) describes the
area-wide practice, and the [nationwide page](/locations/nationwide/)
explains how the same structure serves venues across the country.
