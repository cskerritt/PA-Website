## What remote-first means in practice

Most of the work behind a defensible opinion is document work: medical records, employment files, earnings history, prior evaluations. That work is done the same way for a Wabasha evaluee as for one in a major city, under a defined scope set at intake. Structured interviews proceed by video or phone where appropriate; when the referral questions require direct observation, the evaluation is conducted in person in the evaluee's own community. The firm holds no office in Wabasha and claims none, a statement it makes plainly because honesty about presence is part of the product.
