## One checklist for writing and for reviewing

Whether the firm is authoring an opinion or auditing one, the questions are identical. Was the scope defined before the work began? Does every restriction, every placement conclusion, and every recommended item of care trace back to the medical record and to a named data source? Could the author defend each step in deposition without reaching beyond the file? A Dodge Center report from Purinton Analytics is built to pass that audit because the audit comes first, not afterward; a rebuttal assignment applies the same checklist to the opposing expert's work and reports plainly on what survives it.
