When an opposing expert's report lands in a Duluth case, counsel often need
more than a competing number: they need a disciplined review of the
methodology behind it. That is a common entry point for Purinton Analytics
in the Duluth, MN-WI metro, where
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) work sits
alongside first-chair
[vocational evaluation and testimony](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. The firm accepts referrals from plaintiff and defense
counsel alike, and the same written report is meant to withstand
cross-examination from either direction.

## Courts serving the Duluth metro

Engagements are accepted for matters in the District Court sitting in
St. Louis County and Carlton County, and in the United States District
Court for the District of Minnesota. Because the metro crosses the state
line, referrals arising in Douglas County, Wisconsin, are accepted on the
same terms. Depositions and trial testimony are provided in person or by
remote appearance where the court permits, and matters arising elsewhere in
the state are handled through the same intake process described on the
[Minnesota locations page](/locations/minnesota/).

## How by-appointment service works here

Duluth meetings are by appointment. The firm keeps no staffed office in the
metro and publishes no street address for this location; candor about
presence is part of the same standard that governs the reports themselves.
In practice, most of an engagement proceeds without travel: records review,
evaluee interviews by secure video under publisher protocols, and
counsel conferences by phone or video. When the referral questions call for
in-person evaluation, or when deposition and trial dates require presence,
travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office and planned around the
case calendar set at intake.

## Labor market evidence for a regional economy

A regional market like Duluth's demands more care in labor market analysis,
not less, because placement options and wage structures differ from those
in a large metropolitan core. Employability and earning capacity findings
rely on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage
estimates for the Duluth metropolitan area and for Minnesota statewide,
state workforce agency labor market information, and direct labor market
research, including employer contact and job posting analysis, when the
referral questions require evidence about actual hiring in the communities
where the evaluee lives and works. Findings are stated qualitatively in the
report only where the data supports them, and the underlying sources are
cited so opposing counsel can check the work.
