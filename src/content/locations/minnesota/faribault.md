District Court sitting in Rice County hears the injury, employment, and
disability matters that arise in Faribault, Northfield, and the
surrounding communities, and federal matters proceed in the United States
District Court for the District of Minnesota. Purinton Analytics accepts
engagements in both venues from plaintiff and defense counsel, providing
[vocational evaluation and expert testimony](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/),
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) of opposing
expert reports, and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic

## A single-county metro, served completely

The Faribault-Northfield micropolitan area consists of Rice County, which
keeps the venue picture simple and the service commitment clear:
depositions and trial testimony are provided in person or by remote
appearance where the court permits, and evaluation sessions are held in
the evaluee's community, at counsel's offices, or by secure video under
publisher protocols. Matters that arise outside Rice County are covered
through the same intake process, described statewide on the
[Minnesota locations page](/locations/minnesota/).

## Presence stated plainly

Faribault meetings are by appointment. The firm has no staffed office in
the area and publishes no street address for this location, because
precision about presence is part of the same discipline that governs every
report the practice signs. Counsel gets a defined engagement instead of a
storefront: conflict checks answered promptly, scope confirmed in writing,
evaluation dates set against the disclosure calendar at intake, and travel
scheduled from the Kansas City home office whenever in-person evaluation,
deposition, or trial testimony requires it.

## Labor market research beyond the county line

An evaluee living in Rice County may reasonably seek work across a wider
region, and the analysis follows the evaluee rather than an arbitrary
boundary. Employability and earning capacity findings rest on Bureau of
Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the
relevant area and for Minnesota statewide, together with state workforce
agency labor market information and direct labor market research,
including employer contact and job posting analysis, when the referral
questions require evidence about actual hiring within the evaluee's
realistic commuting range. The report describes those findings
qualitatively, cites its sources, and draws no conclusion the data cannot
carry, so the opinion holds up whichever party commissioned it and
however hard the other side presses on methodology.

## Starting a Faribault engagement

Referrals from Faribault and Northfield counsel most often involve
personal injury, wrongful death, employment, and disability claims, and
the firm works for either side of the caption. Every engagement begins
with a documented conflict check, a defined scope, and a records request
tailored to the claims, so the written opinion rests on a record both
sides can examine. Submit the caption or party names through the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and note any imminent disclosure,
deposition, or trial dates. CVs and fee information follow promptly, and
the [national coverage page](/locations/nationwide/) explains remote
evaluation logistics, travel, and the practice's broader reach.
