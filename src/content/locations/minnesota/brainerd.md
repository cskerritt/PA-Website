## Honest logistics for a smaller market

Brainerd meetings are by appointment. The firm keeps no staffed office here
and publishes no street address for this location, because a report that
will be cross-examined should come from a practice that is exact about its
own footprint. Most engagement work proceeds without travel at all: records
review, interviews by secure video, and counsel conferences by phone. When
the referral questions call for in-person evaluation, or when deposition
and trial dates require presence, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City
home office and set against the case calendar established at intake, so
that distance never becomes an excuse for delay.

## Reading a rural and regional labor market

Employability and earning capacity in a market like Brainerd's cannot be
inferred from big-city data. Placement options are fewer in some
occupations and concentrated in others, and the analysis has to measure
that reality rather than assume it away. Vocational findings rest on
Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for
the relevant area and for Minnesota statewide, on state workforce agency
labor market information, and on direct labor market research, including
employer contact and job posting analysis, when the referral questions
require evidence about actual hiring in the communities at issue. The report states its findings qualitatively and cites the
sources behind them.
