An East Grand Forks case raises a venue question before it raises a damages
question. The town lies on the Minnesota side of a metropolitan area whose
principal city, Grand Forks, sits across the state line, so an injured
worker may live under Minnesota law while earning wages in a market
anchored in North Dakota. Purinton Analytics prepares
[vocational expert opinions](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) and
[life care plans](/services/life-care-planning/) that hold up on either
side of that line and on either side of the caption, for plaintiff and
defense counsel alike, because the method does not change with the
courthouse: documented conflict check, defined scope, and a records
request tailored to the claims at issue.

## Minnesota forums for a border-town caption

East Grand Forks sits in Polk County, and state-court civil matters
arising here proceed in the District Court serving Polk County. On the
federal side, the United States District Court for the District of
Minnesota is the available Minnesota forum; which court actually controls
a given caption, and any registration or qualification requirement that
attaches to it, is identified and confirmed during the conflict check and
intake. Counsel whose case crosses into the North Dakota courts will find
the firm's coverage of the wider metro described on the
[Grand Forks page](/locations/north-dakota/grand-forks/), and coverage of
the rest of the state on the [Minnesota page](/locations/minnesota/).

## Earning capacity when the market crosses a state line

A residual earning capacity opinion for an East Grand Forks evaluee cannot
stop at the municipal boundary. The relevant labor market is the
metropolitan market anchored by Grand Forks, and the analysis says so
explicitly rather than treating the state line as an economic wall.
Vocational findings rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational
employment and wage estimates for the Grand Forks metropolitan area and
for Minnesota statewide, supplemented by state workforce agency labor
market information and direct labor market research when the disputed
occupations warrant it. Where the damages model requires economic
projection, the firm offers
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Appointments, not an address

The firm keeps no office in East Grand Forks and will not pretend
otherwise; accuracy about its own footprint is part of the same discipline
that governs its reports. Most engagement phases run remotely, including
records review, structured vocational interviews, and standardized testing
administered under publisher protocols where remote administration is
supported. When the referral questions call for an in-person evaluation,
the evaluation is scheduled in the evaluee's own community, and when
deposition or trial testimony requires presence, travel is arranged from
the Kansas City home office against the dates counsel provides. The full
scope of this by-appointment model, applied across the country, is
described on the [nationwide page](/locations/nationwide/).
