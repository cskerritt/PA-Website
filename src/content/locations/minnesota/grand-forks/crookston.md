For a Crookston evaluee, the question that usually decides the vocational
opinion is access: which occupations in the surrounding region remain
realistically reachable for this person, with this medical history, from
this address. A small Polk County community cannot supply a full labor
market on its own, and an opinion that pretends it can will not survive
cross-examination. Purinton Analytics builds its
[vocational expert opinions](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) and
[life care plans](/services/life-care-planning/) for Crookston matters
around that access question, and writes them to be read critically by
whichever side did not retain the firm, since plaintiff and defense
referrals are accepted on equal terms.

along with any venue-specific qualification requirement, is settled during

## Testing what the regional market actually offers

Crookston belongs to the metropolitan labor market anchored by Grand
Forks, and the earning capacity analysis measures that market rather than
the town alone. The foundation is the Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates for the Grand Forks
metropolitan area and for Minnesota statewide, read alongside state
workforce agency labor market information and, where the contested
occupations justify it, direct employer-level labor market research. The
analysis then applies the evaluee's restrictions, transferable skills, and
location to that data, separating occupations that exist in the abstract
from those genuinely open to this person, and citing a source for each
step. The firm's work across the metro's principal city and surrounding
communities is described on the
[Grand Forks page](/locations/north-dakota/grand-forks/). When counsel
also needs the loss reduced to present value, the firm arranges
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.
