## Itasca County courts and venue

State court matters involving Cohasset parties proceed in District Court,
which serves Itasca County. Federal-question and diversity matters may be
brought in the United States District Court for the District of Minnesota;
the controlling venue, the governing scheduling order, and any
judge-specific disclosure expectations are identified and confirmed during
the conflict check and intake process. Reports are prepared to the same
standard regardless of forum: a defined scope agreed with retaining counsel,
a records request tailored to the claims, and written opinions that let
either side trace every conclusion back to its documented source. That
discipline is deliberate, because the report that survives
cross-examination is the one whose reasoning was visible from the start.

## Cohasset in the Grand Rapids labor market

For earning-capacity questions, Cohasset is analyzed as part of the
commuting market anchored by Grand Rapids, the principal city of the
surrounding area. A Cohasset evaluee's realistic access to employment is not
confined to positions inside town boundaries, and it is not fairly measured
by a national average either. Vocational findings therefore rest on Bureau
of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the
relevant area and for Minnesota statewide, state workforce agency labor
market information, and direct employer contact where the referral questions
warrant it. The transferable-skills analysis and placeability findings
describe the market the evaluee actually faces, including any limits that
documented medical restrictions place on commuting and sustained work
activity. When counsel needs lost earnings or care costs reduced to a
damages figure, the firm offers
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Evaluation logistics for a Cohasset evaluee

Purinton Analytics keeps no staffed office in Cohasset and publishes no
local street address, because accuracy about presence belongs to the same
discipline that governs its reports. Most engagements begin remotely:
records are transmitted through secure channels, and the clinical interview
and vocational testing can proceed by video when the referral questions
allow it. When an in-person evaluation is appropriate, the appointment is
scheduled in the evaluee's own community, which keeps the assessment
grounded in the conditions the evaluee actually lives and works in. Travel
for evaluations, depositions, and trial testimony is scheduled from the
Kansas City home office, and counsel are told early what dates are
practical so that disclosure deadlines are protected rather than discovered.
