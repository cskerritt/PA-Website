The product of a Grand Rapids engagement is a written opinion built to be
examined: methodology stated, sources cited, limitations acknowledged, and
conclusions that follow from the record rather than from the side that paid
for them. That is what Purinton Analytics delivers to plaintiff and defense
counsel in the Grand Rapids, MN area through
[vocational expert witness](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) work,
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Itasca County and its courts

The Grand Rapids micropolitan area consists of Itasca County. The firm
accepts matters venued in the District Court there and in the United States
District Court for the District of Minnesota, and it writes each opinion to
the standard of whichever forum will test it. Counsel handling related
matters elsewhere in the state will find the firm's statewide coverage
described on the [Minnesota locations page](/locations/minnesota/), and a
conflict check can address several venues at once.

## Appointments, not an office

Purinton Analytics maintains no staffed office in Grand Rapids and
publishes no street address for this location; meetings are by appointment.
The same candor that keeps invented facts out of the firm's reports keeps
implied offices off its website. In practice, most of the work never
requires a conference room: records analysis, structured interviews by
secure video, and standardized vocational testing under publisher protocols
where remote administration is supported. When the referral questions call
for evaluation in person, or when deposition or trial dates put the expert
in front of the court, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office
and confirmed against the case calendar early, so that logistics never
become the story.

## Reading the Grand Rapids labor market

Vocational conclusions about an Itasca County evaluee depend on the labor
market that evaluee actually faces. The firm grounds those conclusions in
Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for
Minnesota statewide and for the regional area that includes Itasca County,
in state workforce agency labor market information, and in direct labor
market research where warranted, with every figure tied to a cited source
in the report itself. Life care plans and medical cost projections for
residents of the area are priced to the evaluee's own market using
provider-specific inquiries and published fee data for the region, because
the cost of future care is a local question, not a national one.
