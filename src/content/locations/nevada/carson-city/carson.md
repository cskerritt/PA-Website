## Courts and venue for Carson matters

Carson lies within the jurisdiction of Carson City, and state-court matters arising here proceed in the District Court that serves it. Federal civil litigation for the state is heard in the United States District Court for the District of Nevada; which forum controls a particular matter is identified during the conflict check and intake rather than assumed. Reports are prepared to the same standard for either forum and either side: a written opinion that discloses its sources and methods so that opposing counsel can test it line by line. Any venue-specific registration or qualification requirement is identified and confirmed during the conflict check and intake process.

## Grounding a Carson opinion in labor market data

Vocational findings for a Carson evaluee rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Carson City metropolitan area and for Nevada statewide, supplemented by state workforce agency labor market information and by direct labor market research when the referral questions warrant it. The opinion does not generalize from national averages when area data exist, and it does not speculate about the local economy beyond what those sources support: transferable skills are mapped to occupations the published estimates actually document for the area, and placeability is assessed against work a person with the evaluee's restrictions could realistically pursue. When the damages theory also requires an economic calculation, the firm offers [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement structure.

## Evaluating an evaluee who lives in Carson

Most engagements begin remotely: records review, a structured vocational interview by secure video, and standardized testing administered under protocols designed for remote use. When the referral questions call for in-person evaluation, the firm conducts it in the evaluee's own community, which keeps the assessment grounded in the setting where the person actually lives and seeks work. Purinton Analytics keeps no staffed office in Carson or anywhere else in Nevada and publishes no street address here; candor about presence is part of the same discipline that governs the reports. In-person evaluations, depositions, and trial appearances are scheduled as travel from the Kansas City home office, and those logistics are discussed candidly at intake so counsel can plan around disclosure and hearing dates. The same model serves counsel [across Nevada](/locations/nevada/) and [nationwide](/locations/nationwide/).

## Starting a Carson engagement

Early retention protects the record: it allows the vocational interview, testing, and any in-person evaluation to be completed well before disclosure deadlines, and it gives rebuttal work time to be thorough rather than rushed. To begin, [request a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) with the case caption and party names, and note any imminent disclosure, deposition, or trial dates. The firm responds promptly with clearance status, a proposed scope, and a records request tailored to the claims in the matter.
