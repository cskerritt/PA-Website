## The Carson City venue

Carson City is its own jurisdiction, and the metropolitan area is defined by it alone, which makes the venue picture unusually clean: state-court matters proceed in the District Court in Carson City, and federal matters proceed in the United States District Court for the District of Nevada. The firm accepts plaintiff and defense engagements in each forum, along with [rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) assignments evaluating an opposing expert's vocational or life care methodology. Because the same standards apply regardless of who retains the firm, a Carson City report reads the same whether it was commissioned to support a claim or to challenge it.

## By appointment, from the Kansas City home office

The firm maintains no staffed office in Carson City and publishes no street address for this location; meetings are by appointment. That candor is intentional. Counsel are not buying a lobby, they are buying a defensible file, and nearly all of the work that produces it, records review, vocational testing coordination, interviews, plan development, and attorney conferences, is handled remotely by secure video and telephone. When in-person evaluation is warranted or when testimony requires presence, travel is arranged from the Kansas City home office with enough lead time to protect the court's schedule and the client's budget.

## Labor market evidence for Carson City evaluees

Vocational conclusions for evaluees in the Carson City area are grounded in Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Carson City metropolitan area and for Nevada statewide, alongside state workforce agency labor market information and targeted employer research where the referral questions justify it. Earning capacity is analyzed from the evaluee's actual work history and documented functional limitations, never from assumptions about what a claimant of a given age or background "should" earn. On the care side, [medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/) and life care plans price future needs from the treating record and cited clinical sources. Where the case requires an economist's present-value analysis, [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) bring in independent economist partners who perform and sign the economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement structure.
