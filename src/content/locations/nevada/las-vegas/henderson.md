The metropolitan area that frames this region's labor data is titled
Las Vegas-Henderson-North Las Vegas, and the second name in that title is
the point: Henderson is one of the largest cities in Nevada, an employment
center that a credible damages opinion must take seriously on its own terms
rather than treat as an afterthought to the principal city. Purinton
Analytics is retained by plaintiff counsel and defense counsel in Henderson
matters for [vocational expert opinions](/services/vocational-expert-witness/)
on employability and lost earning capacity, for
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) after catastrophic
injury, and for
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. The retention letter can come from either table; the
methods and the writing standard do not change.

## Clark County venue for Henderson matters

Henderson sits in Clark County, where civil actions under Nevada law are
heard in the District Court. Where a claim carries federal jurisdiction,
the United States District Court for the District of Nevada is the federal
forum available in the state, and the venue that will actually control a
given case, along with any registration or qualification requirement it
imposes on a testifying expert, is identified and confirmed during the
conflict check and intake. Every report is drafted so that whichever bench
receives it can test it: methods disclosed, sources cited, conclusions held
to the scope of the engagement letter. The surrounding regional practice is
described on the [Las Vegas metro page](/locations/nevada/las-vegas/), and
statewide coverage on the [Nevada page](/locations/nevada/).

## Reading metro-wide labor data for a Henderson evaluee

Vocational findings for a Henderson evaluee rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the
Las Vegas-Henderson-North Las Vegas metropolitan area and for Nevada
statewide, on state workforce agency labor market information, and on
direct labor market research when the referral questions call for it.
Because those estimates describe the metropolitan labor market as a whole,
the report must state, and then defend, how much of that market is
genuinely open to this particular evaluee after injury: whether
restrictions, transportation, and documented work history support
employment across the metro or confine the realistic search closer to
home. That framing decision is made explicitly and anchored in the record
rather than in convenience, because a framing chosen for convenience
rarely survives a capable cross-examination, whichever side offered the
opinion.

## Assessing a Henderson evaluee

The firm keeps no Henderson office and publishes no local address, because
accuracy about presence belongs to the same discipline that governs the
reports. Most engagements proceed remotely: records review, conferences
with counsel, and the bulk of vocational interviewing move over secure
video without any loss of rigor. When the referral questions justify
in-person work, the evaluation is conducted in the evaluee's own community,
where daily function and actual access to services can be observed
directly, with travel scheduled from the Kansas City home office.
Deposition and trial appearances are planned the same way, around the dates
the court has already set.
