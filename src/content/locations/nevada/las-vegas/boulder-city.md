file, a trial setting that will not move. Purinton Analytics organizes its

## Where a Boulder City case is heard

Boulder City belongs to Clark County, and civil actions under Nevada law
are heard there in the District Court. When a claim rests on federal
jurisdiction, the United States District Court for the District of Nevada
stands as the federal forum available in the state; which venue will
control the matter at hand, and any expert qualification or registration
requirement that venue carries, is identified and confirmed during the
conflict check and intake. The disclosure and discovery schedule of the
controlling court then drives the work plan, from the records request
through the final signed report. Counsel can review the broader regional
practice on the [Las Vegas metro page](/locations/nevada/las-vegas/) and
the statewide practice on the [Nevada page](/locations/nevada/).

## Placing a Boulder City evaluee in the metro labor market

A small city on the edge of a major metropolitan labor market poses a
question every honest earning-capacity opinion must confront: which jobs
count as genuinely available to this evaluee, and which exist only on
paper? Vocational findings are built from Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates for the Las Vegas-Henderson-North
Las Vegas metropolitan area and for Nevada statewide, from state workforce
agency labor market information, and from direct labor market research
where the referral questions warrant it. Whether the analysis treats the
evaluee as limited to nearby work or as able to commute into the wider
metro is stated outright and supported by restrictions, transportation,
and work history, never assumed for either side's benefit.

## Evaluation without a local office

The firm keeps no office in Boulder City and publishes no street address
for it; every meeting is by appointment. That candor costs counsel
nothing in practice, because the engagement is structured to run remotely:
secure video carries the records conferences, counsel discussions, and
most vocational interviews. Where the referral questions call for
in-person evaluation, it is completed in the evaluee's own community, with
observation of daily function where it actually occurs, and travel is
scheduled from the Kansas City home office, including for deposition and
trial appearances tied to the court's dates.
