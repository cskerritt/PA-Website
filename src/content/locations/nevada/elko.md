A remote-first practice serves a micropolitan venue on the same terms it serves a major metropolitan one, and that parity is the premise of this page. Purinton Analytics serves the Elko micropolitan area, spanning Elko County and Eureka County, with [vocational expert witness](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) analysis, [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and [expert testimony and litigation consulting](/services/expert-testimony-litigation-consulting/) for plaintiff and defense counsel alike. The methodology is the same described on the [Nevada](/locations/nevada/) page and applied [across the country](/locations/nationwide/); the location of the courthouse changes the logistics, never the standard.

## Venues in Elko County and Eureka County

Engagements are accepted for matters in the District Court in Elko County and Eureka County and in the United States District Court for the District of Nevada. Because the firm works either side of the caption, its reports are drafted for the reader most motivated to find fault in them: opposing counsel. That means disclosed methodology, cited sources, and conclusions that go no further than the record allows. Counsel who already face an adverse vocational or life care opinion can retain the firm for [rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/), which tests that opinion against the same published standards the firm applies to its own work.

## Serving a distant venue without pretending to be local

The firm has no office in Elko and publishes no street address for this location; there is no staffed local presence to claim, and the practice claims none. What counsel get instead is a working model suited to a geographically large service area: records review, interviews, vocational analysis, and attorney conferences conducted by secure video and telephone, with document exchange handled electronically. When the referral questions call for an in-person evaluation of the evaluee, or when a deposition or trial requires the expert in the courtroom, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office and planned early, because reaching a rural venue takes coordination that last-minute retention does not leave room for.

## Labor market analysis in a rural economy

Vocational findings for evaluees in the Elko area rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for Nevada statewide, together with state workforce agency labor market information and direct labor market research targeted to the communities where the evaluee actually lives and can reasonably work. In a rural labor market that last step matters: published statewide estimates describe occupations broadly, and a defensible placeability opinion has to test whether those occupations exist within the evaluee's realistic commuting range. Care needs are handled with the same locality discipline, with [medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/) and life care plans priced from documented sources. When the damages model requires economic analysis, [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) bring in independent economist partners who perform and sign the economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement structure.

## Beginning an Elko-area engagement

Rural venues reward early retention: evaluation travel, records collection from dispersed providers, and disclosure deadlines all take longer to align than they do in a large city. To begin, [request a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) with the case caption and parties, noting any scheduled disclosure, deposition, or trial dates. Once conflicts clear, counsel receive a written scope, a fee schedule, and a records request tailored to the claims.
