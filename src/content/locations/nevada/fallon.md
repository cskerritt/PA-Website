Counsel in the Fallon area retain Purinton Analytics from opposite directions: plaintiff firms seeking a documented earning capacity or future care opinion, and defense firms seeking either an independent analysis or a disciplined critique of an opinion already served. The firm accepts each kind of engagement on identical terms, and that symmetry is the point. An expert whose methodology changes with the retaining party is not offering expertise, and the firm's [vocational expert witness](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) work, [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and [rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) assignments in the Fallon micropolitan area follow the same published standards regardless of the caption's side, consistent with the firm's [Nevada practice](/locations/nevada/) and its [work nationwide](/locations/nationwide/).

## Churchill County and its courts

The Fallon micropolitan area is defined by Churchill County, and engagements are accepted for matters in the District Court in Churchill County and in the United States District Court for the District of Nevada. Files here follow the sequence the firm uses everywhere: a documented conflict check, a defined scope, a records request tailored to the claims pleaded, and a written opinion whose methodology is disclosed so either side can trace how each conclusion was reached. Where testimony is needed, [expert testimony and litigation consulting](/services/expert-testimony-litigation-consulting/) support runs from deposition preparation through trial.

## Local appointments without a local office

Purinton Analytics keeps no staffed office in Fallon and publishes no street address for this location; meetings are by appointment. The firm says so directly because honesty about presence is inseparable from honesty in the opinions themselves. In practice, the absence of a storefront changes little: records analysis, interviews, vocational assessment, plan development, and conferences with counsel proceed remotely by secure video and telephone, and the file develops on the same timeline it would in a large metro. When an in-person evaluation is warranted, or when a deposition or trial date requires the expert's presence in Churchill County, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office with the lead time a smaller venue's calendar deserves.

## Grounding opinions in the regional labor market

Vocational findings for Fallon-area evaluees rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for Nevada statewide, supplemented by state workforce agency labor market information and by direct labor market research aimed at the employment actually available within the evaluee's reach. In a smaller labor market, the difference between a published occupational category and a real, obtainable job can be decisive, and the analysis is built to distinguish them. Future care is handled with matching rigor: [medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/) and life care plans draw their recommendations from the treating record and cited clinical sources, not from templates. When the damages model requires economic computation, [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) bring in independent economist partners who perform and sign the economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement structure.

## How to begin

Whether the firm is the originating expert or the responding expert, early involvement protects the schedule. To begin a local or national engagement, [request a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) with the case caption and parties, noting any disclosure deadlines, depositions, mediations, or trial settings already on the calendar. Once conflicts clear, counsel receive a written scope and fee schedule, and the records request follows immediately.
