## Elko County Venue and Courts

West Wendover lies in Elko County, and state-court matters involving its
residents proceed in the District Court serving Elko County. On the federal
side, the United States District Court for the District of Nevada is the
federal forum available for Nevada matters; the controlling venue for any
particular case, along with any venue-specific registration or qualification
requirement, is identified and confirmed during the conflict check and
intake process. Every report is written to be examined by opposing counsel:
a documented conflict check, a defined scope, a records request tailored to
the claims, and opinions that trace each conclusion to its source so that
cross-examination meets a record rather than an assertion.

## West Wendover in the Elko Area Labor Market

West Wendover is part of the Elko, NV area, whose principal city is Elko.
Vocational findings for a West Wendover evaluee rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for that area and for
Nevada statewide, supplemented by state workforce agency labor market
information and direct labor market research where the published estimates
run thin. That last step matters in a small community: area-wide wage
tables describe a labor market centered on the principal city, and a
transferable-skills analysis that stops there can overstate or understate
what work is realistically available to someone who lives in West Wendover.
The firm's earning-capacity opinions therefore address the evaluee's actual
labor market, the occupations genuinely open within it, and the vocational
consequences of the claimed limitations, stated qualitatively and grounded
in cited sources.
