## Courts serving Washoe, Storey, and Lyon Counties

Engagements are accepted for matters in the District Court in Washoe County, Storey County, and Lyon County, and in the United States District Court for the District of Nevada. Because the firm works for either side of the caption, every report is written on the assumption that opposing counsel will read it closely: sources are cited, methodology is disclosed, and opinions stop where the record stops. Where counsel already have an opposing vocational or life care opinion in hand, [rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) engagements examine that opinion's methodology against the same published standards the firm applies to its own work.

## How a Reno engagement actually runs

Reno meetings are by appointment. The firm keeps no staffed office in the metro and publishes no local street address, because accuracy about presence belongs to the same discipline that governs the reports themselves. Most of the work in a typical engagement is not local at all: records review, transferable skills analysis, plan drafting, and conferences with counsel proceed remotely on a secure platform, which keeps costs proportionate and timelines predictable. When the referral questions call for in-person evaluation, or when deposition and trial dates require presence, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office and coordinated with counsel well before the applicable deadline.

## Labor market data for the Reno area

Vocational findings for Reno-area evaluees rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Reno metropolitan area and for Nevada statewide, together with state workforce agency labor market information and direct labor market research where warranted. The point of that layered sourcing is durability under cross-examination: an earning capacity opinion tied to published, replicable data survives scrutiny that an impressionistic opinion does not. Life care plans and [medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/) follow the same rule, pricing future care from documented sources rather than assumption. Where the damages model requires present-value or wage-loss economics, [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) bring in independent economist partners who perform and sign the economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement structure.

## Retaining the firm for a Reno matter

The best time to involve a vocational or life care planning expert is before the disclosure calendar compresses. To begin, [request a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) with the case caption and parties, and note any scheduled mediation, disclosure, deposition, or trial dates. Counsel receive a written scope and fee schedule once conflicts clear, followed by a records request tailored to the claims, so the file is complete before opinions are drafted rather than after.
