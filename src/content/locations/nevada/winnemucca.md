## Labor market foundations for a rural evaluee

Vocational findings for Winnemucca-area evaluees rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for Nevada statewide, on state workforce agency labor market information, and on direct labor market research directed at the jobs genuinely reachable from the evaluee's community. Statewide statistics describe an occupation's presence in Nevada broadly; a defensible opinion about this evaluee must go further and test whether that occupation exists, and is attainable, within a realistic commuting range and within the limitations the medical record documents. Life care plans follow the same evidentiary rule, pricing recommendations from the treating record and cited clinical sources. When the case needs wage-loss or present-value economics, [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) bring in independent economist partners who perform and sign the economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement structure.
