Tennessee splits its civil work in ways that shape how an expert engagement
runs: trial practice divides between the Circuit and Chancery Courts, and
federal matters fall to separate district courts in the eastern, middle,
and western parts of the state. Purinton Analytics writes for all of those
forums with a single discipline. Plaintiff and defense counsel across
Tennessee retain the firm for
[vocational expert](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) opinions,
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

Engagements are accepted for matters in the Circuit and Chancery Courts and
in the United States District Court for the Eastern District of Tennessee,
the United States District Court for the Middle District of Tennessee, and

## Serving Tennessee from a national practice

There is no staffed Purinton Analytics office in Tennessee, and the firm
publishes no street address in the state; every meeting is by appointment.
That candor is deliberate, because a report is only as credible as the firm
that signs it. Most Tennessee engagements begin remotely, with records
review, structured interviews, and standardized instruments administered by
secure video under publisher protocols. Where the referral questions
require in-person evaluation, the evaluation can be scheduled in the
evaluee's own community, and travel for evaluations, depositions, and trial
testimony is arranged from the Kansas City home office. Vocational findings
for Tennessee evaluees rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational
employment and wage estimates for the relevant metropolitan area and for
Tennessee statewide, together with state workforce agency labor market
information and direct labor market research where warranted, so the
opinion reflects the labor market the evaluee actually faces rather than a
national average. How this model operates across the country is described
on the [nationwide coverage page](/locations/nationwide/).

## Qualification for Tennessee matters

The firm's board certifications are issued by national credentialing
bodies and are not limited to any one state, and admission of expert
testimony is decided by the forum under its own evidentiary standards.
Rather than leaving that question for motion practice, the firm addresses
it at the outset: any venue-specific registration or qualification
requirement is identified and confirmed during the conflict check and
intake process, alongside disclosure deadlines and deposition and trial
settings.

## Beginning a Tennessee engagement

Whichever side of the caption retains the firm, the first step is the
conflict check. [Request a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) with the case
caption and parties, and note any imminent disclosure, deposition, or
trial dates. Availability, CVs, and fee information follow promptly, with
a records checklist tailored to the claims once the conflict check clears.
