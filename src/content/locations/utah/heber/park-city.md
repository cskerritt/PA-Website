## Summit County courts and the federal forum

Park City sits in Summit County, and state-law claims arising in the town
proceed in the District Court there. Matters that belong in federal court
are heard in the United States District Court for the District of Utah; the
controlling venue for a given caption, along with any venue-specific
qualification or registration requirement, is identified and confirmed
during the conflict check and intake process rather than assumed in
advance. Reports are drafted to a single disclosure standard whichever
forum controls, so removal does not force a rewrite. Where the assignment
is instead to test an opposing expert's conclusions, the firm accepts
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) engagements
scoped to methodology. Coverage of venues elsewhere in the state is
described on the [Utah locations page](/locations/utah/).

## A town read against the Heber area market

For statistical purposes Park City belongs to the Heber micropolitan area,
a market named for its principal city rather than for Park City itself.
That designation matters to method. An earning capacity opinion for a Park
City evaluee cannot simply borrow conditions from the state's large urban
centers, and it cannot treat the town as an island either; the relevant
labor market is defined from the evaluee's documented residence and
commuting pattern, then tested against the data. Findings rest on Bureau of
Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the
relevant area and for Utah statewide, on state workforce agency labor
market information, and on direct employer research where the referral
questions warrant it. Where lost earnings must be reduced to present value,
the firm offers
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Evaluation logistics, stated plainly

The firm keeps no staffed office in Park City and publishes no street
address for this location; candor about presence is part of the same
discipline that governs the reports. Most engagements proceed remotely,
through records review, structured interviews, and standardized vocational
testing under publisher-supported remote protocols. When the referral
questions call for in-person evaluation, it is conducted in the evaluee's
own community, and when deposition or trial testimony requires presence,
travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office and confirmed against
counsel's litigation calendar. The same structure serves the wider
[Heber area](/locations/utah/heber/) and, through the firm's
[nationwide practice](/locations/nationwide/), venues across the country.
