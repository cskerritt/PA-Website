Distance is a design constraint, not an obstacle. Purinton Analytics built
its practice to serve markets like St. George, where counsel need
[vocational expert](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) and
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) opinions of the same
quality available in the largest metros, without waiting on an expert's
travel schedule. Plaintiff and defense counsel in the St. George area retain
the firm for earning capacity evaluation, care-plan development, and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Washington County venues

The St. George metropolitan statistical area consists of Washington County.
The firm accepts matters venued in the District Court in Washington County
and in the United States District Court for the District of Utah. Whichever
forum the case sits in, and whichever party issues the retention, the report
follows the same template of documented sources, defined scope, and
disclosed methodology, so that the opinion withstands examination by the
side that did not commission it. Assignments limited to reviewing an
opposing expert's report are handled as
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) engagements with
their own defined scope.

## Remote-first, present when it matters

The firm publishes no street address in St. George and keeps no staffed
office there; meetings are by appointment, and most of the work never
requires a meeting room at all. Records analysis, structured interviews, and
standardized testing proceed remotely under publisher protocols where remote
administration is supported, which keeps the schedule in counsel's hands
rather than the airline's. When the referral questions require an in-person
evaluation of the evaluee, or when a deposition or
[trial appearance](/services/expert-testimony-litigation-consulting/) demands
presence, travel is planned from the Kansas City home office and locked to
the litigation calendar during intake. Reports, exhibits, and testimony
preparation arrive on the same schedule and to the same standard counsel
would receive in any major metro.

## Pricing findings to the St. George market

A vocational opinion about a St. George evaluee has to describe the labor
market that evaluee actually faces. Findings rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the St. George
area and for Utah statewide, state workforce agency labor market
information, and direct labor market research where warranted, with every
source cited in the report. Life care plans and
[medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/) apply the same
rule to care costs: pricing comes from provider-specific inquiries and
published fee data for the evaluee's own region, because a national average
tells the trier of fact little about what care will cost in Washington
County.

## Retaining the firm for a St. George matter

Conflict clearance comes before any discussion of the merits. Submit the
caption or party names through the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/),
identify the venue, and note any imminent disclosure, deposition, or trial
dates so that scheduling and any venue-specific qualification requirement
can be confirmed during intake. Counsel handling matters elsewhere in the
state will find the full scope of coverage on the
[Utah practice page](/locations/utah/), and the firm's
[national coverage](/locations/nationwide/) page describes the same
remote-first structure applied nationwide.
