The remote-first model exists for towns like Hyde Park. A small community
rarely supports a resident vocational expert, yet its residents are injured
on the job, terminated in dispute, and hurt on the road just as city
residents are, and their cases deserve the same quality of expert analysis.
Purinton Analytics closes that gap without pretending to a local presence
it does not have: records move electronically, interviews and testing
proceed by video under publisher protocols where supported, and the
finished [vocational opinion](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) or
[life care plan](/services/life-care-planning/) reads identically to one
prepared across the street. Hyde Park counsel on either side of a case can
retain the firm through its [Logan metro practice](/locations/utah/logan/).

## What remote-first means in practice

It does not mean the expert never appears. It means the sequence is
deliberate: the conflict check, scope definition, records request, and
analysis all happen at a distance, where they are done best, and travel is
reserved for the steps that genuinely require presence. When a Hyde Park
evaluee needs an in-person assessment, or when deposition or trial
testimony is set, the expert travels from the Kansas City home office on a
schedule fixed with counsel. No office is kept in Hyde Park and no address
is published for it; the practice describes itself as carefully as it
describes an evaluee. The [nationwide page](/locations/nationwide/) sets
out this model in full, and the [Utah practice page](/locations/utah/)
shows how it applies across the state.

## The courts serving Hyde Park

Hyde Park is part of Cache County, and civil matters from the town are
generally brought in the District Court in Cache County. For federal
claims, the United States District Court for the District of Utah is the
available forum; the venue that controls any particular case is confirmed
during the conflict check and intake, along with any venue-specific
qualification requirement.

## Vocational evidence at metro scale

An earning capacity opinion for a Hyde Park resident looks outward to the
metropolitan labor market around the principal city of Logan, because that
is where the town's workers actually compete. Findings are grounded in
Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for
the Logan metropolitan area and for Utah statewide, in state workforce
agency labor market information, and in direct labor market research when
a placement dispute justifies it. The methodology is documented step by
step so opposing counsel can retrace it against the same public sources.

## When the engagement widens

Some Hyde Park referrals need more than a single discipline. Through
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/),
independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis
while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement
structure. The firm also accepts
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) assignments
limited to methodology critique of an opposing expert's report, on the
same conflict-checked terms as an affirmative engagement.

A conflict check is the gateway to everything else. Submit the caption or
party names through the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/), identify
