Every report Purinton Analytics prepares for a Wellsville matter is
drafted on the assumption that its most attentive reader will be the

marketing claim could: sources are cited so they can be pulled, testing

## Why the opposing reader is the right standard

An expert opinion that persuades only the side that paid for it is worth
little at mediation and less at trial. Writing for the opposing reader
forces methodological choices into the open: why these records were
requested, why this test battery, why this occupational base. Counsel
retaining the firm for a Wellsville matter receives a report whose
reasoning can be defended line by line, which is equally useful when the
task is proving a loss and when it is testing an inflated one.

confirmed at the same stage.

## Situating a Wellsville worker in the regional market

Wellsville is a small community, and its residents' employment prospects
are properly measured against the wider metropolitan labor market centered
on Logan. Vocational findings draw on Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates for the Logan metropolitan area

information, and on direct labor market research where the referral
questions require it. No figure appears in a report without a source the
other side can check.
