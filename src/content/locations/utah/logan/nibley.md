## From work history to earning capacity

Transferable skills analysis is the discipline's answer to speculation.
The evaluee's past occupations are examined for the skills and demands
they actually involved, those skills are matched against the requirements
of occupations the evaluee can still perform given the documented
restrictions, and the resulting occupational base is tested against real
labor market evidence. Every step is written down and sourced, so that
opposing counsel can pull the same references and retrace the reasoning.
An opinion assembled this way serves whichever side retained it, because
its authority comes from the record rather than the retention.

## Venue for Nibley matters

Nibley sits in Cache County, and civil actions arising in the town are
ordinarily filed in the District Court in Cache County. The United States
District Court for the District of Utah remains the available federal
forum for claims that belong in federal court, and the controlling venue
for any given case is identified during the conflict check and intake,
along with any venue-specific qualification requirement. Nothing in the
firm's method changes with the forum; the report is written for the most
demanding reader it will meet.

## Where a Nibley evaluee's prospects are measured

Logan is the principal city of the metro that counts Nibley among its
communities, and earning capacity is assessed against that regional
market rather than against any single municipality. The firm grounds its
findings in Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage
estimates for the Logan metropolitan area and for Utah statewide,
supplemented by state workforce agency labor market information and by
direct labor market research when a specific vocational question demands
it. Nibley's modest size enters the report only as context; the
conclusions turn on what those sources show a person with the evaluee's
documented restrictions can actually do, never on what a damages theory
needs them to show.
