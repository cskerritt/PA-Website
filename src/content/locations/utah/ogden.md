The Ogden metropolitan statistical area spans Weber County, Davis County, and
Morgan County, and Purinton Analytics accepts vocational expert and life care
planning engagements arising in all of them, for plaintiff and defense
counsel alike. The work most often requested from the area includes
[earning capacity and employability evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/)
in personal injury and employment matters,
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) for catastrophic
injuries, and [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## State and federal venues for Ogden matters

Matters from the metro proceed in the District Court in Weber County, Davis
County, and Morgan County, and in the United States District Court for the
District of Utah, and the firm accepts referrals venued in any of them. The
written opinion is prepared so that opposing counsel can trace every finding
to a documented source, which means the same report serves equally well in a
state courtroom or a federal one and holds its shape whichever side of the
caption retained it. When the assignment is to evaluate another expert's
work rather than an evaluee, the firm offers
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) scoped to
methodology critique.

## No storefront, no pretense

Ogden meetings are by appointment. The firm keeps no staffed office in the
metro and publishes no street address for this location, because candor about
presence is part of the discipline counsel are paying for. Most engagements
from the area proceed remotely from intake through report: records review,
structured interviews, and standardized testing under publisher protocols
where remote administration is supported. When an in-person evaluation is
warranted, or when a deposition or trial appearance requires presence, travel
is arranged from the Kansas City home office and confirmed against counsel's
deadlines at scheduling. Counsel work with the same point of contact from
conflict check through [testimony](/services/expert-testimony-litigation-consulting/).

## Labor market evidence for the Ogden area

Vocational findings for evaluees in the metro are built on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Ogden area and
for Utah statewide, together with state workforce agency labor market
information and direct labor market research where the referral questions
call for it. Transferable skills conclusions follow from the evaluee's
documented work history, not from generalized assumptions. On the
care-planning side, life care plans and
[medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/) are priced to
the evaluee's own market using provider-specific inquiries and published
regional fee data, so that the cost figures presented at deposition trace to
identifiable local sources rather than national averages.

## Beginning an engagement in the Ogden area

Substantive review starts only after a documented conflict check clears.
Counsel can submit the caption or party names through the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/), noting the venue and any imminent
disclosure, deposition, or trial dates; intake then confirms scope, fee terms, and any
venue-specific qualification requirement in writing before substantive work
begins. Coverage across the rest of the
state is described on the [Utah practice page](/locations/utah/), and the
firm's [national coverage](/locations/nationwide/) page explains how the same
engagement structure operates in other jurisdictions.
