Counsel in a Herriman case does not need a vocational expert who happens to
live nearby; counsel needs an opinion that will hold up in court. Purinton
Analytics runs a [national practice](/locations/nationwide/) on that
premise. The firm serves Herriman the same way it serves matters across the
country: a remote-first engagement structure, by-appointment presence when
the case requires it, and a written opinion held to the standard of the
opposing side's scrutiny. Referrals come from plaintiff counsel and defense
counsel in comparable measure, and the work product does not change with the
retaining party.

## The county and courts behind a Herriman caption

Herriman lies within Salt Lake County, so its state-court civil matters are
ordinarily heard in the District Court in Salt Lake County. Claims with a
federal hook may proceed in the United States District Court for the
District of Utah; which forum controls is determined during the conflict
check and intake, not assumed. Reports are drafted so that either courtroom
receives the same thing: a stated methodology, cited sources, and
conclusions that stop where the record stops.

## Reading a suburban evaluee's real market

A town is where an evaluee lives, not the boundary of where that person can
work, so the [vocational evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/)
treats the metropolitan area as the relevant market for a Herriman evaluee.
Employability and earning capacity findings are grounded in Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Salt Lake
City area and for Utah statewide, in state workforce agency labor market
information, and in direct labor market research where the questions
warrant it. The analysis runs from the evaluee's documented history
outward, never from generalizations about suburban workforces.

## Remote-first, without shortcuts

Distance from the expert's home office changes logistics, never method. A
Herriman engagement typically begins with records review and a structured
interview conducted remotely, followed by standardized vocational testing
under publisher protocols where remote administration is supported. If the
referral questions require an in-person evaluation, or if deposition or
trial demands the expert's presence, travel is booked from the Kansas City
home office and fitted to counsel's calendar. No staffed office exists in
Herriman and no street address appears on this page, because honesty about
presence is part of the same discipline that governs the findings. The
[Salt Lake City metro page](/locations/utah/salt-lake-city/) and the
[Utah practice page](/locations/utah/) show how this town-level work fits
the wider footprint.
