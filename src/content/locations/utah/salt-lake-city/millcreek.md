No vocational opinion in a Millcreek case is stronger than the record
beneath it. Before anyone opines about what an injured or displaced person
can still earn, the file has to exist: the work history as employers
documented it, the restrictions as treating providers recorded them, the
education and training the evaluee can actually show. Purinton Analytics
begins every Millcreek engagement with that record, requesting it early and
reading it whole, and accepts these assignments from plaintiff counsel and
defense counsel alike through its
[Salt Lake City metro practice](/locations/utah/salt-lake-city/).

## The record before the opinion

A [vocational evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) from the
firm proceeds in a deliberate order. The conflict check and a defined scope
come first, then a records request tailored to the claims, so gaps surface
while there is still time to cure them. Structured interview and
standardized testing under publisher protocols follow, and transferable
skills analysis works outward from the documented history rather than
inward from assumptions about what any suburb's residents do for a living.
In catastrophic matters, a [life care plan](/services/life-care-planning/)
is developed the same way, with each projected need traced to the treating
record and clinician consultation. What the record cannot support, the
report does not say.

## Salt Lake County venue for Millcreek matters

Millcreek is in Salt Lake County, and its state-court civil litigation
ordinarily proceeds in the District Court in Salt Lake County. Where a
claim qualifies, the United States District Court for the District of Utah
is the available federal forum, and the venue that actually controls a
given matter is identified during the conflict check and intake. The report
is drafted for either courtroom without any change in standard: methodology
stated, sources cited, conclusions bounded by the evidence.

## Labor market findings a stranger to the case can verify

Employability and earning capacity for a Millcreek evaluee are measured
against the metropolitan labor market, using sources the other side can
pull and check. Findings rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational
employment and wage estimates for the Salt Lake City area and for Utah
statewide, on state workforce agency labor market information, and on
direct labor market research where the referral questions warrant it. The
reasoning stays qualitative, and every conclusion carries its citation,
because a finding that cannot be traced is a finding that cannot be
defended.

[Utah practice page](/locations/utah/) and the
