Every expert report in a Taylorsville case will eventually be read by the
lawyer whose job is to take it apart. Purinton Analytics writes for that
reader. The firm accepts vocational and life care planning engagements from
plaintiff counsel and from defense counsel, and the report each side
receives is built the same way: methodology stated up front, sources cited
line by line, and opinions that go no further than the record allows. That
symmetry is the practice's credibility, in Taylorsville and throughout the
[Salt Lake City metro practice](/locations/utah/salt-lake-city/) it belongs
to.

## Why two-sided work produces better opinions

An expert who works only one side of the caption learns to argue; an expert
who works both learns to verify. Defense retention teaches which plaintiff
assumptions collapse under scrutiny, and plaintiff retention teaches which
defense critiques are substance and which are noise. Both lessons are built
into the firm's [vocational evaluations](/services/vocational-expert-witness/):
standardized testing under publisher protocols, transferable skills analysis
tied to the documented work history, and labor market findings a stranger to
the case can reproduce from the citations.

## The labor market context for Taylorsville evaluees

Taylorsville workers move through the valley's economy rather than a single
municipal one, so employability and earning capacity are assessed against
the metropolitan market. The evidence base is qualitative and cited: Bureau
of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Salt
Lake City area and for Utah statewide, state workforce agency labor market
information, and direct labor market research where the referral questions
justify it. In catastrophic matters,

add independent economist partners who perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.
