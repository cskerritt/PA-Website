## Courts hearing Murray matters

Murray lies in Salt Lake County. Civil actions arising here generally
proceed in the District Court in Salt Lake County, and the United States
District Court for the District of Utah is available as the federal forum
where a claim supports it; the venue that actually controls is worked out
during the conflict check and intake rather than presumed. The firm's
reports carry the same architecture into either courtroom: a documented
conflict check behind them, a defined scope, a stated methodology, and
citations that let opposing counsel test every finding.

## Evidence over labels in the labor market analysis

What a Murray evaluee can earn is a question the data answers, not the
map. Employability and earning capacity findings are grounded in Bureau of
Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Salt
Lake City area and for Utah statewide, in state workforce agency labor
market information, and in direct labor market research when the referral
questions call for it. Transferable skills analysis begins with the
evaluee's own documented work history and tested aptitudes, and the
analysis measures the metropolitan labor market those sources describe. No
characterization of Murray as a place enters the file unless a cited
source puts it there, which is the same restraint the rest of the report
observes.

## When the referral asks for more, or for less

Some Murray referrals reach wider than a single vocational opinion, and
some are deliberately narrow. On the wider side, a catastrophic injury can
put future care and its funding into dispute along with earning capacity;
there the firm structures
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. On the narrow side, the assignment may be to examine
a report the other side has already served.
[Rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) work stays
inside the four corners of that report, testing whether its methods carry
its conclusions, and it passes through the same conflict check before it
begins.
