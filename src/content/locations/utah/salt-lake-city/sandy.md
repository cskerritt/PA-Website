A civil case from Sandy is tried where the venue rules put it, and the expert
file should be built for that courtroom from the first day. Sandy sits in
Salt Lake County, so its state-court matters are heard in the District Court
in Salt Lake County, while the United States District Court for the District
of Utah serves as the federal forum when a claim belongs there; the
controlling venue for any specific matter is settled during the conflict
check and intake. Purinton Analytics prepares vocational and life care
planning opinions for those courtrooms as part of its
[Salt Lake City metro practice](/locations/utah/salt-lake-city/), working for
whichever side of the caption retains it.

## What a court-ready opinion looks like

Judges and opposing counsel read expert reports differently than clients do,
and the firm writes for that audience. Each engagement opens with a
documented conflict check and a defined scope. The
[vocational evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) then proceeds
in a fixed order: records review, structured interview, standardized testing
under publisher protocols, transferable skills analysis, and labor market
research, with every source cited in the report so a finding can be traced to
its origin from the witness stand. Opinions are confined to what the record
supports, which is what lets them survive cross-examination.

## Sandy evaluees and the metro labor market

Sandy is a sizable suburb whose residents work throughout the valley, so
employability and earning capacity are measured against the metropolitan
market. The analysis draws on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational
employment and wage estimates for the Salt Lake City area and for Utah
statewide, on state workforce agency labor market information, and on direct
employer contact where the referral questions call for it. In catastrophic
injury matters, [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) is built
from the treating record and consultation rather than from a template, and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
add independent economist partners who perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Appointments in Sandy, no storefront claimed

The firm has no staffed office in Sandy and lists no street address for this
page; candor about presence is part of the same discipline the reports are
held to. Most work begins remotely, and a Sandy evaluee can complete records
review, interview, and supported testing without leaving the community. When
the questions require an in-person evaluation, or when a deposition or trial
date requires the expert in the room, travel is scheduled from the Kansas
City home office around counsel's calendar. The
[Utah state page](/locations/utah/) and the
[nationwide practice page](/locations/nationwide/) describe the same model at

## Reviewing the other side's expert

When an opposing vocational or life care planning report is already on file
in a Sandy matter, the firm accepts
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) engagements
limited to methodology critique: whether the testing was appropriate, whether
the labor market sources say what the report claims, and whether the
conclusions follow from the data relied upon.

## Opening the file

Venue, deadlines, and the evaluee's situation all shape scope, so the
engagement starts with intake rather than assumptions. Submit the caption or
party names through the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/), identify the
court where the matter is pending, and note any disclosure or trial dates
already set.
