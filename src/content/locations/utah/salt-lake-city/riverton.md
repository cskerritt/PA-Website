The referrals that arrive from Riverton tend to fall into familiar
categories: personal injury claims where earning capacity is disputed,
employment matters where mitigation and reemployment prospects are at
issue, and catastrophic injury cases that need a lifetime care analysis
alongside the vocational one. Purinton Analytics handles each category for
plaintiff and defense counsel alike, folding Riverton work into its
[Salt Lake City metro practice](/locations/utah/salt-lake-city/) and
applying the same written-opinion standard across all of them.

## Matching the engagement to the case

In an injury case, the core product is a
[vocational evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/): standardized
testing under publisher protocols, transferable skills analysis from the
documented work history, and employability findings tied to cited labor
market sources. In an employment dispute, the same tools address mitigation
and the realistic path back to work. In catastrophic matters, a
[life care plan](/services/life-care-planning/) grounded in the treating
record joins the vocational opinion, and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
bring in independent economist partners who perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. When the assignment is to evaluate an opposing
expert's disclosure instead,
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) is offered as a
methodology critique.

## Riverton's place in Salt Lake County litigation

Riverton belongs to Salt Lake County, and its state-court civil cases are
ordinarily filed in the District Court in Salt Lake County. The United
States District Court for the District of Utah stands as the federal forum
for claims that reach it; which court controls a given matter is confirmed
during the conflict check and intake. Every report is written to be read in
either forum, and by the opposing side in both.

## The labor market beyond the town line

A Riverton evaluee's job market is the valley, not the suburb, and the
analysis treats it that way. Findings draw on Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates for the Salt Lake City area and
for Utah statewide, on state workforce agency labor market information, and
on direct labor market research where the referral questions warrant it.
The reasoning stays qualitative and sourced, so that a finding about what
work exists for this evaluee can be checked against the cited record rather
than taken on faith.

## Serving Riverton without pretending to be there

No staffed office exists in Riverton and no street address appears on this
page; the firm treats candor about presence as inseparable from candor
about findings. Engagements are appointment-based and usually begin
remotely, through records review, structured interview, and testing where
remote administration is supported. In-person evaluation and appearances at
deposition or trial are met by travel from the Kansas City home office,
timed to counsel's calendar. How this model scales is described on the
[Utah practice page](/locations/utah/) and the
[nationwide practice page](/locations/nationwide/).
