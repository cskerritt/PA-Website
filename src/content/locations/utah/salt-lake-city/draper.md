Ask what a vocational expert's report in a Draper case should contain, and
the answer defines the engagement: a stated methodology, testing conducted
under the instrument publisher's protocols, a transferable skills analysis
anchored to the documented work history, labor market findings tied to
cited sources, and conclusions that end where the evidence ends. Purinton
Analytics builds every Draper opinion to that specification, whether the
retaining party is plaintiff or defense, as part of its
[Salt Lake City metro practice](/locations/utah/salt-lake-city/).

## The report, section by section

A [vocational evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) from the
firm opens with the referral questions and the records relied upon, so the
reader knows the opinion's boundaries before its conclusions. Interview and
standardized testing results follow, then the transferable skills analysis,
then the labor market research, with each source cited where it is used
rather than gathered in an appendix no one checks. A
[life care plan](/services/life-care-planning/) in a catastrophic matter
follows the same logic: needs traced to the treating record and clinician
consultation, costs traced to identified sources, nothing free-floating.
The aim is a document opposing counsel can audit, because a report that
can be audited is a report that can be defended.

## Draper's county and courts

Draper is situated in Salt Lake County, and its state-court civil matters
are ordinarily brought in the District Court in Salt Lake County. Where a
claim arises under federal law or diversity, the United States District
Court for the District of Utah is the available forum; the controlling
venue for a specific case is fixed during the conflict check and intake.
The report specification described above does not vary by forum.

## Labor market grounding for Draper evaluees

Draper's workforce participates in the broader valley economy, so
employability and earning capacity are evaluated against the metropolitan
labor market. Findings rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational
employment and wage estimates for the Salt Lake City area and for Utah
statewide, on state workforce agency labor market information, and on
direct labor market research where the referral questions require it,
always qualitatively and always with the source named.

## Logistics: appointment-based, remote-first

The firm maintains no staffed office in Draper and publishes no street
address for this location. Most engagements begin remotely, with records
review, structured interview, and testing under protocols that support
remote administration; in-person evaluation and courtroom appearances are
handled by travel from the Kansas City home office, scheduled around
counsel's dates. Statewide and national context for this model is on the
[Utah practice page](/locations/utah/) and the
[nationwide page](/locations/nationwide/).

## Economics and rebuttal, when the case calls for them

Through [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/),
independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis while
the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement
structure. And when the question is the other side's report rather than a
new evaluation, [rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/)
assignments critique methodology against the same specification the firm
applies to itself.
