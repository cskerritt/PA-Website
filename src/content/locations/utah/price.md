When an injury changes what work a Carbon County resident can realistically
perform, the damages question turns on evidence about that resident's own
labor market, not a national abstraction. Purinton Analytics builds Price
engagements around that principle, serving plaintiff and defense counsel
with [vocational expert evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/)
of employability and earning capacity,
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) in catastrophic injury
matters, [expert testimony and litigation consulting](/services/expert-testimony-litigation-consulting/)
through deposition and trial, and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under a
single engagement structure.

## Courts serving Carbon County matters

The Price micropolitan area consists of Carbon County. The firm accepts
matters venued in the District Court in Carbon County and in the United
States District Court for the District of Utah, together with arbitrations
and administrative claims arising locally. Whichever side of the caption
retains the firm, the opinion is drafted for the reader across the aisle:
every conclusion traces to records, standardized testing, or published
data that opposing counsel can check independently. That discipline is the
same in a Carbon County matter as in any other venue, which is why the
resulting reports travel well between forums. Counsel with matters
elsewhere in the state can start with the
[Utah state page](/locations/utah/), which describes the statewide
practice.

## How a Price engagement actually runs

Price meetings are by appointment. There is no staffed Purinton Analytics
office in Price and no published street address for this location; the
firm states that plainly because accuracy about presence is part of the
credibility it asks courts to extend to its opinions. Most of the work
proceeds remotely: records review, structured interviews, and
standardized vocational testing under publisher protocols that support
remote administration. Where the referral questions call for evaluation in
person, or where deposition and trial dates require the expert at counsel
table, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office with the
litigation calendar driving the itinerary rather than the reverse. Counsel
are told at intake which dates the firm can commit to, so scheduling risk
is surfaced before retention rather than after.

## Grounding opinions in the local labor market

Vocational findings for Price-area evaluees rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the relevant
area and for Utah statewide, supplemented by state workforce agency labor
market information and direct labor market research where warranted. The
inquiry is concrete: what occupations exist within the evaluee's commuting
area, what those occupations pay, and whether residual capacities and
transferable skills give the evaluee realistic access to them. Life care
plans and medical cost projections for Carbon County residents are priced
through provider-specific inquiries and published fee data for the region,
so the projected cost of care reflects where that care will actually be
obtained. When local availability is thin, the report says so and explains
the consequence, rather than assuming access that does not exist.
