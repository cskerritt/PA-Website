Litigation touching Santa Clara is, for venue purposes, Washington County
litigation, and Purinton Analytics approaches it that way from the outset:
the county, the court, and the deadlines come before any discussion of the
evaluee. The firm serves plaintiff and defense counsel in Santa Clara
matters with
[vocational expert services](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[expert testimony and litigation consulting](/services/expert-testimony-litigation-consulting/),
and it treats the modest size of the town as irrelevant to the standard of
the work.

## Washington County courts and the federal forum

State-court matters from Santa Clara proceed in the District Court serving
Washington County. Utah's federal trial forum is the United States District
Court for the District of Utah; whether a particular dispute belongs there
or in state court is a question of jurisdiction and strategy that counsel
decide, and the firm confirms the controlling venue, along with any expert
qualification requirement it carries, during the conflict check and intake.
Statewide coverage is described on the [Utah page](/locations/utah/).

## Small town, regional market

Santa Clara's own employment base is modest, and a report that pretended
otherwise would discredit itself. Earning capacity findings for a Santa
Clara evaluee therefore look to the regional market centered on St. George,
resting on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage
estimates for the St. George metropolitan area and for Utah statewide, with
state workforce agency labor market information and direct labor market
research added when the referral questions require it. Each finding cites
the geography it comes from, which lets either side probe the data instead
of the assumptions. The [St. George metro page](/locations/utah/st-george/)
describes the firm's practice across the region.

## How evaluation and testimony are handled

The firm has no Santa Clara office and publishes no address for this
location; honesty about presence is part of the same discipline that
governs the written opinions. Records analysis, interviews, and most
standardized testing are handled remotely under publisher protocols. When an
in-person evaluation is warranted, it is conducted in the evaluee's own
community, and when depositions or trial dates require presence, travel is
scheduled from the Kansas City home office that also supports the firm's
[nationwide practice](/locations/nationwide/). Counsel receive the
logistics in writing at engagement, so nothing about scheduling is left to
inference.

## Opening a Santa Clara matter

Counsel open a matter the same way from either side of the caption:
[request a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) with the caption or party names,
and note any imminent disclosure, deposition, or trial dates. After
clearance, the firm confirms scope and fees in writing and issues a records
request tailored to the claims. Where a full damages model is needed,
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
place the economic analysis with independent economist partners, who perform
and sign it, while the vocational and care-planning work continues under one
engagement structure.
