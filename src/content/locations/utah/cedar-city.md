## Iron County courts, state and federal

The Cedar City micropolitan statistical area consists of Iron County. The
firm accepts matters venued in the District Court in Iron County and in the
United States District Court for the District of Utah. Because every report
is drafted to be examined by the side that did not commission it, the
opinion does not soften or sharpen depending on which party retained the
firm; the same sourcing, the same disclosed assumptions, and the same
defined scope appear in every file. Counsel who need an existing expert
report evaluated rather than an evaluee assessed can retain the firm for
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) confined to
methodology critique.

## Meetings by appointment only

The firm maintains no staffed office in Cedar City and publishes no street
address for this location; meetings are by appointment, and that fact is
stated here plainly because honesty about presence is part of the same
standard that governs the reports. The practical consequence for counsel is
flexibility rather than absence: records review, structured interviews, and
standardized testing proceed remotely under publisher protocols where remote
administration is supported, and the file advances on the litigation
calendar rather than a travel calendar. When an in-person evaluation is
warranted, or when a deposition or
[trial appearance](/services/expert-testimony-litigation-consulting/)
requires presence, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office and
confirmed at intake.

## The Iron County labor market, described accurately

Employability and earning capacity findings for Cedar City evaluees rest on
Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for
the relevant area and for Utah statewide, state workforce agency labor
market information, and direct labor market research where the referral
questions warrant it. A smaller market is described as what it is; the
report never assumes that opportunities available in Utah's largest metros
exist in Iron County, and it cites the sources behind every conclusion. Life
care plans and [medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/)
carry the same locality discipline, pricing future care through
provider-specific inquiries and published fee data for the evaluee's own
community rather than national averages.
