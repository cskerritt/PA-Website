Catastrophic injury claims, employment disputes, and wrongful death matters
from the Provo-Orem-Lehi area generate the same expert questions they
generate anywhere else: what work an evaluee can still perform, what that
work pays, and what future care will cost. Purinton Analytics answers those
questions for plaintiff and defense counsel through
[vocational expert evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Courts serving Utah County and Juab County

The Provo-Orem-Lehi metropolitan statistical area comprises Utah County and
Juab County. Engagements are accepted for matters venued in the District
Court in Utah County and in Juab County, and in the United States District
Court for the District of Utah. The report standard does not change with the
forum: the same defined scope, the same documented sources, and the same
disclosure discipline apply whether the matter proceeds in state or federal
court, and whether the retaining party sits at the plaintiff's table or the
defense table. Counsel who need a fresh set of eyes on an existing expert
report can also retain the firm for
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) limited to
methodology.

## How the firm appears in Provo

Provo meetings are by appointment only. The firm maintains no staffed office
in the metro and publishes no street address for this location, a policy that
reflects the same commitment to accuracy that governs its written opinions.
Records review, structured interviews, and standardized testing are conducted
remotely where publisher protocols support remote administration, which is
how most engagements from the area begin. Where the referral requires an
in-person evaluation, or where deposition and
[trial testimony](/services/expert-testimony-litigation-consulting/) require
presence, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office with enough
lead time to protect counsel's disclosure calendar.

## Grounding findings in the local labor market

Vocational conclusions for evaluees in the Provo area are grounded in Bureau
of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the metro
and for Utah statewide, supplemented by state workforce agency labor market
information and direct employer research where the questions warrant it. The
analysis names its sources so that either side can verify them, and where a
position's local availability is disputed, the report documents the survey
method and employer contacts behind the finding rather than asserting it.
Care-planning work follows the same principle: life care plans and medical
cost projections for area residents are priced to the evaluee's own market
through provider-specific inquiries and published regional fee data, not
national averages, because a plan that cannot survive a pricing challenge is
not worth serving.
