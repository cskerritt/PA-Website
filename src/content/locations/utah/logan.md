The Logan metropolitan statistical area crosses a state line: it comprises
Cache County, Utah, and Franklin County, Idaho, and litigation from the area
can therefore land in the courts of either state. Purinton Analytics serves
plaintiff and defense counsel on both sides of that line with
[vocational expert evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Venues on the Utah side and the Idaho side

For matters arising in Cache County, the firm accepts engagements venued in
the District Court in Cache County and in the United States District Court
for the District of Utah. For matters arising in Franklin County, Idaho,
venue and any state-specific qualification questions are identified and
confirmed during the conflict check and intake process, and the engagement
proceeds under the same scope discipline once they are resolved. The
methodology does not change at the border: the report cites its sources,
defines its scope, and is written to be examined by the party that did not
retain the firm. Counsel needing critique of an existing expert report can
retain the firm for [rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/)
on the same terms.

## Appointments, not an office

Logan meetings are by appointment. The firm has no staffed office in the
metro and publishes no street address for this location; stating that
plainly is part of the same accuracy standard that governs the reports
themselves. Engagements from the area typically run remotely from intake
onward, with records review, structured interviews, and standardized testing
conducted under publisher protocols where remote administration is
supported. When an in-person evaluation is warranted or a deposition or
[trial date](/services/expert-testimony-litigation-consulting/) requires
presence, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office against the
dates counsel supplies at intake. Counsel work with the same point of
contact and receive the same report format from intake through testimony,
wherever the work is performed.

## A labor market that spans the state line

Because the metro includes counties in Utah and in Idaho, vocational
findings are grounded in Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment
and wage estimates for the Logan area together with the statewide estimates
that match the evaluee's residence, supplemented by state workforce agency
labor market information from the relevant state and direct labor market
research where the referral questions warrant it. Life care plans and
[medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/) follow the
evaluee as well: care pricing is developed through provider-specific
inquiries and published fee data for the evaluee's own community, whichever
state that community sits in.

## Starting a Logan area engagement

Every matter begins with a documented conflict check and a defined scope
before substantive work starts. Submit the caption or party names through
the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/), identify whether the matter sits
in Utah or Idaho, and flag any imminent disclosure, deposition, or trial
dates. Statewide coverage is described on the
[Utah practice page](/locations/utah/), and the firm's
[national coverage](/locations/nationwide/) page explains how the same
engagement structure reaches every other jurisdiction, Idaho included.
