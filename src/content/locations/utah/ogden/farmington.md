on the other side. Purinton Analytics writes every opinion as if opposing

counsel will be. That standard governs the firm's
[vocational expert services](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),

## Courts serving Farmington litigation

A Farmington matter filed in state court proceeds in the District Court
serving Davis County; Utah's federal cases are heard in the United States
District Court for the District of Utah. The firm does not opine on which
forum controls until intake: venue, and any expert qualification or
registration requirement the venue carries, is identified and confirmed
during the conflict check and intake process. The rest of the state
practice is described on the [Utah page](/locations/utah/).

## Wage and employment evidence that holds up

An opinion built to withstand cross-examination cannot rest on
impressions of the local economy. Vocational findings for a Farmington
evaluee are grounded in Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational
employment and wage estimates for the Ogden metropolitan area and for
Utah statewide, augmented by state workforce agency labor market
information and direct labor market research where the referral questions
demand it. The report discloses methodology as well as results:
transferable skills analysis, labor market access, and placement
prospects are each traced to identified data, and the choice of
geography is explained rather than assumed. Opinions are stated only as
strongly as the record supports, which is precisely what makes them
useful when the record supports them fully. The firm's footprint across
the region appears on the [Ogden metro page](/locations/utah/ogden/).

## By appointment, without pretense

Purinton Analytics has no office in Farmington and prints no street
address for this location; a firm that will not stretch a wage statistic
does not stretch its map either. Counsel meetings run by appointment, scheduled around the case calendar
rather than around a building.
Records move through secure channels, structured interviews and most
standardized testing proceed remotely under publisher protocols, and
in-person evaluation is conducted in the evaluee's own community when the
referral questions require it. Deposition and trial appearances are
supported by travel scheduled from the Kansas City home office, the base
of the firm's [nationwide practice](/locations/nationwide/).

## Where a Farmington engagement begins

Before strategy, before records, before any discussion of the merits,
comes the conflict check. Counsel should
[submit the caption or party names](/refer-a-case/) and note imminent
expert disclosure, deposition, or trial dates so the firm can be honest
about what the schedule permits. Once the check clears, scope and fee
terms are confirmed in writing and a records request tailored to the
claims is issued. If the damages theory also requires an economic loss
calculation, the firm arranges
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.
