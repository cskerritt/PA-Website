Venue comes before methodology. A Roy case belongs to Weber County, and
the expectations of the court that will eventually read the expert report
shape how that report should be written from the outset. Purinton
Analytics provides
[vocational expert services](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[expert testimony and litigation consulting](/services/expert-testimony-litigation-consulting/)
for Weber County matters, accepting retention from plaintiff and defense
counsel and writing to the same standard for both.

## Weber County's courts and Utah's federal forum

State-court litigation arising in Roy is heard in the District Court
serving Weber County. Federal matters in Utah proceed in the United
States District Court for the District of Utah. The firm treats forum as
a fact to be confirmed, not inferred: the controlling venue for a
specific dispute, and any expert qualification or registration
requirement that venue carries, is identified during the conflict check
and intake process. The firm's statewide coverage is described on the
[Utah page](/locations/utah/), and its work across the surrounding
communities on the [Ogden metro page](/locations/utah/ogden/).

## Measuring a Roy worker's market

Roy's workforce is part of the broader Ogden metropolitan labor market,
and the firm's earning capacity opinions are built on data scaled to that
reality. The foundation is Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational
employment and wage estimates for the Ogden metropolitan area and for
Utah statewide, joined by state workforce agency labor market information
and, when the referral questions call for it, direct employer contact.
Every figure that appears in a report carries its source, because a
number that cannot be traced is a number that cannot be defended in a
Weber County courtroom or anywhere else. Transferable skills analysis
follows the same rule: the occupations considered, the skills matched,
and the placement prospects assessed are each documented well enough for
the other side's expert to retrace the path and dispute it on the
merits.

## Appointment-based service, honestly described

Purinton Analytics does not maintain an office in Roy and does not
publish a street address for this location. What Weber County counsel get
instead is a process designed to make the office unnecessary: secure
remote records exchange, structured interviews and standardized testing
administered under publisher protocols, and in-person evaluation
conducted in the evaluee's own community whenever the referral questions
require it. For depositions and trial appearances, travel is scheduled
from the Kansas City home office, the hub of the firm's
[nationwide practice](/locations/nationwide/).

## From conflict check to work plan

Nothing substantive happens on a Roy matter until the conflict check
clears. Counsel begin by
[submitting the caption or party names](/refer-a-case/), together with
the expert disclosure deadline and trial setting if they are already
fixed, so the firm can say plainly whether the calendar supports the full
scope of work or a narrower one. After clearance, scope and fee terms are
confirmed in writing and a records request tailored to the claims is
issued. Where the case also demands economic loss analysis, the firm
arranges
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.
