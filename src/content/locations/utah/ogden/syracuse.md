District Court serving Davis County. Utah's federal trial forum is the

## A small city inside a larger labor market

Syracuse is a smaller community whose workers participate in a much
broader regional economy, and honest earning capacity analysis has to
reflect that. Findings for a Syracuse evaluee draw on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Ogden
metropolitan area and for Utah statewide, alongside state workforce
agency labor market information and direct labor market research where
warranted. The report states which geography supports each conclusion, a
habit that matters most when the town itself is small: transferable
skills and placement prospects are measured against the market an evaluee
can actually reach, not against an abstraction, and the reasoning for
that market frame is set out in the report where it can be examined. The metro-wide practice
is described on the [Ogden metro page](/locations/utah/ogden/).

## What retention looks like without a local office

The firm has no staffed office in Syracuse and does not publish a street
address here; claiming a local presence it does not keep would sit poorly
beside reports that trade on accuracy. Counsel work with the firm by
appointment, largely through secure remote channels. Depositions, trial
testimony, and any evaluation session that must happen in person are
supported by travel scheduled from the Kansas City home office, which
also anchors the firm's [nationwide practice](/locations/nationwide/) for
referring attorneys across the country.

## Starting the engagement

A Syracuse matter opens the way every matter opens here: with a
documented conflict check on the caption or party names before the merits
are discussed. Counsel should
[request a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) and note any expert disclosure
deadline, deposition setting, or trial date already on the calendar, so
scope can be matched honestly to the time available. Once cleared, the
firm confirms scope and fee terms in writing and issues a records request
tailored to the claims. If the case also needs an economic loss
calculation, the firm arranges
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.
