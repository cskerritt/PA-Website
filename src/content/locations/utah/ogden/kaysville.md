United States District Court for the District of Utah. The firm confirms
the controlling forum for each specific case, and any expert
qualification or registration requirement that forum imposes, during the

Statewide engagements are described on the [Utah page](/locations/utah/).

## From records to labor market

Once the record is mastered, the labor market analysis is layered on top
of it. Findings rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational
employment and wage estimates for the Ogden metropolitan area, where
Kaysville's workforce participates, and for Utah statewide, supported by
state workforce agency labor market information and direct labor market
research where the referral questions justify the added depth. The
written report ties each conclusion to its source and its geography, so
that a reader preparing cross-examination can follow the chain from
record to data to opinion without a gap. Where the record is incomplete,
the report says what is missing and what turns on it, rather than
smoothing over the hole with an assumption. Work across the wider region is
described on the [Ogden metro page](/locations/utah/ogden/).

## Logistics for Kaysville counsel and evaluees

The firm maintains no office in Kaysville and publishes no local street

same category as candor about data. Most of the engagement moves through
secure remote channels: records exchange, structured interviews, and
standardized testing under publisher protocols. In-person evaluation,
when the referral questions call for it, is conducted in the evaluee's

travel from the Kansas City home office that also serves the firm's
[nationwide practice](/locations/nationwide/).

## Opening the file

Because the method starts with records, the engagement starts with
clearance to read them. Counsel should
[request a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) with the case caption or party
names and identify the expert disclosure deadline and any trial setting,
so the depth of the records review can be matched to the calendar. After
the check clears, the firm confirms scope and fee terms in writing and
issues a records request tailored to the claims pleaded, itemized so
counsel can see exactly what the analysis will rest on. Where the
damages case also requires economic analysis, the firm arranges
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.
