support an expert opinion about a North Salt Lake evaluee. Purinton

## State and federal forums for North Salt Lake matters

A North Salt Lake case filed in state court proceeds in the District
Court serving Davis County. Utah's federal trial forum is the United
States District Court for the District of Utah. As with data geography,
the firm treats venue as something to establish rather than assume: the
controlling forum, and any expert qualification or registration
requirement it imposes, is confirmed during the conflict check and
intake process. Statewide coverage is outlined on the
[Utah page](/locations/utah/).

## Choosing the right data geography

When a report about a North Salt Lake evaluee cites wage evidence, it
explains why the cited geography fits the evaluee's actual market. The
foundation is Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and
wage estimates for the Ogden metropolitan area and for Utah statewide,
with state workforce agency labor market information layered in and
direct labor market research undertaken where the referral questions
require it. Where an evaluee's work history or restrictions make a
different market frame more defensible, the report says so and shows the
reasoning, because the choice of geography is exactly the kind of
methodological decision opposing counsel probes. Defending that choice
in the report itself, before anyone asks, is cheaper than defending it
for the first time at deposition. The regional practice
is described on the [Ogden metro page](/locations/utah/ogden/).

## Starting a North Salt Lake engagement

Every engagement opens with conflict clearance. Counsel should
[submit the caption or party names for a conflict check](/refer-a-case/)
and identify the expert disclosure deadline, deposition settings, and
any trial date already fixed, so scope can be sized to the calendar
honestly. Once cleared, scope and fee terms are confirmed in writing and
a records request tailored to the claims is issued, with the analysis
staged to meet the disclosure date. If the damages case
also requires economic modeling, the firm arranges
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.
