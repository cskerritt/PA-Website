Smaller markets deserve the same expert discipline as major metros, and the
Heber micropolitan area, covering Wasatch County and Summit County, is
exactly the kind of market the firm's remote-first structure was designed to
reach. Purinton Analytics provides
[vocational expert evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
to plaintiff and defense counsel in the area, with independent economist
partners performing and signing the economic analysis while the vocational
and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement structure.

## Wasatch County and Summit County venues

Engagements are accepted for matters venued in the District Court in Wasatch
County and in Summit County, and in the United States District Court for the
District of Utah. The size of the venue never changes the standard of the
work: a report prepared for a Heber area matter carries the same defined
scope, documented sourcing, and disclosure discipline as a report prepared
for the largest urban docket, and it is written on the assumption that the
party who did not retain the firm will read every line. Where the
assignment is to test another expert's conclusions, the firm accepts
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) engagements
scoped to methodology. Disclosure and report deadlines are treated as
engagement terms, confirmed in writing at intake and tracked through
delivery.

## Serving the area by appointment

The firm keeps no staffed office in the Heber area and publishes no street
address for this location; meetings are by appointment, and saying so
directly is part of the candor counsel should expect from an expert's
marketing as much as from an expert's report. In practice, most engagements
proceed remotely: records review, structured interviews, and standardized
testing under publisher protocols where remote administration is supported.
When an in-person evaluation is warranted, or when deposition or
[trial testimony](/services/expert-testimony-litigation-consulting/) requires
presence, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office and confirmed
against counsel's litigation calendar during intake.

## Reading a smaller labor market honestly

Vocational findings for evaluees in a micropolitan market require particular
care, because opportunity in a smaller labor market cannot simply be
extrapolated from statewide conditions. The analysis rests on Bureau of
Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the relevant
area and for Utah statewide, state workforce agency labor market
information, and direct labor market research where the referral questions
warrant it, and the report states plainly which findings come from which
source. Life care plans and
[medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/) apply the
same locality principle, pricing future care through provider-specific
inquiries and published fee data for the evaluee's own community rather than
national averages.
