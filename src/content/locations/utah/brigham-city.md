Every engagement Purinton Analytics accepts from the Brigham City area
begins the same way: a documented conflict check, a scope defined in
writing, and a records request tailored to the claims. That process serves
plaintiff and defense counsel equally, and it applies whether the referral
calls for [vocational expert evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/)
of earning capacity, [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) for
a catastrophic injury, or a
[coordinated damages engagement](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## A micropolitan area that straddles a state line

The Brigham City micropolitan statistical area comprises Box Elder County,
Utah, and Oneida County, Idaho. For Box Elder County matters, the firm
accepts engagements venued in the District Court in Box Elder County and in
the United States District Court for the District of Utah. For matters
arising in Oneida County, Idaho, the correct venue and any state-specific
qualification requirements are identified and confirmed during the conflict
check and intake process before substantive work begins. On either side of
the line, the report is built to the same standard: sources cited,
assumptions disclosed, conclusions stated so that opposing counsel can test
them. The firm also takes
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) assignments
limited to evaluating another expert's methodology.

## Presence without pretense

Brigham City meetings are by appointment. There is no staffed office in the
area and no published street address for this location, because an expert
whose reports demand precision should describe his own footprint with the
same precision. Most engagements are completed remotely, through records
review, structured interviews, and standardized testing under publisher
protocols where remote administration is supported. When the referral
questions require an in-person evaluation, or when a deposition or
[trial appearance](/services/expert-testimony-litigation-consulting/) calls
for presence, travel is arranged from the Kansas City home office and
committed to counsel's dates at intake.

## Labor market research on both sides of the line

Vocational findings for evaluees in the area draw on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the relevant area
and for the evaluee's state of residence, whether that is Utah or Idaho,
along with state workforce agency labor market information and direct labor
market research where warranted. Transferable skills conclusions proceed
from the evaluee's documented work history and tested aptitudes, not from
assumptions about what a small market ought to offer. The report identifies which estimates
describe which market, so the trier of fact is never asked to assume that
conditions in a small market mirror statewide figures. Life care plans and
[medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/) are priced to
the evaluee's own community through provider-specific inquiries and
published regional fee data.
