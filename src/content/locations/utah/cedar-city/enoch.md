## Reading the Cedar City Area Labor Market

Vocational findings for an Enoch evaluee rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the [Cedar City area](/locations/utah/cedar-city/) and for Utah statewide, supplemented by state workforce agency labor market information and by direct employer contact when the published estimates are too aggregated to describe a small market honestly. That last step matters here. When supplemental research is needed to close that gap, the report presents it and says why, because an opinion that hides its seams invites the cross-examination it deserves.
