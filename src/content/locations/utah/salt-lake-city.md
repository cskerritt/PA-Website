Purinton Analytics serves plaintiff and defense counsel in the Salt Lake
City-Murray metropolitan area as part of its [Utah practice](/locations/utah/),
by appointment and remotely. Referrals from the metro most
often call for [vocational expert evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/)
of earning capacity and employability in injury and employment litigation,
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) in catastrophic injury
matters, and [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Venue footprint in Salt Lake and Tooele counties

The Salt Lake City-Murray metropolitan statistical area comprises Salt Lake
County and Tooele County. The firm accepts matters venued in the District
Court in Salt Lake County and in Tooele County, and in the United States
District Court for the District of Utah. Because the same methodology and the
same disclosure discipline govern every file, counsel litigating parallel
state and federal matters receive a consistent written opinion in both
forums, and a report prepared for either side of the caption is built to read
the same way under cross-examination. Where an opposing expert's report is
already on file, the firm also accepts
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) assignments
limited to methodology critique.

## Meetings by appointment, work built for distance

Salt Lake City meetings are by appointment. The firm keeps no staffed office
in the metro and publishes no street address for this location, because
accuracy about presence belongs to the same discipline that governs its
reports. Most engagements begin remotely: records review, structured
interviews, and standardized testing under publisher protocols where remote
administration is supported. When the referral questions require in-person
evaluation, or when deposition and trial dates require presence, travel is
scheduled from the Kansas City home office and coordinated around counsel's
calendar rather than the firm's. Counsel receive the same report standard and
the same point of contact regardless of where the work is performed.

## Labor market research for Salt Lake City evaluees

Vocational findings for evaluees in the metro rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Salt Lake City
area and for Utah statewide, state workforce agency labor market information,
and direct labor market research where the referral questions warrant it.
Transferable skills analysis proceeds from the evaluee's documented work
history rather than from assumptions about the regional economy, and every
source relied upon is cited in the report so that opposing counsel can trace
each finding to its origin. Life care plans and
[medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/) for Utah
residents are priced to the evaluee's own market using provider-specific
inquiries and published fee data for the region rather than national
averages.

## Starting a Salt Lake City engagement

Every engagement begins with a documented conflict check, a defined scope,
and a records request tailored to the claims, so that the written opinion
rests on a record both sides can examine. Submit the caption or party names
through the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and note any imminent
disclosure, deposition, or trial dates so scheduling can be confirmed at
intake. Counsel with matters beyond Utah can review the firm's
[national coverage](/locations/nationwide/) for the same service structure in
other venues.
