When a Springville family is living with a catastrophic injury, the
hardest litigation question is often not fault but the future: what
care, equipment, therapy, and support the injured person will need for
the rest of their life, and what happens if the case undervalues it.
[Life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) is the discipline
that answers that question in writing, and Purinton Analytics prepares
those plans, alongside
[vocational expert opinions](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) and
[medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/), for
plaintiff and defense counsel handling Springville matters.

## Springville litigation and its courts

Springville's state-court civil cases are filed in the District Court
that serves Utah County, the county in which the city sits. The federal
forum for Utah matters is the United States District Court for the
District of Utah. As with every engagement, the firm verifies the
controlling venue, and any expert qualification requirement attached to
it, during the conflict check and intake stage. Coverage of the rest of
the state is collected on the [Utah overview page](/locations/utah/).

## A care plan that can be cross-examined

A life care plan earns its weight by being checkable. Each recommended
item of care is tied to the medical record or to treating-provider
input, described specifically enough that opposing counsel can locate
its basis and challenge it if they can. The vocational side of a
Springville engagement receives the same treatment: employability and
earning capacity findings are grounded in Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates for the Provo-Orem-Lehi
metropolitan area and for Utah statewide, plus state workforce agency
data and direct market research where warranted, because Springville
residents participate in an employment market far larger than the city
itself. The firm's metro-wide practice is described on the
[Provo metro page](/locations/utah/provo/).

## Evaluating an injured Springville resident

Catastrophically injured evaluees should not have to travel to be
assessed. The firm structures its Springville work accordingly: records
analysis, family and evaluee interviews, and most standardized testing
are completed remotely under publisher protocols, and in-person
evaluation, when the referral requires it, comes to the evaluee's own
community. The firm keeps no Springville office and lists no local
address, a fact it states outright because credibility is cumulative.
Travel for evaluations and for deposition or trial testimony is
scheduled from the Kansas City home office that also serves the firm's
[national caseload](/locations/nationwide/).

## Beginning a Springville engagement

The intake sequence protects both sides: counsel
[request a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) with the caption or party
names before any discussion of the merits, and after clearance the firm
confirms scope and fee terms in writing and issues a records request
matched to the claims. Flagging expert disclosure and trial dates at
intake lets the care-planning and vocational work run on a schedule the
court's calendar will actually accommodate. Where lifetime care costs
and lost earnings must be reduced to present economic terms, the firm
coordinates
[combined damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis, keeping the medical, vocational, and economic strands of the
damages case aligned but separately authored.
