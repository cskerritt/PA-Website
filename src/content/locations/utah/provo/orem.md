Orem is among the largest cities in the Provo-Orem-Lehi metropolitan area,
and that scale matters to expert work: an opinion about what an injured
Orem resident can still earn has to hold up against a substantial local
employment base, not just a set of statewide averages. Purinton Analytics
brings [vocational expert](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) and
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) services to plaintiff
and defense counsel handling Orem matters, with every report written to
survive examination by the lawyer on the other side of the caption.

## Utah County venue and the federal forum

Civil matters arising in Orem are heard in the District Court sitting in
Utah County. Utah's federal forum is the United States District Court for
the District of Utah; which court controls a particular dispute, and what
expert qualification or disclosure requirements that court imposes, is
confirmed during the conflict check and intake rather than assumed. The
firm's statewide practice, including its work in Utah's other metropolitan
areas, is described on the [Utah locations page](/locations/utah/).

## Labor market evidence for Orem findings

A defensible earning capacity conclusion names its data. Findings for Orem
evaluees draw on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and
wage estimates covering the Provo-Orem-Lehi metropolitan area and Utah as
a whole, supplemented by state workforce agency labor market information
and, where the referral questions warrant it, direct contact with
employers. Because Orem workers participate in an employment market that
extends well beyond the city itself, the written report identifies which
geography supports each finding, so that opposing counsel can test the
data rather than speculate about it. Metro-wide engagements are described
on the [Provo metro page](/locations/utah/provo/).

## Evaluation without a local storefront

The firm keeps no office in Orem and publishes no local street address,
because claiming a presence it does not maintain would undercut the
accuracy its reports depend on. Counsel instead get a remote-first
process: records analysis, structured vocational interviews, and most
standardized testing proceed under publisher protocols regardless of
where the evaluee sits. When the questions presented require an in-person
evaluation, the appointment is arranged in the evaluee's own community.
Depositions, trial testimony, and site visits are scheduled with travel
from the Kansas City home office, the same base from which the firm
serves counsel [nationwide](/locations/nationwide/).

## Beginning with a conflict check

An Orem engagement starts the same way every engagement in the practice
starts: counsel [request a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) with the case
caption or party names before any substantive discussion occurs. Once the
check clears, scope and fee terms are confirmed in writing and a records
request tailored to the claims goes out. Counsel should flag expert
disclosure deadlines and trial settings at intake so the work plan can be
built backward from the dates that already bind the case. Where the
damages model calls for economic analysis alongside the vocational
opinion, the firm offers
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure, keeping each discipline within its own
qualifications and each signature where it belongs.
