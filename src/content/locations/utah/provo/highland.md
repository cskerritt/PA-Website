A Highland case does not get a scaled-down expert because Highland is a
smaller community. Purinton Analytics runs a national practice from a
Kansas City home office, and the report prepared for a Highland matter
follows the same protocols, draws on the same data discipline, and meets
the same publication standard as the firm's work in the largest venues
in the country. Counsel for either party can retain the firm here for
[vocational expert opinions](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[litigation and testimony support](/services/expert-testimony-litigation-consulting/).

## Highland's courts

Highland sits in Utah County. Its state-court civil matters are heard in
the District Court serving the county, and the federal forum for Utah
cases is the United States District Court for the District of Utah.
Venue is confirmed, never presumed: the controlling court and its expert
qualification and disclosure requirements are pinned down during the
conflict check and intake that opens every engagement. The firm's
statewide work is summarized on the [Utah page](/locations/utah/).

## Highland evaluees, metro data

Highland residents earn their livings across the Provo-Orem-Lehi
metropolitan area, so the firm evaluates their employability against
that regional market. The underlying sources are the Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the
metropolitan area and for Utah as a whole, state workforce agency labor
market information, and direct labor market research when the referral
questions require detail the published series cannot supply. Reports
state which geography each finding rests on, an ordinary-sounding habit
that does substantial work under cross-examination. Engagements across
the surrounding metro are described on the
[Provo metro practice page](/locations/utah/provo/).

## Remote-first, and honest about it

The national structure is what makes Highland service practical. The
firm does not open satellite offices and does not publish local
addresses it does not have; it discloses instead that all Highland work
is by appointment. Nearly the entire evaluation sequence, from records
analysis through structured interviews to most standardized testing
under publisher protocols, is completed remotely with no loss of rigor,
and remote administration follows the publishers' own validity
conditions so the results carry the same evidentiary weight.
Where the referral requires an in-person evaluation, the firm holds it
in the evaluee's own community, and travel for that work, and for
depositions and trial testimony, is scheduled from the Kansas City home
office. The same model supports the firm's
[engagements throughout the country](/locations/nationwide/).

## Retaining the firm for a Highland matter

The first exchange is administrative by design: counsel
[request a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) with the case caption or the
parties' names, and the merits wait until the check clears. The firm
then documents scope and fee terms, issues a records request fitted to
the claims, and calendars the evaluation and report against the expert
disclosure and trial dates counsel identifies. If the damages
presentation requires economic analysis, the firm coordinates
[a combined damages engagement](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds in
parallel, one structure with every opinion signed inside its author's
own field.
