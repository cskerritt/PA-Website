Ask which side retained the expert and you learn nothing about how a
Purinton Analytics report on a Pleasant Grove evaluee was prepared. The
records request, the interview structure, the testing protocols, and the
data sources are identical whether the retention letter comes from
plaintiff's counsel or the defense, because a methodology that bends to
its client is not a methodology. Pleasant Grove attorneys on either side
of the caption can engage the firm for
[vocational expert opinions](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care plans](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[peer review of opposing reports](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/), and
should expect the same disciplined product in each posture.

## Pleasant Grove's place in the court system

Pleasant Grove is a Utah County community, and its state-court civil
matters are heard in the District Court serving the county. Federal
cases proceed in the United States District Court for the District of
Utah, the federal forum for the state. The firm confirms which court
governs a given engagement, and what expert qualification or disclosure
rules apply there, during the conflict check and intake rather than by
assumption. The firm's full Utah footprint appears on the
[Utah state page](/locations/utah/).

## One market, read the same way for both sides

Earning capacity findings for Pleasant Grove evaluees are grounded in
Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates
for the Provo-Orem-Lehi metropolitan area and for Utah statewide,
supported by state workforce agency labor market information and by
direct labor market research when the referral questions call for it.
The same datasets serve plaintiff and defense engagements alike; what
changes is never the data but the questions posed to it. Since Pleasant
Grove residents work throughout the surrounding metro, each written
finding states the geographic market it describes. Metro-level
engagements are covered on the
[Provo-Orem-Lehi page](/locations/utah/provo/).

## Appointments, not an office

The firm will not tell Pleasant Grove readers it has an office here,
because it does not. Its evaluations are by appointment: remote where
the instruments and interview format permit, which covers most of the
work, and in the evaluee's own community when the referral requires
in-person assessment. That structure serves defense-retained
examinations and plaintiff-retained evaluations equally well, and the
evaluee experiences the same process in either case: the same
instruments, the same interview structure, the same documentation. Travel
for in-person work, depositions, and trial testimony is arranged from
the Kansas City home office, the base for the firm's
[cases across the country](/locations/nationwide/).

## Retention, from either chair

The path in is the same for everyone. Counsel
[request a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) with the caption or party
names; the firm runs it before any substantive discussion; and after
clearance, scope and fees are documented in writing with a records
request tailored to the pleaded claims. Disclosure deadlines and trial
settings identified at intake control the work plan. When a complete
damages presentation also needs economic analysis, the firm offers
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/):
independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis
while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure, a division of labor that protects the record no
matter which side built it.
