Expert work in Lehi litigation is governed less by geography than by the
scheduling order. Disclosure deadlines, deposition windows, and trial
settings determine whether a vocational opinion arrives as a considered
piece of evidence or a rushed one, which is why Purinton Analytics asks
counsel about the calendar before almost anything else. The firm serves
both sides of the caption in Lehi matters with
[vocational expert witness work](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) of opposing
expert reports, each engagement planned backward from the dates the court
has already fixed.

## Venue for Lehi disputes

A civil case arising in Lehi proceeds in the District Court serving Utah
County when filed in state court. On the federal side, the available forum
in the state is the United States District Court for the District of
Utah. The firm treats venue as a fact to be verified, not presumed: the
controlling court, and any registration or qualification requirement
particular to it, is identified during the conflict check and intake. For
engagements elsewhere in the state, see the
[Utah practice page](/locations/utah/).

## The data behind a Lehi earning capacity opinion

Lehi sits within the Provo-Orem-Lehi metropolitan area, and its residents
compete in a labor market that crosses city boundaries freely. Vocational
findings therefore rest on occupational employment and wage estimates
published by the Bureau of Labor Statistics for that metropolitan area
and for the state of Utah, together with state workforce agency
information and targeted labor market research when a specific occupation
or employer question requires it. No figure appears in a report without a
source the other side can pull and check. The firm's broader work across
the metro is set out on the
[Provo-Orem-Lehi metro page](/locations/utah/provo/).

## What retention looks like for a Lehi evaluee

There is no Lehi office and no published local address; the firm is
candid that its presence here is by appointment, because candor about
small things is how a reader learns to trust the large ones. In practice
this costs counsel nothing. Records review, vocational interviews, and
most standardized testing are completed remotely under the test
publishers' own protocols, and when an in-person evaluation is warranted
the firm comes to the evaluee's community rather than summoning an
injured person to a distant office. Travel for evaluations, depositions,
and trial appearances originates from the Kansas City home office that
anchors the firm's [national practice](/locations/nationwide/).

## Reserving the calendar

Because disclosure dates drive everything, early retention preserves
options that late retention forecloses. Counsel should
[submit a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) with the caption or party names
and note any expert deadlines, mediation dates, or trial settings already
on the docket. After clearance, the firm confirms scope in writing,
issues a tailored records request, and builds the evaluation and drafting
schedule so the report is complete before the disclosure date rather than
on it. When the case also needs an economic loss calculation, that
analysis is performed and signed by independent economist partners
through a
[coordinated damages engagement](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/),
with the vocational and care-planning foundation and the economic
projections moving on one synchronized schedule.
