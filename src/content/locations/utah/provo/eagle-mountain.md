For many Eagle Mountain residents, the workday happens somewhere else in
the metro. That commuting reality is the most important fact in an
Eagle Mountain earning capacity analysis: the relevant question is rarely
what work exists inside the city, but what work exists within the labor
market an evaluee can reasonably reach, and how an injury changes that
reach. Purinton Analytics frames its
[vocational expert opinions](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) around
that commuting market for plaintiff and defense counsel alike, and pairs
them with [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) when the
injuries call for a lifetime of future care.

## Courts serving Eagle Mountain

Eagle Mountain lies in Utah County, and state-court civil actions arising
here are filed in the District Court that serves the county. Federal
matters in the state proceed in the United States District Court for the
District of Utah. Which forum governs, and what that forum requires of a
testifying expert, is settled during the conflict check and intake
process rather than guessed from an address. Counsel with matters across
the state will find the firm's statewide coverage on the
[Utah page](/locations/utah/).

## Measuring a commuter's labor market

An opinion that treats a commuter town as a closed economy fails on
cross-examination. The firm instead builds Eagle Mountain findings on
Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for
the Provo-Orem-Lehi metropolitan area and for Utah statewide, layered
with state workforce agency labor market information and direct research
into specific occupations where the referral requires it. The report then
addresses how the evaluee's restrictions interact with commuting itself:
whether driving tolerance, scheduling limits, or stamina findings shrink
the practically available market. Related engagements throughout the
metro are described on the
[Provo metro practice page](/locations/utah/provo/).

## Bringing the evaluation to the evaluee

The firm maintains no office in Eagle Mountain and will not print a local
address it does not occupy. What it offers instead is designed for
exactly this kind of community: a remote-first engagement in which record
review, structured interviews, and most standardized testing are
completed from wherever the evaluee lives, under the publishers'
administration protocols. If the referral questions require face-to-face
evaluation, the appointment is held in the evaluee's own community, so an
injured person is not asked to make the very commute that may be at issue
in the case. When testimony or in-person work is scheduled, travel is
arranged from the firm's Kansas City home office, which also anchors its
[nationwide engagements](/locations/nationwide/).

## First step: the conflict check

No substantive conversation happens before clearance. Counsel begin by
[requesting a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) with the caption or the
names of the parties, along with any expert disclosure or trial dates
already set. Once cleared, scope and fees are confirmed in writing and a
records request tailored to the claims follows. If the damages
presentation also requires an economist, the firm coordinates
[a combined damages engagement](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
under which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis, so the vocational findings, the care plan, and the economic
projections arrive as a coherent whole without any expert opining outside
their own discipline.
