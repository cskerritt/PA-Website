Most American Fork injury and employment cases eventually reduce to a
short list of questions. Can this person return to their former work? If
not, what work remains within their restrictions, education, and
experience? What would that work pay relative to what they earned before?
And what will their future care require? Purinton Analytics exists to
answer those questions with evidence, offering
[vocational expert analysis](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) and
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) to plaintiff and
defense counsel in American Fork matters, and
[rebuttal review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) when the question is
whether an opposing expert answered them soundly.

## The forums for American Fork litigation

American Fork sits in Utah County, whose District Court hears the
state-court civil docket arising here. In the federal system, the forum
available in Utah is the United States District Court for the District of
Utah. The firm does not assume which court will control or what that
court will ask of a testifying expert; both are confirmed as part of the
conflict check and intake. Engagements in Utah's other regions are
addressed on the [statewide Utah page](/locations/utah/).

## Answering the earning capacity question with data

The earning capacity answer is only as good as the market data beneath
it. For American Fork evaluees the firm relies on occupational employment
and wage estimates from the Bureau of Labor Statistics covering the
Provo-Orem-Lehi metropolitan area and the state of Utah, augmented by
state workforce agency labor market information and direct labor market
research where the case requires it. Because American Fork workers move
freely through the metro for employment, the analysis considers the
market an evaluee can actually access under their restrictions, and the
report discloses the geographic scope of every finding. Work across the
broader metro is described on the
[Provo metro services page](/locations/utah/provo/).

## Answering the future care question

Where injuries are catastrophic, the employability questions are joined
by care questions: what treatment, equipment, and support the evaluee
will need over a lifetime. The firm's life care plans are built from the
medical record and treating-provider input, itemized so that each
recommendation can be traced and tested. There is no American Fork
office; evaluations are by appointment, conducted remotely under
publisher protocols where the instruments allow, and held in the
evaluee's own community when in-person work is required. Travel is
scheduled from the Kansas City home office that serves the firm's
[engagements nationwide](/locations/nationwide/).

## Putting the questions to the firm

Retention begins with a
[conflict check request](/refer-a-case/) carrying the caption or party
names, followed after clearance by written scope and fee confirmation
and a records request shaped to the claims. Counsel should identify the
disclosure and trial dates already fixed so the evaluation and report
schedule can be built around them. If the case requires translating
vocational and care findings into an economic loss figure, that
translation is not performed by the firm itself: through
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/),
independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis
while the vocational and care-planning foundations proceed under one
engagement structure, keeping every opinion inside its author's
qualifications.
