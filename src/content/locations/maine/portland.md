Purinton Analytics serves plaintiff and defense counsel in the
Portland-South Portland metro area with vocational expert and life care
planning services built for litigation. Referrals from the region most often
involve [earning capacity and employability evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/)
in personal injury and employment disputes,
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) and
[medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/) in
catastrophic injury matters, and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. The same report standard applies whether the referral
arrives from Portland or from anywhere else in the firm's
[Maine practice](/locations/maine/).

## Venue footprint: Cumberland, Sagadahoc, and York counties

Engagements are accepted for matters venued in the Superior Court in
Cumberland County, Sagadahoc County, and York County, and in the United
States District Court for the District of Maine. The written opinion is
prepared to the same standard regardless of forum: a documented conflict
check, a defined scope, a records request tailored to the claims, and a
report that either side of the caption can examine line by line. Where a
matter proceeds to deposition or trial, testimony is scheduled around the
court's calendar, and [rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/)
assignments are accepted on the same terms as affirmative vocational work.

## Portland meetings are by appointment

The firm keeps no staffed office in Portland and publishes no street address
for this location, because accuracy about presence is part of the same
discipline that governs its reports. Most engagements begin remotely, with
records review, structured interviews, and standardized testing administered
under publisher protocols where remote administration is supported. When the
referral questions call for in-person evaluation, or when deposition and
trial dates require presence, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home
office and planned around the litigation calendar rather than around office
hours. Counsel receive a single point of contact and a consistent report
format wherever the work is performed.

## Labor market research for Portland evaluees

Vocational findings for evaluees in the metro rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the
Portland-South Portland metropolitan area and for Maine statewide,
supplemented by state workforce agency labor market information and by
direct employer contact where the referral questions warrant it. The point
of grounding the analysis in the evaluee's own labor market is
cross-examination: opinions about employability and earning capacity should
survive questions about where the jobs actually are. Life care plans and
medical cost projections for Maine residents are priced to the evaluee's own
market using provider-specific inquiries and published fee data rather than
national averages, so the resulting figures reflect what care costs where
the evaluee lives.

## Starting a Portland engagement

Counsel on either side of the caption can begin with a
[conflict check](/refer-a-case/): submit the caption or party names and note
any imminent disclosure, deposition, or trial dates, since scheduling is
often the immediate practical question in a retained-expert engagement. Once
conflicts clear, intake defines the scope, the records request, and the
delivery timeline in writing before analysis begins. Matters outside the
metro, in Maine or beyond it, are handled through the firm's
[national practice](/locations/nationwide/) under the same intake, the same
methodology, and the same publication standard.
