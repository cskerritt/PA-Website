A vocational referral from Bangor starts the same way every engagement in
this practice starts: a documented conflict check, a scope defined in
writing, and a records request tailored to the claims actually pleaded. That
discipline matters more, not less, when the expert works at a distance,
because the written opinion has to stand on a record that plaintiff and
defense counsel alike can examine. On that foundation, Purinton Analytics
offers Bangor area attorneys
[vocational expert evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) of
employability and earning capacity, [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/)
for catastrophic injury, and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Penobscot County and the District of Maine

The firm accepts matters venued in the Superior Court in Penobscot County
and in the United States District Court for the District of Maine, working
for whichever side of the caption retains it. Assignments range from
affirmative evaluation of an injured or displaced worker to
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) of an opposing
expert's report, and the methodology is the same in either posture:
findings tied to records, testing, and published data, stated so that
cross-examination tests the analysis rather than uncovering it. Counsel
elsewhere in the state can start from the firm's
[Maine practice page](/locations/maine/), which describes the statewide
venue coverage.

## Serving Bangor without a local office

There is no staffed Purinton Analytics office in Bangor, and the firm
publishes no street address for this location; candor about presence is part
of the same standard that governs its reports. Meetings are by appointment.
The bulk of most engagements proceeds remotely, through records review,
structured interviews, and standardized testing under publisher protocols
where remote administration is supported. When an in-person evaluation is
warranted, or when a deposition or trial date requires the expert in the
room, travel is arranged from the Kansas City home office with enough lead
time built into the schedule to protect the litigation calendar.

## Labor market evidence for the Bangor area

Employability and earning capacity opinions for Bangor area evaluees are
grounded in Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage
estimates for the Bangor metropolitan area and for Maine statewide, along
with state workforce agency labor market information and direct labor
market research where the referral questions call for it. Life care plans
and [medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/) are
priced to the evaluee's own market through provider-specific inquiries and
published fee data, because the cost of future care in the evaluee's
community, not a national average, is what the trier of fact needs.

## Beginning a Bangor engagement

The gateway to any engagement is the [conflict check](/refer-a-case/).
Submit the caption or party names, identify the referral questions if they
are already framed, and flag any imminent disclosure, deposition, or trial
dates. Intake then fixes the scope, the records list, and the delivery
schedule in writing before analysis begins. Counsel with matters beyond
Maine will find the same intake and the same report standard described on
the firm's [nationwide page](/locations/nationwide/).
