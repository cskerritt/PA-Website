The practical question for counsel retaining an expert in an Old Town
matter is often logistical before it is analytical: the evaluee lives in a
small Penobscot County community, the retaining office may sit far from
it, and the disclosure calendar will not wait on anyone's travel
arrangements. Purinton Analytics is organized for that shape of case. The
practice works remote-first from its Kansas City home office, serves every
other location by appointment, and keeps no storefront in Old Town or
anywhere else it does not staff. What counsel gets instead is a documented
conflict check, a defined scope, and a written opinion prepared for either
side of the caption, whether the referral is a
[vocational assessment](/services/vocational-expert-witness/), a
[life care plan](/services/life-care-planning/), or a
[coordinated damages engagement](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## County and court context for an Old Town claim

Old Town sits in Penobscot County, and a state-law claim arising there is
tried in Maine's Superior Court. Claims carrying federal jurisdiction have
the United States District Court for the District of Maine available as a
forum; which court ultimately controls, and what qualification or
registration a venue asks of a testifying expert, is confirmed during the
conflict check and intake rather than assumed at retention. Every report
is written to be read aloud in either courtroom, with each conclusion
traced to a stated method and a cited source. The
[Bangor metro page](/locations/maine/bangor/) collects the regional court
picture, and the [Maine page](/locations/maine/) frames statewide
practice.

## Earning capacity in a smaller community

An evaluee's town is not the boundary of the analysis, but it is the
starting point. For an Old Town resident, employability and earning
capacity depend on what work is reachable and sustainable from a small
community within the Bangor metropolitan area, and the opinion has to say
so with evidence rather than impression. The file rests on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates covering the Bangor
metropolitan area and Maine as a whole, on state workforce agency labor
market information, and on direct employer research when the question is
whether specific positions are genuinely open to someone with this
evaluee's restrictions and history. Opposing counsel should be able to
retrace every step, which is why each source is named in the report.

## Scheduling the evaluation around the case calendar

Life care planning and vocational interviews for an Old Town evaluee are
planned against the deadlines that already bind the case, not the other
way around. Secure video carries much of the work: records review,
counsel conferences, and many interviews proceed without anyone
traveling, which keeps costs proportionate for a smaller matter. Where
the referral requires in-person evaluation, or where a deposition or
trial date requires presence, travel is arranged from the Kansas City
home office and the evaluation takes place in the evaluee's own
community, where daily routines, care needs, and realistic access to work
can be observed firsthand. The absence of a local office is stated
plainly because a report is only as credible as the practice behind it.
