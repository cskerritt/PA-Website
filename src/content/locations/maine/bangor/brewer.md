and for a Brewer case that courtroom belongs to Penobscot County. Purinton

and disability matters arising in this small city within the Bangor

## The Penobscot County courthouse and the federal alternative

State-law claims for a Brewer party proceed in Maine's Superior Court, the
trial bench that hears Penobscot County disputes. Where a claim carries
federal jurisdiction, the available forum is the United States District
Court for the District of Maine; the court that controls a particular
caption, along with any registration or qualification requirement that
venue places on a testifying expert, is identified during the conflict
check and intake. Reports are drafted so the same document serves either
bench without revision, with methods stated plainly enough for a judge to
test and for opposing counsel to probe. Regional venue detail appears on
the [Bangor metro page](/locations/maine/bangor/), and the statewide

## A small city attached to a regional employment center

Brewer's workforce does not stop at the city line, and neither can the
analysis. An earning-capacity opinion for a Brewer evaluee first defines
the labor market this particular person can realistically reach and
sustain after injury, given restrictions, education, and work history,
then documents what that market offers with sources the other side can
verify: Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage
estimates for the Bangor metropolitan area and for Maine statewide, state
workforce agency labor market information, and direct labor market
research when a referral question turns on whether particular work exists
in practice rather than on paper. No figure enters the report without a
citation, and no citation is offered that cannot be pulled and checked.

## Seeing the evaluee in Brewer

There is no Purinton Analytics office in Brewer, and the firm publishes no
local address anywhere it does not keep staff; that candor is deliberate,
because accuracy about presence belongs to the same discipline that
governs the reports. Most of the work travels well. Records analysis,
transferable-skills assessment, and counsel conferences proceed remotely,
and secure video handles many vocational interviews at a cost both sides
can defend. When the referral calls for an in-person evaluation, the
assessment is conducted in the evaluee's own community, with travel
scheduled from the Kansas City home office, so functional capacity and
realistic commuting conditions are observed rather than assumed.
Deposition and trial appearances are planned the same way, against the
dates the court has fixed.
