## Sequencing the work against the case schedule

A disciplined engagement runs in a fixed order: conflict clearance, scope
definition, a records request tailored to the claims, the vocational
interview, labor market research, then the written opinion, with time
reserved for counsel to review the draft against the record before
disclosure. Compressing that sequence is sometimes unavoidable, and remote
workflow makes short timelines workable, but the order itself does not
change, because each step is the foundation the next one cites. Counsel
who flag mediation, deposition, and trial settings at intake get a
schedule engineered to meet them.

## Venue context for a Saco caption

Saco is a York County city, and state-law injury, employment, and
disability claims arising there are heard in Maine's Superior Court. On
the federal side, Maine's forum is the United States District Court for the
District of Maine; which court controls a particular case, and any expert
registration or qualification requirement attached to that venue, is
confirmed during the conflict check and intake rather than assumed in
advance. The surrounding regional venue picture is drawn on the
[Portland metro page](/locations/maine/portland/), and the statewide
summary appears on the [Maine page](/locations/maine/).

## Labor market evidence for a Saco evaluee

What a Saco resident can earn after injury is a question about a real
commuting range, not a slogan about the regional economy. The analysis
establishes the employment the evaluee can sustain given documented
restrictions and history, supported by Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates for the Portland and South
Portland metropolitan area and for Maine statewide, by state workforce
agency labor market information, and by direct labor market research when
an opinion turns on specific jobs with specific employers. Every source is
cited for verification, because a conclusion opposing counsel cannot check
is a conclusion the court should not credit.
