## Sagadahoc County and the courts that serve it

Bath sits in Sagadahoc County, and a state-law injury, employment, or
disability claim arising in the city proceeds in Maine's Superior Court,
with the county supplying the local venue context. Claims with federal
jurisdiction look to the United States District Court for the District of
Maine, the state's federal trial forum; the court that actually controls a
given caption, and any registration or qualification requirement that
venue imposes on a testifying expert, is confirmed as part of the conflict
check and intake. Because both possibilities are anticipated from the
outset, the report is drafted once, to the stricter standard, rather than
adapted after the forum is known.

## Scale honesty in the earning-capacity analysis

For a Bath evaluee, the difference between a fair opinion and a lazy one
is scale. A small city's residents work within a commuting range shaped by
their own restrictions and circumstances, and an analysis that silently
substitutes the principal city's opportunities overstates or understates
capacity depending on which side commissioned it. The firm documents the
market the evaluee can genuinely reach, using Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates for the Portland and South
Portland metropolitan area and for Maine statewide, state workforce agency
labor market information, and direct labor market research when the
referral questions require confirming particular jobs with particular
employers. Each source appears in the report with a citation opposing
counsel can follow.

## Serving Bath by appointment

No office is kept in Bath and no street address is published for this
location; the firm describes its footprint as carefully as it describes an
evaluee's. Most of the engagement travels well over secure video, records
review and counsel conferences especially, which keeps costs in proportion
to the case. When the referral calls for in-person evaluation, or when a
deposition or trial appearance is on the calendar, the expert travels from
the Kansas City home office and conducts the assessment in the evaluee's
own community, where home layout, caregiving demands, and everyday
routines can be seen directly. That observed detail is what separates a
defensible life care plan from a priced list of services.
