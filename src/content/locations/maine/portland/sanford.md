A vocational evaluation should come to the evaluee, not the other way
around, and that principle matters most in a community like Sanford, where
demanding that an injured worker travel to an expert's office would itself
distort the picture of what that worker can do. Purinton Analytics builds
its Sanford engagements around that logistics discipline while delivering
the full scope of the practice:
[vocational expert opinions](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) on
employability and earning capacity, [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/)
for catastrophic injury and chronic illness, and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis under one engagement structure alongside the vocational and
care-planning work. Referrals are accepted from plaintiff counsel and
defense counsel on identical terms.

## How a Sanford evaluee is assessed

There is no Sanford office and no published local address; the firm states
that plainly because accuracy about presence is part of the same standard
applied to every opinion it signs. What counsel gets instead is a process
designed around the evaluee. Records analysis, attorney conferences, and
many vocational interviews are handled by secure video. When the referral
questions call for in-person testing or observation, the expert travels
from the Kansas City home office and conducts the evaluation in Sanford
itself, in the setting where the evaluee actually lives and would actually
work. A life care planning interview conducted in the home reveals the
caregiving arrangements, architectural barriers, and daily routines that
no questionnaire captures.

## Venue context in York County

Sanford sits in York County. State-law injury, employment, and disability
claims arising there proceed in Maine's Superior Court, while Maine's
federal trial forum is the United States District Court for the District of
Maine. The controlling venue for any particular caption, together with any
expert registration or qualification requirement that venue carries, is
confirmed during the conflict check and intake rather than presumed at the
outset. Because the report is written to survive scrutiny in either
system, counsel does not need to know the final forum before retaining the
expert. Regional context appears on the
[Portland metro page](/locations/maine/portland/), and the statewide
overview is on the [Maine page](/locations/maine/).

## Earning capacity in a market of its own

Sanford's workforce cannot simply be assigned the principal city's job
market. An honest earning-capacity analysis asks what employment exists
within the commuting range this evaluee, with these restrictions and this
work history, can realistically sustain, and it documents the answer from
sources both sides can verify: Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational
employment and wage estimates for the Portland and South Portland
metropolitan area and for Maine statewide, state workforce agency labor
market information, and direct labor market research where the opinion
depends on specific employers or specific jobs. Nothing in the file rests
on impression; every figure in the report traces to a cited source.
