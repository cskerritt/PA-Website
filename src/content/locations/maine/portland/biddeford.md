Start with the courtroom. A Biddeford injury, employment, or disability
claim under state law belongs to Maine's Superior Court, York County being
the county in which the city sits, and a claim carrying federal
jurisdiction looks to the United States District Court for the District of
Maine as the state's federal forum. Which venue controls a given caption,
and whether that venue imposes a registration or qualification requirement
on a testifying expert, is settled during the conflict check and intake,
not guessed at retention. Purinton Analytics writes for either bench from
the first draft, preparing
[vocational expert opinions](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) on
employability and earning capacity, [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/)
for catastrophic injury, and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## A report built for cross-examination

Retaining counsel in Biddeford, whether representing the injured party or
defending the claim, receives the same product: a written opinion whose
methodology an opposing expert can retrace step by step. Transferable
skills are analyzed against the evaluee's documented work history,
restrictions come from the medical record rather than advocacy, and every
conclusion is stated with its basis. That symmetry is deliberate. An
expert whose analysis depends on who signs the retention letter has
surrendered the only asset that matters at trial, and the firm's practice
is organized to keep that asset intact.

## Biddeford's place in the regional labor market

The earning-capacity question for a Biddeford evaluee is bounded by what
that person can sustain, not by municipal lines. The analysis defines a
realistic commuting range in light of the evaluee's restrictions,
education, and history, then documents the work available inside it using
Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for
the Portland and South Portland metropolitan area and for Maine statewide,
supplemented by state workforce agency labor market information and by
direct employer research when a referral question turns on whether
particular jobs are genuinely open to a person with the evaluee's profile.
Citations are precise enough for opposing counsel to pull each source and
test the reasoning against it.

## Logistics without a local storefront

The firm maintains no Biddeford office and lists no street address here;
plainness about presence is part of the credibility the reports depend on.
In practice the absence costs counsel nothing. Secure video carries the
records conferences and most vocational interviews, and when in-person
evaluation is warranted, or a deposition or trial appearance is set, the
expert travels from the Kansas City home office and meets the evaluee in
the evaluee's own community. Home-based life care planning interviews
document caregiving demands and daily function where they occur, which is
where a defensible plan has to begin.
