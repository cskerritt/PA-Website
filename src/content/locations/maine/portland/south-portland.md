South Portland is written into the name of its metropolitan area, and that
is worth taking literally: the Portland and South Portland region is defined
by both cities, not by Portland alone. Counsel handling an injury,
employment, or disability matter for a South Portland party can retain
Purinton Analytics for [vocational expert opinions](/services/vocational-expert-witness/)
on employability and earning capacity, [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/)
after catastrophic injury, and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. The same file is prepared whether the retaining party
sits at the plaintiff table or the defense table, because an opinion that
shifts with the caption is not worth defending at deposition.

## Where a South Portland matter is heard

South Portland lies in Cumberland County, and a state-law claim arising
there is tried in Maine's Superior Court. For claims carrying federal
jurisdiction, the state's federal forum is the United States District Court
for the District of Maine; which court controls a given caption, and any
registration or qualification requirement that venue imposes on a
testifying expert, is identified and confirmed during the conflict check
and intake. Reports are drafted to the same standard for either setting.
The broader venue picture for the region appears on the
[Portland metro page](/locations/maine/portland/), and statewide practice is
summarized on the [Maine page](/locations/maine/).

## The labor market question for a South Portland evaluee

An earning-capacity opinion for a South Portland evaluee cannot treat the
city as an afterthought of its larger neighbor, and it cannot assume the
whole metro is within reach either. The analysis defines the labor market
the evaluee can actually sustain, given restrictions, education, and work
history, and then documents what that market offers. Sources are
qualitative and checkable: Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational
employment and wage estimates for the Portland and South Portland
metropolitan area and for Maine statewide, state workforce agency labor
market information, and direct employer contact when a referral question
turns on whether specific jobs exist in practice rather than on paper.
Every source is cited so opposing counsel can pull it and retrace the
reasoning.

## Meeting the evaluee where the case arose

The firm keeps no office in South Portland and publishes no local address,
because candor about presence belongs to the same discipline that governs
the reports. Records review, counsel conferences, and most vocational
interviews proceed by secure video, which holds costs proportionate for
both sides. When the referral requires an in-person evaluation, or when a
deposition or trial date puts the expert in the room, travel is scheduled
from the Kansas City home office and the assessment is conducted in the
evaluee's own community, where functional limitations and the practical
demands of daily life can be seen firsthand rather than assumed. Life care
planning interviews follow the same rule, meeting the evaluee where daily
care actually happens.
