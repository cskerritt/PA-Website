## Measuring the market a Westbrook evaluee can reach

The labor market analysis begins with the person, not the map. Medical
restrictions, transferable skills, education, and work history define the
range of employment an evaluee can sustain; only then does the research
establish what that range contains. The evidentiary base is qualitative
and fully cited: Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and
wage estimates for the Portland and South Portland metropolitan area and
for Maine statewide, state workforce agency labor market information, and
direct contact with employers when a referral question hinges on whether
identified jobs are truly available to someone with the evaluee's
limitations. Opposing counsel can pull every source the report cites,
which is precisely the point.

## Cumberland County venue context

Westbrook lies in Cumberland County, and state-law injury, employment, and
disability litigation arising in the city proceeds in Maine's Superior

the District of Maine; the venue that controls a specific caption, along
with any registration or qualification requirement it places on a
testifying expert, is identified and confirmed through the conflict check

## By-appointment service, honestly described

There is no staffed Westbrook office and no published street address for
this location; the firm treats claims about its own presence with the same
rigor it applies to claims in its reports. Engagements run remotely where
remote work serves the case: secure video handles records conferences and
most vocational interviews, keeping fees proportionate for both sides of
the caption. Where the referral requires hands-on evaluation, or where
deposition and trial dates demand attendance, travel is arranged from the
Kansas City home office and the evaluee is seen in the evaluee's own
community, so that functional capacity, caregiving arrangements, and
realistic commuting conditions are observed at first hand rather than
inferred from records.
