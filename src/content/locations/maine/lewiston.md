## Androscoggin County venue coverage

Engagements are accepted for matters in the Superior Court in Androscoggin
County and in the United States District Court for the District of Maine.
The work spans the life of a case: evaluation and written opinion during
discovery, [rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) when
the opposing expert's report arrives, and deposition and trial testimony
when the matter goes the distance. Whichever side of the caption issues the
retention, the report is built the same way, from a documented conflict
check through a defined scope to findings tied to records, testing, and
published sources. Coverage across the rest of the state is described on
the firm's [Maine practice page](/locations/maine/).

## What by-appointment service means here

The firm has no staffed office in Lewiston or Auburn and publishes no
street address for this location. That is stated outright because honesty
about presence belongs to the same discipline that governs the reports
themselves. In-person work still happens when it should: if the referral
questions require face-to-face evaluation, or if a deposition or trial
date requires attendance, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home
office and planned against the court's calendar. The by-appointment model
is a description of logistics, not a limit on service: evaluation quality
depends on the records, the testing, and the analysis, none of which
requires a storefront. Counsel work with a single point of contact from
intake through testimony, and the report format does not change with the
meeting format.

## Grounding opinions in the Lewiston-Auburn labor market

Employability and earning capacity conclusions for Androscoggin County
evaluees draw on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and
wage estimates for the Lewiston-Auburn metropolitan area and for Maine
statewide, together with state workforce agency labor market information
and direct labor market research where the questions warrant it. For
care-related damages, [medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/)
and life care plans are priced to the evaluee's own market through
provider-specific inquiries and published fee data, since a projection
built on national averages invites exactly the cross-examination a
well-founded report should never face.

The starting point is a [conflict check](/refer-a-case/): submit the
caption or party names, note the referral questions if they are framed, and
identify any imminent disclosure, deposition, or trial dates. Intake then
sets the scope, the records request, and the delivery timeline in writing.
Counsel whose dockets extend beyond Maine can rely on the same intake and
the same publication standard nationwide, described on the firm's
[national coverage page](/locations/nationwide/).
