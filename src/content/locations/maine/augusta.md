The Superior Court sitting in Kennebec County, together with the United
States District Court for the District of Maine, hears the injury,
employment, and disability disputes that most often generate expert
referrals from the Augusta-Waterville area. For those matters, Purinton
Analytics provides plaintiff and defense counsel with
[vocational expert evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) of
employability and earning capacity, [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/)
and [medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/) in
catastrophic injury cases, and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Courts and postures in the Augusta-Waterville area

Within Kennebec County, engagements are accepted in any litigation posture:
affirmative vocational evaluation of an injured or terminated worker,
life care planning for a catastrophically injured evaluee, or
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) of the opposing
expert's methodology. Federal matters proceed in the United States District
Court for the District of Maine under the same scope-and-records discipline
as state court work. Because the practice is retained by either side of the
caption, every report is written on the assumption that opposing counsel
will read it closely, and every opinion is tied to records, testing, and
published data that both parties can inspect. The firm's
[Maine practice page](/locations/maine/) describes coverage beyond Kennebec
County.

## Appointment-based logistics, stated plainly

Purinton Analytics maintains no staffed office in Augusta or Waterville and
publishes no street address for this location. Meetings happen by
appointment, and most of the work happens remotely: records analysis,
structured interviews, and standardized testing under publisher protocols
where remote administration is supported. When the referral calls for an
in-person evaluation, or when a deposition or trial requires the expert
present, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office and
coordinated with counsel's litigation deadlines. Stating these logistics
plainly, rather than implying a local storefront, is deliberate: an expert
whose office claims cannot survive scrutiny should not expect the rest of
the report to fare better.

## Labor market foundations for Kennebec County evaluees

Opinions about what work an evaluee can perform, and what it pays, rest on
Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for
the Augusta-Waterville area and for Maine statewide, supplemented by state
workforce agency labor market information and direct labor market research
where warranted. The published data anchors the opinion; the direct
research confirms that the anchor holds for this evaluee, in this market,
with these restrictions. Care-cost findings follow the same logic: life
care plans and medical cost projections are priced through provider-specific inquiries
and published fee data for the evaluee's own market, so that the projection
reflects the cost of care where the evaluee actually lives rather than a
national blend.

## Opening an Augusta engagement

Every engagement begins with a [conflict check](/refer-a-case/). Counsel
should submit the caption or party names, describe the referral questions
as currently framed, and note any imminent disclosure, deposition, or trial
dates so scheduling can be confirmed at intake. Scope, records, and
delivery dates are then fixed in writing before analysis starts. The same
process, methodology, and report standard extend to matters anywhere in the
country through the firm's [national practice](/locations/nationwide/).
