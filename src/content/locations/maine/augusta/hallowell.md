## Placing Hallowell in the metro labor market

A town of Hallowell's size does not contain its own labor market; its
residents work within the wider Augusta-Waterville, ME metropolitan
economy, and the analysis says so plainly. Vocational findings rest on
Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for
that metropolitan area and for Maine statewide, supplemented by state
workforce agency labor market information and by direct labor market
research when the referral questions require it. What the report never
does is assume the whole metro is open to a given evaluee: access is
tested against restrictions, transferable skills, and transportation, and
the chosen market frame is stated and defended rather than implied.

## Evaluation without a local office

The firm maintains no staffed location in Hallowell and publishes no
street address here, because accuracy about presence belongs to the same
discipline as accuracy about data. Meetings are by appointment. Secure
video carries the bulk of the work, from records conferences to counsel
discussions to many interviews, and when the referral questions justify an
in-person evaluation, the evaluee is seen in the community where daily
function actually occurs. Travel for evaluations, depositions, and trial
testimony is scheduled from the Kansas City home office against the dates
the court has set.
