## Where a Waterville matter is heard

Waterville sits in Kennebec County, and civil actions under Maine law
proceed in the Superior Court. For claims with a federal footing, the
United States District Court for the District of Maine is the forum
available within the state; the venue that controls a particular case,
together with any registration or qualification requirement it imposes on
a testifying expert, is identified and confirmed during the conflict check
and intake. Because a case filed in one forum can end up decided in
another, every report is written to a single standard: reproducible
methods, cited sources a paralegal can pull, and no opinion beyond the
disclosed scope. The surrounding region is covered on the
[Augusta metro page](/locations/maine/augusta/) and the state as a whole
on the [Maine page](/locations/maine/).

## The market frame for earning capacity

The retaining side does not change the data. Vocational findings for a
Waterville evaluee rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational
employment and wage estimates for the Augusta-Waterville, ME metropolitan
area and for Maine statewide, on state workforce agency labor market
information, and on direct labor market research where the referral
questions warrant it. The report states explicitly whether the analysis
was framed to the town, the metro, or the state, and defends that choice,
because the frame is often where opposing experts genuinely disagree and
where an unexamined assumption is easiest to impeach.

## Appointments, evaluation, and travel

The firm keeps no office in Waterville and does not pretend otherwise;
meetings are by appointment, and candor about presence is part of the same
discipline that governs the written work. Most engagements proceed
remotely, with secure video handling records conferences, counsel
discussions, and many vocational interviews. When the referral calls for
in-person evaluation, it takes place in the evaluee's own community, where
daily function can actually be observed, with travel scheduled from the
Kansas City home office. Deposition and trial appearances are planned the
same way, around the dates the court has set.
