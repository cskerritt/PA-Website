Much of what decides whether an expert opinion actually helps in a
Gardiner case is settled by the calendar before the expert is ever
retained: how much runway remains before disclosure, whether the records
that ground the opinion can be requested, received, and reviewed in time,
and whether the deposition schedule leaves room to supplement. Counsel who
raise the vocational or care-planning question early get a report built on
a complete record; counsel who call late get an honest answer about what
can and cannot be done responsibly in the time left. Purinton Analytics
gives both, in [vocational expert](/services/vocational-expert-witness/)
engagements on employability and earning capacity, in
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) for serious injury,
and in [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Gardiner's courts and the calendar

Gardiner is a Kennebec County community, and civil claims under Maine law
are heard in the Superior Court. Where a case carries a federal basis, the
United States District Court for the District of Maine is the federal
forum the state offers; which bench controls a given matter, and what
qualification or registration it asks of a testifying expert, is confirmed
during the conflict check and intake rather than assumed. Each forum
brings its own disclosure and discovery rhythm, which is why intake begins
with dates: the scope agreed at retention is sized to the schedule the
court has already imposed. Regional context sits on the
[Augusta metro page](/locations/maine/augusta/), and statewide coverage on
the [Maine page](/locations/maine/).

## Labor market evidence that holds up

Whether the engagement letter comes from a plaintiff firm or a defense
firm, the labor market evidence is drawn from the same sources: Bureau of
Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the
Augusta-Waterville, ME metropolitan area and for Maine statewide, state
workforce agency labor market information, and direct employer research
where the questions demand it. For a Gardiner evaluee the honest analytic
question is access: which parts of the metro market are realistically
open, given restrictions, transportation, and the evaluee's actual work
history. The report answers that question expressly instead of burying it
in an assumption.

## How a Gardiner evaluee is seen

There is no Gardiner office, no local storefront, and no published street
address for this location; representing otherwise would be inconsistent
with the accuracy the reports themselves claim. Engagements are handled
remotely wherever remote work serves the opinion, with secure video for
records conferences and most interviews. When an in-person evaluation is
warranted, it is conducted in the evaluee's own community, and travel is
arranged from the Kansas City home office, the same arrangement that
governs deposition and trial appearances.
