Purinton Analytics answers those questions through [vocational expert](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) opinions and [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) for plaintiff and defense engagements alike, and every Auburn matter starts the way matters start everywhere else in the practice, with a documented conflict check and a scope defined in writing before any records are reviewed.

## Androscoggin County venue and the courts serving Auburn

Auburn sits in Androscoggin County, and state-court matters arising here proceed in Maine's Superior Court, where earning-capacity and future-care disputes are tried on the strength of the written record. For federal matters, the United States District Court for the District of Maine is the federal forum available in the state, and the controlling venue and division for any particular caption are identified and confirmed during the conflict check and intake. Reports are drafted to the same standard regardless of forum, so an opinion prepared for a Superior Court matter withstands the scrutiny a federal courtroom would apply, and the reverse.

## Auburn's place in the Lewiston-Auburn labor market

A vocational opinion about an Auburn resident has to be grounded in the labor market that person actually faces, which includes Lewiston, the metro's principal city, and the broader Lewiston-Auburn, ME area. The firm's findings for Auburn evaluees rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Lewiston-Auburn area and for Maine statewide, on state workforce agency labor market information, and on direct labor market research where the referral questions warrant it. Because Auburn is a small city, the analysis pays particular attention to which occupations are realistically available without relocation, and the written report shows that reasoning rather than asserting a conclusion. ## Evaluating an Auburn resident

Most engagements begin remotely. Records review, vocational interviews, and standardized testing can proceed by secure videoconference, which keeps costs proportionate for counsel and avoids unnecessary travel for an evaluee recovering from injury. When the referral questions call for an in-person evaluation, the firm schedules it in the evaluee's own community rather than requiring travel to a distant office. Purinton Analytics maintains no staffed office in Auburn and publishes no local street address, because accuracy about presence is part of the same discipline that governs its reports; in-person work, along with deposition and trial appearances, is scheduled by appointment with travel arranged from the Kansas City home office.
