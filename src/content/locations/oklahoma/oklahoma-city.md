Purinton Analytics serves plaintiff and defense counsel across the Oklahoma
City metropolitan area with
[earning capacity and employability evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/)
in injury and employment matters,
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) and
[medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/) in catastrophic
injury cases, and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. The practice is national in scope, described on the
[nationwide coverage page](/locations/nationwide/), and its
[Oklahoma engagements](/locations/oklahoma/) follow the same documented
methodology counsel would encounter in any other venue.

## Venue footprint across the metro

Engagements are accepted for matters venued in the District Court of
Oklahoma County and the district courts of the counties that make up the
metropolitan statistical area, including Canadian County, Cleveland County,
Grady County, Lincoln County, Logan County, and McClain County. Federal
matters are supported throughout the state, in the United States District
Court for the Western District of Oklahoma, the United States District Court
for the Northern District of Oklahoma, and the United States District Court
for the Eastern District of Oklahoma. The forum does not change the work
product: every engagement begins with a documented conflict check, a defined
scope, and a records request tailored to the claims, so the written opinion
rests on a record either side of the caption can examine.

## Meetings by appointment, work without a storefront

Oklahoma City meetings are by appointment. The firm keeps no staffed office
in the metro and publishes no street address for this location, because
accuracy about presence is part of the same discipline that governs its
reports. Most engagements begin remotely, with records review, structured
interviews, and standardized testing under publisher protocols where remote
administration is supported. When the referral questions call for in-person
evaluation, or when deposition and trial dates require attendance, travel is
scheduled from the Kansas City home office. Counsel receive one report
standard and one point of contact regardless of where the work is performed.

## Labor market research for Oklahoma City evaluees

Vocational findings for evaluees in the metro rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Oklahoma City
metropolitan area and for Oklahoma statewide, state workforce agency labor
market information, and direct labor market research where the referral
questions warrant it. Life care plans and medical cost projections for
Oklahoma residents are priced to the evaluee's own market using
provider-specific inquiries and published fee data for the region rather
than undifferentiated national averages, and the sourcing for every figure
is documented in the report so that opposing counsel can trace each entry
to its basis.

## Starting an Oklahoma City engagement

Availability for a new matter is confirmed through a conflict check before
any substantive discussion of the case. Submit the caption or party names
through the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and note any imminent
disclosure, deposition, or trial dates so scheduling can be assessed at the
same time. Once cleared, intake defines the referral questions, the records
needed, and the evaluation format, and the engagement proceeds under the
same written protocols the firm applies to matters nationwide, whether the
retaining party sits at the plaintiff table or the defense table.
