Often the first expert question in a Muldrow case is not "what is the
loss" but "is the other side's loss opinion sound." Purinton Analytics
is retained in both postures. Counsel who already hold an opposing
vocational or care-planning report can engage the firm for
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/), a
methodical audit of the opinion's sources, methods, and internal
consistency; counsel building a case in chief can retain the firm for
an original [vocational evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/)
or [life care plan](/services/life-care-planning/). Because the firm
works for plaintiffs and defendants in comparable measure, its rebuttal
work critiques methodology rather than a side, and the same standards
it applies to others' reports govern its own.

## Venue questions answered at intake

Muldrow matters filed in state court proceed in the District Court for
Sequoyah County. Oklahoma's federal forums are the United States
District Court for the Eastern District of Oklahoma, the United States
District Court for the Northern District of Oklahoma, and the United
States District Court for the Western District of Oklahoma; which of
them controls a given case, and what registration or qualification
requirements attach in that forum, are questions resolved during the
conflict check and intake, before scheduling promises are made. Counsel
handling matters elsewhere in the state can start from the
[Oklahoma page](/locations/oklahoma/).

## Testing an opinion against the real market

Whether the assignment is an original opinion or a critique of someone
else's, the benchmark is the same body of data. A Muldrow evaluee's
realistic market is the regional economy anchored by Fort Smith, so the
firm works from Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and
wage estimates for the Fort Smith metropolitan area and for Oklahoma
statewide, from state workforce agency labor market information, and
from direct labor market research where a disputed occupation or
employer deserves closer study. A rebuttal report shows where an
opposing opinion departed from that record; an affirmative report shows
that it never did. Either way the reasoning is exposed for
cross-examination, consistent with the standards of the firm's
[metro practice](/locations/arkansas/fort-smith/).

## Presence, honestly stated

The firm keeps no staffed office in Muldrow and publishes no street
address for this location. Engagements are conducted by appointment:
records review, interviews, and most standardized testing are completed
remotely under applicable publisher protocols, in-person evaluations
are held in the evaluee's own community when the referral questions
require them, and travel for evaluations or testimony is scheduled from
the Kansas City home office. These are the same working arrangements
the firm describes for its [national practice](/locations/nationwide/),
applied without dilution to a small Sequoyah County town.

## How to retain the firm

A conflict check precedes everything, including any review of the
opposing expert's report. Counsel should
[submit the caption or party names](/refer-a-case/) along with the
posture of the engagement, affirmative or rebuttal, and any response or
disclosure deadline already running. On clearance the firm confirms a
written scope, and where full damages coordination is needed it offers
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.
