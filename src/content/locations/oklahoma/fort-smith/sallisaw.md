## An economy measured across the border

Because Sallisaw workers participate in the cross-border regional
economy, an earning capacity opinion confined to Oklahoma data would
miss much of the market a Sallisaw evaluee actually faces. The firm
therefore grounds its vocational findings in Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates for the Fort Smith
metropolitan area, which spans the state line, alongside estimates for
Oklahoma statewide, state workforce agency labor market information, and
direct labor market research where the referral questions call for it.
The report states which geography supports each finding, so the
cross-border character of the market is documented rather than waved at.
The metro-wide practice is described on the
[Fort Smith metro page](/locations/arkansas/fort-smith/).
