Purinton Analytics is retained by plaintiff counsel and by defense counsel
alike, and that even-handed posture shapes every Muskogee engagement:
opinions are written to survive scrutiny from whichever table did not
commission them. For matters arising in the Muskogee micropolitan
area, that means vocational and life care planning work built on a
documented conflict check, a defined scope, and a records request tailored
to the claims, delivered to the same standard the firm applies across its
[national practice](/locations/nationwide/).

## Engagement types for Muskogee matters

Counsel in the area typically retain the firm for
[vocational expert opinions](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) on
employability and earning capacity in injury and employment litigation, for
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) and
[medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/) in
catastrophic injury cases, and for
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. [Rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/)
of opposing expert reports is accepted under the same conflict-check
discipline as affirmative assignments.

## State and federal venues

State court matters are accepted in the District Court of Muskogee County,
the trial court serving the micropolitan statistical area. Federal
engagements are supported throughout Oklahoma, in the United States
District Court for the Eastern District of Oklahoma, the United States
District Court for the Northern District of Oklahoma, and the United States
District Court for the Western District of Oklahoma. The report format and
methodology remain identical from one forum to the next; what changes is
only the caption. The firm's statewide footprint is described on the
[Oklahoma page](/locations/oklahoma/).

## Presence, stated accurately

Muskogee meetings are by appointment. The firm keeps no staffed office here
and publishes no street address for this location, because accuracy about
presence belongs to the same discipline that governs its written opinions.
Most engagements begin remotely: records review, structured interviews, and
standardized testing under publisher protocols where remote administration
is supported. When the referral questions call for in-person evaluation, or
when deposition and trial dates require attendance, travel is scheduled
from the Kansas City home office, and counsel work with one point of
contact from intake through testimony.

## Labor market research for Muskogee County evaluees

Vocational findings rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational
employment and wage estimates and Oklahoma statewide data, supplemented by
state workforce agency labor market information and direct labor market
research where warranted. Employability and earning capacity conclusions
are tied to the occupations actually available in the evaluee's own labor
market, a distinction that matters in smaller regional economies and that
opposing counsel probe first at deposition. Life care plans and medical
cost projections are priced to the evaluee's market through
provider-specific inquiries and published fee data, with every entry
sourced in the report.

## Requesting availability

Availability for a Muskogee County matter is confirmed through a conflict
check before any substantive discussion. Submit the caption or party names
through the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/), noting any imminent
disclosure, deposition, or trial deadlines. Once conflicts clear, intake
defines the referral questions and records, and the engagement proceeds
under the firm's standard written protocols, whichever side of the caption
made the call.
