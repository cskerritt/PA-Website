What a McAlester referral ultimately buys is a document: a written opinion
that will be produced in discovery, tested at deposition, and read aloud at
trial. Purinton Analytics builds that document for plaintiff and defense
counsel in Pittsburg County matters, supported by
[vocational expert evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/),
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) of opposing
opinions, and [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds within a single
engagement structure. The firm's broader coverage of the state is described
on its [Oklahoma page](/locations/oklahoma/).

## Venues for Pittsburg County litigation

Engagements are accepted for matters in the District Court sitting in
Pittsburg County and in the federal courts of Oklahoma, including the United
States District Court for the Eastern District of Oklahoma, the United States
District Court for the Northern District of Oklahoma, and the United States
District Court for the Western District of Oklahoma. The report is written
to a standard that serves any of those forums: methodology disclosed,
data sources cited, assumptions stated, and conclusions that follow from the
record rather than from the retaining party's theory of the case.

## Testimony logistics without a local office

The firm does not maintain an office in McAlester and does not publish a
street address for this location; meetings are by appointment, and that
candor about presence is deliberate. The analytical work in most Pittsburg
County engagements proceeds remotely from the Kansas City home office,
through records review, structured interviews, and standardized testing under
publisher protocols where remote administration is supported. Depositions,
trial testimony, and in-person evaluations are handled by scheduled travel
from Kansas City, planned with counsel against the case deadlines so that
presence is arranged when it matters rather than promised in the abstract.

## Labor market evidence for the McAlester area

Earning capacity and employability opinions for evaluees in the McAlester
area rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage
estimates, read at the Oklahoma statewide level and through the area
estimates that cover Pittsburg County, together with state workforce agency
labor market information and direct labor market research where warranted.
Because the surrounding market is micropolitan in scale, the analysis pays
particular attention to which occupations are genuinely present, what the
realistic commuting range looks like, and how the evaluee's transferable
skills map onto the work that exists. Life care plans and medical cost
projections are priced to the evaluee's own region using provider-specific
inquiries and published fee data.

## How retention begins

Retention for a McAlester matter starts exactly where it starts across the
firm's [national practice](/locations/nationwide/): with conflicts. To begin,
[request a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) with the case caption and parties,
and identify any imminent disclosure, deposition, or trial dates. When the
check clears, counsel receive a defined scope and a records request tailored
to the claims, and any venue-specific registration or qualification
requirement is identified and confirmed during the conflict check and intake
process. Plaintiff and defense engagements proceed under the same terms and
the same report standard.
