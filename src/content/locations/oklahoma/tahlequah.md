Analytics accepts. Whether the referral arrives from plaintiff or defense
counsel, the work follows the same sequence: a documented conflict check, a
defined scope, a records request tailored to the claims, and only then the
analysis itself, whether that analysis is an
[earning capacity and employability evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
a [life care plan](/services/life-care-planning/), a
[medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/), or a
[coordinated damages engagement](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds within a single
engagement structure. Coverage of the wider state is described on the
[Oklahoma page](/locations/oklahoma/).

## Where Tahlequah matters are heard

The firm accepts matters venued in the District Court sitting in Cherokee
County as well as in Oklahoma's federal courts, including the United States
District Court for the Northern District of Oklahoma, the United States
District Court for the Eastern District of Oklahoma, and the United States
District Court for the Western District of Oklahoma. Forum choice changes the
procedural posture of a case, but it does not change the firm's product: the
written opinion is prepared so that its data sources, reasoning, and
conclusions can be examined line by line by the court and by opposing
counsel.

## Appointment-based presence, honestly described

There is no Purinton Analytics office in Tahlequah, staffed or otherwise, and
the firm publishes no street address for this location. Meetings are by
appointment, and most engagements from Cherokee County proceed remotely from
start to finish: records review, structured vocational interviews by video,
and standardized testing under publisher protocols where remote
administration is supported. When the questions in the referral require an
in-person evaluation, or when a deposition or trial date puts the expert in
the courtroom, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office. Counsel
receive the same report standard and the same point of contact either way.

## Reading the Cherokee County labor market

Vocational findings for evaluees in the Tahlequah area draw on Bureau of
Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates, pairing the
Oklahoma statewide tables with the area estimates that cover Cherokee County,
and on state workforce agency labor market information and direct employer
research where the case warrants it. In a micropolitan market, the
transferability of an evaluee's skills and the real availability of suitable
positions deserve closer attention than broad occupational averages provide,
and the analysis is built accordingly. Care-related costs in life care plans
and medical cost projections are priced to the evaluee's own region using
provider-specific inquiries and published fee data.

## Opening an engagement from Tahlequah

Tahlequah referrals enter the same intake process used across the firm's
[national practice](/locations/nationwide/). Send the caption or party names
through the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/), together with any imminent
disclosure, deposition, or trial dates. Once conflicts clear, the firm
returns a defined scope and a tailored records request, and the engagement
proceeds on a schedule aligned with the litigation calendar. Plaintiff and
defense referrals are accepted on identical terms, and any venue-specific
registration or qualification requirement is identified and confirmed during
the conflict check and intake process.
