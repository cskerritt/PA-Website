## How a small-town evaluee fits the wider labor market

Earning capacity for a Fort Gibson resident is rarely measured against
Fort Gibson alone. The relevant labor market is the one an evaluee can
actually reach, which for most residents means the commuting area anchored
by Muskogee, the principal city of the surrounding
[Muskogee metro area](/locations/oklahoma/muskogee/). Vocational findings
therefore rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and
wage estimates for that area and for Oklahoma statewide, supplemented by
state workforce agency labor market information and by direct labor market
research where the referral questions warrant it. The analysis stays
qualitative about place and rigorous about method: what work exists within
a reasonable commute, whether the evaluee's residual capacity matches it,
and what the record supports on both points.
