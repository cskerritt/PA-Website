The referrals that reach Purinton Analytics from Ardmore tend to turn on a
few recurring questions: what an injured worker can still earn, what a
catastrophically injured evaluee will need over a lifetime of care, and
whether an opposing expert's damages opinion survives scrutiny. The firm
answers those questions for plaintiff and defense counsel alike through
[vocational expert evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/),
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/), and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds within a single
engagement structure. Statewide coverage is described on the firm's
[Oklahoma page](/locations/oklahoma/).

## Courts serving Carter County

Matters are accepted from the District Court sitting in Carter County and
from Oklahoma's federal bench, including the United States District Court for
the Eastern District of Oklahoma, the United States District Court for the
Western District of Oklahoma, and the United States District Court for the
Northern District of Oklahoma. Because the same report may be read by a state
district judge, a federal magistrate, and opposing counsel in the same week,
every opinion is written to a single standard: documented methodology, cited
data sources, and conclusions that follow from the record rather than from
the identity of the retaining party.

## How the firm appears in Ardmore

Ardmore meetings are by appointment. The firm keeps no staffed office in the
city and publishes no local street address, a deliberate choice that reflects
the same accuracy it applies to its opinions. The working assumption is
remote-first: records analysis, structured interviews, and standardized
testing under publisher protocols where remote administration is supported,
which keeps costs proportionate for counsel in a micropolitan market. Where
the referral calls for an in-person evaluation, or where a deposition or
trial requires the expert in the room, travel is arranged from the Kansas
City home office and confirmed against the case schedule at intake.

## Grounding opinions in the local labor market

Employability and earning capacity findings for Ardmore-area evaluees are
grounded in Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage
estimates, using the Oklahoma statewide tables together with the area
estimates covering Carter County, alongside state workforce agency labor
market information and direct labor market research where warranted. The
analysis asks what work actually exists for this evaluee in this market, not
what a national average implies. Life care plans and medical cost projections
follow the same principle, pricing future care to the evaluee's own region
through provider-specific inquiries and published fee data rather than
generic national schedules.

## Requesting a conflict check

Every Ardmore engagement begins the way engagements begin across the firm's
[national practice](/locations/nationwide/): with a conflict check before any
substantive discussion of the case. To start,
[request a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) with the case caption and parties,
and flag any disclosure deadlines, hearing dates, or scheduled depositions.
After clearance, counsel receive a defined scope and a records request
tailored to the claims, and the matter proceeds under the same engagement
structure and report standard the firm applies to plaintiff and defense work
in every venue it serves.
