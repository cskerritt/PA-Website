## The courts behind a Bethany caption

Bethany lies within Oklahoma County, and state-court matters proceed in
the District Court for Oklahoma County. Oklahoma's federal bench sits in
the United States District Court for the Western District of Oklahoma,
the United States District Court for the Northern District of Oklahoma,
and the United States District Court for the Eastern District of
Oklahoma. The firm resolves which forum controls a given Bethany case
during the conflict check and intake, together with any

## Method over storefront

Purinton Analytics keeps no office in Bethany and publishes no local
street address, stating that plainly because a firm that shades small
facts invites doubt about large ones. The working process is

deposition-ready throughout, with test data, notes, and source materials
