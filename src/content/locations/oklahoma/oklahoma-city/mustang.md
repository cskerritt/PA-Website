In a community the size of Mustang, the lawyers on opposite sides of a
case often know each other, and an expert whose opinions bend toward
whoever paid the retainer is quickly found out. Purinton Analytics
accepts retention from either side of the caption in Mustang matters,
and the reports read identically either way: the same
[vocational assessment methodology](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
the same [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) standards,
and the same willingness to say when the data does not support the
larger number. That symmetry is not a slogan; it is what makes the
opinions useful in front of a jury.

## Where Mustang disputes are decided

Mustang sits in Canadian County, and its state-court civil matters are
heard in the District Court for Canadian County. Federal cases in
Oklahoma proceed in the United States District Court for the Northern
District of Oklahoma, the United States District Court for the Eastern
District of Oklahoma, or the United States District Court for the
Western District of Oklahoma, and the firm confirms the controlling
forum, along with any expert registration or qualification requirement
it imposes, during the conflict check and intake rather than inferring
it from an address. Engagements across the rest of the state are
described on the [Oklahoma page](/locations/oklahoma/).

## A small suburb, a metro-sized market

Mustang itself is a small suburb, and no honest employability opinion
pretends its town-limits job base defines what an evaluee can earn.
The relevant market is the regional one, and the firm measures it with
Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates
for the Oklahoma City metropolitan area and for Oklahoma statewide,
augmented by state workforce agency labor market information and direct
labor market research where the referral questions require it. The
report identifies the geography behind every finding, and transferable
skills analysis connects the evaluee's history to occupations that
exist in that market rather than in the abstract. The firm's
regional work is set out on the
[Oklahoma City metro page](/locations/oklahoma/oklahoma-city/).

## Appointments, not offices

The firm maintains no staffed location in Mustang and publishes no
street address for this page, because claiming an office it does not
keep would contradict the accuracy its reports are built on. Early-phase
work is remote: records analysis, structured interviews, and most
standardized testing under publisher protocols. In-person evaluation,
when warranted, happens in the evaluee's own community, and deposition
or trial appearances are scheduled as travel from the Kansas City home
office, the hub of the firm's
[nationwide practice](/locations/nationwide/).

## Engaging the firm from Mustang

Whichever party seeks the retention, the sequence is identical: a
documented conflict check on the caption or party names comes before any
discussion of the merits. Counsel should
[request a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) and note imminent disclosure
deadlines, depositions, or trial settings. After clearance, scope and
fee terms are confirmed in writing and a tailored records request
issues. Where economic loss analysis is also needed, the firm arranges
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.
