Norman is not a satellite that borrows its economy from somewhere else. It
is one of the largest cities in the Oklahoma City metropolitan area, with
an employment base substantial enough that a vocational opinion about a
Norman evaluee must be defended on Norman's own terms as well as the
metro's. Purinton Analytics serves plaintiff and defense counsel in
Cleveland County matters with
[vocational expert services](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[expert testimony](/services/expert-testimony-litigation-consulting/)
prepared to the same standard whichever side of the caption issues the
retention.

## Cleveland County courts and the federal forums

State-court matters arising in Norman proceed in the District Court for
Cleveland County. In federal practice, Oklahoma offers the United States
District Court for the Western District of Oklahoma, the United States
District Court for the Eastern District of Oklahoma, and the United States
District Court for the Northern District of Oklahoma. The firm does not
assume from a map which of these controls a given case; the proper forum,
and any expert registration or qualification requirement that forum
imposes, is confirmed during the conflict check and intake. Statewide
engagements are described on the [Oklahoma page](/locations/oklahoma/).

## Reading a large suburb's labor market honestly

Because Norman supports significant employment of its own while remaining
tied to the wider regional economy, earning capacity findings here rest on
Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for
the Oklahoma City metropolitan area and for Oklahoma statewide, together
with state workforce agency labor market information and direct labor
market research where the referral questions require it. Each finding in
the written report identifies the geography that supports it, so
cross-examination can test the data rather than guess at it. The firm's
metro-wide practice is described on the
[Oklahoma City metro page](/locations/oklahoma/oklahoma-city/).

## How a Norman evaluee is served

There is no Purinton Analytics office in Norman, and the firm publishes no
street address here, because it will not imply a presence it does not
keep. What counsel gets instead is a remote-first process that loses
nothing to distance: records analysis, structured interviews, and most
standardized testing proceed under publisher protocols from wherever the
evaluee is, and when the referral questions call for in-person evaluation,
it is conducted in the evaluee's own community rather than in a distant
conference room. Travel for evaluations, depositions, and trial testimony
is scheduled from the Kansas City home office, the same base that
supports the firm's [nationwide practice](/locations/nationwide/).

## Opening a Norman engagement

Every Norman matter begins with a conflict check on the caption or party
names, before any substantive discussion. Counsel should
[request a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) and identify the deadlines
already fixed in the scheduling order, particularly expert disclosure
dates and trial settings, so the work plan can be built backward from
them. Once cleared, the firm confirms scope and fee terms in writing and
issues a records request tailored to the claims. Where the damages model
also requires economic analysis, the firm arranges
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.
