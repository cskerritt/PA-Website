## Federal districts and the controlling forum

Oklahoma's federal trial courts are the United States District Court
for the Western District of Oklahoma, the United States District Court
for the Northern District of Oklahoma, and the United States District
Court for the Eastern District of Oklahoma. Each is an available forum
for Oklahoma litigation; none is assumed to control an El Reno case
from geography alone. The governing district, or the state forum in
the District Court for Canadian County, is identified during the
conflict check and intake, along with any registration or qualification
requirement that forum imposes on a testifying expert. Broader Oklahoma
coverage is described on the [state page](/locations/oklahoma/).
