When a Moore injury case is serious enough to require expert damages
evidence, that evidence usually arrives in pieces: an opinion about what
the evaluee can still earn, and an opinion about what future care will
cost. Retained separately, the experts behind those opinions can
contradict each other, and opposing counsel will find the seam.
Purinton Analytics prepares
[vocational expert opinions](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) and
[life care plans](/services/life-care-planning/) for Moore matters on a
shared factual record, so the functional restrictions that limit earning
capacity are the same restrictions that shape the care plan. Plaintiff
and defense counsel receive the same rigor, and
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) is available
when the assignment is testing an opposing expert's damages evidence.

## Venue for a Moore case

Moore is a Cleveland County community, and civil matters filed in state
court proceed in the District Court for Cleveland County. Oklahoma's
federal forums are the United States District Court for the Western
District of Oklahoma, the United States District Court for the Eastern
District of Oklahoma, and the United States District Court for the
Northern District of Oklahoma. Which forum controls a particular Moore
case, and what that forum requires of a testifying expert by way of
registration or qualification, is confirmed during the conflict check
and intake rather than presumed from the address on the complaint. The
firm's work across the rest of the state is described on the
[Oklahoma page](/locations/oklahoma/).

## Data behind the earning capacity finding

Earning capacity and employability findings for Moore evaluees rest on
Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates
for the Oklahoma City metropolitan area and for Oklahoma statewide,
supplemented by state workforce agency labor market information and
direct labor market research where the referral questions call for
occupation-level detail. The written report identifies the geography
that supports each finding, and transferable skills analysis connects
the evaluee's documented history and residual capacity to occupations
the data can actually place within reach. Because the care plan and the
vocational opinion draw on the same record, the file counsel produces
does not argue with itself. The firm's regional practice is described
on the [Oklahoma City metro page](/locations/oklahoma/oklahoma-city/).

## Where the evaluation happens

Purinton Analytics keeps no office in Moore and publishes no street
address for this location; candor about presence is part of the same
discipline that governs the opinions. Engagements run remote-first,
with records analysis, structured interviews, and most standardized
testing completed under publisher protocols without moving the evaluee.
When the referral questions warrant in-person evaluation, it is
conducted in the evaluee's own community, and appearances for
deposition and trial are scheduled as travel from the Kansas City home
office that anchors the firm's
[nationwide practice](/locations/nationwide/).

## Opening a Moore engagement

No merits discussion happens before the conflict check clears. Counsel
should [request a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) with the caption or
party names and identify the expert disclosure deadlines, deposition
windows, and trial setting already fixed, so the work plan can be built
to meet them. On clearance, the firm confirms scope and fee terms in
writing and issues a records request tailored to the claims. Where the
damages model also requires an economist's loss calculation, the firm
arranges
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.
