Sometimes the first document counsel sends from a Del City case is not a
medical file but an opposing expert's report. Purinton Analytics is
retained in that posture regularly:
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) engagements
that test whether an existing vocational or life care opinion follows
its own cited methodology, uses the labor market data it claims to use,
and reaches conclusions the record can carry. The firm performs that
review with the same rigor it applies to its own affirmative
[vocational expert](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) and
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) work, and it
accepts the assignment from plaintiff or defense counsel without
adjusting the standard to the client.

## Oklahoma County venue, federal options

Del City matters filed in state court proceed in the District Court for
Oklahoma County. When a case belongs in federal court, Oklahoma's
districts are the United States District Court for the Eastern District
of Oklahoma, the United States District Court for the Western District
of Oklahoma, and the United States District Court for the Northern
District of Oklahoma. The firm identifies the controlling district, and
any qualification or registration rule that district applies to
testifying experts, during the conflict check and intake; it does not
guess venue from the town's position on a map. The firm's statewide
engagement is described on the [Oklahoma page](/locations/oklahoma/).

## What the data must show

Whether reviewing another expert's opinion or writing its own, the firm
holds the analysis to the same evidentiary floor: Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Oklahoma
City metropolitan area and for Oklahoma statewide, state workforce
agency labor market information, and direct labor market research where
occupation-specific questions arise. A rebuttal that merely disagrees is
worthless; the value lies in showing precisely where an opinion departs
from its data, and the firm's reports are structured to make that
demonstration, section by section, with citations. Regional practice
details appear on the
[Oklahoma City metro page](/locations/oklahoma/oklahoma-city/).

## Serving Del City without pretending an office

Purinton Analytics has no office in Del City and publishes no street
address for this location. Evaluations for affirmative opinions run
remote-first, with records analysis, structured interviews, and most
standardized testing completed under publisher protocols; in-person
evaluation, when the referral questions call for it, takes place in the
evaluee's own community. Rebuttal work, by its nature, travels even
lighter. When depositions or trial require presence, travel is scheduled
from the Kansas City home office that anchors the firm's
[national engagement](/locations/nationwide/).

## Bringing the firm a Del City file

The conflict check comes before the file does: counsel should
[request a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) with the caption or party
names, noting whether the assignment is affirmative, rebuttal, or both,
and flagging any disclosure or trial dates already set. Once the check
clears, the firm confirms scope and fees in writing. If the damages
model also requires an economist, the firm arranges
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.
