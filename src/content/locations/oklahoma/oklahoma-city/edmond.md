An Edmond case is usually an Oklahoma County case, and that is where the
analysis should start. The District Court for Oklahoma County is the
state forum for most civil matters arising here, and it is a venue where
expert opinions are tested by counsel who know the difference between a
conclusion and a methodology. Purinton Analytics writes for that
audience. The firm provides
[vocational expert](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) and
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) opinions to Edmond
plaintiff and defense counsel alike, along with
[medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/) where the
question is narrower than a full plan.

## Venue first, then the work plan

Beyond the District Court for Oklahoma County, Oklahoma's federal forums
are the United States District Court for the Western District of
Oklahoma, the United States District Court for the Northern District of
Oklahoma, and the United States District Court for the Eastern District
of Oklahoma. Which of them governs a particular Edmond dispute is a
question of venue law and the parties, not of geography alone, so the
controlling court, and any qualification or registration requirement it
places on a testifying expert, is settled during the conflict check and
intake rather than presumed. Work across the rest of the state is
described on the [Oklahoma page](/locations/oklahoma/).

## Edmond wage evidence

Edmond residents earn their livings across the whole regional economy,
so the firm anchors its wage and employability findings in Bureau of
Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates covering the
Oklahoma City metropolitan area, supplemented by Oklahoma statewide
estimates, state workforce agency labor market information, and targeted
labor market research when a specific occupation or employer practice is
at issue. No figure enters the report without its source and geography
attached, and no occupation is proposed that the data cannot place
within the evaluee's reach. The broader regional engagement is set out
on the
[Oklahoma City metro page](/locations/oklahoma/oklahoma-city/).

## By appointment, and honestly so

The firm keeps no staffed location in Edmond and lists no local street
address, a policy of candor that extends to every representation in its
reports. Engagements proceed remotely through their early phases, from
records review through structured interview and most standardized
testing under publisher protocols. If the referral questions warrant
in-person evaluation, the evaluation comes to the evaluee's community.
Depositions and trial appearances are scheduled as travel from the
Kansas City home office, on the same footing as the firm's
[national practice](/locations/nationwide/).

## Retaining the firm for an Edmond matter

Retention begins with a documented conflict check, run on the caption or
party names before any merits discussion takes place. Counsel can
[submit a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) and should flag imminent
disclosure deadlines, deposition dates, or trial settings at the same
time. After clearance, scope and fees are confirmed in writing and a
tailored records request issues. When the case also needs an economic
loss calculation, the firm offers
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure, giving counsel a single point of coordination
without blurring authorship of the opinions.
