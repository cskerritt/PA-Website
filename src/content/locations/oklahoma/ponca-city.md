Distance is the practical problem a Ponca City referral has to solve, and
Purinton Analytics solves it with structure rather than with a storefront.
The firm delivers vocational expert and life care planning services to
plaintiff and defense counsel in the Ponca City micropolitan area through a
remote-first model, providing
[earning capacity and employability evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/),
[medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/), and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic

## Kay County courts and the federal bench

The firm accepts matters pending in the District Court sitting in Kay County
and in Oklahoma's federal trial courts, including the United States District
Court for the Northern District of Oklahoma, the United States District Court
for the Western District of Oklahoma, and the United States District Court
for the Eastern District of Oklahoma. Each engagement, in whichever forum, is
built on the same foundation: a documented conflict check, a defined scope,
and a records request tailored to the claims, so that the resulting opinion
can withstand cross-examination from either side of the caption.

## Remote work as the default, travel as the plan

Most of the work in a Ponca City engagement never requires anyone to be in
Ponca City. Records review, structured vocational interviews conducted by
video, and standardized testing under publisher protocols where remote
administration is supported all proceed from the firm's Kansas City home
office, which keeps fees proportionate to the case. Meetings in the city are
by appointment; the firm has no staffed office in Ponca City and publishes no
street address for this location. When an in-person evaluation is warranted
or when deposition and trial dates require presence, travel is scheduled from
Kansas City and confirmed with counsel against the litigation calendar.

## Wage and labor data for Kay County evaluees

Vocational conclusions for evaluees in the Ponca City area are anchored in
Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates,
combining the Oklahoma statewide tables with the area estimates that cover
Kay County, and are checked against state workforce agency labor market
information and direct employer contact where the referral questions call for
it. The point of the research is concrete: identifying the occupations
actually open to the evaluee in this labor market, at wages the data will
support under oath. Life care plans and medical cost projections apply the
same local discipline, pricing future care to the evaluee's own region
through provider-specific inquiries and published fee data.

## Beginning a Kay County engagement

Ponca City matters follow the same path as every case in the firm's
[national practice](/locations/nationwide/). Submit the caption or party
names through the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and note any imminent
disclosure, deposition, or trial dates. Once the conflict check clears,
counsel receive a defined scope, a tailored records request, and a single
point of contact from intake through testimony, with the same report standard
applied whether the firm is retained by the plaintiff or the defense.
