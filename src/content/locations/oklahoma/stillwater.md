A vocational or life care planning opinion prepared for a Stillwater case
is held to the same standard as one prepared for the largest metropolitan
docket in the country: a documented conflict check, a defined scope, a
records request tailored to the claims, and a written report that opposing
counsel can test line by line. Purinton Analytics brings that standard to
plaintiff and defense engagements in the Stillwater micropolitan area,
centered on Payne County, as part of a
[national practice](/locations/nationwide/) built to deliver consistent
work product wherever the venue sits.

Referrals typically call for

## Appointments, not an office

Stillwater meetings are by appointment. The firm keeps no staffed office
here and publishes no street address for this location, because accuracy
about presence belongs to the same discipline that governs its reports.
Most engagements begin remotely, with records review, structured
interviews, and standardized testing under publisher protocols where remote
administration is supported. When the referral questions call for in-person
evaluation, or when deposition and trial dates require attendance, travel
is scheduled from the Kansas City home office. The location of the work
never changes the report: one standard, one point of contact.

## Reading the Stillwater labor market honestly

Vocational findings for Payne County evaluees rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates and on Oklahoma
statewide data, supplemented by state workforce agency labor market
information and direct labor market research where warranted. In a smaller
regional market, the difference between a defensible opinion and a
vulnerable one is often whether the analysis reflects the occupations
actually present in the evaluee's community; the firm's transferable skills
work is grounded there, not in national averages. Life care plans and cost
projections are likewise priced to the evaluee's own market through
provider-specific inquiries and published fee data.

## How an engagement begins

The first step is always clearance, never a case discussion. Submit the
caption or party names through the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and flag any imminent disclosure,
deposition, or trial dates. Once conflicts are cleared, intake defines the
referral questions, the records needed, and the evaluation format, and the
matter proceeds under the same written protocols the firm applies to
engagements nationwide, whichever party has retained it.
