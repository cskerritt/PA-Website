At the center of a Tonkawa case is a person who, in most instances, still
lives in Tonkawa. An earning capacity or life care plan opinion is not an
abstraction about a market; it is a documented account of what a
particular individual can do, earn, and require after injury, and the
quality of that account depends on meeting the evaluee where the evaluee
actually is. Purinton Analytics structures its Tonkawa work accordingly,
bringing the evaluation to the individual rather than asking a small-town
resident to organize a life around expert appointments. Counsel on either
side of the caption engage the firm for
[vocational expert services](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) of the
opinions offered against their clients.

## Where a Tonkawa case is heard

A Tonkawa matter filed in state court proceeds in the District Court for
Kay County. In federal practice, the available Oklahoma forums are the
United States District Court for the Western District of Oklahoma, the
United States District Court for the Northern District of Oklahoma, and
the United States District Court for the Eastern District of Oklahoma;
the district that governs a given case, along with any expert
registration or qualification requirement that venue carries, is
identified during the conflict check and intake process. Reports are
prepared to the same standard for each forum. The firm's work throughout
the state is summarized on the [Oklahoma page](/locations/oklahoma/).

## A small town inside a regional labor market

Tonkawa is a small Kay County community, and its residents work in a
labor market that extends well beyond it, with Ponca City as the
principal city of the surrounding metro economy. When the firm assesses
transferable skills and residual earning capacity for a Tonkawa evaluee,
the analysis draws on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment
and wage estimates for the Ponca City area and for Oklahoma statewide,
on state workforce agency labor market information, and on direct labor
market research where warranted, always stated qualitatively and tied in
the report to the geography each source covers. How that regional
approach applies across neighboring communities is set out on the
[Ponca City metro page](/locations/oklahoma/ponca-city/).

## Presence by appointment, not by address

There is no staffed Purinton Analytics office in Tonkawa, and the firm
publishes no street address here, because claiming an office it does not
keep would sit poorly beside reports that trade on accuracy. What
counsel gets instead is a remote-first process: records analysis,
structured interviews, and most standardized testing are completed under
publisher protocols without travel, and when the referral questions call
for in-person evaluation, the appointment is arranged in the evaluee's
own community. Depositions and trial appearances are scheduled as travel
from the Kansas City home office, the same base that serves the firm's
[national practice](/locations/nationwide/).

## Starting with the conflict check

Before the merits are discussed, the firm runs a conflict check on the
caption or party names. Counsel can
[request a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) and should identify any
disclosure deadline, deposition setting, or trial date already on the
docket so the engagement can be scoped to meet it. After clearance, scope
and fee terms are confirmed in writing and a tailored records request
issues. Where the case also needs an economist, the firm arranges
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.
