## Kay County venue and the federal forums

State-court matters arising in Blackwell proceed in the District Court
for Kay County, and every report is written to withstand the scrutiny
that court applies to expert opinion. Where a Blackwell dispute belongs
in federal court, Oklahoma offers the United States District Court for
the Northern District of Oklahoma, the United States District Court for
the Eastern District of Oklahoma, and the United States District Court
for the Western District of Oklahoma as available forums. Which district
controls a particular case, and what registration or qualification
requirement it imposes on a testifying expert, is confirmed during the
conflict check and intake rather than assumed from a map. The firm's
statewide practice is described on the
[Oklahoma page](/locations/oklahoma/).

## Opening the file

Everything starts with a conflict check run on the caption or party
names before any discussion of the merits. Counsel should
[request a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) and flag the deadlines the
scheduling order has already fixed, including disclosure, deposition,
and trial dates. Once the check clears, the firm confirms scope and fee
terms in writing, issues a tailored records request, and, where the
damages model also requires an economist, arranges
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.
