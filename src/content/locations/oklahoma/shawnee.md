The questions that bring Shawnee counsel to a vocational expert are
concrete: whether an injured worker can return to any of the occupations
the local economy actually offers, what earning capacity remains after a
permanent restriction, what a lifetime of medically necessary care will
cost, and whether an opposing expert's answers to those questions hold up.
Purinton Analytics addresses each of them for plaintiff and defense counsel
in the Shawnee micropolitan area, applying the same documented methodology
described on the firm's [nationwide coverage page](/locations/nationwide/)
to matters arising in Pottawatomie County.

## Answering the referral questions

Employability and earning capacity questions are addressed through
[vocational expert evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/)
grounded in records review, structured interviews, standardized testing,
and transferable skills analysis. Future care questions are addressed
through [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) and
[medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/). Where a full
damages picture is needed, the firm offers
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. Opinions already served by the other side can be
tested through [rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/).

## Where Shawnee matters are heard

State court engagements are accepted in the District Court of Pottawatomie
County, the trial court serving the micropolitan statistical area. Federal
work is supported throughout the state, in the United States District Court
for the Western District of Oklahoma, the United States District Court for
the Northern District of Oklahoma, and the United States District Court for
the Eastern District of Oklahoma. The firm's full
[Oklahoma coverage](/locations/oklahoma/) spans venues well beyond the
metro.

## No storefront, and no pretense of one

Shawnee meetings are by appointment. The firm maintains no staffed office
in the area and publishes no street address for this location; plain
statements about presence are part of the same discipline that governs the
firm's reports. Engagements generally begin remotely, with records review,
interviews, and testing under publisher protocols where remote
administration is supported. When in-person evaluation is warranted, or
when deposition and trial calendars require attendance, travel is scheduled
from the Kansas City home office, and counsel keep a single point of
contact throughout.

## Local labor market, national method

Vocational findings for Pottawatomie County evaluees rest on Bureau of
Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates and Oklahoma
statewide data, supplemented by state workforce agency labor market
information and direct labor market research where the referral questions
warrant it. The analysis is deliberately local: employability conclusions
turn on the occupations present in the evaluee's own labor market, not on
national listings. Life care plans and cost projections are priced to that
same market through provider-specific inquiries and published fee data, and
every figure is sourced in the report so it can be traced and tested.

## The first step is a conflict check

Substantive case discussion waits until conflicts are cleared. To begin a
Shawnee engagement, [request a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) with the
case caption and party names, noting any imminent disclosure, deposition,
or trial dates. After clearance, intake defines scope, records, and
evaluation format, and the work proceeds under the same written protocols
the firm applies to every matter it accepts, for either side of the
caption.
