## Stephens County venue and the courts that serve Marlow

Marlow matters in state court proceed in the District Court, Oklahoma's trial
court of general jurisdiction, with Stephens County as the local venue for
claims arising in the community. When a claim belongs in federal court,
Oklahoma offers the United States District Court for the Western District of
Oklahoma, the United States District Court for the Eastern District of
Oklahoma, and the United States District Court for the Northern District of
Oklahoma as available forums; the controlling district and division for a
particular matter are confirmed during the conflict check and intake, along
with any venue-specific registration or qualification requirement. Reports
are prepared to the same standard regardless of forum, so an opinion drafted
for a Stephens County jury holds up if the case is removed or refiled.

## Marlow's place in the Duncan area labor market

Employability and earning capacity opinions for a Marlow evaluee are grounded
in the labor market the evaluee actually faces, not a generic statewide
abstraction. Marlow sits within the Duncan, OK metropolitan area, and the
analysis draws on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage
estimates for that area and for Oklahoma statewide, supplemented by state
workforce agency labor market information and direct labor market research
where the referral questions warrant it. In a smaller market like this one,
the distance between an occupation existing somewhere in Oklahoma and that
occupation being realistically available to a specific evaluee can decide a
case, so the written opinion addresses transferable skills, local occupational
demand, and any commuting pattern the record supports, and it is documented so
that opposing counsel can trace every conclusion to its source. Context on the
principal city's market is set out on the [Duncan page](/locations/oklahoma/duncan/).

## Evaluating a Marlow resident

Most engagements begin remotely: records review, counsel conferences, and
scheduling are handled by video and phone, which keeps costs proportionate for
a small-community case. When the referral questions call for in-person
vocational testing or a home assessment for a [life care plan](/services/life-care-planning/),
the evaluation is conducted in the evaluee's own community by appointment,
because assessing function where a person actually lives and works produces a
more defensible record than requiring travel to a distant office. The firm
keeps no office in Marlow and publishes no local address; travel for
evaluations, depositions, and trial testimony is scheduled from the Kansas
City home office. When damages require economic modeling, the firm offers
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis
while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement
structure.
