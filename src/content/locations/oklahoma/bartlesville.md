## The engagement menu

Bartlesville referrals typically involve
[vocational expert evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) of
employability and earning capacity in injury and employment matters,
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) and
[medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/) in
catastrophic injury cases, and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. When an opposing expert's report is already on file,
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) is available
under the same conflict-check discipline.

## A candid word about offices

Bartlesville meetings are by appointment. The firm keeps no staffed office
in the area and publishes no street address for this location, because a
practice that asks jurors to trust its numbers cannot be casual about its
own facts. What counsel get instead of a storefront is a remote-first
engagement model, records review, structured interviews, and standardized
testing under publisher protocols where remote administration is supported,
with in-person evaluation and courtroom attendance scheduled as travel from
the Kansas City home office when the case requires it, and one point of
contact throughout.

## Labor market research done locally

Vocational findings for Washington County evaluees rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates and Oklahoma
statewide data, supplemented by state workforce agency labor market
information and direct labor market research where warranted. Employability
and earning capacity conclusions are tied to the occupations actually
present in the evaluee's own regional economy, because in a smaller market
that is where an opinion is won or lost under cross-examination. Life care
plans and medical cost projections are priced to the same local market
through provider-specific inquiries and published fee data, with each entry
sourced in the report.

## From conflict check to opinion

No substantive case discussion occurs before clearance. To open a
Bartlesville engagement, submit the caption or party names through the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and note any imminent disclosure,
deposition, or trial dates. Once conflicts clear, intake defines the
referral questions, the records needed, and the evaluation format, and the
matter proceeds under the same standard the firm applies to every
engagement it accepts, whichever side of the caption retained it.
