The Weatherford micropolitan area spans Custer County and Washita County, and
that paired-county footprint is where Purinton Analytics focuses this page of
its practice. The firm provides vocational expert and life care planning
services to plaintiff and defense counsel across the area, including
[earning capacity and employability evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) in catastrophic injury
matters, and [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds within a single
engagement structure. Counsel with matters elsewhere in the state will find
the firm's full coverage on its [Oklahoma page](/locations/oklahoma/).

## Courts across Custer and Washita counties

Referrals are accepted for matters in the District Court sitting in Custer
County, in the District Court sitting in Washita County, and in Oklahoma's
federal trial courts, including the United States District Court for the
Western District of Oklahoma, the United States District Court for the
Northern District of Oklahoma, and the United States District Court for the
Eastern District of Oklahoma. Whichever courthouse the caption names, the
engagement follows the same sequence: a documented conflict check, a defined
scope, a records request tailored to the claims, and a written opinion
prepared for examination by either side.

## Presence in Weatherford, stated plainly

Weatherford meetings are by appointment only. The firm keeps no staffed
office in either county, publishes no local street address, and does not
suggest otherwise, because honesty about logistics belongs to the same
discipline that governs its opinions. Engagements from the area generally
begin remotely, with records review, structured vocational interviews, and
standardized testing under publisher protocols where remote administration is
supported. When an evaluation needs to happen in person, or when deposition
and trial dates require the expert's presence, travel is scheduled from the
Kansas City home office and coordinated with counsel well ahead of the
relevant deadlines.

## A labor market spanning neighboring counties

Vocational findings for evaluees in the Weatherford area draw on Bureau of
Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates, combining the
Oklahoma statewide tables with the area estimates that cover Custer County
and Washita County, and are supplemented by state workforce agency labor
market information and direct employer research where the referral warrants
it. Because employment conditions can differ even between neighboring
counties, the analysis is grounded in the evaluee's actual community and
commuting range rather than in an averaged regional picture. Life care plans
and medical cost projections apply the same local rigor, pricing future care
to the evaluee's own region through provider-specific inquiries and published
fee data.

## Starting an engagement in the Weatherford area

Matters from Custer County and Washita County enter the same intake used
throughout the firm's [national practice](/locations/nationwide/). Submit the
caption or party names through the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/),
noting any imminent disclosure, deposition, or trial dates. Once conflicts
clear, counsel receive a defined scope, a tailored records request, and a
single point of contact through testimony, with plaintiff and defense
referrals handled on identical terms and to the same report standard.
