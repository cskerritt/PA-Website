## The county courthouse and the federal alternatives

State-court matters arising in Tecumseh proceed in the District Court
for Pottawatomie County. Where a case belongs in federal court instead,
Oklahoma offers the United States District Court for the Northern
District of Oklahoma, the United States District Court for the Eastern
District of Oklahoma, and the United States District Court for the
Western District of Oklahoma as available forums. Which of those
districts controls a given Tecumseh matter, and what registration or
qualification requirements it imposes on a testifying expert, is
confirmed during the conflict check and intake rather than assumed. The
firm's statewide practice is summarized on the
[Oklahoma page](/locations/oklahoma/).

## Reading the labor market around Tecumseh

Tecumseh sits within the Shawnee metropolitan area, and an earning
capacity analysis for a Tecumseh evaluee has to reflect the employment
that area actually offers rather than a generic statewide abstraction.
Vocational findings rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational
employment and wage estimates covering the relevant Oklahoma area and
the state as a whole, supplemented by state workforce agency labor
market information and by direct labor market research when the
referral questions warrant it. Each report identifies the geography
behind every figure it relies on, so opposing counsel can trace a
conclusion back to its source. The practice across the wider metro is
described on the [Shawnee metro page](/locations/oklahoma/shawnee/).
