What a McLoud worker can earn is rarely decided inside McLoud's own city
limits. The town belongs to the Shawnee metropolitan labor market, and
the jobs, wages, and employers that define an injured worker's residual
earning capacity are spread across that commuting area rather than
concentrated in any single town. Purinton Analytics builds its opinions
around that reality, offering plaintiff and defense counsel in McLoud
matters [vocational expert services](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) grounded in
the market an evaluee actually faces.

## A commuting-area analysis, documented

Because McLoud residents participate in an employment market centered
on Shawnee, the principal city of the metro, an earning capacity
opinion limited to the town itself would understate or misstate the
alternatives open to an evaluee. The firm therefore draws on Bureau of
Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the
relevant Oklahoma area and for the state as a whole, together with
state workforce agency labor market information and direct labor market
research where the referral questions call for it. The report ties each
finding to the geography that supports it, a habit that holds up under
cross-examination. Work throughout the surrounding metro is described
on the [Shawnee metro page](/locations/oklahoma/shawnee/).

## Where a McLoud dispute is heard

McLoud lies in Pottawatomie County, and state-court matters proceed in
the District Court for Pottawatomie County. On the federal side,
Oklahoma's available forums are the United States District Court for
the Western District of Oklahoma, the United States District Court for
the Eastern District of Oklahoma, and the United States District Court
for the Northern District of Oklahoma. The district that governs a
particular McLoud case, along with any expert registration or
qualification requirement that forum imposes, is identified during the
conflict check and intake process, never assumed from the map. Broader
Oklahoma engagements are covered on the
[state page](/locations/oklahoma/).

Purinton Analytics maintains no storefront in McLoud and lists no local

standard of accuracy that the reports must meet. In practice the
absence of an office costs counsel nothing: records analysis,
structured interviews, and most standardized testing are completed
remotely under publisher protocols, and when the referral questions
