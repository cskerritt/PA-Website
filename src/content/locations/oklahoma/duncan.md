## Stephens County venues, state and federal

Referrals are accepted for matters in the District Court sitting in Stephens
County and for cases filed in the United States District Court for the
Western District of Oklahoma, the United States District Court for the
Eastern District of Oklahoma, and the United States District Court for the
Northern District of Oklahoma. The same disciplines apply in each forum:
methodology stated in the report, data sources cited so they can be checked,
and conclusions that would read the same way if the opposing party had
retained the firm instead.

## Earning capacity in the Duncan labor market

An earning capacity opinion for a Duncan-area evaluee has to reflect the
market the evaluee actually lives in. The firm grounds its vocational
findings in Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage
estimates, working from the Oklahoma statewide tables and the area estimates
covering Stephens County, and adds state workforce agency labor market
information and direct labor market research where warranted. The micropolitan
scale of the market shapes the analysis: which occupations are genuinely
represented locally, how far the evaluee can reasonably commute, and what
placement realistically looks like. Life care plans and medical cost
projections are priced to the evaluee's own region using provider-specific
inquiries and published fee data.
