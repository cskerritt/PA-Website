For counsel in the Lawton metropolitan area, the practical question is
rarely whether qualified vocational and life care planning expertise
exists, but how to bring it to a case without the delay and expense of
distance. Purinton Analytics answers that with a remote-first model:
records review, structured interviews, and standardized testing conducted
under publisher protocols where remote administration is supported, with
in-person evaluation and testimony scheduled from the Kansas City home
office when the referral questions or the court calendar require presence.
Plaintiff and defense counsel in Comanche County and Cotton County receive
the same report standard the firm delivers across its
[national practice](/locations/nationwide/).

## Services for Lawton area matters

Engagements from the metro typically involve
[earning capacity and employability evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/)
in injury and employment litigation,
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) and
[medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/) in
catastrophic injury matters, and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. Each assignment starts from a defined scope and a
records request tailored to the claims, so the written opinion rests on a
record both sides can examine.

## Honesty about presence

Lawton meetings are by appointment. The firm maintains no staffed office in
the metro and publishes no street address for this location; candor about
where the firm does and does not sit is part of the same discipline that
governs its reports. What counsel get instead is a single point of contact,
a documented methodology, and travel from the Kansas City home office
scheduled around depositions, evaluations, and trial dates rather than
around office hours.

## Labor market evidence for Lawton evaluees

Vocational findings rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational
employment and wage estimates for the Lawton metropolitan area and for
Oklahoma statewide, state workforce agency labor market information, and
direct labor market research where the referral questions warrant it.
Because smaller regional markets can differ sharply from statewide
patterns, the analysis is anchored to the occupations and employers
actually available to the evaluee rather than to national abstractions.
Life care plans and cost projections are priced to the evaluee's own market
using provider-specific inquiries and published fee data for the region.

## Beginning an engagement

Availability is confirmed through a conflict check before any substantive
case discussion. To begin,
[request a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) with the caption and party
names, and note any imminent disclosure, deposition, or trial dates so
scheduling can be assessed alongside clearance. From intake forward, the
engagement follows the same written protocols the firm applies nationwide,
for either side of the caption.
