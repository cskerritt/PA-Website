Counsel handling injury and employment litigation in Durant often have to look
well beyond the immediate area for vocational and life care planning
expertise, and Purinton Analytics is built for exactly that referral. The
firm serves plaintiff and defense counsel in the Durant micropolitan area
with [earning capacity and employability evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) in catastrophic injury
matters, and [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds within a single
engagement structure. The practice covers the rest of the state through its
[Oklahoma page](/locations/oklahoma/) and accepts matters nationally.

## Venue footprint in Bryan County

Engagements are accepted for matters pending in the District Court sitting in
Bryan County and in Oklahoma's federal trial courts, including the United
States District Court for the Eastern District of Oklahoma, the United States
District Court for the Northern District of Oklahoma, and the United States
District Court for the Western District of Oklahoma. The work product does
not change with the forum: every opinion rests on a documented conflict
check, a defined scope, and a records request tailored to the claims, so that
the written report stands up to examination from either side of the caption.

## Meetings by appointment, not a storefront

The firm maintains no staffed office in Durant and publishes no street
address for this location, because accuracy about presence is part of the
same discipline that governs its reports. Durant meetings are scheduled by
appointment. Most engagements from the area begin remotely, with records
review, structured vocational interviews, and standardized testing
administered under publisher protocols where remote administration is
supported. When an in-person evaluation is warranted, or when deposition and
trial dates require presence in the courtroom, travel is scheduled from the
Kansas City home office and planned around the litigation calendar rather
than around office hours.

## Labor market research for a smaller market

Vocational opinions for evaluees in the Durant area rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates, drawing on the
statewide Oklahoma tables and the smaller-area estimates that cover Bryan
County, supplemented by state workforce agency labor market information and
direct employer contact where the referral questions warrant it. A
micropolitan labor market is not simply a scaled-down version of a large
metro, and the analysis reflects that: transferable skills, commuting
patterns, and the actual availability of suitable work are examined against
the evaluee's own market rather than against national assumptions. Life care
plans and medical cost projections are priced to the evaluee's region using
provider-specific inquiries and published fee data.

## Starting a Durant engagement

The engagement process is the same whether the matter is venued in Bryan
County or anywhere else in the firm's [national practice](/locations/nationwide/).
Submit the caption or party names through the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and note any imminent disclosure,
deposition, or trial dates so scheduling can be confirmed at intake. Once the
conflict check clears, counsel receive a defined scope, a records request
tailored to the claims, and a single point of contact from retention through
testimony. Both plaintiff and defense referrals are welcome, and the same
report standard applies regardless of which side retains the firm.
