## Comanche County venue and the federal forums

Elgin lies in Comanche County, and state-law claims arising there are heard in the District Court. Cases that belong in federal court may proceed in the United States District Court for the Northern District of Oklahoma, the United States District Court for the Eastern District of Oklahoma, or the United States District Court for the Western District of Oklahoma; which of those districts controls a given Elgin matter is confirmed during the conflict check and intake, together with any registration or qualification requirement the chosen forum imposes. Because the forum shapes disclosure obligations and report format, the firm treats venue confirmation as part of scoping rather than an afterthought, and every written opinion is prepared to be examined by the court and by opposing counsel in equal measure.
