For the person whose employability is in dispute, the expert process should not begin with a long trip to an unfamiliar office. Purinton Analytics serves Cache matters on a by-appointment basis designed around the evaluee: records are gathered and reviewed remotely, interviews and testing are arranged in or near the evaluee's own community, and the resulting [vocational expert](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) and [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) opinions are written to withstand cross-examination regardless of which side issued the retention letter. Plaintiff referrals and defense referrals receive an identical process, because the methodology does not know who is paying for it.

## Venue context for a Cache caption

Cache is part of Comanche County, where state-court claims proceed in the District Court. Federal filings from the area may be brought in the United States District Court for the Northern District of Oklahoma, the United States District Court for the Eastern District of Oklahoma, or the United States District Court for the Western District of Oklahoma, and the district that actually controls a particular Cache case is identified during the conflict check and intake rather than assumed. Any venue-specific registration or qualification requirement is confirmed at the same stage. Whatever the forum turns out to be, the report is built so a judge can follow each conclusion back to the underlying records, test results, and published sources without needing the author in the room.

## A small community inside a regional employment market

Cache is a small community, and its residents compete for work in the regional market that surrounds Lawton rather than in a market defined by the town limits. Vocational and earning-capacity findings therefore draw on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the relevant Oklahoma area, supplemented by state workforce agency labor market information and, where warranted, direct labor market research. Scale and opportunity are described qualitatively, and nothing in the opinion is allowed to rest on an unstated assumption about what work exists nearby. Counsel can find the regional picture on the [Lawton metro page](/locations/oklahoma/lawton/) and the statewide practice summary on the [Oklahoma page](/locations/oklahoma/).

## What the evaluee and counsel can expect

There is no Purinton Analytics office in Cache, and the firm does not publish a local address, because it will not imply a storefront that does not exist. What counsel gets instead is a defined sequence: a records request tailored to the claims, remote conferences at whatever pace the case requires, an in-person evaluation in the evaluee's community when the referral questions call for one, and appearance at deposition or trial with travel scheduled from the Kansas City home office. That sequence is documented as it happens, so the file shows how each opinion was formed. When the damages theory also needs economic quantification, [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) let independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement structure. The identical remote-first model supports engagements [nationwide](/locations/nationwide/), which is why a Cache evaluee is assessed with the same rigor as one in any large city.
