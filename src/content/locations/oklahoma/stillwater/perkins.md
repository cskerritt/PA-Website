## Where a Perkins case is heard

Perkins lies in Payne County, and civil matters arising in town proceed
in the District Court at the state level. Oklahoma's federal bench is
divided among the United States District Court for the Western District
of Oklahoma, the United States District Court for the Northern District
of Oklahoma, and the United States District Court for the Eastern
District of Oklahoma, each an available forum depending on how the case
is postured. The firm does not infer the controlling district from a map:
venue, and any registration or qualification requirement the forum
imposes on a testifying expert, is identified during the conflict check
and intake. Engagements across the state are described on the
[Oklahoma page](/locations/oklahoma/).

## A working community within the Stillwater market

Perkins functions as a small residential community within the broader
regional economy anchored by Stillwater, the metro's principal city, and
its residents' employment prospects are shaped by that wider market. The
firm's vocational analyses therefore rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates covering the Stillwater
metropolitan area and Oklahoma statewide, along with state workforce
agency labor market information and, where warranted, direct employer
contact and labor market research. Qualitative honesty matters here:
the report describes the character and reach of the market a Perkins
evaluee faces and cites the estimates behind each finding. The full
metro practice is set out on the
[Stillwater metro page](/locations/oklahoma/stillwater/).

## How an evaluation actually happens

There is no Purinton Analytics office in Perkins, and none is implied;
the firm publishes no local address because it maintains none. Service
is structured around that fact rather than in spite of it. Interviews,
records review, and most standardized testing are completed remotely
under publisher protocols, which keeps early-phase costs proportionate
to the case. When the referral questions require an in-person
evaluation, the evaluee is seen in his or her own community, and
appearances for deposition or trial are scheduled as travel from the
Kansas City home office. The same model supports the firm's
[national practice](/locations/nationwide/), so a Perkins file receives
the identical process a metropolitan file would.

## Retaining the firm for a Perkins matter

Before any discussion of the merits, the firm runs a documented conflict
check on the caption or party names. To start,
[submit a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) and note any imminent
disclosure, deposition, or trial dates so scheduling can be confirmed
alongside clearance. After the check clears, scope and fee terms are set
in writing and a tailored records request follows. If the case also
needs economic loss testimony, the firm arranges
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.
