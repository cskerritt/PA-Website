## Reading a small labor market honestly

Earning-capacity opinions for a New Cordell evaluee turn on what employment genuinely exists within reach of a small rural community, not on statewide averages applied uncritically. The analysis draws on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the applicable Oklahoma areas and for Oklahoma statewide, on state workforce agency labor market information, and on direct labor market research when the case warrants contacting employers about actual openings and requirements. Both sides of the caption benefit from that restraint: a plaintiff's residual capacity is not inflated by jobs that exist only in distant cities, and a defendant is not charged with losses the local market does not support.
