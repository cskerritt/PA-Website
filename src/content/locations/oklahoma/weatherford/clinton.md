## Clinton's place in the Weatherford area labor market

Clinton is a small community, and honest earning-capacity analysis here has to reflect that. A transferable-skills finding that assumes a metropolitan job base would overstate what is realistically available to a Clinton evaluee, while an analysis that ignores work within commuting reach of the town would understate it. Vocational findings therefore rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the relevant Oklahoma areas and for the state as a whole, supplemented by state workforce agency labor market information and direct labor market research where the referral questions warrant it. Every wage and employment conclusion is sourced so opposing counsel can trace it.
