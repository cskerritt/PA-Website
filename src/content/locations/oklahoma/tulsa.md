Litigation in the Tulsa metropolitan area moves through the District Court
of Tulsa County and the district courts of the surrounding counties, and
Purinton Analytics accepts vocational and life care planning engagements
from either side of the caption in all of them. The counties of the
metropolitan statistical area include Creek County, Okmulgee County, Osage
County, Pawnee County, Rogers County, and Wagoner County alongside Tulsa
County itself, and federal matters are supported in the United States
District Court for the Northern District of Oklahoma as well as the United
States District Court for the Eastern District of Oklahoma and the United
States District Court for the Western District of Oklahoma. Details of the
firm's statewide work appear on the [Oklahoma page](/locations/oklahoma/).

## What Tulsa counsel retain the firm to do

Referrals from the metro typically involve
[vocational expert opinions](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) on
employability and earning capacity in injury and employment matters,
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) and
[medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/) in
catastrophic injury cases, and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. [Rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/)
assignments, testing the methodology of an opposing expert's report, are
accepted under the same conflict-check discipline as affirmative work.

## By-appointment presence, stated plainly

The firm keeps no staffed office in Tulsa and publishes no street address
for this location. Meetings are by appointment, and most engagements begin
remotely: records review, structured interviews, and standardized testing
under publisher protocols where remote administration is supported. When
the referral questions require in-person evaluation, or when depositions
and trial dates require attendance, travel is scheduled from the Kansas
City home office and planned around the court calendar rather than around
office hours. Stating this arrangement plainly is deliberate; the same
insistence on accuracy that keeps a street address off this page governs
every figure and citation in the firm's written opinions, and counsel keep
a single point of contact from intake through testimony.

## Grounding findings in the Tulsa labor market

Vocational conclusions for evaluees in the metro rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Tulsa
metropolitan area and for Oklahoma statewide, supplemented by state
workforce agency labor market information and direct labor market research
where warranted. Transferable skills analysis considers the occupations
actually present in the regional economy rather than abstract national
listings. On the care side, life care plans and cost projections are priced
to the evaluee's own market through provider-specific inquiries and
published fee data, and each entry in the plan is sourced so that the
opinion withstands cross-examination.

## Opening a Tulsa engagement

Every engagement, local or national, begins the same way. Counsel
[request a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) with the case caption and party
names, flagging any imminent disclosure, deposition, or trial deadlines.
After clearance, intake defines the referral questions, the records
required, and whether evaluation will proceed remotely or in person. The
resulting report follows the single standard the firm applies across its
[national practice](/locations/nationwide/), written to be read closely by
opposing counsel and defended at deposition, whichever party retained the
work.
