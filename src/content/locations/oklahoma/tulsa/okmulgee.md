Some Okmulgee referrals arrive as vocational questions, but the hardest
ones arrive as care questions: after a catastrophic injury, what will
this person need for the rest of a life, and does the record support
each item of that care? Purinton Analytics builds
[life care plans](/services/life-care-planning/) and
[medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/) for
Okmulgee evaluees on exactly that record, and pairs them with
[vocational expert opinions](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) when
earning capacity is also in dispute, serving plaintiff and defense
counsel alike.

## A care plan built from the record

A life care plan is a clinical document before it is a litigation
document. Needs are identified from the medical records and the treating
and consulting recommendations within them, stated with their sources,
and carried forward only where the foundation holds. A medical cost
projection applies the same discipline to a narrower question.
Recommendations without support are left out, and disagreements with an
opposing planner's items are explained rather than merely asserted. The
goal is a plan either side of an Okmulgee caption can audit line by
line.

## Wage evidence held to the care-plan standard

When earning capacity is disputed alongside care, the vocational side
of an Okmulgee engagement is sourced the way the care plan is: no
recommendation without a citation, and no wage finding without its
data. Those findings rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational
employment and wage estimates for the Tulsa metropolitan area, the
regional market that includes Okmulgee, and for Oklahoma statewide,
alongside state workforce agency labor market information and direct
labor market research where a referral question calls for verification
of a particular occupation. Each finding names its dataset and its
geography inside the report itself, so nothing about where an Okmulgee
evaluee can work rides on an unstated assumption. The
[Tulsa metro page](/locations/oklahoma/tulsa/) describes the regional
practice these matters draw on.

## Reaching Okmulgee by appointment

The firm keeps no staffed office in Okmulgee and publishes no street
address for this location. Care planning in particular rewards
evaluee-centered logistics: when the referral questions require seeing
the evaluee's daily circumstances firsthand, the assessment is scheduled
in the evaluee's own community, while records analysis, structured
interviews, and most standardized testing proceed remotely under
publisher protocols. Travel for evaluations, depositions, and trial
testimony is scheduled from the Kansas City home office that supports
the firm's [national practice](/locations/nationwide/).

## From conflict check to signed plan

Nothing substantive is discussed until a documented conflict check on
the caption or party names has cleared. Counsel should
[submit a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) with the case's controlling
dates, including disclosure deadlines and trial settings, so feasibility
is confirmed alongside clearance. After clearance come written scope and
fee terms and a records request tailored to the claims. Where opposing
opinions are already served, the firm accepts
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) referrals,
and where the damages model also requires economic analysis it arranges
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.
