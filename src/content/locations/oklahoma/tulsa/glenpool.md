Retaining counsel's side of the caption is the least interesting fact
about a Glenpool engagement. Purinton Analytics accepts referrals from
plaintiff and defense firms alike, and the work product does not change
shape with the client: the same methodology, the same data sources, the
same willingness to say what the record cannot support. Glenpool
referrals include
[vocational expert opinions](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) of opposing
experts' work.

## Why the opinion does not bend toward the client

The protections are structural rather than rhetorical. Every engagement
opens with a documented conflict check, proceeds on a defined written
scope, and rests on a records request tailored to the claims, so the
opinion is anchored to a reviewable record before advocacy pressure can
reach it. Where the evidence stops, the analysis stops with it,
whichever party would have preferred another page. Symmetry is the
simplest test of that discipline: had the other side of the same
Glenpool caption retained the firm on the same facts, the working file,
the instruments chosen, and the reasoning behind each finding would
look the same.

## The evidence behind a Glenpool wage opinion

Earning capacity findings for Glenpool evaluees rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Tulsa
metropolitan area and for Oklahoma statewide, together with state
workforce agency labor market information and direct labor market
research where a referral question requires verification of a specific
occupation or employer type. Each finding is tied in the report to the
geography and data source that support it, so neither side has to guess
at the foundation. The [Tulsa metro page](/locations/oklahoma/tulsa/)
sets out the regional analysis in full.

## What counsel gets instead of a local office

No staffed Purinton Analytics office exists in Glenpool, and no street
address is published for this location; the firm says so plainly because
candor about presence belongs in the same ledger as candor in a report.
What counsel gets instead is a process that travels: records analysis,
structured interviews, and most standardized testing conducted remotely
under publisher protocols, in-person evaluation in the evaluee's own
community when the referral questions warrant it, and travel for
depositions and trial testimony scheduled from the Kansas City home
office behind the firm's [national practice](/locations/nationwide/).

## The same entry point for either side

Plaintiff or defense, the door in is identical: a documented conflict
check on the caption or party names before any discussion of the merits.
Counsel should [request a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) and include
imminent disclosure, deposition, or trial dates so scheduling
feasibility comes back with clearance. Once the check clears, scope and
fee terms are confirmed in writing and a tailored records request
issues. Where economic testimony is also required, the firm arranges
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.
