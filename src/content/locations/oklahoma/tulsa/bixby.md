The person at the center of a Bixby case is not a file; it is an injured
worker, a catastrophically hurt child, or a spouse whose earning capacity
is suddenly in dispute, and the evaluation should come to that person
rather than the other way around. Purinton Analytics structures its Bixby
engagements around the evaluee: remote where remote is sound, in the
evaluee's own community when the questions demand presence. Plaintiff and
defense counsel retain the firm for
[vocational evaluation and testimony](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/) in matters
arising in and around Bixby.

## Evaluation logistics for a Bixby evaluee

Most of an engagement travels well. Records analysis, structured
interviews, and the majority of standardized testing are conducted
remotely under publisher protocols, which keeps early-phase costs
proportionate and timelines short. Families coordinating care around
medical appointments can schedule sessions in segments rather than losing
a full day to travel. Where the referral questions call for
face-to-face assessment, or where a care plan requires observation of the
evaluee's actual environment, the evaluation is scheduled in the
community where the evaluee lives. Travel for those visits, and for
depositions and trial appearances, runs from the Kansas City home office,
the hub of the firm's [nationwide practice](/locations/nationwide/). The
firm maintains no staffed office in Bixby and publishes no local street
address, because accuracy about presence belongs in the same category as
accuracy in a report.

## Where a Bixby case is heard

Bixby lies in Tulsa County, so state-court matters proceed in the District
Court for Tulsa County. In federal court, Oklahoma matters may proceed in
the United States District Court for the Eastern District of Oklahoma, the
United States District Court for the Northern District of Oklahoma, or the
United States District Court for the Western District of Oklahoma. All are
available forums; which one controls a given Bixby caption, and what that
forum requires of a testifying expert, is identified and confirmed during
the conflict check and intake process. Statewide coverage is described on
the [Oklahoma page](/locations/oklahoma/).

## Labor market evidence with a regional reach

A Bixby evaluee competes for work across the Tulsa metropolitan area, and
the data should match. Vocational findings rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Tulsa
metropolitan area and for Oklahoma statewide, together with state
workforce agency labor market information and targeted labor market
research when a specific occupation or employer question needs direct
verification. The [Tulsa metro page](/locations/oklahoma/tulsa/) explains
the metro-level approach that Bixby matters draw on.

## Starting with a conflict check

Before any discussion of facts or theories, the firm runs a documented
conflict check on the caption or party names. Counsel can
[request that check](/refer-a-case/) online and should note upcoming
disclosure, deposition, or trial dates in the same submission. Once
cleared, the engagement proceeds on a written scope and a records request
tailored to the claims. When the damages picture also requires economic
analysis, the firm offers
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.
