## Tulsa County venue and the federal forums

State-court matters arising in Broken Arrow proceed in the District Court
for Tulsa County. Federal practice in Oklahoma is divided among the United
States District Court for the Northern District of Oklahoma, the United
States District Court for the Eastern District of Oklahoma, and the United
States District Court for the Western District of Oklahoma. Which district
controls a particular Broken Arrow case, and any registration or
qualification requirement it imposes on a testifying expert, is identified
and confirmed during the conflict check and intake process, never assumed
from geography. The firm's statewide work is described on the
[Oklahoma page](/locations/oklahoma/).

## A large suburb inside a larger market

Broken Arrow residents earn their livings throughout the Tulsa
metropolitan area, so an earning capacity opinion limited to city limits
would understate the market an evaluee actually faces. Vocational findings
for Broken Arrow evaluees therefore rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates for the Tulsa metropolitan area
and for Oklahoma statewide, supplemented by state workforce agency labor
market information and by direct labor market research where the referral
questions warrant it. Each report states which geography supports each
finding, and when restrictions narrow the search to lighter or sedentary
work, transferable skills analysis identifies the occupations that remain
realistically open. The metro-wide practice is described on the
[Tulsa metro page](/locations/oklahoma/tulsa/).

## Opening a Broken Arrow matter

Every engagement begins with a documented conflict check on the caption or
party names, before any discussion of the merits. Counsel should
[request a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) and flag imminent disclosure,
deposition, or trial dates so scheduling can be confirmed alongside
clearance. Once the check clears, scope and fee terms are confirmed in
writing and a records request tailored to the claims follows. Where the
damages model also requires an economist, the firm arranges
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
