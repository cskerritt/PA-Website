Expert opinions are governed by calendars as much as by facts. A Jenks
case arrives with a scheduling order already in place, or soon to be, and
the disclosure deadline, the deposition window, and the trial setting
shape what a retained expert can responsibly promise. Purinton Analytics
plans every Jenks engagement backward from those dates, so that the
[vocational expert](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) report, the
[life care plan](/services/life-care-planning/), or the
[rebuttal review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) is complete, supported,
and served on time for either side of the caption.

## Working to the disclosure calendar

Intake captures the operative dates first. From there the firm sequences
the work: records request tailored to the claims, remote interviews and
standardized testing under publisher protocols, analysis, and a written
report finished with enough margin for counsel to review it before
disclosure. Supplementation and deposition preparation are scheduled
against the same order, and
[testimony and litigation consulting](/services/expert-testimony-litigation-consulting/)
support continues through trial. Late referrals are evaluated honestly:
if the remaining runway cannot support a defensible opinion, the firm
says so at intake rather than after retention.

## Jenks venues, state and federal

Jenks is a Tulsa County community, and state-court matters arising there
proceed in the District Court for Tulsa County. The state's federal
forums are the United States District Court for the Western District of
Oklahoma, the United States District Court for the Northern District of
Oklahoma, and the United States District Court for the Eastern District
of Oklahoma. Which of them governs a particular Jenks matter, and any
venue-specific registration or qualification requirement for a testifying
expert, is identified and confirmed during the conflict check and intake
process. The firm's full [Oklahoma practice](/locations/oklahoma/) is
described at the state level.

## Reading the Tulsa labor market from Jenks

Earning capacity findings for a Jenks evaluee are grounded in Bureau of
Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Tulsa
metropolitan area and for Oklahoma statewide, alongside state workforce
agency labor market information and direct labor market research where a
referral question turns on a specific occupation. The report ties each
finding to its data source and geography, so cross-examination meets
documentation rather than assertion. The
[Tulsa metro page](/locations/oklahoma/tulsa/) describes that regional
foundation in more depth.

## By-appointment service, honestly framed

The firm has no staffed office in Jenks and lists no street address here;
meetings are by appointment, and most phases of the work never require
one. When in-person evaluation is warranted it takes place in the
evaluee's own community, and travel for evaluations, depositions, and
trial testimony is scheduled from the Kansas City home office that anchors
the firm's [national practice](/locations/nationwide/).
