County, while the economy that determines what a Sapulpa evaluee can earn
is the Tulsa metropolitan area. Getting both halves right, without letting
either one blur the other, is the core of sound expert work here.
Purinton Analytics keeps the forum analysis and the labor market analysis
distinct and documented, serving plaintiff and defense counsel in Sapulpa
matters with [vocational expert opinions](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),

## A Creek County forum

State-court matters arising in Sapulpa proceed in the District Court for
Creek County. In federal practice, Oklahoma offers the United States
District Court for the Northern District of Oklahoma, the United States
District Court for the Eastern District of Oklahoma, and the United
States District Court for the Western District of Oklahoma as available
forums; the one that controls a given Sapulpa caption, together with any
registration or qualification requirement it imposes on a testifying
expert, is identified and confirmed during the conflict check and intake
process. Matters elsewhere in the state are covered through the firm's
[Oklahoma practice](/locations/oklahoma/).

## An economy anchored across the county line

Sapulpa workers participate in a labor market centered on Tulsa, and an
earning capacity opinion confined to Creek County data would misstate the
opportunities and wages a Sapulpa evaluee actually faces. The firm
therefore draws on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and
wage estimates for the Tulsa metropolitan area and for Oklahoma
statewide, state workforce agency labor market information, and direct
labor market research where the referral questions require verification
of a particular occupation or employer type. Each report identifies the
geography behind every finding, so the regional character of the market
is documented rather than assumed. If the parties dispute how far the
evaluee can reasonably travel for work, the report addresses that dispute
directly instead of burying it inside an assumption. The
[Tulsa metro page](/locations/oklahoma/tulsa/) sets out that regional
practice.
