## Requesting a conflict check

A documented conflict check on the caption or party names precedes any
discussion of the merits. Counsel should
[submit the conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) with the parties and any
imminent disclosure, deposition, or trial dates. Clearance is followed by
a written scope, fee terms, and a records request tailored to the claims.
Where the case also calls for economic testimony, the firm arranges
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.
