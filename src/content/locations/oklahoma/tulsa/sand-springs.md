The product of a Sand Springs engagement is a written report, and the
report is where the discipline shows. Methods named, instruments
identified, data sourced, limits acknowledged: a Purinton Analytics
opinion is assembled so that both sides of a Sand Springs caption can
trace every finding back to the record. That standard applies whether the
referral is for a
[vocational expert opinion](/services/vocational-expert-witness/), a
[life care plan](/services/life-care-planning/), a
[medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/), or a
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) of an opposing
expert's work.

## What the report contains

A defined scope comes before the analysis; the records request is
tailored to the claims rather than boilerplate. Interviews and
standardized testing follow publisher protocols, and the resulting
opinion separates what the data supports from what it does not. When the
assignment is rebuttal, the same standard is applied to the opposing
report: methodology checked against its own cited sources, conclusions
tested against the underlying records, and disagreements stated with
their reasons. Nothing in a report is written that the author would not
defend line by line at deposition. Appendices identify the records
reviewed, so the foundation of the opinion is inspectable at a glance.

## Courts serving Sand Springs

Sand Springs is part of Tulsa County, and state-court matters arising
there proceed in the District Court for Tulsa County. Federal matters in
Oklahoma may be heard in the United States District Court for the
Northern District of Oklahoma, the United States District Court for the
Western District of Oklahoma, or the United States District Court for the
Eastern District of Oklahoma; which forum controls, and what it requires
of a testifying expert, is identified and confirmed during the conflict
check and intake process. The statewide practice is described on the
[Oklahoma page](/locations/oklahoma/).

## Remote first, in person when warranted

The firm keeps no staffed office in Sand Springs and publishes no street
address here. Engagements run remote-first: records analysis, structured
interviews, and most standardized testing proceed at a distance under
publisher protocols. In-person evaluation, when the referral questions
justify it, is conducted in the evaluee's own community, with travel
scheduled from the Kansas City home office that serves the firm's
[national caseload](/locations/nationwide/).

## The intake sequence

Intake follows a fixed order: conflict check, then scope, then work.
Counsel should [start the conflict check](/refer-a-case/) with the
caption or party names and note controlling dates, especially disclosure
deadlines and trial settings. Cases that also need an economist proceed
as
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.
