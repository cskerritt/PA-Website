Purinton Analytics serves plaintiff and defense counsel throughout the
St. Louis region: St. Louis City, St. Louis County, St. Charles County, and
the surrounding eastern Missouri circuits, together with the Metro East
communities of Madison and St. Clair counties on the Illinois side of the
river. Referrals from the region reach the same evaluator and the same
documented methodology as every other engagement in the firm's practice,
with [vocational evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[rebuttal review of opposing expert reports](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/)
available to either side of the caption.

## Where the firm appears

St. Louis work spans two state court systems and two federal districts. The
firm accepts engagements venued in Missouri circuit courts and Illinois
circuit courts across the metro area, in the United States District Court for
the Eastern District of Missouri, and in the United States District Court for
the Southern District of Illinois. Because the evaluee pool crosses the river
as readily as the case filings do, reports for St. Louis matters state plainly
which side of the bi-state market grounds the vocational findings.

## Meetings and evaluations

The St. Louis presence operates by appointment; there is no permanently
staffed street office, and the firm does not publish a street address it does
not staff. In practice this changes little for counsel: evaluations are
scheduled in the evaluee's community or at counsel's offices, records review
and interviews proceed remotely where protocols permit, and Jason Purinton
travels from the Kansas City home office for evaluations, depositions, and
trial testimony across eastern Missouri and southern Illinois. Scheduling is
confirmed at intake alongside the disclosure calendar.

## A bi-state labor market, measured honestly

Earning capacity opinions in this region turn on which labor market actually
applies to the evaluee. The analysis uses Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates for the St. Louis metropolitan
area, statewide data for Missouri and for Illinois, and state workforce
agency labor market information from each state. Where an evaluee's
commutable market includes both Missouri and Metro East employers, the report
documents that choice and its effect on the numbers, so the opinion holds up
whether it is examined by plaintiff or defense counsel.
