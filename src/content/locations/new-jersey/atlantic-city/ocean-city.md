Venue is what immediately separates an Ocean City case from most of
its neighbors in this metropolitan area: Ocean City lies in Cape May
County, so the county courthouse that hears its state-court disputes is
not the one serving the towns clustered around Atlantic City. Purinton
Analytics pays attention to that kind of detail because its reports are
written to be examined by opposing counsel, and it brings the same care to
[vocational expert evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) for plaintiff
and defense engagements alike.

## Cape May County courts and the federal forum

State-court litigation involving Ocean City parties proceeds in the
Superior Court, New Jersey's trial court of general jurisdiction, sitting
for Cape May County. Federal claims go to the United States District Court
for the District of New Jersey, the state's federal trial forum; the
vicinage that will control a particular caption, and any qualification or
registration requirement the venue imposes on a testifying expert, is
identified during the conflict check and intake process rather than
guessed at. Coverage of the neighboring communities and the wider region
appears on the [Atlantic City metro page](/locations/new-jersey/atlantic-city/)
and the [New Jersey state page](/locations/new-jersey/).

## A labor market that crosses the county line

The county boundary that determines the courthouse does not determine
where an Ocean City evaluee can work. Commuting patterns tie the town into
the metropolitan employment economy whose principal city is Atlantic City,
and the vocational analysis follows the worker, not the map. Findings rest
on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates
for the metropolitan area and for New Jersey statewide, on state workforce
agency labor market information, and on direct labor market research when
a current placement answer is needed. The report characterizes market
scale qualitatively and keeps its focus on the individual: what this
evaluee's medical restrictions permit, which skills transfer from the
actual work history, and which occupations survive both filters, with the
chain of reasoning exposed at every link.

## Appointments, not an office

There is no Purinton Analytics office in Ocean City and no published
street address anywhere in New Jersey; meetings here are by appointment,
and the firm states that plainly because honesty about presence belongs to
the same discipline that governs its opinions. Most engagements move
remotely through records review, structured interviews, and standardized
testing under publisher protocols where remote administration is
supported. When the referral calls for an in-person evaluation, it takes
place in the evaluee's own community, and when depositions or trial dates
require presence, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office.
For damages models that need signed economic analysis, the firm offers
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign that analysis
while vocational and care-planning work continues under one engagement
structure. How the practice operates
[across the country](/locations/nationwide/) is described on the
nationwide page.

## Opening steps in an Ocean City case

Begin with the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/), providing the
caption or party names and noting any imminent disclosure, deposition, or
trial dates. Nothing substantive happens before the conflict check clears
and a written scope and fee agreement is in place; after that, a records
request tailored to the claims sets the foundation, and the schedule is
built backward from the deadlines counsel actually faces.
