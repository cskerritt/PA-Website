## Absecon's place in the court system

Absecon is located in Atlantic County, and its state-court cases are heard
in the Superior Court, New Jersey's trial court of general jurisdiction.
Claims supporting federal jurisdiction proceed in the United States
District Court for the District of New Jersey; which vicinage governs a
particular caption, and whether the venue imposes any registration or
qualification requirement on a testifying expert, is determined during the
conflict check and intake process rather than assumed from the map. The
firm's coverage of nearby communities appears on the
[Atlantic City metro page](/locations/new-jersey/atlantic-city/), and its
statewide approach on the [New Jersey state page](/locations/new-jersey/).

## From scope agreement to labor market evidence

Once the conflict check clears, a written scope and fee agreement defines
the assignment before any records change hands, and the records request
that follows is tailored to the claims rather than copied from a template.
The vocational analysis for an Absecon evaluee then proceeds against the
regional employment economy centered on Atlantic City, using Bureau of
Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the
metropolitan area and for New Jersey statewide, state workforce agency
labor market information, and direct employer research where the question
warrants it. The report characterizes scale qualitatively, never
numerically, and works from the individual outward: restrictions
established by the medical record, skills established by the work history,
and the realistic occupational base that remains when both are applied.

## What by-appointment service means here

The firm's only office is in Kansas City. It maintains no Absecon
location, publishes no local address, and does not pretend otherwise,
because a practice whose product is credibility cannot be casual about its
own facts. Interviews and standardized testing are handled remotely under
publisher protocols where supported; in-person evaluation, when the
referral requires it, takes place in the evaluee's own community. Travel
for evaluations, depositions, and trial testimony is scheduled from the
home office against the dates counsel identifies at intake. When damages
require signed economic analysis, the firm structures
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. The same intake-to-opinion pipeline serves matters
venued anywhere in the country, as the
[nationwide page](/locations/nationwide/) describes.

## Requesting the conflict check

Everything above begins at the same door: the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/). Provide the caption or party names,
a short summary of the claims, and any disclosure, deposition, or trial
dates already scheduled. Counsel receives a clear answer on conflicts, then
a written scope, and only then does the substantive work begin, which is
exactly the order opposing counsel would expect a careful expert to follow.
