## Opinions built to survive cross-examination

What holds up in a deposition room is method, so the vocational findings
for a Margate City evaluee are assembled from sources the opposing expert
can pull and check: Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and
wage estimates for the metropolitan area whose principal city is Atlantic
City and for New Jersey statewide, state workforce agency labor market
information, and direct labor market research where a placement question
needs a current answer. Scale is stated qualitatively, never as a figure.
The chain from medical restriction to transferable skill to remaining
occupational base is written out link by link, and the report concedes
what the record does not establish, because a concession made in the
report costs less than one extracted at trial.
