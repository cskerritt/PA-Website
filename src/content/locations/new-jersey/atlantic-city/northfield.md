Every report Purinton Analytics writes for a Northfield matter is drafted
on a single assumption: the most careful reader will be the expert
retained by the other side. That assumption shapes the citations, the
stated limitations, and the refusal to reach past the record, and it is
why the same work product serves plaintiff counsel in one Northfield case
and defense counsel in the next. The practice covers
[vocational expert evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/),
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/), and
[medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/).

Northfield sits in Atlantic County. Its state-court matters are heard in

## Defensible labor market findings

A vocational opinion about a Northfield evaluee survives cross-examination
only if its market evidence is sourced and its inferences are visible. The
firm builds that evidence from Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational
employment and wage estimates for the metropolitan area whose principal
city is Atlantic City and for New Jersey statewide, from state workforce
agency labor market information, and from direct labor market research
when published series cannot answer a placement question. Scale is
described qualitatively. Each conclusion is tied to something checkable:
a restriction to a page of the medical record, a transferable skill to a
documented job duty, an occupational match to a defined source, so that
disagreement between experts becomes a dispute about identified evidence
rather than about assertion.

## Presence, plainly stated

The firm serves Northfield by appointment from its Kansas City home
office, its only office anywhere. No Northfield address is published and
no local presence is implied, because an expert who shades the facts about
an office invites the inference that the reports are shaded too. Records
review, structured interviews, and standardized testing proceed remotely
under publisher protocols where remote administration is supported;
in-person evaluation happens in the evaluee's own community when the
referral questions require it, and deposition or trial travel is scheduled
from the home office against counsel's dates. Where damages require an
economist's signature, the firm arranges
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. The [nationwide page](/locations/nationwide/) shows
the same standard applied across the country.

## Beginning a Northfield engagement

The route in is the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/): caption or
party names, the nature of the claims, and any disclosure, deposition, or
trial dates already on the calendar. A cleared conflict check is followed
by a written scope and fee agreement before records move. From that point
forward the file is built the way it will eventually be read, which is to
say adversarially, and the report is written so that reading holds up.
