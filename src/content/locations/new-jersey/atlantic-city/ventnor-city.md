## Venue and the schedule it sets

Ventnor City belongs to Atlantic County, and its state-court litigation
proceeds in the Superior Court, New Jersey's trial court of general
jurisdiction, under the case management orders that court issues. Federal
matters go to the United States District Court for the District of New
Jersey, where scheduling orders impose their own expert deadlines; the
vicinage that controls a given caption, and any venue-specific
qualification requirement, is confirmed during the conflict check and
intake process. The [Atlantic City metro page](/locations/new-jersey/atlantic-city/)
and the [New Jersey state page](/locations/new-jersey/) describe how the
firm covers the courts of the surrounding region.

## Labor market analysis on a litigation clock

Meeting a deadline never justifies thinning the analysis. For a Ventnor
City evaluee the firm assembles the same foundation it would assemble with
unlimited time: Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and
wage estimates for the metropolitan area whose principal city is Atlantic
City and for New Jersey statewide, state workforce agency labor market
information, and direct labor market research where a placement question
requires it. Market scale appears in qualitative terms only. The opinion
connects the evaluee's documented restrictions and demonstrated skills to
the occupations realistically available across the regional commuting
market, and it shows its work, because the disclosure that follows will
put every inference in front of the opposing expert.

## Fitting the evaluation to the deadline

Remote-first practice is what makes compressed schedules workable. Records
review, structured interviews, and standardized testing under publisher
protocols proceed without travel time where remote administration is
supported; when the referral requires an in-person evaluation, it is
conducted in the evaluee's own community, and travel is scheduled from the
Kansas City home office, which is the firm's only office. No Ventnor City
address is published because none exists, and the firm says so rather than
implying otherwise. Deposition and trial dates are locked in at intake so
testimony never collides with the report schedule. Where the damages model

[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure, keeping a single timeline across all components. The
