## Venue for Pleasantville matters

Pleasantville sits in Atlantic County, and state-court litigation

federal jurisdiction, the United States District Court for the District of

that will actually control a given caption, and any venue-specific
qualification requirement that comes with it, is identified during the

## Reading the commuting market honestly

Because Pleasantville workers participate in a regional employment
economy, the data foundation for a vocational opinion here is regional as
well. The firm works from Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational
employment and wage estimates for the metropolitan area and for New Jersey
statewide, adds state workforce agency labor market information, and runs
direct employer research when a placement question needs an answer the
published series cannot supply. Scale is always described qualitatively.
What makes the opinion individual rather than generic is the sequence
applied to this evaluee: functional restrictions taken from the medical
record, a transferable skills analysis built on the actual work history,
and a survey of the occupations that survive both, each step documented so
the opposing expert can trace it.
