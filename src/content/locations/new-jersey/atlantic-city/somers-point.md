## The courts that serve Somers Point

Somers Point is an Atlantic County community, and its state-court disputes
are heard in the Superior Court, New Jersey's trial court of general
jurisdiction. Matters supporting federal jurisdiction proceed in the
United States District Court for the District of New Jersey; the vicinage
controlling a specific caption, together with any venue-particular
qualification requirement for a testifying expert, is settled during the

## Placing the evaluee in a regional market

An earning-capacity opinion for a Somers Point resident cannot be written
from the town alone, because the realistic job search reaches across the
metropolitan area anchored by Atlantic City. The firm therefore draws its
labor market foundation from Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational
employment and wage estimates covering the metropolitan area and New
Jersey statewide, from state workforce agency labor market information,
and from direct employer inquiry when a placement question demands a
current answer. Scale is described in qualitative terms throughout. The
analysis itself stays individual: restrictions documented in the medical
record, skills demonstrated in the work history, and the occupational
ground where restriction and skill overlap, presented so that the opposing expert can retrace every step and
the trier of fact can follow the logic without translation.

## Logistics, travel, and the care plan

The firm's only office is in Kansas City; Somers Point is served by
appointment, with no local address published and none implied. That
arrangement is stated openly because the reports depend on the same habit
of accuracy. Depositions and trial appearances are scheduled from the home
office against the dates counsel supplies at intake. When future care
enters the damages picture, the life care plan is developed from the
treating record with clinician input and priced to the evaluee's own
market, and where the model requires economic analysis the firm arranges
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/):
independent economist partners perform and sign the economic work while
the vocational and care-planning components proceed under one engagement
structure. The [nationwide page](/locations/nationwide/) shows how the
identical process serves matters venued far from New Jersey.
