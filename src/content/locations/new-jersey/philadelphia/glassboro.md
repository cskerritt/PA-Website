For an injured worker or accident victim living in Glassboro, the
immediate practical question about a vocational evaluation is what it
will actually involve. In this practice the answer is deliberately
unremarkable: a structured interview, standardized testing, a careful
review of the medical and earnings records, and a written opinion that
either side of the caption can examine line by line. Purinton Analytics
provides [vocational expert evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/),
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/), and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. Plaintiff and defense counsel retain the firm on
identical terms, and the methodology never bends toward the side paying
for it.

## Gloucester County and its forums

Glassboro sits in Gloucester County, and state-court matters involving
Glassboro parties are heard in the Superior Court. Federal matters
proceed in the United States District Court for the District of New
Jersey; which forum controls a given caption, and whether it imposes any
venue-specific registration or qualification requirement, is identified
and confirmed during the conflict check and intake process. Each court
brings its own disclosure schedule, and the firm maps that schedule at
intake so report deadlines and deposition dates shape the work plan from
the outset instead of surprising it near the end.

## A community inside a regional market

Glassboro is a modest-sized community, but no credible opinion about a
Glassboro evaluee stops at the municipal line. Earning capacity is
measured against the commuting economy of the metropolitan area whose
principal city is Philadelphia, and the data behind that measurement is
named in the report: Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment
and wage estimates for the metropolitan area and for New Jersey
statewide, state workforce agency labor market information, and direct
labor market research when the referral questions warrant it. Regional
scale is characterized in qualitative terms only. What keeps the opinion
individual rather than generic is the evaluee-level work: transferable
skills, functional restrictions tied to the treating records, and
placement prospects tested against jobs that actually exist within reach
of Gloucester County.

## How a Glassboro evaluee is seen

The firm's only office is its Kansas City home office; Glassboro is
served by appointment, with no local address published and none implied,
because honesty about presence belongs in the same category as honesty
about data. Most engagements open remotely, using interview and testing
protocols validated for remote administration. Where the questions
presented require an in-person evaluation, the evaluation is conducted in
the evaluee's own community rather than asking the evaluee to travel, and
when deposition or trial testimony calls for a courtroom appearance,
travel is arranged from Kansas City to meet the litigation schedule. The
approach mirrors the [national practice](/locations/nationwide/): the
venue changes, the method does not.
