For counsel with an injured or displaced worker in Hackettstown, the practical question usually comes before the legal question: how does an evaluee in a small Warren County town actually get evaluated without turning the assessment itself into a burden? Purinton Analytics is built around that question. Interviews, records analysis, and most working sessions with counsel proceed remotely, and when an in-person [vocational evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) or [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) assessment is warranted, it is conducted in the evaluee's own community rather than requiring travel to a distant office. Plaintiff and defense referrals are handled under the same protocol.

## How a Hackettstown evaluation proceeds

An engagement here follows the sequence used everywhere in the practice: a documented conflict check, a defined scope, and a records request tailored to the claims. The evaluee interview covers work history, education, transferable skills, and functional limitations as documented by treating and examining sources; the resulting report separates what the records establish from what the evaluation adds, so that the opinion can withstand examination by whichever side did not retain it. Where future medical needs are at issue, [medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/) work follows the same documented-source discipline. The firm maintains no Hackettstown office and lists no local address, a policy explained on the [nationwide practice page](/locations/nationwide/); presence, when needed, is scheduled from the Kansas City home office.

## Warren County courts and the federal forum

State court matters arising in Hackettstown proceed in the Superior Court, and the firm accepts Warren County engagements from either side of the caption. For claims belonging in federal court, the United States District Court for the District of New Jersey is among the available forums; which court controls a given matter is a venue question confirmed during the conflict check and intake, not something assumed from geography. Counsel handling matters elsewhere in the state will find the broader picture on the [New Jersey locations page](/locations/new-jersey/).

## Reading the metropolitan labor market from Hackettstown

Hackettstown sits at the New Jersey edge of the Allentown-Bethlehem-Easton metropolitan area, a regional economy whose principal city is Allentown, and its residents work across a commuting shed that a credible earning capacity analysis has to map honestly. Vocational conclusions rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for that metropolitan area and for New Jersey statewide, along with state workforce agency labor market information and, where the referral requires it, direct labor market research into specific occupations and employers. Scale is treated qualitatively: the report describes the character and reach of the market an evaluee can access, never a manufactured statistic, and each conclusion is tied to a cited source both parties can check. When damages require economic modeling, the firm offers [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement structure. The regional context, including the towns on the Pennsylvania side of the metro, is set out on the [Allentown metro page](/locations/pennsylvania/allentown/).
