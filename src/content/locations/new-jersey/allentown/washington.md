An expert opinion in a Washington case is only as useful as its timing. Whether the matter is a personal injury claim, an employment dispute, or a wrongful death action, counsel retaining a vocational expert or life care planner in this Warren County town is working backward from dates: a discovery cutoff, a report deadline, a deposition, a trial setting. Purinton Analytics structures each engagement around that calendar from the outset, so that [expert testimony and litigation consulting](/services/expert-testimony-litigation-consulting/) support arrives when the schedule needs it rather than when it happens to be finished. Retention is open to plaintiff and defense counsel alike, and the working method does not change with the side of the caption.

## Working to the disclosure calendar

Intake for a Washington matter fixes the operative dates before any analysis begins. The records request goes out early because treating records, employment files, and educational documentation drive both the [vocational assessment](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) and any [life care plan](/services/life-care-planning/), and gaps discovered late are what push reports past deadlines. Draft timing, supplementation, and deposition preparation are planned against the schedule counsel provides, and if the assignment is instead a [rebuttal or peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) of an opposing expert's report, the same calendar discipline applies to the response.
