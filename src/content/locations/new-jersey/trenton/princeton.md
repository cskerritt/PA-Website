## Venue for Princeton matters

Princeton sits in Mercer County, and state-court litigation involving
Princeton parties proceeds in the Superior Court in Mercer County. New
Jersey's federal forum is the United States District Court for the District
of New Jersey, and the venue that actually controls a given caption, along
with any registration or qualification requirement particular to it, is
identified and confirmed during the conflict check and intake rather than
assumed from an address. Whichever court the caption lands in, the file is
built to the same standard: sources cited so they can be pulled, methods
described so they can be replicated, and opinions stated no more strongly
than the record supports.

## Labor market research at regional scale

Vocational findings for a Princeton evaluee rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Trenton
metropolitan area and for New Jersey statewide, supplemented by state
workforce agency labor market information and by direct labor market
research when the referral questions warrant it. Regional conditions are
characterized qualitatively and conservatively; the individual analysis is
where the depth goes. Transferable skills, medically documented
restrictions, and realistic placement options are worked up at the level of
the specific evaluee, because that is the level at which cross-examination
happens and at which an opinion must hold.

## Appointments, not an office

The firm keeps no Princeton office and publishes no street address for this
location, because candor about presence belongs to the same discipline that
governs the reports. Princeton work is served by appointment from the
Kansas City home office. Most engagements open remotely, with records
review, structured interviews, and standardized testing administered under
publisher protocols that support remote use. When the questions presented
call for in-person evaluation in the evaluee's own community, or when
deposition and trial dates require presence, travel is scheduled from
Kansas City and coordinated with counsel's calendar. The remote posture
changes nothing about the record: every instrument, interview, and data
source is documented as fully as it would be in person.
