calendar. Disclosure deadlines, deposition settings, and trial dates arrive
on the same clock whether the plaintiff lives in a small community or in the
principal city, and a report served late or thin is a problem no venue will
forgive. Purinton Analytics takes Hightstown engagements from plaintiff and
defense counsel alike, delivering [vocational expert
evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/), [life care

## Where Hightstown cases are heard

Hightstown is a Mercer County town, and state-court actions involving
Hightstown parties are tried in the Superior Court in Mercer County.
Federal matters proceed in the United States District Court for the
District of New Jersey; which forum controls a particular caption, and
whatever qualification or registration formalities attach to it, gets
settled during the conflict check and intake rather than presumed. The
drafting standard does not vary by courthouse. A report is written so that
the opposing expert can trace every figure to its source and the opposing
lawyer can test every step of the method, because that is the reader the
report will eventually face.

## A small town inside a regional market

Hightstown itself is a small community, which is precisely why the labor
market analysis cannot stop at its boundaries. An evaluee's realistic
employment options are assessed across the commuting region anchored by
Trenton, using Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage
estimates for the Trenton metropolitan area and for New Jersey statewide,
alongside state workforce agency labor market information and targeted
direct research where the referral questions call for it. The regional
picture is drawn in careful, qualitative terms; the close analysis belongs
to the individual, whose transferable skills, restrictions, and placement
prospects are documented at the depth cross-examination demands.

## Scheduling around the case, not around an office

There is no Hightstown office to visit. The firm operates from its Kansas
City home office, states that plainly, and serves this location by
appointment. In practice the schedule bends to the litigation: records
review and structured interviews typically begin remotely, standardized
instruments are administered under publisher protocols that permit remote
testing, and when the referral requires an in-person evaluation in the
evaluee's own community or testimony requires presence at deposition or
trial, travel from Kansas City is arranged against counsel's deadlines.
Remote or in person, the documentation is identical in rigor, so the
posture of the evaluation never becomes a line of attack.
