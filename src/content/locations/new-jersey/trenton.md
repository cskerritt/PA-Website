When counsel in the Trenton-Princeton metro area retain a vocational expert,
the referral questions tend to sound the same from either side of the
caption: what work the evaluee can still perform, what that work pays in the
relevant labor market, and what care an injury will require over a lifetime.
Purinton Analytics answers those questions for plaintiff and defense
attorneys through [vocational expert witness work](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. The metro is served as part of the firm's broader
[New Jersey practice](/locations/new-jersey/), under the same methodology the
firm applies everywhere it works.

## Venue footprint in Mercer County

Engagements are accepted for matters venued in the Superior Court in Mercer
County and in the United States District Court for the District of New
Jersey. The work product is built for either forum: a written opinion
grounded in the records, standardized assessment instruments, and a
methodology disclosed plainly enough for opposing counsel to test it.
Because the same report standard applies to plaintiff and defense referrals
alike, each side of a Trenton caption receives the same documentation of
sources, the same explanation of methods, and the same clear separation
between the vocational findings and the economic calculations that
independent economist partners sign.

## Meetings by appointment, not a storefront

Trenton meetings are by appointment. The firm keeps no staffed office in the
metro and publishes no street address for this location, because accuracy
about presence is part of the same discipline that governs its reports. Most
Mercer County engagements begin remotely: records review, structured
vocational interviews, and standardized testing administered under publisher
protocols where remote administration is supported. When the referral
questions call for in-person evaluation, or when deposition and trial
calendars require attendance, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home
office and planned around counsel's deadlines rather than around office
hours the firm does not keep.

## Labor market research for Trenton evaluees

Vocational findings for evaluees in the metro rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the
Trenton-Princeton metropolitan area and for New Jersey statewide, state
workforce agency labor market information, and direct labor market research
where the referral questions warrant it. Life care plans and
[medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/) for New
Jersey residents are priced to the evaluee's own market using
provider-specific inquiries and published fee data, so the projected costs
reflect what care actually costs where the evaluee lives rather than a
national average.

## Starting a Trenton engagement

Every engagement begins the same way regardless of venue: a documented
conflict check, a defined scope, and a records request tailored to the
claims, so the resulting opinion rests on a record both sides can examine.
Counsel with a matter pending in Mercer County or in federal court can
[request a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) with the case caption and party
names, and should note any imminent disclosure, deposition, or trial dates
so scheduling can be confirmed at intake. Attorneys weighing whether the
firm fits a matter venued elsewhere can review its
[national coverage](/locations/nationwide/), which describes how the same
remote-first model serves courts well beyond New Jersey.
