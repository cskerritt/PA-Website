The Atlantic City-Hammonton metro area spans Atlantic County and Cape May
County, and Purinton Analytics serves counsel across both from a single
point of contact. Whether a matter is venued in Atlantic County or in Cape
May County, the firm delivers the same
[earning capacity and employability evaluations](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) work to
plaintiff and defense counsel alike. When a referral also requires economic
loss calculations, the firm offers
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. Engagements range from pre-suit consulting reviews to
testifying assignments, and the deliverable in every posture is a written
opinion prepared for scrutiny by the other side of the caption.

## Courts serving the metro's counties

The firm accepts engagements for matters venued in the Superior Court in
Atlantic County and Cape May County, and in the United States District
Court for the District of New Jersey. Reports are written to a single
standard regardless of forum or retaining side: sources documented,
instruments identified, and reasoning laid out so that opposing counsel can
follow every step from the records to the opinion. That standard does not
change between a state court filing and a federal one, and it does not
change depending on which side of the caption sends the retention letter.

## How a by-appointment metro is served

There is no staffed Purinton Analytics office in Atlantic City, and the
firm publishes no street address for this location; candor about presence
is part of the same discipline that shapes its written opinions. Meetings
throughout the metro are by appointment. The working model is remote-first:
records analysis, structured interviews, and standardized testing under
publisher protocols where remote administration is supported, with results
delivered on the schedule counsel's case demands. When an evaluation is
better performed in person, or when a deposition or trial requires the
expert in the room, travel is arranged from the Kansas City home office and
confirmed against the court's calendar at intake.

## Grounding opinions in the local labor market

A defensible vocational opinion for an Atlantic County or Cape May County
evaluee has to reflect the labor market that evaluee actually faces.
Findings rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and
wage estimates for the Atlantic City-Hammonton metropolitan area and for
New Jersey statewide, supplemented by state workforce agency labor market
information and direct labor market research where warranted. Life care
plans and medical cost projections are priced to the evaluee's own region
through provider-specific inquiries and published fee data, rather than
imported from national figures that may not describe the local market.

## Referrals and conflict checks

Engagement begins with a documented conflict check and a defined scope, so
that the eventual opinion rests on a record both sides can examine. Counsel
with matters in Atlantic County, Cape May County, or federal court can
[submit the caption or party names](/refer-a-case/) and flag any imminent
disclosure, deposition, or trial dates. The metro pages sit within the
firm's [New Jersey practice](/locations/new-jersey/), and attorneys with
cases beyond the state line will find the same engagement model described
on the [nationwide coverage page](/locations/nationwide/).
