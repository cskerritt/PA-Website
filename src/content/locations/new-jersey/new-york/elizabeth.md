Most expert retentions in Elizabeth are driven by a date: a disclosure
deadline approaching, a deposition noticed, a trial setting that suddenly
makes the damages proof urgent. Purinton Analytics builds its Elizabeth
engagements backward from those dates. At intake, counsel's calendar goes
into the written scope, and the delivery schedule for the
[vocational expert witness](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) report or
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) opinion is set against
the deadlines that actually govern the case. Plaintiff and defense referrals
are handled on the same terms and the same timelines.

## Union County courts and the federal alternative

Elizabeth civil claims filed in state court are heard in the Superior Court,
which tries matters arising in Union County under New Jersey's expert
disclosure and deposition rules. Cases in federal court proceed in the
United States District Court for the District of New Jersey, and the
question of which forum controls a particular dispute, together with any
expert registration or qualification requirement that forum imposes, is
answered during the conflict check and intake rather than presumed on a
landing page. In either court system the engagement runs the same way:
conflicts documented, scope defined in writing, records requested against
the claims, and only then an opinion.

## The market evidence behind an Elizabeth opinion

An Elizabeth evaluee's employment options extend well past the city itself,
into a metropolitan economy that ranks among the largest in the country.
Vocational conclusions are grounded in Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates for the New York metropolitan
area and for New Jersey statewide, joined with state workforce agency labor
market information and, where the referral warrants, direct labor market
research. The report says which labor market it measured, why that market
fits the evaluee's skills and realistic commuting range, and how the
transferable-skills analysis follows from it, so that the opinion can be
probed on its stated terms instead of on guesswork. The wider metropolitan
picture is drawn on the [New York metro page](/locations/new-york/new-york/).

## Appointments, travel, and remote work

Purinton Analytics has no office in Elizabeth and does not advertise one;
by-appointment service, honestly labeled, is the model for every location
outside the Kansas City home office. Evaluations are available by secure
video or in person in the evaluee's own community, and travel for
evaluations, depositions, and trial testimony is scheduled from Kansas City
with the case calendar in hand. When counsel needs the economic loss
quantified alongside the vocational and care findings, the firm arranges
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work continues under a
single engagement structure. Venue notes for the rest of the state appear on
the [New Jersey state page](/locations/new-jersey/), and the
[nationwide overview](/locations/nationwide/) explains how the practice
covers other jurisdictions.
