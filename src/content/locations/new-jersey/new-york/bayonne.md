Every expert report in a Bayonne case will eventually be read by someone
whose job is to take it apart. Purinton Analytics writes for that reader.
Its [vocational expert witness](/services/vocational-expert-witness/)
reports and [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) opinions
are drafted on the assumption that opposing counsel, an opposing expert,
and the court will each test the sourcing, the math behind the reasoning,
and the fit between record and conclusion. Because the firm is retained by
plaintiffs in some Bayonne matters and defendants in others, that
adversarial reading is not hypothetical; it is the standing condition of
the work.

## Hudson County venue for Bayonne cases

Bayonne sits in Hudson County, and its state-court civil claims are tried
in the Superior Court under New Jersey's framework for expert disclosure
and deposition practice. When a Bayonne dispute is in federal court, the
in-state forum is the United States District Court for the District of New
Jersey, and the venue that actually controls, along with any registration
or qualification requirement it places on a testifying expert, is settled
during the conflict check and intake. Forum questions change the calendar;
they do not change the standard. Each engagement begins with a documented
conflict check, a scope agreed in writing, and a records request built from
the pleadings, so that cross-examination finds a record, not improvisation.

## Bayonne work histories, metropolitan wages

Evaluees from Bayonne commonly bring work histories that reach past the
city itself into the surrounding metropolitan economy, and the vocational
analysis has to measure earning capacity against the market the evaluee can
realistically reach rather than against the city limits. The firm relies on Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates for the New York metropolitan
area and for New Jersey statewide, together with state workforce agency
labor market information and, when warranted by the referral questions,
direct labor market research. Each report identifies the market it
measured and the reasoning that connects the evaluee's skills and
restrictions to opportunities within it. The metropolitan setting is
covered on the [New York metro page](/locations/new-york/new-york/).

## Serving Bayonne without a storefront

There is no Bayonne office to visit, and the firm says so rather than
implying otherwise, because overstated presence would undercut reports
that turn on precise statements of fact. Bayonne engagements run by
appointment: secure video evaluations where they serve the case, in-person
evaluations in the evaluee's own community where observation matters, and
travel from the Kansas City home office for depositions and trial dates.
For matters that also need the loss quantified in economic terms, counsel
can add [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work advances under a
single engagement structure. Statewide practice notes appear on the
[New Jersey state page](/locations/new-jersey/), and the firm's coverage
elsewhere is outlined in the [nationwide overview](/locations/nationwide/).
