## Venue for Clifton claims

Clifton lies in Passaic County, and its state-court civil matters are heard
in the Superior Court, subject to New Jersey's rules on expert disclosure,
deposition, and trial testimony. Where a claim proceeds in federal court,
the forum available in the state is the United States District Court for
the District of New Jersey; determining which venue governs a particular
case, and what expert registration or qualification that venue may require,
is part of the conflict check and intake, not a matter of assumption. The
work product is designed so that a judge or opposing expert in any of those
forums can trace each conclusion back to a named source.

## Placing a Clifton evaluee in the regional economy

The vocational question for a Clifton evaluee is usually regional in scale,
because residents work across a metropolitan economy far larger than the
city itself. The firm's answer is built from Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates for the New York metropolitan
area and for New Jersey statewide, layered with state workforce agency
labor market information and direct labor market research when the referral
questions require ground-level confirmation. Market definition, commuting
range, and transferable skills are addressed expressly in the report, in
that order, so the earning-capacity conclusion follows visibly from stated
premises. Context for the metropolitan area as a whole is available on the
[New York metro page](/locations/new-york/new-york/), with statewide venue
material on the [New Jersey state page](/locations/new-jersey/).

## Logistics stated plainly

No Clifton office exists and none is claimed; the firm treats accuracy
about its own footprint as a small daily proof of the accuracy it promises
in reports. Clifton engagements are served by appointment from the Kansas
City home office, with evaluations offered by secure video or conducted in
person in the evaluee's own community, and with travel for depositions and
trial planned around the court's schedule. If the damages presentation also
