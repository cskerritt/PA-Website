When the injured person lives in Paterson, the evaluation should meet the
evaluee where they are. Purinton Analytics structures its Paterson
engagements around the person being evaluated: interviews and assessments
are offered by secure video for evaluees who can participate that way, and
in person in the evaluee's own community when records, function, or
credibility questions make direct observation the better course. That
evaluee-first posture supports both the
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) and the
[vocational expert witness](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) sides of
the practice, and it is available to plaintiff and defense counsel on equal
terms.

## Passaic County venue for Paterson matters

Paterson is a Passaic County city, and civil claims arising there in state
court are heard in the Superior Court under New Jersey's rules for expert
disclosure and deposition practice. Federal matters proceed in the United
States District Court for the District of New Jersey; the controlling venue
for any specific case, and any registration or qualification requirement
that venue imposes on a testifying expert, is worked out during the conflict
check and intake. The courthouse does not change the method. A documented
conflict check, a written statement of scope, and a records request matched
to the pleaded claims come before analysis in every engagement, so the
finished opinion stands on a record both parties can inspect.

## Reading Paterson's labor market honestly

Paterson is a substantial city in its own right, and its residents also draw
work from across the wider metropolitan economy, so a defensible earning
capacity opinion has to be explicit about which market it is measuring. The
firm anchors its vocational conclusions in Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates for the New York metropolitan
area and for New Jersey statewide, adds state workforce agency labor market
information, and conducts direct labor market research when the referral
questions justify the added depth. Transferable skills are assessed against
that stated market, with the evaluee's realistic commuting range treated as
evidence rather than assumption. Metro-wide context appears on the
[New York metro page](/locations/new-york/new-york/).

## Scheduling around the case, not the expert

The firm keeps no office in Paterson, lists no local address, and will not
imply a storefront presence it does not have; candor about logistics is of
a piece with candor in reports. What counsel gets instead is a national,
by-appointment practice: travel for in-person evaluations, depositions, and
trial testimony is scheduled from the Kansas City home office and planned
against the dates that matter in the case. Counsel whose damages theory also
needs an economist can use
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/),
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. How the same model serves courts across the country is
described in the [nationwide overview](/locations/nationwide/), and
statewide venue notes are collected on the
[New Jersey state page](/locations/new-jersey/).
