In a catastrophic injury case out of East Orange, the hardest questions are
about the future: what care the injured person will need across a lifetime,
what that care consists of, and what work, if any, remains realistically
open to them. Purinton Analytics addresses those questions through
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) grounded in the medical
record and through
[vocational expert witness](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) analysis
of residual earning capacity, prepared for whichever side of the caption
issues the retention. The care plan and the vocational opinion are built to
be read together, and each is built to be read critically.

## Where East Orange matters are litigated

East Orange is an Essex County city; its state-court civil claims proceed in
the Superior Court under New Jersey's rules governing expert disclosure,
depositions, and trial testimony. For federal litigation, New Jersey's forum
is the United States District Court for the District of New Jersey, and the
venue controlling a specific case, together with any expert registration or
qualification requirement attached to it, is confirmed as part of the
conflict check and intake. Whichever court is involved, the file is
assembled the same way: documented conflict clearance, written scope, and a
records request drawn from the claims, so that the opinions rest on
evidence the parties can both examine.

## Vocational evidence from the regional market

Residual earning capacity for an East Orange evaluee is measured against
the metropolitan economy the evaluee can actually reach, not against a
national abstraction. The firm draws on Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates for the New York metropolitan
area and for New Jersey statewide, supplements them with state workforce
agency labor market information, and adds direct labor market research when
the referral questions call for it. Restrictions from the medical record,
transferable skills, and realistic commuting range are analyzed together,
and the report shows that chain of reasoning step by step. The surrounding
metropolitan market is described on the
[New York metro page](/locations/new-york/new-york/).

## How the firm reaches East Orange

Purinton Analytics maintains its home office in Kansas City and serves East
Orange by appointment; no local office or address exists, and none is
claimed. Life care planning and vocational evaluations are conducted by
secure video or in person in the evaluee's own community, whichever the
case requires, and travel for evaluations, depositions, and trial
appearances is scheduled from the home office around the litigation
calendar. When the damages presentation needs supporting economic
calculations, the firm arranges
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. Statewide venue information appears on the
[New Jersey state page](/locations/new-jersey/), and national coverage is
summarized in the [nationwide overview](/locations/nationwide/).
