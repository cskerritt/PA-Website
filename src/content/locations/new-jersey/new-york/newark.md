An earning-capacity opinion is only as strong as its definition of the labor
market, and for a Newark evaluee that definition is the first substantive
choice in the file. Purinton Analytics makes the choice in writing and
defends it in the report. The firm provides
[vocational expert witness](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) analysis
and [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) for Newark matters
on referral from plaintiff and defense counsel alike, and it treats the
market question the same way regardless of which side sends the retention:
name the market, name the sources, and let the conclusion be tested on its
stated terms.

## Essex County venue and the federal forum

Newark sits in Essex County, and civil claims arising in the city proceed in
state court in the Superior Court, where New Jersey's rules govern expert
disclosure, deposition practice, and trial testimony. Matters in federal
court are heard in the United States District Court for the District of New
Jersey. Which forum controls a particular case, and whatever registration or
qualification requirement that forum places on a testifying expert, is
identified and confirmed during the conflict check and intake. The forum
sets the calendar and the disclosure format; it does not alter the method.
Every Newark engagement opens with a documented conflict check, a defined
written scope, and a records request tailored to the pleaded claims, so the
eventual opinion rests on a record both parties can examine.

## Naming the market behind the opinion

Newark's residents work within one of the largest metropolitan economies in
the country, which makes an unstated market definition a genuine analytical
failure rather than a technicality. Vocational findings for Newark evaluees
rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage
estimates for the New York metropolitan area and for New Jersey statewide,
supplemented by state workforce agency labor market information and by
direct labor market research where the referral questions warrant it. The
report identifies the market it measured, treats the commuting range
realistic for the particular evaluee as evidence to be established rather
than assumed, and draws its transferable-skills conclusions from that
defined market instead of from national averages. Broader coverage of the
metropolitan area appears on the
[New York metro page](/locations/new-york/new-york/).
