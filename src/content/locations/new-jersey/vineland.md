## Reading the Vineland labor market

Employability and earning capacity conclusions for Cumberland County
evaluees are grounded in Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment
and wage estimates for the Vineland metropolitan area and for New Jersey
statewide, together with state workforce agency labor market information
and direct labor market research where the referral warrants it. The same

projections are priced to the evaluee's own community through

describes the market the evaluee will actually use rather than a
generalized national one.
