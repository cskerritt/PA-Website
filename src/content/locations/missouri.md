Missouri is home ground. Purinton Analytics works from its Kansas City home office, and the practice's national engagement model was built here before it was extended anywhere else. For Missouri counsel on either side of the caption, that means the shortest travel logistics in the practice, paired with the same discipline the firm applies nationally: a documented conflict check, a defined scope, and a records request tailored to the claims.

## Missouri venues served

The firm accepts engagements for matters pending in the Circuit Court throughout Missouri and in the United States District Court for the Eastern District of Missouri and the United States District Court for the Western District of Missouri. Vocational and life care planning opinions figure in personal injury, employment, family, and disability-related matters across these forums, and each report is written to be read closely by opposing counsel before it is ever read aloud in court.

Metro-level detail is available on dedicated pages for [St. Louis, MO-IL](/locations/missouri/st-louis/), [Kansas City, MO-KS](/locations/missouri/kansas-city/), Springfield, MO, Columbia, MO, Joplin, MO-KS, Jefferson City, MO, St. Joseph, MO-KS, Cape Girardeau, MO-IL, Farmington, MO, Branson, MO, Warrensburg, MO, Fort Leonard Wood, MO, Sikeston, MO, Rolla, MO, and Sedalia, MO. Matters elsewhere in the state are served identically; those pages record logistics, not limits.

## Serving the home state

Even at home, the work is remote-first, because that is what keeps the analysis efficient and the file clean. Records review, transferable skills analysis, labor market research, and drafting proceed by secure document exchange, and most conferences with counsel are held by videoconference. The difference in Missouri is proximity: when a referral calls for in-person evaluation in the evaluee's community, or when deposition and trial dates require presence, travel from the Kansas City home office is a matter of scheduling rather than itinerary planning.

The home office is the firm's only office. Outside it, the firm publishes no Missouri street addresses and maintains no satellite storefronts, and meetings around the state are by appointment. Missouri engagements are part of the same [nationwide practice](/locations/nationwide/) counsel encounter in any other state. For matters that also require economic damages testimony, the firm offers coordinated damages engagements in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under a single engagement structure.

Labor market findings for Missouri evaluees rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the relevant Missouri metropolitan areas and for the state as a whole, alongside state workforce agency labor market information and direct labor market research where warranted. Sources are cited so that any finding can be checked against its origin.

## Qualification and disclosure in Missouri matters

Disclosure practice and scheduling expectations differ between the Circuit Court and the federal districts, and from division to division within them. Reports are drafted to satisfy the more exacting standard, and any venue-specific registration or qualification requirement is identified and confirmed during the conflict check and intake process. Sharing disclosure deadlines at intake allows the work plan to be sequenced backward from the dates that control the case.

## Starting a Missouri engagement

To begin a local or national engagement, [request a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) with the case caption and parties. Note the venue, the referral questions as presently framed, and any imminent disclosure, deposition, or trial dates. The firm answers with a conflict determination, a proposed scope, and a records request tailored to the claims, so the written opinion rests on a record both sides can examine.
