An earning capacity opinion is only as good as its understanding of the
person it describes, and the evaluee at the center of a Great Bend case
lives and works in Barton County, not in a statistical abstraction. Purinton
Analytics builds its
[vocational evaluations and expert testimony](/services/vocational-expert-witness/)
around that person: their work history, restrictions, transferable skills,
and the employment realistically open to them in their own community. The
firm serves plaintiff and defense counsel in the Great Bend, KS micropolitan
area with the same care, adding
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) and
[medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/) when the
future-care side of damages is in dispute.

## Barton County venues

The firm accepts engagements for matters in the District Court of Barton
County and for federal litigation in the United States District Court for
the District of Kansas. Regardless of forum, the engagement follows the
practice's standing structure: a documented conflict check, a defined
scope, and a records request tailored to the claims, producing a written
opinion that rests on a record either side of the caption can examine. When
economic loss calculations are needed alongside the vocational and
care-planning work, the firm offers
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds within the
same engagement structure. Statewide coverage is outlined on the
[Kansas practice page](/locations/kansas/).

## Appointments, evaluations, and travel

Great Bend meetings are by appointment. The firm has no staffed office in
Great Bend and publishes no street address for this location; describing
presence accurately is part of the discipline counsel are paying for.
Records review, analysis, drafting, and conferences with counsel are
conducted remotely on a schedule set when the engagement opens. In-person
vocational evaluations of Barton County evaluees, and appearances for
deposition or trial, are scheduled from the Kansas City home office and
planned against the litigation calendar from the beginning, so evaluation
logistics never crowd a disclosure deadline.

## The labor market behind the opinion

Vocational findings for Great Bend evaluees rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for Kansas, on state
workforce agency labor market information, and on direct labor market
research where the record warrants it. Because published statewide figures
describe Kansas as a whole rather than any particular community, the
analysis works from those sources down to the evaluee's actual
circumstances: which occupations fit the documented restrictions, which
employers hire for them within a realistic commute, and what the evidence
shows about wages for that work. Every step is cited, so the opinion can be
tested by opposing counsel and reviewed by the court, the same standard the
firm holds across its [national practice](/locations/nationwide/).

## Engaging the firm for a Barton County case

Start with the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/): submit the caption or
party names and identify any imminent disclosure, deposition, or trial
dates. After conflicts clear, the firm confirms scope and schedule in an
engagement letter, issues the records request, and delivers the written
opinion against the court's deadlines. Counsel who need an opposing
expert's report examined instead can request
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) through the same
intake.
