Counsel in the Emporia, KS micropolitan area retain Purinton Analytics for
matters that arise in Lyon County and Chase County, where a disputed earning
capacity or a contested plan of future care can decide the value of a case.
The firm provides
[vocational evaluation and expert testimony](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/) to plaintiff
and defense counsel alike, and it coordinates
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds within the
same engagement structure.

## Venue footprint in Lyon and Chase Counties

Engagements are accepted for matters in the District Court of Lyon County
and the District Court of Chase County, and for federal litigation in the
United States District Court for the District of Kansas. The work product is
built for either side of the caption: a documented conflict check, a defined
scope, a records request tailored to the claims, and a written opinion that
rests on a record opposing counsel can examine line by line. Reports are
prepared with deposition and cross-examination in mind, because that is
where they will be tested. Counsel with matters elsewhere in the state can
review the [Kansas practice page](/locations/kansas/) for statewide venue
coverage.

## Meetings by appointment, not a storefront

Emporia meetings are by appointment. The firm maintains no staffed office in
Emporia and publishes no street address for this location, because accuracy
about presence is part of the same discipline that governs its reports. Most
phases of an engagement, from intake and records review through report
drafting and conferences with counsel, proceed remotely on a schedule set at
the outset. When an in-person vocational evaluation is warranted, or when a
deposition or trial date requires attendance, travel is scheduled from the
Kansas City home office and planned around the litigation calendar rather
than around office hours.

## Labor market research for Emporia evaluees

Vocational findings for evaluees in the Emporia area rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for Kansas, on state
workforce agency labor market information, and on direct labor market
research where the record warrants it. In a smaller labor market, published
statewide estimates are a starting point rather than an answer, so the
analysis documents how an evaluee's transferable skills, restrictions, and
commuting realities interact with the work that actually exists in the
community. Every source is cited so that the opinion can be replicated, and
challenged, by the other side. The same methodology supports the firm's
[national practice](/locations/nationwide/), which keeps small-venue opinions
consistent with those offered in the largest metropolitan markets.

## Starting an Emporia engagement

The starting point is always the same. Submit the caption or party names through
the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and note any imminent disclosure,
deposition, or trial dates so the schedule can be confirmed before the
engagement letter issues. Once conflicts clear, the firm defines the scope
in writing, issues a tailored records request, and sets the reporting
timeline against the court's deadlines. Counsel receive a written opinion
prepared to the same standard whether the matter resolves at mediation in
Lyon County or proceeds to trial, and
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) of an opposing
expert's report is available under the same conflict-check process.
