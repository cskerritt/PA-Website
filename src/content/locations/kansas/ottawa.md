Every Ottawa engagement starts with process, not promises: a documented
conflict check, a scope fixed in writing, and a records request tailored to
the claims. That sequence is how Purinton Analytics serves counsel in the
Ottawa, KS micropolitan area, and it is deliberately identical for the
plaintiff bar and the defense bar. On that foundation the firm delivers
[vocational evaluation and expert testimony](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/) for Franklin
County litigation, along with
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) when the
assignment is to test an opposing expert's work rather than to offer an
affirmative opinion.

## Franklin County courts and beyond

Engagements are accepted for matters in the District Court of Franklin
County and for federal cases in the United States District Court for the
District of Kansas. Where a matter needs the full damages picture, the firm
structures
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds within the
same engagement structure, so counsel manage a single expert relationship
without blurring professional boundaries. For matters filed elsewhere in the
state, the [Kansas practice page](/locations/kansas/) sets out the firm's
statewide venue coverage.

## What by-appointment service means here

The firm maintains no staffed office in Ottawa and publishes no street
address for this location. That candor is intentional: an expert who is
careful about the record should be equally careful about how it describes
its own presence. Meetings are by appointment, and most of the engagement,
intake, records review, analysis, drafting, and conferences with counsel,
proceeds remotely on a schedule established at the outset. When an
evaluation should be conducted in person, or when a deposition or trial
date in Franklin County requires attendance, travel is arranged from the
Kansas City home office and confirmed against the court's calendar early in
the engagement.

## Evidence about the Ottawa labor market

Earning capacity findings for Franklin County evaluees are grounded in
Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for
Kansas, in state workforce agency labor market information, and in direct
labor market research when the referral questions warrant it. The analysis
asks the practical question a jury will ask: given this evaluee's
restrictions, training, and work history, what employment is realistically
available in and around this community, and at what earnings. Each answer
is tied to a cited source so the opinion can be checked, replicated, and
cross-examined. The same evidentiary standard governs the firm's
[national practice](/locations/nationwide/), so an Ottawa report carries the
same weight as one prepared for a major metropolitan venue.

## Requesting a conflict check for an Ottawa matter

The engagement begins when conflicts clear, and conflicts begin with the
caption. Submit the case caption or party names through the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/), and include any imminent disclosure
deadlines, deposition settings, or trial dates so scheduling can be
confirmed at the same time. Once cleared, the firm issues an engagement
letter with a defined scope, sends its tailored records request, and commits
to a reporting timeline that respects the court's deadlines, whichever side
of the caption made the call.
