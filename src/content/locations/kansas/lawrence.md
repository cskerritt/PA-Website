A vocational opinion in a Lawrence case has to answer concrete questions: what
work the evaluee can still perform, what that work actually pays in the market
the evaluee can reach, and what the injury has done to earning capacity over a
working life. Purinton Analytics answers those questions through
[vocational evaluation and expert testimony](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
and pairs them where the case requires with
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/),
[medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/), and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis
while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement
structure. Referrals come from plaintiff and defense counsel alike, and the
answers do not change with the caption side.

## Douglas County and the federal district

The Lawrence metro is compact: its state-court work runs through the District
Court of Douglas County, and federal matters proceed in the United States
District Court for the District of Kansas. That compactness simplifies
engagement logistics but changes nothing about the standard of work. Lawrence
matters sit within the firm's statewide [Kansas practice](/locations/kansas/),
so a case that adds venues, parties, or related filings elsewhere in the state
remains with the same expert and the same file conventions. Disclosure
deadlines, deposition notices, and trial settings are collected at intake, and
the written opinion is scheduled to be complete before testimony is required.

## Meetings by appointment only

The firm keeps no office in Lawrence and does not pretend otherwise; no street
address is published for this location because honesty about presence is part
of the discipline the reports are built on. Meetings are by appointment, at
counsel's offices, at agreed professional space, or in the evaluee's own
community. Remote evaluation is available whenever the referral questions and
the testing protocols permit it, and most records conferences, attorney
meetings, and pre-deposition preparation sessions are handled by
videoconference. When in-person evaluation is warranted, or when a deposition
or trial date requires presence, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home
office against the court's calendar.

## Reading the Lawrence labor market

Earning capacity findings for Lawrence evaluees are grounded in Bureau of
Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Lawrence
metropolitan area and for Kansas statewide, in state workforce agency labor
market information, and in direct labor market research when the referral
calls for it. The report defines the evaluee's commutable labor market
explicitly and explains the choice, and every wage or employment figure that
appears in the analysis carries its source. The goal is an opinion whose
foundation opposing counsel can inspect line by line and still find intact.

## Opening an engagement

Lawrence counsel engage the same national practice that handles referrals
[nationwide](/locations/nationwide/), with the same methodology and reporting
standards in every venue. To begin, submit the caption or party names through
the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and identify any imminent
disclosure, deposition, mediation, or trial dates. Once the conflict check
clears, counsel receive a defined scope and a records checklist tailored to
the claims, and any venue-specific qualification requirement is identified and
confirmed during the intake process.
