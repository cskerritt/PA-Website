Wichita counsel retain Purinton Analytics across the full scope of the
practice: [vocational evaluation and expert testimony](/services/vocational-expert-witness/)
on employability and earning capacity,
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) for catastrophic injury,
[medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/),
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) of opposing expert
reports, and [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis
while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement
structure. The same written methodology governs work for plaintiff and defense
alike, and every report is prepared on the assumption that opposing counsel
will read it closely.

## Courts served across the Wichita metro

Engagements are accepted for matters pending in the District Court of Sedgwick
County and in the district courts of Butler County, Harvey County, and Sumner
County. Federal matters proceed in the United States District Court for the
District of Kansas. The metro work operates within the firm's broader
[Kansas practice](/locations/kansas/), so counsel whose cases move between
Wichita and other venues in the state work with the same expert, the same file
conventions, and the same reporting standards throughout. Deposition and trial
settings are confirmed at intake, and the report timeline is set against the
operative disclosure calendar rather than left open-ended.

## By-appointment meetings in Wichita

Wichita meetings are by appointment. The firm keeps no staffed office in the
city and publishes no street address for this location, because accuracy about
presence is part of the same discipline that governs its written opinions.
Evaluations are scheduled at counsel's offices, at agreed professional space,
or in the evaluee's own community, and remote evaluation is available when the
referral questions and the testing protocols permit it. When the work calls
for in-person evaluation, or when deposition and trial dates require presence,
travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office. Records conferences and
pre-deposition preparation are ordinarily handled by videoconference, so that
in-person time is reserved for the steps that genuinely require it.

## Labor market research for Wichita evaluees

Employability and earning capacity findings for Wichita evaluees rest on
Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the
Wichita metropolitan area and for Kansas statewide, on state workforce agency
labor market information, and on direct labor market research where the
referral warrants it. Each report identifies the evaluee's commutable labor
market and explains why that market was selected, so the foundation of the
opinion is visible to the court, to retaining counsel, and to the opposing
expert. Transferable skills analysis follows the same documented sequence in
every case, whichever party has made the referral.

## Starting a Wichita engagement

The practice serves counsel [nationwide](/locations/nationwide/) from its
Kansas City home office, and a Wichita matter proceeds on the same terms as
any other engagement. Submit the caption or party names through the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and note any imminent disclosure,
deposition, or trial dates. Once the conflict check clears, counsel receive a
defined scope, a records checklist tailored to the claims, and a schedule
built backward from the dates that control the case. Questions about
qualification or venue-specific requirements are identified and confirmed
during the conflict check and intake process, before any opinion work begins.
