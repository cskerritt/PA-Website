The question a Newton referral usually asks is deceptively short: can this
person still work, and at what? Answering it defensibly takes more than a
transferable-skills printout. Purinton Analytics starts from the medical
restrictions as documented, maps them against the evaluee's actual work
history, and only then turns to the labor market data, so the conclusion is
an argument that can be examined step by step rather than an assertion.
Plaintiff and defense counsel retain the firm on identical terms for
[vocational expert testimony](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Where Newton matters are heard

Newton lies in Harvey County, and civil claims arising under Kansas law
proceed there in the District Court. For matters with a federal basis, the
United States District Court for the District of Kansas is the forum
available in the state; which court controls a given case, and any
venue-specific qualification requirement that follows from it, is
identified and confirmed during the conflict check and intake. Because the
same written opinion may be tested before either bench, the firm does not
write differently for different venues: each report carries a replicable
methodology, complete source citations, and conclusions confined to the
disclosed scope. Regional context appears on the
[Wichita metro page](/locations/kansas/wichita/), and statewide coverage is
described on the [Kansas page](/locations/kansas/).

## Reading the labor market from Harvey County

Newton is a modest-sized community whose residents participate in a labor
market that extends well beyond Harvey County. A credible vocational
opinion has to respect both facts at once: the local employment base the
evaluee can actually reach, and the metro market that shapes wages and
openings. The firm's findings rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates covering the Wichita, KS
metropolitan area and Kansas as a whole, on labor market information
published by the state workforce agency, and on targeted employer research
where the referral questions warrant it. Each report distinguishes what the
metro data shows from what the statewide data shows, and explains why the
chosen frame fits this evaluee.

## Meeting the evaluee

There is no Purinton Analytics office in Newton; meetings happen by
appointment, and saying so plainly is part of the practice's credibility.
Most of the work travels well over secure video: records analysis,
attorney conferences, and the majority of vocational interviews. Where the
case calls for an in-person evaluation, the assessment is conducted in the
evaluee's own community, with travel arranged from the Kansas City home
office, so that function is observed in the setting where the person
actually lives and would actually seek work. Deposition and trial
appearances are scheduled the same way.
