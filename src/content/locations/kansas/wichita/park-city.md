## The courts behind the calendar

Those deadlines issue from identifiable benches. Park City is in Sedgwick
County, where Kansas state-law claims proceed in the District Court, and
the United States District Court for the District of Kansas is the federal
forum the state affords when a matter belongs in federal court. Which
venue governs a particular case, and any registration or qualification
requirement that travels with it, is confirmed during the conflict check
and intake. The firm writes one kind of report for all of them:
methodology stated openly, sources cited for verification, conclusions
held inside the disclosed scope. The regional venue landscape is mapped on
the [Wichita metro page](/locations/kansas/wichita/), and the state as a
whole on the [Kansas page](/locations/kansas/).

## Labor market evidence

Park City is a small community whose labor market is effectively the
metropolitan one, and the vocational question is what work remains
realistically available to the evaluee from here, given the documented
restrictions. The firm's answer rests on Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates, drawing on both the Wichita,
KS metropolitan area series and the Kansas statewide series, together with
state workforce agency labor market information and direct labor market
research when the referral requires it. Because the report must survive an
opposing expert working from the same public data, the analysis shows its
work: which series, which occupations, and why they fit this evaluee.

## Scheduling the evaluation

There is no Park City office; the firm meets by appointment and is candid
about that, because overstating presence would sit poorly next to reports
that demand precision from everyone else. Remote process carries most of
the engagement, with secure video for records conferences and the bulk of
vocational interviews. Where the case needs an in-person assessment, the
evaluation is held in the evaluee's own community, and travel, including
travel for deposition and trial testimony, is scheduled from the Kansas
City home office with the court's dates fixed first.
