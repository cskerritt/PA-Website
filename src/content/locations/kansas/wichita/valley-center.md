Remote-first is not a slogan at Purinton Analytics; it is how a Valley
Center engagement actually runs, and it is why a small Sedgwick County
community gets the same expert bench a major city would. Records analysis,
counsel conferences, and most vocational interviews proceed over secure
video, which compresses timelines and holds costs proportionate to the
case. In-person work is reserved for the moments that need it, and it
happens where it should: in the evaluee's community or in the courtroom.
On those terms the firm serves plaintiff and defense counsel alike, in
[vocational expert engagements](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/),
[medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/), and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## County and courthouse

Valley Center lies in Sedgwick County, and state-law civil claims arising
there proceed in the District Court. For cases with a federal basis, the
United States District Court for the District of Kansas is the federal
forum available in the state; which court controls a particular matter,
together with any registration or qualification requirement a testifying
expert must satisfy there, is confirmed during the conflict check and
intake. The remote posture never loosens the written standard. Every
report is drafted for adversarial reading: methodology set out step by
step, sources cited for retrieval, opinions limited to the disclosed
scope. Regional venue coverage sits on the
[Wichita metro page](/locations/kansas/wichita/), and the statewide
framework on the [Kansas page](/locations/kansas/).

## Labor market grounding

However the interview is conducted, the labor market analysis is built the
same way. Valley Center residents work within a metropolitan economy, and
the operative question is what employment remains realistically open to
this evaluee from this community under the restrictions the medical record
supports. The firm answers with Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational
employment and wage estimates, working from the Wichita, KS metropolitan
area series and the Kansas statewide series, supplemented by state
workforce agency labor market information and by direct employer research
when the referral questions justify it. The report links each conclusion
to its source, because an opinion that cannot show its chain of reasoning
invites the cross-examination it deserves.

## Remote and in-person work

The firm keeps no office in Valley Center and says so without hedging;
meetings are by appointment. What remote process cannot do, in-person
process does: where the case calls for face-to-face evaluation, the
assessment is conducted in the evaluee's own community, with daily
function observed in context, and travel is scheduled from the Kansas City
home office. Depositions and trial testimony are attended in person
whenever counsel and the court require it, with travel planned against
dates fixed early in the engagement.
