Every opinion Purinton Analytics writes for a Wellington matter is
drafted on the assumption that its author will someday defend it under
oath. That assumption disciplines the engagement from the first records
request: nothing goes into the report that the expert would not repeat at
deposition, and nothing essential to the opinion is held back to be
improvised at trial. On those terms the firm is retained by plaintiff and
defense counsel alike, for
[vocational expert engagements](/services/vocational-expert-witness/)
addressing employability and earning capacity, for
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) in serious injury
litigation, and for
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Sumner County and the federal alternative

For a Wellington case, the state route runs to the District Court in
Sumner County, and the federal route, where jurisdiction supports it,
runs to the United States District Court for the District of Kansas, the
federal forum available within the state. The firm's written standard
does not change between those routes, but procedure does, so the
controlling venue, together with any registration or qualification
requirement it imposes on a testifying expert, is pinned down during the
conflict check and intake, before the work plan is built. What the routes
share is an eventual reader in a robe and an adversary with a copy of the
report, and the drafting anticipates both of them. Counsel weighing
venue questions across the region will find the metro treated as a whole
on the [Wichita metro page](/locations/kansas/wichita/) and the state
summarized on the [Kansas page](/locations/kansas/).

## A market opinion that survives cross-examination

The question that opens most vocational cross-examinations is where,
exactly, the evaluee is supposed to find this work. For a Wellington
evaluee the question has real content. The town is a small community in
Sumner County within the Wichita, KS metropolitan area, and whether the
realistic employment base is local, metropolitan, or a defensible blend
is a finding the expert must make and support, never a default. The firm
supports it with Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and
wage estimates, read across the Wichita, KS metropolitan area series and
the Kansas statewide series, with labor market information from the state
workforce agency, and with direct labor market research where the
referral questions justify it. The written opinion then walks the reader
from restriction to occupation to market, because that walk is precisely
the one cross-examination will force the expert to take aloud.

## Between retention and the witness stand

Between retention and any witness stand lies a long stretch of ordinary
work, and the firm is candid about where that work happens. There is no
Purinton Analytics office in Wellington; meetings are by appointment, and
the engagement is structured so an injured evaluee travels as little as
possible. Records are gathered and analyzed without anyone leaving home,
and the vocational interview is usually conducted remotely as well, under
conditions that preserve its rigor. If the referral calls for an
evaluation in person, the firm goes to the evaluee rather than the
reverse: the assessment takes place in the evaluee's own community, and
travel for that visit, and for the deposition and trial dates that may
follow, is scheduled from the Kansas City home office against the
calendar the court controls.

## The first commitment

The last commitment in a Wellington engagement may be testimony; the
first is always clearance. Whether the matter stays in Sumner County or
belongs to the national practice outlined on the
[nationwide page](/locations/nationwide/), counsel should
[request a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) with the case caption and the
parties, and should name the disclosure deadline, deposition window, or
trial setting up front, because a testimony commitment made at retention
is only worth what the calendar behind it will bear.
