In an Augusta case, the retaining party changes; the opinion does not.
Purinton Analytics is engaged by plaintiff firms and defense firms in
comparable measure, and the methodology, the sources, and the willingness
to state limits are identical no matter which side sends the engagement
letter. That symmetry is a practical asset for Augusta counsel: an expert
whose conclusions do not bend toward the client is harder to impeach.
Referrals here typically involve
[vocational expert opinions](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) on
employability and earning capacity,
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) after serious injury,
and [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Augusta's venue context

Augusta sits in Butler County, and civil claims under Kansas law proceed
there in the District Court. For matters with a federal footing, the
United States District Court for the District of Kansas is the forum
available within the state; the venue that controls a specific case, along
with any qualification or registration requirement it imposes on a
testifying expert, is identified and confirmed during the conflict check
and intake. Since either side may later move the fight to a different
bench than the one anticipated, the firm writes to a standard that
satisfies both: methods a rival expert can reproduce, citations a paralegal
can pull, and no opinion outside the disclosed scope. The wider region is
covered on the [Wichita metro page](/locations/kansas/wichita/) and the
state on the [Kansas page](/locations/kansas/).

## The labor market on both sides

Whether the retaining party wants earning capacity shown to be diminished
or preserved, the data source is the same. The firm works from Bureau of
Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the
Wichita, KS metropolitan area and for Kansas statewide, from state
workforce agency labor market information, and from direct employer
research where the questions demand it. For an Augusta evaluee the
analysis must be honest about geography: Butler County offers a real but
limited employment base, and access to the larger metro market depends on
the evaluee's restrictions, transportation, and history. The report states
which market frame was applied and defends the choice explicitly.

## Evaluating an Augusta resident

The firm maintains no Augusta office and does not imply one; meetings are
by appointment. Most engagements proceed remotely, with secure video
carrying records conferences, counsel discussions, and the majority of
vocational interviews. When an in-person evaluation is justified, it is
performed in the evaluee's own community, where actual daily function can
be observed, and travel is arranged from the Kansas City home office. The
same arrangements govern deposition and trial appearances, planned around
the dates the court has already set.
