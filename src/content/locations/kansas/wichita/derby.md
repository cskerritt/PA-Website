Derby is one of the larger suburban communities in the Wichita metropolitan
area, and its injured workers and accident victims tend to have working
lives that span the whole region rather than the town limits. That is the
frame Purinton Analytics brings to a Derby referral: the evaluee lives in
Derby, but the employability question is answered against the metro
commuting market. Counsel on either side of the caption retain the firm for
[vocational expert opinions](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) on earning
capacity and employability, for [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/)
in catastrophic injury matters, and for
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Sedgwick County venue and courts

Derby sits in Sedgwick County, and a state-law claim arising there proceeds
in the District Court. Where federal jurisdiction exists, the United States
District Court for the District of Kansas is the forum available within the
state; the controlling venue for a particular matter, along with any
registration or qualification requirement it carries, is identified during
the conflict check and intake. The firm writes every report to be read in
either setting, with a methodology section an opposing expert can retrace
and sources cited precisely enough to be pulled and checked. Counsel
working across the region will find the broader venue picture on the
[Wichita metro page](/locations/kansas/wichita/) and the statewide summary
on the [Kansas page](/locations/kansas/).

## Derby in the Wichita labor market

A vocational opinion for a Derby evaluee that treats the town as a closed
labor market would be wrong, and one that treats the metro as uniformly
accessible would be careless. The analysis has to say what work is
realistically open to this person, with these restrictions and this work
history, from this community. To ground that answer, the firm relies on
Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for
the Wichita, KS metropolitan area and for Kansas statewide, supplemented by
state workforce agency labor market information and by direct labor market
research when the referral questions call for it. The report explains the
reasoning that connects those sources to the evaluee, so the opinion
withstands the obvious cross-examination rather than inviting it.

## Evaluations for Derby residents

The firm keeps no office in Derby and does not pretend otherwise; accuracy
about presence is part of the same discipline that governs the reports.
Records review, conferences with counsel, and most vocational interviews
proceed by secure video, which shortens timelines and reduces cost for both
sides. When the referral requires in-person assessment, or when a
deposition or trial setting requires the expert in the room, travel is
scheduled from the Kansas City home office, and the evaluation takes place
in the evaluee's own community, where daily function and actual commuting
realities can be observed rather than assumed.
