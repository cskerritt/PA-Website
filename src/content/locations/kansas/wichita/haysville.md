## Venue: Sedgwick County

Haysville lies in Sedgwick County, so a Kansas state-law claim arising here
proceeds in the District Court. Federal-question and diversity matters have
the United States District Court for the District of Kansas available as
the state's federal forum; the venue that actually controls a given case,
and any registration or qualification requirement tied to it, is
identified during the conflict check and intake. The firm's written work
does not change with the courthouse: methodology is disclosed, sources are
cited to be checked, and the opinion stays within the scope stated in the
engagement. For the regional venue picture, see the
[Wichita metro page](/locations/kansas/wichita/); for statewide coverage,
the [Kansas page](/locations/kansas/).

## Haysville and the metro labor market

Haysville is a small suburb inside a substantial metropolitan employment
market, and the gap between those scales is where imprecise vocational
opinions break down. What matters is the work realistically available to
this evaluee, from this community, under these documented restrictions.
The firm anchors that inquiry in Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational
employment and wage estimates for the Wichita, KS metropolitan area and
for Kansas statewide, in state workforce agency labor market information,
and in direct labor market research when the referral questions call for
it. Every conclusion is tied back to its data source in the report itself,
so opposing counsel can test the chain rather than guess at it.

## The evaluation itself

The firm keeps no office in Haysville and claims none; that candor is
deliberate. A typical engagement runs remote-first: records are reviewed
and discussed by secure video, and most vocational interviews are
conducted the same way, which spares the evaluee travel and keeps costs
proportionate. Where an in-person evaluation is warranted, it takes place
in the evaluee's own community, so daily function is observed where the
person actually lives. Travel for evaluations, depositions, and trial
testimony is scheduled from the Kansas City home office and planned around
the court's calendar, not the other way around.
