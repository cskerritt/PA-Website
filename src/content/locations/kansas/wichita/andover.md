state-law claim will proceed even though the evaluee's economic life is
lived across the Wichita metropolitan area. Purinton Analytics treats that

## Butler County courts

State-court matters arising in Andover proceed in the District Court in
Butler County. Where a claim has a federal basis, the United States
District Court for the District of Kansas is the available federal forum in
the state, and the controlling venue for any particular matter, together
with whatever registration or qualification requirement attaches to it, is
identified during the conflict check and intake. The firm's reports are
drafted to hold up wherever the matter lands: methodology stated so an
opposing expert can retrace it, sources cited so they can be pulled, and
opinions kept inside the scope counsel disclosed. Venue context for the
region as a whole is collected on the
[Wichita metro page](/locations/kansas/wichita/) and the
[Kansas page](/locations/kansas/).

## A Butler County address in a metro labor market

For employability purposes, Andover cannot be analyzed as an island. Its
residents work throughout the metropolitan area, and a defensible opinion
has to describe the work realistically reachable from this community by
this evaluee, given the documented restrictions. The firm grounds those
findings in Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage
estimates for the Wichita, KS metropolitan area and for the state of
Kansas, in state workforce agency labor market information, and in direct
labor market research when the questions presented require it. The written
report walks through the connection between data and conclusion, because a
number without reasoning is exactly what cross-examination is for.

## Evaluation logistics

Andover meetings are by appointment; the firm maintains no local office and
publishes no street address here, because being precise about presence is
part of being precise generally. In practice the engagement runs
remote-first. Records review and counsel conferences proceed by secure
video, and vocational interviews are usually conducted the same way. When
the referral calls for in-person evaluation, the assessment happens in the
evaluee's own community rather than in a distant office, with travel
scheduled from the Kansas City home office, and the same travel practice
covers depositions and trial testimony.
