When the other side's expert report lands in an El Dorado file, counsel
needs to know whether it will survive scrutiny, and the fastest way to
find out is to apply that scrutiny before the deposition does. Purinton
Analytics offers exactly that service: a
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) engagement
examines an opposing expert's methodology, data, and inferences against
the same standards the firm applies to its own affirmative opinions, and
delivers a critique counsel can put to work in cross-examination. The
affirmative side of the practice covers
[vocational expert opinions](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) on
employability and earning capacity,
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) after catastrophic
injury, and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. Plaintiff and defense firms alike retain the
practice, and a review reads the same no matter which side requested it.

## Where the critique gets tested

Any opinion in an El Dorado matter, the firm's or an opponent's, will face
the same benches. Civil claims under Kansas law belong to the District
Court sitting in Butler County; a matter proceeding on federal
jurisdiction has the United States District Court for the District of
Kansas as the federal forum available within the state. Which court
controls a given case, and what registration or qualification requirement
attaches to a testifying expert there, is settled during the conflict
check and intake rather than assumed. Venue matters to a rebuttal
assignment for a practical reason: a critique is only useful if it would
persuade the judge who actually rules on the challenge, so the firm writes
its reviews, like its affirmative reports, as documents fit for the record
rather than advocacy memos. Neighboring venue context is collected on the
[Wichita metro page](/locations/kansas/wichita/), with the statewide
picture on the [Kansas page](/locations/kansas/).

## The market-frame question

The weakness a reviewer looks for first in a vocational report is the
labor market frame. El Dorado is a small city with an employment base of
its own, set within the Wichita, KS metropolitan area, and an opinion that
silently confines the evaluee to Butler County, or just as silently grants
access to the entire metro, has skipped a finding that must be made on
evidence. In its own work the firm makes that finding openly. The
evaluee's documented restrictions, work history, and transportation
determine the realistic market, and the market is then described through
Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for
the Wichita, KS metropolitan area and for Kansas statewide, state
workforce agency labor market information, and direct employer research
where the referral questions require it. A report that shows this
reasoning is hard to attack; a report that hides it invites the attack.

## Presence, candidly

Purinton Analytics keeps no office in El Dorado, and this page will not
suggest otherwise; meetings happen by appointment. Little of an engagement
genuinely requires the expert in the room. The reading, the analysis, and
most conversations with counsel and with the evaluee happen over secure
remote connections, which protects the schedule and the budget on both
sides of the caption. What does require presence gets it without
shortcuts: an in-person evaluation is conducted in the evaluee's own
community, where function can be observed where the person actually
lives, and attendance at deposition or trial is planned with travel from
the Kansas City home office once the court's dates are fixed.
