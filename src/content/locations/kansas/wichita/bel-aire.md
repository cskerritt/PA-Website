Purinton Analytics has no office in Bel Aire, and this page will not
pretend otherwise. What Bel Aire counsel get instead of a storefront is a
disciplined national practice that treats a small Sedgwick County community
with the same rigor as a major city: a documented conflict check, a defined
scope, records requested against the actual claims, and a written opinion
built to be examined by the other side. Both plaintiff and defense firms
retain the practice here, principally for
[vocational expert opinions](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## County and court context

Because Bel Aire is in Sedgwick County, its state-law civil docket runs
through the District Court. The United States District Court for the
District of Kansas stands as the federal forum available in the state for
matters that belong there; determining which court controls a specific
case, and satisfying any venue-linked registration or qualification
requirement, is part of the conflict check and intake rather than an
afterthought. One consequence of writing for adversarial review is that
the firm's reports read the same regardless of forum: transparent method,
verifiable sources, and no opinion beyond the disclosed scope. Broader
regional venue coverage appears on the
[Wichita metro page](/locations/kansas/wichita/) and statewide detail on
the [Kansas page](/locations/kansas/).

## Labor market research

Bel Aire's working residents are participants in the metropolitan economy,
so the employability inquiry is regional even when the caption is local.
The firm's vocational findings draw on the Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates, using the series for the
Wichita, KS metropolitan area alongside the Kansas statewide series, and
supplement that federal data with state workforce agency labor market
information and, where warranted, direct employer research. What
distinguishes a durable opinion is not the data, which both sides can
obtain, but the disclosed reasoning that connects the data to this
particular evaluee's restrictions, education, and work history. That
reasoning appears in the report, in full.

## Logistics for Bel Aire evaluees

Meetings are by appointment. The engagement ordinarily proceeds remotely:
secure video handles records conferences, attorney communication, and most
vocational interviews without sacrificing rigor, and it spares an injured
evaluee unnecessary travel while discovery is still moving. When an assessment should
be conducted in person, the firm evaluates in the evaluee's own community,
observing capacity and daily function in context rather than in an
unfamiliar office. Travel for in-person evaluations, depositions, and
trial appearances is arranged from the Kansas City home office, scheduled
against the litigation calendar counsel identifies at intake.
