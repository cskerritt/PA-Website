When a case involves an evaluee who lives in Baldwin City, the practical question counsel asks first is rarely about methodology; it is about logistics. How does a national expert practice evaluate someone in a small Kansas town without burning weeks of the disclosure calendar on scheduling? Purinton Analytics has built its workflow around that question. Records arrive electronically, vocational interviews are conducted by secure video at times that suit the evaluee, and when an in-person assessment is warranted it takes place in the evaluee's own community rather than requiring a trip to a distant office. Retention is open to plaintiff and defense counsel alike, and the report that results is written to withstand scrutiny from whichever side did not commission it.

## The county and courts behind a Baldwin City case

Baldwin City sits in Douglas County, and state-court matters arising there proceed in the District Court serving Douglas County. Baldwin City matters with a federal basis proceed in the United States District Court for the District of Kansas. Which court controls a given dispute is confirmed during the conflict check and intake, together with any qualification or registration requirement particular to the venue. Deadlines drive everything in expert work, so intake also captures the scheduling order: disclosure dates, discovery cutoffs, and any deposition or trial settings that will shape when the report must be complete. The working file is assembled the same way for either forum, beginning with a documented conflict check, a defined scope, and a records request tailored to the claims.

## Reading a small-town evaluee against the metro market

An earning capacity analysis for a Baldwin City resident cannot stop at the town line. The firm frames the evaluee's employment prospects against the [Lawrence metropolitan area](/locations/kansas/lawrence/), using Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for that metro and for Kansas as a whole, alongside state workforce agency labor market information and, where the questions call for it, direct contact with employers. The analysis weighs what a smaller community can offer locally against what becomes available with a reasonable commute, and it documents how the evaluee's restrictions, education, and work history interact with that geography. Nothing in the opinion rests on an unstated assumption about where jobs are; every source is cited so opposing counsel can check the work.

## Appointments, travel, and the life care planning side

The firm maintains its single home office in Kansas City and keeps no storefront or street address in Baldwin City, Lawrence, or anywhere else; saying so plainly is part of the credibility the reports depend on. In-person needs, whether an evaluation, a deposition, or trial testimony, are met by travel scheduled from the Kansas City home office and calendared at intake against the deadlines the scheduling order imposes, so that presence is planned rather than improvised. Life care planning engagements follow the same by-appointment model, and when the damages picture requires economic projections, counsel can retain [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement structure.
