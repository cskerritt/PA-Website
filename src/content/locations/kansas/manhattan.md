The Manhattan metro reaches across Riley County, Pottawatomie County, and
Geary County, and counsel practicing in each of those district courts can
retain Purinton Analytics for
[vocational evaluation and expert testimony](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/),
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/), and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis
while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement
structure. Plaintiff and defense referrals proceed under the same written
methodology, because an opinion that changes with the retaining party is not
an opinion a court should rely on.

## District and federal courts

State-court engagements in the metro are accepted for matters in the District
Court of Riley County, the District Court of Pottawatomie County, and the
District Court of Geary County. Federal matters proceed in the United States
District Court for the District of Kansas. Manhattan work is part of the
firm's wider [Kansas practice](/locations/kansas/), so counsel handling
related matters in other venues across the state deal with the same expert
file and a consistent reporting standard. At intake the firm collects the
disclosure calendar and any noticed deposition or trial dates, and the report
schedule is built backward from those dates rather than left to drift.

## How meetings and evaluations work here

Manhattan meetings are by appointment. The firm maintains no staffed office in
the metro and publishes no local street address, because candor about presence
belongs to the same standard of accuracy the reports are held to. What counsel
get instead is a practice built for distance: remote evaluation when the
referral questions and testing protocols permit it, evaluations conducted in
the evaluee's own community when they do not, and records conferences and
pre-deposition preparation by videoconference. When testimony or in-person
evaluation requires presence, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home
office and coordinated with counsel's deadlines.

## Labor market evidence for Manhattan evaluees

Employability and earning capacity opinions for evaluees in the metro rest on
Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the
Manhattan metropolitan area and for Kansas statewide, supplemented by state
workforce agency labor market information and by direct labor market research
where the referral warrants it. The report states which labor market the
evaluee can reasonably reach and why, then ties every wage and employment
conclusion to a cited source. That transparency is deliberate: the analysis is
written so that the opposing expert can trace each step, and so that
cross-examination tests the evaluee's actual capacity rather than the
expert's paperwork.

## Requesting a conflict check

The practice accepts Manhattan referrals on the same footing as engagements
[nationwide](/locations/nationwide/). To begin, send the caption or the party
names through the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and note any imminent
disclosure, deposition, or trial settings. When the conflict check clears,
counsel receive a defined scope, a records checklist tailored to the claims,
and a firm schedule. Any venue-specific registration or qualification
requirement is identified and confirmed during the conflict check and intake
process.
