deadline, a deposition setting, a mediation, or a trial docket that fixes
when the written opinion must exist. Purinton Analytics builds Olathe
engagements backward from that calendar, so that the
[vocational evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) or

researched, drafted, and reviewed on schedule rather than assembled against

## Courts serving Olathe

Olathe lies in Johnson County, and state-court personal injury, employment,
and disability matters arising here are generally heard in the District
Court sitting in Johnson County. Where a caption is federal, the United
States District Court for the District of Kansas is the forum the state
offers; which court controls a particular matter, and which disclosure and
discovery schedule follows from it, is confirmed during the conflict check
and intake process. That schedule then governs the engagement: report
deadlines, supplementation, and deposition preparation are all set against
it. The surrounding regional practice is described on the
[Kansas City metro page](/locations/missouri/kansas-city/), and work across
the rest of the state on the [Kansas page](/locations/kansas/).

## Olathe in the commuting market

Olathe is a substantial city in its own right, and its workers move through
a labor market that extends well beyond it. Vocational findings for Olathe
evaluees rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and
wage estimates for the Kansas City metropolitan area and for Kansas
statewide, together with state workforce agency labor market information
and, where the referral questions require it, direct contact with employers.
The report identifies the occupations genuinely open to the evaluee within
a demonstrated commuting range and explains the basis for each conclusion,
because a list of job titles without reasoning is exactly what
cross-examination is for. When counsel need loss quantification as well,
the firm offers
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Evaluation logistics for Olathe evaluees

There is no Purinton Analytics storefront in Olathe and no published street
address for this location. The firm's home office is elsewhere in the same
metro, which keeps logistics simple: in-person vocational and life care
planning evaluations are scheduled by appointment in professional space
convenient to the evaluee, counsel meetings and pre-deposition sessions can
be arranged on short notice, and remote evaluation is available when the
referral questions and testing protocols permit it. Counsel are told at
intake what the calendar can absorb before deadlines make the answer
expensive.

## Retaining the firm for an Olathe matter

Whether the engagement is for the party asserting the loss or the party
testing it, the opening step is a documented conflict check with a defined
scope. Use the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) to send the caption or
party names, summarize the referral questions, and list every disclosure,
deposition, and trial date already fixed. The firm replies with scope and
fee terms, and the resulting report is written to be read by opposing
counsel.
