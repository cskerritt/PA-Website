## A distinct community inside a large market

Leavenworth is a smaller city than the metro's suburban giants, and its
evaluees often present mixed work histories: some careers built locally,
others carried on a commute toward the metro's employment centers. The
vocational analysis takes that seriously. Findings rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Kansas City
metropolitan area and for Kansas statewide, together with state workforce
agency labor market information and direct labor market research where the
referral questions warrant it. The report defines the evaluee's commutable
market from the record, then explains which occupations within that market
remain realistic given residual capacity, credentials, and transportation,
so the conclusion can be examined instead of merely announced. Where
counsel also need the loss reduced to numbers, the firm coordinates
[forensic economic damages](/services/forensic-economic-damages/) analyses
performed and signed by independent economist partners; the vocational
opinion and the economic opinion stay in separate, properly credentialed
hands.
