## Review work and affirmative work, same standard

A rebuttal engagement here follows the same discipline as an affirmative
one: the opposing report is tested against its own cited sources, its
occupational conclusions are checked against the labor market data, and
the written critique is confined to what the record supports. The point is
not advocacy but audit, which is why the same analysis serves whichever
side commissions it. Affirmative engagements, in turn, are drafted knowing
that a reviewer of equal care may be reading on the other side.

## Earning capacity in a metro-wide market

Leawood evaluees participate in a labor market far larger than the city
itself, and disputes over earning capacity turn on defining that market
honestly. The analysis rests on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational
employment and wage estimates for the Kansas City metropolitan area and
for Kansas statewide, with state workforce agency labor market information
and direct employer research where the referral questions require it. The
report states which market governs this evaluee and why, and treats each
occupational alternative as a claim needing support. When quantification
of the loss is also required, counsel may add a
[coordinated damages engagement](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.
