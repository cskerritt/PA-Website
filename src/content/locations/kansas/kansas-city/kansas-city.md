## Wyandotte County venue context

State-court injury, employment, and disability matters arising in Kansas
City, Kansas are generally heard in the District Court sitting in Wyandotte
County. On the federal side, the United States District Court for the
District of Kansas is the forum the state offers, and the controlling venue
for a given caption, together with its expert disclosure schedule, is
identified during the conflict check and intake process. Because so much
litigation in this metro crosses the state line, the firm's practice is built

## The evaluee's market, not the metro's average

A vocational opinion about a Kansas City, Kansas worker begins with the work
history in the record and the commuting pattern it demonstrates. The analysis
draws on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage
estimates for the Kansas City metropolitan area and for Kansas statewide,
along with state workforce agency labor market information and direct labor
market research where warranted. The report then explains which portion of
the metro market is realistically open to this evaluee, given the residual
capacities, credentials, and transportation facts the record supports,
instead of averaging the metro and calling it an answer. Where the referral
also requires present-value calculation of losses, the firm coordinates
[forensic economic damages](/services/forensic-economic-damages/) analyses
that are performed and signed by independent economist partners; Jason C.
Purinton does not issue economist opinions.
