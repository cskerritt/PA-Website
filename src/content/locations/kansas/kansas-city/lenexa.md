Retaining a national expert usually means budgeting for travel; retaining
Purinton Analytics for a Lenexa matter does not. The firm's home office is
in the Kansas City metro, so the distance between counsel and expert is a
calendar entry rather than a flight itinerary: evaluation appointments,
records conferences, and pre-deposition preparation are scheduled the way
local work should be. Lenexa counsel on either side of the caption engage
the firm for [vocational expert witness](/services/vocational-expert-witness/)
work and [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) under the
same methodology the practice applies everywhere, described on its
[nationwide page](/locations/nationwide/).

## What proximity does and does not mean

Proximity does not mean a Lenexa office. The firm keeps no storefront here
and publishes no street address for this location, because candor about
presence belongs to the same discipline that governs its reports. What
proximity does mean is practical: in-person vocational and life care
planning evaluations arranged by appointment in professional space
convenient to the evaluee, short lead times when a disclosure deadline or
deposition setting moves, and remote evaluation whenever the referral
questions and testing protocols make it appropriate. Every engagement
still begins with a documented conflict check and a defined scope, because
convenience never substitutes for process.

## Johnson County courts and the federal alternative

Lenexa is a Johnson County city, and state-court injury, employment, and
disability matters arising here are generally heard in the District Court
sitting in Johnson County. The United States District Court for the
District of Kansas is the federal forum the state offers; whether a
particular caption belongs there is a question for counsel, with the
controlling venue and its expert disclosure schedule confirmed during the
conflict check and intake process. Regional coverage is set out on the
[Kansas City metro page](/locations/missouri/kansas-city/), and the rest of
the state on the [Kansas page](/locations/kansas/).

## Grounding the opinion in the metro market

A Lenexa evaluee's realistic alternatives are rarely confined to Lenexa.
Vocational findings therefore rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates for the Kansas City metropolitan
area and for Kansas statewide, alongside state workforce agency labor
market information and direct employer research where the referral
questions require it. Because the commutable market spans the state line,
the written report says which market applies to this evaluee and why, and
it treats every occupational alternative as a conclusion to be defended
rather than a placeholder. When the presentation of damages also calls for
economic quantification, counsel may add a
[coordinated damages engagement](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Opening a Lenexa engagement

Plaintiff and defense referrals begin identically: a documented conflict
check, a defined scope, and a records request tailored to the claims. Send
the caption or party names through the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/), summarize the referral questions,
and identify any dates already fixed for disclosure, deposition, or trial.
The firm responds with scope and fee terms, and the report that follows is
written to withstand the reader who was not the retaining party.
