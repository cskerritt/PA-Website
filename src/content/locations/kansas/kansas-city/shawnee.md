For a Shawnee evaluee, the vocational evaluation is usually the part of the
lawsuit that happens closest to home: an interview, standardized testing
where the protocol calls for it, and a careful walk through work history,
education, and daily function. Purinton Analytics conducts that evaluation
in the evaluee's own community by appointment, and builds the
[vocational expert](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) or
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) opinion on what the
record and the evaluation actually show. Plaintiff and defense counsel are
served on identical terms, under the methodology described on the firm's
[nationwide page](/locations/nationwide/).

## How a Shawnee evaluation proceeds

The firm keeps no storefront in Shawnee and publishes no street address for
this location. Because the home office is in the Kansas City metro,
in-person evaluation is arranged in appropriate professional space
convenient to the evaluee, without air travel or extended scheduling lead
time, and remote evaluation is available when the referral questions and
testing protocols permit it. Records review precedes the appointment, so
the interview tests the record rather than substituting for it, and
counsel on the retaining side receive a report whose factual basis is
documented page by page. Where the medical file leaves a functional
question open, the report says so and explains what additional
documentation would resolve it, rather than papering over the gap.

## Venue: Johnson County and the District of Kansas

Shawnee sits in Johnson County. State-court injury, employment, and
disability claims arising here are generally heard in the District Court
sitting in Johnson County, and the United States District Court for the
District of Kansas is the federal forum the state offers; the controlling
venue for any given caption, and the expert disclosure schedule that comes
with it, is identified during the conflict check and intake process.
Matters elsewhere in the region are addressed on the
[Kansas City metro page](/locations/missouri/kansas-city/), and statewide
work on the [Kansas page](/locations/kansas/).

## The labor market a Shawnee worker actually faces

Shawnee is a suburban community whose residents work throughout the metro,
and the analysis reflects that. Employability and earning-capacity findings
rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage
estimates for the Kansas City metropolitan area and for Kansas statewide,
supported by state workforce agency labor market information and direct
labor market research where warranted. The report maps transferable skills
to occupations available within the evaluee's demonstrated commuting range
and states plainly why each alternative is or is not realistic, because
qualitative candor holds up better under cross-examination than borrowed
precision. Where losses must also be reduced to present value, counsel may
add a
[coordinated damages engagement](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Beginning a Shawnee engagement

The engagement starts the same way for either side of the caption: a
documented conflict check, a defined scope, and a records request tailored
to the claims. Submit the caption or party names through the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/), describe the referral questions, and
note any imminent disclosure, deposition, or trial dates. The firm answers
with a scope proposal and fee terms, and the opinion that results follows
the record wherever it leads.
