## Johnson County and the federal forum

Overland Park sits in Johnson County, and state-court injury, employment, and
disability matters arising there are generally heard in the District Court
sitting in Johnson County. For federal captions, the United States District
Court for the District of Kansas is the forum the state offers; the
controlling venue for any particular matter is confirmed during the conflict
check and intake process, along with the expert disclosure schedule that
governs it. Counsel working across the wider metro will find that footprint
described on the [Kansas City metro page](/locations/missouri/kansas-city/),
and statewide coverage on the [Kansas page](/locations/kansas/).

## Where Overland Park sits in the metro labor market

Employability and earning-capacity findings for Overland Park evaluees are
grounded in Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage
estimates for the Kansas City metropolitan area and for Kansas statewide,
supplemented by state workforce agency labor market information and direct
employer research where the referral questions call for it. Because the
commutable market crosses the state line, the written report is explicit
about which market defines the evaluee's realistic options and why.
Transferable skills are mapped to occupations actually available within the
evaluee's demonstrated commuting pattern, and each alternative is explained
rather than assumed. When the damages presentation also requires economic
quantification, counsel may add a
[coordinated damages engagement](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.
