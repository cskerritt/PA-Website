A smaller venue deserves the same expert discipline as a major one. That
principle is why Purinton Analytics accepts engagements in the Dodge City,
KS micropolitan area on the same terms, and to the same written standard, as
it does across its [national practice](/locations/nationwide/). Counsel in
Ford County retain the firm for
[vocational evaluation and expert testimony](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/), in plaintiff
and defense engagements alike, with every opinion written on the assumption
that opposing counsel will read it closely.

## Courts serving Ford County matters

Engagements are accepted for cases in the District Court of Ford County and
in the United States District Court for the District of Kansas. Each file
opens with a documented conflict check, a scope defined in writing, and a
records request tailored to the claims, so that the resulting opinion rests
on a record either side of the caption can examine. Where the damages
analysis requires an economist, the firm structures
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds within the
same engagement structure. The [Kansas practice page](/locations/kansas/)
describes the firm's statewide venue coverage.

## By-appointment service, honestly described

The firm keeps no staffed office in Dodge City and publishes no street
address for this location. That is stated plainly because accuracy about
presence belongs to the same discipline that governs the reports themselves.
What counsel get instead is a remote-first engagement: intake, records
review, drafting, and conferences proceed by phone and secure exchange on a
schedule fixed at the outset. When the referral questions call for an
in-person evaluation of the evaluee, or when a deposition or trial setting
requires attendance in Ford County, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City
home office and confirmed against the litigation calendar early, so a
Dodge City courtroom date never becomes a logistical surprise.

## Reading the Dodge City labor market

Earning capacity opinions for evaluees in the Dodge City area are grounded
in Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for
Kansas, in state workforce agency labor market information, and in direct
labor market research when the record warrants it. In a smaller labor
market, the analysis has to test whether an evaluee's restrictions and
transferable skills fit the work that is genuinely available, not the work
a statewide table abstractly lists.
The report shows that reasoning step by step, with every source cited, so
the opinion can be replicated by a reviewing expert on either side.

## Opening a Ford County engagement

Begin with the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/): submit the caption or
party names and note any imminent disclosure, deposition, or trial dates.
Once conflicts clear, the firm issues an engagement letter with a defined
scope, sends a tailored records request, and commits to a reporting timeline
built around the court's deadlines. Counsel who need an adverse report examined
rather than an affirmative opinion can request
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) of an opposing
expert's report through the same intake process.
