In a Garden City case, the labor market question often decides the damages
analysis: which jobs the evaluee can actually reach from Finney County, what
those jobs pay, and whether an opinion built on a distant market's assumptions
can withstand cross-examination. Purinton Analytics builds its
[vocational evaluations and expert testimony](/services/vocational-expert-witness/)
around that question from the outset, and adds
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/),
[medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/), and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis
while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement
structure. Plaintiff and defense referrals are accepted on identical terms.

## Venue and court coverage

State-court engagements in the Garden City area are accepted for matters in
the District Court of Finney County, and federal matters proceed in the United
States District Court for the District of Kansas. Garden City work is handled
within the firm's statewide [Kansas practice](/locations/kansas/), under the
same file conventions and reporting standards applied in every other venue,
so a report prepared for a Finney County caption reads the same way one
prepared for any other court does. At intake the firm collects the scheduling
order, disclosure deadlines, and any noticed deposition or trial dates, and
commits to a report timeline measured against them rather than left
open-ended.

## Appointments and travel logistics

The firm keeps no office in Garden City and publishes no street address for
this location; meetings are by appointment, and the practice is candid about
that because accuracy about presence reflects the same discipline that governs
its reports. Distance is managed deliberately. Records conferences, attorney
meetings, and pre-deposition preparation run by videoconference, and remote
evaluation is available when the referral questions and testing protocols
permit it. When the evaluation should be conducted in person, it is scheduled
in the evaluee's own community or at counsel's offices, with travel arranged
from the Kansas City home office, and testimony appearances are planned well
ahead against the court's calendar so that deposition and trial dates are
never left to chance.

## Data before opinion

Findings for Garden City evaluees rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates for Kansas statewide, including the
nonmetropolitan area estimates that cover Finney County, on state workforce
agency labor market information, and on direct labor market research where the
referral warrants it, including employer contact when published data leave a
genuine gap. The report defines the evaluee's commutable labor market
explicitly, defends the definition, and separates what the data show from what
the expert concludes, so the opinion's foundation is open to inspection by the
court and the opposing expert alike. That transparency serves both sides of
the caption: counsel who retained the firm can build on the analysis, and
counsel who oppose it can test every step without guessing at the method.
