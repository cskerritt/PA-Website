## Saline and Ottawa county venues

Engagements in the Salina area are accepted for matters in the District Court
of Saline County and the District Court of Ottawa County, with federal matters
proceeding in the United States District Court for the District of Kansas.
Salina work is one part of the firm's statewide [Kansas coverage](/locations/kansas/),
and cases that spread into other Kansas venues remain with the same expert and
the same file. Intake captures the scheduling order, any noticed depositions,
and the trial setting, and the engagement letter fixes a report timeline

hear.

## Presence, honestly described

The firm keeps no staffed office in Salina and publishes no street address
here; every meeting is by appointment, and the page says so because accuracy
about presence is part of the same discipline that governs the reports.
Early-stage work rarely requires travel at all: records conferences, attorney
meetings, and pre-deposition preparation run by videoconference, and remote
evaluation is available when the referral questions and testing protocols
permit it. When the case calls for in-person evaluation, the firm comes to the
evaluee's community or to counsel's offices, with travel scheduled from the
Kansas City home office, and testimony appearances are planned the same way.

## Wage and employment evidence for Salina evaluees

Vocational findings for Salina evaluees are grounded in Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for Kansas statewide,
including the nonmetropolitan area estimates covering Saline County and Ottawa
County, alongside state workforce agency labor market information and direct
labor market research where the referral requires it. The report defines the
evaluee's commutable labor market and explains why, and it distinguishes what
the data show from what the expert infers, so cross-examination can test the
inference without discovering the data were misstated.
