Testimony is where an expert opinion earns its keep, and it is the lens
through which Purinton Analytics approaches every Hays engagement. Reports
prepared for Ellis County litigation are written from the outset to be
defended at deposition and trial, whether the firm is retained by plaintiff
or defense counsel. The practice covers
[vocational evaluation and expert testimony](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/), and its
[expert testimony and litigation consulting](/services/expert-testimony-litigation-consulting/)
work extends to helping counsel prepare cross-examination of an opposing
expert as well as presenting the firm's own findings.

## Venue coverage for Ellis County

Engagements are accepted for matters in the District Court of Ellis County
and in the United States District Court for the District of Kansas. The
file structure is uniform across venues: a documented conflict check, a
scope defined in writing, and a records request tailored to the claims, so
that the written opinion rests on a record either side of the caption can
examine. When the damages picture requires an economist, the firm offers
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds within the
same engagement structure. Coverage of other Kansas venues is described on
the [Kansas practice page](/locations/kansas/).

## Presence in Hays, stated accurately

Hays meetings are by appointment. The firm keeps no staffed office in Hays
and publishes no street address for this location, because an expert whose
credibility rests on precise statements should not be imprecise about its
own footprint. The bulk of an engagement, intake, records review, analysis,
drafting, and counsel conferences, is handled remotely on a schedule agreed
at the start. When the referral questions call for an in-person vocational
evaluation, or when a deposition or trial setting in Ellis County requires
attendance, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office and
coordinated with the court's calendar well before the date arrives.

## Labor market analysis for the Hays area

Vocational findings for evaluees in the Hays area rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for Kansas, on state
workforce agency labor market information, and on direct labor market
research where warranted by the record. A regional community's labor market
does not mirror statewide averages, so the analysis examines which
occupations consistent with the evaluee's restrictions and transferable
skills are actually hiring within a realistic commuting range, and the
report documents that reasoning source by source. The goal is an opinion a
court can rely on and an opposing expert can test, the same standard the
firm applies across its [national practice](/locations/nationwide/).

## Beginning an Ellis County engagement

To retain the firm, or to check availability before recommending it to
co-counsel, submit the caption or party names through the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and note any imminent disclosure,
deposition, or trial dates. After conflicts clear, the engagement letter
fixes the scope, the tailored records request issues, and the reporting
timeline is built backward from the court's deadlines. Counsel facing an
adverse report rather than needing an affirmative opinion can request
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) through the same
process.
