## Crawford County and the federal district

State-court engagements in the Pittsburg area are accepted for matters in the
District Court of Crawford County, and federal matters proceed in the United
States District Court for the District of Kansas. Pittsburg referrals sit
within the firm's statewide [Kansas practice](/locations/kansas/), which means
the reporting standards, file conventions, and disclosure tracking that govern
the firm's metro work govern its work here without dilution. The scheduling
order and any noticed deposition or trial dates are collected at intake, and
the report is timed to be served, reviewed, and defensible before testimony is
required.

## By-appointment service in Pittsburg

There is no Purinton Analytics office in Pittsburg; the firm publishes no
street address for this location and will not imply a local storefront it
does not keep. Meetings are by appointment, and the practice is structured so
distance rarely slows a case. Records conferences, attorney meetings, and
pre-deposition preparation are conducted by videoconference, and remote
evaluation is available when the referral questions and testing protocols
permit it. Where in-person evaluation is warranted, it takes place in the
evaluee's own community or at counsel's offices, with travel scheduled from
the Kansas City home office, and deposition or trial appearances are planned
against the court's calendar the same way.

## The labor market behind the opinion

Employability and earning capacity findings for Pittsburg evaluees draw on
Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for
Kansas statewide, including the nonmetropolitan area estimates that cover
Crawford County, together with state workforce agency labor market information
and direct labor market research when the referral warrants it. The report is
explicit about the evaluee's commutable labor market and why it was defined
that way, and every wage or employment conclusion is tied to a cited source,
so that whichever side retained the firm, the opposing expert can trace the
analysis step by step.
