Galena lies in Cherokee County, and its state-court civil docket runs
through the District Court that serves the county. If the claim belongs in
federal court instead, the United States District Court for the District
of Kansas is the forum available within the state; the venue that actually
controls, together with any qualification or registration requirement
attached to it, is identified during the conflict check and intake. The
firm's answer to venue uncertainty is a single disciplined product: an

whose conclusions never wander past the disclosed scope. Neighboring

## The market a Galena evaluee actually faces

Galena is a small community whose realistic job search extends into
Joplin and the wider metro around it, and a credible earning capacity
opinion has to model that reach rather than treat the town as an island.
The firm's findings rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational
employment and wage estimates for the Joplin, MO-KS metropolitan area and
for Kansas statewide, on state workforce agency labor market information,
and on direct labor market research when the referral questions demand
employer-level detail. Every report distinguishes what the metro data show
from what the statewide data show, and it explains why the selected
occupations match this evaluee's restrictions and history, so the opinion
withstands the predictable attack that it describes a market the evaluee
cannot actually enter.

## Presence, travel, and testimony

The firm maintains no Galena office and no local storefront, and it
declines to imply one; plain statements about presence are part of what
makes the written work trustworthy. By-appointment service means counsel
conferences and interviews proceed by secure video on the parties'
schedule, while depositions, trial appearances, and any in-person
evaluation are handled through travel arranged from the Kansas City home
office. Distance is managed as logistics; it is never allowed to thin the
record on which the opinion stands.
