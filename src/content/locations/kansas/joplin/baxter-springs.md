Baxter Springs sits on the Kansas side of a metropolitan area whose
principal city, Joplin, lies across the state line in Missouri. That
geography shapes the vocational questions before any complaint is filed: a
Baxter Springs evaluee may hold restrictions assessed under Kansas law
while the jobs that matter to earning capacity sit in another state
entirely. Purinton Analytics writes opinions built for that two-state
reality, and it accepts them from either side of the caption. Plaintiff
and defense counsel retain the firm on identical terms for
[vocational expert opinions](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) on
employability and earning capacity, for
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) in catastrophic injury
matters, and for
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

be pulled and checked, and conclusions confined to the disclosed scope.

## Earning capacity across two states

A commuting market that ignores the state line is the central analytical
fact of a Baxter Springs claim. The firm grounds its findings in Bureau of
Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the
Joplin, MO-KS metropolitan area and for Kansas statewide, supplemented by
state workforce agency labor market information and by direct employer
research where the referral questions call for it. Each opinion explains
why a given data series was chosen and what it does and does not capture,
so that cross-examination about the difference between metro-wide and
Kansas-only figures meets reasoning that was already in the report rather
than improvised at deposition.

## Serving Baxter Springs by appointment

The firm keeps no office in Baxter Springs and does not pretend
otherwise; candor about presence is part of the same discipline that
governs its written work. Counsel conferences, records review, and most
vocational interviews are conducted by secure video. When the referral
calls for in-person assessment, or when a deposition or trial setting
requires the expert in the room, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City
home office, and evaluations take place in the evaluee's own community so
that daily function is observed where it actually occurs.
