Purinton Analytics prepares [vocational expert](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) and [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) opinions for Wamego matters that take the commuting economy seriously, whether the retaining party sits on the plaintiff side or the defense side of the caption.

## Where a Wamego case is heard

Wamego is in Pottawatomie County. State court matters proceed in the District Court, which in Kansas is the trial court of general jurisdiction. Where federal jurisdiction exists, the United States District Court for the District of Kansas stands as the available federal forum, and the venue that actually controls a given case is confirmed during the conflict check and intake, together with any venue-specific qualification requirement. The firm's reports are drafted for scrutiny in either system, with a documented records basis and a scope defined in writing at the outset.

## The commuting market around Manhattan

Vocational findings for a Wamego evaluee begin with the work available in Pottawatomie County and extend to the broader [Manhattan metropolitan area](/locations/kansas/manhattan/), where Manhattan is the principal city and a substantial share of the region's employment is found. The foundation is qualitative and sourced: Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the metro area and for Kansas as a whole, state workforce agency labor market information, and direct employer contact when a specific placement question calls for it. Transferable skills analysis then connects the evaluee's actual work history to occupations that exist in this market, not to a hypothetical national one.
