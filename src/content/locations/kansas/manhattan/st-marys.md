Counsel with an injured client or an opposing expert's report in St. Marys often assume that retaining a credentialed vocational expert or life care planner means sending the evaluee to a distant city for evaluation. It does not. Purinton Analytics structures engagements so that the evaluation comes to the evaluee: records move electronically, interviews and counsel conferences run remotely, and when an in-person assessment is warranted it takes place in the evaluee's own community rather than in an office the client would have to travel to reach. That evaluation-first framing shapes how the firm serves St. Marys matters for [vocational expert witness](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) and [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) work on behalf of plaintiff and defense retentions alike.

## The evaluation itself

A vocational evaluation covers work history, education, transferable skills, and functional considerations documented in the medical records; a life care planning evaluation adds future care needs sourced to the treating record and clinical foundation. For a St. Marys evaluee, conducting that assessment locally matters, because the interview should reflect the person's actual circumstances, household demands, and realistic geography of work. Assignments limited to records, including [medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/) and [rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) of an opposing expert's report, proceed without any appointment at all. There is no staffed St. Marys office and no local street address; in-person work is scheduled by appointment with travel from the Kansas City home office, and the firm says so plainly because accuracy about presence is part of the same discipline that governs its opinions.

## County, courthouse, and forum

St. Marys lies in Pottawatomie County, and Kansas state court litigation arising there proceeds in the District Court, the state's trial court of general jurisdiction. Matters with a federal hook may be brought in the United States District Court for the District of Kansas, and the controlling venue for a specific case, along with any qualification or registration requirement that venue imposes, is identified during the conflict check and intake. Reports carry the same structure regardless of forum: defined scope, documented sources, and conclusions an opposing expert can test line by line.
