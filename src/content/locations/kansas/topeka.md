A Topeka engagement begins with process, not promises: a documented conflict
check, a written scope, and a records request built around the actual claims,
completed before any opinion work starts. That discipline carries through
[vocational evaluation and expert testimony](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/),
[medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/), and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis
while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement
structure. Counsel on either side of the caption retain the firm on identical
terms, and each report is written to hold up under cross-examination rather
than to please the party that commissioned it.

## Venue footprint around Topeka

The Topeka metro's litigation runs through the District Court of Shawnee
County and the district courts of Jackson County, Jefferson County, Osage
County, and Wabaunsee County. Federal matters proceed in the United States
District Court for the District of Kansas. Because these venues sit within the
firm's statewide [Kansas coverage](/locations/kansas/), a case that is
transferred, consolidated, or refiled elsewhere in the state stays with the
same expert and the same file. Disclosure deadlines and testimony dates are
gathered at intake so the written opinion is complete, served, and defensible
before a deposition is ever noticed.

## Appointments, not an office

There is no Purinton Analytics office in Topeka, and the firm says so plainly
rather than implying a storefront it does not keep. Meetings are by
appointment: at counsel's offices, at agreed professional space, or in the
evaluee's community. Remote evaluation is available when the referral
questions and testing protocols permit, which keeps early-stage work moving
without travel on either side. When in-person evaluation is warranted, or when
deposition and trial settings require presence, travel is arranged from the
Kansas City home office and planned around the court's calendar. Records
review, attorney conferences, and pre-deposition preparation are routinely
conducted by videoconference.

## Grounding opinions in the Topeka labor market

Vocational findings for Topeka evaluees draw on Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates for the Topeka metropolitan area
and for Kansas statewide, on state workforce agency labor market information,
and on direct labor market research where the referral requires it. No wage or
employment conclusion is asserted without a cited source, and the report
explains how the evaluee's commutable labor market was defined. The same
sequence of transferable skills analysis, labor market research, and earning
capacity synthesis applies whether the retaining party is plaintiff or
defense, which is precisely what makes the resulting opinion useful in
negotiation as well as at trial.

## Beginning a Topeka matter

Topeka counsel work with the same practice that serves referrals
[nationwide](/locations/nationwide/): the same methodology, the same reporting
standards, the same insistence on a clean record. To begin, submit the caption
or party names through the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and flag any
imminent disclosure, deposition, mediation, or trial dates. Once the conflict
check clears, counsel receive a defined scope and a records checklist tailored
to the claims, and any venue-specific qualification requirement is identified
and confirmed during intake.
