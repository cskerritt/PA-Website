When a McPherson case turns on what an injured person can still earn, or on
what a lifetime of care will require, counsel need an expert whose file will
hold up under the other side's scrutiny. Purinton Analytics provides
[vocational evaluation and expert testimony](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/),
[medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/), and
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) to attorneys in
the McPherson, KS micropolitan area, accepting work from plaintiff and
defense counsel on identical terms. For matters that also require economic
loss calculations, the firm arranges
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds within the
same engagement structure.

## Where McPherson County cases are heard

The firm accepts engagements for matters in the District Court of McPherson
County and for federal litigation in the United States District Court for
the District of Kansas. Venue does not change the method. Every engagement
opens with a documented conflict check, a defined scope, and a records
request tailored to the claims, and every opinion is written so that a
reviewing expert on either side of the caption can trace each conclusion
back to the record. Counsel handling cases in other Kansas venues will find
the firm's statewide coverage on the
[Kansas practice page](/locations/kansas/).

## Appointment-based practice in McPherson

Purinton Analytics has no staffed office in McPherson and publishes no
street address for this location; saying so plainly is part of the same
candor the reports are built on. Meetings are by appointment. The working
phases of an engagement, intake, records review, analysis, drafting, and
conferences with counsel, are conducted remotely, which serves a
smaller-market docket well: timelines stay short and costs stay
proportionate to the case. When an evaluation should happen in person, or
when deposition and trial dates require presence in McPherson County, travel
is arranged from the Kansas City home office and locked to the litigation
calendar at the start of the engagement.

## Labor market evidence for McPherson evaluees

The vocational analysis for a McPherson County evaluee draws on Bureau of
Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for Kansas,
state workforce agency labor market information, and direct labor market
research where the referral questions warrant it. Statewide tables alone do
not answer what work a specific evaluee, with specific restrictions and a
specific commuting radius, can realistically obtain in this community, so
the report connects each data source to the evaluee's actual circumstances
and cites everything it relies on. The methodology is the same one applied
throughout the firm's [national practice](/locations/nationwide/), which
means a McPherson opinion reads no differently than one prepared for the
largest urban venue.

## How to refer a McPherson matter

Referrals begin with the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/). Provide the
caption or party names, note any looming disclosure, deposition, or trial
dates, and the firm will confirm conflicts and availability before an
engagement letter issues. The scope is then fixed in writing, the records
request goes out, and a reporting schedule is set against the court's
deadlines, so both retaining and opposing counsel know when the written
opinion will be on the table.
