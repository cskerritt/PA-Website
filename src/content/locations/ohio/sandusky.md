The Sandusky, OH metropolitan area spans Erie County and Ottawa County, and
Purinton Analytics accepts engagements across that full footprint rather than
treating the principal city as the boundary of the practice. Counsel on
either side of the caption retain the firm for
[vocational evaluation and expert testimony](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/), with the same
documented conflict check and defined scope that govern every matter
described on the [Ohio practice page](/locations/ohio/).

## State and federal venues for the metro

Within the metro, state court engagements proceed in the Erie County Court of
Common Pleas and the Ottawa County Court of Common Pleas. Federal matters
connected to the area may be venued in the United States District Court for
the Northern District of Ohio or the United States District Court for the
Southern District of Ohio; the correct district and any venue-specific
qualification requirement are confirmed during intake. For cases that need
economic damages testimony, the firm structures
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure, so counsel manages a single engagement while each
opinion carries its proper signature.

## By-appointment service, honestly described

The firm has no staffed office in Sandusky and publishes no street address
for this location. That is a deliberate disclosure, not an omission: accuracy
about presence is part of the same discipline that produces defensible
reports. Sandusky-area engagements are served by appointment. Records
review, conferences with counsel, and appropriate evaluee interviews are
conducted remotely by secure video, and when an assignment calls for
in-person evaluation in Erie County or Ottawa County, or when deposition and
trial dates require attendance, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City
home office around the dates the litigation sets.

## Labor market analysis across the counties

Employability and earning capacity opinions for Sandusky-area evaluees rest
on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for
the metropolitan area and for Ohio statewide, supplemented by state
workforce agency labor market information and targeted labor market research
where the questions require it. Because the metro's workforce is spread
across both of its counties, the report states which geographic market the
analysis uses for the particular evaluee and why, so the foundation of the
opinion is visible to plaintiff and defense reviewers alike rather than
buried in an appendix.

## Beginning an engagement in the Sandusky metro

Erie County and Ottawa County referrals move through the same intake process
as the firm's [nationwide practice](/locations/nationwide/). To begin,
[request a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) with the case caption and
parties, and identify any disclosure deadlines, depositions, or trial dates
already scheduled. Confidential records should be held until the engagement
is confirmed in writing; once it is, counsel receives a tailored records
request, a defined scope, and a delivery schedule built backward from the
court's calendar.
