The Cincinnati metropolitan area spans Ohio, Kentucky, and Indiana, and
litigation here frequently involves parties and evaluees on different sides
of a state line. Purinton Analytics is built for that kind of geography. The
firm provides [vocational expert](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) and
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) services to plaintiff and
defense counsel throughout the metro, along with
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under a
unified engagement structure. Wherever in the metro a matter is venued, the
written opinion is prepared to the same standard.

## A venue footprint that crosses state lines

On the Ohio side of the metro, engagements are accepted for matters in the
Court of Common Pleas of Hamilton County and in the common pleas courts of
Butler, Warren, Clermont, and Brown counties. Matters arising in the
Kentucky portion of the region, including Boone County, Kenton County, and
Campbell County, Kentucky, and in the Indiana portion, including Dearborn
County, Franklin County, and Ohio County, Indiana, are accepted through the
same intake process. Ohio federal matters proceed in the United States
District Court for the Southern District of Ohio or the United States
District Court for the Northern District of Ohio, depending on where the
case is filed, and the firm's reports are written to satisfy the scrutiny of
either forum. Counsel with matters elsewhere in the state are served under
the same practice model described on the [Ohio locations page](/locations/ohio/).

## Meetings by appointment, work without a storefront

Cincinnati meetings are by appointment. The firm keeps no staffed office in
the metro and publishes no street address for this location, because
accuracy about presence is part of the same discipline that governs its
reports. Most engagements begin remotely: records review, structured
vocational interviews, and standardized testing under publisher protocols
where remote administration is supported. When the referral questions call
for in-person evaluation, or when deposition and trial dates require
presence, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office and planned
around the court's calendar rather than the firm's. Counsel receive a
consistent report format and a direct point of contact regardless of where
the work is performed or which side of the caption retained the firm.

## Labor market research for a metro that crosses borders

Vocational findings for evaluees in the region rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Cincinnati
metropolitan area and for the relevant state, whether that is Ohio,
Kentucky, or Indiana, supplemented by state workforce agency labor market
information and direct labor market research where warranted. Life care
plans and [medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/) are
priced to the evaluee's own community using provider-specific inquiries and
published fee data, since the cost of future care can differ meaningfully
from county to county within the same metro. The data sources behind every
figure are cited in the report so that opposing counsel can trace each
conclusion back to its foundation.

## Starting a Cincinnati engagement

Availability is confirmed through a conflict check before any substantive
discussion of the case. Submit the caption or party names through the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and note any imminent disclosure,
deposition, or trial dates so that scheduling can be addressed at the
outset. For matters beyond the metro, the firm's
[national coverage](/locations/nationwide/) describes how the same
remote-first practice serves venues across the country.
