## The Tuscarawas County bench and the federal alternatives

State-court claims arising in Dover are heard in the Tuscarawas County Court
of Common Pleas, and reports are written with that county courtroom in mind:
an opinion that will survive cross-examination before local jurors has to be
plain about its sources and candid about its limits. When a matter proceeds
federally, Ohio's district courts, the United States District Court for the
Northern District of Ohio and the United States District Court for the
Southern District of Ohio, are the available forums; the district and
division controlling a particular Dover filing, along with any
venue-specific registration or disclosure requirement, are confirmed with
counsel during the conflict check and intake. The methodology does not bend
to the forum. The same defined scope and documented sourcing apply wherever
the report is filed.

## A paired employment base

An earning capacity analysis for a Dover worker begins from the recognition
that Dover and neighboring New Philadelphia function together as the
employment center of the surrounding county, so the commutable market is
drawn around the worker rather than around a municipal boundary. Vocational
findings rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and
wage estimates for the metro area and for Ohio statewide, supplemented by
state workforce agency labor market information and by direct labor market
research when the referral questions warrant it. The written opinion
identifies the market it used, the occupations it considered and rejected,
and the origin of every wage observation, so the analysis can be tested
rather than taken on faith.

## Appointments, not an office

The firm keeps no office in Dover and publishes no local street address,
because a practice that trades on verifiable statements should not print an
unverifiable one. Records review, counsel conferences, and deposition
preparation proceed remotely, which most attorneys find faster than
scheduling around a building. When an in-person vocational or life care
planning evaluation serves the case, it is arranged in or near Dover in a
setting suited to the evaluee's circumstances, and when deposition or trial
testimony requires presence, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home
office. Where the damages theory calls for economic modeling, the firm
offers [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.
