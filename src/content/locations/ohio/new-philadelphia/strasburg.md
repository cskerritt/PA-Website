Strasburg is a village whose paychecks are largely earned somewhere else,
and that single observation should discipline every earning capacity
opinion written about a Strasburg resident: the relevant labor market is
the commute, not the village limits. Purinton Analytics brings that
market-first framing to its [vocational expert work](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[litigation consulting](/services/expert-testimony-litigation-consulting/)
for counsel on either side of the caption, through the
[New Philadelphia and Dover metro practice](/locations/ohio/new-philadelphia/)
and the firm's [Ohio state page](/locations/ohio/).

## Courts that hear Strasburg matters

In state court, a Strasburg claim proceeds in the Tuscarawas County Court
of Common Pleas. In federal litigation, Ohio counsel choose between, or are
assigned to, the United States District Court for the Northern District of
Ohio and the United States District Court for the Southern District of
Ohio; which district and division actually govern the filing is a question
the firm resolves with counsel at the conflict check and intake stage,
where any venue-specific qualification or disclosure requirement is also
identified. The forum determines formatting and deadlines. It never
determines conclusions, which rest on the record and the method regardless
of where the caption is filed.

## Drawing the commutable market honestly

Because Strasburg workers routinely travel to jobs across Tuscarawas County
and the surrounding region, the vocational analysis gives its closest
attention to how the commutable market is defined for this particular
evaluee: age, restrictions, transportation, and work history all bear on
how far a realistic job search extends. Within that defined market, the
firm relies on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage
estimates for the metro area and for Ohio statewide, on state workforce
agency labor market information, and on direct labor market research where
the referral questions call for it. Every occupational match and every
wage observation in the report is traceable to its source, and the report
says so explicitly, because traceability is what separates an opinion from
an assertion.

## Presence by appointment only

The firm does not maintain a Strasburg office and will not pretend to;
accuracy about geography is a small, visible test of the accuracy counsel
are buying. Most of the engagement is conducted remotely, from records
analysis through deposition preparation, and remote conferencing has made
that the faster path for most attorneys. Where the referral requires an
in-person vocational or life care planning evaluation, the firm comes to
the evaluee, scheduling the assessment in or near the evaluee's own
community. Testimony and other required appearances are planned with
travel from the Kansas City home office, arranged far enough ahead to
protect the court's schedule. The same commitments hold across the firm's
[work for counsel nationwide](/locations/nationwide/).

Every Strasburg engagement starts with clearance:
