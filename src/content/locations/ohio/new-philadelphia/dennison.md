## From the Court of Common Pleas to the district courts

The state forum for a Dennison matter is the Tuscarawas County Court of
Common Pleas. If the case is federal, Ohio's available district forums are
the United States District Court for the Northern District of Ohio and the
United States District Court for the Southern District of Ohio, and the
firm confirms with counsel during the conflict check and intake which
district and division control the filing and what venue-specific
qualification or disclosure rules follow from that answer. Whatever the
courtroom, the expert file is built to the same standard: a defined scope
agreed in writing, a records request tailored to the claims, and an
opinion the other side can examine line by line.

## A village worker in a county market

Dennison is a small village, and its residents' employment prospects are
properly measured against the county and regional market they can actually
reach, not against the village itself. The firm's vocational conclusions
draw on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage
estimates for the surrounding metro area and for Ohio statewide, on the
state workforce agency's labor market information, and on direct labor
market inquiry when the referral questions require it. The written opinion
sets out the commutable market it adopted and why, the occupations
considered for this evaluee, and the provenance of each wage observation,
so that cross-examination finds documentation where it expected
assumptions.

The firm maintains no Dennison office and lists no address here; the same
insistence on accuracy that governs its reports governs its map. Records
review, conferences with counsel, and deposition preparation are handled
remotely as a matter of course. When the referral calls for an in-person
vocational interview or life care planning assessment, the appointment is
arranged in or near Dennison, shaped around the evaluee's medical
condition and ability to travel, because the quality of an evaluation
depends in part on meeting the person where life actually happens.

never put at risk by the expert's. These practices mirror the firm's
