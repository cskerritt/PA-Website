## Labor market foundations for local opinions

Vocational findings for evaluees in the New Philadelphia-Dover area are
grounded in Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage
estimates covering the area and Ohio statewide, together with state
workforce agency labor market information and direct labor market research
where the referral questions warrant it. The written opinion identifies the
geographic labor market analyzed for the particular evaluee, the occupations
considered, and the data behind each conclusion, so that plaintiff and
defense reviewers can trace the reasoning rather than take the bottom line
on faith.
