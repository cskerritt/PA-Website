A vocational opinion earns nothing from the size of the town it concerns;
it earns credibility from its method. Purinton Analytics writes reports
for Shadyside matters to the same standard it applies in the largest
markets it serves: a documented conflict screen, a defined scope, a
records request tailored to the claims, and conclusions that either side
of the caption can trace to their sources. For Shadyside counsel that
discipline covers
[vocational evaluation and expert testimony](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) of opposing
reports, delivered through the firm's
[Wheeling metro practice](/locations/west-virginia/wheeling/) and its
statewide [Ohio coverage](/locations/ohio/).

## Belmont County venue, stated precisely

Shadyside sits in Belmont County, and its state-court matters are heard in
the Belmont County Court of Common Pleas. Ohio's federal trial forums are
the United States District Court for the Northern District of Ohio and the
United States District Court for the Southern District of Ohio; the
district that controls a given filing, and any venue-specific
qualification or registration requirement that comes with it, is
identified and confirmed with counsel during the conflict check and
intake. The firm does not guess at venue any more than it guesses at
wages, and the report that reaches the courthouse is written so its
reasoning holds up wherever the caption lands.

## Shadyside's place in the surrounding labor market

Shadyside is a small community whose evaluees typically look for work
across the wider Wheeling metro, a market that spans West Virginia and
Ohio. Employability and earning capacity findings rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Wheeling
metropolitan area and for Ohio statewide, on state workforce agency labor
market information, and on direct labor market research when published
sources do not answer the referral questions. Scale is characterized in
qualitative terms, every wage observation is tied to its source, and the
report sets out how the commutable market was drawn for this particular
evaluee, so the analysis can be probed from either counsel table without
losing its footing. Where a damages figure is required, the firm arranges
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under a
single engagement structure.

## What appointment-based service means in practice

There is no Purinton Analytics office in Shadyside, no storefront, and no
published local address, and the firm says so because honesty about its
footprint is continuous with honesty in its findings. Case records arrive
electronically under a written scope. Counsel conferences, opinion
reviews, and deposition preparation take place by video and telephone.
In-person vocational evaluations, when warranted, are conducted in the
evaluee's own community, and testimony obligations are met by travel
scheduled from the Kansas City home office once dates are fixed. Shadyside
receives the identical service model that runs through the firm's
[national practice](/locations/nationwide/).

## The route to retention

Plaintiff and defense counsel retain the firm on the same terms, and no
analysis begins until the conflict screen has cleared and the scope is
memorialized in writing. Counsel ready to proceed should
[submit a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) with the caption or party names,
a concise statement of the claims, and any disclosure, deposition, or
trial dates already set, and the firm will confirm availability before any
confidential material is transmitted.
