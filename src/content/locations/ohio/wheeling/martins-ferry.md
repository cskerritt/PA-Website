Martins Ferry is an Ohio town inside a metropolitan area anchored across

Virginia, while a Martins Ferry caption is filed under Ohio law in Ohio
courts. That split is the threshold question Purinton Analytics sorts out
for counsel here, because
[vocational expert opinions](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) and

forum that will actually test them. The town's work sits within the firm's
[Wheeling metro practice](/locations/west-virginia/wheeling/) and its
broader [Ohio coverage](/locations/ohio/), and the state-line geography
changes the paperwork, not the standard of the analysis.

## The Ohio courts behind a Martins Ferry case

Martins Ferry lies in Belmont County, and state-court matters proceed in
the Belmont County Court of Common Pleas. On the federal side, Ohio offers
its own forums: the United States District Court for the Northern District
of Ohio and the United States District Court for the Southern District of
Ohio. Which district controls a particular filing, and whether any
venue-specific qualification or disclosure requirement applies, is
confirmed with counsel during the conflict check and intake rather than
assumed from a map. The report itself is drafted so that its methods,
sources, and reasoning read the same way in a county courtroom as they
would in federal court, because opposing counsel in either forum is
entitled to trace every conclusion back to its record.

## A labor market that ignores the state line

Employability and earning capacity for a Martins Ferry evaluee are
questions about where that person can realistically work, and the honest
answer often crosses into West Virginia. Opinions are grounded in Bureau
of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the
Wheeling metropolitan area and for Ohio statewide, supplemented by state
workforce agency labor market information and by direct labor market
research when the referral questions require it. The report explains how
the commutable market was drawn, which occupations were considered and
why, and where each wage observation originates, so both plaintiff and
defense counsel can audit the path from data to opinion. When a claim
also needs economic quantification, the firm offers
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.
