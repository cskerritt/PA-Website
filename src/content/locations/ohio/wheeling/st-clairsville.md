## Venue and courthouse context in Belmont County

St. Clairsville sits in Belmont County, where state-court matters are
heard in the Belmont County Court of Common Pleas. Federal matters from
Ohio proceed in the United States District Court for the Northern District
of Ohio or the United States District Court for the Southern District of
Ohio, and the district that controls a given filing, along with any
venue-specific registration or qualification requirement, is identified
and confirmed during the conflict check and intake. Knowing the forum
early matters because each court's scheduling practice shapes when the
written opinion must be final, and the firm treats that date as the spine
of the whole engagement.

## Placing a St. Clairsville evaluee in the regional market

Vocational findings for a St. Clairsville evaluee rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Wheeling
metropolitan area, which spans West Virginia and Ohio, and for Ohio
statewide, together with state workforce agency labor market information
and direct labor market research where the referral warrants it. The
analysis is qualitative about scale and explicit about method: it states
how the commutable labor market was delineated, why particular occupations
were included or excluded, and what source stands behind each wage
observation. Where lost earnings must be reduced to a damages figure,
the firm coordinates
[damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) in which
independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis
while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under the same
engagement structure.

## How by-appointment service works here

There is no staffed Purinton Analytics office in St. Clairsville, and the
firm says so plainly rather than implying a storefront it does not keep.
Counsel send records electronically against a defined scope; strategy
conferences and deposition preparation run by video and telephone, which
tends to be faster than coordinating office visits. In-person vocational
evaluations, when the questions require them, are arranged in the
evaluee's own community, and appearances for deposition, hearing, or trial
are scheduled as travel from the Kansas City home office once dates are
fixed. St. Clairsville is served exactly as the rest of the firm's
[national practice](/locations/nationwide/) is served, on the timeline the
court has already imposed.
