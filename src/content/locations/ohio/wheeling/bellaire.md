Earning capacity is a question about a market, not a mailing address, and
for a Bellaire evaluee that market is the commuting region of the Wheeling
metro, which reaches across West Virginia and Ohio. Purinton Analytics
approaches Bellaire referrals from that premise: the
[vocational opinion](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) begins with a
defensible picture of where this evaluee can actually obtain work, and the
[life care plan](/services/life-care-planning/) begins with what treatment
and support this evaluee will actually need. Bellaire assignments are part
of the firm's [Wheeling metro practice](/locations/west-virginia/wheeling/)
and its [Ohio coverage](/locations/ohio/), and each is scoped in writing
before the analysis starts.

## Courts serving Bellaire litigants

Bellaire lies in Belmont County. State-court matters are heard in the
Belmont County Court of Common Pleas, while federal matters from Ohio
proceed in the United States District Court for the Northern District of
Ohio or the United States District Court for the Southern District of
Ohio. The controlling district for a particular caption, together with any
venue-specific registration or qualification requirement, is identified
and confirmed during the conflict check and intake. Whatever the forum,
the written opinion is prepared for the sternest audience it could face,
because a report that survives cross-examination has to be built that way
from the initial records request.

## Delineating the commutable market

The analytical core of a Bellaire vocational opinion is the delineation of
the evaluee's commutable labor market, and the report shows its work:
which occupations were considered, why each was included or excluded
against the evaluee's education, work history, and residual capacities,
and what source supports every wage observation. Those observations come
from Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates
for the Wheeling metropolitan area and for Ohio statewide, from state
workforce agency labor market information, and from direct labor market
research when published estimates leave a question open. Scale is
described qualitatively, and no figure appears without a citation behind
it. When the referral also requires present-value damages, the firm offers
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Meeting counsel and evaluees where they are

Purinton Analytics keeps no office in Bellaire and claims none; the
practice is remote-first by design. Records move electronically, and
conferences with counsel, draft discussions, and deposition preparation
are conducted by video or telephone. If the referral questions require an
in-person vocational evaluation, it is scheduled in the evaluee's own
community rather than in a rented conference room chosen for the firm's
convenience. Depositions, hearings, and trial appearances are handled as
travel from the Kansas City home office once the controlling dates are
set. Bellaire counsel get the same service model, and the same report
discipline, as every engagement in the firm's
[national practice](/locations/nationwide/).

## From inquiry to engagement

The firm accepts Bellaire work from plaintiff and defense counsel on the
same terms, and every engagement begins with a documented conflict screen
and a written scope. The practical starting point is to
[request a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) with the case caption or party
names, a brief description of the claims, and any disclosure, deposition,
or trial dates already on the docket, so the firm can confirm availability
and propose a schedule before confidential records change hands.
