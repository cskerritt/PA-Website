Counsel on either side of the caption come to the firm with the same need in
Toledo matters: an expert whose findings hold up regardless of who issued
the retention letter. Purinton Analytics provides
[vocational expert evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/) for plaintiff
and defense engagements across the Toledo metro, applying the same
methodology, the same sourcing discipline, and the same report conventions
no matter which party retained the firm. That symmetry is not a slogan; it
is what makes the work useful in settlement discussions as well as at trial.

## Courts serving the Toledo metro

Engagements are accepted for matters in the Lucas County Court of Common
Pleas and in the common pleas courts of Wood County and Fulton County. In
federal court, Ohio matters proceed in the United States District Court for
the Northern District of Ohio or the United States District Court for the
Southern District of Ohio, depending on where the case is filed. Reports are
prepared with the disclosure requirements of federal practice in mind even
when a matter sits in state court, because opinions written to the higher
standard travel well between forums. The broader statewide practice,
including other metros, is described on the
[Ohio locations page](/locations/ohio/).

## By-appointment service, stated plainly

Toledo meetings are by appointment. There is no staffed Purinton Analytics
office in the metro and no published street address for this location, and
the firm says so directly because accuracy about presence reflects the same
discipline that governs its reports. Engagements typically open remotely:
records are reviewed as they arrive, structured vocational interviews are
conducted by video, and standardized testing is administered under publisher
protocols where remote administration is supported. When the case requires
an in-person evaluation or courtroom presence for deposition or trial,
travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office and coordinated with
counsel well in advance, so appearances are planned rather than improvised.
Throughout the engagement, scheduling, status updates, and draft timelines
are handled directly between the expert and the retaining office.

## Toledo labor market foundations

Vocational conclusions for Toledo metro evaluees rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Toledo
metropolitan area and for Ohio statewide, supplemented by state workforce
agency labor market information and direct labor market research where the
referral questions warrant it. Life care plans and cost projections are
priced to the evaluee's own community using provider-specific inquiries and
published fee data for the region rather than national averages. Each figure
in the report carries its citation, which allows opposing counsel to test
the analysis on its merits and allows retaining counsel to defend it the
same way.

The engagement process begins with a documented conflict check, a defined
scope, and a records request tailored to the claims. To confirm
availability, submit the caption or party names through the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and note any imminent disclosure,
deposition, or trial dates. Counsel handling related matters in other
states can review the firm's [national coverage](/locations/nationwide/)
for how the same practice model extends to venues across the country.
