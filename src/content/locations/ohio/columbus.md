The questions Columbus counsel bring to the firm are the questions that
decide damages anywhere: what work an injured or displaced person can still
perform, what that work pays in the market where the person lives, and what
future care will cost. Purinton Analytics answers them for plaintiff and
defense counsel across the Columbus metro through
[earning capacity and employability evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/), with
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
available when the case also requires economic analysis performed and signed
by independent economist partners coordinated by the firm.

## Courts of the Columbus metro

Engagements are accepted for matters in the Franklin County Court of Common
Pleas and in the common pleas courts of the surrounding counties, including
Delaware County as well as Fairfield, Licking, Madison, Morrow, Perry,
Pickaway, Hocking, and Union counties. In federal court, Ohio matters proceed in the United States
District Court for the Southern District of Ohio or the United States
District Court for the Northern District of Ohio, depending on where the
case is filed, and the firm's written opinions are prepared to withstand
examination in either forum. The same methodology and report standard apply
statewide, and counsel with matters outside the metro can begin from the
[Ohio locations page](/locations/ohio/).

## By-appointment presence, honestly described

There is no staffed Purinton Analytics office in Columbus, and the firm
publishes no street address for this location. Meetings are by appointment,
because candor about presence belongs to the same discipline that governs
the firm's opinions. In practice, most Columbus engagements proceed
remotely from the start: records are transmitted electronically, structured
interviews are conducted by video, and standardized testing is administered
under publisher protocols where remote administration is supported. When an
in-person evaluation is warranted, or when deposition and trial appearances
are required, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office and
coordinated with counsel's litigation calendar. The result for retaining
counsel is a consistent work product and a direct line to the expert,
regardless of where any given step of the engagement takes place.

## Labor market data for Columbus metro evaluees

Vocational findings for Columbus metro evaluees rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Columbus
metropolitan area and for Ohio statewide, together with state workforce
agency labor market information and direct labor market research where the
referral questions warrant it. Life care plans and cost projections are
priced to the evaluee's own community using provider-specific inquiries and
published fee data rather than national averages, and every source is cited
in the report so that either side of the caption can verify the figures
independently. Where an opposing expert has already opined, the same data
discipline supports rebuttal and peer review work.

## Starting a Columbus engagement

Every engagement begins with a documented conflict check, a defined scope,
and a records request tailored to the claims. To confirm availability for a
Columbus matter, submit the caption or party names through the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and note any imminent disclosure,
deposition, or trial dates. Counsel with related matters in other states
will find the same practice model described under the firm's
[national coverage](/locations/nationwide/).
