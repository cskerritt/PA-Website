An earning-capacity opinion about an Ontario resident is, at bottom, an opinion about the Mansfield area labor market, because a worker living in Ontario looks for employment across the metro rather than inside town lines. Purinton Analytics starts from that premise. Its [vocational expert](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) analyses ask what jobs the wider commuting area actually offers a person with the evaluee's limitations and skills, and its [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) work asks what future care will cost to deliver where that person lives. Both services are available to plaintiff and defense counsel alike, and Ontario matters are handled within the firm's [Mansfield metro practice](/locations/ohio/mansfield/).

## A metro market, measured qualitatively

Because Ontario functions as part of the Mansfield metropolitan area's employment base, the vocational analysis draws on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Mansfield metropolitan area and for Ohio statewide, together with state workforce agency labor market information. Where the referral questions turn on a specific occupation or a specific employer practice, the firm conducts direct labor market research rather than resting on published tables alone. The written opinion characterizes the market in qualitative terms, ties each conclusion to an identified source, and flags the points where reasonable experts could weigh the evidence differently, so cross-examination tests the reasoning rather than discovering it.

## Courts serving Ontario parties

Ontario is a Richland County community, and state-court civil litigation involving Ontario parties proceeds in the Richland County Court of Common Pleas. Ohio's federal forums, the United States District Court for the Northern District of Ohio and the United States District Court for the Southern District of Ohio, are available where federal jurisdiction exists, and the controlling venue for a given case is confirmed during the conflict check and intake process along with any qualification requirement that venue imposes. The firm prepares every report to the same disclosure standard, so a matter that begins in common pleas court and later moves does not require the opinion to be rebuilt.

## How an Ontario evaluee is assessed

There is no Purinton Analytics office in Ontario, and the firm publishes no local street address anywhere it works; candor about presence is treated as part of the same discipline that governs the opinions themselves. In practice, that means records are collected and reviewed remotely, the vocational interview and any testing take place by secure video or in person in the evaluee's own community, and travel for in-person assessment or testimony is arranged from the Kansas City home office. Counsel is told at intake which components will be remote and which in person, and the schedule is set against the litigation calendar rather than around it.

When counsel needs the vocational and care findings expressed as an economic loss figure, the firm offers [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement structure. The disciplines stay separate; the assumptions stay aligned.
