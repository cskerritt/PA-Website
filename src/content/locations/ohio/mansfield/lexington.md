Lexington is a small Richland County community in the Mansfield metropolitan area, and civil claims involving its residents and businesses are tried in the Richland County Court of Common Pleas unless federal jurisdiction moves them elsewhere. Purinton Analytics prepares [vocational expert](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) and [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) opinions for that docket the way it prepares them for any other: written to withstand examination by whichever side did not retain the firm. Lexington engagements are managed through the [Mansfield metro practice](/locations/ohio/mansfield/).

## State and federal forums

Beyond the Richland County Court of Common Pleas, Ohio's federal trial forums are the United States District Court for the Northern District of Ohio and the United States District Court for the Southern District of Ohio. Which court controls a particular Lexington matter, and whether that venue imposes any registration or qualification requirement on a testifying expert, is identified and confirmed during the conflict check and intake process rather than assumed. Because forum questions sometimes resolve late, the firm drafts every report so that its methodology section, source list, and stated assumptions satisfy the stricter disclosure expectations from the start.

## What the metro means for earning capacity

A Lexington evaluee's realistic employment options extend across the Mansfield area, and the vocational analysis is scoped accordingly. Opinions rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Mansfield metropolitan area and for Ohio statewide, on state workforce agency labor market information, and on direct labor market research where a referral question demands more than the published series can answer. The report speaks qualitatively about occupational fit, transferable skills, and the practical availability of work for a person with the evaluee's capacities; it does not dress judgment up as data, and it says plainly which conclusions rest on which sources.

## Meeting the evaluee where they live

Purinton Analytics maintains no Lexington office and lists no address for this location. That is deliberate: a firm whose product is accurate statements should not imply presence it does not have. Assessment is organized around the evaluee instead. Records are exchanged and reviewed remotely, interviews and testing proceed by secure video or in person in the evaluee's own community as the case requires, and any travel for in-person evaluation, deposition, or trial is scheduled from the Kansas City home office. For counsel, the practical effect is that a retention in Lexington moves on the same timeline the firm keeps in its home market, with dates committed at intake.
