## Where Shelby cases are heard

Shelby sits in Richland County, and civil actions in state court proceed in the Richland County Court of Common Pleas. Matters raising federal claims may be filed in the United States District Court for the Northern District of Ohio or the United States District Court for the Southern District of Ohio; the controlling venue, along with any registration or qualification requirement it carries, is identified and confirmed during the conflict check and intake process. The report itself does not change with the forum. Methodology is stated, sources are disclosed, and conclusions are separated from assumptions so that either a common pleas jury or a federal bench can see exactly how the opinion was built.

## Reading the labor market around a Shelby evaluee

Shelby is a small city within the Mansfield metropolitan area, and an earning-capacity analysis for a Shelby resident has to reflect the market that person can actually reach. Vocational findings rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Mansfield metropolitan area and for Ohio statewide, supplemented by state workforce agency labor market information and by direct labor market research when the referral questions warrant it. The resulting opinion describes, in qualitative terms, what work exists for a person with the evaluee's residual functional capacity, education, and work history, and it is candid about where the published data end and professional judgment begins.

## Scheduling the evaluation itself

The firm keeps no office in Shelby and publishes no local address, a policy applied to every location it serves because overstated presence would sit poorly next to a practice built on verifiable statements. Records review proceeds remotely from the outset. The clinical interview and any standardized testing are conducted by secure video or in person in the evaluee's own community, whichever the case requires, and when in-person work, deposition, or trial testimony calls for travel, it is scheduled from the Kansas City home office. Counsel receives a defined timeline at intake rather than an open-ended estimate.
