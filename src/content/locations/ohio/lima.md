## The Lima venue picture

State court matters in the Lima, OH metropolitan area proceed in the Allen
County Court of Common Pleas. Federal cases arising from the area may be
venued in the United States District Court for the Northern District of Ohio
or the United States District Court for the Southern District of Ohio, and
intake confirms the governing district together with any venue-specific
registration or qualification requirement. Broader Ohio venue context,
including how the firm approaches the state's trial and federal courts as a
whole, is collected on the [Ohio practice page](/locations/ohio/). Where the
damages case requires economic testimony, the firm offers
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## What by-appointment service means in Lima

The firm keeps no staffed office in Lima and publishes no street address for
this location; claiming otherwise would sit poorly beside reports that
demand precision from everyone else. Service is by appointment. In practice,
most of the engagement is location-independent: records analysis, strategy
conferences with counsel, and many evaluee interviews proceed by secure
video. When the referral questions call for in-person evaluation, or when
deposition and trial dates in Allen County require presence, travel is
scheduled from the Kansas City home office and coordinated with the
deadlines counsel is already managing.

## Data behind Lima labor market opinions

Vocational findings for Lima-area evaluees are anchored in Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the metropolitan
area and for Ohio statewide, joined with state workforce agency labor market
information and direct labor market research where warranted. The written
opinion sets out which occupations were analyzed, which data sources were
consulted, and how the evaluee's restrictions and transferable skills map
onto the market studied, so opposing counsel can trace every conclusion to
its source instead of arguing with a summary.

## Referring an Allen County case

To open a Lima engagement, [request a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) with
the case caption and parties, and note any imminent disclosure, deposition,
or trial dates. The firm responds once the conflict check clears with a
proposed scope, fee structure, and schedule. Confidential materials,
including medical records, should not be sent until the engagement is
confirmed in writing; the records request that follows confirmation is
drawn to the claims actually pleaded, which keeps the file lean and the
opinion focused.
