Parma is among the largest suburban communities in the Cleveland metro, which
means its residents appear in injury, employment, and disability litigation
often enough that counsel on either side regularly needs vocational and
care-planning testimony grounded in this labor market. Purinton Analytics
provides [vocational evaluation and expert testimony](/services/vocational-expert-witness/)
and [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) for Parma cases as
part of its [Cleveland metro practice](/locations/ohio/cleveland/) and its
broader [Ohio coverage](/locations/ohio/), accepting engagements from
plaintiff and defense counsel alike.

## Where Parma cases are decided

Parma lies in Cuyahoga County, so its state-court matters proceed in the
Cuyahoga County Court of Common Pleas, a venue where opposing experts are
scrutinized closely and written opinions are expected to hold up under
cross-examination. Ohio's federal trial forums are the United States
District Court for the Northern District of Ohio and the United States
District Court for the Southern District of Ohio; which district controls a
particular case is confirmed during the conflict check and intake, along
with any venue-specific qualification or disclosure requirement. The firm
writes to the same standard for either forum: sources identified,
methodology reproducible, conclusions that follow from the record.

## Earning capacity in a large suburban workforce

A Parma evaluee typically competes for work across the whole metro rather
than within municipal lines, so employability and earning capacity analyses
draw on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage
estimates for the Cleveland metropolitan area and for Ohio statewide,
supplemented by state workforce agency labor market information and direct
labor market research when the referral questions warrant it. Every opinion
documents how the commutable labor market was defined and which occupations
were tested, because those choices are where competing vocational opinions
usually diverge, and the trier of fact is entitled to see the reasoning
rather than a bare conclusion.

## Serving Parma by appointment

The firm keeps no office in Parma and publishes no local address, because
accuracy about presence belongs to the same discipline that governs its
reports. Records review, counsel conferences, and most evaluee interviews
proceed by video and telephone. When an in-person vocational evaluation is
appropriate, it is arranged in the evaluee's own community at a suitable
professional setting. Deposition and trial appearances are scheduled with
travel from the Kansas City home office, the same arrangement that supports
the firm's [nationwide practice](/locations/nationwide/), so a Parma
engagement is staffed no differently than one across the country.

## Opening a Parma engagement

Useful referrals arrive early: an evaluation takes time to schedule, and a
report takes time to write well. Send the case caption or party names
through the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/), identify the claims and
the side represented, and flag any disclosure, deposition, or trial dates
already on the calendar. Once the conflict screen clears, the firm defines
the scope in writing, issues a records request tailored to the claims, and
begins work on a schedule that respects the court's deadlines. Counsel
receive candid preliminary impressions before drafting begins, so a written
opinion is never a surprise to the attorney who commissioned it, and the
finished report rests on a record both sides can examine.
