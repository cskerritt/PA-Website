## Cuyahoga County venue for Euclid claims

Euclid is in Cuyahoga County, and its state-court litigation is heard in
the Cuyahoga County Court of Common Pleas. Ohio's federal bench sits in the
United States District Court for the Northern District of Ohio and the
United States District Court for the Southern District of Ohio; which
district governs a given case is determined during the conflict check and
intake, together with any venue-specific expert disclosure requirement.
Reports are prepared to the stricter reading of the applicable standards,
because a report written for the harder audience needs no revision for the
easier one.

## The labor market behind the numbers question

Vocational findings for Euclid residents draw on Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates for the Cleveland metropolitan
area and for Ohio statewide, on state workforce agency labor market
information, and on direct labor market research when the case turns on
whether real employers would hire this person for real work. The firm
describes the regional economy qualitatively and precisely, documents how
the commutable market was drawn for this evaluee, and resists the
temptation to decorate the analysis with figures the methodology cannot
carry. The same restraint governs the firm's
[statewide Ohio practice](/locations/ohio/) and its
[national work](/locations/nationwide/).

## How a Euclid evaluee is actually assessed

Purinton Analytics keeps no office in Euclid and none anywhere nearby; the
community is served by appointment, and the firm says so directly because
counsel planning an engagement deserve the real logistics, not an implied
storefront. In practice, most assessment steps proceed by video and
telephone, which most evaluees find convenient and which keeps engagement
costs proportionate. When the referral questions require an in-person
evaluation, the appointment is arranged in the evaluee's own community
rather than requiring travel to a distant office, and when deposition or
trial testimony calls for the expert in the courtroom, travel is scheduled
from the Kansas City home office around the dates the court has fixed. The
interview follows a documented protocol, the records reviewed are listed in
the report, and the functional and vocational conclusions are tied to
specific evidence, so that opposing counsel reading the file can trace
every finding to its source. Where treating providers supply restrictions,
the report says what was relied on; where the record is silent, the report
says that too.

## What to send with a Euclid referral

Begin with the conflict screen: submit the caption or party names through
the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/), note the claims, and indicate
whether the engagement contemplates evaluation, records-based review, or
testimony. Helpful early materials include medical records bearing on
function, employment and wage documentation, and any prior expert reports
in the case. After conflicts clear, the firm confirms scope and schedule in
writing and arranges the evaluee contact promptly, because the earlier the
assessment happens, the more options counsel keeps open as the discovery
calendar tightens and the disclosure deadline approaches.
