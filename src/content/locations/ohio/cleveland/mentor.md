## Lake County's courthouse and the federal forums

Mentor's state-court matters are tried in the Lake County Court of Common
Pleas. In the federal system, Ohio's available forums are the United States
District Court for the Northern District of Ohio and the United States
District Court for the Southern District of Ohio; the controlling district
for any particular case, along with the expert disclosure rules that come
with it, is confirmed during the conflict check and intake. Because the
practice regularly writes for unfamiliar benches across the country, its
reports assume a skeptical reader by default: methods stated, sources
cited, limitations acknowledged in the text rather than discovered on
cross.

## Coordinated damages for a Mentor case

Serious injury cases from Mentor frequently need more than a vocational
opinion standing alone. In coordinated engagements, independent economist
partners perform and sign the economic analysis while the vocational and
care-planning work proceeds under one engagement structure, giving counsel
a damages presentation whose components rest on consistent assumptions.
The vocational foundation comes from Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates for the Cleveland metropolitan
area and for Ohio statewide, from state workforce agency labor market
information, and from direct labor market research where the referral
questions call for evidence about actual hiring. Scale and opportunity are
characterized qualitatively, never with invented figures.

## Practical arrangements for Mentor counsel

The firm maintains no office in Mentor or anywhere else in the metro; every
location outside the Kansas City home office is served by appointment, and
the firm says so plainly because honest logistics are part of an honest
opinion. Day-to-day work runs remotely by video, telephone, and secure
records exchange. In-person evaluations are held in the evaluee's own
community when the assessment requires them, and testimony travel is
scheduled from Kansas City when deposition or trial dates are set. The
model is described in full on the firm's
[nationwide page](/locations/nationwide/), and its Ohio footprint on the
[state page](/locations/ohio/).

## Referring from Lake County

A Mentor engagement begins with the conflict screen. Provide the caption or
party names through the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/), summarize
the claims and injuries, and identify what the case needs: an original
evaluation, a life care plan, an economic analysis coordinated with both,
or a review of opinions already served. Note any dates the court has fixed.
The firm responds with a written scope and a schedule it can keep, and if
the matter is one the firm should not take, whether for conflict or
capacity, counsel hears that promptly instead of after the calendar has
run.
