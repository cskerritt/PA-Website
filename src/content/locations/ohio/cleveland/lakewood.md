Ask where a Lakewood resident works and the answer is rarely Lakewood
alone: the community functions as part of a metro-wide commuting market,
and that fact shapes every credible vocational opinion written about one of
its residents. Defining the commutable labor market too narrowly
understates opportunity; defining it too broadly overstates it; either
error is an invitation to cross-examination. Purinton Analytics addresses
the question explicitly in each
[vocational evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) it prepares
for Lakewood counsel, and pairs that work with
[forensic economic damages coordination](/services/forensic-economic-damages/)
when lost earnings must be reduced to a defensible figure, serving
whichever side of the caption brings the referral.

## Cuyahoga County courts and the federal alternative

As a Cuyahoga County community, Lakewood sends its state-court disputes to
the Cuyahoga County Court of Common Pleas. Ohio's federal forums are the
United States District Court for the Northern District of Ohio and the
United States District Court for the Southern District of Ohio; the
district with authority over a specific matter, and any qualification step
it requires of a testifying expert, is settled during the conflict check
and intake. The firm's written opinions carry full sourcing and a
reproducible method precisely so that the choice of forum never changes
what the report can withstand.

## Measuring a commuter's earning capacity

For a Lakewood evaluee, the analytical frame is the regional economy. The
firm works from Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage
estimates for the Cleveland metropolitan area and for Ohio statewide, from
state workforce agency labor market information, and from direct labor
market research when a referral question requires evidence about actual
openings. On the economic side, independent economist partners perform and
sign the economic analysis in coordinated engagements, while the vocational
and care-planning work proceeds under the same engagement structure, so
counsel manage a coherent damages presentation rather than a set of
disconnected experts.

## Appointment-based service, honestly described

The firm has no Lakewood office and lists no local address, and it treats
that transparency as part of its credibility. Most of an engagement is
conducted remotely: counsel conferences and evaluee interviews by video or
telephone, records exchanged securely, drafts reviewed against the file.
Where an in-person evaluation serves the referral questions, it is held in
the evaluee's own community. Testimony travel originates at the Kansas City
home office, exactly as it does for the firm's
[nationwide engagements](/locations/nationwide/), and Lakewood counsel can
see the full regional context on the
[Cleveland metro page](/locations/ohio/cleveland/) and the
[Ohio state page](/locations/ohio/).

## Referring a Lakewood matter

Every Lakewood engagement opens the same way: a conflict screen, a written
scope, and a records request built for the claims at hand. Use the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) to submit the caption or party names,
describe the injuries or employment events at issue, and identify any dates
that already bind the schedule. Counsel weighing whether the case needs a
vocational opinion, a life care plan, an economic analysis, or all of them
together can raise that question at intake, and the firm will give a direct
answer, including when the honest answer is that the case does not need
what the caller expected.
