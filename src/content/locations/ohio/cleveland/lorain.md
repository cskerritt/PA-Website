Every sound vocational opinion about a Lorain worker begins in the past
tense. What jobs has this person actually held, what skills did that work
demonstrably build, and what does the medical record say those skills can
still be applied to: those questions come before any statement about future
earnings, and Purinton Analytics answers them in that order. The firm
brings [vocational evaluation and expert testimony](/services/vocational-expert-witness/)
and [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) to Lorain matters
through its [Cleveland metro practice](/locations/ohio/cleveland/), and it
takes referrals from plaintiff counsel and defense counsel on identical
terms.

## Where a Lorain claim is heard

Lorain's state-court civil docket belongs to the Lorain County Court of
Common Pleas. In the federal system, Ohio litigants file in the United
States District Court for the Northern District of Ohio or the United
States District Court for the Southern District of Ohio; the conflict
check and intake settles which district reaches a given case and whether
that venue asks anything additional of a testifying expert. Counsel choose
the forum. The expert's obligation is narrower and stays constant across
courthouses: a written opinion with its sources listed and its method
reproducible, delivered on the schedule the chosen court imposes and able
to withstand the reader who wants it to fail.

## Prior work, present restrictions, future earnings

The analysis moves in sequence. First the employment history is
reconstructed from records and a structured interview; then the
transferable skills that history genuinely supports are mapped against the
restrictions the medical evidence documents; only after both steps does
the opinion turn to the market. Market findings for a Lorain resident draw
on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates
for the Cleveland metropolitan area and for Ohio statewide, on state
workforce agency labor market information, and, when a referral question
concerns actual openings for a person with specific restrictions, on
direct labor market research whose method and results the report states
plainly. Numbers enter the report only when a cited dataset supplies them,
scale is characterized qualitatively, and the opinion explains how the
geographic reach of the job search was defined for this particular
evaluee, since that definition is where opposing vocational experts most
often disagree.

## The practical shape of a Lorain engagement

Service in Lorain is by appointment only. The single staffed Purinton
Analytics location is the Kansas City home office, so this page carries no
Lorain street address; printing one would be an invention, and an expert
practice cannot afford inventions. Day to day, the engagement runs over
secure remote channels: conferences with counsel, records exchange, and
many evaluee interviews proceed by video or telephone. An in-person
evaluation, when the referral questions require one, is convened where the
evaluee actually lives, and testimony travel is booked from Kansas City
once deposition or trial dates are set. None of this is arranged specially
for Lorain; it is the standing structure described on the firm's
[nationwide practice](/locations/nationwide/) page, applied here without
modification.
