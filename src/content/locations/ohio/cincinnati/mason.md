## What by-appointment service actually means

The firm keeps no staffed office in Mason and publishes no local street
address, and it says so directly because honesty about presence is part of
the discipline that makes an expert report worth reading. Records review,
scoping calls, counsel conferences, and pre-deposition preparation move by
video and telephone, which keeps the litigation calendar moving without
travel delays. In-person vocational or care-planning assessment, when the
referral requires it, is scheduled where the evaluee lives. Testimony
appearances for deposition, hearing, or trial are planned from the Kansas
City home office and coordinated with the operative court dates.

## Warren County venue and the federal side

Mason sits in Warren County, and state-court matters arising there are heard
in the Court of Common Pleas. In federal practice, Ohio offers the United
States District Court for the Northern District of Ohio and the United
States District Court for the Southern District of Ohio as available forums,
and the district that actually controls a given matter is confirmed during
the conflict check and intake. Forum aside, every file follows the same
sequence: documented conflict screen, written scope, records request
tailored to the claims. When the damages theory extends into economic
modeling, the firm offers
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## The labor market a Mason evaluee actually faces

Mason is a suburban community within the Cincinnati metropolitan
area, and its residents work across that wider market. Vocational findings
therefore rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and
wage estimates for the Cincinnati metropolitan area and for Ohio statewide,
augmented by state workforce agency labor market information and by direct
labor market research where the questions in the referral call for it. The
report identifies the evaluee's commutable labor market and the reasons for
drawing it as drawn, and it anchors each wage and employment observation to
a named published source, so the analysis can be verified by the counsel
who commissioned it and challenged by the counsel who did not.
