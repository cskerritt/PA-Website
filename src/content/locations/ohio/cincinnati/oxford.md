were at the doorstep, nor written off as if the town were the whole market.

## A defensible market definition

Vocational findings for an Oxford evaluee are grounded in Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Cincinnati
metropolitan area and for Ohio statewide, together with state workforce
agency labor market information and, where the referral warrants it, direct
labor market research. The critical step is the one many reports skip: the
opinion states what portion of the metropolitan market is realistically
commutable for this particular evaluee, given the evidence in the record,
and explains why. Every wage and employment figure is tied to a published
source. An assumption that cannot be sourced is not used, because opposing
counsel will look for exactly that seam.

## Courts serving Oxford matters

Oxford is part of Butler County, and state-court litigation arising there
proceeds in the Court of Common Pleas. On the federal side, the United
States District Court for the Northern District of Ohio and the United
States District Court for the Southern District of Ohio are the available
Ohio forums, with the controlling venue for any given caption identified
during the conflict check and intake. In either setting the engagement
begins identically: a documented conflict screen, a written scope, and a
records request built around the claims. Counsel who need economic modeling
alongside the vocational and care-planning work can structure the matter as
a [coordinated damages engagement](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Reaching an Oxford evaluee

No staffed Purinton Analytics office exists in Oxford, and the firm
publishes no street address here; plain statements about presence are part
of the same practice of accuracy the reports follow. The bulk of the
engagement, records analysis, counsel conferences, and deposition
preparation, is conducted by video and telephone. When an in-person
assessment is called for, the evaluation is brought to the evaluee's own
community, so that distance from the metro's center never becomes a barrier
to a complete file. Travel for deposition, hearing, or trial testimony is
arranged from the Kansas City home office and keyed to the dates the court
has set.
