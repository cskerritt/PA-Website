## Venue for Lebanon litigation

Lebanon lies in Warren County, and state-court matters arising there are
heard in the Court of Common Pleas. Ohio's federal forums are the United
States District Court for the Northern District of Ohio and the United
States District Court for the Southern District of Ohio; which of them
controls a particular case is a question answered during the conflict check
and intake, never assumed. The forum does not alter the method. A file opens
with a documented conflict screen and a scope set in writing, and the
records request is shaped by the claims themselves, so the eventual opinion
stands on a record either party can inspect. When the damages case requires
economic modeling, counsel may structure the referral as a
[coordinated damages engagement](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Grounding the labor market analysis

Lebanon is a smaller community within the Cincinnati metropolitan area, and
an honest earning capacity analysis has to account for both facts at once.
Findings for Lebanon evaluees draw on Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates for the Cincinnati metropolitan
area and for Ohio statewide, supported by state workforce agency labor
market information and by direct labor market research where the referral
questions require it. The report sets out the evaluee's commutable labor
market and the evidence behind that boundary, and it cites a published
source for each wage and employment figure it uses, because a number
without a source is an invitation the firm declines to extend to
cross-examining counsel.

## By appointment in Lebanon

Purinton Analytics has no staffed office in Lebanon and publishes no local
street address, stated here plainly because the reports' credibility begins
with accuracy about small things. Day-to-day engagement work proceeds
remotely: records review, conferences with counsel, and pre-deposition
preparation are carried by video and telephone. If the referral calls for
in-person assessment, the evaluation is conducted in the evaluee's own
community rather than at a distant office. Appearances for deposition,
hearing, or trial are scheduled from the Kansas City home office and
coordinated with the court's calendar.
