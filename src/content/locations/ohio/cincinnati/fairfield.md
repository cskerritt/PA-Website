community sits inside the Cincinnati commuting market, and its workers hold
jobs throughout the metropolitan area, which means the central question in a
vocational file, what work this person can still perform and what it pays,
has to be answered at the scale of the metro rather than the town. Purinton

## Defining the commutable market honestly

Employability and earning capacity findings for a Fairfield evaluee begin
with Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates
for the Cincinnati metropolitan area and for Ohio statewide, supplemented by
state workforce agency labor market information and by direct labor market
research when the referral questions require it. The report does not simply
gesture at a large metro and stop. It states which labor market the evaluee
can realistically reach, explains the basis for that boundary, and sources
every figure to a publication opposing counsel can pull. A commuter's market
is an evidentiary question, and the analysis treats it as one.

## Butler County courts and the Ohio federal districts

Fairfield lies in Butler County, and state-court litigation arising there is
heard in the Court of Common Pleas. Federal matters in Ohio proceed in the
United States District Court for the Northern District of Ohio or the United
States District Court for the Southern District of Ohio; the controlling
district for any particular caption is identified during the conflict check
and intake. The engagement structure does not vary by forum. Each file
starts with a documented conflict screen, a scope fixed in writing, and a
records request matched to the claims, and when the case needs economic
modeling the firm provides
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Seeing the evaluee without moving the evaluee

Purinton Analytics maintains no staffed office in Fairfield and lists no
street address for it, a fact stated plainly because accuracy about presence
is part of the credibility the reports depend on. Most engagement work,
records analysis, conferences with counsel, and preparation for deposition,
is conducted remotely by video and telephone. Where an in-person assessment
is warranted, the evaluation comes to the evaluee's own community instead of
requiring an injured person to travel. Appearances for deposition, hearing,
or trial are planned from the Kansas City home office around the dates the
court and the parties have already fixed.
