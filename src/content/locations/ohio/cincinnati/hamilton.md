## Butler County venue and the federal forums

State-court matters arising in Hamilton proceed in the Court of Common Pleas
in Butler County. On the federal side, Ohio offers the United States District
Court for the Northern District of Ohio and the United States District Court
for the Southern District of Ohio as available forums, and the district that
controls a given caption is confirmed during the conflict check and intake
rather than assumed from a map. Whichever courtroom the case lands in, the
file is built the same way: a documented conflict screen before any records
are reviewed, a scope defined in writing, and a records request tailored to
the claims, so the finished opinion rests on materials either side can
examine line by line. Where the damages theory calls for economic modeling,
the firm offers
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Hamilton workers in the Cincinnati labor market

Hamilton is a sizable community on the Ohio side of the Cincinnati
metropolitan area, and its workers participate in a labor market that
crosses county lines. Employability and earning capacity findings for a
Hamilton evaluee draw on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment
and wage estimates for the Cincinnati metropolitan area and for Ohio
statewide, together with state workforce agency labor market information and
direct labor market research when the referral questions warrant it. Each
report states how the evaluee's commutable labor market was defined and why,
and every wage or employment figure that enters the analysis carries a named,
published source that opposing counsel can retrieve and check.

## Appointments, not an office

The firm keeps no staffed office in Hamilton and publishes no street address
here, because candor about presence belongs to the same discipline that
governs the reports. Records review, counsel conferences, and
pre-deposition preparation are handled by video and telephone. When the
referral requires an in-person vocational or care-planning assessment, the
evaluation is arranged in the evaluee's own community, which spares an
injured person the burden of traveling to a distant office. When deposition,
hearing, or trial dates require presence in Butler County or elsewhere in
Ohio, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office and planned
against the operative case calendar.

## Starting a Hamilton engagement

The firm accepts referrals from either side of the caption, and the intake
sequence does not change with the identity of the retaining party. Early
referral preserves the widest window for records collection, evaluation,
drafting, and review before expert disclosure deadlines arrive. To begin,
[request a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) with the case caption or party
names, a brief description of the claims, and any dates already set for
disclosure, deposition, or trial, so the engagement can be scheduled around
them from the first day.
