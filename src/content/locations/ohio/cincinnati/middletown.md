## Where a Middletown case is heard

Middletown sits in Butler County, and state-court matters arising there
proceed in the Court of Common Pleas. Federal practice in Ohio runs through
the United States District Court for the Northern District of Ohio and the
United States District Court for the Southern District of Ohio; which
district governs a particular case is a venue question resolved during the
conflict check and intake, not a fact assumed from geography. The forum
changes the deadlines, not the method. Every file opens with a documented
conflict screen and a written scope, and the records request is tailored to
the claims so that the resulting opinion stands on a record both sides can
test. Where economic modeling is part of the damages picture, counsel can
add
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Earning capacity in a metro-wide market

A Middletown worker's employment prospects are not confined to city limits.
Middletown is a substantial community within the Cincinnati metropolitan
area, and vocational findings for its residents rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for that metropolitan
area and for Ohio statewide, alongside state workforce agency labor market
information and targeted labor market research where the referral calls for
it. The report defines the evaluee's commutable labor market expressly,
explains the reasoning, and ties every wage and employment observation to a
published source, because an earning capacity opinion that cannot be traced
to its data does not survive a careful cross-examination and should not.

## How the work reaches Middletown

There is no staffed Purinton Analytics office in Middletown and no local
street address to publish; saying so plainly is part of the same honesty the
reports practice. The working rhythm is remote by design: video and
telephone carry records conferences, counsel updates, and pre-deposition
preparation without waiting on travel. In-person evaluation, when the
referral requires it, is conducted in the evaluee's own community rather
than in a distant office lobby. Testimony travel, whether for a Butler
County courtroom or a federal proceeding, is scheduled from the Kansas City
home office against the dates the court has set.

## Beginning a Middletown engagement

Because the calendar drives everything, the most valuable thing counsel can
do is refer early. The firm takes engagements from either side of the
caption, and the intake steps are identical for each. To open a file,
[submit a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) with the caption or party names, a
short summary of the claims, and every date already on the docket for
disclosure, deposition, or trial. Scheduling starts from those dates the day
the conflict screen clears.
