Counsel in the Springfield, OH metropolitan area engage Purinton Analytics for
a defined scope of work: [vocational evaluation and expert testimony](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis
while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement
structure. The practice accepts plaintiff and defense engagements alike, and
every report is written on the assumption that opposing counsel will read it
closely. That standard, described in more detail on the
[Ohio practice page](/locations/ohio/), does not change with the size of the
venue.

## Venue footprint in Clark County

Springfield engagements typically arise in the Clark County Court of Common
Pleas, the state trial court for the metro. Federal matters connected to the
area may proceed in the United States District Court for the Northern District
of Ohio or the United States District Court for the Southern District of Ohio,
and the correct district for a given case is confirmed during intake rather
than assumed. The work product is the same in either forum: a documented
conflict check before the engagement opens, a records request tailored to the
claims actually pleaded, and a written opinion that sets out its methods and
sources so that both sides of the caption can examine the record behind it.

## Meetings by appointment, not a storefront

The firm keeps its only staffed office in Kansas City and publishes no street
address for Springfield, because accuracy about presence is part of the same
discipline that governs its reports. Local meetings, evaluations, and
testimony are arranged by appointment. Much of the work proceeds remotely:
records review, vocational interviews by secure video where appropriate, and
report drafting do not require travel. When the referral calls for in-person
evaluation, or when deposition and trial dates require presence in Clark
County, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office and planned
around the litigation calendar counsel provides.

## Labor market research for Springfield evaluees

Vocational findings for evaluees in the Springfield area rest on Bureau of
Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the
metropolitan area and for Ohio statewide, supplemented by state workforce
agency labor market information and direct labor market research where the
questions warrant it. Estimates are cited qualitatively in this setting and
precisely in the report itself, with each source identified so that the
opinion can be tested on cross-examination. Where an evaluee's realistic
labor market extends beyond the metro, the analysis says so and documents the
basis, rather than treating the local market as a fixed boundary.

## Starting a Springfield engagement

Because the practice is [national in scope](/locations/nationwide/), Clark
County referrals follow the same intake path as any other matter. Submit the
caption or party names through the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and
note any imminent disclosure, deposition, or trial dates. The firm responds
after the conflict check clears, with a proposed scope, a fee structure, and
a schedule matched to the deadlines already on the docket. Please do not send
medical records or other confidential materials until the engagement is
confirmed in writing.
