Every opinion the firm issues is written on the assumption that opposing
counsel will read it line by line, and that assumption shapes how Cleveland
engagements are run. Purinton Analytics serves plaintiff and defense counsel
across the Cleveland metro with
[employability and earning capacity evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) of opposing
expert reports. Each engagement begins the way it does everywhere else in
the practice: with a documented conflict check, a defined scope, and a
records request tailored to the claims, so the written opinion rests on a
record either side can examine.

## Venue footprint across the Cleveland metro

Engagements are accepted for matters in the Cuyahoga County Court of Common
Pleas and in the common pleas courts of the surrounding counties, including
Ashtabula County as well as Lorain, Lake, Medina, and Geauga counties. Ohio federal matters
proceed in the United States District Court for the Northern District of
Ohio or the United States District Court for the Southern District of Ohio,
depending on where the case is filed, and the firm applies the same
disclosure-ready report standard in state and federal court alike. Counsel
whose dockets extend beyond the metro will find the statewide practice
described on the [Ohio locations page](/locations/ohio/).

## No storefront, and none implied

Cleveland meetings are by appointment. The firm maintains no staffed office
in the metro and publishes no street address for this location; stating that
plainly is part of the same discipline that governs its reports. Most
engagements begin remotely, with records review, structured vocational
interviews conducted by video, and standardized testing administered under
publisher protocols where remote administration is supported. When the
referral calls for an in-person evaluation, or when a deposition or trial
appearance requires presence, travel is arranged from the Kansas City home
office and scheduled around the court's calendar. Counsel receive the same
report structure and the same direct access to the expert whether the work
is performed on site or at a distance.

## Grounding findings in the Cleveland labor market

Vocational conclusions for Cleveland metro evaluees rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Cleveland
metropolitan area and for Ohio statewide, supplemented by state workforce
agency labor market information and direct labor market research where the
questions in the case warrant it. Life care plans and medical cost
projections are priced to the evaluee's own community through
provider-specific inquiries and published fee data, not lifted from national
averages, and each figure in the report is tied to a cited source. That
sourcing discipline is what allows the same work product to serve either
side of the caption without adjustment.

Availability for a Cleveland matter is confirmed through a conflict check
