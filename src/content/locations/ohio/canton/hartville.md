## Hartville and the Canton-Massillon employment market

Employability findings for a Hartville evaluee are framed by the
Canton-Massillon, OH metropolitan labor market and by Ohio as a whole. The
evidentiary spine is the Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment
and wage estimates for the metro area and the state, joined by state
workforce agency labor market information and, when a question is narrow
enough to demand it, direct labor market research with documented employer
contacts. Market scale and job availability are characterized
qualitatively; the reports carry no population counts, no percentages, and
no figure that cannot be traced to a named source.
