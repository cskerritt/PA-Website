People who live in North Canton rarely confine their working lives to it.
An evaluee with a North Canton address typically holds, or held, employment
somewhere in the broader Canton-Massillon, OH metro, and that commuting
reality is where a credible earning-capacity analysis has to begin. Purinton
Analytics prepares [vocational expert](/services/vocational-expert-witness/)
opinions and [life care plans](/services/life-care-planning/) for North
Canton litigation with the labor market defined the way the evaluee actually
experiences it, and the work is available to plaintiff and defense counsel
alike under an identical report standard.

## Venue: Stark County and Ohio's federal districts

North Canton's state-court matters are heard in the Stark County Court of
Common Pleas, where the firm accepts retentions from either side of the
caption. For federal litigation, Ohio's available forums are the United
States District Court for the Northern District of Ohio and the United
States District Court for the Southern District of Ohio; the controlling
district for any particular case, and any registration or qualification
requirement specific to that forum, is confirmed as part of the conflict
check and intake. Counsel with matters elsewhere in the state will find the
full picture on the [Ohio locations page](/locations/ohio/).

## A suburb inside a metro-wide labor market

Because North Canton functions as part of a larger employment market, the
firm's vocational findings draw on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational
employment and wage estimates for the Canton-Massillon metropolitan area and
for Ohio statewide, together with state workforce agency labor market
information. Where the referral question is concrete, whether particular
jobs exist for a person with particular restrictions, direct labor market
research supplements the published estimates. No figure appears in a report
without a cited source, and no conclusion is offered that the underlying
data cannot carry.

## Logistics for North Canton evaluees

The firm maintains no office in North Canton and lists no street address
here; every meeting is by appointment. In practice most of the work is
remote. Records are reviewed, interviews conducted, and standardized
instruments administered under publisher protocols that support remote
administration, which keeps costs down and timelines short. When an
evaluation is better done face to face, it is scheduled in the evaluee's own
community, with travel arranged from the Kansas City home office. Deposition
and trial appearances are handled the same way. Regional detail sits on the
[Canton metro page](/locations/ohio/canton/); the firm's
[national practice](/locations/nationwide/) runs on the same chassis.

A North Canton referral starts with clearance, not commitment. Counsel
submits the caption or party names for a

disclosure calendar, and a written scope follows. If the case also needs

independent economist partners perform and sign the economic opinion while
the vocational and care-planning work moves forward under one engagement
