work history, standardized testing under published protocols, labor market
findings tied to cited government data, and a report written to withstand
the scrutiny of whichever lawyer did not hire the firm. Purinton Analytics
offers [vocational expert](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) and

matters to plaintiff and defense counsel on equal footing, and the small

## County and court

Minerva's state-court matters are venued in Stark County and heard in the
Stark County Court of Common Pleas. Ohio's federal bench sits in the United
States District Court for the Northern District of Ohio and the United
States District Court for the Southern District of Ohio; rather than
guessing which district reaches a given Minerva dispute, the firm confirms
the controlling venue, and any qualification requirement that forum
imposes, during the conflict check and intake process. Engagements
elsewhere in the state are described on the
[Ohio locations page](/locations/ohio/).

## Assessing earning capacity from a village address

The labor market for a Minerva evaluee is regional. The firm's findings
draw on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage
estimates for the Canton-Massillon, OH metropolitan area and for Ohio
statewide, on state workforce agency labor market information, and on
direct employer research when a referral question requires evidence about
actual, present openings. Scale is described qualitatively, small community,
regional employment center, statewide market, because the firm's reports
contain no unsourced figures and no invented local color. What the data
cannot support, the report does not say.

## Where and how the evaluation happens

Minerva meetings are by appointment; the firm has no staffed office in the
village and publishes no street address for this location. The working
default is remote: records review, interviews, and standardized instruments
administered under remote protocols the test publishers sanction. When the
referral requires an in-person evaluation, the firm comes to the evaluee's
community, and when depositions or trial require presence, travel is
scheduled from the Kansas City home office. The
[Canton metro page](/locations/ohio/canton/) sets out the regional
logistics, and the [nationwide page](/locations/nationwide/) shows the same
system serving counsel across the country.
