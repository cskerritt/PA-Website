Carrollton belongs to Carroll County, which sets it apart from most of the
Canton-Massillon, OH metro's communities and shapes how its litigation is
venued. Purinton Analytics accepts Carrollton referrals from plaintiff and
defense counsel for
[vocational expert evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) and
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), pairing a
Carroll County venue posture with labor market analysis that reflects the
metro-scale economy Carrollton residents actually work in. The county line
changes the courthouse; it does not change the standard of the work.

## Carroll County's court and the federal forums

State-court matters arising in Carrollton proceed in the Carroll County
Court of Common Pleas. Federal litigation belongs to one of Ohio's district
courts, the United States District Court for the Northern District of Ohio
or the United States District Court for the Southern District of Ohio, and
the firm identifies the controlling district for a specific Carrollton
filing, together with any registration or qualification requirement that
forum imposes on a testifying expert, during the conflict check and intake
process. The firm's practice across the rest of the state is described on
the [Ohio locations page](/locations/ohio/).

## A rural county tied to a metro labor market

Although Carrollton is a small community, its evaluees are assessed against
the regional economy the Canton-Massillon, OH metropolitan area defines.
Vocational conclusions rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational
employment and wage estimates for that metro and for Ohio statewide,
supported by state workforce agency labor market information and by direct
employer inquiry when a referral question turns on whether particular work
is genuinely available to a person with the evaluee's limitations. Where
rural access to employment, transportation, or care is relevant, the report
addresses it qualitatively and ties every characterization to a cited
source rather than to impression.

## Evaluating a Carrollton resident

The firm maintains no office in Carrollton, and no street address is
published for this location; meetings occur by appointment. Engagements
generally begin remotely, through records review, structured interviews,
and standardized testing administered under publisher-sanctioned remote
protocols, which spares a rural evaluee unnecessary travel. When an
in-person evaluation better serves the referral, it is conducted in the
evaluee's own community, and appearances for deposition or trial are
scheduled with travel from the Kansas City home office. Regional logistics
appear on the [Canton metro page](/locations/ohio/canton/), and the
[nationwide page](/locations/nationwide/) shows the same approach applied
across the country.

## Starting with a conflict check

Every Carrollton matter begins with clearance of the parties. Submit the
caption or party names through the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/), identify the side of the caption
issuing the retention, and list the dates already fixed for disclosure,
deposition, or trial. If the damages case requires economic modeling, the
firm offers
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning components are completed
under one engagement structure.
