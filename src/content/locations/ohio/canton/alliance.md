Expert disclosure calendars drive most referrals the firm receives from
Alliance. By the time counsel in an injury or employment case starts looking
for a [vocational expert](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) or a
[life care planner](/services/life-care-planning/), a report deadline is
usually already on the docket, and the question is whether a defensible
opinion can be produced inside it. Purinton Analytics is structured for
exactly that situation: intake, conflict check, records request, and scoping
happen quickly, and the remote-first workflow means the Alliance evaluation
can begin while records are still arriving. Plaintiff and defense retentions
are handled with the same methodology and the same report standard.

## County and courthouse context for Alliance matters

Alliance sits in Stark County, and its state-court litigation proceeds in
the Stark County Court of Common Pleas. In federal practice, Ohio offers the
United States District Court for the Northern District of Ohio and the
United States District Court for the Southern District of Ohio as its trial
forums; which of them is the controlling venue for a given Alliance case is
settled during the conflict check and intake, along with any
forum-specific qualification requirement. Statewide venue coverage is
outlined on the [Ohio page](/locations/ohio/).

## Reading the labor market from Alliance

A vocational opinion for an Alliance evaluee is anchored in the
Canton-Massillon, OH metropolitan labor market rather than in the town by
itself. The firm relies on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational
employment and wage estimates for the metro and for Ohio as a whole, on
state workforce agency labor market information, and on targeted employer
contact when a referral question turns on whether specific work is actually
available to a person with the evaluee's restrictions. Conclusions are
stated qualitatively and sourced explicitly, so that the opinion reads the
same way to the retaining lawyer and to the lawyer across the aisle.

## How an Alliance evaluee is evaluated

There is no Purinton Analytics office in Alliance, and the firm does not
publish a street address for this location. What counsel gets instead is a
by-appointment practice: interviews and standardized testing are conducted
remotely where the test publishers' protocols permit, and in-person
evaluation in the evaluee's own community is arranged when the referral
requires it. Travel for evaluations, depositions, and trial testimony is
scheduled from the Kansas City home office. The
[Canton metro page](/locations/ohio/canton/) describes the regional
logistics, and the [nationwide page](/locations/nationwide/) explains how
the same model serves counsel in every state.

## Requesting a conflict check

Every Alliance engagement opens the same way, whatever the side of the
caption: a conflict check against the parties, then a scope tailored to the
claims. Where economic loss calculations are needed alongside the vocational
work, [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
bring in independent economist partners who perform and sign the economic
analysis under a single engagement structure. Send the caption and party
names through the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/), and identify the
disclosure or hearing dates that the work must meet so scheduling can be
confirmed at the outset.
