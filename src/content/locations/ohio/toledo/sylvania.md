For the injured person at the center of a Sylvania case, the evaluation is
often the most personal step in the litigation: a structured interview about
work history, education, daily function, and what a changed body or mind now
permits. Purinton Analytics designs that step around the evaluee. Interviews
are conducted by secure video or in person in the evaluee's own community,
scheduled at times that respect treatment calendars and family obligations,
and documented so thoroughly that counsel on either side can see exactly what
was asked and answered. That evaluee-centered process feeds the firm's
[vocational expert opinions](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care plans](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/).

## From interview to opinion

The interview is one input among several, never the whole basis. Before it
occurs, the firm completes a documented conflict screen, agrees to a written
scope, and issues a records request tailored to the claims; afterward, the
interview record is checked against the medical, employment, and educational
files rather than taken on its own terms. Where the interview and the records
diverge, the report says so plainly. That practice serves plaintiff and
defense retention equally, because it produces findings that neither side has
to defend as advocacy.

## Where Sylvania cases are heard

Sylvania is part of Lucas County, and state-court matters arising here are
tried in the Court of Common Pleas. Federal actions in Ohio proceed in the
United States District Court for the Northern District of Ohio or the United
States District Court for the Southern District of Ohio, with the controlling
district for any particular caption confirmed during the conflict check and
intake. The firm's Ohio venue experience is summarized on its
[statewide page](/locations/ohio/), and its work throughout this metro on the
[Toledo page](/locations/ohio/toledo/).

## Sylvania workers in the Toledo economy

A Sylvania evaluee's earning capacity is assessed against the labor market
the evaluee can actually access, which for this suburb means the Toledo
metropolitan economy as a whole. The firm relies on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Toledo
metropolitan area and for Ohio statewide, on state workforce agency labor
market information, and on direct labor market research when a specific
occupation or employer question warrants it. Reports carry citations for
every figure they use; this page, by design, carries none.

## Presence, plainly described

There is no Purinton Analytics office in Sylvania, and the firm publishes no
street address for this location. The absence is stated because accuracy
about offices belongs to the same discipline that governs the firm's
opinions. Analysis and drafting proceed remotely; in-person evaluation
happens in the evaluee's community when the referral questions require it;
and travel for depositions and trial is scheduled from the Kansas City home
office. If the damages theory extends to lost earnings modeling, counsel may
add a [coordinated damages assessment](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work continues under one
engagement structure, the same arrangement offered across the firm's
[national practice](/locations/nationwide/).
