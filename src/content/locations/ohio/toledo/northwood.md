The file comes before the opinion. In a Northwood case, Purinton Analytics
will not offer a view of earning capacity or future care until the medical
records, employment history, educational documentation, and prior evaluations
have been requested, received, and read, because an expert conclusion is only
as good as the record beneath it. That sequencing discipline is the
foundation of the firm's
[vocational expert practice](/services/vocational-expert-witness/), its
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and its
[medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/), whether the
retaining lawyer sits at the plaintiff table or the defense table.

## How a Northwood file is assembled

Intake follows a fixed order. A documented conflict screen clears first. The
scope is then defined in writing, so both the firm and counsel know what
question is being answered and what question is not. A records request
tailored to the claims goes out next, and analysis begins only when the
record is in hand. Gaps in the record are flagged to counsel rather than
papered over with assumptions, and where an assumption is unavoidable it is
identified as one in the report. The result is an opinion that opposing
counsel can audit, which is the standard the firm applies across its
[Ohio practice](/locations/ohio/) and its
[national practice](/locations/nationwide/).

## Wood County and the federal alternatives

Northwood sits in Wood County, and state-court actions arising here proceed
in the Court of Common Pleas. Ohio's federal trial forums are the United
States District Court for the Northern District of Ohio and the United
States District Court for the Southern District of Ohio; identifying which
district governs a particular caption is part of the conflict check and
intake, not something the firm presumes in advance. The reporting standard
does not vary by forum, so a Northwood opinion moves intact from state to
federal court if the case does.

## Employment data for a Northwood evaluee

Northwood's workforce belongs to the labor market of the surrounding Toledo
metropolitan area, and the firm evaluates earning capacity against that
market. The data foundation is the Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational
employment and wage estimates for the Toledo metropolitan area and for Ohio
statewide, joined by state workforce agency labor market information and
direct labor market research where the transferable skills analysis needs
occupation-level confirmation. Figures appear only in reports, cited to
their sources; the description here stays qualitative by rule. The metro
market as a whole is discussed on the firm's
[Toledo page](/locations/ohio/toledo/).

## Presence and travel, stated accurately

Purinton Analytics keeps no office in Northwood and publishes no street
address for this location; claiming one would sit poorly beside a practice
that demands sourcing for every factual claim. Records analysis and drafting
are remote. Evaluee interviews are held by secure video or in person in the
evaluee's own community when direct observation is warranted, and deposition
or trial appearances are handled as scheduled travel from the Kansas City
home office. Where the damages theory includes economic modeling, a
[coordinated damages engagement](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
places that work with independent economist partners who perform and sign
the economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning components stay
under one engagement structure.
