When a catastrophic injury reshapes a life in Whitehouse, the litigation
eventually has to answer a question no liability finding resolves: what will
this person's care actually require, year over year, for as long as the need
lasts? [Life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) is the discipline
that answers it, and it is often the reason counsel in Whitehouse matters
first contact Purinton Analytics. The plan itemizes future medical care,
therapies, equipment, medications, and attendant support, each element tied
to the clinical record and to published cost sources, and it is prepared to
the same standard whether the retaining party is plaintiff or defense. Around
that core, the firm provides
[vocational evaluation and testimony](/services/vocational-expert-witness/)
and [medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/) when the
damages picture calls for them.

## Care needs, documented for adversarial review

A care plan earns its weight by being checkable. Every recommendation in a
Whitehouse plan is anchored to the medical record or to a treating or
consulting source, every cost is cited, and the report distinguishes what the
record establishes from what remains a clinical judgment. Before any of that
work begins, the firm completes a documented conflict screen, sets the scope
in writing, and issues a records request tailored to the claims, so that the
finished plan can be examined by the opposing side without a single
untraceable line.

Whitehouse sits in Lucas County, and state-court matters arising here are

the United States District Court for the Northern District of Ohio and the

controlling district for any given caption confirmed during the conflict
check and intake. The firm's venue experience across the state appears on
its [Ohio page](/locations/ohio/).

## Work and wages after injury

Where the injury also disrupts a career, the vocational analysis measures a
Whitehouse evaluee against the employment market of the surrounding Toledo
metropolitan area. That analysis draws on Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates for the Toledo metropolitan area
and for Ohio statewide, on state workforce agency labor market information,
and on direct labor market research when specific occupations are at issue.
The metro market is treated in depth on the firm's
[Toledo page](/locations/ohio/toledo/); consistent with the practice's
standards, no figures are quoted outside a sourced report.

Purinton Analytics maintains no office in Whitehouse and publishes no street

evaluee assessment is conducted by secure video or in person in the

analysis while the care-planning and vocational work continues under one
engagement structure, mirroring the firm's
[national practice](/locations/nationwide/).
