Perrysburg is a suburb whose working life is woven into the wider Toledo
metropolitan economy: people who live here hold jobs across the metro, and
people who work here commute in from elsewhere. That commuting pattern is not
background color for a damages case; it defines the labor market in which a
Perrysburg evaluee's earning capacity must be measured. Purinton Analytics
frames its [vocational evaluations](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care plans](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/) around that
market, for plaintiff and defense engagements alike.

## The commuting market, measured properly

A transferable skills analysis limited to a suburb's own boundaries would
understate what a Perrysburg resident can actually reach, and an analysis that
ignores commuting realities would overstate it. The firm works from Bureau of
Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Toledo
metropolitan area and for Ohio statewide, together with state workforce agency
labor market information, and conducts direct labor market research when the
referral questions turn on specific occupations or employers. Each data point
in the resulting report is cited to its source so that either side can check
the arithmetic. The metro-wide view of this market is described on the firm's
[Toledo metro page](/locations/ohio/toledo/), and the statewide practice on
the [Ohio page](/locations/ohio/).

## Venue for Perrysburg matters

Perrysburg sits in Wood County, and state-court cases arising here are tried
in the Court of Common Pleas. Ohio's federal forums are the United States
District Court for the Northern District of Ohio and the United States
District Court for the Southern District of Ohio; which district governs a
given case is a venue question resolved during the conflict check and intake,
not a fact the firm presumes. Reports are drafted to the same standard for
either forum, and for arbitration or mediation as well, because the audience
the firm writes for is always the same: opposing counsel with a red pen.

## What retention looks like from Perrysburg

Counsel do not need the firm to be local, and the firm does not pretend to be.
It keeps no office in Perrysburg, publishes no street address here, and
handles the substantial majority of each engagement remotely: records review,
skills analysis, research, and drafting all move by secure channels on
counsel's schedule. Evaluee interviews are conducted by secure video or in
person in the evaluee's community, whichever the case requires, and
appearances for deposition or trial are scheduled as travel from the Kansas
City home office. When a claim needs economic modeling alongside the
vocational and care-planning opinions, the firm offers a
[coordinated damages assessment](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) in
which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis
while the remaining work continues under one engagement structure. The same
model serves referring counsel throughout the firm's
[national practice](/locations/nationwide/).

## Opening a Perrysburg file

Retention begins with a conflict screen, and nothing substantive happens
before it clears. Send the caption or the party names through the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/), describe the injury and the disputed
damages issues in general terms, and flag any dates the court has already
fixed. The firm replies with clearance status, a proposed scope in writing,
and the records request that will anchor the opinion.
