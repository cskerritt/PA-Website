## What a review of an opposing opinion examines

A rebuttal is not a louder version of the other report. The review works
through the served opinion the way a careful cross-examiner would: whether
the records relied on are the records that exist, whether the transferable
skills reasoning follows from the documented work history, whether each
recommended care item is tied to a clinical source, and whether the wage data
cited actually describe the market the evaluee would work in. Where the
opposing opinion is sound, the review says so, because attacking solid work
costs the retaining side more credibility than it gains. Where the opinion
overreaches, the written critique identifies the specific step at which the
method failed, in terms a judge can follow.

Court for the Northern District of Ohio or the United States District Court

conflict screen, written scope, records requested against the claims, so
that the finished opinion can be audited by the lawyers on the other side of
the caption.

## Starting a Wauseon engagement

Begin with the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/): the caption or party
names, a short description of the injury and the damages in dispute, and any
disclosure, deposition, or trial dates already set. If an opposing report is
in the file, say so. The response includes conflict clearance, a proposed
written scope, and a schedule matched to the court's calendar.
