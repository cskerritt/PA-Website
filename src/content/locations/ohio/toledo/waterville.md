Very little of a well-run expert engagement actually requires anyone to be
standing in Waterville. Records arrive electronically, analysis happens at a
desk, drafts move by secure transfer, and conferences with counsel run by
video. Purinton Analytics builds its practice around that reality, reserving
in-person work for the steps that genuinely demand it: certain evaluee
evaluations, depositions, and trial. For a small Lucas County suburb like
Waterville, this remote-first design means counsel choose an expert on
method rather than mileage. The firm offers
[vocational evaluation and testimony](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/) to plaintiff
and defense counsel on equal terms.

## What happens remotely, and what does not

The remote portion is the analytical core: conflict screening documented
before records are touched, a written scope, a records request tailored to
the claims, transferable skills analysis, care-plan research, and report
drafting. The in-person portion is chosen case by case. An evaluee interview
may proceed by secure video, or in the evaluee's own community when the
referral questions call for direct observation of function. Testimony
obligations are met wherever the court sits, with travel scheduled from the
Kansas City home office. What the firm never does is stage a local presence:
there is no Waterville office and no published street address, because
honesty about logistics is part of the product.

## Venue for Waterville cases

Waterville is in Lucas County, and state-court matters arising here are
heard in the Court of Common Pleas. Ohio's federal forums are the United
States District Court for the Northern District of Ohio and the United
States District Court for the Southern District of Ohio, and the district
controlling a specific caption is confirmed during the conflict check and
intake. The firm's broader Ohio venue work is described on its
[statewide page](/locations/ohio/), with metro-level detail on the
[Toledo page](/locations/ohio/toledo/).

## Grounding the wage analysis

Waterville is part of the Toledo metropolitan area, and that is the labor
market in which a Waterville evaluee's earning capacity is measured.
Findings rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage
estimates for the Toledo metro area and for Ohio statewide, on state
workforce agency labor market information, and on direct labor market
research where an occupation-specific question warrants it. Where an
evaluee's restrictions, licensure, or realistic commuting range narrow the
market below the metro as a whole, the report says so and shows the
reasoning. This page keeps its description qualitative on purpose; sourced
figures belong in the report, where they can be examined.

## Coordinated economics when the claim needs it

When lost earnings must be reduced to present-value economics, counsel can
retain a
[coordinated damages assessment](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning opinions proceed under one
engagement structure. The arrangement keeps a single point of coordination
without blurring who authored what, a distinction the firm maintains across
its [national practice](/locations/nationwide/).

## Referring a Waterville matter

The engagement starts the same way regardless of distance: submit the
caption or party names through the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/),
summarize the injury and the damages issues in dispute, and identify any
disclosure or deposition dates already scheduled. The firm answers with
conflict clearance, a written scope, and a plan that states plainly which
steps will happen remotely and which in person.
