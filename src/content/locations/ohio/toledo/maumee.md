Ask who retains Purinton Analytics for Maumee matters and the honest answer is
both sides, in comparable measure. Plaintiff firms bring the firm in to
establish what an injury has taken from a client's working life; defense
counsel and insurers bring it in to test whether a claimed loss is what the

## Why two-sided retention matters in practice

An expert whose conclusions can be predicted from the caption is an expert a
jury learns to discount. The firm's answer is procedural: a documented
conflict screen before any records arrive, a scope defined in writing, a
records request tailored to the claims, and findings that follow the record
wherever it goes. When the record supports a smaller loss than the plaintiff
alleges, the report says so; when it supports a larger care need than the
defense expects, the report says that too. Counsel in Maumee cases get the
benefit either way: an opinion that was not written to please them.
