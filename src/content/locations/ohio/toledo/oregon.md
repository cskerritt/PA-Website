## Built to survive the other table

Durability under scrutiny is a product of method, not confidence. Each
engagement opens with a documented conflict screen, a scope fixed in writing,
and a records request drawn to the claims, so the opinion rests on a record
both sides hold. Assumptions are stated rather than buried, and the reasoning
from record to conclusion is laid out step by step. When the assignment is
rebuttal, the same standard runs in reverse: the firm tests the opposing
expert's sources, math, and logic and reports what checks out along with what
does not, a candor that tends to matter to judges and juries alike.

## Lucas County venue and Ohio's federal forums

Oregon lies in Lucas County, and state-court actions arising here proceed in
the Court of Common Pleas. On the federal side, Ohio's forums are the United
States District Court for the Northern District of Ohio and the United States
District Court for the Southern District of Ohio; the district with authority
over a specific caption is identified during the conflict check and intake.
The firm's reporting format does not change with the courthouse, which is one
reason its opinions transfer cleanly between state and federal proceedings
across its [Ohio practice](/locations/ohio/).

## The labor market behind an Oregon opinion

Vocational conclusions for an Oregon evaluee are grounded in Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Toledo
metropolitan area and for Ohio statewide, in state workforce agency labor
market information, and in direct labor market research where the transferable
skills profile calls for it. The town's workforce participates in the broader
metro economy centered on Toledo, and the analysis treats that full market,
not the municipal boundary, as the relevant frame. Sourcing is qualitative
here by design; the numbers live in the report, each one cited. Metro-level
context appears on the [Toledo page](/locations/ohio/toledo/).
