When a catastrophic injury reshapes a Cortland resident's future, the
central damages document is often the life care plan: the itemized,
sourced projection of the medical care, equipment, therapies, and supports
the person will need for the rest of their life. Purinton Analytics prepares
[life care plans](/services/life-care-planning/) for Cortland matters to a
litigation standard, with each recommendation tied to the medical record and
each cost element traceable to a stated source, and pairs that work with
[vocational evaluation and testimony](/services/vocational-expert-witness/)
when lost earning capacity is also in dispute. Retention is open to counsel
on either side of the case.

## Trumbull County proceedings

Cortland's state-court civil litigation arises in Trumbull County and is
heard in the Court of Common Pleas. Where a Cortland case proceeds
federally, Ohio's district courts are the United States District Court for
the Northern District of Ohio and the United States District Court for the
Southern District of Ohio; the firm leaves the identification of the
controlling district to the conflict check and intake stage, where it
belongs, and then aligns the work plan with that court's disclosure
requirements. A statewide view of Ohio venues is maintained on the
[Ohio locations page](/locations/ohio/).

## Vocational context from the regional economy

Where earning capacity is contested, the analysis for a Cortland evaluee
looks to the labor market of the broader
[Youngstown-Warren metro](/locations/ohio/youngstown/). Its foundations are
qualitative on this page and quantitative only in the report itself: Bureau
of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the
Youngstown-Warren metropolitan area and for Ohio, state workforce agency
labor market information, and direct labor market research where the
referral requires evidence about particular occupations. The opinion
explains the commutable market selected for the evaluee and why it fits the
facts.

## Serving Cortland by appointment

Purinton Analytics states its footprint accurately: the staffed home office
is in Kansas City, and Cortland is served by appointment with no local
office and no published street address. Life care planning interviews,
collateral interviews with caregivers, and records conferences are
conducted remotely where appropriate; home-based or community-based
assessment of the evaluee is scheduled in Cortland when the plan requires
firsthand observation of function and environment. Travel for depositions
and trial comes from the Kansas City home office, timed to the case
schedule. Plan updates remain available as the medical picture evolves, so
the document presented at mediation or trial reflects current treatment
rather than a snapshot that has aged out of accuracy. The whole model is
documented on the [nationwide page](/locations/nationwide/).

## Bringing a Cortland case to the firm

Cases needing a present-value economic analysis of the plan proceed as
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/),
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the life care planning and vocational work continues under
the same engagement. The path in is the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/): caption or party names, the nature of
the claims, and the dates that will govern disclosure. Once cleared,
Cortland counsel receive a records checklist and a delivery date the firm
stands behind.
