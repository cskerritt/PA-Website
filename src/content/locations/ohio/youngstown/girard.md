## Venue context for Girard litigation

Girard is located in Trumbull County, where state-court civil matters are
heard in the Court of Common Pleas. On the federal side, Ohio litigants
appear in the United States District Court for the Northern District of Ohio
or the United States District Court for the Southern District of Ohio; the
firm does not guess at venue, and the district that will actually govern a
Girard case is nailed down during the conflict check and intake. Opinions are
drafted to satisfy the expert-evidence standards of state and federal courts
alike, and the [Ohio page](/locations/ohio/) collects the statewide picture
for counsel weighing forum questions.

## Grounding opinions in the regional market

A vocational opinion about a Girard evaluee is credible only if it reflects
the work that exists within reach of Girard. The firm builds its
employability and earning capacity findings on the Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the
Youngstown-Warren metropolitan area, on Ohio statewide estimates, and on
state workforce agency labor market information, adding direct labor market
research when a referral question demands it. The
[Youngstown-Warren metro page](/locations/ohio/youngstown/) explains the
regional framing; the Girard report applies it to the specific evaluee,
stating the commutable market chosen and the reasons for choosing it.

## How the firm appears in Girard

Honesty about presence comes before anything else: the firm has no Girard
office, staffed or otherwise, and its only staffed location is the Kansas
City home office.
What Girard counsel get instead is a remote-first engagement that moves
quickly. Conferences, records work, and most interviews are handled
remotely; in-person evaluation is arranged in the evaluee's community when
testing protocols require it; and testimony travel is scheduled from Kansas
City around the dates the court has set. The model is described in full on
the [nationwide practice page](/locations/nationwide/), and it exists
precisely so that a case's location never dilutes the quality of its
evidence.

## From inquiry to engagement

For matters that also need economic testimony, the firm offers
[coordinated damages assessment](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/),
with independent economist partners performing and signing the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning opinions proceed in
parallel. To put a Girard matter in motion, provide the caption or party
names through the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) along with the
claims and any dates already fixed by scheduling order. Clearance precedes
everything else; a tailored records checklist and a report timeline the
firm commits to and keeps follow close behind, so that Girard counsel know
early what the engagement will require and when the opinion will arrive.
