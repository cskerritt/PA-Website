What can this person still earn, and where? For a Canfield evaluee, that
question cannot be answered from a national table, because the answer lives
in the specific mix of occupations reachable from Canfield across the
Youngstown-Warren region. Purinton Analytics builds its
[vocational expert opinions](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) from the
labor market outward: residual functional capacities, then transferable
skills, then the disciplined match of both against the work that the
regional economy actually offers. The same evaluee-specific rigor carries
into the firm's [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and it
is available to whichever side of the caption retains the firm.

## County and courthouse for Canfield matters

Canfield is a Mahoning County community, and its state-court civil cases are
heard in the Court of Common Pleas. Ohio's federal trial forums are the
United States District Court for the Northern District of Ohio and the
United States District Court for the Southern District of Ohio; the firm
treats the question of which district controls a particular Canfield case as
an intake fact to be confirmed during the conflict check, not a detail to be
presumed. Reports are written to hold up wherever the case lands, and the
[Ohio state page](/locations/ohio/) gives counsel the statewide venue
overview.

## Sourcing the earning capacity analysis

The data behind a Canfield opinion is named in the opinion itself: Bureau of
Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the
Youngstown-Warren metropolitan area, corresponding Ohio statewide estimates,
state workforce agency labor market information, and direct labor market
research where a contested placement issue justifies it. The report
identifies the commutable labor market it uses for the evaluee and defends
that choice explicitly, because opposing counsel will probe it. Regional
context for the whole metro appears on the
[Youngstown-Warren page](/locations/ohio/youngstown/).

## Where and how evaluations happen

No Purinton Analytics office exists in Canfield, and the firm says so
plainly rather than borrowing the appearance of a local presence. The
staffed home office is in Kansas City; Canfield engagements run by
appointment from there. Interviews and standardized testing proceed remotely
whenever the protocols allow, and when an in-person evaluation better serves
the referral questions, it is held in the evaluee's own community. Travel
for testimony is planned from Kansas City against the deposition and trial
dates counsel provide, a model the
[nationwide page](/locations/nationwide/) describes end to end. Counsel are
told at intake which components of the evaluation can proceed remotely and
which cannot, so logistics are settled before the work begins rather than
renegotiated midstream.

## Engaging the firm from Canfield

Cases that pair vocational loss with economic loss can proceed as
[coordinated damages assessments](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/),
where independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis
while the vocational and care-planning work moves forward within the same
engagement. To start, submit the caption or the party names through the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) with a short description of the claims
and the schedule. After clearance, Canfield counsel receive a records
checklist matched to the claims and a report date the firm treats as a
commitment.
