deadline, a scheduled deposition, a mediation the parties want supported by a
real damages record. Purinton Analytics is built around that reality. From the
moment a Niles matter clears intake, the work is scheduled backward from the

[vocational opinion](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) or

buzzer. Counsel for plaintiffs and counsel for defendants receive the same

## Where Niles cases are heard

Niles is a Trumbull County city, and its state-court civil docket runs
through the Court of Common Pleas. In federal litigation, Ohio offers its
parties the United States District Court for the Northern District of Ohio
and the United States District Court for the Southern District of Ohio as
trial forums; the district that governs a particular Niles case is settled
during the conflict check and intake, and the report is written to the
standards either court applies to expert evidence. Statewide venue context
for Ohio counsel is collected on the [Ohio locations page](/locations/ohio/).

## Reading the labor market around Niles

A Niles evaluee competes for work across the surrounding metro, and the
employability analysis reflects that. Findings rest on occupational
employment and wage estimates published by the Bureau of Labor Statistics for
the Youngstown-Warren metropolitan area, supplemented by Ohio statewide
estimates, state workforce agency labor market information, and targeted
labor market research where the referral requires it. No wage figure or job
count appears in a report without a cited source, and the opinion explains
how the evaluee's residual capacities map onto occupations that actually
exist in this market. The [metro page](/locations/ohio/youngstown/) describes
the regional labor market framing that Niles matters inherit.

## Meeting and evaluating a Niles evaluee

The firm maintains its home office in Kansas City and keeps no local office
in Niles; every meeting is by appointment, and no street address is published
for this location because none exists to publish. In practice that costs
counsel nothing. Records review, attorney conferences, and most vocational
interviews proceed remotely, testing protocols permitting, and when the
referral calls for an in-person evaluation it is scheduled in the evaluee's
own community. Deposition and trial appearances are planned from Kansas City
against the dates counsel supply. Draft opinions reach counsel far enough
ahead of the deadline to allow questions about scope and sourcing, which is
where deadline-driven engagements are most often won or lost. The
[nationwide page](/locations/nationwide/) sets out how the same model serves
every market in the practice.
