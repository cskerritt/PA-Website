In a community the size of Campbell, the lawyers on opposite sides of a case
often know each other well, and an expert whose opinions bend toward whoever
retained him is quickly found out. Purinton Analytics works for plaintiffs
and for defendants in Campbell matters, and its answer to the credibility
question is structural: the same
[vocational methodology](/services/vocational-expert-witness/), the same
[life care planning standards](/services/life-care-planning/), and the same
written product no matter which side signs the engagement letter. An opinion
that would change depending on the client is not an opinion the firm will
issue.

## The courts that hear Campbell cases

Campbell sits in Mahoning County, and its state-court civil litigation is
tried in the Court of Common Pleas. Federal cases involving Ohio parties are
heard in the United States District Court for the Northern District of Ohio
and the United States District Court for the Southern District of Ohio;
rather than assert which district reaches a given Campbell dispute, the firm
resolves the question of controlling venue during the conflict check and
intake, and prepares the opinion to the evidentiary standard of the forum
that emerges. Counsel comparing venues across the state will find the
broader map on the [Ohio locations page](/locations/ohio/).

## Employability analysis in the surrounding market

For a Campbell evaluee, the relevant labor market extends across the
Youngstown-Warren region, and the vocational findings are sourced to match:
Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for
the Youngstown-Warren metropolitan area, Ohio statewide estimates, state
workforce agency labor market information, and direct research with
employers when a placement question turns on it. The
[metro page](/locations/ohio/youngstown/) sets out the regional labor market
approach; the Campbell report shows its application, occupation by
occupation, so that either side can trace every conclusion back to a source.
When testimony is required, the report's author appears at deposition or
trial prepared to walk the file through, page by page, with whoever asks.

## By-appointment service, honestly described

The firm keeps no office in Campbell and lists no address here, because it
has none; its staffed home office is in Kansas City, and everything else in
the practice is by appointment. For Campbell counsel this means remote
conferences and remote evaluation where protocols permit, evaluation in the
evaluee's own community where they do not, and travel from the Kansas City
home office for depositions, trials, and in-person assessments. Scheduling
is driven by the case calendar counsel supply at intake, and the
[nationwide page](/locations/nationwide/) documents how the model performs
across the whole practice.

## Retaining the firm for a Campbell matter

Where economic loss testimony is needed alongside the vocational and
care-planning work, the firm arranges
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis under a unified engagement structure. Every Campbell matter starts
at the same door: the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/), with the
caption or party names, a summary of the claims, and any deadlines the court
has already imposed. Clearance, a claims-tailored records checklist, and a
firm report timeline follow in that order.
