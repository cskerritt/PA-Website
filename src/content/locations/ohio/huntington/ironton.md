## Ironton's Place in the Huntington-Ashland Labor Market

Ironton sits on the Ohio side of a metropolitan labor market whose principal
city is Huntington, and vocational conclusions for an Ironton evaluee have to
respect that geography. Vocational findings rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates for the Huntington-Ashland
metropolitan area and for Ohio statewide, supplemented by state workforce
agency labor market information and direct employer research where warranted.
The analysis stays qualitative about the market and specific about the
evaluee: transferable skills, medically supported restrictions, and the
commuting range that is realistic for the individual determine which
occupations count as genuinely available. That discipline matters in a market
that crosses state lines, where an opinion built on the wrong geography
invites cross-examination.
