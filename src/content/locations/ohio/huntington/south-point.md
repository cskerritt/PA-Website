South Point is a small Ohio community whose working life is measured in
commutes. Its residents participate in the Huntington-Ashland labor market, a
metropolitan economy organized around Huntington, the metro's principal city,
and a damages analysis that treats the town as a self-contained economy will
misstate what an injured worker there can actually earn. Purinton Analytics
serves attorneys with South Point matters, in plaintiff and defense
engagements alike, with vocational evaluations, life care plans, and damages
coordination grounded in the market as it actually operates.

## Venue for South Point Matters

State court litigation arising in South Point is heard in Lawrence County,
where the trial court of general jurisdiction is the Court of Common Pleas. On
the federal side, Ohio's district courts are the United States District Court
for the Northern District of Ohio and the United States District Court for the
Southern District of Ohio; the district that controls any particular South
Point filing is confirmed during the conflict check and intake, together with
any venue-specific qualification requirement. Because the surrounding metro
spans neighboring states, a South Point caption sometimes travels with related
matters filed elsewhere in the region; the firm's reports use the same
structure and sourcing wherever the case lands, so counsel can rely on
consistency across forums.

## A Labor Market That Crosses State Lines

For a South Point evaluee, the relevant market is the Huntington-Ashland
metropolitan area, not the town in isolation. Vocational findings draw on
Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for that
metropolitan area and for Ohio statewide, along with state workforce agency
labor market information and, where warranted, direct contact with employers.
The commuting patterns that define daily life here also define the analysis:
whether an occupation is genuinely available to a specific evaluee depends on
transferable skills, documented restrictions, and a realistic commuting
radius, assessed individually rather than assumed. The firm describes the
market qualitatively and ties every conclusion about the individual to the
record, a practice designed to survive scrutiny from the opposing table.

## How a South Point Evaluation Proceeds

There is no Purinton Analytics office in South Point, and the firm does not
pretend otherwise; every meeting is by appointment, and no local street
address is published. Most engagements begin remotely, with records review and
a structured vocational interview conducted by video at the evaluee's
convenience. When an in-person assessment is warranted, it takes place in the
evaluee's own community rather than requiring travel to a distant office, with
the evaluator traveling from the firm's Kansas City home office. The same
travel arrangements cover depositions and trial testimony. Broader regional
logistics appear on the
[Huntington-Ashland metro page](/locations/west-virginia/huntington/), and the

figures alongside the vocational work,
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
are available, with independent economist partners performing and signing the
economic analysis while vocational and care-planning work proceeds under a
single engagement structure.
