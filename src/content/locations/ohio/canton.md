When the evaluee lives in Canton or Massillon, the analysis should reflect
the labor market where that person actually lives and works, not a national
abstraction. That principle organizes everything Purinton Analytics does in
the Canton-Massillon metro, where the firm provides
[vocational expert evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) for plaintiff
and defense counsel alike. An earning capacity opinion or a care plan is
only as strong as its connection to the evaluee's own community, and the
firm builds that connection into the record from the initial records request.

## Stark and Carroll county venues

Engagements are accepted for matters in the Stark County Court of Common
Pleas and the Carroll County Court of Common Pleas. In federal court, Ohio
matters proceed in the United States District Court for the Northern
District of Ohio or the United States District Court for the Southern
District of Ohio, depending on where the case is filed, and the firm's
reports are drafted to meet the disclosure expectations of either forum.
Counsel whose practices extend past the metro will find the firm's broader
statewide model, and its other Ohio metros, on the
[Ohio locations page](/locations/ohio/).

## Appointment-based service in the metro

Canton meetings are by appointment. The firm keeps no staffed office in the
metro and publishes no street address for this location, and it says so
plainly because honesty about presence is part of the same discipline that
governs its written work. For a local evaluee, that model works in both
directions: much of the engagement proceeds remotely, through records
review, structured interviews by video, and standardized testing under
publisher protocols where remote administration is supported, while
in-person evaluation, when the referral questions warrant it, is conducted
in the evaluee's own community after travel from the Kansas City home
office. Deposition and trial appearances are scheduled the same way, with
dates confirmed early so counsel can plan around them.

## Pricing the local market, not the national average

Vocational findings for evaluees in the metro rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the
Canton-Massillon metropolitan area and for Ohio statewide, supplemented by
state workforce agency labor market information and direct labor market
research where warranted. Life care plans and medical cost projections are
priced to the evaluee's own community using provider-specific inquiries and
published fee data for the region, since the realistic cost of future care
here is a local fact that must be established, not assumed. Because each
figure is cited to its source, the resulting report can be tested by
opposing counsel and relied on by retaining counsel in equal measure.
