Every Streetsboro engagement Purinton Analytics accepts follows the same sequence, and the sequence is the point: conflicts cleared and documented, scope defined in writing, records requested against the claims, assessment completed, opinion delivered. That procedural discipline is what makes the firm's [vocational expert](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) and [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) opinions usable by plaintiff or defense counsel in a Streetsboro case, and it is the reason the reports read the same to both sides. Streetsboro is served within the firm's [Akron metro practice](/locations/ohio/akron/).

## From intake to opinion

Intake fixes the referral questions and the delivery schedule. The records phase gathers medical, employment, educational, and earnings documentation matched to the claims, and the report inventories what was reviewed. The assessment phase evaluates the evaluee's residual capacity, transferable skills, and future care needs, and where the case requires it, [medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/) work is prepared on the same documented footing. The written opinion then states its methodology, cites its sources, and stops where the evidence stops.

The firm's process, and the report it produces, do not change between forums.

## Labor market foundations

A Streetsboro evaluee's employment prospects extend across the Akron metropolitan commuting area, and the vocational analysis is anchored there. Its data spine is Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Akron metropolitan area and for Ohio statewide, reinforced by state workforce agency labor market information and by direct labor market research when the referral questions turn on particular occupations or employers. Market conditions are described qualitatively, and each description carries its citation, so the opinion invites verification instead of resisting it.
