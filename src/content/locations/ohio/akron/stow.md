## Earning capacity in the wider metro

A Stow resident's employment prospects are metro-wide, and the analysis treats them that way. Vocational conclusions rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Akron metropolitan area and for Ohio statewide, together with state workforce agency labor market information and, where the questions require it, direct contact with employers in the relevant occupations. The report describes qualitatively what the local market offers a person with the evaluee's residual capacity, training, and work history, and it distinguishes clearly between what the data show and what remains judgment.
