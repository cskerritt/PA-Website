Not every Aurora case needs an original expert opinion; some need an existing one taken apart. Purinton Analytics does both. Alongside its [vocational expert](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) and [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) practice, the firm performs [rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) of opposing vocational and care-planning reports for Aurora counsel, applying the same methodological yardstick to the other side's work that it applies to its own. Aurora is covered through the firm's [Akron metro practice](/locations/ohio/akron/).

## Rebuttal with a straight face

A rebuttal assignment for an Aurora case examines whether the opposing report's data sources support its conclusions, whether its transferable skills reasoning is traceable, and whether its care projections follow from the medical record. The review says so plainly when the opposing work holds up, because a rebuttal that finds fault on command is worthless to the court and, eventually, to the client. That evenhandedness is possible because the firm works for plaintiff and defense counsel alike and expects each report it writes to be dissected by the other side.

## Portage County and the federal courts

Aurora lies in Portage County, and its state-court civil matters proceed in the Portage County Court of Common Pleas. Federal litigation involving Aurora parties may be venued in the United States District Court for the Northern District of Ohio or the United States District Court for the Southern District of Ohio; the controlling district for a specific matter, and any venue-specific qualification requirement, is identified and confirmed during the conflict check and intake process. Original and rebuttal work alike are drafted to serve either forum without adjustment.

## Grounding opinions in the metro market

Whether the assignment is original analysis or review of someone else's, the labor market reference points are the same: Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Akron metropolitan area and for Ohio statewide, state workforce agency labor market information, and direct labor market research where a referral question requires it. An Aurora evaluee's realistic options span the metro's commuting area, and the firm characterizes those options qualitatively with every claim tied to a source, which is also the standard it holds opposing reports to.
