A useful test for any expert report is whether opposing counsel could reconstruct its conclusions from its stated sources. That test governs every Hudson engagement Purinton Analytics accepts. The firm provides [vocational expert](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) and [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) services for Hudson litigation, retained by plaintiff or defense, and its working assumption is that whichever side did not retain it will read the report most carefully. Hudson matters fall within the firm's [Akron metro coverage](/locations/ohio/akron/).

## Method before opinion

Each Hudson assignment starts from the same sequence: a documented conflict check, a scope defined in writing, a records request tailored to the claims, and only then an assessment. Vocational conclusions are built from a structured interview, transferable skills analysis, and labor market data that is cited, not gestured at. Life care plans tie every projected service to the medical record and to clinical reasoning stated in the report itself. When the assignment is testing someone else's work rather than building an original opinion, the firm's [rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) practice applies the identical standard in reverse.

## Courts serving Hudson

Hudson is in Summit County, and its state civil litigation proceeds in the Summit County Court of Common Pleas. Federal matters involving Hudson parties are heard in Ohio's federal forums, the United States District Court for the Northern District of Ohio and the United States District Court for the Southern District of Ohio, with the controlling venue and any venue-specific qualification requirement identified and confirmed during the conflict check and intake process. Nothing in the report's rigor depends on which of these courts it is filed in.

## Reading the labor market honestly

For a Hudson evaluee, employability and earning capacity are assessed against the Akron metropolitan labor market as a whole, since that is where Hudson residents actually work. The firm draws on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Akron metropolitan area and for Ohio statewide, on state workforce agency labor market information, and on direct labor market research when a referral question demands it. The report characterizes occupational demand qualitatively and attributes every characterization to its source, because an unsourced market claim is an invitation to impeachment.

## Presence, honestly stated

The firm has no Hudson office and claims none. Meetings are by appointment; evaluations are conducted by secure video or in person in the evaluee's own community; and when evaluation, deposition, or trial work requires being in Summit County, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office. Cases needing economic quantification can proceed as [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/), in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement structure.
