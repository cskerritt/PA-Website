Damages in a serious Kent case rarely rest on a single expert opinion. Earning capacity, future care, and economic loss each demand their own analysis, and the seams between those analyses are where cross-examination probes first. Purinton Analytics structures Kent engagements so the seams hold. The firm provides [vocational expert](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) and [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) opinions for plaintiff and defense counsel, and when the case needs economic quantification as well, it offers [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement structure. Kent is served within the firm's [Akron metro practice](/locations/ohio/akron/).

## Portage County venue, Ohio's federal forums

Kent's county is Portage County, and civil actions filed in state court proceed in the Portage County Court of Common Pleas. Federal matters in Ohio are heard in the United States District Court for the Northern District of Ohio and the United States District Court for the Southern District of Ohio; the forum that controls a given Kent case, together with any venue-specific qualification requirement, is identified and confirmed during the conflict check and intake process. The firm's written work does not vary by forum: methodology stated, data sources cited in full, and conclusions held to the record, so the opinion reads the same to retaining counsel, opposing counsel, and the court.

## One coordination point, separate signatures

In a coordinated engagement, the vocational findings and the life care plan feed the economist partners' loss calculations on a shared factual foundation, so counsel is not reconciling experts on the eve of disclosure and the opposing side cannot pry the damages presentation apart at its joints. Each report still carries its own author's signature and stays within its own discipline. The economic analysis is the economists' work, performed and signed independently, while Purinton Analytics coordinates scope, scheduling, and the exchange of assumptions. Counsel manages a defined engagement structure instead of juggling separate retentions that may drift apart.

## Kent's labor market, read regionally

Vocational findings for a Kent evaluee rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Akron metropolitan area and for Ohio statewide, joined by state workforce agency labor market information and direct labor market research when a referral question turns on the availability of specific work. The analysis stays qualitative where the discipline requires it: it names the occupations open to the evaluee given residual capacity, education, and work history, characterizes the demand for them, and cites the source behind each characterization rather than gesturing at the market in general.
