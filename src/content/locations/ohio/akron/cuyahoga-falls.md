In a catastrophic injury case, the life care plan is often the exhibit that gets read hardest. Every projected therapy, every equipment replacement schedule, every category of attendant care is an invitation to cross-examination, and a plan that cannot defend its line items can pull the rest of the damages case down with it. Purinton Analytics prepares [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) and [vocational expert](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) opinions for Cuyahoga Falls litigation with that reading in mind, serving plaintiff and defense counsel alike as part of its [Akron metro practice](/locations/ohio/akron/) and its [nationwide](/locations/nationwide/) coverage.

## A care plan tied to the medical record

A life care plan prepared for a Cuyahoga Falls evaluee begins with the medical record, not with a template. Future care needs are itemized against the treating providers' documentation, the clinical reasoning behind each recommended service is stated in the report itself, and where the case calls for valuation of future medical needs, [medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/) work is prepared on the same documented footing. The plan separates what the record establishes from what clinical judgment infers, and it says plainly which is which, because a plan that blurs that line invites the deposition question it cannot answer.

## Earning capacity in the Akron labor market

The vocational side of the analysis treats the labor market as regional, because the occupations realistically open to an injured worker are not confined to the town line. Findings for a Cuyahoga Falls evaluee rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Akron metropolitan area and for Ohio statewide, supplemented by state workforce agency labor market information and direct labor market research where a referral question requires it. Demand is characterized qualitatively, each characterization is tied to a named source, and pre-injury and post-injury capacity are compared on the same data foundation, so the loss opinion is traceable from conclusion back to data.
