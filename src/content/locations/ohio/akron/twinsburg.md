## A commuter's labor market, measured properly

The starting point for any Twinsburg earning capacity opinion is Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Akron metropolitan area and for Ohio statewide. State workforce agency labor market information adds occupational outlook context, and direct labor market research fills the gaps when a referral question turns on specific employers or specific work. The analysis matches the evaluee's residual functional capacity, education, and work history against occupations that exist in this commuting region, describes demand for them qualitatively, and shows its sources at every step. Pre-injury and post-injury capacity are compared on the same data foundation, so the loss opinion is a difference between documented figures of merit, not an impression.
