Expert retention in a Green case is usually driven by a date on the docket: an expert disclosure deadline, a discovery cutoff, a mediation the report must be ready for. Purinton Analytics builds its Green engagements backward from those dates. The firm provides [vocational expert](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) and [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) services to plaintiff and defense counsel alike, and it treats the disclosure calendar as a design constraint from the first call, not a complication discovered later. Green sits within the firm's [Akron metro practice](/locations/ohio/akron/).

## Working to the calendar

Once the conflict check clears, scope and delivery dates are set against the court's schedule: records requests go out early enough to be answered, the evaluee assessment is placed on the calendar with the deadline in view, and drafting time is reserved so the written opinion is complete before disclosure rather than finalized against it. Rebuttal timelines get the same treatment; when the assignment is responding to an opposing expert, the firm's [rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) work is scoped to the reply deadline from the outset.

## Venue for Green matters

Green is a Summit County city, and its state-court civil cases are heard in the Summit County Court of Common Pleas. Federal matters may proceed in the United States District Court for the Northern District of Ohio or the United States District Court for the Southern District of Ohio; the forum that controls, and any qualification or registration requirement particular to it, is identified and confirmed during the conflict check and intake process. The report itself is forum-neutral in its rigor: stated methodology, cited sources, and opinions confined to what the record supports.

## The labor market a Green evaluee actually faces

Green's workforce participates in the broader Akron metropolitan labor market, and vocational conclusions are grounded accordingly. The firm relies on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Akron metropolitan area and for Ohio statewide, on state workforce agency labor market information, and on direct labor market research where a specific occupation or employer question warrants it. Scale and demand are described qualitatively, consistent with the firm's practice of writing every sentence so opposing counsel can check it.

## Evaluation without a local office

The firm has no office in Green and publishes no address there; meetings are by appointment only. Evaluations are conducted by secure video or in person in the evaluee's community, and when a deposition, trial, or in-person assessment requires attendance in Summit County, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office. For matters needing economic quantification, [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) are available, in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement structure. The firm's statewide coverage is outlined on the [Ohio page](/locations/ohio/) and its national reach on the [nationwide page](/locations/nationwide/).
