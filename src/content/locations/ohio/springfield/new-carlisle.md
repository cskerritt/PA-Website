## Where a New Carlisle dispute is heard

New Carlisle sits in Clark County, and state-law claims are tried in the
Clark County Court of Common Pleas. On the federal side, Ohio's available
forums are the United States District Court for the Northern District of
Ohio and the United States District Court for the Southern District of
Ohio; which district controls a particular filing is confirmed with counsel
during the conflict check and intake rather than assumed. The forum shapes
the disclosure calendar and any venue-specific qualification requirement,
but it never changes the work product: the same defined scope, documented
methodology, and source-by-source citation appear whether the reader is a
common pleas judge or a federal one.

## Reading a small town against a metro labor market

Employability is never judged against New Carlisle's own borders. A
resident's realistic labor market is the commutable area, which reaches
into Springfield and across the surrounding metro, and the firm's opinions
are grounded accordingly: Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational
employment and wage estimates for the Springfield metropolitan area and
for Ohio statewide, state workforce agency labor market information, and
direct labor market research when the referral questions call for it. The
report explains how the commutable market was drawn, why particular
occupations were included or excluded, and where each wage observation
originates, so opposing counsel can trace every step from data to opinion
rather than take any of it on faith.

## Evaluation logistics without a local office

The firm keeps no office in New Carlisle and publishes no Ohio street
address, because accuracy about its own footprint is part of the same
discipline that governs its reports. Records arrive electronically and are
reviewed against a written scope. Conferences with counsel, opinion
discussions, and deposition preparation proceed by video and telephone.
When the referral requires an in-person vocational evaluation, it is
arranged in the evaluee's own community, so an injured or displaced worker
is assessed where daily life actually happens rather than in an unfamiliar
city. When deposition or trial dates demand physical presence, travel is
scheduled from the Kansas City home office against the court's calendar.
This is the same model the firm applies across its
[national practice](/locations/nationwide/), and New Carlisle is served no
differently.
