## Forum questions for a Rittman caption

Rittman lies in Wayne County, so a state-court civil action involving a Rittman party is ordinarily filed in the Wayne County Court of Common Pleas. Federal claims proceed in the United States District Court for the Northern District of Ohio or the United States District Court for the Southern District of Ohio, and the firm does not guess between them: the controlling forum, and whatever qualification or disclosure obligations attach to it, are confirmed during the conflict check and intake process. Whichever court ends up with the case, the expert file is built to a single consistent standard, with methodology stated on the face of the report and every source identified so opposing counsel can retrace the analysis.

## Measuring what the commuting market offers

The analytical work in a Rittman matter starts from published data and ends in disclosed judgment. Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Wooster area and for Ohio statewide anchor the occupational analysis; state workforce agency labor market information adds the administrative picture; and where the referral questions justify it, the firm conducts direct labor market research with employers in the relevant occupations. Conclusions are expressed qualitatively: what kinds of work remain open to a person with the evaluee's capacities and history, how transferable the skills are, and where the data leave off and professional judgment begins. No claim is made that a spreadsheet cannot support.
