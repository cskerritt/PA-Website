Doylestown is a Wayne County community, and the injury, employment, and disability disputes that arise here are tried in Wayne County's courts unless federal jurisdiction pulls them elsewhere. Purinton Analytics serves counsel in those disputes from within its [Wooster metro practice](/locations/ohio/wooster/), providing [vocational expert](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) and [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) opinions written for the courtroom the case will actually occupy, and written so that either side of the caption can probe them line by line.

## From the Court of Common Pleas to the federal districts

The state trial forum for a Doylestown civil matter is the Wayne County Court of Common Pleas. If the claim belongs in federal court, Ohio offers the United States District Court for the Northern District of Ohio and the United States District Court for the Southern District of Ohio as its available forums; which of them controls a given caption is a question the firm resolves with counsel during the conflict check and intake process, together with any venue-specific registration or qualification requirement. Because the forum is settled at intake, the report is drafted from the outset against the disclosure practice of the court that will receive it, rather than retrofitted late in the schedule.

## A village evaluee, a regional market

Doylestown is a small community, and the honest analytical consequence is that its residents' employment prospects must be measured against the wider region rather than the village itself. The firm's occupational analysis draws on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Wooster area and for Ohio statewide, on state workforce agency labor market information, and on direct labor market research where the referral questions warrant that step. What the opinion offers is qualitative and testable: the categories of work realistically available to the evaluee given residual capacity, training, and history, with the boundary between published data and professional judgment marked rather than blurred.

## Where and how the assessment takes place

Counsel retaining the firm for a Doylestown evaluee should expect no local storefront and no published street address here; the firm states that plainly because overstating presence would sit poorly beside a practice built on verifiable opinions. Assessment is delivered where the evaluee is. Records move electronically and are reviewed remotely, the interview and any testing occur by secure video or in person in the evaluee's own community, and in-person work, depositions, and trial appearances are traveled from the Kansas City home office on dates coordinated with the court's schedule. The same structure supports [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement structure, keeping every expert within their own discipline.
