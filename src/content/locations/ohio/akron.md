Expert deadlines, not distance, are usually the pressing question when Akron
counsel reach out: whether a disclosure date can be met, whether an
evaluation can be completed before the discovery cutoff, and whether the
expert will be available when the case is tried. Purinton Analytics
structures its Akron work around those dates, providing
[vocational expert evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[expert testimony and litigation consulting](/services/expert-testimony-litigation-consulting/)
to plaintiff and defense counsel across the metro on schedules that are
committed to at intake and kept.

## The Akron metro's venue footprint

Engagements are accepted for matters in the Summit County Court of Common
Pleas and the Portage County Court of Common Pleas. Ohio federal matters
proceed in the United States District Court for the Northern District of
Ohio or the United States District Court for the Southern District of Ohio,
depending on where the case is filed, and the firm prepares every report to
meet the stricter forum's disclosure standards rather than the more lenient
forum's. Counsel whose cases reach beyond the metro will find the
statewide practice described on the [Ohio locations page](/locations/ohio/),
with the same intake process and the same report conventions across venues.

## Presence by appointment

Akron meetings are by appointment. The firm keeps no staffed office in the
metro and publishes no street address for this location; that candor about
presence is part of the same discipline that governs its written opinions.
The practical consequence for counsel is flexibility rather than absence.
Records review, structured vocational interviews, and standardized testing
under publisher protocols that support remote administration all proceed
without travel, which protects the calendar in deadline-driven cases. When
an in-person evaluation is warranted, or when deposition or trial testimony
requires the expert in the room, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City
home office with enough lead time to honor the commitments made at intake.

## Labor market research for Akron evaluees

Vocational findings for evaluees in the metro rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Akron
metropolitan area and for Ohio statewide, together with state workforce
agency labor market information and direct labor market research where the
referral questions call for it. Life care plans and medical cost projections
are priced to the evaluee's own community through provider-specific
inquiries and published fee data rather than national assumptions. Where the
timeline is short, the research is sequenced so that preliminary findings can
inform mediation while the full report is completed for disclosure. Because
every figure is tied to a cited source, the report serves either side of the
caption equally well, and it arrives in a form that supports
cross-examination rather than inviting it.

## Starting an Akron engagement

Availability is confirmed through a conflict check before the merits of the
case are discussed. Submit the caption or party names through the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and state the operative deadlines,
including any imminent disclosure, deposition, or trial dates, so that the
schedule can be evaluated honestly before the engagement is accepted. For
related matters venued outside Ohio, the firm's
[national coverage](/locations/nationwide/) describes how the same practice
model serves courts nationwide.
