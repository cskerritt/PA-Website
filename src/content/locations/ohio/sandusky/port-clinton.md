## Ottawa County courts and the federal alternatives

Port Clinton matters filed in state court proceed in the Court of Common
Pleas in Ottawa County. Ohio's federal trial forums are the United States
District Court for the Northern District of Ohio and the United States
District Court for the Southern District of Ohio; which of them governs a
given caption is a venue question confirmed during the conflict check and
intake, never guessed at. What stays constant across forums is the working
method. Before any record is reviewed, the conflict screen is documented. The
scope is reduced to writing. The records request is built from the pleadings,
so the eventual report rests on materials both parties can examine and
challenge.
