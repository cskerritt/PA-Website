In a small community like Oak Harbor, the commute defines the labor market
more than the town limits do. Residents work across the Sandusky metropolitan
area, and a credible earning capacity opinion has to reflect that reach
rather than treat the village as its own economy. That is the analytical
starting point when Purinton Analytics accepts an Oak Harbor referral, whether
it comes from plaintiff or defense counsel, and whether the assignment is
[vocational evaluation and expert testimony](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), or a
[medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/).

## The courts that hear Oak Harbor disputes

Oak Harbor lies in Ottawa County, where state-court claims are tried in the
Court of Common Pleas. Where a claim is federal, Ohio's forums are the United
States District Court for the Northern District of Ohio and the United States
District Court for the Southern District of Ohio; the controlling venue for
any particular matter is settled during the conflict check and intake. The
firm's habits do not change with the courthouse. A documented conflict screen
precedes any review of records, the engagement scope is fixed in writing, and
the file is assembled so that each finding in the eventual report traces to a
source available to both sides of the case.

## Measuring earning capacity from a small town

Because Oak Harbor's own employer base is limited, the vocational analysis
leans on regional data with disclosed sources. Occupational employment and
wage estimates from the Bureau of Labor Statistics for the Sandusky
metropolitan area, read alongside Ohio statewide series, form the foundation;
state workforce agency labor market information and direct labor market
research fill in where the evaluee's transferable skills raise questions the
published tables cannot answer. The result is an opinion about what work
exists within realistic commuting range and what it pays, stated with
citations a cross-examiner can verify line by line. Counsel comparing venues
will find the same methodology described on the
[Sandusky metro page](/locations/ohio/sandusky/) and across the firm's
[Ohio coverage](/locations/ohio/).
