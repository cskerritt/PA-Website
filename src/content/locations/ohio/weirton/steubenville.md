## Reading a labor market that crosses a state line

A residual earning capacity opinion for a Steubenville evaluee has to
describe the labor market the evaluee actually faces, which in this metro
extends beyond the town and beyond Ohio. Vocational findings rest on Bureau
of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the
Weirton-Steubenville metropolitan area and for Ohio statewide, supplemented
by state workforce agency labor market information and direct labor market
research where the referral questions warrant it. The analysis stays
qualitative about character and explicit about sources: transferable skills
are mapped against occupations that actually exist in the regional economy,
and every data source is cited so the opinion can be tested on
cross-examination rather than taken on faith.
