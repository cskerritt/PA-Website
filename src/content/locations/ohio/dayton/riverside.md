Most of the work in a Riverside expert engagement never happens in a
conference room. It happens in the file: medical records read closely, work
histories reconstructed, labor market sources pulled and cited, drafts checked
against the record before anything is signed. Purinton Analytics runs its
[vocational expert](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) and
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) practice remote-first for
exactly that reason, which lets Riverside counsel on either side of a case
retain the firm without geography adding cost or delay.

## The courts a Riverside case answers to

Riverside is a Montgomery County community, and civil matters arising here are
generally venued in the Montgomery County Court of Common Pleas, where the
firm accepts personal injury, employment, workers' compensation, and family
law engagements. Federal claims in Ohio are heard in the United States
District Court for the Northern District of Ohio or the United States District
Court for the Southern District of Ohio; the firm confirms which district
controls a particular matter during the conflict check and intake process,
never by assumption, and identifies any venue-specific registration or
qualification requirement at the same time. The remote posture changes
nothing about the standard of the written opinion, which is prepared for
examination by both sides.

## Labor market data without a windshield survey

Distance does not weaken a labor market analysis when the method is sound.
Vocational findings for Riverside evaluees rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates for the Dayton metropolitan area
and for Ohio statewide, on state workforce agency labor market information,
and on direct labor market research when the referral questions call for it.
The evaluee's position in that regional market is assessed through documented
functional capacity and transferable skills, and the conclusions are stated
qualitatively and conservatively, so that the opinion claims no more than its
sources can prove.

## What remote-first means in practice

The firm keeps no staffed office in Riverside and publishes no street address
for this location; meetings are by appointment, and the practice says so
plainly rather than implying a storefront that does not exist. A typical
engagement proceeds through secure video interviews and electronic records
exchange, with written opinions delivered on the disclosure schedule counsel
sets. Remote-first is not remote-only: when the referral questions require an
in-person interview or evaluation, it is arranged in the evaluee's own
community, and when a deposition or trial date requires the expert present,
travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office. Counsel can also add a
[medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/) or retain
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis
while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement
structure.
