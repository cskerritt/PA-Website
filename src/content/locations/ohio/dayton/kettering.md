## Montgomery County venues for a Kettering matter

Civil claims arising in Kettering are generally venued in the Montgomery County
Court of Common Pleas, and engagements are accepted for personal injury,
employment, workers' compensation, and family matters heard there. Ohio's
federal forums are the United States District Court for the Northern District
of Ohio and the United States District Court for the Southern District of Ohio;
rather than assuming which district a particular case belongs to, the firm
confirms the controlling forum during conflict check and intake, along with any
venue-specific registration or qualification requirement. Whatever the forum,
the work product is the same: a defined scope, a records request tailored to
the claims, and a written opinion whose sources opposing experts can retrace.

## Reading the labor market around an anchor suburb

A Kettering evaluee typically works somewhere in the wider
Dayton-Kettering-Beavercreek labor market, whether in town or across the line
in Dayton itself, so vocational findings are grounded in Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Dayton
metropolitan area and for Ohio statewide, supplemented by state workforce
agency labor market information and by direct labor market research when the
referral questions warrant it. The analysis proceeds from documented
functional capacity through transferable skills to the realistic availability
of work, and every step is cited, because the opinion has to survive
cross-examination, not just reach a number that pleases the retaining side.

## How the firm appears in Kettering

Meetings in Kettering are by appointment only. The firm keeps no staffed
office here and publishes no street address for this location, because
accuracy about presence is part of the same discipline that governs the
reports. Most engagements proceed remotely, through records review, secure
video interviews, and written opinions delivered on the disclosure schedule
counsel sets. When the referral calls for an in-person interview, a testing
session, or testimony, the appearance is arranged in the evaluee's own
community or at the courthouse, with travel scheduled from the Kansas City
home office. Counsel whose matters pair earning capacity with future medical
needs can add a [medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/)
or a full life care plan under the same engagement, and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
are available in which independent economist partners perform and sign the
economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under
one engagement structure.
