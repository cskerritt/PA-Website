A Trotwood engagement with Purinton Analytics follows a fixed sequence, and
the sequence is the point. Conflicts are checked before any substance is
discussed. Scope is defined in writing before records move. The records
request is tailored to the claims, the analysis is documented as it is built,
and the [vocational expert](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) or
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) opinion that results is
signed only when the file supports it. Plaintiff and defense counsel in
Trotwood retain the same process, because process is what makes an expert
worth retaining.

## Venue in Montgomery County

Trotwood civil matters are generally heard in the Montgomery County Court of
Common Pleas, where the firm accepts engagements in personal injury,
employment, workers' compensation, and domestic cases. For claims that proceed
federally, Ohio's forums are the United States District Court for the Northern
District of Ohio and the United States District Court for the Southern
District of Ohio, and the district that governs a specific Trotwood matter is
one more thing the intake process confirms rather than assumes, along with any
registration or qualification requirement particular to the venue. The
disclosure rules differ between forums; the discipline behind the report does
not.

## The labor market step of the sequence

When the analysis reaches the labor market, it reaches for sources both sides
can verify. Vocational findings for a Trotwood evaluee rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Dayton
metropolitan area and for Ohio statewide, on state workforce agency labor
market information, and on direct labor market research where the referral
questions warrant it. Because Trotwood workers participate in the employment
market of the wider Dayton-Kettering-Beavercreek region, the analysis is
framed regionally and described qualitatively, and it connects functional
capacity, transferable skills, and available work in an order an opposing
expert can audit step by step.

## Where and how meetings happen

The firm keeps no staffed office in Trotwood and publishes no street address
for this location. Meetings are by appointment, and most engagements proceed
remotely, through secure video interviews, electronic records review, and
written opinions delivered on the disclosure schedule counsel sets. When the
referral questions call for an in-person interview or evaluation, it is
arranged in the evaluee's own community, and travel for evaluations,
depositions, and trial testimony is scheduled from the Kansas City home
office. Counsel building a complete damages presentation can add a
[medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/) or use
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis
while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement
structure, keeping the sequence intact across disciplines.

## Step one, always

Every Trotwood engagement opens the same way: the caption or party names go
through the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/), together with any
disclosure, deposition, or trial dates counsel already has on the calendar.
Nothing else happens until conflicts clear. From there, the practice described
on the [Dayton metro page](/locations/ohio/dayton/) and the
[Ohio state page](/locations/ohio/) is available locally, and the
[nationwide practice](/locations/nationwide/) applies the identical sequence
to matters venued anywhere in the country.
