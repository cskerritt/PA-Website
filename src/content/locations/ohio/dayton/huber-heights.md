Strip a Huber Heights damages case down to its expert questions and a short
list remains: what could the injured person earn before, what can they earn
now, and what will their future care require. Everything else in the file
exists to answer that list, and so does Purinton Analytics. The firm prepares
[vocational expert](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) and
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) opinions for plaintiff and
defense counsel alike, each one written so the answers can be traced back to
the record that produced them.

## Where a Huber Heights claim is heard

Huber Heights sits in Montgomery County, and civil matters arising here are
generally venued in the Montgomery County Court of Common Pleas, where
engagements are accepted for personal injury, employment, workers'
compensation, and family law matters. Federal claims in Ohio proceed in the
United States District Court for the Northern District of Ohio or the United
States District Court for the Southern District of Ohio, and the firm treats
the question of which district controls a particular case as something to be
confirmed during conflict check and intake, never assumed. Every opinion is
prepared as if the toughest reader will be opposing counsel, because in this
work that reader always is.

## Answering the earning capacity questions

The earning questions are answered against the regional market. Vocational
findings for a Huber Heights evaluee rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates for the Dayton metropolitan area
and for Ohio statewide, along with state workforce agency labor market
information and, where the referral questions justify it, direct labor market
research. Scale and geography are described qualitatively. The method is what
carries the weight: functional capacity drawn from the medical record,
transferable skills identified from the actual work history, and conclusions
limited to what a vocational expert can properly say about the work realistically
available in the Dayton-Kettering-Beavercreek regional economy.

## Answering the future care question

The care side of the list gets the same treatment. A life care plan built here
itemizes future medical and support needs from the treating record and
clinical foundation, not from templates, and counsel who need the cost side
documented can add a [medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/).
The firm maintains no staffed office in Huber Heights and publishes no street
address for this location; that candor is deliberate, and meetings are by
appointment. Most engagements proceed remotely, through records review, secure
video interviews, and written opinions delivered on the disclosure schedule
counsel sets. When an in-person interview or testing session is warranted, it
is arranged in the evaluee's own community, and when deposition or trial
requires presence, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office. Where
the full damages picture is in play,
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
are available in which independent economist partners perform and sign the
economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under
one engagement structure.
