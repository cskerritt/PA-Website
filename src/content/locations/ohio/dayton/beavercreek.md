## The Greene County answer: venue

Civil matters arising in Beavercreek are generally heard in the Greene County
Court of Common Pleas, and engagements are accepted for personal injury,
employment, workers' compensation, and domestic matters venued there. When a
claim belongs in federal court, Ohio offers the United States District Court
for the Northern District of Ohio and the United States District Court for the
Southern District of Ohio; the firm does not assume which district controls a
given Beavercreek matter, and instead confirms the forum, together with any
registration or qualification requirement it carries, during the conflict
check and intake process. The report is drafted to the same standard either
way, with a documented methodology and sources cited so that opposing experts
can retrace every step.

## The regional answer: labor market

Earning capacity is not a courthouse question, so the analysis widens its
frame. Vocational findings for a Beavercreek evaluee rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Dayton
metropolitan area and for Ohio statewide, alongside state workforce agency
labor market information and direct labor market research when the referral
questions call for it. Geography stays qualitative; method stays rigorous. The
analysis moves from functional capacity as documented by treating and
examining sources, through transferable skills, to the work actually available
to this evaluee in this market, and it declines to speculate where the record
runs out.

## Appointments, not an office

The firm keeps no staffed office in Beavercreek and publishes no street
address for this location; meetings are by appointment, and candor about that
arrangement is part of the same discipline the reports themselves follow. In
practice, most Beavercreek engagements are handled remotely, with records
review, secure video interviews, and written opinions delivered on the
disclosure schedule counsel sets. Where the questions require an in-person
evaluation, or where deposition and trial dates require presence, travel is
scheduled from the Kansas City home office and the meeting takes place in the
evaluee's own community. Counsel can pair the vocational opinion with a
[medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/) or a life care
plan, and [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
are available in which independent economist partners perform and sign the
economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under
one engagement structure.
