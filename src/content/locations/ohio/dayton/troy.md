The most useful place to evaluate a Troy evaluee is Troy. An injury is lived
where the person lives, and an in-person interview or testing session carries
more weight when it happens in the evaluee's own community rather than in a
distant office chosen for the expert's convenience. Purinton Analytics builds
its [vocational expert](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) and
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) practice around that
principle, serving plaintiff and defense counsel in Troy matters with opinions
grounded in the record and, when warranted, in an evaluation conducted where
the evaluee actually is.

## Miami County venue for Troy matters

Civil claims arising in Troy are generally heard in the Miami County Court of
Common Pleas, and the firm accepts engagements there in personal injury,
employment, workers' compensation, and domestic matters. Where a claim
proceeds federally, Ohio's forums are the United States District Court for the
Northern District of Ohio and the United States District Court for the
Southern District of Ohio; the controlling district for any particular Troy
case, and any venue-specific registration or qualification requirement, is
identified and confirmed during the conflict check and intake process rather
than presumed from geography. The written opinion is built for whichever
courtroom receives it, with a documented methodology both sides can examine.

## A labor market larger than the town

However local the evaluation, the labor market analysis cannot stop at the
municipal limits. Vocational findings for a Troy evaluee are anchored in
Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the
Dayton metropolitan area and for Ohio statewide, in state workforce agency
labor market information, and in direct labor market research where the
referral questions warrant it. Scale is described qualitatively rather than
counted. What matters is the chain of reasoning, from functional capacity
documented by treating and examining sources, through transferable skills, to
work the evaluee can realistically obtain within the
Dayton-Kettering-Beavercreek regional economy, with each link cited so an
opposing expert can test it.

## How a local evaluation is arranged

The firm has no staffed office in Troy and publishes no street address for
this location; meetings are by appointment, and saying so plainly is part of
the practice's discipline. An engagement typically proceeds remotely through
records review, secure video interviews, and written opinions delivered on the
disclosure schedule counsel sets, and the in-person evaluation is added only
when the referral questions call for it. When it is, the meeting is arranged
in the evaluee's own community, and when a deposition or trial date requires
the expert present, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office.
Matters that join lost earning capacity to future medical needs can add a
[medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/), and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
are available in which independent economist partners perform and sign the
economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under
one engagement structure.
