In a Centerville damages case, whichever table retains the expert, the report
lands on the other one. Purinton Analytics builds its practice around that
fact: the firm's [vocational expert](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) and
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) opinions are offered on
identical terms to plaintiff and defense counsel, follow the same methodology
regardless of who signs the retention letter, and are written so the lawyer
who did not hire the firm can still trace every conclusion to its source.
Centerville counsel on either side of the caption engage the same practice.

## The Montgomery County forum

Centerville sits in Montgomery County, and civil matters arising here are
generally venued in the Montgomery County Court of Common Pleas, where the
firm accepts engagements in personal injury, employment, workers'
compensation, and family matters. Ohio's federal trial courts are the United
States District Court for the Northern District of Ohio and the United States
District Court for the Southern District of Ohio, and the district that
controls a particular Centerville claim is confirmed during the conflict check
and intake process, together with any registration or qualification
requirement the forum carries. Neither side of the caption gets a different
report; the venue only determines the disclosure rules the same opinion must
meet.

## One market, one method

Vocational findings for a Centerville evaluee are grounded in the regional
economy shared across the Dayton-Kettering-Beavercreek metropolitan area:
Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the
Dayton metropolitan area and for Ohio statewide, state workforce agency labor
market information, and direct labor market research when the referral
questions call for it. The same sequence applies whether the retaining party
hopes the earning capacity is high or low. Functional capacity comes from the
medical record, transferable skills come from the documented work history, and
the employment outlook is stated qualitatively, only as far as the data will
carry it.

## Meeting Centerville evaluees

The firm keeps no staffed office in Centerville and publishes no street
address for this location; meetings are by appointment, and that honesty about
presence is part of what makes the opinions credible. Most engagements are
conducted remotely, through records review, secure video interviews, and
written opinions delivered on the disclosure schedule counsel sets. Where an
in-person interview or evaluation is warranted, it takes place in the
evaluee's own community, and travel for evaluations, depositions, and trial is
scheduled from the Kansas City home office. Counsel can pair the vocational
work with a [medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/) or
retain [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis
while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement
structure.
