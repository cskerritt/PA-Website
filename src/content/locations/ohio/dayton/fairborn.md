Every expert report in a Fairborn case eventually faces the same test: a
deposition in which opposing counsel walks through it line by line, looking
for the assumption that was never checked and the source that was never cited.
Purinton Analytics writes for that moment. The firm's
[vocational expert](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) and
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) opinions are prepared for
Fairborn counsel on either side of the caption, and each one is drafted so the
cross-examiner finds a documented basis wherever they press.

## Greene County courts and the federal forums

Fairborn matters are generally venued in the Greene County Court of Common
Pleas, where the firm accepts engagements in personal injury, employment,
workers' compensation, and domestic cases. Ohio's federal trial courts are the
United States District Court for the Northern District of Ohio and the United
States District Court for the Southern District of Ohio, and rather than
mapping towns to districts by assumption, the firm confirms the controlling
forum for each matter during the conflict check and intake process, along with
any registration or qualification requirement the venue imposes. State or
federal, the report follows the same construction: defined scope, tailored
records request, cited sources, and opinions that stay inside the vocational
and care-planning record.

## Testimony rests on labor market method

What survives cross-examination is method, and the method here starts with the
regional economy Fairborn belongs to. Vocational findings rest on Bureau of
Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Dayton
metropolitan area and for Ohio statewide, joined by state workforce agency
labor market information and direct labor market research when the referral
questions require it. From that base the analysis works through the evaluee's
documented functional capacity and transferable skills to the employment
actually within reach, describing the market in qualitative terms and
declining to state what the data cannot support. An opinion built this way
reads the same on direct and on cross.

## Preparing the Fairborn evaluee's file

Meetings in Fairborn are by appointment; the firm keeps no staffed office here
and publishes no street address for this location, because accuracy about
presence belongs to the same discipline that governs testimony. Most
engagements proceed remotely, through records review, secure video interviews,
and written opinions delivered on the schedule counsel sets, and when an
in-person evaluation is warranted it is arranged in the evaluee's own
community with travel scheduled from the Kansas City home office. The same is
true when a deposition or trial date requires the expert in the room. Counsel
can extend the engagement with a
[medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/) or through
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis
while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement
structure.
