Begin where a Xenia case begins: with the docket. Civil claims arising here
are generally filed in the Greene County Court of Common Pleas, and from the
day of filing the calendar starts to run toward disclosure deadlines,
deposition notices, and a trial date. Purinton Analytics fits its work to that
calendar, delivering [vocational expert](/services/vocational-expert-witness/)
and [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) opinions that arrive
when counsel needs them and hold together when the other side reads them,
whichever side of the caption sends the retention.

## Venue and the shape of the record

For matters heard in the Greene County Court of Common Pleas, the firm accepts
engagements in personal injury, employment, workers' compensation, and
domestic cases. Claims that proceed in federal court reach one of Ohio's
district courts, the United States District Court for the Northern District of
Ohio or the United States District Court for the Southern District of Ohio,
and which of them controls a given Xenia matter is confirmed during the
conflict check and intake process rather than assumed, along with any
venue-specific registration or qualification requirement. In every forum the
opinion is assembled the same way: a written scope, a records request tailored
to the claims, and conclusions that never outrun the file.

## The regional market behind a Xenia opinion

Employment prospects for a Xenia evaluee are evaluated against the
Dayton-Kettering-Beavercreek regional economy, not the town alone. The firm
grounds its vocational findings in Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational
employment and wage estimates for the Dayton metropolitan area and for Ohio
statewide, adds state workforce agency labor market information, and conducts
direct labor market research when the referral questions justify it. The
market is characterized qualitatively, the reasoning is documented link by
link, and the resulting opinion distinguishes carefully between what the
evaluee can no longer do and what the labor market still offers, because the
difference between those is where most damages disputes are actually decided.

## Logistics for evaluations and testimony

Meetings in Xenia are by appointment; the firm maintains no staffed office
here and publishes no street address for this location, a plainness about
presence that matches the plainness of the reports. Most engagements proceed
remotely through records review, secure video interviews, and written opinions
delivered on the schedule counsel sets. In-person interviews and testing, when
warranted, are arranged in the evaluee's own community, and travel for
evaluations, depositions, and trial appearances is scheduled from the Kansas
City home office. Where the damages case requires it, counsel can add a
[medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/) or use
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis
while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement
structure.
