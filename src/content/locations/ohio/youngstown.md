The Youngstown-Warren metro presents a compact venue map, anchored by
Mahoning County and Trumbull County, and Purinton Analytics serves it with
the same depth it brings to much larger markets. Plaintiff and defense
counsel in the metro retain the firm for
[earning capacity and employability evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under a
unified engagement structure. A smaller metro does not mean a smaller
record: the workup here follows the identical protocol used nationwide.

## Venues in Mahoning and Trumbull counties

Engagements are accepted for matters in the Mahoning County Court of Common
Pleas and the Trumbull County Court of Common Pleas. Ohio federal matters
proceed in the United States District Court for the Northern District of
Ohio or the United States District Court for the Southern District of Ohio,
depending on where the case is filed, and reports are drafted to satisfy
federal disclosure expectations even when the matter remains in state
court. Counsel practicing across the state will find the rest of the
firm's Ohio footprint, including its other metro pages, described on the
[Ohio locations page](/locations/ohio/).

## Serving the metro without a local office

Youngstown meetings are by appointment. The firm maintains no staffed
office in the metro and publishes no street address for this location,
because plain statements about presence belong to the same discipline that
shapes its opinions. Engagements ordinarily begin remotely: records review,
structured vocational interviews by video, and standardized testing under
publisher protocols where remote administration is supported. When an
evaluation is better conducted in person, in the evaluee's own community,
or when a deposition or trial date requires the expert's presence, travel
is scheduled from the Kansas City home office and confirmed with counsel
early enough to protect the litigation calendar. The point of contact and
the report standard stay constant throughout.

## Labor market evidence for the Youngstown-Warren area

Vocational findings for evaluees in the metro rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the
Youngstown-Warren metropolitan area and for Ohio statewide, together with
state workforce agency labor market information and direct labor market
research where the referral questions call for it. Life care plans and
medical cost projections are priced to the evaluee's own community through
provider-specific inquiries and published fee data, because the cost and
availability of care in this metro are local questions that national tables
do not answer. Every conclusion is sourced in the report itself, so that
either side of the caption can trace the analysis without a deposition.

Availability is confirmed through a conflict check before any substantive
discussion of the case. Submit the caption or party names through the

deposition, or trial dates so the schedule can be assessed candidly at the
outset. For related matters venued outside Ohio, the firm's
