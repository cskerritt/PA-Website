An earning capacity opinion is a comparison built from evidence: the work an
evaluee could have performed but for the events at issue, set against the work
the record shows remains open afterward. Neither side of that comparison can
be assumed, and a Dublin case is no exception. Purinton Analytics constructs
each side from documents and data for counsel on either side of the caption,
through its
[vocational evaluation and expert testimony](/services/vocational-expert-witness/)
and [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) practice in Dublin and
across the [Columbus metro](/locations/ohio/columbus/).

## Franklin County venue, state and federal

Dublin matters proceeding in state court are generally filed in the Franklin
County Court of Common Pleas, and the firm accepts engagements for cases
venued there from plaintiff or defense counsel. Ohio's federal forums are the
United States District Court for the Northern District of Ohio and the United
States District Court for the Southern District of Ohio; the district with
jurisdiction over a given Dublin matter is identified during the conflict
check and intake, not presumed on this page. Whatever the forum, the work
product is the same: a signed report with a stated methodology, an inventory
of the records reviewed, and conclusions that opposing counsel can test line
by line.

## Building the comparison from the record

The before picture starts with documents: earnings history, education and
credentials, and the demands of the jobs the evaluee actually held. The after
picture starts with functional capacity as documented by treating and
examining sources, then asks what work those findings still support. Each
side is measured against the market the evaluee can realistically reach,
using Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates
for the Columbus metropolitan area and for Ohio statewide, state workforce
agency labor market information, and direct labor market research where the
occupation is specialized enough to warrant it. The firm does not inflate or
discount a career path to fit a damages theory; where the earnings history is
irregular or the record is incomplete, the report says so and explains how
the gap was treated, so the valuation can be examined rather than merely
argued about.

## How Dublin engagements are staffed

There is no staffed Purinton Analytics office in Dublin, and the firm
publishes no local street address, a policy it applies everywhere outside its
Kansas City home office. Counsel get a remote-first engagement instead:
secure document transfer, video interviews with the evaluee, and drafts
delivered against the case schedule. In-person evaluation, deposition
testimony, and trial appearances are scheduled from the Kansas City home
office when the matter requires presence. Where damages extend beyond
earnings into future care, the firm prepares life care plans and
[medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/), and it offers
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) in
which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis
while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement
structure.
