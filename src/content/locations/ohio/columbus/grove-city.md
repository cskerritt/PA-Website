## Where Grove City cases are heard

Grove City sits in Franklin County, and state-court matters arising here are
generally venued in the Franklin County Court of Common Pleas. In federal
practice, Ohio is served by the United States District Court for the Northern
District of Ohio and the United States District Court for the Southern District
of Ohio; the district that governs a specific Grove City matter is identified
during the conflict check and intake rather than announced here. The firm's
reports are drafted for either court system from the outset, because a
disclosure-ready report cannot be retrofitted for admissibility after the
deadline has passed.

## Sequencing the work against the calendar

A defensible vocational opinion has stages that cannot be compressed without
showing: records collection, evaluee interview, transferable skills analysis,
labor market research, and a written report that ties them together. At intake
the firm maps those stages onto the dates counsel provides, identifies which
records must arrive by when, and flags early if the schedule requires an
interim disclosure strategy. Labor market findings for Grove City evaluees
draw on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates
for the Columbus metropolitan area and for Ohio statewide, state workforce
agency labor market information, and targeted employer contact when the
occupation demands it, all of it cited so the opinion can be checked by the
other side. If a compressed schedule genuinely cannot support a full
evaluation, the firm says so at intake rather than delivering thin work on
time.

frequently what a tight calendar needs: secure records transfer starts
immediately, video interviews are scheduled within days rather than around
travel, and drafts move by encrypted channels. Where the referral questions
