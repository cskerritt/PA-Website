## What a rebuttal actually examines

A useful review does not quarrel with an opposing expert's conclusions in the
abstract; it audits the path to them. Did the opinion account for the full
records set? Were the transferable skills findings tied to the evaluee's
actual work history? Do the labor market claims match what the Bureau of
Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Columbus
metropolitan area and for Ohio statewide actually show, and was state
workforce agency labor market information used or ignored? Where the opposing
opinion is sound, the review says so, which is what makes the criticisms
credible where it is not. The deliverable can be a formal rebuttal report for
disclosure or a confidential consulting memorandum for deposition
preparation, whichever the litigation posture calls for. The same data
discipline supports the firm's affirmative opinions for Westerville evaluees,
whose employment prospects are evaluated at the metro-market level where
hiring genuinely occurs.
