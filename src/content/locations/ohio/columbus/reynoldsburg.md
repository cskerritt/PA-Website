The same Reynoldsburg case looks different depending on which table counsel
sits at, but the vocational facts should not. Purinton Analytics is retained
by plaintiff firms and defense firms in roughly equal spirit, and its
[vocational expert](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) and
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) opinions for Reynoldsburg
matters are written so that neither side has to apologize for them: the
methodology is stated, the sources are cited, and the conclusions follow from
the record whether they help or hurt the retaining party's theory.

## Franklin County and the federal forums

Reynoldsburg is part of Franklin County, and state-court matters arising here
are generally filed in the Franklin County Court of Common Pleas. Ohio's
federal trial courts are the United States District Court for the Northern
District of Ohio and the United States District Court for the Southern
District of Ohio; rather than guessing at jurisdiction on a landing page, the
firm confirms the controlling venue for each matter during the conflict check
and intake. That habit of confirming rather than assuming runs through the
entire engagement, which is precisely why the reports travel well between
courts and between sides of the caption.

## Reading the labor market from either table

A Reynoldsburg evaluee is evaluated against the wider Columbus employment
market rather than the town alone, because that is the level where hiring
actually happens. Findings rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational
employment and wage estimates for the Columbus metropolitan area and for Ohio
statewide, on state workforce agency labor market information, and on direct
labor market research when a specific occupation or employer question needs
it. For a plaintiff referral the analysis tests whether claimed losses are
supported; for a defense referral it tests whether claimed limitations
foreclose the work the data say exists. The method is identical either way,
and the report says so, which means a retaining attorney learns early, not at
mediation, where the vocational evidence actually points.

## Appointment-based service, stated plainly

There is no Purinton Analytics storefront in Reynoldsburg and no published
local address, because implying an office that does not exist is the kind of
soft misstatement the firm's whole practice is built against. Engagements run
remote-first: secure records exchange, video interviews with the evaluee, and
report drafts delivered on the disclosure schedule. In-person evaluations are
held in the evaluee's own community when warranted, and travel for
evaluations, depositions, and trial is scheduled from the Kansas City home
office. Counsel who need the damages picture completed can add a
[medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/) or structure a
[coordinated damages engagement](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) in
which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis
while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement
structure.
