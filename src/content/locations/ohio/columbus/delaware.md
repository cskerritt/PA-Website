In a Delaware case, the place names themselves call for precision: the city
of Delaware sits within Delaware County, which shares its name, and a report
that is careless about which of them a fact belongs to has already handed
opposing counsel a foothold. Purinton Analytics treats precision about place
as part of its method, providing
[vocational expert](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) and
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) opinions for plaintiff
and defense counsel in Delaware and across the
[Columbus metro](/locations/ohio/columbus/).

## Delaware County courts and the federal alternative

State-court matters arising in Delaware are generally heard in the Delaware
County Court of Common Pleas, and the firm accepts engagements for matters
venued there. For claims in federal court, Ohio's forums are the United
States District Court for the Northern District of Ohio and the United States
District Court for the Southern District of Ohio; the controlling district
for any particular matter is confirmed during the conflict check and intake.
Reports are drafted to hold up wherever the case lands, with methodology,
sources, and limitations stated on the face of the document rather than
reconstructed at deposition.

## Which labor market, and why

The same precision governs the market analysis. Which labor market applies
to a given evaluee is an evidentiary question, not a default, and the opinion
answers it from the record. The firm's analyses use Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Columbus
metropolitan area alongside the Ohio statewide series, plus state workforce
agency labor market information and direct employer research where the
referral questions require it. The commuting radius an evaluee can
realistically sustain, before and after injury, is documented from the record
rather than assumed, and the resulting opinion says plainly which markets
were considered and why. If the record does not support a commuting
assumption, the opinion narrows accordingly; the analysis follows the
evidence about this particular evaluee rather than a generic profile of the
region.

## Serving Delaware evaluees by appointment

The firm maintains no office in Delaware and lists no local address; every
location outside the Kansas City home office is served by appointment, and
saying so plainly is part of the practice's credibility. Records review and
evaluee interviews proceed remotely in most engagements, over secure channels
and on the schedule the litigation demands. When counsel or the referral
questions call for in-person evaluation, the session is arranged in the
evaluee's own community, with travel scheduled from the Kansas City home
office. Counsel needing damages support beyond the vocational opinion can
pair it with a [medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/)
or a full life care plan, or structure a
[coordinated damages engagement](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) in
which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis
while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement
structure.
