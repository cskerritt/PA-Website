## Venue for Gahanna matters

Gahanna is part of Franklin County, and state-court cases arising here are
generally filed in the Franklin County Court of Common Pleas. In the federal
system, Ohio's trial courts are the United States District Court for the
Northern District of Ohio and the United States District Court for the
Southern District of Ohio; which district controls a given Gahanna matter is
settled during the conflict check and intake, not presumed in marketing copy.

## Testimony that matches the report

Preparation for deposition begins when the report is drafted, not when the
notice arrives. Because the written opinion inventories its records, cites its
labor market sources, and states its assumptions, the testimony can simply
walk the same path. Labor market findings for Gahanna evaluees rest on Bureau
of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the
Columbus metropolitan area and for Ohio statewide, supported by state
workforce agency labor market information and direct labor market research
where the referral questions require it. Gahanna's workforce is woven into the
metro-wide employment market, and the opinion evaluates the evaluee's options
at that level, which is also where opposing counsel's cross-examination will
try to live. Exhibits are prepared to match the report's citations exactly, so
the witness, the document, and the underlying data never diverge on the
record.
