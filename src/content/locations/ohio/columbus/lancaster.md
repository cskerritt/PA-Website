An injured worker or catastrophically hurt client in Lancaster should not
have to organize their recovery around trips to a distant expert's office,
and with Purinton Analytics they do not. The firm brings the evaluation to
the evaluee: remote sessions where remote work is appropriate, in-person
sessions arranged in the evaluee's own Fairfield County community when the
referral questions call for them. That evaluee-centered logistics model
anchors the firm's
[vocational expert](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) and
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) work for Lancaster
counsel on both sides of the docket.

## Fairfield County venue and the state's federal districts

Lancaster matters proceeding in state court are generally heard in the
Fairfield County Court of Common Pleas, and the firm accepts engagements for
cases venued there. Federal claims in Ohio proceed in the United States
District Court for the Northern District of Ohio or the United States
District Court for the Southern District of Ohio; the district controlling
any given Lancaster matter is identified during the conflict check and
intake. In either system the deliverable is the same disciplined document: an
opinion whose methodology, records inventory, and reasoning are laid out for
the opposing expert to examine.

## Which labor market the record supports

A vocational opinion for a Lancaster evaluee has to decide which labor market
the evidence supports: work close to home in Fairfield County, work across
the wider Columbus metro, or a combination the record itself defines. The
firm treats that decision as an evidentiary question rather than an
assumption. Its labor market analysis draws on Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates for the Columbus metropolitan area
and for Ohio statewide, on state workforce agency labor market information,
and on direct labor market research where a particular trade or employer
question requires it, with the distance an evaluee can realistically travel
for work established from the record, not presumed. Where treating providers
have restricted driving or endurance, the labor market analysis narrows to
what those restrictions actually leave reachable, and the report shows the
narrowing step explicitly.

## What evaluation looks like in practice

Interviews and testing are scheduled around the evaluee's medical
appointments and functional limits, not the other way around. Records move by
secure transfer, video interviews cover most referral questions, and when an
in-person session is warranted it takes place in the evaluee's community,
with the firm's travel scheduled from the Kansas City home office. Purinton
Analytics keeps no staffed office in Lancaster and publishes no street
address here; by-appointment service, honestly described, is the model
everywhere outside Kansas City. Where the case requires a full damages
picture, the firm prepares
[medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/) and life care
plans, and offers
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) in
which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis
while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement
structure.
