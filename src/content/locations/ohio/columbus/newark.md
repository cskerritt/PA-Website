A Newark referral asks the expert to keep separate frames of reference
straight. The case itself proceeds through Licking County's courts, while the
employment evidence that will decide it is measured across the Columbus labor
market, and an opinion that blurs the line between where a case is heard and
where its evaluee finds work invites cross-examination. Purinton Analytics
keeps that line documented, providing
[vocational expert](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) and
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) opinions to plaintiff and
defense counsel in Newark and across the
[Columbus metro](/locations/ohio/columbus/).

## Venue runs through Licking County

State-court matters arising in Newark are generally heard in the Licking
County Court of Common Pleas. In the federal system, Ohio's trial forums are
the United States District Court for the Northern District of Ohio and the
United States District Court for the Southern District of Ohio; the district
that controls a particular Newark matter is confirmed during the conflict
check and intake rather than presumed on a landing page. Whatever the forum,
the deliverable is built for scrutiny in it: a stated methodology, an
inventory of the records reviewed, and sources cited so the opposing expert
can retrace every step of the analysis without guessing at what was done.

## The labor market frame is wider than the venue

The employment side of a Newark case is analyzed where hiring actually
happens. Vocational findings for a Newark evaluee rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Columbus
metropolitan area and for Ohio statewide, supported by state workforce agency
labor market information and direct labor market research when the referral
questions require it. The method underneath is constant: transferable skills
drawn from the evaluee's documented work history, functional capacity as
established by treating and examining sources, and a realistic accounting of
the work those findings leave reachable. The report states which market was
used and why, so the framing itself can be examined by the other side rather
than merely argued about at deposition.
