Ask what a vocational expert report for a Hilliard case should contain, and the
answer is more specific than most retention letters assume: a documented
records inventory, a functional profile drawn from treating and examining
sources, a transferable skills analysis, labor market findings with citations,
and opinions that connect those pieces without skipping steps. That document is
what Purinton Analytics delivers to plaintiff and defense counsel in Hilliard,
as part of its [vocational expert](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) and
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) practice across the
[Columbus metro](/locations/ohio/columbus/).

## The venues a Hilliard report must satisfy

Hilliard sits in Franklin County, and state-court matters arising here are
generally venued in the Franklin County Court of Common Pleas. Federal matters
in Ohio proceed in the United States District Court for the Northern District
of Ohio or the United States District Court for the Southern District of Ohio,
with the controlling district for a particular case confirmed during the
conflict check and intake. The report is engineered for scrutiny in any of
them: every source is cited, every assumption is labeled as such, and the
scope of the opinion is stated so the other side knows exactly what is and is
not being claimed.

## From records to labor market findings

The analysis begins with the file, not the conclusion. Medical records,
employment and earnings history, educational credentials, and prior vocational
documentation establish what the evaluee could do before the events at issue
and what capacity remains. Labor market findings for a Hilliard evaluee then
rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates
for the Columbus metropolitan area and for Ohio statewide, on state workforce
agency labor market information, and on direct labor market research when the
occupation is narrow enough to need it. Because Hilliard workers participate
in the metro-wide market, the analysis is framed at that level, and the report
explains the framing rather than leaving it implicit. Gaps in the record are
handled the same way: identified, disclosed, and either resolved through a
supplemental records request or carried as stated limitations on the opinion.
