When an Upper Arlington family is reorganized around a catastrophic injury,
the litigation eventually has to answer a question medicine alone cannot:
what will a lifetime of care actually require?
[Life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) is the discipline that
answers it, item by item, from the medical record and the treating providers'
recommendations, and it anchors the services Purinton Analytics offers Upper
Arlington counsel, alongside the firm's
[vocational expert](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) practice, in
matters for both plaintiffs and defendants.

## The courts behind an Upper Arlington case

Upper Arlington is a Franklin County community, and state-court matters
arising here are generally venued in the Franklin County Court of Common
Pleas. Where a claim proceeds federally, Ohio's forums are the United States
District Court for the Northern District of Ohio and the United States
District Court for the Southern District of Ohio, and the controlling
district for a particular matter is identified during the conflict check and
intake. A life care plan offered in any of these courts must survive an
opposing expert's line-by-line audit, so each recommendation in the plan is
sourced to the record, priced by documented method, and distinguishable from
advocacy. Recommendations the record cannot support are left out, whatever
the retaining side might prefer.

## Care needs and earning capacity, analyzed together

Catastrophic cases rarely present a care question without a work question.
The firm evaluates both: the future care architecture through the life care
plan or a narrower
[medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/), and the
evaluee's remaining earning capacity through vocational analysis grounded in
Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the
Columbus metropolitan area and for Ohio statewide, state workforce agency
labor market information, and direct labor market research where warranted.
For an evaluee whose career ran through the metro employment market, the
interplay between residual capacity and realistic market access is often
where the damages dispute is actually decided, and the reports address it
head-on. Because the care plan and the vocational opinion are prepared under
a common records set, the assumptions in each are reconciled before
disclosure instead of colliding on cross-examination.

## How the firm attends an Upper Arlington matter

Service is by appointment, and the firm is direct about what that means: no
staffed office in Upper Arlington, no published local address, no implied
storefront. Records and interviews move through secure remote channels for
most of the engagement; in-person evaluation, when the referral questions
require it, is conducted in the evaluee's own community, and travel for
evaluations, depositions, and trial is scheduled from the Kansas City home
office. When counsel needs the economic loss quantified, the firm offers
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) in
which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis
while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement
structure.
