An injured Wellsville resident should not have to travel to a distant city
to be evaluated properly. Purinton Analytics structures its town-level work
around the evaluee: the interview and, where appropriate, the in-person
assessment happen in or near Wellsville, in a setting suited to the
person's condition, while the resulting
[vocational opinion](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) or
[life care plan](/services/life-care-planning/) is prepared to the same
standard the firm applies in its largest engagements. Counsel for injured
plaintiffs and counsel defending claims retain the firm on identical terms,
and [rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) of an
opposing expert's report is available where that is the assignment.

## The courts that will read the report

Wellsville is a Columbiana County community, so state-court litigation
proceeds in the Court of Common Pleas, the same bench addressed by the
firm's [Salem area practice](/locations/ohio/salem/). Federal matters have
Ohio's district courts available as forums, the United States District
Court for the Northern District of Ohio and the United States District
Court for the Southern District of Ohio, with the controlling district and
division for any given case, and any venue-specific qualification
requirement, confirmed during the conflict check and intake. The report is
drafted for whichever courtroom will receive it, but its method is fixed
before the venue question is even asked.

## A small town, measured against a real market

Wellsville is a small town, and pretending otherwise would discredit the
analysis before the deposition began. What matters for earning capacity is
the labor market an evaluee can reach from Wellsville, and the firm maps
that market with Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and
wage estimates for the surrounding region and for Ohio statewide, alongside
state workforce agency labor market information and, where the questions
call for it, direct contact research with employers. The written opinion
sets out the commutable market it assumed, the occupations it tested, and
the source of every wage figure it relied on, because a transparent method
is the only kind worth defending under oath.

## Logistics without pretense

The firm has no Wellsville office and claims none; publishing an address it
does not staff would contradict the accuracy its reports are built on.
Records analysis, counsel conferences, and testimony preparation are
conducted remotely, and travel for depositions, hearings, or trial is
scheduled from the Kansas City home office when presence is required. The
firm's [Ohio page](/locations/ohio/) covers its statewide approach, and the
[nationwide engagement model](/locations/nationwide/) explains how the same
remote-first structure serves counsel across the country. Where economic
damages modeling is needed alongside the vocational and care-planning work,
the firm offers
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.
