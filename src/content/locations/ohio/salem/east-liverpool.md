## The forums that hear East Liverpool claims

East Liverpool sits in Columbiana County, and state-court matters arising
there proceed in the Court of Common Pleas. A report headed for that bench
is drafted so that a local jury can follow its reasoning and opposing
counsel can trace every source it cites, because an opinion that cannot be
traced cannot be defended. For federal litigation, Ohio's district courts,
the United States District Court for the Northern District of Ohio and the
United States District Court for the Southern District of Ohio, are the
available forums; the district and division that control a particular
filing, together with any venue-specific qualification or disclosure
requirement, are confirmed with counsel during the conflict check and
intake. The forum changes the caption, never the method.

## Measuring an East Liverpool earning capacity

Earning capacity for an East Liverpool worker is measured against the
market that worker can actually reach, not against the town line. The
analysis draws on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and
wage estimates for the surrounding area and for Ohio statewide, on state
workforce agency labor market information, and on direct labor market
research when the referral questions call for it. The written opinion
states which market definition it used, which occupations were considered
and why some were rejected, and where each wage observation came from, so
that cross-examination tests the analysis rather than uncovers it.

## Starting an East Liverpool engagement

Every engagement begins the same way: with clearance. Submit the caption or
party names through the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and note any
report deadlines, depositions, or trial settings already on the calendar.
The firm confirms conflicts, defines the scope in writing, and only then
requests records, so that the opinion that ultimately reaches the court
rests on a record both sides can examine. Counsel receive candid scheduling
estimates at intake; a promised date the work cannot honestly meet serves
neither the client nor the court.
