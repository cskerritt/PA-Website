## Where a Columbiana case is tried

State-court claims from the town proceed in the Columbiana County Court of
Common Pleas, and the firm's broader
[Salem area practice](/locations/ohio/salem/) describes how it approaches
that county bench. Matters that belong in federal court have Ohio's
district courts available to them, the United States District Court for the
Northern District of Ohio and the United States District Court for the
Southern District of Ohio; which district and division control a given
filing, and whether any venue-specific registration or qualification
requirement applies, is identified and confirmed during the conflict check
and intake process. The [Ohio practice page](/locations/ohio/) sets out how
the firm handles venue questions across the state, and the
[nationwide page](/locations/nationwide/) explains the engagement model
that travels with every file.

## The market a Columbiana worker actually faces

A Columbiana resident's employment prospects are not confined to the town
itself; the relevant question is what work exists within a reasonable
commute, at what wages, and whether the evaluee's residual capacities and
transferable skills give access to it. Vocational findings rest on Bureau
of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the
surrounding area and for Ohio as a whole, supplemented by state workforce
agency labor market information and by direct employer research where the
questions warrant it. Each report discloses its market definition and its
sources so that the opposing expert can check the arithmetic instead of
guessing at it.

## By appointment, honestly stated

There is no Purinton Analytics office in Columbiana, and the firm will not
print an address it does not keep. Counsel conferences, records analysis,
and deposition preparation run remotely on the attorney's schedule. When an
in-person evaluation would strengthen the record, it is arranged in or near
the town in a setting suited to the evaluee's condition, and appearances
for deposition or trial are scheduled as travel from the Kansas City home
office. When the case also requires economic damages testimony, the firm
arranges
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## The conflict check comes before everything

Retention starts with clearance, not with commitments.
[Request a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) with the case caption and party
names, and identify any disclosure deadlines, depositions, or hearings that
bear on timing. Once conflicts are cleared, the firm confirms a written
scope, requests the records the questions require, and gives counsel a
schedule it can keep. That sequence protects the eventual opinion: a report
built on a defined scope and a documented record answers cross-examination
in a way that improvised work never can, and it reads the same whether the
reader retained the firm or opposes it.
