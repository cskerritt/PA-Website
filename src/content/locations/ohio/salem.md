An injured evaluee in the Salem, OH micropolitan area does not need a
different kind of expert than an evaluee in a major city; the evaluee needs
the same disciplined evaluation, delivered without inflated logistics. That
is how Purinton Analytics structures its Columbiana County work: the
evaluee is interviewed and, where appropriate, assessed in a setting
convenient to the case, while the resulting
[vocational opinion](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) or
[life care plan](/services/life-care-planning/) is prepared to the same
standard the firm applies everywhere. Retention is open to plaintiff and
defense counsel alike, and the firm also provides
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) of opposing
expert reports.

## Columbiana County courts and beyond

Salem-area state court matters proceed in the Columbiana County Court of
Common Pleas. Federal cases connected to the area may be venued in the
United States District Court for the Northern District of Ohio or the
United States District Court for the Southern District of Ohio; the correct
district, and any venue-specific qualification requirement, is identified
and confirmed during the conflict check and intake process. The firm's
approach to Ohio venues generally is described on the
[Ohio practice page](/locations/ohio/). When economic damages testimony is
needed, the firm arranges
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Appointment-based service, no local storefront

There is no Purinton Analytics office in Salem, and the firm publishes no
street address for this location. Stating that plainly is part of the
practice's credibility: a report is only as trustworthy as the firm behind
it, and the firm does not embellish its own footprint. Salem engagements
are served by appointment, with records review, counsel conferences, and
suitable interviews handled remotely by secure video. When in-person
evaluation in Columbiana County is warranted, or when deposition and trial
dates require attendance, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home
office against the litigation calendar.

## Reading the Salem-area labor market

Employability and earning capacity findings for Salem-area evaluees rest on
Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates
covering the area and Ohio statewide, along with state workforce agency
labor market information and direct labor market research where the
referral questions call for it. In a micropolitan market, the report is
explicit about the geography of the analysis: which labor market the
evaluee can realistically access, what data describe it, and how the
restrictions in the medical record narrow or preserve that access. Both
sides of the caption can then test the opinion on its stated foundation.

## How to refer a Salem matter

Columbiana County referrals join the intake queue of the firm's
[nationwide practice](/locations/nationwide/). Submit the caption or party
names through the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/), identify any
disclosure, deposition, or trial dates that are already set, and hold
confidential records until the engagement is confirmed in writing. Counsel
then receives a defined scope, a fee structure, and a records request
tailored to the claims, so the engagement starts on the record it will
finish on.
