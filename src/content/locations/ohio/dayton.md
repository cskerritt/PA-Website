Most Dayton engagements begin at a distance: records arrive electronically,
interviews are scheduled by video, and standardized testing is administered
under publisher protocols that support remote use. That remote-first
workflow is how Purinton Analytics serves plaintiff and defense counsel
throughout the Dayton-Kettering-Beavercreek metro, delivering
[vocational expert evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[forensic economic damages](/services/forensic-economic-damages/) analyses
that are performed and signed by independent economist partners coordinated
by the firm. Distance changes the logistics of an engagement here, never the
method or the report standard.

## Where Dayton matters are venued

The firm accepts engagements for matters in the Montgomery County Court of
Common Pleas and in the common pleas courts of Greene County and Miami
County. In federal court, Ohio matters proceed in the United States District
Court for the Southern District of Ohio or the United States District Court
for the Northern District of Ohio, depending on where the case is filed, and
the firm's written opinions are built for the disclosure requirements of
either forum. The counties of the metro are served under the same statewide
practice model described on the [Ohio locations page](/locations/ohio/), so
counsel with a mixed docket deal with a common intake process and a
consistent work product across venues.

## Appointments, not an office

Dayton meetings are by appointment. The firm has no staffed office in the
metro and publishes no street address for this location, because being
precise about presence is part of the same discipline the firm applies to
its opinions. When the referral questions require an in-person vocational
evaluation, when a life care planning assessment is best conducted in the
evaluee's home, or when deposition and trial dates require the expert in the
courtroom, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office and planned
around the litigation calendar. Between those milestones, the work proceeds
remotely without loss of rigor, and counsel keep a direct line to the expert
at every stage.

## Dayton labor market and care cost research

Vocational findings for evaluees in the metro rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Dayton
metropolitan area and for Ohio statewide, along with state workforce agency
labor market information and direct labor market research where the case
calls for it. Life care plans and medical cost projections are priced to the
evaluee's own community using provider-specific inquiries and published fee
data for the region, since the cost of future care in the Dayton area is a
question about the local market, not the national average. Every figure is
traceable to a cited
source, which is what lets the same report withstand review from whichever
side of the caption did not retain the firm.

Every engagement opens with a conflict check, completed before any substantive
discussion of the case. Submit the caption or party names through the

deposition, or trial dates so scheduling commitments can be made honestly.
Counsel with related matters outside Ohio can review the firm's
[national coverage](/locations/nationwide/) for how the same remote-first
