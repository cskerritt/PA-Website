An expert report earns its place in an Indiana courtroom the same way it does anywhere else: by surviving the reader who wants it excluded. Purinton Analytics writes vocational expert and life care planning opinions for Indiana matters with that reader in mind. Every opinion is documented so that opposing counsel can trace each conclusion to the record, and engagements are accepted from plaintiff and defense counsel on the same terms, because a methodology that serves only its retaining side is not a methodology.

## Where Indiana engagements are accepted

The firm accepts matters pending in Indiana's Circuit and Superior Courts and in the United States District Court for the Northern District of Indiana and the United States District Court for the Southern District of Indiana, with deposition and trial testimony offered in each forum. Indiana's metropolitan areas are covered in depth on their own pages: Indianapolis, Fort Wayne, South Bend-Mishawaka, Evansville, Lafayette-West Lafayette, Elkhart-Goshen, Terre Haute, Bloomington, Muncie, Michigan City-La Porte, Columbus, Kokomo, Warsaw, Marion, and Richmond. A case arising outside those areas is handled under the same statewide approach.

## Working with the firm from anywhere in Indiana

There is no staffed Purinton Analytics office in Indiana, and no street address is published for the state; meetings are by appointment, and the firm says so directly because accuracy about presence is part of the discipline that gives its reports weight. Indiana counsel work with the same structure that supports the [nationwide practice](/locations/nationwide/): a documented conflict check, a written scope, records requests tailored to the claims, and evaluee interviews conducted by secure video or arranged in the evaluee's own community. When the referral calls for in-person evaluation, or when deposition and trial dates require presence, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office around the controlling calendar.

Labor market findings for Indiana evaluees are grounded in Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for Indiana statewide and for its metropolitan areas, in state workforce agency labor market information, and in direct labor market research where the questions warrant it. When a matter needs economic analysis alongside the vocational and care-planning work, the firm offers [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under the same engagement structure.

## Venue requirements and disclosure practice

Indiana's state and federal forums do not run on identical rules, and the firm does not treat them as if they did. Any venue-specific registration or qualification requirement is identified and confirmed during the conflict check and intake process. Disclosure deadlines are confirmed at retention, and report drafting is scheduled backward from those dates so that the opinion is finished, reviewed, and served on time.

## How an Indiana engagement begins

Before any records are reviewed, the conflict question gets answered. Submit the caption or the party names through the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/), name the court, and flag any imminent disclosure, deposition, or trial dates. Once conflicts clear, scope, fee structure, and schedule are confirmed in writing, and the work begins on a record both sides will eventually examine.
