Ask which side Purinton Analytics works for in Oxford and the answer is the
same as everywhere else in the practice: whichever side retains it first,
on identical terms. The firm's opinions are built to survive the reader who
did not commission them, which is why counsel on either side of the caption
engage it for
[vocational evaluation and expert testimony](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under a single
engagement structure. A report that reads the same to both counsel tables
is the point, not a slogan.

## Oxford's courts, state and federal

The Oxford area takes in Lafayette County and Yalobusha County, and
engagements are accepted for matters venued in the Circuit Court of each.
In federal practice, the firm serves counsel in the United States District
Court for the Northern District of Mississippi, and it accepts matters in
the United States District Court for the Southern District of Mississippi
when a related case is venued there. The written opinion is prepared to a
single standard across forums: identified sources, stated assumptions, and
reasoning that can be followed from the record to the conclusion without
gaps. Any venue-specific registration or qualification requirement is
identified and confirmed during the conflict check and intake process.

## What by-appointment service means here

There is no staffed Purinton Analytics office in Oxford, and the firm
publishes no street address for this location. It says so directly because
a practice that expects its data to be verified should invite the same
verification of itself. Counsel work with the firm by appointment: video
conference for most records reviews, strategy discussions, and
pre-deposition preparation, and in-person work when the referral requires
it. Evaluations can be scheduled in the evaluee's community, and travel for
depositions, mediations, and trial settings is arranged from the Kansas
City home office with the disclosure calendar governing the schedule.

## Labor market data for Oxford matters

Vocational findings for Oxford-area evaluees rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the relevant area
and for Mississippi statewide, state workforce agency labor market
information, and direct labor market research where the referral questions
warrant it. The report states which market defines the evaluee's commutable
labor market and why, and it distinguishes between what the published data
show and what required original research, so that cross-examination finds a
documented trail rather than an undifferentiated assertion.

## Beginning an Oxford engagement

Oxford referrals enter the same intake that serves all of
[Mississippi](/locations/mississippi/) and the firm's
[national practice](/locations/nationwide/). To begin, submit the caption
or party names through the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and note
any imminent disclosure, deposition, mediation, or trial dates. Once the
conflict check clears, the firm confirms scope and fee terms in writing and
issues a records checklist tailored to the claims, and the report timeline
is set against the deadlines that actually govern the case rather than
left to drift.
