## Where Baldwyn cases are heard

On the state side, matters arising in Baldwyn proceed in the Circuit Court serving Prentiss County, and the firm takes engagements for that forum from either party to the caption. Mississippi's federal forums are the United States District Court for the Northern District of Mississippi and the United States District Court for the Southern District of Mississippi; which of them controls a given case, and what expert registration or qualification rules attach there, is identified and confirmed during the conflict check and intake process. Counsel with matters across the state can consult the [Mississippi locations page](/locations/mississippi/) for the firm's broader coverage.

## The evaluation itself

A Baldwyn engagement typically unfolds in stages. Records review, transferable skills analysis, and [medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/) work proceed from the Kansas City home office, where the file is built before anyone meets. The evaluee interview follows, by video when appropriate or in person in Baldwyn or nearby, on a trip scheduled from Kansas City; the firm keeps no office in the town and publishes no local address, and it states that outright because the same candor governs every page of its reports. Site visits, day-in-the-life observation, and testimony dates are folded into the same travel planning so that counsel's calendar, not the expert's, drives the schedule.
