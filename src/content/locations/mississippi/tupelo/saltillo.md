Saltillo is a commuting town: many of its residents build their working lives around jobs spread across the Tupelo area rather than inside the town limits, and any credible opinion about a Saltillo evaluee's earning capacity has to begin from that fact. Purinton Analytics analyzes the market an evaluee can actually reach, not an abstraction, and brings that analysis to [vocational expert witness](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) and [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) engagements for Saltillo matters, retained by plaintiff or defense counsel and written to survive scrutiny from whichever side did not retain the firm.

## Lee County courts and the federal alternatives

State-court matters arising in Saltillo proceed in the Circuit Court serving Lee County, and the firm accepts engagements for that court from either side of the caption. Federal filings in Mississippi may proceed in the United States District Court for the Northern District of Mississippi or the United States District Court for the Southern District of Mississippi; the forum that controls a particular case, along with any expert registration or qualification requirement it carries, is identified and confirmed during the conflict check and intake process. Coverage for matters elsewhere in the state is described on the [Mississippi locations page](/locations/mississippi/).

## Earning capacity in a commuter's market

Because Saltillo workers routinely look beyond the town for employment, vocational findings here are grounded in Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Tupelo metropolitan area, read alongside Mississippi statewide estimates, state workforce agency labor market information, and direct labor market research when the referral questions warrant it. Transferable skills analysis then connects the evaluee's documented work history to the occupations that data describes, so the resulting opinion identifies actual openings and actual wage levels rather than hypothetical ones. The broader regional picture, including the communities that share this commuting market, appears on the [Tupelo metro page](/locations/mississippi/tupelo/). When the damages model requires economic testimony, [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) let independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning work continues under the same engagement structure.

## Logistics for a Saltillo evaluee

Purinton Analytics maintains no staffed office in Saltillo and publishes no local street address, a matter of accuracy rather than modesty. Most phases of the work are remote by design: records analysis, labor market research, and [medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/) proceed from the Kansas City home office, and evaluee interviews can be conducted by video where appropriate. If an in-person evaluation is warranted, it is held in or near the evaluee's community, on a trip scheduled from Kansas City and built around the deadlines counsel has already docketed. Deposition and trial appearances are addressed the same way; raise them at intake so the dates are protected early through the firm's [testimony and litigation consulting](/services/expert-testimony-litigation-consulting/) work.
