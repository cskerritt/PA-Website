Expert opinions in a Guntown case are governed by the scheduling order long before they are governed by cross-examination, and counsel who retain late pay for it in compressed records requests and rushed evaluations. Purinton Analytics builds its Guntown engagements around the disclosure calendar from the first call: the conflict check, the records request, the evaluee interview, and the written report are each placed against the dates counsel already has on the docket. Within that framework the firm provides [vocational expert witness](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) and [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) opinions for plaintiff and defense counsel alike, in a form the opposing party is expected to examine closely.

## The courts that hear Guntown matters

Guntown lies in Lee County, and its state-court litigation proceeds in the Circuit Court serving that county; the firm accepts engagements for that court from either side of the caption. Federal matters in Mississippi may be heard in the United States District Court for the Northern District of Mississippi or the United States District Court for the Southern District of Mississippi, and the venue controlling a particular case, together with any expert registration or qualification requirement it imposes, is identified and confirmed during the conflict check and intake process. Statewide coverage is described on the [Mississippi locations page](/locations/mississippi/).

## Timing the analysis of a regional labor market

The labor market analysis for a Guntown evaluee cannot be rushed into a template, because the relevant market runs from local employment into the commuting area centered on Tupelo. The firm grounds its findings in Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Tupelo metropolitan area and for Mississippi statewide, in state workforce agency labor market information, and in direct labor market research where the referral questions require it, and it sequences that research so the data is current when the report is disclosed. The shared regional picture is set out on the [Tupelo metro page](/locations/mississippi/tupelo/). When an economic loss calculation must be disclosed on the same calendar, [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) allow independent economist partners to perform and sign the economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning work advances under a single engagement structure, with the deadlines aligned across both workstreams.

## Scheduling the evaluee's evaluation

The firm keeps no office in Guntown and publishes no address for the town, stating so directly because its reports depend on the same habit of precision. Records-based phases, from file review through [medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/), run remotely from the Kansas City home office. Interviews are held by video where the referral questions permit; when an in-person evaluation is warranted, it takes place in the evaluee's own community on a trip planned from Kansas City, booked early enough to leave room between evaluation, report, and disclosure. [Rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) work, being records-driven, can often be completed on shorter timelines.

Send the caption or party names through the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and include every date that is already fixed: disclosure deadlines, deposition settings, trial. Once the documented conflict check clears, intake confirms scope, records, and venue requirements against that calendar.
