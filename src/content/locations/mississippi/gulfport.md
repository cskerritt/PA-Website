The Gulfport-Biloxi metropolitan area spans Harrison County, Jackson County,
Hancock County, and Stone County, and litigation filed across that footprint
frequently turns on what an injured or displaced person can still do for
work and what future care will be required. Purinton Analytics answers those
questions for plaintiff and defense counsel alike through
[vocational evaluation and expert testimony](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under a single
engagement structure.

## Courts served across the metro

State court engagements are accepted for matters in the Circuit Court of
each county in the metro. Federal engagements are served in the United
States District Court for the Southern District of Mississippi, and the firm
also appears in the United States District Court for the Northern District
of Mississippi when a related matter is venued upstate. Because the same
report standard applies in every forum, counsel can disclose the written
opinion in state or federal court without reworking it, and opposing counsel
can test its foundations the same way in either setting. Venue-specific
registration or qualification requirements, where they exist, are identified
and confirmed during the conflict check and intake process.

## How appointments work here

There is no staffed Purinton Analytics office in Gulfport or Biloxi, and the
firm publishes no street address for this location. Saying so plainly is
deliberate: the same insistence on accuracy governs every figure and source
in the firm's reports. Counsel meet with the firm by appointment, most often
by video conference for records review and preparation sessions. When the
referral calls for in-person evaluation of an evaluee, or when a deposition
or trial date requires presence in the metro, travel is scheduled from the
Kansas City home office with enough lead time to protect the disclosure
calendar.

## Grounding opinions in the local labor market

Vocational findings for evaluees in the Gulfport-Biloxi area rest on Bureau
of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the metro
and for Mississippi statewide, state workforce agency labor market
information, and direct labor market research where the referral questions
warrant it. The report identifies the evaluee's commutable labor market and
explains the basis for that definition, rather than assuming a market and
leaving the assumption unstated. Where transferable skills analysis is part
of the referral, the report shows the pathway from the evaluee's work
history to the occupations considered, step by step.

## Beginning an engagement

Gulfport-Biloxi referrals move through the same intake as matters from the
rest of [Mississippi](/locations/mississippi/) and the firm's
[national practice](/locations/nationwide/). Counsel begin by submitting the
caption or party names through the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/),
noting any imminent disclosure, deposition, mediation, or trial dates. After
the conflict check clears, the firm confirms scope and fee terms in writing
and issues a records checklist tailored to the claims, so the file that
supports the opinion is assembled deliberately from the outset. The
engagement is structured so that either side of the caption could have
commissioned the same report and received the same answer.
