Counsel on opposite sides of a Ridgeland case should be able to read the
same expert report and disagree only about its implications, not its
sources. That is the standard Purinton Analytics writes to, and it is why
the practice is retained by plaintiff and defense counsel alike for
[vocational evaluation and expert testimony](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[coordinated damages assessment](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/),
where independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis
while the vocational and care-planning opinions are developed under the
same engagement structure.

## Where Ridgeland cases are heard

Ridgeland lies in Madison County, and engagements are accepted for matters
in the Circuit Court of Madison County as well as in Mississippi's federal
forums, the United States District Court for the Northern District of
Mississippi and the United States District Court for the Southern District
of Mississippi. Rather than presume which forum controls, the firm
identifies the governing court, and any qualification requirement it
imposes on a testifying expert, during the conflict check and intake
process. Matters pending in other parts of the
[Jackson metro](/locations/mississippi/jackson/) or elsewhere in
[Mississippi](/locations/mississippi/) can be added through the same
intake conversation.

## Grounding opinions in the regional market

Ridgeland sits within the employment market that surrounds Jackson, and
earning capacity opinions for Ridgeland evaluees are grounded in Bureau of
Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Jackson
metropolitan area and for Mississippi statewide, supported by state
workforce agency labor market information and, where a referral question
demands it, direct labor market research. The analysis never stops at
regional averages: it asks which occupations remain accessible to the
specific evaluee after accounting for restrictions, transferable skills,
and commuting realities, and the report cites each source at a level of
detail that lets the opposing expert retrace every step of the reasoning.

## Evaluation logistics, stated plainly

The firm has no staffed office in Ridgeland and publishes no local street
address, a fact stated here because accuracy about presence is part of the
same discipline the reports observe. Work is by appointment and, in most
engagements, fully remote: records analysis, structured vocational
interviews over secure video, and written reporting keyed to the case
schedule. If an in-person evaluation is warranted, or if deposition or
trial testimony requires attendance, travel is arranged from the Kansas
City home office and the evaluation is held in the evaluee's own community
rather than an unfamiliar setting. [Medical cost
projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/) assignments follow the same
treatment-record-first discipline, and testimony is prepared with the
expectation that every exhibit and source will be examined closely.
