Where an evaluee lives shapes how an evaluation should run, and for a Pearl
evaluee the practical questions come first: whether the vocational interview
happens by secure video or in person, where standardized testing takes
place, and which labor market the transferable skills analysis should
actually describe. Purinton Analytics starts there. The firm serves
plaintiff and defense counsel in Pearl with
[vocational evaluation and expert testimony](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and coordinated damages
work in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis under a single engagement structure.

## Rankin County venue and the federal forums

Pearl sits in Rankin County, and engagements are accepted for matters
proceeding in the Circuit Court of Rankin County as well as in Mississippi's
federal forums, the United States District Court for the Northern District
of Mississippi and the United States District Court for the Southern
District of Mississippi. The firm does not presume which court controls a
given dispute; the venue, and any expert qualification requirement it
imposes, is identified and confirmed during the conflict check and intake
process. Counsel handling related matters elsewhere in the
[Jackson metro](/locations/mississippi/jackson/) or across
[Mississippi](/locations/mississippi/) can route them through the same
intake.

## Reading the labor market from Pearl

Earning capacity findings for Pearl evaluees rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Jackson
metropolitan area and for Mississippi statewide, supplemented by state
workforce agency labor market information and direct labor market research
where the referral questions call for it. Pearl workers participate in a
regional employment market centered on Jackson, so the analysis weighs what
a specific evaluee can realistically reach given restrictions,
transportation, and work history rather than treating the metro as a single
undifferentiated pool. Every report states which market each conclusion
draws on and cites its sources in enough detail for opposing counsel to
retrace the work.

## How a Pearl evaluation proceeds

The firm keeps no staffed office in Pearl and publishes no street address
for this location; candor about presence is part of the same discipline
that governs the written opinions. Meetings are by appointment. Most files
proceed remotely from start to finish, with records review, a structured
interview by secure video, and written reporting moving on the litigation
calendar. When an in-person evaluation is warranted, or when a deposition
or trial setting requires attendance, travel is scheduled from the Kansas
City home office and the evaluation is arranged in the evaluee's own
community, so that observed functioning reflects the environment where the
evaluee actually lives and works. [Medical cost
projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/) and life care planning
assignments follow the same logistics, grounded in the treatment sources
the record supports.

## Starting a Pearl engagement

Referrals are welcome from either side of the caption, on affirmative
evaluations and on [rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/)
assignments alike, and the [nationwide practice](/locations/nationwide/)
applies the same written methodology in every venue, so a Rankin County file
is handled no differently from a matter in the firm's home jurisdiction. To
begin, [request a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) with the case caption and
party names, and flag any disclosure deadline, deposition setting, or trial
date that should drive the schedule. Scope and fee terms follow in writing
once the conflict check clears.
