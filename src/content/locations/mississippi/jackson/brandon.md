Many Brandon households earn their living across county lines, in the wider
employment market that surrounds Jackson, and that commuting pattern is
often the central analytical problem in a Brandon damages case: which
market does a diminished earning capacity claim actually describe, the work
available near home or the work the evaluee once drove to? Purinton
Analytics addresses that question for plaintiff and defense counsel through
[vocational evaluation and expert testimony](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and coordinated
engagements in which independent economist partners perform and sign the
economic analysis alongside the vocational and care-planning work.

## Venue in Rankin County and beyond

Brandon matters are accepted for the Circuit Court of Rankin County and, in

District of Mississippi or the United States District Court for the
Southern District of Mississippi, whichever the parties' posture supplies.

[Jackson metro](/locations/mississippi/jackson/) or across

## Commuting patterns and the earning capacity analysis

Because a Brandon evaluee's realistic labor market may extend well past the
town itself, vocational conclusions here rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates for the Jackson metropolitan
area and for Mississippi statewide, along with state workforce agency labor
market information and direct labor market research where warranted. The
transferable skills analysis then narrows from the regional data to the
individual: restrictions, driving tolerance, schedule limits, and work
history all determine how much of the metro market remains genuinely open
to this evaluee. The written opinion documents that narrowing step by step,
because it is precisely where cross-examination tends to concentrate.

## Meeting and evaluating in Brandon

Purinton Analytics is candid that it has no staffed office and no street
address in Brandon; local meetings occur by appointment only. That candor
costs nothing, because the work is built to travel: records review,
secure-video interviews, standardized testing where appropriate, and
written reporting proceed remotely in most files. When the referral
questions require an in-person evaluation, or when deposition or trial
attendance is set, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office and
the evaluation takes place in the evaluee's own community, so functional
observations match the setting in which the evaluee actually lives.
[Medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/) engagements
run on the same record-driven footing, with every projected item traced to
a treating provider recommendation or an equivalent documented source.
