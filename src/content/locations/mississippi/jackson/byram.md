## Byram's courts: Hinds County and the federal districts

Byram sits in Hinds County, and referrals are accepted for matters in the
Circuit Court of Hinds County and for federal cases in the United States
District Court for the Northern District of Mississippi or the United
States District Court for the Southern District of Mississippi. Venue is
never assumed from a mailing address: the controlling court for each
matter, and any registration or qualification requirement it imposes on a
testifying expert, is identified during the conflict check and intake
process. Counsel can bring related matters from across the
[Jackson metro](/locations/mississippi/jackson/) or the rest of
[Mississippi](/locations/mississippi/) through that same intake.

## A small community inside a regional market

Byram is a small suburb, but its residents work within the regional
employment market centered on Jackson, and vocational opinions are built
accordingly. Findings rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational
employment and wage estimates for the Jackson metropolitan area and for
Mississippi statewide, with state workforce agency labor market information
and direct labor market research added where the referral questions require
it. The transferable skills analysis pays particular attention to what a
given evaluee can reach from Byram in practice, weighing restrictions,
transportation, and prior work history before any occupation is treated as
available. Reports state the basis for each conclusion so that either side
of the caption can test it.

## What remote-first means for a Byram evaluee

The firm keeps no staffed office in Byram, publishes no street address for
this location, and meets by appointment only; it says so plainly because
honest description of presence is part of the product. In the typical file,
nothing is lost by distance: records review, a structured vocational
interview conducted over secure video, standardized testing where
appropriate, and the written report all proceed without travel. Where an
in-person evaluation genuinely serves the referral, or where deposition or
trial attendance is required, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home
office and the evaluation is conducted in the evaluee's own community.
[Medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/) work draws
strictly on the treatment record.
