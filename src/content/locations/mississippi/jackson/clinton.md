Clinton litigation is Hinds County litigation, and that venue carries its
own rhythms: a jury pool with its own sense of work and wages, dockets that
move on their own schedule, and opposing experts who know the ground.
Purinton Analytics supports plaintiff
and defense counsel in Clinton with
[vocational evaluation and expert testimony](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which the economic analysis is performed and signed by independent
economist partners while the vocational and care-planning opinions proceed
under the same engagement.

## Hinds County courts and Mississippi's federal districts

Referrals are accepted for matters in the Circuit Court of Hinds County and
for cases proceeding federally in the United States District Court for the
Northern District of Mississippi or the United States District Court for
the Southern District of Mississippi. The firm treats venue as a fact to be
confirmed, not assumed: the controlling court for a given matter, and any
qualification requirement that venue imposes on a testifying expert, is
identified during the conflict check and intake process. Counsel with
companion cases across the [Jackson metro](/locations/mississippi/jackson/)
or elsewhere in [Mississippi](/locations/mississippi/) can bring them
through the same channel.

## What the labor market looks like from Clinton

Vocational findings for Clinton evaluees are grounded in Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Jackson
metropolitan area and for Mississippi statewide, along with state workforce
agency labor market information and direct employer research where the
questions in the referral justify it. Clinton residents work within a
regional market anchored by Jackson, and the analysis distinguishes
between the jobs that exist somewhere in that market and the jobs a
particular evaluee can realistically secure given restrictions,
transportation, and vocational history. Each report shows its data trail so
that either side of the caption can test the reasoning.

## Conducting the evaluation itself

Purinton Analytics maintains no staffed office in Clinton and lists no
street address for this location; meetings happen by appointment, and the
firm says so directly because honesty about presence is part of its
publication standard. In most files the entire engagement runs remotely,
from records review through a secure-video vocational interview to the
written report. Standardized testing is added where the referral questions
make it useful, and the testing choices are documented alongside the
results. When an in-person evaluation better serves the referral, or
when deposition or trial testimony requires presence, travel proceeds from
the Kansas City home office and the evaluation is scheduled in the
evaluee's own community, keeping observed functioning consistent with daily
life. [Medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/) and
care-planning work draw on the same record-first approach.

## Beginning a Clinton matter

Affirmative evaluations, [rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/)
of opposing expert reports, and testimony-only assignments are all accepted
on the same terms, and the [nationwide practice](/locations/nationwide/)
applies an identical written methodology wherever the case sits. To start,
[request a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) with the case caption and party
names, noting any imminent disclosure deadline, deposition, or trial
setting. Once the conflict check clears, scope and fee terms are confirmed
in writing before work begins.
