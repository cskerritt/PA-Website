## Answering the earning capacity question for a Richland evaluee

Richland is a small suburb whose workers draw their opportunities from the
regional market around Jackson, so the earning capacity analysis starts
wide and narrows carefully. It begins with Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates for the Jackson metropolitan
area and for Mississippi statewide, adds state workforce agency labor
market information, and turns to direct labor market research when a
referral question needs employer-level answers. It then narrows to the
individual: what this evaluee's restrictions, skills, education, and
transportation actually permit. The written opinion preserves that whole
chain, because an earning capacity conclusion that cannot show its steps
will not survive a competent cross-examination and should not be offered.
