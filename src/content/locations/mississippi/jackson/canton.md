The forum question comes early in many Canton cases: Mississippi maintains
both the United States District Court for the Northern District of
Mississippi and the United States District Court for the Southern District
of Mississippi, state claims proceed in circuit court, and where a Canton
matter lands affects everything from disclosure format to trial logistics.
Purinton Analytics prepares its opinions to hold up in any of those forums,
serving plaintiff and defense counsel with
[vocational evaluation and expert testimony](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[coordinated damages assessment](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis.

## Madison County trial courts and forum selection

For state court matters, Canton engagements proceed in the Circuit Court of
Madison County; for federal matters, in whichever of Mississippi's district
courts the case's posture supplies. The firm treats the controlling venue
as an intake question, not an assumption: it is identified and confirmed
during the conflict check and intake process, together with any
registration or qualification requirement that court imposes on a
testifying expert. Counsel with parallel matters elsewhere in the
[Jackson metro](/locations/mississippi/jackson/) or in other
[Mississippi](/locations/mississippi/) venues can bring them through the
same process.

## Canton evaluees and the surrounding labor market

Canton is a smaller community within the metropolitan region that surrounds
Jackson, and its workers' realistic opportunities span both nearby
employment and the broader regional market. Earning capacity opinions
therefore rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and
wage estimates for the Jackson metropolitan area and for Mississippi
statewide, joined by state workforce agency labor market information and
direct labor market research where the referral questions justify the added
step. What separates a defensible opinion from a vulnerable one is the
bridge between regional data and the individual evaluee, so each report
spells out how restrictions, transferable skills, and access to
transportation narrow the market for this particular person.

## Appointments, evaluations, and travel

Purinton Analytics keeps no staffed office in Canton and publishes no
street address for this location. Meetings are by appointment, and the firm
discloses that structure candidly because factual precision about presence
belongs to the same standard its written work observes. Engagements
typically proceed remotely, from records review through secure-video
vocational interviews to the finished report. When an in-person evaluation
is warranted, or when a deposition or trial setting requires presence,
travel originates at the Kansas City home office and the evaluation is
held in the evaluee's own community so that observed functioning reflects
daily reality rather than an unfamiliar clinical setting. Care-planning
and [medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/)
assignments follow the same appointment structure.

Affirmative evaluations, [rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/)

from either side of the caption, and the
[nationwide practice](/locations/nationwide/) holds each one to a single
written methodology regardless of venue. Begin by
[submitting a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) with the case caption and
party names, flagging any disclosure deadline, deposition, or trial date
that should control the calendar. Once the check clears, scope and fee
terms follow in writing before any substantive work starts.
