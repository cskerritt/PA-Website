## Courts serving Madison County litigants

Matters arising in Madison are accepted for the Circuit Court of Madison
County and for Mississippi's federal forums, the United States District
Court for the Northern District of Mississippi and the United States
District Court for the Southern District of Mississippi. Which court
controls a particular dispute is a question the firm answers file by file:
the venue, along with any registration or qualification requirement it
places on a testifying expert, is confirmed during the conflict check and
intake process. Related matters pending elsewhere in the
[Jackson metro](/locations/mississippi/jackson/) or in other parts of
[Mississippi](/locations/mississippi/) move through the same intake without
a separate engagement.

## Madison evaluees in a regional market

Madison is a suburban community whose working residents draw on the broader
employment market surrounding Jackson, and vocational conclusions here have
to respect that structure. Findings rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates for the Jackson metropolitan
area and for Mississippi statewide, together with state workforce agency
labor market information and targeted labor market research where a
referral question warrants it. The transferable skills analysis asks what
work a specific evaluee can actually obtain given restrictions, commuting
capacity, and work history, and the report records which data source
supports each step so the opinion can withstand cross-examination.

## Appointment logistics for Madison files

There is no staffed Purinton Analytics office in Madison, and the firm
publishes no street address here, because accuracy about presence belongs
to the same standard that governs its reports. Engagements ordinarily run
remotely: records review, interviews by secure video, standardized testing
where appropriate, and written reporting all proceed on the litigation
schedule. Where the referral calls for an in-person evaluation, or where a
deposition or trial date requires attendance, travel is arranged from the
Kansas City home office and the evaluation is set in the evaluee's own
community. [Medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/)
work follows the same pattern, tied to the treatment recommendations the
record actually contains.
