## Sourcing the labor market analysis

Yazoo City sits within the Jackson, MS metropolitan area, and the
vocational analysis for a Yazoo City evaluee draws on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Jackson
metropolitan area and for Mississippi statewide, on state workforce
agency labor market information, and on direct labor market research
where the referral questions call for ground-level verification. The
written opinion states which source supports each finding, and the
transferable skills analysis distinguishes what the broader market
contains from what this evaluee can actually reach, given restrictions,
work history, and circumstances documented in the record. Where the
record does not support a conclusion, the report says so; that candor is
the same discipline the intake sequence is built on.
