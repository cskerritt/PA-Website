In catastrophic injury litigation, the care plan often drives the damages
model, and Flowood counsel frequently come to Purinton Analytics with the
care questions first: what treatment the record supports, what it will
plausibly require over a lifetime, and how a jury should hear it explained.
The firm answers with [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/)
and [medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/) built
strictly from the treatment record, paired with
[vocational evaluation and expert testimony](/services/vocational-expert-witness/)
and with coordinated engagements in which independent economist partners
perform and sign the economic analysis under the same engagement structure,
for plaintiff and defense counsel alike.

## Rankin County venue for Flowood matters

Flowood cases are accepted for the Circuit Court of Rankin County and for
federal matters in the United States District Court for the Northern
District of Mississippi or the United States District Court for the
Southern District of Mississippi. The firm does not guess at which forum
controls: the governing court, along with any expert qualification or
registration requirement that venue imposes, is confirmed during the
conflict check and intake process. Related files pending elsewhere in the
[Jackson metro](/locations/mississippi/jackson/) or across
[Mississippi](/locations/mississippi/) can be consolidated into the same
intake conversation.

## Care planning and the vocational picture together

A life care plan for a Flowood evaluee begins and ends with the medical
record: treating provider recommendations, therapy histories, equipment and
medication needs, and the functional limitations they document. The
vocational side of the same file draws on Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates for the Jackson metropolitan
area and for Mississippi statewide, plus state workforce agency labor
market information and direct labor market research where warranted, since
Flowood workers participate in the regional market surrounding Jackson.
Keeping both analyses inside one engagement means the restrictions the care
plan documents and the restrictions the earning capacity opinion applies
never drift apart, a consistency opposing counsel will look for and should
find.

## Seeing the evaluee in Flowood

There is no staffed Purinton Analytics office in Flowood and no published
street address here; the firm meets by appointment and states that fact
plainly, because describing presence accurately is part of the discipline
its reports are built on. Most engagements run remotely, with records
review, secure-video interviews, and written reporting proceeding on the
litigation calendar. When the referral requires an in-person evaluation, or
when deposition or trial dates require attendance, travel is scheduled from
the Kansas City home office and the evaluation is arranged in the evaluee's
own community, where observed functioning is most reliable.
