In a Greenville case, the dispute over earning capacity usually comes down
to the labor market itself: what work exists for this evaluee, in this
place, with these restrictions. Purinton Analytics starts there. Vocational
opinions for Washington County matters are built from published labor
market data and documented research before they are built into a report,
which is why they survive contact with opposing counsel. The firm supports
plaintiff and defense engagements across the Greenville micropolitan area
with [vocational expert evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[forensic economic damages coordination](/services/forensic-economic-damages/),
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under a
single engagement structure.

## How the firm shows up in Greenville

Purinton Analytics keeps no staffed office in Greenville and publishes no
street address for this location; stating that plainly is part of the same
discipline that governs the reports. Service is by appointment and built
for distance. Records review, attorney conferences, and standardized
testing under publisher remote-administration protocols proceed without
travel where appropriate, and evaluations can be scheduled in the
evaluee's own community. When an in-person evaluation is warranted, or
when a deposition or trial date requires the expert's presence, travel is
scheduled from the Kansas City home office and confirmed against the
litigation calendar during intake. The logistics are the same ones the
firm runs for its [nationwide practice](/locations/nationwide/); Greenville
is simply one of the communities that practice reaches.

## Data before opinion

Employability and earning capacity findings for Greenville-area evaluees
rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage
estimates from the OEWS program for the relevant Mississippi area and for
the state as a whole, along with state workforce agency labor market
information and direct labor market research where the referral questions
warrant it. Each source is cited in the report so that either side can
trace a conclusion back to the underlying data, and the analysis describes
the market the evaluee can realistically reach rather than a national
abstraction.
