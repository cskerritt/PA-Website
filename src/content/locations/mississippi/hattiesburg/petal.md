## Forrest County Courts and the Available Forums

State-court matters involving Petal parties are heard in the Circuit Court,
and Forrest County is the venue reference point for most of them. Federal
practice in the state is divided between the United States District Court for
the Northern District of Mississippi and the United States District Court for
the Southern District of Mississippi; both are available forums for
Mississippi litigation, and the controlling venue for a particular Petal case
is identified during the conflict check and intake. Reports are prepared to
the same standard for either system: a defined scope, a documented records
basis, and opinions written so that opposing counsel can trace every
conclusion back to its source. Any venue-specific registration or
qualification requirement is likewise confirmed during intake, without
guesswork.

## Where Petal Sits in the Hattiesburg Labor Market

For earning-capacity purposes, Petal is not analyzed in isolation. Its
residents participate in the labor market centered on Hattiesburg, the
principal city of the metro, and a transferable-skills analysis for a Petal
evaluee has to reflect the occupations realistically reachable within that
commuting pattern. Vocational findings rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates for the Hattiesburg metropolitan
area and for Mississippi statewide, supplemented by state workforce agency
labor market information and direct employer research where the referral
questions warrant it. The qualitative picture matters as much as the data
source: a smaller community adjoining a regional employment center presents a
narrower occupational base than a major city, and the written opinion says so
plainly rather than importing assumptions from larger markets. The
[Hattiesburg metro page](/locations/mississippi/hattiesburg/) describes the

## Evaluation Logistics for a Petal Evaluee

The firm keeps no office in Petal or anywhere else in Mississippi, and it says
so plainly because accuracy about presence belongs in the same discipline that
governs its reports. Most engagements proceed remotely: records review,
structured vocational interviews by video, and written opinions delivered on
the disclosure calendar counsel sets. When the referral calls for in-person
evaluation, the assessment is arranged in the evaluee's own community, and
travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office for evaluations,
depositions, and trial testimony alike. Where a case pairs vocational and
care-planning opinions with economic loss questions, the firm offers
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis
while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement
structure. The same model serves counsel [nationwide](/locations/nationwide/),
so a Petal case with out-of-state parties or parallel filings does not require
a separate expert relationship.
