counsel in the Meridian area typically reach out with a scheduling order
already in hand. Purinton Analytics builds each engagement backward from
those dates: the conflict check, records collection, evaluation, and report
drafting are sequenced against the operative calendar rather than left
open-ended. Within that structure, plaintiff and defense counsel retain the
firm for [vocational evaluation and expert testimony](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),

[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)

## The Meridian venue footprint

The Meridian area takes in Lauderdale County and Clarke County, and
engagements are accepted for matters venued in the Circuit Court of each.
In federal practice the firm serves counsel in the United States District
Court for the Southern District of Mississippi, and it accepts matters in
the United States District Court for the Northern District of Mississippi
when a case is venued in that district. The report standard does not change
with the forum: every opinion identifies its sources, states its
assumptions, and shows its reasoning, so that it holds up whether it is
read by a Circuit Court jury or tested in a federal expert challenge.

## Labor market foundations for Meridian evaluees

Employability and earning capacity conclusions for evaluees in the Meridian
area are grounded in Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and
wage estimates for the relevant area and for Mississippi statewide, along
with state workforce agency labor market information and direct labor
market research where the referral questions require it. The report is
explicit about the evaluee's commutable labor market and the reasons for
defining it as it does, which lets opposing counsel test the definition
directly instead of guessing at it.

## Referring a Meridian matter

Meridian engagements use the same intake as the rest of
[Mississippi](/locations/mississippi/) and the firm's
[national practice](/locations/nationwide/). Submit the caption or party
names through the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and flag any
imminent disclosure, deposition, mediation, or trial dates so the timeline
can be built around them from the first call. Once the conflict check
clears, scope and fee terms are confirmed in writing and a records
checklist tailored to the claims issues promptly. Any venue-specific
registration or qualification requirement is identified and confirmed
during the conflict check and intake process, and the same intake serves
either side of the caption.
