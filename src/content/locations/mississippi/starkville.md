The Starkville micropolitan area takes in Oktibbeha County and Webster
County, and the firm accepts matters venued in the Circuit Court serving
each. Federal matters arising in the region may proceed in the
United States District Court for the Northern District of Mississippi or the
United States District Court for the Southern District of Mississippi, and
the firm works in both. Because retention comes from plaintiff and defense
counsel alike, the same records-driven method applies regardless of which

## By appointment, without a local storefront

The firm maintains no staffed office in Starkville and publishes no street
address for this location; candor about presence is part of the same
discipline that governs the reports themselves. Meetings with counsel and
evaluations are scheduled by appointment. Most engagements begin remotely,
with records review, structured interviews, and standardized testing
administered under publisher protocols where remote administration is
appropriate. When the referral questions call for in-person evaluation, or
when deposition and trial dates require presence, travel is scheduled from
the Kansas City home office and planned around the litigation calendar
rather than around office hours.

## Labor market research for Starkville evaluees

Employability and earning capacity conclusions for evaluees in Oktibbeha
County and Webster County rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational
employment and wage estimates from the OEWS program for the relevant
Mississippi area and for the state as a whole, supplemented by state
workforce agency labor market information and direct labor market research
where the referral questions warrant it. The analysis addresses the market
where the evaluee actually lives and works, not a generic national profile,
and the sources are cited so that either side can trace every conclusion
back to published data. The same standards apply whether the matter is
local to Starkville or part of the firm's
[nationwide practice](/locations/nationwide/).

## Starting a Starkville engagement

Retention begins with a documented conflict check, a defined scope, and a
records request tailored to the claims. Submit the caption or party names
through the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and note any imminent
disclosure, deposition, or trial dates so scheduling can be confirmed at
intake. Any venue-specific registration or qualification requirement is
identified and confirmed during the conflict check and intake process, and
counsel receive a written scope and fee structure before work begins.
