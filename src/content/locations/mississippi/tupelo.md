Counsel in smaller markets often face a practical problem: the vocational
and care-planning experts they need are based elsewhere, and engaging one
should not mean accepting a thinner work product. The Tupelo micropolitan
area is served by Purinton Analytics on exactly the same terms as the
largest metros in the national practice. The firm provides
[vocational evaluation and expert testimony](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under a single
engagement structure, for plaintiff and defense counsel alike.

## Venues in and around Tupelo

The Tupelo area comprises Lee County, Itawamba County, and Prentiss County,
and engagements are accepted for matters venued in the Circuit Court of
each. Federal matters are served in the United States District Court for
the Northern District of Mississippi, and the firm also accepts engagements
in the United States District Court for the Southern District of
Mississippi when a related case sits there. Whatever the forum, the written
opinion follows the same structure: identified sources, stated assumptions,
and a reasoning path that opposing counsel can follow from the record to
the conclusion.

## Remote-first, in person when it matters

Much of a vocational or life care planning engagement travels well: records
review, counsel conferences, methodology discussions, and pre-deposition
preparation are handled by video conference, which keeps a Tupelo file
moving without waiting on travel. The firm keeps no staffed office in
Tupelo and publishes no street address for this location, and it says so
plainly because accuracy about presence belongs to the same discipline that
governs its reports. When the referral questions require in-person
evaluation, or when deposition and trial dates require attendance, travel
is scheduled from the Kansas City home office and planned around the
disclosure calendar, so counsel know early which steps happen remotely and
which require a visit.

## Reading the Tupelo labor market honestly

An earning capacity opinion for an evaluee in a micropolitan area cannot
simply borrow big-city data. Vocational findings for Tupelo-area evaluees
rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage
estimates for the relevant area and for Mississippi statewide, state
workforce agency labor market information, and direct labor market research
where the referral warrants it. The report defines the evaluee's commutable
labor market, explains why that definition fits the evaluee's circumstances,
and keeps the occupational conclusions tied to sources that either side can
verify.

## How to refer a Tupelo matter

Engagements from the Tupelo area begin the same way as matters from the
rest of [Mississippi](/locations/mississippi/) and the firm's
[national practice](/locations/nationwide/): submit the caption or party
names through the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and note any
imminent disclosure, deposition, mediation, or trial dates. Once the
conflict check clears, the firm confirms scope and fee terms in writing and
issues a records checklist tailored to the claims. Any venue-specific
registration or qualification requirement is identified and confirmed
during the conflict check and intake process, so nothing about the
engagement's foundation is left to be discovered late.
