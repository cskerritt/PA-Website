The Natchez micropolitan area does not stop at the state line, and neither
do the cases that come out of it. The area spans Adams County and Jefferson
County in Mississippi and Concordia Parish in Louisiana, so a single
incident can generate filings on either side of the state boundary.
Purinton Analytics serves plaintiff and defense counsel throughout this
cross-state region with
[vocational expert evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under a
single engagement structure.

## A venue footprint in two states

On the Mississippi side, the firm accepts matters venued in the Circuit
Court serving Adams County and Jefferson County, and in the United States
District Court for the Southern District of Mississippi and the United
States District Court for the Northern District of Mississippi as venue
dictates. Matters arising in Concordia Parish, Louisiana are accepted on
the same terms, with any Louisiana-specific venue questions addressed at
intake. Because the practice is built for cross-jurisdiction work, counsel
whose case crosses the state line retain an expert whose method does not
change with the forum. The broader state picture, including other
Mississippi metros, is laid out on the
[Mississippi locations page](/locations/mississippi/).

## Presence by appointment, stated plainly

The firm keeps no staffed office in Natchez and publishes no street address
for this location. That candor is deliberate: a practice whose reports are
built to withstand cross-examination cannot afford to imply offices it does
not have. Counsel and evaluees are served by appointment. Records review,
attorney conferences, and standardized testing under publisher remote
protocols are handled from a distance where appropriate, and when the
referral requires an in-person evaluation or the calendar requires a
witness at deposition or trial, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City
home office around the court's dates.

## Labor market analysis for a cross-state region

Employability and earning capacity findings for evaluees in Adams County,
Jefferson County, or Concordia Parish rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates from the OEWS program for the
relevant areas of Mississippi and Louisiana and for each state, along with
state workforce agency labor market information and direct labor market
research where warranted. A cross-state metro is exactly where that
precision matters: the market an evaluee can actually reach may sit in
either state, and the analysis follows the evaluee's real commuting
patterns rather than an arbitrary boundary. These are the same sourcing
standards the firm applies across its
[nationwide practice](/locations/nationwide/).

## Starting a Natchez engagement

Engagements begin the way they do everywhere else in the practice: with a
documented conflict check, a defined scope, and a records request tailored
to the claims. Submit the caption or party names through the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/), note which court the matter is
filed in, and flag any imminent disclosure, deposition, or trial dates.
Any venue-specific registration or qualification requirement, in either
state, is identified and confirmed during the conflict check and intake
process.
