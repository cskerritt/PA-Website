## Labor market research behind the opinions

Vocational findings for evaluees in Carroll County and Leflore County rest
on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates
from the OEWS program for the relevant Mississippi area and for the state
as a whole, supplemented by state workforce agency labor market
information and direct labor market research where the referral questions
warrant it. Where transferable skills analysis is part of the referral,
it proceeds from documented work history rather than assumption. The
analysis speaks to the employment actually available in
the evaluee's own market, and every source is cited so the path from data
to conclusion stays open to inspection by either side. Those standards
hold across the firm's [nationwide practice](/locations/nationwide/) and
they hold here.
