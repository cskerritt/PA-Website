A vocational or life care planning opinion is only as strong as the record
underneath it, and that is the standard Purinton Analytics brings to
Hattiesburg matters. Every engagement in the metro begins the way it does
everywhere else in the practice: with a documented conflict check, a defined
scope, and a records request tailored to the claims, so that the written
opinion rests on a file both sides can examine. Plaintiff and defense
counsel retain the firm here for
[vocational evaluation and expert testimony](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under a single
engagement structure.

## Where Hattiesburg matters are heard

The Hattiesburg metropolitan area takes in Forrest County, Lamar County, and
Perry County, and engagements are accepted for matters venued in the Circuit
Court of each. In federal practice, the firm serves counsel in the United
States District Court for the Southern District of Mississippi and accepts
matters in the United States District Court for the Northern District of
Mississippi when a case is venued there. Reports are written to a single
standard across forums, so an opinion disclosed in a county Circuit Court
matter is prepared with the same rigor counsel would expect in federal
court. Any venue-specific registration or qualification requirement is
identified and confirmed during the conflict check and intake process.

## Appointments, not an office

The firm maintains no staffed office in Hattiesburg and publishes no street
address for this location; candor about presence is part of the same
discipline that governs its reports. Counsel work with the firm by
appointment: video conferences carry most records review, strategy, and
pre-deposition preparation, and in-person work is scheduled when the
referral requires it. Evaluations can be conducted in the evaluee's
community, and when deposition or trial settings call for attendance,
travel is arranged from the Kansas City home office against the case's
disclosure calendar rather than on an open-ended basis.

## Labor market evidence for Hattiesburg evaluees

Findings on employability and earning capacity are grounded in Bureau of
Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the
Hattiesburg metropolitan area and for Mississippi statewide, supplemented by
state workforce agency labor market information and by direct labor market
research where the referral questions warrant it. The report states which
labor market defines the evaluee's commutable area and why, and it ties
each occupational conclusion to identified sources, so that the analysis
can be tested on cross-examination rather than taken on faith.

## Opening a Hattiesburg file

Referrals from the metro enter the same intake used across
[Mississippi](/locations/mississippi/) and the firm's
[national practice](/locations/nationwide/). To begin, submit the caption or
party names through the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and note any
imminent disclosure, deposition, mediation, or trial dates. Once the
conflict check clears, scope and fee terms are confirmed in writing, a
records checklist issues, and the report timeline is set against the
deadlines that govern the case. The same intake serves either side of the
caption, and the finished opinion is written to be read closely by the side
that did not commission it.
