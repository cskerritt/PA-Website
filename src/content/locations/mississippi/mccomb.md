Counsel on either side of a McComb caption retain the same expert practice
on the same terms. Purinton Analytics works for plaintiffs and for
defendants across the McComb micropolitan area, and treats that
availability to both sides as a methodological commitment: the records request, the
evaluation protocol, and the report format are identical whichever party
signs the engagement letter. A report written that way needs no adjustment
when the other side reads it, because it was written to be read by the
other side from the outset. Referrals from Pike County and Walthall County
typically involve
[earning capacity and employability evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) in catastrophic injury
matters, and [rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/)
of opposing vocational and care-planning opinions.

## Where McComb matters are heard

The McComb micropolitan area comprises Pike County and Walthall County, and
the firm accepts matters venued in the Circuit Court serving each. Federal
matters from the area may proceed in the United States District Court for
the Southern District of Mississippi, and the firm likewise appears in the
United States District Court for the Northern District of Mississippi when
a related matter is venued there. Engagements are also accepted for
McComb-area parties whose litigation is pending elsewhere in the state or
out of state; the [Mississippi locations page](/locations/mississippi/)
describes the statewide practice.

## By-appointment service, honestly described

Purinton Analytics keeps no staffed office in McComb and publishes no
street address for this location. The firm states that plainly because
accuracy about presence belongs to the same discipline that governs its
written opinions. In practice, distance rarely drives cost or delay: records
review and attorney conferences are remote, structured interviews and
standardized testing are administered under publisher protocols remotely
where appropriate or scheduled in the evaluee's community, and when an
evaluation, deposition, or trial requires the expert in person, travel is
arranged from the Kansas City home office and confirmed against the
litigation calendar at intake.

## Reading the McComb labor market

Vocational findings for evaluees in Pike County and Walthall County rest on
Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates from
the OEWS program for the relevant Mississippi area and for the state as a
whole, supplemented by state workforce agency labor market information and
direct labor market research where the referral questions warrant it. The
opinions describe the employment realistically available to the specific
evaluee in the market where that person lives, with every source cited so
that opposing counsel can trace the analysis. Those standards do not vary
by venue; they are the same ones applied throughout the firm's
[national practice](/locations/nationwide/).

## Starting a McComb engagement

Whether the inquiry comes from plaintiff or defense counsel, retention
starts with a documented conflict check, a defined scope, and a records
request tailored to the claims. To begin, submit the caption or party names
through the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and note any imminent
disclosure, deposition, or trial dates. Any venue-specific registration or
qualification requirement is identified and confirmed during the conflict
check and intake process, and a written scope and fee structure precedes
any substantive work.
