The Jackson, MS metropolitan area generates the full range of matters in which
vocational and care-planning evidence is contested: personal injury, workers'
compensation, employment, and family law. Counsel on either side of the
caption retain Purinton Analytics here for
[vocational evaluation and expert testimony](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under a single
engagement structure. The methodology matches the approach applied throughout
[Mississippi](/locations/mississippi/) and the national practice.

## Venue footprint in the Jackson metro

Engagements are accepted for matters venued in the Circuit Court of the
counties that make up the metro: Hinds County, Madison County, Rankin County,
Copiah County, Simpson County, Scott County, Holmes County, and Yazoo County.
Federal matters are served in the United States District Court for the
Southern District of Mississippi and, where a case arises elsewhere in the
state, the United States District Court for the Northern District of
Mississippi. The written report is prepared to the same standard regardless
of forum, so that an opinion disclosed in a state court matter withstands the
scrutiny expected in federal practice, and the reverse.

## By-appointment service, stated plainly

The firm keeps no staffed office in Jackson and publishes no street address
for this location, because accuracy about presence is part of the same
discipline that governs its reports. Meetings with counsel are held by
appointment, by video conference, or at counsel's offices. When the referral
questions call for in-person evaluation, or when deposition and trial
settings require attendance, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home
office and is planned against the operative disclosure calendar rather than
left open-ended. Records conferences and pre-deposition preparation are
routinely handled remotely, which keeps the engagement moving between
scheduled trips.

## Labor market research for Jackson evaluees

Employability and earning capacity findings for evaluees in the metro are
grounded in data specific to this market. The analysis draws on Bureau of
Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Jackson
metropolitan area and for Mississippi statewide, state workforce agency labor
market information, and direct labor market research where the referral
requires it. The report is explicit about which market defines the evaluee's
commutable labor market and why, so that both retaining and opposing counsel
can trace every conclusion back to an identified source rather than to
assertion.

## Starting a Jackson engagement

Every engagement begins with a documented conflict check, a defined scope,
and a records request tailored to the claims. Counsel with matters anywhere
in the metro, or elsewhere through the firm's
[national practice](/locations/nationwide/), can begin by submitting the
caption or party names through the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and
noting any imminent disclosure, deposition, mediation, or trial dates. Once
the conflict check clears, the firm confirms scope and fee terms in writing,
issues a records checklist, and sets the report timeline against the
deadlines that actually govern the case. Plaintiff and defense referrals
proceed through the identical intake, and the resulting opinion is written to
be read closely by whichever side did not retain it.
