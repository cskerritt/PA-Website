indistinguishable in rigor from an opinion prepared for any courtroom in the
country, and that consistency is what Purinton Analytics offers counsel
here. The firm runs a single methodology across its
[national practice](/locations/nationwide/): the same intake, the same
records discipline, the same report structure, whether the matter is venued
in a Mississippi Circuit Court or a distant federal district. Plaintiff and
defense counsel in the Columbus area engage the firm for
[vocational evaluation and expert testimony](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)

## Venues for Columbus-area matters

The Columbus area comprises Lowndes County and Noxubee County, and
engagements are accepted for matters venued in the Circuit Court of each.
Federal matters are served in the United States District Court for the
Northern District of Mississippi, and the firm also accepts engagements in
the United States District Court for the Southern District of Mississippi
when a case is venued in that district. Any venue-specific registration or
qualification requirement is identified and confirmed during the conflict
check and intake process, so forum-specific questions are resolved before
the substantive work begins.

## Honest logistics, by appointment

Purinton Analytics maintains no staffed office in Columbus and publishes no
street address for this location. The firm states that plainly because
accuracy about its own footprint is inseparable from the accuracy it claims
for its reports. Engagements proceed by appointment: records review,
counsel conferences, and pre-deposition preparation are handled by video
conference, evaluations can be conducted in the evaluee's community, and
when depositions, mediations, or trial settings require attendance, travel
is scheduled from the Kansas City home office against the operative
disclosure calendar.

## Labor market research for Columbus evaluees

Employability and earning capacity findings for evaluees in the Columbus
area are grounded in Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and
wage estimates for the relevant area and for Mississippi statewide, state
workforce agency labor market information, and direct labor market research
where the referral questions require it. The report defines the evaluee's
commutable labor market, explains the basis for that definition, and ties
each occupational conclusion to sources that either side can pull and
check. National methodology does not mean generic data; it means a
disciplined way of reading the local market.

## Referring a Columbus matter

Columbus engagements begin through the same intake used across
[Mississippi](/locations/mississippi/) and the wider practice. Submit the
caption or party names through the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/)
and note any imminent disclosure, deposition, mediation, or trial dates.
Once the conflict check clears, the firm confirms scope and fee terms in
writing and issues a records checklist tailored to the claims, and the
report timeline is set against the case's governing deadlines. The intake
is identical for plaintiff and defense referrals, and the finished opinion
is written for the scrutiny of the side that did not retain the firm.
