Small cities are where a [national expert practice](/locations/nationwide/)
proves whether its structure actually works. Waveland is a small city
in the Gulfport-Biloxi metropolitan area, and a Waveland referral gets
the identical treatment a case from a major metropolitan market gets: the same conflict check, the same
defined scope, the same records discipline, and the same willingness to
be examined by whichever side did not do the retaining. Purinton
Analytics takes
[vocational expert](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) and
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) engagements from
Waveland counsel without a minimum case size and without treating the
matter as an outpost assignment.

## Where Waveland cases are decided

Waveland lies in Hancock County, so its civil actions are tried in the
Circuit Court of Hancock County. When a matter belongs in federal court,
Mississippi's available forums are the United States District Court for
the Northern District of Mississippi and the United States District
Court for the Southern District of Mississippi; the controlling district
for any specific case, and any expert qualification requirement that
venue enforces, is worked out during the conflict check and intake
process rather than presumed from geography. Opinions are drafted so
that the analysis reads the same in either courthouse.

## Honest measurement of a coastal labor market

An evaluee living in Waveland competes for work across the
Gulfport-Biloxi metropolitan area, and the vocational analysis is
scoped to match. Findings rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates for the Gulfport-Biloxi
metro area and for Mississippi statewide, on state workforce agency
labor market information, and on direct labor market research when the
referral raises a question those sources cannot settle. Where the
injury forecloses past work, the report says which occupations remain
and why; where the data will not support a conclusion, the report says
that too. The methodology section explains how each source was used,
which lets the opinion be evaluated on its reasoning instead of its
signature.

## Assembling the full damages picture

Waveland cases that involve permanent injury often require earnings,
care, and economic loss to be presented as a coherent whole. In
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/),
independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis
while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure, so counsel receive an integrated analysis in which
every expert remains separately accountable for his or her own
methods. That division of labor is disclosed openly, because it is a
strength, not a footnote.

## Scheduling and first contact

There is no firm office in Waveland, and none is claimed; the practice
operates by appointment from its Kansas City home office, handling
records and interviews remotely where that serves the case and
traveling for in-person evaluation, deposition, or trial when the
referral or the calendar requires it. To engage the firm,
[request a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) with the caption or party
names and the case's expert deadlines; a prompt reply confirms whether
the firm can take the matter and on what schedule. The
[Gulfport-Biloxi metro page](/locations/mississippi/gulfport/) describes
coverage along the coast, and the
[Mississippi page](/locations/mississippi/) sets out the statewide
practice.
