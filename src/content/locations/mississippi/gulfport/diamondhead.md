A vocational opinion earns its keep in a Diamondhead case only if it
would read the same had the other side commissioned it. That is the
test Purinton Analytics writes to. The plaintiff firm asking what its
injured client can still earn and the defense firm asking whether the
claimed loss is real are posing the same question, and the firm answers
it the same way for either caller: through a
[vocational evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) that
applies documented restrictions to occupations that actually exist,
through a [life care plan](/services/life-care-planning/) whose every
item traces to the medical record, and, when the opposing expert has
disclosed first, through
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) that grades
the disclosed report against its own cited sources and says where it
holds as candidly as where it breaks.

## The courthouse for a Diamondhead claim

Diamondhead is a Hancock County city, and civil actions arising there are
tried in the Circuit Court of Hancock County. Mississippi's federal trial
courts are the United States District Court for the Northern District of
Mississippi and the United States District Court for the Southern
District of Mississippi; which district controls a given matter, and what
registration or qualification formality that venue imposes on a
testifying expert, is identified and confirmed during the conflict check
and intake process. Findings and reasoning are documented so the opinion
carries between state and federal practice without rework, and
[expert testimony and litigation consulting](/services/expert-testimony-litigation-consulting/)
support continues through deposition and trial in whichever forum the
case lands.

## Measuring the market a Diamondhead evaluee faces

Diamondhead is a small city, and its residents' employment prospects are
properly measured across the Gulfport-Biloxi metropolitan area rather
than within municipal limits. Vocational findings for a Diamondhead
evaluee rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and
wage estimates for the Gulfport-Biloxi metropolitan area and for
Mississippi statewide, on state workforce agency labor market
information, and on direct labor market research when a disputed
occupation calls for it. The same sourcing discipline applies in review
work: when an opposing report asserts a labor market conclusion, the
rebuttal traces that assertion back to the data and says plainly whether
the data supports it. Market scale and conditions are described
qualitatively, and the path from work history to conclusion is written
out so a reader need not take anything on trust.

## Distance, stated plainly

The firm's staffed office is in Kansas City, and this page does not
dress that up: Diamondhead engagements run by appointment, and no local
address is published because none exists. In practice the distance
rarely shows. The case file is electronic from intake onward, the
evaluee interview proceeds over secure video where the referral permits
it, and in-person assessment, when warranted, is conducted in the
evaluee's own community rather than in a borrowed conference room. When
deposition or trial dates harden, travel from the Kansas City home
office puts the expert wherever the testimony is taken. Counsel are
buying the analysis, not the letterhead, and the analysis does not
depend on a coastal address.
