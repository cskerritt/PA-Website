Expert deadlines drive Ocean Springs referrals more than geography does.
By the time counsel reach out, a scheduling order usually already fixes
when the vocational or care-planning opinion must be disclosed, and the
engagement is planned backward from that date: records requested at
intake, the evaluee interview set early, the draft delivered with room
for counsel's questions before service. Purinton Analytics runs Ocean
Springs engagements on that calendar discipline for either side of the
caption, whether the assignment is a full
[vocational evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) or a
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) of the
opposing expert's disclosed report.

## The venues an Ocean Springs case may see

Civil matters from Ocean Springs are heard in the Circuit Court of
Jackson County. Mississippi's federal trial courts, the United States
District Court for the Northern District of Mississippi and the United
States District Court for the Southern District of Mississippi, stand as
the available federal forums; the one that actually controls a case is
identified during the conflict check and intake process, and any
registration or qualification requirement particular to that venue is
confirmed at the same time. Deadlines differ by forum, which is another
reason the calendar is settled before the analysis begins.

## What the labor market data can and cannot say

Ocean Springs sits within the Gulfport-Biloxi metropolitan area, so an
honest earning-capacity analysis looks at the region an evaluee can
commute across, not at the town in isolation. The firm grounds vocational
findings in Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage
estimates for the Gulfport-Biloxi metro area and for the state of
Mississippi, in state workforce agency labor market information, and in
direct labor market research when a specific occupation needs testing.
Just as important, the report refuses to stretch the data: where the
sources are silent, the opinion says so, because an overreached
conclusion is the first thing a competent cross-examiner will find.

## Seeing the evaluee without a local office

The firm has no staffed location in Ocean Springs and lists no address
here; its home office is in Kansas City, and every coastal Mississippi
engagement is served by appointment. Record review and interviews are
conducted remotely when remote handling meets the standard of care for
the assignment. When it does not, or when deposition and trial dates
require presence, travel from Kansas City is scheduled so that the
evaluation happens in the evaluee's own community and the testimony
happens where the court convenes. Counsel are told at engagement which
parts of the workup will be remote and which in person, so the plan is
on the record before the other side asks about it.
