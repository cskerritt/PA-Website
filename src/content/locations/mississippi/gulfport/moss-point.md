In a catastrophic injury case out of Moss Point, the hardest questions
are usually about care: what the injured person will need, for how long,
delivered by whom, and at what foundation in the medical record. Purinton
Analytics is retained by counsel on both sides of such cases to answer
those questions through
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) that ties every
recommended item of future care to clinical evidence, and through
[medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/) when the
dispute has narrowed to pricing alone. The vocational question, what work
if any remains open to the evaluee, is answered in the same document set
with the same sourcing discipline.

## Jackson County trial courts and the federal map

Moss Point matters proceed in the Circuit Court of Jackson County, and
the firm's reports are drafted to that court's disclosure practice from
the start. Where a case belongs in federal court, Mississippi's forums
are the United States District Court for the Northern District of
Mississippi and the United States District Court for the Southern
District of Mississippi; the conflict check and intake process settles
which district governs and surfaces any venue-specific qualification
requirement before the engagement letter is signed. The substance of the
opinion is venue-independent by design.

## Work capacity measured against a regional market

Moss Point is a smaller Jackson County community within the
Gulfport-Biloxi metropolitan area, so residual earning capacity is
assessed against the regional market an evaluee can realistically
reach. That assessment stands on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational
employment and wage estimates for the Gulfport-Biloxi metro area and for
Mississippi statewide, on state workforce agency labor market
information, and on direct labor market research where the case calls
for it. The report presents the market qualitatively and traces each
conclusion to its source, which lets opposing counsel test the opinion
on its merits instead of its adjectives. Vocational and care-planning
findings are cross-checked against each other before service, so the
document set tells a single consistent story about the same evaluee.

## One record, several experts, no shortcuts

When care costs must become a present-value damages figure, the
economics is not done in-house. Through
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/),
independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis
while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement
structure. The care planner does not vouch for the economist and the
economist does not vouch for the care planner; each opinion is built to
stand alone under cross-examination, which is what coordination is for.
