A Long Beach resident's realistic job market does not stop at the city
line. People who live here work across the coastal metro, and any
credible opinion about lost earning capacity has to be measured against
that wider commuting market rather than against the town alone. That is
the analytical starting point for every
[vocational expert engagement](/services/vocational-expert-witness/)
Purinton Analytics accepts from Long Beach counsel: define the market the
evaluee can actually reach, apply the documented restrictions, and report
what remains, in terms that hold up whether the retaining party sits at
the plaintiff's table or the defense's.

## Harrison County and the federal courts of Mississippi

Long Beach lies in Harrison County, and its civil disputes are tried in
the Circuit Court of Harrison County. For matters with a federal hook,
Mississippi's district courts are the United States District Court for
the Northern District of Mississippi and the United States District Court
for the Southern District of Mississippi; determining which of them
governs a specific case, and whether the venue imposes its own
qualification formalities on a testifying expert, is part of the conflict
check and intake process. The firm has structured its reports so that
none of that variation touches the substance of the opinion.

## Data first, conclusions second

The market analysis is grounded in Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates published for the
Gulfport-Biloxi metropolitan area and for Mississippi statewide, in state
workforce agency labor market information, and in direct labor market
research when a referral question turns on a particular occupation or
employer type. Scale and opportunity are described qualitatively, sources
are cited in the report itself, and the transferable skills analysis is
laid out step by step so that the path from work history to residual
capacity can be followed, and challenged, by anyone reading it. If the
evaluee's restrictions narrow the field to a few occupations, the report
names them; if nothing realistic remains, it says that plainly.

## When the case needs more than a vocational number

Serious injury litigation rarely ends at earning capacity. Life care
planning and economic loss analysis frequently ride on the same facts,
and through
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis
while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement
structure. Counsel get an integrated damages picture without any expert
vouching for another's methods, which preserves each opinion's
independence at deposition.
