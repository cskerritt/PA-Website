Before anything else, counsel with a Gautier referral usually want to know
how the evaluee will actually be seen. The answer is that most of the
workup happens without anyone traveling: medical and employment records
arrive electronically, the vocational interview runs over secure video at
a time that suits the evaluee, and drafting proceeds on the litigation
calendar. When the questions in the referral require observation in
person, the evaluation happens in Gautier itself, in the community where
the evaluee lives and would look for work, with travel scheduled from the
firm's Kansas City home office. That model serves
[vocational expert](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) and
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) engagements for
plaintiff and defense counsel alike, and it keeps the schedule in
counsel's control rather than the airline's.

## Jackson County courts and the federal alternatives

Gautier is a Jackson County community, and civil claims arising there are
heard in the Circuit Court of Jackson County. At the federal level,
Mississippi is served by the United States District Court for the Northern
District of Mississippi and the United States District Court for the
Southern District of Mississippi; the controlling venue for a given case,
together with any venue-specific qualification requirement, is confirmed
during the conflict check and intake process. The firm's written work
holds to the same standard in every one of those rooms.

## A commuter's labor market, measured honestly

Gautier is a smaller city inside the Gulfport-Biloxi metropolitan area,
and its residents' realistic employment options spread across the metro's
commuting range. Vocational conclusions therefore rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the
Gulfport-Biloxi metro area and for Mississippi statewide, alongside state
workforce agency labor market information and direct labor market research
where warranted. The report states which occupations remain open after
restrictions, shows where each finding comes from, and avoids the
temptation to describe a market more generous or more bleak than the data
support. If retraining or accommodation would change the picture, the
analysis addresses that possibility directly rather than leaving it for
cross-examination to raise.

## Future care, priced for the record

Where a Gautier case involves lasting injury, the life care plan sets out
future medical and support needs with each item tied to the record and to
clinical foundation. When the dispute narrows to the pricing of future
care rather than its scope, the firm accepts
[medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/) assignments
as a focused alternative. Either way, the deliverable is written so that
opposing counsel can check the arithmetic of care against its sources,
which is precisely what makes it useful to the side that retained it.
