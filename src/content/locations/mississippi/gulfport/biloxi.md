## Harrison County venue and the federal forums

Biloxi civil matters proceed in the Circuit Court of Harrison County, where
vocational and care-planning opinions are disclosed, deposed, and tried
like any other expert evidence. Mississippi's federal forums are the United
States District Court for the Northern District of Mississippi and the
United States District Court for the Southern District of Mississippi; the
district that controls a particular Biloxi case, along with any
venue-specific registration or qualification requirement, is identified and
confirmed during the conflict check and intake process. Nothing about the
report's method or candor changes between the county courthouse and the
federal bench, and nothing about it changes with the identity of the
retaining party.

## Reading the coastal labor market

An earning-capacity opinion for a Biloxi evaluee has to describe the labor
market that person actually faces, not a generic one. Vocational findings
here rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage
estimates for the Gulfport-Biloxi metropolitan area and for Mississippi
statewide, supplemented by state workforce agency labor market information
and by direct labor market research when the referral questions warrant
it. The report characterizes the market qualitatively, names its sources,
and applies the evaluee's documented restrictions to occupations that
exist in the region, so opposing counsel can trace each conclusion to its
data rather than to the expert's say-so.

## Appointments, not an office

The firm's only staffed office is its Kansas City home office. It keeps no
Biloxi storefront and publishes no street address here, because accuracy
about presence belongs to the same discipline that governs the reports.
Records move electronically, vocational interviews proceed by secure video
when that serves the case, and when the referral calls for an in-person
evaluation, a deposition, or trial testimony, travel is scheduled from
Kansas City so the expert meets the evaluee in Biloxi and appears where the
court sits. Counsel get a national practice's consistency without paying
for a local office that would add nothing to the opinion.
