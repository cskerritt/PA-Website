No qualified vocational expert lives in every city where a case arises,
and D'Iberville counsel do not need one to. What they need is an opinion
that survives contact with opposing counsel, delivered on the court's
schedule. Purinton Analytics supplies that from a deliberately
remote-first structure: the file is worked wherever the records are, the
evaluee is interviewed by secure video or seen in person in D'Iberville
as the referral requires, and the resulting
[vocational evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) or
[life care plan](/services/life-care-planning/) reads identically whether
the engagement letter came from a plaintiff firm or a defense firm.

## The courts serving Harrison County litigants

Civil actions arising in D'Iberville are heard in the Circuit Court of
Harrison County. On the federal side, Mississippi offers the United
States District Court for the Northern District of Mississippi and the
United States District Court for the Southern District of Mississippi as
its available forums; the conflict check and intake process establishes
which venue controls the matter at hand and whether it carries any
expert registration or qualification formality. Counsel are told at
intake exactly what the disclosure package will contain, so there are no
surprises when the report is served.

## Situating a D'Iberville evaluee in the metro economy

D'Iberville is a smaller Harrison County city inside the Gulfport-Biloxi
metropolitan area, which means an evaluee's employment prospects are
regional by nature. The firm's vocational conclusions draw on Bureau of
Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the
Gulfport-Biloxi metro area and for Mississippi statewide, on state
workforce agency labor market information, and on direct labor market
research where a disputed occupation deserves closer study. Every
placement or preclusion finding in the report is tied to a named source,
because an unsourced vocational opinion is an invitation to exclusion.
The analysis also documents its own limits, distinguishing what the
data establish from what they merely suggest.

## Answering the other side's expert

A meaningful share of D'Iberville referrals arrive after the opposing
expert has already disclosed. For those cases the firm performs
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) work,
examining the disclosed report's methodology, data currency, and internal
consistency, and stating candidly where the opposing opinion is sound as
well as where it is not. That candor is deliberate: a rebuttal that
concedes nothing persuades no one, least of all a judge weighing
reliability.
