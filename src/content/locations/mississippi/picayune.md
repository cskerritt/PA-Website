An evaluee in Picayune does not need to travel to a distant office to be
evaluated. Purinton Analytics structures its Pearl River County engagements
around the person being assessed: records are reviewed remotely, interviews
and standardized testing are scheduled in the evaluee's own community or by
secure remote administration, and the resulting report reaches counsel on
the litigation calendar. Referrals from the Picayune micropolitan area
typically call for
[vocational expert evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) of
employability and earning capacity, [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/)
for catastrophic injuries, and
[medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/) where future
care is disputed but a full life care plan is not required.

## Courts serving Picayune and Pearl River County

The Picayune micropolitan area consists of Pearl River County, and the firm
accepts matters venued in the Circuit Court there. Federal matters from the
region may be heard in the United States District Court for the Southern
District of Mississippi, and the firm also appears in the United States
District Court for the Northern District of Mississippi when a case is
venued upstate. Plaintiff and defense counsel are served on the same terms:
the method, the sources, and the report format do not change with the
client, which is why the work holds up when the other side reads it.
Counsel handling matters across the rest of the state can start from the
[Mississippi locations page](/locations/mississippi/).

## Logistics without a local office

There is no staffed Purinton Analytics office in Picayune, and the firm
publishes no street address for this location. Saying so plainly matters:
accuracy about presence is part of the same discipline that governs the
opinions. What counsel get instead is a by-appointment practice built for
distance. Records review, conferences with counsel, and much of the
evaluation work proceed remotely. When an in-person evaluation is warranted,
or when a deposition or trial requires the expert in the room, travel is
scheduled from the Kansas City home office with dates confirmed against the
court's schedule at intake.

## Grounding opinions in the local labor market

Vocational conclusions for Pearl River County evaluees rest on Bureau of
Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates from the OEWS
program for the relevant Mississippi area and for the state, together with
state workforce agency labor market information and direct labor market
research where the referral questions warrant it. The point of that
grounding is simple: an employability opinion is only as good as the market
it describes, so the analysis addresses the jobs realistically available to
this evaluee, in this labor market, with citations either side can check.
The same sourcing standards apply across the firm's
[national practice](/locations/nationwide/).

## Starting a Picayune engagement

Every file begins with a documented conflict check, a defined scope, and a
records request tailored to the claims. To begin, submit the caption or
party names through the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and flag any
imminent disclosure, deposition, or trial dates. Any venue-specific
registration or qualification requirement is identified and confirmed
during the conflict check and intake process, and counsel receive a written
scope and fee structure before the work starts.
