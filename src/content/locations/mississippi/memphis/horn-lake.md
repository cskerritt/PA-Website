## Where a Horn Lake case is heard

Horn Lake sits in DeSoto County, and state-law matters arising there
proceed in the Circuit Court serving that county. When a claim carries
federal jurisdiction, the available Mississippi forums are the United
States District Court for the Northern District of Mississippi and the
United States District Court for the Southern District of Mississippi; the
controlling venue for a specific caption, together with any qualification
or registration requirement it imposes on a testifying expert, is
identified during the conflict check and intake. The report itself is
venue-agnostic by design, written and sourced so that either a county jury
or a federal judge can follow every step.

## Measuring earning capacity in a commuter suburb

Few Horn Lake evaluees work only in Horn Lake. The town belongs to a
metropolitan labor market centered on Memphis, and an honest transferable
skills analysis has to weigh openings across that region against the
documented restrictions that determine what commute, schedule, and physical
demands the evaluee can actually sustain. The data foundation is the
Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for
the Memphis metropolitan area and for Mississippi statewide, alongside
state workforce agency labor market information and direct employer
research where the referral questions require it, all presented
qualitatively and cited to the source. Counsel can read the fuller
regional treatment on the [Memphis metro page](/locations/tennessee/memphis/)
and the statewide overview on the [Mississippi page](/locations/mississippi/).

## Evaluation in the evaluee's own community

When an in-person vocational interview or life care planning assessment is
warranted, it is arranged where the evaluee lives, and travel for that
visit, and for any later deposition or trial appearance, is scheduled from
the Kansas City home office. Everything else in the engagement, from
records review to counsel conferences to draft delivery, is handled
remotely on the litigation calendar. The firm keeps no Horn Lake office
and never suggests otherwise; stating that plainly is part of the same
discipline the reports are held to. Referrals from outside the region
follow the identical model, described on the
[nationwide practice page](/locations/nationwide/).
