Most expert problems in Olive Branch litigation are really calendar
problems. The disclosure deadline, the deposition setting, and the trial
date are usually fixed long before the expert is retained, and a report
rushed to meet them reads exactly like what it is. Purinton Analytics
builds each engagement backward from those dates, so that
[vocational evaluation and testimony](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/),
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure, arrive with time left for counsel to actually use
them. Retentions come from either side of the caption, on the same terms
and to the same standard.

## Courts serving Olive Branch litigation

An injury, employment, or disability matter arising in Olive Branch under
state law proceeds in the Circuit Court serving DeSoto County. Where
federal jurisdiction attaches, Mississippi offers the United States
District Court for the Northern District of Mississippi and the United
States District Court for the Southern District of Mississippi as forums;
which of them controls a given case, and what expert qualification or
registration steps that venue requires, is settled during the conflict
check and intake process. Because the answer can shift the disclosure
sequence, the intake conversation covers scheduling in the first call, not
after the retention letter.

## Olive Branch in the regional employment picture

An Olive Branch evaluee competes for work in a market that reaches across
DeSoto County, into Memphis, and through the wider region, and a credible
earning-capacity opinion has to define that territory rather than gesture
at it. The analysis rests on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational
employment and wage estimates for the Memphis metropolitan area and for
Mississippi statewide, supplemented by state workforce agency labor market
information and by direct labor market research when specific placements
are in dispute. Conclusions are stated qualitatively and sourced line by
line; nothing enters the report that opposing counsel cannot pull and
verify. The broader regional analysis appears on the
[Memphis metro page](/locations/tennessee/memphis/), with the firm's
statewide practice described on the [Mississippi page](/locations/mississippi/).

## How the evaluation work gets scheduled

Purinton Analytics maintains no office in Olive Branch and publishes no
local address, because there is none; the home office is in Kansas City.
Records review and transferable skills analysis proceed remotely as soon as
the file arrives. If the referral questions warrant an in-person vocational
interview or a life care planning assessment, the appointment is set in the
evaluee's own community at a point in the calendar that leaves room for
drafting, counsel review, and disclosure. Travel for that appointment, and
later for deposition or trial, is scheduled from the Kansas City home
office. The same calendar-first approach serves referrals across the
country, as the [nationwide practice page](/locations/nationwide/) explains.

## Beginning the engagement

The first step is not a retainer; it is a documented conflict check with a
defined scope behind it. Counsel should
[submit the caption or party names](/refer-a-case/) and list every date
