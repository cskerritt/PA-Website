For counsel practicing in Hernando, the venue question usually comes
first, so this page does too. A state-law injury, employment, or
disability claim arising in this small DeSoto County town proceeds in the
Circuit Court serving DeSoto County, while claims with federal
jurisdiction look to the United States District Court for the Northern
District of Mississippi or the United States District Court for the
Southern District of Mississippi; the district that governs a particular
case, and any expert qualification step that forum requires, is confirmed
during the conflict check and intake. Purinton Analytics writes for
whichever bench and jury the caption produces, and it is retained by
plaintiff and defense counsel on equal footing.

## Hernando workers and the metro labor market

Hernando is a small town inside a large metropolitan economy, and both
halves of that sentence matter to an earning-capacity opinion. Confining
the analysis to local openings would understate the market an evaluee can
reach; assuming effortless access to every job in greater Memphis would
overstate it, especially where documented restrictions limit commuting or
sustained physical demands. The opinion draws the line with evidence:
Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for
the Memphis metropolitan area and for Mississippi statewide, state
workforce agency labor market information, and direct labor market
research when a disputed placement calls for employer-level verification.
Scale and trend are described qualitatively, never as unsourced figures.

[Memphis metro page](/locations/tennessee/memphis/), and the firm's work
across the state is outlined on the [Mississippi page](/locations/mississippi/).
