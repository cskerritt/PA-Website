Southaven sits on the Mississippi side of a metropolitan economy anchored in
Memphis, and that single fact of geography runs through nearly every damages
question that arises there. An injured worker may live under Mississippi law
while earning a living across the state line, and an opinion that treats the
town as a self-contained job market will not hold up when the opposing table
starts asking where the evaluee actually worked. Purinton Analytics is
retained by plaintiff and defense counsel on identical terms for
[vocational expert opinions](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## DeSoto County venue for Southaven matters

State-law claims arising in Southaven proceed in the Circuit Court serving
DeSoto County. Matters carrying federal jurisdiction may be filed in the
United States District Court for the Northern District of Mississippi or the
United States District Court for the Southern District of Mississippi; the
district that controls a particular caption, along with any expert
registration or qualification requirement that venue imposes, is confirmed
during the conflict check and intake rather than assumed. The written work
does not change with the forum: every source is cited precisely enough for
an opposing expert to retrace it, whether the report is headed for a DeSoto
County jury or a federal courtroom.

## A labor market that crosses the state line

Earning-capacity analysis for a Southaven evaluee has to be honest about
where the jobs are. Realistic alternatives may sit in Mississippi, in
Memphis, or elsewhere in the surrounding region, and restrictions that make
a commute unsustainable can shrink that territory considerably. The
vocational foundation stays qualitative and verifiable: Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Memphis
metropolitan area and for Mississippi statewide, state workforce agency
labor market information, and direct employer contact when the referral
questions call for it. The regional picture is developed further on the
[Memphis metro page](/locations/tennessee/memphis/), and statewide practice
is summarized on the [Mississippi page](/locations/mississippi/).

## Appointment-based logistics for Southaven evaluees

There is no Purinton Analytics office in Southaven, and the firm says so
plainly; its home office is in Kansas City, and accuracy about presence is
part of the same discipline that governs its reports. Records review,
transferable skills analysis, and conferences with counsel are handled
remotely on the litigation calendar. When the referral requires an
in-person vocational interview or a life care planning assessment, it is
arranged in the evaluee's own community, and travel for evaluations,
depositions, and trial testimony is scheduled from the Kansas City home
office. Counsel with matters beyond this region will find the same model
described on the [nationwide practice page](/locations/nationwide/).

Every Southaven engagement opens the same way: a documented conflict check,
