Behind every referral from the Laurel area is a person whose working life
or future care needs are in dispute, and the evaluation of that person is
where the engagement begins. Purinton Analytics structures its Laurel work
around the evaluee: records are gathered before conclusions are formed, the
evaluation is conducted where the evaluee actually lives and works, and the
resulting opinion describes what the evidence supports rather than what
either side hoped to hear. On that foundation, plaintiff and defense
counsel retain the firm for
[vocational evaluation and expert testimony](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic

engagement structure.

## Courts serving the Laurel area

The Laurel area comprises Jones County and Jasper County, and engagements
are accepted for matters venued in the Circuit Court of each. Federal
matters are served in the United States District Court for the Southern
District of Mississippi, and the firm also accepts engagements in the
United States District Court for the Northern District of Mississippi when
a case sits in that district. Any venue-specific registration or
qualification requirement is identified and confirmed during the conflict
check and intake process, before the work begins rather than after a
question arises.

## Meeting evaluees and counsel by appointment

The firm has no staffed office in Laurel and publishes no street address
for this location; describing its presence accurately is part of the same
discipline it applies to sourcing a report. Evaluations can be conducted in
the evaluee's community, which often produces a more accurate picture of
daily function and local work options than requiring travel to a distant
office. Counsel conferences, records reviews, and pre-deposition
preparation proceed by video conference, and when depositions or trial
settings require attendance, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home
office against the case's disclosure calendar.

## The labor market a Laurel evaluee actually faces

Employability and earning capacity findings are grounded in Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the relevant area
and for Mississippi statewide, state workforce agency labor market
information, and direct labor market research where the referral questions
call for it. The report defines the evaluee's commutable labor market and
explains that definition, then ties each occupational conclusion to
identified sources. An opinion about what work exists for a Laurel evaluee
should rest on evidence about this market, and the report shows that it
does.
