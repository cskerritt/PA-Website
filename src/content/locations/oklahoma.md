An expert opinion earns nothing from the courtroom it is filed in; it earns
everything from the record beneath it. Purinton Analytics serves Oklahoma
counsel on that premise, providing vocational expert and life care planning
services statewide, accepting engagements from either side of the caption,
and applying the same documented methodology in every venue the state's
dockets present, state or federal.

## Oklahoma Venues Served

Engagements are accepted for matters in the District Court of any Oklahoma
county and in each of the state's federal trial courts: the United States
District Court for the Northern District of Oklahoma, the United States
District Court for the Eastern District of Oklahoma, and the United States
District Court for the Western District of Oklahoma. Because vocational and
care-planning opinions rest on records and published labor market data
rather than on proximity to a courthouse, the venue itself changes the
logistics of testimony, not the substance of the analysis.

## How the Firm Serves Oklahoma

The firm maintains its home office in Kansas City and keeps no staffed
office in Oklahoma; every Oklahoma meeting is by appointment. Most of an
engagement proceeds without travel at all: records review, transferable
skills analysis, labor market research, and report drafting are performed
remotely, and evaluee interviews are conducted by secure video conference or
scheduled in person in the evaluee's own community. When the referral
questions call for in-person evaluation, or when deposition and trial dates
require presence, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office.

Vocational findings for Oklahoma evaluees rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates for the relevant Oklahoma
metropolitan areas and for the state as a whole, supplemented by state
workforce agency labor market information and direct employer research where
the referral questions warrant it. Where the damages analysis requires
economic projection, the firm offers coordinated engagements in which
independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis while
the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement
structure. The same structure and the same disclosure practices apply to
plaintiff and defense engagements alike, and every report is written so that
it can withstand examination by the opposing side.

## Qualification and Disclosure

Expert disclosure practice differs between Oklahoma's state and federal
courts, and scheduling orders differ from judge to judge. Counsel are asked
to share the operative scheduling order at intake so that report and
disclosure deadlines are calendared from the start. Any venue-specific
registration or qualification requirement is identified and confirmed during
the conflict check and intake process.

## Starting an Oklahoma Engagement

Engagements begin with a documented conflict check, not a sales
conversation. Submit the caption or party names through the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and note any imminent disclosure,
deposition, or trial dates; the firm responds with a defined scope and a
records request tailored to the claims. Counsel with matters beyond Oklahoma
can review the [nationwide practice overview](/locations/nationwide/) for
the full geographic scope of the firm's work.
