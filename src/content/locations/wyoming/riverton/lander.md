## Lander in the regional labor market

Lander is part of the Riverton, WY area, a rural labor market in which Riverton serves as the principal city and employment options are narrower than in Wyoming's larger centers. That narrowness matters analytically. An earning capacity opinion for a Lander evaluee has to engage honestly with what work actually exists within a reasonable commuting range, not with occupations that appear only in distant metros. Vocational findings therefore rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the relevant area and for Wyoming statewide, on state workforce agency labor market information, and on direct labor market research when the published estimates are too coarse for a rural community.
