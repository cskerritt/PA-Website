A vocational opinion is only as durable as the questions it answers, and in
Gillette matters those questions are usually concrete: what work can this
evaluee still perform, what will it pay, and what treatment and support
will the coming decades require. Purinton Analytics prepares written
answers to those questions for attorneys on either side of the caption,
through [vocational expert witness work](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) of opposing
reports. Where a full damages picture is needed, the firm structures
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Campbell County venue coverage

Matters are accepted in the District Court for Campbell County and in the
United States District Court for the District of Wyoming. Whether the case
sits in state or federal court, the working assumption is the same: the
report will be read by opposing counsel, so it must disclose its records,
its methods, and its sources completely enough that a stranger to the file
can retrace the analysis. That assumption governs plaintiff retentions and
defense retentions equally. Counsel handling Wyoming matters outside the
Gillette area will find the statewide picture on the firm's
[Wyoming page](/locations/wyoming/).

## Presence in Gillette, stated plainly

The firm keeps no staffed office in Gillette and publishes no street
address for this location; every meeting is by appointment. Stating that
plainly is deliberate, because honesty about logistics belongs to the same
discipline that governs the opinions themselves. Most Campbell County
engagements are conducted remotely, through secure records transfer,
structured interviews by video, and standardized testing under publisher
protocols where remote administration is supported. When the referral
questions require an in-person evaluation, or when deposition and trial
dates require the expert's presence, travel is scheduled from the Kansas
City home office and confirmed against the court's calendar at intake.

## Labor market evidence for a regional economy

Vocational findings for Gillette evaluees are grounded in Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates covering the Gillette
area and Wyoming statewide, in state workforce agency labor market
information, and in direct labor market research where the referral
warrants it. The analysis asks what occupations actually exist in the
regional economy and what they actually pay, rather than importing a
national profile that no local employer recognizes. Life care plans and
medical cost projections for Campbell County residents are priced to the
evaluee's own market using provider-specific inquiries and published fee
data, so that availability and cost reflect the community where care will
actually be delivered.

## Beginning with a conflict check

Every Gillette engagement begins with a documented conflict check and a
defined scope before any records are reviewed. Submit the caption or party
names through the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and note any
imminent disclosure, deposition, or trial dates so the schedule can be
evaluated candidly before retention. Any venue-specific registration or
qualification requirement is identified and confirmed during the conflict
check and intake process. Counsel with related matters in other states can
review the firm's [national coverage](/locations/nationwide/) to see how
the same remote-first practice and the same report standard apply
nationwide.
