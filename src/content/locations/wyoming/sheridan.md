Purinton Analytics is retained in Sheridan by both sides of the bar, and
that fact shapes everything about how the work is done. A firm that
expects to be hired by plaintiff counsel in this case and defense counsel
in the next cannot write advocacy dressed as analysis; it has to write
opinions that hold their shape regardless of who paid for them. For
Sheridan County matters that means
[vocational expert evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/) built on
disclosed records and cited sources, plus
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Sheridan County venue footprint

Engagements are accepted for matters venued in the District Court for
Sheridan County and in the United States District Court for the District
of Wyoming. The same report serves either forum, and the same report
serves either side of the caption, because it is written to be examined:
the records reviewed are enumerated, the interview and testing are
documented, and the labor market and cost data are cited to sources
opposing counsel can retrieve. Counsel with matters elsewhere in the state
can begin with the firm's [Wyoming page](/locations/wyoming/), which
describes the statewide practice.

## What by-appointment actually means here

There is no staffed Purinton Analytics office in Sheridan, and the firm
publishes no street address for this location. Meetings are by
appointment, and the firm states that without embellishment, because
honesty about presence is part of the credibility it asks courts to
extend to its opinions. Most Sheridan County files are worked remotely
from intake through the written report: secure records transfer,
structured video interviews, and standardized testing under publisher
protocols where remote administration is supported. When an in-person
evaluation is warranted, or when deposition or trial requires attendance,
travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office around the court's
calendar.

## Evidence from the Sheridan labor market

Employability and earning capacity findings for Sheridan evaluees rest on
Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates
covering the Sheridan area and Wyoming statewide, on state workforce
agency labor market information, and on direct labor market research
where the referral questions warrant it. Conclusions are framed against
the occupations and wages of the regional economy rather than national
composites that no local employer would recognize. Life care plans for
Sheridan County residents apply the same locality discipline on the cost
side, using provider-specific inquiries and published fee data for the
evaluee's own market so the projection reflects local availability and
local pricing.

## The conflict check comes before everything

No records are reviewed and no opinions are scoped until conflicts are
cleared. To begin, [request a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) with the
case caption and parties, and identify any imminent disclosure,
deposition, or trial dates so feasibility can be addressed at the same
time. Any venue-specific registration or qualification requirement is
identified and confirmed during the conflict check and intake process.
The firm's [national coverage](/locations/nationwide/) page describes how
the same intake, methodology, and report standard extend to engagements
across the country.
