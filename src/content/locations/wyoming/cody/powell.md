A vocational opinion written for a Powell case has to survive the same cross-examination as one written for a case in a major city, and the analysis is harder, not easier, when the evaluee lives in a small Park County town. The temptation in rural files is to lean on broad national tables and call the work done; the discipline is to define the labor market the evaluee can actually reach and to document why. Purinton Analytics brings that discipline to [vocational expert witness](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) and [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) engagements arising in Powell, accepting work from plaintiff and defense counsel alike and writing every report so the other side of the caption can test it line by line.

## A small town in the Cody area labor market

Earning capacity for a Powell evaluee cannot be read off a national average. Powell is a small community, and the employment realistically available to a resident depends on the occupations present in the surrounding Park County market, the commuting range the evaluee's restrictions permit, and the transferability of the skills the evaluee actually holds. Vocational findings therefore rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates covering the Cody area and Wyoming statewide, supplemented by state workforce agency labor market information and direct labor market research when the referral questions call for it. The wider market context, anchored by the principal city of Cody, is addressed on the [Cody metro page](/locations/wyoming/cody/). Where the damages case requires an economic dimension, the firm offers [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement structure.

## How a Powell evaluee is evaluated

The firm keeps no office in Powell and publishes no local address; candor about presence is part of the same standard that governs the reports themselves. Records review, transferable skills analysis, labor market research, and [medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/) work proceed from the Kansas City home office, and interviews are conducted by video when the referral questions permit. When an in-person evaluation is warranted, it is scheduled in the evaluee's own community, with travel planned from Kansas City around the case's disclosure and deposition calendar. Counsel anticipating a need for [testimony or litigation consulting](/services/expert-testimony-litigation-consulting/) at deposition or trial should raise dates during intake so appearances are protected early.
