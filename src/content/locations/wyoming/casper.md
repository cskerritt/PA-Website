Most Casper referrals arrive as a question about damages that the records
alone cannot answer: whether an evaluee can return to past work, what
earning capacity survives an injury, and what a lifetime of care will
require. Purinton Analytics answers those questions for plaintiff and
defense counsel through
[earning capacity and employability evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/), and it offers
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Courts serving the Casper metro

The firm accepts matters venued in the District Court for Natrona County
and in the United States District Court for the District of Wyoming.
Because the same written opinion may be examined by retaining and opposing
counsel alike, every report is built on a record both sides can inspect:
the reviewed records are enumerated, the interview and testing are
documented, and the published sources behind each labor market and cost
finding are cited. The methodology does not shift between state and federal
court, and it does not shift between the plaintiff and defense sides of the
caption. Statewide coverage beyond the Casper metro area is described on
the firm's [Wyoming page](/locations/wyoming/).

## How a by-appointment practice serves Casper

There is no staffed Purinton Analytics office in Casper, and the firm
publishes no street address here; meetings occur by appointment. That
candor matters, because an expert whose website invents a local office
invites the cross-examination it deserves. In practice, most Natrona County
engagements are handled remotely: secure records exchange, structured
video interviews, and standardized testing under publisher protocols where
remote administration is supported. When the referral calls for in-person
evaluation, or when a deposition or trial requires the expert in the room,
travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office with the court's
calendar, not the firm's, setting the dates.

## Grounding opinions in the regional labor market

Vocational conclusions for Casper evaluees rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Casper
metropolitan area and for Wyoming statewide, together with state workforce
agency labor market information and direct labor market research where the
questions warrant it. Employability findings are tied to occupations that
exist in the regional economy, not to a national abstraction. On the care
side, life care plans for Natrona County residents are priced to the
evaluee's own market through provider-specific inquiries and published fee
data, so the projection reflects local availability and local cost rather
than an average drawn from somewhere else.

## Opening a file with the firm

The path into an engagement is deliberately uniform: conflicts before
commitments, scope before records. Counsel can
[request a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) with the case caption and
parties, and should flag any disclosure deadline, deposition setting, or
trial date at the same time. Any venue-specific registration or
qualification requirement is identified and confirmed during the conflict
check and intake process. For matters beyond Wyoming, the firm's
[national coverage](/locations/nationwide/) page explains how the identical
report standard travels: the same intake, the same documentation, and the
same willingness to be read closely by whichever side did not retain the
firm.
