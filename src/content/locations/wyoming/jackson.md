The Jackson area is unusual among the firm's Wyoming locations because it
crosses a state line: the metro area takes in Teton County, Wyoming and
Teton County, Idaho, and counsel on either side of that line can retain the
same practice through a single intake. Purinton Analytics serves plaintiff
and defense attorneys throughout the area with
[vocational expert evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[expert testimony and litigation consulting](/services/expert-testimony-litigation-consulting/),
and it offers
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Venues on both sides of the state line

On the Wyoming side, engagements are accepted for matters in the District
Court for Teton County, Wyoming and in the United States District Court
for the District of Wyoming. Matters arising in Teton County, Idaho are
evaluated through the same intake, with the correct forum and any
Idaho-specific requirements confirmed during the conflict check rather
than assumed. Wherever the caption is filed, the report is prepared to the
same standard: a record both sides can examine, methods described in full,
and findings tied to sources opposing counsel can check. The firm's
[Wyoming page](/locations/wyoming/) covers the rest of the state under the
same approach.

## By-appointment service, honestly described

The firm keeps no staffed office in Jackson and publishes no street
address for this location; meetings are by appointment. Most engagements
in the area proceed remotely, through secure records exchange, structured
video interviews, and standardized testing under publisher protocols where
remote administration is supported, so an evaluee is ordinarily assessed
without leaving their own community. When the referral questions call for
in-person evaluation, or when deposition and trial dates require presence,
travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office and planned early,
with scheduling constraints discussed candidly at intake instead of
surfacing as a problem later in the litigation calendar.

## Labor market analysis where the market is distinctive

Vocational findings for Jackson-area evaluees rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates covering the Jackson
area and the relevant statewide data, on state workforce agency labor
market information, and on direct labor market research where the referral
warrants it. That last layer matters here, because an opinion about what
work is available to an evaluee must reflect the actual mix of occupations
and wages in this regional economy rather than a generic profile. Life
care plans and medical cost projections are priced to the evaluee's own
market through provider-specific inquiries and published fee data, so that
projected costs describe care as it is delivered and billed locally.

## Starting a Jackson-area engagement

Whether the matter sits in Wyoming or Idaho, the entry point is the same:
submit the caption or party names through the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and note any imminent disclosure,
deposition, or trial dates. Any venue-specific registration or
qualification requirement is identified and confirmed during the conflict
check and intake process. Counsel with matters farther afield can review
the firm's [national coverage](/locations/nationwide/) for how the same
remote-first model and report standard serve every state where the firm
accepts work.
