Counsel litigating in Cheyenne retain Purinton Analytics when a case turns on
what an injured or displaced person can still earn, what work remains open to
them, and what their future care will require. The firm provides
[vocational expert](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) and
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) services to plaintiff and
defense attorneys alike, and it structures
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. Every Cheyenne matter starts the same way the practice
starts everywhere: a documented conflict check, a defined scope, and a
records request tailored to the claims.

## Venue footprint in Laramie County

Engagements are accepted for matters venued in the District Court for
Laramie County and in the United States District Court for the District of
Wyoming. The report standard does not change with the forum. Opinions are
written so that either side of the caption can trace each conclusion back to
the records, the interview, the testing, and the published data on which it
rests, because the same document must hold up in disclosure, at deposition,
and on cross-examination. Counsel with matters elsewhere in the state,
beyond the Cheyenne metro area, can begin from the firm's
[Wyoming page](/locations/wyoming/), which describes statewide coverage
under the same methodology.

## Meetings by appointment, not a storefront

Cheyenne meetings are by appointment. The firm maintains no staffed office
in the city and publishes no street address for this location, because
accuracy about presence is part of the same discipline that governs its
reports. Most engagements proceed remotely from intake through the written
opinion: secure records transfer, structured interviews by video, and
standardized testing under publisher protocols where remote administration
is supported. When the referral questions call for in-person evaluation, or
when deposition and trial calendars require attendance, travel is scheduled
from the Kansas City home office and planned around the court's dates rather
than the firm's convenience.

## Labor market research for Cheyenne evaluees

Vocational findings for evaluees in the Cheyenne area rest on Bureau of
Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the
Cheyenne metropolitan area and for Wyoming statewide, supplemented by state
workforce agency labor market information and direct employer contact where
the referral questions warrant it. Transferable skills analysis considers
the occupations actually present in the regional economy rather than a
generic national profile. Life care plans and medical cost projections for
Laramie County residents are priced to the evaluee's own market through
provider-specific inquiries and published fee data, so that the plan
reflects what care actually costs where the evaluee lives rather than a
national average.

## Starting a Cheyenne engagement

Retention begins with conflicts, not with commitments. Submit the caption or
party names through the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and note any
imminent disclosure, deposition, or trial dates so scheduling can be
addressed honestly at the outset. Any venue-specific registration or
qualification requirement is identified and confirmed during the conflict
check and intake process. Counsel weighing whether a matter outside Wyoming
fits the practice can review the firm's
[national coverage](/locations/nationwide/), which explains how the same
remote-first model and the same report standard extend to every state where
the firm accepts work.
