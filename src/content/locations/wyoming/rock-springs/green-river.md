## Green River in the Sweetwater County labor market

Green River is a small community that shares a working labor market with
Rock Springs, the metro's principal city, and a credible opinion about a
Green River evaluee has to reflect that shared market rather than a
national composite. Vocational findings rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates covering the Rock Springs area
and Wyoming statewide, supplemented by state workforce agency labor market
information and by direct labor market research when the referral questions
warrant it. The analysis asks what work actually exists within the
evaluee's reach, on what terms, and with what transferable skills, and it
says so in language either side can test. Life care plan costs are priced
to the evaluee's own market through provider-specific inquiries and

where the evaluee lives.
