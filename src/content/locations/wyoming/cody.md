The Cody micropolitan area is a small market by any measure, and Purinton
Analytics treats that as a reason for more rigor, not less. A vocational or
life care planning opinion offered in a Park County courtroom will be read by
the same class of opposing counsel, and tested by the same cross-examination,
as an opinion offered in a major metropolitan docket, so the firm prepares it
the same way. Counsel in Cody engage the practice for
[vocational evaluation and expert testimony](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis
while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement
structure. Both plaintiff and defense referrals are accepted, and every report
is written so that either side of the caption can examine the record it rests
on.

## Venue footprint in Park County

Engagements are accepted for matters pending in the District Court serving
Park County and in the United States District Court for the District of
Wyoming. The venue does not change the method. Each engagement begins with a
documented conflict check, a defined scope, and a records request tailored to
the claims, whether the matter involves personal injury, workers'
compensation, employment, or family law questions about earning capacity.
Where the referral requires economic damages calculations, the firm
coordinates the engagement so that the economist's work and the vocational and
care-planning work move on the same disclosure calendar, under the state's
procedural deadlines rather than around them.

## Meetings and evaluations by appointment

Cody meetings are by appointment. The firm keeps no staffed office in Cody and
publishes no street address for this location, because accuracy about presence
is part of the same discipline that governs its reports. Most of an
engagement, from records review through report drafting and pre-deposition
conferences, proceeds remotely without any loss of quality. When the referral
questions call for in-person evaluation, or when deposition and trial settings
require presence, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office and
planned against the operative litigation calendar. Evaluations can be
conducted in the evaluee's own community, which keeps testing conditions
practical for the evaluee and keeps the resulting findings grounded in the
labor market the evaluee actually faces.

## Labor market research for Cody matters

Employability and earning capacity findings for evaluees in the Cody area rest
on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for
Wyoming statewide, state workforce agency labor market information, and direct
labor market research where the referral requires it. In a small labor market,
published estimates alone can miss how few positions of a given kind actually
exist within commuting distance, so the report is explicit about how the
commutable labor market was defined, what sources describe it, and where
direct employer contact supplemented the published data. That transparency is
what allows the opinion to withstand scrutiny from either side.
