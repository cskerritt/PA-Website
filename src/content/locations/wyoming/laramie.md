## Albany County and federal venues

Matters are accepted in the District Court for Albany County and in the
United States District Court for the District of Wyoming. The discipline
that governs the analysis does not vary with the courthouse: reviewed
records are listed, interview and testing procedures are documented, and
labor market and cost findings carry citations that opposing counsel can
verify independently. A report written that way serves the retaining side
precisely because it can withstand the other side, in disclosure, at
deposition, and on cross-examination. The statewide practice, for counsel
whose matters sit outside the Laramie area, is described on the firm's
[Wyoming page](/locations/wyoming/).

## No local office, and why that is stated

Laramie meetings are by appointment. The firm maintains no staffed office
here and publishes no street address for this location, because accuracy
about offices belongs to the same discipline that governs its reports. Most
Albany County engagements proceed remotely: secure records transfer,
structured interviews by video, and standardized testing under publisher
protocols where remote administration is supported. When the referral
questions require an in-person evaluation, or when deposition and trial
dates require presence in Wyoming, travel is scheduled from the Kansas
City home office and confirmed against counsel's litigation calendar
during intake rather than left to chance.

## Data sources for Laramie labor market findings

Every employability and earning capacity opinion for a Laramie evaluee is
anchored in Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage
estimates covering the Laramie area and Wyoming statewide, in state
workforce agency labor market information, and in direct labor market
research where the referral warrants it. The analysis describes the
occupations and wages of the regional economy the evaluee actually
occupies, not a national composite. Life care plans and medical cost
projections for Albany County residents follow the same locality rule:
costs are developed through provider-specific inquiries and published fee
data for the evaluee's own market, so the projection reflects care as it
is available and priced where the evaluee lives.

## Requesting a conflict check

The intake sequence is fixed by design. Counsel
[request a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) with the case caption and
parties, note any imminent disclosure, deposition, or trial dates, and
receive a candid answer on both conflicts and feasibility before any
commitment is made. Any venue-specific registration or qualification
requirement is identified and confirmed during the conflict check and
intake process. For matters beyond Wyoming, the firm's
[national coverage](/locations/nationwide/) page explains how the same
sequence, the same documentation, and the same report standard operate in
every state where the firm accepts engagements.
