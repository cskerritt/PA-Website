The most practical question counsel ask before retaining an expert for an Evansville matter is where the work will actually happen. The answer, for this small Natrona County town in the Casper, WY metropolitan area, is deliberately unglamorous: most of it happens wherever the records are, and the part that must happen in person happens where the evaluee lives. Purinton Analytics structures its [vocational expert witness](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) and [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) engagements around that principle, serving plaintiff and defense counsel in Evansville cases with reports written to survive scrutiny from whichever side did not retain the firm.

## Remote work, local evaluations

Purinton Analytics maintains no Evansville office and lists no local address here, a matter of accuracy rather than modesty. Document-intensive tasks, including records review, transferable skills analysis, and [medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/), are completed from the Kansas City home office, and many evaluee interviews proceed by video where the referral questions allow. When the case warrants an in-person evaluation, the appointment is set in the evaluee's own community rather than forcing a trip to a distant city, and travel from Kansas City is scheduled with the deposition and disclosure calendar in view. If [testimony or litigation consulting](/services/expert-testimony-litigation-consulting/) is anticipated, trial and hearing dates raised at intake are protected from the outset.
