Ask where a Mills case will be tried, and the answer nearly always begins with Natrona County. The town is a small community within the Casper, WY metropolitan area, and the injury, employment, and death cases that arise here tend to be litigated close to home, before judges and juries drawn from the same county. Purinton Analytics accepts Mills engagements from counsel on both sides of the caption, providing [vocational expert witness](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) work and [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) written to the standard a small venue demands: an opinion the opposing party can examine line by line and still find methodologically sound.

## The Natrona County venue picture

State-court matters arising in Mills proceed in the District Court serving Natrona County, and the firm takes plaintiff and defense retentions for that court alike. On the federal side, the United States District Court for the District of Wyoming is among the forums available for Wyoming matters; the controlling venue for any particular case, and any registration or qualification requirement it imposes, is identified and confirmed during the conflict check and intake process. Because expert disclosure deadlines in either forum shape the entire engagement, counsel are encouraged to share the scheduling order at intake so the report and any rebuttal work can be calendared backward from the dates that bind. Coverage for matters elsewhere in the state is described on the [Wyoming locations page](/locations/wyoming/).

## Reading the Casper labor market from Mills

A Mills evaluee's earning capacity cannot be measured against the town alone. The relevant market is the one the evaluee can realistically reach, which for most Mills residents means the employment base centered on Casper, the metro's principal city. Vocational findings therefore rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Casper metropolitan area and for Wyoming statewide, supplemented by state workforce agency labor market information and direct labor market research where the referral questions warrant it. The wider market context is set out on the [Casper metro page](/locations/wyoming/casper/). When the damages picture requires an economic dimension, the firm offers [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement structure.

## How a Mills evaluation proceeds

The firm keeps no office in Mills and publishes no local address; candor about presence is part of the same discipline that governs its reports. Records review, transferable skills analysis, [medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/), and labor market research are performed from the Kansas City home office, and interviews may be conducted by video when the referral questions permit. Where an in-person evaluation is warranted, it is scheduled in the evaluee's own community, with travel planned from Kansas City around the case's deposition and disclosure calendar. Counsel anticipating a need for [testimony or litigation consulting](/services/expert-testimony-litigation-consulting/) can flag hearing and trial dates during intake so appearances are protected early.
