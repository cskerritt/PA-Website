For an evaluee in Riverton, a credible evaluation should not require a long
trip to an unfamiliar office. Purinton Analytics designs Fremont County
engagements around the person being evaluated: interviews and standardized
testing are conducted remotely under publisher protocols where remote
administration is supported, and when the referral questions require it,
the evaluation takes place in the evaluee's own community. On that
foundation the firm delivers
[vocational expert opinions](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care plans](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/) for
plaintiff and defense counsel, along with
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Fremont County courts and coverage

The firm accepts matters venued in the District Court for Fremont County
and in the United States District Court for the District of Wyoming. In
either forum the written opinion is prepared to be read by both sides of
the caption: the record reviewed is enumerated, the methods are described,
and every labor market and cost finding is tied to a cited source that
opposing counsel can pull and check. Counsel with Wyoming matters beyond
the Riverton area can start from the firm's
[Wyoming page](/locations/wyoming/), which describes statewide coverage
under the same methodology.

## Appointment-based logistics, described accurately

Purinton Analytics keeps no staffed office in Riverton and publishes no
street address for this location. Meetings are by appointment, and the firm
says so plainly, because a practice that asks jurors to trust its
measurements should not blur the facts about its own footprint. The
working sequence for most Fremont County files is remote from start to
finish: secure records exchange, structured video interviews, and remote
testing where the instruments permit it. Where presence is needed, whether
for an in-person evaluation, a deposition, or trial, travel is scheduled
from the Kansas City home office and coordinated with counsel around the
court's dates.

## Local labor market data behind each opinion

Vocational findings for Riverton evaluees are built on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates covering the
Riverton area and Wyoming statewide, on state workforce agency labor
market information, and on direct labor market research where the referral
warrants it. Employability conclusions are stated against the occupations
present in the regional economy, and earning capacity is assessed with the
same locality discipline. Life care plans for Fremont County residents are
priced to the evaluee's own market using provider-specific inquiries and
published fee data, so that the projected costs describe care as it is
actually available where the evaluee lives.

and confirmed during the conflict check and intake process. Counsel
