Evanston sits on a state line, and litigation there reflects it. The
micropolitan area takes in Uinta County, Wyoming, and Rich County, Utah, which
means an evaluee's residence, workplace, and treating providers can fall on
different sides of the border, and the commutable labor market rarely respects
it at all. Purinton Analytics serves counsel on both sides of that line, for
plaintiff and defense engagements alike, with
[vocational evaluation and expert testimony](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis
while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement
structure.

## Venue footprint across the border

Engagements are accepted for matters pending in the district courts serving
Uinta County, Wyoming, and Rich County, Utah, and in the United States
District Court for the District of Wyoming for federal matters arising on the
Wyoming side. The cross-state character of the area shapes the intake itself:
the conflict check and scoping call establish which state's court the matter
sits in, which state's law frames the damages questions, and which labor
market defines the evaluee's opportunities, before any records are requested.
The written opinion then states those framing choices openly, so that
opposing counsel can see exactly how the border was handled rather than
discovering it at deposition.

## How by-appointment service works here

Meetings in the Evanston area are by appointment. The firm maintains no
staffed office in Evanston and publishes no street address for this location;
candor about presence is part of the same discipline the reports are built on.
The working relationship is remote-first: records review, counsel conferences,
report drafting, and pre-deposition preparation proceed by phone and video on
the litigation calendar. When the referral questions call for in-person
evaluation, or when a deposition or trial setting requires presence, travel is
scheduled from the Kansas City home office. Evaluations can also be conducted
in the evaluee's own community, on whichever side of the state line the
evaluee lives, which keeps logistics simple for counsel and keeps the
evaluation grounded where the evaluee actually works and seeks work.

## Labor market research across two states

Vocational findings for Evanston-area evaluees draw on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for Wyoming statewide
and for Utah statewide, state workforce agency labor market information from
both states, and direct labor market research where warranted. Because the
metro spans a border, the report is explicit about which market defines the
evaluee's commutable labor market and why, and it does not blend the states'
data without saying so. In a small market, that explicitness matters: the
question is not only what occupations exist in the published estimates but
whether such positions are realistically available within the evaluee's
reach, and the methodology section shows how that question was answered.
