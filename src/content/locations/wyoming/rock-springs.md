Every opinion Purinton Analytics issues is written to be read by opposing
counsel, and that standard does not change with the venue. For plaintiff
and defense counsel litigating in Rock Springs and the rest of Sweetwater
County, the firm provides
[vocational expert evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[expert testimony and litigation consulting](/services/expert-testimony-litigation-consulting/)
through a remote-first practice built on documented method rather than
proximity, and it offers
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Where Rock Springs cases are heard

Engagements are accepted for matters in the District Court for Sweetwater
County and in the United States District Court for the District of Wyoming.
The forum changes the procedural posture, never the product: each report
enumerates the records reviewed, documents the interview and testing, and
cites the published data behind every labor market and cost conclusion, so
that either side of the caption can test the opinion on its stated terms.
The firm's [Wyoming page](/locations/wyoming/) describes how the same
approach covers matters venued elsewhere in the state.

## Remote-first, with honest travel logistics

The firm has no staffed office in Rock Springs and publishes no street
address for this location; meetings happen by appointment. Most Sweetwater
County engagements never require the evaluee to leave their community:
records move by secure transfer, interviews are conducted by video on a
structured protocol, and standardized instruments are administered under
publisher protocols where remote administration is supported. When the
referral questions call for an in-person evaluation, or when a deposition
or trial requires attendance, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home
office. Scheduling realities are discussed at intake rather than
discovered later, which is the version of convenience that actually serves
a litigation calendar.

## Reading the Sweetwater County labor market

Employability and earning capacity findings for Rock Springs evaluees rest
on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates
covering the Rock Springs area and Wyoming statewide, on state workforce
agency labor market information, and on direct labor market research where
the questions warrant it. The point of that layering is fidelity to the
regional economy: an opinion about what work exists for an evaluee must
describe the labor market the evaluee actually lives in, not a national
composite. Life care plans and medical cost projections for Sweetwater
County residents are likewise priced to the evaluee's own market through
provider-specific inquiries and published fee data rather than national
averages, so that projected costs describe care as it is actually billed
where the evaluee receives it.

## From inquiry to engagement

An inquiry becomes an engagement only after a documented conflict check and
a defined scope. Counsel can [request a conflict check](/refer-a-case/)
with the case caption and parties, flagging any disclosure, deposition, or
trial dates that bear on feasibility. Any venue-specific registration or
qualification requirement is identified and confirmed during the conflict
check and intake process. For the broader practice beyond Wyoming, the
firm's [national coverage](/locations/nationwide/) page sets out how the
same model serves counsel in every state where engagements are accepted.
