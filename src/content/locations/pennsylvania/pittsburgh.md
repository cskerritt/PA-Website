Pittsburgh referrals tend to bundle questions rather than pose them in
isolation: whether an evaluee can return to work in the regional labor
market, what future care the injury will require, and what the resulting
loss is worth. Purinton Analytics answers the vocational and care-planning
questions directly through
[vocational expert evaluations](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) and
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and structures the
damages question as
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. Plaintiff and defense counsel are served on the same
terms.

## Courts across the Pittsburgh metro

Engagements are accepted for matters in the Court of Common Pleas in
Allegheny County, Armstrong County, Beaver County, Butler County, Fayette
County, Lawrence County, Washington County, and Westmoreland County. Federal
matters most often proceed in the United States District Court for the
Western District of Pennsylvania, and the same availability extends to the
United States District Court for the Middle District of Pennsylvania and the
United States District Court for the Eastern District of Pennsylvania when a
case is filed elsewhere in the Commonwealth. Coverage across the rest of the
state is described on the [Pennsylvania page](/locations/pennsylvania/).

## Presence stated plainly

Pittsburgh meetings are by appointment. The firm maintains no staffed local
office here and publishes no street address for this location, because
accuracy about presence belongs to the same discipline counsel are retaining
for the report itself. Depositions and trial testimony are provided in
person or by remote appearance where the court permits, evaluation sessions
are held in the evaluee's community, at counsel's offices, or by secure
video under publisher protocols, and travel is scheduled from the Kansas
City home office when the referral questions call for in-person evaluation
or when deposition and trial dates require presence.

## Labor market research for Pittsburgh evaluees

Employability and earning capacity findings rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Pittsburgh
metropolitan area and for Pennsylvania statewide, on state workforce agency
labor market information, and on direct labor market research, including
employer contact and job posting analysis, when the referral requires
evidence about actual hiring rather than published averages. Transferable
skills analysis is grounded in the evaluee's documented work history and
tested capacities, not in assumptions about what the regional economy used
to be or ought to become, so the resulting opinion holds up whether it is
offered by the plaintiff or examined by the defense.

## Starting a Pittsburgh engagement

Referrals arise most often in personal injury, medical malpractice, wrongful
death, employment, and disability matters, from either side of the caption.
When an opposing expert's employability opinion or life care plan is already
on file, [rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) can be
scoped within the same intake. Every engagement begins with a documented
conflict check, a defined scope, and a records request tailored to the
claims. To begin, [request a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) with the case
caption and parties, and note any imminent disclosure, deposition, or trial
dates. The [national coverage page](/locations/nationwide/) describes remote
evaluation logistics and the firm's reach beyond western Pennsylvania.
