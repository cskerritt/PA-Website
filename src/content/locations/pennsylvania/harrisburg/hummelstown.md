Future care, not past earnings, is often the largest disputed element in the catastrophic injury and medical negligence matters that bring Hummelstown counsel to Purinton Analytics. The firm's [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) practice addresses that dispute with a plan built from the medical record and treating-provider foundation, itemized by category of care, and priced through methodology disclosed in the report, with [medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/) available where the assignment is narrower. Every care item in the plan is tied to a stated medical foundation, and items without foundation are left out, which is why the same plan architecture serves plaintiff development and defense review alike. Vocational loss is analyzed alongside the care plan when the injuries reach earning capacity, and both plaintiff and defense counsel retain the firm for each kind of work.

## Evaluations close to home

Catastrophically injured evaluees rarely travel easily, so the firm's logistics start from the evaluee's circumstances. ## First, the conflict check

Care-planning engagements reward early retention, and retention starts with a [conflict check](/refer-a-case/): the caption or parties, a summary of the injuries and claims, and the disclosure dates already on the calendar. Once conflicts clear, the firm defines its scope in writing and issues the records request. Where the damages picture requires economic modeling of the care plan or the earnings loss, the firm offers coordinated damages engagements in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement structure.
