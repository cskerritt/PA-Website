Ask what a defensible vocational expert report in a Lemoyne case should contain, and the answer is a list, not a sales pitch: a scope defined in writing before work begins, a records request tailored to the claims, an evaluation matched to the questions presented, data sources named where they are used, and conclusions that stop where the record stops. Purinton Analytics builds its [vocational opinions](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) and [life care plans](/services/life-care-planning/) to that list for retaining counsel on either side of the caption, and it holds opposing experts' files to the same list in its [rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) assignments.

## Venue in Cumberland County and beyond

Lemoyne is a Cumberland County borough; its state-court matters are heard in the Cumberland County Court of Common Pleas. Beyond the county courthouse, Pennsylvania's federal forums are the United States District Court for the Eastern District of Pennsylvania, the United States District Court for the Middle District of Pennsylvania, and the United States District Court for the Western District of Pennsylvania. The firm leaves the mapping of a given case to its controlling district where it belongs, in the conflict check and intake, where any venue-specific qualification requirement is also confirmed. The report a court receives is the same either way: complete, cited, and prepared for adversarial reading.

## Grounding opinions in labor market data

The employment analysis for a Lemoyne evaluee runs on the regional market. Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Harrisburg-Carlisle metropolitan area and for Pennsylvania statewide anchor the findings, with state workforce agency labor market information and direct labor market research added when the referral requires more than the published series shows. Employability and earning capacity conclusions are tied to occupations that exist in that market and to the evaluee's documented education and work history, never to assumptions about what a small borough should look like. When published estimates point in different directions, the report says so and explains which source carried more weight and why, rather than quietly selecting the favorable figure; that transparency costs less than credibility lost on the stand. The regional approach is set out on the [metro page](/locations/pennsylvania/harrisburg/).

## Meeting the evaluee

By-appointment service is the honest description of the firm's presence here: no Lemoyne office, no published local address, and no suggestion of one. The practical consequence for evaluees is favorable. Remote evaluation covers many referral questions; where in-person assessment is warranted, it happens in the evaluee's own community, scheduled around treatment. Where testimony is required, the expert travels from the Kansas City home office, the anchor of both the [Pennsylvania practice](/locations/pennsylvania/) and the firm's [national footprint](/locations/nationwide/). Counsel are conferenced remotely throughout, and drafts move on the disclosure calendar fixed at intake.
