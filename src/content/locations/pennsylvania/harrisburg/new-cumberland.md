Where an evaluee lives and where an evaluee can work are different questions, and the distinction matters in New Cumberland cases. The borough is small; the labor market its residents participate in is regional, spanning the wider Harrisburg-Carlisle economy. Purinton Analytics keeps that distinction at the center of its [vocational expert work](/services/vocational-expert-witness/): residual earning capacity is assessed against the jobs the region actually offers, not against the boundaries of a town, and the resulting opinion is written so that counsel on either side of the caption can trace each conclusion back to a source.

## Venue for New Cumberland matters

New Cumberland litigation in the state system proceeds through the Cumberland County Court of Common Pleas. In the federal system, Pennsylvania's trial courts are the United States District Court for the Eastern District of Pennsylvania, the United States District Court for the Middle District of Pennsylvania, and the United States District Court for the Western District of Pennsylvania. The firm names all of them here deliberately: which one controls a specific matter is a filing question, settled during the conflict check and intake along with any registration or qualification requirement the venue imposes. Whatever the answer, the report's methodology, citations, and disclosure posture stay constant.

## The metro labor market as the unit of analysis

Because commuting patterns tie a small borough to a regional economy, the firm's data follow suit. Vocational conclusions are grounded in Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Harrisburg-Carlisle metropolitan area and for Pennsylvania statewide, in state workforce agency labor market information, and in direct labor market research when a referral question demands more granularity than published estimates provide. Loss of earning capacity, employability, and placeability are each addressed at that regional scale, and the [metro page](/locations/pennsylvania/harrisburg/) describes the surrounding practice in fuller detail. Methodology sections identify every dataset by program and coverage area, so an opposing expert can replicate the search rather than argue about what was searched.

## Evaluation without unnecessary travel

The firm's presence in New Cumberland is by appointment only; there is no local office and no local address, in this borough or anywhere else outside the Kansas City home office. Most engagement phases are remote by design, which spares evaluees travel and spares clients cost. In-person evaluation, when the questions require it, is conducted in the evaluee's own community, and courtroom or deposition appearances are met with travel scheduled from Kansas City. [Life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) engagements follow the same pattern, with the care-plan interview and any home assessment arranged around the evaluee's treatment schedule. The model is uniform across the [Pennsylvania practice](/locations/pennsylvania/) and the firm's [nationwide work](/locations/nationwide/).
