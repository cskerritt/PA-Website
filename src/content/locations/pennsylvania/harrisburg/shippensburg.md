## Requesting a conflict check

Every engagement opens with a conflict check. Send the caption or party names through the [referral form](/refer-a-case/), state the questions the case presents, and identify any imminent disclosure or deposition dates. After clearance, the firm defines the scope in writing, issues its records request, and, where the case requires economic analysis, coordinates a damages engagement in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic work while the vocational and care-planning analysis proceeds under one engagement structure. Clearance decisions are communicated promptly in either direction, so counsel who need to look elsewhere lose no time doing so.
