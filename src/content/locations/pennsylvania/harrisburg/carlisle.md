## Cumberland County venue and the federal forums

State-court matters arising in Carlisle proceed through the Cumberland County Court of Common Pleas. On the federal side, Pennsylvania's bench sits as the United States District Court for the Eastern District of Pennsylvania, the United States District Court for the Middle District of Pennsylvania, and the United States District Court for the Western District of Pennsylvania. Which of those forums controls a particular case is a question of where and how the matter is filed, and it is confirmed during the conflict check and intake rather than assumed in advance. The firm's process does not vary with the courthouse: the same scope definition, records protocol, and disclosure discipline apply in the county courthouse and in federal court alike.

## Requesting a conflict check

Engagements begin with a conflict check, not a consultation. Submit the caption or party names through the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/), identify the venue if it is known, and note any imminent disclosure, deposition, or trial dates so scheduling can be confirmed against the calendar. Once conflicts clear, the firm defines the scope in writing, issues a records request tailored to the claims, and sets a delivery date for the report that respects the deadlines Carlisle counsel are working under.
