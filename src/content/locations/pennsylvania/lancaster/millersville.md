After a catastrophic injury, the hardest questions in a Millersville case are usually about the future: what care will this person need for the rest of their life, who will provide it, and what will it cost where they actually live? Purinton Analytics answers those questions through [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) and [medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/) grounded in the treating record and priced with provider-specific inquiries and published fee data for the evaluee's own region, not a national average that no local family will ever be billed. Both plaintiff and defense counsel retain the firm for this work, and the plan reads the same either way: sourced, itemized, and ready for examination.

## Vocational analysis alongside the care plan

Future care is only half of most damages models; the other half is what the evaluee can still earn. The firm's [vocational expert work](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) for Millersville evaluees rests on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Lancaster metropolitan area and Pennsylvania statewide, on state workforce agency labor market information, and on direct labor market research when the referral requires it. When counsel needs the full damages picture in a single coordinated structure, independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement structure through the firm's [coordinated damages assessments](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/).

## Seeing the evaluee where they live

No staffed office exists in Millersville, and the firm does not manufacture the impression of one. The practice is remote-first: records analysis, research, drafting, and most interviews and standardized testing proceed at a distance under protocols the test publishers support. What does not happen at a distance is the in-person evaluation a referral sometimes demands, which is conducted in the evaluee's own community, or courtroom testimony, for which travel is arranged from the Kansas City home office once dates are confirmed at intake. A care plan gains credibility when its author has documented the evaluee's actual circumstances, and the logistics are built to make that documentation possible.
