## A commuting workforce, measured honestly

Because the relevant market is regional, the vocational data must be too. Findings for Elizabethtown evaluees draw on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates covering the Lancaster metropolitan area and Pennsylvania statewide, alongside state workforce agency labor market information and targeted employer research when a specific placement question warrants it.
