Sometimes the assignment is not to build an opinion but to test one. Lititz counsel who receive an opposing expert's vocational report or life care plan retain Purinton Analytics for [rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/): a methodical audit of the sources cited, the testing relied on, the transferable skills logic, and the pricing behind projected care. The same rigor runs the other direction when the firm is engaged affirmatively, producing [vocational expert opinions](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) that anticipate exactly that kind of audit. Plaintiff and defense retain the practice in comparable measure, and the review checklist does not change with the client.

## Where Lititz disputes are litigated

A Lititz case filed in state court is heard in the Lancaster County Court of Common Pleas. If it belongs in federal court instead, Pennsylvania presents its district courts as the available forums: the United States District Court for the Eastern District of Pennsylvania, the United States District Court for the Middle District of Pennsylvania, and the United States District Court for the Western District of Pennsylvania. Rather than assume which of these governs, the firm confirms the controlling venue, and any qualification or registration requirement particular to it, during the conflict check and intake. The broader court and county landscape for the region appears on the [Lancaster metro page](/locations/pennsylvania/lancaster/).

## Labor market evidence both sides can use

Whether offered affirmatively or in rebuttal, vocational conclusions about a Lititz evaluee stand on published, checkable data: Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Lancaster metropolitan area and for Pennsylvania statewide, state workforce agency labor market information, and direct employer research where a contested question deserves primary sourcing. That transparency is deliberate. A rebuttal opinion that cannot show its own sources is no better than the report it criticizes, and an affirmative opinion built on the same open record gives cross-examining counsel nothing hidden to find.

## By-appointment presence, stated plainly

The practice publishes no Lititz address because it has none; every representation about presence is kept as accurate as the citations in its reports. Engagements run remotely by default, from records review through drafting, with structured interviews and standardized testing administered remotely under publisher protocols where supported. When an in-person evaluation is warranted, it takes place in the evaluee's own community, and when testimony requires the expert in a Lancaster County courtroom or deposition suite, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office with dates locked against the case calendar at intake. Counsel dealing with catastrophic injury can pair this work with [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) under the same engagement.
