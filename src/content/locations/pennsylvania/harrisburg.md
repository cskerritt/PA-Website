Plaintiff and defense counsel in the Harrisburg-Carlisle metro want the same
things from a retained expert: candor about method, a record that supports
every conclusion, and an opinion that survives the other side's scrutiny.
Purinton Analytics is built around that expectation, offering
[vocational expert evaluations](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) to either side
of the caption. Where a referral also needs economic damages, the firm
offers [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Venues in Dauphin, Cumberland, and Perry Counties

Engagements are accepted for matters in the Court of Common Pleas in
Dauphin County, Cumberland County, and Perry County, covering Harrisburg,
Carlisle, and the communities around them. Federal matters proceed in the
United States District Court for the Middle District of Pennsylvania, and
the same availability extends to the United States District Court for the
Eastern District of Pennsylvania and the United States District Court for
the Western District of Pennsylvania when a referral is filed elsewhere in
the Commonwealth. For coverage beyond the metro, the
[Pennsylvania overview](/locations/pennsylvania/) lists the firm's presence
across the state.

## What by-appointment means in Harrisburg

Harrisburg and Carlisle meetings are by appointment. The firm has no staffed
office in the metro and publishes no street address for this location,
because honesty about presence is part of the same discipline that governs
its written opinions. What counsel gets instead is a practice organized for
distance: conflict checks answered promptly, evaluation dates set against
the disclosure calendar at intake, depositions and testimony in person or by
remote appearance where the court permits, and travel scheduled from the
Kansas City home office whenever the referral calls for in-person evaluation
or courtroom presence.

## Labor market research for metro evaluees

Employability and earning capacity findings rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Harrisburg
metropolitan area and for Pennsylvania statewide, together with state
workforce agency labor market information and direct labor market research,
including employer contact and job posting analysis, when the referral
questions require evidence about actual hiring. The same restraint that
keeps invented offices off this page keeps invented labor market
assumptions out of the reports: findings are tied to published estimates and
documented research, so both sides can check the work.

## Starting a Harrisburg engagement

Referrals from the metro most often involve personal injury, medical
malpractice, wrongful death, employment, and disability matters. Every
engagement begins with a documented conflict check, a defined scope, and a
records request tailored to the claims, so the eventual opinion rests on a
record both parties can examine. To begin,
[request a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) with the case caption and party
names, and note any imminent disclosure, deposition, or trial dates so the
schedule can be set realistically at intake. Fee information and CVs follow
promptly, and the [national coverage page](/locations/nationwide/) describes
remote evaluation logistics and the rest of the firm's geographic reach.
