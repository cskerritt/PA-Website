Distance does not decide whether an evaluation happens. For counsel in the
Scranton and Wilkes-Barre region, Purinton Analytics conducts
[vocational evaluations](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) and
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) assessments by secure
video under publisher protocols, in the evaluee's own community, or at
counsel's offices, with travel scheduled from the Kansas City home office
when the referral calls for in-person work or when deposition and trial
dates require presence. The same engagement can add
[rebuttal or peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) of an opposing
expert's report, or expand into
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Courts serving Lackawanna, Luzerne, and Wyoming Counties

Engagements are accepted for matters in the Court of Common Pleas in
Lackawanna County, Luzerne County, and Wyoming County, and in the United
States District Court for the Middle District of Pennsylvania. When a
referral from the region is filed elsewhere in the Commonwealth, the same
availability extends to the United States District Court for the Eastern
District of Pennsylvania and the United States District Court for the
Western District of Pennsylvania. Work from both plaintiff and defense
counsel is accepted on identical terms, and the
[Pennsylvania overview](/locations/pennsylvania/) describes the firm's
coverage across the rest of the state.

## By-appointment presence in Scranton and Wilkes-Barre

Meetings in the metro are by appointment. The firm keeps no staffed office
in Scranton or Wilkes-Barre and publishes no street address for this
location; stating that plainly is part of the same discipline that governs
the reports themselves. In practice, counsel here work with a firm organized
for distance from the outset: prompt conflict checks, evaluation dates set
against the disclosure calendar at intake, testimony delivered in person or
by remote appearance where the court permits, and in-person evaluation
sessions arranged in the evaluee's community when testing or observation
requires it.

## Labor market research for the region

Employability and earning capacity findings rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Scranton
metropolitan area and for Pennsylvania statewide, on state workforce agency
labor market information, and on direct labor market research, including
employer contact and job posting analysis, when the referral questions
require evidence of actual hiring rather than published averages. Findings
describe the job market a particular evaluee realistically faces, given
documented restrictions, work history, and transferable skills, rather than
generalized impressions of the regional economy, and each step is documented
so opposing counsel can retrace it.

## Starting an engagement in the metro

Referrals from the region most often involve personal injury, medical
malpractice, wrongful death, employment, and disability matters, from either
side of the caption. Every engagement begins with a documented conflict
check, a defined scope, and a records request tailored to the claims.
Submit the caption or party names through the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/), and note any imminent disclosure,
deposition, or trial dates so scheduling is realistic from the start. CVs
and fee information follow promptly, and the
[national coverage page](/locations/nationwide/) explains remote evaluation
logistics, travel, and the firm's reach beyond northeastern Pennsylvania.
