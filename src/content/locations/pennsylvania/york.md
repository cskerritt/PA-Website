Some York-Hanover referrals begin not with an injury but with a report: an
opposing expert's employability opinion or life care plan is already on
file, and counsel needs it examined before a deadline. Purinton Analytics is
retained in exactly that posture for
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/), testing an
opposing report's methodology, sources, and internal consistency, and it
performs the affirmative work as well:
[vocational expert evaluations](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic

engagement structure. Plaintiff and defense engagements are accepted on the
same terms.

## Venue context for York County matters

State-court engagements proceed in the Court of Common Pleas in York
County, which serves York, Hanover, and the surrounding communities that
make up the metro. Federal matters proceed in the United States District
Court for the Middle District of Pennsylvania, with the same availability in
the United States District Court for the Eastern District of Pennsylvania
and the United States District Court for the Western District of
Pennsylvania when a referral is filed elsewhere in the Commonwealth. Any
venue-specific registration or qualification requirement is identified and
confirmed during the conflict check and intake process, and the
[Pennsylvania overview](/locations/pennsylvania/) collects the firm's
statewide coverage.

## By-appointment logistics for York and Hanover

Meetings in the metro are by appointment. The firm keeps no staffed office
in York or Hanover and publishes no street address for this location;
describing presence accurately is part of the same discipline that governs
its reports. Counsel can instead rely on process: conflict checks answered
promptly, evaluation dates set against the disclosure calendar at intake,
testimony provided in person or by remote appearance where the court
permits, and evaluation sessions held in the evaluee's community, at
counsel's offices, or by secure video under publisher protocols. Travel is
scheduled from the Kansas City home office when in-person evaluation or
courtroom presence is required.

## Labor market research for York County evaluees

Employability and earning capacity findings rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the York
metropolitan area and for Pennsylvania statewide, together with state
workforce agency labor market information and direct labor market research,
including employer contact and job posting analysis, when the referral
requires evidence of actual hiring rather than published averages. The same
standard applies whether the firm is writing the affirmative opinion or
reviewing someone else's: each finding is tied to identified sources, so
that a reader on either side of the caption can retrace the analysis step
by step.

## Starting a York engagement

Referrals from the metro most often involve personal injury, medical
malpractice, wrongful death, employment, and disability matters. Every
engagement, affirmative or rebuttal, begins with a documented conflict
check, a defined scope, and a records request tailored to the claims.
Submit the caption or party names through the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and note any imminent disclosure,
deposition, or trial dates, particularly where a rebuttal deadline is
already running. CVs and fee information follow promptly, and the
[national coverage page](/locations/nationwide/) describes remote
evaluation logistics and the firm's reach beyond south-central
Pennsylvania.
