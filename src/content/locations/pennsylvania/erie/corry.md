An approaching expert disclosure deadline is usually what brings counsel with a Corry matter to this practice, and the calendar is where every Corry engagement is planned from. Purinton Analytics maps the report date, the likely deposition window, and any trial setting before the records request goes out, so that [vocational expert witness](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) and [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) opinions arrive with time for counsel to use them rather than merely file them. Plaintiff and defense referrals from Corry are taken on identical terms, and the schedule discipline is the same for both.

## Timelines, Then the Erie County Forum

Once the calendar is fixed, the forum shapes the work. Corry sits in Erie County, and state-court claims proceed in the Court of Common Pleas. Where a claim is federal, Pennsylvania offers the United States District Court for the Eastern District of Pennsylvania, the United States District Court for the Middle District of Pennsylvania, and the United States District Court for the Western District of Pennsylvania as available forums; which of them controls a particular caption, and whether any venue-specific qualification requirement applies, is identified and confirmed during the conflict check and intake. The written product is prepared for whichever setting the case lands in, with methodology stated plainly enough for opposing counsel to test it.

## A Corry Evaluee in the Wider Labor Market

Corry is a small city, and its residents' realistic employment prospects extend past its borders into the broader Erie, PA metropolitan labor market anchored by the principal city of Erie. Vocational findings for a Corry evaluee therefore rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Erie, PA metropolitan area and for Pennsylvania statewide, along with state workforce agency labor market information and direct employer contact where the referral questions call for it. The opinion addresses transferable skills, residual capacity, and the occupations the data genuinely supports, and it stays qualitative about the region while remaining specific about the person.

## Scheduling the Evaluation and Any Appearance

Meetings in Corry are by appointment only. The practice maintains no staffed location there and lists no local street address, because being accurate about presence is part of being accurate generally. Records review, structured interviews, and most collaboration with counsel proceed remotely over secure video, which protects the disclosure calendar from travel friction. When the questions require an in-person evaluation, or when a deposition or trial date requires attendance, travel is arranged from the Kansas City home office and set around the court's schedule. In-community evaluation can be arranged so the functional assessment reflects the setting where the evaluee actually lives and seeks work. The same calendar-driven approach serves counsel [across the Erie metro](/locations/pennsylvania/erie/), [throughout Pennsylvania](/locations/pennsylvania/), and [nationwide](/locations/nationwide/).

## Opening a Corry Engagement

Every engagement starts with a documented conflict check, a defined scope, and a records request tailored to the claims, so the finished opinion rests on a record both sides can examine. Where the damages picture requires economic analysis, the firm offers [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement structure. To begin, [submit a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) with the caption and party names, and flag any imminent disclosure, deposition, or trial dates so the schedule is built into the engagement from the start.
