A report worth retaining is one the other side cannot take apart, and that standard governs every Girard engagement Purinton Analytics accepts. Whether the referral concerns [vocational expert witness](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) work, [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), or [rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) of another expert's opinion, the product is written on the assumption that opposing counsel will read it line by line: sources cited, assumptions declared, methodology stated in terms a cross-examiner can test. Girard referrals arrive from plaintiff counsel and defense counsel alike, and the writing standard does not change with the retaining side.

## What Discipline Looks Like on the Page

The discipline is procedural before it is rhetorical. A Girard engagement begins with a documented conflict check, a defined scope, and a records request tailored to the claims, so the eventual opinion rests on a record both sides can examine rather than on materials selected to flatter a conclusion. Findings that the record does not support are not offered; limits on the data are acknowledged in the report itself. That posture serves the retaining party best precisely because it leaves the least for the other side to attack.

## The Courts Serving Girard

Girard is part of Erie County, and its state-court matters proceed in the Court of Common Pleas. Federal claims arising in Pennsylvania may be brought in the United States District Court for the Eastern District of Pennsylvania, the United States District Court for the Middle District of Pennsylvania, or the United States District Court for the Western District of Pennsylvania; the forum that actually controls a given Girard caption, and any qualification requirement that forum imposes, is confirmed during the conflict check and intake so the report is fitted to its destination from the outset.

## Grounding Girard Findings in Regional Data

A borough the size of Girard participates in the labor market of the larger Erie, PA metropolitan area, with the principal city of Erie at its center, so vocational conclusions rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for that metropolitan area and for Pennsylvania statewide, alongside state workforce agency labor market information and direct employer research when warranted. Regional description remains qualitative; the individual analysis remains concrete about transferable skills, residual capacity, and realistic placement. When a matter also needs a signed economic analysis, the firm offers [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement structure.

## Meeting Counsel and the Evaluee

Candor extends to logistics: there is no staffed Purinton Analytics location in Girard and no published local address. Work proceeds remotely by default, through secure video interviews and structured records review, with in-person meetings by appointment. When an evaluation is better conducted in the evaluee's own community, it is arranged there; when deposition or trial requires presence, travel is planned from the Kansas City home office against the court's dates. The same standards apply [across the Erie metro](/locations/pennsylvania/erie/), [throughout Pennsylvania](/locations/pennsylvania/), and [nationwide](/locations/nationwide/).

## Referring a Girard Matter

Counsel weighing an expert for a Girard case should [request a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) with the caption and party names, attaching nothing confidential at that stage, and should identify any looming disclosure, deposition, or trial dates. Scope, records, and scheduling are then set at intake, where they belong.
