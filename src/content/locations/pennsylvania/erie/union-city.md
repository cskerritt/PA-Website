Nearly every serious injury or employment case from Union City turns on a pair of questions: what can this person still earn, and what will their future care cost? Purinton Analytics exists to answer both, pairing [vocational expert witness](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) analysis of earning capacity with [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) that itemizes future care needs from the medical record. The answers are prepared for counsel on whichever side of the caption retains the practice, in the same form and to the same standard, because a defensible answer does not depend on who asked the question.

## Two Questions, One Engagement Structure

The earning question and the care question interact, so the practice keeps them under a single engagement rather than splitting them across unconnected vendors. Vocational findings inform the care plan's assumptions about function and daily life; the care plan's demands feed back into what work is realistic. When the case also requires those findings converted into an economic damages analysis, the firm offers [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement structure, giving counsel a coherent set of opinions instead of a stack of experts who never compared notes.

## Union City's Venue Landscape

Union City lies in Erie County, and state-court claims proceed in the Court of Common Pleas. If a claim sounds in federal law, Pennsylvania's forums are the United States District Court for the Eastern District of Pennsylvania, the United States District Court for the Middle District of Pennsylvania, and the United States District Court for the Western District of Pennsylvania; the district that controls a particular Union City caption, along with any qualification requirement specific to that venue, is identified during the conflict check and intake. Each report is prepared with its destination court in mind, sourced and reasoned so opposing counsel can retrace it.

## Earning Capacity Against the Erie Metro

Union City is a small borough, and the earning question cannot be answered inside its limits alone: the relevant market is the Erie, PA metropolitan area, anchored by the principal city of Erie, together with the state as a whole. Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for that metropolitan area and for Pennsylvania statewide supply the foundation, supplemented by state workforce agency labor market information and direct employer contact when the questions call for it. The region is described qualitatively; the evaluee's transferable skills, residual capacity, and realistic occupations are described exactly.

## Seeing the Evaluee, Serving the Case

Union City meetings are by appointment; the practice has no staffed office there and lists no local address, stating so plainly because accuracy about presence belongs to the same discipline as accuracy about damages. The care-planning interview and much of the vocational workup proceed by secure video, and an in-person evaluation can be conducted in the evaluee's own community when the questions require it. Depositions and trial appearances are reached by travel from the Kansas City home office, planned against the court's dates. The same paired analysis is available [across the Erie metro](/locations/pennsylvania/erie/), [throughout Pennsylvania](/locations/pennsylvania/), and [nationwide](/locations/nationwide/).
