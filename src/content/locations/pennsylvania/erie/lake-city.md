## How Remote-First Reaches Lake City

The model is deliberately independent of geography. Records arrive electronically under a request tailored to the claims; interviews with the evaluee proceed by secure video at scheduled times; counsel confers with the expert without anyone crossing the state. What cannot be done remotely is not forced into that mold: in-person functional evaluation, deposition attendance, and trial testimony are scheduled as travel from the Kansas City home office, timed to the court's calendar. The practice keeps no staffed space in Lake City and prints no local street address, a fact stated openly because opinions and logistics are held to the same standard of accuracy. When it serves the analysis, the evaluation itself can be held in the evaluee's own community.
