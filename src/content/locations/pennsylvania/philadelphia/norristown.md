## Sources an examiner can check

Norristown evaluees work within the labor market of the Philadelphia-Camden-Wilmington metropolitan area, and earning capacity conclusions are anchored there. Vocational findings rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Philadelphia metropolitan area and for Pennsylvania statewide, on state workforce agency labor market information, and on direct labor market research when a disputed occupation calls for original inquiry. Because the sources are published and reproducible, an examiner can retrace every step from data to conclusion, and the methodology does not change with the retention letter: a plaintiff-side loss analysis and a defense-side mitigation critique draw on the same well. When the damages case requires economic quantification, the firm offers [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement structure.
