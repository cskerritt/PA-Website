For a Pottstown worker, the relevant labor market is wider than Pottstown itself. The community lies within the Philadelphia-Camden-Wilmington metropolitan area, and the honest vocational question after an injury is what employment remains realistic across that commuting market, given the evaluee's restrictions, transferable skills, and tolerance for travel to work. That framing shapes how Purinton Analytics approaches Pottstown referrals from plaintiff and defense counsel alike, whether the assignment is a [vocational expert opinion](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) on employability and earning capacity, a [life care plan](/services/life-care-planning/) after catastrophic injury, or a [rebuttal review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) of the opposing expert's methodology.

## Montgomery County courts and the federal districts

Pottstown sits in Montgomery County, and state-court matters proceed in the Court of Common Pleas. Where a case belongs in federal court, Pennsylvania offers the United States District Court for the Eastern District of Pennsylvania, the United States District Court for the Middle District of Pennsylvania, and the United States District Court for the Western District of Pennsylvania as its available forums; the controlling district for a particular matter, and any registration or qualification requirement that venue imposes, is identified during the conflict check and intake rather than assumed in advance. Counsel working across the wider region will find the county-by-county picture on the [Philadelphia metro page](/locations/pennsylvania/philadelphia/).

## A commuting market, measured honestly

A loss-of-earning-capacity opinion that treats Pottstown as an island understates or overstates the case, depending on who wrote it. The firm instead grounds its findings in Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Philadelphia metropolitan area and for Pennsylvania statewide, in state workforce agency labor market information, and in direct labor market research where a disputed occupation demands it. Restrictions that limit commuting are treated as evidence to be documented, not assumed away; access to the broader metro market is likewise a finding, not a default. Because the sources are public and the method is the same for either side of the caption, the resulting opinion can be examined line by line in deposition.

## How the work reaches Pottstown

The firm maintains no Pottstown office and lists no local address, and it prefers to say that plainly rather than imply a storefront that does not exist. Engagements run remote-first: intake and scoping by phone or video, records analysis and drafting on a schedule set by the court's deadlines, and written opinions delivered with time to prepare the witness. In-person evaluation, when the referral questions require it, is conducted in the evaluee's own community, and testimony travel to Montgomery County is arranged from the Kansas City home office. When the damages case also needs economic quantification, [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) place that analysis with independent economist partners who perform and sign it, keeping the vocational and care-planning opinions and the economic model under one engagement structure without blurring who authored what.
