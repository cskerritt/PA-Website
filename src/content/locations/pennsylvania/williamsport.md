## Labor market foundations for Williamsport opinions

A vocational opinion survives scrutiny when its data can be traced.
Employability and earning capacity findings for Williamsport evaluees rest on
Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the
Williamsport metropolitan area and for Pennsylvania statewide, on state
workforce agency labor market information, and on direct labor market
research where warranted, each source identified in the report. Life care
plans and medical cost projections are priced to the evaluee's own market,
using regional fee data and provider-specific inquiry, so the cost analysis
describes care as it is actually purchased where the evaluee lives.
