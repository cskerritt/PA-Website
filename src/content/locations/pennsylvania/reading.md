Expert referrals rarely arrive early. When Reading counsel contact Purinton
Analytics with a disclosure deadline already on the calendar, intake is
organized around that date: the conflict check is answered promptly, the
scope is defined in writing, and the records request goes out immediately so
the evaluation can proceed without slack in the schedule. Within that
structure the firm provides
[vocational expert evaluations](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[rebuttal or peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) of opposing
reports, along with
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. Plaintiff and defense referrals are welcome on the
same terms.

## Venue context for Berks County matters

State-court engagements proceed in the Court of Common Pleas in Berks
County, and federal matters in the United States District Court for the
Eastern District of Pennsylvania. When a Reading referral is filed elsewhere
in the Commonwealth, the same availability extends to the United States
District Court for the Middle District of Pennsylvania and the United States
District Court for the Western District of Pennsylvania. Any venue-specific
registration or qualification requirement is identified and confirmed during
the conflict check and intake process. Coverage across the rest of the state
is collected on the [Pennsylvania overview](/locations/pennsylvania/).

## Presence in Reading, described accurately

Reading meetings are by appointment. The firm maintains no staffed office in
Berks County and publishes no street address for this location, because a
practice that asks courts to trust its reports should be equally precise
about its own footprint. Evaluations are held in the evaluee's community, at
counsel's offices, or by secure video under publisher protocols, and
depositions and trial testimony are given in person or by remote appearance
where the court permits. When the referral questions require in-person
evaluation, or when hearing dates require presence, travel is scheduled from
the Kansas City home office as part of the engagement plan.

## Labor market research for Reading evaluees

Employability and earning capacity findings rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Reading
metropolitan area and for Pennsylvania statewide, on state workforce agency
labor market information, and on direct labor market research, including
employer contact and job posting analysis, when the referral questions call
for evidence of actual hiring rather than published averages. Conclusions
about what an evaluee can earn are anchored to documented restrictions,
work history, and transferable skills, and every source is cited so
opposing counsel can audit the path from record to opinion.

## Starting a Reading engagement

Berks County referrals most often involve personal injury, medical
malpractice, wrongful death, employment, and disability matters, and either
side of the caption may retain the firm. Each engagement begins with a
documented conflict check, a defined scope, and a records request tailored
to the claims, so the finished opinion rests on a record both parties can
examine. To begin, [request a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) with the case
caption and parties, and identify any imminent disclosure, deposition, or
trial dates so the schedule is realistic from the outset. The
[national coverage page](/locations/nationwide/) describes remote evaluation
logistics and the firm's reach beyond Berks County.
