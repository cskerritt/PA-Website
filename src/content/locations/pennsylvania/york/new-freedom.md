An injured person in New Freedom should not have to travel to an expert's city to be evaluated fairly, and with Purinton Analytics they do not. The firm's practice is built around the evaluee's circumstances: interviews conducted remotely where appropriate, in-person [vocational evaluations](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) held in the evaluee's own community, and [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) assessments that account for how care is actually delivered where the person lives. That evaluee-centered posture is not a courtesy; it produces better evidence, because an assessment rooted in the claimant's real surroundings is harder to attack in deposition than one conducted in an unfamiliar office far from home.

## York County courts and the federal alternatives

New Freedom sits in York County, so its state-court claims proceed in the York County Court of Common Pleas. Where a claim carries a federal basis, Pennsylvania offers the United States District Court for the Eastern District of Pennsylvania, the United States District Court for the Middle District of Pennsylvania, and the United States District Court for the Western District of Pennsylvania as potential forums. The firm takes no position on which court governs a New Freedom dispute until the conflict check and intake confirm it, together with any venue-specific qualification requirement. ## What the labor market data must show

However small the borough, the earning capacity question is answered with the same rigor applied in major cities. Vocational conclusions for a New Freedom evaluee rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the York-Hanover metropolitan area and for Pennsylvania statewide, supplemented by state workforce agency labor market information and, where a referral question warrants it, direct labor market research. The report describes scale in qualitative terms, cites its sources, and never assumes that a small community means a small analysis. Because the opinion is built the same way regardless of who retained it, plaintiff and defense engagements are equally at home in the practice.

## Serving New Freedom by appointment

The firm maintains no office in New Freedom and publishes no local address; candor about presence is part of the same discipline that governs its written opinions. All meetings are by appointment. The bulk of the work, records review, transferable skills analysis, and drafting, proceeds remotely on a secure footing, and when depositions or trial dates require an appearance in the region, travel is arranged from the Kansas City home office and coordinated with the case schedule. Nothing about the firm's distance changes the evaluee's experience, which remains local by design.
