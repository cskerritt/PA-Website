## From the file to the labor market

Once the record is complete, the analysis moves outward to the market. A Hallam evaluee's residual earning capacity is measured against Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the York-Hanover metropolitan area and for Pennsylvania statewide, informed by state workforce agency labor market information and, where the referral questions call for it, by direct labor market research. The borough's modest scale is acknowledged qualitatively, never quantified, and the transferable skills analysis connects documented work history to occupations the data shows actually exist in the region. Every inference in the chain is written down so that it can be checked, which is the firm's working definition of an expert opinion. Where the record is thin, the report says so and explains what additional documentation would resolve the gap, because an opinion that overstates its foundation invites the cross-examination it deserves.
