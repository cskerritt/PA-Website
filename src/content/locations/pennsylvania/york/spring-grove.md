## Keeping the damages chain consistent with the data

Coordination is worthless if the underlying market analysis is soft. Vocational findings for a Spring Grove evaluee are grounded in Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the York-Hanover metropolitan area and for Pennsylvania statewide, supported by state workforce agency labor market information and direct labor market research when the referral questions warrant employer-level inquiry. Scale is described qualitatively, and each expert's opinion stays inside that expert's discipline, which is precisely what keeps the package defensible.
