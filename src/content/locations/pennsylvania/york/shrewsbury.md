## Measuring a commuter's earning capacity

The data has to match the market. Vocational findings for a Shrewsbury evaluee draw on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the York-Hanover metropolitan area and for Pennsylvania statewide, refined by state workforce agency labor market information and by direct labor market research when a referral question demands evidence of actual hiring practice. Commuting range is treated as an analytical variable, not an afterthought: restrictions that shrink the reachable market are documented, and capacity that survives them is credited. Scale is always described qualitatively, and every source is named so the opposing expert can check the work, because a market conclusion that cannot be replicated from its cited sources is an invitation to exclusion.
