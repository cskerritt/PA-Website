Hanover shares top billing in the York-Hanover, PA metropolitan area's own name, and the practice treats it accordingly: not as a satellite of York, but as a substantial borough whose injury, employment, and disability docket generates its own demand for damages experts. When a Hanover case turns on what a claimant can still earn, or on what a lifetime of care will require, Purinton Analytics supplies the answering opinion for either side of the caption through [vocational expert witness work](/services/vocational-expert-witness/), [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and [medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/), prepared to the same standard the firm applies nationally.

## Where Hanover cases are heard

State-court matters from Hanover proceed in the York County Court of Common Pleas, since the borough lies in York County. Claims with a federal basis may be brought in the United States District Court for the Eastern District of Pennsylvania, the United States District Court for the Middle District of Pennsylvania, or the United States District Court for the Western District of Pennsylvania. Which of those forums controls a given Hanover dispute is a question the firm leaves to the record; it is settled during the conflict check and intake, along with any venue-specific registration or qualification requirement. Coverage of the wider region appears on the [York-Hanover metro page](/locations/pennsylvania/york/), and statewide practice is described on the [Pennsylvania page](/locations/pennsylvania/).

## Hanover's labor market in a vocational opinion

An earning capacity opinion for a Hanover evaluee starts from the market that person actually faces rather than a generic statewide abstraction. Vocational findings rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the York-Hanover metropolitan area and for Pennsylvania as a whole, supported by state workforce agency labor market information and by direct labor market research when the referral questions call for it. The write-up stays qualitative about scale and explicit about sources, so the same analysis holds up whether counsel is proving a loss or challenging one. Where damages must also be expressed in economic terms, the firm offers [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement structure.

## Meeting and evaluation logistics

There is no Purinton Analytics office in Hanover, and the firm will not pretend otherwise; every location outside the Kansas City home office is served by appointment. Records review, transferable skills analysis, and report drafting travel well over a secure connection, and remote interviews are available where the referral questions permit. In-person vocational evaluations are conducted in the evaluee's own community rather than at a distant office, and when a deposition or trial date requires presence in the region, travel is planned from Kansas City against the case calendar rather than improvised at the last moment.

## Opening a Hanover engagement

Every engagement opens with a documented conflict check. Counsel should [submit the caption or party names](/refer-a-case/) and note any disclosure, deposition, or trial dates already set. After conflicts clear, the firm defines the scope and the records request in writing before any opinion work begins, so that the resulting report rests on a record both sides can examine. The [national practice overview](/locations/nationwide/) explains the standards behind that sequence; they do not change for a Hanover file.
