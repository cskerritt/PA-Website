Counsel in the Philadelphia-Camden-Wilmington metro practice under
Pennsylvania, New Jersey, Delaware, and Maryland law, sometimes within the
same firm and occasionally within the same matter. Purinton Analytics
supports that multi-state practice with
[employability and earning capacity evaluations](/services/vocational-expert-witness/)
and [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) for plaintiff and
defense engagements alike, along with
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) of opposing
reports and [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## A venue footprint that crosses state lines

Engagements are accepted throughout the metro. On the Pennsylvania side,
that means the Court of Common Pleas in Philadelphia County, Bucks County,
Chester County, Delaware County, and Montgomery County. In New Jersey, the
firm serves counsel in Burlington County, Camden County, Gloucester County,
and Salem County, along with New Castle County in Delaware and Cecil County
in Maryland. Federal matters proceed in the United States District Court for
the Eastern District of Pennsylvania, and when a referral arises elsewhere
in the Commonwealth, in the United States District Court for the Middle
District of Pennsylvania or the United States District Court for the Western
District of Pennsylvania. The [Pennsylvania overview](/locations/pennsylvania/)
describes statewide coverage beyond the metro.

## Meetings by appointment, no local storefront

Philadelphia meetings are by appointment. The firm keeps no staffed office
in the city and publishes no street address for this location; candor about
presence is part of the same discipline that governs the reports themselves.
What counsel can rely on instead is responsiveness. Conflict checks are
answered promptly, evaluation dates are set against the disclosure calendar
at intake, and travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office when
depositions, trials, or in-person evaluations require presence anywhere in
the metro. Evaluations may also proceed by secure video under publisher
protocols, or in the evaluee's own community when in-person testing is
warranted.

## Labor market research across a multi-state metro

Employability and earning capacity findings for metro evaluees rest on
Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for
the Philadelphia metropolitan area, supplemented by statewide estimates for
Pennsylvania, New Jersey, Delaware, and Maryland when an evaluee's realistic
job market crosses a state line. State workforce agency labor market
information and direct labor market research, including employer contact and
job posting analysis, are added when the referral questions require evidence
about actual hiring rather than published averages. This is one of the
largest labor markets in the country, and that depth cuts both ways in
litigation: broader post-injury placement options for some evaluees, sharper
wage stratification for others. The analysis measures rather than assumes.

## Starting a Philadelphia engagement

Referrals here most often arise in personal injury, medical malpractice,
wrongful death, employment, and disability matters, and the firm accepts
work from either side of the caption. Every engagement begins with a
documented conflict check, a defined scope, and a records request tailored
to the claims, so that the written opinion rests on a record both sides can
examine. Submit the caption or party names through the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and note any imminent disclosure,
deposition, or trial dates. CVs and fee information follow promptly, and the
[national coverage page](/locations/nationwide/) describes remote evaluation
logistics, travel, and the rest of the firm's geographic reach.
