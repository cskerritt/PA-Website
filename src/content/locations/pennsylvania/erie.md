When counsel in Erie retain an expert, what they are really retaining is a
written opinion that will sit in the record long after the evaluation itself
is over. Purinton Analytics builds that document to be examined:
[vocational expert work](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) that traces
employability and earning capacity conclusions back to the record and the
labor market data,
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) in catastrophic and
serious injury matters,
[medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/) where future
care is the contested item, and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under a single
engagement structure. The same file discipline applies whether the retention
comes from plaintiff or defense counsel, because every report is prepared on
the assumption that the other side of the caption will read each page.

## Where Erie engagements are venued

Referrals from the region most often involve matters in the Court of Common
Pleas of Erie County. Federal engagements are accepted wherever the case sits
among Pennsylvania's district courts: the United States District Court for
the Western District of Pennsylvania, the United States District Court for
the Middle District of Pennsylvania, and the United States District Court for
the Eastern District of Pennsylvania. The venue changes the deadlines and the
disclosure rules, not the analysis, which follows the same written standard
in every forum. Counsel with matters elsewhere in the Commonwealth can review
the firm's [Pennsylvania coverage](/locations/pennsylvania/) for statewide
context.

## By appointment in Erie

The firm maintains no staffed office in Erie and publishes no street address
for this location, because accuracy about presence belongs to the same
discipline that governs its reports. Meetings are by appointment. Most
engagements begin remotely: a documented conflict check, a records request
tailored to the claims, structured interviews conducted by secure video, and
standardized testing under publisher protocols where remote administration is
supported. When the referral questions call for in-person evaluation, or when
deposition and trial calendars require presence, travel is scheduled from the
Kansas City home office and planned around the court's dates rather than the
firm's convenience.

## Erie labor market research

Vocational findings for Erie County evaluees rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Erie
metropolitan area and for Pennsylvania statewide, supplemented by state
workforce agency labor market information and direct employer contact where
the referral questions warrant it. Employability and placeability opinions
are grounded in the work that actually exists in the evaluee's labor market,
not in national generalities. Life care plans and medical cost projections
are likewise priced to the evaluee's own market rather than to national
averages, so the resulting analysis reflects what care costs where the
evaluee lives and receives treatment. Where the record involves
self-employment or irregular earnings, the earnings analysis is documented
from tax and business records rather than assumption.
