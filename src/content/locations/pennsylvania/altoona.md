Expert deadlines drive most referrals, and Purinton Analytics builds its
Blair County engagements backward from the docket: disclosure dates,
deposition windows, and trial settings come out at intake, and the work plan
is set against them. Within that schedule the firm delivers
[vocational expert evaluations](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) of
employability and earning capacity,
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) for serious injury,
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) when the question
is the soundness of an opposing expert's work, and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under a single
engagement structure. The practice serves plaintiff and defense counsel on
identical terms, and every report is written to be read by the other side.

## Blair County venues

Altoona matters in state court proceed in the Court of Common Pleas of Blair
County. Federal engagements are accepted across Pennsylvania's districts,
whether the case is filed in the United States District Court for the Middle
District of Pennsylvania, the United States District Court for the Western
District of Pennsylvania, or the United States District Court for the Eastern
District of Pennsylvania. Forum selection changes the disclosure obligations
and the calendar, not the method or the report format. How the firm serves
the balance of the Commonwealth is set out on its
[Pennsylvania page](/locations/pennsylvania/).

## Scheduling and presence in Altoona

The firm keeps no staffed office in Altoona and publishes no street address
for this location; describing its presence accurately is part of the same
discipline that governs its opinions. Meetings are by appointment. The
typical engagement opens remotely, with a documented conflict check, a
defined scope, and a records request tailored to the claims, followed by
secure video interviews and standardized testing under publisher protocols
where remote administration is supported. When the referral requires an
in-person evaluation, or when a Blair County deposition or trial date
requires attendance, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office and
locked to the court's calendar early, which is why deadline information at
intake matters.

## Labor market data for Altoona evaluees

Vocational opinions are grounded in Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational
employment and wage estimates for the Altoona metropolitan area and for
Pennsylvania statewide, in state workforce agency labor market information,
and in direct labor market research where the questions warrant it. Earning
capacity conclusions are tied to occupations documented in the evaluee's own
market and to the evaluee's documented work history. Life care plans and
medical cost projections are priced to the region where the evaluee lives and
receives care, so the projected costs describe an actual market rather than a
national abstraction. Each source is cited in the report, so opposing counsel
can trace every finding to its origin.

## Getting on the calendar

Because scheduling is the constraint, the conflict check should come as soon
as an expert need is identified. Submit the caption or party names through
the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) with any disclosure, deposition, or
trial dates already ordered. For related matters outside Pennsylvania, the
firm's [national practice](/locations/nationwide/) runs on the same
remote-first model with the same report standard.
