## Defining the Labor Market Defensibly

A frequent line of attack on earning capacity opinions is the definition of the relevant labor market: draw it too narrowly and the opinion understates opportunity, draw it too broadly and it assumes jobs the evaluee could never realistically reach. The firm's answer in Westmont engagements is methodological, not anecdotal. The analysis defines the market at the level where reliable government data exists, resting on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Johnstown metro area and for Pennsylvania statewide, supplemented by state workforce agency labor market information and direct employer research when a disputed placement question calls for it. Every source is cited so the opposing expert can check the work. Where the damages claim requires economic modeling, the firm offers [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement structure.
