Purinton Analytics is built to be retained by either side of the caption,
because an expert whose conclusions depend on who signs the engagement
letter is not much use to anyone. Plaintiff and defense counsel in Lebanon
County matters are offered the same scope discipline and the same written
standard. For Lebanon litigation the firm provides
[vocational expert evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) of
employability and earning capacity,
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) in serious injury cases,
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) of opposing
expert work, and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under a single
engagement structure. Whichever side refers the case, the method, the
sources, and the report format are identical.

## By-appointment presence in Lebanon

There is no staffed Purinton Analytics office in Lebanon, and no street
address is published for this location; the firm treats accuracy about its
own footprint as part of the credibility it asks courts to extend to its
reports. Meetings are by appointment. Engagements generally open remotely
with a documented conflict check, a defined scope, and a records request
tailored to the claims. Interviews proceed by secure video, and standardized
testing is administered under publisher protocols where remote administration
is supported. When the questions call for an in-person evaluation, or when
deposition and trial dates require presence in Lebanon County, travel is
scheduled from the Kansas City home office with the court calendar as the
fixed point. Counsel work with a consistent point of contact throughout the
engagement, wherever the work itself is performed.

## Labor market grounding for Lebanon evaluees

Vocational conclusions rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational
employment and wage estimates for the Lebanon metropolitan area and for
Pennsylvania statewide, on state workforce agency labor market information,
and on direct labor market research where the referral warrants it. The point
of that grounding is simple: an employability opinion should describe the
labor market the evaluee actually faces. Life care plans and medical cost
projections follow the same principle, priced to the evaluee's own region so
the projected cost of care matches the market where care will be delivered.
Sources are cited in the report so that either side can trace every finding.
