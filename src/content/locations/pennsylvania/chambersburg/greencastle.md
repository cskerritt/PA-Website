## The courts that hear Greencastle claims

Greencastle lies within Franklin County, so state-court litigation arising in the borough is heard in the Franklin County Court of Common Pleas. Where a claim carries a federal basis, Pennsylvania offers several district forums: the United States District Court for the Eastern District of Pennsylvania, the United States District Court for the Middle District of Pennsylvania, and the United States District Court for the Western District of Pennsylvania. Which of them controls a particular case is a question the firm never assumes in advance; the controlling venue, and any registration or qualification requirement that attaches to it, is confirmed during the conflict check and intake. ## A small-town evaluee in a regional market

An earning capacity analysis for a Greencastle evaluee must be sized to the market the evaluee can actually reach. That market is the Chambersburg metropolitan area rather than the borough alone, and the opinion says so plainly instead of leaning on local anecdote. Findings draw on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates covering the Chambersburg area and Pennsylvania statewide, on state workforce agency labor market information, and on direct labor market research when the questions in the referral call for it. Plaintiff work and defense work use the identical sources and the identical method. When the case also needs the loss expressed in economic terms, counsel may elect [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement structure.

There is no Purinton Analytics office in Greencastle, and the firm will not pretend otherwise with a rented address; accuracy about presence is part of the credibility it sells.
