## Reading a borough's earning capacity honestly

Waynesboro is a borough, not a metropolis, and an earning capacity opinion that pretends otherwise fails in deposition. Its workers participate in the labor market of the Chambersburg metropolitan area, so vocational findings rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for that area and for Pennsylvania statewide, supplemented by state workforce agency labor market information and direct labor market research where the referral questions warrant it. The analysis is qualitative about scale and explicit about sources, and it reads the same whether the retaining party is proving a loss or testing a mitigation claim. Where damages must be reduced to economic terms, the firm offers [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement structure.

## How a Waynesboro evaluee is actually seen
