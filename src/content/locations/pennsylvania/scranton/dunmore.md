## Venue for Dunmore claims

Dunmore is a Lackawanna County community, and its state-court litigation proceeds in the Court of Common Pleas. On the federal side, Pennsylvania's district courts sit as the available trial forums: the United States District Court for the Middle District of Pennsylvania, the United States District Court for the Western District of Pennsylvania, and the United States District Court for the Eastern District of Pennsylvania. The firm does not announce which of them governs a Dunmore filing; controlling venue is a case-specific determination confirmed with counsel during the conflict check and intake, along with any qualification step the chosen forum requires of a testifying expert.

## Interview and evaluation logistics

Purinton Analytics keeps no office in Dunmore and publishes no local address, because implying a storefront that does not exist would sit poorly next to reports that are otherwise scrupulous about facts. Counsel meetings are held by video or scheduled by appointment. Trial and deposition travel into Lackawanna County is planned from Kansas City around the court's calendar, under the same model the firm applies to its [nationwide practice](/locations/nationwide/).

## Starting the engagement

The way in is a conflict check, not a consultation call. Submit party names or the caption through the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/), and include the posture of the case, the venue if known, and every date that constrains the work, from disclosure deadlines to a set trial term. After clearance, scope and fees are confirmed in writing, the records request goes out, and Dunmore counsel receive a schedule that accounts for evaluation logistics before the report is due rather than after.
