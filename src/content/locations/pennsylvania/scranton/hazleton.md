An earning capacity opinion built on the wrong market definition invites cross-examination it cannot survive, so Purinton Analytics starts there. ## Courts that hear Hazleton matters

Hazleton sits in Luzerne County, and its state-court cases proceed in the Court of Common Pleas. Federal filings raise a separate venue analysis: Pennsylvania is served at the trial level by the United States District Court for the Eastern District of Pennsylvania, the United States District Court for the Middle District of Pennsylvania, and the United States District Court for the Western District of Pennsylvania, and the firm treats the question of which district controls a given Hazleton case as something to be confirmed with counsel during the conflict check and intake. Forum-specific qualification or registration requirements are resolved at that stage as well, before any opinion work begins.

## Seeing the evaluee in Hazleton

Interviews and functional-history reviews are conducted where they yield the most reliable information. For many referrals that is a structured remote interview; for others, counsel wants the evaluee seen in person, and in those engagements the evaluation is arranged in the evaluee's own community rather than requiring travel to a distant office. Purinton Analytics maintains no Hazleton office and lists no local address, a fact stated here directly because accuracy about presence belongs in the same category as accuracy about wages. Report drafting, records analysis, and testimony preparation run from the Kansas City home office, which also anchors the firm's [nationwide coverage](/locations/nationwide/), and travel for depositions or trial is planned around the court's schedule.

## Conflict check and intake

A Hazleton engagement opens the same way every engagement opens: with a documented conflict check. Counsel submits the caption or party names through the [referral form](/refer-a-case/), notes the forum if it is known, and lists the dates that matter, especially expert disclosure deadlines. Once screening clears, the firm defines scope in writing, requests the records the opinion will rest on, and commits to a delivery schedule that leaves room for deposition preparation on either side of the report.
