## Nanticoke cases in the Court of Common Pleas

Nanticoke litigation in the state system belongs to the Court of Common Pleas of Luzerne County, and reports for those cases are prepared with Pennsylvania state-court disclosure practice in view. Federal matters are a different track. Pennsylvania's district-level forums are the United States District Court for the Western District of Pennsylvania, the United States District Court for the Eastern District of Pennsylvania, and the United States District Court for the Middle District of Pennsylvania; the firm makes no assumption on this page about which of them would receive a Nanticoke case, because controlling venue is identified with counsel during the conflict check and intake, together with any expert registration the forum requires.

## Evaluation options for Nanticoke residents

Counsel choosing between a remote and an in-person evaluation should know both are available on the same terms. The firm operates from its Kansas City home office with no Nanticoke location, no published local address, and no pretense of either; what it offers instead is a structured process that travels well. Records analysis and drafting are done remotely; structured interviews run by video where appropriate; and when the referral questions call for face-to-face evaluation, the appointment is set in or near the evaluee's community so that mobility limits, transportation realities, and local conditions inform the findings. Testimony in Luzerne County is scheduled from Kansas City, as it is across the firm's [national practice](/locations/nationwide/).
