## Lackawanna County and the federal forums

Archbald's home county is Lackawanna County, and its state-court cases are tried in the Court of Common Pleas. Federal litigation involving an Archbald party would proceed in a Pennsylvania district court, and the commonwealth's trial-level forums are the United States District Court for the Western District of Pennsylvania, the United States District Court for the Middle District of Pennsylvania, and the United States District Court for the Eastern District of Pennsylvania. Which district controls is not something this page decides; it is confirmed, case by case, during the conflict check and intake, together with any registration or qualification requirement the presiding forum places on outside experts.
