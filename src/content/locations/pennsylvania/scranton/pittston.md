A serious damages case in Pittston rarely rests on an expert discipline working in isolation. Lost earning capacity, future medical and support needs, and present value are separate opinions that must nonetheless agree with each other, because the side that did not commission them will read them side by side and press on every seam. Purinton Analytics is retained by plaintiff counsel assembling that presentation and by defense counsel testing it, and in either posture the firm offers [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under a unified engagement structure, so the assumptions line up across reports instead of contradicting each other on the stand.

## Courts that hear Pittston disputes

Pittston is in Luzerne County, and state-court proceedings there run through the Court of Common Pleas. Federal matters go to a United States district court, and in Pennsylvania the trial-level forums are the United States District Court for the Eastern District of Pennsylvania, the United States District Court for the Middle District of Pennsylvania, and the United States District Court for the Western District of Pennsylvania. The firm leaves the question of which district reaches a given Pittston filing where it belongs, with counsel, and confirms controlling venue during the conflict check and intake, along with any qualification formality that forum applies to testifying experts.

## Wage evidence across the metro

Pittston evaluees are analyzed within the labor market of the Scranton and Wilkes-Barre metropolitan area, using the Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for that area and for Pennsylvania statewide, together with state workforce agency labor market information. The frame is identical whether the retention comes from the plaintiff or the defense, which is exactly what makes it defensible: the data do not change with the client, and neither does the method. When a coordinated engagement is underway, the vocational findings hand off cleanly to the economist partners; the occupations, the wage bases, and the work-life assumptions the economic analysis uses are the ones the vocational report established, on the record and with sources shown. ## Evaluation and meeting logistics

The firm's only office is its Kansas City home office; there is no Pittston location, and no address is published for this page, deliberately. Engagements run remote-first, with records analysis, drafting, and counsel conferences handled from Kansas City by video and correspondence. In-person vocational evaluations and care-planning assessments, where the referral requires them, take place in the evaluee's own community, and travel for Luzerne County depositions or trial is arranged from the home office around the docket. The structure scales to the firm's [nationwide coverage](/locations/nationwide/), which matters when a Pittston case has parallel proceedings elsewhere.

## Requesting the conflict check

Coordinated engagements make early screening even more important, because economist partners must clear conflicts too, and a practice that accepts work from both sides of the caption cannot treat the gate as a formality. Counsel should [request a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) with the caption and party names, state the posture of the retention, indicate whether vocational, care-planning, and economic components are all in play, and list the governing deadlines. Once screening clears across the engagement, scope and sequencing are confirmed in writing so each report is complete before the next one depends on it.
