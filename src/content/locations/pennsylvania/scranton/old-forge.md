By the time some Old Forge files reach this practice, an expert report already sits in them, and the question is whether it holds up. Purinton Analytics is retained in that posture regularly: [rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) engagements that examine an opposing vocational opinion or life care plan for methodological soundness, sourcing, and internal consistency. The same firm also builds affirmative opinions from scratch, through [vocational expert witness](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) and [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) engagements, and it accepts both kinds of retention from either side of an Old Forge caption, subject always to conflict clearance.

## Where an Old Forge matter is tried

Old Forge belongs to Lackawanna County, and its state-court cases are heard in the Court of Common Pleas. A federal filing implicates a different set of forums, and Pennsylvania supplies the candidates at the trial level: the United States District Court for the Middle District of Pennsylvania, the United States District Court for the Eastern District of Pennsylvania, and the United States District Court for the Western District of Pennsylvania. This page takes no position on which district an Old Forge case lands in; that determination, along with any venue-specific qualification requirement for a testifying expert, is confirmed during the conflict check and intake with the retaining counsel.

## Testing opinions against the regional market

Whether the assignment is affirmative or rebuttal, the reference data are the same: Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Scranton and Wilkes-Barre metropolitan area and for Pennsylvania statewide, supplemented by state workforce agency labor market information. In rebuttal work, the review asks whether the opposing expert used the market an Old Forge evaluee actually faces or substituted a more convenient one; in affirmative work, the report shows its own frame selection so that question never has traction against it. The regional data foundation is described on the [Scranton metro page](/locations/pennsylvania/scranton/), and the commonwealth-wide practice on the [Pennsylvania state page](/locations/pennsylvania/).

## Logistics for evaluations and testimony

Purinton Analytics does not keep an office in Old Forge and does not print a local address anywhere, because a firm that audits other experts' assertions cannot be casual about its own. Service is by appointment under a remote-first model based at the Kansas City home office. Record review, transferable skills analysis, and report production happen there; in-person vocational evaluations and home assessments, when the referral calls for them, are conducted in the evaluee's community; and appearances for deposition or trial in Lackawanna County are scheduled from Kansas City against the court's dates. The identical model serves the firm's [nationwide practice](/locations/nationwide/), so Old Forge counsel with matters in other jurisdictions can use the same process there.

## Opening a file

Conflict screening precedes every substantive conversation, including a rebuttal consult. Old Forge counsel should [start with a conflict check](/refer-a-case/), supplying the caption or parties, the posture, and the operative deadlines, and noting whether an opposing report is already in hand. After clearance, the firm confirms scope in writing, whether that scope is a full evaluation or a focused review, and commits to dates that fit the disclosure calendar.
