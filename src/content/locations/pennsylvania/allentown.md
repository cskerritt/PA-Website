A vocational opinion is only as good as its performance under
cross-examination. Counsel in the Allentown-Bethlehem-Easton metro retain
Purinton Analytics for
[employability and earning capacity evaluations](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[rebuttal or peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) written to that
standard: a defined scope, documented methodology, and conclusions the
report can defend line by line. When the referral also requires economic
damages, the work is structured as
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. Both plaintiff and defense engagements are accepted.

## A metro that reaches into New Jersey

The metro's Pennsylvania venues include the Court of Common Pleas in Lehigh
County, Northampton County, and Carbon County, and the firm also serves
counsel across the state line in Warren County, New Jersey. Federal matters
proceed in the United States District Court for the Eastern District of
Pennsylvania, with the same availability in the United States District Court
for the Middle District of Pennsylvania and the United States District Court
for the Western District of Pennsylvania when a case is filed elsewhere in
the Commonwealth. The [Pennsylvania overview](/locations/pennsylvania/)
collects the firm's coverage across the rest of the state.

## By-appointment logistics in Allentown, Bethlehem, and Easton

Meetings throughout the metro are by appointment. The firm keeps no staffed
office in Allentown, Bethlehem, or Easton and publishes no street address
for this location; being accurate about presence is part of the discipline
that makes the reports reliable. Conflict checks are answered promptly,
evaluation dates are set against the disclosure calendar at intake, and
travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office when depositions,
trials, or in-person evaluations require it. Where in-person work is not
required, evaluations proceed by secure video under publisher protocols or
in the evaluee's own community.

## Labor market evidence, not labor market folklore

Employability and earning capacity findings for metro evaluees rest on
Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for
the Allentown metropolitan area and for Pennsylvania statewide, with New
Jersey statewide estimates considered when an evaluee's realistic job market
crosses the state line. State workforce agency labor market information and
direct labor market research, including employer contact and job posting
analysis, are added when the referral questions require evidence of actual
hiring. The point is the same in every venue: the opinion should describe
the labor market an evaluee actually faces, documented well enough that
opposing counsel can trace every step.

## Starting an engagement in the metro

Referrals most often arise in personal injury, medical malpractice, wrongful
death, employment, and disability matters, from either side of the caption.
Every engagement begins with a documented conflict check, a defined scope,
and a records request tailored to the claims. Submit the caption or party
names through the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and flag any
imminent disclosure, deposition, or trial dates so scheduling can be built
around them. CVs and fee information follow promptly, and the
[national coverage page](/locations/nationwide/) explains remote evaluation
logistics and the firm's geographic reach beyond eastern Pennsylvania.
