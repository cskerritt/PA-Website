Monroeville generates the kinds of disputes that need vocational evidence: workplace injuries, motor vehicle collisions involving working-age claimants, and employment matters where lost earning capacity is contested. Purinton Analytics accepts those referrals from plaintiff and defense counsel alike, delivering [vocational expert](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) opinions, [life care plans](/services/life-care-planning/), and [medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/) written to withstand cross-examination. The question the firm answers is narrow and consequential: what could this person earn before, what can they earn now, and what will their care cost.
