Earning capacity is a comparison, and the method matters more than the conclusion. For a McKeesport evaluee, Purinton Analytics constructs the comparison from documented halves: the capacity demonstrated by the actual pre-injury work history, and the capacity that remains given the medical restrictions, education, skills, and the jobs genuinely available to this person now. The difference between those halves, if any, is the vocational loss, and it is presented the same way whether the retaining counsel represents the plaintiff or the defense. McKeesport referrals commonly involve [vocational expert witness](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) work, [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and [rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) of an opposing expert's methodology.

## Where a McKeesport case is heard

McKeesport is part of Allegheny County, and its state-court civil actions ordinarily proceed in the Court of Common Pleas of Allegheny County. Pennsylvania's federal bench sits in the United States District Court for the Western District of Pennsylvania, the United States District Court for the Middle District of Pennsylvania, and the United States District Court for the Eastern District of Pennsylvania; identifying which district governs a particular McKeesport matter is part of the conflict check and intake, and the answer shapes the disclosure package the firm prepares. Nothing in the analysis changes with the forum. What changes is packaging, timing, and the mechanics of testimony.

## Measuring the market a McKeesport evaluee competes in

McKeesport is a small city within the Pittsburgh, PA metropolitan area, and its residents' employment prospects are properly measured against that regional market. The firm's labor market findings draw on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Pittsburgh metropolitan area and for Pennsylvania statewide, on state workforce agency labor market information, and on direct labor market research when the referral turns on whether specific employers actually hire for specific work. Because the ultimate question is what this evaluee can obtain, not what exists somewhere in theory, the analysis stays local to the region and qualitative in its statements, with every source identified so the opposing expert can retrace the path. ## Conducting the evaluation for a McKeesport resident

The firm's home office is in Kansas City; it maintains no McKeesport location and will not imply one, since a report that begins with an inflated address would deserve the skepticism it got. Standard practice pairs encrypted records exchange with a vocational interview by secure video conference, which most evaluees complete from home. In-person evaluation, when the referral requires it, takes place in the evaluee's own community in an accessible professional setting, with travel scheduled from the Kansas City home office; the same arrangements govern deposition and trial appearances.
