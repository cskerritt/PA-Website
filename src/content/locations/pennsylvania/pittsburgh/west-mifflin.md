Physical demands are where many West Mifflin vocational disputes are won or lost. When an evaluee's pre-injury work involved lifting, standing, climbing, or sustained exertion, the central question becomes whether documented restrictions still permit that work or any work like it, and answering that question carelessly invites impeachment. Purinton Analytics matches medical restrictions against the demand characteristics of specific occupations, occupation by occupation, in [vocational expert witness](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) engagements for West Mifflin counsel on either side of the caption, and pairs that analysis with [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) when future care is in dispute.
