## Lawrence County courts and the federal alternatives

State-court civil actions from New Castle are heard in the Court of Common Pleas of Lawrence County. When a matter belongs in federal court, Pennsylvania offers its district courts as the available forums: the United States District Court for the Western District of Pennsylvania, the United States District Court for the Middle District of Pennsylvania, and the United States District Court for the Eastern District of Pennsylvania. The firm does not guess at venue; the controlling court for each engagement is identified during the conflict check and intake, and the disclosure schedule, report format, and testimony logistics follow from that determination. Counsel practicing in a smaller county's bar often value an expert who adapts to local scheduling realities instead of imposing big-city assumptions, and that adaptability is built into the intake process.

## A small city inside a large regional market

New Castle is a small city whose residents look both to local employers and to the wider Pittsburgh, PA metropolitan labor market for work. That dual reality shapes the vocational analysis: earning capacity findings draw on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Pittsburgh metropolitan area and for Pennsylvania statewide, on state workforce agency labor market information, and on direct labor market research when the question is whether particular work actually exists within a reasonable commuting range of the evaluee. The report describes those findings qualitatively and cites every source, so the labor market picture can be tested rather than taken on faith.
