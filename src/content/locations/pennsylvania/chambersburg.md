Referrals from Chambersburg tend to arrive as questions. Can the evaluee
return to any of the work identified in the record? What has the injury done
to earning capacity? What will future care cost, and on what clinical basis?
Purinton Analytics answers those questions for Franklin County litigation
through [vocational expert evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/), and, where the
damages model requires an economist, through
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under a single
engagement structure. The answers are written for scrutiny from either side
of the caption, because the firm is retained by plaintiff and defense counsel
in comparable measure.

## Venues serving Franklin County

Matters referred from Chambersburg are most often venued in the Court of
Common Pleas of Franklin County. Federal engagements are accepted throughout
the Commonwealth, whether filed in the United States District Court for the
Middle District of Pennsylvania, the United States District Court for the
Eastern District of Pennsylvania, or the United States District Court for the
Western District of Pennsylvania. Counsel handling cases in other parts of
the state will find the same engagement terms described on the firm's
[Pennsylvania page](/locations/pennsylvania/).

## Appointments, not an office

Purinton Analytics keeps no staffed office in Chambersburg and publishes no
street address for this location. Plain statements about presence are part of
the same discipline the firm applies to its opinions. Work ordinarily begins
remotely: a documented conflict check, a defined scope, a records request
tailored to the claims, and interviews by secure video, with standardized
testing administered under publisher protocols where remote administration is
supported. If the referral questions require an in-person evaluation, or if a
deposition or trial in Franklin County requires attendance, travel is
scheduled from the Kansas City home office around the dates the court has
set. Counsel receive a consistent point of contact from intake through
testimony, wherever the work is performed.

## Franklin County labor market research

Employability and earning capacity findings for Chambersburg evaluees draw on
Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the
Chambersburg metropolitan area and for Pennsylvania statewide, on state
workforce agency labor market information, and on direct labor market
research where warranted. The analysis names its sources so that either side
can check them. Life care plans and medical cost projections are priced to
the evaluee's own market, reflecting the cost of care where the evaluee
actually lives and is treated rather than a national average that fits no
particular community. Where the record raises self-employment or seasonal
work, the earnings history is documented from tax and payroll records rather
than assumption.
