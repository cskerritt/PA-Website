An expert opinion is worth little if the people who must weigh it cannot follow it. Purinton Analytics writes its Laureldale reports for the reader who actually decides the case: the judge ruling on admissibility, the arbitrator working through a stack of exhibits, the juror to whom vocational methodology is entirely new. Every conclusion is stated in plain language and traced to its source, because clarity is not a courtesy in litigation; it is what allows a fact-finder to credit the opinion at all. On that footing the firm serves plaintiff and defense counsel alike, providing [vocational expert witness services](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) addressing employability and earning capacity, [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) in catastrophic injury matters, and [expert testimony and litigation consulting](/services/expert-testimony-litigation-consulting/) when the analysis must be explained aloud under oath.

## Reports built to be understood

Clarity is a discipline, not a simplification. A transferable skills analysis means nothing to a lay reader until the report explains which skills the work history actually built, which occupations draw on them, and why the match holds. Laureldale reports therefore define their terms as they go, walk through the method in the order the work was performed, and keep the record's facts visibly separate from the conclusions drawn from them. Jargon that cannot be translated is cut. The approach pays at every stage of the case: an opinion the fact-finder can follow is also an opinion opposing counsel finds hard to mischaracterize, because its reasoning sits on the page rather than in the expert's head. What survives that transparency is what belongs in the report.

## The data behind the opinions

Whatever the posture of a Laureldale referral, the evidentiary spine is the same: Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Reading metropolitan area and for Pennsylvania statewide, state workforce agency labor market information, and direct labor market research where warranted. Figures enter the report with citations attached, so a reader who wants to verify a data point can go to the table it came from without asking the expert for directions. When a matter needs full economic quantification, the firm arranges [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis while vocational and care-planning components proceed under one structure.
