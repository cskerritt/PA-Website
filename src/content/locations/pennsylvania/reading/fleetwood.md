Every Fleetwood engagement follows the same sequence, and the sequence is the product. Conflict check before any substantive conversation; a written scope before any opinion work; a records request tailored to the pleaded claims before any conclusion is drafted. That order protects counsel as much as it protects the firm, because an opinion whose provenance is clean is an opinion that survives discovery. Within that structure, Purinton Analytics serves plaintiff and defense counsel with [vocational expert opinions](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) on employability and earning capacity, [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) in catastrophic injury matters, and [expert testimony and litigation consulting](/services/expert-testimony-litigation-consulting/) through deposition and trial.

## Why the sequence matters

Scope creep is how expert opinions get excluded. An expert asked at deposition why the report addresses questions no referral posed, or relies on records nobody produced, has no good answer. By defining scope in writing at the outset and tying the records request to the claims, the firm ensures that a Fleetwood report can account for itself: what was asked, what was reviewed, what was concluded, and why. Amendments to scope happen the same way the original did, in writing, so the file tells a coherent story from retention to testimony. Supplemental opinions follow the same discipline and issue only when new records or rulings change the analytical foundation.

## Venue for Fleetwood litigation

Fleetwood civil matters filed in state court are heard in Berks County's Court of Common Pleas. For federal claims, Pennsylvania's district courts stand available: the United States District Court for the Eastern District of Pennsylvania, the United States District Court for the Middle District of Pennsylvania, and the United States District Court for the Western District of Pennsylvania. Determining which forum controls is part of intake, not assumption; the governing court and any registration or qualification requirement it imposes are confirmed during the conflict check. ## Labor market evidence for Fleetwood evaluees

Employability and earning capacity findings draw on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Reading metropolitan area and for Pennsylvania statewide, together with state workforce agency labor market information and direct labor market research where the referral questions justify it. The same sources support an affirmative loss opinion for a plaintiff and a mitigation analysis for a defendant; what changes between retentions is the question, never the method. When economic quantification is needed, the firm arranges [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning work runs under the same engagement.
