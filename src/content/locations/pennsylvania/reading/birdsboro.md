When the person at the center of a case lives in Birdsboro, the evaluation should meet that person where life actually happens, not require a day of travel to a distant office. That is how Purinton Analytics structures its work: in-person vocational assessment in the evaluee's own community when the referral warrants it, remote interviewing and records analysis when it does not, and always a written product that holds up in front of both sides of the caption. Counsel retain the firm for [vocational expert opinions](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) on employability and earning capacity, for [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) after catastrophic injury, and for [rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) of opposing expert work.

## An evaluee-centered process

The sequence is deliberate. Records arrive before opinions form; a structured vocational interview follows, conducted by video or, where the questions demand it, in person near the evaluee's home; testing and transferable skills analysis come after that, and the report is drafted only when the record supports it. An evaluee with a skilled-trades history presents different transferable skills questions than one with clerical experience, and the interview is where those distinctions surface. Accommodations and interpreters are arranged in advance when the evaluee needs them, so the assessment measures capacity rather than logistics. Nothing in the process changes with the side that pays for it, which is precisely why the resulting opinion travels well in litigation.

## Venue for Birdsboro disputes

Birdsboro sits in Berks County, and state-court matters proceed in the county's Court of Common Pleas. Where a claim is federal, Pennsylvania's district courts are the available forums: the United States District Court for the Eastern District of Pennsylvania, the United States District Court for the Middle District of Pennsylvania, and the United States District Court for the Western District of Pennsylvania. The firm leaves the venue determination to the conflict check and intake stage, where the controlling court and any court-specific qualification requirements are confirmed with counsel. ## What the labor market data shows

Employability conclusions for a Birdsboro evaluee rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Reading metropolitan area and for Pennsylvania as a whole, joined with state workforce agency labor market information and targeted labor market research when a specific occupation or employer question needs a direct answer. The data is qualitative context and quantitative foundation at once, but it is always cited, so a cross-examiner can pull the same tables the expert used. If the engagement extends to economic damages, independent economist partners perform and sign that analysis within a [coordinated damages engagement](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) that keeps vocational, care-planning, and economic work under a common structure.
