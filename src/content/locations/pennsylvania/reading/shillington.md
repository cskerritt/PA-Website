## The same standard, wherever the caption arises

Uniformity is a substantive commitment, not a slogan. The vocational interview of a Shillington evaluee follows the same structure the firm uses everywhere; the transferable skills analysis applies the same method; sources are cited the same way, so a cross-examiner can pull exactly what the expert pulled. The commitment also runs to candor: where the record cannot carry the opinion counsel hopes for, the firm says so before the work is commissioned rather than after the report is due. ## Earning capacity measured against the metro

Vocational findings for a Shillington evaluee are grounded in Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Reading metropolitan area and for Pennsylvania statewide, because that is the market in which the evaluee actually competes for work, supplemented by state workforce agency data and, where warranted, direct employer research. Because the same datasets serve an affirmative loss opinion and a mitigation critique, the analysis reads identically whether the retention letter comes from the plaintiff or the defense. When counsel also needs the loss reduced to present value, the firm coordinates [damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis alongside the vocational work.
