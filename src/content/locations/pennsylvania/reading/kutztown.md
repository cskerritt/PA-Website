Purinton Analytics keeps no office in Kutztown, and it will not pretend otherwise. Plenty of expert marketing implies a local presence that evaporates on inspection; the firm treats that kind of inflation as a credibility problem, because an expert caught shading the truth about an address will be presumed to shade it elsewhere. What Kutztown counsel actually get is a remote-first national practice with disciplined travel: [vocational expert opinions](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) on employability and earning capacity, [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) for catastrophic injury, and [rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) of opposing expert work, delivered for plaintiff and defense retention alike.

## What remote-first means in practice

Records analysis, transferable skills work, labor market research, and report drafting proceed from the firm's own systems, with counsel conferences held by video at whatever cadence the case requires. Drafts reach counsel on a schedule agreed at intake, and the working file is maintained so production obligations can be met without delay. Distance disappears from the parts of the work where it never mattered. It reappears only where physical presence genuinely matters, and there the firm travels: in-person vocational evaluation is conducted in the evaluee's own community when the referral questions warrant it, and deposition or trial testimony in Berks County is scheduled as travel from the Kansas City home office, booked against dates counsel confirms in advance.

## Grounding opinions in public data

For a Kutztown evaluee, employability and earning capacity conclusions rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Reading metropolitan area and for Pennsylvania statewide, supplemented by state workforce agency labor market information and by direct labor market research when a referral question demands verification the published tables cannot supply. Public data keeps the opinion checkable, which is the quality that matters most once the other side's expert starts reading. Assumptions are stated as assumptions, and gaps in the record are flagged rather than papered over. Where full economic damages are required, [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) put the calculation in the hands of independent economist partners, who perform and sign the economic analysis within the same engagement structure as the vocational work.
