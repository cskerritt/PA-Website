Expert work runs on the court's calendar, not the expert's. Disclosure deadlines, deposition notices, and trial listings arrive on their own schedule, and an expert who cannot deliver inside those dates is a liability no matter how good the analysis. Purinton Analytics builds its Hamburg engagements backward from the dates that bind them: report delivery set against the disclosure deadline, evaluation scheduled early enough to inform it, and testimony preparation timed to the notice. Within that frame the firm provides [vocational expert opinions](/services/vocational-expert-witness/), [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and [expert testimony support](/services/expert-testimony-litigation-consulting/) to plaintiff and defense counsel alike.

## Working against the disclosure calendar

The intake conversation starts with dates. Counsel identify what is already fixed, expert disclosure, discovery cutoff, arbitration or trial listing, and the firm maps the engagement onto that timeline before accepting it, so a Hamburg referral is never taken on a schedule it cannot meet. Records requests go out immediately after scoping, because records drive everything downstream. When a matter arrives late, the firm says whether the remaining runway supports a defensible opinion; taking the engagement anyway and rushing the work would betray the discipline the reports depend on. Once retained, the firm tracks the docket alongside counsel and flags promptly any input, a missing record, an unscheduled evaluation, that threatens a date.

## Labor market foundations

Vocational findings for Hamburg evaluees are grounded in Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Reading metropolitan area and Pennsylvania statewide, alongside state workforce agency labor market information and, where a referral question requires it, direct research into specific occupations. Those foundations do not shift with the retaining party, and the research is logged as it proceeds, so the methodology section of the report reflects what was actually done rather than a reconstruction. Where lost earnings must be converted into an economic damages figure, [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) place that calculation with independent economist partners who perform and sign the economic analysis under the same engagement structure as the vocational work.
