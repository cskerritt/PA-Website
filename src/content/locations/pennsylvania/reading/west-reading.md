## The commuting market as the unit of analysis

The analytical frame matters because it decides what counts as available work. Vocational conclusions for West Reading evaluees are anchored in Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Reading metropolitan area and for Pennsylvania statewide, layered with state workforce agency labor market information and direct labor market research where a referral question turns on a particular occupation. That framing serves plaintiffs establishing loss and defendants testing mitigation with equal force, because the market is what it is regardless of who asks. Opinions state their sources so the data can be checked by anyone who wants to check it, and where the record already contains vocational testing or a prior evaluation, those materials are reconciled against the published data rather than accepted on faith.
