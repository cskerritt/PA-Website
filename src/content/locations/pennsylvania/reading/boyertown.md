Catastrophic injury litigation lives or dies on the future: what care the injured person will need across a lifetime, who will provide it, and what happens to earning capacity in the meantime. For Boyertown cases where those stakes are present, Purinton Analytics prepares [life care plans](/services/life-care-planning/) that tie every projected item of care to the medical record and treating recommendations, [medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/) when the dispute is narrower than a full plan, and [vocational opinions](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) on what work, if any, remains realistic. Retention comes from plaintiff and defense counsel in comparable measure, and the plans read the same either way.

## Building a plan that survives scrutiny

A life care plan is an evidentiary document, not a wish list. Each category, physician services, therapies, medications, equipment, home modifications, attendant care, appears in the plan only when the clinical record or a treating source supports it, and each is priced through documented sourcing rather than assumption. On the defense side the same discipline runs in reverse: reviewing an opposing plan item by item, separating what the record supports from what it does not. Duplications across categories are reconciled so the plan claims only what the record supports, and updates are prepared when the medical picture changes materially before trial. Boyertown counsel receive the analysis in a form built for deposition, because a plan that cannot be defended line by line is worth little to either side.

## Earning capacity alongside the care plan

Where injury forecloses past work, the vocational question runs parallel to the care question. Findings on employability for Boyertown evaluees are grounded in Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Reading metropolitan area and Pennsylvania statewide, with state workforce agency labor market information and direct labor market research added where the referral requires it. When the lost earnings and the care costs must be brought together into a damages figure, the firm structures [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement structure.
