## What a rigorous review looks for

A useful rebuttal is specific. It identifies the data source an opposing report relied on and checks whether the source says what the report claims; it tests whether stated restrictions were carried through to the occupational conclusions or quietly dropped; it distinguishes methodological error from mere professional disagreement, and says so plainly when the opposing work is sound. The review also notes what the opposing expert did well, because overreach in a rebuttal damages credibility as surely as overreach in an affirmative report. Counsel in Sinking Spring matters receive that assessment in writing, early enough to shape deposition strategy rather than merely react at trial.
