The Lancaster metro is coextensive with Lancaster County, which gives
litigation here an unusually clear venue picture and lets an expert
engagement focus immediately on the merits. Purinton Analytics serves
Lancaster County counsel with
[employability and earning capacity evaluations](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) of opposing
reports, accepting work from plaintiff and defense alike. When a matter also
requires economic damages, the firm structures
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Venue context for Lancaster County matters

State-court engagements proceed in the Court of Common Pleas in Lancaster
County. Federal matters proceed in the United States District Court for the
Eastern District of Pennsylvania, and when a Lancaster referral is filed
elsewhere in the Commonwealth, the same availability extends to the United
States District Court for the Middle District of Pennsylvania and the United
States District Court for the Western District of Pennsylvania. Because the
metro and the county share the same boundaries, scoping is usually quick: the venue,
disclosure schedule, and applicable standards are identified at intake, and
any venue-specific registration or qualification requirement is identified
and confirmed during the conflict check and intake process. The
[Pennsylvania overview](/locations/pennsylvania/) describes the firm's
coverage across the rest of the state.

## Meetings by appointment, offices described honestly

Lancaster meetings are by appointment. The firm has no staffed office in the
county and publishes no street address for this location, because accuracy
about presence is part of the discipline counsel is retaining. Evaluation
sessions are held in the evaluee's community, at counsel's offices, or by
secure video under publisher protocols, and depositions and trial testimony
are provided in person or by remote appearance where the court permits.
When the referral questions call for in-person evaluation, or when hearing
and trial dates require presence, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City
home office and is built into the engagement plan rather than improvised
late.

## Labor market research for Lancaster County evaluees

Employability and earning capacity findings rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Lancaster
metropolitan area and for Pennsylvania statewide, on state workforce agency
labor market information, and on direct labor market research, including
employer contact and job posting analysis, when the referral questions
require evidence about actual hiring in the county rather than published
averages alone. Transferable skills analysis starts from the evaluee's
documented work history and tested capacities, and every source relied on is
cited in the report so that both sides can examine the basis for each
conclusion.

## Starting a Lancaster engagement

Referrals most often involve personal injury, medical malpractice, wrongful
death, employment, and disability matters, and either side of the caption
may engage the firm. Each engagement begins with a documented conflict
check, a defined scope, and a records request tailored to the claims. To
begin, [request a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) with the case caption and
parties, noting any imminent disclosure, deposition, or trial dates. CVs and
fee information follow promptly, and the
[national coverage page](/locations/nationwide/) covers remote evaluation
logistics and the firm's geographic reach beyond Lancaster County.
