Purinton Analytics serves Wilson the way it serves most of the country: remote-first. The bulk of a sound vocational or life care planning opinion is built from records, structured interviews, and published labor market data, none of which requires the expert and the evaluee to share a room. So for a Wilson matter, the early phases proceed by secure video and document exchange, keeping costs proportionate to the case, and in-person work is reserved for the moments where it genuinely changes the analysis or the testimony. Retaining counsel on either side of the caption gets the same structure and the same candor about what each phase will and will not accomplish.

## Venue Context in Northampton County

Wilson is a Northampton County borough. Its state-court litigation proceeds in the Court of Common Pleas, and federal claims arising under Pennsylvania venue rules may land in the United States District Court for the Eastern District of Pennsylvania, the United States District Court for the Middle District of Pennsylvania, or the United States District Court for the Western District of Pennsylvania. The practice does not guess at the controlling forum; venue and any court-specific qualification requirements are confirmed during the conflict check and intake, and the report is drafted to hold up wherever the case is actually heard.

## Labor Market Data for a Wilson Claim

The economically meaningful market for a Wilson evaluee is the Allentown-Bethlehem-Easton, PA-NJ metropolitan area, and [vocational findings](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) are therefore anchored in Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for that metro and for Pennsylvania statewide, supplemented by state workforce agency labor market information and by direct labor market research where warranted. The analysis names its sources, states its assumptions, and distinguishes between what the data shows and what it merely suggests, because that distinction is exactly where cross-examination goes. Transferable skills, residual capacity, and realistic placement prospects are addressed in the report itself, not saved for direct examination.

## In-Person Work When It Matters

There is no Wilson office, no local storefront, and no published street address, and the practice states that plainly rather than implying a presence it does not have. When an engagement calls for physical presence, such as a functional evaluation, a home and care-environment assessment supporting a [life care plan](/services/life-care-planning/), or deposition and trial testimony, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office with enough lead time to protect the court's calendar. Evaluations can be held in the evaluee's own community, which strengthens the factual foundation of the opinion. The same service model covers the [full metro](/locations/pennsylvania/allentown/), the [rest of Pennsylvania](/locations/pennsylvania/), and [engagements nationwide](/locations/nationwide/).

## Submitting a Referral

A Wilson referral begins with the caption. [Send a conflict check request](/refer-a-case/) listing the parties, the nature of the claims, and any scheduled expert disclosure, deposition, or trial dates. After conflicts clear, counsel receives a written scope with a tailored records request, so the engagement's boundaries, deliverables, and timing are documented before analysis begins. Plaintiff and defense referrals move through the same intake on the same terms, and questions about fit, timing, or scope are welcome before any formal retention is made.
