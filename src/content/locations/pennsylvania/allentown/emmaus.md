An evaluee living in Emmaus should not have to organize their evaluation around an expert's convenience. Purinton Analytics structures its Emmaus engagements around the person being assessed: interviews are scheduled at hours that respect treatment and work obligations, remote sessions are offered whenever the referral questions permit, and when an in-person evaluation is needed it can be conducted in the evaluee's own community rather than a distant office. Counsel for either party gets the same benefit from that approach, because an assessment grounded in the evaluee's actual daily environment is harder to impeach than an assessment abstracted from it.

## Lehigh County Courthouse Context

Emmaus is a Lehigh County borough, and state-court claims arising there are heard in the Court of Common Pleas. Federal matters may proceed in the United States District Court for the Eastern District of Pennsylvania, the United States District Court for the Middle District of Pennsylvania, or the United States District Court for the Western District of Pennsylvania; the district that actually controls venue for a specific caption, and any expert qualification requirement particular to it, is identified during the conflict check and intake rather than assumed. The written work is prepared to the same standard regardless of forum, because the audience is always the same: a fact-finder, and opposing counsel reading closely.
