Expert disclosure deadlines drive the schedule in Easton litigation, and a vocational or life care planning opinion that arrives late, thin, or unsupported creates problems no continuance fully repairs. Purinton Analytics is built around that calendar. From the initial referral of an Easton matter, the practice works backward from the disclosure, deposition, and trial dates counsel identifies, so that records are requested early, the evaluation is scheduled with margin, and the written report is complete before the deadline rather than beside it. That discipline serves plaintiff and defense engagements alike.

## Courts Serving Easton

Easton litigants proceed in the Court of Common Pleas for state-court matters in Northampton County. When a claim belongs in federal court, Pennsylvania offers the United States District Court for the Eastern District of Pennsylvania, the United States District Court for the Middle District of Pennsylvania, and the United States District Court for the Western District of Pennsylvania as available forums; which district controls a particular case is a venue question resolved by counsel and confirmed during the conflict check and intake, together with any registration or qualification requirement the forum imposes. The report format does not change with the courthouse: cited sources, stated assumptions, and a methodology section written for cross-examination.

## The Easton Workforce in Metro Context

Easton is part of the Allentown-Bethlehem-Easton, PA-NJ metropolitan area, and an earning-capacity opinion for an Easton evaluee must reflect that regional market rather than a market imagined as ending at the municipal boundary. The practice grounds its [vocational expert](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) findings in Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the metro and for Pennsylvania statewide, along with state workforce agency labor market information and, where the questions call for it, direct labor market research. No wage figure or employment count appears without a source, and no occupation is offered as a placement target unless the data and the evaluee's residual capacity both support it.

## Beginning the Engagement

Every Easton engagement opens the same way: a documented conflict check, then a defined scope, then a records request tailored to the claims. Submit the caption or party names through the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and flag any imminent disclosure, deposition, or trial dates in the submission itself. Once conflicts clear, counsel receives a proposed scope and schedule keyed to the dates provided, so both the retaining attorney and the expert know what will be delivered and when, before any substantive work begins.
