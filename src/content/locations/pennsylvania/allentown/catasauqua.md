## Courts Available to Catasauqua Litigants

Catasauqua lies in Lehigh County, where state-court matters are heard in the Court of Common Pleas. Pennsylvania's federal forums are the United States District Court for the Eastern District of Pennsylvania, the United States District Court for the Middle District of Pennsylvania, and the United States District Court for the Western District of Pennsylvania; the district that governs a particular Catasauqua case is a venue determination made by counsel and confirmed during the conflict check and intake, along with any qualification or registration step the chosen forum requires. Whatever the courthouse, the deliverable is the same: a written opinion with cited sources and stated assumptions, prepared for an audience that includes opposing counsel.

Because Catasauqua is a small suburb inside a regional employment market, the vocational analysis reads metro-level and Pennsylvania statewide data through the lens of the individual evaluee: what occupations their work history and residual capacity support, what those occupations pay in this region, and how the gap between pre-injury and post-injury capacity should be described to a fact-finder.
