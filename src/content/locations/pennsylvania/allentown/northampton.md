A worker who lives in the borough of Northampton typically sells their labor across the Allentown-Bethlehem-Easton, PA-NJ metropolitan area, and an earning-capacity opinion that pretends otherwise understates or overstates the loss. That commuting reality is where Purinton Analytics starts when counsel refers a Northampton matter: the question is never what jobs exist inside the borough line, but what the regional market offers a person with this evaluee's education, work history, and post-injury capacity. The answer is developed the same way for plaintiff and defense clients, from the same data, under the same [vocational expert](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) methodology.

## A Shared Name: Borough and County

The borough of Northampton lies within Northampton County, and its state-court disputes are heard in the Court of Common Pleas. On the federal side, Pennsylvania's trial courts are the United States District Court for the Eastern District of Pennsylvania, the United States District Court for the Middle District of Pennsylvania, and the United States District Court for the Western District of Pennsylvania; which of them supplies the controlling venue for a given complaint is settled by counsel and verified during the conflict check and intake, together with any forum-specific qualification step. Because borough and county share a name, captions and records requests are drafted with care to keep the geography unambiguous.

## Commuting Range and Earning Capacity

Assessing what a Northampton evaluee can earn means assessing the market within realistic commuting range, and the practice does that with Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the metro and for Pennsylvania statewide, state workforce agency labor market information, and targeted employer research when the referral questions demand it. Transferable skills analysis connects the evaluee's demonstrated work history to occupations the regional data actually documents; nothing is asserted about the market that a cited source does not support, and limitations are stated as plainly as capacities.

## Meeting the Evaluee

Purinton Analytics has no office in Northampton and does not pretend to. Appointments are arranged as the case requires: secure video for records-driven interviews and counsel conferences, in-community sessions when a functional evaluation or a [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) home assessment needs to happen where the evaluee actually lives. Travel for evaluations, depositions, and trial testimony is scheduled from the Kansas City home office. When the damages model also needs an economist, the firm arranges [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement structure. Coverage across the [surrounding metro](/locations/pennsylvania/allentown/), [across Pennsylvania](/locations/pennsylvania/), and [nationally](/locations/nationwide/) follows the same model.
