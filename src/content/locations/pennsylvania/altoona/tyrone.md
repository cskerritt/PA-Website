Counsel in Tyrone work against the same calendar of disclosures, depositions, and trial listings as counsel anywhere in Pennsylvania, and Purinton Analytics builds its engagements around those dates from the first call. The firm supplies [vocational expert opinions](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) addressing employability and residual earning capacity, [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) where an injury or illness creates lifelong care needs, and [expert testimony and litigation consulting](/services/expert-testimony-litigation-consulting/) when the opinion must survive examination by the other side. Plaintiff and defense engagements are accepted on identical terms, because a method that changes with the client is not a method a court should trust.

## A commuting-market view of earning capacity

An evaluee living in Tyrone competes for work across the Altoona metropolitan labor market, not merely within the borough itself, and the firm's opinions reflect that commuting reality. Employability and earning capacity findings rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates covering the Altoona metropolitan area and Pennsylvania statewide, on labor market information published by the state workforce agency, and, where the assignment warrants it, on direct labor market research documenting what employers in the region actually require. Because the underlying data are cited in the report, opposing counsel can check every step. If lost earnings or the cost of future care must be reduced to present value, the firm offers [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement structure.

## Meeting the evaluee where they live

The firm maintains no office in Tyrone, holds no storefront anywhere in Blair County, and says so plainly, since candor about presence is part of the credibility it offers counsel. Most of the work travels well: records analysis, transferable skills assessment, report drafting, and attorney conferences all proceed remotely by video and secure exchange. An in-person vocational evaluation, when the questions call for it, is conducted in the evaluee's own community so that participation does not depend on the evaluee's ability to travel. Testimony is a scheduling matter, handled early: once deposition or trial dates firm up, the expert's travel is planned from the Kansas City home office to meet them.
