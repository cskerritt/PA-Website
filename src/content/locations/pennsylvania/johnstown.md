Catastrophic injury cases carry the heaviest evidentiary burdens, and they
are the matters that most often bring Cambria County counsel to Purinton
Analytics. A serious injury claim needs a
[life care plan](/services/life-care-planning/) grounded in the clinical
record, a [medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/)
priced to the market where care will actually be delivered, and a
[vocational evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) that says
plainly what work remains open to the evaluee and what has been lost. Where
the damages model also requires economic analysis, the firm offers
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under a single
engagement structure. These cases arrive from plaintiff counsel and defense
counsel alike, and the file is built to the same standard for each.

## Cambria County and the federal districts

Johnstown referrals are most commonly venued in the Court of Common Pleas of
Cambria County. Federal matters are accepted wherever they sit among the
Commonwealth's districts: the United States District Court for the Western
District of Pennsylvania, the United States District Court for the Middle
District of Pennsylvania, and the United States District Court for the
Eastern District of Pennsylvania. The report standard does not vary by forum; the
calendar and the disclosure obligations do. Engagement terms for the rest of
the state appear on the firm's
[Pennsylvania page](/locations/pennsylvania/).

## Evaluations by appointment

Purinton Analytics staffs no office in Johnstown and publishes no street
address for this location. Saying so directly is part of the same habit of
accuracy the firm's reports depend on. Most Cambria County engagements begin
remotely, with a documented conflict check, a defined scope, and a records
request tailored to the claims; interviews proceed by secure video, and
standardized testing is administered under publisher protocols where remote
administration is supported. In catastrophic cases the evaluation often needs
to happen where the evaluee is, and when it does, or when deposition and
trial dates require presence, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home
office around the court's calendar.

## Pricing care and work to the local market

Life care plans and medical cost projections for Johnstown evaluees are
priced to the evaluee's own region, using provider-specific inquiry and
published fee data rather than national averages. Vocational findings rest on
Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the
Johnstown metropolitan area and for Pennsylvania statewide, on state
workforce agency labor market information, and on direct labor market
research where the referral questions warrant it. The result is an analysis
tied to the community where the evaluee lives, works, and receives care,
which is where cross-examination inevitably goes. Where home modification,
attendant care, or equipment replacement drives the plan, vendors serving the
evaluee's area are identified and their pricing documented.
