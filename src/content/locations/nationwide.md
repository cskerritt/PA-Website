Purinton Analytics is a national practice. The firm is based in Kansas City,
Missouri, and accepts [vocational expert](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[forensic economic damages](/services/forensic-economic-damages/) engagements

economic damages testimony, the analyses are performed and signed by independent
economist partners coordinated by the firm. This page explains how a national
practice actually works: where the firm appears, how evaluations are conducted
at a distance and in person, and how labor market and medical cost research
stays local to the venue and to the evaluee.

## Engagements in all 50 states

The firm serves counsel in federal and state matters nationwide. Engagements
have a common structure wherever they arise: a documented conflict check, a
defined scope, a records request tailored to the claims, a written report with
stated methodology, and availability for deposition and trial. That structure
travels well because the underlying data does. The occupational taxonomies,
wage surveys, and standardized instruments that support a vocational opinion
are national systems with state and local detail, so the same documented
method can be applied to a claim venued in any federal district court, state
trial court, workers' compensation system, or administrative forum.

Referrals most often arise in personal injury, medical malpractice, wrongful
death, employment, and disability matters. The firm accepts single-expert
referrals and [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which vocational, life care planning, and independent economic analyses are
sequenced together while each expert stays within a separate professional role.

## Remote and in-person evaluation options

Most engagements can begin remotely. Records review, clinical interviews, and
many standardized vocational instruments can be administered by secure video
under publisher protocols, which shortens timelines and reduces cost for
counsel in states distant from the firm's offices. In-person evaluation is
scheduled when the referral questions, testing protocols, or counsel's
preference require it, at one of the firm's meeting locations, at counsel's
offices, or in the evaluee's community. The written report identifies how each
component of the evaluation was conducted so the record is clear.

## Labor market research that stays local

A national practice is credible only if its labor market findings are local.
Employability and earning capacity opinions are tied to the labor market where
the evaluee actually lives and works, using published government data that can
be checked by opposing counsel. Wage and employment figures come principally
from the [Bureau of Labor Statistics Occupational Employment and Wage
Statistics (OEWS) program](https://www.bls.gov/oes/), which reports estimates
at the national, state, and metropolitan area level. Occupational requirements
and transferable skills are analyzed through the United States Department of
Labor taxonomies, including [O\*NET](https://www.onetonline.org/) and the
Dictionary of Occupational Titles. Where the referral questions require it,
the analysis incorporates state workforce agency labor market information and
direct labor market research, such as employer contact and job posting
analysis, for the relevant region. Every figure in the report is cited to its
source so the analysis can be traced from conclusion to data.

## Geographic medical cost research

Life care plans and medical cost projections are priced for the region where
the evaluee lives and is expected to receive care, not from national averages
alone. Future care costs are researched through provider-specific pricing
inquiries in the evaluee's market, published fee schedule data, and recognized
national reference sources such as the
[Centers for Medicare and Medicaid Services](https://www.cms.gov/), with the
sources for each cost item identified in the plan itself. When a life care
plan or [medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/) feeds an
economic damages calculation, the present value analysis is performed and
signed by an independent economist partner, keeping the cost research and the
economic methodology in separate, properly credentialed hands.

## Licensure and professional qualification

Counsel evaluating an out-of-state expert should know exactly how
qualification works. Professional counseling licensure is state-based; Jason
C. Purinton is a Licensed Professional Counselor in Missouri. His board
certifications, including Certified Rehabilitation Counselor, Certified
Vocational Evaluator, Certified Life Care Planner, and Fellow of the American
Board of Vocational Experts, are issued by national credentialing bodies and
are not limited to any one state. Admission of expert testimony is governed by
the forum's rules of evidence, such as Federal Rule of Evidence 702 and its
state analogues, rather than by licensure alone. He also holds
workers' compensation rehabilitation credentials in Missouri, Kansas, and
Nebraska. Any venue-specific registration or qualification requirement is
identified and confirmed during the conflict check and intake process.
