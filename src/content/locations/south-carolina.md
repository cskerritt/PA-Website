What counsel receive from Purinton Analytics in South Carolina is a written
opinion built for cross-examination: a defined scope, a documented
methodology, sources cited so the opposing side can check them, and
conclusions that do not reach past the record. The firm provides vocational
expert and life care planning services throughout the state on that
standard, and because it accepts work from plaintiff and defense
engagements alike, every report is drafted knowing that the other side of
the caption may be a future client.

## How the Firm Serves South Carolina

The firm operates from its home office in Kansas City and keeps no staffed
office in South Carolina. Meetings in the state are by appointment only,
and no South Carolina street address is published; the firm treats honesty
about where it actually sits as part of the same discipline it applies to
its opinions. The engagement model is remote-first. Records review,
transferable skills analysis, labor market research, and report drafting
proceed without travel, and evaluee interviews are conducted by secure
video conference or in person in the evaluee's own community. When the
referral questions call for in-person evaluation, or when deposition and
trial dates require the expert's presence, travel is scheduled from the
Kansas City home office.

Vocational findings for South Carolina evaluees rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the relevant
South Carolina metropolitan areas and for the state as a whole, along with
state workforce agency labor market information and direct labor market
research where warranted. Where the case also requires economic projection
of lost earnings or the cost of future care, the firm offers coordinated
damages engagements in which independent economist partners perform and
sign the economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning work
proceeds under one engagement structure.

## Qualification and Disclosure

Expert disclosure practice differs between the Court of Common Pleas and
the federal district, and the operative scheduling order controls the
timing in either forum, so counsel are asked to provide that order at
intake. Report and disclosure deadlines are calendared at the outset of the
engagement. Any venue-specific registration or qualification requirement is
identified and confirmed during the conflict check and intake process.

outside South Carolina, the
