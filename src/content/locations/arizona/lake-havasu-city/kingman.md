Counsel preparing a Mohave County case usually comes to the expert question
with a courtroom already in mind. For Kingman matters, Purinton Analytics
starts from that same place: the venue, its disclosure deadlines, and the
standard the written opinion will have to survive there. The firm provides
[vocational expert evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) and
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) to plaintiff and
defense counsel alike, and it structures
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under a
single engagement structure. Every report is written on the assumption
that opposing counsel will read it closely, because that is the only
assumption that produces durable opinions.

## The venue picture for Kingman matters

State-court litigation involving Kingman parties proceeds in the Superior
Court, and the firm accepts personal injury, employment, and family law
engagements venued there. In federal practice, the United States District
Court for the District of Arizona is the available federal forum; which
court will actually control a given matter is identified during the
conflict check and intake, along with any venue-specific registration or
qualification requirement. Intake follows the same sequence in every
engagement: a documented conflict check, a defined scope in writing, and a
records request tailored to the claims. How the broader metropolitan area
is served is set out on the
[Lake Havasu City metro page](/locations/arizona/lake-havasu-city/).

## Labor market evidence for Kingman evaluees

When earning capacity is disputed, the fight is usually about data, so the
data comes first. The firm grounds its vocational findings for Kingman
evaluees in Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage
estimates for the Lake Havasu City-Kingman metropolitan area and for
Arizona statewide, together with state workforce agency labor market
information. Where those published sources leave a genuine gap, direct
labor market research fills it, and the report says so explicitly.
Transferable skills, residual functional capacity as documented in the
record, and the occupations actually available in the regional market are
analyzed separately and then connected, step by step, in the written
opinion.

## How an evaluation reaches Kingman

The firm maintains its home office in Kansas City and keeps no staffed
location in Kingman; it publishes no local street address because none
exists. Service here is by appointment and by design. Records review,
counsel conferences, and much of the interview and testing work proceed
remotely under publisher protocols. When the referral questions require an
in-person evaluation, or when deposition and trial dates require presence,
the appointment is held in the evaluee's own community and travel is
scheduled from the Kansas City home office. The same model serves counsel
statewide, described on the [Arizona page](/locations/arizona/), and
nationally, described on the
[nationwide practice page](/locations/nationwide/).

## Requesting a conflict check

Engagements open with a conflict check rather than a sales conversation.
To begin, [request a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) with the case caption
and parties, and identify any disclosure, deposition, or trial dates
already on the calendar. Counsel receives a written scope and records
request in response, so both sides of the caption can see, before any
analysis begins, exactly what questions the opinion will answer and what
record it will rest on.
