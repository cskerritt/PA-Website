The practical question counsel tend to raise first about a Pima referral is
logistical: how does a rigorous forensic evaluation get done for an evaluee
who lives in a small Graham County town rather than a metropolitan center?
The answer is that the file drives the work, not the address. Purinton
Analytics accepts Pima matters from plaintiff and defense counsel alike and
delivers [vocational expert evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[expert testimony](/services/expert-testimony-litigation-consulting/)
through a remote-first process that treats a Pima evaluee's record with the
same rigor a big-city file would receive.

## Courts that hear Pima cases

Litigation arising in Pima belongs, on the state side, to the Superior Court
in Graham County. On the federal side, Arizona's federal trial forum is the
United States District Court for the District of Arizona, and the venue
that will actually control a given matter is identified during the conflict
check and intake, together with any registration or qualification
requirement the forum imposes. That early venue determination is not a
formality. Disclosure deadlines, report content expectations, and the
mechanics of expert discovery differ between the state and federal systems,
and an opinion scoped to the wrong set of rules costs counsel time at
exactly the point in the calendar when time is scarce.

## Reading the labor market from Pima

A vocational opinion about a Pima resident cannot borrow conclusions from a
large-city labor market and call them local. The relevant market is the one
reachable from Pima, centered on Safford as the principal city of the
micropolitan area, and the analysis describes it with the sources built for
that purpose: Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage
estimates for the area and for Arizona statewide, state workforce agency
labor market information, and direct employer research when published
estimates leave gaps. Smaller labor markets narrow some occupational paths
and leave others open, and the written opinion says which is which for this
evaluee, with the reasoning laid out so the other side can probe it at
deposition rather than guess at it.

## The evaluation itself

Records review, interviews, and conferences with counsel are conducted
remotely by default, through video meetings and secure document exchange, so
a Pima evaluee is not asked to organize a claim around travel to an expert.
When the referral questions require an in-person assessment, the firm comes
to the evaluee's community instead. There is no staffed Purinton Analytics
office in Pima or elsewhere in Arizona, and none is claimed; the sole home
office is in Kansas City, and travel for evaluations, depositions, and trial
is scheduled from there. The [Safford metro page](/locations/arizona/safford/)
describes the regional practice around Pima, and the
[Arizona page](/locations/arizona/) covers the statewide framework.

## Requesting a conflict check

Every engagement, local or part of the firm's
[national practice](/locations/nationwide/), begins with the same gate: a
documented conflict check. To refer a Pima matter,
[request a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) with the case caption and party
names, and note the disclosure, deposition, and trial dates already on the
calendar. Intake then fixes the referral questions, the records request, and
the applicable disclosure schedule before analysis begins, so the resulting
opinion arrives scoped to the forum, grounded in the record, and ready for
the scrutiny of whichever side did not send it.
