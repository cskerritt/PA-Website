Thatcher is a small Graham County town, and a claim that arises there is
measured against the working life of the county as a whole rather than
against the town limits. When counsel on either side of the caption refer a
Thatcher matter, Purinton Analytics answers with
[vocational expert evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. The product is a written opinion built to withstand
examination by the opposing party, not advocacy dressed as analysis.

## Venue for Thatcher matters

State-court cases from Thatcher are heard in the Superior Court in Graham
County. Federal cases proceed in the United States District Court for the
District of Arizona, the state's federal trial forum, with the controlling
venue confirmed during the conflict check and intake along with any
venue-specific registration or qualification requirement. The forum shapes
the engagement from its first day: state and federal courts run different
disclosure calendars and expect different things from an expert report, so
scope, report format, and testimony preparation are set to the rules of the
court that will actually hear the case rather than adjusted after the fact.

## Where Thatcher sits in the Safford labor market

An earning-capacity opinion for a Thatcher evaluee has to describe the
market that person can realistically reach, which in Graham County means the
employment base anchored by Safford, the principal city of the surrounding
micropolitan area. Vocational findings therefore rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the area and for
Arizona statewide, on state workforce agency labor market information, and
on direct labor market research where the published series run thin, as they
often do in smaller areas. The analysis stays qualitative about geography
and precise about method: transferable skills are assessed against
occupations that actually exist within a reasonable commuting range, and any
assumption about relocation or remote work is stated openly so opposing
counsel can test it.

## Evaluating an evaluee who lives in Thatcher

The firm's only home office is in Kansas City, and it keeps no staffed
office in Thatcher or anywhere else in Arizona; candor about that is part of
the same discipline that governs the reports. Most engagement work proceeds
remotely, with records review, vocational interviewing, and counsel
conferences handled by video and secure document exchange. When the referral
questions call for an in-person evaluation, the evaluation is scheduled in
the evaluee's own community, and travel is arranged from the Kansas City
home office around deposition, hearing, and trial dates. Counsel who want
the broader regional context can review the
[Safford practice page](/locations/arizona/safford/), and the
[Arizona overview](/locations/arizona/) describes how the same approach
applies statewide.
