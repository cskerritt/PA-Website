Testimony is where a damages opinion is tested, and engagements in the
Sierra Vista-Douglas metropolitan area are built with that endpoint in view
from the first records request. Purinton Analytics serves plaintiff and
defense counsel across Cochise County with
[vocational expert evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[expert testimony and litigation consulting](/services/expert-testimony-litigation-consulting/)

## Cochise County venues and forums

Engagements are accepted for matters venued in the Superior Court in
Cochise County and in the United States District Court for the District of
Arizona. Whatever the forum, each engagement begins with a documented
conflict check, a scope defined in writing, and a records request tailored
to the claims, so that the written opinion rests on a record both sides can
examine. Counsel with matters elsewhere in the state will find the firm's
statewide approach described on the [Arizona page](/locations/arizona/).

## Appearing where the case requires

The firm keeps no staffed office in Sierra Vista or Douglas and publishes no
street address for this location; meetings are by appointment. Most of the
work in a typical engagement is performed remotely, including records
review, structured vocational interviews, and standardized testing under
publisher protocols where remote administration is supported. Depositions
are routinely taken by video where the parties agree. When the case requires
physical presence, whether for an in-person evaluation or for trial, travel
is scheduled from the Kansas City home office and confirmed against the
court's calendar early enough to avoid scheduling pressure.

## Labor market foundations for Cochise County

Vocational findings for evaluees in the metro rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Sierra
Vista-Douglas metropolitan area and for Arizona statewide, together with
state workforce agency labor market information and direct labor market
research where the referral questions warrant it. Because cross-examination
often turns on whether the data actually describe the evaluee's market, the
report is explicit about geographic coverage for every source it relies on.
Life care plans and
[medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/) are priced to
the evaluee's own market using provider-specific inquiries and published fee
data.

## Qualification for Arizona forums

Jason Purinton's certifications are issued by national credentialing bodies
rather than by any single state. Whether an expert may testify in a given
matter is decided by the forum under its own rules of evidence, and any
venue-specific registration or qualification requirement is identified and
confirmed during the conflict check and intake process.
