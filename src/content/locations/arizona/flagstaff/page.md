When the person whose earning capacity or future care is in dispute lives in
Page, the practical question for counsel is how a rigorous evaluation gets
done without turning the evaluee's participation into its own hardship.
Purinton Analytics is built around that question. The practice is
remote-first by design, so an
[earning capacity and employability evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/)
or a [life care plan](/services/life-care-planning/) for a Page evaluee
starts with records review and structured remote interviews rather than a
summons to a distant office. Plaintiff and defense counsel receive the same
product: a written opinion that states its methodology, cites its sources,
and separates what was observed from what is concluded.

## The county and courts behind a Page case

Page sits in Coconino County, and civil matters arising there are accepted
for the Superior Court in Coconino County. For federal claims, the United
States District Court for the District of Arizona is the available federal
forum, and the controlling venue for any particular matter is identified and
confirmed during the conflict check and intake. Reports are prepared to the
same disclosure standard regardless of forum, so that an opinion drafted for
a state court case does not need rebuilding if the matter is removed.
Counsel weighing venue questions elsewhere in the state can start from the
firm's [Arizona page](/locations/arizona/), which maps the statewide
landscape.

## Reading the labor market from Page

Vocational opinions for a Page evaluee are grounded in the labor market that
evaluee actually faces, not in an abstraction borrowed from a larger city.
The analytical base is the Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational
employment and wage estimates for the Flagstaff metropolitan area and for
Arizona statewide, supplemented by state workforce agency labor market
information and by direct labor market research where the referral questions
warrant it. The firm's [Flagstaff metro page](/locations/arizona/flagstaff/)
describes the regional market in fuller detail; the town-level task is
narrower and more demanding, which is to test transferable skills against
openings a Page resident could realistically pursue and to document every
step of that test.

## Evaluation logistics for a Page evaluee

Most engagements proceed through remote interview and records-based
analysis, which keeps costs proportionate and timelines short. When the
referral questions call for in-person evaluation, the evaluation is
conducted in the evaluee's own community, scheduled with travel from the
firm's Kansas City home office. The firm keeps no office, storefront, or
street address in Page and does not pretend otherwise; accuracy about
presence is part of the same discipline that governs the reports themselves.
Deposition and trial testimony are supported through
[testimony and litigation consulting](/services/expert-testimony-litigation-consulting/),
and where a full damages picture is needed, counsel can structure
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Starting a Page engagement

Every engagement begins the same way, whether the caption is local or one of
the matters the firm handles [nationwide](/locations/nationwide/): a
documented conflict check, a defined scope, and a records request tailored
to the claims. Submit the caption or party names through the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/), note any imminent disclosure,
deposition, or trial dates, and identify where the evaluee lives so that
evaluation logistics can be scoped from the outset.
