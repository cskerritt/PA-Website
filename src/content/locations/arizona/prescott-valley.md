Litigation arising anywhere in the Prescott Valley-Prescott metropolitan
area, which lies within Yavapai County, reaches Purinton Analytics through
the same door. The firm provides
[vocational expert evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
to plaintiff and defense counsel across the metro, with independent
economist partners performing and signing the economic analysis while the
vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement structure.
Because the practice is remote-first by design, counsel in Prescott Valley
receive the same report standard and the same turnaround discipline as
counsel in the largest markets the firm serves.

## Where Yavapai County matters are heard

Engagements are accepted for matters venued in the Superior Court in
Yavapai County and in the United States District Court for the District of
Arizona. The firm's written opinions are prepared for either side of the
caption and for any forum, from state court bench trials to federal jury
trials to arbitration. Counsel handling matters elsewhere in the state can
consult the firm's [Arizona overview](/locations/arizona/) for how
engagements outside the metro are structured.

## By-appointment service, stated plainly

Purinton Analytics keeps no staffed office in Prescott Valley or Prescott
and publishes no street address for this location. Saying so plainly is part
of the same discipline that governs the reports themselves. Meetings are by
appointment, and the bulk of most engagements proceeds remotely: records
review, structured vocational interviews, and standardized testing under
publisher protocols where remote administration is supported. When the
referral questions require an in-person evaluation, or when deposition and
trial dates require presence, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home
office and planned around the litigation calendar.

## Labor market evidence for a mid-sized metro

Vocational findings for evaluees in the metro rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Prescott
Valley-Prescott metropolitan area and for Arizona statewide, alongside state
workforce agency labor market information and direct employer contact where
warranted. In a market of this size, careful sourcing matters: the report
documents which estimates describe the local area and which describe the
state, so that opposing counsel can trace every figure to its source. Life
care plans and [medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/)
are priced to the evaluee's own market rather than to a national composite.

## Qualification and intake

Jason Purinton holds certifications issued by national credentialing bodies
rather than by any single state. Whether an expert may testify in an Arizona
matter is decided by the forum under its own rules of evidence, and any
venue-specific registration or qualification requirement is identified and
confirmed during the conflict check and intake process before substantive
work begins.
