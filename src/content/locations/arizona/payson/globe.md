## Globe in the Payson Area Labor Market

For earning capacity questions, Globe is treated as its own labor market within the broader [Payson, Arizona area](/locations/arizona/payson/), not as an interchangeable suburb of a large city. Vocational findings for a Globe evaluee rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the surrounding area and for Arizona statewide, on state workforce agency labor market information, and on direct labor market research where the published series are too coarse to describe a smaller community honestly. The distinction matters in practice: transferable skills that command steady demand in a major metropolitan market may translate differently in a smaller regional market, and the written opinion says so plainly rather than importing big-city assumptions into a Gila County case.
