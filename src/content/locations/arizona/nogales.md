litigation should read the same whether the retaining party sits at the
plaintiff table or the defense table. Purinton Analytics writes to that
standard for counsel on either side of the caption, offering
[vocational expert witness work](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),

[medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/) for cases
arising in the Nogales micropolitan area, alongside
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)

## Courts served in Santa Cruz County and beyond

Engagements are accepted for matters in the Superior Court in Santa Cruz
County and in the United States District Court for the District of Arizona.
The firm also takes [rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/)
assignments in which the task is not an original opinion but a disciplined
examination of someone else's: whether the methods are the ones the
profession actually uses, whether the data supports the conclusions, and
whether the report says what the underlying records say. Counsel handling
matters elsewhere in the state will find the full
[Arizona practice](/locations/arizona/) described on its own page, with the
same intake path and the same report standard.

## An honest account of presence

Nogales meetings are by appointment. The firm keeps no staffed office in
Nogales and publishes no street address for this location, a matter of
policy rather than oversight: a practice that asks courts to trust its
written opinions should describe its own footprint with the same precision.
What counsel get instead of a storefront is a remote-first workflow that
handles most of an engagement, records review, structured interviews, and
standardized testing under publisher protocols where remote administration
is supported, with in-person evaluation and courtroom appearances scheduled
as travel from the Kansas City home office when the referral questions or
the trial calendar require presence.

## Labor market and cost research

Vocational findings for Nogales evaluees are grounded in Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the area and for
Arizona statewide, together with state workforce agency labor market
information and direct labor market research where warranted. The analysis
is explicit about sources and about the limits of published data in a
smaller labor market, because an opinion that will be cross-examined should
carry its own citations. Life care plans and medical cost projections for
Santa Cruz County residents are priced to the evaluee's own market through
provider-specific inquiries and published fee data, rather than assuming
that national averages describe local care costs.

## Requesting a conflict check

Every Nogales engagement opens with a conflict check, before availability or
scope is discussed. Submit the caption or party names
through the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and note any imminent
disclosure, deposition, or trial dates so scheduling can be addressed
honestly from the outset. Any venue-specific registration or qualification
requirement is identified and confirmed during the conflict check and intake
process. Counsel weighing whether the firm fits a case can also review the
scope of its [nationwide practice](/locations/nationwide/), which applies
the same methods and the same disclosure discipline whether the matter is
venued in Nogales or across the country.
