## Measuring the market, not assuming it

For a Taylor evaluee, the vocational question is concrete: what work remains open given this person's restrictions, education, and transferable skills, in the market this person can actually reach. Answering it starts with Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for Arizona and for the area that takes in Navajo County, continues through state workforce agency labor market information, and extends to direct labor market research when the referral questions justify the added step. Findings are expressed qualitatively and tied to their sources. An opinion that survives in a small-market case is one in which every step from data to conclusion can be walked backward by opposing counsel and arrive at the same place.
