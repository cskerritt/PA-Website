## Navajo County venue and the scheduling order

Snowflake lies in Navajo County, where state-court civil actions are heard in the Superior Court. Matters with a federal hook may proceed instead in the United States District Court for the District of Arizona, and the controlling venue, along with the disclosure obligations that come with it, is confirmed during the conflict check and intake. Whatever the forum, the engagement letter fixes the scope in writing before analysis begins, and the records request is tailored to the claims so the resulting opinion rests on a record either party can examine. ## Reading the labor market around Snowflake

A Snowflake earning-capacity opinion is only as strong as its labor market foundation. Vocational findings are grounded in Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for Arizona and for the area covering Navajo County, in state workforce agency labor market information, and in direct labor market research where the referral questions warrant it. Conclusions are stated qualitatively and sourced, because a figure that cannot be traced will not survive cross-examination, and a rural labor market described with big-city assumptions will not survive it either. Where the question is medical rather than vocational, care needs and their costs are documented with the same sourcing discipline.
