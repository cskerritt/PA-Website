## Winslow's place in the regional labor market

An earning-capacity opinion for a Winslow evaluee cannot borrow its assumptions from the state's large metropolitan areas. Vocational findings here rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for Arizona and for the area that includes Navajo County, on state workforce agency labor market information, and on direct labor market research where the record warrants it. The analysis addresses the occupations realistically open to a person with the evaluee's restrictions, transferable skills, and commuting realities, and it says so in sourced, qualitative terms that opposing counsel can trace back to the underlying data rather than in assertions that stand on the expert's say-so.
