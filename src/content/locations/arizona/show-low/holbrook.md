## The labor market behind a Holbrook opinion

Holbrook is a small community, and pretending otherwise is the fastest way for a vocational opinion to fail. Employability and earning-capacity findings draw on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for Arizona and for the area encompassing Navajo County, supplemented by state workforce agency labor market information and by direct labor market research when the referral questions demand it. The opinion describes, in qualitative and sourced terms, what work exists within reach of the evaluee, what the evaluee's restrictions and transferable skills permit, and how the local picture differs from the statewide one, so that neither side has to guess where a conclusion came from.

## Appointments, evaluations, and travel

There is no Purinton Analytics office in Holbrook, and the firm publishes no street address for this location; stating that plainly is part of the same accuracy that the reports themselves are held to. The approach mirrors the firm's [national practice](/locations/nationwide/): the same methodology in every venue, with logistics adapted to the place rather than the other way around.
