## Labor market evidence built for a small town

Published wage and employment estimates describe areas, not towns, and an
honest report says so. Vocational findings for a Chino Valley evaluee draw
on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates
for the Prescott Valley-Prescott metropolitan area and for Arizona
statewide, together with state workforce agency labor market information
and, where the questions warrant, direct contact with employers. Rather
than pretending town-level precision exists where it does not, the report
documents which sources describe the metro and which describe the state, a
transparency that holds up when the other side's expert goes looking for
gaps.
