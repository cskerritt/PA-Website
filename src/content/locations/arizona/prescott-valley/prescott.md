Some Prescott referrals arrive before any expert has been disclosed; others
arrive with the opposing expert's report already on the desk. Purinton
Analytics is built to serve Yavapai County counsel in both postures. The
firm prepares
[vocational expert evaluations](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) and
[life care plans](/services/life-care-planning/) designed to be read line by
line by the other side, and it performs
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) of opposing
experts' opinions under the same methodological standards it applies to its
own work. Plaintiff and defense counsel retain the firm on equal terms,
because a method that only holds up for one side is not a method.

## Venue for Prescott matters

State-court matters arising in Prescott are heard in the Superior Court in
Yavapai County, and the firm prepares its reports to that court's disclosure
and testimony expectations. For claims that belong in federal court, the
United States District Court for the District of Arizona is the available
federal forum, and the controlling venue for any particular case is
confirmed during the conflict check and intake. Counsel with matters
elsewhere in the region can consult the
[Prescott Valley metro page](/locations/arizona/prescott-valley/) or the
firm's [Arizona overview](/locations/arizona/) for how those engagements are
structured.

## Reading the labor market from Prescott

Prescott is among the larger communities in the Prescott Valley-Prescott
metropolitan area, and residents work both in town and across the wider
metro. Vocational findings for a Prescott evaluee therefore rest on Bureau
of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the
metropolitan area and for Arizona statewide, supplemented by state workforce
agency labor market information and direct employer contact where the
referral questions warrant it. The report states which estimates describe
the local area and which describe the state, so that every figure can be
traced to a published source under cross-examination.

## How a Prescott evaluation proceeds

Most of the work in a typical engagement is remote by design: records
review, structured vocational interviews by video, and standardized testing
under publisher protocols where remote administration is supported. When the
referral questions call for an in-person evaluation, the firm evaluates the
evaluee in his or her own community rather than requiring travel to a
distant office, and when deposition or trial dates require presence in
Yavapai County, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office around
the litigation calendar. The firm keeps no staffed office in Prescott and
publishes no street address here; stating that plainly is part of the same
discipline that governs the reports themselves.

## Qualification, stated without shortcuts

Jason Purinton holds certifications issued by national credentialing bodies
rather than by any single state. Forensic economic analyses are always
performed and signed by independent economist partners coordinated by the
firm, typically within
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under the same
engagement structure. Whether an expert may testify in a given Arizona
matter is decided by the forum under its own rules, and any venue-specific
registration or qualification requirement is identified and confirmed during
intake before substantive work begins.
