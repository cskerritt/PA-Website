Every Cottonwood engagement at Purinton Analytics begins with a written
scope: the specific questions the referral asks, the records needed to
answer them, and a documented conflict check completed before any
substantive work starts. Framing the questions first keeps the file clean,
whether counsel needs a
[vocational evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) of earning
capacity, a [life care plan](/services/life-care-planning/) for future care
needs, or [rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) of an
opposing expert's report. Referrals are accepted from either side of the
caption, and the deliverable answers the questions counsel actually asked
rather than the ones an expert might prefer to answer.

## The county and its courts

State-court matters arising in Cottonwood proceed in the Superior Court in
Yavapai County, and each report is prepared to the disclosure and testimony
expectations of the court that will receive it. Federal claims proceed,
when they proceed at all, in the United States District Court for the
District of Arizona, and which court controls a given case is pinned down
during the conflict check and intake rather than assumed. Counsel comparing
venues across the region can start with the
[Prescott Valley-Prescott metro overview](/locations/arizona/prescott-valley/).

## A small town measured honestly

Cottonwood is a small town within the Prescott Valley-Prescott
metropolitan area, and the firm's labor market analysis treats it that way
rather than borrowing big-city assumptions. Findings rest on Bureau of
Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the
metropolitan area and for Arizona statewide, on state workforce agency
labor market information, and on direct employer inquiry when a placement
question turns on it. Each report identifies the geographic level of every
estimate it relies on, because an opinion that blurs metro data into
town-level claims will not survive a careful cross-examination, and should
not.

## Scheduling the evaluation itself

There is no Purinton Analytics office in Cottonwood, staffed or otherwise,
and the firm publishes no local address; it says so plainly because
accuracy about presence belongs to the same discipline as accuracy in the
report. The working sequence runs remote-first: records review, structured
vocational interviewing by video, and standardized testing under publisher
protocols where remote administration is supported. If the referral calls
for an in-person evaluation, the appointment is set in the evaluee's own
community, and any appearance in Yavapai County for deposition or trial is
handled as scheduled travel from the Kansas City home office.

## When the damages picture needs an economist

Where lost earnings must be reduced to present value, counsel can convert
the referral into a
[coordinated damages engagement](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/):
independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis
while the vocational and care-planning work continues under the same
engagement structure. Jason Purinton's certifications come from national
credentialing bodies rather than any single state, and any forum-specific
registration or qualification requirement is identified and confirmed
during intake, before substantive work begins.

the [Arizona overview](/locations/arizona/) and, for matters in other
