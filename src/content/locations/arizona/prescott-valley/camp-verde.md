An injured worker or catastrophically injured plaintiff living in Camp
Verde should not have to organize his or her recovery around an expert's
travel preferences. Purinton Analytics designs the evaluation around the
evaluee: the interview, testing, and functional history are gathered where
the person actually lives and works, and the resulting
[vocational opinion](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) or
[life care plan](/services/life-care-planning/) reflects that person's own
community and its services. Referrals are welcome from plaintiff counsel
and defense counsel alike, and the report reads the same either way.

## How a Camp Verde evaluation is staged

Most phases of an engagement proceed remotely: medical and employment
records are reviewed, structured vocational interviews are conducted by
video, and standardized instruments are administered under publisher
protocols wherever remote administration is supported. When the referral
questions require presence, the evaluation is scheduled in the evaluee's
own community in Camp Verde rather than in a distant office, with travel
arranged from the Kansas City home office. The firm keeps no staffed
location in Camp Verde and publishes no street address here; that candor
is deliberate, because a report is only as credible as the practice that
produced it.

## County, courthouse, and federal forum

Camp Verde lies within Yavapai County, and state-court matters from the
town are heard in the Superior Court there. Federal claims have the United
States District Court for the District of Arizona as their available
forum, and the venue that actually controls a given dispute is confirmed
during the conflict check and intake. Reports and disclosures are prepared
to the receiving court's standards, and the
[metro page for Prescott Valley-Prescott](/locations/arizona/prescott-valley/)
explains how the firm serves the surrounding county under the same
engagement model.

## Earning capacity in a small labor market

Camp Verde is a small town, and honest earning-capacity analysis for its
residents accounts for the wider metropolitan labor market a resident can
realistically reach. The firm grounds its findings in Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Prescott
Valley-Prescott metropolitan area and for Arizona statewide, alongside
state workforce agency labor market information and targeted employer
contact where warranted. Life care plans and
[medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/) are priced
to the evaluee's own market, not to a national composite, and the report
discloses the geographic scope of every source so both sides can test it.

## The economist question, answered cleanly

When counsel needs lost earnings carried through to a signed economic
analysis, the firm offers
[coordinated damages assessment](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/),
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic work
while the vocational and care-planning pieces proceed under the same
engagement. Jason Purinton holds certifications issued by national
credentialing bodies rather than by any single state, and any Arizona
forum's registration or qualification requirement is identified and
confirmed during intake before substantive work begins.

Engagements open with a [conflict check](/refer-a-case/): send the caption
