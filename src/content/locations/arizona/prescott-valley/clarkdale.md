The smaller the town, the more a vocational opinion depends on method
rather than proximity. Clarkdale is a very small community within the
Prescott Valley-Prescott metropolitan area, and no expert can claim a
storefront's worth of local knowledge about a place this size without
overreaching. What Purinton Analytics offers Clarkdale counsel instead is
disciplined method: transferable skills analysis, labor market sourcing
that names its geographic limits, and
[vocational opinions](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) and
[life care plans](/services/life-care-planning/) written to withstand
scrutiny from whichever side of the caption did not retain the firm.

## Transferable skills across the metro

For a Clarkdale evaluee, the analysis asks what work the person can still
perform and where that work exists within reach, which in practice means
the surrounding metropolitan labor market. The firm grounds those findings
in Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates
for the Prescott Valley-Prescott metropolitan area and for Arizona
statewide, in state workforce agency labor market information, and in
direct employer contact when a specific placement question requires it.
Every source is cited with its geographic scope stated, because in a very
small town the gap between metro data and local reality is exactly where
cross-examination will go.

[metro page](/locations/arizona/prescott-valley/); the

## No office here, and no pretense of one

The firm has no staffed office in Clarkdale and lists no address for this
location. That is stated outright because honesty about presence is part
of the same standard that governs every citation in the report. The
practice is remote-first: records review, structured vocational
interviews, and standardized testing under publisher protocols proceed by
video and secure exchange wherever remote administration is supported.
When an in-person evaluation is warranted, it takes place in the evaluee's
own community, and courtroom or deposition appearances in Yavapai County
are planned as travel from the Kansas City home office.
