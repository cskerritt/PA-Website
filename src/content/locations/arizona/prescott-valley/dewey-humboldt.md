## The forum for a Dewey-Humboldt dispute

Dewey-Humboldt is part of Yavapai County, so state-court litigation from
the town is heard in the Superior Court there. Claims arising under
federal law have the United States District Court for the District of
Arizona as their available forum, and which court controls a particular
case is established during the conflict check and intake. Disclosures,
report format, and testimony preparation follow the receiving court's
expectations; the
[Prescott Valley-Prescott metro page](/locations/arizona/prescott-valley/)
sets out how the firm serves the county as a whole.

## A very small town inside a working metro

Dewey-Humboldt is a very small town, and its residents participate in the
labor market of the broader Prescott Valley-Prescott metropolitan area.
Vocational findings therefore rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates for that metropolitan area and
for Arizona statewide, corroborated by state workforce agency labor market
information and by direct employer inquiry when a referral question turns
on actual hiring practice. The report never presents metro-level data as
town-level fact; it labels the geography of every estimate so the
foundation survives examination by the side that did not commission it.
