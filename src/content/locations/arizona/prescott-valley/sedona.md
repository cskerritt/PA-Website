## What the labor market data can and cannot say

A defensible opinion about a Sedona evaluee begins by admitting what
published data measures. Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational
employment and wage estimates describe the Prescott Valley-Prescott
metropolitan area and Arizona statewide, not any single small town, so the
firm pairs those estimates with state workforce agency labor market
information and, when the referral questions justify it, direct employer
contact. The report then labels each source by its geographic scope. That
restraint is not caution for its own sake; it is what allows the opinion
to stand unshaken when opposing counsel probes the foundation.

## Appointments, not an office

Purinton Analytics maintains no staffed office in Sedona and publishes no
street address for this location, and it says so on the page rather than
implying otherwise. Engagements run remotely wherever the work permits:
