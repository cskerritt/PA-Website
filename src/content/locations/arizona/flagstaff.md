A vocational or life care planning report earns its weight in a Flagstaff
courtroom the same way it does anywhere: through method that can be traced
and sources that can be checked. That is the product Purinton Analytics
delivers to plaintiff and defense counsel across the Flagstaff metropolitan
area, in the form of
[earning capacity and employability evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. Each report states its methodology, cites its data,
and separates observation from opinion, because the reader the firm writes
for is opposing counsel.

## Coconino County venues

The firm accepts engagements for matters venued in the Superior Court in
Coconino County and in the United States District Court for the District of
Arizona. Personal injury, employment, and family law matters all pass
through the same intake: a documented conflict check, a defined scope, and a
records request tailored to the claims. Counsel with matters elsewhere in

[testimony and litigation consulting](/services/expert-testimony-litigation-consulting/)

## An honest account of presence

Purinton Analytics keeps no staffed office in Flagstaff and publishes no
street address for this location. Meetings are by appointment. The firm
treats accuracy about offices as part of the same standard that governs its
reports, so the arrangement is stated rather than obscured. In practice most
engagements are remote from start to finish: records review, structured
interviews, and standardized testing under publisher protocols where remote
administration is supported. Where the referral questions call for in-person
evaluation, or where deposition and trial dates require attendance, travel
is scheduled from the Kansas City home office.

## Labor market data behind the findings

Vocational conclusions for Flagstaff-area evaluees rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Flagstaff
metropolitan area and for Arizona statewide, along with state workforce
agency labor market information and direct labor market research where
warranted. The report identifies which estimates describe the local market
and which describe the state as a whole, a distinction that matters when
employability is contested. Life care plans and
[medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/) are priced to
the evaluee's own market through provider-specific inquiries and published
fee data rather than national composites.

## Qualification for Arizona matters

Jason Purinton's certifications are issued by national credentialing bodies
rather than by any single state, and whether an expert may testify in a
given Arizona matter is a question for the forum under its own rules of
evidence. Any venue-specific registration or qualification requirement is
identified and confirmed during the conflict check and intake process.
