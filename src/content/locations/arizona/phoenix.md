The Phoenix-Mesa-Chandler metropolitan area is one of the largest labor
markets in the country, and the litigation it produces spans nearly every
setting in which earning capacity and future care become contested questions.
Purinton Analytics serves plaintiff and defense counsel throughout the metro
with [vocational expert evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. Every matter begins the same way regardless of caption
side: a documented conflict check, a defined scope, and a records request
tailored to the claims at issue.

## Venue footprint across the metro

Engagements are accepted for matters venued in the Superior Court in
Maricopa County and in Pinal County, and in the United States District Court
for the District of Arizona. The same engagement structure supports personal
injury, employment, and family law matters wherever they are filed, and
counsel with cases elsewhere in the state can review the firm's broader
[Arizona coverage](/locations/arizona/) for how work outside the metro is
handled. Reports are written to a single standard so that the analysis holds
up whether it is examined in state court, federal court, or arbitration.

## Meetings by appointment, work without borders

Phoenix meetings are by appointment. The firm maintains no staffed office in
the metro and publishes no street address for this location, because
accuracy about presence is part of the same discipline that governs its
written opinions. Most engagements proceed remotely from the outset: records
review, structured vocational interviews, and standardized testing under
publisher protocols where remote administration is supported. When the
referral questions call for in-person evaluation, or when deposition and
trial calendars require presence, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City
home office and coordinated around counsel's deadlines rather than around
office hours.

## Labor market research for Phoenix evaluees

Vocational findings for evaluees in the metro rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the
Phoenix-Mesa-Chandler metropolitan area and for Arizona statewide,
supplemented by state workforce agency labor market information and direct
employer research where the referral questions warrant it. A market this
large supports meaningful analysis of transferable skills and placement
prospects across a wide range of occupations, and the report documents the
sources relied upon so that either side of the caption can trace every
finding. [Medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/) and
life care plans are priced to the evaluee's own market rather than to
national averages.

## Qualification and disclosure

Jason Purinton's certifications are issued by national credentialing bodies
rather than by any single state, and qualification to testify in an Arizona
matter is determined by the forum under its own rules of evidence. Any
venue-specific registration or qualification requirement is identified and
confirmed during the conflict check and intake process, before substantive
work begins.

## Starting a Phoenix engagement

To explore availability for a matter in Maricopa County or Pinal County,
submit the caption or party names through the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and note any imminent disclosure,
deposition, or trial dates. Counsel with matters beyond Arizona can review
the firm's [national coverage](/locations/nationwide/) for how multi-state
dockets are supported from the Kansas City home office.
