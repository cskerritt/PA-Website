Counsel in Tucson referring a vocational or life care planning question
usually arrive with a deadline already on the calendar: a disclosure date, a
deposition, a mediation that needs a defensible damages picture. Purinton
Analytics is built for that posture. The firm accepts plaintiff and defense
engagements throughout the Tucson metropolitan area for
[earning capacity and employability evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. Intake starts with a documented conflict check and a
scope defined in writing, so the work product can withstand examination by
whichever side did not retain it.

## Courts serving Pima County matters

The firm accepts engagements for matters venued in the Superior Court in
Pima County and in the United States District Court for the District of
Arizona. Personal injury, employment, and family law matters all move through
the same engagement structure, and the written opinion is prepared to a
single standard whatever the forum. Counsel whose dockets reach beyond the
metro will find the firm's statewide approach described on the
[Arizona page](/locations/arizona/), which covers how matters outside Tucson
are staffed and scheduled.

## Presence in Tucson, honestly described

There is no staffed Purinton Analytics office in Tucson, and the firm
publishes no street address for this location. Meetings happen by
appointment, and most engagements never require one: records review,
structured interviews, and standardized testing under publisher protocols
proceed remotely where remote administration is supported. When an in-person
evaluation is warranted, or when deposition and trial dates require
attendance, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office. Counsel
retain a single point of contact throughout, and the report reads the same
whether the underlying work was performed remotely or in the evaluee's own
community.

## Grounding findings in the Tucson labor market

Vocational conclusions for Tucson-area evaluees are grounded in Bureau of
Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Tucson
metropolitan area and for Arizona statewide, together with state workforce
agency labor market information and direct labor market research where the
referral questions call for it. Life care plans and
[medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/) are priced to
the evaluee's own market using provider-specific inquiries and published fee
data, because the cost of future care is a local question, not a national
average.

## Qualification for Arizona forums

Certifications held by Jason Purinton are issued by national credentialing
bodies rather than by any single state, and admissibility in an Arizona
matter is a question for the forum under its own rules of evidence. Any
venue-specific registration or qualification requirement is identified and
confirmed during the conflict check and intake process, before the
substantive work begins.
