San Luis is among the larger communities in the Yuma metropolitan area, and
its workers move through the same regional labor market that Purinton
Analytics studies whenever a Yuma County case requires an opinion on earning
capacity. Counsel litigating an injury, employment, or disability matter
involving a San Luis plaintiff or defendant can retain the firm for
[vocational expert evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/), with every
engagement built on a documented conflict check, a written scope, and a
records request tailored to the claims. The resulting report is drafted so
that either side of the caption can trace each conclusion to its source.

## County and courthouse context for San Luis

Civil matters arising in San Luis are venued in the Superior Court in Yuma
County, and the United States District Court for the District of Arizona is
available as a federal forum; the controlling venue for a given case is
identified during the conflict check and intake. The firm's written opinions
are prepared to the same standard in either setting, and
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) work is
available where counsel already hold an opposing expert's report and need
its methodology examined. How the broader region is served is described on
the [Yuma metro page](/locations/arizona/yuma/), and matters filed elsewhere
in the state are covered on the [Arizona page](/locations/arizona/).

## San Luis in the regional labor market

Because San Luis workers participate in a labor market that extends across
Yuma County toward the principal city of Yuma, a residual earning capacity
opinion for a San Luis evaluee cannot stop at the town line. Vocational
findings rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage
estimates for the Yuma metropolitan area and for Arizona statewide, on state
workforce agency labor market information, and on direct labor market
research where the referral questions warrant it. Transferable skills
analysis considers the occupations actually available to the evaluee in this
market, not a hypothetical national average, and any venue-specific
qualification requirement is identified and confirmed during intake.

## Evaluation logistics for a San Luis evaluee

Meetings are by appointment. The firm keeps no staffed office in San Luis
and publishes no street address here, because accuracy about presence is
part of the same discipline that governs its reports. Interviews and
testing can proceed remotely where the referral questions permit, and when
in-person evaluation is warranted the assessment is scheduled in the
evaluee's own community, with travel arranged from the Kansas City home
office. Deposition and trial appearances are planned the same way, and the
firm's national practice, described on the
[nationwide page](/locations/nationwide/), applies an identical protocol
whether the caption sits in Arizona or elsewhere. Where a damages figure is
also required, counsel can add
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds within the
same engagement structure.
