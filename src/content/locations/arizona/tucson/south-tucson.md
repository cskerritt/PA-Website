South Tucson is a small community within the Tucson metropolitan area, and
the size of the town has no bearing on the rigor its cases deserve. A damages
opinion for a South Tucson evaluee is not a scaled-down exercise: the records
request is as complete, the methodology as documented, and the report as
ready for hostile reading as it would be in any major city the firm serves.
Purinton Analytics takes referrals from counsel on both sides of South Tucson
matters for
[earning capacity and employability evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) of opposing
expert work. The surrounding metro practice is described on the
[Tucson page](/locations/arizona/tucson/).

## Where South Tucson cases are heard

South Tucson lies within Pima County, so its state-court civil matters
ordinarily proceed in the Superior Court in Pima County. Federal claims have
the United States District Court for the District of Arizona available as a
forum, and the venue that actually controls a given case is settled during
the conflict check and intake, along with any registration or qualification
requirement the forum imposes. Whatever the courtroom, the opinion is
structured the same way: questions defined in writing, sources cited so they
can be checked, and conclusions that do not outrun the record. Counsel
handling matters across the state can review the broader practice on the
[Arizona page](/locations/arizona/).

## A small town inside a large labor market

Although South Tucson itself is compact, its residents work within the
economy of the wider metro, and vocational conclusions are drawn against
that larger market. The firm relies on Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates covering the Tucson metropolitan
area and Arizona as a whole, on state workforce agency labor market
information, and on direct labor market research when the referral questions
require it. The analysis asks what work exists across the metro, what it
demands physically and cognitively, and whether the evaluee's education,
history, and residual capacity open or foreclose that work, with every
answer tied to a source the other side can pull and verify.

## Reaching a South Tucson evaluee

Accessibility matters in every evaluation, and the firm's model is built so
that the evaluee's circumstances never dictate the quality of the
assessment. Purinton Analytics has no office in South Tucson, only a home
office in Kansas City, and it says so plainly rather than implying a local
storefront. Remote interviews and records-based analysis carry most
engagements from intake to signed report; when an in-person evaluation is
warranted, the firm comes to the evaluee's community, with travel scheduled
from the Kansas City home office around the case deadlines. Forensic
economic analyses, when the damages picture requires them, are performed and
signed by independent economist partners coordinated by the firm. The
national reach behind this model appears on the
[nationwide page](/locations/nationwide/).

## Beginning with a conflict check

Every South Tucson engagement starts the same way: a documented conflict
check before any substantive discussion of the merits. Provide the caption
or party names through the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/), describe
the questions the case presents, and identify any disclosure or trial dates
already set. The firm confirms scope and fees in writing, and the work
begins on a record both sides will eventually examine.
