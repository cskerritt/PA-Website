## Sahuarita's county and courthouse context

Sahuarita is a Pima County community, and its state-court matters are
ordinarily venued in the Superior Court in Pima County. The United States
District Court for the District of Arizona stands as the federal forum
available to parties in the state, and the controlling venue for a specific
case, together with any qualification requirement particular to it, is
identified during the conflict check and intake. Disclosure and
expert-designation deadlines tend to arrive faster than expected in any
forum, so early retention protects the record: it leaves room for a complete
records request, a properly scheduled evaluation, and a report that is not
written under avoidable time pressure. Statewide coverage beyond the Tucson

## A commuter town measured against the metro market

Sahuarita's workforce is tied to the larger Tucson economy, and an honest
vocational opinion measures the evaluee against that full market. The firm
works from the Bureau of Labor Statistics OEWS program's estimates for the
Tucson metropolitan area and the state of Arizona, from state workforce
agency labor market information, and from targeted employer research when the
questions justify it. No wage or employment figure appears in a report
without a named, dated source, and labor market scale is characterized in
qualitative terms that the underlying data will support under
cross-examination.

The firm maintains no office in Sahuarita and claims none; its single home
office is in Kansas City, and all local presence is by appointment. In most
engagements the record work, the structured interview, and the report are

opinion in proportion to the dispute. Where the referral requires an
in-person assessment, or where a deposition or trial appearance is set, the

the community where the evaluee actually lives. The same operating model,
