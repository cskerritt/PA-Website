A vocational question that begins in Marana rarely stays inside the town
limits. Marana is one of the larger suburban communities in the Tucson
metropolitan area, and the working lives of its residents, the jobs they held
before an injury and the jobs realistically open to them afterward, stretch
across the whole metro commuting market. Purinton Analytics evaluates that
market rather than the town in isolation. The firm accepts plaintiff and
defense engagements from Marana counsel for
[earning capacity and employability evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. The firm's approach to the metro as a whole is
described on the [Tucson page](/locations/arizona/tucson/).

## Venue for Marana matters

Marana sits in Pima County, and civil matters arising there are typically
heard in the Superior Court in Pima County. On the federal side, the United
States District Court for the District of Arizona is the forum available to
Arizona litigants, and the controlling venue for any particular case is
identified during the conflict check and intake. The written opinion is
prepared to the same standard for either bench: a defined scope, a documented
methodology, and conclusions built to hold when the side that did not retain
the firm begins cross-examination. Counsel whose dockets reach beyond the
metro will find statewide coverage described on the
[Arizona page](/locations/arizona/).

## Marana in the Tucson labor market

Vocational findings for a Marana evaluee rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates for the Tucson metropolitan area
and for Arizona statewide, supplemented by state workforce agency labor
market information and direct employer research where the referral questions
warrant it. Because Marana workers participate in the broader metro economy,
transferable skills and placeability are assessed against the occupations
actually hiring across that market, not against an artificially narrow local
radius. Every source that reaches the report is identified, dated, and
reproducible, so opposing counsel can trace each input back to its origin and
the opinion does not depend on anything the record cannot show.

## Evaluation logistics for Marana evaluees

There is no staffed Purinton Analytics office in Marana or anywhere else in
the metro; the firm's home office is in Kansas City, and every meeting
elsewhere is by appointment. Most engagements proceed remotely from records
review through report delivery, with structured interviews conducted by
video, which keeps costs proportionate to the questions asked. When the
referral calls for in-person evaluation, or when deposition and trial dates
require presence, the assessment is scheduled in the evaluee's own community
and travel is arranged from the Kansas City home office. Counsel who practice
across state lines can see how the same model operates nationally on the
[nationwide page](/locations/nationwide/).

## Starting a Marana engagement

Engagements open with a documented conflict check, not a sales call. Submit
the caption or party names through the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/), note any disclosure, deposition, or
trial dates already set, and describe the vocational or care-planning
questions in play. The firm responds with a written scope and fee structure
before substantive work begins, so both the retaining party and the record
know exactly what was asked and what will be answered.
