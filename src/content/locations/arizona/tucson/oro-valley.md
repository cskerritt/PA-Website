## Pima County courts and the federal forum

Oro Valley lies in Pima County, and its civil disputes generally move through
the Superior Court in Pima County. The United States District Court for the
District of Arizona is the federal forum available to litigants in the state;
which court controls a given matter, along with any venue-specific
registration or qualification requirement, is confirmed during the conflict
check and intake. Reports are drafted from the first page for examination by
opposing counsel, whichever party retained the firm and whichever bench will
hear the case. How the practice operates across the rest of the state is set
out on the [Arizona page](/locations/arizona/).

## The labor market an Oro Valley evaluee actually faces

An earning capacity opinion for an Oro Valley resident is grounded in the
Tucson metropolitan labor market as a whole, because that is the market in
which the evaluee will or will not find work. The analysis draws on
occupational employment and wage estimates published by the Bureau of Labor
Statistics for the Tucson metropolitan area, alongside Arizona statewide
data, state workforce agency labor market information, and, where the
referral questions call for it, direct contact with employers. Scale is
described qualitatively and sourced precisely: which occupations exist in the
market, what they demand, and whether the evaluee's residual capacities and
transferable skills give genuine access to them.

## Requesting a conflict check

The first step in any Oro Valley matter is a documented conflict check. Send
the caption or the party names through the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/), together with the questions to be
answered and any deadlines already fixed on the calendar. Scope and fees are
confirmed in writing before analysis begins, and the engagement proceeds on
the schedule the court has set rather than the one that is merely convenient.
