## Where Bisbee cases are heard

Bisbee is a Cochise County community, and state-court litigation arising
there proceeds in the Superior Court. The United States District Court for
the District of Arizona is the federal forum for the state, and the
controlling venue for any specific matter is confirmed during the conflict
check and intake rather than inferred from a map. Forum choice changes
deadlines and disclosure obligations, not the firm's method: a documented
conflict check, a written scope, and a records request built around the
actual claims come before any opinion work, in every court.
