alongside the principal city, and the litigation that arises there raises
the same expert questions found in far larger venues: what work an injured
or displaced person can still perform, what that work actually pays in the
place where the person lives, and what future care will cost. Purinton
Analytics answers those questions for plaintiff and defense counsel alike,
bringing [vocational expert evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/)
and [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) to Douglas matters

## Venue context for Douglas matters

Douglas sits in Cochise County, and state-court cases arising there are
heard in the Superior Court. Matters filed in federal court proceed in the
United States District Court for the District of Arizona, and the
controlling venue for any particular case is identified during the conflict
check and intake review rather than assumed from geography. Whatever the
forum, the engagement follows the same sequence: a documented conflict
check, a scope defined in writing, and a records request tailored to the
claims, so that the written opinion rests on materials either side of the
caption can examine and test.

## Reading the labor market from Douglas

A vocational opinion about a Douglas evaluee has to describe the employment
actually reachable from Douglas, not a statewide abstraction. Findings rest
on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates
for the Sierra Vista-Douglas metropolitan area and for Arizona as a whole,
supplemented by state workforce agency labor market information and by
direct employer research when the referral questions warrant it. The report
states plainly which geography each data source covers, because
cross-examination in a smaller market so often turns on whether metro-level
figures fairly represent the openings available to a worker who lives at
the edge of that market rather than at its center.

## Evaluation logistics in Douglas

The firm keeps no staffed office in Douglas and publishes no street address
for this location; candor about presence is part of the same discipline
that governs the reports themselves. Records review, structured vocational
interviews, and standardized testing are handled remotely where publisher
protocols support remote administration, which spares most evaluees any
travel at all. When the referral calls for an in-person evaluation, it is
conducted in the evaluee's own community, and when deposition or trial
requires physical presence, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home
office and confirmed against the court's calendar early. Counsel elsewhere
in the state will find the statewide approach on the
[Arizona page](/locations/arizona/).
