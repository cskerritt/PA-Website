## The county and its courts

Benson is a small Cochise County town, and civil matters arising there are
heard in the Superior Court at the state level. Federal filings proceed in
the United States District Court for the District of Arizona; which forum
controls a given dispute is settled during the conflict check and intake,
never presumed. The firm's working method does not change with the
courthouse. Every file opens with a documented conflict check and a written
scope, and every opinion is drafted on the assumption that opposing counsel
will read it line by line, which is exactly how a defensible opinion should
be written in the first place.

## A small town inside a metro market

Benson belongs to the Sierra Vista-Douglas metropolitan area, which means
the labor market evidence for a Benson evaluee starts with Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for that metro and
for Arizona statewide. Metro data alone can flatter or understate a small
town's real options, so the analysis also draws on state workforce agency
labor market information and, where warranted, direct contact with
employers to establish what work is genuinely available within a
reasonable commute of Benson. Each source is cited with its geographic
coverage stated, so the trier of fact can see precisely what market the
numbers describe without taking the expert's word for it.
