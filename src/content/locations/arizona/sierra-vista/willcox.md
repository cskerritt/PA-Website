## Cochise County forums for Willcox litigation

Willcox lies in Cochise County, where the Superior Court hears state-court
civil matters. Cases in federal court are brought in the United States
District Court for the District of Arizona; whether a particular dispute
belongs in state or federal court is a question resolved with counsel
during the conflict check and intake, not assumed in advance. In either
forum the deliverable is the same: a written opinion whose scope was
defined at the outset and whose record was assembled through a documented
request tailored to the pleadings, ready for scrutiny from the other side
of the caption.

## Labor market data for a Willcox evaluee

Willcox belongs to the Sierra Vista-Douglas metropolitan area, and the
published labor market data that covers the town is collected at that
metro level and at the level of Arizona as a whole. The vocational
analysis is candid about that fact. Earning capacity and employability
findings draw on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and
wage estimates for the metro and for Arizona statewide, on state
workforce agency labor market information, and on direct labor market
research when the referral question is what work is genuinely open to
someone living in Willcox rather than what a broader dataset averages
across the area. Every source appears in the report with its geographic
coverage identified, so the trier of fact and opposing counsel can test
the fit between the data and the evaluee instead of taking it on faith.

## Serving a Willcox evaluee by appointment

No staffed office exists in Willcox and no street address is published for
this location; the firm states that plainly because overstating presence
would undercut the credibility the reports depend on. The working model is
remote-first. Document review, vocational interviews, and standardized
testing under remote-capable publisher protocols proceed without travel,
and an in-person evaluation is arranged in the evaluee's community when
the referral requires one. For depositions and trial appearances that must
happen in person, travel is planned from the Kansas City home office and
locked to the court's calendar early enough that scheduling never crowds
the analysis. The broader regional practice is set out on the
[Sierra Vista metro page](/locations/arizona/sierra-vista/) and the
statewide approach on the [Arizona page](/locations/arizona/).
