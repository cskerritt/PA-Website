Retention discipline is the starting point for every Yuma engagement. Before
any substantive work begins on a Yuma County matter, Purinton Analytics runs
a documented conflict check, defines the scope in writing, and issues a
records request tailored to the claims, so that the eventual opinion rests
on a record either side of the caption can examine. On that foundation the
firm provides
[vocational expert evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. The same process serves plaintiff and defense counsel
alike, which is precisely why it is worth describing first.

## Where Yuma matters are venued

Engagements are accepted for matters in the Superior Court in Yuma County
and in the United States District Court for the District of Arizona. The
written opinion is prepared to a single standard whatever the forum, from
state court to federal court to arbitration, and
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) engagements are
available where counsel need an existing opposing report examined rather
than a new evaluation performed. Matters filed elsewhere in the state are
handled as described on the firm's [Arizona page](/locations/arizona/).

## By-appointment presence in Yuma

The firm has no staffed office in Yuma and publishes no street address for
this location. Meetings are by appointment, and stating that plainly is part
of the same discipline the reports themselves are held to. Most engagements
proceed remotely: records review, structured vocational interviews, and
standardized testing under publisher protocols where remote administration
is supported. When the referral questions require in-person evaluation, or
when deposition and trial dates call for presence, travel is scheduled from
the Kansas City home office with enough lead time to protect the litigation
calendar.

## Labor market evidence for Yuma County

Vocational findings for Yuma-area evaluees rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Yuma
metropolitan area and for Arizona statewide, together with state workforce
agency labor market information and direct employer research where the
referral questions warrant it. The report distinguishes local data from
statewide data so that employability and placement conclusions can be tested
against their sources. Life care plans and
[medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/) are priced to
the evaluee's own market using provider-specific inquiries and published fee
data for the region.

## Qualification and intake for Arizona forums

Jason Purinton's certifications are issued by national credentialing bodies
rather than by any single state. Qualification to testify in an Arizona
matter is determined by the forum under its own rules of evidence, and any
venue-specific registration or qualification requirement is identified and
confirmed during the conflict check and intake process, before the work
begins.

## Starting a Yuma engagement

To begin, submit the caption or party names through the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and note any imminent disclosure,
deposition, or trial dates. Counsel whose dockets extend beyond Arizona can
review the firm's [national coverage](/locations/nationwide/) for how the
same engagement model is delivered across state lines from the Kansas City
home office.
