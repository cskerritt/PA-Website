A referral from Safford usually arrives as a question rather than a case
file: what can this person earn now, what work can they still perform, what
will their care cost over a lifetime. Purinton Analytics answers those
questions for plaintiff and defense counsel in the Safford micropolitan
area through [vocational expert evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. The answer takes the form of a written opinion built
on a record both sides can examine, whichever side of the caption sends the
referral.

## Where Safford matters are heard

Engagements are accepted for matters in the Superior Court in Graham County
and in the United States District Court for the District of Arizona. State
and federal venues impose different disclosure schedules and different
expectations about expert reports, and the engagement is scoped to the forum
from the start so that the written opinion, the disclosure, and any
deposition or trial testimony line up with what the court actually
requires. Counsel with matters elsewhere in the state can review the firm's
[Arizona practice](/locations/arizona/) page, which describes the same
services applied statewide.

## How the firm appears in Safford

The firm's only home office is in Kansas City, and it keeps no staffed
office in Safford and publishes no street address for this location.
Meetings are by appointment, and stating that plainly is deliberate: a
practice whose product is accurate written opinions should be equally
accurate about its own presence. Most Safford engagements are conducted
remotely, through records review, structured vocational interviews, and
standardized testing administered under publisher protocols where remote
administration is supported. Where the referral questions call for an
in-person evaluation of the evaluee, or where deposition and trial dates
require attendance, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office
around the demands of the litigation calendar.

## Grounding findings in the Safford labor market

Vocational opinions for Graham County evaluees rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the area and for
Arizona statewide, state workforce agency labor market information, and
direct labor market research where the occupation or the referral questions
warrant it. In a smaller market the published estimates do not cover every
occupation at every level of detail, and the report says so where it
matters, identifying the source and the limits of each data point rather
than presenting borrowed precision. Life care plans and medical cost
projections are priced to the evaluee's own community through
provider-specific inquiries and published fee data, so the projected costs
describe care as it is actually available to the evaluee.

Send the caption or party names through the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/), flag any imminent disclosure,
