For an injured worker or a catastrophically injured person living in
Gilbert, the practical questions in litigation are concrete ones: where the
vocational evaluation will happen, who has to travel, and whether the
report will be defensible when the other side's expert reads it. Purinton
Analytics is built around those questions. The firm provides
[vocational expert work](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) and
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) to plaintiff and
defense counsel alike, serving Gilbert as part of its
[Phoenix metro practice](/locations/arizona/phoenix/), and it designs each
engagement so that the evaluee's logistics never compromise the record.

## The county and its courts

Gilbert lies within Maricopa County, and its civil matters proceed in the
Superior Court there. Federal claims may be filed in the United States
District Court for the District of Arizona, which the firm treats as an
available forum rather than a presumed one: the controlling venue, like any
registration or qualification requirement that attaches to it, is
identified during the conflict check and intake. Whatever the forum, the
deliverable is the same kind of document, a written opinion with a defined
scope, a disclosed methodology, and sources that opposing counsel can pull
and check. Statewide venue context appears on the
[Arizona page](/locations/arizona/).

## A suburb inside a large labor market

Gilbert is a sizable suburban city, but no vocational analysis treats it as
a labor market unto itself. Earning capacity for a Gilbert resident is
measured against the employment opportunities of the surrounding metro,
using Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates
for the Phoenix-Mesa-Chandler metropolitan area and for Arizona statewide,
alongside state workforce agency labor market information. Where a referral
question turns on a specific occupation or a contested placement claim, the
firm adds direct labor market research and documents it, so the opinion
reflects real hiring conditions rather than assumptions.

## How the evaluation reaches the evaluee

The firm keeps no staffed office in Gilbert and will not pretend otherwise;
meetings happen by appointment. In most cases nothing about the evaluation
requires the evaluee to leave home: records are reviewed in advance, the
vocational interview is conducted over secure video, and standardized
instruments are administered under protocols suited to remote use. When the
questions in the case make an in-person evaluation the sounder course, it
is scheduled in Gilbert or wherever the evaluee lives, with the evaluator
traveling from the Kansas City home office. The same travel arrangements
cover depositions and trial testimony. Counsel who need economic damages
quantified can pair the vocational and care-planning work with
[coordinated damages assessment](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/),
under which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis within a single coordinated engagement.
