Most Goodyear referrals arrive as a version of the same question: what can
this person earn now, and what could they have earned but for the injury?
Plaintiff counsel needs the answer to prove loss; defense counsel needs it
to test the loss being claimed. Purinton Analytics answers that question
for either side, through
[vocational evaluation and expert testimony](/services/vocational-expert-witness/)
and, where future care is at issue,
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), serving Goodyear
within its [Phoenix metro practice](/locations/arizona/phoenix/).

## The Goodyear case in its county and courts

Goodyear belongs to Maricopa County, so its state-court civil actions are
tried in the Superior Court for the county, while the United States
District Court for the District of Arizona stands as the available federal
forum. The firm takes no position on controlling venue until the conflict
check and intake, where that question is confirmed with counsel along with
any venue-specific registration or qualification requirement. Intake also
fixes the scope in writing, so the earning capacity question is framed
precisely before any records are read. Forums elsewhere in the state
appear on the [Arizona page](/locations/arizona/).

## Answering the earning capacity question with evidence

The answer for a Goodyear evaluee is built in layers. The work history
establishes what skills exist; the medical record establishes what
restrictions apply; and the labor market evidence establishes what
accessible work pays. For that final layer the firm cites Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the
Phoenix-Mesa-Chandler metropolitan area and for Arizona statewide,
together with state workforce agency labor market information, expressing
its findings qualitatively rather than through invented precision.
Goodyear is a sizable city inside one of the region's broadest commuting
markets, so the accessible-work analysis considers the metro as a whole.
Direct labor market research fills any gap the published estimates leave,
and the research trail stays in the file.

## Logistics that fit the evaluee

The evaluation is arranged around the person being evaluated, not around
an office, because the firm has no office in Goodyear and does not
advertise one. Standard practice is remote: records exchanged
electronically, a structured interview by secure video at a set
appointment, and instruments administered under remote protocols. Cases
that genuinely require an in-person evaluation get one in the evaluee's
own community, with the evaluator traveling from the Kansas City home
office, an arrangement that likewise covers deposition and trial
appearances. When the matter calls for the loss to be carried through to
an economic calculation, the firm structures
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning components proceed under
one engagement structure.
