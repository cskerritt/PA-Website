## Maricopa County venue for Surprise cases

Surprise is a Maricopa County city, and civil litigation arising there is
heard in the county's Superior Court. Claims within federal jurisdiction
have the United States District Court for the District of Arizona as their
available forum. The firm leaves the controlling-venue question where it
belongs, with counsel, and confirms it during the conflict check and
intake together with any registration or qualification requirement the
chosen forum imposes. Whichever court receives the report, its contents
follow the same rule: opinions supported by cited records and data, in a
form the opposing expert can check. The firm's statewide venue coverage is
described on the [Arizona page](/locations/arizona/).

## Earning capacity in a metro-wide market

Vocational conclusions about a Surprise resident are benchmarked against
the employment market of the whole metropolitan region, because that is
the market a real job search would cover. The firm relies on Bureau of
Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the
Phoenix-Mesa-Chandler metropolitan area and for Arizona statewide, plus
state workforce agency labor market information, and it states its
occupational and wage findings qualitatively with the sources shown.
Where a case turns on facts the published series cannot resolve, the firm
runs direct labor market research, documenting the inquiries so that the
resulting testimony has something concrete under it.

## Remote by default, present when it counts

For most Surprise engagements, no travel occurs at all. The evaluee is
interviewed by secure video at a scheduled appointment, standardized
instruments are administered under protocols validated for remote use, and
counsel receives the report electronically. The firm keeps no office in
Surprise and never lists one, which is exactly what it tells juries about
itself if asked. When the referral questions require a face-to-face
evaluation, the firm comes to the evaluee's community rather than
summoning an injured person across the metro, with travel scheduled from
the Kansas City home office; depositions and trial dates are covered the
same way. Cases that need a complete damages package can be structured as
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/),
where independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis and the vocational and care-planning work travels with it under
one engagement structure.
