What retaining counsel in Scottsdale actually receives from Purinton
Analytics is a document: a written vocational or life care planning
opinion built to survive the scrutiny of a capable opposing expert. The
firm's Scottsdale work, part of its
[Phoenix metro practice](/locations/arizona/phoenix/), is available to
plaintiff and defense counsel in equal measure and spans
[vocational evaluation and testimony](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[review of the other side's expert reports](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/).
Everything else on this page describes how that document gets made.

## Forum and county footing

A Scottsdale civil case in state court proceeds in the Superior Court in
Maricopa County. If the matter is federal, the United States District
Court for the District of Arizona is the available federal forum; the firm
does not guess at controlling venue but confirms it, along with any
registration or qualification requirement the venue imposes, during the
conflict check and intake. The opinion is drafted with the forum's
disclosure expectations in mind from the outset, which is considerably
easier than retrofitting a report after the fact. Other Arizona venues are
covered on the [state page](/locations/arizona/).

## Labor market evidence behind the opinion

Scottsdale is a large city embedded in a much larger employment region,
and the firm's earning capacity findings reflect that structure. The
statistical backbone is the Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational
employment and wage estimates for the Phoenix-Mesa-Chandler metropolitan
area and for Arizona statewide, read together with state workforce agency
labor market information. On top of that backbone the firm layers
case-specific analysis: which occupations the evaluee's skills transfer
to, which of those occupations remain accessible given documented
restrictions, and what the accessible work pays in qualitative terms
supported by the cited sources. Direct labor market research is added when
a referral question demands evidence the published estimates cannot give.

## Appointments, not an address

The firm maintains no Scottsdale office and lists no street address in the
city, and it says so plainly rather than implying a presence it does not
have. Meetings with counsel and evaluations are by appointment. The
default working mode is remote, which serves most cases well: secure video
interviews, electronic records exchange, and delivery on the agreed
schedule. Where the case is better served by an in-person evaluation, the
firm conducts it in the evaluee's home community, traveling from the
Kansas City home office; testimony at deposition or trial is handled with
the same travel practice. When counsel needs the economic loss itself
calculated, the firm arranges
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
under which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds in
parallel within the same engagement.

## The route to retention

Retention runs through a conflict check, not a consultation pitch. Counsel
should submit the caption or party names via the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/), noting any disclosure, deposition,
or trial dates already on the calendar; the firm responds with
availability, scope, and a records request. How the same standards travel
to every other jurisdiction the firm serves is set out on the
[nationwide page](/locations/nationwide/).
