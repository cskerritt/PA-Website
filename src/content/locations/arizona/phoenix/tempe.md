A vocational opinion is only as sound as the work history underneath it.
Before any labor market source is opened in a Tempe case, Purinton
Analytics reconstructs what the evaluee actually did for a living: the
duties performed, the skills exercised, and the demands of each job as
the records, the testimony, and the clinical interview document them.
Transferable skills, occupational access, and earning capacity are then
built on that reconstruction rather than on a job title taken at face
value. The firm brings this record-first method to Tempe matters for
plaintiff counsel and defense counsel alike, providing
[vocational expert evaluation and testimony](/services/vocational-expert-witness/)
and [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) within its
[Phoenix metro practice](/locations/arizona/phoenix/).

## Where a Tempe matter is heard

Tempe lies in Maricopa County. A civil action filed in state court
proceeds in the Superior Court, while a federal claim is heard in the
United States District Court for the District of Arizona. The
distinction matters to an expert because each forum carries its own
disclosure expectations, and a report drafted without knowing its
destination tends to need repair on the eve of a deadline. The firm
confirms the controlling forum with counsel at intake, and any
venue-specific registration or qualification requirement is identified
and confirmed during the same conflict check and intake process. Counsel
with matters elsewhere in the state will find the wider court landscape
described on the [Arizona page](/locations/arizona/).

## From work history to labor market

Once the evaluee's occupational history is established, the firm tests
it against published data. Findings rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates for the Phoenix-Mesa-Chandler
metropolitan area and for Arizona statewide, read alongside state
workforce agency labor market information. Tempe is a large city, and
its residents hold work histories that range across the full breadth of
the metro economy, so the analysis names each occupation considered,
names the source behind every finding, and keeps its conclusions
qualitative and within what the cited estimates will support. Medical
restrictions documented in the record then narrow the occupational field
to what remains genuinely available to this evaluee, not to a
hypothetical worker. Where a referral question reaches past the
published estimates, the firm conducts direct labor market research and
preserves the results in the file so that either side can examine them.

## Logistics for the Tempe evaluee

Purinton Analytics keeps no office in Tempe and says so plainly;
meetings with counsel and evaluees are by appointment, and no local
street address is published. The standard evaluation is remote,
pairing electronic records review with a secure video interview and
remotely administered instruments, which spares an injured evaluee
unnecessary travel. When the referral questions call for an in-person
evaluation, it takes place in the evaluee's own community, with travel
scheduled from the Kansas City home office, and the same travel practice
covers deposition and trial appearances. Where lost earnings must be
reduced to figures, the firm offers
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.
