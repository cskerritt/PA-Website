Begin with the courtroom, because that is where a Peoria case ends up if
it does not settle. Peoria lies in Maricopa County, so a state-court civil
action arising here is tried in the Superior Court, and a federal claim
finds its forum in the United States District Court for the District of
Arizona. Purinton Analytics prepares vocational and life care planning
opinions for exactly those settings, retained by plaintiff counsel and
defense counsel alike, and treats the eventual trier of fact as the real
audience for every page it writes. The firm's Peoria work is part of its
[Phoenix metro practice](/locations/arizona/phoenix/).

## County, courthouse, and qualification

Because venue shapes disclosure obligations, the firm settles forum
questions at the start of an engagement rather than the end. Which court
controls, whether any venue-specific registration or qualification
requirement applies, and what the operative scheduling order demands are
all identified during the conflict check and intake. State-court matters
in the Superior Court and federal matters in the District of Arizona
receive the same underlying work product: a scope agreed in writing, a
records request matched to the claims, and an opinion whose reasoning is
laid out step by step for the other side to probe. The firm's coverage of
Arizona forums generally is summarized on the
[Arizona page](/locations/arizona/).

## What Peoria's labor market looks like in evidence

Peoria is a large city on the metro's roster of major municipalities, and
its residents participate in an employment market that operates at
regional scale. In the firm's reports that market appears as cited
evidence: Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage
estimates for the Phoenix-Mesa-Chandler metropolitan area and for the
state of Arizona, state workforce agency labor market information, and,
when a question requires it, direct labor market research conducted and
documented for the specific case. Conclusions about transferable skills,
post-injury occupational access, and earning capacity are framed
qualitatively and tied to those sources, never floated as unsupported
professional impressions.

## Getting the evaluation done

Peoria evaluees are typically evaluated without leaving home. The firm
works remote-first: counsel transmits records electronically, the
vocational interview runs over secure video, and standardized instruments
are administered under remote-appropriate protocols. An in-person
evaluation, when the case warrants one, is conducted in the evaluee's own
community, with the evaluator traveling from the Kansas City home office,
the same arrangement that covers deposition and trial appearances. The
firm has no office in Peoria and claims none; meetings are by appointment
only. Cases requiring a full damages calculation can be structured as
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/),
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) and
[vocational components](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) proceed
under one engagement structure.
