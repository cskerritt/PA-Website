Process is the product in expert work, and a Buckeye engagement with
Purinton Analytics shows its process before it shows anything else: a
documented conflict check, a scope reduced to writing, a records request
matched to the claims, and only then an opinion. Counsel for injured
plaintiffs and counsel defending claims retain the firm on identical
terms for
[vocational expert services](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) and
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and Buckeye sits
squarely within the firm's
[Phoenix metro coverage](/locations/arizona/phoenix/).

## Buckeye's place in the county court system

As a Maricopa County city, Buckeye sends its state-court civil disputes
to the Superior Court for the county. The federal forum available in the
state is the United States District Court for the District of Arizona.
Which of these controls a given matter, and whether the venue carries any
registration or qualification requirement for the expert, are settled
facts by the end of the conflict check and intake, not open questions at
disclosure time. The opinion that follows is drafted so that its
reasoning, sources, and limits are visible to the court and to the
opposing expert alike. The remainder of the state's forums are mapped on
the [Arizona page](/locations/arizona/).

## Measuring work opportunity from Buckeye

Buckeye is a sizable city whose residents draw their employment options
from the wider metropolitan market, and the firm measures those options
with the standard federal series: Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational
employment and wage estimates for the Phoenix-Mesa-Chandler metropolitan
area and for Arizona statewide, complemented by state workforce agency
labor market information. Findings about what occupations remain open to
an evaluee, and how the accessible range compares with pre-injury work,
are stated qualitatively and pinned to the cited sources. If a disputed
issue outruns the published data, the firm conducts direct labor market
research and keeps the documentation, because an opinion is only as
strong as what can be produced behind it.

## Bringing the evaluation to Buckeye

The firm operates no Buckeye location and puts no local address into the
world; every meeting is by appointment, and the absence of a storefront
is disclosed as readily as any credential. In the ordinary course an
evaluee never travels: the vocational interview is held by secure video,
records are reviewed in advance, and testing proceeds under remote
protocols. Where an in-person evaluation serves the case better, it is
conducted in the community where the evaluee lives, with travel scheduled
from the Kansas City home office, and the same scheduling practice
carries the firm to depositions and trial. Matters needing a quantified
loss figure can be organized as
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/),
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while vocational and care-planning work moves in step under one
engagement structure.
