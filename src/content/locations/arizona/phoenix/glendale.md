Municipal boundaries mean very little to a labor market. A Glendale
resident's realistic employment options are defined by where that person
can commute, not by the city line, and a vocational opinion that ignores
this is vulnerable on cross-examination. Purinton Analytics writes
opinions for Glendale cases with the commuting market as the unit of
analysis, offering
[vocational expert services](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) and
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) to counsel on either
side of a dispute, within the metro-wide practice described on the
[Phoenix page](/locations/arizona/phoenix/).

## Courts serving Glendale

Glendale sits in Maricopa County, and its state-court civil litigation is
conducted in the Superior Court for that county. Where a claim belongs in
federal court, the United States District Court for the District of
Arizona is the forum available in the state; which court actually controls
a particular case is a question the firm resolves with counsel during the
conflict check and intake, along with any venue-specific registration or
qualification requirement. Nothing in the firm's method changes between
forums. The scope is defined in writing, the records request is tailored
to the claims, and the resulting opinion is one that opposing counsel can
audit line by line. Counsel can survey the rest of the state's forums on
the [Arizona page](/locations/arizona/).

## The commuting market as the measure

When the firm assesses what work a Glendale evaluee can perform and what
it pays, the evidence comes from Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational
employment and wage estimates covering the Phoenix-Mesa-Chandler
metropolitan area and Arizona statewide, augmented by state workforce
agency labor market information. Glendale is itself a large city, but the
analysis never stops at its borders: transferable skills are matched to
occupations across the metro, and access to those occupations is evaluated
in light of the evaluee's restrictions, education, and work history. Where
the published data cannot answer a referral question on its own, direct
labor market research supplies the missing evidence and is documented so
it can be tested.

## Meeting the evaluee where they are

The firm holds no office space in Glendale; all meetings are by
appointment, and no local address appears anywhere in its materials
because none exists. That candor costs nothing and buys credibility.
Evaluations typically proceed remotely, with records reviewed beforehand
and the vocational interview conducted by secure video, which spares an
injured evaluee unnecessary travel. When an in-person evaluation better
serves the case, it is held in the community where the evaluee lives, and
the travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office, as are
deposition and trial appearances. For cases that require the damages to be
reduced to numbers, the firm coordinates
[damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) in which
independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis
while the vocational and life care planning work runs alongside it in one
engagement structure.
