Expert retention in Chandler litigation is usually driven by a calendar.
A scheduling order sets the disclosure date, the disclosure date sets the
report date, and the report date determines when records must be gathered,
the evaluee interviewed, and the analysis completed. Purinton Analytics
plans every Chandler engagement backward from those dates. The firm serves
counsel for plaintiffs and for defendants with
[vocational expert evaluation and testimony](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) of opposing
expert work, as part of the broader
[Phoenix metro practice](/locations/arizona/phoenix/).

## Where Chandler cases are decided

The state forum for Chandler civil matters is the Superior Court in
Maricopa County. For federal claims, the United States District Court for
the District of Arizona stands available, with the controlling venue for a
given case confirmed during the conflict check and intake rather than
assumed in advance. That intake step also surfaces any registration or
qualification requirement particular to the venue, so nothing about the
expert's standing becomes a surprise at the disclosure stage. A summary of
forums across the state is maintained on the
[Arizona page](/locations/arizona/).

## Reading the labor market around Chandler

Chandler is a large city named in its metro's own official designation,
and its residents work throughout the surrounding region, so earning
capacity questions are answered at the metro scale. The firm draws on
Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for
the Phoenix-Mesa-Chandler metropolitan area and for Arizona as a whole,
together with state workforce agency labor market information. Those
sources are treated as evidence to be cited, not authority to be invoked:
every occupational finding in a Chandler report identifies the data behind
it, and where published estimates leave a genuine question open, direct
labor market research closes it and is documented in the file.

## Scheduling the evaluation itself

Because the report date governs, evaluation logistics are settled at the
outset. Remote evaluation is the default and is often the fastest path to
a complete record: records review, a structured interview by secure video,
and standardized testing administered remotely under appropriate
protocols. If the case calls for an in-person evaluation, it takes place
in the evaluee's community on a date that protects the disclosure
deadline, with travel from the Kansas City home office. The firm maintains
no office in Chandler and publishes no local address, a fact stated
plainly because candor about presence is part of what makes the rest of
the file credible. Matters that need economic quantification can proceed
as [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/),
with independent economist partners performing and signing the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning components move on the
same schedule.
