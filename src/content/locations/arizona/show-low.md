## Navajo County venues

Engagements are accepted for matters venued in the Superior Court in Navajo
County and in the United States District Court for the District of Arizona.
Every matter opens the same way: a documented conflict check, a scope
defined in writing, and a records request tailored to the claims, so the
opinion that follows rests on a record either side can examine. The firm's
[Arizona page](/locations/arizona/) describes how the same structure serves
matters filed elsewhere in the state.

## Remote-first service, travel when it counts

Purinton Analytics keeps no staffed office in Show Low and publishes no
street address for this location. Meetings are by appointment, and the firm
says so directly because candor about presence is part of the discipline its
reports are held to. Remote-first practice works in the firm's favor here:
records review, structured vocational interviews, and standardized testing
under publisher protocols proceed without anyone traveling, where remote
administration is supported. When an in-person evaluation is warranted, or
when deposition and trial dates require attendance, travel is scheduled from
the Kansas City home office and planned around the court's calendar.

## Reading a smaller labor market honestly

Employability conclusions in a smaller market demand particular care about
data coverage. Vocational findings for Show Low-area evaluees rest on Bureau
of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the area
and for Arizona statewide, together with state workforce agency labor market
information and direct labor market research where the referral questions
warrant it. The report states plainly which sources describe the local
market and which describe broader geography, so that placement and earning
capacity opinions can be tested rather than taken on faith. Life care plans
and [medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/) are
priced to the evaluee's own market through provider-specific inquiries and
published fee data.
