Purinton Analytics designed its Oregon practice to be remote-first from the
start, rather than adapting an office-bound model after the fact. A
vocational or life care planning engagement proceeds on the strength of the
records and the published labor market data: the evaluee is interviewed by
secure video conference or in person in the community where they actually
live, counsel meet the expert by appointment, and the expert appears in
person when deposition or trial requires it. Plaintiff and defense
referrals are accepted on the same terms.

## Courts and Communities Across Oregon

Engagements are accepted for matters in the Circuit Court of any Oregon
county and in the United States District Court for the District of Oregon.
Both plaintiff and defense counsel refer cases, and the written opinion is
prepared to the same standard for each: a documented methodology, a defined
scope, and conclusions that rest on a record both sides can examine.

## How the Firm Serves Oregon

The firm's only home office is in Kansas City; it keeps no staffed office
anywhere in Oregon, publishes no Oregon street address, and says so plainly,
because accuracy about offices is part of the same discipline that governs
its reports. Records review, transferable skills analysis, labor market
research, and report drafting are handled remotely. Evaluee interviews are
scheduled by video conference or conducted in person in the evaluee's own
community, and when depositions, trials, or referral questions require the
expert's presence, travel is arranged from the Kansas City home office.

Vocational findings for Oregon evaluees rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates for the relevant Oregon
metropolitan areas and for Oregon statewide, together with state workforce
agency labor market information and direct labor market research where
warranted. When a matter also requires economic projection of losses, the
firm offers coordinated damages engagements in which independent economist
partners perform and sign the economic analysis while the vocational and
care-planning work proceeds under one engagement structure.

## Qualification and Disclosure in Oregon Matters

Disclosure obligations and scheduling practices differ between Oregon's
state and federal courts, so counsel are asked to provide the operative
scheduling order at intake and to flag any expert disclosure deadline that
is already set. Any venue-specific registration or qualification requirement
is identified and confirmed during the conflict check and intake process,
before the engagement is accepted rather than after.

## Starting an Oregon Engagement

To begin, [request a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) with the case caption
and parties, and note any imminent disclosure, deposition, or trial dates.
Once the conflict check clears, the firm issues a defined scope and a
records request tailored to the claims, and the work begins without waiting
on travel plans. The [nationwide practice page](/locations/nationwide/)
describes how the same engagement structure operates for counsel with
matters outside Oregon.
