Smaller markets deserve the same caliber of expert work as the largest ones,
and the remote-first structure of Purinton Analytics is what makes that
practical for the Keene, NH Micropolitan Statistical Area. Counsel in the
area retain the firm without paying for a local storefront they do not need:
records review, conferences, and most of the analytic work proceed remotely,
while [vocational evaluation and expert testimony](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure are all available to plaintiff and defense counsel on
the same terms offered in any major metro.

## Where Keene matters are heard

Engagements are accepted for matters arising in Cheshire County, whether
they proceed in the Superior Court or in the United States District Court
for the District of New Hampshire. The referral questions, not the venue,
shape the work: earning capacity in a personal injury claim, employability
after termination in an employment dispute, future care needs in a
catastrophic injury case, or a
[rebuttal assignment](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) testing an opposing
expert's opinion all follow the same documented methodology. Keene referrals
are part of the firm's wider [New Hampshire practice](/locations/new-hampshire/),
which keeps a single expert available to counsel whose dockets reach beyond
the county.

## Presence, stated plainly

The firm has no staffed office in Keene and publishes no street address for
this location. That is stated plainly because honesty about presence is part
of the same discipline that produces the reports. What counsel get instead
is responsiveness: remote conferences on short notice, records checklists
delivered promptly after the conflict check clears, and written opinions
that move on the disclosure calendar. When in-person evaluation is
warranted, or when deposition and trial dates require attendance, travel is
scheduled from the Kansas City home office with enough lead time that the
logistics never crowd the deadlines. Evaluations can be conducted in the
evaluee's own community when that serves the referral questions best.

## Labor market evidence for a smaller market

In a compact labor market, loose assumptions about available work are easy
for opposing counsel to attack, so the analysis is built to be specific.
Vocational findings for Keene-area evaluees draw on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the area and for
New Hampshire statewide, state workforce agency labor market information,
and direct labor market research where warranted. The report defines the
evaluee's commutable labor market expressly and explains the definition, so
that conclusions about employability and earning capacity rest on cited
data rather than on impressions of what a smaller market must look like.

## Opening an engagement in Keene

Keene counsel engage the same office, methodology, and reporting standards
that support the firm's [national practice](/locations/nationwide/). Begin
by submitting the caption or party names through the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/), and note any imminent disclosure,
deposition, or trial dates so the report timeline can be set against the
operative calendar. Once the conflict check clears, the engagement proceeds
under a defined written scope, with a records request tailored to the
claims, so the finished opinion rests on a record both sides can examine.
