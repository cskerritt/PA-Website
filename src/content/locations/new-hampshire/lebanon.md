The Lebanon-Claremont, NH-VT Micropolitan Statistical Area crosses a state
line, and litigation here often does the same. Purinton Analytics is built
for that: a single expert engagement can address an evaluee who lives on the
Vermont side and worked on the New Hampshire side, with the written opinion
explicit about which state's labor market and which venue's requirements
govern. Plaintiff and defense counsel throughout the area retain the firm
for [vocational evaluation and expert testimony](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## A cross-state venue footprint

On the New Hampshire side, engagements are accepted for matters in Grafton
County and Sullivan County, in the Superior Court and in the United States
District Court for the District of New Hampshire. On the Vermont side, the
area takes in Orange County and Windsor County, and the firm accepts
retention for Superior Court matters there under the same methodology and
reporting standards. Where the caption, the evaluee's residence, and the
place of injury fall in different states, the report addresses the choice of
labor market directly rather than leaving it for cross-examination to
expose. Counsel with related matters elsewhere in the state can work through
the firm's [New Hampshire practice](/locations/new-hampshire/) with the same
expert.

## By-appointment service, honestly described

The firm maintains no staffed office in Lebanon, Claremont, or anywhere else
in the area, and it publishes no local address, because candor about
presence belongs to the same discipline that governs its opinions. Most of
an engagement proceeds remotely: records review, counsel conferences,
methodology discussions, and pre-deposition preparation are handled by video
and telephone on short scheduling cycles. In-person evaluation, deposition
attendance, and trial testimony are scheduled as travel from the Kansas City
home office, coordinated in advance against the disclosure calendar so that
the distance never becomes the opposing side's argument.

## Labor market data on both sides of the line

Vocational findings for evaluees in the area rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the region and for
New Hampshire and Vermont statewide, together with state workforce agency
labor market information from the state that defines the evaluee's
commutable market, supplemented by direct labor market research where
warranted. Because the area spans states, the report identifies the
commutable labor market expressly and explains the selection, so that the
earning capacity conclusions survive scrutiny from either direction.
[Rebuttal and peer review work](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) in the area
applies the same sources when an opposing expert's market definition is the
question.

## Starting an engagement in the Lebanon area

Retention begins with a documented conflict check, a defined scope, and a
records request tailored to the claims, in this area as in the firm's
[national practice](/locations/nationwide/). Send the caption or party names
through the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/), identify the venue and
the evaluee's state of residence, and flag any imminent disclosure,
deposition, or trial dates. Counsel then receive a records checklist and a
report timeline set against the operative calendar, and the engagement
proceeds under a written scope that either side of the caption can examine.
