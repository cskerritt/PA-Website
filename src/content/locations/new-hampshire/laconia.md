to organize their case around an expert's geography. Purinton Analytics
structures its work around the person being evaluated: interviews and
testing can be conducted remotely when the referral questions and protocols
permit, or in the evaluee's own community when they do not, and the
resulting opinions support
[vocational evaluation and expert testimony](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/),

[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic

engagement structure. Plaintiff and defense referrals are accepted on
identical terms.

## The Belknap County venue picture

The Laconia area is anchored by Belknap County, and engagements are accepted
for matters in the Superior Court and in the United States District Court
for the District of New Hampshire. Administrative and pre-suit matters are
served as well: a workers' compensation claim, a disability dispute, or a
demand-stage damages evaluation gets the same methodology as a case already
set for trial. Because Laconia referrals belong to the firm's statewide
[New Hampshire practice](/locations/new-hampshire/), counsel who practice
across county lines can retain a single expert with a consistent record
across their matters.

## What by-appointment coverage means in Laconia

The firm keeps no staffed office in Laconia and publishes no street address
for this location; stating that outright is part of the same discipline that
governs the reports themselves. Coverage instead means a working rhythm
counsel can rely on: remote records conferences and preparation sessions
scheduled quickly, written work product delivered against the disclosure
calendar, and in-person appearances planned rather than improvised. When an
evaluation must happen face to face, or when a deposition or trial setting
requires presence, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office and
confirmed well before the operative dates.

## Reading the Laconia labor market honestly

Employability and earning capacity findings for evaluees in the area are
grounded in Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage
estimates for the area and for New Hampshire statewide, state workforce
agency labor market information, and direct labor market research where the
referral warrants it. The report states how the evaluee's commutable labor
market is defined and why that definition fits the record, because a market
conclusion that cannot be traced to its source is an invitation to
cross-examination. The same standard applies in reverse: in
[rebuttal and peer review assignments](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/), the
firm tests whether an opposing expert's market assumptions would survive the
scrutiny its own reports are built for.

## How a Laconia engagement starts

Laconia matters run on the same intake as the firm's
[national practice](/locations/nationwide/): a documented conflict check
first, then a defined scope and a records request tailored to the claims.
Submit the caption or party names through the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and note any imminent disclosure,
deposition, or trial dates. Once the conflict check clears, counsel receive
a records checklist and a proposed timeline keyed to the case calendar, and
the engagement proceeds under a written scope that either side of the
caption can examine.
