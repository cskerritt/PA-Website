Every Concord engagement begins the way it does everywhere else in the
practice: with a documented conflict check, a defined scope, and a records
request tailored to the claims, so that the written opinion rests on a
record both sides can examine. That discipline is the product counsel in the
Concord, NH Micropolitan Statistical Area are buying, whether the referral
is for [vocational evaluation and expert testimony](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), a
[medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/), or
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. The firm accepts work from plaintiff and defense
counsel in equal measure.

## Courts serving Merrimack County matters

The Concord area is anchored by Merrimack County, and engagements are
accepted for matters proceeding in the Superior Court and in the United
States District Court for the District of New Hampshire. The venue does not
change the method: the same evaluation protocol, the same data sources, and
the same reporting standards apply whether the matter is a personal injury
claim, an employment dispute, or a workers' compensation case headed for
hearing. Concord referrals sit within the firm's statewide
[New Hampshire practice](/locations/new-hampshire/), so counsel managing
matters in more than a single county can keep continuity of expert across
them.

## How by-appointment service works here

There is no staffed Purinton Analytics office in Concord, and the firm
publishes no street address for this location; describing presence
accurately is part of the same standard that governs its reports. In
practice, that costs counsel little. Records analysis, conferences, and
deposition preparation are conducted remotely on short scheduling cycles,
and written work product moves on the case calendar rather than on travel
logistics. When the referral questions call for in-person evaluation, or
when a deposition or trial setting requires presence, travel is arranged
from the Kansas City home office and confirmed well ahead of the operative
deadlines. Evaluations can also be conducted in the evaluee's own community
or at counsel's offices.

## Grounding vocational findings in the Concord market

Employability and earning capacity conclusions for Concord-area evaluees are
grounded in Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage
estimates for the area and for New Hampshire statewide, state workforce
agency labor market information, and direct labor market research where the
referral warrants it. The report defines the evaluee's commutable labor
market explicitly and shows how each conclusion traces to a cited source, an
approach that serves the retaining party on direct examination and holds up
under cross. The same sourcing discipline supports
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) of opposing
vocational and care-planning opinions in Concord matters.

## Beginning a Concord engagement

Concord counsel work with the same office and the same methodology that
serve the firm's [national practice](/locations/nationwide/). To begin,
submit the caption or party names through the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and note any imminent disclosure,
deposition, or trial dates. Once the conflict check clears, counsel receive
a records checklist tailored to the claims and a proposed report timeline
set against the disclosure calendar, and the engagement proceeds under a
written scope from the outset.
