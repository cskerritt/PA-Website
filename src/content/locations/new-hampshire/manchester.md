## Meetings and evaluations by appointment

Manchester work is handled by appointment. The firm keeps no staffed office
in the city and publishes no local street address, because accuracy about
presence is part of the same discipline that governs its reports. Records
review, attorney conferences, and preparation sessions proceed remotely by
default, which keeps timelines short and costs predictable. When the
referral questions call for in-person evaluation, or when deposition and
trial settings require attendance, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City
home office and planned around the operative disclosure calendar.
Evaluations can be conducted at counsel's offices or in the evaluee's own
community, and remote evaluation is available when the referral questions
and testing protocols permit it.

## Labor market research for Manchester evaluees

Employability and earning capacity findings for evaluees in the metro are

## Starting a Manchester engagement

Every engagement begins the same way, in Manchester as everywhere else in
the [national practice](/locations/nationwide/): a documented conflict
check, a defined scope, and a records request tailored to the claims. Submit
the caption or party names through the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and note any imminent disclosure,
deposition, or trial dates so scheduling can be confirmed against the
deadlines that matter. Once the conflict check clears, counsel receive a
records checklist and a proposed report timeline keyed to the case calendar,
and the engagement proceeds under a written scope both sides of the caption
can examine.
