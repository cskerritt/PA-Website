## Labor market evidence for Danville evaluees

Whether the assignment is an affirmative opinion or a critique of someone
else's, the labor market analysis follows the same path. Employability and
earning capacity findings rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational
employment and wage estimates for the Danville area and for Illinois
statewide, state workforce agency labor market information, and direct labor
market research, including employer contact and job posting analysis, when
the referral questions require evidence about actual hiring in Vermilion
County. In review work, the same sources become the yardstick: an opposing
report's occupational assumptions, wage bases, and placement conclusions are
checked against the published data and the evaluee's documented
restrictions, and the resulting critique states plainly what the record
supports and what it does not.
