An engagement in the Rockford, IL metropolitan area can enter the practice
at any posture: as an independent
[vocational evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) of
employability and earning capacity, as a
[life care plan](/services/life-care-planning/) in a catastrophic matter, or
as a [rebuttal or peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) when an
opposing expert's report is already on file and counsel needs its methods
examined on their merits. Purinton Analytics
accepts that work from either side of the caption, for plaintiff and defense
counsel alike, and applies the same written methodology whichever party
issues the retention letter. The firm's broader
[Illinois practice](/locations/illinois/) is described on the state page;
this page covers how the work runs in the Rockford, IL metropolitan area
specifically.

## Winnebago and Boone County venues

Engagements are accepted for matters in the Circuit Court in Winnebago
County and Boone County, and in federal court across the state: the United
States District Court for the Northern District of Illinois, the United
States District Court for the Central District of Illinois, and the United
States District Court for the Southern District of Illinois. Because the
same expert file supports state and federal work, a case that is removed or
refiled does not need a new expert or a rebuilt record. Testimony is
provided in person at deposition and trial, or by remote appearance where
the court permits, with exhibits prepared to the same standard either way.

## How by-appointment service works in Rockford

The firm maintains no staffed office in Rockford and publishes no street
address for this location. Saying so plainly is deliberate: accuracy about
presence is part of the same discipline that governs the reports themselves.
Meetings are by appointment, and evaluations take place in the evaluee's own
community, at counsel's offices, or by secure video under publisher
protocols. When the referral questions call for in-person evaluation, or
when deposition and trial dates require presence, travel is scheduled from
the Kansas City home office with enough lead time to protect the court's
schedule.

## Grounding opinions in the Rockford labor market

Vocational findings for Rockford area evaluees rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates from the OEWS program
for the Rockford, IL metropolitan area and for Illinois statewide, alongside
state workforce agency labor market information and direct labor market
research where warranted. Opinions on employability, labor market access,
and post-injury earning capacity are stated qualitatively where the data are
qualitative and are cited to their sources throughout, so that
cross-examination can test the foundations rather than guess at them. When a
matter extends past the metro or the state line, the
[nationwide practice](/locations/nationwide/) carries the same method to any
venue.

## Opening a Rockford file

The first step is always a documented conflict check with a defined scope,
followed by a records request tailored to the claims. To begin,
[request a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) with the case caption and
parties, and flag any disclosure deadlines, deposition settings, or trial
dates that will shape the schedule. Any venue-specific registration or
qualification requirement is identified and confirmed during the conflict
check and intake process, and counsel receive written confirmation of scope
before substantive work begins. In catastrophic matters,
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
are available, in which independent economist partners
perform and sign the economic analysis while the vocational and
care-planning work proceeds under one engagement structure.
