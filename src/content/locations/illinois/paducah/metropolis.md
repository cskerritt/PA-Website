## Massac County and its courts

State-court litigation arising in Metropolis is heard in the Circuit Court
of Massac County, a small-town venue where an expert's overstatement is
remembered longer than an expert's name. For captions filed federally,
Illinois makes available the United States District Court for the Northern
District of Illinois, the United States District Court for the Central
District of Illinois, and the United States District Court for the Southern
District of Illinois; which district controls a particular Metropolis matter
is confirmed during the conflict check and intake rather than presumed in
advance. Venue never changes the working method: a documented conflict
screen, a scope reduced to writing, and a records request matched to the
claims come before any opinion is drafted, so the finished report stands on
materials both parties can inspect.

## A labor market that crosses the state line

For a Metropolis evaluee, the relevant labor market does not stop at the
Illinois border. Employability and placeability findings are grounded in
Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for
the Paducah metropolitan area, which spans Kentucky and Illinois, together
with Illinois statewide estimates, state workforce agency labor market
information, and direct employer contact when the referral questions call
for it. Because commuting patterns in this metro routinely carry workers
toward the principal city, a credible transferable skills analysis has to
weigh openings on both sides of the line, and the report identifies the
data source behind each conclusion so that opposing counsel can trace every
figure to its origin.
