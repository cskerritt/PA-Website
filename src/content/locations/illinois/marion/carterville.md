across a wider area than the town itself, and a credible vocational opinion
has to reflect that reality. When counsel on either side of a caption needs
an injured Carterville worker's earning capacity assessed, or a lifetime of
future care priced with sources shown, Purinton Analytics provides
[vocational expert evaluation and testimony](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),

[medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/), as part of the
firm's [Marion metro practice](/locations/illinois/marion/), its
[Illinois statewide coverage](/locations/illinois/), and its
[nationwide practice](/locations/nationwide/).

## Courts serving Carterville matters

Carterville sits in Williamson County, and state-court litigation involving
its residents is heard in the Circuit Court of Williamson County. Federal
questions have their own set of forums: the United States District Court for
the Northern District of Illinois, the United States District Court for the
Central District of Illinois, and the United States District Court for the
Southern District of Illinois are all available in the state, and the
controlling venue for any particular matter is confirmed during the conflict
check and intake. Nothing about the opinion changes with the courtroom.
Retentions from plaintiff firms and defense firms follow an identical
protocol, which is the point: an expert whose conclusions depend on who is
paying is not an expert either side can safely disclose.

## A commuting workforce and its data

Carterville's position in the Marion-Herrin metropolitan area shapes the
vocational analysis. Employability for a Carterville evaluee is measured
against the metro commuting market, with Marion as the principal city, so
transferable skills findings consider the jobs an evaluee can actually reach
rather than only those inside the town. The data foundation is qualitative
and documented: Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage
estimates covering the metro area and Illinois statewide, state workforce
agency labor market information, and targeted labor market research where a
specific placement question calls for it. Every source appears in the report
by name, so opposing counsel can pull the same tables the expert used.

## How evaluations proceed

There is no Purinton Analytics office in Carterville, and the firm does not
pretend otherwise. Records review, interviews, and much of the testing
proceed remotely; when the referral questions require an in-person
evaluation, it takes place in the evaluee's own community, with travel
arranged from the Kansas City home office. Where the case also requires the
loss quantified in dollars, counsel can structure the referral as a
[coordinated damages engagement](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/),
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. Counsel facing an opposing expert's report rather than
needing an affirmative opinion can request a
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) of the file's
methods and sources.

## Requesting a conflict check

An engagement begins with clearance, not a sales call. Send the caption or
the party names through the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/), flag any
disclosure, deposition, or trial dates already on the calendar, and the firm
replies with a conflict determination and a written scope before any records
change hands. That sequence protects privilege, keeps the retention clean,
and gives counsel in Carterville a defensible answer to the question a court
will eventually ask: what was this expert retained to do, and when.
