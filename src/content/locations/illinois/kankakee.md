counsel there receive the same expert file the firm builds anywhere in its

## Kankakee County courts, state and federal

Engagements are accepted for matters in the Circuit Court in Kankakee
County and in the federal forums serving Illinois: the United States
District Court for the Northern District of Illinois, the United States
District Court for the Central District of Illinois, and the United States
District Court for the Southern District of Illinois. Reports are drafted
against the disclosure standard of the forum that will receive them, and
because the underlying record work does not vary by court, removal or
transfer does not force a change of expert. Deposition and trial testimony
are given in person or by remote appearance where the court permits.

## What counsel should expect about presence

Purinton Analytics has no staffed office in Kankakee and publishes no
street address for this location. The firm says so directly because honesty
about presence is part of the same discipline that governs its written
opinions. Meetings are by appointment, and evaluation sessions take place
in the evaluee's own community, at counsel's offices, or by secure video
under publisher protocols. When the referral questions call for in-person
evaluation, or when deposition and trial dates require presence, travel is
scheduled from the Kansas City home office and coordinated with counsel so
that court commitments are protected.

## Labor market foundations for Kankakee opinions

Vocational findings for Kankakee County evaluees rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates from the OEWS program
for the Kankakee, IL metropolitan area and for Illinois statewide, together
with state workforce agency labor market information and direct labor
market research where warranted. The report ties employability and earning
capacity conclusions to occupations documented in the evaluee's record, and
it cites every source, because an opinion that cannot be traced is an
opinion that cannot be defended. Where an evaluee's realistic labor market
extends beyond the county, the analysis says so and follows the evidence
rather than the map.

## Opening a Kankakee County engagement

Every file begins with a documented conflict check, a defined scope, and a
records request tailored to the claims. Submit the caption or party names
through the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and note any imminent
disclosure, deposition, or trial dates. Any venue-specific registration or
qualification requirement is identified and confirmed during the conflict
check and intake process. When the matter also needs economic damages
testimony, the firm offers
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.
