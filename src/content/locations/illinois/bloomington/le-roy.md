Retaining an expert for a client who lives in Le Roy raises a practical
concern before any methodological question: how the evaluation will
actually happen for an evaluee in a small McLean County town, without
asking that person to organize their recovery around an expert's travel
schedule. Purinton Analytics builds its practice around that concern.
[Vocational evaluations](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) and
[life care plans](/services/life-care-planning/) for Le Roy evaluees
proceed remotely by secure video under publisher protocols, or in person in
the evaluee's own community, and the choice is made with counsel at intake
rather than dictated by the expert's location. Plaintiff and defense
referrals are welcomed on the same terms.

## Where a Le Roy case is heard

Le Roy civil matters proceed in the Circuit Court serving McLean County,
and every report is written for the scrutiny of the courtroom: a defined
scope, a documented records basis, and conclusions that opposing counsel
can trace to their sources. Federal claims from Illinois counsel may be
filed in the United States District Court for the Northern District of
Illinois, the United States District Court for the Central District of
Illinois, or the United States District Court for the Southern District of
Illinois. Which district controls a given case, and whether any
venue-specific qualification requirement applies, is settled during the
conflict check and intake. The [Illinois page](/locations/illinois/) sets
out the firm's statewide court coverage.

## Small-town evaluees, metro-level data

A Le Roy evaluee lives in a small community but works, or worked, in a
wider market that includes Bloomington, the metro's principal city.
Vocational findings therefore draw on Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates for the Bloomington
metropolitan area and for Illinois statewide, on state workforce agency
labor market information, and on direct labor market research when the
referral calls for evidence of actual hiring within realistic commuting
range. The distinction matters in a town this size: post-injury
employability cannot be assumed from national tables built on metropolitan
density, and the opinion is careful to describe what the accessible market
genuinely offers rather than what a larger city would.

## Scheduling, travel, and the case calendar

There is no Purinton Analytics office in Le Roy or elsewhere in Illinois;
the firm's staffed home office is in Kansas City, and all other locations
are served by appointment, a fact stated plainly because accuracy about
offices belongs to the same discipline that governs the reports. Deposition
and trial appearances are booked out of Kansas City once the case calendar
is established at intake, so disclosure deadlines are known before the
evaluation date is chosen. Where economic loss is also claimed, the matter
can proceed as a
[coordinated damages engagement](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/),
with independent economist partners performing and signing the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work moves under one
engagement structure.
