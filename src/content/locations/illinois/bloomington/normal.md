working assumption: the reader most likely to study it line by line is the
counsel who did not commission it. Purinton Analytics writes to that
reader. The firm provides
[vocational expert witness](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) and

and defense counsel with Normal cases, and every engagement begins the same

opinion rests on a record both sides can examine.

Normal sits in McLean County, and civil matters are heard in the Circuit
Court there. Reports and testimony are prepared as courtroom documents:
methods stated, sources cited, and limits acknowledged. For federal
filings, Illinois counsel may proceed in the United States District Court
for the Northern District of Illinois, the United States District Court for
the Central District of Illinois, or the United States District Court for

matter, along with any venue-specific registration or qualification

## Reading the Bloomington and Normal labor market

Employability and earning capacity opinions for a Normal evaluee are
grounded in Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage
estimates for the Bloomington metropolitan area and for Illinois statewide,
supplemented by state workforce agency labor market information and, where
the referral questions warrant it, direct labor market research such as
employer contact and job posting analysis within the evaluee's commuting
range. Bloomington, the principal city of the metro, gives those estimates
their coverage area, and the same sources support opinions for evaluees who
live in Normal and work elsewhere in the region. For each referral the
analysis turns on the questions the data can actually answer: which
occupational paths remain open after injury, which restrictions genuinely
foreclose work, and how transferable the evaluee's skills prove in the
market the estimates describe. The written opinion states what the data
supports and stops there.

## Appointments, evaluations, and travel

The firm maintains no office in Normal and publishes no local address,
because it has none; its only staffed office is the Kansas City home
office, and Normal is served by appointment. That candor is deliberate, and
it costs counsel nothing in practice. Evaluations proceed by secure video
under publisher protocols or in person in the evaluee's own community or at
counsel's offices, and when a deposition or trial date requires presence,
travel is scheduled from Kansas City against the deadlines set at intake.
Matters that combine vocational and care-planning issues with economic loss
claims may be structured as
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and life care planning work proceeds under
one engagement structure.
