## Where Morrison Cases Are Heard

State-law claims involving Morrison parties proceed in the Circuit Court,
with Whiteside County as the local forum. On the federal side, Illinois is
served by the United States District Court for the Northern District of
Illinois, the United States District Court for the Central District of
Illinois, and the United States District Court for the Southern District of
Illinois; which district governs a given filing, and whether any
venue-specific registration applies, is confirmed during the conflict check
and intake process rather than assumed. Morrison lies within the Sterling,
IL area, and the firm's [Sterling page](/locations/illinois/sterling/)
describes the regional engagement footprint while the
[Illinois page](/locations/illinois/) covers the statewide venue landscape.

## Earning Capacity in a Small-Town Market

Morrison is a small town, and small-town earning-capacity analysis demands
its own rigor. The question is never what an evaluee might earn somewhere
else in theory; it is what employment is realistically open to this person,
with these restrictions, in the labor market this community actually
belongs to. To answer it, the practice draws on Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates for Illinois and the surrounding
area definitions, supplements them with state workforce agency labor market
information, and conducts direct labor market research when published data
cannot carry the weight alone. Opinions distinguish plainly between what
the data show and what professional judgment adds, so the basis for each
step is visible to the reader.

## Appointments, Not an Office

The firm maintains no office in Morrison and lists no street address here;
saying so plainly is part of the credibility the reports depend on. Work
is remote-first: secure records exchange, video interviews where the
referral questions permit, and in-person evaluation arranged in the
evaluee's own community when the questions require it. Deposition and
trial appearances are planned from the Kansas City home office and
confirmed against the litigation calendar at intake. Where the referral
also calls for economic loss calculations,
[forensic economic damages](/services/forensic-economic-damages/) analyses
are performed and signed by independent economist partners whom the firm
coordinates, keeping the vocational, care-planning, and economic opinions
consistent without blurring who authored what.
