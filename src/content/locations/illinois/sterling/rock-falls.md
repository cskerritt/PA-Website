When injury or illness interrupts a working life in Rock Falls, the damages
question turns on matters a jury cannot resolve by intuition: what work the
evaluee can still perform, what that work actually pays where the evaluee
lives, and how the difference compounds over the remainder of a career.
Purinton Analytics addresses those questions for counsel on either side of
the caption, providing
[vocational evaluation and expert testimony](/services/vocational-expert-witness/)
and [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) in matters involving
Rock Falls evaluees. This page is part of the firm's
[Sterling area practice](/locations/illinois/sterling/) and its statewide
[Illinois practice](/locations/illinois/).

## Whiteside County Venue and the Courts Serving Rock Falls

Rock Falls sits in Whiteside County, and claims arising there are most often
tried in the Circuit Court. Reports prepared for that venue follow the same
protocol the firm applies nationally: a documented conflict check, a defined
scope, records requests tailored to the claims at issue, and a written
opinion built so that opposing counsel can trace every conclusion back to
its source. For matters that proceed federally, Illinois's federal trial
forums are the United States District Court for the Northern District of
Illinois, the United States District Court for the Central District of
Illinois, and the United States District Court for the Southern District of
Illinois; the district that controls a particular case, along with any
venue-specific qualification requirement, is identified during the conflict
check and intake process.

## A Small-City Workforce in a Regional Labor Market

Rock Falls is a small city whose workers move within the wider Sterling
area labor market, and earning-capacity opinions here must reflect that
market rather than a national abstraction. Vocational findings rest on
Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for
Illinois and for the area definitions covering the region, on state
workforce agency labor market information, and on direct labor market
research where the published series run thin, as they can in smaller
markets. Transferable skills are tested against work that actually exists
within a realistic commute of Rock Falls, not against a hypothetical
big-city job base, and every source is cited in the report so that both
sides can examine it.

## How an Evaluation Proceeds

Most engagements are handled remotely: records move through secure
channels, and interviews and standardized instruments are administered by
video under accepted protocols. When the referral questions call for
in-person evaluation of a Rock Falls evaluee, the evaluator comes to the
evaluee's community rather than requiring the evaluee to travel. The firm
keeps no office in Rock Falls and publishes no local address, because
accuracy about presence is part of the same discipline that governs its
reports; meetings are by appointment, and deposition or trial appearances
are scheduled from the Kansas City home office. Where a matter also
requires economic loss analysis, the firm offers
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.
