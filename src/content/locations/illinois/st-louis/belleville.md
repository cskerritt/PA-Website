## Venue in St. Clair County and beyond

State-court matters arising in Belleville proceed in the Circuit Court of St.
Clair County. On the federal side, Illinois offers the United States District
Court for the Northern District of Illinois, the United States District Court
for the Central District of Illinois, and the United States District Court for
the Southern District of Illinois as available forums; which district controls
a particular caption is confirmed during the conflict check and intake rather
than assumed from geography. The forum never changes the method. Every file
opens with a documented conflict screen, a scope agreed in writing, and a
records request tailored to the claims, so the written opinion rests on a
record that either side can examine and challenge. When the damages theory
requires economic modeling, the firm offers
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Reading the labor market from the Illinois side

Employability and earning capacity findings for a Belleville evaluee rest on
Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the
St. Louis metropolitan area and for Illinois statewide, supplemented by state
workforce agency labor market information and by direct labor market research
when the referral questions call for it. Because the metropolitan area is
bi-state, a Belleville worker's commutable labor market can include employers
on either side of the state line, and each report states expressly how that
commutable market was defined and why, instead of treating residence in a
large metro as the end of the inquiry. No wage or employment figure enters
the analysis without a named source that opposing counsel can pull and
verify.

## Evaluation logistics for a Belleville evaluee

Most of the work in a vocational or care-planning file is remote by design.
Records review, counsel conferences, and pre-deposition preparation are
conducted by video and telephone, which keeps a litigation schedule moving
without waiting on airfares. When the referral calls for in-person
assessment, the evaluation is arranged in the evaluee's own community rather
than requiring travel to a distant office. The firm keeps no staffed office
in Belleville and publishes no local street address, because accuracy about
presence belongs to the same discipline that governs the reports themselves.
When deposition, hearing, or trial dates require an appearance, travel is
scheduled from the Kansas City home office and planned against the operative
calendar.
