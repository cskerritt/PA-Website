## Where an O'Fallon case is heard

O'Fallon sits in St. Clair County, and claims filed in state court proceed in
the Circuit Court of St. Clair County. Federal claims have the United States
District Court for the Northern District of Illinois, the United States
District Court for the Central District of Illinois, and the United States
District Court for the Southern District of Illinois available as forums, and
the controlling venue for a given matter is identified during the conflict
check and intake rather than presumed from a map. Whatever the forum, the
report is built to the same standard: a documented conflict screen at the
front, a defined scope, and a records request matched to the pleadings, so
that every conclusion can be traced to material both parties possess.

## A commuting market larger than the town

Vocational conclusions about an O'Fallon evaluee begin with Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the St. Louis
metropolitan area and for Illinois statewide, alongside state workforce
agency labor market information and direct labor market research where the
questions warrant it. The commuting geography matters here. Workers from a
suburban community like O'Fallon routinely reach employers across the
metropolitan area, including across the state line toward St. Louis, so the
report defines the evaluee's commutable labor market on the record and
explains the definition, in terms restrictions actually documented in the
file. Qualitative context is sourced the same way as everything else, and
nothing is asserted about local employers or industries that a named,
verifiable source does not support.

## Bringing the evaluation to the evaluee

Counsel conferences, records analysis, and deposition preparation are handled
by video and telephone, so the file advances between hearing dates without
travel delays. If the referral questions require an in-person interview or
assessment, it is arranged in the evaluee's own community; nobody is asked to
drive to a distant office to be evaluated. The firm maintains no staffed
office in O'Fallon and lists no street address for this location, a matter of
candor that carries into the reports. For testimony, whether at deposition or
trial, travel is arranged from the Kansas City home office and calendared
against the case's operative deadlines.
