## Appointment-based work in the Marion-Herrin area

Meetings in Marion are by appointment. The firm keeps no staffed office in
the metro and publishes no street address for this location, because a
practice that documents every opinion should be equally exact about where it
does and does not sit. In place of a storefront, counsel gets a working
process: conflict checks answered promptly, evaluation dates fixed against
the disclosure calendar at intake, and travel from the Kansas City home
office scheduled when depositions, trial dates, or in-person evaluations
call for presence in Williamson County. Life care planning evaluations in
particular are often conducted in the evaluee's own community, where the
home environment, care arrangements, and daily demands can be observed
directly; secure video under publisher protocols is available when travel
is not warranted.

## Grounding opinions in the local labor market

Vocational findings for Marion-Herrin evaluees rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the area and for
Illinois statewide, state workforce agency labor market information, and
direct labor market research, including employer contact and job posting
analysis, when the referral questions require evidence about actual hiring
in Williamson County. Care-planning opinions are costed against sources
appropriate to the evaluee's region rather than national averages applied
uncritically, and every source is cited in the report so that opposing
counsel can trace each figure to its origin.
