Scope discipline is the organizing commitment of Purinton Analytics' work
in the Bloomington, IL metropolitan area: conflict checks are answered
promptly, scope is fixed in writing at intake, and report timelines are set
against the court's calendar rather than negotiated afterward. Counsel here
retain the firm for
[vocational evaluations](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) in both
plaintiff and defense matters, with the wider state practice described on
the [Illinois page](/locations/illinois/).

## McLean County and the courts beyond it

Engagements are accepted for matters in the Circuit Court in McLean County
and, when a case is filed or removed to federal court, throughout the United
States District Court for the Central District of Illinois, the United
States District Court for the Northern District of Illinois, and the United
States District Court for the Southern District of Illinois. The expert file
is assembled to satisfy whichever forum receives it, and deposition and
trial testimony are available in person or by remote appearance where the
court permits. Counsel on either side of the caption receive the same
methodology, the same citation practice, and the same willingness to say
what the record does not support.

## No storefront, and why that is stated

There is no staffed Purinton Analytics office in Bloomington, and the firm
publishes no street address here. That candor is intentional: a practice
whose reports depend on verifiable statements should describe its own
presence with the same accuracy. Meetings are by appointment. Evaluations
are held in the evaluee's own community, at counsel's offices, or by secure
video under publisher protocols, and when the referral questions call for
in-person evaluation, or when deposition and trial dates require presence,
travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office with the court's
schedule as the fixed point.

## The labor market behind the opinions

Vocational findings for Bloomington evaluees rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates from the OEWS program
for the Bloomington, IL metropolitan area and for Illinois statewide, joined
with state workforce agency labor market information and direct labor market
research where warranted. Employability, labor market access, and earning
capacity conclusions are expressed qualitatively where the data are
qualitative, and every source is cited in the report so cross-examination
can test the foundation directly. Matters that reach beyond McLean County
or beyond Illinois are handled through the firm's
[nationwide practice](/locations/nationwide/) without any change in
standards.

## Retaining the firm for a Bloomington case

Every engagement begins with a documented conflict check, a defined scope,
and a records request tailored to the claims. Submit the caption or party
names through the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and note any
imminent disclosure, deposition, or trial dates so the schedule can be
confirmed at intake. Any venue-specific registration or qualification
requirement is identified and confirmed during the conflict check and
intake process. For matters that require economic damages testimony, the
firm offers
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.
