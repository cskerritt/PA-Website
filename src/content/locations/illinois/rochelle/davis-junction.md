## Working Backward from the Disclosure Date

Intake for a Davis Junction matter fixes the sequence in writing: when
records must arrive, when the evaluee interview and any standardized
assessment occur, when the draft analysis is complete, and when the signed
report is delivered against the disclosure deadline. Because the schedule
is explicit, counsel can see early whether a timeline is realistic, and the
firm says so plainly when it is not. Late-arriving referrals are sometimes
better served by
