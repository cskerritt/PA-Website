An earning capacity opinion for a Byron evaluee cannot rest on national
averages alone. Vocational findings are grounded in Bureau of Labor

and for Illinois statewide, supplemented by state workforce agency labor
market information and by direct labor market research when the referral
questions warrant it. Byron belongs to the Rochelle, IL area, whose principal
city is Rochelle, and the analysis reflects the occupations realistically
open to a worker living there rather than a distant city's job mix. The

## Evaluation Logistics Without a Local Office

The firm keeps no office in Byron and publishes no street address anywhere in
Ogle County, because accuracy about presence is part of the same discipline
that governs its reports. What counsel gets instead is a defined process:
records are requested and reviewed before any interview, standardized
assessment proceeds by secure video where appropriate, and in-person
evaluation, when the questions call for it, happens in the evaluee's own
community. When deposition or trial testimony requires presence, travel is
scheduled from the Kansas City home office. The same structure serves
matters everywhere the practice works, as outlined on the
[nationwide page](/locations/nationwide/). When a case also needs a damages
figure, counsel can add
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Starting a Byron Engagement

The engagement begins with clearance, not commitment. Submit the caption or
party names through the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/), note any
pending disclosure, deposition, or trial dates, and identify whether the
referral concerns earning capacity, future care, or both. Intake confirms
scope, venue, and timeline before records are exchanged, so counsel on
either side of the caption knows exactly what the opinion will cover and
