Mount Morris is a small community, but the labor market its residents
actually work in is regional, and that distinction decides cases. When an
injured worker from Mount Morris can no longer perform past work, the
question of what remains is answered not by the employers within the
community's boundaries but by the broader Rochelle, IL area market they
commute into. Purinton Analytics builds its
[vocational opinions](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) for Mount Morris
evaluees on that regional reality, and offers the same analysis to counsel
prosecuting a claim and counsel defending one.

## Ogle County Courts and the Federal Alternatives

Before the market analysis matters, the forum does. Mount Morris matters
filed in state court proceed in the Circuit Court, and the firm's reports
are drafted to be examined there. Illinois also offers federal forums: the
United States District Court for the Northern District of Illinois, the
United States District Court for the Central District of Illinois, and the
United States District Court for the Southern District of Illinois. The
controlling venue for any particular Mount Morris case, and any
registration or qualification requirement that venue imposes, is identified
and confirmed during the conflict check and intake. Venue patterns across
the state are collected on the
[Illinois locations page](/locations/illinois/).

## Measuring a Regional Market Honestly

With the forum identified, the measurement tools are named in the report so
they can be checked. Vocational findings rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates for the relevant area and for
Illinois statewide, on state workforce agency labor market information, and
on direct labor market research where the referral questions warrant it.
For a Mount Morris evaluee that means asking what work exists within a
realistic commuting range of the community, including the principal city of
Rochelle, and whether the evaluee's residual capacities, education, and work
history give access to it. The area-wide analysis is described on the
[Rochelle metro page](/locations/illinois/rochelle/), and none of it is
reported as raw optimism: transferable skills are traced from documented
work history, and every placement conclusion is tied to identifiable
occupational data.

## Future Care Priced for Where the Evaluee Lives

The same local honesty governs
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) and
[medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/) work. A care
plan for a Mount Morris resident prices attendant care, therapies,
equipment, and replacement services as they are actually obtained by someone
living in a small Ogle County community, not as they would be obtained
downtown in a major city. Each recommended item originates in the medical
record or a treating provider's recommendation, and each cost carries a
documented source, so the plan reads as evidence rather than advocacy.

## Serving Mount Morris by Appointment

The firm maintains no office in Mount Morris and claims none; its national
practice, described on the [nationwide page](/locations/nationwide/), runs
on a remote-first model. Records review, counsel conferences, and most
interviews proceed by secure video, in-person evaluations are conducted in
the evaluee's own community when warranted, and appearance at deposition or
trial is handled through travel from the Kansas City home office. Where
lost earnings must become a damages number, the referral can include
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.
