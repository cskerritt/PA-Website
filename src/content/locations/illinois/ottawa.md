## By-appointment logistics in the Ottawa area

The firm keeps no staffed office in Ottawa and publishes no street address
for this location; accuracy about presence is part of the same discipline
that governs its reports. Rather than asking the evaluee to come to the
expert, the evaluation comes to the evaluee: sessions are held in the
evaluee's own community, at counsel's offices, or by secure video under
publisher protocols. When the referral questions call for in-person
evaluation, or when deposition and trial dates require presence, travel is
scheduled from the Kansas City home office and confirmed with counsel in
writing before dates are represented to the court.

Vocational findings for Ottawa area evaluees rest on Bureau of Labor

covering the Ottawa, IL area and Illinois statewide, along with state
workforce agency labor market information and direct labor market research

not less, and the report distinguishes clearly between published estimates

that extend past the Ottawa area proceed through the firm's
[nationwide practice](/locations/nationwide/) under the same method.
