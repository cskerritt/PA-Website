Most of what a court will ever learn about a Chatham evaluee arrives on
paper. The interview matters and the testimony matters, but the opinion
that survives scrutiny is the one whose underlying record was assembled
deliberately: medical documentation, an employment and earnings history,
education and training records, each item requested because the claims at
issue made it relevant. Purinton Analytics builds its
[vocational expert](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) and
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) work for Chatham
matters on that record-first discipline, so that counsel on either side of
the caption receives a written opinion whose every conclusion can be traced
back to a documented source. The discipline is the same whether the firm is
retained early, while the record is still being shaped, or late, when the
question is what the existing record will actually support.

## Sangamon County venue for Chatham claims

Chatham sits in Sangamon County, and state court matters proceed in the
Circuit Court under Illinois procedure. Federal claims arising in Illinois
may be heard in the United States District Court for the Northern District
of Illinois, the United States District Court for the Central District of
Illinois, or the United States District Court for the Southern District of
Illinois; which district controls a particular Chatham filing is confirmed
during the conflict check and intake, along with any venue-specific
registration or qualification requirement. The forum's disclosure rules
then govern the report's format, its stated basis, and the timing of any
supplementation.

## Sourcing the labor market analysis

Employability and earning capacity conclusions for a Chatham evaluee are
grounded in the Springfield metropolitan labor market of which the
community is a part. The firm relies on Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates for the Springfield
metropolitan area and for Illinois statewide, together with state
workforce agency labor market information and direct labor market research
where the referral questions call for it. The
[Springfield metro page](/locations/illinois/springfield/) sets out the
regional method in more detail. No figure enters a report without a
citation, and the same sourcing standard governs when the firm is asked to
test an opposing expert's analysis through
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) work.

## Evaluation logistics in Chatham

The firm maintains no Chatham office and publishes no local address;
meetings are by appointment, and stating that plainly is part of the same
accuracy the reports themselves are held to. Vocational interviews and
life care planning assessments usually proceed by secure video, and
in-person evaluation in the evaluee's own community is arranged when the
impairments or the referral questions require direct observation.
Deposition and trial appearances are supported by travel scheduled from
the Kansas City home office and confirmed with counsel well ahead of the
date. Where the case also needs economic loss testimony, counsel can add
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

Chatham referrals draw on the national practice described on the
