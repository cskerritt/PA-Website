## The county's forums

State court matters proceed in the Circuit Court under Illinois procedure.
Where a Riverton case raises federal claims, Illinois presents the United
States District Court for the Northern District of Illinois, the United
States District Court for the Central District of Illinois, and the United
States District Court for the Southern District of Illinois as its
available forums; the controlling district for any particular filing, and
any qualification requirement attached to it, is settled during the
conflict check and intake rather than inferred from location. Testimony is
given in person or, where the presiding court allows, by remote appearance,
with report formats matched to the governing disclosure rules.

## A village workforce, a metropolitan market

Riverton's workers participate in the Springfield regional economy, and
vocational conclusions about a Riverton evaluee are therefore benchmarked
against it. The firm relies on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational
employment and wage estimates for the Springfield metropolitan area and
for Illinois statewide, state workforce agency labor market information,
and direct labor market research where the referral questions call for it,
following the regional method laid out on the
[Springfield metro page](/locations/illinois/springfield/). Sourcing is
absolute: a figure without a citation does not go in the report, whether
the report supports a claim or rebuts an opposing expert through
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) work.

## Practical arrangements

Purinton Analytics maintains no Riverton office and lists no address in the
village; representing otherwise would sit poorly with a practice built on
verifiable statements. Evaluations are conducted by secure video or, when
warranted by the impairments at issue, in person in the community where
the evaluee actually lives, so that functional observations reflect real
conditions rather than an unfamiliar clinical setting. Travel for evaluations, depositions, and trial
is arranged from the Kansas City home office and confirmed with counsel
well before the date at issue. For cases needing a full
damages picture, the firm offers
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning components proceed under
one engagement structure.
