Beneath every Pawnee case sits the same short set of questions: what work
the person at its center can still perform, what that work would actually
pay, and what care the injuries will demand over a lifetime. Verdicts and
settlements move on the answers, and the answers are only as good as the
method behind them. Purinton Analytics exists to answer those questions
defensibly, providing
[vocational expert](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) and
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) opinions to counsel
handling Pawnee matters on either side of the caption, with every
conclusion documented, sourced, and written in the expectation that a
skilled opponent will probe it. When the record cannot support an answer,
the firm says so, because an honest limitation is worth more to counsel
than a conclusion that collapses at deposition.

## Forum questions come before opinion questions

Venue is treated as something to be confirmed, never assumed. A Pawnee
claim filed in state court proceeds in the Circuit Court, the trial forum
for Sangamon County under Illinois procedure. Federal practice in Illinois
is divided among the United States District Court for the Northern
District of Illinois, the United States District Court for the Central
District of Illinois, and the United States District Court for the
Southern District of Illinois; rather than guessing which of those will
hold a particular filing, intake settles it with counsel as part of the
conflict check, together with any registration or qualification
requirement the venue imposes. Only then are the report's format, its
disclosure content, and its supplementation plan fixed, because those
choices belong to the forum, not to the expert.

## Answering the earning capacity question

What a Pawnee evaluee can earn is not measured against the village alone,
since its residents participate in the wider Springfield regional economy,
and the analysis reflects that reality. Employability and earning capacity
opinions draw on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and
wage estimates for the Springfield metropolitan area and for Illinois
statewide, on state workforce agency labor market information, and on
direct labor market research when the referral questions require it,
following the regional method described on the
[Springfield metro page](/locations/illinois/springfield/). Where the case
also needs the economic loss question answered, counsel can add
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure, so the experts share records and assumptions instead
of contradicting each other.

## Answering the care question where the evaluee lives

Life care planning turns on what treatment and support the evaluee will
actually need and can realistically obtain, so assessment is organized
around the person rather than around an office. The firm keeps no office
in Pawnee and would never claim one; appointments are how the firm meets
everyone, everywhere it works. Vocational interviews and care-planning
assessments ordinarily proceed by secure video, which keeps the cost of
the work proportionate to the case, and when direct observation matters,
the evaluation is conducted in person in the evaluee's own community.
Depositions, trial testimony, and any other setting that requires the firm
in the room are supported by travel arranged from the Kansas City home
office and confirmed with counsel well ahead of the date.
