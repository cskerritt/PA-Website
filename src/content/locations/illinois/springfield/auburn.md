The person at the center of an Auburn case does not stop being an Auburn
resident when litigation begins, and a credible vocational or life care
planning opinion has to account for that. An injured worker or disabled
claimant living in this small Sangamon County city faces treatment
logistics, transportation realities, and job search conditions that differ
from those of an evaluee in the principal city, and Purinton Analytics
builds its [vocational expert](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) and
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) opinions around the
evaluee's actual circumstances rather than a generic metropolitan profile.

## How an Auburn evaluee is assessed

Assessment begins with the record: medical documentation, employment
history, and educational background gathered through a records request
tailored to the claims. The vocational interview itself is usually
conducted by secure video, which spares an evaluee with mobility or
transportation limitations an unnecessary trip; when the impairments or the
referral questions justify it, the evaluation takes place in person in the
evaluee's own community. Life care planning assessments follow the same
principle, grounding future care recommendations in the treatment resources
an Auburn resident can realistically reach. In-person meetings and
testimony dates are supported by travel scheduled from the Kansas City home
office. Counsel for plaintiffs and counsel for defendants receive precisely
the same process, the same protocols, and the same willingness to say when
the record does not support an opinion.

## County, courts, and controlling venue

Auburn matters filed in state court proceed in the Circuit Court, Sangamon
County's trial forum under Illinois procedure. On the federal side,
Illinois cases may proceed in the United States District Court for the
Northern District of Illinois, the United States District Court for the
Central District of Illinois, or the United States District Court for the
Southern District of Illinois; identifying which district governs a
particular Auburn claim is part of the conflict check and intake, as is any
venue-specific qualification requirement. The finished report conforms to
the disclosure standard of the forum that actually holds the case.

## The labor market question

An Auburn evaluee's earning capacity is tested against the wider region.
Opinions draw on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and
wage estimates for the Springfield metropolitan area and for Illinois
statewide, together with state workforce agency labor market information
and direct labor market research where warranted, as outlined on the
[Springfield metro page](/locations/illinois/springfield/). When the case
also requires economic loss calculations, the firm coordinates
[a combined damages engagement](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.
