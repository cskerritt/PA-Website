An evaluation is only as good as the evaluee's participation in it, and for
many Waukegan claimants a long trip to an unfamiliar office is a real
barrier. Purinton Analytics removes that barrier in either of the usual
ways: the [vocational interview](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) and
[life care planning assessment](/services/life-care-planning/) are
conducted by secure video under publisher protocols, or in person in the
evaluee's own community in Waukegan, so the record reflects the person
rather than the logistics. Counsel for plaintiffs and defendants engage the
firm on identical terms.

## Lake County venue notes

Waukegan lies in Lake County, and Illinois state court matters there
proceed in the Circuit Court. Federal litigation in the state is spread
across the United States District Court for the Northern District of
Illinois, the United States District Court for the Central District of
Illinois, and the United States District Court for the Southern District of
Illinois; which district holds a particular Waukegan claim is a question
the firm answers during the conflict check and intake instead of assuming.
Reports conform to the disclosure requirements of the confirmed forum, and
testimony is delivered in person or remotely as the court permits.

## Labor market evidence, not labor market lore

Waukegan is a substantial city within the Chicago metropolitan region, and
its residents' employment prospects are analyzed against that regional
market, whose overall treatment appears on the
[Chicago metro page](/locations/illinois/chicago/). The evidence base is
consistent from case to case: Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational
employment and wage estimates for the Chicago metropolitan area and for
Illinois, state workforce agency labor market information, and direct
research such as employer contact or job posting review when a specific
placement question needs a specific answer. Opinions state what the data
show and stop there, which is also the standard applied when reviewing an
opposing expert's work in a
[rebuttal or peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) engagement.

## By appointment, and honestly so

The firm does not operate an office in Waukegan, staffs no storefront, and
publishes no local address, because a practice that certifies the accuracy
of its reports should be equally accurate about its footprint. Meetings
with counsel are by appointment, most collaboration is remote, and when a
deposition, trial date, or in-person assessment requires presence, travel
comes from the Kansas City home office on a schedule fixed against the
case's disclosure calendar at intake.

or party names through the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/), noting
imminent deadlines. Statewide availability is covered on the
[Illinois page](/locations/illinois/), and the
