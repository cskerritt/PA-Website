Not every Arlington Heights case needs the same expert work, and sorting
that out early is half the value of the intake call. A contested return to
work points toward a
[vocational expert opinion](/services/vocational-expert-witness/); a
catastrophic injury adds [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/);
a disputed damages model may call for
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) of the other
side's expert. Purinton Analytics helps Arlington Heights counsel, whether
retained for the plaintiff or the defense, define that scope before any
records move, so the engagement fits the case instead of inflating it.

## Venue considerations in Cook County

Arlington Heights is located in Cook County, where state court claims
proceed in the Circuit Court. At the federal level, Illinois litigants may
find themselves in the United States District Court for the Northern
District of Illinois, the United States District Court for the Central
District of Illinois, or the United States District Court for the Southern
District of Illinois; the firm identifies the controlling district for each
matter during the conflict check and intake, and shapes its disclosures to
the confirmed court. Deposition and trial testimony are offered in person
or by remote appearance where the presiding court permits it.

## Earning capacity against a regional backdrop

An Arlington Heights evaluee competes in the labor market of the wider
Chicago metropolitan area, and opinions about that competition are built
from evidence rather than impression: Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates for the Chicago metropolitan
area and for Illinois statewide, state workforce agency labor market
information, and direct labor market research where a question demands
proof of actual hiring. The metro-level methodology is described on the
[Chicago page](/locations/illinois/chicago/); each Arlington Heights
referral narrows it to a single worker's restrictions, transferable
skills, and realistic options.

## Scheduling and evaluation logistics

Arlington Heights meetings are by appointment. The firm neither staffs nor
claims an office in the village, on the view that a practice built on
verifiable reports should have a verifiable footprint. Evaluations proceed
by secure video under publisher protocols or in person in the evaluee's own
community; records are exchanged securely; and where an assessment,
deposition, or trial requires attendance, travel is planned from the Kansas
City home office around the dates locked in at intake.

Every file starts the same way: the

analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. The [Illinois page](/locations/illinois/) covers the
