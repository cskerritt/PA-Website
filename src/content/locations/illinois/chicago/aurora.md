Aurora is one of the largest cities in Illinois in its own right, and the
injury, employment, and wrongful death cases that arise there deserve expert
support scaled to that reality rather than borrowed from a downtown Chicago
template. Purinton Analytics provides
[vocational expert](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) and
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) services to Aurora
counsel on either side of the caption, with the same documented methodology
described on the [Chicago metro page](/locations/illinois/chicago/) applied
to the specific record of each Aurora claim.

## Kane County venue context

Aurora sits in Kane County, and state court matters proceed in the Circuit
Court under Illinois procedure. Federal claims from Illinois reach the firm
through the United States District Court for the Northern District of
Illinois, the United States District Court for the Central District of
Illinois, and the United States District Court for the Southern District of
Illinois; which of those forums controls a given case is confirmed during
the conflict check and intake rather than assumed from geography. Reports
are prepared to the disclosure standards of whichever court holds the
matter, and testimony is offered in person or by remote appearance where the
court permits.

## A workforce that crosses the metro

An Aurora evaluee rarely competes for work only inside city limits. The
relevant labor market usually spans the wider Chicago metropolitan area,
and the analysis treats it that way. Employability and earning capacity
opinions rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage
estimates for the Chicago metropolitan area and for Illinois statewide,
supplemented by state workforce agency labor market information and, when
the referral questions warrant it, direct labor market research such as job
posting analysis. No figure appears in a report without a cited source, and
the same discipline governs
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) work when an
opposing expert's market assumptions need testing.

## Evaluating an Aurora evaluee

Meetings in Aurora are by appointment only. The firm maintains no staffed
office there and publishes no local address, because candor about presence
is part of the credibility it sells. What counsel gets instead is
flexibility: vocational interviews and life care planning assessments can be
conducted by secure video under publisher protocols, or in person in the
evaluee's own community when the case calls for it. When depositions, trial
dates, or in-person evaluations require presence, travel is scheduled from
the Kansas City home office, and the timeline is set against the disclosure
calendar identified at intake.

Catastrophic Aurora cases often need more than a single discipline, and the
firm structures
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)

analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. Whatever the scope, the sequence is the same: submit
the caption or party names through the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/), note any imminent disclosure or
trial deadlines, and expect a prompt answer with CV and fee information.
