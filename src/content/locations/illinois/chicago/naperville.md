## DuPage County and the available federal forums

Naperville lies in DuPage County, where state court claims proceed in the
Circuit Court. On the federal side, Illinois offers the United States
District Court for the Northern District of Illinois, the United States
District Court for the Central District of Illinois, and the United States
District Court for the Southern District of Illinois as potential forums;
the venue that actually controls a Naperville matter is pinned down during
the conflict check and intake, not presumed. Opinions are drafted to survive
scrutiny in any of them, with methodology, sources, and reasoning laid out

## Where the evaluation happens

There is no Purinton Analytics office in Naperville, and the firm says so

secure video under publisher protocols or in person in the evaluee's
