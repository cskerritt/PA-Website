When an Elgin worker is hurt, the loss is rarely confined to a job in
Elgin. Many residents earn their living somewhere else in the metropolitan
region, and an injury that limits driving, standing, or sustained
concentration can shrink a commuting radius as surely as it shrinks a task
list. Purinton Analytics builds its Elgin
[vocational opinions](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) around that
reality, asking not only what work exists but what work remains reachable
for this evaluee, on this record, with these restrictions. Plaintiff and
defense counsel receive the same analysis.

## County and courthouse picture

Elgin is a Kane County city, and state law claims proceed in the Circuit
Court. For federal matters, Illinois counsel choose among the United States
District Court for the Northern District of Illinois, the United States
District Court for the Central District of Illinois, and the United States
District Court for the Southern District of Illinois; the district that
governs any specific Elgin case is identified during the conflict check and
intake rather than asserted in advance. Whatever the forum, the written
opinion follows its disclosure rules, and testimony can be given live or by
remote appearance where the court allows.

## Measuring a commuter's labor market

Because Elgin sits within one of the largest labor markets in the country,
the data foundation is broad: Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational
employment and wage estimates for the Chicago metropolitan area and for
Illinois statewide, state workforce agency labor market information, and
job posting or employer research where the questions turn on actual hiring.
The metro-wide methodology is set out on the
[Chicago page](/locations/illinois/chicago/); at the town level the task is
narrower and harder, separating what the regional market offers in theory
from what this claimant can obtain in practice. That distinction is often
where an opposing report fails, and it is the focus of
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) assignments.

## How an Elgin evaluation proceeds

Elgin meetings are by appointment; the firm has no staffed location there
and does not pretend otherwise. Most engagements begin remotely, with
records exchanged through secure channels and interviews conducted by
secure video under publisher protocols. When the case needs an in-person
assessment, the evaluation is held in the evaluee's community, and travel
is arranged from the Kansas City home office, timed to the deposition and
disclosure dates confirmed at intake.
[Life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) referrals follow the
same path, from records review through costed plan.
