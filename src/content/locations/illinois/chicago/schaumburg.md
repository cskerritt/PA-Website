Schaumburg referrals reach Purinton Analytics from both directions: counsel
proving a loss and counsel testing one. The firm considers that balance a
feature of the practice, not a complication, because an
[employability and earning capacity analysis](/services/vocational-expert-witness/)
that only works for one side is not an analysis at all. Whether the
assignment is an affirmative opinion, a
[life care plan](/services/life-care-planning/), or a
[review of the opposing expert's report](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/),
the method and the candor are identical.

## Where Schaumburg cases are heard

Schaumburg sits in Cook County, whose state court claims proceed in the
Circuit Court. Illinois also offers federal forums in the United States
District Court for the Northern District of Illinois, the United States
District Court for the Central District of Illinois, and the United States
District Court for the Southern District of Illinois; instead of declaring
which district a Schaumburg dispute belongs to, the firm confirms the
controlling venue during the conflict check and intake and prepares
disclosures to that court's standard. Testimony is available at deposition
and trial, in person or by remote appearance where permitted.

## A suburb inside a very large market

For labor market purposes, a Schaumburg evaluee is a participant in the
broader Chicago metropolitan economy, one of the largest in the nation, and
the analysis reflects that breadth without being seduced by it. Findings
are anchored in Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage
estimates for the Chicago metropolitan area and for Illinois statewide,
supported by state workforce agency labor market information and by direct
labor market research when the case turns on whether specific work is
genuinely open to a specific person. The regional framework behind these
town-level applications is laid out on the
[Chicago metro page](/locations/illinois/chicago/).

## Practical arrangements for counsel and evaluees

The firm holds Schaumburg meetings by appointment only and neither
maintains nor advertises an office there; stating that plainly is part of
the same discipline the reports themselves follow. Day to day, engagements
run remotely, with secure records transfer and interviews by secure video
under publisher protocols. In-person needs, whether an evaluation in the
evaluee's community, a deposition, or trial testimony, are met by travel
from the Kansas City home office, scheduled around the dates confirmed
when the file opens.

## From conflict check to opinion

Intake follows a fixed order: conflict check, scope, records, analysis,
report. Submit the caption or party names through the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and identify any disclosure or trial
dates already set. Where full economic damages are in play, the firm
arranges
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. Coverage across the state appears on the
[Illinois page](/locations/illinois/), and the
[nationwide page](/locations/nationwide/) describes the remote-first
practice that supports it.
