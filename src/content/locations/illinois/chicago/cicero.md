Every opinion Purinton Analytics writes for a Cicero case is written on the
assumption that the other side will read it line by line. That assumption
disciplines the work: a defined scope agreed at intake, a records request
tailored to the claims actually pleaded, and a
[vocational](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) or
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) report in which every
conclusion traces to a cited source. Cicero counsel on either side of the
caption receive the same product, because the methodology does not change
with the client.

## Cook County courts serving Cicero

Cicero is a Cook County community, and its state court litigation proceeds
in the Circuit Court. Illinois federal practice offers the United States
District Court for the Northern District of Illinois, the United States
District Court for the Central District of Illinois, and the United States
District Court for the Southern District of Illinois as available forums;
the firm treats the controlling venue for any Cicero matter as an intake
question, confirmed during the conflict check rather than inferred. Once
the forum is fixed, the disclosure format follows it, and testimony is
provided live or by remote appearance where the court allows.

## Cicero workers in a metropolitan market

As part of one of the country's largest labor markets, Cicero produces
evaluees whose employment histories draw on the full metropolitan economy. The firm's regional framework is described on the
[Chicago metro page](/locations/illinois/chicago/); in an individual Cicero
case it is applied through Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational
employment and wage estimates for the Chicago metropolitan area and for
Illinois, state workforce agency labor market information, and, where the
dispute requires it, direct labor market research into actual openings and
hiring practices. Wage stratification in a market this deep can cut for or
against either party, which is precisely why the analysis measures instead
of assumes.

## Evaluation without a storefront

There is no Purinton Analytics office in Cicero. Meetings are by
appointment, and the firm declines to publish an address it does not staff,
treating honesty about presence as an extension of report accuracy.
Interviews and assessments run by secure video under publisher protocols or
in person in the evaluee's community; records travel by secure transfer;
and when presence is needed for an evaluation, deposition, or trial, travel
is scheduled from the Kansas City home office against the deadlines
documented at intake. Narrower care-cost questions can be handled through
[medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/).

## Submitting a Cicero referral

For damages models that require an economist, the firm structures
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. The path in is the same for every matter: send the
caption or party names through the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/)
with any court-set dates, and clearance, CV, and fees follow quickly. See
the [Illinois page](/locations/illinois/) for statewide coverage and the
[national page](/locations/nationwide/) for the firm's full geographic
