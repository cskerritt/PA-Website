Where the expert sits matters far less to an Evanston case than what the
expert can document. Records review, the vocational interview, labor market
research, and report drafting all happen wherever the file is, which is why
Purinton Analytics runs Evanston engagements remote-first: secure records
transfer, interviews by secure video under publisher protocols, and
[written opinions](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) that carry their
support with them. Presence is reserved for the moments that genuinely
require it, and both plaintiff and defense counsel retain the firm on the
same footing.

## The courts behind an Evanston caption

Evanston belongs to Cook County, and state law claims proceed in the
Circuit Court. Federal options in Illinois consist of the United States
District Court for the Northern District of Illinois, the United States
District Court for the Central District of Illinois, and the United States
District Court for the Southern District of Illinois; determining which of
these forums controls an Evanston matter is part of the conflict check and
intake, and the firm does not shortcut that determination. The confirmed
venue then dictates disclosure format, and testimony follows the court's
practice, live or by remote appearance where allowed.

## Reading the labor market for an Evanston evaluee

Evanston is a sizable community within the Chicago metropolitan region, so

[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) and

dispute is about future care rather than future work.

## When presence is worth the trip

The firm has no Evanston office and does not imply one; meetings are by
appointment. What justifies travel is the case itself: an in-person
evaluation conducted in the evaluee's community, a deposition, or trial
testimony. Those trips originate at the Kansas City home office and are
booked against the disclosure calendar established at intake, so counsel
knows early which dates involve the expert in the room and which proceed
by video.
