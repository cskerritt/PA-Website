Ask which side retains Purinton Analytics in Rockton cases and the answer is
both, on identical terms. The firm's vocational expert and life care
planning opinions are built to read the same whether the retaining lawyer
represents the injured worker or the defendant, and in a village the size of
Rockton, where counsel on opposite sides of a case may well meet again in
the next one, that consistency is worth more than advocacy. The opinion says
what the record supports, and stops there.

## Why Retention Side Does Not Change the Opinion

Methodology is fixed before the engagement begins: a documented conflict
check, a written scope, a records request tailored to the claims, review
before interview, and citations for every data point. A defense referral
gets no softer plan and a plaintiff referral gets no rounder numbers,
because the same report may be examined by the retaining side's opponent
today and by that opponent's future client tomorrow. This is the discipline
described across the firm's [Illinois practice](/locations/illinois/), and
Rockton matters receive it in full.
