A vocational opinion is only as portable as the record beneath it, and for
South Beloit cases that principle does the heavy lifting. Purinton Analytics
runs a records-first practice: medical files, employment and earnings
histories, educational documents, and prior expert reports are gathered and
read before any conclusion is formed, so the written opinion that reaches
counsel in a South Beloit matter can travel to mediation, deposition, or
trial without changing shape. Plaintiff and defense referrals are handled
under the same protocol, because the protocol is the point.

## From Records Request to Written Opinion

Every engagement opens with a documented conflict check and a defined
scope, followed by a records request tailored to the claims actually pled.
Gaps in the file are flagged to counsel early rather than papered over
later. Only after the record is complete does the evaluee interview occur,
in the person's own community or by secure video, with standardized
vocational testing added when the referral questions require it. The
resulting report shows its work: methodology, sources, and reasoning are
laid out for the other side to test.

## Labor Market Evidence, Sourced and Qualitative

Earning capacity findings for South Beloit evaluees are anchored to Bureau
of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the
Rockford metropolitan area and for Illinois statewide, augmented by state
workforce agency labor market information and, where warranted, direct
contact with regional employers. Published data is preferred, deviations
are explained, and no figure enters the report without a citation an
opposing expert could pull and check.

## Care Plans and Coordinated Economics

Life care planning for a South Beloit matter follows the same evidentiary
discipline: future care items come from the medical record and treating
providers, and each is priced from documented cost sources. Where a full
damages presentation needs economic loss analysis, counsel can engage the
firm's [coordinated damages
service](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/), under which independent
economist partners perform and sign the economic opinions while the
vocational and care-planning work runs in parallel. The firm keeps no
staffed office in South Beloit; meetings are by appointment, and testimony
travel originates from the Kansas City home office.
