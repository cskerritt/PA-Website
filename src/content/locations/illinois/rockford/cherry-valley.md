When a Cherry Valley family confronts a catastrophic injury, the central
evidentiary document in the case is often the life care plan, and a plan
built on templates collapses under cross-examination. Purinton Analytics
prepares life care plans for Cherry Valley matters the demanding way: every
item of future care originates in the medical record or a treating
provider's recommendation, every cost is drawn from a documented source, and
the whole plan is written so that opposing counsel can trace each line to
its basis. Vocational expert opinions for the village are held to the same
standard, and both are available to either side of the caption.

## Anatomy of a Defensible Care Plan

The plan begins with the record: treatment history, current providers,
stated restrictions, and prognosis as the physicians describe it. From
there, categories of future care are identified, each tied to a medical
foundation, and priced from published and verifiable cost sources with
attention to where a Cherry Valley resident could actually obtain the
recommended care. Nothing is padded for effect and nothing is trimmed for
convenience; the same document must survive review by the retaining side's
opponent.

## Winnebago County Venue and Federal Alternatives

Cherry Valley is a Winnebago County village, and its state-court civil
matters are heard in the Circuit Court. Illinois federal litigation
proceeds, depending on the case, in the United States District Court for
the Northern District of Illinois, the United States District Court for the
Central District of Illinois, or the United States District Court for the
Southern District of Illinois; the controlling venue for any Cherry Valley
matter is settled during the conflict check and intake. Counsel comparing
forums statewide can consult the [Illinois page](/locations/illinois/).
