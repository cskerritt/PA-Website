Few Roscoe residents work only in Roscoe. Like many communities in the
[Rockford metro](/locations/illinois/rockford/), the
village sends much of its workforce outward each morning, and any honest earning
capacity analysis has to follow those commuting patterns rather than stop at
the village limits. Purinton Analytics builds its Roscoe vocational opinions
around that reality, defining the relevant labor market by where an evaluee
could reasonably work, and it accepts these referrals from plaintiff and
defense counsel on equal terms.

## Commuting Range as the Unit of Analysis

The transferable skills analysis for a Roscoe evaluee starts with the
person's work history and restrictions, then maps them onto occupations
across the regional market. The qualitative foundation comes from Bureau of
Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Rockford
metropolitan area and for Illinois statewide, alongside state workforce
agency labor market information. Where a referral question is narrower, such
as whether a particular accommodation is realistic with area employers,
direct labor market research supplements the published data. Every source is
cited; nothing rests on the expert's say-so.

## Venue for Roscoe Disputes

Roscoe is part of Winnebago County, so state civil actions arising there
are heard in the Circuit Court. Federally, Illinois presents the United
States District Court for the Northern District of Illinois, the United
States District Court for the Central District of Illinois, and the United
States District Court for the Southern District of Illinois as available
forums; the venue controlling a specific Roscoe matter is confirmed during
the conflict check and intake. Statewide venue notes appear on the
[Illinois page](/locations/illinois/).

## Life Care Planning Without Shortcuts

For catastrophic injuries, the care plan is assembled the slow way: from the
medical record, from treating provider recommendations, and from documented
cost sources for each item of future care. A plan for a Roscoe evaluee
accounts for where recommended treatment can realistically be obtained,
and it is written so that either side of the caption could hand it to a
reviewing expert without embarrassment. When lifetime costs must be reduced
to present value, the firm arranges [coordinated damages
engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) in which the
economic analysis is performed and signed by independent economist partners
rather than by the vocational expert.

## Logistics, Stated Plainly

There is no Purinton Analytics office in Roscoe, and the firm will not
imply one into existence. Evaluations happen in the evaluee's community or
over secure video; counsel meetings are by appointment; and when a
deposition or trial requires the expert in the room, travel is scheduled
from the Kansas City home office with the case calendar in view. This
by-appointment model is the same one the practice uses
[across the country](/locations/nationwide/), which keeps the local work
consistent with the national standard.

## Getting a Roscoe Matter Cleared

The first step is always the [conflict check](/refer-a-case/): send the
caption or the parties' names, note any dates already fixed for disclosure
or testimony, and the firm will confirm clearance and propose a scope. A
defined scope at the outset is what keeps the eventual opinion narrow,
supportable, and useful.
