should not have to travel far, or at all, to be properly evaluated. Purinton

interviews and vocational testing are brought to the person's own community

## Earning Capacity in the Surrounding Market

A residual earning capacity opinion for a Machesney Park evaluee has to
describe work that person can actually reach, which means the analysis looks
at the Rockford area labor market as a whole rather than at village
boundaries. The firm relies on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational
employment and wage estimates for the Rockford metropolitan area and for
Illinois statewide, together with state workforce agency labor market
information, and adds direct employer contact when the referral question
turns on whether a specific job actually exists in the region. Sources are
cited so both sides can verify them.

## How the Evaluation Proceeds

After clearance and a defined scope, the sequence is fixed: records first,
then the clinical interview, then standardized testing where indicated, then
the written opinion. Because there is no staffed office in Machesney Park,
and the firm does not pretend otherwise, meetings with counsel run by
appointment and testimony travel is arranged from the Kansas City home
office. Life care planning follows the same discipline, with each item of
future care tied to the medical record and to documented cost sources
rather than to a template. When the damages picture requires present-value
economics, [coordinated damages
engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) bring in independent
economist partners who perform and sign the economic analysis within the
same engagement structure.
