A vocational opinion is only as strong as the questions it was scoped to
answer, so Purinton Analytics begins every Springfield engagement by
defining, in writing, what will be evaluated and what will not. Within that
scope the firm delivers
[vocational evaluations](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) of
employability and earning capacity,
[life care plans](/services/life-care-planning/) in catastrophic injury
matters, and [rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) of
opposing expert reports for counsel in Sangamon County and Menard County.
Plaintiff and defense retention are accepted on the same terms, and the
Springfield, IL metropolitan area is served as part of the firm's
[Illinois practice](/locations/illinois/).

## The Springfield venue footprint

Engagements are accepted for matters in the Circuit Court in Sangamon
County and Menard County, and in federal court throughout the state: the
United States District Court for the Central District of Illinois, the
United States District Court for the Northern District of Illinois, and the
United States District Court for the Southern District of Illinois. Reports
are prepared with the disclosure requirements of the receiving forum in
mind, and testimony is available in person or by remote appearance where the
court permits. Because the underlying analysis does not change with the
forum, a removal, transfer, or refiling leaves the expert work intact.

## Appointment-based service, honestly described

Purinton Analytics maintains no staffed office in Springfield and publishes
no street address for this location. The firm states that plainly because
accuracy about presence belongs to the same discipline that governs its
written opinions. Meetings here are by appointment, and evaluation sessions
are conducted in the evaluee's own community, at counsel's offices, or by
secure video under publisher protocols. When the referral questions call for
in-person evaluation, or when deposition and trial dates require presence,
travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office and confirmed with
counsel in advance so court dates are never left exposed.

## Labor market evidence for Springfield evaluees

Vocational findings for Springfield area evaluees rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates from the OEWS program
for the Springfield, IL metropolitan area and for Illinois statewide, along
with state workforce agency labor market information and direct labor market
research where warranted. Transferable skills, labor market access, and
placeability conclusions are tied to occupations documented in the record
and to sources cited in the report itself, so both sides of the caption can
audit the path from data to opinion. When a Springfield matter involves
parties or labor markets elsewhere, the firm's
[nationwide practice](/locations/nationwide/) applies the same method in any
venue.

## How to retain the firm for a Springfield matter

To begin a local or national engagement,
[request a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) with the case caption and
parties, and identify any disclosure, deposition, or trial dates that bear
on scheduling. Every file opens with a documented conflict check, a defined
scope, and a records request tailored to the claims, and any venue-specific
registration or qualification requirement is identified and confirmed during
the conflict check and intake process. Where economic damages are in issue,
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
are available, in which independent economist partners perform and sign the
economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under
one engagement structure.
