## Presence and travel

By-appointment service means the engagement is designed to need presence
only where presence adds value. Records move by secure transfer, counsel
conferences and most interviews run over secure video, and
[testimony and litigation consulting](/services/expert-testimony-litigation-consulting/)
support continues remotely between court dates. When an in-person
evaluation in the evaluee's community is warranted, or when a deposition or
trial requires attendance, the firm travels from its Kansas City home
office on a schedule set by the deadlines counsel identifies at intake, not
by convenience.
