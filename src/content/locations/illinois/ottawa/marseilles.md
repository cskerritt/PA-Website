An injured person in Marseilles should not have to organize their recovery
around an expert's travel preferences. Purinton Analytics structures its
Marseilles engagements around the evaluee: interviews at times that work
around treatment schedules, assessments conducted by secure video or in
person in the community where the person actually lives, and a
[life care plan](/services/life-care-planning/) built from that person's
documented medical needs rather than from a template. The same
evaluee-centered process serves defense counsel testing a claimed level of
need as well as plaintiff counsel proving one.

## Evaluating close to home

The firm has no Marseilles office and does not imply one; by-appointment
service is stated plainly because credibility on small facts protects
credibility on large ones. For questions narrower than a full plan, a
standalone [medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/) can
price the disputed items of future care. Records flow through secure
transfer, most conferences happen remotely, and when the work requires
hands-on presence, whether for evaluation, deposition, or trial, the trip is
arranged from the Kansas City home office and slotted against the case
deadlines counsel supplies at intake.
