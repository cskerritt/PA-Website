Whichever side of a Spring Valley caption retains Purinton Analytics, the
work product is the same. That is not a slogan but a constraint the firm
imposes on itself: the [vocational](/services/vocational-expert-witness/)
methodology, the data sources, and the reporting standards do not bend
toward the retaining party, because an opinion that would change with the
client is not an expert opinion. For counsel in this Bureau County
community, that constraint is the point; it is what makes the report worth
disclosing.

## State and federal venues

A Spring Valley dispute filed in the Illinois courts proceeds in the
Circuit Court serving Bureau County. Federal jurisdiction in the state runs
through the United States District Court for the Northern District of
Illinois, the United States District Court for the Central District of
Illinois, and the United States District Court for the Southern District of
Illinois, each an available forum; the district that actually governs a
Spring Valley filing is identified during the conflict check and intake and
never assumed in advance. Disclosure formatting, report timing, and the
mechanics of testimony, live or remote where allowed, all follow from that
venue determination.

## Labor market evidence for Spring Valley evaluees

The employment questions in a Spring Valley case reach past the town into
the surrounding regional economy, and the firm answers them with published,
checkable sources: Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and
wage estimates for the area and for Illinois statewide, state workforce
agency labor market information, and direct labor market research where the
dispute demands evidence about particular jobs. The
[Ottawa metro page](/locations/illinois/ottawa/) explains the regional
framework shared by the nearby communities. When the other side's expert
has already characterized this market, counsel can also commission a
[rebuttal or peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) that tests those
conclusions against the same published record.

## Appointments, not an office

Spring Valley has no Purinton Analytics office, and the firm will not
manufacture the appearance of one. What counsel gets instead is a portable,
documented process: records exchanged over secure channels, interviews and
assessments conducted by secure video or brought in person to the community
where the evaluee lives, and conferences with counsel held remotely so the
schedule serves the case rather than the geography. Travel for in-person
evaluation, deposition, or trial originates at the Kansas City home office
and is booked around the deadlines recorded when the engagement opens.

## The conflict check comes before anything else

Where the damages theory needs an economist, the firm assembles
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. No substantive discussion of a Spring Valley matter
happens before clearance, so the practical way to start is simple:
[submit a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) with the caption or party names
and any dates the court has already fixed. For the firm's broader coverage,
see the [Illinois page](/locations/illinois/) and the
[nationwide page](/locations/nationwide/).
