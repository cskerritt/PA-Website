Most of what makes a vocational or care-planning opinion reliable happens on
paper: medical records read closely, work histories verified against
documents, published labor market data checked line by line. That is why
Purinton Analytics can serve Mendota well without being based there. For
this LaSalle County community, the firm delivers
[vocational expert](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) and
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) opinions assembled from
the record itself, so that plaintiff and defense counsel alike can trace
every conclusion back to a source they can inspect.

## Venue context for Mendota litigation

Mendota's state court disputes are heard in the Circuit Court serving
LaSalle County. At the federal level, the available Illinois forums are the
United States District Court for the Northern District of Illinois, the
United States District Court for the Central District of Illinois, and the
United States District Court for the Southern District of Illinois. Rather
than presuming which district a Mendota case belongs to, the firm settles
venue as part of the conflict check and intake, and only then locks the
report format to the receiving court's disclosure standards. Where a court
permits remote testimony, the firm appears that way; where it requires
presence, the firm travels.

## Reading the Mendota labor market

For a Mendota evaluee, the operative labor market is regional, and the firm
reads it through published evidence: Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates for the area and for Illinois
statewide, state workforce agency labor market information, and direct
inquiry into openings and employer requirements when a contested placement
question needs primary evidence. The
[Ottawa area metro page](/locations/illinois/ottawa/) describes how the same
data discipline applies across the neighboring towns; on any single
engagement the data is filtered through the evaluee's actual restrictions
and transferable skills, which is where generic market summaries fail and
individualized analysis begins.

## Remote-first, in person when it counts

No Purinton Analytics office exists in Mendota, and none is claimed.
Engagements default to secure remote workflows because they are faster and
better documented: encrypted records transfer, video interviews under
controlled protocols, and remote counsel conferences at every stage. In-person
steps are reserved for what genuinely benefits from them, such as an
evaluation conducted in the evaluee's own community or attendance at
deposition and trial, and those trips are organized from the Kansas City
home office with the case deadlines already mapped.
