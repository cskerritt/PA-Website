The Quincy, IL-MO micropolitan area spans a state line, and litigation there
often does the same. A claim can be filed in an Illinois courtroom while the
evaluee lives, works, or treats in Missouri, and counsel need an expert whose
methodology does not change at the boundary. Purinton Analytics serves Quincy
attorneys on either side of the caption with
[vocational expert evaluations](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## A venue footprint on both sides of the line

The metro consists of Adams County in Illinois and Lewis County in Missouri,
and engagements are accepted for matters in the circuit courts of both: the
Circuit Court of Adams County on the Illinois side, and circuit court matters
arising in Lewis County, Missouri, across the line. Federal referrals are
accepted wherever the case is pending, including the United States District
Court for the Northern District of Illinois, the United States District
Court for the Central District of Illinois, and the United States District
Court for the Southern District of Illinois. Depositions and trial testimony
are provided in person or by remote appearance where the court permits, and
the [Illinois locations page](/locations/illinois/) describes coverage
across the rest of the state when a referral arises outside the metro.

## Meetings by appointment, stated plainly

Quincy meetings are by appointment. The firm keeps no staffed office in
Quincy and publishes no street address for this location, because accuracy
about presence is part of the same discipline that governs the written
reports. What counsel can rely on instead is a defined process: conflict
checks answered promptly, evaluation dates set against the disclosure
calendar at intake, and travel scheduled from the Kansas City home office
when depositions, trial dates, or in-person evaluations require presence in
the Quincy area. Evaluation sessions are held in the evaluee's community, at
counsel's offices, or by secure video under publisher protocols, whichever
the referral questions and the record support.

## Labor market research that respects the state line

Employability and earning capacity findings for Quincy-area evaluees rest on
Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for
the area and for Illinois and Missouri statewide, together with state
workforce agency labor market information from both states and direct labor
market research, including employer contact and job posting analysis, when
the referral questions require evidence about actual hiring. A labor market
that crosses a state line is analyzed the way evaluees actually experience
it: transferable skills, commuting realities, and post-injury placement
options are examined and documented rather than assumed, so the resulting
opinion holds up whether it is read by retaining counsel or by the other
side of the caption.

## Starting a Quincy engagement

Quincy referrals commonly arise in personal injury, medical malpractice,
wrongful death, employment, and disability matters, and the firm accepts
work from plaintiff and defense counsel on the same terms. Every engagement
begins with a documented conflict check, a defined scope, and a records
request tailored to the claims, so the written opinion rests on a record
both sides can examine. Submit the caption or party names through the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and note any imminent disclosure,
deposition, or trial dates; CVs and fee information follow promptly. The
[national coverage page](/locations/nationwide/) explains remote evaluation
logistics, travel, and the firm's reach beyond Illinois and Missouri.
