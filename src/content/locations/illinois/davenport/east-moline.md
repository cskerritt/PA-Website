Where can this person still work, and what can that work pay? For an East
Moline evaluee, those questions cannot be answered from a single town's job
postings, because East Moline belongs to a commuting economy that crosses a
state line: residents routinely hold jobs elsewhere in the metro, on either
side of the Illinois-Iowa boundary. Purinton Analytics builds its
[vocational expert](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) opinions for East
Moline cases around that wider market, and it does so for whichever party
retains it first, plaintiff or defendant, under the same methodology.

## The Labor Market Comes First

Transferable skills analysis for an East Moline worker starts with the
worker's actual vocational history and then tests it against the
occupational landscape of the full metro region, not a fragment of it.
The data foundation is the Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational
employment and wage estimates for the metro area and for Illinois
statewide, joined with state workforce agency labor market information and,
where the referral questions justify it, direct contact with employers.
Because the metro's economic center of gravity includes its Iowa side,
counsel may also find the firm's [metro area page](/locations/iowa/davenport/)
useful when a case involves parties or employment on the Iowa side of the
boundary.

## Rock Island County Venue and the Federal Alternatives

East Moline lies in Rock Island County, so state-law claims are generally
filed in the Circuit Court of Rock Island County. On the federal side,
Illinois is divided among the United States District Court for the Northern
District of Illinois, the United States District Court for the Central
District of Illinois, and the United States District Court for the Southern
District of Illinois; identifying which district properly hears an East
Moline dispute is part of the conflict check and intake, never a guess made
in a report. The firm's [statewide Illinois practice](/locations/illinois/)
keeps its work consistent with what courts across the state expect from
retained experts.

## Life Care Planning With Local Delivery in Mind

For catastrophic injury matters, the firm prepares
[life care plans](/services/life-care-planning/) that price future care in
the context of where the evaluee actually lives and receives treatment.
A plan drafted for an East Moline resident considers the practical
availability of services in the region, and where
[medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/) is the better
fit for the case posture, the firm scopes the narrower product instead of
overselling the broader one.

## Appointments, Not Addresses

Purinton Analytics keeps no storefront in East Moline and lists no local
address, a deliberate choice: an expert whose credibility rests on accuracy
should not imply offices that do not exist. Evaluations requiring physical
presence take place in the evaluee's own community, scheduled around
treatment and work obligations. Everything else, from records review to
attorney conferences, is handled remotely, and travel for testimony is
staged from the Kansas City home office. The same model serves counsel
throughout the firm's [nationwide coverage](/locations/nationwide/).
