## Courts Serving Moline Litigation

Moline sits in Rock Island County, and civil actions arising in the city are
ordinarily heard in the Circuit Court of Rock Island County. Where a matter
belongs in federal court instead, Illinois offers the United States District
Court for the Northern District of Illinois, the United States District
Court for the Central District of Illinois, and the United States District
Court for the Southern District of Illinois as potential forums; which of
them controls a given Moline case is confirmed during the conflict check and
intake rather than assumed. The Moline practice operates as part of the
firm's broader [Illinois practice](/locations/illinois/), so reports and
testimony follow conventions Illinois judges and juries have seen before.

## A Labor Market That Spans the Metro

An earning capacity opinion for a Moline evaluee cannot stop at the city
limits. Workers here participate in a commuting economy that reaches across
the whole [Davenport metro area](/locations/iowa/davenport/), including its
Iowa side, and the firm's vocational findings reflect that reality. Analyses
draw on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage
estimates for the metro area and for Illinois statewide, supplemented by
state workforce agency labor market information and by direct employer
research when the referral questions call for it. Every figure that appears
in a report is sourced so that opposing counsel can trace it, which is the
standard the firm applies whether the retaining side is plaintiff or
defense.

## Meeting With Moline Evaluees

The firm maintains no staffed office in Moline and publishes no local street
address, because claiming a presence it does not keep would be inconsistent
with the accuracy it promises in its reports. What counsel receives instead
is a remote-first engagement model: records review, vocational interviews,
and counsel conferences proceed by secure video and telephone, and when the
assignment requires an in-person evaluation, the evaluation is conducted in
the evaluee's own community. Travel for evaluations, depositions, and trial
testimony is arranged from the Kansas City home office and planned around
the case calendar, not the other way around.

## Requesting a Conflict Check for a Moline Matter

Retention begins with clearance, not commitment. Submit the caption or the
party names through the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/), and identify
any disclosure deadline, deposition setting, or trial date that is already
on the calendar. Once conflicts are cleared, the firm responds with a
defined scope, a records request tailored to the claims, and a schedule
built to meet the dates the court has set.
