Expert disclosure deadlines do not shrink because a case comes from a small
suburb. A Silvis matter moves on the same calendar of disclosures,
depositions, and pretrial orders as any case filed from a courthouse
downtown, and the most common expert problem the firm sees is not bad
analysis but late retention. Purinton Analytics builds its Silvis
engagements backward from the dates that already bind counsel: report due
dates drive records deadlines, records deadlines drive evaluation
scheduling, and every step is committed to writing at intake. The firm is
retained by plaintiff counsel and by defense counsel alike, and its
calendar discipline does not vary with the side.

## Working Backward From the Disclosure Date

When a [vocational expert](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) or
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) referral arrives for a
Silvis evaluee, the first substantive conversation after conflict clearance
is about time: what has been disclosed, what remains, and when the opinion
must be served. From there the firm fixes the sequence of records
collection, interview, testing, and drafting so the report is complete
before the deadline rather than at it. Rush work is sometimes unavoidable
in litigation, but a report assembled under a written schedule reads
differently on cross-examination than one assembled in a scramble, and
judges can tell the difference.

## Where Silvis Cases Are Heard

Silvis sits within Rock Island County, and its state-law civil claims are
ordinarily brought in the Circuit Court of Rock Island County. Illinois
federal matters are distributed among the United States District Court for
the Northern District of Illinois, the United States District Court for the
Central District of Illinois, and the United States District Court for the
Southern District of Illinois; the district with authority over a specific
Silvis dispute is settled during the conflict check and intake. Counsel
handling matters elsewhere in the state can consult the firm's
[Illinois overview](/locations/illinois/) for the same services in other
venues.

## Presence by Appointment

The firm does not keep an office in Silvis and does not pretend otherwise;
by-appointment service is the honest description of how it operates
everywhere outside its Kansas City home office, and travel to evaluations
or testimony is planned from that home office. In-person evaluation
sessions are held in the evaluee's community. Remote conferencing covers
the rest, which is how the firm keeps small-venue engagements economical
across its [national practice](/locations/nationwide/).
