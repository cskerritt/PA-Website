A defensible expert report has the same anatomy in Geneseo as it does
anywhere: a documented conflict check, a scope defined before the work
begins, records requested against the claims actually pleaded, methods that
other professionals in the field would recognize, and conclusions each
traceable to a source. Purinton Analytics brings that anatomy, unchanged,
to Henry County litigation. The firm writes every Geneseo opinion as if the
sharpest reader will be the lawyer on the other side, because in practice
that is exactly who reads it, and it accepts engagements from plaintiff and
defense counsel without altering its approach for either.

## Method Before Venue

The firm's Geneseo work spans

catastrophic injuries, and

engagement structure.

## Henry County's Courts, and the State's Federal Bench

Geneseo civil matters in state court are heard in the Circuit Court of
Henry County. If federal jurisdiction attaches, Illinois presents its
district courts as the available forums: the United States District Court
for the Northern District of Illinois, the United States District Court for
the Central District of Illinois, and the United States District Court for
the Southern District of Illinois. Which forum controls, and whether any
venue-specific qualification requirement applies, is identified and
confirmed during the conflict check and intake process. The firm's wider
[Illinois coverage](/locations/illinois/) keeps its formats aligned with
practice across the state.

## Reading the Regional Labor Market Honestly

Geneseo is a smaller Henry County community whose workers look toward the
larger [metro economy](/locations/iowa/davenport/) that surrounds it, and a
sound earning capacity opinion must weigh both the local and the regional
picture. The firm grounds that weighing in Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates for the metro area and for
Illinois statewide, in state workforce agency labor market information, and
in direct labor market research where the referral questions call for it.
It characterizes market scale in words rather than unsupported figures, and
it cites what it uses.

Honesty about method extends to honesty about geography: the firm has no
Geneseo office and claims none. Evaluations that must occur in person are
held in the evaluee's own community, and the balance of the engagement runs
remotely by secure video and telephone. Travel for evaluations, deposition,
and trial testimony originates from the Kansas City home office, the same
arrangement described on the firm's
[nationwide page](/locations/nationwide/).
