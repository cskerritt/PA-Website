Getting a credible expert in front of a Kewanee evaluee has historically
meant an unwelcome compromise: ask an injured person to travel a long way,
or accept an opinion formed entirely from paper. Purinton Analytics rejects
both options. For Kewanee matters, the firm conducts in-person vocational
evaluations in the evaluee's own community when the assignment requires
them, and it structures the rest of the engagement remotely so that a Henry
County case never pays a premium simply for sitting outside a metro core.
Plaintiff firms and defense firms retain the practice on the same terms and
receive the same product.

## Evaluation Logistics Designed Around the Evaluee

The working sequence for a Kewanee referral is deliberate. Records arrive
first, under a request tailored to the claims. Interviews and standardized
vocational testing follow, conducted by secure video where appropriate or
face to face in the community where the evaluee lives, scheduled around
medical treatment. Counsel conferences happen by video or phone at any
stage. When deposition or trial requires the expert in the courtroom,
travel is arranged from the Kansas City home office with lead time
protected for the court's schedule. What the firm never does is publish a
Kewanee address or imply a staffed local office, because it keeps none, and
saying so is part of the discipline counsel is buying.

## A Small Town Tied to a Larger Market

Although Kewanee has its own employment base, its residents participate in
the labor market of the broader
[Davenport metro region](/locations/iowa/davenport/), and an earning
capacity analysis that ignored that connection would understate or
overstate what work is realistically available. The firm's
[vocational opinions](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) for Kewanee
evaluees rely on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and
wage estimates for the metro area and for Illinois statewide, alongside
state workforce agency labor market information, with direct labor market
research added when the questions demand it. Scale is described
qualitatively and every data point is cited to its source.
