Most Milan cases that reach Purinton Analytics turn on a short list of hard
questions. Can this person return to the job held before the injury? If
not, what other work do their skills, education, and restrictions actually
support? What would that work pay, and what does the gap mean over a
working lifetime? What will future care cost, and who says so? The firm
exists to answer those questions with evidence, and it answers them the
same way whether the retaining lawyer represents the injured worker or the
party alleged to be responsible.

## From Question to Opinion

For the return-to-work and earning capacity questions, the firm's
[vocational expert witness](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) practice
pairs a structured review of the evaluee's work and medical history with
standardized vocational testing and a transferable skills analysis. For the
future care questions, its
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) practice translates the
treating record and clinical consultation into a costed plan of care. Where
the case needs the full damages picture, independent economist partners
perform and sign the economic analysis within
[coordinated engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/), so
the disciplines stay distinct while the presentation stays coherent.

## Milan's Venue Within Rock Island County

Milan is a small Rock Island County community, and its state-court civil
matters proceed in the Circuit Court of Rock Island County. Federal claims
arising from Milan parties or events may be lodged in the United States
District Court for the Northern District of Illinois, the United States
District Court for the Central District of Illinois, or the United States
District Court for the Southern District of Illinois, with the controlling
venue established during the conflict check and intake. The firm supports
counsel in each of these forums as part of its
[Illinois practice](/locations/illinois/).

## What the Data Says About Work Near Milan

Answering the pay question for a Milan evaluee requires regional data,
because Milan's workforce is woven into the
[Davenport metro labor market](/locations/iowa/davenport/) that stretches
across the Illinois-Iowa state line. The firm relies on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the metro area
and for Illinois statewide, on state workforce agency labor market
information, and on direct labor market research when the referral
questions require employer-level detail. Reports describe the market in
qualitative terms and footnote every source, so cross-examination finds a
trail instead of a gap.

## Seeing the Evaluee Without a Local Office

The firm keeps no office in Milan and prints no local address, stating that
fact as plainly as it states its findings. Records work and conferences run
remotely; in-person evaluation, when the assignment calls for it, takes
place in the community where the evaluee lives. Travel for evaluation
sessions and courtroom appearances is coordinated from the Kansas City home
office, following the model the firm applies across its
[national engagement footprint](/locations/nationwide/).
