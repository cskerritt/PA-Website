East Peoria's workforce moves across the metro every working day, and that
daily movement is exactly what a credible earning capacity opinion has to
capture. When an East Peoria worker is injured, the vocational question is
not what jobs exist inside the town limits but what the whole Peoria
commuting market realistically offers someone with that person's skills and
restrictions. Purinton Analytics answers that question for plaintiff and
defense counsel alike, pairing
[vocational expert services](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) with
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) under the methodology
described on the [Peoria metro page](/locations/illinois/peoria/).

## Reading the commuter labor market

Vocational findings for East Peoria evaluees rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Peoria
metropolitan area and for Illinois statewide, together with state workforce
agency labor market information and direct employer research where the
referral questions warrant it. Because commuting patterns define the metro,
the analysis treats the labor market as a regional whole: transferable
skills are matched against occupations across the commuting area, and
residual earning capacity is stated in terms a trier of fact can test
against the published data rather than against impressions about any particular
town.

## Tazewell County courts and the federal forums

East Peoria sits in Tazewell County, and state court matters proceed in the
Circuit Court under Illinois procedure. On the federal side, Illinois
litigation is distributed among the United States District Court for the
Northern District of Illinois, the United States District Court for the
Central District of Illinois, and the United States District Court for the
Southern District of Illinois; identifying the controlling forum for a
particular East Peoria matter is part of the conflict check and intake, not
an assumption made from a map. Reports follow the disclosure standards of
whichever court holds the case, and testimony is offered in person or by
remote appearance where the court permits.

## How an East Peoria evaluee is evaluated

There is no staffed Purinton Analytics office in East Peoria, and the firm
publishes no local street address, because accuracy about presence belongs
in the same category as accuracy about data. Meetings are by appointment.
Records review and vocational interviews proceed remotely in most files, and
in-person evaluation, when the questions require it, is conducted in the
evaluee's own community rather than forcing travel on an injured person.
When hearing or trial dates demand physical presence, travel is scheduled
from the Kansas City home office. Where damages must be reduced to numbers
for the jury, [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
bring in independent economist partners who perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work continues under the
same engagement.
