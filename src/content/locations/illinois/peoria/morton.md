Most calls about a Morton case arrive with a date attached: an expert
disclosure deadline, a discovery cutoff, a mediation already on the
calendar. Purinton Analytics is built for that reality. Intake, conflict
checking, and scoping are designed to move quickly without shortcuts, so
that Morton counsel on either side of a claim can retain a
[vocational expert](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) or a
[life care planner](/services/life-care-planning/) with enough runway to
produce an opinion that survives scrutiny, using the methodology set out on
the [Peoria metro page](/locations/illinois/peoria/).

## Working backward from the disclosure calendar

A defensible report cannot be compressed into the final week before
disclosure, but it can be planned. At intake the firm maps the records
request, evaluee contact, testing, and drafting against the case schedule,
and tells counsel plainly whether the timeline supports a full evaluation or
only a narrower scope such as a
[rebuttal or peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) of an opposing
report. That candor at the front end is the same discipline that shows up in
the written opinion, which is drafted so opposing counsel can trace every
conclusion to its sources.

## Tazewell County and the Illinois federal districts

Morton lies in Tazewell County, where state court matters proceed in the
Circuit Court under Illinois procedure. Federal practice in the state runs
through the United States District Court for the Northern District of
Illinois, the United States District Court for the Central District of
Illinois, and the United States District Court for the Southern District of
Illinois. Which of those forums governs a particular Morton matter, and any
venue-specific qualification requirement that follows from it, is identified
and confirmed during the conflict check and intake process. Reports conform
to the disclosure rules of the court that actually holds the case.

## The labor market behind a Morton opinion

Earning capacity conclusions for Morton evaluees are anchored in Bureau of
Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Peoria
metropolitan area and for Illinois statewide, along with state workforce
agency labor market information and targeted labor market research when the
referral questions call for it. The analysis reflects the metro-wide market
a Morton resident can reach, matching documented transferable skills and
medically established restrictions against occupations across the commuting
area, and it is expressed qualitatively and quantitatively in the report in
a form both sides can test.

## Meetings, evaluations, and travel

The firm maintains no staffed office in Morton and lists no local address,
because it will not imply a presence it does not have. Consultations are by
appointment, and most of the work, records analysis, interviews, and
conferencing with counsel, proceeds remotely. In-person evaluation of a
Morton evaluee is held in the evaluee's own community when the questions
require it, and appearances for deposition or trial are scheduled with
travel from the Kansas City home office. Statewide context is available on
the [Illinois page](/locations/illinois/), and the
[nationwide overview](/locations/nationwide/) describes the same practice
standards applied across the country.
