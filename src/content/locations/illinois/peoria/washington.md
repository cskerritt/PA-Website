The person at the center of a Washington case is usually not a lawyer. It is
an injured worker, a surviving spouse, or a family managing a permanent
disability, and the quality of the expert work depends on evaluating that
person carefully and fairly. Purinton Analytics builds its
[vocational evaluations](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) and
[life care plans](/services/life-care-planning/) for Washington evaluees
around that principle, serving plaintiff and defense counsel with the same
methodology documented on the [Peoria metro page](/locations/illinois/peoria/).

## Evaluating a Washington evaluee

An evaluation begins with the record: medical documentation, employment
history, education, and earnings evidence gathered under a records request
tailored to the claims. Interviews and standardized vocational testing are
conducted remotely in most files, which spares an injured Washington
resident unnecessary travel; when the referral questions require in-person
assessment, the evaluation is scheduled in the evaluee's own community. Life
care planning follows the same pattern, grounding every projected item of
future care in the medical foundation, with a
[medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/) available when
counsel needs a narrower costing analysis rather than a comprehensive plan.

## Where a Washington case is heard

Washington is a Tazewell County community, and state court matters proceed
in the Circuit Court under Illinois procedure. Illinois federal litigation
is divided among the United States District Court for the Northern District
of Illinois, the United States District Court for the Central District of
Illinois, and the United States District Court for the Southern District of
Illinois; rather than presume which district reaches a given Washington
dispute, the firm confirms the controlling venue, and any qualification
requirement that attaches to it, during the conflict check and intake.
Reports are written to the disclosure standard of the court that holds the
matter and are structured for cross-examination from the outset.

## The market a Washington worker actually faces

Employability and earning capacity opinions are only as strong as the labor
market data beneath them. For Washington evaluees the firm relies on Bureau
of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the
Peoria metropolitan area and for Illinois statewide, supplemented by state
workforce agency labor market information and direct labor market research
where warranted. Because Washington residents work throughout the metro, the
analysis evaluates occupations across the regional commuting market against
the evaluee's transferable skills and documented restrictions, and states
its conclusions in terms opposing counsel can check against the same public
sources.

## Presence, candidly stated

There is no Purinton Analytics office in Washington, and the firm publishes
no street address here; meetings are by appointment, and travel for
depositions, trials, or in-person evaluations is arranged from the Kansas
City home office. When a case also requires economic quantification, the
firm coordinates [combined damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis alongside the vocational and care-planning work.
