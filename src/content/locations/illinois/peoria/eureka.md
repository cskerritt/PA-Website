## Woodford County venue context

State court matters arising in Eureka proceed in the Circuit Court under
Illinois procedure, and reports are formatted to that court's disclosure
expectations when it holds the case. On the federal side, Illinois offers
the United States District Court for the Northern District of Illinois, the
United States District Court for the Central District of Illinois, and the
United States District Court for the Southern District of Illinois as
potential forums; the firm confirms which district controls a particular
Eureka matter, along with any venue-specific qualification requirement,
during the conflict check and intake process rather than assuming the answer
from geography.

## A Woodford County evaluee in a regional market

Although Eureka sits in Woodford County, its workers participate in the
broader Peoria commuting market, and a sound earning capacity opinion has to
reflect that reach. Vocational findings rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates for the Peoria metropolitan area
and for Illinois statewide, supplemented by state workforce agency labor
market information and direct labor market research where the referral
questions warrant it. Transferable skills from the evaluee's documented work
history are matched against occupations across the region, filtered through
the restrictions the medical record supports, so the conclusion describes
the market the evaluee can actually enter.

## How the work reaches Eureka

The firm keeps no staffed office in Eureka and publishes no street address
for this location; that plain statement reflects the same standard of
accuracy the reports observe. Consultations are by appointment. Records
analysis, vocational interviewing, and much of the testing proceed remotely,
which keeps costs proportionate for a smaller-venue case, and in-person
evaluation is conducted in the evaluee's own community when the referral
questions require it. Travel for depositions and trial testimony is
scheduled from the Kansas City home office. Where the damages picture also
demands economic modeling, the firm arranges
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds in parallel.
