## Built to survive cross-examination

A report that cannot be defended at deposition is worse than no report at
all. Bartonville engagements therefore begin with a documented conflict
check, a written scope, and a records request tailored to the claims, so the
final opinion rests on a foundation both sides can inspect. The same
standard governs [rebuttal and peer review work](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/):
when counsel retains the firm to examine an opposing expert's report, the
critique is confined to methodology and record support, the categories a
court can act on, rather than advocacy dressed as analysis.
