A small community should not have to accept diluted expert work because it
sits outside the orbit of a major litigation center. Chillicothe cases
receive the same rigor Purinton Analytics applies anywhere in the country: a
remote-first practice model means the size of the town where an evaluee
lives has no bearing on the depth of the analysis. Plaintiff and defense
counsel in Chillicothe matters retain the firm for
[vocational expert work](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) and
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) under the approach
described on the [Peoria metro page](/locations/illinois/peoria/).

## Remote-first, by design

The working file in a Chillicothe engagement is built almost entirely at a
distance: records are exchanged securely, vocational interviews and many
standardized instruments are administered remotely, and conferences with
counsel happen by video on the case's schedule rather than the travel
calendar's. This is not a compromise adopted for outlying towns; it is how
the practice runs everywhere, and it is why a Chillicothe file gets the same
attention as a case in a major metro. When the referral questions do require
face-to-face assessment, the evaluation is conducted in the evaluee's own
community, and courtroom or deposition appearances are supported by travel
from the Kansas City home office.

## Peoria County venue context

Chillicothe lies in Peoria County, and state court matters proceed in the
Circuit Court under Illinois procedure. The federal forums for Illinois
litigation are the United States District Court for the Northern District of
Illinois, the United States District Court for the Central District of
Illinois, and the United States District Court for the Southern District of
Illinois; the firm treats the controlling venue for any particular
Chillicothe case as a question for the conflict check and intake, not for
assumption. Every report is prepared to the disclosure standard of the court
that actually holds the matter, with sources cited so either side can
retrace the reasoning.

## Situating Chillicothe in the regional market

Vocational conclusions about a Chillicothe evaluee are grounded in Bureau of
Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Peoria
metropolitan area and for Illinois statewide, along with state workforce
agency labor market information and direct labor market research where the
questions warrant it. The commuting reach of the metro, rather than the
boundaries of the town, defines the occupations considered, and the analysis
matches those occupations against the evaluee's transferable skills and the
restrictions established in the medical record.
