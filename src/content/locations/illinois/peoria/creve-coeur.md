## From conflict check to signed report

The intake sequence is not paperwork for its own sake. The conflict check
protects the engagement from disqualification; the written scope fixes what
questions the expert will and will not answer; and the tailored records
request ensures the opinion rests on the actual medical, employment, and
earnings record rather than on counsel's summary of it. Drafting proceeds
from that record, and the finished report cites its sources so that the
attorney across the aisle can verify every step. The same structure governs
narrower assignments, including
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) of opposing
expert reports and stand-alone
[medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/).
