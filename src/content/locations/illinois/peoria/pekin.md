## Venue context for Pekin matters

State court matters arising in Pekin proceed in the Circuit Court under
Illinois procedure, and reports are prepared to the disclosure expectations
that court applies. Federal claims from Illinois may reach the United States
District Court for the Northern District of Illinois, the United States
District Court for the Central District of Illinois, or the United States
District Court for the Southern District of Illinois; the firm does not
assume which forum controls a given Pekin case, because that question is
confirmed during the conflict check and intake rather than inferred from
geography. Whatever the venue, the written opinion is structured so that
methodology, sources, and reasoning can be examined by the court and by
opposing counsel without translation.

## Pekin within the Peoria labor market

A Pekin resident's earning capacity is rarely defined by Pekin alone.
Vocational findings for evaluees here rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates for the Peoria metropolitan area
and for Illinois statewide, supplemented by state workforce agency labor
market information and direct labor market research where the referral
questions warrant it. The analysis considers the occupations realistically
available to the evaluee across the metro commuting area, the transferable
skills documented in the work history, and the functional limitations
established by the medical record, so that conclusions about employability
and earning capacity reflect the market the evaluee actually faces.

## Evaluation logistics in Tazewell County

Purinton Analytics keeps no staffed office in Pekin and publishes no street
address for this location; candor about presence is part of the same
discipline that governs the reports. Meetings are by appointment. Most
records review, vocational interviewing, and testing can be completed
remotely, and when the referral questions call for in-person evaluation of a
Pekin evaluee, that evaluation is scheduled in the evaluee's own community.
Deposition and trial appearances are planned around the court's calendar,
with travel arranged from the Kansas City home office when presence is
required. For matters that also need economic quantification, the firm
offers [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.
