The Peoria, IL metropolitan area spreads litigation across a wide multi-county
footprint, and counsel in Peoria County, Tazewell County, Woodford County,
Marshall County, and Stark County retain Purinton Analytics for the same
reasons counsel do anywhere else in [Illinois](/locations/illinois/): a
[vocational evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) of
employability and earning capacity, a
[life care plan](/services/life-care-planning/) in catastrophic matters, or a
[rebuttal review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) of an opposing expert's
report. Plaintiff and defense engagements are accepted on equal terms, and
every report is written so that either side of the caption can examine its
foundations.

## Courts serving the Peoria area

Engagements are accepted for matters in the Circuit Court in each of the
metro's counties: Peoria County, Tazewell County, Woodford County,
Marshall County, and Stark County. Federal
matters are served as well: the firm accepts engagements throughout the
United States District Court for the Central District of Illinois, the
United States District Court for the Northern District of Illinois, and the
United States District Court for the Southern District of Illinois, so a
removal or transfer does not interrupt the work. Depositions and trial
testimony are given in person or by remote appearance where the court
permits, and the analysis is documented the same way regardless of forum.

## Meetings by appointment, not a storefront

There is no staffed Purinton Analytics office in Peoria, and the firm
publishes no street address for this location, because accuracy about
presence is part of the same discipline that governs its opinions. Meetings
here are by appointment. Evaluations are conducted in the evaluee's own
community, at counsel's offices, or by secure video under publisher
protocols, whichever the case requires. When an engagement calls for
in-person evaluation, or when deposition and trial dates require presence,
travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office and planned around the
court's calendar rather than the firm's convenience.

## Labor market research for Peoria area evaluees

Vocational findings for Peoria area evaluees rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates from the OEWS program
for the Peoria, IL metropolitan area and for Illinois statewide,
supplemented by state workforce agency labor market information and direct
labor market research where the referral questions warrant it. Transferable
skills analysis, labor market access, and placeability opinions are tied to
the occupations actually documented in the record, and the sources for every
figure in a report are cited so opposing counsel can trace them. Where a
matter reaches beyond the metro, the same method extends through the firm's
[nationwide practice](/locations/nationwide/) without any change in
standards.

## Starting a Peoria engagement

Every engagement begins with a documented conflict check, a defined scope,
and a records request tailored to the claims. Submit the caption or party
names through the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and note any
imminent disclosure, deposition, or trial dates so scheduling can be
confirmed at intake. Counsel receive a written engagement confirmation
before any substantive work begins, and any venue-specific registration or
qualification requirement is identified and confirmed during the conflict
check and intake process. From that point the file proceeds under the same
scope discipline whether the matter sits in a Peoria County courtroom or a
federal one.
