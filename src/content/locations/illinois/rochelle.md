Every Purinton Analytics engagement starts the same way, whether the caption
is filed in a major metro or in Ogle County: a documented conflict check, a
defined scope, and a records request tailored to the claims. Rochelle
counsel get that identical structure behind
[vocational expert evaluations](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),

[forensic economic damages](/services/forensic-economic-damages/) analyses
that are performed and signed by independent economist partners coordinated

terms, and the smaller size of the Rochelle, IL micropolitan area changes
none of it.

## Courts serving Ogle County matters

Engagements are accepted for matters in the Circuit Court of Ogle County and
in federal court, whether the case is pending in the United States District
Court for the Northern District of Illinois, the United States District
Court for the Central District of Illinois, or the United States District
Court for the Southern District of Illinois. Depositions and trial testimony
are provided in person or by remote appearance where the court permits.
Counsel whose practices extend past the metro will find the same services
statewide; the [Illinois locations page](/locations/illinois/) sets out that
coverage.

## What retaining Rochelle counsel can expect

Rochelle meetings are by appointment. There is no staffed office in Rochelle
and no street address published for this location; the firm states that
directly because the habit of saying only what is true is the same habit
that produces defensible reports. After the conflict check clears, intake
fixes the scope in writing, identifies the records needed, and sets the
evaluation date against the disclosure calendar. Evaluation sessions are
held in the evaluee's community, at counsel's offices, or by secure video
under publisher protocols, and travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home
office when a deposition, trial date, or in-person evaluation requires
presence in Ogle County. Any venue-specific registration or qualification
requirement is identified and confirmed during the conflict check and intake
process.

## Labor market data behind Rochelle opinions

Employability and earning capacity findings for Rochelle-area evaluees rest
on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for
the area and for Illinois statewide, state workforce agency labor market
information, and direct labor market research, including employer contact
and job posting analysis, when the referral questions require evidence about
actual hiring in Ogle County. Where a coordinated engagement includes
economic damages, the vocational findings hand off cleanly to the
independent economist partner who performs and signs the economic analysis,
so each discipline stays within its own competence and the combined
presentation remains internally consistent.
