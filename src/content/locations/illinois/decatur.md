What a Decatur attorney actually buys from Purinton Analytics is a record:
a written opinion whose scope, sources, and reasoning are documented well
enough for opposing counsel to examine every step. That record can take the
form of a [vocational evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) of
employability and earning capacity, a
[life care plan](/services/life-care-planning/) in a catastrophic injury
matter, or a [rebuttal or peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) of
an opposing expert's report. The firm accepts plaintiff and defense
engagements in the Decatur, IL metropolitan area on the same terms it
applies across its [Illinois practice](/locations/illinois/).

## Macon County venue coverage

Engagements are accepted for matters in the Circuit Court in Macon County
and in the federal courts that serve Illinois: the United States District
Court for the Central District of Illinois, the United States District
Court for the Northern District of Illinois, and the United States District
Court for the Southern District of Illinois. The expert file is prepared to
meet the disclosure practice of the receiving forum, and testimony is
available in person or by remote appearance where the court permits. A
matter that moves between state and federal court keeps its expert work
intact, because the analysis was built on the record rather than on the
forum.

## By-appointment meetings in Decatur

The firm maintains no staffed office in Decatur and publishes no street
address for this location. It states that outright because accuracy about
presence is part of the same discipline that governs its reports. Meetings
are by appointment, and evaluation sessions are conducted in the evaluee's
own community, at counsel's offices, or by secure video under publisher
protocols. When the referral questions call for in-person evaluation, or
when deposition and trial dates require presence, travel is scheduled from
the Kansas City home office and confirmed in writing before counsel commits
dates to the court.

## Labor market analysis for Decatur evaluees

Vocational findings for Decatur evaluees rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates from the OEWS program for the
Decatur, IL metropolitan area and for Illinois statewide, supported by
state workforce agency labor market information and direct labor market
research where warranted. Conclusions about employability, labor market
access, and post-injury earning capacity are stated in qualitative terms
where the underlying data are qualitative, and every source appears in the
report's citations so that both sides of the caption can retrace the
analysis. When a Decatur matter reaches other labor markets, the firm's
[nationwide practice](/locations/nationwide/) extends the same method
wherever the case requires.
