## Kankakee County venue, Illinois forums

Momence is a Kankakee County town, and its state court litigation proceeds
in the Circuit Court under Illinois procedure. Federal litigation from
Illinois may be venued in the United States District Court for the Northern
District of Illinois, the United States District Court for the Central
District of Illinois, or the United States District Court for the Southern
District of Illinois; rather than guessing from geography, the firm
confirms the controlling district during the conflict check and intake and
then conforms the report and disclosure package to that court's
requirements. Testimony is given in person or by remote appearance where
the presiding judge allows.

## Small-town evaluees, metro-scale data

Earning capacity opinions for a Momence evaluee are anchored in sources
that can survive cross-examination: Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational
employment and wage estimates for the Kankakee metropolitan area and for
Illinois statewide, state workforce agency labor market information, and
case-specific research such as job posting analysis when the referral
warrants it. Qualitative judgment about a small community's employment
options is always tethered to those cited sources, and the identical
standard is applied in reverse when counsel needs
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) of an opposing
expert's report: every assumption is traced back to its source, and any
gap between the data and the conclusion is documented for the record.

## Logistics for a Momence engagement

All Momence meetings are by appointment; the firm has no office in the
town, publishes no local address, and frames every travel plan honestly
from its Kansas City home office. In practice, most of an engagement never
requires travel at all: records review, counsel conferences, vocational
interviews, and care-planning assessments are handled remotely under
secure, publisher-compliant protocols. In-person evaluation in the
evaluee's community, site inspection, deposition attendance, and trial
testimony are scheduled as travel when the case requires them, sequenced
against the deadlines confirmed at intake.
