For an evaluee living in Manteno, the most practical fact about a Purinton
Analytics engagement is that the evaluation comes to the evaluee, not the
other way around. A catastrophically injured person in a small village
should not have to organize long-distance travel to be interviewed, so
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) assessments and
[vocational expert](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) interviews are
built around secure video sessions conducted under publisher protocols,
with in-person evaluation in the evaluee's own community available whenever
the referral questions require direct observation. Counsel for plaintiff
and defense alike retain the firm on those terms, under the same
methodology set out on the
[Kankakee metro page](/locations/illinois/kankakee/).

## The county and its courts

Manteno belongs to Kankakee County, where state court matters proceed in
the Circuit Court under Illinois procedure. On the federal side, Illinois
offers the United States District Court for the Northern District of
Illinois, the United States District Court for the Central District of
Illinois, and the United States District Court for the Southern District of
Illinois as possible forums; the one that actually controls a Manteno
matter is pinned down during the conflict check and intake. Whichever court
holds the case, the written opinion is prepared to that court's disclosure
standards and defended at deposition or trial, in person or by remote
appearance where permitted.

## Where a Manteno worker actually competes

The labor market relevant to a Manteno evaluee is defined by the evidence,
not by village limits. Analyses draw on Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates for the Kankakee metropolitan
area and for Illinois statewide, alongside state workforce agency labor
market information and direct research such as job posting review when the
questions call for it. That evidentiary floor cuts both ways: it supports
affirmative opinions on employability and earning capacity, and it supplies
the yardstick for
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) when an
opposing expert's labor market assumptions deserve scrutiny.

## Presence, candidly stated

The firm's only staffed office is its Kansas City home office; there is no
Manteno location and no published local address, and the firm says so
plainly because overstating presence would sit poorly next to reports that
demand precision from everyone else. By-appointment meetings, remote
conferencing with counsel, and scheduled travel for depositions, trial
testimony, and in-person evaluations cover the practical distance. Travel
is arranged from Kansas City against the deadlines the case actually has,
which are gathered at intake before the work plan is set, so that report
delivery, supplementation, and testimony each land inside the disclosure
window rather than against its edge.
