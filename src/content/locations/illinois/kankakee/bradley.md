Bradley sits squarely in the orbit of Kankakee, the metro's principal city,
and the venue picture for a Bradley case is correspondingly settled: the
village is part of Kankakee County, and state court matters belong to the
Circuit Court under Illinois procedure. What is less settled in most files is
the damages picture, and that is where Purinton Analytics is retained. The
firm supplies [vocational expert](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/) work for
Bradley matters, accepting engagements from plaintiff and defense counsel
alike under the practice standards described on the
[Kankakee metro page](/locations/illinois/kankakee/).

## Venue and disclosure posture

For state claims, the Circuit Court is the trial forum, and reports are
prepared to Illinois disclosure practice. Federal matters proceed in one of
the state's district courts: the United States District Court for the
Northern District of Illinois, the United States District Court for the
Central District of Illinois, or the United States District Court for the
Southern District of Illinois. Which forum governs a given Bradley case is
confirmed during the conflict check and intake, and the report format,
disclosure content, and testimony plan follow the controlling court's
requirements rather than a generic template.

## Reading the Bradley labor market honestly

A village economy tied to a larger metro demands a labor market analysis
that respects both scales. Opinions on employability and earning capacity
for Bradley evaluees are grounded in Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates for the Kankakee metropolitan
area and for Illinois as a whole, supplemented with state workforce agency
labor market information and targeted job market research where the
questions require it. Nothing is asserted about the local market that the
sources cannot support, a constraint that matters as much in
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) assignments as
in affirmative reports. Each cited estimate is tied to the occupations the
evaluee can actually perform, as established by the records, the interview,
and any transferable skills analysis the referral requires.

## How evaluations are conducted

There is no Purinton Analytics office in Bradley, and the firm does not
pretend otherwise; meetings happen by appointment. Interviews and
assessments are typically completed by secure video, which spares an
injured evaluee unnecessary travel, and in-person evaluation in the
evaluee's home community is arranged when the referral calls for it. Trial
testimony, depositions, and site visits are staged as travel from the
Kansas City home office, scheduled around the deadlines counsel identifies
at intake, with remote appearance available wherever the presiding court
allows it.
