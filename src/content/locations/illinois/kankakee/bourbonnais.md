## Kankakee County courts and federal forums

Bourbonnais lies in Kankakee County, and state court matters proceed in the
Circuit Court under Illinois procedure. Federal claims from Illinois are
heard in the United States District Court for the Northern District of
Illinois, the United States District Court for the Central District of
Illinois, and the United States District Court for the Southern District of
Illinois; the district that controls a particular Bourbonnais filing is
identified during the conflict check and intake rather than assumed from a
map. Reports are drafted to the disclosure requirements of the forum that
actually holds the case, and testimony is offered in person or, where the
court permits, by remote appearance.

## A commuting market, not a village economy

An injured Bourbonnais resident rarely competes for work inside town
boundaries alone, so the analysis does not stop there either.
Employability and earning capacity findings rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Kankakee
metropolitan area and for Illinois statewide, together with state workforce
agency labor market information and, where the referral questions warrant,
direct labor market research such as employer contact and job posting
review. Every figure in a report carries a cited source, and the same
sourcing discipline applies when the firm is retained for
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) of an opposing
expert's conclusions.

## Meeting and evaluating in Bourbonnais

Meetings in Bourbonnais are by appointment. The firm keeps no staffed
office in the town and publishes no local street address, because accuracy
about presence is part of the same discipline that governs its reports.
Counsel gets flexibility instead: vocational interviews and life care
planning assessments proceed by secure video under publisher protocols, or
in person in the evaluee's own community when the referral questions call
for direct observation. When a deposition, trial date, or in-person
evaluation requires presence, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home
office and planned against the case calendar established at intake.
