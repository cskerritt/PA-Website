For an injured person in a small village, the expert evaluation itself can become a hardship: hours of interviews and testing, scheduled in a distant city, for someone whose injury already makes travel difficult. Purinton Analytics designs its Tilton engagements to remove that burden from the evaluee rather than shift it onto the case. Interviews and standardized vocational assessment are conducted by secure video when the referral questions permit, and when they do not, the examiner comes to the evaluee's community instead of the reverse. On that foundation the practice delivers [vocational expert](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) opinions and [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) for Tilton matters, accepting retention from plaintiff and defense counsel on the same terms.

## Reading the Danville Labor Market from Tilton

Tilton belongs to the Danville, IL micropolitan area, whose principal city is Danville, and a defensible opinion about a Tilton evaluee's earning capacity must be built from that market's actual composition. The analysis draws on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Danville area and for Illinois statewide, on labor market information from the state workforce agency, and on direct research, including contact with employers and review of current postings, when the referral questions turn on whether particular work is genuinely available. Scale matters here: in a compact labor market, an occupational title that exists in the published estimates may correspond to few real openings, and the report addresses that distance explicitly rather than leaving it for cross-examination to expose.

## How an Evaluation Proceeds

No staffed office exists in Tilton, and the firm will not pretend otherwise; appointments are how the location is served, and that plain statement is part of the credibility the reports depend on. After conflicts clear and scope is defined, records are requested and reviewed before any evaluation session is calendared. The session itself, remote or in person, covers work history, transferable skills, functional considerations documented in the records, and standardized assessment as warranted, and travel for in-person work or courtroom testimony is arranged from the Kansas City home office. When the damages picture requires economic quantification, counsel may pair the file with [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement structure. The same standards apply across the [nationwide practice](/locations/nationwide/), so a Tilton report reads no differently from one prepared for a major metropolitan venue.
