## Georgetown Work in the Danville Commuting Market

Georgetown is a small community in the Danville, IL micropolitan area, and the jobs realistically open to a Georgetown evaluee are the jobs of that market, in which Danville serves as the principal city. Vocational conclusions therefore rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Danville area and for Illinois statewide, on state workforce agency labor market information, and, when the referral questions demand it, on direct labor market research such as employer contact and job posting analysis. The written report keeps the chain visible from data source to occupational base to placement conclusion, because in a smaller market the credibility of that chain, not the size of the data set, is what a fact finder is being asked to trust.

## Evaluation Practicalities for Georgetown Cases

Georgetown is served by appointment. The practice maintains no staffed location there and publishes no local address, an accuracy that counsel can hold up against any other representation in the file. Evaluations proceed remotely when the referral questions permit, using structured interview and standardized assessment by secure video; when the assignment requires an in-person session, it is conducted in the evaluee's own community, and travel for evaluations, depositions, and trial testimony is scheduled from the Kansas City home office. Records are requested against a scope defined at intake, so the evaluation date is set only after counsel and the expert agree on what must be reviewed beforehand, and the timeline runs backward from the operative disclosure deadline rather than forward from a signature on a retention letter.
