quickly. Occupations that are plentiful in a major metro may be thin on the
ground in Jackson County, and the difference goes straight to damages.
Purinton Analytics serves Carbondale counsel with
[vocational expert evaluations](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) that
measure the local market rather than assume it, together with
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) for catastrophic claims
and [rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) when an
opposing report imports big-city placement assumptions into a Carbondale
case. Plaintiff and defense engagements are accepted on the same terms.

## By appointment in Carbondale

Carbondale meetings are by appointment. The firm keeps no staffed office in
Carbondale and publishes no street address for this location, because
precision about presence belongs to the same discipline that governs its
labor market findings. Counsel can instead count on process: conflict checks
answered promptly, evaluation dates set against the disclosure calendar at
intake, and travel scheduled from the Kansas City home office when
depositions, trial dates, or in-person evaluations require presence in
Jackson County. Evaluation sessions are held in the evaluee's community, at
counsel's offices, or by secure video under publisher protocols, and the

## Measuring the market instead of assuming it

Employability and earning capacity findings for Carbondale-area evaluees
rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage
estimates for the area and for Illinois statewide, state workforce agency
labor market information, and direct labor market research, including
employer contact and job posting analysis, when the referral questions
require evidence about actual hiring in Jackson County. In a smaller market
that last step carries particular weight: whether a specific occupation is
genuinely available to a specific evaluee, at what wage, within a
reasonable commute, is treated as a question of evidence, not of
generalization from statewide tables. The written opinion documents what was
searched, what was found, and what was not.
