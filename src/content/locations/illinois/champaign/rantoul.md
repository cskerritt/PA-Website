For a Rantoul evaluee, the practical question counsel asks first is where
the evaluation will happen. Purinton Analytics answers it plainly: most
interviews and standardized testing are completed remotely, and when the
referral questions require an in-person assessment, the evaluation takes
place in the evaluee's own community rather than requiring a trip to a
distant office. On that logistical foundation the firm delivers
[vocational expert witness](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) opinions,
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[expert testimony and litigation consulting](/services/expert-testimony-litigation-consulting/)
for Rantoul matters, accepting work from whichever side of the caption
extends the retention.

## Where a Rantoul case is heard

Rantoul sits in Champaign County, and state-court litigation proceeds in
the Circuit Court that serves the county. Federal engagements are accepted
across Illinois, where the available forums are the United States District
Court for the Northern District of Illinois, the United States District
Court for the Central District of Illinois, and the United States District
Court for the Southern District of Illinois; the district that governs a
given matter is confirmed with counsel during the conflict check and
intake. That intake conversation also surfaces any venue-specific
qualification or registration requirement before the engagement letter is
signed, so nothing about the forum comes as a surprise at disclosure time.

## Employability measured against the metro

Rantoul workers participate in a labor market that extends well beyond the
town limits, and a defensible opinion has to account for that reach. The
firm builds its vocational findings on Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates for the Champaign-Urbana
metropolitan area and for Illinois as a whole, on state workforce agency
labor market information, and on targeted labor market research when the
dispute turns on whether specific work is actually available. Those sources
are cited qualitatively and methodically, and the resulting analysis is
described in the report in enough detail that an opposing expert can test
it, which is precisely the point.
