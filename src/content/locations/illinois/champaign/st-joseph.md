Whoever retains the expert, the other side gets the report. That is the
premise behind every opinion Purinton Analytics writes for a St. Joseph
case: the analysis is drafted to read identically under direct examination
and under cross, which is why the firm can and does accept retention from
plaintiff and defense counsel alike. For St. Joseph matters it provides
[vocational expert witness](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) opinions
on employability and earning capacity,
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) in catastrophic injury
litigation, and
[expert testimony and litigation consulting](/services/expert-testimony-litigation-consulting/)
when counsel needs the analysis defended at deposition or trial.

## Champaign County and the federal forums

St. Joseph lies in Champaign County, and its state-court matters are heard
in the Circuit Court serving the county. Federal work is accepted
throughout Illinois in the United States District Court for the Northern
District of Illinois, the United States District Court for the Central
District of Illinois, and the United States District Court for the
Southern District of Illinois. Which forum controls
a specific St. Joseph matter, and what registration or qualification steps
that forum requires of an expert, are settled with counsel during the
conflict check and intake instead of being assumed in advance.

## Grounding opinions in the market around St. Joseph

Vocational conclusions for a St. Joseph evaluee are built on Bureau of
Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the
Champaign-Urbana metropolitan area and for Illinois statewide, along with
state workforce agency labor market information and direct labor market
research when a placement question is squarely disputed. The analysis
describes the market qualitatively, connects the evaluee's documented
skills and history to work that exists within reach of this community, and
cites its sources so that the opposing expert's critique, whenever it
comes, has something concrete to engage with. The firm applies the same
scrutiny in reverse when engaged for
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/).

## The conflict check comes first

Neither side's retention proceeds without clearance, so the engagement
starts when counsel
[submits the caption or party names for a conflict check](/refer-a-case/),
including any disclosure, deposition, or trial dates already fixed. The
firm then returns a defined scope and a records request tailored to the
claims. Where the case requires economic loss testimony alongside the
vocational and care-planning work, the firm offers
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure, keeping each professional's signature on each
professional's opinion.
