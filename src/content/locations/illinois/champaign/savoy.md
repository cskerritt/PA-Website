Most calls about a Savoy case come with a date attached: an expert
disclosure deadline, a deposition already noticed, a trial setting that is
no longer theoretical. Purinton Analytics builds its Savoy work backward
from those dates, sequencing records review, evaluation, and report
drafting so the written opinion is complete and defensible before the
calendar forces anyone's hand. Within that schedule the firm provides
[vocational expert witness](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) opinions,
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[expert testimony and litigation consulting](/services/expert-testimony-litigation-consulting/),
retained by plaintiff or defense counsel with no difference in method
between them.

## The venue picture for a Savoy dispute

Savoy is a Champaign County community, and its state-court matters proceed
in the Circuit Court serving that county. In federal practice the firm
works throughout Illinois, where the available forums are the United States
District Court for the Northern District of Illinois, the United States
District Court for the Central District of Illinois, and the United States
District Court for the Southern District of Illinois. Rather than assert
from geography which district a Savoy case belongs in, the firm confirms
the controlling venue during the conflict check and intake, and it
identifies at the same stage any registration or qualification requirement
the chosen forum imposes.

## Labor market evidence on a litigation clock

Working against a disclosure deadline does not loosen the evidentiary
standard. Vocational findings for a Savoy evaluee rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the
Champaign-Urbana metropolitan area and for Illinois statewide, on state
workforce agency labor market information, and, when a specific placement
dispute justifies the time, on direct labor market research. Each source is
identified in the report so the analysis can be replicated and challenged.
When counsel needs the mirror image of this work, the firm applies the same
tests to an opposing expert's opinion through
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/).

## Reserving the dates that matter

Because scheduling is the point of leverage in a deadline-driven case, the
sensible first step is to
[submit a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) with the caption or party names
and every date already fixed on the docket. Once conflicts clear, the firm
returns a defined scope and a tailored records request, and evaluation
windows are reserved against the disclosure calendar. If the damages
theory also requires economic testimony, the firm arranges
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure, so the whole damages record moves on a single
timeline.
