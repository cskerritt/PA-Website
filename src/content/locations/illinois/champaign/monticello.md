A Monticello case starts with a venue fact that distinguishes it from much
of the surrounding metro: this is Piatt County litigation. The county line
matters to counsel choosing experts, because a report prepared for a Piatt
County jury should reflect the community the evaluee actually lives and
works in, not a template written for the principal city. Purinton Analytics
prepares its
[vocational expert witness](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) opinions,
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/) work for
Monticello matters with that audience in mind, and it takes referrals from
both sides of the caption on identical terms.

## Piatt County courts and the federal alternatives

State-court matters arising in Monticello proceed in the Circuit Court
serving Piatt County. Federal engagements are accepted statewide in the
United States District Court for the Northern District of Illinois, the
United States District Court for the Central District of Illinois, and the
United States District Court for the Southern District of Illinois. The firm treats the controlling district as a
question of record, settled with counsel during the conflict check and
intake rather than guessed from a map, and any venue-specific qualification
or registration requirement is confirmed in the same conversation.

## A small county inside a regional economy

Monticello is a small community whose workers are tied into the broader
Champaign-Urbana regional economy, and the vocational analysis has to hold
both facts at once. The firm grounds its findings in Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the
Champaign-Urbana metropolitan area and for Illinois statewide, in state
workforce agency labor market information, and in direct labor market
research when a genuinely disputed placement question calls for it. The
resulting opinion describes, in qualitative terms, what work exists within
reach of a Monticello evaluee and what that means for earning capacity, in
language an opposing expert can test and a jury can follow.

## Serving Piatt County by appointment

The firm has no office in Monticello and publishes no address here; it
serves this community by appointment and says so without hedging. Remote
interviews and standardized testing cover most engagements, in-person
evaluation is arranged in the evaluee's own community when the referral
questions require it, and deposition or trial appearances are scheduled
with travel from the Kansas City home office. Counsel can place this page
in context through the

## From caption to defined scope

Engagement begins when counsel
[requests a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) with the case caption and
parties, along with any disclosure, deposition, or trial dates already set.
The firm clears conflicts, defines the scope in writing, and issues a
records request tailored to the claims before opinion work starts, the same
sequence whether the retention comes from plaintiff or defense. When
economic loss testimony belongs in the case as well, the firm offers
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure, preserving the separation of roles that
cross-examination will probe.
