which district governs a Paxton dispute; the controlling venue is

## Earning capacity where the market is thin

Small-town cases put real pressure on labor market evidence, because the
question of what work exists nearby cannot be waved away with national
statistics. For a Paxton evaluee the firm works from Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the
Champaign-Urbana metropolitan area and for Illinois statewide, from state
workforce agency labor market information, and from direct labor market
research when the dispute turns on actual local availability.
Transferable skills analysis then maps the evaluee's history against that
evidence, and the report states its reasoning qualitatively, step by
step, so the path from data to conclusion is visible to both sides.

## What to send first

To open a Paxton file, counsel should
[submit the caption through the conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and
note any imminent disclosure, deposition, or trial dates. After conflicts
clear, the firm issues a written scope and a records request tailored to
the claims, and only then does opinion work begin. If the case also calls
for present-value economic testimony, the firm can structure
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure, giving a Ford County case the full damages record a
metropolitan case would receive.
