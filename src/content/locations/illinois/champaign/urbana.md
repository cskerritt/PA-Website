[vocational expert witness](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) opinions

[Champaign-Urbana metropolitan practice](/locations/illinois/champaign/).

## Courts serving Champaign County

State-court matters from Urbana proceed in the Circuit Court serving
Champaign County. On the federal side, the available forums are the United
States District Court for the Northern District of Illinois, the United
States District Court for the Central District of Illinois, and the United
States District Court for the Southern District of Illinois. Which of them controls a particular case is a question the firm
resolves with counsel during the conflict check and intake, not one it
assumes from a map. Any venue-specific registration or qualification
requirement is likewise identified and confirmed at that stage.

## Reading the regional labor market from its center

An earning-capacity opinion for an Urbana evaluee draws on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the
Champaign-Urbana metropolitan area and for Illinois statewide, supplemented
by state workforce agency labor market information and by direct labor
market research when a placement question is genuinely contested.
Transferable skills analysis ties the evaluee's documented work history to
occupations that actually exist in this market, so the opinion describes
real hiring conditions rather than national averages. The same qualitative
discipline applies on rebuttal, where the firm offers
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) of opposing
vocational and care-planning work.

## Appointments, not an office

The firm maintains no staffed office in Urbana and publishes no local street
address, because accuracy about presence is part of the same discipline that
governs its reports. Interviews and standardized testing are typically
completed remotely; when the referral questions call for in-person
evaluation, it is conducted in the evaluee's own community. Deposition and
trial appearances are scheduled with travel from the Kansas City home
office. Counsel weighing coverage beyond this metro can review the
[Illinois overview](/locations/illinois/) or the
[nationwide practice](/locations/nationwide/), which describe the same
remote-first structure applied across the country.

## Beginning an Urbana engagement

The first step is a conflict check, not a consultation. Submit the caption
or party names through the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and flag
any imminent disclosure, deposition, or trial dates so scheduling can be
confirmed up front. The firm responds with a defined scope and a records
request tailored to the claims before any opinion work begins. Where the
damages picture also requires economic testimony, the firm structures
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure, keeping professional roles distinct while giving
counsel a coherent damages record.
