Before any opinion about a Tolono worker is worth writing, the data behind
it has to be named. Purinton Analytics builds its Tolono analyses on
identified, checkable sources: Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational
employment and wage estimates for the Champaign-Urbana metropolitan area
and for Illinois statewide, state workforce agency labor market
information, and direct labor market research when a contested question
justifies it. From that foundation the firm delivers
[vocational expert witness](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) opinions,
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/) for Tolono
matters, on referral from either party to the litigation.

## Venue for Tolono matters

Tolono is part of Champaign County, and its state-court cases proceed in
the Circuit Court serving the county. Federal engagements are taken across
Illinois, in the United States District Court for the Northern District of
Illinois, the United States District Court for the Central District of
Illinois, and the United States District Court for the Southern District
of Illinois. The firm confirms the
controlling venue for each matter with counsel during the conflict check
and intake, and it identifies at the same time any expert qualification or
registration requirement the forum imposes, so venue never becomes a
late-breaking problem.

## What the numbers-free report still proves

A vocational report can be rigorous without ever resting on decoration.
For a Tolono evaluee the firm's transferable skills analysis starts from
the evaluee's documented education, training, and work history, tests that
profile against occupations present in the surrounding regional market,
and states in plain qualitative terms what employment remains open and
what has been foreclosed. Care-planning opinions follow the same
discipline, tying every recommended service to the medical record. The
methodology is spelled out so it can be challenged, which is the standard
the firm also enforces when reviewing an opposing expert's work through
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/).

## Referring a Tolono case

The route in is the same for both sides:
[request a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) with the case caption and
parties, and note any disclosure, deposition, or trial dates that are
already set, since evaluation windows are reserved against those dates. Once conflicts are cleared, counsel receives a defined scope
and a records request tailored to the claims before opinion work begins.
When economic testimony is part of the damages picture, the firm
structures
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure, a division of labor the report itself makes
explicit.
