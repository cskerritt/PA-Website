A defensible opinion about a Mahomet evaluee begins with the person, not
the place. Before any labor market source is opened, Purinton Analytics
assembles the individual's documented education, training, certifications,
and work history, because a transferable skills analysis is only as strong
as the record beneath it. On that foundation the firm provides
[vocational expert witness](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) opinions
on employability and earning capacity,
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) in catastrophic injury
litigation, and [medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/)
where future care is the disputed issue, and it accepts Mahomet referrals
from plaintiff and defense counsel on identical terms.

## Where Mahomet cases are litigated

Mahomet lies within Champaign County, so state-court matters proceed in
the Circuit Court serving the county. For federal matters the firm accepts
engagements throughout Illinois, in the United States District Court for
the Northern District of Illinois, the United States District Court for
the Central District of Illinois, and the United States District Court for
the Southern District of Illinois. The firm does not presume which
district controls a given dispute; that determination, along with any
venue-specific qualification or registration requirement, is made with
counsel during the conflict check and intake so the engagement is aligned
with the forum from the outset.

## From documented record to labor market

Once the evaluee's vocational profile is established from the underlying
documents, it is tested against the market. The firm draws on Bureau of
Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the
Champaign-Urbana metropolitan area and for Illinois statewide, on state
workforce agency labor market information, and on direct labor market
research when a placement question is genuinely contested. The report
moves in a visible sequence, profile first, then market evidence, then
conclusions, with each step stated qualitatively and each source
identified so an opposing expert has something concrete to test. The same
sequence runs in reverse when counsel retains the firm for
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) of another
expert's opinion, where the opening question is whether that expert's
profile of the evaluee actually matches the documents in the file.

## By-appointment service, stated plainly

Purinton Analytics keeps no office in Mahomet and publishes no local
address; service here is by appointment, and the firm says so directly
rather than implying a presence it does not have. Interviews and
standardized testing are ordinarily completed remotely. When the referral
questions call for an in-person evaluation, it is conducted in the
evaluee's own community, and deposition or trial appearances are met with
travel from the Kansas City home office. The

## Opening a Mahomet file

The engagement sequence is fixed: conflict check, written scope, tailored
records request, and only then the analytical work. Counsel can
[request a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) with the case caption and
parties, flagging any imminent disclosure, deposition, or trial dates so
scheduling is settled early. Where the damages case also requires economic
testimony, the firm structures
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure, keeping each professional's signature on that
professional's opinion while counsel manages a single coherent damages
record.
