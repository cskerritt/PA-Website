Purinton Analytics built its practice to work at a distance, and that design
is what it offers New Mexico counsel: records review, structured interviews,
standardized testing under publisher protocols where remote administration is
supported, and written opinions produced to one report standard wherever the
case is venued. Plaintiff firms and defense firms alike retain the practice
for earning capacity and employability questions, life care planning in
catastrophic injury matters, and coordinated damages engagements in which
independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis while
the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement
structure.

## How the firm serves New Mexico

The firm keeps no staffed office anywhere in New Mexico and publishes no
street address for the state, because accuracy about presence belongs to the
same discipline that governs its reports. Meetings are by appointment. Most
engagements begin and often finish remotely, with counsel receiving a single
point of contact from conflict check through testimony. When the referral
questions call for in-person evaluation of an evaluee in their own
community, or when deposition and trial calendars require presence, travel
is scheduled from the Kansas City home office and planned around the
litigation deadlines counsel identify at intake. Vocational findings for New
Mexico evaluees rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment
and wage estimates for New Mexico and its metropolitan areas, state
workforce agency labor market information, and direct labor market research
where warranted, so that the labor market described in the report is the one
the evaluee actually faces. Life care plans and medical cost projections for
New Mexico residents are priced to the evaluee's own market using
provider-specific inquiries and published fee data for the region, rather
than to a national average that may fit no one.

## Qualification and disclosure

Professional certifications held within the practice are issued by national
credentialing bodies rather than by any single state, and qualification to
testify in a New Mexico matter is determined by the forum under its own
rules of evidence. Any venue-specific registration or qualification
requirement is identified and confirmed during the conflict check and
intake process. Disclosure materials, prior testimony lists, and fee
schedules are provided in the form the venue and the parties require, and
the written opinion is prepared so that either side of the caption can
examine the record behind every conclusion.

## Starting a New Mexico engagement

Every engagement begins with a documented conflict check and a defined
scope. Submit the caption or party names through the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and note any imminent disclosure,
deposition, or trial dates so scheduling can be confirmed up front. Once the
conflict check clears, counsel receive a written scope and fee agreement
before any records are reviewed, so the boundaries of the engagement are
documented from the first day. Counsel
weighing a multi-state or out-of-state matter can review the firm's
[national coverage](/locations/nationwide/) for how the same engagement
structure operates across the country.
