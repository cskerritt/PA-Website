For counsel in the Beaver Dam, WI micropolitan area, the path to retaining
Purinton Analytics is deliberately short: a caption submitted for a conflict
check, a defined scope in writing, and a records request tailored to the
claims. From that foundation the firm delivers
[vocational expert](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) opinions on
employability and earning capacity,
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) for catastrophic
injuries, and [rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/)
of opposing expert work, for plaintiff and defense counsel in comparable
measure. Where economic damages are part of the assignment,
[coordinated damages assessments](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
engage independent economist partners who perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under the
same engagement structure.

## Where matters are venued

Beaver Dam engagements are accepted in the Circuit Court for Dodge County
and in the federal courts of Wisconsin, meaning the United States District
Court for the Eastern District of Wisconsin and the United States District
Court for the Western District of Wisconsin, as venue dictates. The report
a Dodge County jury sees is held to the same standard as a report filed in
any federal district: sourced, scoped, and written to survive the other
side's scrutiny. Counsel with matters spread across the state can find the
firm's full court coverage on the
[Wisconsin locations page](/locations/wisconsin/).

## Appointments, not an office

The firm has no staffed location in Beaver Dam and publishes no street
address here; claiming a local office it does not keep would sit poorly
with the accuracy its reports depend on. Work proceeds by appointment and,
for most engagements, remotely: structured video interviews, records review
on counsel's production schedule, and standardized testing under publisher
protocols where remote administration is supported. When an evaluation is
better conducted in person, it can be held in the evaluee's own community,
and deposition or trial appearances are scheduled from the Kansas City home
office to match the dates counsel supplies.

## Labor market research for Dodge County evaluees

Vocational findings for evaluees in the Beaver Dam area are grounded in
Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for
the area and for Wisconsin statewide, with state workforce agency labor
market information and direct employer contact used where published data
for a smaller market needs corroboration. Placeability and wage conclusions
are tied to the market the evaluee actually faces rather than to statewide
abstractions. Life care plans and
[medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/) for Dodge
County residents are priced through provider-specific inquiries and
published fee data for the evaluee's own region.

## Next steps for counsel

Availability is confirmed only after conflicts clear, so the engagement
starts with the parties, not the merits. To begin,
[request a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) with the case caption and
parties, and note any imminent disclosure, deposition, or trial dates so
scheduling can be assessed in the same pass. Any venue-specific
registration or qualification requirement is identified and confirmed
during the conflict check and intake process. Counsel handling matters
outside Wisconsin can review the firm's
[national coverage](/locations/nationwide/) for the same model applied
across other jurisdictions.
