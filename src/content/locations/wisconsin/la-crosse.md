The La Crosse-Onalaska metropolitan area crosses a state line, taking in
La Crosse County and Vernon County in Wisconsin and Houston County,
Minnesota.
Litigation follows that geography, and counsel need an expert practice
indifferent to which side of the line a case is filed on. Purinton Analytics
serves plaintiff and defense counsel throughout the metro with
[vocational expert](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) and
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) services, and with
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Venues on both sides of the line

Wisconsin matters are accepted for the La Crosse County Circuit Court and
the Vernon County Circuit Court, with federal cases proceeding in the United
States District Court for the Western District of Wisconsin; the firm also
appears in the United States District Court for the Eastern District of
Wisconsin when a matter is venued there. Cases arising in Houston County,
Minnesota are handled through the same national practice, with the proper
forum confirmed during intake. Any venue-specific registration or
qualification requirement, in either state, is identified and confirmed
during the conflict check and intake process. The
[Wisconsin page](/locations/wisconsin/) describes the firm's statewide
coverage.

## Presence by appointment

La Crosse meetings are by appointment, and the firm publishes no street
address for this location because it maintains no staffed office in the
metro; plain statements about geography belong in the same category as
plain statements about methodology. Most engagements run remotely from
intake onward: electronic records exchange, structured interviews by video,
and standardized testing under publisher protocols where remote
administration is supported. When an in-person evaluation is warranted on
either side of the state line, or when deposition or trial requires
attendance, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office. The
state line changes the forum, not the logistics: counsel in Wisconsin and
Minnesota work with the same team on the same terms.

## Labor market research across a state line

Vocational conclusions for metro evaluees rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates for the La Crosse-Onalaska area,
supplemented by the statewide estimates for Wisconsin or Minnesota that
match the evaluee's labor market, state workforce agency labor market
information, and direct labor market research where warranted. Life care
plans and [medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/) are
priced to the evaluee's own community through provider-specific inquiries
and published regional fee data, since a resident of Houston County,
Minnesota and a resident of La Crosse County may face different provider
networks despite sharing a metro.
