Smaller venues deserve the same expert discipline as the largest ones, and
the Whitewater-Elkhorn, WI micropolitan area is served on exactly that
premise. Counsel in Walworth County matters retain Purinton Analytics for
[vocational expert](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) evaluations of
employability and earning capacity, [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/)
in serious injury cases, and
[coordinated damages assessments](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under the same
engagement structure. Referrals come from plaintiff and defense counsel
alike, and the reports are written for the reader who will try to take them
apart.

## Courts serving Walworth County

Whitewater and Elkhorn matters are accepted in the Circuit Court for
Walworth County and, for federal cases, in the United States District Court
for the Eastern District of Wisconsin or the United States District Court
for the Western District of Wisconsin, according to where the action is
venued. A case in a smaller circuit receives the same conflict check,
defined scope, and documented methodology as a case in a major metropolitan
court; forum size never sets the standard of care. Statewide coverage,
including the other Wisconsin courts the firm serves, is described on the
[Wisconsin locations page](/locations/wisconsin/).

## By appointment in the Whitewater area

The firm keeps no staffed office in Whitewater or Elkhorn and publishes no
street address for this location. Meetings are by appointment, and the
by-appointment model is a genuine advantage in a smaller market: rather than
asking an evaluee to travel to a distant office, the firm conducts most work
remotely and, when an in-person evaluation is warranted, comes to the
evaluee's community. Records review, structured interviews, and standardized
testing under publisher-supported remote protocols proceed without travel at
all. Deposition and trial appearances are scheduled from the Kansas City
home office around counsel's dates.

## Researching a smaller labor market

Labor market analysis for Walworth County evaluees is anchored in Bureau of
Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the area and
for Wisconsin statewide, with state workforce agency labor market
information and direct employer research filling in where published
estimates for a smaller market run thin. That layered approach matters in a
micropolitan economy, where an individual occupation's local prospects can differ
sharply from statewide patterns. Life care plans and
[medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/) are likewise
priced to the evaluee's own market using provider-specific inquiries and
published fee data rather than metropolitan or national averages.

## Starting the conflict check

An engagement begins when the parties clear conflicts, so the caption comes
before the conversation. Submit the case caption or party names through the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and note any imminent disclosure,
deposition, or trial dates so scheduling can be reviewed at the same time.
Any venue-specific registration or qualification requirement is identified
and confirmed during the conflict check and intake process. Counsel who also
practice outside Wisconsin can consult the firm's
[national coverage](/locations/nationwide/) to see how the same remote-first
engagement model reaches courts across the country.
