## How a Holmen evaluation actually proceeds

Most of the file work in a vocational or life care planning engagement, records review, research, report drafting, requires no travel at all, and the clinical interview itself is frequently conducted by structured remote session with the same instruments and the same documentation standards as an in-person meeting. Where the referral questions call for direct observation or a hands-on assessment, the evaluation is scheduled in the evaluee's community rather than requiring a trip to a distant office, and the evaluator travels from the Kansas City home office. The firm keeps no staffed location in Holmen and prints no local address, because it will not imply a presence it does not maintain.
