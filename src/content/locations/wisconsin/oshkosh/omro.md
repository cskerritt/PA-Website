## A Small Town Inside a Metro Labor Market

Although Omro is a small community, its residents compete in the labor market of the wider Oshkosh-Neenah metropolitan area, and the vocational analysis reflects that. Findings rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for that metro area and for Wisconsin statewide, together with state workforce agency information and direct labor market research when warranted.
