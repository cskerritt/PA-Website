Weston sits in Marathon County, and civil matters arising there ordinarily proceed in the Circuit Court, whose scheduling orders govern when expert disclosures and reports come due. ## Evaluations in the evaluee's community

The firm keeps no office in Weston and says so plainly, because accuracy about presence belongs to the same discipline that governs its reports. Most engagements proceed remotely, with secure video interviews and records exchanged through counsel. When the referral questions call for an in-person evaluation, it is conducted in the evaluee's own community rather than forcing travel on an injured person, and when deposition or trial testimony requires physical presence, that travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office against the court's calendar. Files that also need an economic damages calculation proceed as [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/), in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning work continues under one engagement structure.
