When a matter in the Dane County Circuit Court or the United States District
Court for the Western District of Wisconsin needs a vocational expert or a
life care planner, Purinton Analytics accepts the engagement from either
side of the caption. The Madison practice covers
[vocational expert witness work](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) on
earning capacity and employability,
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) in catastrophic injury
matters, and [coordinated damages assessment](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/),
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. The opinions are written to be read by opposing
counsel, which is the only durable standard an expert report can have.

## Counties and courts of the Madison metro

The Madison metropolitan area reaches beyond Dane County into Columbia
County, Green County, and Iowa County, and engagements are accepted for
matters in the circuit courts of each. Federal cases from the region
generally proceed in the United States District Court for the Western
District of Wisconsin; the firm also appears in the United States District
Court for the Eastern District of Wisconsin when a matter is venued there.
Statewide context, including the other Wisconsin metros the practice serves,
is collected on the [Wisconsin page](/locations/wisconsin/). Any
venue-specific registration or qualification requirement is identified and
confirmed during the conflict check and intake process.

## A practice built for appointments, not an office

There is no staffed Madison office and no published street address for this
location; saying so plainly is part of the same discipline the firm applies
to its reports. Meetings happen by appointment, and most of the work does
not require them: records review, structured interviews by video, and
standardized testing under publisher protocols where remote administration
is supported all proceed at a distance. When an evaluation must be conducted
in person, or when a deposition or trial date requires presence in a Madison
courtroom, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office and planned
around the litigation calendar rather than the other way around.

## Grounding opinions in the Madison labor market

Employability and earning capacity conclusions for Madison area evaluees are
grounded in Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage
estimates for the Madison metropolitan area and for Wisconsin statewide,
along with state workforce agency labor market information and direct labor
market research where the questions call for it. Life care plans and medical
cost projections are priced to the evaluee's own market using
provider-specific inquiries and published regional fee data. When testimony
is needed, [expert testimony and litigation consulting](/services/expert-testimony-litigation-consulting/)
carries the same findings through deposition and trial without changing the
underlying method.

## Beginning with a conflict check

Retention starts the same way for plaintiff and defense counsel alike:
[request a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) with the case caption and party
names, and flag any disclosure, deposition, or trial dates already on the
calendar. Once the check clears, scope and fee terms are confirmed in
writing before any substantive work begins. Counsel with related matters in
other states can review the firm's
[national coverage](/locations/nationwide/) to see how the same structure
extends across the country.
