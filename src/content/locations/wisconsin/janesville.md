Matters venued in the Circuit Court for Rock County are where most
Janesville-Beloit, WI engagements for Purinton Analytics begin. The firm
provides [vocational expert witness](/services/vocational-expert-witness/)
opinions, [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) of opposing
expert work for counsel on either side of the caption, and it structures
every engagement so the resulting opinion can stand up to the scrutiny of
the lawyers who did not retain it. Where a matter requires economic
damages, [coordinated damages assessments](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
pair the vocational and care-planning work with independent economist
partners who perform and sign the economic analysis under a unified
engagement structure.

## Courts served across Rock County

State court work in the metro centers on the Circuit Court for Rock County,
covering the injury, employment, and family matters in which vocational and
care-planning evidence typically arises. Federal matters are accepted in the
United States District Court for the Eastern District of Wisconsin and the
United States District Court for the Western District of Wisconsin,
whichever holds the case. Because the practice is built around a written
methodology rather than a courthouse, counsel in Janesville and Beloit
receive the same disclosure-ready work product used in courts throughout the
state; the [Wisconsin page](/locations/wisconsin/) describes that statewide
footprint.

## How meetings and evaluations are scheduled

There is no staffed Janesville office and no published street address for
this location. Meetings happen by appointment, and most steps of an
engagement proceed remotely: records are reviewed as they are produced,
interviews are conducted by video under a structured protocol, and
standardized instruments are administered remotely where the publisher
supports it. When the referral questions call for an in-person evaluation of
the evaluee, or when a deposition or trial requires physical presence,
travel is arranged from the Kansas City home office and planned around the
court's calendar. Counsel keep the same point of contact from intake through
testimony.

## Grounding opinions in the regional labor market

Employability and earning capacity findings for Rock County evaluees are
built on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage
estimates for the area and for Wisconsin as a whole, together with state
workforce agency labor market information and direct labor market research
when the questions warrant it. The analysis names its sources so opposing
counsel can check them. Life care plans and
[medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/) for area
residents are priced against the evaluee's own regional market through
provider-specific inquiries and published fee data, since local care costs
can diverge from national figures in either direction.

## Requesting a conflict check

Engagements open with a conflict check rather than a sales conversation.
Send the caption or the party names through the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/), flag any disclosure deadline,
deposition, or trial date that is already set, and the firm will confirm
availability and scope before any substantive discussion of the case. Any
venue-specific registration or qualification requirement is identified and
confirmed during the conflict check and intake process. For counsel whose
dockets extend beyond Wisconsin, the [nationwide page](/locations/nationwide/)
explains how the same engagement model travels to other jurisdictions.
