Greenville is a commuter community, and that fact shapes how a Greenville earning-capacity question has to be answered. A resident's pre-injury work life and post-injury alternatives both spread across the [Appleton metro area](/locations/wisconsin/appleton/), so an opinion that treats the town as its own labor market would misstate the evaluee's real options. Purinton Analytics builds its Greenville [vocational expert witness](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) opinions and [life care plans](/services/life-care-planning/) around the commuting market the evaluee actually faces, for plaintiff and defense engagements alike.

## Measuring the market a Greenville worker occupies

The analytical foundation is Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Appleton metropolitan area and for Wisconsin statewide, read together with state workforce agency labor market information. Transferable-skills analysis matches the evaluee's demonstrated skills against occupations present in that regional data, and where the referral questions justify it, direct labor market research tests whether the identified occupations are hiring in practice, not just listed in a table. Scale is described qualitatively and every figure-bearing source is cited rather than summarized, so opposing counsel can pull the same data and check the work.

## Where a Greenville matter is heard

Greenville lies in Outagamie County, and civil matters involving its residents commonly proceed in the county's Circuit Court. Wisconsin's federal forums, the United States District Court for the Eastern District of Wisconsin and the United States District Court for the Western District of Wisconsin, are available where jurisdiction supports them; the venue that controls a given case is settled during the conflict check and intake rather than asserted in advance. Whatever the forum, the written opinion is prepared as if the most exacting admissibility standard applies, a practice described further on the [Wisconsin locations page](/locations/wisconsin/).

## Appointment-based service, honestly described

The firm maintains no Greenville office and does not manufacture the appearance of one. ## From vocational findings to damages

Vocational conclusions often need an economic layer before they become a damages number. For those cases the firm offers [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement structure. The division of labor is explicit in the reports: the vocational expert opines on capacity and access to employment, the life care planner on future care needs, the economist partners on valuation, and each opinion stands on its own record.
