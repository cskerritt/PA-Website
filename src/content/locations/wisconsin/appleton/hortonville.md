## Reviewing the opposing file

A rebuttal engagement for a Hortonville matter examines the opposing expert's methodology the way a court would: were the referral questions answered or reframed, do the cited sources say what the report claims, does the transferable-skills analysis reflect the evaluee's actual record, and are the care-plan items tied to the medical evidence or merely listed? The review is written with the same restraint the firm applies to its own opinions, because an overreaching critique is as impeachable as an overreaching report. Where the opposing work is sound, the review says so; counsel is better served by an honest assessment than a flattering one, and a credibility budget spent on weak criticisms is not available for the strong ones.
