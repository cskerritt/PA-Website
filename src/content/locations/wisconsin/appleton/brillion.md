Catastrophic injury cases from small communities carry the same lifetime-care questions as cases from anywhere else, and for a Brillion family those questions are urgent long before trial. Purinton Analytics builds [life care plans](/services/life-care-planning/) for Brillion evaluees that document future medical, therapeutic, and support needs item by item against the medical record, and pairs that work with [vocational expert witness](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) analysis when earning capacity is also in dispute. Plaintiff and defense counsel retain the firm on identical terms.

## What a defensible care plan looks like

A life care plan for a Brillion matter is not a wish list; it is a sourced projection. Each recommended item traces to a treating provider's records, a published standard of care, or a clinical rationale stated in the plan itself, and each is priced from disclosed sources rather than asserted. The plan distinguishes what the medical evidence requires from what it merely permits, because a plan that cannot make that distinction will not survive the opposing expert's review. The same documentation discipline governs the firm's vocational reports, where post-injury earning capacity is reasoned from the evaluee's record and the regional data, never assumed.

## Earning capacity in the regional economy

Although Brillion is a small Calumet County community, its workers participate in the wider labor market of the [Appleton metro area](/locations/wisconsin/appleton/), and vocational conclusions are calibrated to that reality. The firm relies on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Appleton metropolitan area and for Wisconsin statewide, supplemented by state workforce agency labor market information and direct labor market research where the referral questions warrant it. Market conditions are characterized qualitatively in the narrative, with quantitative support confined to cited exhibits.
