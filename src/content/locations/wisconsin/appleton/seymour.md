## Data first, then opinion

The order matters. A transferable-skills analysis for a Seymour evaluee begins with the person's documented education, training, and work history, maps those against occupations present in the regional data, and only then reaches conclusions about post-injury earning capacity. Where the referral questions justify it, direct labor market research tests whether the occupations the tables identify are actually hiring within a reasonable commute of Seymour. Scale and availability are characterized qualitatively in the prose, with the underlying sources cited so that opposing counsel can reproduce the search rather than take the expert's word for it.

## A small town tied to a regional market

Seymour is a small Outagamie County community within the [Appleton metro area](/locations/wisconsin/appleton/), and its residents' employment realities span both the local economy and the wider region.
