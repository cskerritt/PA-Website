## What each engagement stage produces

The conflict check produces a clean record that neither side's confidences are compromised. The scope letter fixes the referral questions, so the opinion answers what was asked and nothing it was not. The records request is tailored to the claims, because a vocational file padded with irrelevant records is as vulnerable as one missing the essential ones. The report itself states its methodology, lists its sources, and reasons in the open. And testimony preparation treats the deposition as the report's proving ground rather than an afterthought. Counsel in a Combined Locks case can rely on each stage arriving in order, on the calendar the scheduling order dictates.
