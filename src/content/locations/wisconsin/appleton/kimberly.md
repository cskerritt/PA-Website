Expert disclosure deadlines drive Kimberly referrals more often than anything else, and Purinton Analytics organizes its Kimberly work around that calendar. At intake the firm maps the scheduling order: when the report is due, when depositions are likely, when a rebuttal window opens. Scope and records requests are then sized so the [vocational expert witness](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) opinion or [life care plan](/services/life-care-planning/) arrives complete, not rushed, whichever side of the caption issued the retention.

## Working backward from the scheduling order

A defensible opinion cannot be compressed into the final week before disclosure, so the firm sequences Kimberly engagements deliberately: records are requested immediately after the conflict check clears, the evaluee interview is set early, and draft-stage analysis leaves time for counsel to understand the findings before they are served. When a matter arrives late, the firm says so plainly and defines what can responsibly be opined on the time remaining rather than promising a full workup that the calendar cannot support. That candor is the same discipline that governs the reports themselves.

## The labor market behind the opinion

Kimberly is a small community within the [Appleton metro area](/locations/wisconsin/appleton/) commuting market, and its residents' employment options are evaluated at that regional scale. Vocational conclusions rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Appleton metropolitan area and for Wisconsin statewide, on state workforce agency labor market information, and on direct labor market research when the referral questions warrant confirmation that identified occupations are truly attainable. Findings are stated qualitatively where precision is unsupported, and every quantitative source is cited for verification.

## Logistics without a local office

The firm keeps no office in Kimberly and does not imply one. Records work, conferences, and most interviews are handled remotely under secure protocols; in-person evaluation, when needed, is arranged in the evaluee's own community; and testimony travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office. The model scales from small Wisconsin communities to the firm's [nationwide practice](/locations/nationwide/) without changing shape. Where the damages case requires economic quantification, [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) bring in independent economist partners who perform and sign the economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement structure.
