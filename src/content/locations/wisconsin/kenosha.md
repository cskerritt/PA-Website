Attorneys litigating injury, employment, and disability matters in the
Kenosha, WI metropolitan area engage Purinton Analytics for
[vocational expert](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) opinions on
employability and earning capacity, [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/)
in catastrophic injury cases, and
[coordinated damages assessments](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under the same
engagement structure. The practice takes referrals from plaintiff and defense
counsel alike, and every report is drafted on the assumption that the other
side of the caption will read it closely.

## Venue footprint in Kenosha County

Engagements are accepted for matters venued in the Circuit Court for Kenosha
County and in Wisconsin's federal courts, whether a given case proceeds in
the United States District Court for the Eastern District of Wisconsin or in
the United States District Court for the Western District of Wisconsin, as
venue dictates. The work product does not change with the
forum: the same conflict check, the same defined scope, and the same
documented methodology apply whether the matter is a personal injury claim
in state court or an employment dispute in federal court. Counsel with
related matters in other parts of the state can consult the
[Wisconsin locations page](/locations/wisconsin/) for statewide coverage.

## By-appointment meetings, without a local storefront

Kenosha meetings are scheduled by appointment. The firm maintains no staffed
office in the metro and publishes no street address for this location,
because accuracy about presence is part of the same discipline that governs
its reports. Most engagements begin remotely with records review, structured
interviews, and standardized testing administered under publisher protocols
where remote administration is supported. When an in-person evaluation is
warranted, or when deposition and trial calendars require attendance, travel
is scheduled from the Kansas City home office and coordinated around
counsel's dates. The result is the same report standard and the same point
of contact regardless of where any given step of the work is performed.

## Labor market research for Kenosha evaluees

Vocational findings for evaluees in the Kenosha area rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the metro area and
for Wisconsin statewide, supplemented by state workforce agency labor market
information and direct employer contact where the referral questions warrant
it. Transferable skills analysis is tied to the evaluee's documented work
history rather than to generic occupational assumptions, and placeability
conclusions are grounded in the labor market the evaluee actually faces.
Life care plans and [medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/)
for Kenosha County residents are priced to the evaluee's own market using
provider-specific inquiries and published fee data rather than national
averages.

## Starting an engagement

Every Kenosha matter begins with a conflict check, not a consultation,
because availability cannot be confirmed until the parties are
cleared. Submit the caption or party names through the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and note any imminent disclosure,
deposition, or trial dates so scheduling can be assessed at the same time.
Any venue-specific registration or qualification requirement is identified
and confirmed during the conflict check and intake process. Counsel weighing
whether the practice fits a matter venued outside Wisconsin can review the
firm's [national coverage](/locations/nationwide/), which describes how the
same remote-first model serves courts across the country.
