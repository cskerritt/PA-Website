A life care plan is a lifetime document. For a catastrophically injured resident of Oregon, Wisconsin, it must anticipate a lifetime of treatment, equipment, home modification, and attendant care, and it must do so in prices and provider realities that will be interrogated line by line at deposition. Purinton Analytics prepares [life care plans](/services/life-care-planning/) and [medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/) for Oregon matters with that interrogation in mind, and pairs them with vocational analysis of what the injury has done to the evaluee's working life. The practice takes these engagements from counsel for the injured person and from counsel defending the claim, applying an identical methodology in either posture.

## The venue picture for an Oregon case

Oregon is a Dane County community, and civil actions from the town are ordinarily filed in the Circuit Court serving Dane County. When a case belongs in federal court, Wisconsin's trial forums are the United States District Court for the Western District of Wisconsin and the United States District Court for the Eastern District of Wisconsin; the district that actually governs is worked out during the conflict check and intake, not presumed from the map. Life care planning testimony travels well between forums because the plan's foundations, the medical record and the published cost data, do not change with the caption.

## Vocational loss measured against the metro

Care needs are personal, but employment prospects are regional. When the engagement includes earning capacity, the analysis situates the Oregon evaluee in the labor market of the surrounding Madison area, using Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Madison metropolitan area and for Wisconsin statewide, state workforce agency labor market information, and direct labor market research where the questions require more than published tables. The metro's overall market and the counties within it are treated on the [Madison metro page](/locations/wisconsin/madison/), and the [vocational expert witness](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) service page describes the employability methodology itself.

## Assessment in the evaluee's own setting

A life care plan benefits from seeing how the evaluee actually lives, which is why in-person assessment for an Oregon evaluee happens in the evaluee's home community when the referral warrants it, with travel scheduled from the Kansas City home office. Where remote assessment serves the case, interviews and record reviews proceed by secure video. The firm keeps no office in Oregon and publishes no address there; counsel engage a national by-appointment practice, described on the [nationwide page](/locations/nationwide/), whose local presence is the evaluation itself rather than a storefront.

## Bringing the practice into the case

Catastrophic injury cases reward early expert involvement, while records are fresh and treatment paths are still being established. Oregon counsel can begin by submitting the caption or party names through the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/), noting the nature of the injuries and any case management deadlines. After clearance come scope, fee terms, and a records request shaped to the claims. The practice's Wisconsin coverage as a whole is outlined on the [state page](/locations/wisconsin/). The plan that results is written so that every projected cost can be defended by pointing to a source, which is what separates a life care plan from a wish list.
