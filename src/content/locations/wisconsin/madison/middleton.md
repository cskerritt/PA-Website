## Courts available to Middleton litigants

Middleton is a Dane County community, and state court matters from the town proceed in the Circuit Court for that county. On the federal side, Wisconsin is served by the United States District Court for the Western District of Wisconsin and the United States District Court for the Eastern District of Wisconsin; both stand as available forums, and the venue that governs a specific dispute is pinned down with counsel during the conflict check and intake. Because deposition and trial testimony are part of the engagement when needed, scheduling for court appearances is addressed at retention rather than discovered at the close of discovery.

## Logistics that survive scrutiny too

Cross-examiners probe logistics as readily as methodology, so the practice keeps its logistics plain. There is no Middleton office and no local address to publish; meetings are by appointment, evaluations are conducted remotely by secure video or in person in the evaluee's community, and any travel runs from the Kansas City home office.
