The referral question in a Windsor case is usually easy to state and hard to answer: what could this person have earned but for the injury, and what can they earn now? Everything else in a damages case hangs on how carefully that gap is measured. Purinton Analytics answers it for Windsor counsel through [vocational expert witness](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) analysis grounded in the record and the market, through [rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) when the question has already been answered badly by someone else, and through coordinated work with [forensic economic damages](/services/forensic-economic-damages/) analyses that independent economist partners perform and sign while the vocational assessment proceeds under the same engagement. Both sides of the caption are welcome to ask.

## Windsor's courts, state and federal

As a Dane County community, Windsor sends its state court civil matters, in the ordinary course, to the Circuit Court serving that county. Federal litigation involving Windsor parties has Wisconsin's trial-level forums available to it: the United States District Court for the Eastern District of Wisconsin and the United States District Court for the Western District of Wisconsin. Which district controls a specific case is a venue determination made with counsel during the conflict check and intake. The measurement of an earning capacity gap does not vary by forum, and neither does the practice's documentation of it.

## Sizing the gap against the regional market

Windsor is a smaller community whose labor market is, functionally, the Madison area's. Pre-injury capacity is reconstructed from the evaluee's earnings history, education, and skills; post-injury capacity is tested against occupations that actually exist in the surrounding regional market. The published foundation is Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Madison metropolitan area and for Wisconsin statewide, augmented by state workforce agency labor market information and, where a referral turns on it, direct inquiry with employers. A fuller account of that regional market appears on the [Madison metro page](/locations/wisconsin/madison/).

## Getting the evaluation done, wherever the evaluee is

Windsor engagements run on the practice's standard by-appointment model. The document work is remote from the start; vocational interviews are held by secure video unless the referral questions justify in-person assessment, in which case the evaluation comes to the evaluee's community and travel is arranged from the Kansas City home office. The practice has no Windsor office and no local address to print, and says so directly, on the theory that a report is only as credible as the practice behind it. The architecture of the national practice is described on the [nationwide page](/locations/nationwide/).

To put the earning capacity question, or any damages question, to the practice, Windsor counsel should start with the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/), providing the caption or party names and a brief description of the claims and posture.
