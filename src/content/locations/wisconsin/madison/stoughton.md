Purinton Analytics keeps no office in Stoughton, publishes no Stoughton address, and does not dress its coverage of the town up as anything other than what it is: a national, by-appointment practice that serves Stoughton counsel remotely and, when the work requires presence, in person. The firm says this plainly because candor about its own footprint comes from the same habit of mind that governs its reports. Counsel who retain the practice for [vocational expert witness](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) opinions or [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) in a Stoughton matter get that habit applied to every page of the file, and retention is open to plaintiff and defense counsel on identical terms.

## The county and the courts behind a Stoughton case

Stoughton lies in Dane County, and civil matters arising in the community are ordinarily filed in the Circuit Court serving that county. Cases that belong in federal court have Wisconsin's district courts to consider, the United States District Court for the Western District of Wisconsin and the United States District Court for the Eastern District of Wisconsin, and the practice treats the choice between them as counsel's venue call to confirm at intake, not a detail to be guessed at. Reports carry the full expanded court name and the operative caption from the outset, together with a sourcing appendix, so nothing in the document has to be retrofitted once the venue picture settles.

## Working with the grain of the published data

Labor market data is published at the scale of metropolitan areas and states, not small cities, and the practice works with that grain rather than against it. An earning capacity opinion for a Stoughton evaluee therefore rests on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Madison metropolitan area and for Wisconsin statewide, joined with state workforce agency labor market information and with direct labor market research when a referral question turns on hiring realities the published tables cannot show. The evaluee's own work history, skills, and residual capacity then determine which parts of that market are realistically open, and the report says so in terms that can be checked against the sources. The regional market, and the counties the practice covers around the principal city, are described on the [Madison metro page](/locations/wisconsin/madison/).

## What by-appointment service looks like from Stoughton

Most phases of an engagement never needed an office. Records analysis and transferable skills work proceed remotely from the start, and vocational interviews are held by secure video at times built around the evaluee's treatment and work obligations. When the referral calls for an in-person evaluation, the assessment takes place in the evaluee's own community rather than requiring the evaluee to travel to the expert, and the firm's travel is scheduled from its Kansas City home office. Deposition and trial appearances are calendared with counsel the same way. The structure of the national practice behind this model, and the reasoning for building it without branch offices, is set out on the [nationwide page](/locations/nationwide/).

## Opening a Stoughton engagement

Everything waits on the conflict check. Submit the caption or the party names through the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/), together with a short description of the claims and any dates the court has already fixed. Once conflicts clear, counsel receive a defined scope, fee terms, and a records request tailored to the case. Coverage across the rest of the state is collected on the [Wisconsin page](/locations/wisconsin/). The commitment made at intake is the one kept at trial: every opinion sourced, every limitation acknowledged, and nothing asserted, about the firm or about the evaluee, that the record cannot support.
