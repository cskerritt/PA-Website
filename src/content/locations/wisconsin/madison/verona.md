Verona's workforce does not stop at the municipal line, and neither should an earning capacity analysis. A Verona evaluee's realistic employment options are spread across the Madison area, which means a credible vocational opinion has to be built on the metro market rather than a town-sized slice of it. Purinton Analytics approaches [vocational expert witness](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) engagements from Verona on exactly that footing, for plaintiffs establishing a loss and for defendants testing one, and pairs the vocational work with [coordinated damages assessment](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) when counsel want the full damages picture: independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement structure.

## Venue for Verona disputes

Verona belongs to Dane County, and civil actions arising in the town are generally filed in the county's Circuit Court. Federal-question and diversity matters have Wisconsin's federal trial courts available to them, the United States District Court for the Western District of Wisconsin and the United States District Court for the Eastern District of Wisconsin, with the venue that actually controls confirmed as part of the conflict check and intake. The practice's reports are prepared to the same evidentiary standard for either system, so a removal or remand mid-case changes the caption but not the analysis.

## Measuring the commuter's market

Because Verona residents commute throughout the region, the practice measures opportunity where the jobs actually are. That means Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Madison metropolitan area and for Wisconsin statewide, state workforce agency labor market information, and direct labor market research when a disputed occupation requires evidence about real hiring practice rather than published averages. Transferable skills analysis then connects the evaluee's work history and residual capacity to occupations that exist in that market in meaningful numbers, qualitatively assessed and fully sourced. The [Madison metro page](/locations/wisconsin/madison/) describes the regional market and the counties it spans.

## Evaluations without a local office

The practice tells Verona counsel the same thing it tells counsel everywhere: there is no local storefront, and none is needed. Records analysis and vocational interviews run remotely by secure video as the default; when an in-person evaluation better serves the referral questions, it takes place in the evaluee's own community, with travel scheduled from the Kansas City home office. Life care planning site visits and home assessments follow the same model. The full remote-first architecture of the practice is laid out on the [nationwide page](/locations/nationwide/), and it exists so that geography never dilutes the quality of the file.

## Opening a Verona engagement

The engagement opens with conflicts, not with commitments. Submit the caption or the names of the parties through the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/), note the claims and the posture of the case, and identify any disclosure or deposition dates already set. After clearance, counsel receive a scope, a fee agreement, and a records request matched to the questions asked. Statewide coverage, including the rest of Wisconsin served by the practice, is summarized on the [Wisconsin page](/locations/wisconsin/). Whether the case settles in mediation or is tried to verdict, the analysis is built to hold either way.
