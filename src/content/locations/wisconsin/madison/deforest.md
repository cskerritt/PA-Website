Before any opinion is written in a DeForest matter, the file has to be built. Purinton Analytics starts with the paper: conflicts cleared against the parties, a written scope, and then medical, employment, educational, and earnings records requested with the claims in mind rather than by generic checklist. Only after the record is assembled do the analytical products follow, whether that is a [vocational expert witness](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) opinion on employability and earning capacity or a [medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/) quantifying disputed future care. DeForest counsel on the plaintiff side and the defense side are served on the same terms, because a file-first practice has no reason to care who retained it.

## County and courthouse context for DeForest

DeForest is located in Dane County, and state court proceedings from the community are generally brought in that county's Circuit Court. Where federal jurisdiction exists, the available forums are the United States District Court for the Western District of Wisconsin and the United States District Court for the Eastern District of Wisconsin, and the district controlling any specific case is confirmed during the conflict check and intake rather than assumed. Because the practice's opinions are records-driven, they transfer cleanly between forums; a change of venue changes the caption page and nothing in the analysis.

## From the record to the regional market

A DeForest evaluee's earning history comes from the file; the earning future has to come from the market. The practice connects record and market with Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Madison metropolitan area and for Wisconsin statewide, state workforce agency labor market information, and direct labor market research when the referral turns on a specific occupation or employer practice. Every market observation in the report is tied to its source the same way every functional limitation is tied to a medical record. The surrounding regional market and the counties it includes are described on the [Madison metro page](/locations/wisconsin/madison/).

## Where the evaluation happens

The records phase is fully remote, and the interview phase usually is too, conducted by secure video at times that fit the evaluee's treatment and work schedule. When the referral questions justify an in-person evaluation, the practice comes to the evaluee's community; travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office. There is no DeForest office, no local address, and no suggestion of either, because the same care that keeps invented facts out of the report keeps them off the website. How that remote-first structure supports engagements nationally is described on the [nationwide page](/locations/nationwide/).

## Opening the file

The document that opens every engagement is the conflict check. DeForest counsel should submit the caption or party names through the [referral form](/refer-a-case/), summarize the claims, and identify any disclosure or trial dates already scheduled. Clearance is followed by scope and fee terms and by the records request that starts the file in earnest. Coverage across the rest of the state, including the other communities served, appears on the [Wisconsin page](/locations/wisconsin/). What DeForest counsel receive at the end is a report whose every sentence can be traced backward to something in the file, which is the only kind of report worth serving.
