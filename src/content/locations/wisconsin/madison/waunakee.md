Plaintiff and defense counsel retain vocational experts for different reasons, but the method cannot change with the client. In a Waunakee injury or employment case, the plaintiff's lawyer needs a loss established from the record; the defense lawyer needs the claimed loss stress-tested against the same record. Purinton Analytics does both kinds of work, often in the same week, and the discipline that makes that possible is that the answer is never chosen before the analysis is done. The practice offers [vocational expert witness](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) opinions, life care planning, and [rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) of opposing experts' reports, on identical methodological terms for either side of the caption.

## The courts that hear Waunakee matters

Waunakee sits within Dane County, whose Circuit Court is the usual state forum for civil matters arising in the community. Wisconsin's federal bench sits in the United States District Court for the Western District of Wisconsin and the United States District Court for the Eastern District of Wisconsin; both are available forums for cases that belong in federal court, and the district that governs a particular filing is settled with counsel during the conflict check and intake. Rebuttal assignments frequently arrive after venue is fixed and the opposing disclosure is in hand, and the practice is accustomed to entering a case at that later stage.

## A small community inside a large market

Waunakee is a smaller suburban community whose residents draw their employment from the broader Madison area. When the question is what work an evaluee can perform and what it pays, the analysis rests on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Madison metropolitan area and for Wisconsin statewide, along with state workforce agency labor market information and direct research where a specific placement question demands it. The same sources are used to audit an opposing expert's opinion: a rebuttal that cannot cite better data than the report it criticizes is advocacy, not analysis. The regional market is described on the [Madison metro page](/locations/wisconsin/madison/).

## Appointments, video, and travel

Waunakee counsel and evaluees deal with the practice by appointment. There is no local office and no published address in the community, and the practice says so rather than implying otherwise. Interviews and record reviews proceed by secure video in the ordinary course; in-person evaluation, when the referral warrants it, happens in the evaluee's own community with travel arranged from the Kansas City home office. Deposition and trial appearances are calendared the same way. The structure of the national practice behind those logistics is set out on the [nationwide page](/locations/nationwide/).

## Retention and the conflict check

Because the practice serves both sides of the bar, the conflict check is not a formality; it is the gate everything else waits behind. Counsel should submit the caption or party names through the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) before sending any substantive material, and note whether the assignment is an affirmative opinion or a review of an opposing report. Wisconsin-wide coverage and venue context appear on the [state page](/locations/wisconsin/). Once cleared, the engagement proceeds under a written scope that both sides of the caption could read without objection.
