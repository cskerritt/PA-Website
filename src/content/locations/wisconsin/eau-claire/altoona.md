Catastrophic injury litigation puts two forecasts in front of the same jury: what work remains within the injured person's capacity, and what care the injury will demand for the rest of their life. When those forecasts are built by strangers to each other, their assumptions drift apart, and the gap becomes a cross-examination exhibit. For Altoona matters, Purinton Analytics builds them together. The practice prepares [vocational expert witness](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) opinions on employability and earning capacity alongside [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) grounded in the same medical record, for counsel on either side of the caption, so the work half and the care half of the damages case can be examined as a coherent whole.

## Venue begins with Eau Claire County

Altoona lies within Eau Claire County, and a civil action rooted in the community will usually be commenced in the Circuit Court serving that county. If the claim carries a federal basis, Wisconsin offers the United States District Court for the Western District of Wisconsin and the United States District Court for the Eastern District of Wisconsin as its trial-level forums. Which court governs, and whether it imposes any qualification requirement on the experts appearing before it, is confirmed with counsel during the conflict check and intake; paired opinions are only useful if both are prepared to the standard of the bench that will receive them.

## Two analyses, two evidentiary anchors

The earning-capacity analysis for an Altoona evaluee is anchored in the regional labor market centered on Eau Claire, not in the town alone. Its published foundation is the Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates, taken for the Eau Claire metropolitan area and for Wisconsin statewide; state workforce agency labor market information adds occupational depth, and direct employer inquiry answers the questions no published source reaches. The life care plan draws its authority from a different record: the medical file, treating-provider input, and documented functional limitations, with each projected item of care traceable to a stated need. When the combined loss must be expressed in economic terms, the firm arranges [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under a single engagement structure. The counties and courts of the surrounding region are mapped on the [Eau Claire metro page](/locations/wisconsin/eau-claire/).

## Reaching an Altoona evaluee

Neither analysis obliges the evaluee to leave home. Document review and labor market research travel with the file, not with the person; the vocational interview is usually held by secure video, and when the referral questions justify an in-person assessment, the appointment takes place in the evaluee's own community rather than in an office rented for the occasion. Purinton Analytics maintains no Altoona office and prints no Altoona address, and it says so because a practice that overstated its footprint would invite doubt about everything else it states. Travel for assessments, depositions, and trial testimony originates at the Kansas City home office, and meetings with counsel are by appointment.
