## Measuring earning capacity beyond the town line

For an evaluee from a small community, the relevant labor market is regional, and the analysis treats it that way. Findings rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Racine-Mount Pleasant metropolitan area and for Wisconsin statewide, supplemented by state workforce agency labor market information and direct labor market research where the referral questions require it. The question is always concrete: which occupations, within a commute this evaluee can sustain, are realistically open at what earnings. When those findings must become a present-value damages figure, [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) place the economic analysis with independent economist partners, who perform and sign it while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement structure.
