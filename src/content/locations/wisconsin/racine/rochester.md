An opposing vocational report may treat a Rochester evaluee as interchangeable with a big-city worker, overstate the local availability of accommodating employers, or skip the commuting analysis a small community demands, and each of those choices is testable. ## Review that meets the same standard as authorship

A rebuttal assignment follows the identical discipline as an original opinion: documented conflict check, defined written scope, and access to the record the opposing expert relied on. The review asks whether the methodology was applied as described, whether the data sources support the conclusions drawn from them, and whether the opinion accounts for this evaluee rather than a generic one. Findings are stated so that either side of the caption could verify them, because a critique that cannot survive its own cross-examination helps no one.
