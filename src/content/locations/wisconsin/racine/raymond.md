Labor market data does not stop at a town line, and for a community the size of Raymond that is precisely the point. A vocational opinion about a Raymond evaluee stands or falls on how honestly it uses regional evidence: Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Racine-Mount Pleasant metropolitan area and for Wisconsin statewide describe the market a rural evaluee actually enters, and an expert who pretends the town itself is the market has already handed cross-examination material to the other side. Purinton Analytics builds its [vocational expert witness](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) and [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) work for Raymond matters on that regional foundation, serving plaintiff and defense counsel through its [Racine metro practice](/locations/wisconsin/racine/) and its [Wisconsin practice](/locations/wisconsin/).

## From published estimates to a defensible opinion

The methodology is layered and disclosed. Published Bureau of Labor Statistics estimates establish the occupational and wage landscape; state workforce agency labor market information refines it; and direct labor market research, where the referral questions warrant it, tests whether specific opportunities are real rather than statistical. Each layer is cited in the report, so the opinion's path from data to conclusion is visible to both sides of the caption. The same transparency governs the file itself: a documented conflict check, a written scope, and a records request matched to the claims precede any analysis.

## Evaluating a rural evaluee without shortcuts

Distance is not a reason to thin the record. Raymond evaluees are typically evaluated remotely, through structured interview and standardized instruments completed at home, with records exchanged over secure channels. When an in-person evaluation is justified, it is conducted in the evaluee's own community at an appropriate professional setting, with travel scheduled from the Kansas City home office. The firm maintains no office in Raymond and claims none; the same standard of accuracy the report applies to the evaluee applies to the firm's description of itself. Deposition and trial testimony are calendared against the scheduling order counsel provides.
