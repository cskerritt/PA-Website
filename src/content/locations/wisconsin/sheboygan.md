Retention in the Sheboygan, WI metropolitan area runs in both directions:
plaintiff firms retain Purinton Analytics to establish vocational loss and
future care needs, and defense counsel retain the same practice to test
those claims. That balance of retention shapes the work product, because an
expert who expects to be retained by either side cannot afford advocacy in
the report. Sheboygan engagements typically involve
[vocational expert witness](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) opinions,
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) of opposing
expert reports, with
[coordinated damages assessments](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
available when independent economist partners are needed to perform and sign
the economic analysis alongside the vocational and care-planning work under
a unified engagement structure.

## The venue footprint

The Circuit Court for Sheboygan County hears the state-court matters that
most often generate these referrals. Federal engagements are accepted in
the United States District Court for the Eastern District of Wisconsin and
in the United States District Court for the Western District of Wisconsin
alike; where a particular case is venued is a question for counsel, and the
answer does not alter the analysis. State and federal matters receive the
same methodology, the same
sourcing discipline, and the same disclosure-ready format. The
[Wisconsin locations page](/locations/wisconsin/) collects the firm's
statewide court coverage for counsel whose matters sit outside the county.

## Appointment logistics and travel

Sheboygan has no staffed Purinton Analytics office, and the firm publishes
no street address for this location; meetings occur by appointment. In
practice most engagements are conducted remotely from start to finish:
records review, structured video interviews, and standardized testing under
publisher protocols where remote administration is supported. When an
evaluation must happen in person, it can be conducted in the evaluee's own
community, and when depositions or trial testimony require presence, travel
is scheduled from the Kansas City home office to fit the court's calendar.
Counsel work with the same expert and the same report standard throughout.

## Labor market and cost research

Findings about employability and earning capacity for Sheboygan County
evaluees rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and
wage estimates for the metro area and for Wisconsin statewide, supported by
state workforce agency labor market information and direct labor market
research where the referral questions call for it. The analysis is written
so its sources can be pulled and checked. Life care plans and
[medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/) price future
care to the evaluee's own regional market using provider-specific inquiries
and published fee data, since local costs are not reliably captured by
national averages.

## Opening an engagement

Because the practice serves both sides of the caption, a conflict check
comes before any discussion of the merits. Counsel should submit the caption
or party names through the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/), noting any
disclosure deadlines, depositions, or trial settings already on the
calendar. Any venue-specific registration or qualification requirement is
identified and confirmed during the conflict check and intake process. For
matters venued beyond Wisconsin, the firm's
[nationwide coverage](/locations/nationwide/) describes how the identical
engagement model is delivered in other jurisdictions.
