A report prepared for an Eau Claire courtroom is held to the same standard
as a report prepared for any courtroom in the country: a documented conflict
check, a defined scope, methodology stated plainly, and conclusions built to
survive cross-examination from whichever side did not retain the firm.
Purinton Analytics brings that standard to western Wisconsin through
[vocational expert witness services](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Courts serving the Eau Claire metro

Engagements are accepted for matters venued in the Eau Claire County Circuit
Court and the Chippewa County Circuit Court. Federal matters from this part
of the state generally proceed in the United States District Court for the
Western District of Wisconsin, and the firm also appears in the United
States District Court for the Eastern District of Wisconsin when a case is
venued there. Any venue-specific registration or qualification requirement
is identified and confirmed during the conflict check and intake process.
For the full picture of the firm's work across the state, including the
other metros it serves, see the [Wisconsin page](/locations/wisconsin/).

## Distance practice, by appointment

Eau Claire meetings are by appointment, and the firm publishes no street
address for this location because it maintains no staffed office here.
Stating that outright is deliberate: an expert practice that shades the
truth about its own geography should expect its opinions to be read with
suspicion. The working model is remote-first. Records review, structured
interviews by video, and standardized testing under publisher protocols
where remote administration is supported all proceed without travel. When
the referral questions require an in-person evaluation, or when deposition
and trial dates require presence, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City
home office and planned against the deadlines counsel already carry.

## Labor market research for western Wisconsin

Employability and earning capacity opinions for Eau Claire area evaluees are
grounded in Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage
estimates for the Eau Claire metropolitan area and for Wisconsin statewide,
supplemented by state workforce agency labor market information and direct
labor market research where warranted. Life care plans and
[medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/) are priced to
the evaluee's own regional market using provider-specific inquiries and
published fee data. Because the report standard is uniform, a critique
assignment receives the same rigor as an affirmative one:
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) tests opposing
reports against exactly the sources the firm would use itself.

## Starting with the conflict check

Plaintiff and defense counsel open engagements the same way: submit the
caption or party names through the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and identify any imminent disclosure,
deposition, or trial dates. Scope and fee terms follow in writing once the
check clears, before substantive work begins. Counsel weighing a referral
outside Wisconsin can consult the firm's
[national coverage](/locations/nationwide/) to see the same standard applied
across the country.
