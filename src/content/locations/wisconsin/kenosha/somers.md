Venue shapes an expert engagement before any record is requested, so for Somers matters it is worth naming the forums at the outset. Somers is part of Kenosha County, and civil litigation touching the village is most often filed in the Circuit Court serving Kenosha County, with the United States District Court for the Eastern District of Wisconsin and the United States District Court for the Western District of Wisconsin available when a case belongs in federal court; the district that actually controls is pinned down during the conflict check and intake. Against that backdrop, Purinton Analytics offers [vocational expert witness](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) and [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) services to plaintiff and defense counsel handling Somers parties, within its [Kenosha metro practice](/locations/wisconsin/kenosha/).

## An opinion built to survive the courtroom

Whatever the forum, the working file is assembled the same way: a documented conflict check before any substantive discussion, a scope letter that states the referral questions in writing, and a records request tailored to the claims actually pleaded. The finished report cites its sources, explains its reasoning step by step, and is drafted on the assumption that the sharpest reader will be the lawyer on the other side. That assumption is not defensive posturing; it is what makes the opinion useful to whichever party retained it.

## Somers within the Kenosha labor market

A Somers evaluee's earning capacity is assessed against the labor market they can realistically reach, not against the village alone. The analysis rests on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Kenosha metropolitan area and for Wisconsin statewide, joined with state workforce agency labor market information and, where the questions warrant, direct contact with employers. Transferable skills, residual functional capacity, and the occupations hiring within a reasonable commute are weighed openly, with every source identified. When lost earnings need conversion to present value, counsel can commission a [coordinated damages assessment](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/), under which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis while vocational and care-planning work moves forward in the same engagement structure.

## No local office, and why that is stated plainly

Purinton Analytics does not keep an office in Somers and does not print a local address anywhere, because accuracy about presence belongs to the same habit of mind that governs the reports. Evaluations are conducted remotely wherever the referral questions permit, using secure video interviews, standardized testing, and encrypted records exchange. Where an in-person evaluation adds real value, it takes place in the evaluee's community, with travel scheduled from the Kansas City home office; deposition and trial appearances are calendared the same way, keyed to the dates the court has set.
