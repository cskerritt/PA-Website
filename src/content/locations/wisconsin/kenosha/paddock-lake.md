## A small village, a metro-sized labor market

Because Paddock Lake itself supports a limited employment base, the earning-capacity analysis necessarily looks outward to the metro. The question is always concrete: given this evaluee's residual capacity and transferable skills, which occupations within a realistic commute are actually attainable, and at what general wage level do they cluster, described qualitatively and sourced precisely.
