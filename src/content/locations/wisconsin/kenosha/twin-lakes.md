A scheduling order usually exists before anyone in a Twin Lakes case starts looking for a vocational expert or life care planner, and every step that follows, from records collection to report signature, has to fit inside it. Purinton Analytics collects disclosure deadlines, deposition windows, and trial settings at intake and lets those dates drive the work plan from the outset. Counsel on either side of a Twin Lakes matter can retain the firm for [vocational expert witness](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) or [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) work through its [Kenosha metro practice](/locations/wisconsin/kenosha/).

## The courts that set those calendars

Twin Lakes belongs to Kenosha County, and its civil disputes are typically heard in the Circuit Court serving Kenosha County. Cases with a federal basis may proceed in the United States District Court for the Eastern District of Wisconsin or the United States District Court for the Western District of Wisconsin; identifying the controlling forum is part of the conflict check and intake, never an assumption. The engagement itself follows the same sequence in any court: conflicts documented before substantive work, scope reduced to writing, and a records request drawn to match the claims, so the eventual opinion stands on a record both sides can inspect.

## Grounding the analysis in the regional market

For a Twin Lakes evaluee, the relevant labor market is regional. Vocational findings draw on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Kenosha metropolitan area and for Wisconsin statewide, on state workforce agency labor market information, and on direct labor market research where the referral questions justify it. The report ties residual capacity and transferable skills to occupations genuinely open within commuting distance of the village, and it shows its arithmetic of reasoning without showing off. Counsel needing present-value figures can add a [coordinated damages assessment](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/), in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis alongside the vocational and care-planning work, coordinated by the firm from intake through testimony.

## Meeting the evaluee where they are

Purinton Analytics has no Twin Lakes office and publishes no street address for the village, a fact stated here because candor costs nothing and vagueness costs credibility. Interviews and testing proceed remotely by default, over secure video with encrypted records handling. When the questions call for an in-person evaluation, it is conducted in the evaluee's own community; when depositions or trial testimony require the expert in the room, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office with the court's calendar, not the firm's convenience, controlling.
