A commuting map explains Bristol better than a village boundary does. Residents of this small Kenosha County community routinely earn their livings across the wider metro, which means the central question in a Bristol earning-capacity dispute is rarely what work exists in Bristol; it is what work the evaluee can still reach, perform, and hold across the region they actually travel for employment. Purinton Analytics frames its [vocational expert witness](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) and [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) opinions around that commuting reality, serving plaintiff and defense counsel through its [Kenosha metro practice](/locations/wisconsin/kenosha/).

## Measuring the market an evaluee can actually reach

The commuting-market question is answered with sources, not impressions. Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Kenosha metropolitan area and for Wisconsin statewide anchor the analysis, with state workforce agency labor market information layered on and direct employer research added when the referral questions require it. Transferable skills and residual functional capacity are then matched against occupations hiring within a defensible commuting radius of Bristol, and every step of that matching is documented so cross-examination finds method rather than guesswork. Where the case also needs damages reduced to present value, a [coordinated damages assessment](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) brings in independent economist partners who perform and sign the economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning components proceed together.

## Venue for Bristol disputes

Bristol sits in Kenosha County, so its civil matters are commonly filed in the Circuit Court serving Kenosha County. Federal questions and diversity cases may belong in the United States District Court for the Eastern District of Wisconsin or the United States District Court for the Western District of Wisconsin; the controlling district for a specific matter is settled during the conflict check and intake rather than declared from a map. The opinion is engineered to be forum-independent: documented conflicts, a written scope, and a claims-matched records request precede all analysis, and the conclusions follow the record wherever it leads.
