The measure of an expert report is how it reads under cross-examination, and
that standard governs every opinion Purinton Analytics prepares for the
Wausau, WI metropolitan area. Whether the assignment is an
[employability and earning capacity evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
a [life care plan](/services/life-care-planning/), or
[expert testimony and litigation consulting](/services/expert-testimony-litigation-consulting/),
the engagement starts with a documented conflict check and a defined scope,
so that the written opinion rests on a record both sides can examine. The
practice works for plaintiff and defense counsel in comparable measure, and
when economic damages are needed,
[coordinated damages assessments](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
bring in independent economist partners who perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under the same
engagement structure.

## Marathon County and the federal districts

Wausau engagements are accepted for matters in the Circuit Court for
Marathon County and in the federal courts of the state: the United States
District Court for the Western District of Wisconsin and the United States
District Court for the Eastern District of Wisconsin, depending on where the
case is venued. The methodology does not vary by forum. Reports are prepared
to the same disclosure standard in state and federal court, and counsel
handling matters elsewhere in the state will find the broader court coverage
described on the [Wisconsin locations page](/locations/wisconsin/).

## Presence by appointment, not by address

The firm keeps no staffed office in Wausau and publishes no street address
for this location; candor about presence is part of the same discipline the
reports are built on. Engagements ordinarily begin remotely, with records
review, structured interviews by video, and standardized testing under
publisher protocols where remote administration is supported. In-person
evaluation is available when the referral questions require it, and
deposition or trial attendance is scheduled from the Kansas City home
office around the dates counsel provides. The distance changes the travel
plan, not the report standard.

## Labor market evidence for Wausau evaluees

Vocational conclusions for evaluees in the Wausau area draw on Bureau of
Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the metro
area and for Wisconsin statewide, along with state workforce agency labor
market information and direct labor market research where warranted. Every
wage and employment source is cited so that opposing counsel can retrace the
analysis. Care-related costs in a
[medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/) or life care
plan are researched against the evaluee's own market through
provider-specific inquiries and published fee data, rather than assumed from
national averages that may not reflect local pricing.

## Beginning a Wausau engagement

Availability for a Marathon County matter is confirmed through a conflict
check, which is always the starting point. Submit the caption or party
names through the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and include any
imminent disclosure, deposition, or trial dates so the schedule can be
evaluated alongside the conflicts. Any venue-specific registration or
qualification requirement is identified and confirmed during the conflict
check and intake process. Counsel comparing venues or handling parallel
matters in other states can review the firm's
[national coverage](/locations/nationwide/) for how the same model operates
outside Wisconsin.
