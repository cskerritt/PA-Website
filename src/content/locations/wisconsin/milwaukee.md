Purinton Analytics serves plaintiff and defense counsel across the
Milwaukee-Waukesha metropolitan area with vocational expert and life care
planning services. Referrals from southeastern Wisconsin most often involve
[earning capacity and employability evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/)
in injury and employment litigation, [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/)
in catastrophic injury matters, [medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/)
where future care drives the dispute, and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. Every matter begins with a documented conflict check
and a defined scope, so the written opinion rests on a record either side
can examine.

## Venue footprint in southeastern Wisconsin

Engagements are accepted for matters venued in the Milwaukee County Circuit
Court and in the circuit courts of Waukesha County, Ozaukee County, and
Washington County. Federal matters from the metro proceed in the United
States District Court for the Eastern District of Wisconsin, and the firm
also appears in the United States District Court for the Western District of
Wisconsin when a case is venued there. The same intake process, methodology,
and report standard apply regardless of forum. Counsel with matters
elsewhere in the state will find the statewide practice described on the
[Wisconsin page](/locations/wisconsin/), which collects the metros the firm
serves across the state.

## Meetings by appointment, work at a distance

Milwaukee meetings are by appointment. The firm keeps no staffed office in
the metro and publishes no street address for this location, because
accuracy about presence is part of the same discipline that governs its
reports. Most engagements begin remotely: records transfer electronically,
structured interviews are conducted by video, and standardized testing is
administered under publisher protocols where remote administration is
supported. When the referral questions call for in-person evaluation, or
when deposition and trial dates require presence, travel is scheduled from
the Kansas City home office. Counsel receive the same report structure and
the same responsiveness whether the work is performed on site or at a
distance.

## Milwaukee labor market research

Vocational findings for evaluees in the metro rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the
Milwaukee-Waukesha metropolitan area and for Wisconsin statewide,
supplemented by state workforce agency labor market information and direct
labor market research where the referral questions warrant it. Life care
plans and medical cost projections for Wisconsin residents are priced to the
evaluee's own market rather than to national averages, using
provider-specific inquiries and published fee data for the region. Where an
opposing expert has already produced a report,
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) applies the same
sources and the same method to test the opinions already on the record.

## Starting a Milwaukee engagement

The practice accepts work from either side of the caption, and the initial
step is identical for both: submit the caption or party names through the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and note any imminent disclosure,
deposition, or trial dates. Any venue-specific registration or qualification
requirement is identified and confirmed during the conflict check and intake
process. For counsel weighing whether a matter outside Wisconsin fits the
practice, the [national coverage page](/locations/nationwide/) sets out how
this structure supports engagements in courts throughout the country.
