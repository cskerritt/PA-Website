Expert deadlines decide more Milton cases than expert opinions do. A scheduling order sets the disclosure date, the disclosure date sets the report date, and the report date sets how much record development is still possible; counsel who retain late inherit whatever the calendar left behind. Purinton Analytics builds its [vocational expert](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) and [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) engagements for Milton matters backward from those dates, for plaintiff and defense referrals alike, so the written opinion is complete before the deadline pressure starts making decisions for anyone.

## Working backward from the scheduling order

Intake begins with the dates. During the conflict check, counsel identifies the disclosure, deposition, and trial settings already fixed, and the firm maps the work plan against them: records requests first, the evaluee interview and any testing next, then drafting with enough margin for counsel's review of the factual record before the opinion is served. Supplementation and [rebuttal or peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) of an opposing expert's report are scheduled the same way, against the deadline that governs them rather than against convenience.

## The courts that set those deadlines

Milton is a Rock County community, and its civil actions ordinarily proceed in the Circuit Court, which controls the state-court calendar an expert must honor. Federal filings belong in one of Wisconsin's two districts, the United States District Court for the Eastern District of Wisconsin or the United States District Court for the Western District of Wisconsin; the firm does not guess at which one governs a given Milton claim, and the controlling venue, together with any qualification requirement particular to it, is confirmed with counsel during the conflict check and intake. Broader venue context for the state appears on the [Wisconsin page](/locations/wisconsin/).

## Milton wage evidence on a defensible footing

A Milton evaluee's earning capacity is measured against the regional market, not the municipal boundary, because that is where the jobs are and where a jury will expect the analysis to look. The firm relies on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Janesville-Beloit metropolitan area and for Wisconsin statewide, corroborated by state workforce agency labor market information and by direct labor market research when a disputed occupation calls for it. The [Janesville-Beloit metro page](/locations/wisconsin/janesville/) sets out the regional market that frames this analysis.

## Appointments, not an address

The firm holds no office in Milton and does not manufacture the appearance of one. Engagements run remotely by default, with secure video interviews and document exchange through counsel; when an in-person evaluation better serves the referral questions, it is held in the evaluee's community, and when testimony requires presence, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office with the court's calendar as the fixed point. Files that need an economic damages figure alongside the vocational and care-planning opinions proceed as [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/), with independent economist partners performing and signing the economic analysis inside a single engagement structure.
