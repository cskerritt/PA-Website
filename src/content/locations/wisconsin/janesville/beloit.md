## Rock County venue and the courts serving Beloit

Beloit sits in Rock County, and civil actions arising there are ordinarily filed in the Circuit Court, Wisconsin's trial court of general jurisdiction. A claim that proceeds in federal court will land in the United States District Court for the Eastern District of Wisconsin or the United States District Court for the Western District of Wisconsin, and the firm does not presume to say which; the controlling district for a particular Beloit dispute is a question for counsel, confirmed during the conflict check and intake along with any registration or qualification requirement particular to the forum. The report itself does not vary by courthouse. Every opinion is drafted on the assumption that opposing counsel and the bench will read it closely, so methodology, sourcing, and limitations are stated the same way in every venue.

## Reading the Beloit labor market honestly

Vocational findings in Beloit cases rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Janesville-Beloit metropolitan area and for Wisconsin statewide, supplemented by state workforce agency labor market information and by direct labor market research where the referral questions warrant it. The published estimates, not impressions of the town, carry the analysis: which occupations exist within a reasonable commute of a Beloit resident, which remain available given documented restrictions, and what the wage evidence shows for those that do. Where an opinion cannot be supported by that record, it is not offered.
