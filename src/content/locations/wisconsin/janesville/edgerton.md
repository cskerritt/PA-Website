## How an Edgerton evaluation actually proceeds

Most of the work happens remotely. Records arrive through counsel, the clinical interview and vocational testing are conducted by video where appropriate, and the draft opinion is built on documents both sides can examine. When the referral questions require observation that video cannot support, an in-person session is scheduled in Edgerton or wherever the evaluee can reasonably travel, with the firm's travel arranged from its Kansas City home office. Purinton Analytics keeps no office in Edgerton and publishes no local address, because accuracy about presence belongs in the same category as accuracy about earnings: it is not negotiable. For files that also need economic modeling, [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) bring in independent economist partners who perform and sign the economic analysis under a single engagement structure.

## Where Edgerton cases are heard

Edgerton lies in Rock County, and its civil disputes are ordinarily filed in the Circuit Court there. Federal matters proceed in one of Wisconsin's two districts, the United States District Court for the Eastern District of Wisconsin or the United States District Court for the Western District of Wisconsin, with the controlling venue confirmed during the conflict check and intake rather than assumed. Any venue-specific qualification requirement is identified at the same stage. The firm's reports are written for cross-examination from the first draft: methodology stated, sources cited, limitations acknowledged.
