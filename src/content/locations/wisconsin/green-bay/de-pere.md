## Brown County venue for De Pere matters

De Pere lies in Brown County, and civil claims arising there ordinarily proceed in the Circuit Court, Wisconsin's trial court of general jurisdiction. Federal filings present a venue question of their own: the United States District Court for the Eastern District of Wisconsin and the United States District Court for the Western District of Wisconsin both sit as Wisconsin's federal trial courts, and the firm does not presume which of them governs a given De Pere dispute. The controlling venue, together with any registration or qualification requirement particular to it, is identified with counsel during the conflict check and intake. The report itself is drafted to the same standard regardless of forum, because its assumed readers always include opposing counsel and the court.

## The labor market a De Pere evaluee actually faces

Earning capacity for a De Pere resident is not measured against the town line. Vocational findings rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Green Bay metropolitan area and for Wisconsin statewide, supplemented by state workforce agency labor market information and by direct employer contact when the referral questions call for it. The analysis asks what occupations exist within a reasonable commute, which of them remain open under the documented restrictions, and what the published wage evidence shows for those that do; it does not decorate the answer with local color the data cannot support. Matters filed elsewhere in the state draw on the same sources, as the [Wisconsin state page](/locations/wisconsin/) explains.

## Evaluations and appearances by appointment

The firm keeps no office in De Pere and implies none; candor about presence is part of the discipline that governs the written work. Most engagements proceed remotely, with records exchanged through counsel and interviews conducted over secure video. Where an in-person evaluation is warranted, it is scheduled in the evaluee's own community, and travel for evaluations, depositions, and trial testimony is arranged from the Kansas City home office around counsel's calendar. When damages require an economic model as well as a vocational and care-planning foundation, [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) are available, in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement structure.
