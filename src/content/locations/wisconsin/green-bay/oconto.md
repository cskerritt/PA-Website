## Oconto County venue

Civil claims arising in Oconto are ordinarily brought in the Circuit Court of Oconto County, Wisconsin's trial court of general jurisdiction for that county. Federal-court matters look to Wisconsin's district courts, the United States District Court for the Eastern District of Wisconsin and the United States District Court for the Western District of Wisconsin; the firm takes no position on which district governs an Oconto case until the conflict check and intake, where counsel and the firm confirm the controlling venue together and identify any registration or qualification requirement that attaches to it. The report reads identically well in a county courtroom and in a federal courtroom, because it is written for skeptical readers in both.

## A small city inside a metro orbit

Oconto is a small city, and its residents' employment prospects reach beyond it toward the larger Green Bay labor market. Vocational conclusions therefore draw on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Green Bay metropolitan area and for Wisconsin statewide, alongside state workforce agency labor market information and direct labor market research when the referral questions call for that added depth. For an Oconto evaluee, the honest questions concern commuting tolerance as much as occupational fit: which occupations exist within realistic reach, which the documented restrictions still permit, and what the published wage evidence shows.
