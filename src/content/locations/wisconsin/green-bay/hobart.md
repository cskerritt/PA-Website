An expert opinion earns its keep at deposition, and that is the standard Purinton Analytics applies to every Hobart engagement from the day the file opens. Each conclusion in a [vocational expert witness](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) report or a [life care plan](/services/life-care-planning/) is written so that its source, its method, and its limits can be stated under oath without retreat. Hobart counsel, plaintiff or defense, retain that discipline through the regional practice described on the [Green Bay metro page](/locations/wisconsin/green-bay/); the same discipline supports [rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) when the assignment is to test another expert's work rather than to build an opinion from scratch.

## Hobart in Brown County's court system

Hobart's civil disputes ordinarily belong to the Circuit Court of Brown County, the general-jurisdiction trial court in Wisconsin's system. When federal jurisdiction attaches, the available district courts are the United States District Court for the Eastern District of Wisconsin and the United States District Court for the Western District of Wisconsin. Which of them controls a specific Hobart case is not something the firm assumes; the answer is settled with counsel at the conflict check and intake stage, where any registration or qualification requirement of the chosen forum is flagged as well. Cross-examination looks the same in every courtroom, so the preparation does too.

## Labor market foundations that survive scrutiny

Opinions that hold up share a trait: their data can be checked. Vocational findings for Hobart evaluees are built on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Green Bay metropolitan area and for Wisconsin statewide, on state workforce agency labor market information, and, when the referral justifies it, on direct labor market research documented well enough for the other side to retrace. The analysis stays within commuting-distance realities and documented restrictions, and it refuses conclusions the published estimates cannot carry, because an overreaching sentence is the tool opposing counsel is looking for. Statewide application of these sources is addressed on the [Wisconsin state page](/locations/wisconsin/).

## Logistics without a local office

Candor extends to geography: there is no Hobart office, no local address, and no pretense of either. Engagements run remotely wherever the work allows, with records moving through counsel and interviews conducted by secure video, and they move in person when the questions require it, through evaluations scheduled in the evaluee's own community. Travel for those appointments, and for deposition or trial testimony, originates at the Kansas City home office and is planned around the litigation calendar. Where the damages model calls for economics, [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) place that analysis with independent economist partners, who perform and sign it while the vocational and care-planning work continues under one engagement structure.

## Requesting a conflict check

Testing this practice costs counsel little. Route the caption or the party names through the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/), state the disclosure, deposition, or trial dates already in play, and expect a direct answer on conflicts, fit, and delivery. If a Hobart matter later crosses county or state lines, the engagement follows the national intake explained on the [nationwide practice page](/locations/nationwide/) without a change of expert.
