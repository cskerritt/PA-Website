## Ashwaubenon's place in the Brown County courts

Ashwaubenon sits in Brown County, so state-court claims arising there are ordinarily heard in the Circuit Court, Wisconsin's trial court of general jurisdiction. Wisconsin's federal bench is organized into the United States District Court for the Eastern District of Wisconsin and the United States District Court for the Western District of Wisconsin, and the firm treats the choice between them as a question for counsel, settled during the conflict check and intake along with any registration or qualification requirement the chosen forum imposes. Whichever court ultimately controls, the analysis and the report are built to the same evidentiary discipline.

## What the wage data can and cannot say

Opinions about an Ashwaubenon resident's earning capacity are anchored in Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Green Bay metropolitan area and for Wisconsin statewide, with state workforce agency labor market information and direct labor market research layered in when the referral questions justify it. Those sources support conclusions about which occupations exist within commuting reach, which remain accessible given the documented restrictions, and what the published estimates show such work pays. They do not support guesses about individual employers or neighborhood conditions, and the reports decline to make them. ## Evaluation logistics, stated plainly

The firm maintains no Ashwaubenon office and publishes no local address, because accuracy about presence belongs to the same discipline as accuracy in the opinions. In practice, most of an engagement is location-independent: records travel through counsel, and interviews proceed by secure video. When an in-person evaluation is indicated, it is arranged in the evaluee's own community at a scheduled appointment, with travel planned from the Kansas City home office; the same planning covers depositions and trial testimony so that appearances land on counsel's calendar without drama. Where the damages picture requires economics, [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) bring in independent economist partners who perform and sign the economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement structure.
