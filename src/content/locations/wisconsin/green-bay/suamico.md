## Suamico venue and the Brown County courts

Suamico lies within Brown County, where civil actions ordinarily proceed in the Circuit Court, Wisconsin's court of general trial jurisdiction. For federal claims, Wisconsin offers the United States District Court for the Eastern District of Wisconsin and the United States District Court for the Western District of Wisconsin as its district-level forums; the firm leaves the venue determination to counsel and confirms the controlling court during the conflict check and intake, where any forum-specific registration or qualification requirement is also identified.
