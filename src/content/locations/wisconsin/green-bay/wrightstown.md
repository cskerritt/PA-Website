A village the size of Wrightstown is a fair test of whether an expert practice is honest about geography. No national firm keeps an office in a small Wisconsin village, and Purinton Analytics does not pretend to; what it offers Wrightstown counsel instead is a practice engineered so that office location stops mattering. Records, interviews, and analysis move through secure remote channels, presence is supplied by appointment when the case genuinely needs it, and the substantive work, [vocational expert witness](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) analysis and [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) for plaintiff and defense engagements, meets the standard set across the practice described on the [Green Bay metro page](/locations/wisconsin/green-bay/).

## Where Wrightstown claims are litigated

Wrightstown is part of Brown County, so its civil disputes ordinarily go to the Circuit Court, the trial court of general jurisdiction in Wisconsin. A matter proceeding federally will land in the United States District Court for the Eastern District of Wisconsin or the United States District Court for the Western District of Wisconsin, and the firm declines to predict which; counsel and the firm establish the controlling venue during the conflict check and intake, along with any qualification or registration requirement the forum imposes. A village address on the complaint changes none of the writing standards: the opinion is composed for the court and for opposing counsel in the same breath.

## Small village, metro labor market

Nothing about a small community shrinks the labor market analysis. A Wrightstown evaluee's realistic employment options are evaluated across the Green Bay metropolitan area, using Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for that metropolitan area and for Wisconsin statewide, state workforce agency labor market information, and direct labor market research where a referral question warrants it. The inquiry weighs what work exists within a reasonable commute, what the documented restrictions leave open, and what the published data show it pays, and it stops where the data stop. ## The remote-first model in practice

Remote-first does not mean remote-only. The default posture keeps costs proportionate to a village-sized case: records arrive through counsel, interviews run on secure video, and drafts move electronically. When the referral requires an in-person evaluation, the appointment is held in the evaluee's own community rather than forcing travel on an injured person, and the firm's own travel, for evaluations, depositions, or trial, is scheduled from the Kansas City home office. Cases that need economics alongside the vocational and care-planning work use [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/), in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis under one engagement structure with the rest of the work.
