When a catastrophic injury reaches a Pulaski household, the litigation questions become lifelong ones: what care will be needed, what it will cost to obtain, and what earning capacity remains. Those are planning questions before they are courtroom questions, and Purinton Analytics approaches Pulaski engagements in that order, building [life care plans](/services/life-care-planning/) and [vocational expert](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) opinions that counsel on either side can put in front of a jury. Where future medical costs need pricing without a full plan, a [medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/) covers the narrower question. The surrounding regional practice is described on the [Green Bay metro page](/locations/wisconsin/green-bay/).

## Pulaski litigation and the Brown County courts

Pulaski's civil matters arise in Brown County, where the Circuit Court serves as Wisconsin's trial court of general jurisdiction. Federal jurisdiction brings Wisconsin's district courts into view, the United States District Court for the Eastern District of Wisconsin and the United States District Court for the Western District of Wisconsin, and the firm makes no assumption about which applies to a given Pulaski case. Venue is confirmed with counsel as part of the conflict check and intake, which also surfaces any registration or qualification requirement particular to the forum, so that nothing procedural surprises either side after retention.

## Work and wages beyond the village line

A care plan addresses cost; the vocational analysis addresses capacity, and for a Pulaski evaluee capacity is measured against the Green Bay metropolitan area rather than the village itself. The evidentiary base is Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Green Bay metropolitan area and for Wisconsin statewide, joined by state workforce agency labor market information and by direct labor market research when the referral calls for it. Conclusions stay inside what the published estimates and the documented restrictions will support, which is what lets them survive the scrutiny both sides are entitled to apply. The [Wisconsin state page](/locations/wisconsin/) treats the statewide picture.

## Care planning and evaluations close to home

Purinton Analytics maintains no Pulaski office and states as much without hedging; the by-appointment model is the honest description of how a national practice serves a small village. For an injured evaluee, that model is also the humane choice: records and interviews are handled remotely through counsel and secure video wherever possible, and in-person evaluation, when the plan requires it, comes to the evaluee's own community instead of demanding travel from a person with significant care needs. The firm's travel, for evaluations and for deposition or trial testimony, is arranged from the Kansas City home office. Where lifetime losses need an economic model, [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) add independent economist partners who perform and sign the economic analysis while the care-planning and vocational work proceeds under one engagement structure.

## Submitting a conflict check

The gravity of a catastrophic case does not change the way in; it makes the discipline more important. Counsel submit the caption or party names through the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/), flag disclosure, deposition, or trial dates, and receive a straightforward answer on conflicts, scope, and timeline.
