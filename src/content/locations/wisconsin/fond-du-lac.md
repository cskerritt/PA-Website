A Fond du Lac, WI engagement usually starts at a desk, not in a conference
room: records arrive, a conflict check clears, a scope is defined in
writing, and the analysis is underway before anyone books travel. That
remote-first sequence is how Purinton Analytics delivers
[vocational expert](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) opinions,
[life care plans](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[expert testimony support](/services/expert-testimony-litigation-consulting/)
to counsel in the Fond du Lac area, on either side of the caption. When a
matter also needs economic damages,
[coordinated damages assessments](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
add independent economist partners who perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work continues under the
same engagement structure.

## Venues in Fond du Lac County

The firm accepts matters venued in the Circuit Court for Fond du Lac County
and in Wisconsin's federal courts: the United States District Court for the
Eastern District of Wisconsin and the United States District Court for the
Western District of Wisconsin, whichever the case calls for. Injury,
employment, and disability matters in state and federal court receive the
same documented methodology and the same disclosure-ready reporting. The
[Wisconsin locations page](/locations/wisconsin/) sets out the rest of the
firm's statewide court coverage for counsel with matters beyond the county.

## Remote-first, by appointment

There is no staffed Fond du Lac office and no published street address for
this location, and the firm says so plainly because honesty about presence
belongs in the same category as honesty in a report. Meetings are held by
appointment. Interviews are conducted by video under a structured protocol,
standardized instruments are administered remotely where the publisher
supports it, and records are reviewed as counsel produces them. When the
referral questions require an in-person evaluation, it can take place in the
evaluee's own community; when a deposition or trial requires attendance,
travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office around the dates the
court has set.

## Wage and employment research for Fond du Lac evaluees

Employability and earning capacity conclusions rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Fond du Lac
area and for Wisconsin statewide, alongside state workforce agency labor
market information and direct labor market research where warranted. Sources
are cited so that opposing counsel can verify each figure's provenance in
the underlying data. For future care needs, life care plans and
[medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/) are priced to
the evaluee's regional market through provider-specific inquiries and
published fee data, not borrowed from national averages that may misstate
local costs in either direction.

## How to begin

Substantive discussion waits until conflicts are cleared. Counsel should
[request a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) with the case caption and
parties, flagging any disclosure deadline, deposition, or trial setting that
is already on the calendar so availability and schedule can be confirmed
together. Any venue-specific registration or qualification requirement is
identified and confirmed during the conflict check and intake process. For
a view of how the same engagement model operates outside Wisconsin, see the
firm's [nationwide coverage](/locations/nationwide/).
