Expert opinions earn their keep in deposition rooms and jury boxes, and
counsel retaining a national practice for a Green Bay case want certainty
that the expert will be there when the calendar demands it. Purinton
Analytics builds that certainty into its northeastern Wisconsin work: the
analysis proceeds remotely, appearances happen by appointment, and testimony
dates are treated as fixed points the rest of the engagement is planned
around. Plaintiff and defense counsel alike use the practice for
[vocational expert](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) opinions on
earning capacity and employability, for
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) in catastrophic injury
matters, and for
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Where the firm appears

Engagements are accepted for matters venued in the Brown County Circuit
Court and in the circuit courts of Kewaunee County and Oconto County.
Federal matters from the Green Bay area proceed in the United States
District Court for the Eastern District of Wisconsin, and the firm appears
in the United States District Court for the Western District of Wisconsin
when a case is venued on that side of the state. The forum changes nothing
about the work: the same intake, the same methodology, and the same report
discipline apply in every court. The [Wisconsin page](/locations/wisconsin/)
describes how the practice covers the rest of the state.

## In person when it matters

The firm maintains no staffed Green Bay office and publishes no street
address here; candor about geography is part of the credibility the reports
depend on. What counsel gets instead is a by-appointment practice that
travels when travel earns its place. In-person vocational evaluations,
on-site life care planning assessments, and appearances for deposition or
trial are scheduled from the Kansas City home office and coordinated with
the litigation calendar. Between appearances, the work runs at a distance:
records arrive electronically, structured interviews are conducted by
video, and standardized testing is administered under publisher protocols
where remote administration is supported, so distance never becomes an
excuse for delay or silence.

## Labor market evidence for northeastern Wisconsin

Vocational conclusions for Green Bay area evaluees rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Green Bay
metropolitan area and for Wisconsin statewide, together with state workforce
agency labor market information and direct labor market research where the
referral questions warrant it. [Medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/)
and life care plans are priced to the evaluee's own regional market through
provider-specific inquiries and published fee data rather than borrowed
national averages, because a plan that ignores local pricing invites
cross-examination it cannot survive.

## Referral and conflict check

Whether the referral comes from the plaintiff side or the defense side, the
engagement opens with a
[conflict check](/refer-a-case/): submit the caption or party names and note
any imminent disclosure, deposition, or trial dates. Any venue-specific
registration or qualification requirement is identified and confirmed
during the conflict check and intake process, before substantive work
begins. Counsel handling matters beyond Wisconsin can see how the same
model operates elsewhere on the
[national coverage page](/locations/nationwide/).
