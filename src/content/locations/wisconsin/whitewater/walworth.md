The village of Walworth shares its name with the county that surrounds it, and a hurried reading of a caption can conflate village and county. Purinton Analytics treats that distinction the way it treats every fact in a damages file: worth verifying before it is written down. For counsel litigating matters that involve village of Walworth parties, the firm provides [vocational expert](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) analysis and [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) directly, along with [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement structure. A substantial share of the work arrives as review: an opposing expert's report is already on file, and counsel needs to know whether it holds.

## Venue: the village, the county, the districts

State-court cases involving village of Walworth parties are heard in the Circuit Court serving Walworth County. Federal matters proceed in the United States District Court for the Eastern District of Wisconsin or the United States District Court for the Western District of Wisconsin, and the firm confirms the controlling district during the conflict check and intake instead of asserting it from a map. Each engagement is documented from the start, with the conflict check, scope definition, and records requests preserved so the opinion's foundation is itself discoverable and defensible.

## Testing the labor market claims in a damages file

Whether the firm is writing the vocational opinion or reviewing someone else's, the standard is identical. Earning capacity conclusions for a Walworth evaluee rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Whitewater-Elkhorn area and for Wisconsin statewide, together with state workforce agency labor market information and direct labor market research where warranted. In [rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) engagements, the firm traces the opposing report's sources, checks its transferable skills logic against the record, and states plainly where the analysis holds as well as where it fails; a review that only criticizes is not credible, and a credible review is the useful kind.

## Reaching a Walworth evaluee

The firm keeps no office in the village and does not imply otherwise. Evaluations proceed remotely in most engagements, combining structured interview, standardized testing, and record review; in-person evaluation, when the referral questions require it, is conducted in a professional setting in the evaluee's own community, with travel arranged from the Kansas City home office. Testimony for deposition or trial is scheduled against the court's calendar through the [Whitewater-Elkhorn metro practice](/locations/wisconsin/whitewater/), which serves the county's villages by appointment and says so without embellishment.
