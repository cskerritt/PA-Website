A Genoa City expert file is written to hold up no matter which side retains it and no matter which forum receives it. The firm accepts plaintiff engagements and defense engagements on identical terms, and it makes no assumption about venue either: Wisconsin's federal trial forums are the United States District Court for the Eastern District of Wisconsin and the United States District Court for the Western District of Wisconsin, the state-court path runs through the Circuit Court, and the venue that actually governs a Genoa City case is identified during the conflict check and intake. Purinton Analytics prepares [vocational expert](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) opinions and [life care plans](/services/life-care-planning/) to a uniform disclosure standard and structures [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement structure.

## Genoa City in Walworth County's courts

Genoa City is a Walworth County village, and its state-court civil matters are heard in the Circuit Court serving the county. Removal, diversity, and federal-question cases move the same disputes into the federal districts named above, each with its own scheduling practices and disclosure expectations, which is why the firm treats venue confirmation as an intake task rather than an afterthought. From the outset the file carries a documented conflict check, a defined scope, and records requests tailored to the claims, so the opinion stands wherever the case ultimately lands and reads the same whether it is offered by the plaintiff or tested by the defense.

## The employment market beyond the village

A Genoa City evaluee's earning capacity is analyzed against the regional market rather than the village itself. Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Whitewater-Elkhorn area and for Wisconsin statewide form the foundation, supplemented by state workforce agency labor market information and direct labor market research when the referral questions call for it. The transferable skills analysis connects the evaluee's documented history and residual capacity to occupations that exist within a sustainable commute, and the resulting market picture is expressed qualitatively, in language a jury can weigh and opposing counsel can test against the cited sources.

## Evaluations by appointment, not storefront

There is no Genoa City office, and the firm's honesty on that point is deliberate: accuracy about presence is the same habit that keeps the reports accurate. Evaluations generally proceed remotely, through structured interview, standardized testing, and full record review; when in-person assessment serves the referral questions better, it is conducted in a professional setting in the evaluee's community, with travel scheduled from the Kansas City home office. Testimony follows the court's calendar, and engagements across the area are managed through the [Whitewater-Elkhorn metro practice](/locations/wisconsin/whitewater/), which serves the surrounding communities on the same by-appointment basis.
