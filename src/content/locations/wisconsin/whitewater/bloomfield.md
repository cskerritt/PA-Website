Bloomfield is a small Walworth County community, and nothing in Purinton Analytics' method shrinks to match it. A catastrophic injury or a contested termination raises the same damages questions in a village that it raises in a major city: what work the injured person can still perform, what care the medical record supports, and what those answers are worth over a working life. The firm answers them with the same architecture everywhere it practices: [vocational expert](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) analysis and [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) performed directly, and [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement structure.

## Bloomfield's courts

State-court matters involving Bloomfield parties proceed in the Circuit Court serving Walworth County. Cases that belong in federal court are filed in the United States District Court for the Eastern District of Wisconsin or the United States District Court for the Western District of Wisconsin, and the firm does not guess at which: the controlling venue, with its disclosure rules and deadlines, is confirmed during the conflict check and intake. Whatever the forum, the file is built the same way, beginning with a documented conflict check, a defined scope, and a records request tailored to the pleaded claims, so the finished opinion can be examined by either side without surprise.

## A village evaluee, a regional market

Bloomfield's size means its residents' employment prospects are defined regionally. Vocational conclusions rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Whitewater-Elkhorn area and for Wisconsin statewide, with state workforce agency labor market information and targeted labor market research layered in as the referral questions require. The transferable skills analysis identifies work the evaluee's history and restrictions support and then verifies that such work exists within a realistic commuting range of Bloomfield, so the opinion describes an actual market rather than an abstraction. Scale is conveyed in words, never in recited figures, and each characterization traces to a cited source.

## Serving Bloomfield by appointment

The firm has no Bloomfield office and states as much; candor about presence is part of the product. Evaluations ordinarily proceed remotely through structured interview, standardized testing, and record review. When in-person evaluation is warranted, it takes place in a professional setting within the evaluee's own community, with travel arranged from the Kansas City home office, and testimony for deposition or trial is scheduled the same way.
