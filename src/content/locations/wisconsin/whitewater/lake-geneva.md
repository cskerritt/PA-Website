## The labor market behind a Lake Geneva opinion

An earning capacity conclusion for a Lake Geneva evaluee is grounded in Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates covering the Whitewater-Elkhorn area and Wisconsin statewide, along with state workforce agency labor market information and, where the questions demand it, direct labor market research. The analysis reads the local market qualitatively: what kinds of work the region supports, how the evaluee's transferable skills map onto it, and whether the identified occupations lie within a commute the evaluee can sustain given the medical restrictions actually documented in the record. Nothing is asserted about the market that a cited source cannot support.

## How the evaluation is scheduled

Most Lake Geneva evaluations proceed remotely, combining structured interview, standardized testing, and complete record review on a schedule built around the evaluee's treatment calendar. When an in-person evaluation better serves the referral questions, the firm conducts it in a professional setting in the evaluee's community; there is no Lake Geneva office and none is claimed, and travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office. The same logistics govern testimony: deposition and trial appearances are calendared against the court's schedule, with the coordinated experts sequenced so their opinions arrive as a consistent whole rather than a set of freestanding documents.
