## Where East Troy cases are heard

East Troy lies in Walworth County, and its state-court litigation proceeds in the Circuit Court serving the county. When a case belongs in federal court, Wisconsin's trial forums are the United States District Court for the Eastern District of Wisconsin and the United States District Court for the Western District of Wisconsin; the district that actually controls, and the expert disclosure requirements that attach to it, are identified during the conflict check and intake. Deadlines differ between forums, and plaintiff and defense disclosures often fall on different dates in the same case, which is why the firm asks for the scheduling order at the outset of every engagement and confirms the report dates in writing before analysis begins.

## Labor market evidence on a deadline

Speed never substitutes for method, because a report delivered on time still has to survive examination by the other side. Vocational findings for an East Troy evaluee rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Whitewater-Elkhorn area and for Wisconsin statewide, joined by state workforce agency labor market information and direct labor market research where the referral questions require it. East Troy is a small community whose workers routinely look beyond it for employment, so the transferable skills analysis tests each identified occupation against the commuting range the evaluee can sustain. The market is characterized qualitatively, and every source is cited so the opinion can be verified line by line under cross-examination.
