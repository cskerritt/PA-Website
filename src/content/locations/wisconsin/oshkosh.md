Damages litigation often needs more than a single discipline: a vocational
opinion on what work remains open to the evaluee, a life care plan for
future needs, and an economic analysis that reduces both to present value.
For Oshkosh and Neenah area counsel, Purinton Analytics structures that work
as [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/),
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the [vocational](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) and
[care-planning](/services/life-care-planning/) work proceeds under one
engagement structure. Each discipline remains independently defensible; the
coordination lives in scheduling, records handling, and scope, never in
borrowed or shared conclusions. Plaintiff and defense referrals are accepted on the
same terms.

## Winnebago County and its courts

State court engagements are accepted for matters venued in the Winnebago
County Circuit Court. Federal matters from the Oshkosh-Neenah area proceed
in the United States District Court for the Eastern District of Wisconsin,
and the firm also appears in the United States District Court for the
Western District of Wisconsin when a case is venued there. Any
venue-specific registration or qualification requirement is identified and
confirmed during the conflict check and intake process. The
[Wisconsin page](/locations/wisconsin/) collects the rest of the firm's
statewide coverage for counsel whose dockets reach beyond this metro.

## Appointments, not an office

There is no staffed Purinton Analytics office in Oshkosh, and no street
address is published for this location; the firm treats accuracy about its
own footprint as part of the discipline it applies to every opinion.
Meetings are by appointment. The bulk of an engagement proceeds remotely,
with electronic records exchange, structured interviews by video, and
standardized testing administered under publisher protocols where remote
administration is supported. When an evaluation is better conducted in
person, or when deposition and trial dates require attendance, travel is
scheduled from the Kansas City home office and set against the case
calendar. Coordination among the disciplines happens behind the scenes, so
counsel deal with a unified schedule rather than juggling separate expert
calendars.

## Oshkosh and Neenah labor market data

Vocational findings for Winnebago County evaluees rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Oshkosh-Neenah
metropolitan area and for Wisconsin statewide, together with state workforce
agency labor market information and direct labor market research where the
referral questions warrant it. Life care plans and
[medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/) are priced to
the evaluee's own market through provider-specific inquiries and published
regional fee data, so the care side of a coordinated engagement stands on
evidence as local as the vocational side.

## Requesting a conflict check

A coordinated engagement, like a single-discipline one, opens with a
conflict check: submit the caption or party names through the
[referral form](/refer-a-case/) and note any disclosure, deposition, or
trial dates already set. Once the check clears, the engagement structure,
scope, and fee terms are confirmed in writing before substantive work
begins. Counsel with matters beyond Wisconsin can see how the same
coordinated model operates nationally on the
[national coverage page](/locations/nationwide/).
