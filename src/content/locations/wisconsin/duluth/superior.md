Superior is the Wisconsin side of a metropolitan area whose principal city, Duluth, sits across the state line in Minnesota, and that geography shapes everything about how a Superior case should be handled. The evaluee may work in either state, the labor market data spans both, and the lawsuit itself proceeds under Wisconsin venue rules even while the economic life of the community is tied to a Minnesota city. Purinton Analytics accepts [vocational expert](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) and [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) referrals for Superior matters from plaintiff and defense counsel alike, and treats the state line as a fact to be analyzed rather than a complication to be ignored.

## Douglas County venue for a cross-border community

Civil actions arising in Superior are ordinarily filed in the Circuit Court, Wisconsin's trial court of general jurisdiction, sitting for Douglas County. When a claim belongs in federal court, the Wisconsin forums are the United States District Court for the Eastern District of Wisconsin and the United States District Court for the Western District of Wisconsin; the firm does not presume which district controls a particular Superior dispute, and the governing venue, together with any registration or qualification requirement specific to that forum, is identified and confirmed during the conflict check and intake. Because a Superior evaluee's employment history may cross into Minnesota, the written opinion is built to survive scrutiny in whichever courtroom the caption lands, with methodology stated plainly enough for opposing counsel to test it.

## A labor market that does not stop at the state line

Vocational findings for a Superior evaluee rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Duluth metropolitan area, which covers communities on both sides of the border, alongside Wisconsin statewide estimates and state workforce agency labor market information. The honest question is what work actually remains available to this person within a reasonable commute, and for a Superior resident that inquiry cannot be confined to Wisconsin employers when the metro's principal city lies in Minnesota. The analysis names its sources, keeps community description qualitative, and reserves quantitative claims for what the published estimates support. The [Duluth metro page](/locations/minnesota/duluth/) describes the regional market in fuller detail, and the [Wisconsin state page](/locations/wisconsin/) explains how the same sources extend to matters filed elsewhere in the state.

## Evaluating a Superior resident without a local office

The firm maintains no office in Superior and publishes no street address here; every meeting is by appointment, and saying so directly is part of the same discipline that governs the reports themselves. In practice, most engagements proceed remotely: records move through counsel, and interviews and vocational testing are conducted by secure video when the referral questions permit. Where an in-person evaluation is warranted, it is scheduled in the evaluee's own community, with travel arranged from the Kansas City home office and coordinated around deposition and trial dates already on counsel's calendar. Files that also require economic modeling are handled through [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement structure.
