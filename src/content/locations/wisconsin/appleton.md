Expert disclosure and trial calendars rarely leave slack, and a referral
that sits unassigned costs counsel options. Appleton attorneys engage
Purinton Analytics when injury, employment, or disability litigation in
Outagamie County or Calumet County requires a
[vocational expert](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) opinion on earning
capacity and employability, a
[life care plan](/services/life-care-planning/), or a
[medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/). Where a full
damages picture is needed,
[coordinated engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) pair
that work with independent economist partners who perform and sign the
economic analysis under one engagement structure, so the disciplines stay
aligned without any of them borrowing another's conclusions.

## Venues served from the Appleton metro

State court engagements are accepted for matters in the Outagamie County
Circuit Court and the Calumet County Circuit Court. Federal matters from the
area proceed in the United States District Court for the Eastern District of
Wisconsin, and the firm also appears in the United States District Court for
the Western District of Wisconsin when a case is venued there. Any
venue-specific registration or qualification requirement is identified and
confirmed during the conflict check and intake process. Counsel with matters
elsewhere in the state can start from the
[Wisconsin page](/locations/wisconsin/), which maps the statewide practice.

## Appointment logistics and honest geography

Appleton meetings are by appointment. The firm has no staffed office in the
metro, publishes no local street address, and will not pretend otherwise;
the same insistence on accuracy governs every line of its reports. In
practice, the absence of a storefront rarely slows a case. Records review,
structured interviews, and standardized testing under publisher protocols
proceed remotely where remote administration is supported, and when the
referral questions require an in-person evaluation, or when deposition and
trial dates demand presence, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home
office around the deadlines that prompted the engagement. Counsel keep the
same point of contact and the same report standard whether the work is
performed on site or at a distance.

## Reading the Appleton labor market

Employability and earning capacity findings for Appleton area evaluees are
built on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage
estimates for the Appleton metropolitan area and for Wisconsin statewide,
supplemented by state workforce agency labor market information and direct
labor market research where warranted. Care-related opinions are priced to
the evaluee's own market using provider-specific inquiries and published
regional fee data. The same sources support
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) when the
assignment is to test an opposing expert's report rather than to produce an
affirmative one, which keeps critique and construction on identical
methodological footing.

## Getting a conflict check started

Because timing drove the referral, the intake is built to be fast without
being casual: submit the caption or party names through the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/), identify the disclosure, deposition,
or trial dates already set, and note whether the assignment is affirmative
or rebuttal. Scope and fee terms are confirmed in writing once the check
clears. Plaintiff and defense counsel are served on the same terms, in
Appleton and across the firm's
[national practice](/locations/nationwide/).
