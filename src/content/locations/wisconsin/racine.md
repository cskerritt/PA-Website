An evaluation should come to the evaluee, not the other way around. For
Racine litigation, Purinton Analytics conducts vocational and life care
planning evaluations remotely or in the evaluee's own community, so that an
injured worker or a catastrophically injured plaintiff is assessed against
the labor market and care resources where they actually live. Counsel on
either side of the caption use the practice for
[earning capacity and employability evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[coordinated damages assessment](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/),
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Racine County venue coverage

Engagements are accepted for matters venued in the Racine County Circuit
Court. Federal cases from the Racine-Mount Pleasant area proceed in the
United States District Court for the Eastern District of Wisconsin, and the
firm also appears in the United States District Court for the Western
District of Wisconsin when a matter is venued there. Whatever the forum, the
report follows the same structure, discloses the same methodology, and cites
the same classes of sources, because a report written differently for
different courts is a report that invites impeachment. The broader statewide
practice is described on the [Wisconsin page](/locations/wisconsin/).

## By appointment only, and said plainly

The firm keeps no staffed office in Racine and lists no street address for
this location. That fact is stated here rather than papered over because
honesty about presence is part of the same discipline that governs the
firm's opinions. Meetings are by appointment; most of the engagement
proceeds without one. Records are reviewed remotely, interviews are
conducted by video, and standardized testing follows publisher protocols
where remote administration is supported. When an in-person evaluation is
warranted, or when a deposition or trial requires attendance, travel is
scheduled from the Kansas City home office and confirmed against the case
calendar, so counsel never discover a scheduling conflict after the
disclosure deadline has passed.

## Local labor market, national method

Vocational findings for Racine evaluees rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates for the Racine-Mount Pleasant
metropolitan area and for Wisconsin statewide, along with state workforce
agency labor market information and direct labor market research where the
referral questions call for it. Life care plans and
[medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/) are priced to
the evaluee's own market through provider-specific inquiries and published
regional fee data. The method is national; the data is local; the report
explains both, so that either side's counsel can trace every conclusion
back to its source.

## Opening an engagement

Every Racine matter, plaintiff or defense, starts with the same step:
[request a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) with the case caption and party
names, noting any disclosure, deposition, or trial dates already scheduled.
Any venue-specific registration or qualification requirement is identified
and confirmed during the conflict check and intake process. Counsel with
related matters outside Wisconsin can review the firm's
[national coverage](/locations/nationwide/) for how the same evaluee-centered
model operates elsewhere.
