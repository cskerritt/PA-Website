Not every referral arrives as a blank slate. Often the file already contains an opposing expert's vocational report or life care plan, and the immediate need is a disciplined answer to it. For Brookfield matters, Purinton Analytics performs [rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) that tests the opposing opinion's methodology, sourcing, and internal consistency, and it prepares original [vocational](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) and care-planning opinions when the case calls for an affirmative analysis instead. Because the firm is retained by plaintiff and defense counsel alike, its critiques are methodological rather than positional: the same standards apply no matter who wrote the report under review.

## Venue for Brookfield disputes

Brookfield is a Waukesha County municipality, and its state-court civil litigation is generally venued in the Circuit Court serving Waukesha County. Where a case belongs in federal court, Wisconsin's trial forums are the United States District Court for the Eastern District of Wisconsin and the United States District Court for the Western District of Wisconsin. The firm does not guess at the controlling forum from a marketing page; venue, along with any expert qualification requirement it carries, is identified during the conflict check and intake, and the report or rebuttal is then written to that forum's disclosure standard.

## What the labor market data must show

Whether the task is an original opinion or a critique of someone else's, the labor market evidence is judged the same way: is each finding traceable to a source? For a Brookfield evaluee, the relevant market is the Milwaukee-Waukesha regional economy, and the firm's analyses rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Milwaukee metropolitan area and for Wisconsin statewide, state workforce agency labor market information, and direct labor market research where warranted. A rebuttal asks whether the opposing report meets that traceability standard; an affirmative report is built to meet it from the start. Numbers are reserved for signed opinions, where they can be sourced and cross-examined.

## Logistics without a local office

The firm has no Brookfield office and claims none; meetings are by appointment, and stating that honestly is part of the credibility it sells. Records review, scope conferences, and evaluee interviews ordinarily proceed remotely by video, which keeps the timeline predictable for both a fast-moving rebuttal and a full evaluation. In-person assessment, when it will strengthen the record, is conducted in the evaluee's own community with travel scheduled from the Kansas City home office, and the same travel practice covers depositions and trial. Cases needing economic damages can proceed as [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement structure.
