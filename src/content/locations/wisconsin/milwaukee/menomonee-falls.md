## The courts behind the calendar

Those deadlines issue from a specific bench. Menomonee Falls sits in Waukesha County, and its state-court civil actions are generally heard in the Circuit Court serving Waukesha County. Matters within federal jurisdiction proceed in the United States District Court for the Eastern District of Wisconsin or the United States District Court for the Western District of Wisconsin; the forum that controls a particular case, and the expert qualification and disclosure rules that follow from it, are confirmed during the conflict check and intake. Knowing the forum early matters because disclosure standards differ, and the report must be built for the standard that will actually judge it.
