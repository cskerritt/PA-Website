Earning capacity, future care, and their economic value are separate opinions that must nonetheless agree with each other. When the vocational expert, the life care planner, and the economist are retained piecemeal, their assumptions can drift apart, and cross-examination finds the seams. For New Berlin matters, Purinton Analytics addresses that risk at the level of engagement design: the firm prepares the [vocational evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) and the [life care plan](/services/life-care-planning/), and through [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement structure. Plaintiff and defense counsel retain the same coordination on the same terms.

## Forum questions answered at intake

New Berlin lies in Waukesha County, so a state-court action arising in the city ordinarily proceeds in the Circuit Court serving Waukesha County, while matters within federal jurisdiction belong to the United States District Court for the Eastern District of Wisconsin or the United States District Court for the Western District of Wisconsin. In a coordinated engagement the forum question carries extra consequence, because a shared disclosure calendar governs several deliverables at once: the vocational evaluation, the care plan, and the economist partners' analysis must all arrive on it together, in a form the controlling court will accept. The firm therefore treats venue as an intake question, confirmed during the conflict check along with any expert qualification or registration requirement the forum imposes, before scope and schedule are fixed. Regional venue context sits on the [Milwaukee metro page](/locations/wisconsin/milwaukee/), and the statewide court structure on the [Wisconsin page](/locations/wisconsin/).

## A regional market, documented sources

Vocational and earning capacity findings for a New Berlin evaluee are grounded in Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Milwaukee metropolitan area and for Wisconsin statewide, supplemented by state workforce agency labor market information and by direct labor market research when the referral questions call for evidence about actual employer requirements. Because these analyses feed a coordinated damages presentation, sourcing discipline carries extra weight: every input the economist partners rely on must trace back to a vocational or care-planning finding that is itself documented. That chain is also why this page carries none of the underlying data. Printed here, a number would stand apart from its source; the signed reports are built on the opposite habit.

## Evaluations in the evaluee's community

The firm maintains no office in New Berlin and does not suggest otherwise. Service is by appointment, and most of an engagement is conducted remotely by design: a documented conflict check, an engagement letter with a defined scope, a records request tailored to the claims, and interviews by video as the default. Some referral questions do call for in-person assessment, and those evaluations happen where the evaluee lives, not in a borrowed conference room presented as a local branch. The evaluator makes that trip from the Kansas City home office, which is also the point of departure for deposition and trial appearances. [Expert testimony](/services/expert-testimony-litigation-consulting/) is prepared with the full coordinated file in view, so the opinions presented at deposition or trial remain consistent with each other and with the record that produced them.
