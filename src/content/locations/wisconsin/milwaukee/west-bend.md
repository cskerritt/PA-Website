A vocational report earns its keep in the deposition room. Long before trial, the opinions the firm signs for West Bend matters will be read line by line by opposing counsel, and the questions are predictable: where did this figure come from, why this occupation, what did the expert not review. Purinton Analytics writes every [vocational evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) and [life care plan](/services/life-care-planning/) to answer those questions before they are asked, with sources named, scope defined in the engagement letter, and methodology stated in the report itself. The standard is the same whether plaintiff or defense counsel makes the referral, because the cross-examiner changes but the questions do not.

## The labor market a cross-examiner will probe

For labor market purposes West Bend belongs to the Milwaukee-Waukesha metropolitan area, and a West Bend evaluee's employment prospects are read at that regional scale while the venue analysis stays with Washington County. Vocational findings rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Milwaukee metropolitan area and for Wisconsin statewide, on state workforce agency labor market information, and on direct labor market research where published estimates cannot answer the referral questions. Every data choice is open to testing at deposition, so the report records why a source was used and what it covers. What counsel will not find here is a wage or employment figure of any kind: a number on a marketing page cannot be checked against anything, which is exactly the defect the reports are written to avoid. ## By-appointment service, stated plainly

Little undermines an expert's credibility faster than an office that does not exist, so the firm says plainly that it keeps none in West Bend and publishes no local address. Service is by appointment: a documented conflict check, an engagement letter with a defined scope, records review, and video interviews as the default. An in-person assessment is added when the record needs one, and it takes place wherever the evaluee actually lives; those visits are scheduled out of the firm's Kansas City home office, and depositions and trial attendance are handled on the same basis. Where the damages case requires economic valuation, [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) place that work with independent economist partners who perform and sign the economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement structure.
