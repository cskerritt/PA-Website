The referrals that reach Purinton Analytics from Greenfield counsel tend to reduce to a handful of questions. Can this person return to the old job, and if not, to what work? What has the injury done to earning capacity over a working lifetime? What will future care require, and who will deliver it? Each is answerable, but only by method: a [vocational evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/), a [life care plan](/services/life-care-planning/), or both, built on the record and written to be read by opposing counsel. The firm takes those questions from whichever side of the caption asks them, and the method does not change with the client.

## The forums where the answers are tested

Greenfield is part of Milwaukee County, and its state-court civil actions are generally venued in the Circuit Court serving Milwaukee County. Wisconsin's federal trial courts, the United States District Court for the Eastern District of Wisconsin and the United States District Court for the Western District of Wisconsin, hear the matters that belong in federal court. Rather than assert from a web page which forum will control, the firm resolves venue during the conflict check and intake, along with any expert qualification requirement the chosen forum applies, and then writes the report to that forum's disclosure standard.

## What "earning capacity" means here

For a Greenfield evaluee, earning capacity is measured against the Milwaukee-Waukesha regional labor market, because that is the market a resident of the city actually works in. The firm's answer draws on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Milwaukee metropolitan area and for Wisconsin statewide, on state workforce agency labor market information, and on direct labor market research when the questions demand evidence beyond published estimates. Care-related questions get the same treatment: the availability and cost structure of attendant care, therapies, and equipment are researched, not assumed. Consistent with the firm's publication standard, the figures themselves appear only in signed reports where each is tied to its source.

## The practical arrangements

Greenfield counsel will find no local Purinton Analytics office, because none exists; the firm treats that candor as inseparable from the reliability of its opinions. Service is by appointment. Most engagements run remotely from conflict check through defined scope, records review, and video interview. Where the referral questions justify in-person assessment, the evaluation is carried out in the evaluee's own community, with travel scheduled from the Kansas City home office, as are appearances for deposition and trial. Where economic damages must be quantified, the firm structures [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement structure.
