For an evaluee in West Allis, that means reconstructing what the person actually did for a living, what skills that work built, and what those skills transfer to now that injury or illness has changed the picture. Purinton Analytics builds [vocational evaluations](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) for West Allis matters from that record forward, documenting each step so the resulting opinion holds up whether the firm was retained by the plaintiff or by the defense.

## Where a West Allis case is heard

West Allis sits in Milwaukee County, and civil actions arising here are generally tried in the Circuit Court serving Milwaukee County. Federal-question and diversity matters may instead proceed in the state's federal trial courts, the United States District Court for the Eastern District of Wisconsin or the United States District Court for the Western District of Wisconsin; the forum that will actually control, together with any qualification requirement particular to it, is confirmed during the conflict check and intake. The engagement is then scoped to that forum's disclosure expectations, because a report written for the wrong standard costs counsel time that compressed schedules rarely allow.

## Transferable skills in a regional market

Once the work history is established, the question becomes what employment remains realistically available, and that question is answered at the scale of the Milwaukee-Waukesha regional economy rather than a single municipality. The analysis draws on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Milwaukee metropolitan area and for Wisconsin as a whole, on state workforce agency labor market information, and on direct labor market inquiry where the referral warrants it. No figures appear on this page by design; in the signed report, every wage and employment finding is tied to a published estimate or a documented source that opposing counsel can check.
