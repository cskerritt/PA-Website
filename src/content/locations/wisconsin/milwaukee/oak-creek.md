Most of what a vocational expert or life care planner does never requires being in the same room as anyone. Records are reviewed, scopes are defined, interviews are conducted by video, and drafts are checked against sources, all of it documented. That is why Purinton Analytics can serve Oak Creek counsel rigorously without pretending to a local presence it does not have: the remote-first model is not a compromise, it is how disciplined [vocational](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) and [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) work is done, for plaintiff and defense referrals alike, with in-person steps added deliberately where they earn their place.

## Oak Creek's courts

When an Oak Creek dispute is filed in state court, it is generally heard in the Circuit Court serving Milwaukee County, the county in which the city sits. Federal matters proceed in the United States District Court for the Eastern District of Wisconsin or the United States District Court for the Western District of Wisconsin, depending on the case; the venue that will actually govern, and any expert registration or qualification requirement it imposes, is confirmed as part of the conflict check and intake. That confirmation happens before drafting, because the disclosure standard of the controlling forum shapes the report from its structure down to its citations.

## Data before opinion

An Oak Creek evaluee's employment prospects are evaluated against the Milwaukee-Waukesha regional economy, the market in which such an evaluee actually competes. The evidentiary base is consistent across engagements: Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Milwaukee metropolitan area and for Wisconsin statewide, state workforce agency labor market information, and direct labor market research when the referral questions require testing what published estimates cannot show. The order matters: data is gathered before conclusions are formed, and the signed report shows the path from source to finding. This page, by the firm's own rule, presents no figures at all.

## When in-person work is worth it

Some things video cannot capture, and the firm says so as plainly as it says it has no Oak Creek office and publishes no local address. Where functional observation, a home assessment for a care plan, or the dynamics of a family interview would materially strengthen the record, the evaluation is conducted in the evaluee's own community, with travel scheduled from the Kansas City home office. Deposition and trial attendance follow the same practice. And when the case requires economic valuation alongside the vocational and care-planning opinions, [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) place that analysis with independent economist partners who perform and sign it while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement structure.
