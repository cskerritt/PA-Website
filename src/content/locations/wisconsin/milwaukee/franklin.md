An expert opinion is only as defensible as the process that produced it. For Franklin referrals, Purinton Analytics runs the same sequence every time: documented conflict check, engagement letter with a defined scope, records request tailored to the claims, analysis, and a signed report whose findings trace to their sources. The sequence does not bend for the retaining party; plaintiff and defense counsel purchase the identical discipline. What varies is the question presented, whether it calls for a [vocational evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/), a [life care plan](/services/life-care-planning/), or [expert testimony](/services/expert-testimony-litigation-consulting/) on opinions already formed.

## Franklin's place in the court system

Franklin belongs to Milwaukee County, and civil actions arising there are generally heard in the Circuit Court serving Milwaukee County. For matters within federal jurisdiction, Wisconsin offers the United States District Court for the Eastern District of Wisconsin and the United States District Court for the Western District of Wisconsin. Identifying which forum governs a specific caption is itself part of the process: it happens during the conflict check and intake, together with confirmation of any venue-specific expert qualification requirement, so that scope, methodology, and disclosure format are aligned with the controlling court before any analysis begins.

## Sourcing the labor market step

Within that sequence, the labor market step has its own rules. A Franklin evaluee's employment prospects are analyzed against the Milwaukee-Waukesha regional economy using Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Milwaukee metropolitan area and for Wisconsin statewide, supplemented by state workforce agency labor market information and, where the referral questions require it, direct labor market research with documented contacts. The output of the step is a set of findings each tied to a published estimate or a recorded inquiry, which is why the firm publishes no figures on pages like this: unsourced numbers on a website would contradict the standard the reports are held to.

## Where the process meets the evaluee

Nothing in the process requires a Franklin storefront, and the firm keeps none, publishes no local address, and says so directly. Interviews and assessments default to video, which serves most referral questions well and keeps costs proportionate. The process adds in-person steps when they earn their place: an evaluation in the evaluee's own community, a home assessment for a care plan, or attendance at deposition and trial, each with travel scheduled from the Kansas City home office. When economic valuation belongs in the case, the firm arranges [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement structure.
