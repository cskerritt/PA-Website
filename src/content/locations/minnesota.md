Most of the work behind a defensible vocational opinion happens at a desk, not a courthouse: records review, transferable skills analysis, labor market research, and careful drafting. That is why Purinton Analytics can serve Minnesota counsel, plaintiff and defense alike, without pretending to a local storefront. The analysis is remote-first and fully documented; presence in Minnesota is reserved for the moments that actually require it, such as in-person evaluation, deposition, or trial.

## Minnesota courts and communities served

The practice accepts engagements for matters pending in the District Court throughout Minnesota and in the United States District Court for the District of Minnesota. Vocational and life care planning questions arise in personal injury, employment, family, and disability-related litigation in all of these forums, and each written opinion is prepared with the expectation that opposing counsel will read it closely.

Dedicated pages describe service in Minneapolis-St. Paul-Bloomington, MN-WI, Duluth, MN-WI, Rochester, MN, St. Cloud, MN, Mankato, MN, Brainerd, MN, Faribault-Northfield, MN, Fergus Falls, MN, Winona, MN, Red Wing, MN, Bemidji, MN, Grand Rapids, MN, Willmar, MN, Austin, MN, and Alexandria, MN. Counsel in communities beyond those metros receive the same service; the pages describe logistics, not limits.

## The engagement model in Minnesota

Minnesota engagements run on the structure the firm applies across its [national practice](/locations/nationwide/): a documented conflict check, a defined scope, and a records request tailored to the claims. Secure document exchange and videoconference carry most of the collaboration with counsel, which keeps the timeline in counsel's control and the cost proportionate to the dispute. Vocational interviews are conducted remotely where appropriate, or in person in the evaluee's own community when the referral questions warrant direct observation.

The firm keeps no staffed office in Minnesota and publishes no street address for the state, because candor about presence belongs to the same discipline that governs the reports themselves. Meetings are by appointment. When testimony or evaluation requires travel, it is scheduled from the Kansas City home office around the case calendar. For matters that also require economic damages analysis, the firm offers coordinated damages engagements in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under a single engagement structure.

Labor market findings for Minnesota evaluees rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the relevant Minnesota metropolitan areas and for the state as a whole, together with state workforce agency labor market information and, where warranted, direct employer contact. Sources are cited in full so that every conclusion can be traced back to its data.

## Qualification and disclosure in Minnesota matters

Scheduling orders and expert disclosure expectations vary between the District Court and the federal forum, and from judge to judge. The firm drafts to the stricter standard as a matter of habit, and any venue-specific registration or qualification requirement is identified and confirmed during the conflict check and intake process. Raising disclosure deadlines at intake lets the work plan run backward from the dates that bind it.

## Starting a Minnesota engagement

To retain the firm for a Minnesota matter, on either side of the caption, submit the caption or party names through the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and note the venue, the referral questions, and any imminent disclosure, deposition, or trial dates. Counsel receive a conflict determination, a proposed scope, and a records request tailored to the claims, so the eventual opinion stands on a record both sides can examine.
