Bowling Green anchors a working regional economy in south-central Kentucky,
and the injury, employment, and disability disputes that arise from it need
the same expert rigor that counsel in the largest cities expect. Purinton
Analytics provides that rigor to plaintiff and defense counsel across the
Bowling Green metropolitan area:
[vocational expert evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) of
employability and earning capacity,
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) for catastrophic
injuries, and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Courts serving Warren County and its neighbors

The metro comprises Warren County, Allen County, Butler County, and
Edmonson County. Engagements are accepted for matters venued in the Circuit
Court of Warren County and in the circuit courts of the neighboring
counties, as well as in the United States District Court for the Western
District of Kentucky. Matters venued in the United States District Court
for the Eastern District of Kentucky are accepted through the same intake.
Whatever the forum, the deliverable is the same: a written opinion with a
documented record and a stated methodology, prepared on the assumption that
opposing counsel will read every line of it.

## By-appointment service, honestly described

There is no staffed Purinton Analytics office in Bowling Green, and the
firm publishes no street address for this location. That candor is
deliberate; an expert whose website invents offices invites questions about
what else has been embellished. Engagements typically proceed remotely,
with records review, structured interviews by secure video, and
standardized testing under publisher protocols where remote administration
is supported. When an in-person evaluation better serves the referral
questions, or when depositions and trial settings require attendance,
travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office and planned against
the case calendar rather than left to chance. The engagement keeps the same
report standard and the same point of contact from intake through
testimony, whether the work is performed remotely or in the region.

## Reading the south-central Kentucky labor market

Vocational conclusions for area evaluees are grounded in Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Bowling Green
metropolitan area and for Kentucky statewide, in state workforce agency
labor market information, and in direct labor market research when the
questions demand it. That grounding matters in a regional employment
center, where the mix of occupations open to an injured worker differs from
the mix in a major metropolitan core. Life care plans and
[medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/) are priced
to the evaluee's own community using provider-specific inquiries and
published fee data, not to a national average that no local provider
actually charges.

work. Counsel can [request a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) with the case

dates already on the calendar. The firm's work across the rest of the state
is described on the [Kentucky page](/locations/kentucky/), and its approach
to matters beyond Kentucky is set out under
