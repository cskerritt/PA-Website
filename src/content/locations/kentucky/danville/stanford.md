usually a problem of scale: an expert who treats a Lincoln County plaintiff as if
they lived in a major city produces an opinion that falls apart on
cross-examination. Purinton Analytics builds the file the other way, starting
from the evaluee's actual community and the work that is realistically

## Lincoln County venue context

Stanford sits in Lincoln County, and a state-law personal injury,
employment, or disability matter arising there is tried in the Circuit
Court. When a claim belongs in federal court, Kentucky's available forums are the
United States District Court for the Eastern District of Kentucky and the
United States District Court for the Western District of Kentucky; the
district that controls a given caption, along with any registration or
qualification requirement the venue imposes on a testifying expert, is
identified and confirmed during the conflict check and intake. Reports are
prepared to the same disclosure standard for either setting, with sources
and reasoning laid out so an opposing expert can retrace each step. Counsel
looking at the wider region will find the surrounding venue picture on the
[Danville metro page](/locations/kentucky/danville/) and the statewide
practice summary on the [Kentucky page](/locations/kentucky/).

## Where Stanford stands in the Danville area market

An honest earning-capacity opinion for a Stanford evaluee has to describe
the labor market that person can actually reach, which extends beyond the
town itself toward Danville and the surrounding area without assuming every
position in the region is within a sustainable commute. The analysis rests
on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates
for the Danville area and for Kentucky statewide, supplemented by state
workforce agency labor market information and, when the referral questions
warrant it, direct labor market research that contacts employers about the
specific jobs the opinion depends on. Findings stay qualitative until the
data supports them, and every source is cited precisely enough for the
other side to pull it and test the conclusion.

## Evaluating an evaluee in Stanford

The firm keeps no office in Stanford and publishes no local street address;
accuracy about presence is part of the same discipline that governs the
reports. Records review, conferences with counsel, and vocational
interviews proceed by secure video in most engagements, which keeps costs
proportionate to the case. When the referral calls for in-person
assessment, or when a deposition or trial date requires the expert in the
room, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office and the
evaluation takes place in the evaluee's own community, where daily
routines, functional limits, and real commuting conditions can be observed
rather than assumed. Life care planning interviews follow the same rule and
meet the evaluee where care is actually delivered.
