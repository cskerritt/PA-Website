St. Matthews lies in Jefferson County, and state-law claims arising there
are tried in the Jefferson County Circuit Court. When a case is federal,
Kentucky's trial-level forums are the United States District Court for the
Western District of Kentucky and the United States District Court for the
Eastern District of Kentucky; the district that actually controls, and any
venue-specific qualification requirement that comes with it, is confirmed
as part of the conflict check and intake. Reports are drafted to hold up
under either court's scrutiny, and under the sharper scrutiny of the

## Grounding the vocational analysis

An evaluee who lives in St. Matthews works, searches, and retrains in the
labor market of the greater Louisville area. The firm's opinions draw on
Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for
the Louisville metropolitan area and for Kentucky statewide, on state
workforce agency labor market information, and on direct labor market
research where the referral questions justify it. No figure is quoted
without its source, and no conclusion is offered without the reasoning
that ties the data to this particular person's restrictions, education,
and history.
