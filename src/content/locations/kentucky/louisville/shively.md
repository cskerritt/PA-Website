The most reliable place to understand what an injury has done to a working
life is the place where that life is lived. For a Shively evaluee, that
means the assessment happens in Shively: when an engagement calls for
in-person evaluation, Purinton Analytics conducts it in the evaluee's own
community, where daily function, home layout, and the practical realities
of getting to work can be observed rather than reconstructed from records.
Counsel for plaintiffs and counsel for defendants retain the firm on equal
terms for [vocational expert opinions](/services/vocational-expert-witness/)
on employability and earning capacity, for
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) in serious injury
matters, and for
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## The courts that serve Shively

Shively is part of Jefferson County, so a state-law claim arising there is
tried in the Jefferson County Circuit Court. Kentucky's federal trial
courts are the United States District Court for the Western District of
Kentucky and the United States District Court for the Eastern District of
Kentucky; rather than presume which forum a matter belongs in, the firm
resolves the controlling venue, and any qualification requirement tied to
it, during the conflict check and intake. The written product is the same
either way: a report an opposing expert can retrace, with every source
citable and every inference explained. Counsel can situate a Shively case
in its regional context through the
[Louisville metro page](/locations/kentucky/louisville/) and the
[Kentucky page](/locations/kentucky/).

## What the labor market data must show

Shively workers participate in the employment market of the greater
Louisville area, and a credible opinion treats them that way. The firm
anchors its vocational findings in Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational
employment and wage estimates for the Louisville metropolitan area and for
Kentucky statewide, together with state workforce agency labor market
information and, when the referral warrants, direct labor market research.
The data alone is not the opinion; the opinion is the demonstrated fit
between the data and this evaluee's documented restrictions, skills, and
work history, stated plainly enough that either side can test it.

## Remote by default, present when it matters

There is no local storefront and no published Shively address, and the firm
regards that candor as part of its professional product. Records review,
conferences with counsel, and most interviews proceed by secure video,
which holds down expense for whichever side has retained the firm. In-person
work, whether a community-based evaluation or testimony at deposition and
trial, is scheduled as travel from the Kansas City home office and
coordinated with counsel's calendar.
