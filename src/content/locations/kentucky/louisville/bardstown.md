Counsel in smaller communities have long faced a structural problem: the
vocational and life care planning experts tend to cluster in major cities,
while the cases arise everywhere. Purinton Analytics built its practice to
close that gap. From Bardstown, at the Nelson County edge of the Louisville
metropolitan area, a referring attorney gets the same expert product as a
firm in any principal city, delivered through a remote-first national model
described on the [nationwide page](/locations/nationwide/). The practice
accepts work from both sides of the caption:
[vocational expert opinions](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) on
employability and earning capacity,
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) for catastrophic
injury, and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Nelson County venue and Kentucky's federal courts

State-law claims arising in Bardstown are heard in the Nelson County
Circuit Court. In federal litigation, Kentucky's forums are the United
States District Court for the Western District of Kentucky and the United
States District Court for the Eastern District of Kentucky, and the
question of which one governs a particular case is answered during the
conflict check and intake, together with any qualification or registration
requirement the venue imposes. Reports are prepared to a courtroom
standard in either system: methodology stated openly, sources identified
precisely, opinions bounded by the record. For the surrounding region, see
the [Louisville metro page](/locations/kentucky/louisville/); for the
Commonwealth as a whole, the [Kentucky page](/locations/kentucky/).

## A Bardstown evaluee's real labor market

Bardstown lies within the Louisville metropolitan area, so the employment
picture for a local evaluee includes both work near home and work within
commuting reach of the larger regional market. The firm's vocational
findings rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and
wage estimates for the Louisville metropolitan area and for Kentucky
statewide, on state workforce agency labor market information, and on
direct labor market research when the referral questions call for it. The
analysis names what is realistically available to this person, in this
place, with these restrictions, and shows its work at every step, so that
the opinion invites verification rather than resisting it.

## Distance without disadvantage

The firm holds no office in Bardstown and does not manufacture the
impression of one. What counsel gets instead is a process engineered for
distance: secure video for interviews and conferences, electronic records
handling, and disciplined written communication that keeps both sides'
costs proportionate. When in-person work is required, whether an
evaluation conducted in the evaluee's own community or testimony at
deposition or trial, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office
and planned well ahead of the controlling dates.
