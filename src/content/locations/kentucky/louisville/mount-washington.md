Many of Mount Washington's workers cross the county line every morning: they
live in Bullitt County and earn their wages in the wider Louisville
metropolitan market. That commuting pattern is not background color in a
damages case, it is the analysis. Where a person can realistically work
after an injury depends on the market they actually participate in, not the
one drawn by municipal boundaries, and Purinton Analytics builds its
opinions around that distinction. Counsel on either side of the caption
retain the firm for
[vocational expert testimony](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) on
employability and earning capacity, for
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) when injuries create
lifelong care needs, and for
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Where a Bullitt County claim is heard

State-law matters arising in Mount Washington proceed in the Bullitt County
Circuit Court. Federal-question and diversity cases have two possible homes
in the Commonwealth, the United States District Court for the Western
District of Kentucky and the United States District Court for the Eastern
District of Kentucky; the district that governs a specific matter, together
with any venue-specific qualification requirement, is settled during the
conflict check and intake rather than assumed. Every report is drafted so
it reads the same in either courthouse, with methodology an opposing expert
can walk through step by step. The
[Louisville metro page](/locations/kentucky/louisville/) covers the
regional venue picture, and the [Kentucky page](/locations/kentucky/)
addresses the statewide practice.

## The commuting market and earning capacity

For a Mount Washington evaluee, the honest labor market is regional. The
firm's vocational findings rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational
employment and wage estimates for the Louisville metropolitan area and for
Kentucky as a whole, alongside state workforce agency labor market
information and targeted direct research where the questions demand it.
What matters is not reciting those sources but connecting them: the report
explains why particular occupations remain open or closed to this person,
given documented restrictions, education, and work history, so the opinion
survives the cross-examination it will certainly meet.

## Interviews, evaluations, and appearances

The firm maintains no office in Mount Washington and says so plainly.
Records analysis, attorney conferences, and most vocational interviews are
handled by secure video, which serves both plaintiff and defense budgets.
When an in-person evaluation is warranted, it happens in the evaluee's own
community, where daily function and real commuting distances can be
observed instead of estimated; travel for those evaluations, and for
depositions and trial appearances, is scheduled from the Kansas City home
office.
