## Bullitt County and the two federal districts

A state-law claim arising in Shepherdsville proceeds in the Bullitt County
Circuit Court. Matters in federal court land in one of Kentucky's two
districts, the United States District Court for the Western District of
Kentucky or the United States District Court for the Eastern District of
Kentucky; the firm treats the question of which district controls as an
intake fact to be confirmed, not assumed, and resolves it, along with any
venue-specific qualification requirement, during the conflict check. Since
opposing counsel will read every page, the reports carry their methodology
on the surface: what was reviewed, what was relied on, and how each
conclusion follows. The wider regional practice appears on the
[Louisville metro page](/locations/kentucky/louisville/), and statewide

## Earning capacity in the Louisville orbit

Shepherdsville sits inside the Louisville metropolitan area, and its
residents' realistic employment options are regional, not municipal. To
describe those options honestly, the firm works from Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Louisville
metropolitan area and for Kentucky statewide, from state workforce agency
labor market information, and from direct labor market research when a
referral question requires original inquiry. Each source is cited with
enough precision that opposing counsel can pull it and check it, and the
report explains how the data applies to this evaluee's documented
restrictions and work history. Whether the analysis helps or hurts a
client's position is not the firm's concern; whether it is defensible
under oath is.

## How the work gets done

The firm keeps no Shepherdsville office and represents none. Most of an
engagement, records analysis, conferences, and vocational interviews, runs
over secure video, which both sides' budgets appreciate. Where the
referral calls for an in-person evaluation, it takes place in the
evaluee's own community; where deposition or trial testimony is required,
travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office around the dates the
court has set.
