Louisville litigation crosses a state line as a matter of routine. The
Louisville/Jefferson County, KY-IN metropolitan area takes in counties on the
Kentucky side and the Indiana side alike, and Purinton Analytics serves
plaintiff and defense counsel throughout it with
[earning capacity and employability evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/)
in injury and employment matters,
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) in catastrophic injury
cases, and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## A venue footprint on both sides of the line

Engagements are accepted for matters venued in the Circuit Court of
Jefferson County and in the circuit courts of the surrounding Kentucky
counties of the metro, including Bullitt County, Henry County, Meade County,
Nelson County, Oldham County, Shelby County, and Spencer County. On the
Indiana side, the practice serves counsel with matters arising in
Clark County, Floyd County, Harrison County, and Washington County,
Indiana.
Federal matters are accepted in the United States District Court for the
Western District of Kentucky and, where a case is venued there, in the
United States District Court for the Eastern District of Kentucky. The same
report standard applies regardless of which courthouse will receive the
opinion: a written opinion that rests on a documented record, states its
methodology plainly, and is prepared to be examined by the other side.

## Meetings by appointment, not a storefront

The firm keeps no staffed office in Louisville and publishes no street
address for this location, because accuracy about presence is part of the
same discipline that governs the reports. Most engagements from the metro
begin remotely: records review, a structured vocational interview, and
standardized testing administered under publisher protocols where remote
administration is supported. When the referral questions call for in-person
evaluation, or when deposition and trial dates require presence, travel is
scheduled from the Kansas City home office and coordinated around counsel's
calendar. Counsel receive the same report standard and the same point of
contact whether the work is performed remotely or on site.

## Labor market research where the state line runs through the data

A metro that spans Kentucky and Indiana requires care in sourcing.
Vocational findings for Louisville area evaluees rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Louisville
metropolitan area, supplemented by state workforce agency labor market
information from the state where the evaluee actually works and by direct
labor market research where the referral questions warrant it. Life care
plans and [medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/) are
priced to the evaluee's own market rather than to national averages, using
provider-specific inquiries and published fee data for the region, so that
the resulting figures can be defended line by line at deposition.

## Starting a Louisville engagement

Every engagement begins the way it does everywhere else in the practice:
with a documented conflict check, a defined scope, and a records request
tailored to the claims. Submit the caption or party names through the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and note any imminent disclosure,
deposition, or trial dates. Counsel with matters elsewhere in the state
will find the broader venue picture on the [Kentucky page](/locations/kentucky/),
and support for litigation that reaches beyond the region is described
under the firm's [national coverage](/locations/nationwide/).
