Radcliff's workforce does not stop at the city line. People who live in
Radcliff work across Hardin County and the wider Elizabethtown metro, and
workers from elsewhere in the region commute the other way, which means an
earning-capacity question about a Radcliff evaluee is almost never a
question about Radcliff alone. Purinton Analytics frames its vocational
opinions around that commuting reality: what work the evaluee can still
perform, where that work actually exists within realistic reach of home,
and what it pays in the market the evaluee genuinely faces. Counsel on
either side of the caption retain the firm on identical terms, most often
for [vocational expert opinions](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) on
employability and earning capacity,
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) where injuries are
catastrophic, and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Hardin County courts and the federal forums

Radcliff sits in Hardin County, and state-court civil matters proceed in
the Circuit Court there. In the federal system, Kentucky's available forums
are the United States District Court for the Eastern District of Kentucky
and the United States District Court for the Western District of Kentucky;
which district controls a given case, along with any venue-specific
registration or qualification requirement, is confirmed during the conflict
check and intake. Because the same report may be examined in either
setting, each opinion is built to a court-ready standard: a methodology an
opposing expert can retrace, sources cited precisely enough to be pulled
and checked, and conclusions that stay inside the disclosed scope. Related
venues throughout the region appear on the
[Elizabethtown metro page](/locations/kentucky/elizabethtown/) and the
statewide [Kentucky page](/locations/kentucky/).

## Earning capacity in a commuter market

A defensible opinion about a Radcliff worker has to describe the labor
market as the worker experiences it, not as a spreadsheet abstracts it.
The firm grounds that description in Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates for the Elizabethtown, KY
metropolitan area and for Kentucky statewide, supplemented by state
workforce agency labor market information and by direct employer research
when the referral questions call for it. Reports distinguish metro-level
findings from statewide ones and explain why each source applies to this
evaluee, so the analysis cannot be dismissed as either too broad to be
local or too local to be representative.

## Meeting Radcliff evaluees where they are

Purinton Analytics keeps no office in Radcliff and publishes no local
address, because accuracy about presence belongs to the same discipline
that governs its reports. Engagements run remote-first: records review,
counsel conferences, and most vocational interviews proceed by secure
video on the case schedule. When the questions require in-person
assessment, or when a deposition or trial date puts the expert in the
courtroom, travel is arranged from the Kansas City home office and the
evaluation itself is conducted in the evaluee's own community, where
transportation, daily routine, and actual job access can be observed
rather than assumed.
