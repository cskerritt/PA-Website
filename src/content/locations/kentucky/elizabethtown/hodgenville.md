## Hodgenville workers in a regional economy

Hodgenville is a small community whose residents look to the surrounding
Elizabethtown metro for much of their employment, and a vocational
opinion that ignores that geography misstates the evaluee's real options.
The firm analyzes what work remains open to the individual, given the
documented restrictions and the commute the person can actually sustain,
using Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage
estimates for the Elizabethtown, KY metropolitan area and for Kentucky
statewide, together with state workforce agency labor market information
and targeted employer research when a referral demands it. Where the
regional figures and the statewide figures diverge, the report says so
plainly and gives the reason one set fits this evaluee better than the
other. An opinion that papers over that tension invites the
cross-examination it could have prevented.

## Evaluation logistics for Larue County evaluees

The firm maintains no Hodgenville office and claims none; candor about
