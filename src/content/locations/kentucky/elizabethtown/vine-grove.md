## The labor market behind a Vine Grove opinion

Small-town residence inside a larger metropolitan economy is precisely the
setting in which careless vocational conclusions come apart. What matters
for a Vine Grove worker is not the total volume of jobs somewhere in the
region but the work realistically open to this person, with these
restrictions and this commute. The firm's findings rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the
Elizabethtown, KY metropolitan area and for Kentucky statewide, on state
workforce agency labor market information, and on direct labor market
research where the referral requires it. Every report explains the chain
of reasoning from source to conclusion so that opposing counsel can test
each link rather than argue with a summary.
