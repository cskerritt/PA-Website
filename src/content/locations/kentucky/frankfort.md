## How meetings and evaluations are handled

Frankfort meetings are by appointment. The firm maintains no staffed office
in the area and publishes no local street address; candor about presence
belongs in the same category as candor about methodology. In practice, most
of the work is remote. Records are reviewed, vocational interviews are
conducted by video, and reports and pre-deposition conferences are
completed on the schedule the litigation sets. When the referral questions
warrant an in-person evaluation, or when a deposition or trial setting
requires attendance, travel is arranged from the Kansas City home office
and confirmed with counsel against the case calendar. Evaluees can be seen
in their own community when travel would be a hardship.

## Labor market data behind the findings

Vocational findings for Frankfort area evaluees rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the area and for
Kentucky statewide, state workforce agency labor market information, and
direct labor market research where the referral requires it. No figure
enters a report without a stated source, and the definition of the
evaluee's commutable labor market is explained rather than assumed. That
same discipline applies when the assignment is to review another expert's
work: the analysis addresses what the data will and will not support, in
writing, so counsel can decide how to use it. Where the referral requires
it, transferable skills analysis and employer research are documented step
by step and retained in a file that can be produced in discovery.
