Expert deadlines do not shrink for smaller venues. A scheduling order
entered in a Paducah case sets disclosure, deposition, and trial dates as
firm as any entered in a large city, and counsel need an expert who treats
those dates as fixed. Purinton Analytics runs its Paducah practice on that
premise. Plaintiff and defense counsel across the Paducah, KY-IL
metropolitan area retain the firm for
[vocational expert evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) of
employability and earning capacity,
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure, each engagement scoped at intake so the work product
lands when the scheduling order says it must.

## Venues on the Kentucky and Illinois sides

On the Kentucky side, the metro takes in McCracken County, Ballard County,
Carlisle County, and Livingston County, and engagements are accepted for
matters venued in the Circuit Court of McCracken County and the circuit
courts of the neighboring counties. Across the line, the practice serves
counsel with matters arising in Massac County, Illinois. Federal matters
are accepted in the United States District Court for the Western District
of Kentucky, and cases venued in the United States District Court for the
Eastern District of Kentucky proceed through the same intake. In each
forum the opinion is built on a documented record and a stated
methodology, ready for examination by either side of the caption.

## Remote-first, with travel when it matters

The firm keeps no staffed office in Paducah and publishes no street
address for the location; counsel are told this directly rather than left
to infer it. The remote-first model carries most of the work: records
review and analysis, structured vocational interviews by secure video or
telephone, and standardized testing under publisher protocols where remote
administration is supported. When the referral questions require an
in-person evaluation of the evaluee, or when deposition and trial dates
require presence in the region, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City
home office with lead time built around the court's calendar rather than
the firm's convenience.

## Labor market evidence across the state line

Employability and earning capacity conclusions for Paducah area evaluees
rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage
estimates for the relevant Kentucky and Illinois areas and for each state
as a whole, on state workforce agency labor market information from the
state where the evaluee actually works, and on direct labor market
research where warranted. Life care plans and
[medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/) are priced
to the evaluee's own community using provider-specific inquiries and
published fee data, because care costs in far western Kentucky are not a
rounding of national averages and should never be treated as such.
