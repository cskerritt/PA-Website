When a Glasgow area case turns on what an injured or disabled person can
still earn, or on what their future care will actually require, the answer
should come from an expert whose conclusions rest on a record both sides
can examine. Purinton Analytics provides that work for Barren County and
Metcalfe County litigation through
[vocational evaluation and expert testimony](/services/vocational-expert-witness/)
and [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and offers
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. Plaintiff and defense referrals are handled with the
same methodology and the same reporting standard.

## The venue footprint

Engagements are accepted for matters in the Circuit Courts of Barren
County and Metcalfe County, the counties of the Glasgow micropolitan area,
and in the United States District Court for the Western District of
Kentucky and the United States District Court for the Eastern District of
Kentucky when the matter is federal. Referrals typically arise in personal
injury, workers' compensation, employment, and family law cases where
employability, earning capacity, or the cost of future care is in dispute.
Counsel evaluating an opposing expert's report can also engage the firm for
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/), and the
[Kentucky state page](/locations/kentucky/) sets out the statewide practice
behind the local work.

## Meetings, evaluations, and travel

Glasgow meetings are by appointment. The firm keeps no staffed office in
the area and publishes no local street address, because being accurate
about presence is part of the same discipline that opposing counsel will
probe in the report. Most engagements proceed remotely, with records
review, video interviews, and report preparation running on the schedule
the litigation sets. Where the referral questions call for an in-person
evaluation, or where a deposition or trial setting requires attendance,
travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office and confirmed with
counsel before deadlines tighten. Evaluees who cannot travel are seen in
their own community.

## How the labor market analysis is built

Vocational findings for Glasgow area evaluees rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the area and for
Kentucky statewide, state workforce agency labor market information, and
direct labor market research where warranted. The report identifies the
labor market the evaluee can reasonably reach, explains why it was defined
that way, and ties each conclusion to a cited source, keeping the analysis
open to examination instead of resting on the expert's say-so. When
transferable skills analysis or employer contact is part of the method, the
steps and results are documented in the file.

## Starting an engagement

Every matter opens with a documented conflict check, followed by a
defined scope and a records request tailored to the claims; the report
timeline is then set against the operative disclosure calendar rather
than left open-ended, so counsel know from the outset when the written
opinion will arrive. The same
team serves counsel across the country through the firm's
[national practice](/locations/nationwide/). Submit the caption or party
names through the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and note any
imminent disclosure, deposition, or trial dates.
