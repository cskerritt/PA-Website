## A small city measured against a regional market

Oak Grove is a small city within the Clarksville, TN-KY metropolitan area,
a regional economy whose principal city is Clarksville and whose formal
name joins Tennessee and Kentucky. For a vocational analysis, that setting
demands
precision rather than shortcuts. An opinion that scores only the
opportunities inside a small city risks understating what a particular
evaluee can realistically do; an opinion that credits the entire
metropolitan economy to every worker risks the opposite error. The firm
resolves that tension evaluee by evaluee, against the specific
restrictions, transferable skills, and work history in the record, using
Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for
the Clarksville, TN-KY metropolitan area and for Kentucky statewide, state
workforce agency labor market information, and direct labor market
research where the referral questions warrant it. Each source is cited
precisely enough for opposing counsel to pull it and check the work.

The firm keeps no office in Oak Grove and publishes no local address,
because candor about presence belongs to the same discipline that governs
its opinions. In practice, most of the engagement moves by secure video:
records review, conferences with counsel, and the majority of vocational
interviews, which spares an injured person unnecessary travel and keeps
costs proportionate for both sides. When the referral questions require
in-person evaluation, or when a deposition or trial appearance calls for
the expert in the room, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home
office and the evaluation is conducted in the evaluee's own community,
where daily function can be observed rather than inferred.
