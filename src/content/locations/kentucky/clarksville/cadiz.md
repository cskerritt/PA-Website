## A small community inside a metro economy

Cadiz is a small community, and that fact cuts in both directions in a
vocational analysis. Opportunity within Trigg County itself may be
genuinely limited for a worker with significant restrictions, yet the town
sits within the Clarksville, TN-KY metropolitan area, and jobs elsewhere
in that regional economy may or may not be realistically reachable
depending on the evaluee's restrictions, transportation, and work history.
The firm's opinions confront that tension directly instead of papering
over it, drawing on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and
wage estimates for the Clarksville, TN-KY metropolitan area and for
Kentucky statewide, on state workforce agency labor market information,
and on direct labor market research where the referral questions require
it. Nearby dockets in Hopkinsville and Oak Grove are served on the same
basis.

## Evaluating a Trigg County resident

Remote-first practice suits a community like Cadiz. Secure video handles
records conferences, counsel discussions, and most vocational interviews
without asking an injured person to travel, and it keeps expert costs in
proportion to the case. The firm has no office in Cadiz and claims none;
that candor about presence reflects the same standard applied to every
opinion it signs. Where in-person assessment is warranted, or where a
deposition or courtroom appearance requires attendance, travel originates
at the Kansas City home office and the evaluation is conducted in the
evaluee's own community, where the practical realities of daily function
and access to work can be seen firsthand.
