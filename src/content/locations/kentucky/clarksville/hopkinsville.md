## An employment base that faces both states

Hopkinsville functions as a regional employment center on the Kentucky side
of the Clarksville, TN-KY metropolitan area, which means a vocational
analysis here has to be honest in both directions: some evaluees work
within the community, others hold jobs that sit across the state line, and
the opinion must say which market actually applies to this person, with
these restrictions and this work history. To ground that judgment, the
firm relies on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage
estimates for the Clarksville, TN-KY metropolitan area and for Kentucky
statewide, supplemented by state workforce agency labor market information
and by direct labor market research when the referral questions call for
it. Sources are cited precisely enough for opposing counsel to pull and
verify them.

## Meeting and evaluation logistics

The firm maintains no staffed office in Hopkinsville and publishes no local
address, because accuracy about presence is part of the same discipline
that governs the reports. Records review, attorney conferences, and most
vocational interviews proceed by secure video, which keeps timelines short
and costs proportionate for both sides. When the referral calls for
in-person assessment, or when a deposition or trial date requires the
expert in the courtroom, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home
office, and the evaluation itself takes place in the evaluee's own
community, where daily function and actual commuting realities can be
observed rather than assumed.
