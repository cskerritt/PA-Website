## Venue context in Madison County

Berea lies in Madison County, and a state-law injury, employment, or
disability matter arising in town proceeds in the Circuit Court. Where a
claim carries federal jurisdiction, it proceeds in the United States
District Court for the Eastern District of Kentucky or in the United
States District Court for the Western District of Kentucky. Which court
controls a particular caption, and whether that venue imposes any
registration or qualification requirement on a testifying expert, is
confirmed during the conflict check and intake rather than assumed. The
report itself is drafted for either setting, with a methodology an opposing
expert can retrace step by step. The surrounding venue picture appears on
the [Richmond metro page](/locations/kentucky/richmond/), and the statewide
summary of Kentucky practice is on the [Kentucky page](/locations/kentucky/).

## How Berea fits the regional labor market

An earning-capacity opinion for a Berea evaluee has to answer a specific
question honestly: what work exists for this person, with these
restrictions, this education, and this work history, within the commuting
range that person can actually sustain. Berea's workers move through a
market that includes Richmond and the broader region, so the analysis
neither confines itself to the town limits nor assumes every job in the
metro is within reach. The foundation is qualitative and sourced: Bureau of
Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the
Richmond and Berea area and for Kentucky statewide, state workforce agency
labor market information, and direct labor market research when the
referral questions warrant contacting employers about the jobs the opinion
depends on. Each source is cited precisely enough for opposing counsel to
pull it and check the reasoning.

## Evaluating a Berea resident

The firm maintains no office in Berea and publishes no local address,
because claims about presence are held to the same standard as claims in a
report. Most stages of an engagement, records review, counsel conferences,
and the vocational interview, proceed by secure video, which keeps
timelines short and costs proportionate for both sides of the caption. When
the referral calls for in-person assessment, or when a deposition or trial
date requires the expert at the table, travel is arranged from the Kansas
City home office and the evaluation is conducted in the evaluee's own
community, where functional capacity and real commuting conditions can be
observed instead of inferred. Life care planning interviews follow the same
practice, meeting the evaluee where daily routines and caregiving actually
happen.
