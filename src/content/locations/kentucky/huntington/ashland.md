## The labor market from an Ashland vantage

An Ashland evaluee's realistic employment options are defined by the
Huntington-Ashland commuting market, not by the town alone, and a credible
opinion has to say what work remains open to this specific person, with
these restrictions and this work history, from this community. Vocational
findings for Ashland matters rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates for the Huntington-Ashland
metropolitan area and for Kentucky statewide, on state workforce agency
labor market information, and on direct labor market research where the
referral questions call for it. The report walks through the reasoning
that connects those sources to the evaluee, because a conclusion that
cannot show its path invites the cross-examination it should have
foreclosed.

## Meetings and evaluations in Ashland

The firm keeps no staffed office in Ashland and publishes no local street
address, because candor about presence belongs to the same discipline that
governs the written work. Records review, conferences with counsel, and
most vocational interviews proceed by secure video, which compresses
timelines and holds down cost for both sides of a case. When the referral
requires in-person assessment, or when a deposition or trial date puts the
expert in the room, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office
and the evaluation is conducted in the evaluee's own community, where
daily function and actual commuting realities can be observed rather than
assumed.
