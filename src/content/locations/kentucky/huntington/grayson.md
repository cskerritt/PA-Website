## What the regional market means for a Grayson worker

Grayson is a small community within the Huntington-Ashland, WV-KY-OH
metropolitan area, and an honest employability opinion must account for
both facts at once: the limited job base close to home and the wider
market a worker can realistically reach. The firm grounds that analysis in
Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for
the Huntington-Ashland metropolitan area and for Kentucky statewide, in
state workforce agency labor market information, and in direct labor
market research when the referral questions require it. Numbers never
substitute for reasoning in the written product; the opinion explains why
particular occupations are or are not open to this evaluee, given the
restrictions, the work history, and the commute each option would demand.

## Evaluation logistics from Grayson

The practice maintains no office in Grayson, and it does not pretend to.
Secure video carries most of the workload, from records conferences with
counsel to the bulk of vocational interviewing, which keeps costs
proportionate for parties on both sides. Where the referral calls for
in-person evaluation, the expert comes to the evaluee's own community
rather than summoning an injured person to a distant office, with travel
scheduled from the Kansas City home office. Deposition and trial
appearances follow the same travel practice when the case calendar
requires them.
