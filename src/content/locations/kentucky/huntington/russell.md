Expert work runs on the litigation calendar, and a Russell engagement is
scheduled backward from the dates that cannot move: the expert disclosure
deadline, the discovery cutoff, the trial setting. Purinton Analytics
scopes each Russell referral so that the records request, the evaluation,
and the written report land ahead of those dates with room for counsel to
prepare. On that footing, lawyers for injured plaintiffs and lawyers
defending claims retain the firm for
[vocational expert opinions](/services/vocational-expert-witness/), for
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) when injuries carry
lifelong care needs, and for
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Venue for Greenup County matters

Russell sits in Greenup County, where a state-law claim proceeds in the
Circuit Court. When federal jurisdiction is in play, the forums Kentucky
offers are the United States District Court for the Eastern District of
Kentucky and the United States District Court for the Western District of
Kentucky; the district that actually governs, and any registration or
qualification requirement that comes with it, is pinned down during the
conflict check and intake. Each forum brings its own disclosure schedule
and testimony expectations, which is exactly why the calendar work above
starts with the venue question. The wider market context is set out on the
[Huntington metro page](/locations/west-virginia/huntington/), and the
state-level practice on the [Kentucky page](/locations/kentucky/).

## Grounding opinions in the regional market

A Russell evaluee competes for work across the Huntington-Ashland
metropolitan area, and the vocational opinion must reflect that market
rather than an abstraction. The firm draws on Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates for the Huntington-Ashland
metropolitan area and for Kentucky statewide, on state workforce agency
labor market information, and on direct labor market research when the
referral questions justify the added step. What separates a defensible
opinion from a vulnerable one is the chain of reasoning between those
sources and this particular worker, so the report documents that chain
explicitly instead of asking the reader to trust it.

## Where and how evaluations happen

No Purinton Analytics office exists in Russell, and none is claimed;
stating that plainly is part of the same accuracy the reports practice.
The efficient path for most tasks is secure video, which covers records
review sessions, conferences with counsel, and most vocational
interviews without imposing travel costs on either party. In-person
evaluation, when the referral warrants it, takes place in the evaluee's
own community so that daily function and commuting realities are observed
firsthand, with travel arranged from the Kansas City home office. The
same arrangements cover depositions and trial testimony, booked as soon
as the dates are certain rather than left to the end of discovery.
