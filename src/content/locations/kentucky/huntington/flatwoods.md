## Where a Flatwoods case is heard

Flatwoods lies in Greenup County, so a state-law claim arising here
proceeds in the Circuit Court. If the matter belongs in federal court,
Kentucky offers the United States District Court for the Eastern District
of Kentucky and the United States District Court for the Western District
of Kentucky as its forums; which one controls, and what registration or
qualification requirements attach, is settled during the conflict check
and intake rather than presumed. The firm treats venue as a fact to
confirm, not a detail to guess, and the same habit runs through the
citations in its reports. Counsel weighing the regional picture can start
from the [Huntington metro page](/locations/west-virginia/huntington/) or
the statewide overview on the [Kentucky page](/locations/kentucky/).

## Commuting patterns and the employability question

For a Flatwoods evaluee, the employability question is answered against
the commuting market of the Huntington-Ashland metropolitan area, since a
small community's own job base rarely defines what a worker can actually
reach. That framing is grounded in Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational
employment and wage estimates for the Huntington-Ashland metropolitan area
and for Kentucky statewide, supplemented by state workforce agency labor
market information and, where the questions warrant it, direct contact
with employers in the relevant occupations. The written opinion states not
just the conclusion but the route to it, so that an opposing expert can
retrace each step and a jury can follow the logic without taking anything
on faith.

## Serving the evaluee where they live

There is no Purinton Analytics office in Flatwoods, and the firm says so
plainly rather than implying a storefront it does not keep. Most of the
work travels well over secure video: records analysis, attorney
conferences, and the majority of vocational interviews. What does not
travel is the evaluee's daily reality, so when in-person assessment is
warranted the appointment happens in the evaluee's own community, with
travel arranged from the Kansas City home office. The same travel
practice covers depositions and trial testimony when the calendar demands
the expert's presence, and appearance logistics are confirmed with counsel
well before the date arrives.
