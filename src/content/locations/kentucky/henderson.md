The Henderson practice is built remote-first. Records review, vocational
interviews by video, written reporting, and pre-deposition conferences all
proceed on the litigation calendar without travel driving the timeline,
which is how a national firm serves a market it does not live in without
cutting corners. On that foundation, Purinton Analytics offers Henderson
area counsel
[vocational evaluation and expert testimony](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Where the work is venued

Engagements are accepted for matters in the Circuit Courts of Henderson
County and Webster County, the counties that make up the Henderson
micropolitan area, and in the United States District Court for the Western
District of Kentucky and the United States District Court for the Eastern
District of Kentucky when a matter is filed federally. Referrals come from
plaintiff and defense counsel in personal injury, workers' compensation,
employment, and family law cases where employability, earning capacity, or
the cost of future care is disputed. Counsel can also refer an opposing
expert's report for [rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/).
The [Kentucky state page](/locations/kentucky/) covers the statewide
practice in which the Henderson work sits.

## Appointments, travel, and candor about presence

Henderson meetings are by appointment. The firm keeps no staffed office in
the area and publishes no local street address; saying so plainly is part
of the same discipline that governs the reports. When the referral
questions call for an in-person evaluation, or when deposition and trial
settings require attendance, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home
office and coordinated with counsel against the case calendar. Evaluees who
cannot reasonably travel are seen in their own community, and attorney
conferences are held by video or phone whenever that serves the schedule
better than a trip does.

## Labor market research for Henderson evaluees

Vocational findings for evaluees in the Henderson area rest on Bureau of
Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the area
and for Kentucky statewide, state workforce agency labor market
information, and direct labor market research where the referral warrants
it. The report states which labor market the evaluee can reasonably reach,
explains why that market was chosen, and cites every source relied upon, so
the opinion can be tested by the other side rather than merely asserted.
Where a transferable skills analysis or employer survey is performed, the
method and results are documented in the file and available for production.
Care-planning recommendations follow the same rule: each item is tied to
the medical record and to an identified cost source rather than asserted.

## Starting an engagement

An engagement opens with a documented conflict check, a written scope, and
a records request tailored to the claims; the report timeline is then set
against the operative disclosure deadlines rather than left open-ended.
The same team and the same standards are available to counsel nationwide
through the firm's [national practice](/locations/nationwide/). To begin,
[request a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) with the case caption and
parties, noting any imminent disclosure, deposition, or trial dates.
