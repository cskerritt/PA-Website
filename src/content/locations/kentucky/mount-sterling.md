The Mount Sterling micropolitan area gathers Bath County, Menifee County,
and Montgomery County into a single market, and Purinton Analytics serves
counsel across that whole footprint through a single engagement process. The firm
provides
[vocational evaluation and expert testimony](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/) for matters
throughout the area, and offers
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. Plaintiff and defense counsel receive the same
methodology, and every report is written for the reader most likely to
attack it.

## Circuit and federal venues served

Engagements are accepted for matters in the Circuit Courts of Bath County,
Menifee County, and Montgomery County, and in the United States District
Court for the Eastern District of Kentucky and the United States District
Court for the Western District of Kentucky when the action is federal.
Typical referrals involve personal injury, workers' compensation,
employment, and family law disputes in which employability, earning
capacity, or the cost of future care must be established or challenged.
The [Kentucky state page](/locations/kentucky/) describes the statewide
practice these engagements belong to, including how the firm handles
qualification questions during intake.

## Presence in the area, stated accurately

Mount Sterling meetings are by appointment. The firm has no staffed office
in any of the area's counties and publishes no local street address, and it
says so plainly: accuracy about presence is part of the same discipline
that governs the opinions themselves. Day to day, the work runs remotely.
Records are reviewed, vocational interviews are conducted by video, and
reports and pre-deposition conferences are completed on the litigation
schedule. When the referral calls for an in-person evaluation, or when
deposition or trial dates require attendance, travel is scheduled from the
Kansas City home office in coordination with counsel, and evaluees who
cannot travel are seen in their own community.

## Labor market foundations

Employability and earning capacity findings for evaluees in the Mount
Sterling area rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment
and wage estimates for the area and for Kentucky statewide, state
workforce agency labor market information, and direct labor market
research where the referral warrants it. The report defines the labor
market the evaluee can reasonably reach across the area's counties,
explains that definition, and cites each source, so the analysis can be
examined line by line. The same evidentiary discipline carries into
care-planning work, where recommendations are tied to the medical record
and to identified cost sources, and into rebuttal assignments, where the
question is whether an opposing expert's data actually supports the
conclusions drawn from it.

## Starting an engagement

Every engagement begins with a documented conflict check, a defined scope,
and a records request tailored to the claims, with the report timeline set
against the operative disclosure deadlines. Counsel with matters elsewhere
can engage the same team through the firm's
[national practice](/locations/nationwide/). To begin,
[request a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) with the case caption and
parties, and note any imminent disclosure, deposition, or trial dates.
