## Scott County venue and the courts beyond it

A claim under Kentucky law arising in Georgetown proceeds in the Circuit
Court serving Scott County. Where a matter belongs in federal court, the
forums within the state are the United States District Court for the
Eastern District of Kentucky and the United States District Court for the
Western District of Kentucky; which court controls a particular case, and
any registration or qualification requirement that comes with it, is
confirmed during the conflict check and intake. The report is written the
same way for either setting: a methodology section an opposing expert can
retrace, sources cited precisely enough to be pulled and checked, and
conclusions that stop where the record stops. The regional venue picture
appears on the [Lexington metro page](/locations/kentucky/lexington/),
and the statewide summary on the [Kentucky page](/locations/kentucky/).

## Reading the labor market for a Scott County evaluee

Georgetown is among the larger communities in the Lexington-Fayette, KY
metropolitan area, and a Georgetown resident's realistic job search runs
across that regional market rather than stopping at the city limits. The
firm grounds its findings in Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational
employment and wage estimates covering the Lexington-Fayette metropolitan
area and Kentucky statewide, supplements them with state workforce agency
labor market information, and conducts direct labor market research when
the referral questions warrant it. The written opinion then explains why
those sources describe work this particular evaluee can realistically
obtain, given the restrictions, transferable skills, and work history the
record establishes. That explanatory step is what separates an opinion
that survives cross-examination from a table of statistics that does not.

## Meeting and evaluating in Scott County

The firm maintains no office in Georgetown and says so plainly; candor
about presence is part of the same discipline that governs the reports.
Most of the work travels well: records review, counsel conferences, and
the majority of vocational interviews are conducted by secure video,
which keeps costs proportionate for both sides. When an in-person
evaluation is warranted, or when a deposition or trial date requires the
expert in the room, travel is arranged from the Kansas City home office
and the evaluation is held in the evaluee's own community, where daily
function and practical access to work can be observed rather than
assumed.
