the damages claim lives in a small Woodford County community, and the

## The Woodford County setting

State-law claims arising in Versailles proceed in the Circuit Court
serving Woodford County. When a matter belongs in federal court, the
forums within Kentucky are the United States District Court for the
Eastern District of Kentucky and the United States District Court for the
Western District of Kentucky; the venue that controls, along with any
qualification requirement particular to it, is settled during the
conflict check and intake. The firm's reports do not change register
between forums. Each one is written for the reader who wants it to fail:
methodology stated, sources checkable, and the limits of the data
acknowledged on the page. Broader regional context is collected on the
[Lexington metro page](/locations/kentucky/lexington/), and the statewide
practice is described on the [Kentucky page](/locations/kentucky/).

## A small town inside a large market

Versailles is a small community, but its labor market is not the town
itself; residents work throughout the Lexington-Fayette, KY metropolitan
area, and an employability analysis must reflect that reach without
overstating it. The firm draws on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational
employment and wage estimates for the Lexington-Fayette metropolitan area
and for Kentucky statewide, on state workforce agency labor market
information, and on direct labor market research when a referral question
turns on specific hiring practice. The analytical task is honesty in both
directions: neither treating a Woodford County resident as locked inside
a small-town job market, nor assuming every metro opportunity is
practically available to a person with documented restrictions and a
particular work history.

## How a Versailles evaluation proceeds

Much of the engagement runs remotely by design: secure video interviews,
electronic records handling, and conferences with counsel scheduled
around litigation deadlines. The firm has no office in Versailles and
never implies one; by-appointment service, described candidly, is the
model for every location outside the home base. When the referral calls
for face-to-face assessment, or when deposition or trial testimony
requires physical presence, the expert travels from the Kansas City home
office, and in-person evaluations are conducted in the evaluee's own
community, where the realities of daily function and access to work can
be seen firsthand.
