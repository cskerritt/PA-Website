## Jessamine County and the available forums

Claims under Kentucky law arising in Nicholasville proceed in the Circuit
Court serving Jessamine County. On the federal side, the United States
District Court for the Eastern District of Kentucky and the United States
District Court for the Western District of Kentucky are the forums
available within the state; the controlling venue for a given dispute,
together with any venue-specific qualification requirement, is identified
during the conflict check and intake process. Reports are drafted so they
hold up in whichever courtroom the case lands: assumptions stated,
methods disclosed, and every source citation complete enough for opposing
counsel to verify. Counsel with matters spread across the region can
consult the [Lexington metro page](/locations/kentucky/lexington/) and
the [Kentucky state page](/locations/kentucky/) for the wider footprint.

## Earning capacity against the metro market

A Nicholasville evaluee rarely works only in Nicholasville. The city sits
inside the Lexington-Fayette, KY metropolitan area, so a defensible
employability opinion has to weigh access to the broader regional market
as well as work available close to home. The firm relies on Bureau of
Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the
Lexington-Fayette metropolitan area and for Kentucky statewide, on state
workforce agency labor market information, and on direct employer
research where the questions call for it. What the report never does is
recite raw statistics without connecting them to the evaluee's
restrictions, education, and vocational history; the connective reasoning
is the opinion, and it is written so that both retaining and opposing
counsel can follow every step. The same standard governs rebuttal work:
an opposing report is measured against the data it claims to rest on, not
against rhetoric.

## Seeing the evaluee, not just the file

Purinton Analytics keeps no Nicholasville office and does not suggest
otherwise. The working default is remote: secure video for interviews and
counsel conferences, electronic records exchange, and telephone
availability between deadlines. Remote-first does not mean records-only.
When the referral requires an in-person vocational evaluation, or when
testimony must be given live, the expert travels from the firm's Kansas
City home office and conducts the evaluation in the evaluee's own
community, where functional claims can be checked against observable
daily life.
