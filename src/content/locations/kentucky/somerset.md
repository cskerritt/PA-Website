Purinton Analytics brings its full scope of work to Somerset and the rest
of Pulaski County:
[vocational evaluation and expert testimony](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/),
[medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/), and
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) of opposing
expert reports. Where a complete damages presentation is needed, counsel
may structure
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. The practice accepts referrals from plaintiff and
defense firms alike, and each report is written to withstand examination by
whichever side did not commission it.

## Pulaski County venue context

Somerset engagements center on matters in the Pulaski County Circuit Court,
and federal matters are supported in the United States District Court for
the Eastern District of Kentucky and the United States District Court for
the Western District of Kentucky, according to where the case is pending.
Typical referrals involve personal injury, workers' compensation,
employment, and family law disputes in which employability, earning
capacity, or the cost of future care is at issue. Because the Somerset
micropolitan area is a single-county market, venue questions tend to be
straightforward; the analysis itself receives the same depth as it would in
any larger metro. The [Kentucky state page](/locations/kentucky/) describes
the statewide practice that stands behind the local work.

## By-appointment presence, stated plainly

Somerset meetings are by appointment. The firm keeps no staffed office in
Pulaski County and publishes no local street address, because honesty about
presence is part of the discipline that opposing counsel will look for in
the report itself. The working model is remote-first: records review,
video interviews, and written reporting proceed on the litigation calendar
without waiting on travel. When the referral calls for an in-person
evaluation, or when a deposition or trial date requires attendance, travel
is scheduled from the Kansas City home office with enough lead time to
protect the operative deadlines. An evaluee who cannot travel can be
evaluated in their own community.

## Grounding opinions in the local labor market

Employability and earning capacity findings for Somerset evaluees rest on
Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for
the area and for Kentucky statewide, state workforce agency labor market
information, and direct labor market research where warranted. The report
defines the evaluee's commutable labor market and explains the basis for
that definition, identifies every data source relied upon, and keeps the
underlying file in a form that can be produced in discovery. Qualitative
conclusions are tied to the data rather than to impressions, which is what
allows the same methodology to serve either side of the caption.

## Starting an engagement

Every matter opens with a documented conflict check, a defined scope, and a
records request tailored to the claims, with the report timeline set
against the disclosure calendar. Counsel with matters beyond Kentucky can
engage the same team through the firm's
[national practice](/locations/nationwide/). To begin,
[request a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) with the case caption and party
names, and flag any imminent disclosure, deposition, or trial dates.
