A vocational report filed in an Owensboro case will be read most carefully
by the lawyer who did not commission it. Purinton Analytics writes for that
reader. Plaintiff and defense counsel across the Owensboro metropolitan
area retain the firm for
[earning capacity and employability evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) in catastrophic injury
matters, and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic

together under an unfriendly reading.

## Daviess County and McLean County courts

The Owensboro metro consists of Daviess County and McLean County, and
engagements are accepted for matters venued in the Circuit Court of
Daviess County and the Circuit Court of McLean County. Federal cases are
accepted in the United States District Court for the Western District of
Kentucky, and matters venued in the United States District Court for the
Eastern District of Kentucky are handled through the same intake. State or
federal, the opinions carry the same architecture: a documented record, a
disclosed methodology, and conclusions that either side can trace and
test.

## Presence in Owensboro, stated accurately

The firm has no staffed office in Owensboro and publishes no street
address for this location. Overstating presence would sit poorly beside
reports that demand precision from everyone else's numbers, so the firm
does not do it. Engagements generally begin remotely, with records review,
structured vocational interviews conducted by secure video or telephone,
and standardized testing administered under publisher protocols where
remote administration is supported. When an in-person evaluation is the
better instrument, or when a deposition or trial date requires attendance,
travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office and locked to the
litigation calendar. Counsel deal with the same point of contact from the
records request to the stand, and the report standard stays constant
whether the work happens remotely or in the courtroom's own county.

## Western Kentucky labor market research

Vocational findings for Owensboro area evaluees are grounded in Bureau of
Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the
Owensboro metropolitan area and for Kentucky statewide, in state workforce
agency labor market information, and in direct labor market research when
the referral questions warrant the extra step. Every labor market fact in
a report carries a citation an opposing expert can check. Life care plans
and [medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/) are
priced to the evaluee's own market through provider-specific inquiries and
published fee data for the region, not lifted from national tables that no
local provider follows.
