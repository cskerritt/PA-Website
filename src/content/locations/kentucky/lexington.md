Counsel in Lexington retain vocational experts for a familiar set of
questions: what work an injured person can still perform, what that work
pays, and what it will cost to care for someone whose injuries are
permanent. Purinton Analytics answers those questions for plaintiff and
defense counsel across the Lexington-Fayette metropolitan area through
[earning capacity and employability evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Central Kentucky venues

The metro's counties are Fayette County, Bourbon County, Clark County,
Jessamine County, Scott County, and Woodford County, and engagements are
accepted for matters venued in the Circuit Court of Fayette County and in
the circuit courts of the surrounding counties. Federal matters proceed in
the United States District Court for the Eastern District of Kentucky, and
the firm also accepts cases venued in the United States District Court for
the Western District of Kentucky when a Lexington area party or evaluee is
involved. In every forum, the written opinion is built to the same
standard: a documented record, a stated methodology, and conclusions that
follow from evidence both sides can examine.

## How the firm appears in Lexington

Lexington meetings are by appointment. There is no staffed local office and
no published street address here, and the firm says so plainly rather than
implying a presence it does not keep. The working pattern is remote-first:
medical and employment records are reviewed, the vocational interview is
conducted by secure video or telephone, and standardized testing is
administered under publisher protocols where remote administration is
supported. In-person evaluation, deposition testimony, and trial appearance
are scheduled as the case requires, with travel arranged from the Kansas
City home office. Counsel dealing with compressed disclosure deadlines
should raise them at intake so the schedule can be built around them.
Wherever the work is performed, counsel receive the same report standard
and the same point of contact from records request through testimony.

## Grounding opinions in the Lexington labor market

Employability and earning capacity conclusions for central Kentucky
evaluees rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and
wage estimates for the Lexington-Fayette metropolitan area and for Kentucky
statewide, on state workforce agency labor market information, and on
direct labor market research when the referral questions call for it. Life
care plans and [medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/)
for area residents are priced to the evaluee's own market using
provider-specific inquiries and published fee data for the region, because
a plan priced to a national average is an invitation to cross-examination.

## Beginning an engagement

An engagement here starts with a conflict check, not a consultation. Send the case
caption or the party names through the
[conflict check referral form](/refer-a-case/), together with a short
description of the claims and any dates already set for disclosure,
deposition, or trial. Matters arising elsewhere in the state are covered on
the firm's [Kentucky overview](/locations/kentucky/), and engagements that
span jurisdictions are handled as described on the
[nationwide practice page](/locations/nationwide/).
