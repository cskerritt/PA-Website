## Webster County venue and the courts that hear Providence cases

Providence sits in Webster County, and state court matters arising there are heard in the Circuit Court. Federal question and diversity matters from Kentucky proceed in the United States District Court for the Eastern District of Kentucky or the United States District Court for the Western District of Kentucky; the controlling district and division for a particular Providence case are identified during the conflict check and intake rather than assumed. Reports are drafted to the same standard in either forum: a stated methodology, sources listed, and conclusions that follow from the record, so the opinion reads the same way to the retaining attorney and to opposing counsel. Any venue-specific registration or qualification requirement is likewise identified and confirmed during the conflict check and intake process.

## Reading a small labor market honestly

A Providence evaluee does not work in an abstract national economy. Earning capacity questions turn on the work that realistically exists for this evaluee, in and around this evaluee's own community, and on what the residual capacities documented in the record actually allow. Vocational findings rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the relevant Kentucky areas and for the state as a whole, on state workforce agency labor market information, and on direct labor market research where the published estimates are too coarse for a community of this size. The report says which sources were used and why, and it does not stretch broader-area data to fit a small-town evaluee without saying so. When damages require an economic loss calculation, the firm offers [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement structure.

## Evaluating a Providence resident

The firm keeps no office in Providence and publishes no local street address, because accuracy about presence is part of the same discipline that governs its reports. What counsel gets instead is flexible logistics built around the evaluee. Records review, vocational interviews, and most [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) work proceed remotely, which spares the evaluee unnecessary travel and keeps costs proportionate to the case. When the referral questions call for in-person evaluation, the assessment is scheduled in or near the evaluee's own community, and when deposition or trial dates require presence, travel is arranged from the Kansas City home office.
