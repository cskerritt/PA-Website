For an injured person living in Independence, the most useful expert
evaluation is the one that comes to them. Independence is a residential
community within the Cincinnati metropolitan area, and its evaluees are
workers and families whose daily lives, treatment schedules, and caregiving
arrangements are anchored at home, not in the principal city. Purinton
Analytics builds its Independence engagements around that fact: remote
work wherever the case allows, and in-person assessment in the evaluee's
own community when the referral questions require it. Attorneys on either
side of the caption receive the same evaluation, documented the same way,
because a report written for one audience fails in front of the other.

## Where Independence matters are heard

Independence sits in Kenton County, and its civil matters proceed in the
Circuit Court, Kentucky's trial court for the county. When a claim
presents a federal question or otherwise belongs in federal court, the
United States District Court for the Eastern District of Kentucky and the
United States District Court for the Western District of Kentucky are the
available forums; the controlling venue, and any venue-specific
qualification requirement that comes with it, is identified and confirmed
during the conflict check and intake process. Nothing about the analysis
bends to the forum. The same records discipline, the same sourcing, and
the same willingness to say what the data do not show apply whether the
opinion is filed in state or federal court. The surrounding metro practice
is described on the [Cincinnati metro page](/locations/ohio/cincinnati/),
and statewide engagements on the [Kentucky page](/locations/kentucky/).

## Reading the labor market from a residential suburb

A suburb's labor market is borrowed from its region. Independence workers
participate in an employment market that spans Ohio, Kentucky, and
Indiana, which is precisely why the geographic frame of a vocational
opinion must be chosen deliberately rather than by default. Findings for
Independence evaluees rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational
employment and wage estimates for the Cincinnati metropolitan area and for
Kentucky statewide, with state workforce agency labor market information
and direct labor market research added where the questions warrant. The
report explains which geography governs each conclusion, so a
cross-examiner looking for an unstated assumption will not find one.

## Evaluations in the evaluee's own community

The firm maintains no office in Independence and lists no local address;
by-appointment service is stated plainly because overstating presence
would undercut the credibility the reports depend on. Records review and
analysis proceed remotely, interviews are held by secure video when
appropriate, and when an in-person functional or vocational assessment is
needed, travel is arranged from the Kansas City home office so the
evaluation happens where the evaluee actually lives. That setting matters
most in catastrophic injury cases, where counsel typically pair
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) with an
[earning capacity evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/), and
often structure [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.
