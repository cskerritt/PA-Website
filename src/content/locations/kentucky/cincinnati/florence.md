The question a Florence vocational report has to answer is where a Boone
County worker can realistically be employed after an injury, and the honest
answer rarely stops at the city limits. Florence workers compete in a
commuting market that reaches across the Cincinnati metropolitan area, and
a placeability opinion that ignores that reach, in either direction,
invites impeachment. Purinton Analytics prepares
[earning capacity and employability evaluations](/services/vocational-expert-witness/)
for Florence matters that define the evaluee's labor market explicitly,
document the commuting assumptions, and let both sides test them. The
practice serves plaintiff and defense referrals alike, and the report reads
the same way no matter which side signed the retention letter.

any venue-specific registration or qualification requirement, is identified

## Reading the commuting market honestly

Because the metropolitan area spans Ohio, Kentucky, and Indiana, the data
question in a Florence case is which geography actually describes this
evaluee. Vocational findings rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates for the Cincinnati metropolitan
area and for Kentucky statewide, alongside state workforce agency labor
market information and direct labor market research where the referral
calls for it. The report states, occupation by occupation, whether the
metro figures or the statewide figures better describe the evaluee's
realistic market and why, so the transferable skills analysis lands on
jobs a Florence worker could actually reach rather than a statistical
abstraction. That transparency is deliberate: an opinion whose geographic
assumptions are explicit is harder to distort and easier to defend.

## How evaluations proceed for Florence evaluees

There is no staffed Purinton Analytics office in Florence, and the firm
publishes no local address, because it will not imply a presence it does
not maintain. Most engagements run remotely from the records request
through report delivery, with interviews by secure video where
appropriate. When the referral questions require an in-person assessment,
or when testimony dates demand attendance, travel is scheduled from the
Kansas City home office and the evaluation is conducted in the evaluee's
own community. In catastrophic injury matters, counsel frequently combine
the vocational work with [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/)
or retain [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.
