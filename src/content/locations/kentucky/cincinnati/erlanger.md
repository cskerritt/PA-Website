Most Erlanger referrals arrive with a calendar attached: an expert
disclosure deadline, a mediation date, a trial setting. Purinton Analytics
scopes its Erlanger engagements to that calendar from the first call,
because a vocational or life care planning opinion delivered after the
deadline that mattered is not an opinion at all. Intake establishes what
must be disclosed and when, the records request is tailored to reach the
documents that the timeline allows, and the written report is committed to
a schedule counsel can represent to the court. Plaintiff and defense
lawyers alike retain the firm on those terms, and the discipline is the
same regardless of which side is paying.

## Erlanger's county and courthouse context

Erlanger is a Kenton County community, and its civil litigation proceeds
in the Circuit Court, the Kentucky trial court serving the county. Claims
with a federal basis may instead belong in the United States District
Court for the Eastern District of Kentucky or the United States District
Court for the Western District of Kentucky; the forum that controls a
particular case, and any qualification requirement specific to that venue,
is identified and confirmed during the conflict check and intake process
rather than assumed. Forum choice shapes the disclosure schedule more than
it shapes the analysis: the report's methodology, sources, and limits are
stated the same way for every court. Work throughout the wider region is
described on the [Cincinnati metro page](/locations/ohio/cincinnati/), and
the firm's other Kentucky venues on the [Kentucky page](/locations/kentucky/).

## Labor market evidence for Erlanger evaluees

Erlanger workers earn their livings in a metropolitan labor market that
spans Ohio, Kentucky, and Indiana, and the evidentiary question is always
which slice of that market describes the person in the case. Vocational
conclusions rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and
wage estimates for the Cincinnati metropolitan area and for Kentucky
statewide, joined by state workforce agency labor market information and,
where the referral requires it, direct labor market research. Each report
identifies its geographic frame and defends the choice, because an
[earning capacity opinion](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) whose
data boundaries are explicit survives cross-examination, and one whose
boundaries are implied does not.

## Scheduling, travel, and remote work

There is no Purinton Analytics office in Erlanger, no storefront, and no
published local address; the firm states its by-appointment model plainly
rather than dressing it up. That model is also what makes tight schedules
workable. Records analysis and report drafting proceed remotely, secure
video handles most interviews, and when the case needs an in-person
evaluation or courtroom testimony, travel is planned from the Kansas City
home office around the dates that govern the case. In catastrophic injury
matters the timeline usually carries more than a single expert task:
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) alongside the
vocational work, and frequently
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.
