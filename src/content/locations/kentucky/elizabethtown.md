An Elizabethtown engagement starts the same way every Purinton Analytics
engagement starts: with a documented conflict check, a defined scope, and a
records request tailored to the claims. From that foundation, the firm
provides plaintiff and defense counsel in the Elizabethtown metropolitan
area with
[vocational expert evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) of
employability and earning capacity,
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) for catastrophically
injured evaluees, and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. Method comes before conclusions, in that order, on
every file.

## Hardin County and Larue County venues

The Elizabethtown metro consists of Hardin County and Larue County, and
engagements are accepted for matters venued in the Circuit Court of Hardin
County and the Circuit Court of Larue County. Federal matters are accepted
in the United States District Court for the Western District of Kentucky,
and cases venued in the United States District Court for the Eastern
District of Kentucky come through the same intake. The forum changes the
caption, not the work product: in each court the opinion rests on a
documented record and a methodology stated openly enough for the other
side to probe it.

## What by-appointment service means here

The firm maintains no staffed office in Elizabethtown and publishes no
street address for the location, a fact stated outright because accuracy
about presence is part of the credibility an expert brings to the stand.
Engagements ordinarily run remote-first. Records review, structured
vocational interviewing by secure video or telephone, and standardized
testing under publisher protocols where remote administration is supported
all proceed without travel. When the referral questions are better served
by an in-person evaluation, or when deposition and trial settings require
presence, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office and
coordinated with counsel well before the date arrives. Throughout the
engagement, counsel keep the same point of contact and receive the same
report standard regardless of where any step is performed.

## Sourcing the labor market data

Vocational opinions for Elizabethtown area evaluees rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Elizabethtown
metropolitan area and for Kentucky statewide, on state workforce agency
labor market information, and on direct labor market research where the
questions in the referral call for it. No wage or employment figure enters
a report without a source that can be produced. Life care plans and
[medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/) follow the
same rule, priced to the evaluee's own market through provider-specific
inquiries and published fee data for the region rather than borrowed
national averages.

## The conflict check comes before everything

Because the practice serves both sides of the caption, no substantive
conversation happens before the conflict check clears. Counsel can
[request a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) by submitting the case caption
and party names, along with any imminent disclosure, deposition, or trial
dates. For matters arising elsewhere in the state, the firm's
[Kentucky overview](/locations/kentucky/) maps the broader venue coverage,
and the [nationwide page](/locations/nationwide/) describes how identical
standards apply wherever the case is pending.
