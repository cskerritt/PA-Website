The evaluee is the center of a vocational case, and for matters arising in
the Richmond-Berea area that person usually lives, works, and treats close
to home in Madison County or its neighbors. Purinton Analytics builds its
opinions around that reality, offering plaintiff and defense counsel
[earning capacity and employability evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. The evaluation comes to the evaluee's community; the
report is written for the courtroom.

## Madison County and surrounding venues

Engagements are accepted for matters venued in the Circuit Court of Madison
County and in the circuit courts of Estill County and Rockcastle County,
the counties that make up the Richmond-Berea micropolitan area. Federal
matters proceed in the United States District Court for the Eastern
District of Kentucky, and referrals venued in the United States District
Court for the Western District of Kentucky are accepted through the same
intake. The firm treats each of these forums identically: the written
opinion documents its record, discloses its methodology, and is drafted so
that opposing counsel can trace each conclusion back to evidence.

## Appointments, not an address

There is no staffed Purinton Analytics office in Richmond or Berea, and no
street address is published for this location. The firm states that
plainly because honesty about presence belongs to the same discipline that
governs its reports. In practice, most of the work happens remotely:
records are reviewed off-site, vocational interviews proceed by secure
video or telephone, and standardized testing is administered under
publisher protocols where remote administration is supported. Where the
referral questions call for an in-person evaluation of the evaluee, or
where a deposition or trial requires attendance, travel is scheduled from
the Kansas City home office and set against counsel's deadlines. Counsel
work with the same point of contact throughout, and the report standard
does not change with the location of the work.

## The labor market an area evaluee actually faces

Employability and earning capacity findings for Richmond area evaluees are
grounded in Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage
estimates for the relevant Kentucky areas and for the state as a whole,
supplemented by state workforce agency labor market information and by
direct labor market research when the referral questions warrant it. Life
care plans and [medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/)
are priced to the evaluee's own market with provider-specific inquiries and
published fee data for the region, since the cost and availability of care
in a smaller community rarely match national published averages.

## From referral to opinion

Work begins with a documented conflict check, a defined scope, and a
records request tailored to the claims. To start,
[request a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) with the case caption and the
parties, and identify any disclosure, deposition, or trial dates that are
already set. The firm's statewide availability is described on the
[Kentucky page](/locations/kentucky/), and counsel with matters outside the
state can consult the [nationwide coverage page](/locations/nationwide/)
for the same engagement process at national scale.
