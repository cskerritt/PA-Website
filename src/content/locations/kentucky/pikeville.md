Counsel who retain a vocational expert for a Floyd County or Pike County
matter are retaining a witness whose opinions will be tested by the other
side, and Purinton Analytics writes for exactly that audience. The firm
provides [vocational evaluation and expert testimony](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. Referrals come from plaintiff and defense firms alike,
and every report is written so that either side of the caption can examine
the record behind it.

## Courts serving the Pikeville area

Engagements are accepted for matters in the Circuit Courts of Floyd County
and Pike County and, when a case is filed federally, in the United States
District Court for the Eastern District of Kentucky or the United States
District Court for the Western District of Kentucky, wherever the matter is
pending. The work spans personal injury, workers' compensation, employment,
and family law disputes in which employability, earning capacity, or the
cost of future care is contested. Counsel who need an independent review
of an opposing expert's report can also engage the firm for
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/). The
[Kentucky state page](/locations/kentucky/) describes the statewide
practice; Pikeville engagements follow the same intake, the same
methodology, and the same reporting standards.

## Meetings by appointment

Pikeville meetings are by appointment. The firm keeps no staffed office in
the area and publishes no local street address, because accuracy about
presence is part of the same discipline that governs its reports. Most work
proceeds remotely: records review, vocational interviews by video, and
report preparation follow the litigation calendar rather than a travel
schedule. When the referral questions call for in-person evaluation, or
when deposition or trial dates require attendance, travel is scheduled from
the Kansas City home office and coordinated with counsel well ahead of the
operative deadlines. Evaluees who cannot travel can be seen in their own
community, and pre-deposition conferences are held by video or phone at
counsel's convenience.

## Labor market research for Pikeville evaluees

Employability and earning capacity findings for evaluees in the Pikeville
micropolitan area rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational
employment and wage estimates for the area and for Kentucky statewide,
state workforce agency labor market information, and direct labor market
research where warranted. The report identifies the evaluee's commutable
labor market and states why it was defined that way, so the opinion can be
examined on its own terms rather than taken on faith. Where transferable
skills analysis or employer contact is needed, the methodology section
documents the sources consulted and the steps taken, and the file is kept
in a form that can be produced when the other side asks for it.

## Starting an engagement

Every engagement begins with a documented conflict check, a defined scope,
and a records request tailored to the claims, and the report timeline is
set against the operative disclosure calendar rather than left open-ended.
The same structure supports counsel far beyond Kentucky through the firm's
[national practice](/locations/nationwide/). Submit the caption or party
names through the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and note any
imminent disclosure, deposition, or trial dates so scheduling can be
confirmed at the outset.
