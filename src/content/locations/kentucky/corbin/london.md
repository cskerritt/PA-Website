somewhere along the corridor that connects London with Corbin and the rest

## Laurel County venue and the courts that hear London cases

A state-law injury, employment, or disability matter arising in London
proceeds in the Circuit Court serving Laurel County. Claims carrying
federal jurisdiction may be heard in the United States District Court for
the Eastern District of Kentucky or the United States District Court for
the Western District of Kentucky; which district controls a given caption,
and whether that venue imposes any expert registration or qualification
requirement, is confirmed during the conflict check and intake rather than
assumed in the report. Opinions are drafted to the same standard for either
forum, with sources cited precisely enough that an opposing expert can
retrace every step. Counsel weighing the broader regional picture will find
it on the [Corbin metro page](/locations/kentucky/corbin/), with statewide
practice summarized on the [Kentucky page](/locations/kentucky/).

## Reading the Corbin area labor market from London

London workers participate in a labor market that extends well past the
town limits, and the analysis has to reflect that honestly in both
directions: it cannot confine an evaluee to openings inside London, and it
cannot assume every position in the region is within a sustainable commute
for a person with documented restrictions. The vocational foundation is
qualitative and verifiable: Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational
employment and wage estimates for the relevant area and for Kentucky
statewide, state workforce agency labor market information, and direct
labor market research where the referral questions call for contacting
employers about specific jobs. No figure enters the opinion without a
source opposing counsel can pull and check.

## How a London evaluation proceeds

The firm keeps no office in London and does not pretend otherwise; its home
office is in Kansas City, and that candor is part of the same discipline
that governs its reports. Records review, transferable skills analysis,
and counsel conferences are handled remotely on a schedule set by the
litigation calendar. When an in-person vocational interview or a life care
planning assessment is warranted, it is arranged in the evaluee's own
community, so the findings describe the person's actual circumstances
rather than a clinic setting far from home. Travel for evaluations,
depositions, and trial testimony is scheduled from the Kansas City home
office. The same model supports referrals far beyond Kentucky, as the
[nationwide practice page](/locations/nationwide/) describes.
