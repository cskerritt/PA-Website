## Knox County courthouse context

Barbourville are heard in the Circuit Court serving Knox County. Where a
claim supports federal jurisdiction, Kentucky's federal forums are the
United States District Court for the Eastern District of Kentucky and the
United States District Court for the Western District of Kentucky; the
district that controls a specific caption, together with any registration
or qualification requirement that venue imposes on a testifying expert, is
confirmed during the conflict check and intake. The written product does
not change with the forum: a methodology laid out step by step, every
source disclosed, and conclusions an opposing expert can attack on the

## The labor market beyond the Knox County line

Barbourville is a small community, and pretending its town limits define an
evaluee's employment prospects would misstate the market in whichever
direction happened to help the retaining party. Workers there move within a
regional economy oriented toward Corbin and the surrounding area, and an
honest earning-capacity opinion tests candidate occupations against that
commuting reality as well as against the evaluee's restrictions, education,
and work history. The evidentiary base is the Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates for the relevant area and for
Kentucky statewide, state workforce agency labor market information, and
direct labor market research when a specific job opportunity needs
verification. All of it is treated qualitatively and cited so that
opposing counsel can pull each source and check it.
