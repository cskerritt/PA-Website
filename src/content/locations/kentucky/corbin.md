Distance from a major metropolitan area should not lower the standard of
expert work a case receives. Counsel in the Corbin micropolitan area of
southeastern Kentucky retain Purinton Analytics on the same terms as
counsel anywhere else in the country: for
[vocational expert evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) of
employability and earning capacity, for
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) in catastrophic injury
matters, and for
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. Both plaintiff and defense referrals are welcome, and
the reports read the same way regardless of who sent the retainer.

## Venues across the Corbin area counties

The Corbin area takes in Whitley County, Knox County, Laurel County, and
Clay County. Engagements are accepted for matters venued in the circuit
courts of those counties, including the Circuit Court of Whitley County and
the Circuit Court of Laurel County, and in the United States District Court
for the Eastern District of Kentucky. Cases venued in the United States
District Court for the Western District of Kentucky are accepted through
the same intake process. Each opinion is written against the possibility of
cross-examination: the record is documented, the methodology is stated, and
the reasoning is laid out so that either side of the caption can test it.

## Serving the area without pretending to an office

Purinton Analytics keeps no staffed office in Corbin and publishes no
street address for this location. What counsel gets instead is a
remote-first practice built for exactly this kind of geography: records
review and analysis performed off-site, structured vocational interviews by
secure video or telephone, and standardized testing administered under
publisher protocols where remote administration is supported. When the
referral questions require an in-person evaluation, or when a deposition or
trial setting requires attendance, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City
home office with enough lead time to protect the case calendar. Counsel
keep the same point of contact and receive the same report standard from
records request through testimony, wherever the work is performed.

## Local labor market, national method

An earning capacity opinion for a southeastern Kentucky evaluee has to
reflect the work that actually exists there. Vocational findings rest on
Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for
the relevant Kentucky areas and for the state as a whole, on state
workforce agency labor market information, and on direct labor market
research where warranted. Life care plans and
[medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/) are priced
to the evaluee's own community through provider-specific inquiries and
published fee data, because care availability and cost in a rural region
cannot be assumed from national tables.

## Opening a Corbin area engagement

The practice opens every matter with a documented conflict check and a
defined scope. To begin, [request a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) with
the case caption and party names, and flag any imminent disclosure,
deposition, or trial dates. Counsel handling matters in other parts of the
state can review the firm's [Kentucky overview](/locations/kentucky/), and
its [national practice](/locations/nationwide/) page explains how the same
engagement structure travels to any venue.
