A vocational or life care planning opinion disclosed in a Billings courtroom
will be read most closely by the lawyer who did not commission it, and every
report this practice signs is written for that reader. Plaintiff and defense
counsel in the Billings metro retain Purinton Analytics for
[vocational expert evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) of
employability and earning capacity,
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) in catastrophic injury
matters, and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. The same disciplined approach used
[across Montana](/locations/montana/) applies to every Billings file.

## Venue footprint in the Billings metro

The Billings metropolitan area takes in Yellowstone County, Carbon County,
and Stillwater County, and the firm accepts matters venued in the District
Court in each of them. Federal matters proceed in the United States District
Court for the District of Montana. Because opinions rest on records,
standardized methodology, and published data rather than on local presence,
the same report format serves a state court jury trial and a federal
bench proceeding equally well, and counsel on either side of the caption
receive the same analysis from the same record.

## Meetings by appointment, not a storefront

There is no staffed Purinton Analytics office in Billings, and the firm
publishes no street address for this location, because accuracy about
presence is part of the same discipline that governs its reports. Most
engagements proceed remotely from intake through disclosure: secure records
transfer, structured vocational interviews by video, and standardized
testing administered under publisher protocols where remote administration
is validated. When the referral calls for in-person evaluation of an
evaluee, or when deposition and trial calendars require presence in a
Billings courtroom, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office and
planned around the court's dates rather than the firm's convenience.

## Labor market research for Billings evaluees

Employability and earning capacity findings for evaluees in the Billings
area rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage
estimates for the Billings metropolitan area and for Montana statewide,
supplemented by state workforce agency labor market information and direct
labor market research where the referral questions warrant it. That grounding
matters in a regional economy where an evaluee's realistic options may sit
in Billings itself or in the surrounding counties, and it allows the written
opinion to describe transferable skills and placement prospects in terms
opposing counsel can trace back to published sources rather than to
impression or anecdote.

## Starting a Billings engagement

Early retention protects the record: it allows the vocational interview,
testing, and labor market research to be completed before disclosure
deadlines compress the schedule, and it gives the life care plan time to
reflect treating-provider input rather than assumption. To begin, submit the
caption or party names through the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and note any imminent disclosure,
deposition, or trial dates. Counsel with matters beyond the Billings area
can review the firm's [nationwide practice](/locations/nationwide/), which
operates on the same remote-first model with travel scheduled wherever the
case requires it.
