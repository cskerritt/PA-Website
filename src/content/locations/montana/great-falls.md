## Cascade County and federal venues

The Great Falls metropolitan area consists of Cascade County, and the firm
accepts matters venued in the District Court there. Federal matters proceed
in the United States District Court for the District of Montana. The report
delivered is the same in either forum: a written opinion that identifies
the records reviewed, the instruments administered, the labor market data
consulted, and the reasoning connecting them, so that cross-examination
finds a documented trail rather than an undisclosed judgment call.

## Appointment-based service, honestly described

The firm has no staffed office in Great Falls and publishes no street
address for this location; overstating presence would be a strange habit
for a practice whose value lies in careful statements. Great Falls matters
are served by appointment. The typical engagement runs remotely, from
secure records transfer through vocational interviews by video and
standardized testing under publisher protocols where remote administration
is validated. When the referral questions require meeting the evaluee in
person, or when a deposition or trial puts the expert in a Cascade County
courtroom, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office with the
court's calendar as the fixed point. In-person evaluations can be arranged
in the evaluee's own community, which keeps the assessment grounded in the
setting where the evaluee actually lives and works.

## Wage and employment data for Cascade County

Employability and earning capacity findings for Great Falls evaluees are
grounded in Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage
estimates for the Great Falls metropolitan area and for Montana statewide,
along with state workforce agency labor market information and direct labor
market research, including employer contact where warranted, when the
referral questions require it. Because the plausibility of a vocational
opinion turns on whether the cited occupations actually exist in the
evaluee's labor market at the wages claimed, the analysis stays within the
coverage of the published data and identifies every source precisely enough
for opposing counsel to reproduce the lookup.
