## Belgrade in the Bozeman Labor Market

A Belgrade evaluee's earning capacity is analyzed against the labor market where that person actually works and seeks work, which for most Belgrade residents means the broader Bozeman commuting area anchored by Bozeman itself. Vocational findings rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the metropolitan area and for Montana statewide, supplemented by state workforce agency labor market information and direct labor market research where the referral questions warrant it. The analysis stays qualitative about the community and quantitative only where the published data supports it: transferable skills, occupational access, and placeability are assessed for the evaluee's specific work history, not for a generic worker. Where a claim requires economic damages, the firm offers [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement structure.
