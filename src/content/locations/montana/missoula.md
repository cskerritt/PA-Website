Missoula counsel referring an injury, employment, or disability matter can
place the vocational and the care-planning sides of the damages case with a
single practice, whichever side of the caption they represent. Purinton
Analytics serves the Missoula metro through
[vocational expert evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. Each engagement begins the way it does
[everywhere in Montana](/locations/montana/): a documented conflict check, a
defined scope, and a records request tailored to the claims.

## Courts served from Missoula referrals

The Missoula metropolitan area comprises Missoula County and Mineral County,
and the firm accepts matters venued in the District Court in both. Matters
filed federally proceed in the United States District Court for the District
of Montana. The work product does not change with the forum: the same
written opinion, grounded in records and published data, is prepared to
withstand cross-examination whether it is disclosed in a state court injury
case or a federal employment matter, and it is available to plaintiff and
defense counsel alike.

## How a Missoula engagement runs

Missoula meetings are by appointment. The firm maintains no staffed office
in the city and publishes no street address for this location; candor about
presence is part of the same standard that governs every report it signs.
In practice, most of the work happens at a distance: counsel transfer
records through a secure channel, the vocational interview is conducted by
video, and standardized instruments are administered under publisher
protocols where remote administration is validated. Where the referral
questions require seeing the evaluee in person, or where a deposition or
trial requires the expert in the room, travel is arranged from the Kansas
City home office and coordinated with the court's calendar well in advance.

## Grounding opinions in the local labor market

Vocational findings for evaluees in the Missoula area rest on Bureau of
Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Missoula
metropolitan area and for Montana statewide, along with state workforce
agency labor market information and direct labor market research where
warranted. The purpose is simple: an opinion about what an evaluee can earn
must describe the labor market that evaluee actually faces, not a national
abstraction. Every wage source and occupational classification cited in the
report is identified so that opposing counsel can check the analysis against
the same published data.

## Requesting a conflict check

The most useful time to retain a vocational expert or life care planner is
before disclosure deadlines begin to dictate the schedule, while there is
still room for records collection, testing, and treating-provider input. To
begin a Missoula engagement,
[request a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) with the case caption and
parties, and flag any dates already set for disclosure, deposition, or
trial. Counsel handling matters beyond the Missoula metro will find the
same model, and the same standards, described on the firm's
[nationwide page](/locations/nationwide/).
