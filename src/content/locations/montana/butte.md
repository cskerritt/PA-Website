The written opinion is the product here, and everything about a Butte
engagement is organized to make that opinion hold up: complete records
before conclusions, standardized instruments before impressions, published
labor market data before generalization. Purinton Analytics brings that
approach to Silver Bow County counsel through
[vocational expert evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. Butte referrals are handled within the firm's
[Montana practice](/locations/montana/) and accepted from plaintiff and
defense counsel on identical terms.

## Where Butte matters are heard

The Butte-Silver Bow area consists of Silver Bow County, and the firm
accepts matters venued in the District Court there. Cases filed in federal
court proceed in the United States District Court for the District of
Montana. The forum changes the procedural posture but not the work: in
either court, the disclosed report sets out its records, methods, and data
sources in enough detail that the opposing party can retrace the analysis
step by step, which is the only kind of vocational opinion worth putting in
front of a fact-finder.

## No local office, and no pretense of one

Butte meetings are by appointment. The firm maintains no staffed office in
the city and publishes no street address for this location; a report that
demands precision from medical records and wage tables should not be
introduced by a website that fudges geography. In practice, engagements
run remotely by default, through secure records transfer, vocational
interviews conducted by video, and standardized testing administered under
publisher protocols where remote administration is validated. When the
referral requires an in-person evaluation of a Silver Bow County evaluee,
or when deposition or trial testimony requires presence in the courtroom,
travel is arranged from the Kansas City home office around the dates the
court has set. Evaluations conducted in person are scheduled in the
evaluee's own community whenever practical, and remote deposition
appearances remain available where the parties and the court allow them.

## Occupational data behind Butte opinions

Vocational findings for Butte-area evaluees rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates covering the local
area and Montana statewide, together with state workforce agency labor
market information and direct labor market research where the referral
questions warrant it. Earning capacity conclusions are tied to occupations
the published data actually reports for the region, at wages the tables
actually show, and the report cites each source so that a challenge on
cross-examination becomes a comparison of the opinion against the data
rather than a contest of assertions.
