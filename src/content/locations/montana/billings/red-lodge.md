## Labor market analysis for a small community

A small community presents a distinct vocational question: the local employment base is thin, and an honest earning capacity opinion has to say so rather than paper over it. For a Red Lodge evaluee, the analysis considers the work realistically available in and around the community, the broader Billings metropolitan area market within commuting reach, and the transferability of the evaluee's skills between the two. Findings rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Billings metropolitan area and for Montana statewide, together with state workforce agency labor market information and direct labor market research, which carries particular weight where published estimates thin out at the community level. When the damages model requires economic projection, independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis under a [coordinated damages engagement](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/), with the vocational and care-planning work proceeding alongside it.

## Evaluation logistics without a local office

There is no Purinton Analytics office in Red Lodge, and the firm does not pretend otherwise; meetings happen by appointment because saying so plainly is part of the discipline counsel is hiring. Records review and interviews are handled remotely by secure video wherever that serves the evaluee, which spares an injured or disabled person the burden of traveling for an appointment. When the referral questions require it, the evaluator comes to the evaluee, conducting the in-person assessment in Red Lodge itself so that home, transportation, and daily-function observations reflect real conditions. That travel, like deposition and trial appearances, is scheduled from the Kansas City home office under the same remote-first model described on the [nationwide practice](/locations/nationwide/) page.
