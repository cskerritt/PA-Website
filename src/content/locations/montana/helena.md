The Helena metropolitan area takes in Lewis and Clark County, Jefferson
County, and Broadwater County, and Purinton Analytics accepts engagements
venued in the District Court in each of them, as well as in the United
States District Court for the District of Montana. Within that footprint the
firm provides plaintiff and defense counsel with
[vocational expert evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) of
employability and earning capacity,
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) in catastrophic injury
matters, and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## One standard across the metro's counties

Whether a matter sits in Lewis and Clark County, Jefferson County, or
Broadwater County, the engagement follows the same sequence the firm uses
[across Montana](/locations/montana/): a documented conflict check, a
defined scope, a records request tailored to the claims, and a written
opinion that discloses its methodology and sources. Nothing in the analysis
depends on which side issued the retention, and the report is drafted on the
assumption that the most careful reader will be opposing counsel. That
uniformity is deliberate: counsel who know what the firm's reports look like
in one county know what to expect in the next.

## Presence in Helena, stated plainly

Helena meetings are by appointment. The firm keeps no staffed office in the
city and publishes no street address for this location, because a practice
that audits other people's records for accuracy should describe its own
footprint accurately. Most engagements are conducted remotely from start to
finish: secure records transfer, vocational interviews by video, and
standardized testing administered under publisher protocols where remote
administration is validated. When an in-person evaluation is warranted, or
when deposition or trial testimony requires the expert in a Helena
courtroom, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office and planned
against the court's deadlines, not the other way around.

## Labor market evidence for Helena evaluees

Vocational opinions for evaluees in the Helena area rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Helena area
and for Montana statewide, alongside state workforce agency labor market
information and direct labor market research where the referral questions
call for it. Transferable skills conclusions are checked against the
occupations the regional data actually reports, and each source is cited
with enough specificity that either party can pull the same table and
verify the figure behind every finding. Where a claim would outrun the
published data, the report says so rather than filling the gap with
assumption.

## Getting a Helena matter underway

An engagement retained early can absorb slow records custodians, schedule
valid testing, and gather treating-provider input for the life care plan
without crowding disclosure deadlines; an engagement retained late gets an
honest assessment of what remains achievable on the calendar. To start,
[request a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) with the case caption and party
names, and note any imminent disclosure, deposition, or trial dates. The
same intake path serves counsel far beyond this metro; the firm's
[nationwide page](/locations/nationwide/) describes how the identical
model operates in venues across the country.
