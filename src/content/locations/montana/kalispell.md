## Flathead County courts

The Kalispell micropolitan area consists of Flathead County, and the firm
accepts matters venued in the District Court there. Matters filed federally
proceed in the United States District Court for the District of Montana.
Engagements are taken from plaintiff and defense counsel alike, and the
report reads the same way regardless of who signs the retention letter: a
statement of the records reviewed, the methodology applied, and the
published data relied upon, written so the opposing side can test every
step.

## Serving Kalispell without a storefront

There is no staffed Purinton Analytics office in Kalispell and no street
address published for this location. What counsel get instead is a process
designed for exactly this situation: secure records transfer at intake,
vocational interviews conducted by video, and standardized instruments
administered under publisher protocols where remote administration is
validated. Many files move from conflict check to disclosed report without
anyone boarding a plane, and remote deposition appearances are available
where the parties and the court allow them. When the referral questions
require evaluating a Flathead County resident in person, or when a
deposition or trial requires testimony in the courtroom, travel is
scheduled from the Kansas City home office with enough lead time to respect
the court's calendar, and the evaluation itself can take place in the
evaluee's own community rather than requiring travel from them.

## Labor market data for Flathead County

Employability and earning capacity opinions for Kalispell-area evaluees are
grounded in Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage
estimates covering the Kalispell area and Montana statewide, together with
state workforce agency labor market information and direct labor market
research where the referral warrants it. In a smaller regional labor market,
the difference between a national average and the local wage structure can
be the difference between a defensible opinion and an impeachable one, so
the analysis names its sources and keeps its conclusions inside what the
published data will support.

The engagement runs best when it starts before deadlines force choices:
early retention leaves room for complete records, valid testing, and

or party names through the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and
identify any disclosure, deposition, or trial dates already set. Counsel
with matters elsewhere can review the firm's
[nationwide practice](/locations/nationwide/), which extends this same
model to venues across the country.
