## Flathead County and its courts

Whitefish lies in Flathead County, and civil actions under Montana law are
heard there in the District Court. Where a claim carries a federal footing,
the United States District Court for the District of Montana is the forum
available within the state; the venue that will actually control a given
case, together with any registration or qualification requirement it
imposes on a testifying expert, is identified and confirmed during the
conflict check and intake. The firm writes every report to survive
whichever bench receives it: reproducible methods, cited sources, and
conclusions confined to the disclosed scope. Counsel working across the
region will find the broader coverage described on the
[Kalispell metro page](/locations/montana/kalispell/) and statewide
practice on the [Montana page](/locations/montana/).

## Reading the Whitefish labor market honestly

An earning-capacity opinion for a Whitefish evaluee has to answer a
threshold question before any transferable-skills analysis begins: which
labor market is genuinely open to this person? Vocational findings rest on
Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for
the Kalispell, MT metropolitan area and for Montana statewide, on state
workforce agency labor market information, and on direct employer contact
where the referral questions require it. The report states plainly whether
the analysis treats the evaluee as limited to work close to home, as able
to reach employment toward Kalispell, or as mobile beyond the region, and
it defends that framing with the evaluee's restrictions, transportation,
and documented work history rather than with assumption. That candor
serves whichever side retained the opinion, because a framing chosen for
convenience is the first thing a capable cross-examiner dismantles.

## How a Whitefish evaluee is assessed

Purinton Analytics keeps no office in Whitefish and does not imply one;
every meeting is by appointment, and being plain about presence is part of
the same discipline that governs the written work. Most engagements
proceed remotely, with secure video carrying records conferences,
discussions with counsel, and the majority of vocational interviews. When
the referral questions justify an in-person evaluation, it is performed in
the evaluee's own community, where daily function, home layout, and actual
access to services can be observed rather than inferred, with travel
scheduled from the Kansas City home office. Deposition and trial
appearances are planned the same way, around the dates the court has
already set, so that scheduling never becomes the reason an opinion
arrives late.
