Method comes before geography in this practice, and a Bozeman referral is
handled exactly like one from anywhere else: a documented conflict check, a
defined scope, a records request tailored to the claims, and a written
opinion built to be examined by the other side. Within that structure,
Purinton Analytics offers Bozeman counsel
[vocational expert evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) of
employability and earning capacity,
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) in catastrophic injury
matters, and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. The [Montana state page](/locations/montana/)
describes the statewide practice this metro work sits within.

## Gallatin County venues

The Bozeman metropolitan area consists of Gallatin County, and the firm
accepts matters venued in the District Court there as well as in the United
States District Court for the District of Montana. Plaintiff and defense
engagements are taken on the same terms, and the analysis does not bend to
the side that retained it: the report sets out the records reviewed, the
instruments administered, and the published data relied upon, so that
either side of the caption can trace every conclusion to its source.

## Appointment-based presence in Bozeman

The firm keeps no staffed office in Bozeman and publishes no street address
for this location. That is a deliberate choice: claiming a local office that
does not exist would sit poorly beside reports that demand precision from
everyone else's records. Bozeman meetings are therefore by appointment.
Records review, vocational interviews, and standardized testing under
publisher protocols proceed remotely wherever remote administration is
validated, which is how most engagements run from intake to disclosure.
When an in-person evaluation is warranted, or when deposition and trial
dates require the expert in a Gallatin County courtroom, travel is scheduled
from the Kansas City home office around the case calendar, with remote
deposition appearances available where the parties and the court permit
them.

## Reading the Bozeman labor market

Opinions about employability and earning capacity for Bozeman-area evaluees
are grounded in Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage
estimates for the Bozeman metropolitan area and for Montana statewide,
supplemented by state workforce agency labor market information and, where
the referral questions call for it, direct labor market research. A labor
market can shift faster than any single published table, which is why the
report identifies the vintage and coverage of every source used, and why
transferable skills analysis is run against the occupations actually present
in the regional data rather than against a generic national profile.

## Opening the file

Counsel get the most from a vocational expert or life care planner when
retention comes early enough for testing, records collection, and
treating-provider input to happen before disclosure deadlines arrive. Late
retention is still workable, but it narrows what the record can support,
and the report will say so candidly rather than paper over the gap. To
open a Bozeman engagement,
[request a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) with the case caption and party
names, and note any dates already on the court's calendar. For matters
beyond Montana, the firm's [nationwide practice](/locations/nationwide/)
operates on the same remote-first model with travel wherever the case
requires.
