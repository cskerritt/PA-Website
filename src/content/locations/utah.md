A vocational opinion in a Utah case turns on which Utah the evaluee lives
in: the labor market around Salt Lake City
looks nothing like the markets serving smaller communities such as
Vernal or Price, and an
earning capacity analysis that ignores the difference will not survive
cross-examination. Purinton Analytics prices every opinion to the evaluee's
own market. The firm accepts plaintiff and defense engagements throughout
Utah for [vocational expert](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) work,
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Utah's courts and communities

Engagements are accepted for matters in the District Court, Utah's trial
court of general jurisdiction, and in the United States District Court for
the District of Utah. A single federal district covers the state, so the
same written report serves a case wherever it is filed. Beyond the state
hub, the firm maintains dedicated pages for
Salt Lake City,
Provo-Orem, Ogden,
St. George, Logan,
Heber,
Brigham City,
Cedar City,
Vernal, and Price.
Counsel elsewhere in Utah are served identically, under the same
methodology and report standard.

## Remote-first service, by appointment

Purinton Analytics keeps no staffed office in Utah and publishes no street
address in the state. All meetings are by appointment, and the firm states
that plainly because accuracy about offices is part of the discipline that
opposing counsel will test in every other part of the file. Utah
engagements typically open remotely, with records review, structured
interviews, and standardized vocational instruments administered by secure
video under publisher protocols. Where the referral questions require
in-person evaluation, the evaluation can be conducted in the evaluee's own
community, and travel for evaluations, depositions, and trial appearances
is scheduled from the Kansas City home office against the dates confirmed
at intake. Labor market findings rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates for the relevant Utah
metropolitan area and for the state as a whole, supplemented by state
workforce agency labor market information and direct labor market research
where warranted. The [nationwide coverage page](/locations/nationwide/)
describes how the same model operates across the country.

## Qualification for Utah matters

Counsel weighing an out-of-state expert should be able to answer the
qualification question before signing an engagement letter. The firm's
board certifications are issued by national credentialing bodies and are
not limited to any single state, and admissibility in a Utah forum is
governed by that forum's own evidentiary standards. Any venue-specific
registration or qualification requirement is identified and confirmed
during the conflict check and intake process, together with the disclosure,
deposition, and trial dates that will govern delivery of the written
opinion.

## Opening a Utah engagement

Engagements from either side of the caption begin identically. Submit the
case caption or party names through the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and flag any imminent disclosure,
deposition, or trial settings. The firm responds with availability, CVs,
and fee information, and once the conflict check clears, a records
checklist tailored to the claims follows so the analysis can begin on a
complete record.
