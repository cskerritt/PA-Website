An expert opinion is only as useful as its ability to survive the other side's scrutiny. That standard, not geography, is what Mississippi counsel are actually retaining when they engage Purinton Analytics: vocational expert and life care planning work built for cross-examination, available to plaintiff and defense counsel throughout the state under a documented conflict check and a defined scope.

## Mississippi venues and metro coverage

Engagements are accepted for matters pending in the Circuit Court across Mississippi and in the United States District Court for the Northern District of Mississippi and the United States District Court for the Southern District of Mississippi. Personal injury, employment, family, and disability-related matters in these forums routinely turn on earning capacity, employability, and future care needs, and the firm's reports address those questions in language a fact-finder can follow and an opposing expert can test.

Service in the state's metropolitan areas is described on dedicated pages for Jackson, MS, Gulfport-Biloxi, MS, Hattiesburg, MS, Tupelo, MS, Meridian, MS, Laurel, MS, Oxford, MS, Columbus, MS, Starkville, MS, Picayune, MS, Natchez, MS-LA, McComb, MS, Vicksburg, MS, Greenville, MS, and Greenwood, MS. Matters in smaller communities are welcome on identical terms; the metro pages exist for local detail, not exclusion.

## How the firm works in Mississippi

Mississippi engagements follow the remote-first model of the firm's [nationwide practice](/locations/nationwide/). Records review, transferable skills analysis, labor market research, and report drafting proceed by secure document exchange, and conferences with counsel are held by videoconference unless the case calls for something else. Vocational interviews are conducted remotely where appropriate or in person in the evaluee's own community when direct evaluation better serves the referral questions.

There is no staffed Purinton Analytics office in Mississippi and no published street address in the state; meetings are by appointment, and the firm states that plainly because honesty about presence is part of the credibility it sells. When deposition, trial, or in-person evaluation requires travel, it is scheduled from the Kansas City home office and built around counsel's dates. Where economic damages testimony is also needed, the firm arranges coordinated damages engagements in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under a single engagement structure.

Vocational findings for Mississippi evaluees rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the relevant Mississippi metropolitan areas and for the state as a whole, along with state workforce agency labor market information and direct labor market research where warranted. Each source appears in the report's citations so the path from data to opinion stays visible.

## Qualification and disclosure in Mississippi matters

Expert disclosure practice differs between the Circuit Court and the federal districts, and local scheduling orders control the details. The firm writes every report to withstand the stricter regime, and any venue-specific registration or qualification requirement is identified and confirmed during the conflict check and intake process. Counsel are asked to share disclosure deadlines at intake so the analysis is finished with room to spare.

## Starting a Mississippi engagement

Whether the referral comes from the plaintiff or the defense side of the caption, the process starts the same way. [Request a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) with the case caption and parties, the venue, and any imminent disclosure, deposition, or trial dates. The firm responds with a conflict determination, a defined scope, and a records request tailored to the claims, so the written opinion rests on a record both sides can examine.
