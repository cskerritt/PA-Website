What Delaware counsel are actually retaining, when they retain Purinton

## The Delaware venue landscape

Personal injury, employment, and other damages matters in Delaware's state
system proceed in the Superior Court, and federal matters in the United
States District Court for the District of Delaware. The firm maintains
dedicated pages for the Delaware areas where engagements most often
arise: Dover and
Seaford. Those pages are a convenience
for counsel, not a boundary; a matter venued anywhere in the state moves
through the same intake, the same records process, and the same report
standards. Where an in-person evaluation and a testimony appearance can
be coordinated together, the firm schedules them that way, so Delaware
counsel are not paying for travel the engagement does not need.

## Serving Delaware from a remote-first practice

The firm's only staffed office is its Kansas City home office, and it
publishes no Delaware street address because it has none to publish.
Delaware engagements therefore begin remotely: records review, structured
vocational interviews, and standardized testing under publisher protocols
where remote administration is supported. When the referral questions
call for in-person evaluation, or when deposition and trial dates require
presence, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office and
planned around the court's calendar. Vocational findings for Delaware
evaluees rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and
wage estimates for Delaware and its labor market areas, state workforce
agency labor market information, and direct labor market research where
warranted. Where a matter also requires forensic economic analysis, the
firm offers coordinated damages engagements in which independent
economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis while the
vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement
structure. A Delaware file is handled exactly as a file from any other
state in the firm's [nationwide practice](/locations/nationwide/): same
intake, same quality controls, same publication standard.

## Qualification and disclosure in Delaware

Counsel weighing an out-of-state expert for a Delaware matter can raise
the qualification question at the outset, because the firm already does.
Any venue-specific registration or qualification requirement is
identified and confirmed during the conflict check and intake process,
before the engagement letter is signed and long before any opinion is
disclosed. Disclosure materials, including the expert's qualifications,
prior testimony, and compensation terms, are prepared to the standard the
Superior Court or the federal district and the governing scheduling order
require, so that the opinion arrives with its foundation already
documented.

## Opening a Delaware engagement

The first step in any Delaware matter, for plaintiff or defense counsel,
is a conflict check. Submit the caption or party names through the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and note any imminent disclosure,
deposition, or trial dates, together with the county and court where the
matter is pending. The firm responds with a defined scope, a records
request tailored to the claims at issue, and a schedule that accounts
for any travel the engagement will require.
