Purinton Analytics keeps no office in Maine, and this page says so before it says anything else. Counsel searching for a vocational expert or life care planner deserve the same accuracy from a firm's website that they demand from its reports, so the offer here is stated plainly: a remote-first national practice, meetings in Maine by appointment only, no published street address anywhere in the state, and travel from the Kansas City home office when depositions, trials, or in-person evaluations require presence. Plaintiff and defense referrals are accepted on identical terms.

## Maine courts and venues

Engagements are accepted for matters in the Maine Superior Courts and, for federal litigation, in the United States District Court for the District of Maine. The forum changes the disclosure calendar, not the analysis: every opinion rests on a documented conflict check, a defined scope, and a records request tailored to the claims, so the written product can be examined by either side of the caption.

Dedicated pages cover the metropolitan areas of Portland-South Portland, Bangor, Augusta-Waterville, and Lewiston-Auburn. Those pages exist to explain local logistics, not to mark the edge of coverage: a matter venued anywhere in Maine enters the same intake and is evaluated under the same methodology.

## How the firm serves Maine

Most of the work in a vocational or life care planning engagement is record work, and record work does not depend on geography. Medical records, employment files, wage documentation, and educational histories move through secure document exchange; vocational interviews and life care planning assessments are conducted by videoconference when that serves the evaluee, and in person when the referral questions require it. When presence is required, whether for evaluation, deposition, or trial, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office and planned around the litigation calendar. Counsel receive the same deliverables in either mode: a written report that states its methodology, identifies its sources, and separates findings from assumptions, so that cross-examination tests the analysis rather than surprises it.

Vocational findings for Maine evaluees rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for Maine statewide and for its metropolitan areas, state workforce agency labor market information, and direct labor market research where warranted. When a matter also requires economic damages, the firm offers coordinated damages engagements in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement structure.

## Qualification and disclosure

Disclosure obligations differ between Maine's state and federal forums, and the firm's reports, file materials, and testimony history are maintained so counsel can satisfy either. Any venue-specific registration or qualification requirement is identified and confirmed during the conflict check and intake process.
