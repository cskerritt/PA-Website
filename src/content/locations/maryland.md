A vocational or life care planning opinion filed in a Maryland case will be read most carefully by the lawyer whose job is to take it apart. Purinton Analytics writes for that reader. Every Maryland report states its referral questions, lists the records reviewed, shows the method applied to them, and separates findings from assumptions, so that cross-examination becomes a walk through a documented file rather than a hunt for what was left out. Counsel on either side of the caption can retain the firm on those terms, because the work product does not change with the client.

## Maryland courts and venues

Engagements are accepted for matters pending in the Circuit Courts of Maryland, the state's trial courts of general jurisdiction, and in the United States District Court for the District of Maryland. Dedicated pages describe service to the Baltimore-Columbia-Towson area and to Hagerstown-Martinsburg, Lexington Park, Salisbury, Cumberland, Ocean Pines, Easton, and Cambridge. A matter venued elsewhere in the state is equally welcome: the metro pages explain local logistics, and none of them draws a line around what the practice will accept.

## How the practice serves Maryland

The engagement model is remote-first. Records review, vocational interviews by video, transferable skills analysis, report drafting, and pre-deposition conferences all proceed remotely, on the schedule the litigation sets, so distance does not slow a Maryland case. Maryland meetings are by appointment: the firm keeps no staffed office in the state and publishes no Maryland street address, a fact stated plainly here because accuracy about presence is part of the same discipline that governs the reports. When the referral questions call for in-person evaluation, or when deposition and trial dates require attendance, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office. Vocational findings for Maryland evaluees rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the relevant metropolitan areas and for Maryland statewide, state workforce agency labor market information, and direct labor market research where warranted. When a matter calls for a full damages presentation, independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement structure, which keeps the earning-capacity findings, the care plan, and the economic valuation consistent with the same underlying record.

## Qualification and disclosure

Expert-practice formalities vary by venue, and the firm treats tracking them as its own responsibility. Any venue-specific registration or qualification requirement is identified and confirmed during the conflict check and intake process, and disclosure materials, prior testimony lists, and fee schedules are supplied in the format the venue expects. Intake also confirms scope, deadlines, and the records that must be reviewed before an opinion can responsibly issue, so nothing about the engagement rests on assumption.
