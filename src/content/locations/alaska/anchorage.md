Purinton Analytics serves plaintiff and defense counsel across the Anchorage,
AK metropolitan area with vocational expert and life care planning work built
for litigation. Referrals from Anchorage counsel typically involve
[earning capacity and employability evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/)
in personal injury and employment matters,
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) and
[medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/) in catastrophic
injury cases, and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. Whichever side of the caption issues the retention,
the file begins the same way: a documented conflict check, a defined scope,
and a records request tailored to the claims.

## Courts served across the Anchorage metro

The metropolitan area comprises Anchorage Municipality and the
Matanuska-Susitna Borough, and the firm accepts matters venued in the
Superior Court locations serving both. Federal claims proceed in the United
States District Court for the District of Alaska. Because the same written
methodology travels to every forum, counsel can retain the firm for a
state-court injury matter and a federal employment case in the same season
without reconciling two different report standards. Disclosure deadlines,
independent evaluations, and deposition dates are calendared at intake so
that the venue's schedule, not the expert's, drives the work plan. The
Anchorage practice operates within the firm's broader
[Alaska coverage](/locations/alaska/), so matters that reach beyond the
metro stay under the same engagement.

## By appointment, with honesty about presence

The firm keeps no staffed office in Anchorage and publishes no street
address for this location, because accuracy about offices is part of the
same discipline that governs its reports. Meetings, evaluations, and
testimony appearances are scheduled by appointment. Most engagements begin
remotely, with records review, structured interviews, and standardized
testing under publisher protocols where remote administration is supported.
When the referral questions call for in-person evaluation, or when
deposition and trial dates require presence, travel is planned from the
Kansas City home office and coordinated with counsel well ahead of the
appearance date. Counsel receive a single point of contact and a consistent
report standard regardless of where the work is performed.

## Labor market research for Anchorage evaluees

Vocational findings for evaluees in Anchorage Municipality and the
Matanuska-Susitna Borough rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational
employment and wage estimates for the Anchorage metropolitan area and for
Alaska statewide, supplemented by state workforce agency labor market
information and direct employer contact where the referral questions warrant
it. Transferable skills analysis and employability opinions are grounded in
the occupations actually available to the evaluee's own labor market rather
than national composites. Life care plans and medical cost projections for
Alaska residents are priced to the evaluee's own care market, using
provider-specific inquiries and published fee data, because care
availability and cost in an evaluee's own region can differ from national
averages in either direction depending on the service category.

Availability for a new matter is confirmed through a conflict check before
any substantive discussion of the case. Submit the caption or party names
through the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and note any imminent
disclosure, deposition, or trial dates so scheduling can be assessed at the
same time. Counsel weighing a multi-venue docket can also review the firm's
[national coverage](/locations/nationwide/) to see how the same engagement
structure serves matters outside Alaska.
