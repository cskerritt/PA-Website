Counsel who try cases in the Superior Court in Juneau, or in the United
States District Court for the District of Alaska, retain vocational and
life care planning experts for the record those courts will scrutinize, not
for proximity. Purinton Analytics supports plaintiff and defense
engagements from the Juneau, AK micropolitan area with
[earning capacity and employability evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) of opposing
expert work, each delivered under a methodology written to withstand
cross-examination regardless of which side commissioned it.

## The Juneau venue footprint

The micropolitan area consists of the Juneau City and Borough. The firm
accepts matters venued in the Superior Court serving the borough and in the
United States District Court for the District of Alaska, and it treats the
two forums identically at the level that matters: a documented conflict
check before substantive discussion, a defined scope at retention, and a
records request tailored to the claims, so the written opinion rests on a
record both sides can examine. Juneau engagements are part of the firm's
[Alaska practice](/locations/alaska/), and related claims arising elsewhere
in the state can be folded into the same engagement rather than
re-retained.

## How a by-appointment practice serves Juneau

The firm maintains no staffed office in Juneau and publishes no street
address for this location. That candor is deliberate: accuracy about
presence is part of the same discipline the courts expect from the reports
themselves. Juneau matters typically proceed remotely through most of their
life, with records analysis, structured interviews, and standardized
testing conducted under publisher protocols where remote administration is
supported. When the referral questions require an in-person evaluation, or
when a deposition or trial appearance is set, travel is scheduled from the
Kansas City home office with enough lead time that logistics never dictate
the quality of the appearance. Counsel deal with a consistent report
standard and a single point of contact from intake through testimony.

## Grounding opinions in the Juneau labor market

Vocational opinions for evaluees in the Juneau City and Borough are
grounded in Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage
estimates for the Juneau area and for Alaska statewide, in state workforce
agency labor market information, and in direct labor market research where
warranted. Employability conclusions turn on the occupations genuinely open
to the evaluee in the evaluee's own market, and the report says so
explicitly, source by source. Life care plans and
[medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/) for
Juneau-area residents are priced to the local care market through
provider-specific inquiries and published fee data, since the cost and
availability of care in an evaluee's own community, not a national average,
is what a damages figure must reflect.
