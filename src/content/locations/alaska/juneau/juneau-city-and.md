## The borough and its courts

Juneau sits within the Juneau City and Borough, and civil actions arising
there are tried in the Superior Court. Matters raising federal questions or
invoking diversity jurisdiction may proceed in the United States District
Court for the District of Alaska, and the controlling venue for any
particular caption is identified and confirmed during the conflict check
and intake process rather than assumed. The firm treats the state and
federal forums identically at the level of workmanship: a documented
conflict check before any substantive discussion, a defined scope at
retention, and a written opinion that rests on a record both sides of the
caption can examine. Context for the surrounding market appears on the
[Juneau metro page](/locations/alaska/juneau/), and the statewide venue
picture on the [Alaska state page](/locations/alaska/).

## The labor market behind the opinion

Vocational findings for a Juneau evaluee rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates for the Juneau area and for
Alaska statewide, on state workforce agency labor market information, and
on direct labor market research where the referral questions warrant it.
Juneau is a compact regional market, and a compact market imposes its own
discipline: published estimates thin out for narrow occupations, and where
they do, the report says so and explains what the direct research showed
instead of papering over the gap. Transferable skills analysis, labor
market surveys, and employability conclusions are all tied to occupations
that demonstrably exist in the market where the evaluee actually lives,
because an opinion built on work that is not hiring locally does not
survive a careful cross-examination, whichever side commissioned it.

## Evaluating an evaluee who lives in Juneau

Most of the work proceeds without anyone boarding a plane. Records review,
the diagnostic interview, vocational testing, and counsel conferences are
handled remotely, which keeps schedules predictable and costs proportionate
to the case. When the referral questions call for in-person evaluation, the
assessment is conducted in the evaluee's own community, and when deposition
or trial dates require presence, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City
home office and planned around the litigation calendar. The firm keeps no
staffed office in Juneau and publishes no street address for this location;
accuracy about where the firm actually sits is part of the same discipline
that governs its reports. For matters that pair vocational and
care-planning work with economic loss calculations, the firm offers
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.
