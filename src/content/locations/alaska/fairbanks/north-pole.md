When the injured party in a case lives in North Pole, the vocational and
care-planning questions have to be answered for North Pole, not for a
generic Alaska claimant. A small community does not offer the same mix of
work that Fairbanks, the principal city of its metropolitan area, offers,
and a written opinion that ignores that difference invites the
cross-examination it deserves. Purinton Analytics accepts North Pole
matters for
[vocational expert work](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) and
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) on either side of the
caption, and builds each opinion on the record of the evaluee's own
community: the work available to this person, in this place, given these
restrictions.

## Venue context in the Fairbanks North Star Borough

North Pole sits in the Fairbanks North Star Borough, and state-court
matters involving North Pole parties are typically heard in the Superior
Court serving the borough. Federal matters may proceed in the United States
District Court for the District of Alaska, with the controlling venue for
any particular case identified during the conflict check and intake. The
engagement sequence does not change with the forum: a documented conflict
check, a defined scope, a records request tailored to the claims, and a
report calendar mapped against the venue's disclosure and deposition
deadlines. Matters that reach beyond the borough are handled within the
firm's statewide [Alaska practice](/locations/alaska/) under the same
structure.

## North Pole's place in the Fairbanks labor market

For employability and earning capacity purposes, North Pole is best
understood as part of the labor market of the
[Fairbanks metropolitan area](/locations/alaska/fairbanks/), where
Fairbanks serves as the principal city and the center of gravity for
employment. The analysis therefore asks what work exists within the
evaluee's realistic commuting range, drawing on Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates for the Fairbanks area and for
Alaska statewide, state workforce agency labor market information, and
direct labor market research where the referral questions warrant it. When
a matter pairs vocational findings with economic loss calculations, the
firm offers
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Evaluating an evaluee who lives in North Pole

The firm keeps no staffed office in North Pole or anywhere else in Alaska
and publishes no street address for this location; candor about presence is
part of the same discipline that governs the reports themselves. Most of an
engagement proceeds remotely: records review, counsel conferences,
interviews, and standardized testing under publisher protocols where remote
administration is supported. When the referral questions call for an
in-person evaluation, the evaluee can be seen in or near North Pole rather
than being asked to travel to the expert, and when deposition or trial
dates require presence, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home
office and confirmed with counsel well before the appearance date. The
result is that a North Pole evaluee is assessed in context, without the
case absorbing the cost of an office the firm does not need.
