A Fairbanks retention tests whether an expert practice was actually built
for distance or merely tolerates it. Purinton Analytics was designed
remote-first, which is why counsel in the Fairbanks-College, AK
metropolitan area can retain the firm for
[vocational expert work](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[expert testimony support](/services/expert-testimony-litigation-consulting/)
on the same footing as counsel anywhere else in the country. Records
review, interviews, and standardized testing proceed remotely under
publisher protocols where remote administration is supported, and the
written opinion that results is held to the same standard on either side of
the caption: suitable for the closest reading opposing counsel can give it.

## Venue footprint in the Fairbanks North Star Borough

The metropolitan area consists of the Fairbanks North Star Borough, and the
firm accepts matters venued in the Superior Court serving the borough as
well as in the United States District Court for the District of Alaska.
State and federal engagements follow the same sequence: a documented
conflict check, a defined scope, a records request tailored to the claims,
and a report schedule mapped against the venue's disclosure and deposition
calendar rather than against the expert's convenience. Fairbanks matters
sit within the firm's statewide [Alaska practice](/locations/alaska/), so a
case that adds parties or claims beyond the borough does not require a new
engagement structure.

## Appointments instead of an office

There is no staffed Purinton Analytics office in Fairbanks, and the firm
publishes no street address for this location; stating that plainly is part
of the same discipline that governs its reports. What counsel get instead
is a by-appointment model: remote conferences for most of the life of the
case, and scheduled travel from the Kansas City home office when the
referral questions call for in-person evaluation or when deposition and
trial dates require presence. Travel to Fairbanks is planned early and
confirmed with counsel, so that appearance logistics are settled long
before they can crowd the substantive work. The evaluee can be seen in the
setting that best serves the evaluation, including the evaluee's own
community.

## Fairbanks labor market and cost research

Employability and earning capacity opinions for evaluees in the Fairbanks
North Star Borough rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational
employment and wage estimates for the Fairbanks area and for Alaska
statewide, along with state workforce agency labor market information and
direct labor market research where the questions warrant it. The analysis
turns on the work that actually exists in the evaluee's own market, not on
national assumptions imported from elsewhere. Life care plans and
[medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/) for
Fairbanks-area residents are priced through provider-specific inquiries and
published fee data for the region, so that the projected cost of care
reflects what care actually costs where the evaluee lives.
