The useful measure of an expert report is how it reads to the lawyer on the
other side of the caption, and that standard does not relax because a venue
is small or remote. Purinton Analytics brings the same documented
methodology to matters from the Ketchikan, AK micropolitan area that it
applies everywhere else:
[vocational evaluation of earning capacity and employability](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. Plaintiff and defense counsel are served on identical
terms, and the report is written so that either could have commissioned it.

## Where Ketchikan matters are venued

The micropolitan area consists of the Ketchikan Gateway Borough. The firm
accepts engagements for matters in the Superior Court serving the borough
and, for federal claims, in the United States District Court for the
District of Alaska. Retention starts with a documented conflict check and a
defined scope, followed by a records request tailored to the claims, so
that the eventual opinion rests on a record both sides can examine. The
work is calendared against the forum's disclosure, deposition, and trial
dates from the outset. Ketchikan sits within the firm's wider
[Alaska practice](/locations/alaska/), which keeps multi-venue Alaska
dockets under a single engagement structure.

## No storefront, and no pretense of one

Purinton Analytics keeps no staffed office in Ketchikan and publishes no
street address for this location. Saying so plainly is part of the brand of
accuracy the firm sells: a practice that would not overstate its offices is
also unlikely to overstate its findings. Ketchikan engagements are handled
by appointment. Most of the work, from records analysis through structured
interviews and standardized testing under publisher protocols where remote
administration is supported, proceeds remotely. When an in-person
evaluation is warranted, or when deposition or trial testimony requires
presence, travel is planned from the Kansas City home office and confirmed
with counsel early, so appearance logistics are resolved well before the
substantive deadlines they might otherwise crowd.

## Labor market evidence for Ketchikan evaluees

Employability and earning capacity findings for evaluees in the Ketchikan
Gateway Borough rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment
and wage estimates for Alaska and for the Ketchikan area, on state
workforce agency labor market information, and on direct labor market
research where the referral questions warrant it. The controlling question
is always what work exists for this evaluee in this market, and the report
shows its sources rather than asking the reader to take conclusions on
faith. Life care plans and
[medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/) for
Ketchikan-area residents are priced to the evaluee's own care market
through provider-specific inquiries and published fee data, because the
cost of future care belongs to the place where the care will actually be
delivered.
