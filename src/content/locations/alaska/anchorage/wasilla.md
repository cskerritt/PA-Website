When a Wasilla resident's earning capacity is at issue, the analysis cannot
stop at the town line. Wasilla sits within the Anchorage, AK metropolitan
area, which means the realistic labor market for a Wasilla evaluee extends
across the Matanuska-Susitna Borough and into the commuting reach of
Anchorage itself. Purinton Analytics writes vocational opinions for Wasilla
matters with that commuting geography in view, so that the transferable
skills analysis and the job market conclusions describe where a Wasilla
worker could actually be employed rather than an abstract statewide average.
Referring counsel on either side of the caption typically retain the firm
for [earning capacity and employability evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) in catastrophic injury
cases, and [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

Wasilla civil matters are heard in the Alaska Superior Court, the state's
trial court for the borough. For claims with a federal basis, the United
States District Court for the District of Alaska is the available federal
forum; the controlling venue for any particular matter is identified and
confirmed during the conflict check and intake process, along with any
venue-specific registration or qualification requirement. The firm prepares
the same work product for either forum: a written opinion that discloses its
methodology, cites its sources, and holds up under cross-examination.

## Wasilla's place in the Anchorage labor market

Wasilla is a small city, and small-city labor markets punish shortcuts. A
placeability opinion that simply imports occupational data from the
principal city, or one that ignores the jobs a commuting worker can reach,
invites impeachment. Vocational findings for Wasilla evaluees therefore
rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage
estimates for the Anchorage metropolitan area and for Alaska statewide,
supplemented by state workforce agency labor market information and by
direct labor market research where the referral questions warrant it. The
written report states which data describe the metro, which describe the
state, and why each source applies to this evaluee, so opposing counsel can
trace every conclusion to its foundation.

## Evaluation logistics for Wasilla evaluees

Purinton Analytics keeps no staffed office in Wasilla and publishes no local
address, because accuracy about presence is part of the same discipline that
governs the reports. Most engagements proceed remotely from records review
through report delivery, with vocational interviews conducted by secure
video when the case allows. When the referral calls for an in-person
evaluation, the firm schedules travel from the Kansas City home office and
conducts the assessment in the evaluee's own community, which keeps the
functional and vocational observations tied to the setting where the
evaluee actually lives and works. Deposition and trial testimony are
scheduled the same way, sized to the case rather than to a storefront.
