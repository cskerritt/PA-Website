Smaller markets test an expert practice in ways large cities do not: the
labor market is thinner, the provider network is narrower, and an opinion
built on big-city assumptions falls apart on cross-examination. Purinton
Analytics serves Seaford and the surrounding Sussex County communities with
the same remote-first discipline it applies nationwide, offering
[vocational expert and earning capacity evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/)
in injury and employment matters,
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) and
[medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/) in
catastrophic injury cases, and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. Plaintiff and defense referrals are accepted on the
same terms.

## Venue coverage in Sussex County

Engagements are accepted for matters venued in the Superior Court sitting in
Sussex County and in the United States District Court for the District of
Delaware. The firm's reports are written to a single standard regardless of
forum, so an opinion prepared for a state court personal injury matter holds
up under the same scrutiny it would face in a federal employment case.
Counsel elsewhere in Delaware can review the firm's statewide coverage on
the [Delaware locations page](/locations/delaware/), and matters that cross
state lines are handled through the practice's
[national coverage](/locations/nationwide/).

## Meetings by appointment, work built for distance

There is no staffed Purinton Analytics office in Seaford, and the firm
publishes no street address for this location, because accuracy about
presence is part of the same discipline that governs its written opinions.
Meetings with counsel and evaluees are scheduled by appointment. Most
engagements begin remotely, with records review, structured vocational
interviews, and standardized testing administered under publisher protocols
where remote administration is supported. When the referral questions call
for in-person evaluation of a Sussex County evaluee, or when deposition and
trial dates require presence, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home
office and planned around the litigation calendar rather than the other way
around.

## Labor market research for Sussex County evaluees

Vocational findings for evaluees in the Seaford area rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the area and for
Delaware statewide, supplemented by state workforce agency labor market
information and direct labor market research where the referral questions
warrant it. That last step matters here: employability and placeability
conclusions for a resident of a smaller community must reflect the work that
actually exists within a reasonable commuting range, not an occupational
profile borrowed from a distant metropolitan area. Life care plans and
medical cost projections are priced to the evaluee's own market using
provider-specific inquiries and published fee data, so the projected costs
reflect what care actually costs where the evaluee lives.

## Starting a Seaford engagement

Every engagement, local or national, begins with a documented conflict
check, a defined scope, and a records request tailored to the claims, so
that the written opinion rests on a record both sides of the caption can
examine. Counsel with a matter in Seaford or elsewhere in Sussex County can
begin by submitting the caption or party names through the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/), noting any imminent disclosure,
deposition, or trial dates so scheduling can be confirmed at the outset.
