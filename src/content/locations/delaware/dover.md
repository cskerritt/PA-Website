Purinton Analytics provides vocational expert and life care planning
services for Dover and Kent County litigation, accepting referrals from
plaintiff and defense counsel alike. Engagements from the Dover area
typically involve
[earning capacity and employability evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/)
in injury and employment matters,
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) in catastrophic injury
cases, [rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) of
opposing expert work, and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. Whichever side retains the firm, the report is
written to be read closely by the other.

## The Kent County venue footprint

The firm accepts matters venued in the Superior Court sitting in Kent
County and in the United States District Court for the District of
Delaware. Because the practice works on both sides of the caption, its
methodology does not change with the identity of the retaining party: the
same evaluation protocols, the same sourcing standards, and the same report
structure apply whether the opinion supports a claim or answers one.
Counsel handling matters elsewhere in the state will find the firm's
statewide approach described on the
[Delaware locations page](/locations/delaware/).

## How the firm appears in Dover

Dover meetings are by appointment. Purinton Analytics keeps no staffed
office in the city and publishes no street address for this location,
because candor about presence belongs to the same discipline that governs
its opinions. In practice, most Kent County engagements proceed remotely
from intake through report: records review, structured interviews, and
standardized testing under publisher protocols where remote administration
is supported. When an in-person evaluation is warranted, or when a
deposition or trial appearance requires presence, travel is scheduled from
the Kansas City home office. Counsel work with a single point of contact
and receive the same report standard regardless of where any given step of
the work is performed, in Dover or through the firm's
[national practice](/locations/nationwide/).

## Grounding opinions in the Dover labor market

Vocational conclusions for Kent County evaluees rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Dover
metropolitan area and for Delaware statewide, together with state workforce
agency labor market information and direct labor market research where the
referral questions warrant it. Employability findings are tied to the
evaluee's actual commuting range and transferable skills rather than to
generic national profiles. On the care side, life care plans and medical
cost projections are priced to the evaluee's own market through
provider-specific inquiries and published fee data, so that projected costs
survive comparison with what treatment in the region actually costs.

## Requesting a conflict check

Every Dover engagement begins the way engagements begin everywhere in the
practice: with a documented conflict check, a defined scope, and a records
request tailored to the claims, so the written opinion rests on a record
both parties can examine. To confirm availability for a Kent County matter,
[request a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) with the case caption and party
names, and note any disclosure deadlines, deposition settings, or trial
dates that bear on scheduling. Conflict screening is completed before any
substantive discussion of the case, which protects counsel on both sides of
the dispute.
