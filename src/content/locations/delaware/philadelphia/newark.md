at the city limits or even the state line. Newark lies in the Delaware
portion of the Philadelphia-Camden-Wilmington metropolitan area, a region
defined by commuting ties that link Delaware with Pennsylvania, New
Jersey, and Maryland, and a credible vocational opinion has to take that
geography seriously. Purinton Analytics brings that regional lens to
[vocational expert](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) and
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) engagements arising in
Newark, working for plaintiff and defense counsel in equal measure and
writing every report to withstand the opposing side's reading. Where an
opposing expert has already opined, the firm also provides
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) of that work.

## The New Castle County forums

Newark matters in Delaware's state system proceed in the Superior Court,
and the firm accepts New Castle County engagements on either side of the
caption. In federal practice, the United States District Court for the
District of Delaware stands as the available federal forum; which venue
actually controls a given case is a question answered during the conflict
check and intake rather than assumed in advance. The report standard does
not vary between forums, and counsel whose dispute sits elsewhere in the
state can review the firm's statewide coverage on the
[Delaware locations page](/locations/delaware/).

## Reading the commuting market honestly

Because Newark's workforce participates in a metropolitan economy whose
principal city is Philadelphia, employability and earning capacity
findings for Newark evaluees draw on Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates for the metropolitan area and
for Delaware statewide, supplemented by state workforce agency labor
market information and, where the referral questions require it, direct
labor market research with employers. The analysis asks what work actually
exists within the evaluee's realistic commuting range given the evaluee's
transferable skills and restrictions, not what a national average
suggests. Counsel who want the full regional picture, including how the
practice serves the wider metropolitan area, will find it on the
[Philadelphia metro page](/locations/pennsylvania/philadelphia/).

same discipline that governs its opinions. Meetings are scheduled by
appointment, and the bulk of a typical engagement, from records review
