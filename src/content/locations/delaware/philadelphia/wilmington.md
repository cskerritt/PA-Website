## Superior Court and the federal forum

Wilmington sits in New Castle County, and the firm accepts engagements in
matters before the Superior Court there as well as in the United States
District Court for the District of Delaware, the federal forum available to
Delaware litigants; the venue that controls any particular matter is
identified and confirmed during the conflict check and intake. Methodology
does not shift with the forum or with the retaining side: the same
evaluation protocols, the same sourcing standards, and the same report
structure apply whether the opinion supports a damages claim or answers
one. Counsel handling matters elsewhere in the state will find the
statewide approach described on the
[Delaware locations page](/locations/delaware/).

## A city inside a multistate labor market

Wilmington is a mid-size city, but its labor market is not a closed
circle. The city anchors the Delaware corner of the
Philadelphia-Camden-Wilmington metropolitan area, a commuting region that
reaches across Pennsylvania, New Jersey, Delaware, and Maryland with
Philadelphia as its principal city. Vocational findings for Wilmington
evaluees therefore rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational
employment and wage estimates for the metropolitan area and for Delaware
statewide, together with state workforce agency labor market information
and direct employer research where the referral questions warrant it.
Employability conclusions are tied to the evaluee's realistic commuting
range and transferable skills within that region rather than to a generic
national profile, and the regional practice as a whole is described on the
[Philadelphia metro page](/locations/pennsylvania/philadelphia/).

## Evaluation logistics in Wilmington

Meetings in Wilmington are by appointment. The firm keeps no staffed
office in the city and publishes no local street address, because accuracy
about presence belongs to the same discipline that governs its reports.
Most engagements proceed remotely from intake through report: records
review, structured vocational interviews, and standardized testing under
publisher protocols where remote administration is supported. When the
referral questions call for in-person evaluation, or when a deposition or
trial setting requires attendance, travel is scheduled from the Kansas
City home office. Wilmington counsel work with a single point of contact
and receive the same report standard applied throughout the firm's
[national practice](/locations/nationwide/).

## Starting with a conflict check

Every engagement begins with a documented conflict check, a defined scope,
and a records request tailored to the claims, so the written opinion rests
on a record both parties can examine. To confirm availability for a
Wilmington matter, [request a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) with the
case caption and party names, and note any disclosure deadlines,
deposition settings, or trial dates that bear on scheduling. Conflict
screening is completed before any substantive discussion of the case,
which protects counsel on both sides of the dispute.
