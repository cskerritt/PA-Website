impressions: medical records, employment files, earnings history, and
treatment documentation assembled before any conclusion is drafted.
Purinton Analytics brings that records-first discipline to Milton matters
for counsel on both sides of the bar, whether the referral calls for a
[vocational expert and earning capacity
evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/), a
[life care plan](/services/life-care-planning/), a
[medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/), or a

## Building the record before the opinion

Every Milton engagement opens with a records request tailored to the
claims actually pleaded, because the scope of the documents should follow
the scope of the dispute. The vocational interview and any standardized
testing are layered on top of that documentary base, not substituted for
it, and the finished report cites what it relies on so that the opposing
expert, the deposing attorney, and the court can trace each conclusion to
its source. An opinion assembled this way costs more effort at the front
end and saves the client from surprises at the back.

## Labor market evidence for a small community

Milton is a small town, and pretending otherwise is the fastest way to
lose an employability opinion on cross-examination. The firm's vocational
findings rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and
wage estimates for the area and for Delaware statewide, supplemented by
state workforce agency information and, where a placeability conclusion
requires it, direct research into the employers actually hiring within
commuting range. Life care plan pricing follows the same logic, drawing
on provider-specific inquiries and published fee data from the evaluee's
own market rather than national composites.

## Serving Milton without an office there

The firm maintains no Milton office and prints no local street address,
stating that fact as plainly as it states its findings. Engagements run
remotely by default: document analysis, structured interviews, and
publisher-sanctioned remote testing proceed regardless of distance. When
an in-person evaluation is warranted or testimony is required, the
evaluator travels from the Kansas City home office on a schedule set by
the case, an arrangement that also lets the practice serve referring
counsel through its [national footprint](/locations/nationwide/).
