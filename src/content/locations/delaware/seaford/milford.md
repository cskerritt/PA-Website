When an injury takes a Milford worker out of a job, the dispute that follows
turns on questions a jury cannot answer from intuition alone: what work the
evaluee can still perform, whether that work actually exists within reach of
home, and what the difference is worth over a working lifetime. Purinton
Analytics answers those questions for counsel on either side of the caption,
providing [vocational expert and earning capacity
evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/) for Milford
matters, along with
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under a single
engagement structure.

## Courts serving Milford litigants

Milford sits in Sussex County, and engagements are accepted for matters in
the Superior Court there as well as in the United States District Court for
the District of Delaware, with the controlling venue for any particular
case confirmed during the conflict check. The report itself does not change
with the forum: whether the caption reads state or federal, the opinion is
written to survive the scrutiny of whichever judge and opposing expert will
test it. Counsel handling claims elsewhere in the state will find the
firm's full coverage described on the
[Delaware locations page](/locations/delaware/).

## A small city's labor market, measured honestly

Milford is a small city, and its residents work across a commuting market
that is wider than the town itself. An employability opinion for a Milford
evaluee therefore has to be built from data that matches that market, not
from occupational assumptions imported from a major metropolitan area.
Vocational findings rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational
employment and wage estimates for the area and for Delaware statewide,
supplemented by state workforce agency information and direct labor market
research when the referral questions call for it. The same coverage extends
across the surrounding communities described on the
[Seaford metro page](/locations/delaware/seaford/), and matters that reach
beyond Delaware are handled through the firm's
[national practice](/locations/nationwide/).

## How a Milford evaluee is evaluated

Most of the work happens at a distance by design. Records review, structured
vocational interviews, and standardized testing under publisher protocols
that support remote administration can all be completed without anyone
leaving Milford. The firm maintains no office in the city and publishes no
local address, because claiming a presence it does not have would undercut
the accuracy it sells. When the referral requires an in-person evaluation,
or when a deposition or trial date demands the expert's attendance, travel
is arranged from the Kansas City home office and planned around counsel's
calendar. What counsel gives up in storefront proximity it gains in a
practice built to deliver the same product in every venue it serves.

## Opening a Milford engagement

An engagement begins with a documented conflict check, a defined scope, and
a records request tailored to the claims, so the finished opinion rests on
a record either party can test. To start, submit the caption or party names
through the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and flag any disclosure
deadline, deposition, or trial setting that is already on the docket, so
scheduling commitments can be confirmed before the engagement letter is
signed.
