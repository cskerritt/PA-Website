earning capacity opinion, a catastrophic claim that needs a care plan and
a cost projection, an employment dispute that needs mitigation analysis.
Purinton Analytics is structured so a single referral can cover the set. The
firm provides [vocational expert and earning capacity

engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) in which
independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis
while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under a single
engagement structure. Plaintiff and defense referrals are welcome on the
same terms.

## Matching the expert scope to the Selbyville case

Scope is decided deliberately at intake, not accreted later. Some
Selbyville matters need only a narrow vocational opinion; others need the
full sequence from functional vocational assessment through care planning
to economic loss, each component feeding the next. Defining that scope in
the engagement letter keeps the file clean, keeps the expert's role
inside its lane, and gives the retaining lawyer a clear picture of what
the disclosure will contain before it is due.

## The employment picture around Selbyville

Selbyville is a small town whose workers participate in a regional
economy larger than the town itself, the same regional market surveyed on
the [Seaford metro page](/locations/delaware/seaford/). Vocational
conclusions for a Selbyville evaluee are therefore built from Bureau of
Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the area
and for Delaware statewide, from state workforce agency labor market
information, and from direct labor market research whenever the
employability or placeability question turns on what employers within
commuting distance are actually doing. Cost conclusions in care plans
draw on the evaluee's own provider market through provider-specific
inquiries and published fee data.

## Local evaluees, remote-first practice

No Purinton Analytics office exists in Selbyville, and the firm says so
rather than implying otherwise, because accuracy about small facts is how
an expert earns trust on large ones. Engagements are conducted remotely
wherever the method allows, including records review, structured
vocational interviews, and standardized testing under publisher-approved
remote protocols. Where an in-person evaluation or live testimony is
required, the expert travels from the Kansas City home office on dates
coordinated with counsel's schedule and the court's.
