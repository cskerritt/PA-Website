## An opinion that survives the other side

Writing for the adversary changes how the work is done. Assumptions are
stated rather than buried, methods are named rather than implied, and
every figure in a care plan or earning capacity analysis is tied to a
source the other side can pull and check. When the firm is retained to
review rather than to build, the same lens is applied in reverse,
identifying where an opposing expert's Lewes opinion departs from its own
stated method. Counsel get no advocacy dressed as analysis; they get
analysis solid enough to advocate from.

## Grounding conclusions in the regional market

An earning capacity opinion for a Lewes evaluee must reflect the
employment that exists within a realistic commuting range of a small
town, so the firm starts from Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational
employment and wage estimates for the area and for Delaware statewide,
adds state workforce agency labor market information, and conducts direct
labor market research when a disputed conclusion turns on actual hiring
conditions. The surrounding regional economy, of which Lewes is a part,
is discussed on the [Seaford metro page](/locations/delaware/seaford/).
Care-cost projections are priced from the evaluee's own provider market,
using provider-level inquiry and published fee data.

## By-appointment service, honestly described

The firm does not keep an office in Lewes and does not publish a street
address for the town, and it treats that candor as inseparable from the
credibility of its reports. Counsel and evaluees are seen by appointment.
Remote work covers most of an engagement, from records analysis to
structured interviews to standardized testing administered under
publisher protocols that permit remote delivery, and in-person
evaluations or courtroom appearances are handled through travel scheduled
from the Kansas City home office.

Begin with the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/): the caption or
