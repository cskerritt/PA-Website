## Working to the disclosure calendar

A late expert is a useless expert, so scheduling is treated as part of the
scope. At intake, counsel identifies every date already on the docket, and
the engagement letter reflects when the records must arrive, when the
evaluee will be seen, and when the signed report will be delivered.
Because most of the work proceeds remotely, a Laurel engagement does not
wait on travel windows: analysis begins as soon as the conflict check
clears and the records come in, and the calendar risk that remains is
named early instead of discovered late.

## Laurel's county and courts

Laurel is a small Sussex County town, and matters involving Laurel parties
are accepted for the Superior Court sitting in that county and for the
United States District Court for the District of Delaware, the state's
federal forum; which court controls any given engagement is pinned down
during the conflict check rather than presumed from geography. The firm's
reports are drafted to a standard that does not vary by forum, an approach
counsel can see applied across the state on the
[Delaware locations page](/locations/delaware/) and across the country
through the firm's [nationwide practice](/locations/nationwide/).

## Earning capacity in a small-town market

For a Laurel evaluee, the honest employability question is what work
exists within a realistic commute of a small town, and the answer cannot
be borrowed from a big-city occupational profile. Findings are anchored in
Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for
the area and for Delaware statewide, checked against state workforce
agency data, and extended by direct labor market research whenever a
conclusion depends on the actual hiring behavior of employers within
reach. The broader regional employment picture that Laurel sits inside is
treated on the [Seaford metro page](/locations/delaware/seaford/).

## Meetings and evaluations by appointment

The firm keeps no office in Laurel and publishes no address there;
overstating presence would sit poorly next to reports that trade on
precision. Counsel meetings and evaluee sessions are set by appointment.
Interviews and publisher-approved remote testing are completed at a
distance, and when the referral requires an in-person evaluation or the
case requires live testimony, travel from the Kansas City home office is
booked against the same litigation calendar the engagement was scoped to.
