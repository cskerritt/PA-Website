The path from a Bridgeville referral to a signed expert report runs
through a fixed sequence, and Purinton Analytics runs it the same way for
every client on either side of a case: conflict check, written scope,
tailored records request, analysis, report. That procedural spine carries
each of the firm's services, from
[vocational expert and earning capacity
evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) to
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) to
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) of opinions
served by the other side.

## From referral to report, step by step

Nothing substantive happens before the conflict check clears; that
protects the referring lawyer as much as the firm. Scope comes next, set
out in the engagement letter so the disclosure holds no surprises. The
records request is drafted to the claims actually pleaded in the
Bridgeville matter, the evaluee interview and any standardized testing
follow, and the report is delivered against the dates counsel identified
at intake. Each step leaves a paper trail, because the firm assumes from
the outset that the file will eventually be read by someone trying to
discredit it.

## Venue facts for Bridgeville cases

Bridgeville belongs to Sussex County, and engagements are accepted for
matters in the county's Superior Court and in the United States District
Court for the District of Delaware, with the controlling forum for any
specific case confirmed as part of intake rather than assumed in
advance. The same intake step surfaces any venue-particular registration
or qualification requirement. Coverage across the rest of the state is
collected on the [Delaware locations page](/locations/delaware/), and
the practice's reach beyond Delaware on the
[nationwide page](/locations/nationwide/).

## Measuring work within reach of a small town

Bridgeville is a small town, and its residents' employment prospects are
properly measured against the regional market they actually commute
into, the market described on the
[Seaford metro page](/locations/delaware/seaford/). The firm anchors its
vocational conclusions in Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational
employment and wage estimates for the area and for Delaware statewide,
augments them with state workforce agency data, and performs direct
labor market research when the dispute turns on whether particular work
is truly available nearby. Care costs are projected from the evaluee's
own provider market using provider-level inquiries and published fee
data, never from national averages applied by default.

## No local office, full local service

The firm has no Bridgeville office and publishes no address in the town,
a fact stated rather than obscured. Evaluees and counsel are seen by
appointment. The remote-first design of the practice means a Bridgeville
engagement moves at the speed of the records, not the travel schedule:
interviews and publisher-approved remote testing proceed at a distance,
and when in-person evaluation or courtroom testimony is needed, the
trip is made from the Kansas City home office on dates the case
dictates.

## Setting the sequence in motion

The sequence starts when counsel submits the caption or party names
through the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/), ideally with the
scheduling order dates attached. Everything after that, scope, records,
evaluation, report, follows the same documented path, which is why the
opinions that come out the other end stand up when the other side goes
looking for weaknesses.
