When earning capacity, employability, or the cost of future care becomes a contested question in a Massachusetts case, counsel on either side of the caption need an expert whose conclusions rest on a record both parties can examine. Purinton Analytics provides vocational expert and life care planning services for Massachusetts litigation and offers [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement structure. Every matter starts the same way: a documented conflict check, a defined scope of work, and a records request matched to the claims at issue.

## Massachusetts venues and courts served

Engagements are accepted for matters pending in the Superior Court and in the United States District Court for the District of Massachusetts. The written product is built to the same standard regardless of forum: opinions that state their methodology, cite their sources, and hold up under cross-examination by opposing counsel.

Dedicated pages describe the practice's work in each of the state's metropolitan areas: Boston-Cambridge-Newton, MA-NH, Worcester, MA, Springfield, MA, Barnstable Town, MA, Amherst Town-Northampton, MA, Pittsfield, MA, Greenfield, MA, Vineyard Haven, MA, and Nantucket, MA. Counsel with a matter tied to a listed market can start with the metro page; counsel with a matter elsewhere in Massachusetts can proceed directly through intake, because the methodology does not change with the venue.

## How the firm serves Massachusetts

The practice is remote-first by design. Records review, transferable skills analysis, labor market research, and report drafting proceed without regard to where the evaluee or counsel sits, and conferences with counsel are ordinarily held by videoconference. Massachusetts meetings are by appointment. The firm keeps no staffed office in the state and publishes no Massachusetts street address, because accuracy about presence is part of the same discipline that governs its reports. When the referral questions call for in-person evaluation, or when deposition and trial dates require presence, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office and planned around the litigation calendar counsel provides.

Labor market findings for Massachusetts evaluees rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the relevant metropolitan area and for Massachusetts statewide, on state workforce agency labor market information, and on direct labor market research where the referral questions warrant it. Those sources are cited in the report so that either side can trace every finding back to its origin.

## Qualification and disclosure

Expert disclosure practice differs from forum to forum, and the engagement is run to the disclosure and scheduling requirements of the court where the matter is pending. Any venue-specific registration or qualification requirement is identified and confirmed during the conflict check and intake process, before substantive work begins, so that a late-discovered obstacle never compromises a report or a deadline.

## Starting a Massachusetts engagement

Conflict screening comes before any discussion of the merits. Submit the caption or party names through the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and note any imminent disclosure, deposition, or trial dates so scheduling can be confirmed alongside clearance. Plaintiff and defense engagements are accepted on the same terms. Counsel whose matter sits outside Massachusetts, or whose caseload spans several states, can review the [nationwide practice](/locations/nationwide/) for the same services delivered under the same engagement structure.
