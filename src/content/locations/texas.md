The distances inside Texas are the first practical fact of any expert
engagement there: the evaluee, the courthouse, and retaining counsel can
each be a long drive apart, and the labor market that matters to the
opinion may be none of the ones near the courthouse. Purinton Analytics
builds Texas engagements around that reality, accepting plaintiff and
defense referrals statewide for
[vocational expert](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) opinions,
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)

## Working Texas cases from Kansas City

The firm keeps no staffed office anywhere in Texas and publishes no street
address in the state. Meetings are by appointment, and the firm says so
plainly because honesty about presence belongs to the same discipline that
governs its written opinions. In practice, most Texas engagements begin
remotely: records review, structured interviews, and standardized
vocational instruments administered by secure video under publisher
protocols. When the referral questions call for in-person evaluation, or
when depositions and trial settings require presence, travel is scheduled
from the Kansas City home office and confirmed against the deadlines
recorded at intake. Because Texas contains labor markets as different from
each other as any in the country, vocational findings are grounded in
Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for
the specific metropolitan area at issue and for Texas statewide, along with
state workforce agency labor market information and direct labor market
research where the questions warrant it. The full national service model is
described on the [nationwide coverage page](/locations/nationwide/).

## Qualification and disclosure in Texas

Board certifications held within the firm are issued by national
credentialing bodies rather than by any single state, and whether an expert
may testify in a given Texas forum is a question the forum answers under
its own evidentiary standards. Any venue-specific registration or
qualification requirement is identified and confirmed during the conflict
check and intake process, so counsel on either side of the caption know
before retention exactly what the record will show about the expert's
qualifications.

## Starting a Texas engagement

The path into an engagement is the same for a metro docket and a regional
venue alike. Submit the caption or party names through the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and note any imminent disclosure,
deposition, or trial dates. The firm responds with availability, CVs, and
fee information, followed by a records checklist tailored to the claims
once the conflict check clears.
