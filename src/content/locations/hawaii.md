A vocational opinion stands or falls on the record beneath it: the medical documentation, earnings history, vocational testing, and labor market research that either side of the caption can examine line by line. That record can be assembled for a Hawaii matter with the same rigor the firm applies everywhere else in its [national practice](/locations/nationwide/), because the work is document-driven by design. Purinton Analytics provides vocational expert evaluation and life care planning to plaintiff and defense counsel litigating injury, employment, and disability claims throughout Hawaii.

## Courts and Venues Across Hawaii

Engagements are accepted for matters pending in the Circuit Court and in the United States District Court for the District of Hawaii. The deliverable in either forum is the same: a written opinion with sources cited and assumptions stated, prepared against the disclosure obligations and deadlines of the court in which it will be served. Counsel whose caseload concentrates in a particular market will find dedicated pages for Urban Honolulu, Hilo-Kailua, Kahului-Wailuku, and Kapaa, each describing the venue footprint and appointment logistics for that area.

## How the Firm Serves Hawaii

Hawaii is served on a by-appointment basis. The firm keeps no staffed office in the state and publishes no street address for any Hawaii location; accuracy about presence is part of the same discipline that governs the reports themselves. Most phases of an engagement proceed remotely. Records are transmitted securely, vocational interviews and follow-up conferences are conducted by video when the referral questions permit, and report drafts move against the disclosure calendar without waiting on anyone's travel plans. When the assignment calls for in-person evaluation, or when deposition and trial dates require presence, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office and planned around the litigation calendar rather than improvised against it.

Vocational findings for Hawaii evaluees rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for Hawaii and for its metropolitan areas, state workforce agency labor market information, and direct labor market research where warranted. Life care plans are grounded in the treating record and in sourced pricing for the goods and services each plan recommends. Where lost earnings or the cost of future care must be reduced to a defensible economic figure, the firm offers [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement structure.

## Qualification and Disclosure

Expert qualification is established the same way in every forum: through the expert's file, testimony history, and disclosed methodology rather than through assertion. Any venue-specific registration or qualification requirement is identified and confirmed during the conflict check and intake process. Reports are written to be examined, and the file behind each opinion is kept in a condition that discovery can test, because an opinion that cannot survive cross-examination was never worth serving.

## Starting a Hawaii Engagement

New matters begin with a conflict check rather than a sales conversation. Submit the caption or party names through the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and note any imminent disclosure, deposition, or trial dates so that scheduling can be confirmed before the engagement letter issues. Once the conflict check clears, scope is defined in writing: the referral questions, the records to be requested, and the deliverables are fixed at the outset, whether the retaining party sits at the plaintiff table or the defense table.
