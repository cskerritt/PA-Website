A catastrophic injury or employment case seldom turns on a single expert
discipline. Counsel building or defending the damages picture usually need
an employability and earning capacity opinion, often a life care plan, and
an economic analysis that reduces both to present value, each prepared to
withstand the other side's examination. Purinton Analytics offers South
Dakota counsel that full sequence, for plaintiff and defense matters alike:
[vocational expert witness](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) work,
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## South Dakota venues

Engagements are accepted for matters in the Circuit Court, South Dakota's
trial court of general jurisdiction, and in the United States District
Court for the District of South Dakota. State or federal, the choice of
forum controls the disclosure calendar and the evidentiary standard; it
does not change how the analysis is built, which is on a documented record
that both sides of the caption can examine.

## How the firm works in South Dakota

Meetings in South Dakota are by appointment. The firm keeps no staffed
office in the state and publishes no South Dakota street address, and it
says so directly because accuracy about presence belongs to the same
discipline that governs its written opinions. Engagements generally open
remotely: records analysis, structured vocational interviews by secure
video, and standardized instruments administered under publisher protocols
where remote administration is supported. When the referral questions call
for in-person evaluation, the evaluation is scheduled in the evaluee's own
community, and travel for evaluations, depositions, and trial testimony is
arranged from the Kansas City home office around the deadlines counsel
identify at intake.

Vocational findings for South Dakota evaluees rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for South Dakota
statewide and for its metropolitan areas, on state workforce agency labor
market information, and on direct labor market research where the referral
questions warrant it. Life care plans and future medical cost projections
are priced to the evaluee's own market through provider-specific inquiry
rather than assumed from national tables. The same report standard
described on the firm's [nationwide coverage page](/locations/nationwide/)
governs every South Dakota engagement.

## Qualification and disclosure

Credentials in vocational evaluation and life care planning are issued by
national certifying bodies rather than by any single state, and whether an
opinion is admitted in a South Dakota courtroom is decided by the forum
under its own rules of evidence. Any venue-specific registration or
qualification requirement is identified and confirmed during the conflict
check and intake process, so the question is settled before substantive
work begins rather than litigated after disclosure.

## Starting a South Dakota engagement

An engagement opens with a documented conflict check and a defined scope,
in that order. Send the case caption or party names through the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/), and flag any disclosure deadline,
deposition setting, or trial date that should shape the work plan. Once
conflicts clear, counsel receive a written scope, a fee schedule, and a
records request tailored to the claims; confidential records should not be
transmitted before that point.
