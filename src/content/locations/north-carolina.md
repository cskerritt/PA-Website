Retention in North Carolina comes from both directions: plaintiff firms
proving earning capacity loss and future care needs, and defense firms
testing those same claims. Purinton Analytics takes engagements from either
side, and the reports read the same way regardless of who signs the
retention letter. That symmetry is deliberate. A vocational opinion or life
care plan that survives cross-examination has to rest on a record both
sides can examine, so every North Carolina engagement starts with a
documented conflict check, a defined scope, and a records request tailored
to the claims.

## North Carolina's court landscape

The firm accepts matters venued in the Superior Court, the state's trial
court of general jurisdiction, and in each of the state's federal trial
courts: the United States District Court for the Eastern District of North
Carolina, the United States District Court for the Middle District of North
Carolina, and the United States District Court for the Western District of
North Carolina. Litigation in the state is spread across many centers
rather than concentrated in one, and the practice profiles them

## Serving North Carolina by appointment

There is no staffed Purinton Analytics office in North Carolina and no
published street address, because claiming a local presence the firm does
not keep would be at odds with the accuracy it promises in its reports.
Meetings are by appointment. Most engagements proceed remotely from records
review through report delivery, with standardized testing administered
under publisher protocols where remote administration is supported. When
the referral calls for in-person evaluation of an evaluee in their own
community, or when deposition and trial schedules require presence, travel
is arranged from the Kansas City home office. Vocational findings for North
Carolina evaluees rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational
employment and wage estimates for North Carolina and its metropolitan
areas, state workforce agency labor market information, and direct labor
market research where warranted. Life care plans for North Carolina
residents are priced to the evaluee's own market using provider-specific
inquiries and published fee data for the region. Coordinated damages
engagements are
available as well, in which independent economist partners perform and sign
the economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds
under one engagement structure.

## Qualification and disclosure

Certifications held within the practice come from national credentialing
bodies rather than from any single state, and admissibility in a North
Carolina matter is decided by the forum under its own rules of evidence.
Any venue-specific registration or qualification requirement is identified
and confirmed during the conflict check and intake process. Prior testimony
lists, fee schedules, and disclosure materials are supplied in whatever
form the venue and the parties require.

## Starting a North Carolina engagement

To explore availability, [request a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) with
the case caption and party names, and note any disclosure, deposition, or
trial dates already on the calendar. When the conflict check clears, the
engagement proceeds under a written scope and fee agreement issued before
any records are reviewed. Counsel with matters beyond the state
can review the firm's [national coverage](/locations/nationwide/) to see
how the same engagement structure applies elsewhere.
