A Virginia caption frames the expert question before any disclosure is drafted. Whether a matter proceeds in a Circuit Court or in federal court, the vocational and life care planning issues it raises tend to be the same on either side of the caption: what work the evaluee can still perform, what that work pays in the labor market where the evaluee actually lives, and what future care a permanent condition will require. Purinton Analytics answers those questions for plaintiff and defense counsel throughout Virginia, applying the same documented methodology it brings to every matter in its national practice.

## The Virginia venue landscape

Engagements are accepted for matters in the Circuit Court across the Commonwealth and in the United States District Court for the Eastern District of Virginia and the United States District Court for the Western District of Virginia. Reports and disclosures are prepared to the standards of whichever court holds the case, and each written opinion is built on a record that opposing counsel and the bench can examine line by line.

Because vocational opinions turn on the labor market surrounding the evaluee, coverage within Virginia is organized by metro area. Dedicated pages describe the practice's work in Washington-Arlington-Alexandria, Virginia Beach-Chesapeake-Norfolk, Richmond, Roanoke, Lynchburg, Charlottesville, Blacksburg-Christiansburg-Radford, Winchester, Harrisonburg, Staunton-Stuarts Draft, Danville, Martinsville, and Lake of the Woods. A matter arising anywhere else in Virginia is handled under the same statewide approach, with labor market research scoped to the evaluee's own community.

## How the firm serves Virginia counsel

The practice is remote-first. Records review, vocational interviews, transferable skills analysis, and life care plan development proceed by secure document exchange and scheduled video conference, and many Virginia engagements are completed without any travel at all. The firm maintains no staffed office in Virginia and publishes no Virginia street address; meetings in the Commonwealth are by appointment, and when an in-person evaluation, deposition, or trial appearance requires presence, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office and planned around the court's calendar.

Vocational findings for Virginia evaluees rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the relevant Virginia metro areas and for Virginia statewide, state workforce agency labor market information, and direct labor market research where warranted. When a case requires economic damages testimony as well, counsel may structure [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement structure. The same structure is available wherever the firm works; the [nationwide practice page](/locations/nationwide/) describes it in full.

## Qualification and disclosure in Virginia courts

Every engagement begins with a documented conflict check, a defined scope, and a records request tailored to the claims, so the file supports the opinion from intake forward. Qualifications, prior testimony, and the bases for each opinion are produced with the disclosure in the form the forum requires. Any venue-specific registration or qualification requirement is identified and confirmed during the conflict check and intake process, before substantive work begins, so that admissibility questions are addressed at the start of the engagement rather than on the eve of trial.

## Starting a Virginia engagement

Conflict checking precedes every substantive conversation about a case. Submit the caption or party names through the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and note any imminent disclosure, deposition, or trial dates so scheduling can be confirmed alongside the conflict search. Counsel receive a defined scope and fee agreement before work begins, and the engagement proceeds the same way for either side of the caption.
