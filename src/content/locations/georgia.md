Retaining counsel in Georgia sit on both sides of the caption, and
Purinton Analytics is built to serve either chair without changing its
method. The firm provides vocational expert and life care planning
services to plaintiff and defense counsel across Georgia on identical
terms: the same conflict check before any substantive conversation, the
same defined scope, and the same written opinion standard, prepared on
the assumption that the lawyers across the table will read every page of
it. That symmetry is not a slogan; it is what allows the same expert to
be credible in a plaintiff's catastrophic injury case and in a defense
employability dispute alike.

## Working Georgia matters from a remote-first practice

The firm keeps no staffed office in Georgia and publishes no street
address here; its only home office is in Kansas City, and it says so
plainly because accuracy about presence belongs to the same discipline
as accuracy in a report. Georgia engagements typically begin remotely,
with records review, structured vocational interviews, and standardized
testing under publisher protocols where remote administration is
supported. Vocational findings for Georgia evaluees rest on Bureau of
Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for Georgia
and its metropolitan areas, state workforce agency labor market
information, and direct labor market research where warranted. When
in-person evaluation is called for, or when deposition and trial
calendars require presence, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City
home office. Where the referral also requires forensic economic
analysis, the firm offers coordinated damages engagements in which
independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis
while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. Georgia files are governed by the same intake and
quality controls as the firm's
[nationwide practice](/locations/nationwide/).

## Qualification and disclosure in Georgia

The firm does not leave qualification questions for motion practice to
surface. Any venue-specific registration or qualification requirement is
identified and confirmed during the conflict check and intake process,
and disclosure materials, including the expert's qualifications, prior
testimony, and compensation terms, are assembled to the standard the
venue and the scheduling order require. That holds whether the matter is
pending in a Superior Court or in any of Georgia's federal districts,
and it holds for whichever party issues the engagement.

## Opening a Georgia engagement

A Georgia engagement, for either side, begins with a conflict check.
Submit the caption or party names through the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and note any imminent disclosure,
deposition, or trial dates, along with the county and court where the
matter is venued. The firm responds with a defined scope, a records
request tailored to the claims, and a schedule built around the court's
deadlines and any travel the engagement will involve.
