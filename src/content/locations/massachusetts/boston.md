The Boston-Cambridge-Newton, MA-NH metropolitan area crosses a state line,
and the litigation practice built around it does the same. Counsel in Boston
regularly carry matters from Massachusetts venues into southern New
Hampshire, and Purinton Analytics accepts referrals on either side of that
line and either side of the caption. The core work is the
[vocational evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) of
employability and earning capacity, joined in catastrophic matters by
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) and, where damages
require it, by
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Venues on both sides of the state line

Massachusetts referrals from this metro most often arise in Suffolk County,
Middlesex County, Norfolk County, Essex County, and Plymouth County, in the
Superior Court and in the United States District Court for the District of
Massachusetts. The metro also takes in Rockingham County and Strafford
County in New Hampshire, and engagements there proceed under the same intake
discipline; any venue-specific registration or qualification requirement is
identified and confirmed during the conflict check and intake process. The
[Massachusetts practice page](/locations/massachusetts/) describes coverage
across the rest of the commonwealth.

## By appointment, stated plainly

Boston meetings are by appointment. The firm maintains no staffed office in
the metro and publishes no street address for this location, because
accuracy about presence belongs to the same discipline that governs its
written opinions. What counsel can count on instead is a defined process:
conflict checks answered promptly, evaluation dates set against the
disclosure calendar at intake, and travel scheduled from the Kansas City
home office when depositions, trial testimony, or in-person evaluations
require presence in Massachusetts or New Hampshire. Remote appearance is
available where the court permits it, and
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) assignments are
routinely completed on the record alone.

## Labor market research for a deep regional market

The Boston area is among the larger and more occupationally diverse labor
markets in the country, and that depth cuts both ways in litigation: broader
post-injury placement options for some evaluees, sharper stratification of
wages and credentials for others. The analysis measures rather than assumes.
Employability and earning capacity findings rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Boston
metropolitan area and for Massachusetts statewide, state workforce agency
labor market information, and direct labor market research, including
employer contact and job posting analysis, when the referral questions turn
on actual hiring in a particular sector of the metro.

## Starting a Boston engagement

Referrals from this metro typically involve personal injury, medical
malpractice, wrongful death, employment, and disability matters, and the
firm's availability to plaintiff and defense counsel alike is part of what
makes its opinions durable under cross-examination. Every engagement begins
with a documented conflict check, a defined scope, and a records request
tailored to the claims, so the written opinion rests on a record both sides
can examine.

To begin, submit the caption or party names through the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and note any imminent disclosure,
deposition, or trial dates. CVs and fee information follow promptly, and the
[national coverage page](/locations/nationwide/) explains remote evaluation
logistics and the firm's reach beyond New England.
