Method is the product. What counsel in the Amherst Town-Northampton, MA
metropolitan area retain when they retain Purinton Analytics is not a
conclusion but a process: a documented conflict check, a defined scope, a
records request tailored to the claims, and a written opinion that shows its

underlies each
[vocational evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) of
employability and earning capacity, each
[life care plan](/services/life-care-planning/), and each
[rebuttal or peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) the firm

defense table.

## Hampshire County courts

Engagements are accepted for matters pending in Hampshire County, in the
Superior Court and in the United States District Court for the District of
Massachusetts. Opinions are drafted with the forum in mind: methodology
stated plainly, sources cited, assumptions disclosed, and limits
acknowledged, because an opinion that hides its reasoning does not hold up
at deposition. Testimony is given in person or by remote appearance where
the court permits, and any venue-specific registration or qualification
requirement is identified and confirmed during the conflict check and
intake process. The
[Massachusetts practice page](/locations/massachusetts/) covers matters
arising elsewhere in the commonwealth.

## Appointment-based service, described accurately

The firm keeps no staffed office in Amherst or Northampton and publishes no
street address for this location. An expert practice that trades on
disciplined reporting should describe its own footprint with the same
discipline. Hampshire County counsel instead get a dependable rhythm:
conflict checks answered promptly, evaluation dates and report deadlines set
against the disclosure calendar during intake, evaluations conducted in the
evaluee's community, at counsel's offices, or by secure video under
publisher protocols, and travel scheduled from the Kansas City home office
when a deposition, trial, or in-person evaluation requires presence.

## Labor market data behind every finding

Employability and earning capacity conclusions for Hampshire County evaluees
are grounded in Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage
estimates for the Amherst Town-Northampton metropolitan area and for
Massachusetts statewide, together with state workforce agency labor market
information. Where the referral questions demand evidence of actual hiring
rather than published estimates, the analysis extends to direct labor market
research, including employer contact and job posting review. When a matter
requires a full damages presentation, the firm structures
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure, and
[medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/) work prices
future care against the region the evaluee actually lives in.
