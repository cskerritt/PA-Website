Worcester referrals tend to arrive with a specific pairing in mind: a
[vocational evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) of the
evaluee's employability and earning capacity, and a
[rebuttal or peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) of the report an
opposing expert has already served. Purinton Analytics takes both
assignments, separately or together, for plaintiff and defense counsel in
the Worcester, MA metropolitan area. Catastrophic matters add
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) and, where the damages
model requires it,
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## The Worcester County venue footprint

Engagements are accepted for matters pending in Worcester County, in the
Superior Court and in the United States District Court for the District of
Massachusetts. Depositions and trial testimony are given in person or by
remote appearance where the court permits, and written opinions are prepared
to the same standard regardless of forum: a documented record, a defined
scope, and conclusions that can withstand examination by the other side.
When a referral reaches beyond the county, the
[Massachusetts practice page](/locations/massachusetts/) describes the
firm's coverage across the rest of the commonwealth.

## What by-appointment service means in Worcester

There is no staffed Purinton Analytics office in Worcester, and the firm
publishes no street address for this location. That candor is deliberate:
counsel who retain an expert for credibility should not find the expert's
own marketing overstating its footprint. In place of a storefront, counsel
get a working process. Conflict checks are answered promptly, scheduling is
set against the disclosure calendar at intake, evaluations are held in the
evaluee's community, at counsel's offices, or by secure video under
publisher protocols, and travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home
office when depositions, trials, or in-person evaluations require presence.

## Grounding opinions in the regional labor market

Worcester sits in its own defined metropolitan labor market, and vocational
findings for evaluees here are built on data specific to it rather than
borrowed from elsewhere in the state. Employability and earning capacity
opinions rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage
estimates for the Worcester metropolitan area and for Massachusetts
statewide, supplemented by state workforce agency labor market information
and by direct labor market research, including employer contact and job
posting analysis, when the referral questions call for evidence about actual
hiring rather than published averages. The same sources support
[medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/) and life care
plans when future care must be priced against the region an evaluee actually
lives in.

## Starting a Worcester engagement

Worcester County matters referred to the firm most often involve personal
injury, medical malpractice, wrongful death, employment, and disability
claims. The practice accepts work from either side of the caption, and each
engagement begins the same way: a documented conflict check, a defined
scope, and a records request tailored to the claims, so that the finished
opinion rests on a record both parties can test.

Submit the caption or party names through the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and flag any imminent disclosure,
deposition, or trial dates. CVs and fee information follow promptly, and the
[national coverage page](/locations/nationwide/) covers remote evaluation
logistics and engagements outside Massachusetts.
