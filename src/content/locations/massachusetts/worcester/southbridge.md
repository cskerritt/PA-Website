## Labor market analysis on a litigation timeline

A defensible vocational opinion for a Southbridge evaluee cannot be improvised near a deadline, because its foundation takes time to assemble: Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Worcester metropolitan area and for Massachusetts statewide, state workforce agency labor market information, and, where warranted, direct labor market research. Southbridge is a small city, and the analysis treats its scale honestly, weighing the wider metropolitan market an evaluee can realistically reach against the work actually available close to home. When quantification is required, independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis through [coordinated damages assessment](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/), sequenced so that the vocational, care-planning, and economic deliverables move on the same schedule instead of colliding at the deadline.
