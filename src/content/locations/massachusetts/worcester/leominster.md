## Reading a commuter's labor market

Because Leominster workers routinely cross municipal lines to earn, an earning-capacity opinion limited to jobs inside the city would misstate the market an evaluee actually faces. Vocational findings for Leominster evaluees rest instead on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Worcester metropolitan area and for Massachusetts statewide, on state workforce agency labor market information, and on direct employer contact where the referral questions warrant it. Transferable-skills analysis ties those sources to the individual: prior work history, residual functional capacity, and the occupations realistically open within a reasonable commuting pattern. When the case requires the vocational findings to be reduced to dollars, the firm offers [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement structure.
