## Evaluation in the evaluee's community

Most Gardner work is remote by design: records analysis, video interviews, standardized instruments administered under appropriate conditions, and reports that document every source relied upon. When in-person evaluation is warranted, whether for a life care plan or a vocational assessment, the appointment is set in the evaluee's own community, in a setting suited to the assessment, so that an injured or disabled claimant is not asked to absorb the burden of travel that properly belongs to the expert. The firm keeps no office in Gardner and claims none; when depositions or trial require the expert's presence, that travel originates from the Kansas City home office and is planned around the court's schedule.
