New Bedford anchors the Massachusetts side of the Providence-Warwick metropolitan area: a city large enough to sustain a substantial employer base of its own, yet tied by daily commuting to a regional labor market that crosses the state line into Rhode Island. That dual character matters in litigation, because a New Bedford evaluee's earning capacity cannot be read from city limits alone. Purinton Analytics provides [vocational expert witness](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) and [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) services for injury, employment, and disability matters involving New Bedford parties, accepting referrals from plaintiff and defense counsel as part of a [metro practice centered on Providence](/locations/rhode-island/providence/) and a statewide [Massachusetts practice](/locations/massachusetts/).

## Bristol County venue for a New Bedford case

New Bedford sits in Bristol County, and civil actions involving New Bedford parties are commonly heard in the Superior Court serving Bristol County. Federal claims may proceed in the United States District Court for the District of Massachusetts, with the controlling venue identified during the conflict check and intake rather than assumed from geography. The work product does not change with the forum. Every engagement opens with a documented conflict check, a defined scope, and a records request tailored to the claims, so the written opinion rests on a record that plaintiff and defense counsel can each examine line by line.

## A labor market that crosses a state line

Because the metro spans Massachusetts and Rhode Island, vocational findings for a New Bedford evaluee rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Providence-Warwick metropolitan area and for Massachusetts statewide, supplemented by state workforce agency labor market information and direct labor market research where the referral questions warrant it. The analysis asks where this evaluee could realistically work given residual capacity, transferable skills, and the commuting patterns of the region, not where an abstract worker might. When lost earnings must be reduced to present value, the firm offers [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement structure.

## How a New Bedford evaluee is evaluated

Purinton Analytics keeps no office in New Bedford and publishes no local address, because accuracy about presence belongs to the same discipline that governs its reports. Most evaluations proceed remotely, through structured interview and standardized instruments the evaluee completes from home. When the referral questions call for an in-person evaluation, it is arranged in the evaluee's own community at a suitable professional setting, with travel scheduled from the Kansas City home office. Deposition and trial appearances are planned the same way, around the court's calendar and the disclosure deadlines counsel is working against.
