## Middlesex County litigation from Somerville

Somerville is situated in Middlesex County, and civil suits arising there are generally heard in the Superior Court serving Middlesex County. Where federal jurisdiction attaches, Massachusetts cases proceed in the United States District Court for the District of Massachusetts. Which of those forums controls, and what registration or qualification rules that forum applies to a testifying expert, is worked out during the conflict check and intake, and the answer shapes the report's architecture before any opinion is drafted, whether the assignment is affirmative or a [review of an opposing expert's disclosure](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/). Counsel learn during intake exactly how the practice will appear for that forum's purposes, from report format to testimony logistics, so nothing about the engagement's structure surprises the court later.

## What by-appointment service looks like in practice

A Somerville engagement typically begins and ends without anyone boarding a plane: conflict check, scope definition, electronic records production, structured video interviews, and a report delivered against the disclosure schedule. Video interviewing is treated as method rather than convenience: sessions are structured, documented, and long enough to support the findings drawn from them. When the referral requires a hands-on evaluation, the practice comes to the evaluee's own community rather than summoning the evaluee somewhere convenient to the expert, and travel from the Kansas City home office is likewise scheduled for depositions and trial testimony. Cases that need economic quantification alongside the vocational and care questions are structured as [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/), with independent economist partners performing and signing the economic analysis while the rest of the work remains under one engagement structure.
