Cambridge is not a satellite of its metro; it is one of the communities named in the federal statistical title of the Boston region itself. Counsel litigating a Cambridge injury, employment, or disability matter can retain Purinton Analytics for [vocational evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/), [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and [rebuttal or peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/), with the same engagement discipline offered to plaintiff and defense counsel alike. The work begins the way it begins everywhere in the practice: a documented conflict check, a defined scope, and a records request tailored to the claims, so the written opinion rests on a record either side of the caption can examine.

## Middlesex County venue for Cambridge matters

Cambridge sits in Middlesex County, and state-court civil actions arising there are generally heard in the Superior Court serving Middlesex County. On the federal side, Massachusetts is served by the United States District Court for the District of Massachusetts, and the controlling venue for a given engagement, along with any registration or qualification requirement that venue imposes on a testifying expert, is identified and confirmed during the conflict check and intake. Because disclosure practice differs between the state and federal forums, the report is drafted from the outset for the court that will ultimately test it, whether the assignment is an affirmative opinion or a critique of an opposing expert's disclosure.

## Reading the Cambridge labor market honestly

Vocational findings for a Cambridge evaluee are grounded in Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the [Boston metropolitan area](/locations/massachusetts/boston/) and for Massachusetts statewide, supplemented by state workforce agency labor market information and by direct labor market research when the referral questions call for it. The market description stays qualitative and sourced: Cambridge participates in one of the largest employment regions in the country, and the analysis treats it as part of that wider commuting market rather than as a sealed local economy. Transferable-skills conclusions and placement opinions cite published estimates and documented inquiries, because an opinion that cannot show its sources tends not to survive cross-examination.

## How a Cambridge evaluation proceeds

The firm keeps no staffed office in Cambridge and publishes no local street address; accuracy about presence is part of the same standard that governs the reports. Most engagements proceed remotely, with structured interviews conducted by video and records reviewed against the scope counsel defined. When the referral calls for in-person assessment, the evaluation takes place in the evaluee's own community, and travel for evaluations, depositions, and trial appearances is scheduled from the Kansas City home office. Where the damages question extends beyond vocational capacity into economic valuation, the firm offers [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement structure. Scheduling is candid as well: counsel are told at intake what the calendar will realistically support, and interim findings can be staged ahead of the full report when mediation or an early settlement conference requires them.
