Most damages cases that reach this practice from Haverhill turn on the same pairing of questions: what can this person earn now, and what will their care cost over a lifetime. The earning question is answered through a [vocational evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) of residual capacity, transferable skills, and realistic placement; the care question through a [life care plan](/services/life-care-planning/) that itemizes future needs against the medical record. Purinton Analytics answers both for plaintiff and defense counsel alike, and applies the same evidentiary standard whichever question, or whichever side, the retention presents.
