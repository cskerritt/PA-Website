## Norfolk County and the choice of forum

Quincy sits in Norfolk County, so state-court civil actions arising there generally belong to the Superior Court serving Norfolk County. The federal alternative for Massachusetts litigation is the United States District Court for the District of Massachusetts, and which forum controls a given case, together with any expert qualification requirement that forum imposes, is resolved during the conflict check and intake. The forum determination feeds directly back into scheduling: state and federal disclosure practice diverge enough that the same opinion may need a different structure and a different lead time depending on where the caption lives. The intake step also surfaces scope questions while there is still time to resolve them, which is exactly when they are cheapest to resolve.

## By-appointment presence, remote-first process

Purinton Analytics maintains its home office in Kansas City and no other staffed location, so Quincy work is by appointment and no local address is published. In practice this costs counsel nothing: records review, scope definition, and structured video interviews proceed remotely on whatever cadence the case calendar allows, and when an in-person assessment is warranted it is conducted in the evaluee's own community with travel scheduled from the home office. Depositions and trial appearances are planned the same way, with dates protected early. Counsel receive dated commitments rather than loose estimates, and if a scheduling risk emerges the practice raises it immediately instead of letting everyone discover it together at the deadline.
