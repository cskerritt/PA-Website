Labor market evidence is where vocational opinions are won or lost, and for a Lawrence evaluee that evidence has to be gathered with care. Purinton Analytics grounds every Lawrence [vocational assessment](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) in the Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates covering the [Boston metropolitan area](/locations/massachusetts/boston/) and Massachusetts statewide, layered with state workforce agency labor market information and direct labor market research when the referral questions call for it. The same sourcing rigor supports [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) on the cost-of-care side, and the practice takes retention from plaintiff and defense counsel without distinction.

## Essex County venue and the federal forum

Lawrence belongs to Essex County, and civil actions arising there are generally litigated in the Superior Court serving Essex County. Massachusetts litigants may also find themselves in the United States District Court for the District of Massachusetts, and the venue that actually governs an engagement, along with whatever expert registration or qualification requirement that venue carries, is established during the conflict check and intake. The practice writes each report for its destination court from the first draft, because retrofitting an opinion to a different disclosure standard late in a case invites exactly the kind of scrutiny an expert should never volunteer for. Early forum clarity also tells counsel what the expert timeline must look like, since disclosure sequencing differs meaningfully between the state and federal tracks.

## A commuting market wider than the city line

Earning capacity is measured against the work a person can realistically obtain, and for someone in Lawrence that inquiry extends across the regional commuting market rather than stopping at municipal boundaries. The analysis therefore characterizes opportunity at metro and statewide scale, qualitatively on this page and with full citation in the signed report. Transferable-skills findings, labor market surveys, and placement opinions are all constructed so opposing counsel can trace each conclusion to a published estimate or a documented inquiry; an untraceable assertion is treated in-house as an error, not a shortcut. Where the evaluee's work history includes specialized or licensed occupations, the transferability analysis addresses those credentials directly instead of assuming they carry over to other work.

## How the work reaches Lawrence

There is no local Purinton Analytics office in Lawrence, no storefront, and no published street address, because the practice does not imply presence it does not have. Engagements run remote-first: a documented conflict check, a scope agreed with counsel, electronic records review, and structured interviews by video. Where an in-person evaluation is warranted, it takes place in the evaluee's own community, and travel from the Kansas City home office is scheduled for evaluations, depositions, and trial dates. When economic valuation belongs in the damages case, [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) bring in independent economist partners who perform and sign the economic analysis while vocational and care-planning work continues under one engagement structure. Opposing disclosures can be evaluated separately through [rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/).
