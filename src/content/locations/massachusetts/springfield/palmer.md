## A small-town evaluee in a metro data set

An earning capacity opinion for a Palmer evaluee has to handle scale honestly. The jobs data comes from Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Springfield metropolitan area and for Massachusetts statewide, refined with state workforce agency labor market information and direct labor market research where warranted, and the transferable skills analysis then asks the narrower question: which of those jobs are realistically within this evaluee's reach, given the commuting the evaluee can actually sustain and the restrictions the record supports. Conclusions are stated market by market so the factfinder can see where each finding comes from, and a metro-wide statistic is never passed off as a statement about what is available to this particular person. Where present-value calculations are needed, [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) let independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement structure.
