A referral from Nantucket counsel arrives the same way a referral arrives from anywhere else in the country: a caption, a set of claimed damages, and a calendar that is already moving. Purinton Analytics serves the Nantucket area as part of a national practice built for exactly that posture, providing [vocational expert witness](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) analysis, [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and [medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/) work for counsel on either side of the caption. The practice model is remote-first by design, which is what makes a small venue practical to serve to the same standard as a major metropolitan docket. The scope of that national model is described on the [nationwide services page](/locations/nationwide/), and the broader state practice is covered on the [Massachusetts page](/locations/massachusetts/).

## Venue Footprint in Nantucket County

Engagements are accepted for matters in the Superior Court sitting for Nantucket County and in the United States District Court for the District of Massachusetts. Every engagement begins with a documented conflict check, a defined scope, and a records request tailored to the claims, so the written opinion rests on a record both plaintiff and defense counsel can examine. Reports are drafted for disclosure from the first page: methodology stated, sources cited, and conclusions limited to what the record supports. When the claimed damages include earnings losses, counsel can pair the vocational work through [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement structure. [Rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) assignments, reviewing the opposing expert's file rather than the evaluee, are accepted under the same terms.

## Meetings by Appointment

The firm keeps no staffed office in Nantucket and publishes no street address for this location, because accuracy about offices belongs to the same discipline that governs its reports. Meetings with counsel are held by appointment, and most case work, including records review, counsel conferences, and a substantial share of evaluee interviews, proceeds remotely by secure videoconference. When the referral questions call for in-person evaluation, or when deposition and trial dates require presence, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office and planned early against the court's calendar. Counsel are told plainly what can be done remotely and what warrants travel, and that judgment is documented in the engagement scope rather than improvised later.

## Labor Market Research for Nantucket Evaluees

Vocational findings for evaluees in the Nantucket area rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the area and for Massachusetts statewide, state workforce agency labor market information, and direct labor market research where warranted. Published series for a smaller labor market can run thin, and the analysis says so when they do: rather than stretching a sparse estimate, the work leans on statewide data and direct employer research, and the report identifies which source supports each finding. Transferable skills analysis, employability, and placeability are assessed against the labor market the evaluee actually faces, not a generic national profile, and the reasoning is laid out so that cross-examination can test it step by step.

## Starting an Engagement

The first step for any Nantucket County matter is a conflict check, not a consultation. Submit the caption or party names through the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and note any imminent disclosure, deposition, or trial dates. Once conflicts clear, the firm returns a defined scope, a fee schedule, and a records request, and [expert testimony](/services/expert-testimony-litigation-consulting/) support is scheduled around the deadlines counsel already carry.
