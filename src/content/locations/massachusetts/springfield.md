## Hampden County courts and forums

The firm accepts engagements for matters pending in Hampden County, in the
Superior Court and in the United States District Court for the District of
Massachusetts. Reports are written to survive the forum they are headed for:
a defined scope, a documented factual record, and methodology explained
plainly enough for the trier of fact and rigorously enough for opposing
counsel. Testimony is provided at deposition and trial in person, or by
remote appearance where the court permits. Matters arising elsewhere in the
commonwealth are covered through the
[Massachusetts practice page](/locations/massachusetts/).

## Meetings by appointment, and what that actually means

Springfield meetings are by appointment. The firm keeps no staffed office in
the metro and publishes no local street address, because being precise about
presence is part of the same discipline that governs its written opinions.
What counsel receive instead is predictability. Conflict checks are answered
promptly. Evaluation dates, report deadlines, and testimony dates are mapped
against the scheduling order during intake. Evaluations take place in the
evaluee's community, at counsel's offices, or by secure video under
publisher protocols, and when a deposition, trial, or in-person evaluation
requires presence in Hampden County, travel is scheduled from the Kansas
City home office.

## Labor market evidence for Springfield evaluees

Employability and earning capacity findings for evaluees in the Springfield
area rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage
estimates for the Springfield metropolitan area and for Massachusetts
statewide, state workforce agency labor market information, and direct labor
market research, including employer contact and job posting analysis, when
the referral questions require evidence of actual hiring conditions. Where a
case needs a damages number as well as a vocational opinion, the firm offers
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure, keeping the disclosure calendar in a single set of
hands.

## Starting a Springfield engagement

Referrals from Hampden County most often involve personal injury, medical
malpractice, wrongful death, employment, and disability matters, and the
firm's willingness to work for either side of the caption is part of what
keeps its opinions credible under cross-examination. Every engagement opens
with a documented conflict check, a defined scope, and a records request
tailored to the claims.

To put a matter in motion, send the caption or party names through the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and identify any imminent disclosure,
deposition, or trial dates so scheduling can be confirmed up front. CVs and
fee information follow promptly, and the
[national coverage page](/locations/nationwide/) describes remote evaluation
logistics and the firm's work beyond Massachusetts.
