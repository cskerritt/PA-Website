The Greenfield, MA micropolitan area sits entirely within Franklin County,
and that compactness shapes how Purinton Analytics approaches every
referral here. Employability and earning capacity questions in a market
this size turn on the occupations an evaluee can realistically reach from
home, not on the composition of distant employment centers, so the firm's
[vocational evaluations](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) start from
local evidence and stay tethered to it: the jobs that exist here, the wages
they carry, and the fit between those openings and a specific evaluee's
restrictions. The same evidentiary discipline runs through
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) and
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) work for
plaintiff and defense counsel alike.

## Franklin County venues

Engagements are accepted for matters pending in Franklin County, in the
Superior Court and in the United States District Court for the District of
Massachusetts. Reports are written for adversarial reading: scope defined at
intake, sources cited, methodology explained, and limits acknowledged.
Deposition and trial testimony are provided in person or by remote
appearance where the court permits. Referrals arising elsewhere in the
commonwealth are covered through the
[Massachusetts practice page](/locations/massachusetts/).

## Presence, stated without embellishment

The firm keeps no office in Greenfield and publishes no street address for
this location. Counsel deserve the same accuracy about the firm's footprint
that they expect in its reports, so service here is described exactly as it
works: by appointment. Conflict checks are answered promptly, and
evaluation, report, and testimony dates are set against the disclosure
calendar during intake. Evaluations take place in the evaluee's own
community, at counsel's offices, or by secure video under publisher
protocols, which keeps most engagements entirely travel-free. When
in-person evaluation or courtroom presence in Franklin County is required,
travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office.

## Data before conclusions

Employability and earning capacity findings for Franklin County evaluees
rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage
estimates covering the Greenfield area and Massachusetts statewide, together
with state workforce agency labor market information. Because published
estimates thin out in smaller markets, the analysis adds direct labor market
research where the referral questions warrant it, including employer contact
and job posting review, so conclusions about placement and wages reflect
actual hiring rather than interpolation. When a matter also requires an
economic damages presentation, the firm structures
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Starting a Greenfield engagement

Franklin County referrals most often involve personal injury, medical
malpractice, wrongful death, employment, and disability matters. The firm
accepts retention from either side of the caption, and that two-sided
practice shapes every deliverable: an opinion written for one audience is
advocacy, while an opinion written for both is evidence. Each engagement
begins with a documented conflict check, a defined scope, and a records
request tailored to the claims.

To begin, submit the caption or party names through the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and note any imminent disclosure,
deposition, or trial dates. CVs and fee information follow promptly, and the
[national coverage page](/locations/nationwide/) sets out remote evaluation
logistics and the practice's work beyond Massachusetts.
