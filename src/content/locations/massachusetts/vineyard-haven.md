[Massachusetts practice page](/locations/massachusetts/).

## No local office, and why that is stated plainly

The firm maintains no office in Vineyard Haven and publishes no street
address for this location. Some expert practices imply local presence they
do not have; this one treats accuracy about its own footprint as a preview
of the accuracy counsel can expect in its reports. Service in Dukes County
is by appointment, with conflict checks answered promptly and evaluation,
report, and testimony dates fixed against the disclosure calendar at intake,
so the logistics of a distant venue are resolved before the engagement
begins rather than discovered midstream.

## Careful data work in a compact labor market

A compact labor market demands more careful vocational analysis, not less.
Employability and earning capacity findings for Dukes County evaluees rest
on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates
covering the area and Massachusetts statewide, together with state workforce
agency labor market information, and the analysis extends to direct labor
market research, including employer contact and job posting review, when the
referral questions turn on what hiring actually looks like for a particular
evaluee. Where the case requires a full damages presentation, the firm
offers
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure, and
[medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/) work prices
future care against what the evaluee's region actually charges.
