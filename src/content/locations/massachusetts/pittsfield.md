Counsel in the Pittsfield, MA metropolitan area sometimes assume that a
national expert practice will treat their venue as an afterthought.
Purinton Analytics is built on the opposite premise: the firm works
remote-first everywhere, so a Berkshire County referral receives the same
process, the same report quality, and the same scheduling attention as a
matter in a major coastal market. That coverage spans
[vocational evaluations](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) of
employability and earning capacity,
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) of opposing

## Berkshire County venues

The firm accepts engagements for matters pending in Berkshire County, in the
Superior Court and in the United States District Court for the District of
Massachusetts. Deposition and trial testimony are provided in person or by
remote appearance where the court permits, and written opinions follow the
practice's uniform standard: documented conflict check, defined scope,
records request tailored to the claims, and conclusions the opposing side
can trace and test. Matters arising elsewhere in the commonwealth are
addressed through the
[Massachusetts practice page](/locations/massachusetts/).

## How a distant venue is actually served

There is no Purinton Analytics office in Pittsfield, and the firm publishes
no street address for this location; candor about presence is part of the
product. What makes the distance workable is planning rather than
proximity. At intake, evaluation dates, report deadlines, and likely
testimony dates are mapped against the scheduling order. Evaluations are
held in the evaluee's community, at counsel's offices, or by secure video
under publisher protocols, so most engagements require no travel by anyone.
When an in-person evaluation, deposition, or trial appearance does require
presence in Berkshire County, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home
office and confirmed with counsel well before the date.

## Labor market analysis proportioned to the market

Vocational findings for Berkshire County evaluees rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Pittsfield
metropolitan area and for Massachusetts statewide, alongside state workforce
agency labor market information. In a market of this scale, published
estimates alone can miss what hiring actually looks like, so where the
referral questions warrant it the analysis adds direct labor market
research, including employer contact and job posting review. For matters
that need a complete damages presentation, the firm offers
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure, with
[medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/) priced
against the region where the evaluee will actually receive care.

Berkshire County referrals most often involve personal injury, medical
malpractice, wrongful death, employment, and disability matters, and the
firm accepts retention from either side of the caption. Every engagement
begins the same way regardless of venue: a documented conflict check, a
defined scope, and a records request tailored to the claims.

Submit the caption or party names through the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and note any imminent disclosure,
deposition, or trial dates. CVs and fee information follow promptly, and the
[national coverage page](/locations/nationwide/) explains remote evaluation
logistics and the practice's reach beyond Massachusetts.
