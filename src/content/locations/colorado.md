Colorado litigation is spread across an unusually wide map, and vocational
and life care planning evidence has to travel with it. Purinton Analytics
accepts engagements from plaintiff and defense counsel throughout the
state, in matters venued in its largest metro areas and in communities far
from them. The practice is built for that geography: work that can be done
remotely is done remotely, and work that requires presence is scheduled
deliberately, so the resulting opinion reads the same whether the evaluee
lives in a metro suburb or a rural county.

general jurisdiction, and federal matters in the United States District

## How the firm works Colorado matters

Purinton Analytics keeps no staffed office in Colorado and publishes no
street address here, because accuracy about presence is part of the same
discipline that governs its written opinions. Colorado engagements typically
begin remotely: records review, structured vocational interviews, and
standardized testing under publisher protocols where remote administration
is supported. Vocational findings rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates for Colorado and its metro
areas, state workforce agency labor market information, and direct labor
market research where warranted. When the referral questions call for
in-person evaluation, or when deposition and trial dates require presence,
travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office. Where a matter also
requires forensic economic analysis, the firm offers coordinated damages
engagements in which independent economist partners perform and sign the
economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds
under one engagement structure. Colorado files receive the same intake,
conflict check, and quality controls as the firm's
[nationwide practice](/locations/nationwide/), and every report is written
to withstand examination by opposing counsel in either a District Court
courtroom or a federal one.

## Qualification and disclosure in Colorado

Counsel retaining an out-of-state expert reasonably ask what applies before
an opinion is disclosed in a Colorado forum. The firm treats that as an
intake question rather than an afterthought: any venue-specific
registration or qualification requirement is identified and confirmed
during the conflict check and intake process, and disclosure materials,
including the expert's qualifications, prior testimony, and compensation
terms, are prepared to the standard the venue and the scheduling order
require. The same practice applies whether the matter sits in state court
or in the federal district.

## Starting a Colorado engagement

The first step for any Colorado matter, on either side of the caption, is a
conflict check. Submit the caption or party names through the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and note any imminent disclosure,
deposition, or trial dates, along with the county or district where the
matter is venued. The firm responds with a defined scope, a records
request tailored to the claims, and a schedule that accounts for the
travel the venue may require.
