What is a Quincy worker's earning capacity actually worth after an injury?
That question sits at the center of most referrals the firm receives from
this part of Washington, and answering it honestly requires more than a
national average dressed up in local language. Purinton Analytics provides
[vocational expert witness](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) opinions
and [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) for Quincy matters
on behalf of plaintiff and defense counsel alike, and treats the labor
market analysis as the load-bearing wall of the opinion rather than an
appendix.

## The market a Quincy opinion must describe

Quincy is a small community whose residents work both in town and across
the wider Moses Lake area, so a credible earning-capacity analysis has to
define the commuting market before it counts anything inside it.
Vocational findings rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational
employment and wage estimates for the surrounding region and for Washington
statewide, supplemented by state workforce agency labor market information
and by direct labor market research when the referral questions call for
employer-level detail. Transferable skills are identified from the work
history, matched against occupations that plausibly exist within reach of
Quincy, and tested against the restrictions the medical record supports.
Where the case requires lost earnings to be converted into a present-value
damages figure, the firm offers [coordinated damages
engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) in which
independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis while
the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement
structure, keeping methodology consistent across the disciplines.

## Grant County courts and the choice of forum

Civil claims arising in Quincy are venued in Grant County, where
state-court matters proceed in the Superior Court under Washington's rules
for expert disclosure, deposition, and trial testimony. On the federal
side, Washington offers the United States District Court for the Eastern
District of Washington and the United States District Court for the Western
District of Washington as forums; which district governs a particular
dispute, and what that court requires of a retained expert, is settled
during the conflict check and intake process rather than presumed on a web
page. The report itself is venue-agnostic by design: every opinion states
its methods and sources plainly enough that opposing counsel in either
court system can trace each conclusion back to the record.

## How evaluation reaches Quincy

The firm keeps no office in Quincy and publishes no local street address,
because there is none to publish; every meeting is by appointment. In
practice, most of an engagement moves remotely: records arrive through
counsel, interviews are conducted by video where appropriate, and draft
timelines are managed against the court's schedule. When an in-person
[vocational evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) or life care
planning assessment is warranted, the evaluee is seen in his or her own
community rather than summoned to a distant office, and travel is scheduled
from the Kansas City home office. The same logistics govern testimony:
deposition and trial dates are calendared at intake and protected as the
engagement proceeds, and [rebuttal and peer
review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) assignments follow the identical
scheduling discipline.
