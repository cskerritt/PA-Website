Every expert report filed in a Warden case is written not only for the
counsel who retained it but for the opposing counsel preparing to take it
apart. Purinton Analytics drafts to that hostile reader. Every
[vocational expert witness](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) report
and [life care plan](/services/life-care-planning/) is prepared on the
assumption that the opposing side will read it closely, retain its own
expert, and probe every step at deposition. Because the firm accepts work
from both sides of the caption, its opinions cannot lean on advocacy; the
same methodology produces the analysis whether the client chair holds
plaintiff or defense counsel.

## Building an opinion that survives the courtroom

A Warden engagement opens the way every engagement in the practice does:
with a documented conflict check, a defined written scope, and a records
request tailored to the claims. From there the file is built in layers that
opposing counsel can take apart and reassemble. The medical record
establishes restrictions; the work history establishes transferable
skills; the vocational analysis connects restrictions and skills to the
jobs that actually exist within the evaluee's reach; and a life care plan, where one is
needed, prices future care from cited sources rather than round estimates.
Opinions offered in [rebuttal or peer
review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) get the same construction, since a
critique is only as strong as the methodology behind it. Assumptions are
labeled, alternatives are acknowledged, and conclusions are stated no more
broadly than the record supports.

## Venue in Grant County and the federal courts

Warden is a Grant County community, and civil claims arising there proceed
in state court in the Superior Court, with Washington's rules governing
expert disclosure, deposition practice, and trial testimony. The federal
forums available in Washington are the United States District Court for the
Eastern District of Washington and the United States District Court for the
Western District of Washington; the district that controls any particular
case, together with whatever qualification or registration that court asks
of a testifying expert, is confirmed as part of the conflict check and
intake. The distinction matters for scheduling and disclosure format, not
for rigor, because the report is prepared to a single standard regardless
of forum.

## A small town inside a working regional market

Warden is a small community, and its residents participate in the labor
market of the wider Moses Lake area, so the earning-capacity analysis
begins by defining that regional market honestly. Findings rest on Bureau
of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the
region and for Washington statewide, on state workforce agency labor market
information, and on direct labor market research where the questions
warrant it. Where the damages case requires the vocational conclusions to
be reduced to economic loss, the firm offers [coordinated damages
engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) in which
independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis while
the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement
structure.
