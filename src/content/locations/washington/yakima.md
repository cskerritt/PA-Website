Catastrophic injury cases in the Yakima, WA metropolitan area turn on a
question that outlasts the liability fight: what will this person's care
actually require, and what will it cost where they live? Purinton Analytics
answers that question through [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/)
and [medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/) grounded
in the evaluee's own market, alongside
[vocational expert work](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) on
employability and earning capacity. Plaintiff and defense counsel receive
the same methodology and the same documentation standard; the opinion is
written so that either side of the caption can test it.

## Venues serving Yakima County

Engagements are accepted for matters venued in the Superior Court of Yakima
County and in the federal courts of the state, the United States District
Court for the Eastern District of Washington and the United States District
Court for the Western District of Washington. When a case requires economic
loss figures in addition to the vocational and care-planning analysis, the
firm offers
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. The statewide venue discussion, including the other
metro areas the firm serves, appears on the
[Washington page](/locations/washington/).

## By appointment in Yakima

Yakima meetings are by appointment. The firm has no staffed office in the
metro and publishes no street address for this location; that candor is
deliberate, because a practice that documents other people's economic
realities should describe its own accurately. Most of each engagement is
performed remotely, through records review, structured interviews by video,
and standardized testing under publisher protocols where remote
administration is supported. In-person evaluation in the evaluee's community
is available when the referral questions call for it, and travel for
evaluations, depositions, and trial is scheduled from the Kansas City home
office around the controlling court dates.

## Labor market and cost research for the Yakima metro

Vocational findings for evaluees in the metro rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Yakima
metropolitan area and for Washington statewide, along with state workforce
agency labor market information and direct labor market research where
warranted. A local employment base has its own occupational mix, and the
analysis is built on the market the evaluee actually faces rather than on
statewide generalities. Life care plans and medical cost projections for
Yakima County residents are priced through provider-specific inquiries and
published fee data for the region, so the resulting figures reflect what
care costs where the evaluee lives, not a national composite.

## Starting a Yakima engagement

The path into an engagement is the same for every referral: a conflict
check before any substantive discussion of the case. Submit the caption or
party names through the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and note any
imminent disclosure, deposition, or trial dates. Any venue-specific
registration or qualification requirement is identified and confirmed during
the conflict check and intake process. For matters or co-counsel outside
Washington, the firm's [national coverage](/locations/nationwide/) explains
how the same structure operates across jurisdictions.
