A damages case out of Forks deserves the same expert-witness discipline that
governs litigation in Washington's largest cities, and that is the standard
Purinton Analytics applies. The firm delivers
[vocational expert](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) assessments,

line.

## Venue for a Forks matter

Forks is part of Clallam County, so a state-law claim arising in town is
tried in the Superior Court, where Washington procedure sets the calendar for
expert disclosure, discovery, and testimony. Cases that are removed or filed
under federal law belong in the state's federal forums, the United States
District Court for the Western District of Washington or the United States
District Court for the Eastern District of Washington; the controlling
district for a given case, and any registration or qualification requirement
the presiding court imposes on experts, is identified and confirmed during
the conflict check and intake. The methodology never bends to the venue.
Conflict clearance comes before engagement, a written scope comes before
records, and the records request is tailored to the claims so the resulting
opinion stands on evidence available to each party.

## Taking a thin labor market seriously

The harder analytical problem in a Forks referral is usually the labor
market itself. In a very small community, the local employment base is
narrow, and an earning-capacity opinion must decide whether the realistic
market for this evaluee is the town, the wider commuting area that includes
the principal city of Port Angeles, or a broader regional or statewide
market reached only by relocation. That decision is made explicitly and
defended in the report, not assumed. The underlying data come from Bureau of
Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the relevant
Washington area and statewide, from state workforce agency labor market
information, and from direct labor market research when published sources
run thin, as they often do for small markets. Conditions across the
surrounding area are addressed on the
[Port Angeles metro page](/locations/washington/port-angeles/).

## Service without a storefront

Purinton Analytics maintains no office in Forks and lists no street address
here; candor about presence is part of the same discipline that governs the
reports. Counsel meetings are scheduled by appointment. Evaluee interviews
and assessments are conducted over secure video, or in person in the
evaluee's own community when the referral questions require it, and any
travel for evaluation, deposition, or trial is arranged from the Kansas City
home office. Where the case also needs economic loss quantification, the
firm offers
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. Washington-wide venue context is gathered on the
[state page](/locations/washington/), and the scope of the national practice
is set out in the [nationwide overview](/locations/nationwide/).
