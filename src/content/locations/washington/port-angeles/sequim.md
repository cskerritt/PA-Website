## The labor market question in a small town

Earning-capacity analysis for a Sequim evaluee turns on an honest definition
of the relevant labor market, because employment prospects in a small
community differ from those in the metro's principal city of Port Angeles,
and both differ from statewide conditions. Vocational findings rest on Bureau
of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the
relevant Washington area and for the state as a whole, on state workforce
agency labor market information, and on direct labor market research where
the referral questions warrant it. The report states which market applies and
why, treats the commuting reach realistic for this particular evaluee as part
of that definition, and keeps transferable-skills conclusions inside the
defined market. Context for the surrounding area appears on the
[Port Angeles metro page](/locations/washington/port-angeles/).
