A defensible vocational opinion does not depend on the expert's zip code. It
depends on method: a documented conflict check, a defined scope, records
gathered before conclusions are drawn, and findings tied to sources the
other side can verify. That is what Purinton Analytics brings to Moses Lake
litigation for plaintiff and defense counsel, through
[vocational expert witness services](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Grant County courts and federal venues

The Moses Lake micropolitan area consists of Grant County, and engagements
are accepted for matters venued in the Superior Court of Grant County, the
trial court of general jurisdiction for the area. When a claim proceeds in
federal court, the firm appears in the United States District Court for the
Eastern District of Washington or the United States District Court for the
Western District of Washington, depending on where the case is filed. The
written report follows the same structure in state and federal court, so
that a change of forum never means a change of standard. Counsel weighing
venues elsewhere in the state can start from the firm's
[Washington page](/locations/washington/).

## Remote-first work, appearances when they matter

Moses Lake engagements are handled by appointment; the firm keeps no staffed
office in the area and publishes no street address for this location,
because honesty about presence is part of the same discipline that governs
the opinions. Records review, structured interviews, and standardized
testing under publisher protocols proceed remotely where remote
administration is supported, which lets the analysis begin as soon as the
records arrive. In-person evaluation, deposition attendance, and trial
testimony are scheduled as travel from the Kansas City home office, planned
against the case calendar so that counsel are never guessing about the
expert's availability at a critical date.

## Labor market evidence for Grant County evaluees

Employability and earning capacity conclusions rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the relevant area
and for Washington statewide, on state workforce agency labor market
information, and on direct labor market research when the referral questions
call for contact with actual employers. Where the file requires future care
costing, [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) and
[medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/) are priced to
the evaluee's own market with provider-specific inquiries and published fee
data, since costs in a smaller regional market are not reliably captured by
national averages. Each figure in the report carries its source.

## Opening a Moses Lake engagement

Whether the referral comes from the plaintiff's side or the defense, intake
follows the same path. Send the caption or party names through the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and note any imminent disclosure,
deposition, or trial dates; scope and schedule are confirmed in writing
before work begins. Any venue-specific registration or qualification
requirement is identified and confirmed during the conflict check and intake
process. Firms with matters beyond Washington can review the practice's
[national coverage](/locations/nationwide/) for the same services under the
same report standard.
