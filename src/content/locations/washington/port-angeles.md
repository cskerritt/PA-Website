An evaluee in Port Angeles should not have to travel across the state to be
evaluated, and an opinion about that evaluee's employability should not be
written from a distance without accounting for where the person actually
lives and works. Purinton Analytics structures its Clallam County
engagements around both principles, offering plaintiff and defense counsel
[vocational expert evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Clallam County venues, state and federal

The Port Angeles micropolitan area consists of Clallam County, and matters
in state court are venued in the Superior Court of Clallam County, the trial
court of general jurisdiction for the area. Federal claims proceed in the
United States District Court for the Western District of Washington or the
United States District Court for the Eastern District of Washington,
depending on where the case is filed. Each engagement begins with a
documented conflict check, a defined scope, and a records request tailored
to the claims, so the resulting opinion stands on a record both sides can
examine in any of these forums. The firm's statewide reach is described on
the [Washington page](/locations/washington/).

## How the evaluation reaches the evaluee

Much of the work travels well: records review, structured interviews, and
standardized testing under publisher protocols proceed remotely where remote
administration is supported, which spares evaluees unnecessary trips and
keeps the schedule moving. When the referral questions require an in-person
evaluation, the evaluation is brought to the evaluee's community, with
travel scheduled from the Kansas City home office; the same planning covers
deposition and trial appearances. Port Angeles meetings are by appointment
only. The firm has no staffed office there and publishes no street address
for this location, because being precise about presence is part of the same
discipline that governs its written opinions.

## Labor market research on the ground

Employability and earning capacity findings for Clallam County evaluees rest
on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for
the relevant area and for Washington statewide, on state workforce agency
labor market information, and on direct labor market research when the
referral questions call for contact with employers who actually hire in the
evaluee's market. Life care plans and
[medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/) apply the
same locality standard, pricing future care to the evaluee's own community
through provider-specific inquiries and published fee data instead of
defaulting to national averages. Every input is cited so opposing counsel
can follow the trail from source to conclusion.

## Opening a Port Angeles engagement

Referrals from either side of the caption start with the same step: submit
the caption or party names through the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and note any imminent disclosure,
deposition, or trial dates. Scope, schedule, and fees are confirmed in
writing before analysis begins, and any venue-specific registration or
qualification requirement is identified and confirmed during the conflict
check and intake process. For matters beyond Washington, the firm's
[national coverage](/locations/nationwide/) offers the same services under
the same report standard.
