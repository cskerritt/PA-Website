## Courts serving Spokane Valley matters

Spokane Valley sits in Spokane County, and civil actions arising there are
heard in the Spokane County Superior Court under Washington's rules
governing expert disclosure and testimony. Washington's federal trial
forums are the United States District Court for the Eastern District of
Washington and the United States District Court for the Western District
of Washington; which forum controls a given matter, and any registration
or qualification requirement that forum imposes, is confirmed during the
conflict check and intake rather than assumed. Reports are prepared so
that a trier of fact in any of these courts can follow each finding back
to a named record or published source.
