## The courts behind a Cheney case

Cheney is part of Spokane County, and its state-court civil litigation is
heard in the Spokane County Superior Court under Washington's expert
disclosure and testimony rules. Federal matters in Washington proceed in
the United States District Court for the Eastern District of Washington
or the United States District Court for the Western District of
Washington; identifying which of those forums governs a specific claim,
and any expert qualification requirement that follows from it, is part of
the conflict check and intake rather than a guess made on a landing page.
Whatever the forum, the report is written so its reasoning can be traced
by the judge, the jury, and the opposing expert.

## Earning capacity measured against the regional economy

A Cheney evaluee's realistic employment options usually extend into the
surrounding metropolitan economy, so the vocational analysis is grounded
in Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates
for the Spokane metropolitan area and for Washington statewide, augmented
by state workforce agency labor market information and direct labor
market research when warranted. The report states the assumed commuting
range and explains why it fits the evaluee's circumstances, which keeps
the earning-capacity conclusion anchored to stated premises instead of
impressions. The metro-wide analysis is described on the
[Spokane metro page](/locations/washington/spokane/), and statewide
material appears on the [Washington state page](/locations/washington/).

## What by-appointment service means here

The firm keeps no Cheney office and publishes no local address, because
accuracy about presence is part of the same discipline that governs its
opinions. Engagements are coordinated from the Kansas City home office;
meetings with counsel are by appointment, evaluations are scheduled
around the evaluee's needs, and travel for depositions or trial is
arranged when the litigation calendar requires presence. If the case also
calls for an economic loss analysis, the firm offers
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. The same service model, applied nationally, is
outlined in the [nationwide overview](/locations/nationwide/).
