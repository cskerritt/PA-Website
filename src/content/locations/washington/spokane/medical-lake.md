The record decides what the opinion may say. That is the operating rule
Purinton Analytics applies to every Medical Lake referral: medical
records, employment history, education, and earnings documentation are
requested in writing, reviewed against the claims, and cited in the
report, and no
[vocational expert witness](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) finding
or [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) recommendation is
offered that the assembled file cannot support. Counsel for either party
gets the same treatment, because a record-bound opinion is the only kind
worth sponsoring in front of a jury.

## Where a Medical Lake claim is litigated

Medical Lake lies in Spokane County, and its state-court civil actions
are heard in the Spokane County Superior Court under Washington's expert
disclosure and testimony rules. For federal matters, Washington offers
the United States District Court for the Eastern District of Washington
and the United States District Court for the Western District of
Washington; the forum that controls a specific case, together with any
expert qualification or registration requirement it carries, is
identified during the conflict check and intake rather than presumed
here. The [Washington state page](/locations/washington/) surveys the
statewide venue picture for counsel weighing their options.

## Grounding the vocational opinion in published data

For a Medical Lake evaluee, the employment analysis reaches into the
surrounding metropolitan economy, and the data must reach with it.
Findings rely on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and
wage estimates for the Spokane metropolitan area and for Washington
statewide, on state workforce agency labor market information, and on
direct labor market research when a referral question requires
occupation-level confirmation. Each data source appears in the report by
name, alongside the reasoning that connects it to the evaluee's residual
capacity and transferable skills, so the path from evidence to
earning-capacity conclusion is visible on the page. The metro-wide
treatment is available on the
[Spokane metro page](/locations/washington/spokane/).

## Practical arrangements for a Medical Lake evaluee

There is no Medical Lake office, and the firm does not suggest one;
stating its footprint accurately is part of the same habit of precision
the reports depend on. Engagements are administered from the Kansas City
home office, with evaluations by secure video or in person in the
evaluee's own community, and with travel for depositions and trial
scheduled around the litigation calendar. If economic loss calculations
are needed alongside the vocational and care-planning work, the firm
coordinates
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. The [nationwide overview](/locations/nationwide/)
explains how the model functions in courts across the country.

## Getting the file started

Nothing substantive happens before clearance. Route the caption or party
names through the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/), flag any
imminent disclosure, deposition, or trial dates, and expect a written
scope and a records request tailored to the claims promptly once
conflicts clear. From that point the Medical Lake file grows the
way the final report will read, document by document, with every source
accounted for.
