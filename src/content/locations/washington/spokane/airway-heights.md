Start with the forum, because the forum sets the rules the expert must
satisfy. An Airway Heights civil case in state court will move through
the Spokane County Superior Court, with its own expectations for expert
disclosure, deposition practice, and trial testimony, and a claim in
federal court will answer to a different set of standards. Purinton
Analytics prepares every
[vocational expert witness](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) report
and [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) opinion to hold
up under whichever set applies, for counsel on the plaintiff side or the
defense side, so the choice of venue changes the procedure but never the
rigor.

## State and federal paths for Airway Heights claims

Airway Heights sits in Spokane County, making the Spokane County Superior
Court the state trial forum for most civil disputes arising there.
Washington's federal trial courts are the United States District Court
for the Eastern District of Washington and the United States District
Court for the Western District of Washington; which district controls a
particular case, and whether that court imposes any registration or
qualification step on a testifying expert, is confirmed during the
conflict check and intake. The firm treats venue as a fact to verify, not
to assume, and the engagement letter records the answer before the
analysis begins. Statewide court structure is summarized on the
[Washington state page](/locations/washington/).

## The labor market around a small city

Airway Heights is a small city whose residents draw employment from the
wider metropolitan economy, and the vocational analysis reflects that
reality. Findings rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational
employment and wage estimates for the Spokane metropolitan area and for
Washington statewide, on state workforce agency labor market information,
and on direct labor market research when a referral question turns on a
specific occupation or employer type. The report identifies the market it
uses and defends that choice explicitly, which is what allows the opinion
to survive a methodical cross-examination. Regional context is developed
on the [Spokane metro page](/locations/washington/spokane/).

No Airway Heights office exists, and the firm will not manufacture the

starting with its own footprint. Work is coordinated from the Kansas City
home office, evaluations proceed by secure video or in person in the
