stand is usually the simplest one: what jobs are realistically available
to this person, in this place? A small Stevens County town cannot be
analyzed as if it were a principal city, and an opinion that borrows

## Market definition where the market is thin

The analytical core of a Chewelah engagement is a defensible answer to
the geography question. The firm starts from Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates for the Spokane metropolitan
area and for Washington statewide, then tests those figures against state
workforce agency labor market information and direct labor market
research to establish what an evaluee in a small community can actually
reach. Where commuting assumptions expand or contract the opportunity
set, the report says so explicitly and gives the reasoning, because a
transparent adjustment is persuasive and a silent one is a liability. The
metro-scale analysis that frames this work appears on the
[Spokane metro page](/locations/washington/spokane/).
