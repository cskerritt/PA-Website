## Venue footprint in Cowlitz County

The Longview-Kelso metropolitan area consists of Cowlitz County, and
engagements are accepted for matters venued in the Superior Court of
Cowlitz County, the trial court of general jurisdiction there. Federal
matters proceed in the United States District Court for the Western District
of Washington or the United States District Court for the Eastern District
of Washington, according to where the action is filed. The report is written
to withstand examination in any of these forums: a documented conflict
check, a defined scope, and a records request tailored to the claims come
before any opinion is formed. Statewide venue coverage, including the other
metro areas the firm serves, is described on the
[Washington page](/locations/washington/).

## How by-appointment service works here

There is no staffed Purinton Analytics office in Longview or Kelso, and the
firm publishes no street address for this location; candor about presence is
part of the same discipline that shapes the written work. What counsel get
instead is a remote-first process that begins with records review,
structured interviews, and standardized testing under publisher protocols
where remote administration is supported. When an in-person evaluation is
warranted, or when a deposition or trial date requires the expert in the
room, travel is arranged from the Kansas City home office and coordinated
with counsel's calendar well before the appearance date.

## Grounding opinions in the local labor market

Employability and earning capacity findings for evaluees in the metro draw
on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for
the relevant area and for Washington statewide, together with state
workforce agency labor market information and direct labor market research
where the referral questions justify employer-level inquiry. Life care
plans and [medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/) are
priced to the evaluee's own community using provider-specific inquiries and
published fee data, because the cost of future care in a regional market can
differ materially from national averages. Every source is cited so that
opposing counsel can check the arithmetic and the assumptions behind it.
