An injured worker or catastrophically injured plaintiff in Woodland should
not have to organize a case around an expert's travel preferences. Purinton
Analytics structures its engagements the other way around: the
[vocational expert witness](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) interview
and the [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) assessment are
brought to the evaluee, by secure video or in person in the evaluee's own
community, while the analysis itself is held to the same standard the firm
applies in major-city litigation. Plaintiff and defense counsel retain the
practice on equal terms, and every report is written on the assumption that
opposing counsel will read it closely.

## How a Woodland evaluation actually proceeds

The sequence is deliberately unremarkable. After the conflict check clears,
counsel receives a written scope and a records request tailored to the
claims. Records are reviewed before anyone is interviewed, so the
evaluation tests the file rather than repeats it. The interview and any
in-person assessment are then scheduled around the evaluee, in Woodland or
wherever the evaluee now lives, with travel arranged from the Kansas City
home office when presence is required. The firm keeps no staffed office in
Woodland and publishes no street address here; candor about that is part of
the same discipline that governs the opinions themselves.

## Venue context in Cowlitz County and beyond

Woodland lies in Cowlitz County, and state-court claims arising in town are
tried in the Superior Court under Washington's civil rules for expert
disclosure, deposition, and trial testimony. For matters that belong in
federal court, Washington's available forums are the United States District
Court for the Eastern District of Washington and the United States District
Court for the Western District of Washington; the venue that controls a
given case, and any expert registration or qualification requirement that
venue carries, is identified and confirmed during the conflict check and
intake. Counsel comparing venues across the metro will find the broader
picture on the [Longview metro page](/locations/washington/longview/) and
statewide coverage on the [Washington state page](/locations/washington/).

## Defining the labor market for a small-city evaluee

Woodland is a small city within the Longview-Kelso metro, and its workers
routinely look beyond the town line for employment. An earning-capacity
opinion here must therefore say precisely which market it is measuring.
Vocational findings draw on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational
employment and wage estimates for the Longview area and for Washington
statewide, together with state workforce agency labor market information and
direct labor market research where the questions warrant it. An opinion that quietly
substitutes national averages for the market a Woodland worker actually
faces should not survive a careful deposition. The reports here commit in
writing to the market they measured, and the transferable-skills analysis
extends no further than the employment this particular evaluee can
realistically reach and hold. When the case also requires economic loss
calculations, the firm offers
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. The practice's full geographic reach is described in
the [nationwide overview](/locations/nationwide/).

Before any scheduling conversation, run the conflict check. Send the caption
or party names through the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/), flag any
