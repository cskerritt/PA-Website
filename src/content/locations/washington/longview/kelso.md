## Superior Court venue in Cowlitz County

Kelso civil claims filed in state court are heard in the Superior Court,
where Washington's civil rules govern expert disclosure, deposition
practice, and testimony at trial. Matters that belong in federal court
instead proceed in one of Washington's federal forums, the United States
District Court for the Eastern District of Washington or the United States
District Court for the Western District of Washington. Which venue controls
a particular case, and any registration or qualification requirement that
venue imposes on a testifying expert, is identified and confirmed during the
conflict check and intake process. The working method does not vary with the
courthouse: every engagement opens with a documented conflict check, a
defined written scope, and a records request tailored to the claims, so that
the eventual opinion rests on a record both sides can examine.

## A labor market that carries the town's name

Vocational findings for a Kelso evaluee rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates for the Longview area and for
Washington statewide, supplemented by state workforce agency labor market
information and by direct labor market research when the referral questions
call for it. Market definition is treated in the report as a conclusion to
be defended, not a default to be assumed: no transferable-skills finding is
offered until the opinion has said which market it measured and shown that
this evaluee can realistically work in it. The wider metro context, and how
the surrounding communities relate to that data, is set out on the
[Longview metro page](/locations/washington/longview/).
