Litigation in the Kennewick-Richland, WA metropolitan area moves through the
Superior Court of Benton County, the Superior Court of Franklin County, and
the federal courts of the state, and Purinton Analytics accepts engagements
in all of them, for plaintiff and defense counsel alike. The federal venues
are the United States District Court for the Eastern District of Washington
and the United States District Court for the Western District of Washington.
Whatever the forum, the referral questions usually sound in
[earning capacity and employability](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), or
[medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/), and the
opinion is built the same way in each: defined scope, tailored records
request, documented methodology.

## The venue footprint in Benton County and Franklin County

The firm treats the metro as a unified venue footprint. A matter filed in
Benton County and a companion claim in Franklin County can proceed under a
shared engagement structure, with consistent assumptions and a consistent
record across the related files. Where a case needs economic loss analysis
alongside the vocational and care-planning work, the firm offers
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. Counsel litigating elsewhere in the state can review
the firm's [Washington page](/locations/washington/) for the statewide
venue discussion.

## Appointments in Kennewick and Richland, honestly described

There is no Purinton Analytics office in Kennewick or Richland, and the firm

protocols where remote administration is supported. When in-person

to protect the court's calendar. The report standard and the point of
contact do not change with the venue or the travel arrangements.

## Labor market evidence for the Kennewick-Richland metro

Employability and earning capacity conclusions for evaluees in the metro

estimates and check the analysis against them. Life care plans and medical
