Purinton Analytics serves plaintiff and defense counsel across the
Seattle-Tacoma-Bellevue, WA metropolitan area with
[earning capacity and employability evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/)
in injury and employment matters, [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/)
in catastrophic injury cases, and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. The referral may come from a plaintiff firm in Seattle
or a defense team in Tacoma; either way the engagement begins the same way,
with a documented conflict check, a defined scope, and a records request
tailored to the claims, so that the written opinion rests on a record both
sides can examine.

## Venues in King County, Pierce County, and Snohomish County

Engagements are accepted for matters venued in the Superior Court of King
County, the Superior Court of Pierce County, and the Superior Court of
Snohomish County, and in the federal courts that serve the state, the United
States District Court for the Western District of Washington and the United
States District Court for the Eastern District of Washington. The work
product does not change with the courthouse: the same methodology,
documentation standard, and disclosure practice apply in a state Superior
Court bench trial and a federal jury trial alike. Counsel with matters
elsewhere in the state will find the full venue discussion on the firm's
[Washington page](/locations/washington/).

## By appointment in Seattle, Tacoma, and Bellevue

Meetings across the metro are by appointment. The firm keeps no staffed
office in Seattle, Tacoma, or Bellevue and publishes no street address for
this location, because accuracy about offices is part of the same discipline
that governs its reports. Most engagements proceed remotely from intake
onward: records review, structured interviews by video, and standardized
testing under publisher protocols where remote administration is supported.
When the referral questions call for in-person evaluation, or when deposition
and trial calendars require presence, travel is scheduled from the Kansas
City home office and planned around the court's dates rather than the firm's
convenience. Counsel receive the same report standard and the same point of
contact regardless of where the work is performed.

## Labor market research for the Seattle metro

Vocational findings for evaluees in the metro rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the
Seattle-Tacoma-Bellevue metropolitan area and for Washington statewide,
supplemented by state workforce agency labor market information and direct
labor market research where the referral questions warrant it. Life care
plans and medical cost projections for residents of King County, Pierce
County, and Snohomish County are priced to the evaluee's own market using
provider-specific inquiries and published fee data, not national averages
applied by default. Where a claim turns on transferable skills, the analysis
documents the occupational base actually available to the evaluee rather
than a generic listing.

## Starting a Seattle engagement

The practical starting point for any matter in the metro is a conflict
check. Submit the caption or party names through the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and note any imminent disclosure,
deposition, or trial dates so scheduling can be addressed at intake. Any
venue-specific registration or qualification requirement is identified and
confirmed during the conflict check and intake process. Counsel weighing a
retention that spans jurisdictions can review the firm's
[national coverage](/locations/nationwide/) for how the same engagement
structure operates outside Washington.
