Purinton Analytics works both sides of the caption in Centralia, and the
work product does not change with the client. A plaintiff firm retaining the
practice for [earning capacity and employability evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/)
receives the same methodology, the same sourcing discipline, and the same
report structure as a defense firm retaining it for
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/). The same holds
for [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) in catastrophic
injury matters and for
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Where Centralia cases are heard

The Centralia micropolitan area consists of Lewis County. State court
engagements are venued in the Superior Court of Lewis County, the trial
court of general jurisdiction for the area, and federal matters proceed in
the United States District Court for the Western District of Washington or
the United States District Court for the Eastern District of Washington,
depending on where the action is filed. Every engagement, whatever the
forum, opens with a documented conflict check, a defined scope, and a
records request tailored to the claims, so the written opinion rests on a
record either party can examine. The firm's full footprint in the state is
described on the [Washington page](/locations/washington/).

## Presence, stated plainly

There is no Purinton Analytics office in Centralia. Meetings are by
appointment, and the firm publishes no street address for this location,
because accuracy about presence belongs to the same discipline that governs
every report it signs. Counsel get a remote-first process in its place:
records review, structured interviews with the evaluee, and standardized
testing under publisher protocols where remote administration is supported.
When the referral questions require an in-person evaluation, or when
deposition and trial dates put the expert in the courtroom, travel is
scheduled from the Kansas City home office and confirmed against counsel's
deadlines in advance.

## Reading the Lewis County labor market

Vocational findings for evaluees in the area rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the relevant area
and for Washington statewide, on state workforce agency labor market
information, and on direct labor market research where the referral
questions call for employer-level detail. Future care costs are handled
with the same locality discipline: life care plans and
[medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/) are priced to
the evaluee's own market through provider-specific inquiries and published
fee data rather than borrowed national averages. Sources are cited
throughout, so cross-examination can start from the report itself rather
than from speculation about where its numbers came from.

## Beginning a Lewis County engagement

Intake is identical for plaintiff and defense referrals. Submit the caption
or party names through the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/), note any
imminent disclosure, deposition, or trial dates, and expect scope and
schedule to be confirmed in writing before analysis begins. Any
venue-specific registration or qualification requirement is identified and
confirmed during the conflict check and intake process. For related matters
outside Washington, the firm's [national coverage](/locations/nationwide/)
applies the same standard everywhere the practice is engaged.
