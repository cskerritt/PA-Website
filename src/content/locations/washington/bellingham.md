An expert report earns its weight in the Bellingham, WA metropolitan area
the same way it does anywhere else: by surviving the scrutiny of the lawyer
on the other side. Purinton Analytics writes every opinion to that standard.
Methodology is stated rather than implied, sources are cited so they can be
pulled and checked, and the file is organized for production from the start.
That publication discipline runs through the firm's
[vocational expert work](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) on
employability and earning capacity, its
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) in catastrophic injury
matters, and its [rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/)
of opposing expert reports, for plaintiff and defense counsel alike.

## Whatcom County venues, state and federal

Engagements are accepted for matters venued in the Superior Court of
Whatcom County and in the federal courts serving the state, the United
States District Court for the Western District of Washington and the United
States District Court for the Eastern District of Washington. Where a matter
needs economic loss analysis alongside the vocational and care-planning
work, the firm offers
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. Counsel with matters across the state can consult the
firm's [Washington page](/locations/washington/) for the full venue
discussion.

## Appointments in Bellingham, described accurately

The firm keeps no staffed office in Bellingham and publishes no street
address for this location. Meetings are by appointment, and that fact is
stated plainly because honesty about presence belongs to the same discipline
that governs the reports. Engagements typically proceed remotely from
intake: records review, structured interviews by video, and standardized
testing under publisher protocols where remote administration is supported.
When the referral questions require in-person evaluation, or when deposition
and trial calendars require attendance in Whatcom County, travel is
scheduled from the Kansas City home office with lead time planned around the
court's deadlines. Counsel work with a consistent point of contact
throughout.

## Labor market research for the Bellingham metro

Employability and earning capacity conclusions for evaluees in the metro
rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage
estimates for the Bellingham metropolitan area and for Washington statewide,
supplemented by state workforce agency labor market information and direct
labor market research where the questions warrant it. Because those
estimates are published, opposing counsel can verify every occupational and
wage premise in the report, which is precisely the point of using them. Life
care plans and medical cost projections for Whatcom County residents are
priced to the evaluee's own market through provider-specific inquiries and
published fee data rather than national averages applied by default.
