The practical question counsel usually ask about a Moxee referral is
logistical: how does a national expert practice evaluate someone who lives
in a small town, and what does the evaluee have to do to make that happen?
The answer is that the process comes to the evaluee. Purinton Analytics
handles [vocational expert](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) and
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) engagements for Moxee
matters remotely wherever remote work serves the case, and in the
evaluee's own community when an in-person evaluation is warranted, so that
an injured person is never asked to organize their recovery around an
expert's convenience.

## How the evaluation reaches Moxee

Records review, counsel conferences, and scheduling all proceed remotely
from the start. Clinical and vocational interviews are conducted by
secure video when that is appropriate to the referral questions, and in
person, locally to the evaluee, when the assessment requires direct
observation. Follow-up is handled the same way. When the case moves to
deposition or trial, appearance is arranged with travel scheduled from
the Kansas City home office. The firm keeps no office in Moxee and says
so; what counsel retains is the process, not a building.

## The county and the courts

Moxee sits in Yakima County. Its state-court civil matters proceed in
the Superior Court under Washington's rules governing expert disclosure
and testimony. Federal litigation in the state is divided between the
United States District Court for the Eastern District of Washington and
the United States District Court for the Western District of Washington;
which of those forums controls a specific Moxee case, and whatever
qualification requirements that forum imposes, is identified and
confirmed during the conflict check and intake. Broader venue notes for
the region appear on the [Yakima metro page](/locations/washington/yakima/)
and the [Washington state page](/locations/washington/).

## A small town inside a metro labor market

Moxee is a small community whose workers participate in the Yakima
metropolitan labor market, and the vocational analysis reflects both
facts. Earning-capacity opinions rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates for the Yakima metro area and
for Washington statewide, on state workforce agency labor market
information, and on direct labor market research when the questions
demand it. The opinion describes the work realistically available to
this evaluee from where they live, stated qualitatively and sourced
precisely, without extrapolating from markets the evaluee cannot reach.

## The written product and its standards

Every Moxee engagement produces a report written to be read by opposing
counsel: scope defined at the outset, methodology explained, sources
cited, conclusions confined to the record. The same standard applies
whether the firm is retained by the plaintiff or the defense, and it is
the same standard applied across the firm's
[national practice](/locations/nationwide/). Where the damages
presentation requires economic quantification, independent economist
partners perform and sign that analysis through a
[coordinated damages engagement](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/),
keeping the vocational and care-planning work under one engagement
structure.

names through the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/), describe the
