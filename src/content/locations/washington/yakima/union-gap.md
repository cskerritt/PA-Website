A vocational opinion earns nothing from the size of the town it concerns.
A Union Gap case deserves, and gets, the same report discipline the firm
brings to litigation in the largest markets it serves: a documented
conflict check before substantive work, a defined written scope, a records
request tailored to the claims, and conclusions that follow from the
record. Purinton Analytics provides
[vocational expert witness](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) services
and [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) to plaintiff and
defense counsel in Union Gap matters on exactly those terms.

## What report discipline means in practice

Every opinion is drafted for the reader who wants to take it apart. That
means methodology is described so it can be replicated, data sources are
cited so they can be checked, and the limits of the analysis are
acknowledged rather than papered over. Where the record is thin, the
report says so instead of speculating. Counsel who need an opposing
expert's work examined under the same lens can retain the firm for
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/), and where a
matter needs medical costs projected without a full plan, a
[medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/) can be
scoped separately.

## Courts serving Union Gap

Union Gap's civil docket runs through Yakima County: state-law claims are
heard in the Superior Court under Washington procedure. Federal-question
and diversity matters proceed in the United States District Court for the
Eastern District of Washington or the United States District Court for
the Western District of Washington, and the district that actually
controls a given Union Gap case, along with any expert registration or
qualification requirement, is identified and confirmed during the
conflict check and intake. Venue context for the wider area is collected
on the [Yakima metro page](/locations/washington/yakima/).

## Situating Union Gap in the metro economy

Union Gap is a small city within the Yakima metropolitan labor market,
and the vocational analysis treats it that way: the evaluee's realistic
employment prospects are assessed against Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates for the Yakima metro area and
for Washington statewide, informed by state workforce agency labor market
information and, where warranted, direct contact with the labor market
itself. No figure appears in a report that cannot be traced to a source,
and no market conclusion is asserted that the data will not carry.

## Logistics for counsel and evaluees

As explained on the [Washington state page](/locations/washington/) and
the [nationwide page](/locations/nationwide/), the practice is remote-first
and by appointment everywhere, with no staffed office in Union Gap and no

trial appearances are planned early, with travel from the Kansas City home

Engagement begins with clearance, not commitment. Use the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) to submit the caption or party

trial dates. Once conflicts clear, scope and schedule are confirmed in
