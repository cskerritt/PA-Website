Plaintiff and defense counsel bring different questions to a Wapato
retention. One side asks what an injury has taken from a working life; the
other asks whether the claimed loss is supported by the record and the
labor market. Purinton Analytics is retained by both, and the answer it
gives either side comes from the same methodology, because a
[vocational opinion](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) or
[life care plan](/services/life-care-planning/) that changes with the
retention is not evidence, it is advocacy. That symmetry is the premise of
every Wapato engagement the firm accepts.

## The same analysis from either chair

For a plaintiff referral, the work identifies residual capacity,
transferable skills, realistic post-injury occupations, and the care an
injury will require, all documented against the record. For a defense
referral, the work tests those same elements in an opposing expert's
report, through [rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/)
or an independent evaluation. In both postures the deliverable is
disclosure-ready, the assumptions are stated, and the conclusions are the
ones the record supports, whether or not they are the ones the retaining
lawyer hoped for.

## Where Wapato cases are heard

Wapato is a Yakima County town, and its state-court civil claims proceed
in the Superior Court under Washington procedure. Federal matters belong
to the United States District Court for the Eastern District of
Washington or the United States District Court for the Western District
of Washington; the firm confirms the controlling district for each case,
along with any expert qualification requirement the forum imposes, during
the conflict check and intake. Regional venue context is gathered on the
[Yakima metro page](/locations/washington/yakima/), and statewide practice
is described on the [Washington page](/locations/washington/).

## Grounding the labor market opinion

Wapato is a small town whose residents look to the surrounding Yakima
metropolitan area for much of their employment. Vocational findings
therefore rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and
wage estimates for the Yakima metro area and for Washington statewide,
supported by state workforce agency labor market information and direct
labor market research where the referral warrants it. Market scale is
characterized qualitatively; specific claims are sourced; and the
distance between what the data show and what either side might wish they
showed is left visible in the report.

## Practical arrangements

The firm serves Wapato by appointment as part of a
[national, remote-first practice](/locations/nationwide/). It has no
office in Wapato and does not suggest otherwise. Interviews and
evaluations are conducted remotely or in the evaluee's own community as
the referral requires, and when testimony must be given in person, travel
is scheduled from the Kansas City home office against the court's
calendar. Counsel on either side work with the same engagement structure
from intake through trial.
