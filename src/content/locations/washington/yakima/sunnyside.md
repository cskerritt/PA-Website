When an injury interrupts a working life in Sunnyside, the questions that
decide damages are vocational before they are economic: what work the person
can still perform, what that work actually pays in the market where the
person lives, and what care the injury will demand over a lifetime. Purinton
Analytics answers those questions for counsel on either side of the caption,
providing [vocational expert witness](/services/vocational-expert-witness/)
opinions and [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) for
Sunnyside matters as part of a [national practice](/locations/nationwide/)
built around written opinions that survive cross-examination.

## Yakima County venue and the federal forums

Sunnyside sits in Yakima County, and state-court claims arising there
proceed in the Superior Court, where Washington's civil rules govern expert
disclosure and testimony. Federal practice in the state runs through the
United States District Court for the Eastern District of Washington and the
United States District Court for the Western District of Washington; which
district controls a given matter, and any qualification requirement the
forum imposes on a testifying expert, is identified and confirmed during
the conflict check and intake. The engagement is structured the same way
regardless of forum: a documented conflict check, a defined scope, and a
records request tailored to the claims, so that the opinion rests on a
record both sides can examine.

## Measuring earning capacity where the evaluee lives

A Sunnyside resident competes for work in the broader labor market
described on the [Yakima metro page](/locations/washington/yakima/), and an
earning-capacity opinion that ignores that context invites impeachment.
Vocational findings for Sunnyside evaluees rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Yakima
metropolitan area and for Washington statewide, on state workforce agency
labor market information, and on direct labor market research where the
referral questions warrant it. The analysis is qualitative about place and
rigorous about method: it identifies the occupations realistically open to
the evaluee given residual capacity, transferable skills, and the work that
actually exists within reach of Sunnyside, without inflating or discounting
the market to fit a litigation theory.

## How a Sunnyside evaluation proceeds

Meetings are by appointment. The firm maintains no office in Sunnyside and
publishes no local address, because accuracy about presence is part of the
same discipline that governs the reports. Records review, counsel
conferences, and most interviews proceed remotely without any loss of
rigor. When the referral calls for in-person evaluation, the evaluation is
conducted in the evaluee's own community rather than forcing travel on an
injured person, and when deposition or trial requires presence, travel is
scheduled from the Kansas City home office. The same standards described on
the [Washington state page](/locations/washington/) apply to every
engagement in the state.
