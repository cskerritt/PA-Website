## The forums available to a Toppenish case

the Superior Court. Cases with a federal basis are heard in Washington's
federal forums, the United States District Court for the Eastern District
of Washington and the United States District Court for the Western District
of Washington. The firm does not guess at controlling venue; the district
with jurisdiction over a specific matter, and any expert qualification or
registration requirement that forum imposes, is identified and confirmed
during the conflict check and intake. Wherever the case lands, the working
method is constant: a documented conflict check, a written scope, and a
records request matched to the claims.

## What the opinion must withstand

An expert report in a Toppenish case will be read most closely by the
lawyer who opposes it. The firm therefore treats admissibility and
credibility as drafting constraints, not afterthoughts. Assumptions are
stated rather than buried, methodology is described in enough detail to be
replicated, and every vocational or care-planning conclusion is tied to a
cited record source. Where an opposing report requires scrutiny, the firm
also accepts [rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/)
engagements, applying the same standards to another expert's work that it
applies to its own.
