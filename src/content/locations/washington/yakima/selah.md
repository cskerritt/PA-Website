Selah residents earn their livings across the Yakima metropolitan area,
not just inside the town limits, and any honest measure of earning
capacity has to follow the worker rather than the map. That commuting
reality is where Purinton Analytics starts its
[vocational expert](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) analysis for
Selah matters: the relevant market is the one the evaluee can actually
reach, and the opinion is built on data describing that market rather than
on assumptions about it. The firm serves plaintiff and defense counsel
alike, pairing vocational opinions with
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) when the injuries call
for it.

## Reading the commuting market honestly

Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for
the Yakima metropolitan area, described more fully on the
[metro page](/locations/washington/yakima/), anchor the labor market side
of every Selah opinion, alongside statewide estimates for Washington,
state workforce agency labor market information, and direct labor market
research where the referral questions justify it. The analysis asks what
occupations remain open to the evaluee given residual functional capacity
and transferable skills, and whether those occupations are genuinely
available within a commute the evaluee can sustain. It reports
what the data show, including when the data cut against the retaining
party's theory.

## Venue for Selah claims

Selah is in Yakima County, and its state-court civil litigation proceeds
in the Superior Court under Washington's expert disclosure and testimony
rules. Federal claims are heard in the United States District Court for
the Eastern District of Washington or the United States District Court
for the Western District of Washington; controlling venue for any given
case, and any qualification requirement that forum imposes on a
testifying expert, is identified and confirmed during the conflict check
and intake. The firm's practice across the state's forums is summarized
on the [Washington page](/locations/washington/).

## Care planning built on the same record

When a Selah case involves catastrophic or permanent injury, the life
care plan is developed from the medical record, treating-provider input,
and published cost sources, and it is reconciled with the vocational
findings so the damages presentation holds together as a whole. Where
counsel needs the economic loss quantified, the firm arranges
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Appointments, not an office

The firm is a [national practice](/locations/nationwide/) and does not
maintain a Selah office; saying so plainly is part of the same accuracy
that governs its reports. Most work, from records review to counsel
conferences to many interviews, is done remotely. In-person evaluations,
when warranted, take place in the evaluee's own community, and deposition
or trial appearances are met with travel scheduled from the Kansas City
home office, arranged around the court's calendar.

## Opening a Selah file

Before any discussion of the merits, the firm runs a documented conflict
check. Submit the caption or parties through the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/), note the forum if known, and list
any disclosure or trial dates. Clearance, scope, and schedule come back
in writing, and the engagement proceeds the same way it would in any
venue in the country.
