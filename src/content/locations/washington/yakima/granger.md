Purinton Analytics keeps no office in Granger, and this page will not
pretend otherwise. The firm is a national, by-appointment practice whose
home office is in Kansas City, and it serves Granger the way it serves
every community in its [nationwide footprint](/locations/nationwide/):
remotely by default, in person when the work requires it, and honestly
about which is which. Counsel retaining a
[vocational expert](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) or
[life care planner](/services/life-care-planning/) for a Granger matter
get that candor in the marketing because it is the same candor that goes
into the reports.

## Why the honesty matters

An expert whose website invents a local presence has already shown
opposing counsel how the expert handles inconvenient facts. Accuracy
about offices is a small discipline, but it is continuous with the larger
ones: opinions confined to the record, assumptions disclosed, data
sources cited, and limitations acknowledged. A Granger jury will never
hear about the firm's address; it may well hear about whether the
expert's claims check out. The practice is built for the latter test.

## Granger's place in the court system

Granger lies in Yakima County, so its state-law civil claims are heard
in the Superior Court under Washington's expert disclosure and testimony
rules. Federal claims proceed in the United States District Court for
the Eastern District of Washington or the United States District Court
for the Western District of Washington, and the district controlling a
particular Granger case, together with any registration or qualification
requirement for a testifying expert, is identified and confirmed during
the conflict check and intake. The [Yakima metro page](/locations/washington/yakima/)
describes the surrounding venue landscape in more detail.

## Vocational evidence for a small town

Granger is a small town, and pretending it is a self-contained labor
market would be as false as pretending the firm has an office there. The
earning-capacity analysis for a Granger evaluee draws on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Yakima
metropolitan area and for Washington statewide, together with state
workforce agency labor market information and, where warranted, direct
labor market research. The opinion describes the employment realistically
open to the evaluee from Granger, qualitatively as to scale and precisely
as to source.

## What by-appointment service looks like

Records analysis, conferences with counsel, and much of the interviewing
proceed remotely. When the referral questions call for an in-person
evaluation, it is conducted in the evaluee's own community rather than at
a distant office, and deposition or trial appearances are met with travel
scheduled from the Kansas City home office and planned around the
court's calendar. The full scope of the firm's Washington work, including
the other communities it serves in the state, is set out on the
[Washington page](/locations/washington/), and the engagement structure
counsel sees in a Granger matter is the same structure used everywhere
the practice appears.

## The route to retention

Every engagement, plaintiff or defense, starts with a documented conflict
check. Send the caption or party names through the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/), sketch the claims, and flag any
imminent disclosure, deposition, or trial dates. Clearance, a written
scope, and a delivery schedule follow before substantive work begins.
