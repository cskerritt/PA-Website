Some Federal Way referrals ask Purinton Analytics to build an opinion from
the ground up; others ask the firm to test one that already exists. Both
kinds of work are accepted here, from plaintiff and defense counsel alike:
original [vocational expert witness](/services/vocational-expert-witness/)
opinions and [life care plans](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) of opposing
experts' reports, applying the same methodological standards in both
directions. An expert who critiques others' work for a living has a strong
incentive to keep its own beyond reproach, and that symmetry is the point.

## Venue for Federal Way disputes

Federal Way lies within King County, and state-law claims are heard in the
Superior Court, where Washington's civil rules control the disclosure and
testing of expert opinions. Despite the city's name, whether a given matter
proceeds in federal court is a question of jurisdiction, not geography:
Washington's federal forums are the United States District Court for the
Western District of Washington and the United States District Court for the
Eastern District of Washington, and the controlling venue for each case,
with any expert qualification requirement it brings, is identified during
the conflict check and intake. The file structure is identical either way,
opening with a documented conflict check, a defined written scope, and a
records request shaped to the claims.

## The market questions in a Federal Way file

Whether the assignment is an original opinion or a rebuttal, the labor
market analysis is where most vocational disputes are decided. The firm
grounds its findings in Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment
and wage estimates for the Seattle metropolitan area and for Washington
statewide, in state workforce agency labor market information, and in direct
labor market research when the questions demand it. A rebuttal asks whether
the opposing expert defined a market and stayed within it; an original
opinion defines the market expressly, includes the evaluee's realistic
commuting reach, and keeps its transferable-skills conclusions inside that
definition. Metro-wide discussion appears on the
[Seattle metro page](/locations/washington/seattle/).

## Presence, travel, and coordination

Purinton Analytics staffs no Federal Way office and publishes no local
street address, stating so directly because candor about small facts builds
trust in large ones. Meetings are by appointment; evaluations take place by
secure video or in the evaluee's own community; travel for depositions and
trial testimony originates at the Kansas City home office. Where the damages
model requires economic calculations, the firm offers
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. Statewide coverage is described on the
[Washington state page](/locations/washington/) and the national footprint
in the [nationwide overview](/locations/nationwide/).

## Clearing conflicts first

Whether the request is an original opinion or a review of someone else's,
intake begins identically. Submit the caption or party names through the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and identify the disclosure,
deposition, or trial dates already governing the case. Written scope and
records requests follow clearance, and the analysis follows the record.
