## Snohomish County venue for Marysville claims

Marysville sits in Snohomish County. State-law claims arising there are
heard in the Superior Court, where Washington's civil rules govern expert
disclosure, deposition practice, and trial testimony. Cases that belong in
federal court proceed in the United States District Court for the Western
District of Washington or the United States District Court for the Eastern
District of Washington; which district controls a particular matter, and
what registration or qualification requirements attach to a testifying
expert there, are questions answered during the conflict check and intake
rather than assumed in advance. The engagement sequence described above
holds in each of those forums without modification, because it is the
foundation of the opinion rather than a courtesy to any particular court.
