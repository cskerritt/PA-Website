disclosure deadline set by the case schedule, a deposition window the parties

## Where Bellevue disputes are heard

Bellevue lies in King County, and claims filed in state court proceed in the
Superior Court under Washington's civil rules for expert disclosure and
testimony. Cases belonging in federal court are heard in the United States
District Court for the Western District of Washington or the United States
District Court for the Eastern District of Washington; the controlling forum
for a particular matter, and any expert registration or qualification
requirement it carries, is confirmed during the conflict check and intake
rather than assumed. Whatever the venue, the file is assembled the same way,
beginning with a documented conflict check, a defined scope, and a records
request tailored to the claims at issue.

## Valuing capacity in a demanding market

Earning-capacity disputes involving Bellevue evaluees often turn on careful
distinctions between what a person once did, what the person can still do,
and what the surrounding market will pay for the difference. The analysis
rests on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage
estimates for the Seattle metropolitan area and for Washington statewide,
along with state workforce agency labor market information and direct labor
market research when the questions call for it. Each report defines the
relevant market explicitly, accounts for the evaluee's realistic commuting
reach, and ties transferable-skills findings to that defined market. Metro
context beyond the city appears on the
[Seattle metro page](/locations/washington/seattle/), and the state's full
venue landscape is covered on the
[Washington state page](/locations/washington/).

## Honest logistics for a by-appointment practice

There is no staffed Bellevue office and no published street address here;
accuracy about presence is part of the same discipline that governs the
reports. Counsel meetings occur by appointment, evaluations are conducted by
secure video or in person in the evaluee's own community, and travel for
depositions and trial testimony is scheduled from the Kansas City home
office. Where the case requires economic loss calculations alongside the
vocational and care-planning work, the firm offers
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. The practice's full geographic scope is described in
the [nationwide overview](/locations/nationwide/).
