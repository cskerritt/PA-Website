The contested question at the center of many Redmond vocational disputes is
transferability: which of an evaluee's skills survive the injury, which
occupations those skills actually reach, and what the honest answer does to
the damages model. Purinton Analytics treats that question as an analysis to
be documented, not an impression to be asserted. In
[vocational expert witness](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) engagements
for plaintiff and defense counsel alike, the firm traces each
transferable-skills conclusion from the work history, through the residual
capacities the medical record supports, to occupations that exist in a
defined labor market, so the chain of reasoning is available to both sides.

## From skills to market, on the record

Transferability means little without a market to transfer into. The firm's
analyses rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage
estimates for the Seattle metropolitan area and for Washington statewide,
along with state workforce agency labor market information and direct labor
market research where the referral questions justify it. The applicable
market is defined in the report itself, the evaluee's realistic commuting
reach is incorporated into that definition, and no conclusion is permitted
to drift beyond it. Metro-level context surrounding Redmond appears on the
[Seattle metro page](/locations/washington/seattle/).

## Redmond venue in state and federal court

Redmond is situated in King County, and state-court claims are litigated in
the Superior Court, where Washington's civil rules structure expert
disclosure, depositions, and trial testimony. Washington's federal forums
are the United States District Court for the Western District of Washington
and the United States District Court for the Eastern District of Washington;
the controlling venue for any particular matter, and whatever registration
or qualification requirement that venue applies to a testifying expert, is
identified and confirmed during the conflict check and intake. In every
forum the engagement is built the same way, on a documented conflict check,
a defined written scope, and a records request tailored to the claims.

Where the injury requires lifelong care, the firm prepares

in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. The statewide picture is drawn on the
