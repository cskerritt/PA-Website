Serious injury cases from Auburn rarely need just a single expert
discipline. The same file that requires a vocational opinion usually
requires a care plan, and the care plan and lost earnings usually require an
economist to reduce them to present value. Purinton Analytics structures
Auburn engagements around that reality, offering
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. Counsel for either party retains one coordinated team
instead of assembling and refereeing separate experts, and each discipline's
opinion still stands on its own author's signature.

## The disciplines, separately accountable

Within that structure, the firm's own work is the
[vocational expert witness](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) analysis
and the [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) assessment, each
delivered as a written opinion with its methodology, sources, and
assumptions stated openly. The economic loss calculations are performed and
signed by the independent economist partners, never by the firm itself, so
that every opinion in the damages case is defended by the professional who
authored it. Plaintiff and defense counsel receive the same structure and
the same standards; the practice's credibility depends on the work reading
identically no matter who paid for it.

## Auburn's courts and forums

Auburn lies in King County, and state-law claims are tried in the Superior
Court under Washington's civil rules governing expert disclosure and
testimony. Federal matters proceed in the United States District Court for
the Western District of Washington or the United States District Court for
the Eastern District of Washington; which forum controls, and what expert
registration or qualification it may require, is determined during the
conflict check and intake. The coordinated structure does not loosen the
sequence: a documented conflict check covering the engagement, a written
scope for each discipline, and records requests tailored to the claims come
before any analysis.

## Grounding the damages case in market evidence

Before any economist runs a calculation, the vocational foundation must
hold. That foundation rests on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational
employment and wage estimates for the Seattle metropolitan area and for
Washington statewide, on state workforce agency labor market information,
and on direct labor market research where the referral questions warrant it.
The market is defined expressly, the evaluee's realistic commuting reach is
part of the definition, and transferable-skills conclusions stay within the
defined market. Metro-level discussion appears on the
[Seattle metro page](/locations/washington/seattle/) and statewide venue
coverage on the [Washington state page](/locations/washington/).

## Service by appointment

The firm keeps no staffed office in Auburn and publishes no street address
for this location. Meetings with counsel are by appointment, evaluations of
Auburn evaluees occur by secure video or in person in the evaluee's own
community, and travel for depositions and trial dates is scheduled from the
Kansas City home office, as it is throughout the national practice described
in the [nationwide overview](/locations/nationwide/).

A coordinated engagement begins exactly where a single-discipline one does.
Submit the caption or party names through the
