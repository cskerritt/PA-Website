## How the work actually reaches Kirkland

Meetings with counsel are scheduled by appointment, in person or by video as
the case requires. Evaluations of Kirkland evaluees are conducted by secure
video or in person in the community where the evaluee lives, because
observing a person in their own setting often informs the vocational and
care-planning analysis. Travel for evaluations, depositions, and trial
appearances is planned from the Kansas City home office and calendared
around the deadlines that govern the case. Nothing about this model is
improvised; it is the same structure the firm applies across the country, as
described in the [nationwide overview](/locations/nationwide/).

## The courts behind a Kirkland caption

Kirkland sits in King County, and state-court claims arising there proceed
in the Superior Court under Washington's rules for expert disclosure,
deposition practice, and trial testimony. Federal matters are heard in the
United States District Court for the Western District of Washington or the
United States District Court for the Eastern District of Washington, and the
forum controlling a particular case, along with any registration or
qualification requirement it places on a testifying expert, is settled
during the conflict check and intake. Whatever the court, the record comes
first: a documented conflict check, a written scope, and a records request
tailored to the claims precede every opinion.

## Market evidence for Kirkland evaluees

The vocational analysis for a Kirkland evaluee is anchored in Bureau of
Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Seattle
metropolitan area and for Washington statewide, supported by state workforce
agency labor market information and, where the referral questions justify
it, direct labor market research. The report defines the applicable market
in express terms, treats realistic commuting reach as part of that
definition, and confines its transferable-skills conclusions to the market
so defined. Wider metropolitan context is set out on the
[Seattle metro page](/locations/washington/seattle/), and statewide venue
material on the [Washington state page](/locations/washington/). When
economic loss calculations are also needed, the firm offers
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.
