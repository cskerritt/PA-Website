For the injured worker or catastrophically injured plaintiff who lives in
Kent, the expert evaluation itself is often the most personal step in the
lawsuit: a structured interview, a review of a life's work history, and an
honest accounting of what has changed. Purinton Analytics designs that step
around the evaluee. As a national, by-appointment practice, the firm conducts
[vocational expert witness](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) evaluations
and [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) assessments for Kent
residents by secure video or in person in the evaluee's own community, on
referral from plaintiff or defense counsel, and writes up the findings in
opinions built to withstand examination by the opposing side.

## The courts that serve Kent

Kent is in King County, and state-law claims arising there are tried in the
Superior Court, whose civil rules set the rhythm of expert disclosure,
deposition, and trial testimony. Federal matters proceed in the United States
District Court for the Western District of Washington or the United States
District Court for the Eastern District of Washington, and the forum that
controls a specific case, together with any qualification or registration
requirement it imposes on a testifying expert, is identified during the
conflict check and intake. Venue never changes the method: documented
conflict check, written scope, and a records request fitted to the claims,
in that order, before any opinion work begins.

## Placing a Kent evaluee in the labor market

A Kent evaluee's employment prospects cannot be assessed in isolation from
the metropolitan economy that surrounds the city. Vocational conclusions are
grounded in Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage
estimates for the Seattle metropolitan area and for Washington statewide,
state workforce agency labor market information, and direct labor market
research where warranted by the referral questions. The written analysis
names the market it uses, folds the evaluee's realistic commuting range into
that definition, and derives transferable-skills findings from the defined
market instead of national averages, so the basis of every conclusion is
visible on the page. The wider metropolitan picture is addressed on the
[Seattle metro page](/locations/washington/seattle/).
