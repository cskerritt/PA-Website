title, and the lawyers who try cases there treat the city accordingly: as a
principal city with its own bench, its own bar, and its own jury pools rather
than an outlying satellite of a larger neighbor. Purinton Analytics serves

## Pierce County venue and the federal forums

Tacoma sits in Pierce County, and state-court claims arising in the city
proceed in the Superior Court, where Washington's civil rules govern expert
disclosure, deposition practice, and trial testimony. Washington's federal
trial forums are the United States District Court for the Western District of
Washington and the United States District Court for the Eastern District of
Washington; which of them controls a removed or federal-question case, along
with any registration or qualification requirement the forum imposes on a
testifying expert, is identified and confirmed during the conflict check and
intake. The engagement sequence does not vary by courthouse: a documented
conflict check first, then a defined written scope, then a records request
tailored to the claims, so the finished opinion rests on a record either side
of the caption can examine.

## Tacoma's labor market inside the metro

An earning-capacity opinion for a Tacoma evaluee must decide, and then
defend, what labor market actually applies. Vocational findings rest on
Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the
Seattle metropolitan area and for Washington statewide, supplemented by state
workforce agency labor market information and by direct labor market research
where the referral questions warrant it. The report states the market
definition expressly, treats the commuting reach realistic for the particular
evaluee as part of that definition, and grounds transferable-skills
conclusions in the defined market rather than in national averages. Broader
context for the metropolitan area as a whole appears on the
[Seattle metro page](/locations/washington/seattle/).
