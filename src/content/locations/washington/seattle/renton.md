A vocational opinion earns its fee at the moment opposing counsel starts
asking questions, and that is the standard every Renton report from Purinton
Analytics is written against. Each [vocational expert witness](/services/vocational-expert-witness/)
opinion and [life care plan](/services/life-care-planning/) states its
methodology, identifies its sources, and discloses its assumptions on the
page, so that a cross-examiner can test the work and a fact-finder can
follow it. The practice serves Renton nationally and by appointment, and it
is retained by plaintiff and defense counsel in roughly the manner the work
deserves: for the reliability of the opinion rather than the location of the
office.

## King County process, federal process

Renton is a King County city, and state claims arising there are litigated
in the Superior Court under Washington's civil rules for expert disclosure,
deposition, and trial testimony. On the federal side, Washington's forums
are the United States District Court for the Western District of Washington
and the United States District Court for the Eastern District of Washington;
determining which forum controls a specific case, and what registration or
qualification it may require of a testifying expert, is part of the conflict
check and intake. Neither forum changes the discipline of the file: a
documented conflict check, a written scope, and a claims-tailored records
request come first in every engagement.

## Building the market analysis for a Renton case

The labor market portion of a Renton opinion begins with a definition and
defends it. Findings rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational
employment and wage estimates for the Seattle metropolitan area and for
Washington statewide, on state workforce agency labor market information,
and on direct labor market research where the referral questions require
more granular evidence. The evaluee's realistic commuting reach is built
into the market definition, and transferable-skills conclusions are drawn
from that defined market, never from national averages standing alone. For
the metropolitan context surrounding Renton, see the
[Seattle metro page](/locations/washington/seattle/); the state's venue
landscape is gathered on the
[Washington state page](/locations/washington/).

## What by-appointment service means here

No staffed office operates in Renton and no street address is published for
this location, a fact stated plainly because overstating presence would sit
poorly beside reports that certify facts for a living. Attorney meetings are
held by appointment; evaluations occur by secure video or in person in the
evaluee's own community; travel for depositions and trial dates is scheduled
from the Kansas City home office. Where economic loss calculations are
needed, the firm offers
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. The reach of the whole practice appears in the
[nationwide overview](/locations/nationwide/).

## First step: the conflict check

No scheduling happens before clearance. Provide the case caption or party
names through the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/), noting any
disclosure, deposition, or trial dates already entered. After conflicts
clear, the scope is confirmed in writing, records are requested, and opinion
work proceeds on the agreed schedule.
