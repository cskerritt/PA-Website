Damages cases in Walla Walla rarely need just a single expert discipline; a
serious injury claim can require vocational analysis, a life care plan, and
an economic loss calculation that agree with one another. Purinton Analytics
serves plaintiff and defense counsel in the Walla Walla metropolitan area
with exactly that combination:
[vocational expert evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) of
earning capacity and employability, [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/)
in catastrophic injury matters, and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure, so the pieces of the damages case are built to fit
together rather than reconciled after the fact.

## Venues for Walla Walla County matters

The metro consists of Walla Walla County, and state court engagements are
venued in the Superior Court of Walla Walla County, the trial court of
general jurisdiction for the area. Federal matters are handled in the
United States District Court for the Eastern District of Washington or the
United States District Court for the Western District of Washington,
depending on where the action is filed. Whatever the forum, the engagement
opens with a documented conflict check, a defined scope, and a records
request tailored to the claims, and the report is written so either side of
the caption can trace its reasoning. The firm's other venues in the state
appear on the [Washington page](/locations/washington/).

## Appointment-based logistics, disclosed candidly

Walla Walla meetings are by appointment. The firm keeps no staffed office in
the metro and publishes no street address for this location; that candor
about presence reflects the same discipline the reports themselves are held
to. Engagements ordinarily begin remotely, with records review, structured
interviews, and standardized testing under publisher protocols where remote
administration is supported. In-person evaluations, depositions, and trial
appearances are scheduled as travel from the Kansas City home office and
planned against counsel's disclosure and trial calendar, so the expert's
availability is settled long before it matters.

## Labor market foundations for the opinion

Vocational findings for Walla Walla County evaluees rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Walla Walla
metropolitan area and for Washington statewide, on state workforce agency
labor market information, and on direct labor market research when the
referral questions warrant employer-level inquiry. Life care plans and
[medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/) are priced to
the evaluee's own market with provider-specific inquiries and published fee
data, since the cost of future care in a regional market cannot simply be
read off national averages. Sources are cited throughout the report, which
is written on the assumption that opposing counsel will check them.

Plaintiff and defense referrals follow the same intake path. Submit the
