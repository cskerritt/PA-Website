Vancouver occupies an unusual position in vocational litigation. It is a
large Washington city inside a metropolitan economy whose principal city,
Portland, sits across the state line in Oregon, so counsel handling injury,
employment, and disability matters for Vancouver parties apply Washington
procedure to evaluees who compete in a labor market that does not stop at
the border. Purinton Analytics serves plaintiff and defense counsel in that
setting with [earning capacity and employability evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) in catastrophic injury
cases, and [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Clark County venue and the courts

State-court matters for Vancouver parties proceed in the Superior Court of
Clark County, and reports prepared for that forum are written to be read
closely by opposing counsel: a documented conflict check, a defined scope,
and a records request tailored to the claims come before any opinion. For
federal matters, Washington's federal trial courts, the United States
District Court for the Eastern District of Washington and the United States
District Court for the Western District of Washington, are the available
forums, and the controlling venue for a particular caption is identified
during the conflict check and intake. Because the metro spans states, some
disputes touching Vancouver residents are litigated on the Oregon side
instead; the firm's [Portland metro page](/locations/oregon/portland/)
describes that work, and the [Washington state page](/locations/washington/)
covers engagements elsewhere in the state.

## A labor market that crosses the state line

Employability and earning capacity questions for a Vancouver evaluee rarely
end at the city limits. Residents work throughout Clark County and commute
into Oregon, and employers on either side of the line recruit from the whole
commuting shed, so a placement analysis confined to one state would misstate
the market an evaluee actually faces. Vocational findings therefore rest on
Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for
the Portland-Vancouver-Hillsboro metropolitan area and for Washington
statewide, supplemented by state workforce agency labor market information
and direct labor market research where the referral questions warrant it.
No wage figure or employment count enters a report except from a cited
source, and the same qualitative discipline governs this page.

## Evaluating a Vancouver evaluee

Most engagements begin remotely: records review, a structured vocational
interview by video, standardized testing where appropriate, and
transferable skills analysis proceed without travel. When the referral
questions call for in-person evaluation, the appointment is held in the
evaluee's own community rather than requiring travel to a distant office.
The firm keeps no staffed office in Vancouver and publishes no street
address here, because accuracy about presence is part of the same
discipline that governs its reports; when evaluation, deposition, or trial
dates require presence, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home
office. [Testimony and litigation support](/services/expert-testimony-litigation-consulting/)
are provided in person or by remote appearance as the court permits, and
scheduling is planned around the disclosure calendar counsel identifies at
intake.

## Starting a Vancouver engagement

A Vancouver matter begins the way every engagement does, whether local or
[nationwide](/locations/nationwide/): with a conflict check before any
substantive discussion. Submit the caption or party names through the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and note any imminent disclosure,
deposition, or trial dates, along with whether the referral questions
concern employability, earning capacity, future care needs, or coordinated
economic damages. The firm responds with confirmation of clearance, a
proposed scope, and a records request tailored to the claims.
