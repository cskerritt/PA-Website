## The courts that serve a Benton County town

Prosser is a Benton County community, and state-law claims arising here are
tried in the Superior Court under Washington's civil rules governing expert
disclosure and testimony. On the federal side, Washington's forums are the
United States District Court for the Eastern District of Washington and the
United States District Court for the Western District of Washington; the
district with venue over a specific case, and any qualification or
registration requirement that forum applies to a testifying expert, is
identified and confirmed during the conflict check and intake. The
methodology holds constant across all of them: documented conflict check,
written scope, records request tailored to the claims, and an opinion that
either side of the caption can trace back to its sources.

## Small-town evaluees, metro-scale labor data

A small town's evaluees still work within a metro-scale labor market, and
the analysis must capture that honestly. Vocational findings for a Prosser
evaluee rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage

statewide, supported by state workforce agency labor market information and
direct labor market research where the referral questions justify it. The
report states its market definition outright, accounts for the commuting
distances a Prosser resident would realistically accept, and keeps

[Kennewick-Richland metro page](/locations/washington/kennewick/).
