does nationally: the evaluee is seen by secure video or in person in his or

both sides of the caption to examine. Counsel for plaintiffs and defendants

## Benton County venue and the federal alternatives

Claims arising in West Richland proceed in the Superior Court when they
sound in state law, following Washington's rules for expert disclosure,
deposition, and trial testimony. Matters belonging in federal court are
heard in the United States District Court for the Eastern District of
Washington or the United States District Court for the Western District of
Washington, and the controlling forum for a given case, together with any
expert qualification requirement it carries, is settled during the conflict
check and intake. Wherever the case lands, the sequence is fixed: a
documented conflict check, then a defined scope in writing, then a records
request shaped to the claims, so the opinion rests on evidence rather than
recollection.
