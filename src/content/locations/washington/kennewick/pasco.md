Franklin County supplies its own share of the civil docket in the
Kennewick-Richland metropolitan area, and Pasco, one of the larger cities in
the metro, is where much of that litigation originates. Purinton Analytics
serves Pasco matters as a national, by-appointment practice, providing
[vocational expert witness](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) opinions
and [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) for counsel on
either side of the caption. What retention buys is not proximity but a
written opinion built to withstand the scrutiny of the lawyer who did not
hire the expert.

## Franklin County courts and the federal forums

State-court claims arising in Pasco proceed in the Superior Court, where
Washington's civil rules govern expert disclosure, deposition practice, and
trial testimony. Federal matters in the state are heard in the United States
District Court for the Eastern District of Washington and the United States
District Court for the Western District of Washington; which district
controls a particular case, along with any registration or qualification
requirement the forum imposes on a testifying expert, is identified and
confirmed during the conflict check and intake. The engagement itself runs
the same way in every venue: a documented conflict check first, then a
defined written scope, then a records request tailored to the claims, so
the opinion rests on a record both sides can examine.

## Defining the Pasco labor market

A vocational opinion for a Pasco evaluee must say expressly what labor
market it describes. Findings rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates for the Kennewick-Richland
metropolitan area and for Washington statewide, supplemented by state
workforce agency labor market information and direct labor market research
where the referral questions call for it. Because Pasco workers routinely
hold jobs across the county line elsewhere in the metro, the analysis
treats realistic commuting reach as part of the market definition rather
than an afterthought, and transferable-skills conclusions are drawn from
that defined market instead of national averages. The metro-wide picture,
including the courts and communities on the Benton County side, is
described on the [Kennewick-Richland metro page](/locations/washington/kennewick/).
