Distance is not a limitation on expert work when the methodology is built
for it. Purinton Analytics operates remote-first everywhere it practices,
which means a case arising in Connell, a small Franklin County town in the
Kennewick-Richland metropolitan area, receives the same
[vocational expert witness](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) and
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) analysis as a case in
any major city: records-driven, methodically scoped, and written so the
lawyer on the other side of the caption can check every step. The firm
takes referrals from plaintiff and defense counsel without preference.

## Franklin County trial courts and federal options

State-law claims from Connell are heard in the Superior Court, subject to
Washington's rules for expert disclosure, depositions, and trial testimony.
Federal litigation in the state proceeds in the United States District
Court for the Eastern District of Washington and the United States District
Court for the Western District of Washington; determining which district
has venue over a particular dispute, and satisfying whatever expert
qualification or registration that forum requires, happens during the
conflict check and intake rather than by assumption. The court never alters
the sequence of the work. A documented conflict check opens the file, a
written scope defines it, and a records request tailored to the claims
supplies the record on which the opinion will stand.

## Labor market evidence, not assumptions about the town

A vocational opinion for a Connell evaluee must be able to show where every
input came from. The published foundation is Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates for the Kennewick-Richland
metropolitan area and for Washington statewide, refined with state
workforce agency labor market information and direct labor market research
when the referral questions call for it. What the published tables cannot
supply, the file itself must: this evaluee's work history, education,
transferable skills, transportation, and the commuting range he or she can
realistically sustain are established through the interview and the
records, never presumed from the name of the town on the caption. The
report states the market definition it uses and ties its conclusions to
that documented definition, so opposing counsel can test the analysis
against its sources rather than against the expert's say-so. The metro's
overall venue and labor market picture appears on the
[Kennewick-Richland metro page](/locations/washington/kennewick/).

## How remote-first service actually works

The firm has no office in Connell and claims none; its only staffed
location is the Kansas City home office, and honesty about that is part of
the product. Records review, counsel conferences, and most evaluations
proceed by secure remote means, with in-person evaluation available in the
evaluee's own community when the referral requires it. Depositions and
trial appearances are attended in person, with travel arranged from the
Kansas City home office around the case schedule. Where economic loss
figures are needed,
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
bring in independent economist partners who perform and sign the economic
analysis, keeping vocational, care-planning, and economic workstreams
inside one engagement structure. Statewide coverage is outlined on the
[Washington state page](/locations/washington/), and the
[nationwide overview](/locations/nationwide/) shows the same model applied
across the country.
