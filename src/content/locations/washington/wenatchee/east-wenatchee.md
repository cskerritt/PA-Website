## Douglas County venue and the federal forums

State-court claims from East Wenatchee proceed in the Superior Court, where
Washington's civil rules control expert disclosure, deposition practice, and
testimony at trial. When a dispute belongs in federal court, Washington's
forums are the United States District Court for the Eastern District of
Washington and the United States District Court for the Western District of
Washington; the district that controls a particular case, along with any
registration or qualification requirement a forum imposes on a testifying
expert, is identified and confirmed during the conflict check and intake.
Whatever the venue, the sequence does not change: a documented conflict
check, a defined written scope, and a records request tailored to the
claims, so the opinion rests on a record both sides can examine.

## A namesake city inside a shared labor market

East Wenatchee residents earn their livings across the metro, and a credible
earning-capacity opinion has to define that market before measuring anyone
against it. Vocational findings rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates for the Wenatchee-East Wenatchee
metropolitan area and for Washington statewide, supplemented by state
workforce agency labor market information and direct labor market research
where the referral questions require it. Transferable-skills analysis is run
against occupations that actually exist within realistic commuting reach of
East Wenatchee, including the principal city of Wenatchee, not against
national averages. The full metro picture, courts and market alike, is set
out on the
[Wenatchee-East Wenatchee metro page](/locations/washington/wenatchee/).
