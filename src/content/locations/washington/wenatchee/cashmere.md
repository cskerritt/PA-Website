Small communities are where careless vocational opinions fail fastest,
because a labor market assumed rather than defined is easy to expose on
cross-examination. Cashmere, a small town within the Wenatchee-East
Wenatchee, WA metropolitan area, gets the same rigor from Purinton
Analytics that a big-city docket would demand:
[vocational expert witness](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) opinions
and [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) prepared for
plaintiff and defense counsel alike under a documented, repeatable method.
The premise of the practice is that geography changes the data, never the
discipline.

## Where Cashmere cases are heard

Civil claims arising in Cashmere belong to Chelan County, and state-court
litigation proceeds in the Superior Court under Washington's civil rules
for expert disclosure and testimony. On the federal side, Washington's
forums are the United States District Court for the Eastern District of
Washington and the United States District Court for the Western District of
Washington; the controlling district for any particular matter, and any
expert registration or qualification requirement it imposes, is settled
during the conflict check and intake rather than assumed in advance. Each
engagement is built in the same order regardless of court: documented
conflict clearance, a written scope, and records requested to fit the
claims actually pleaded.

## Defining the market around a Cashmere evaluee

For a Cashmere worker, the relevant labor market rarely stops at the town
line. Vocational findings are grounded in Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates for the Wenatchee-East Wenatchee
metropolitan area and for Washington statewide, alongside state workforce
agency labor market information and targeted labor market research where
warranted. The analysis asks what work a person with this evaluee's
capacities can realistically reach, treating commuting toward the principal
city of Wenatchee and across the metro as evidence to be examined, not an
assumption to be made. How the metro fits together as a venue and as a
market is described on the
[metro page for Wenatchee and East Wenatchee](/locations/washington/wenatchee/).

## Logistics without embellishment

There is no Purinton Analytics storefront in Cashmere and no local address
to publish; the firm describes its own footprint as carefully as it
describes an evaluee's earning capacity. Engagements run by appointment:
counsel conferences by phone or video, evaluee assessments by secure video
or in person in the evaluee's own community, and travel from the Kansas
City home office when depositions or trial dates require attendance. When
damages must be carried into economic terms, the firm offers
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. Statewide context sits on the
[Washington state page](/locations/washington/), and the
[nationwide overview](/locations/nationwide/) covers the rest of the map.
