Expert work in Leavenworth cases is governed less by the map than by the
calendar: disclosure deadlines, deposition notices, and trial settings
arrive on the same schedule whether a claim originates in a small Chelan

fixed in writing at intake, for counsel on either side of the caption.

## Venue for Leavenworth matters

Leavenworth sits in Chelan County, and state-law claims are litigated in
the Superior Court under Washington's rules governing expert disclosure and
trial testimony. Matters in federal court proceed in the United States
District Court for the Eastern District of Washington or the United States
District Court for the Western District of Washington; identifying the
controlling district for a specific case, and confirming any registration
or qualification requirement the forum applies to a testifying expert, is
part of the conflict check and intake, not a guess made on a landing page.
From there the file follows the standing sequence: cleared conflicts, a
defined scope, and a records request matched to the pleadings.

## Labor market evidence for a Leavenworth evaluee

A vocational opinion is only as strong as the labor market it names. For
Leavenworth evaluees, findings rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates for the Wenatchee-East
Wenatchee metropolitan area and for Washington statewide, joined by state
workforce agency labor market information and direct research when the
referral requires it. Because a Leavenworth resident's realistic employment
options can extend across the metro toward the principal city of
Wenatchee, commuting reach enters the market definition explicitly, and
transferability conclusions are confined to that defined market. The
[Wenatchee-East Wenatchee metropolitan overview](/locations/washington/wenatchee/)
describes the surrounding venues and the market as a whole.

## Scheduling, evaluations, and travel

The firm keeps no staffed location in Leavenworth and lists no street
address for it; candor about presence is part of the product. Work
proceeds by appointment against the litigation calendar: records review
and counsel conferences handled remotely, evaluee assessments by secure
video or in person in the evaluee's own community, and appearances for
deposition or trial scheduled as travel from the Kansas City home office
with the operative dates in hand. When the referral also requires an
economic loss calculation, the firm offers
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. The [Washington state page](/locations/washington/)
gathers statewide venue notes, and the
[nationwide overview](/locations/nationwide/) shows the practice's full
footprint.

Start where every engagement starts. Submit the caption or party names
