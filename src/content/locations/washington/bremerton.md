metropolitan area, the logistics of an expert evaluation matter as much as
the credentials behind it. Purinton Analytics structures each engagement
around the evaluee's circumstances: records review and structured interviews

protocols where remote administration is supported, and when the referral

[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and

## Kitsap County venues, state and federal

Engagements are accepted for matters venued in the Superior Court of Kitsap
County and in the federal courts serving the state, the United States
District Court for the Western District of Washington and the United States
District Court for the Eastern District of Washington. Where a matter also
needs economic loss analysis, the firm offers
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. Counsel handling related matters elsewhere in the
state can start from the firm's
[Washington page](/locations/washington/) for the statewide venue picture.

## Appointments in Bremerton, Silverdale, and Port Orchard

The firm keeps no staffed office in Bremerton, Silverdale, or Port Orchard
and publishes no street address for this location. Meetings are by
appointment, and the absence of a storefront is stated plainly because
accuracy about presence is part of the same discipline that governs the
reports themselves. When deposition or trial dates require attendance, or
when an in-person evaluation is scheduled in Kitsap County, travel is
arranged from the Kansas City home office and confirmed with counsel against
the controlling deadlines. The report standard, the file organization, and
the point of contact remain constant whether the work is performed remotely
or on site.

## Labor market research for Kitsap County evaluees

Employability and earning capacity findings for evaluees in the metro rest
on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for
the Bremerton-Silverdale-Port Orchard metropolitan area and for Washington
statewide, together with state workforce agency labor market information and
direct labor market research where warranted. The analysis describes the

for Kitsap County residents are priced to the evaluee's own market through
provider-specific inquiries and published fee data. Opposing counsel can
trace every figure in the report to a named source, which is the test the
firm writes against.

## Starting a Bremerton engagement

Retention starts with a conflict check, not a commitment. Submit the caption
or party names through the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and note
any imminent disclosure, deposition, or trial dates so scheduling risk is
surfaced at intake. Any venue-specific registration or qualification
requirement is identified and confirmed during the conflict check and intake
process. The firm's [national coverage page](/locations/nationwide/)
describes how the same engagement structure serves counsel beyond
Washington.
