Chelan County and Douglas County together make up the Wenatchee-East
Wenatchee metropolitan area, and Purinton Analytics serves counsel across
that footprint on either side of the caption. Referrals from the metro
typically call for [earning capacity and employability evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/)
in injury and employment matters, [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/)
in catastrophic injury cases, and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. Every file begins the same way: a documented conflict
check, a defined scope, and a records request tailored to the claims.

## Courts hearing Wenatchee and East Wenatchee matters

Engagements are accepted for matters venued in the Superior Court of Chelan
County and the Superior Court of Douglas County, the trial courts of general
jurisdiction for the metro, and in federal court in the United States
District Court for the Eastern District of Washington or the United States
District Court for the Western District of Washington, depending on where
the action is filed. The written opinion is prepared to the same standard in
every forum, so that direct and cross-examination can proceed from a record
both sides are able to test. Counsel with matters elsewhere in the state
will find the broader venue picture on the firm's
[Washington page](/locations/washington/).

## Meetings by appointment, work built for distance

Wenatchee meetings are by appointment. The firm keeps no staffed office in
the metro and publishes no street address for this location, because
accuracy about presence is part of the same discipline that governs the
reports. Most engagements begin remotely: records review, structured
interviews with the evaluee, and standardized testing administered under
publisher protocols where remote administration is supported. When the
referral questions call for in-person evaluation, or when deposition and
trial dates require presence, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home
office and planned around counsel's disclosure calendar. Counsel receive a
consistent report format and a direct line to the expert regardless of where
the work is performed.

## Labor market research for the Wenatchee area

Vocational findings for evaluees in the metro rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Wenatchee
metropolitan area and for Washington statewide, supplemented by state
workforce agency labor market information and by direct labor market
research when the referral questions warrant employer-level detail. Life
care plans and [medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/)
are priced to the evaluee's own market using provider-specific inquiries and
published fee data, rather than to national averages that may not describe
the cost of care where the evaluee actually lives. The sources behind every
figure are cited in the report so opposing counsel can trace each input.

## Starting an engagement in Chelan County or Douglas County

Availability for plaintiff and defense work alike is confirmed through the
same intake process, and any venue-specific registration or qualification
requirement is identified and confirmed during the conflict check and
intake. Submit the caption or party names through the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and note any imminent disclosure,
deposition, or trial dates so scheduling can be addressed at the outset.
Firms with related matters outside Washington can review the practice's
[national coverage](/locations/nationwide/) for the same services delivered
under the same engagement structure.
