A national practice exists so that counsel in the Mount Vernon-Anacortes, WA
metropolitan area do not have to settle for whichever expert happens to be
nearby. Purinton Analytics brings the same engagement structure to Skagit
County that it applies in the largest litigation markets it serves:
[vocational evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) of
employability and earning capacity,
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) for catastrophic injury,
and [expert testimony support](/services/expert-testimony-litigation-consulting/)
through deposition and trial. Referrals are welcome from plaintiff and
defense counsel, and the work is documented so that either side of the
caption can examine it.

## Skagit County courts and the federal districts

Engagements are accepted for matters venued in the Superior Court of Skagit
County and in the federal courts of the state, the United States District
Court for the Western District of Washington and the United States District
Court for the Eastern District of Washington. When the damages picture
requires economic analysis in addition to vocational and care-planning
opinions, the firm offers
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. The firm's [Washington page](/locations/washington/)
sets out the statewide venue landscape and the other metro areas served.

## By appointment in Mount Vernon and Anacortes

There is no staffed Purinton Analytics office in Mount Vernon or Anacortes,
and the firm publishes no street address for this location. Meetings are by
appointment, and the firm says so directly because describing its own
footprint accurately is part of the discipline it applies to every opinion.
Most engagements proceed remotely, through records review, structured
interviews by video, and standardized testing under publisher protocols
where remote administration is supported. When in-person evaluation is
warranted, or when deposition or trial attendance is required, travel is
scheduled from the Kansas City home office and confirmed against the dates
the court controls.

## Labor market research for Skagit County evaluees

Vocational conclusions for evaluees in the metro rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Mount
Vernon-Anacortes metropolitan area and for Washington statewide, together
with state workforce agency labor market information and direct labor market
research where the referral questions warrant it. The occupational base of a
regional market differs from that of a major metropolitan center, and the
analysis is grounded in the market the evaluee actually faces. Life care
plans and medical cost projections for Skagit County residents are priced to
the evaluee's own market using provider-specific inquiries and published fee
data, so cost conclusions reflect local conditions rather than a national
composite.

## Starting a Mount Vernon engagement

Engagement begins the same way here as everywhere in the practice: with a
conflict check. Submit the caption or party names through the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and note any imminent disclosure,
deposition, or trial dates so timing questions surface at intake. Any
venue-specific registration or qualification requirement is identified and
confirmed during the conflict check and intake process. Counsel whose
matters extend past Washington can review the firm's
[national coverage](/locations/nationwide/) for the same structure applied
across jurisdictions.
