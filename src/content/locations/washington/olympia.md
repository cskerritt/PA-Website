Counsel in the Olympia-Lacey-Tumwater, WA metropolitan area tend to ask the
same practical questions before retaining an expert: who performs the work,
how the file is documented, and whether the opinion will hold up when the
other side reads it. Purinton Analytics answers those questions in writing,
for plaintiff and defense referrals alike. The practice covers
[vocational expert work](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) on earning
capacity and employability, [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/)
for catastrophic injury, and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Thurston County and the federal courts

Engagements are accepted for matters venued in the Superior Court of
Thurston County and in the federal courts that serve the state, the United
States District Court for the Western District of Washington and the United
States District Court for the Eastern District of Washington. Because
Olympia counsel practice across state and federal dockets, the firm keeps
its disclosure practice uniform: the same methodology statement, the same
sourcing discipline, and the same production-ready file regardless of forum.
Matters arising elsewhere in the state are addressed on the firm's
[Washington page](/locations/washington/), which links the other metro areas
it serves.

## By appointment in Olympia, Lacey, and Tumwater

The firm keeps no staffed office in Olympia, Lacey, or Tumwater and
publishes no street address for this location; describing its presence
accurately is part of the same discipline that governs its opinions.
Meetings are by appointment. Most engagements are handled remotely from the
outset, through records review, structured interviews by video, and
standardized testing under publisher protocols where remote administration
is supported. When in-person evaluation is warranted, or when deposition or
trial attendance is required, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home
office and coordinated against the dates that matter to the court, not the
other way around. Throughout the engagement, counsel work with a consistent
point of contact and receive the same report standard the firm applies in
every jurisdiction it serves.

## Labor market grounding for Thurston County evaluees

Vocational conclusions for evaluees in the metro rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the
Olympia-Lacey-Tumwater metropolitan area and for Washington statewide,
supplemented by state workforce agency labor market information and direct
labor market research where the referral questions call for it. That
grounding matters on cross-examination: an employability opinion tied to
published, area-specific estimates can be tested by either side, which is
the standard the firm writes to. Life care plans and medical cost
projections for Thurston County residents are priced to the evaluee's own
market using provider-specific inquiries and published fee data for the
region.
