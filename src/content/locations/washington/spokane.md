Distance is not a barrier to disciplined expert work, and the
Spokane-Spokane Valley, WA metropolitan area illustrates why. Purinton
Analytics performs most of each engagement remotely, from records review
through structured interviews and standardized testing under publisher
protocols where remote administration is supported, so counsel in Spokane
retain the same [vocational expert](/services/vocational-expert-witness/)
and [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) capability available
in any major market. Plaintiff and defense referrals are accepted on equal
terms, and when a matter needs economic loss figures the firm offers
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Courts serving Spokane County and Stevens County

The firm accepts matters venued in the Superior Court of Spokane County and
the Superior Court of Stevens County, and in the federal courts of the
state, the United States District Court for the Eastern District of
Washington and the United States District Court for the Western District of
Washington. Opinions are written to be examined by opposing counsel in any
of those forums: methodology is stated, sources are cited, and the file is
organized so that cross-examination can trace every conclusion to its
record. The broader statewide picture, including other metro areas, is set
out on the firm's [Washington page](/locations/washington/).

## By-appointment presence in Spokane

Spokane meetings are by appointment. The firm maintains no staffed office in
the metro and publishes no street address for this location; candor about
where the firm actually sits is part of the same discipline that governs its
reports. When the referral questions call for in-person evaluation, or when
deposition and trial dates require attendance, travel is scheduled from the
Kansas City home office and coordinated with counsel well before the
deadline that drives it. Between trips, counsel work with the same point of
contact and receive the same report standard as every other engagement in
the practice, so a Spokane retention is never a diluted version of the
firm's work.

## Labor market research for the Spokane metro

Vocational findings for evaluees in the metro rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the
Spokane-Spokane Valley metropolitan area and for Washington statewide,
together with state workforce agency labor market information and direct
labor market research where warranted. A regional employment base is not
interchangeable with statewide or national figures, and the analysis treats
it accordingly: employability and placeability conclusions are grounded in
the labor market the evaluee actually faces, and life care plans and medical
cost projections are priced to the evaluee's own market using
provider-specific inquiries and published fee data for the region.

## Starting a Spokane engagement

Retention begins with a conflict check rather than a sales call. Send the
caption or party names through the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/),
note any imminent disclosure, deposition, or trial dates, and identify the
referral questions as precisely as the pleadings allow. Any venue-specific
registration or qualification requirement is identified and confirmed during
the conflict check and intake process. For matters that reach beyond
Washington, the firm's [national coverage](/locations/nationwide/) describes
how the same remote-first structure serves counsel in every state.
