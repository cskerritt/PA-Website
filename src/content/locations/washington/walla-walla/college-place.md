## Evaluation logistics for a College Place evaluee

The practice is remote-first. Diagnostic interviews, records review conferences, and counsel briefings proceed by secure video, which most scheduling in a smaller community favors. When the referral questions call for in-person evaluation, the assessment is scheduled in the evaluee's own community rather than requiring travel to a distant office; the firm keeps no staffed office in College Place or anywhere else in Washington and publishes no street address here, because accuracy about presence is part of the same discipline that governs its reports. Travel for evaluations, depositions, and trial testimony is scheduled from the Kansas City home office. When damages require an economic component, the firm offers [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under a single engagement structure.

## Starting a College Place engagement

Timelines in Superior Court matters tend to be set by the disclosure calendar, so early retention protects both the record and the expert's independence. Submit the caption or party names through the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and note any imminent disclosure, deposition, or trial dates. The firm responds with a conflict determination, a proposed scope, and a records request, so that counsel on either side of the caption knows before retention exactly what the opinion will and will not address.
