For an injured person in a small town like Blaine, the evaluation itself is
often the hardest part of the expert phase to arrange, and how an expert
handles that logistics problem says something about how the rest of the
file will be handled. Purinton Analytics evaluates Blaine evaluees by
secure video or in person in the evaluee's own community, so that the
[vocational assessment](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) or
[life care plan](/services/life-care-planning/) reflects the person's
actual circumstances rather than whichever city was convenient for the
expert. Engagements are accepted from plaintiff and defense counsel on the
same terms, and every report is written to withstand the other side's
scrutiny.

## The county and courts behind a Blaine matter

Blaine is part of Whatcom County, and civil claims arising there are tried
in the Superior Court under Washington's rules for expert disclosure,
deposition, and trial testimony. Federal-question and diversity matters
belong in the state's federal forums, the United States District Court for
the Western District of Washington and the United States District Court for
the Eastern District of Washington; the forum that controls a specific
case, and any expert qualification requirement that forum carries, is
identified during the conflict check and intake process. Whatever the
venue, the file is assembled the same way, beginning with a documented
conflict check, a defined scope in writing, and a records request matched
to the claims.

## A small-town evaluee in a metro labor market

Blaine is a small community within the Bellingham metropolitan area, and
the vocational question in most Blaine files is what work is realistically
available to this evaluee across that wider market. The analysis draws on
Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for
the Bellingham metro area and for Washington statewide, on state workforce
agency labor market information, and on direct labor market research where
the referral requires it. Commuting reach is treated as an evidentiary
issue, established for the individual rather than assumed from geography,
and the opinion names its market definition so opposing counsel can engage
with it directly. Regional context for the metro as a whole is set out on
the [Bellingham metro page](/locations/washington/bellingham/).
