Western District of Washington; the district and division controlling a

## A Labor Market That Crosses the State Line

Vocational findings for a Clarkston evaluee rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Lewiston,
ID-WA metropolitan area and for Washington statewide, supplemented by state
workforce agency labor market information and direct labor market research
where the referral questions warrant it. The cross-border character of the
metro matters analytically: a transferable-skills analysis that stopped at
the Washington line would understate the jobs realistically open to a
Clarkston resident, while an analysis that treated the whole region as
interchangeable would ignore differences between the labor markets on each
side of the line. The reports address that geography explicitly, and the same
framework supports the metro-wide practice described on the
[Lewiston metro page](/locations/idaho/lewiston/).
