## Kitsap County venue and the federal forums

Bainbridge Island lies within Kitsap County, and civil actions arising here
are tried in the Superior Court, Washington's court of general jurisdiction.
When a claim proceeds in federal court, Washington offers two forums: the
United States District Court for the Western District of Washington and the
United States District Court for the Eastern District of Washington. Which
of them controls a given matter is confirmed during the conflict check and
intake, and every report is then built to the disclosure and deposition
rules of the court that actually holds the case, not to a generic template.

## How the evaluation itself is handled

There is no staffed Purinton Analytics office on Bainbridge Island, and the
firm publishes no street address here, because candor about presence belongs
in the same category as candor about opinions. Records review, scheduling,
and counsel conferences are handled remotely. Vocational interviews can
proceed by secure video when the referral questions permit, or in person in
the evaluee's community when they do not. When deposition or trial testimony
requires attendance, travel is arranged from the Kansas City home office and
planned against the court's calendar rather than the firm's convenience.
Where damages require economic modeling, counsel can use
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.
