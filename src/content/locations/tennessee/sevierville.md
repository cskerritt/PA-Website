Sevier County is the entire venue footprint of the Sevierville area, and
that concentration keeps engagements here unusually clean: the county's own
courts, a defined local labor market, and a clear set of referral questions.
Purinton Analytics serves plaintiff and defense counsel in that footprint
with [employability and earning capacity evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) in catastrophic injury
matters, and [expert testimony and litigation consulting](/services/expert-testimony-litigation-consulting/)
at deposition and trial. When economic modeling is part of the damages case,
the firm offers [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Sevier County courts and the federal forums

State court matters proceed in the Circuit and Chancery Courts of Sevier
County, and the firm accepts them from either side of the caption. Federal
matters are accepted throughout Tennessee, whether venued in the United
States District Court for the Eastern District of Tennessee, the United
States District Court for the Middle District of Tennessee, or the United
States District Court for the Western District of Tennessee. In every
forum the engagement follows the same sequence: documented conflict check,
written scope, records request tailored to the claims, and a report whose
reasoning is laid out for the opposing side to test. The
[Tennessee page](/locations/tennessee/) describes the statewide practice
this local work belongs to.

## Appointments, not an office

There is no Purinton Analytics office in Sevierville, staffed or otherwise,
and the firm publishes no street address for this location. Meetings are by
appointment, and the working default is remote: records review, structured
vocational interviews, and standardized testing under publisher protocols
that permit remote administration. Where the referral questions are better
served by seeing the evaluee in person, or where deposition and trial
schedules require the expert in the courtroom, travel is arranged from the
Kansas City home office. Counsel are told at intake which parts of the work
will be remote and which will not, so logistics never surprise anyone
mid-engagement.

## Reading the Sevier County labor market

Employability findings for Sevierville area evaluees rest on Bureau of
Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the area
and for Tennessee statewide, on state workforce agency labor market
information, and on direct labor market research where a referral question
turns on specific local hiring. A micropolitan market rewards care: the
written opinion distinguishes between occupations that exist in the data
and occupations realistically open to a particular evaluee, and it says so
in terms that hold up whether the report is offered by the plaintiff or
read against her. Counsel who need an opposing report examined with the
same rigor can engage the firm for
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/).

## Beginning an engagement in Sevierville

The route in is the same as for the rest of the firm's
[national practice](/locations/nationwide/): a conflict check first.
[Request one](/refer-a-case/) with the case caption or party names and any
imminent disclosure, deposition, or trial dates. Once conflicts clear, the
firm confirms scope in writing and issues its records request, so the
analytical work starts while the discovery calendar still leaves room to
use it.
