Plaintiff and defense counsel in Cleveland retain Purinton Analytics for the
same reason: an opinion that holds up when the other side reads it. The firm
provides [vocational expert](/services/vocational-expert-witness/)
evaluations of employability and earning capacity,
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) for catastrophic
injuries, [medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/), and
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) of opposing
experts' reports for matters venued in Bradley and Polk counties. When the
damages picture requires economic modeling, the firm offers
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Venue footprint in Bradley and Polk counties

State court engagements center on the Circuit and Chancery Courts of Bradley
County and the Circuit and Chancery Courts of Polk County. Federal
engagements are accepted throughout Tennessee, in the United States District
Court for the Eastern District of Tennessee, the United States District
Court for the Middle District of Tennessee, and the United States District
Court for the Western District of Tennessee. Every engagement, regardless of
venue, begins the way it does everywhere else in the practice: with a
documented conflict check, a defined scope, and a records request tailored
to the claims. The [Tennessee page](/locations/tennessee/) sets out how that
model applies statewide and lists the other Tennessee markets the firm
serves.

## Meetings by appointment

Cleveland meetings are scheduled by appointment. There is no staffed local
office and no published street address for this location; the firm treats
honesty about its footprint as part of the credibility of its work product.
In practice, most engagements proceed remotely from start to finish, with
records review, structured interviews, and standardized testing administered
under publisher protocols that permit remote use. When an in-person
evaluation better serves the referral questions, or when a deposition or
trial requires the expert's presence, travel is arranged from the Kansas
City home office and scheduled around the litigation calendar rather than
the other way around.

## How the local labor market is analyzed

Employability and earning capacity conclusions for Cleveland area evaluees
are grounded in Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage
estimates for the metropolitan area and for Tennessee statewide,
supplemented by state workforce agency labor market information and by
direct employer research when a referral question turns on the availability
of specific work. The analysis is transparent by design: sources are cited,
assumptions are stated, and the distance between what the data show and
what the opinion concludes is kept short enough for either side to walk on
cross-examination.

## Requesting a conflict check

Cleveland engagements start where every matter in the firm's
[national practice](/locations/nationwide/) starts: with a conflict check.
Counsel should [submit the caption or party names](/refer-a-case/) along
with the nature of the claims and any imminent disclosure, deposition, or
trial dates. After conflicts clear, the firm confirms scope and fee terms
in writing, issues its records request, and commits to a reporting schedule
tied to the court's deadlines, so that retention late in discovery remains
workable when the record allows it.
