What counsel in the Johnson City area need is reliability rather than
proximity, and Purinton Analytics brings the same national methodology to
this metro that it applies everywhere it works: standardized vocational
assessment, transparent sourcing, and reports written for the scrutiny of
the other side. The practice serves plaintiff and defense counsel in equal
measure, whether the referral concerns an
[earning capacity and employability evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
a [life care plan](/services/life-care-planning/), or
[expert testimony support](/services/expert-testimony-litigation-consulting/)
as a case moves toward trial. The metro sits within the firm's broader
[Tennessee practice](/locations/tennessee/), which follows the standards set
out on its [national coverage page](/locations/nationwide/).

## The Johnson City venue footprint

The Johnson City metropolitan area takes in Washington County, Carter
County, and Unicoi County. Engagements are accepted for matters in the
Circuit and Chancery Courts of those counties and in federal litigation in
the United States District Court for the Eastern District of Tennessee, the
United States District Court for the Middle District of Tennessee, and the
United States District Court for the Western District of Tennessee. The
choice of forum does not alter the work: the same written opinion standard,
disclosure practice, and testimony preparation apply in state and federal
court alike.

## Appointments rather than an address

Johnson City meetings are by appointment. The firm keeps no staffed office
in the metro and publishes no local street address, because honesty about
presence is part of the same discipline that governs its reports. Most
engagements begin remotely, through records review, structured interviews,
and standardized testing under publisher protocols where remote
administration is supported. When the referral questions call for in-person
evaluation, or when deposition and trial dates require attendance, travel is
scheduled from the Kansas City home office and arranged around the court's
calendar. Evaluees can be evaluated in their own communities when doing so
strengthens the findings, and counsel keep a single point of contact from
intake through testimony.

## Reading the labor market for Johnson City evaluees

Vocational findings for evaluees in the metro rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates from the OEWS program
for the Johnson City area and for Tennessee statewide, supplemented by state
workforce agency labor market information and direct employer contact where
the referral questions warrant it. Life care plans and
[medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/) are priced to
the evaluee's own market rather than to national averages, with each figure
tied to a cited source that opposing counsel can check. Where a matter also
requires economic loss calculations, the firm offers
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.
