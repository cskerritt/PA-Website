## Courts on both sides of the state line

The Chattanooga metropolitan area sits astride the Tennessee and Georgia
border. In Tennessee it takes in Hamilton County, Marion County, and
Sequatchie County, where engagements are accepted for matters in the Circuit
and Chancery Courts and in federal litigation in the United States District
Court for the Eastern District of Tennessee, the United States District
Court for the Middle District of Tennessee, and the United States District
Court for the Western District of Tennessee. In Georgia the metro includes
Catoosa County, Dade County, and Walker County, and the firm accepts matters
in the state and federal courts serving those counties on the same terms.
Whichever side of the line a case is venued on, counsel receive the same
report format, disclosure practice, and testimony preparation.

## Meetings by appointment

Chattanooga meetings are by appointment. The firm maintains no staffed
office in the metro and publishes no local street address, because honesty
about presence is part of the discipline that governs its written opinions.
Most engagements begin remotely with records review, structured interviews,
and standardized testing under publisher protocols where remote
administration is supported. When the referral questions call for in-person
evaluation, or when deposition and trial dates require attendance, travel is
scheduled from the Kansas City home office and planned against the case
calendar, with enough lead time built in to protect disclosure deadlines.

## Labor market research for Chattanooga evaluees

Vocational findings for evaluees in the metro rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates from the OEWS program
for the Chattanooga area and for the relevant statewide markets on each side
of the border, supplemented by state workforce agency labor market
information and direct employer contact where the referral questions warrant
it. Life care plans and
[medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/) are priced to
the evaluee's own market rather than to national averages, with every source
cited for the other side to examine. When economic loss calculations are
also required, the firm offers
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.
