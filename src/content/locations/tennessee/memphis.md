The Memphis metropolitan area crosses state lines, and litigation there
often does too. A single case can involve an evaluee living in Mississippi,
an employer in Tennessee, and treatment providers in Arkansas, which is why
Purinton Analytics structures Memphis engagements so that the state line
never changes the analysis. Plaintiff and defense counsel throughout the
metro receive the same
[earning capacity and employability evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/)
methodology, the same [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/)
standards, and the same documentation practice described on the firm's
[Tennessee page](/locations/tennessee/), wherever the matter is venued.

## A venue footprint that crosses state lines

On the Tennessee side, the metro takes in Shelby County, Fayette County, and
Tipton County, where engagements are accepted for matters in the Circuit and
Chancery Courts as well as in federal litigation in the United States
District Court for the Western District of Tennessee, the United States
District Court for the Middle District of Tennessee, and the United States
District Court for the Eastern District of Tennessee. Across the state line,
the metro includes DeSoto County, Marshall County, Tate County, Tunica
County, and Benton County in Mississippi, along with Crittenden County in
Arkansas, and the firm accepts matters in the state and federal courts
serving those counties under the same engagement terms. The report format,
disclosure practice, and testimony preparation do not vary by forum.

## By appointment on either side of the line

Memphis meetings are by appointment. The firm keeps no staffed office in the
metro and publishes no local street address, because accuracy about presence
is part of the discipline that governs its written opinions. Most
engagements begin remotely: records review, structured interviews, and
standardized testing under publisher protocols where remote administration
is supported. When the referral questions require in-person evaluation, or
when deposition and trial dates require attendance, travel is scheduled from
the Kansas City home office, and a single trip can cover appointments in
Tennessee, Mississippi, or Arkansas as the case demands.

## Labor market research for Memphis evaluees

Vocational findings for evaluees in the metro rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates from the OEWS program
for the Memphis area and for the relevant statewide markets, supplemented by
state workforce agency labor market information and direct employer contact
where warranted. Because the commuting area spans state lines, the labor
market analysis is framed around where the evaluee actually lives and can
reasonably work rather than around a single state's boundaries. Life care
plans and [medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/) are
priced to the evaluee's own market, and when economic loss calculations are
also needed, the firm offers
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.
