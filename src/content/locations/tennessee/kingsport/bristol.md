Bristol is written into the name of its own metropolitan area. The Kingsport-Bristol, TN-VA metropolitan area carries the town in its title and carries a state line through its middle, and the Tennessee side of that line places Bristol in Sullivan County. For counsel litigating an injury, employment, or disability matter with a Bristol party, that geography matters twice: once for venue, and once for the labor market a damages opinion must describe. Purinton Analytics accepts [vocational expert](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) and [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) referrals involving Bristol parties from plaintiff and defense counsel alike, and it treats both the venue question and the market question as things to be documented, not assumed.

## Sullivan County courts and Tennessee's federal forums

State-court matters arising from Bristol proceed in the Circuit and Chancery Courts serving Sullivan County. In federal practice, Tennessee's available forums are the United States District Court for the Eastern District of Tennessee, the United States District Court for the Middle District of Tennessee, and the United States District Court for the Western District of Tennessee; the district and division that actually control a given case, together with any registration or qualification requirement the venue imposes on a testifying expert, are identified and confirmed during the conflict check and intake process. The written opinion is prepared to the same standard for either forum: a documented record, a defined scope, and sources opposing counsel can trace.

## A labor market that straddles a state line

An earning capacity opinion for a Bristol evaluee cannot stop at the city limits or the state line, because the commuting market named by the metropolitan area does not stop there either. The analysis rests on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Kingsport-Bristol area, anchored by the principal city of Kingsport, and for Tennessee statewide, supplemented by state workforce agency labor market information and direct labor market research where the referral questions warrant it. The output is not a survey of the regional economy; it is a sourced answer to what work remains open to this evaluee, in the market this evaluee can actually reach, given documented restrictions, education, and work history.

The firm's home office is in Kansas City, and it maintains no staffed office in Bristol and publishes no local street address, because candor about presence belongs to the same discipline that governs the reports.
