## Measuring earning capacity from a small town

Scale is a fact a vocational opinion has to respect. A town the size of Mount Carmel does not contain its own labor market; its residents work across the surrounding region anchored by the principal city of Kingsport. The analysis therefore draws on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Kingsport-Bristol metropolitan area and for Tennessee statewide, on state workforce agency labor market information, and on direct labor market research where warranted, and it names each source so the conclusion can be checked. The question is narrow and concrete: which occupations remain within this evaluee's reach after the injury, and what does that work pay in the market this evaluee can actually access.
