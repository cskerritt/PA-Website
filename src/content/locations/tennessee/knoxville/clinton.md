Not every Clinton referral asks for an original opinion. Counsel often arrive with an opposing expert's report already served: an earning capacity figure that seems untethered from the evaluee's documented restrictions, or a future care plan whose recommendations cite no source a reader can check. Purinton Analytics takes those files as readily as it takes affirmative assignments, offering [rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) of opposing experts' work alongside its own [vocational expert](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) opinions and [life care plans](/services/life-care-planning/). The review applies the standard the firm applies to itself, methodology stated, sources cited, conclusions traceable to the record, and it names what holds up as plainly as what does not, because a review that only criticizes is advocacy wearing an expert's credentials. Plaintiff and defense counsel receive that scrutiny on identical terms.

## Anderson County venue and the federal forums

Clinton lies in Anderson County, and state-court damages litigation involving Clinton parties proceeds in the county's Circuit and Chancery Courts. When a matter belongs in federal court, Tennessee's district courts are the United States District Court for the Eastern District of Tennessee, the United States District Court for the Middle District of Tennessee, and the United States District Court for the Western District of Tennessee; which of them controls a particular case, together with any registration or qualification requirement, is confirmed during the conflict check and intake process. Venue changes the calendar and the caption, not the analysis: every opinion and every review is written to the same evidentiary standard regardless of the courthouse that will receive it.

## Reading the labor market around Clinton

Whether the firm is writing an opinion or reviewing someone else's, labor market evidence is the ground the analysis stands on. Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Knoxville metropolitan area and for Tennessee statewide anchor the vocational findings, state workforce agency labor market information supplements them, and direct labor market research is added when the referral questions warrant that step. In a rebuttal posture the same sources become the measuring stick: an opposing opinion is tested against the published data it should have relied on, and the gaps are documented rather than merely asserted. ## Requesting a conflict check

Clearance precedes everything, including a rebuttal review. Send the caption or the party names through the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/), identify the disclosure and testimony dates already on the docket, and hold the opposing report and other confidential records until intake confirms scope and turnaround in writing. That sequence protects privilege, protects the calendar, and tells counsel exactly what the review or opinion will cover, and by when.
