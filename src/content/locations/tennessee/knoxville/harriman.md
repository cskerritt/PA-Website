Serious damages cases from Harriman rarely call for vocational analysis alone. A catastrophic injury or wrongful termination matter typically requires the vocational analysis, the future care plan, and the economic loss calculation to fit together, and opinions assembled by strangers to each other tend to contradict at deposition. Purinton Analytics offers Harriman counsel [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement structure, so the assumptions align and the seams hold. The [vocational](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) and [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) components are equally available on their own, to plaintiff or defense counsel alike.

## The forums for a Harriman matter

Harriman is a Roane County community, and its state-court damages litigation proceeds in the Circuit and Chancery Courts. Federal matters draw on Tennessee's district courts as the available forums, namely the United States District Court for the Eastern District of Tennessee, the United States District Court for the Middle District of Tennessee, and the United States District Court for the Western District of Tennessee. Determining which venue controls, and satisfying whatever qualification or registration requirement it carries, is part of the conflict check and intake process, completed before the substantive work begins.

## Consistent assumptions, sourced findings

Coordination is worthless if the underlying research is weak, so every component of a Harriman damages analysis is independently sourced. Vocational findings rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Knoxville metropolitan area and for Tennessee statewide, joined by state workforce agency labor market information and direct labor market research where the referral questions warrant it; the metro-wide research approach is laid out on the [Knoxville metro page](/locations/tennessee/knoxville/). Care-planning elements are documented against the medical record. The economist partners then build on those inputs rather than inventing parallel ones, which is precisely what keeps a coordinated opinion coherent under cross-examination. When counsel already has an economist, the vocational and care-planning opinions are drafted so that expert can rely on them cleanly, with assumptions stated where they can be found.
