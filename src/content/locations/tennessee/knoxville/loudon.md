Purinton Analytics runs that sequence identically whether the referral arrives from the plaintiff's side or the defense, and whether the question is an [earning capacity evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/), a [life care plan](/services/life-care-planning/), or a [review of an opposing expert's report](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/).

## Grounding the numbers in the regional market

Vocational conclusions about a Loudon evaluee have to reflect a labor market that stretches from Loudon County into the broader region around Knoxville, the metro's principal city. The firm documents that market rather than assuming it: Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Knoxville metropolitan area and for Tennessee statewide supply the foundation, state workforce agency labor market information adds depth, and direct labor market research is undertaken when the referral questions justify it. Occupations are named, transferable skills reasoning is shown, and sources are cited, so the analysis can be audited by anyone with the report in hand.
