## Loudon County venue, federal options

Lenoir City lies in Loudon County, so state-court damages litigation involving Lenoir City parties is tried in the Circuit and Chancery Courts. For federal matters, Tennessee's forums are the United States District Court for the Eastern District of Tennessee, the United States District Court for the Middle District of Tennessee, and the United States District Court for the Western District of Tennessee; the district that controls any particular case, and whatever registration or qualification requirement follows from it, is pinned down during the conflict check and intake process.
