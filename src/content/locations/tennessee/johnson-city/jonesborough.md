Expert retention in a Jonesborough case tends to be a calendar problem before it is anything else: a scheduling order sets the disclosure date, the disclosure date sets the report deadline, and the report deadline decides whether counsel retains early enough to do the work properly. Purinton Analytics builds its [vocational expert](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) and [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) engagements for Jonesborough matters backward from those dates, for plaintiff and defense counsel alike, so that record collection, evaluation, and drafting each get the time a defensible opinion requires instead of being compressed into the final weeks before disclosure.

## Where Jonesborough cases are heard

Jonesborough sits in Washington County, and state-court matters involving Jonesborough parties proceed in the Washington County Circuit and Chancery Courts. On the federal side, Tennessee offers the United States District Court for the Eastern District of Tennessee, the United States District Court for the Middle District of Tennessee, and the United States District Court for the Western District of Tennessee as its trial forums; the district that actually controls a particular matter, together with any registration or qualification requirement specific to that venue, is confirmed during the conflict check and intake process. ## Grounding opinions in the Johnson City area market

A small town inside a metropolitan area borrows its labor market from its neighbors, and an earning-capacity opinion about a Jonesborough evaluee must reflect the employment available across the Johnson City, TN metropolitan area, including work in and around the principal city of Johnson City, rather than inside the town limits alone. That analysis is sourced, not asserted: Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the relevant area and for Tennessee statewide, state workforce agency labor market information, and direct labor market research where the referral questions justify it. Each occupational conclusion is tied to the evaluee's documented restrictions, education, and work history, so that on cross-examination every step from record to opinion can be walked in order.

## Scheduling the evaluation itself

Because the calendar drives the engagement, the evaluation is scheduled to fit it without shortchanging the evaluee. The firm works remote-first from its Kansas City home office; it keeps no staffed office in Jonesborough and publishes no local address, a fact stated plainly because candor about presence belongs in the same category as candor in a report. Records exchange and most interviews proceed through secure channels and secure video. When an in-person evaluation is warranted, or when deposition or trial testimony requires presence, travel is arranged from Kansas City and the evaluation takes place in the evaluee's own community. Cases that also require economic loss analysis can be structured as [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/), with independent economist partners performing and signing the economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning work moves forward under one engagement structure. Counsel with matters outside Tennessee can engage the firm through its [nationwide practice](/locations/nationwide/).
