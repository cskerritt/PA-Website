For an injured person in a very small town like Unicoi, the practical burden of litigation often shows up as travel: to treatment, to depositions, to evaluations arranged for the convenience of everyone but the evaluee. Purinton Analytics designs its engagements to remove that burden wherever possible. Its [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) and [vocational expert](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) work for parties in Unicoi, the town that shares its name with surrounding Unicoi County, runs remote-first, with in-person assessment brought to the evaluee's community when the questions require it. Referrals are welcome from either side of the caption, and the methodology does not shift with the retaining party.

## Conducting the evaluation where the evaluee lives

Most of a Unicoi engagement never requires anyone to leave home. Records move through secure channels, and interviews for both vocational and life care planning purposes are conducted by secure video when video serves the evaluee well. When an in-person evaluation is warranted, the appointment is held in the evaluee's own community, with travel scheduled from the firm's Kansas City home office; the same travel arrangements cover deposition and trial attendance. The firm keeps no staffed office in Unicoi and publishes no local street address, and it says so plainly, because a practice that is careful about every sentence in a report should be equally careful about how it describes its own footprint.

## Venue in Unicoi County and beyond

State-court matters involving Unicoi parties proceed in the Unicoi County Circuit and Chancery Courts. Tennessee's federal forums are the United States District Court for the Eastern District of Tennessee, the United States District Court for the Middle District of Tennessee, and the United States District Court for the Western District of Tennessee; which of them controls a given case, and whether any venue-specific registration or qualification requirement applies, is identified and confirmed during the conflict check and intake process. Counsel with matters elsewhere in the region can consult the [Johnson City metro page](/locations/tennessee/johnson-city/), and the [Tennessee locations page](/locations/tennessee/) addresses the rest of the state.

## Earning capacity from a small-town address

A vocational opinion about a Unicoi evaluee cannot stop at the town line, because a community this small necessarily looks outward for much of its employment, toward the wider Johnson City, TN metropolitan area and its principal city of Johnson City. The firm grounds that analysis in Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the relevant area and for Tennessee statewide, in state workforce agency labor market information, and in direct labor market research when the referral questions demand it. The result is deliberately specific: the occupations open to this person given the documented restrictions, education, and work history, and the pay attached to that work in the market this person can reach. When the matter also calls for economic loss modeling, it can proceed as a [coordinated damages engagement](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/), in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement structure. Identical standards govern engagements nationwide through the firm's [national practice](/locations/nationwide/).
