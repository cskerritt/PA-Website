## The labor market an Elizabethton evaluee faces

Elizabethton is a small city inside the Johnson City, TN metropolitan area, and a credible earning-capacity opinion has to describe employment its residents can actually reach, including work in and around the principal city of Johnson City. Vocational findings rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the relevant area and for Tennessee statewide, on state workforce agency labor market information, and on direct labor market research when the referral questions warrant it. The analysis stays narrow on purpose: given documented restrictions, education, and work history, which occupations remain open to this evaluee, and what does that work pay where this evaluee lives. Broad economic commentary is left out because it does not survive cross-examination and does not help the trier of fact.
