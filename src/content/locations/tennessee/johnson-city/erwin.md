The dispute at the center of most vocational testimony about an Erwin evaluee is geographic: which jobs count as genuinely available to a person living in a small Unicoi County town, and which exist only on paper. Purinton Analytics answers that question with sources rather than impressions, in [vocational expert](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) and [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) engagements accepted from plaintiff and defense counsel on equal terms. An opinion about post-injury employability that ignores where the evaluee actually lives, or one that quietly assumes an unlimited commute, invites the cross-examination it deserves; the firm's practice is to define the reachable market explicitly and defend the definition.

## Erwin's market inside the Johnson City metro

Erwin belongs to the Johnson City, TN metropolitan area, and for many of its residents realistic employment includes the broader metro anchored by the principal city of Johnson City as well as work closer to home. The vocational analysis draws on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the relevant area and for Tennessee statewide, on state workforce agency labor market information, and on direct labor market research when the referral questions call for employer-level detail. Those sources are then filtered through the individual file: restrictions supported by the medical record, education, transferable skills, and actual work history. What survives that filter is the evaluee's residual earning capacity, stated with its assumptions visible so either side can test them.

## How an Erwin evaluee is evaluated

No part of the engagement asks the evaluee to accommodate the expert. The firm operates from its Kansas City home office, keeps no staffed office in Erwin, and publishes no local street address; it says so directly because honesty about presence is part of what makes the reports credible. Records are exchanged through secure channels, and vocational and life care planning interviews are held by secure video whenever video serves the evaluee well. If the referral questions require in-person assessment, or if deposition and trial dates require attendance, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office and the assessment is conducted in the evaluee's own community. Where the case also needs economic loss modeling, it can be organized as a [coordinated damages engagement](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/), in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement structure. The same standards apply to matters anywhere in the country through the firm's [nationwide practice](/locations/nationwide/).
