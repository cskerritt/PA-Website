## A Small Town Inside a Metro Labor Market

Dandridge is a small community, but its residents participate in the labor market of the wider Morristown, TN metropolitan area, whose principal city is Morristown. That distinction matters in litigation. An earning-capacity opinion keyed only to opportunities inside a small town would understate or misstate what is realistically available to the evaluee, so the analysis is framed at the metro and state level, using Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Morristown metropolitan area and for Tennessee statewide, state workforce agency labor market information, and direct labor market research where the questions call for it. The estimates are always characterized qualitatively; the numbers stay in the cited tables where both sides can verify them.
