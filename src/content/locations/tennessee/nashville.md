The Nashville metropolitan area, reaching from Davidson County out through
Murfreesboro and Franklin, sends Purinton Analytics a steady mix of vocational and care-planning questions
from plaintiff and defense counsel alike: an
[earning capacity and employability evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/)
in an injury or employment matter, a
[life care plan](/services/life-care-planning/) in a catastrophic injury
case, or a [rebuttal review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) of an opposing
expert's report. Whatever the posture, the work follows the same standard the
firm applies across its [Tennessee practice](/locations/tennessee/): a
documented conflict check, a defined scope, and a written opinion built to
withstand examination by the other side of the caption.

## Venues across the Nashville metro

The metro's footprint is wide. It takes in Davidson County, Rutherford
County, and Williamson County along with Cannon County, Cheatham County,
Dickson County, Hickman County, Macon County, Maury County, Robertson
County, Smith County, Sumner County, Trousdale County, and Wilson County.
Engagements are accepted for matters in the Circuit and Chancery Courts of
those counties and in federal litigation in the United States District Court
for the Middle District of Tennessee, the United States District Court for
the Eastern District of Tennessee, and the United States District Court for
the Western District of Tennessee. The forum changes nothing about the work
product: the same disclosure practice, report format, and testimony
preparation apply in every courtroom.

## Appointments, not an office

Nashville meetings are by appointment. The firm maintains no staffed office
in the metro and publishes no local street address, because honesty about
presence belongs to the same discipline that governs its reports. Most
engagements begin remotely, with records review, structured interviews, and
standardized testing under publisher protocols where remote administration
is supported. When the referral questions call for in-person evaluation, or
when deposition and trial dates require attendance, travel is scheduled from
the Kansas City home office and planned around the court's calendar. Counsel
work with a single point of contact from intake through testimony,
regardless of where each step is performed.

## Labor market research for Middle Tennessee evaluees

Vocational findings for evaluees in the metro rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates from the OEWS program
for the Nashville area and for Tennessee statewide, supplemented by state
workforce agency labor market information and direct employer contact where
the referral questions warrant it. Life care plans and
[medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/) are priced to
the evaluee's own market rather than to national averages, with every source
cited so opposing counsel can trace each figure to its origin. When a matter
also requires economic loss calculations, the firm offers
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Opening a Nashville engagement

Any venue-specific registration or qualification requirement is identified
and confirmed during the conflict check and intake process, before
substantive work begins. To explore availability for a matter venued in the
Nashville metro, submit the caption or party names through the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and note any imminent disclosure,
deposition, or trial dates so scheduling can be confirmed alongside
conflicts. The same intake path serves counsel anywhere in the country, as
described on the firm's [national coverage page](/locations/nationwide/).
