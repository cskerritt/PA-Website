Damages cases rarely need just one expert discipline. A serious injury claim arising in Brentwood may require a vocational opinion on lost earning capacity, a life care plan for future medical needs, and an economic analysis reducing both to present value, and when those opinions come from experts who never coordinate, the seams show at trial. Purinton Analytics structures Brentwood engagements so the disciplines fit together from intake forward: the [vocational analysis](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) and [life care plan](/services/life-care-planning/) proceed under a defined scope, and where economic valuation is needed, [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) bring in independent economist partners who perform and sign the economic analysis under one engagement structure, with authorship of every opinion disclosed. Plaintiff and defense counsel in Williamson County retain the practice on identical terms.

## Coherence without advocacy

Coordination does not mean the opinions bend toward a result. Each professional forms and defends their own conclusions; what the structure eliminates is the inconsistency that arises when the care plan assumes restrictions the vocational report never found, or the economic model values items no clinician supported. Every input that crosses between disciplines is documented, so opposing counsel can trace exactly what each expert relied on and why.

## The labor market from a Brentwood address

Earning capacity opinions for Brentwood evaluees are grounded in Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Nashville metropolitan area and for Tennessee statewide, plus state workforce agency labor market information and direct labor market research where the referral questions warrant it.
