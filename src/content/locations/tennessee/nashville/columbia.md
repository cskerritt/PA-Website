A vocational opinion is about a person before it is about a place. For a Columbia case, Purinton Analytics starts with the evaluee's own record, the work history, education, documented restrictions, and transferable skills that the file actually contains, and only then asks what the labor market offers someone with exactly that profile. The order matters: an opinion built from the place downward tends to assume opportunities the evaluee cannot use, while an opinion built from the person outward can defend every step it took. The practice writes its Columbia opinions to that standard for plaintiff and defense counsel alike, as part of coverage spanning the [Nashville metro area](/locations/tennessee/nashville/), [all of Tennessee](/locations/tennessee/), and [engagements nationwide](/locations/nationwide/).

## Maury County courts and the federal alternatives

Columbia matters in state court proceed in the Circuit and Chancery Courts serving Maury County. Federal claims may be venued in the United States District Court for the Middle District of Tennessee, the United States District Court for the Eastern District of Tennessee, or the United States District Court for the Western District of Tennessee; which district governs a particular Columbia case, and any expert qualification or registration requirement that forum imposes, is identified and confirmed during the conflict check and intake process. The report is prepared to the receiving court's disclosure standard, whichever forum that turns out to be.

## The person defines the market

Vocational conclusions for Columbia evaluees rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Nashville metropolitan area and for Tennessee statewide, together with state workforce agency labor market information and direct labor market research where warranted. The [transferable skills analysis](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) weighs both the employment available within Maury County and the positions reachable elsewhere in the metro commuting market, and it treats commute feasibility as evidence, not assumption: restrictions, driving capacity, transportation circumstances, and work history all bear on what counts as available work for this person. The geographic scope each conclusion assumes is stated in the report itself, where it can be examined and challenged like any other input.

## Life care planning where the evaluee lives

For serious injury cases, [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) assessments are conducted with the evaluee's actual community in view, because a plan that prices care the person cannot practically reach invites challenge. When an in-person evaluation is warranted, it is arranged in Columbia by appointment, with travel scheduled from the Kansas City home office. The firm has no office in Columbia and publishes no local address; that is stated as a matter of simple fact, because the evaluation happens where the evaluee is, and what counsel actually needs from the expert is a confirmed appointment, not a nearby building. Where the damages case also needs economic valuation, coordinated engagements bring in [independent economist partners](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) who perform and sign the economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds within the same engagement structure.

## What a Columbia referral looks like

Counsel typically arrives with a scheduling order already in place, so intake starts with dates: expert disclosure deadlines, rebuttal windows, and any deposition settings, matched against the work the opinion genuinely requires. If the calendar cannot support defensible work, that judgment is shared before retention rather than discovered after.
