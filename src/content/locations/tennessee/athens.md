Distance is usually the first practical question counsel ask about an
Athens engagement, and the honest answer is that most of the work never
requires anyone to travel. Records review, structured vocational
interviews, and standardized testing under publisher protocols all proceed
remotely when remote administration is sound, which lets Purinton Analytics
serve matters across McMinn and Meigs counties on the same footing as cases
in much larger markets. The firm's work here spans
[vocational evaluation of employability and earning capacity](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/),
[medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/), and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## The venue footprint: McMinn and Meigs counties

State court matters are accepted in the Circuit and Chancery Courts of
McMinn County and the Circuit and Chancery Courts of Meigs County, for
plaintiff and defense engagements alike. In the federal system, the firm
accepts matters across Tennessee, whether venued in the United States
District Court for the Eastern District of Tennessee, the United States
District Court for the Middle District of Tennessee, or the United States
District Court for the Western District of Tennessee. Whatever the forum,
the sequence is fixed: documented conflict check, written scope, records
request tailored to the claims, and a report meant to be read closely by
the other side. The [Tennessee overview](/locations/tennessee/) shows how
this local work fits the statewide practice.

## When presence is needed

Athens meetings are by appointment. The firm keeps no staffed office in the
area and publishes no street address for this location; candor about
presence is part of the same discipline the reports themselves are held to.
When a referral question genuinely calls for an in-person evaluation, or
when a deposition or trial date requires the expert in the room, travel is
scheduled from the Kansas City home office and confirmed with counsel well
ahead of the litigation calendar. The choice between remote and in-person
work is made on the merits of the referral questions, and the report
discloses which was used.

## Evidence about the Athens labor market

Employability and earning capacity opinions for Athens area evaluees are
grounded in Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage
estimates for the area and for Tennessee statewide, in state workforce
agency labor market information, and in direct labor market research when
the questions call for primary evidence. In a micropolitan market the
distance between statewide data and a particular evaluee's realistic
options can be significant, so the written opinion addresses that gap
explicitly rather than papering over it. Counsel confronting an opposing
expert's labor market analysis can retain the firm for
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) under the same
standard.

## Starting an Athens engagement

The path in mirrors the firm's [national practice](/locations/nationwide/):
begin with a conflict check. [Submit the caption or party names](/refer-a-case/)
along with the nature of the claims and any imminent disclosure,
