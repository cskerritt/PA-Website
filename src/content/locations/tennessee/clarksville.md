## Venue footprint across Tennessee and Kentucky

The Clarksville metropolitan area spans the Tennessee and Kentucky line. On
the Tennessee side it takes in Montgomery County and Stewart County, where
engagements are accepted for matters in the Circuit and Chancery Courts and
in federal litigation in the United States District Court for the Middle
District of Tennessee, the United States District Court for the Eastern
District of Tennessee, and the United States District Court for the Western
District of Tennessee. On the Kentucky side the metro includes Christian
County and Trigg County, and the firm accepts matters in the state and
federal courts serving those counties under the same engagement terms. The
analysis, the report format, and the disclosure practice do not change at
the state line.

## In-person work when the case requires it

Clarksville meetings are by appointment. The firm keeps no staffed office in
the metro and publishes no local street address, because accuracy about
presence is part of the discipline behind its reports. When the referral
questions call for in-person evaluation, or when deposition and trial dates
require attendance, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office and
coordinated with counsel's deadlines. Evaluations can be conducted in the
evaluee's own community when that strengthens the reliability of the
findings, and testimony is prepared to the same standard whether it is
delivered in person or by remote appearance where the court allows it.

## Labor market evidence for Clarksville evaluees

Vocational findings for evaluees in the metro rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates from the OEWS program
for the Clarksville area and for the relevant statewide markets in Tennessee
and Kentucky, supplemented by state workforce agency labor market
information and direct employer contact where warranted. Because the
commuting area crosses the state line, the employability analysis is framed
around the labor market the evaluee can actually reach. Life care plans and
[medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/) are priced to
the evaluee's own market, and when economic loss calculations are also
needed, the firm offers
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.
