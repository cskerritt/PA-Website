Injury and employment disputes in the Morristown area tend to converge on
the same questions: what work can this person still perform, what will
future care actually require, and what is the resulting loss. Purinton
Analytics answers those questions for counsel on both sides of the caption
through [vocational evaluation of employability and earning capacity](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/). Where the
damages model calls for economic analysis, the firm arranges
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Venues in Hamblen and Jefferson counties

The firm accepts matters venued in the Circuit and Chancery Courts of
Hamblen County and the Circuit and Chancery Courts of Jefferson County, and
it accepts federal matters across the state, whether filed in the United
States District Court for the Eastern District of Tennessee, the United
States District Court for the Middle District of Tennessee, or the United
States District Court for the Western District of Tennessee. Retention
works identically in each forum: a documented conflict check, a written
scope, a records request tailored to the claims, and a report designed to
survive examination by opposing counsel. Counsel with matters elsewhere in
the state will find the full picture on the
[Tennessee overview](/locations/tennessee/).

## Presence, stated plainly

The firm maintains no staffed office in Morristown and publishes no street
address for this location. Meetings happen by appointment, and most of the
work happens at a distance: records are reviewed, interviews are conducted,
and standardized instruments are administered remotely under publisher
protocols wherever remote administration is sound. When the referral
questions justify an in-person evaluation, or when deposition and trial
dates require attendance, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home
office. Stating that plainly is intentional; a practice that is careful
about how it describes its own footprint is easier to trust about how it
describes an evaluee's earning capacity.

## Grounding opinions in the regional labor market

Vocational conclusions for Morristown area evaluees draw on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the metropolitan
area and for Tennessee statewide, on state workforce agency labor market
information, and on direct labor market research when a specific occupation
or employer question needs a primary source. The written opinion identifies
which occupations were considered, why they were or were not suitable, and
what the wage evidence supports, so that the path from data to conclusion
is visible to both sides. When counsel need an assessment of an opposing
expert's use of the same data, the firm provides
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) under the same
standards it applies to its own reports.

## Opening a Morristown engagement

As with every matter in the firm's [national practice](/locations/nationwide/),
the engagement begins with a conflict check rather than a sales
conversation. [Submit the caption or party names](/refer-a-case/) with a
short description of the claims and any imminent disclosure, deposition, or
trial dates. Once conflicts clear, scope and schedule are confirmed in
writing, and the records request issues so the analysis can begin while
discovery is still open.
