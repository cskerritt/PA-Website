## Hamilton County courts and the federal forums

East Ridge lies in Hamilton County, where state-court matters proceed in the Circuit and Chancery Courts. Tennessee's federal forums are the United States District Court for the Eastern District of Tennessee, the United States District Court for the Middle District of Tennessee, and the United States District Court for the Western District of Tennessee; which of them controls a given case, and any venue-specific registration or qualification requirement that attaches to it, is identified and confirmed during the conflict check and intake process. The written opinion is prepared the same way for either forum: a documented record, a defined scope, and sources the other side can check. ## Defining the labor market for an East Ridge evaluee

Because the metropolitan area crosses a state line, the vocational analysis names its market deliberately. Findings rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Chattanooga metropolitan area and for Tennessee statewide, supplemented by state workforce agency labor market information and direct labor market research where the referral questions call for it. The question is narrow and answerable: given this evaluee's restrictions, education, and work history, which occupations remain open in the market anchored by the principal city of Chattanooga, and what do the public data say about them, described qualitatively and cited to their sources.

## By-appointment service, remote-first work

The firm's only staffed office is its Kansas City home office; it maintains no East Ridge location and publishes no local street address, because candor about presence belongs to the same discipline as candor in a report. Records exchange and counsel conferences run through secure remote channels, and evaluee interviews proceed by secure video when video serves the evaluee well. When an in-person evaluation is warranted, or when deposition and trial dates require attendance, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office and the assessment is conducted in the evaluee's own community. Matters that also require economic loss modeling can be structured as [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/), in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement structure. Referrals beyond Tennessee are handled through the firm's [nationwide practice](/locations/nationwide/).
