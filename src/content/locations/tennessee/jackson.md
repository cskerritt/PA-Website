## Venues in the Jackson metro

The Jackson metropolitan area takes in Madison County, Chester County,
Crockett County, and Gibson County. Engagements are accepted for matters in
the Circuit and Chancery Courts of those counties and in federal litigation
in the United States District Court for the Western District of Tennessee,
the United States District Court for the Middle District of Tennessee, and
the United States District Court for the Eastern District of Tennessee.
Whether a matter proceeds in state or federal court, counsel receive the
same written opinion standard, the same disclosure practice, and testimony
prepared to the same level of rigor.

## By appointment in West Tennessee

Jackson meetings are by appointment. The firm maintains no staffed office in
the metro and publishes no local street address, because accuracy about
presence is part of the discipline that governs its written work. Most
engagements begin remotely, with records review, structured interviews, and
standardized testing under publisher protocols where remote administration
is supported, which keeps early-stage costs proportionate for cases of every
size. When the referral questions call for in-person evaluation, or when
deposition and trial dates require attendance, travel is scheduled from the
Kansas City home office and planned around the court's calendar, and
evaluees can be seen in their own communities when that serves the
evaluation.

## Labor market research for Jackson evaluees

Vocational findings for evaluees in the metro rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates from the OEWS program
for the Jackson area and for Tennessee statewide, supplemented by state
workforce agency labor market information and direct employer contact where
the referral questions warrant it. The analysis reflects the employment
actually available to the evaluee in the regional market rather than
averages drawn from distant metros. Life care plans and
[medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/) are priced to
the evaluee's own market, and when a matter also requires economic loss
calculations, the firm offers
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.
