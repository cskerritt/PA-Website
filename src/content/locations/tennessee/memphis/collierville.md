## The market a Collierville evaluee actually faces

A residual earning capacity that exists only on paper is worthless to either side, so the vocational analysis for a Collierville evaluee is grounded in the metropolitan labor market anchored by Memphis, the principal city, where much of the area's employment is concentrated. The foundation is qualitative and sourced: Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Memphis metropolitan area and for Tennessee statewide, state workforce agency labor market information, and direct labor market research when the referral questions require it. Each conclusion about available occupations and their pay is tied to a cited source, because a deadline met with an undocumented opinion is no better than a deadline missed.

## Appointments, travel, and remote work

There is no staffed Purinton Analytics office in Collierville and no published local address; meetings are by appointment, and honesty on that point is deliberate. Most of the engagement runs remotely, with records moving through secure channels and interviews held by secure video when video serves the evaluee well. Where the case requires an in-person evaluation, or where deposition and trial appearances demand presence, travel is arranged from the Kansas City home office and the evaluation takes place in the evaluee's own community. When economic loss modeling is also needed, the matter can proceed as a [coordinated damages engagement](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis alongside the vocational and care-planning work, all within a single engagement structure. The [nationwide practice](/locations/nationwide/) extends identical service to counsel with matters outside Tennessee.
