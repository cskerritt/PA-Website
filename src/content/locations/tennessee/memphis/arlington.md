## A small suburb inside a large labor market

Arlington may be a small community, but its workers are priced by a metropolitan labor market anchored by Memphis, the principal city, and the vocational analysis is calibrated accordingly. Earning-capacity findings draw on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Memphis metropolitan area and for Tennessee statewide, on state workforce agency labor market information, and on direct labor market research where the referral questions justify it. The output is deliberately narrow: given this evaluee's restrictions, education, and work history, which occupations remain realistically open, and what do they pay in the market this person can reach from where they live.
