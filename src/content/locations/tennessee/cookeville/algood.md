Every earning capacity opinion rests on a definitional choice that deserves more scrutiny than it usually gets: which labor market should this evaluee be measured against? For a small Putnam County town like Algood, that choice can move the entire damages analysis, and Purinton Analytics treats it as a finding to be supported rather than an assumption to be made. The firm prepares [vocational expert](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) and [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) opinions for plaintiff and defense counsel in Algood matters, and each report states which market was used, why it was used, and where the data describing it come from, so the foundation of the opinion can be examined by either side of the caption.

## Defining the Algood evaluee's market

Algood sits within the Cookeville metropolitan area, whose principal city is Cookeville, and the public data that anchor vocational findings are published at that metropolitan level: Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Cookeville metropolitan area and for Tennessee statewide, supplemented by state workforce agency labor market information and by direct labor market research where the referral questions warrant it. Whether a particular evaluee can realistically reach opportunities across that metro market, though, is not presumed from geography. It is developed from the record: documented medical restrictions, education and training, work history, and the other individual facts that intake gathers. A market drawn too wide overstates capacity and a market drawn too narrow understates it, and the written opinion defends its definition either way, in terms both sides can test.
