Expert engagements are won or lost on the calendar as often as on the merits, and that is where Purinton Analytics asks Monterey counsel to start: what disclosure deadline, deposition setting, or trial date is already fixed in this case? A [vocational expert](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) or [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) opinion produced under time pressure without a records foundation invites exclusion, so the firm works backward from the dates counsel supply, sequencing the records request, the evaluee interview, and the draft-to-final report timeline so that the written opinion is complete, sourced, and defensible before it is due. That scheduling discipline is offered equally to plaintiff and defense counsel with Monterey matters.

## Building the report against the disclosure date

Intake for a Monterey engagement fixes the essentials in writing at the outset: the scope of the opinions sought, the governing venue, and the date each deliverable must be ready. From there the file develops in a set order. Records arrive through secure electronic exchange and are logged as received; the evaluee interview is held by secure video or, when the questions require it, in person in the evaluee's own community, with travel scheduled from the Kansas City home office; and the report is written so that every finding traces to a document or a cited public source. The firm maintains no office in Monterey and says so plainly, because a report that will be tested by opposing counsel should come from a practice that states even small facts exactly.
