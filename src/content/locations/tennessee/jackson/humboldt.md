A defensible opinion about a Humboldt worker is assembled from records before it is argued from expertise. That is the order of operations Purinton Analytics follows on every Humboldt referral: the file comes first, built from a records request tailored to the claims, and the [vocational expert](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) or [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) opinion is written only from what that file supports. Treating records, work history, and the evaluee's own account are gathered and cross-checked before conclusions are drafted, so that the finished report can point to a document for every material finding when opposing counsel begins turning its pages.

## Where Humboldt cases are heard

Humboldt lies in Gibson County, and state-court matters arising there proceed in the Circuit and Chancery Courts serving the county. Cases resting on federal-question or diversity grounds may proceed in the United States District Court for the Western District of Tennessee, the United States District Court for the Middle District of Tennessee, or the United States District Court for the Eastern District of Tennessee. The firm resolves which forum controls a particular Humboldt dispute with counsel during the conflict check and intake, along with any registration or qualification requirement the controlling forum imposes. Nothing about the analysis changes with the venue; the same methods and the same documentation standard apply wherever the case is filed.

## The record behind the labor market findings

The same records discipline governs the market side of the opinion. A Humboldt evaluee's employability and earning capacity are measured against the surrounding Jackson metropolitan area, of which Humboldt is one of the smaller communities, and every market conclusion is tied to a named source: Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Jackson area and for Tennessee statewide, state workforce agency labor market information, and direct labor market research where the assignment warrants employer-level inquiry. Scale is described qualitatively, and where the published estimates cannot answer a question, the report identifies the limit instead of guessing past it.

## What counsel receives instead of a local office

There is no Purinton Analytics office in Humboldt, and the firm publishes no local address because it has none; the practice is national, run from a home office in Kansas City, and Humboldt engagements are handled by appointment. Interviews and testing proceed remotely when the defined scope allows, and are conducted in the evaluee's own community when the questions require presence. Depositions and trial testimony in Gibson County are met through travel scheduled from the Kansas City home office once dates are set, arranged in advance rather than left to chance.

## When the file must support a full damages case

Where lost earnings must be reduced to present value, the firm offers [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement structure. Counsel who already hold an opposing disclosure can ask instead for a [rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/), and a [medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/) can be added where future care is disputed. Each component keeps its own boundary and its own signatory, and plaintiff and defense referrals are accepted on the same terms.
