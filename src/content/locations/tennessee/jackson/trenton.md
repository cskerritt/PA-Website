## The venue behind the calendar

Trenton lies in Gibson County, where civil matters proceed in the Circuit and Chancery Courts serving the county. If the case sounds in federal jurisdiction, Tennessee's available federal forums are the United States District Court for the Western District of Tennessee, the United States District Court for the Middle District of Tennessee, and the United States District Court for the Eastern District of Tennessee. The firm takes no position on which district reaches a given dispute until the conflict check and intake confirm it; that confirmation also surfaces any qualification or registration requirement particular to the controlling forum, so nothing about admissibility is discovered late.
