## Venues from Kingsport to Bristol

The Kingsport-Bristol metropolitan area straddles the Tennessee and Virginia
line. In Tennessee it takes in Sullivan County and Hawkins County, where
engagements are accepted for matters in the Circuit and Chancery Courts and
in federal litigation in the United States District Court for the Eastern
District of Tennessee, the United States District Court for the Middle
District of Tennessee, and the United States District Court for the Western
District of Tennessee. In Virginia the metro includes Scott County,
Washington County, and the independent city of Bristol, and the firm
accepts matters in the state and federal courts serving those communities on
the same terms. Counsel on either side of the caption, and either side of
the state line, receive an identical report format and disclosure practice.

## By appointment in the metro

Kingsport and Bristol meetings are by appointment. The firm maintains no
staffed office in the metro and publishes no local street address, because
accuracy about presence belongs to the same discipline that shapes its
opinions. Most engagements begin remotely, with records review, structured
interviews, and standardized testing under publisher protocols where remote
administration is supported. When the referral questions call for in-person
evaluation, or when deposition and trial dates require attendance, travel is
scheduled from the Kansas City home office and planned around the case
calendar, and evaluees can be seen in their own communities when that serves
the evaluation.

## Labor market research on both sides of the line

Vocational findings for evaluees in the metro rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates from the OEWS program
for the Kingsport-Bristol area and for the relevant statewide markets in
Tennessee and Virginia, supplemented by state workforce agency labor market
information and direct employer contact where the referral questions warrant
it. The employability analysis follows the labor market the evaluee can
actually reach rather than an arbitrary state boundary. Life care plans and
[medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/) are priced to
the evaluee's own market, and matters that also require economic loss
calculations can proceed as
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.
