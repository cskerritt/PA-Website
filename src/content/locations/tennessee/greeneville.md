The written report is the product. Every Greeneville engagement Purinton
Analytics accepts is organized around that premise: the opinion must
document its record, show its method, and survive the scrutiny of whichever
side did not commission it. On that foundation the firm offers Greene
County counsel [vocational expert evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/)
of employability and earning capacity,
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) and
[medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/) in serious
injury matters, and [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Where Greeneville matters are heard

State court engagements proceed in the Circuit and Chancery Courts of
Greene County. Federal engagements are accepted wherever in Tennessee they
are venued: the United States District Court for the Eastern District of
Tennessee, the United States District Court for the Middle District of
Tennessee, and the United States District Court for the Western District of
Tennessee. The forum changes the deadlines, not the discipline; the
conflict check, the written scope, and the tailored records request come
first in every court. Counsel weighing venues or handling related matters
elsewhere in the state can start from the
[Tennessee overview](/locations/tennessee/), which maps the markets the
firm serves statewide.

## An honest account of presence

Greeneville meetings are by appointment only. The firm has no staffed
office here, publishes no street address for this location, and does not
pretend otherwise, because a practice that stretches the truth about its
own footprint invites the same question about its findings. The working
model is remote-first: records review, structured interviews, and
standardized testing under publisher protocols where remote administration
is appropriate. When the referral questions call for an in-person
evaluation, or when deposition or trial requires attendance, travel is
scheduled from the Kansas City home office with enough lead time to
protect the litigation calendar.

## Labor market evidence for Greene County evaluees

Vocational findings for evaluees in the Greeneville area are built on
Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for
the area and for Tennessee statewide, on state workforce agency labor
market information, and on direct labor market research when a question
requires primary evidence about local hiring. Because the report is written
for adversarial reading, it names its sources, states its assumptions, and
distinguishes what the data establish from what they merely suggest. The
same standard applies when the assignment runs the other way: counsel may
retain the firm for [rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/)
of an opposing expert's use of labor market data, methodology, and
foundation.

## The first step in a Greeneville engagement

Engagements here begin the way they begin across the firm's
[national practice](/locations/nationwide/): with a conflict check, not a
pitch. [Submit the caption or party names](/refer-a-case/) together with a
brief description of the claims and any imminent disclosure, deposition,
or trial dates. When conflicts clear, the firm confirms scope and schedule
in writing and issues its records request, so the analysis is underway
while discovery still allows the opinion to do its work.
