The Tullahoma-Manchester area carries the names of its principal towns, and
Purinton Analytics serves both, together with the rest of Coffee and Moore
counties, through the same engagement model it applies nationwide. Counsel
for plaintiffs and defendants retain the firm here for
[vocational expert evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) of
employability and earning capacity,
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) in catastrophic injury
cases, and [expert testimony and litigation consulting](/services/expert-testimony-litigation-consulting/)
through deposition and trial. Where the damages case requires economic
modeling, the firm structures
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Venues in Coffee and Moore counties and the federal courts

The firm accepts state court matters in the Circuit and Chancery Courts of
Coffee County and the Circuit and Chancery Courts of Moore County. Federal
matters are accepted throughout Tennessee, whether they proceed in the
United States District Court for the Eastern District of Tennessee, the
United States District Court for the Middle District of Tennessee, or the
United States District Court for the Western District of Tennessee. Each
engagement, in each forum, opens the same way: a documented conflict check,
a written scope, and a records request tailored to the claims, so that the
eventual opinion rests on a record both sides can examine. The
[Tennessee overview](/locations/tennessee/) places this area within the
firm's statewide coverage.

## By appointment from the Kansas City home office

Meetings in Tullahoma or Manchester are by appointment. The firm maintains
no staffed office in either community and publishes no street address for
this location; it describes its footprint exactly as it is, on the theory
that a report writer who is careful with small facts is more likely to be
careful with large ones. Most engagements run remotely, with records
review, structured interviews, and standardized testing administered under
publisher protocols that support remote use. When the referral questions
warrant an in-person evaluation, or when deposition and trial dates demand
presence, travel is planned from the Kansas City home office around
counsel's schedule.

## Labor market research across the area

Vocational conclusions for evaluees in the Tullahoma-Manchester area rest
on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates
for the area and for Tennessee statewide, on state workforce agency labor
market information, and on direct labor market research where a specific
occupational question deserves primary evidence. The written opinion shows
which occupations were considered and why, ties wage conclusions to the
cited sources, and acknowledges the limits of the data in a smaller market
rather than borrowing conclusions from larger ones. Counsel who need an
opposing expert's analysis tested against that standard can engage the firm
for [rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/).

As throughout the firm's [national practice](/locations/nationwide/), the
