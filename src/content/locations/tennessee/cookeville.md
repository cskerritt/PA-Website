Litigation arising in Putnam, Overton, Jackson, and White counties reaches
Purinton Analytics through a deliberately simple channel: counsel on either
side of the caption request a conflict check, define the referral questions,
and receive a written opinion built on a record both parties can examine. For
the Cookeville area that work most often means
[earning capacity and employability evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/)
in injury and employment matters,
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) in catastrophic injury
cases, and [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Courts serving the Cookeville area

Engagements are accepted for matters in the Circuit and Chancery Courts of
Putnam County and in the Circuit and Chancery Courts of the surrounding
counties, including Overton County, Jackson County, and White County. Federal
matters are accepted wherever in the state they are venued, whether in the
United States District Court for the Eastern District of Tennessee, the
United States District Court for the Middle District of Tennessee, or the
United States District Court for the Western District of Tennessee. The
[Tennessee overview page](/locations/tennessee/) describes how the same
intake, methodology, and disclosure practices apply across every venue the
firm serves in the state, so counsel can expect an identical engagement
structure whether the case sits in a county Chancery Court or a federal
courtroom.

## By appointment, without a local storefront

Cookeville meetings are by appointment. The firm keeps no staffed office in
the area and publishes no street address for this location, because accuracy
about presence is part of the same discipline that governs its reports. Most
engagements begin remotely: records review, structured vocational
interviews, and standardized testing under publisher protocols where remote
administration is appropriate. When the referral questions call for
in-person evaluation, or when deposition and trial dates require the expert
in the room, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office and
planned around counsel's calendar. Counsel receive a defined scope and a
records request tailored to the claims before any analysis begins, and the
file documents each step so the resulting opinion can be tested on
cross-examination.

## Labor market research for Cookeville evaluees

Vocational findings for evaluees in the Cookeville area rest on Bureau of
Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the area and
for Tennessee statewide, on state workforce agency labor market information,
and on direct labor market research where the referral questions warrant it.
Because the area is a micropolitan market rather than a major metropolitan
one, the analysis pays particular attention to the occupations actually
available within a reasonable commute of the evaluee's community, and the
written opinion states plainly what the data can and cannot support. Where
an opposing report overstates or understates that market, counsel may also
engage the firm for [rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/)
of the methodology.

## Starting a Cookeville engagement

The first step is the same one used across the firm's
[national practice](/locations/nationwide/): submit the caption or party
names through the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and note any
imminent disclosure, deposition, or trial dates. Once conflicts clear,
counsel receive a defined scope, a schedule that works backward from the
dates that matter, and a consistent point of contact for the life of the
engagement.
