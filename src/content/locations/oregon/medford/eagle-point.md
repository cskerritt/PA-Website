An Eagle Point referral often begins with the other side's expert report already in the file. When it does, Purinton Analytics reads that disclosure the way a cross-examiner would, then tells retaining counsel candidly what it finds: where the opposing methodology holds, where it does not, and whether the case calls for a [rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/), an affirmative opinion of the firm's own, or both. Plaintiff and defense counsel receive the same candor, because a weakness the firm will not name at intake is a weakness opposing counsel will name at deposition.

## Rebuttal work and affirmative opinions

Rebuttal review in an Eagle Point matter examines the opposing expert's sources, methods, and reasoning against the standards of the discipline, and the resulting critique is confined to what the record supports. Where the case also needs the firm's own analysis, a [vocational expert](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) opinion or a [life care plan](/services/life-care-planning/) is prepared under its own separate scope, so the affirmative work stands on its own foundations instead of leaning on the criticism of someone else's.

## Where Eagle Point cases are heard

Civil matters arising in Eagle Point proceed in the Circuit Court serving Jackson County, and the firm calibrates its disclosure and deposition preparation to that court's calendar. A matter presenting federal-question or diversity grounds may instead be litigated in the United States District Court for the District of Oregon, and the conflict check and intake process settles which forum governs before scope and schedule are fixed, so the report is prepared for the court that will actually receive it.

## Grounding the labor market analysis

Eagle Point is a small town within the Medford metropolitan area, and vocational conclusions for an Eagle Point evaluee are grounded in data covering that metropolitan area rather than in impressions of the town. The firm relies on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Medford metropolitan area and for Oregon statewide, supplemented by state workforce agency labor market information and by direct labor market research when the referral questions call for it. The same sourcing standard applies whether the firm is writing its own opinion or testing an opposing expert's, and the report distinguishes what the published data shows from what it cannot.

## By-appointment service in Eagle Point

Purinton Analytics keeps its home office in Kansas City and maintains no location in Eagle Point; counsel should expect appointments, not a storefront, and the firm says so because candor about presence belongs in the same category as candor in an opinion. Records review and rebuttal analysis proceed remotely as a rule. In-person interviewing, testing, or observation, when the referral requires it, is arranged in the evaluee's own community, and travel for deposition or trial testimony is scheduled from the Kansas City home office around the dates the court sets.

## Coordinating the full damages picture

When economic loss is in issue, the firm structures [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement structure.
