A serious injury case out of Ashland tends to raise a question about future care and a question about remaining work at the same time. Purinton Analytics is retained on both. Counsel for a plaintiff or a defendant can engage the firm for a [life care plan](/services/life-care-planning/) that documents what the medical record justifies in future treatment, equipment, and support, for a [vocational assessment](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) of what employment remains realistic for the evaluee, or for both together, with the opinions developed on a shared record so that neither undercuts the other under examination.

## Care needs and work capacity, analyzed together

The life care plan and the vocational opinion answer different questions about the same Ashland resident, and the firm treats consistency between them as a methodological requirement rather than a happy accident. The care plan addresses the treatment and support the injury will demand; the vocational analysis addresses restrictions, transferable skills, and the employment those restrictions still permit; and each opinion cites the records and data behind its conclusions so that both can be checked against the same file. When a referral needs only a single discipline, that discipline is delivered alone under its own defined scope.

## Jackson County courts and the federal alternative

Civil litigation arising in Ashland proceeds in the Circuit Court serving Jackson County, and the firm prepares its disclosures with that court's practices in mind. Where federal-question or diversity jurisdiction exists, a matter may instead be heard in the United States District Court for the District of Oregon; which forum will govern a particular case is resolved during the conflict check and intake, not guessed from the caption. The opinions themselves are written to a standard that does not depend on the forum, so a change of court does not require a change of report.

## The labor market behind an Ashland opinion

Ashland is a small city within the Medford metropolitan area, in which Medford is the principal city, and earning-capacity conclusions for an Ashland evaluee are grounded accordingly. The firm draws on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Medford metropolitan area and for Oregon statewide, on state workforce agency labor market information, and on direct labor market research when the referral questions justify that added step. Every source is identified in the report, and the report is explicit about what the data cannot establish as well as what it can.

## Appointments in Ashland, not an office

The firm has no Ashland office and does not suggest one; its home office is in Kansas City, and Ashland engagements are served by appointment. Records review, research, and drafting proceed remotely. When an in-person interview, testing session, or direct observation is required, it is arranged in the evaluee's own community, and testimony travel from the Kansas City home office is scheduled once deposition or trial dates are entered.

## When the case needs an economist

If the damages theory requires economic loss calculations, the firm offers [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement structure. [Medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/) and [rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) of opposing disclosures carry the same discipline: a defined scope for each assignment and a qualified signatory for every opinion in the file.
