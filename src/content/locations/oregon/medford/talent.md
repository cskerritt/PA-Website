Expert disclosure deadlines decide more Talent cases than trial testimony does, and Purinton Analytics builds its Talent engagements backward from those dates. When counsel retains the firm as a [vocational expert](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) or for a [life care plan](/services/life-care-planning/) in a matter arising in Talent, the intake conversation starts with the scheduling order: when opinions must be disclosed, when depositions are likely to be noticed, and how much record collection has to happen before an evaluation can responsibly be set. Working the calendar in reverse keeps the opinion from being rushed at the end, which is where methodology problems are born.

## The disclosure calendar in Jackson County

Talent civil matters proceed in the Circuit Court serving Jackson County, and that court's scheduling practices set the rhythm for record requests, evaluation, drafting, and disclosure. Cases with federal jurisdiction may instead be heard in the United States District Court for the District of Oregon, the federal forum available to Oregon litigants, where disclosure obligations run on their own track; which venue controls is identified during the conflict check and intake. Whichever court governs, the firm treats the disclosure date as immovable and plans every earlier step against it.

## What the evaluation requires and when

Talent is a small town, and there is no Purinton Analytics office there; the firm's home office is in Kansas City and every Talent appointment is arranged individually, a fact stated plainly because candor about presence is part of the product. Remote evaluation can compress the timeline when the record supports it. When the referral requires in-person interviewing or testing, the session is held in the evaluee's own community and slotted into the case schedule early enough that scoring, analysis, and drafting are not squeezed against the deadline. Testimony travel from the Kansas City home office is likewise planned once deposition or trial dates firm up.

## Labor market analysis for a Talent resident

A Talent evaluee's employment prospects are framed by the Medford metropolitan area's labor market as a whole, not by the town alone. The firm's vocational findings rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Medford metropolitan area and for Oregon statewide, along with state workforce agency labor market information and targeted labor market research where warranted. Sources are cited so that the opinion can be examined line by line, by retaining counsel before disclosure and by opposing counsel after it.
