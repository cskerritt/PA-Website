Scope is where a Jacksonville engagement succeeds or fails, so scope is where Purinton Analytics begins. Before any records arrive in a Jacksonville matter, the firm and retaining counsel settle in writing which questions the [vocational expert](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) will answer, which belong to the [life care planner](/services/life-care-planning/), and which require other professionals entirely. A report that drifts beyond its author's qualifications is a gift to the cross-examiner; a report whose boundaries were drawn at intake is not. That engagement structure, not proximity to any courthouse, is what the firm considers its real product for counsel in a small town like Jacksonville.

## Defining the engagement before the analysis

Each Jacksonville referral starts with a documented conflict check, a written scope, and a records request tailored to the claims actually pleaded. The evaluee interview, any testing, and the labor market research all follow from that scope rather than expanding it, and the final opinion cites the records reviewed and the methods applied so that both sides of the caption can trace every conclusion to its foundation. The firm serves plaintiff and defense engagements on identical terms, and the structure of the work is the same either way.

## Venue context for Jacksonville disputes

Matters arising in Jacksonville are heard in the Circuit Court serving Jackson County, whose calendar the firm tracks when setting evaluation and drafting timelines. For cases with federal-question or diversity grounds, the United States District Court for the District of Oregon serves as the available federal forum in the state; the controlling venue for any specific matter is confirmed during the conflict check and intake. Reports are written once, to a standard fit for either forum, rather than adapted after the fact.

## A small town measured against a metro market

Jacksonville is a small town within the Medford metropolitan area, and its residents' vocational options are properly measured against the regional market that surrounds them. The firm's opinions draw on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Medford metropolitan area and for Oregon statewide, on state workforce agency labor market information, and on direct labor market research where the defined scope calls for it. What the data cannot answer, the report identifies as unanswered instead of estimating around it.

## Evaluations arranged around the evaluee

The firm keeps no office in Jacksonville and describes its presence exactly as it is: a national practice whose home office is in Kansas City, serving southern Oregon by appointment. Interviews and testing proceed remotely when the scope permits, or in the evaluee's own community when in-person evaluation is required, and any appearance for deposition or trial in Jackson County is handled as scheduled travel from the Kansas City home office.

## Completing the damages picture

When the scope extends to economic loss, the firm offers [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement structure. A [medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/) or [rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) of an opposing expert's report can be added, each with its own defined boundary and its own qualified signatory.
