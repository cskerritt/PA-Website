Method comes before geography. Every Eugene-Springfield engagement begins the
way engagements begin everywhere in the practice: a documented conflict check,
a defined scope, and a records request tailored to the claims, so that the
written opinion rests on a record either side of the caption can examine.
From that foundation, Purinton Analytics provides
[vocational expert evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) of earning
capacity and employability, [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/)
for catastrophic injuries, [medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/),
and [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis
while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement
structure.

## Lane County venue footprint

The Eugene-Springfield metropolitan area sits within Lane County, and
engagements are accepted for matters venued in the Circuit Court of Lane
County and in the United States District Court for the District of Oregon.
Plaintiff and defense retention are accepted on the same terms, and the
opinion that results is written to be read closely by whichever side did not
retain the firm. Counsel handling matters in other parts of the state can
review the firm's [Oregon page](/locations/oregon/) for the statewide venue
picture.

## How by-appointment service works here

The firm maintains no staffed office in Eugene or Springfield and publishes no
street address for this location; candor about presence is part of the same
discipline that governs the reports. In practice, most engagements need no
office at all. Records analysis, structured interviews by secure video, and
standardized testing under publisher protocols where remote administration is
supported carry the work from intake through a draft opinion. Where the
referral questions require an in-person evaluation, the firm evaluates in the
evaluee's own community, and when deposition or trial testimony requires
presence, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office to meet the
court's calendar.

## Labor market evidence for Lane County evaluees

Vocational conclusions are anchored in Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational
employment and wage estimates for the Eugene-Springfield metropolitan area and
for Oregon statewide, alongside state workforce agency labor market
information and direct labor market research where warranted. Published
estimates describe the market; the analysis still has to place a specific
person in it, which is why transferable skills analysis, residual functional
capacity, and employer contact work are documented step by step in the report.
Life care plans and cost projections are priced to the evaluee's own market
using provider-specific inquiries and published fee data rather than national
averages, and each source is cited so the figures can be traced and tested.

## Opening a Eugene-Springfield engagement

The most useful time to retain a vocational expert or life care planner is
before the scheduling order makes evaluation difficult. Submit the caption or
party names through the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and note any
imminent disclosure, deposition, or trial dates; scope and fees are confirmed
in writing before records are reviewed. For matters outside Lane County or
outside Oregon entirely, the firm's [national coverage](/locations/nationwide/)
explains how the same remote-first model serves venues across the country.
