outside the major population centers. Attorneys in Klamath Falls do not need

## Courts serving Klamath County

The Klamath Falls micropolitan area consists of Klamath County, and
engagements are accepted for matters in the Circuit Court of Klamath County
and in the United States District Court for the District of Oregon. The
intake process is identical in either forum: a documented conflict check, a
defined scope, and a records request tailored to the claims. Opinions are
written for adversarial testing, which means the report states its methods,
identifies its sources, and confines its conclusions to the record, whether
the firm has been retained by the plaintiff or the defense. Counsel with
matters in other parts of the state can review the firm's full coverage on
the [Oregon locations page](/locations/oregon/).

## Presence, honestly stated

The firm maintains no staffed office in Klamath Falls and lists no street
address for this location. Meetings with counsel are by videoconference or
by appointment, and most consulting-stage work, from records review to
report drafting, is performed remotely without any loss of rigor.
Evaluations of Klamath County evaluees are scheduled remotely when the
referral questions permit and in the evaluee's own community when an
in-person assessment is warranted. When a deposition or trial requires the
expert in the courtroom, travel is planned from the Kansas City home office
with enough lead time to protect the litigation schedule.

## Grounding opinions in the local labor market

Vocational opinions for Klamath County evaluees are grounded in Bureau of
Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for Oregon,
together with state workforce agency labor market information and, where
the referral questions call for it, direct labor market research into the
jobs actually available to a person with the evaluee's documented
restrictions and skills. In a smaller labor market, that last step matters:
statewide averages can misstate what a specific evaluee can realistically
obtain, in either direction, and a defensible opinion has to close that gap
with evidence rather than assumption. Where economic damages are also in
dispute, the firm offers
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Opening a Klamath County engagement

Every engagement starts with a conflict check. Submit the caption or party
names through the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and note any
imminent disclosure, deposition, or trial dates. After conflicts clear,
counsel receives a written scope and fee agreement and a records request
keyed to the claims, and the work proceeds on the schedule the case
demands.
