A vocational or life care planning report is only as useful as it is
defensible, and every Medford engagement is written on the assumption that
opposing counsel will read it closely. That standard, rather than proximity,
is what Purinton Analytics offers counsel in Jackson County: disciplined
[earning capacity and employability evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) in catastrophic injury
matters, [rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) of
opposing expert work, and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis
while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement
structure. Plaintiff and defense retention are accepted on identical terms.

## Jackson County venue and forum

Engagements are accepted for matters venued in the Circuit Court of Jackson
County and in the United States District Court for the District of Oregon.
Forum does not change the method: each opinion rests on a documented conflict
check, a defined scope, and a records request tailored to the claims, so that
the record supporting the conclusions is available to both sides of the
caption. Any venue-specific registration or qualification requirement is
identified and confirmed during the conflict check and intake process. The
statewide venue picture, including other metro areas the firm serves, appears
on the firm's [Oregon page](/locations/oregon/).

## Meetings by appointment only

Purinton Analytics keeps no staffed office in Medford and publishes no street
address for this location. Stating that plainly is deliberate: accuracy about
offices belongs to the same discipline that governs the reports. The work
itself rarely suffers for it. Records review, structured interviews by secure
video, and standardized testing under publisher protocols where remote
administration is supported all proceed without an office. When the referral
questions call for in-person evaluation, the firm evaluates in the evaluee's
own community, and when deposition or trial dates require presence in Jackson
County, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office against the
court's calendar.

## Labor market and cost research

Vocational findings for Medford area evaluees are grounded in Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Medford
metropolitan area and for Oregon statewide, supplemented by state workforce
agency labor market information and direct labor market research where
warranted. The report shows its work: how residual capacities were assessed,
how transferable skills were identified, and how the published series and any
employer contacts support the placement conclusions. Life care plans and
medical cost projections are priced to the evaluee's own market using
provider-specific inquiries and published fee data, with every source cited so
that cross-examination can start from the same page the opinion did.

before any records are reviewed. For related matters outside Jackson County or
outside Oregon, the firm's [nationwide coverage](/locations/nationwide/)
