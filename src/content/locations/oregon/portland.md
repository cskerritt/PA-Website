The Portland-Vancouver-Hillsboro metropolitan area crosses a state line, and
the litigation that arises there does too. Purinton Analytics serves plaintiff
and defense counsel throughout the metro with
[vocational expert evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) of earning
capacity and employability, [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/)
in catastrophic injury matters, and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis
while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement
structure. Whichever side of the caption retains the firm, and whichever side
of the state line the case sits on, the report standard is the same.

## A venue footprint spanning the state line

On the Oregon side, engagements are accepted for matters in the Circuit Court
of Multnomah County and the circuit courts of Clackamas County, Washington
County, Columbia County, and Yamhill County, and in the United States District
Court for the District of Oregon. The metro also reaches Clark County and
Skamania County in Washington, and the firm accepts matters venued in the
state and federal courts serving those counties as well. Cross-state
engagements are routine for a national practice, and the intake process
confirms any venue-specific requirement before work begins. Counsel elsewhere
in the state can start from the firm's [Oregon page](/locations/oregon/).

## By appointment in Portland

Portland meetings are by appointment. The firm keeps no staffed office in the
metro and publishes no street address for this location, because accuracy
about presence belongs to the same discipline that governs its reports. Most
engagements begin remotely: a documented conflict check, a defined scope, a
records request tailored to the claims, and structured interviews conducted by
secure video. Standardized testing is administered under publisher protocols,
remotely where the publisher supports it. When the referral questions call for
in-person evaluation, or when deposition and trial calendars require presence,
travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office and planned around the
court's dates rather than the firm's convenience.

## Labor market research for the metro

Vocational findings for evaluees in the Portland area rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the
Portland-Vancouver-Hillsboro metropolitan area, together with statewide
estimates for Oregon and for Washington when an evaluee's labor market crosses
the state line. State workforce agency labor market information and direct
employer research supplement the published series where the referral questions
warrant it. Life care plans and medical cost projections are priced to the
evaluee's own market rather than to national averages, and every source is
cited in the report so that opposing counsel can trace each figure to its
origin.

## Starting a Portland engagement

Early retention protects the record: it allows evaluation before surveillance
disputes, discovery deadlines, and scheduling conflicts narrow the options.
Submit the caption or party names through the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and note any imminent disclosure,
deposition, or trial dates. The firm responds after the conflict check clears,
with a defined scope and fee structure before any records are reviewed.
Counsel with matters beyond the metro, in either state or elsewhere in the
country, can review the firm's [national coverage](/locations/nationwide/)
for the same intake path.
