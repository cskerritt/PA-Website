The Hermiston-Pendleton micropolitan area spans two counties, and litigation
here can arise in either of them. Purinton Analytics serves plaintiff and
defense counsel across that full footprint with
[vocational evaluation and expert testimony](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. The practice is national and remote-first, described in
full on the [nationwide services page](/locations/nationwide/), and every
engagement follows the same sequence regardless of venue: a documented
conflict check, a defined scope, and a records request tailored to the claims.

## Venue footprint in Morrow and Umatilla Counties

Engagements are accepted for matters in the Circuit Court serving Morrow
County and Umatilla County and in the United States District Court for the
District of Oregon. Personal injury, wrongful death, employment, and workers'
compensation matters each call for somewhat different vocational questions,
and the scope of work is set at intake so that the written opinion addresses
the issues actually in dispute. Because the same methodology is applied for
either side of the caption, reports are written to withstand examination by
opposing counsel and opposing experts alike. Counsel handling matters
elsewhere in the state will find the firm's broader coverage described on the
[Oregon locations page](/locations/oregon/).

## Meetings and evaluations by appointment

There is no staffed Purinton Analytics office in Hermiston, and the firm
publishes no street address for this location, because accuracy about
presence is part of the same discipline that governs its reports. Meetings
with counsel are held by videoconference or by appointment. Vocational
evaluations of local evaluees are conducted remotely where the referral
questions permit, or scheduled in the evaluee's own community when in-person
assessment is warranted. When deposition or trial dates require presence in
Morrow County or Umatilla County, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City
home office and planned around the litigation calendar rather than around
the firm's convenience.

## Labor market research for a two-county area

Vocational opinions for Hermiston-area evaluees rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates covering Oregon,
together with state workforce agency labor market information and direct
labor market research where the referral questions warrant it. In a
micropolitan labor market, the distance between published statewide data and
the jobs actually available to a specific evaluee can be meaningful, so
transferable skills analysis and employability findings are grounded in the
evaluee's documented work history and restrictions rather than in
generalized assumptions. Where future medical and care needs are at issue,
[life care plans](/services/life-care-planning/) and
[medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/) are priced
from sources the report identifies, so that both sides can trace every
figure to its origin.

## Starting a Hermiston-Pendleton engagement

The first step for any matter in Morrow County or Umatilla County is a
conflict check. Submit the caption or party names through the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and note any imminent disclosure,
deposition, or trial dates so scheduling can be confirmed at the outset.
Once conflicts clear, counsel receives a defined scope, a fee agreement, and
a records request, and the work proceeds on the timeline the litigation
requires.
