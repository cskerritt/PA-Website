Counsel in Grants Pass typically retain Purinton Analytics for one of a small
set of questions: what work an injured or displaced person can still perform,
what that capacity is worth in the labor market, and what future care will
cost. The firm answers those questions through
[vocational expert evaluation and testimony](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) of opposing
expert reports, for plaintiff and defense engagements alike. Where a matter
also requires economic damages, the firm offers
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## The Josephine County venue

The Grants Pass metropolitan area consists of Josephine County, and
engagements are accepted for matters in the Circuit Court of Josephine
County and in the United States District Court for the District of Oregon.
Every engagement begins the same way regardless of the court: a documented
conflict check, a written scope, and a records request tailored to the
claims, so that the resulting opinion rests on a record both sides can
examine. Reports are drafted on the assumption that opposing counsel will
read every sentence closely, and the methodology sections are written so a
cross-examiner can follow each step from records to conclusion. Statewide
coverage, including the other Oregon metros the firm serves, is described on
the [Oregon locations page](/locations/oregon/).

## How appointments work here

Purinton Analytics keeps no staffed office in Grants Pass and publishes no
local street address, because candor about presence belongs in the same
category as candor about findings. Attorney conferences are held by
videoconference at whatever stage the case requires: before filing, during
discovery, or in preparation for deposition and trial. Evaluations of
Josephine County evaluees are conducted remotely when the referral questions
permit, and in person in the evaluee's own community when the assessment
requires it. Deposition and trial appearances are scheduled with travel from
the Kansas City home office, arranged around the court's calendar.

## Labor market evidence for Josephine County evaluees

Vocational findings for Grants Pass evaluees draw on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Grants Pass
metropolitan area and for Oregon statewide, along with state workforce
agency labor market information and direct labor market research where
warranted. Employability and earning capacity opinions tie those sources to
the specific evaluee: documented restrictions, education, work history, and
transferable skills. Nothing in a report rests on an unnamed database or an
undisclosed assumption, and the same sourcing discipline applies to
[medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/) and life care
plans, where each cost item is traced to an identified pricing source.

## Requesting a conflict check

Engagement begins with a conflict check, not a sales call. To start a
Josephine County matter, [request a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) with the
case caption and parties, and flag any disclosure deadlines, deposition
settings, or trial dates that bear on scheduling. The firm's national scope
and remote-first structure, described on the
[nationwide page](/locations/nationwide/), mean that a Grants Pass
engagement proceeds with the same tools and the same timeline discipline as
an engagement in any major metro.
