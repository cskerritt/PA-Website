## Where Warrenton cases are heard

Warrenton is a Clatsop County city, and damages litigation arising there under state law proceeds in the Circuit Court. Matters that belong in federal court have the United States District Court for the District of Oregon as the available federal forum; which court controls a given case, and what that court asks of a testifying expert, is settled during the conflict check and intake before any opinion work begins. The [Astoria metro page](/locations/oregon/astoria/) describes the firm's coverage of the surrounding area, and the [Oregon locations page](/locations/oregon/) collects the state's other venues. Wherever the caption lands, the file opens with a written conflict clearance, an agreed scope, and a records request drawn to the claims rather than to a template.

## A commuter's labor market, read carefully

Warrenton sits in a small labor market anchored by Astoria, the area's principal city, and its workers' realistic employment prospects depend on jobs within practical commuting reach as much as on employers in town. Vocational findings therefore rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the area and for Oregon statewide, supplemented by state workforce agency labor market information and by direct labor market research where the referral questions justify it. In a market this size, an honest opinion cannot hide behind aggregates: capacity and earning conclusions are tied to identified occupations, each tested against the evaluee's residual function, education, and work history, and each carrying a cited source that survives scrutiny at deposition.
