An evaluation that never gets close to the evaluee is a weakness opposing counsel will eventually find. That conviction shapes how Purinton Analytics serves Seaside: [vocational expert](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) and [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) engagements are accepted from plaintiff and defense counsel alike, and each is planned from the outset around the person being evaluated, a Seaside resident whose functional capacity, transferable skills, and future care needs have to be assessed in the context of the community where that person actually lives. The opinions are written to be examined, so the evaluation record beneath them is built with the same care as the report that rests on it.

## Clatsop County and the courts that serve Seaside

Seaside lies in Clatsop County, and state-court injury, employment, and disability matters involving its residents proceed in the Circuit Court. For cases that belong in federal court, the United States District Court for the District of Oregon is the federal forum available in the state; the controlling venue for a particular matter, along with any registration or qualification requirement it carries, is identified and confirmed during the conflict check and intake process. The method does not change with the forum. A documented conflict check, a defined scope, and a records request tailored to the claims open every file, so the finished opinion rests on materials either side of the caption can inspect. Counsel with matters elsewhere in the area can consult the [Astoria metro page](/locations/oregon/astoria/), and the [Oregon locations page](/locations/oregon/) maps the rest of the state.

## Seaside's place in the area labor market

Vocational conclusions about a Seaside evaluee turn on what work is realistically available within practical reach of home, and that question cannot be answered with national averages. Findings are grounded in Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the area and for Oregon statewide, in state workforce agency labor market information, and in direct labor market research when the referral questions warrant it. Astoria is the area's principal city, and the analysis weighs work within commuting reach of it alongside employment closer to Seaside itself. Because the market is small, the transferable skills analysis names the specific occupations relied upon and documents why each is or is not within the evaluee's residual capacity, so the opinion can withstand cross-examination occupation by occupation.

## How a Seaside evaluation proceeds

Records move through secure channels, and interviews are conducted by secure video whenever that format serves the evaluee and the referral questions. When those questions require an in-person evaluation, it is scheduled in the evaluee's own community rather than demanding a long trip from Seaside to a distant office. Purinton Analytics maintains its home office in Kansas City, keeps no staffed office in Seaside, and publishes no local street address, because accuracy about presence belongs to the same discipline that governs the reports. Travel for evaluations, depositions, and trial testimony is arranged from the Kansas City home office. Where a life care plan needs pricing support, [medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/) work proceeds under the same engagement, and identical procedures are available to counsel [nationwide](/locations/nationwide/).
