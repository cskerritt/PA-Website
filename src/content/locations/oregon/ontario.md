The Ontario micropolitan area sits astride a state line, taking in Malheur
County, Oregon and Payette County, Idaho, and litigation here can land on
either side of it. A practice built around a single local courthouse would
serve only half this market; Purinton Analytics is structured instead as a
national, remote-first firm whose methodology and engagement process are the
same in every forum, as set out on the
[nationwide services page](/locations/nationwide/). Plaintiff and defense
counsel throughout the two-state area retain the firm for
[vocational evaluation and expert testimony](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) of opposing
expert work.

## A venue footprint on both sides of the line

On the Oregon side, engagements are accepted for matters in the Circuit
Court of Malheur County and in the United States District Court for the
District of Oregon. On the Idaho side, the firm accepts engagements arising
in Payette County, Idaho under the same intake structure; any
venue-specific registration or qualification requirement is identified and
confirmed during the conflict check and intake process. Whatever the forum,
the engagement begins with a documented conflict check, a defined scope,
and a records request tailored to the claims, so the written opinion rests
on a record both sides can examine. Coverage across the rest of the state
is described on the [Oregon locations page](/locations/oregon/).

## Appointments, not an office

Purinton Analytics keeps no staffed office in Ontario and publishes no
street address for this location. Counsel conferences are held by
videoconference, from early case assessment through deposition preparation.
Evaluations of Malheur County and Payette County evaluees are conducted
remotely where the referral questions permit, or arranged in the evaluee's
own community when in-person assessment is warranted. When deposition or
trial testimony requires the expert's presence, travel is scheduled from
the Kansas City home office and coordinated with counsel well ahead of the
court's deadlines.

## Labor market research across two states

A two-state labor market requires two-state data. Vocational opinions for
Ontario-area evaluees draw on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational
employment and wage estimates for Oregon and for Idaho, together with the
labor market information published by each state's workforce agency and
direct labor market research where the referral questions warrant it. An
evaluee living in Malheur County may realistically compete for work in
Payette County and the reverse, and employability findings account for that
cross-border reality rather than stopping at the state line. When the
matter also requires economic quantification, the firm offers
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Starting an Ontario-area engagement

Whether the caption reads Oregon or Idaho, the first step is the same.
Submit the case caption or party names through the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and note any imminent disclosure,
deposition, or trial dates. Once conflicts clear, counsel receives a
written scope, a fee agreement, and a records request keyed to the claims,
and the engagement proceeds on the litigation's timeline in whichever
state the matter is filed.
