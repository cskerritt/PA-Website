point for every Corvallis engagement. Purinton Analytics is retained by
plaintiff counsel to establish what an injury has cost a working life, and by
defense counsel to test whether a claimed loss holds up; the method is the
same in both directions. For Benton County matters the firm provides
[earning capacity and employability evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),

## Benton County venue footprint

The Corvallis metropolitan area sits in Benton County, and engagements are
accepted for matters venued in the Circuit Court of Benton County and in the
United States District Court for the District of Oregon. Whatever the forum,
the opinion is built the same way: a documented conflict check, a defined
scope, and a records request tailored to the claims, producing a written
report whose record both sides can examine. Any venue-specific registration or
qualification requirement is identified and confirmed during the conflict
check and intake process. Counsel with matters elsewhere in the state can
start from the firm's [Oregon page](/locations/oregon/).

## No storefront, by design

There is no staffed Purinton Analytics office in Corvallis, and the firm
publishes no street address for this location, because accuracy about presence
is part of the discipline the reports are built on. Engagements are served by
appointment and, for most of their life, remotely: records analysis,
structured interviews by secure video, and standardized testing under
publisher protocols where remote administration is supported. When the
referral questions call for an in-person evaluation, the firm evaluates in the
evaluee's own community rather than moving the evaluee, and when deposition or
trial dates in Benton County require presence, travel is scheduled from the
Kansas City home office against the court's calendar.

## Labor market analysis for Corvallis evaluees

Vocational conclusions rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational
employment and wage estimates for the Corvallis metropolitan area and for
Oregon statewide, together with state workforce agency labor market
information and direct labor market research where the referral questions
warrant it. The analysis places a specific person, with specific residual
capacities and transferable skills, into a specific market, and the report
documents each step so that cross-examination can engage the substance rather
than guess at it. Life care plans and medical cost projections are priced to
the evaluee's own market through provider-specific inquiries and published fee
data, every source cited.
