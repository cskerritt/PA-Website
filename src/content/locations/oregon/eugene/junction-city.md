## A small town inside a metro labor market

Junction City is a small community whose workers largely look to the Eugene-Springfield, OR labor market, with Eugene as the principal city, for employment beyond the town's own employers. A defensible earning capacity analysis has to model that reality with sources on the table. The firm relies on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the metro area and for Oregon statewide, on state workforce agency labor market information, and on direct labor market research where the referral questions demand more resolution than published estimates give. Qualitative honesty is the rule throughout: the report characterizes the market as the data show it, and it never borrows big-city assumptions for a small-town evaluee.
