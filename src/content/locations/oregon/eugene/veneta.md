## Lane County venue and the federal alternative

Veneta is a Lane County town, and state-court litigation involving Veneta parties is heard in the Circuit Court for Lane County. Federal matters may proceed in the United States District Court for the District of Oregon; which venue controls, and what registration or qualification requirement follows from it, is a question resolved during the conflict check and intake process, not assumed on a website. Counsel researching the broader area will find the surrounding communities treated on the [Eugene metro page](/locations/oregon/eugene/) and the whole state on the [Oregon locations page](/locations/oregon/).

## Labor market evidence that survives scrutiny

Veneta is a small community in the Eugene-Springfield, OR metro, where Eugene is the principal city, and an opinion about a Veneta evaluee's residual earning capacity must square the town's own scale with the wider market reachable from it. The firm supports that opinion with Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the metro area and for Oregon statewide, with state workforce agency labor market information, and with direct labor market research when the referral questions call for confirmation beyond published estimates. Because the report is written for adversarial reading, its labor market characterizations stay qualitative and sourced; a claim about job availability that cannot be tied to data does not go in.

## Seeing the evaluee, stating the logistics

Evaluations are arranged around the person being evaluated. Records travel by secure transfer, interviews proceed by secure video where that serves the evaluee, and in-person assessment, when warranted, is scheduled in the Veneta area. The firm is candid about its footprint because candor is the product: the home office is in Kansas City, there is no staffed Veneta office, and no local street address is published. Deposition and trial appearances are planned as travel from the Kansas City home office. The identical standard of practice is available to counsel [nationwide](/locations/nationwide/).
