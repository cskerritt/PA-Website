## Reading a small labor market honestly

Lincoln City is a small city, and small labor markets punish careless vocational work in both directions: an opinion that assumes big-city job density overstates capacity, while an opinion that ignores work within practical commuting reach of Newport, the area's principal city, understates it. The firm's findings rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the area and for Oregon statewide, on state workforce agency labor market information, and on direct labor market research when the referral questions call for it. Every figure in a report carries a cited source, and the transferable skills analysis is documented step by step, because in a market this size the difference between employable and not often turns on a handful of occupations that must be named and defended.
