Keizer is a city of real size within the Salem metropolitan area, large
enough that its residents' injury, employment, and disability cases raise
the full range of damages questions counsel see anywhere else. When a
Keizer case turns on what an injured person can still earn or what future
care will actually be required, Purinton Analytics provides
[vocational evaluations](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care plans](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/) prepared to
the same testimony standard for plaintiff and defense counsel alike. The
work product is a written opinion with its methodology on the surface, so
that either side of the caption, and the court, can trace every conclusion
back to the record.

## Marion County venue and the courts beyond it

Keizer lies in Marion County, and civil matters arising in the city are
ordinarily heard in the Circuit Court serving Marion County. For matters
that proceed federally, the United States District Court for the District
of Oregon is the forum available statewide, and the controlling venue for
any particular case is identified and confirmed during the conflict check
and intake. Because reports are drafted for cross-examination from the
first page, the same opinion serves whether the dispute is resolved in the
county courthouse, in federal court, or at mediation before either.

## Earning capacity in the Salem commuting market

A Keizer evaluee's employability cannot be assessed against a single city's
job postings. The realistic market is the broader
[Salem metropolitan area](/locations/oregon/salem/), where residents of
Keizer and its neighboring communities compete for the same work.
Vocational findings therefore rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates for the Salem area and for
Oregon statewide, supplemented by state workforce agency labor market
information and direct labor market research where the referral questions
warrant it. The written opinion characterizes that market qualitatively and
cites its sources, avoiding claims the data cannot support; that restraint
is what keeps the opinion standing when the other side's expert reviews it.

## Appointments, evaluations, and honesty about presence

The firm's home office is in Kansas City, and it maintains no staffed
office in Keizer or anywhere in Oregon. Meetings are by appointment, and
the practice states that plainly because accuracy about presence is part of
the same discipline that governs its reports. Most interviews, records
reviews, and counsel conferences proceed by secure video, which usually
serves an injured evaluee better than travel. When the assignment calls for
in-person evaluation, or when a deposition or trial date requires the
expert in the room, the appointment is arranged in the evaluee's own
community and travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office, the
same way the firm's [nationwide practice](/locations/nationwide/) handles
every other by-appointment venue.

analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. To begin, submit the caption or party names through
