A referral from Hubbard counsel follows a short, predictable path: the
caption or party names go through the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/), the firm confirms it can accept the
engagement, scope and deadlines are defined in writing, and a records
request tailored to the claims goes out. Only then does the substantive
work begin. Purinton Analytics keeps that sequence identical for every
matter, plaintiff-side or defense-side, because a
[vocational evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care plan](/services/life-care-planning/), or
[medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/) is only as
strong as the untangled, conflict-cleared foundation it stands on.

## Venue for a Hubbard dispute

Hubbard sits in Marion County, and civil actions arising in the town are
ordinarily filed in the Circuit Court serving Marion County. Oregon's
federal trial forum is the United States District Court for the District of
Oregon, available statewide, and the venue controlling a particular matter
is identified and confirmed during the conflict check and intake; the same
step surfaces any venue-specific registration or qualification requirement.
Because each report is written to survive cross-examination, the choice of
forum changes scheduling, not substance: the opinion's record foundation
and stated methodology are the same wherever the caption lands.

## Situating Hubbard in the metro labor market

Employability findings for a Hubbard evaluee are grounded in the commuting
market of the [Salem metropolitan area](/locations/oregon/salem/), the
economic territory in which the town's residents actually seek and hold
work. The firm's vocational analysis rests on Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates for the Salem area and for
Oregon statewide, on state workforce agency labor market information, and
on direct labor market research where the referral questions warrant it.
The written opinion handles all of this qualitatively, citing sources and
declining to dress estimates up as certainties, because an opinion that
respects its data is far harder to impeach.

## What by-appointment service means in Hubbard

The firm's home office is in Kansas City, and it is the only office the
firm has. There is no Hubbard location, no Oregon storefront, and no
published local address; every meeting is by appointment, and the practice
says this plainly because accuracy about presence is part of the discipline
counsel is hiring. Interviews, evaluations, and conferences proceed by
secure video whenever that serves the evaluee well. When the assignment
requires an in-person evaluation, or when a deposition or trial requires
the expert's attendance, the appointment happens in the evaluee's own
community and travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office,
consistent with the firm's [nationwide practice](/locations/nationwide/)
in every venue it serves.

## From referral to report

After intake, the engagement runs on the disclosure calendar: records
review, evaluation, analysis, and a written report delivered with time for
counsel's review before any deadline, with rebuttal and deposition
preparation available as the case develops. Where the damages theory calls for
economic analysis, the firm arranges
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. Counsel with a Hubbard matter can
[request a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) with the case caption and party
names, noting any imminent disclosure, deposition, or trial dates, and can
find the statewide context for this page in the
[Oregon overview](/locations/oregon/).
