A life care plan is a promise about the future written in the present
tense, and it has to hold up for decades. When a Mount Angel case involves
catastrophic injury, the [life care plan](/services/life-care-planning/)
Purinton Analytics prepares itemizes future medical and support needs from
the treating record and clinical foundation, prices nothing casually, and
documents the basis for every projected need so that opposing counsel can
audit the whole structure. Alongside that care-planning work the firm
provides [medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/) and
[vocational evaluations](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) of lost
earning capacity, and it accepts these engagements from plaintiff and
defense counsel on the same terms, because the plan's credibility depends
on its indifference to who ordered it.

## Where Mount Angel cases proceed

Mount Angel is a Marion County community, and civil matters arising there
are ordinarily heard in the Circuit Court serving Marion County. The United
States District Court for the District of Oregon provides the federal forum
available statewide; the venue that controls a given matter is identified
and confirmed during the conflict check and intake, together with any
venue-specific registration or qualification requirement. Reports are
written for cross-examination from the first draft, which makes them
portable across state court, federal court, and settlement negotiations
alike.

## The vocational side: a metro-wide lens

When lost earning capacity is also in dispute, the analysis for a Mount
Angel evaluee looks to the commuting market of the surrounding
[Salem metropolitan area](/locations/oregon/salem/). Vocational findings
rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage
estimates for the Salem area and for Oregon statewide, on state workforce
agency labor market information, and on direct labor market research where
the referral questions warrant it. Market conditions are described
qualitatively, sources are cited in the report, and no estimate is pushed
beyond what its methodology supports; restraint in the market analysis is
what lets the rest of the opinion be believed.

## Evaluation in the evaluee's world

Care planning in particular benefits from seeing how an evaluee actually
lives, and the firm's logistics respect that. Purinton Analytics has one
home office, in Kansas City; it staffs no office in Mount Angel or
elsewhere in Oregon and publishes no local address, meeting counsel and
evaluees by appointment only. Much of the engagement proceeds by secure
video, and when an in-person evaluation is warranted the appointment is
held in the evaluee's own community, with travel scheduled from the Kansas
City home office just as the firm's
[nationwide practice](/locations/nationwide/) arranges it everywhere else.
Deposition and trial appearances are planned the same way.

## Referring a Mount Angel matter

Future-care opinions take time to build well, so early referral serves the
disclosure calendar. Where the damages theory also needs economic modeling,
the firm arranges
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. Submit the caption or party names through the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) with a note of any imminent
disclosure, deposition, or trial dates; the
[Oregon overview](/locations/oregon/) describes the statewide practice
behind this page.
