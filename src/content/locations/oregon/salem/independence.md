Some experts write differently depending on who signs the retainer.
Purinton Analytics does not, and that is the premise of its work in
Independence: the same
[vocational evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) methodology,
the same [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) standards, and
the same [medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/)
sources apply whether the firm is retained by the plaintiff or by the
defense. Counsel on either side of an Independence matter get an opinion
whose conclusions were reached the same way they would have been for the
opposing party, which is precisely what makes the opinion useful at
deposition, at mediation, and in front of a jury.

## Polk County courts and the federal forum

Independence sits in Polk County, and civil matters arising in the town are
ordinarily filed in the Circuit Court serving Polk County. Oregon's federal
trial forum, the United States District Court for the District of Oregon,
is available statewide, and the venue controlling a particular matter,
together with any venue-specific qualification requirement, is identified
and confirmed during the conflict check and intake. The firm's reports are
drafted to a testimony standard regardless of forum, so a case that moves
from state to federal court, or resolves before either, is served by the
same work product.

## One commuting market, assessed evenhandedly

An earning capacity opinion for an Independence evaluee is anchored in the
commuting market of the
[Salem metropolitan area](/locations/oregon/salem/), the labor market in
which the town actually participates. Vocational findings rest on Bureau of
Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Salem
area and for Oregon statewide, supported by state workforce agency labor
market information and direct labor market research where the referral
questions warrant it. Whichever side retains the firm, the market is
characterized qualitatively and the sources are cited, because a market
description slanted toward the client is the fastest way for an opinion to
lose a courtroom.

## Appointments in fact, not in name

The firm's only office is its home office in Kansas City. It maintains no
staffed location in Independence or elsewhere in Oregon, publishes no local
address, and conducts every Oregon meeting by appointment; stating that
plainly is part of the same accuracy the reports practice. Secure video
handles most interviews, evaluations, and counsel conferences. Where the
assignment calls for an in-person evaluation, or where deposition or trial
requires the expert present, the meeting is set in the evaluee's own
community and travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office, exactly
as the firm's [nationwide practice](/locations/nationwide/) manages its
other venues.

defined scope, tailored records request, evaluation, and a written report

of the damages theory, the firm arranges
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. Submit the caption or party names through the

deposition, or trial dates; the [Oregon overview](/locations/oregon/)
