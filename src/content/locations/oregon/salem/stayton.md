Distance is not the obstacle it once was in expert practice. A Stayton case
can be staffed by a national firm without the file ever waiting on an
airplane: records move electronically, the vocational interview proceeds by
secure video, counsel conferences happen on screen, and the written opinion
arrives on the disclosure calendar regardless of where it was drafted.
Purinton Analytics runs its practice on exactly that model, delivering
[vocational evaluations](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care plans](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/) to plaintiff
and defense counsel in matters far from its Kansas City home office, with
in-person work reserved for the moments that genuinely require it.

## Stayton's courts, state and federal

Civil matters arising in Stayton, a Marion County community, are ordinarily
heard in the Circuit Court serving Marion County. The United States
District Court for the District of Oregon serves as the federal forum
available throughout the state, and the venue that controls any specific
matter is identified and confirmed during the conflict check and intake;
any venue-specific registration or qualification requirement is resolved at
the same point. Since every report is written to be examined by opposing
counsel, forum changes do not force rewrites; the record foundation,
methodology, and sourcing read the same in either courthouse.

## The metro market behind a small-town caption

Stayton is a smaller community within the
[Salem metropolitan area](/locations/oregon/salem/), and its residents'
realistic employment options extend across that metro's commuting market.
The firm's vocational findings accordingly rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Salem area
and for Oregon statewide, together with state workforce agency labor market
information and direct labor market research where the referral questions
warrant it. The written opinion keeps its market characterizations
qualitative and its citations explicit, and it distinguishes clearly
between what the published estimates show and what the evaluee's specific
restrictions imply. It gains nothing from overstatement, and it would
forfeit its credibility by trying.

## When the expert does travel

Remote-first does not mean remote-only. The firm keeps no staffed office in
Stayton or anywhere in Oregon and publishes no local address, because
honesty about presence is part of its professional discipline; meetings are
by appointment. When the referral questions call for an in-person
evaluation, the appointment is held in the evaluee's own community rather
than in a distant office. When deposition or trial dates require the expert
in the courtroom, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office with
the same planning the firm's
[nationwide practice](/locations/nationwide/) applies in every other
venue it serves.

## Starting a Stayton engagement

An engagement opens with a documented conflict check, a defined scope, and
a records request matched to the claims, so the opinion rests on a record
both sides can examine. Where the damages theory requires economic
analysis, the firm arranges
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. To refer a matter, submit the caption or party names
through the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and note any imminent
disclosure, deposition, or trial dates; the statewide practice is described
in the [Oregon overview](/locations/oregon/).
