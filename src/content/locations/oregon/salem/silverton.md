A defensible expert opinion has a visible anatomy: a defined scope, a
records foundation, a stated methodology, sources a reader can check, and
conclusions that follow from what came before. That anatomy, not the
letterhead, is what Purinton Analytics offers counsel with a Silverton
case. Whether the assignment is a
[vocational evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) of lost
earning capacity, a [life care plan](/services/life-care-planning/) for
catastrophic injury, or a
[medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/), the report
is built so that opposing counsel can trace every step, because the firm
writes for both plaintiff and defense engagements and assumes each page
will eventually be read by the other side.

## Venue for Silverton disputes

Silverton lies in Marion County. Civil matters arising in the town are
ordinarily heard in the Circuit Court serving Marion County, while the
United States District Court for the District of Oregon stands as the
federal forum available statewide; which court controls a given case is
identified and confirmed during the conflict check and intake, as is any
venue-specific registration or qualification requirement. A report with its
methodology on the surface transfers cleanly between those settings, and
between litigation and mediation, without revision.

## Reading the labor market from Silverton

The employability of a Silverton evaluee is a question about the
[Salem metropolitan area's](/locations/oregon/salem/) commuting market, of
which Silverton is a part, rather than about any single town. Vocational
findings rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and
wage estimates for the Salem area and for Oregon statewide, joined by state
workforce agency labor market information and, where the referral questions
warrant, direct labor market research. The report characterizes these
sources qualitatively and refuses to press them past their limits. That
refusal is deliberate: an opinion is only as strong as its least defensible
claim, so the firm does not make claims it cannot defend.

## Logistics without pretense

There is no Silverton office to visit, and the firm will not imply one. Its
single home office is in Kansas City; all Oregon meetings are by
appointment; no local street address is published. Most engagements run
largely by secure video, from the vocational interview through strategy
conferences with counsel, which keeps costs down and spares injured
evaluees needless travel. Where an in-person evaluation is warranted, or
where deposition or trial testimony requires the expert's presence, the
appointment is held in the evaluee's own community with travel scheduled
from the Kansas City home office, the same footing on which the firm's
[nationwide practice](/locations/nationwide/) operates everywhere.
