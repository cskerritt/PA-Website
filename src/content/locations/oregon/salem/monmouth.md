For the person being evaluated, an expert engagement is not a filing on a
docket; it is an interview, a records review, and a stranger's written
assessment of their working life. Purinton Analytics designs its process
around that person. A Monmouth evaluee is typically interviewed by secure
video from home, seen in person in their own community when the assignment
requires it, and never asked to organize their recovery around an expert's
travel schedule. On that foundation the firm delivers
[vocational evaluations](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care plans](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/) that serve
retaining counsel on either side of the caption, because a humane process
and a rigorous opinion are not in tension.

## The courts that serve a Monmouth case

Monmouth is in Polk County, and civil matters arising there are ordinarily
filed in the Circuit Court serving Polk County. Federally, the United
States District Court for the District of Oregon is the forum available
across the state, and the venue that controls a specific matter is
identified and confirmed during the conflict check and intake, along with
any venue-specific qualification requirement. The firm writes each report
to a testimony standard from the beginning, so the opinion is equally
usable in the county courthouse, in federal proceedings, or in a mediated
resolution.

## Employability measured across the Salem area

A vocational opinion about a Monmouth resident has to reckon with where
that resident can actually work, and the realistic answer spans the
[Salem metropolitan area](/locations/oregon/salem/) as a whole. The firm's
vocational findings rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational
employment and wage estimates for the Salem area and for Oregon statewide,
state workforce agency labor market information, and direct labor market
research where the referral questions warrant it. Each source is described
qualitatively and cited in the report; the analysis stays inside what the
data can support, which is what allows it to survive an opposing expert's
review intact.

## By appointment, and honestly so

Purinton Analytics has one home office, in Kansas City. It keeps no staffed
office in Monmouth or anywhere else in Oregon, publishes no local street
address, and says so directly, because candor about presence belongs to the
same discipline that governs the reports themselves. Remote work covers
most of an engagement well. When an in-person evaluation is warranted, or
when a deposition or trial requires the expert in the room, travel is
scheduled from the Kansas City home office and the evaluation is held where
the evaluee lives, mirroring how the firm's
[nationwide practice](/locations/nationwide/) serves every venue it
accepts.
