Dallas matters belong to Polk County, and a report headed for the Circuit
Court serving Polk County is written with that courtroom in mind: an
opinion that will be read closely by opposing counsel, examined at
deposition, and, if the case does not resolve, defended at trial. Purinton
Analytics prepares
[vocational evaluations](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care plans](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/) for Dallas
cases on exactly those terms, accepting engagements from plaintiff and
defense counsel alike and applying the same documented methodology
whichever side retains the firm.

## Polk County and the federal alternative

Civil actions arising in Dallas are ordinarily heard in the Circuit Court
serving Polk County. When a matter proceeds in federal court instead,
Oregon offers a single statewide forum, the United States District Court
for the District of Oregon, and the controlling venue for any particular
case is identified and confirmed during the conflict check and intake. Any
venue-specific registration or qualification requirement surfaces at the
same stage. Because the report is drafted to a testimony standard from the
outset, nothing about it needs to be rebuilt if the case moves between
state and federal court.

## Measuring work against the metro, not the town

Dallas is part of the [Salem metropolitan area](/locations/oregon/salem/),
and an honest employability analysis for a Dallas evaluee looks at that
wider commuting market rather than at the town in isolation. Vocational
findings rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and
wage estimates for the Salem area and for Oregon statewide, alongside state
workforce agency labor market information and direct labor market research
where the referral questions warrant it. The opinion presents that market
qualitatively, names its sources, and declines to convert survey estimates
into certainties; a market analysis that overreaches is a gift to the
cross-examining lawyer, and the firm does not give it.

## How evaluation logistics actually work here

The practice is candid about geography. Purinton Analytics keeps one home
office, in Kansas City, maintains no staffed office in Dallas or elsewhere
in Oregon, and meets counsel and evaluees by appointment only. Secure video
carries most of the work, including the vocational interview and counsel
conferences, and often serves an injured evaluee far better than a trip to
anyone's office. Where the assignment requires an in-person evaluation, or
where deposition or trial dates require the expert present, the appointment
is arranged in the evaluee's own community and travel is scheduled from the
Kansas City home office, the same arrangement the firm's
[nationwide practice](/locations/nationwide/) uses in every by-appointment
venue.
