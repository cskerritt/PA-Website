Expert deadlines arrive on their own schedule, not the case's. By the time
a Woodburn matter reaches the point where earning capacity or future care
costs must be quantified, the disclosure calendar is usually already
running, and the expert's work has to fit inside it: records requested and
reviewed, the evaluee interviewed, the analysis completed, and the report
written with time to spare for counsel's review. Purinton Analytics builds
its [vocational evaluations](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care plans](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/) around that
reality, for retaining counsel on either side of the caption, and treats
the calendar as part of the engagement from the first call.

## Where Woodburn matters are heard

Woodburn sits in Marion County, and civil actions arising there are
ordinarily filed in the Circuit Court serving Marion County. Oregon's
federal forum, the United States District Court for the District of Oregon,
is available statewide, and which court controls a given matter is
identified and confirmed during the conflict check and intake rather than
assumed. The report does not change with the forum: it is drafted to a
testimony standard either way, with methodology, sources, and reasoning
laid out so opposing counsel can test each step.

## A labor market wider than the city limits

For a Woodburn evaluee, the question of what work remains realistically
available is answered at the level of the
[Salem metropolitan area](/locations/oregon/salem/), the commuting market
in which Woodburn participates. Vocational findings rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Salem area
and for Oregon statewide, together with state workforce agency labor market
information and, where the referral questions call for it, direct labor
market research. Those sources are characterized qualitatively in the
written opinion; the firm does not stretch published estimates into claims
they cannot carry, because an overstated market analysis is the first thing
a capable opposing expert will dismantle.

## Seeing the evaluee without moving the evaluee

There is no Purinton Analytics office in Woodburn, and the firm does not
pretend otherwise: its one home office is in Kansas City, every Oregon
meeting is by appointment, and no street address is published for this
location. In practice, most of the engagement proceeds by secure video,
from the initial interview through conferences with counsel, which spares
an injured person unnecessary travel. When the referral questions require
an in-person evaluation, or when deposition or trial testimony requires
presence, the evaluation is set in the evaluee's own community and travel
is scheduled from the Kansas City home office, consistent with how the
firm's [nationwide practice](/locations/nationwide/) serves every other
venue it accepts.

## Referring a Woodburn case before the calendar closes

The best time to refer is before the deadline pressure starts compounding.
Where the damages theory needs economic modeling, the firm arranges
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure, so counsel manages a single retention rather than
several. Submit the caption or party names through the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and flag any imminent disclosure,
deposition, or trial dates; the [Oregon overview](/locations/oregon/) sets
out the statewide practice supporting this page.
