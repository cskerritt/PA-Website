For an injured person living in Sweet Home, the least useful part of a forensic evaluation is the trip to reach it. A long drive to a distant office taxes exactly the person whose limitations are the subject of the report, and it tells the expert nothing. Purinton Analytics structures its [vocational expert](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) and [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) work for Sweet Home matters the other way around: the evaluation comes to the evaluee, by secure video or in that person's own community, whether the retaining lawyer sits at plaintiff's table or defense's.

## Where Sweet Home matters are heard

Sweet Home is a small Linn County city, and state-court litigation involving its residents proceeds in the Circuit Court for Linn County. Federal matters in the state have the United States District Court for the District of Oregon as their forum; the venue that actually controls a given case, together with any qualification requirement it imposes on a testifying expert, is identified during the conflict check and intake process. Whatever the forum, the file is built the same way, beginning with a documented conflict check and a records request drawn to the claims, so that each conclusion in the report can be checked against something in the record. The [Albany metro page](/locations/oregon/albany/) describes the surrounding area, and the [Oregon locations page](/locations/oregon/) collects the firm's statewide coverage.

## Earning capacity from a small city

Residual earning capacity for a Sweet Home evaluee cannot be assessed as if the town offered the occupational range of a large metropolitan center. The realistic market is the Albany, OR area, with the principal city of Albany accounting for much of the hiring within practical reach, and the analysis must say so candidly. The firm grounds its vocational findings in Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the relevant area and for Oregon statewide, in state workforce agency labor market information, and in direct labor market research when the referral questions call for it. Candor about a smaller market cuts in both directions, which is why the same method serves either side of the caption.

## An evaluation built around the evaluee

Most engagements open remotely: records arrive through secure channels, and interviews proceed by secure video when video serves the evaluee well. Where an in-person evaluation adds value, it is held at a location convenient to Sweet Home rather than at the end of a long drive. That is not because the firm has a nearby branch; it has none. The home office is in Kansas City, no staffed office exists in Sweet Home or anywhere in Oregon, and no local street address is published, since claiming a presence that does not exist would sit poorly beside reports that trade on accuracy. Depositions and trial testimony are handled through scheduled travel from Kansas City. When economic loss calculations are needed, [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) let independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement structure, and the practice extends the identical approach to counsel [nationwide](/locations/nationwide/).
