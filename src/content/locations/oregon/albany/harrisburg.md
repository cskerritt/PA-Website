## How a small town fits the labor market data

The occupational data that anchors a defensible earning capacity opinion is not published town by town, and no honest analysis pretends otherwise. Harrisburg belongs to the Albany, OR metropolitan area, anchored by the principal city of Albany, so the firm's vocational findings for a Harrisburg evaluee rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for that area and for Oregon statewide, supplemented by state workforce agency labor market information and by direct labor market research when the referral questions call for it. The written opinion is explicit about what area-level estimates can and cannot establish for a particular evaluee with particular restrictions, because an expert who overstates the reach of the data invites the cross-examination that follows.
