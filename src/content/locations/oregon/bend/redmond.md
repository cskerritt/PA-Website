What a Redmond claimant can realistically earn after an injury is rarely a question about Redmond alone. A mid-sized community inside the Bend, OR metropolitan area, Redmond sits within a commuting market that reaches well beyond its own limits, and a defensible earning capacity opinion has to be built at that scale. Purinton Analytics accepts [vocational expert](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) and [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) engagements involving Redmond parties for plaintiff and defense counsel alike, and it frames each analysis around the market an evaluee actually faces rather than the boundary drawn on a map.

## Earning capacity across the Bend area market

Because Redmond workers compete for jobs throughout the metro, with the principal city of Bend anchoring much of the area's hiring, a residual earning capacity opinion limited to employers inside the town would understate or misstate the evaluee's real options. Vocational findings therefore rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the relevant area and for Oregon statewide, supplemented by state workforce agency labor market information and by direct labor market research when the referral questions call for it. Each source is cited in the written report so that opposing counsel can trace every conclusion back to its data, and the transferable skills analysis is documented step by step rather than asserted.
