An evaluation should come to the evaluee, not the other way around. For an injured person living in Sisters, a small Deschutes County town in the Bend, OR metropolitan area, being told to travel to a distant office for an interview adds burden without adding rigor. Purinton Analytics structures its [vocational expert](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) and [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) engagements for Sisters parties, whether retained by plaintiff or defense counsel, so that the interview happens where it is easiest to conduct well: by secure video, or in person in the evaluee's own community.

## How a Sisters evaluation is arranged

Most engagements open with records, not travel. Counsel transmits the file through secure channels, the scope is confirmed in writing, and the vocational or life care planning interview is scheduled by secure video when that format serves the evaluee. Where the referral questions call for in-person assessment, the evaluation is arranged at a location convenient to Sisters rather than at an office the evaluee must find. The firm is candid about its footprint: its home office is in Kansas City, it staffs no office in Sisters or anywhere else in Oregon, and it publishes no local street address, because a practice that demands precision from its reports owes the same precision about where it actually sits. Travel for evaluations, depositions, and trial appearances is scheduled from the Kansas City home office.

## The courts that hear Sisters matters

Sisters lies in Deschutes County, and state-court litigation involving Sisters parties proceeds in the Circuit Court there. In federal practice, the forum available in the state is the United States District Court for the District of Oregon; the controlling venue for a specific case, and any registration or qualification requirement that comes with it, is identified and confirmed during the conflict check and intake process. Either way, the written opinion is prepared for adversarial reading, with a documented conflict check, a defined scope, and a records request tailored to the claims. ## Small-town residence, regional labor market

A Sisters evaluee's earning capacity cannot be read off the town itself. The community is small, and the practical labor market extends into the wider metro area anchored by the principal city of Bend, so the analysis must establish what work is actually reachable from where the evaluee lives. Vocational findings rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the relevant area and for Oregon statewide, on state workforce agency labor market information, and on direct labor market research when the referral questions warrant it. Sources are cited throughout the report so that opposing counsel can follow every conclusion to its origin. When economic damages are part of the theory, the firm offers [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement structure, and it serves counsel [nationwide](/locations/nationwide/) under identical procedures.
