## What a defensible La Pine opinion requires

La Pine is a small Deschutes County community within the Bend, OR metropolitan area, and small-town cases punish shortcuts. An expert who imports big-city assumptions about occupational variety, or who ignores the regional market anchored by the principal city of Bend, hands opposing counsel the cross-examination. The firm's approach is to establish the labor market the evaluee can actually reach and then document it: Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the relevant area and for Oregon statewide, state workforce agency labor market information, and direct labor market research where the referral questions warrant it. The transferable skills analysis is laid out so that its logic can be checked, not merely its conclusion quoted.
