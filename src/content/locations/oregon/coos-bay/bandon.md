## Where Bandon Cases Are Heard

State-court matters arising in Bandon proceed in the Circuit Court of Coos County. A case filed in or removed to federal court is heard by the United States District Court for the District of Oregon, and part of the conflict check and intake is pinning down which court will control the matter, along with any registration or qualification requirement that choice carries for the expert. That early confirmation matters for experts as much as for lawyers: disclosure form and timing differ between systems, and the report is drafted to the operative standard from the outset rather than retrofitted once the venue becomes clear.

## Labor Market Evidence for Bandon Evaluees

Bandon is a small labor market, and its scale is itself a fact in evidence when the question is what work an injured person can still perform and what that work would pay. Vocational conclusions rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Coos Bay-North Bend area and for Oregon statewide, alongside state workforce agency data and direct employer research where warranted. The analysis is explicit about geography: whether an occupation is realistically available to a Bandon resident, whether the relevant market extends toward Coos Bay, and what commuting assumptions the opinion depends on. An earning capacity figure that ignores where the evaluee actually lives invites impeachment, so the report shows its geographic reasoning on the page.

The practice keeps no office in Bandon and lists no street address for this location; every meeting is by appointment, and no local presence is implied that does not exist.
