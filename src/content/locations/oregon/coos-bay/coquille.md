## Earning Capacity in a Small-Town Market

A Coquille evaluee's realistic employment options are not the same as those of an evaluee in a major metropolitan area, and a credible earning capacity opinion has to say so with data rather than assumption. The analysis draws on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Coos Bay-North Bend area and for Oregon statewide, state workforce agency labor market information, and direct labor market research where warranted. Commuting range matters in a market this size: which employers an evaluee can realistically reach, including those in Coos Bay, is treated as an evidentiary question to be examined and documented, not an assumption to be waved through. Where a plan of care is at issue, the life care plan is built the same way, item by item from the medical record and treating-provider input rather than from templates.
