North Bend lends the Coos Bay-North Bend, OR metropolitan area half of its name, and litigation arising there draws on the same compact regional labor market that defines the metro as a whole. Purinton Analytics provides vocational expert and life care planning services for North Bend matters: earning capacity analysis in personal injury and employment cases, employability and transferable skills assessment, and life care plans for catastrophic injury claims. Counsel on either side of the caption receive the same work product, prepared to the same standard, because the report that survives cross-examination is the one written as though opposing counsel were reading over the drafter's shoulder.

The practice serves North Bend on a remote-first model from its Kansas City home office. The regional picture is described on the [Coos Bay metro page](/locations/oregon/coos-bay/), and the statewide practice on the [Oregon state page](/locations/oregon/).

## Coos County Courts

North Bend sits in Coos County, and state-court matters proceed in the Circuit Court of Coos County. In federal practice, the United States District Court for the District of Oregon is the forum available within the state; the controlling venue for any particular case is identified and confirmed during the conflict check and intake. Vocational and life care planning opinions are prepared with the disclosure and testimony obligations of both systems in mind: a defined scope, a documented methodology, and a written report that states its sources so either side can test it.

## Reading the North Bend Labor Market

North Bend and Coos Bay together form the employment core of a small metro area, and questions of earning capacity there cannot be answered from national averages alone. Vocational findings for North Bend evaluees rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Coos Bay-North Bend area and for Oregon statewide, supplemented by state workforce agency labor market information and direct labor market research where the referral questions warrant it. In a market of this scale, the gap between what an occupation pays nationally and what it pays locally can be material to damages, so the analysis names its geographic assumptions instead of leaving them implicit, and it explains why the chosen data source fits the evaluee rather than defaulting to whichever figure favors the retaining party.

## Evaluation Logistics in North Bend

There is no staffed Purinton Analytics office in North Bend or anywhere else in Oregon; meetings are by appointment, and no street address is published for this location, because accuracy about offices belongs to the same discipline that governs the reports. Most engagements proceed remotely from start to finish: records review, vocational interviews by video, and report preparation require no travel at all. When the referral questions call for in-person evaluation of a North Bend evaluee, or when deposition or trial dates require presence, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office and planned around the case calendar rather than the other way around. For catastrophic injury matters, the firm also offers [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement structure.
