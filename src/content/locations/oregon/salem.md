The questions that bring Salem counsel to a vocational expert are usually
narrow and consequential: what work an injured person can still perform, what
that work pays in the labor market where the person actually lives, and what
future care will cost over a lifetime. Purinton Analytics answers those
questions for plaintiff and defense counsel in the Salem area through
[earning capacity and employability evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis
while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement
structure.

## Courts serving the Salem area

Engagements are accepted for matters venued in the Circuit Court of Marion
County and the Circuit Court of Polk County, and in the United States District
Court for the District of Oregon for federal matters arising from the area.
The firm's written opinions are prepared to one national standard regardless
of forum, resting on a documented conflict check, a defined scope, and a
records request tailored to the claims, so that both sides of the caption can
examine the same record the opinion rests on. Counsel with related matters
elsewhere in the state will find the broader venue picture on the firm's
[Oregon page](/locations/oregon/).

## Appointment-based, not storefront

There is no staffed Purinton Analytics office in Salem, and the firm publishes
no street address for this location. Meetings are by appointment, and most of
the work does not require one: records review, structured interviews by secure
video, and standardized testing under publisher protocols where remote
administration is supported all proceed without travel. When an in-person
evaluation is warranted by the referral questions, or when a deposition or
trial requires presence, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office
around the dates the court sets. Counsel receive a single point of contact and
a consistent report format whether the work is performed remotely or in the
evaluee's own community.

## Grounding opinions in the local labor market

Vocational findings for Salem area evaluees rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates for the Salem metropolitan area and
for Oregon statewide, supplemented by state workforce agency labor market
information and direct labor market research where the referral questions
warrant it. The published series are qualitative anchors, not the end of the
analysis: transferable skills, physical and cognitive capacities, and the
actual hiring practices of regional employers all shape what an evaluee can
earn. Life care plans and medical cost projections are priced to the evaluee's
own market using provider-specific inquiries and published fee data, and every
source appears in the report where opposing counsel can test it.

## Beginning an engagement

Retention early in the case preserves options that discovery deadlines later
foreclose: baseline evaluation, records requests that reach the right
custodians, and testing scheduled before the calendar tightens. To begin,
[request a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) with the case caption and parties,
and flag any imminent disclosure or deposition dates. Scope and fee structure
are confirmed in writing before any records are reviewed. The same intake path
serves matters far beyond Marion County and Polk County; the firm's
[nationwide coverage](/locations/nationwide/) describes how distant venues are
served from the Kansas City home office.
