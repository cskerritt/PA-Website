## A small town's workers, a regional market's data

Boardman is a small town whose residents participate in the labor market of the broader Hermiston-Pendleton, OR metropolitan area, anchored by the principal city of Hermiston. Vocational findings for a Boardman evaluee therefore draw on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for that metropolitan area and for Oregon statewide, supplemented by state workforce agency labor market information and by direct labor market research when the referral questions justify it. The firm treats the qualitative character of the market honestly in both directions: an earning-capacity opinion should neither shrink a claimant's prospects to the town limits nor inflate them with occupations the regional economy does not meaningfully offer. Each placeability and transferable-skills conclusion is documented to withstand deposition scrutiny.
