How does an injured worker in Milton-Freewater actually get evaluated? That practical question, more than any legal one, shapes expert work in a small community, because the specialists a case requires rarely keep offices nearby, and asking a claimant with physical limitations to travel long distances for an interview can distort the very evaluation it enables. Purinton Analytics answers the question with a remote-first structure: the [vocational expert](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) and [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) file is built from records and secure video wherever that serves the case, and when the referral questions require an in-person evaluation, the appointment comes to the evaluee's own community rather than the other way around. Plaintiff and defense counsel are retained on identical terms.

## Reading the labor market from a small town

An earning-capacity analysis for a Milton-Freewater evaluee cannot be built on hometown impressions, and it cannot be built on big-city data either. The town is a small Umatilla County community within the Hermiston-Pendleton, OR metropolitan area, whose principal city is Hermiston, and the honest data frame is that regional market. The firm grounds its findings in Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the metro area and for Oregon statewide, in state workforce agency labor market information, and in direct labor market research where the questions call for it. Transferable-skills and placeability conclusions are stated with the limits of a smaller occupational base acknowledged on the page, because an opinion that overstates what a regional market offers will not survive a competent cross-examination and should not.
