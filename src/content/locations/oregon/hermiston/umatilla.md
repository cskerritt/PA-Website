Umatilla shares its name with the county that surrounds it, and in litigation that shared name rewards precision. A pleading, a records subpoena, or an expert designation that says "Umatilla" without more can point at the city or at Umatilla County as a whole, and the expert file should never be the place where that ambiguity survives. Purinton Analytics writes its reports to remove doubt of exactly that kind: parties, venue, and the community where the evaluee actually lives and works are stated plainly at the outset, so that a [vocational expert](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) or [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) opinion for a city of Umatilla matter reads cleanly to the judge, the jury, and the lawyer retained to attack it. Referrals come from both sides of the caption, and both get the same document.

The Circuit Court is the state trial court for Umatilla County, and it is where most state-law claims involving Umatilla residents and employers proceed. ## A small city inside a regional labor market

Umatilla is a small city within the Hermiston-Pendleton, OR metropolitan area, whose principal city is Hermiston, and its workers move through a labor market that is regional rather than local. Vocational findings for an Umatilla evaluee therefore rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the metro area and for Oregon statewide, on state workforce agency labor market information, and on direct labor market research when the referral questions warrant it. Placeability conclusions are tested against the occupations that actually exist in a market of this scale, not against assumptions imported from a big-city economy, and every step of that analysis is documented so it can be examined rather than taken on faith.

## Getting the evaluation done for an Umatilla evaluee

All Umatilla work is by appointment. The firm keeps no office in the city, publishes no local address, and will not suggest otherwise, because candor about logistics belongs in the same discipline that governs its opinions. Records are exchanged through secure channels; interviews proceed by secure video when video serves the case; and where an in-person evaluation is warranted, it is scheduled in or near the evaluee's own community, with travel arranged from the Kansas City home office. Counsel whose damages theory needs present-value calculations can convert the file into [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement structure, a model the firm applies for counsel [nationwide](/locations/nationwide/).
