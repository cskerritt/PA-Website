## Earning capacity in the Hermiston and Pendleton labor market

Pendleton sits within the Hermiston-Pendleton, OR metropolitan area, whose principal city is Hermiston, and its workers compete in that regional market rather than in the town alone. An earning-capacity opinion for a Pendleton evaluee is therefore grounded in Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for that metropolitan area and for Oregon statewide, together with state workforce agency labor market information and, where the referral questions call for it, direct labor market research. Because a regional market of this character offers a narrower occupational base than a large metropolitan economy, the transferable-skills analysis carries particular weight: each occupation identified for the evaluee must actually exist in the market the data describes, and the firm documents that step at a level of detail that stands up to line-by-line cross-examination rather than resting on generalized placement assumptions.
