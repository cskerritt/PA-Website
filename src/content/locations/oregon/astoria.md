Some Astoria referrals begin not with an evaluee but with another expert's
report: counsel needs to know whether the opposing vocational or life care
opinion will hold up, and if not, where it fails. Purinton Analytics
performs that work as
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/), applying the
same standards it applies to its own reports, and it accepts those
engagements from plaintiff and defense counsel alike. Alongside review
work, the firm provides
[vocational evaluation and expert testimony](/services/vocational-expert-witness/)
and [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) for Clatsop County
matters, all under the national, remote-first structure described on the
[nationwide services page](/locations/nationwide/).

## Clatsop County venues

The Astoria micropolitan area consists of Clatsop County, and engagements
are accepted for matters in the Circuit Court of Clatsop County and in the
United States District Court for the District of Oregon. Whether the firm
is retained affirmatively or in rebuttal, the engagement opens the same
way: a documented conflict check, a written scope, and a records request
tailored to the claims, so that every opinion rests on a record both sides
can examine. The remainder of the firm's coverage in the state appears on
the [Oregon locations page](/locations/oregon/).

## Appointment-based practice, no local storefront

Purinton Analytics maintains no staffed office in Astoria and publishes no
street address for this location; candor about presence is part of the
discipline counsel is retaining. Conferences with counsel are held by
videoconference, whether the task is an early opinion on case value, a
walkthrough of a draft report, or preparation for the expert's deposition.
Evaluations of Clatsop County evaluees are conducted remotely where the
referral questions permit and arranged in the evaluee's own community when
in-person assessment is warranted. When testimony requires presence in the
county, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office against the
court's calendar.

## Reading the Clatsop County labor market

Vocational findings for Astoria-area evaluees are built on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for Oregon, state
workforce agency labor market information, and direct labor market
research where the referral questions warrant it. The same sources anchor
the firm's review of opposing reports: an opposing opinion that leans on
generalized data without connecting it to the evaluee's documented
restrictions and work history is a familiar weakness, and identifying it
precisely is more useful to counsel than rhetoric about it. Where damages
must be quantified, the firm offers
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure, with
[medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/) available
when future care is the disputed element.

## Starting a Clatsop County engagement

Review engagements and affirmative engagements start at the same place.
Submit the caption or party names through the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and note any imminent disclosure,
deposition, or trial dates, along with the opposing report's service date
if rebuttal work is contemplated. Once conflicts clear, counsel receives a
defined scope, a fee agreement, and a records request, and the analysis
begins on the schedule the disclosure deadlines require.
