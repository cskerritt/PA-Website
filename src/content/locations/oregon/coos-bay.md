For an injured evaluee in the Coos Bay-North Bend area, the evaluation
itself is often the hardest part of the case to arrange: the person may have
mobility limits, ongoing treatment, or work obligations that make travel to
a distant expert impractical. Purinton Analytics resolves that problem by
bringing the evaluation to the evaluee, remotely where the referral
questions permit and in the evaluee's own community when in-person
assessment is warranted. Around that evaluation the firm delivers its full
scope of work for plaintiff and defense counsel:
[vocational expert evaluation and testimony](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/) built on
identified pricing sources.

## Where Coos County cases are heard

The Coos Bay-North Bend micropolitan area consists of Coos County, and
engagements are accepted for matters in the Circuit Court of Coos County and
in the United States District Court for the District of Oregon. Whichever
side of the caption retains the firm, the engagement follows the same
sequence: a documented conflict check, a defined scope, and a records
request tailored to the claims, so the written opinion rests on a record
both parties can examine. The firm's coverage of the rest of the state is
described on the [Oregon locations page](/locations/oregon/), and its
national structure on the [nationwide page](/locations/nationwide/).

## By-appointment logistics in the Coos Bay-North Bend area

There is no staffed Purinton Analytics office in Coos Bay or North Bend, and
no street address is published for this location. That is a deliberate
statement, not an omission: accuracy about offices is part of the same
discipline that governs the firm's reports. Counsel meet with the expert by
videoconference for case strategy, report review, and deposition
preparation. When testimony requires presence in Coos County, travel is
scheduled from the Kansas City home office and confirmed against the
court's calendar at the time the engagement is scoped, so that trial dates
never depend on last-minute logistics.

## Labor market analysis for Coos County evaluees

Employability and earning capacity opinions for Coos Bay-area evaluees rest
on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates
for Oregon, state workforce agency labor market information, and direct
labor market research where the referral questions warrant it. A
micropolitan labor market rewards that extra step: the occupations open to
an evaluee with specific restrictions may differ substantially from what
statewide data alone would suggest, and opinions in this practice are
written to survive cross-examination on exactly that point. When lost
earnings must be reduced to a damages figure, the firm offers
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Beginning a Coos County engagement

The path into an engagement runs through a conflict check. To begin,
[request a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) with the case caption and party
names, and identify any disclosure deadlines, depositions, or trial
settings already on the calendar. Once conflicts are cleared, counsel
receives the scope, fee agreement, and records request, and evaluation
scheduling for the evaluee begins immediately after the records arrive.
