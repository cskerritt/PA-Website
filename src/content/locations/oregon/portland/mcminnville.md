## The Yamhill County courthouse and the federal option

For most McMinnville parties the answer to the venue question is the Circuit Court serving Yamhill County, which hears the state-court side of the community's litigation. Cases within federal jurisdiction may instead proceed in the United States District Court for the District of Oregon, the federal forum the state offers; which court actually controls, together with any venue-specific qualification or registration requirement, is confirmed during the conflict check and intake. The written opinion is prepared identically for either setting, with methodology disclosed, records itemized, and sources cited so opposing counsel can retrace the analysis line by line. ## The labor market behind a McMinnville opinion

McMinnville is a mid-sized community within the Portland-Vancouver-Hillsboro, OR-WA metropolitan area, and a defensible earning-capacity opinion measures its evaluee against the employment that market actually offers, not against generalizations about small cities or large ones. The firm's vocational findings rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Portland metropolitan area and for Oregon statewide, on state workforce agency labor market information, and on direct labor market research where the referral questions warrant contact with actual employers. The report separates what the data shows from what the evaluator concludes, so the finder of fact can see where evidence ends and expert judgment begins.
