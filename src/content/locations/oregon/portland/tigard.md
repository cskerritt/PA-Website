A scheduling order is usually the reason the phone rings. Counsel handling a Tigard case rarely retains a vocational expert at leisure; the call comes when a disclosure deadline is visible, a mediation is set, or an opposing expert's report has just landed and a response is due. Purinton Analytics organizes its Tigard work around that reality. Intake for every [vocational expert](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) or [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) referral, whether from the plaintiff's side or the defense, begins with the calendar: what is due, when, and in what form. ## The venue question for Tigard parties

Tigard lies in Washington County, and its state-court matters are heard in the Circuit Court serving the county. Cases with a federal basis may proceed in the United States District Court for the District of Oregon, the federal forum for the state; which court actually controls, and any venue-specific qualification requirement that comes with it, is confirmed during the conflict check and intake rather than presumed at the outset. The written opinion is built to the same standard for either forum, with methodology, records, and sources documented for examination by both sides. Neighboring venue coverage is collected on the [Portland metro page](/locations/oregon/portland/), and statewide practice is described on the [Oregon page](/locations/oregon/).

## Earning capacity in Tigard's commuting orbit

Tigard is a mid-sized suburb whose workers move daily through the wider Portland metropolitan labor market, and an earning-capacity opinion has to respect that geography. The firm anchors its findings in Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Portland metropolitan area and for Oregon statewide, adds state workforce agency labor market information, and conducts direct labor market research when the referral questions require contact with actual employers. The analysis of transferable skills and placeability is set out step by step, because an opinion that cannot show its work is not worth serving, and the firm writes on the assumption that opposing counsel will check every step.
