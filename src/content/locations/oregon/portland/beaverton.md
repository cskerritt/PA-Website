Purinton Analytics structures its Beaverton practice around making that step easy for counsel on both sides of the caption: interviews by secure video when the case permits, in-person evaluation arranged in or near the evaluee's own community when the referral questions require it, and records handled through secure channels throughout. ## Measuring work capacity against a metro market

A large suburb like Beaverton offers its residents employment both locally and across the broader Portland metropolitan area, so a defensible earning-capacity opinion has to be scaled to the commuting market the evaluee can actually reach. The firm draws on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Portland metropolitan area and for Oregon statewide, on state workforce agency labor market information, and on direct labor market research where the questions justify employer-level inquiry. Transferable skills, placeability, and labor market access are analyzed and documented so that opposing counsel can audit the path from data to conclusion, which is the standard the firm sets for its own work whether retained by plaintiff or defense.
