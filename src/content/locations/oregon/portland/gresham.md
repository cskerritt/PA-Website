## Scaling the analysis to the market

Residents of a large city participate in a labor market that extends beyond its own boundaries, and the firm sizes its vocational analysis to that reality rather than to city limits. Earning-capacity and placeability findings rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Portland metropolitan area and for Oregon statewide, on state workforce agency labor market information, and on direct labor market research when the referral questions justify employer-level inquiry. Each step from medical restriction to occupational option to wage conclusion is written down in the report itself, because a conclusion that cannot be audited is not evidence, only assertion, and the firm assumes every Gresham report will eventually be read by the lawyer whose job is to break it.
