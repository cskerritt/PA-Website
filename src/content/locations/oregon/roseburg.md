Smaller venues deserve the same expert work as the largest ones, and the
Roseburg micropolitan area is served on exactly that footing. Purinton
Analytics brings the identical report standard to Douglas County that it
applies in major metropolitan litigation: careful
[vocational evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) of earning
capacity and employability, [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/)
in catastrophic injury matters, and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis
while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement
structure. Retention is open to plaintiff and defense counsel alike, on the
same published terms.

## Douglas County courts

Engagements are accepted for matters venued in the Circuit Court of Douglas
County and in the United States District Court for the District of Oregon.
Every opinion begins with a documented conflict check, a defined scope, and a
records request tailored to the claims, so that the written conclusions rest
on a record either side of the caption can examine. Any venue-specific
registration or qualification requirement is identified and confirmed during
the conflict check and intake process. For the courts and metro areas the firm
serves elsewhere in the state, see the firm's
[Oregon page](/locations/oregon/).

## Served by appointment, honestly

The firm keeps no staffed office in Roseburg and publishes no street address
for this location. That candor is intentional: a practice that asks courts to
trust its accuracy should be accurate about where it is. What counsel get
instead of a storefront is a working model built for distance. Records review,
structured interviews by secure video, and standardized testing under
publisher protocols where remote administration is supported carry most
engagements from intake through the written opinion. When an in-person
evaluation is warranted, it is conducted in the evaluee's own community, and
when a deposition or trial in Douglas County requires presence, travel is
scheduled from the Kansas City home office to fit the court's calendar.

## Evidence from the local labor market

Vocational findings for Roseburg area evaluees rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates covering the area and
Oregon statewide, alongside state workforce agency labor market information
and direct labor market research where the referral questions warrant it. In a
regional market, direct employer contact often matters as much as the
published series, and the report documents both, showing how residual
capacities, transferable skills, and actual hiring conditions combine into
placement conclusions. Life care plans and medical cost projections are priced
to the evaluee's own market through provider-specific inquiries and published
fee data, with sources cited so the figures can be traced.

## Opening a Douglas County engagement

Counsel get the most from an expert retained before the scheduling order
narrows the options for evaluation and testing. Submit the caption or party
names through the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and identify any
imminent disclosure, deposition, or trial dates; scope and fee structure are
confirmed in writing before any records are reviewed. The same model serves
matters far beyond Douglas County, as described in the firm's
[national coverage](/locations/nationwide/).
