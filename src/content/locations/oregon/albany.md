## Where Albany matters are heard

The Albany metropolitan area lies in Linn County, and engagements are accepted
for matters venued in the Circuit Court of Linn County and in the United
States District Court for the District of Oregon. The method does not vary by
forum: a documented conflict check, a defined scope, and a records request
tailored to the claims come first, so the written opinion rests on a record
both sides of the caption can examine. Any venue-specific registration or
qualification requirement is identified and confirmed during the conflict
check and intake process. Counsel weighing venues elsewhere in the state can
consult the firm's [Oregon page](/locations/oregon/).

## Presence, stated accurately

The firm has no staffed office in Albany and publishes no street address for
this location; there is one home office, in Kansas City, and every other
location is served by appointment. Most engagements never require a local
office. Records analysis, structured interviews by secure video, and
standardized testing under publisher protocols where remote administration is
supported move the work from intake to draft opinion without travel. Where the
referral questions call for an in-person evaluation, the firm evaluates in the
evaluee's own community, and deposition or trial appearances in Linn County
are met through travel scheduled from the Kansas City home office around the
court's dates.

## Reading the Albany area labor market

Vocational conclusions for Albany area evaluees rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Albany
metropolitan area and for Oregon statewide, together with state workforce
agency labor market information and direct labor market research where the
referral questions warrant it. A smaller regional market is examined on its

documents the path from published estimates and employer contacts to placement
conclusions. Life care plans and medical cost projections are priced to the
evaluee's own market through provider-specific inquiries and published fee
data, each source cited for verification by either side.
