Asking an injured person in a small town like Reedsport to travel
repeatedly for expert appointments is a real cost, and it is one the
evaluation process can largely avoid. Purinton Analytics designs its
engagements so the expert work comes to the evaluee: secure video for the
interview and follow-up, records gathered and reviewed remotely, and
in-person assessment arranged in the evaluee's own community when the
referral questions genuinely require it. That logistics discipline applies
equally to [vocational evaluations](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care plans](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/), and it is
available to plaintiff and defense counsel alike.

## What by-appointment service means here

The firm maintains no office in Reedsport, publishes no street address for
this location, and will not imply a local storefront that does not exist;
its one home office is in Kansas City. In practice that means scheduling is
explicit and put in writing at intake: which steps proceed by video, which
require presence, and how travel from the Kansas City home office is timed
against the litigation calendar. Depositions and trial appearances are
planned the same way, so counsel is never guessing about availability in
the weeks that matter most.

## Douglas County venue for Reedsport matters

Reedsport sits in Douglas County, and civil actions arising there are
ordinarily brought in the Circuit Court serving Douglas County. Oregon's
federal forum, the United States District Court for the District of Oregon,
is available for cases that belong in federal court, and the controlling
venue for a given matter is identified and confirmed during the conflict
check and intake. The report itself is venue-neutral in its rigor:
methodology stated, sources cited, assumptions labeled as assumptions, so
that either forum, and either side's examining counsel, can trace how each
conclusion was reached.

## A small town measured against a wider market

Earning capacity for a Reedsport evaluee is not assessed against the town
alone. The analysis considers the labor market the evaluee can realistically
access, including the broader Roseburg, OR metropolitan area described on
the [Roseburg metro page](/locations/oregon/roseburg/). Findings rest on
Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for
the Roseburg area and for Oregon statewide, together with state workforce
agency labor market information and direct labor market research where
warranted. Every source is characterized qualitatively; where the market a
worker can actually reach is thinner than statewide figures suggest, the
report says so plainly, because opinions that overstate access do not
survive a capable cross-examination.

a records request tailored to the claims. When economic quantification is

analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. To begin, send the caption or party names through the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and identify any imminent disclosure,
deposition, or trial dates. The [Oregon state page](/locations/oregon/)
covers the statewide practice, and the
