vocational or care-planning question can be answered, counsel has already
chosen a forum, and the expert's report will eventually be read in that
forum by a judge, a jury, and an opposing expert looking for weak joints.
Purinton Analytics writes its
[vocational evaluations](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care plans](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/) with that
audience in mind from the outset, and accepts retention from plaintiff and
defense counsel on the same terms.

## The venues that hear Sutherlin disputes

Civil actions arising in Sutherlin are ordinarily filed in the Circuit
Court serving Douglas County, and Oregon's federal forum, the United States
District Court for the District of Oregon, stands available for matters
that belong there. Which court controls a particular dispute is identified
and confirmed during the conflict check and intake, not presumed from
geography. The work product is the same either way: a written opinion with
its methodology, sources, and reasoning set out step by step, so that
cross-examination tests the analysis rather than exposes it. When the
damages theory also requires economic quantification, the firm offers
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Reading the labor market around a small city

Sutherlin is a small city within the Roseburg, OR metropolitan area, and an
injured worker's realistic options are measured against that wider market
rather than against the town alone. Vocational findings rest on Bureau of
Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the
Roseburg area and for Oregon statewide, on state workforce agency labor
market information, and on direct labor market research where the referral
questions warrant it. Those sources are characterized carefully and
qualitatively in the report; published estimates are not stretched into
claims they cannot support, because an inflated market picture is the
easiest thing for a capable opposing expert to take apart. Context for the
surrounding market sits on the
[Roseburg metro page](/locations/oregon/roseburg/).

## How a Sutherlin evaluee is evaluated

The firm keeps no office in Sutherlin and publishes no street address for
this location; its one home office is in Kansas City, and every Oregon
meeting is by appointment. Most engagements proceed largely by secure
video, from the initial interview through working conferences with counsel,
which keeps the schedule moving without imposing travel on an injured
person. When the referral questions call for in-person evaluation, or when
deposition and trial dates require presence, travel is scheduled from the
Kansas City home office and the evaluation is conducted in the evaluee's
own community. That candor about presence is deliberate: a report that
begins with an honest account of logistics is easier to defend in every
later particular.
