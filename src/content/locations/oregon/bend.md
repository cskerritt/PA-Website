Distance is a design constraint the practice was built around. Counsel in the
Bend area do not need a local expert so much as a rigorous one who can serve a
distant venue without friction, and Purinton Analytics structures its work
accordingly: remote-first evaluation, by-appointment meetings, and scheduled
travel when the case requires presence. For plaintiff and defense counsel
alike, the firm provides
[vocational expert evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis
while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement
structure.

## Courts of the Bend metropolitan area

The metro spans Deschutes County, Crook County, and Jefferson County, and
engagements are accepted for matters venued in the circuit courts of each,
including the Circuit Court of Deschutes County, as well as in the
United States District Court for the District of Oregon. The same written
opinion standard applies in each forum: a documented conflict check, a defined
scope, and a records request tailored to the claims, producing a report that
either side of the caption can examine line by line. The firm's
[Oregon page](/locations/oregon/) covers the rest of the state's venues.

## What by-appointment means in practice

There is no staffed Purinton Analytics office in Bend, and the firm publishes
no street address for this location. That is stated plainly because honesty
about presence is part of the same discipline that governs the analysis. Most
engagements proceed remotely from intake onward: records review, structured
interviews by secure video, and standardized testing under publisher protocols
where remote administration is supported. When the referral questions call for
an in-person evaluation, the evaluation is conducted in the evaluee's own
community rather than requiring the evaluee to travel. Depositions and trial
appearances are met through travel scheduled from the Kansas City home office,
planned against the court's calendar with room for the logistics a distant
venue demands.

## Labor market research for a regional market

Vocational findings for Bend area evaluees rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates for the Bend metropolitan area and
for Oregon statewide, together with state workforce agency labor market
information and direct labor market research where the referral questions
warrant it. A regional market is analyzed on its own terms, not assumed to
mirror the state's largest cities, and the report documents how the published
series and any employer contacts support each conclusion. Life care plans and
medical cost projections are likewise priced to the evaluee's own market
through provider-specific inquiries and published fee data, with every source
cited for verification.

## Requesting a conflict check

Engagements move fastest when they begin before disclosure deadlines compress
the schedule. To start,
[request a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) with the case caption and parties,
and identify any imminent disclosure, deposition, or trial dates. The firm
confirms scope and fee structure in writing before reviewing records. Counsel
with matters beyond the Bend area can consult the firm's
[national coverage](/locations/nationwide/) for how the same model serves
venues nationwide.
