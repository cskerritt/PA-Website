Most referrals from the Fort Morgan area turn on a small set of contested
questions: what work the evaluee can still perform, what that work pays,
and what future care will cost. Purinton Analytics answers those questions
for plaintiff and defense counsel in Morgan County matters through
[vocational expert witness](/services/vocational-expert-witness/)
evaluation, [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/), and it
answers them in writing, with a methodology section that either side of the
caption can test line by line.

## Where Fort Morgan matters are heard

Engagements are accepted for civil matters in the District Court sitting in
Morgan County and for matters proceeding in the United States District
Court for the District of Colorado, as well as for arbitrations and
administrative proceedings where vocational or care-planning evidence is
contested. The analysis is built to the same standard in each forum, so a
report prepared for the state district court needs no rework if the case is
removed. The firm's broader work across the state, including its pages for
Colorado's larger markets, is listed on the
[Colorado locations page](/locations/colorado/).

## Serving Morgan County by appointment

Purinton Analytics has no staffed office in Fort Morgan and publishes no
street address for this location; meetings are by appointment, and the firm
prefers to say that directly rather than imply a local presence it does not
maintain. A typical Morgan County engagement runs remotely: counsel's
records are reviewed, structured vocational interviews are conducted by
video, and standardized testing is administered under publisher protocols
where remote administration is supported. When the referral questions
require an in-person evaluation, the firm conducts it in the evaluee's own
community, and when deposition or trial requires the expert's presence,
travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office around the dates
counsel supplies at intake.

## Labor market and cost research

An earning capacity opinion for a Morgan County evaluee is grounded in the
labor market that evaluee actually faces. Vocational findings rest on
Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for
the relevant area and for Colorado statewide, on state workforce agency
labor market information, and on direct employer and labor market research
where warranted. Life care plans and medical cost projections are priced to
the evaluee's own market through provider-specific inquiries and published
fee data rather than national averages. Counsel who need the full damages
picture can retain the firm for
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure, keeping the vocational, care-planning, and economic
strands consistent with each other.

## Starting the engagement

The engagement sequence in Fort Morgan is the sequence used everywhere in
the practice: a documented conflict check, a defined scope, and a records
request tailored to the claims, so that the written opinion rests on a
record both sides can examine. To begin,
[request a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) with the case caption and
parties, and identify any disclosure deadlines, deposition settings, or
trial dates already on the calendar. The firm's
[nationwide coverage](/locations/nationwide/) describes how the same
structure serves counsel in every state.
