## Fitting the evaluation into the case timeline

Because remote evaluation removes travel from the critical path, a Superior evaluee can usually be interviewed and tested from home within the window the disclosure schedule allows, under structured and documented conditions. Testimony dates for deposition and trial are reserved as soon as the court's schedule makes them known.
