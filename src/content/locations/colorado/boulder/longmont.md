Longmont is one of the larger cities in the Boulder metropolitan area and a regional employment center in its own right, which means an earning capacity analysis for a Longmont evaluee cannot treat the town as a bedroom community. The work available locally and the work available across the metro both belong in the analysis, and a report that ignores either will not survive a careful cross-examination. Purinton Analytics provides [vocational expert](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) and [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) services for injury, employment, and disability matters involving Longmont parties, serving plaintiff and defense counsel alike as part of its broader [Boulder metro practice](/locations/colorado/boulder/).

## Boulder County venue for Longmont matters

Longmont sits in Boulder County, and civil actions involving Longmont parties are commonly heard in the District Court serving Boulder County. When a claim arises under federal law or the parties are diverse, the United States District Court for the District of Colorado is the available federal forum; the controlling venue for any particular case is identified during the conflict check and intake rather than assumed in advance. Wherever the matter lands, the work product is built the same way: a documented conflict check, a defined scope, a records request tailored to the claims, and a written opinion that both sides of the caption can examine line by line.

## Measuring a labor market larger than the town

Vocational findings for a Longmont evaluee rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Boulder metropolitan area and for Colorado statewide, supplemented by state workforce agency labor market information and direct labor market research where the referral questions warrant it. Because Longmont supports substantial employment of its own while remaining connected to the wider metro, the transferable skills analysis addresses both markets rather than assuming the evaluee must commute to Boulder. When lost earnings must be reduced to present value, the firm offers [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement structure.

## Evaluation logistics for a Longmont evaluee

The firm keeps no office in Longmont and publishes no local address, because accuracy about presence is part of the same discipline that governs its reports. Most evaluations proceed remotely, through structured interview and standardized testing that the evaluee completes from home on a schedule set around treatment and work obligations. When the referral questions call for an in-person evaluation, it is arranged at a suitable professional setting in the evaluee's own community, with travel scheduled from the Kansas City home office. Deposition and trial appearances are planned the same way, around the court's calendar rather than the firm's.

## Requesting a conflict check

Engagements begin with a conflict check rather than a retainer. Counsel can [request a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) with the case caption and party names, noting any imminent disclosure, deposition, or trial dates so scheduling is settled before the scope issues. Assignments range from first-line evaluations to [medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/) and reviews of opposing experts' reports, on either side of the caption.
