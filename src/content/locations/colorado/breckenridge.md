A vocational opinion should read the same whether it is filed in a major
metropolitan courthouse or in a micropolitan district court, and that uniformity
is the premise of Purinton Analytics' work in the Breckenridge area. The
firm brings its national report standard to Summit County and Lake County
matters, providing [vocational expert witness](/services/vocational-expert-witness/)
evaluation, [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) of opposing
expert work. Plaintiff and defense engagements are accepted on identical
terms, and each report is drafted for the reader most motivated to find its
flaws: opposing counsel.

## Summit and Lake county venues

The firm accepts matters venued in the District Court sitting in Summit
County and the District Court sitting in Lake County, and matters in the
United States District Court for the District of Colorado. It also serves
arbitration and administrative proceedings where employability, earning
capacity, or future care needs are disputed. Counsel with cases spread
across the state can review the firm's other Colorado coverage on the
[Colorado locations page](/locations/colorado/); the engagement structure
does not change from venue to venue.

## Logistics without a storefront

There is no Purinton Analytics office in Breckenridge, no staffed storefront,
and no published street address for this location, because claiming a
presence the firm does not have would sit poorly beside reports that trade
on accuracy. Meetings are by appointment. Most Summit and Lake county
engagements are conducted remotely from intake to report: records review,
structured interviews by video, and standardized testing under publisher
protocols where remote administration is supported. When an evaluation must
happen in person, it happens in the evaluee's own community, and when a
deposition or trial date requires presence, travel is scheduled from the
Kansas City home office with the lead time counsel's calendar demands.

## Occupational data for the Breckenridge area

Earning capacity and employability findings for Breckenridge area evaluees
rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage
estimates for the relevant area and for Colorado statewide, on state
workforce agency labor market information, and on direct labor market
research where the referral questions call for it. The firm does not
substitute national generalities for the market the evaluee actually
occupies. Life care plans and [medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/)
are likewise priced to the evaluee's own market through provider-specific
inquiries and published fee data. Where counsel wants economic loss analysis
alongside the vocational and care-planning work, the firm offers
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Opening the file

Every Breckenridge area engagement opens with a documented conflict check, a
defined scope, and a records request tailored to the claims, so the written
opinion rests on a record both sides can examine. Submit the caption or
party names through the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/), and note any
imminent disclosure, deposition, or trial dates so scheduling commitments
can be confirmed before the engagement letter issues. For matters outside
Colorado, the firm's [nationwide coverage](/locations/nationwide/) describes
the same practice at national scale.
