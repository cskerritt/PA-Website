When earning capacity or the cost of future care is contested in a Montrose
County case, the dispute usually turns on method: how residual employability
was measured, which labor market the findings describe, and whether the care
plan tracks the treating record. Purinton Analytics answers those questions
for Montrose counsel through [vocational expert witness](/services/vocational-expert-witness/)
testimony, [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) of opposing
opinions. Plaintiff firms and defense firms alike bring these disputes to
the practice, and every opinion is written to withstand the closest reading
opposing counsel can give it.

## Montrose County venues

The firm accepts matters venued in the District Court sitting in Montrose

## Appointment-based meetings in Montrose

The firm maintains no staffed office in Montrose and publishes no street
address for this location; meetings are by appointment, and the firm states
that plainly rather than implying a storefront that does not exist. The
working sequence for a typical Montrose County engagement runs remotely:
counsel's records request is fulfilled, the file is analyzed, structured
interviews are conducted by video, and standardized testing is administered
under publisher protocols where remote administration is supported. In-person
evaluation, when the referral questions require it, takes place in the
evaluee's own community, with travel scheduled from the Kansas City home
office. Deposition and trial appearances are calendared the same way, and
early notice of firm dates keeps that travel from ever becoming counsel's
problem.

## Grounding opinions in Montrose area labor data

A transferable skills analysis is only as persuasive as the labor market it
describes. Vocational findings for Montrose County evaluees rest on Bureau
of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the
relevant area and for Colorado statewide, on state workforce agency labor
market information, and on direct employer and labor market research where
warranted. Life care plans and
[medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/) are priced to
the evaluee's own market through provider-specific inquiries and published
fee data. Where a full damages presentation is needed, the firm offers
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure, giving counsel a coherent damages record without
managing multiple retentions.
