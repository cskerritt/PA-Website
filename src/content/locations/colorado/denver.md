Purinton Analytics supports Front Range litigation with vocational expert and
life care planning services for plaintiff and defense counsel across the
Denver metro and the rest of Colorado. Engagements from the region typically
involve [earning capacity and employability evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/)
in injury and employment matters, [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/)
in catastrophic injury cases, and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Serving Denver and the Front Range

The firm accepts matters venued in the District Court for the City and County
of Denver and the other state district courts of the metro area, including
Adams, Arapahoe, Jefferson, Douglas, and Boulder counties, as well as in the
United States District Court for the District of Colorado. Counsel outside
the metro, from Colorado Springs to Fort Collins and the Western Slope, are
served through the same combination of remote work and scheduled travel that
the firm uses nationwide.

## By appointment, and built for distance

Denver meetings are by appointment rather than through a permanently staffed
office, and the firm deliberately publishes no street address here. Most
Colorado engagements begin remotely: records review, structured interviews,
and standardized testing under publisher protocols where remote
administration is supported. When the referral questions call for in-person
evaluation, or when deposition and trial dates require presence, travel is
scheduled from the Kansas City home office, and direct flights make
short-notice appearances practical. Counsel receive one report standard and
one point of contact regardless of where the work is performed.

## Colorado labor market and cost research

Vocational findings for Colorado evaluees rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates for the Denver metropolitan area
and for Colorado statewide, state workforce agency labor market information,
and direct labor market research where warranted. Life care plans and medical
cost projections for Colorado residents are priced to the evaluee's own
market using provider-specific inquiries and published fee data for the
region, since care costs along the Front Range differ from national averages
in both directions depending on the service category.

## Qualification for Colorado matters

Jason Purinton's board certifications are national credentials, and
qualification to testify in a Colorado matter is determined by the forum
under its rules of evidence. Any venue-specific requirement is identified
during intake. To explore availability for a Front Range case, start with a
[conflict check](/refer-a-case/) or review the firm's
[national coverage](/locations/nationwide/).
