Expert work in litigation is governed by dates: the disclosure deadline, the deposition, the trial setting. When counsel in Frisco retains a vocational expert or life care planner, the engagement is planned backward from that calendar. Purinton Analytics scopes each Frisco assignment at intake, whether the need is [vocational evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/), [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), or [rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) of an opposing disclosure, so that the written opinion arrives when the schedule requires it and in the form the forum expects. Plaintiff and defense referrals receive the same method, because the calendar does not care which side served it.

## Venue shapes the report

Frisco is a Summit County town, and civil actions arising there are generally heard in the District Court serving Summit County; the United States District Court for the District of Colorado is the state's federal trial forum. The controlling venue for any particular case, along with any expert registration or qualification requirement it carries, is identified and confirmed during the conflict check and intake process. That confirmation happens early because it changes the work product itself: report structure, the reach of required disclosures, and the sequence of expert discovery all follow the forum, and a report drafted under the wrong set of rules costs counsel time the calendar rarely gives back.

## Labor market evidence for a Frisco case

For vocational purposes, Frisco is analyzed as part of the Breckenridge commuting market rather than in isolation. Opinions on what work a Frisco evaluee can perform, and on the earnings that work supports, are grounded in Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the area and for Colorado statewide, in state workforce agency labor market information, and in direct labor market research where the referral questions justify it. Every market characterization in the report remains qualitative and traceable to a published estimate or a documented inquiry, a standard set with cross-examination in mind rather than met after it begins. The same sourcing discipline applies whether the assignment is a first-line opinion or a critique of an opposing expert's methodology.

## Where the evaluee is seen

The firm keeps no office in Frisco and does not suggest otherwise; its only office is the Kansas City home office, and Frisco is served by appointment. Records analysis and structured interviews ordinarily proceed by video, and many engagements are completed remotely from intake through report delivery, which protects the disclosure schedule. When the assignment calls for in-person assessment, the evaluation is conducted in the evaluee's own community, with travel from Kansas City arranged for evaluations, depositions, and trial testimony. If the damages analysis extends to economic valuation, counsel can add [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement structure.
