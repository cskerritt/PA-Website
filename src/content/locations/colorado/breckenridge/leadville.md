For expert practice, the most important fact about Leadville is jurisdictional: the town sits in Lake County, even though it belongs to the commuting area named for Breckenridge. Purinton Analytics starts every Leadville engagement from that county-level reality, because the county line determines where a state-court action is tried and how a vocational or care-planning opinion must be disclosed and defended. The practice accepts Leadville referrals from plaintiff and defense counsel alike, offering [vocational evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/), [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and [rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/), each under a documented conflict check and a defined scope.

## Lake County venue in practice

State-court civil actions arising in Leadville are ordinarily tried in the District Court serving Lake County. Colorado's federal trial forum is the United States District Court for the District of Colorado, and the controlling venue for a given engagement, together with any registration or qualification requirement it places on a testifying expert, is identified and confirmed during the conflict check and intake process. Writing for the correct forum from the first draft is not a formality: the disclosure a state district court expects and the disclosure federal practice demands are different documents, and an opinion prepared for one but filed in the other invites avoidable impeachment at deposition.

## A Lake County evaluee in a regional labor market

Although Leadville is a small town, its residents' working lives are analyzed against the broader regional market that includes the Breckenridge area, not against the town alone. Vocational findings for a Leadville evaluee draw on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the region and for Colorado statewide, on state workforce agency labor market information, and on direct labor market research where warranted by the referral questions. The analysis names its sources and keeps its market descriptions qualitative, so that on cross-examination each conclusion can be walked back to published data or a documented inquiry, and nothing in the report depends on an impression of the local economy that no source supports.

## Logistics without a local office

Purinton Analytics maintains no office in Leadville and claims none anywhere outside its Kansas City home office; every other location, this one included, is served by appointment. Remote handling covers most of the work: records analysis, structured video interviews, and report delivery on the disclosure schedule counsel sets. When the referral requires an in-person evaluation, it is performed in the evaluee's own community, and travel for evaluations, depositions, and trial testimony is scheduled from Kansas City. Where economic valuation is also needed, the firm provides [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement structure, so each discipline stays within its proper lane.
