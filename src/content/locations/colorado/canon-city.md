The District Court sitting in Fremont County is where most Cañon City civil
matters are decided, and it is the venue Purinton Analytics has in mind when
it prepares vocational and life care planning opinions for the area. The
firm provides [vocational expert witness](/services/vocational-expert-witness/)
evaluation, [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[expert testimony support](/services/expert-testimony-litigation-consulting/)
to plaintiff and defense counsel on equal terms, and it applies the same
documented methodology in a micropolitan venue that it applies in the
largest metros, because an expert's consistency across engagements is
always easy to check.

## Courts served from a national practice

Engagements are accepted for matters in the District Court in Fremont County
and, where a case proceeds federally, in the United States District Court
for the District of Colorado. The firm also takes appointments in
arbitration and administrative forums where employability, earning capacity,
or future care needs are contested. Counsel with matters elsewhere in the
state will find the firm's other Colorado coverage described on the
[Colorado locations page](/locations/colorado/), and the same report
standard applies in every forum.

## No local office, and why

Purinton Analytics keeps no staffed office in Cañon City and publishes no
street address for this location. Meetings are by appointment, and the firm
says so plainly because candor about logistics is part of the credibility it
sells. In practice, most Fremont County engagements proceed remotely from
intake through report: records analysis, structured interviews, and
standardized testing under publisher protocols where remote administration
is supported. When an in-person evaluation is warranted, it is conducted in
the evaluee's own community, and when deposition or trial requires presence,
travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office with enough lead time
to protect counsel's calendar.

## How local labor market data is handled

An earning capacity opinion for a Fremont County evaluee has to survive
cross-examination about where that evaluee could actually work. Vocational
findings therefore rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational
employment and wage estimates for the relevant area and for Colorado
statewide, supplemented by state workforce agency labor market information
and by direct labor market research when the referral questions call for it.
Where the damages theory extends to future medical and attendant care,
[medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/) and life care
plans are priced to the evaluee's own market using provider-specific
inquiries and published fee data rather than national averages. When
economic loss calculations are needed alongside the vocational work, the
firm offers [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

the practice: with a documented conflict check, a defined scope, and a

parties, and flag any disclosure deadline, deposition setting, or trial date
that will shape the schedule. Counsel who want to see how the practice
operates beyond Colorado can review its
