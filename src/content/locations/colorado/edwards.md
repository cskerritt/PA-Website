Counsel handling injury, employment, and disability matters in the Edwards
micropolitan area retain Purinton Analytics for
[vocational expert witness](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) opinions on
employability and earning capacity, [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/)
in catastrophic injury cases, and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. The practice serves plaintiff and defense counsel
alike, and every opinion is written on the assumption that the other side of
the caption will read it closely.

## The Eagle County venue footprint

Civil matters from the Edwards area are heard in the District Court sitting
in Eagle County, and matters that proceed in federal court are venued in the
United States District Court for the District of Colorado. The firm accepts
engagements in either forum, as well as in arbitration and administrative
proceedings where vocational or care-planning evidence is at issue. Because
the report standard does not change with the venue, counsel can expect the
same documented methodology whether the matter stays in Eagle County or is
removed to federal court. Coverage for the rest of the state, including the
metro pages for Colorado's larger markets, is collected on the
[Colorado locations page](/locations/colorado/).

## Meetings by appointment in Edwards

There is no staffed Purinton Analytics office in Edwards, and the firm
publishes no street address for this location, because accuracy about
presence is part of the same discipline that governs its reports. Meetings
are scheduled by appointment. Most engagements begin remotely: records
review, structured vocational interviews, and standardized testing
administered under publisher protocols where remote administration is
supported. When the referral questions call for in-person evaluation, or
when deposition and trial calendars require presence, travel is scheduled
from the Kansas City home office and planned around the deadlines counsel
identifies at intake. The evaluee's own community, rather than the firm's,
is the default setting for any in-person evaluation.

## Labor market research for the Edwards area

Vocational findings for Eagle County evaluees rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the relevant area
and for Colorado statewide, on state workforce agency labor market
information, and on direct labor market research where the referral
questions warrant it. Transferable skills analysis and job search feasibility
are evaluated against the occupations actually available to the evaluee, not
against a generic national profile. Life care plans and
[medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/) are priced to
the evaluee's own market through provider-specific inquiries and published
fee data, so that the resulting figures reflect where the evaluee actually
lives and receives care.

## Starting an engagement

Whether the matter is a catastrophic injury case in the District Court in
Eagle County or a federal employment dispute, the engagement begins the same
way: a documented conflict check, a defined scope, and a records request
tailored to the claims. Submit the caption or party names through the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and note any imminent disclosure,
deposition, or trial dates so scheduling can be confirmed at the outset.
Counsel weighing a multi-venue litigation program can review the firm's
[nationwide coverage](/locations/nationwide/) for how the same engagement
structure operates across the country.
