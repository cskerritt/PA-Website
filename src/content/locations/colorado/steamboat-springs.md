The Steamboat Springs micropolitan area spans both Routt County and Moffat
County, and Purinton Analytics serves counsel across that full footprint
rather than treating the principal city as the edge of the map. Plaintiff
and defense attorneys in either county retain the firm for
[vocational expert witness](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) opinions
on employability and earning capacity, [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/)
in catastrophic injury matters, and
[forensic economic damages](/services/forensic-economic-damages/) support,
with every report written to the same standard regardless of which
courthouse the matter calls home.

## Venues across Routt and Moffat counties

Engagements are accepted for civil matters in the District Court sitting in
Routt County and the District Court sitting in Moffat County, and for
matters proceeding in the United States District Court for the District of
Colorado. Arbitration and administrative forums are covered on the same
terms. The firm's work elsewhere in the state, including the larger
markets it lists separately, is collected on the
[Colorado locations page](/locations/colorado/), and the methodology
described there is the methodology applied here.

## Meetings by appointment only

Purinton Analytics keeps no staffed office in Steamboat Springs and
publishes no street address for this location. Saying so plainly matters:
the firm's credibility rests on precision, and precision includes precision
about where it does and does not maintain a presence. Engagements for
Routt and Moffat county matters typically proceed remotely, through records
analysis, structured vocational interviews conducted by video, and
standardized testing administered under publisher protocols where remote
administration is supported. When the referral requires an in-person
evaluation, it is conducted in the evaluee's own community; when a
deposition or trial requires the expert in the courtroom, travel is
scheduled from the Kansas City home office against the dates counsel
provides at intake.

## Labor market evidence for the Steamboat Springs area

Employability conclusions are anchored to the labor market the evaluee
actually faces. For Steamboat Springs area evaluees, vocational findings
rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage
estimates for the relevant area and for Colorado statewide, on state
workforce agency labor market information, and on direct labor market
research where the referral questions warrant it. Life care plans and
[medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/) are priced to
the evaluee's own market using provider-specific inquiries and published fee
data. When counsel needs the economic loss calculation under the same roof,
the firm offers [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Starting with a conflict check

An engagement here begins the way it begins everywhere in the practice: a
documented conflict check, a defined scope, and a records request tailored
to the claims, so that the written opinion rests on a record both sides can
examine. To check availability for a Routt or Moffat county matter,
[request a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) with the case caption and
parties, and identify any disclosure, deposition, or trial dates already
set. Counsel handling matters beyond Colorado can review the firm's
[nationwide coverage](/locations/nationwide/) for the same engagement
structure applied across the country.
