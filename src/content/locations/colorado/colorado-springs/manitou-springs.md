Consider the practical problem first: an injured person in Manitou Springs needs to be interviewed, tested, and assessed by a qualified expert, and neither the evaluee's recovery nor the litigation budget is improved by unnecessary travel. Purinton Analytics designed its evaluation process around that problem. Interviews and standardized vocational testing are conducted remotely whenever remote administration preserves validity, and an [in-person vocational evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) or [life care planning assessment](/services/life-care-planning/) is brought to the evaluee's community when the assignment demands it. Both plaintiff and defense counsel retain the firm on these terms, under the same practice standards described for the [Colorado Springs metro area](/locations/colorado/colorado-springs/) as a whole.

## What a Manitou Springs evaluation involves

Every engagement opens with a documented conflict check and a defined scope, followed by a records request tailored to the claims. The evaluee interview covers work history, education, medical restrictions as documented in the record, and daily function; standardized testing is selected for the referral questions rather than administered by rote. For catastrophic injuries, the care-planning side of the practice adds [medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/) built from the treatment record and consulting clinical sources, so that the plan's line items can each be traced to a foundation.
