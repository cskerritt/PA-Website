Referrals from Fountain reach Purinton Analytics in more than one posture. Sometimes counsel needs the first expert in the case: a [vocational evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) or [life care plan](/services/life-care-planning/) built from a clean record before anyone else has opined. Just as often, an opposing report is already on file, and the immediate need is a [rebuttal or peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) that tests its methods and sources before the deposition does. The firm accepts both kinds of engagement, from plaintiff and defense counsel alike, and applies the same standards to each that govern its work across the [Colorado Springs metropolitan area](/locations/colorado/colorado-springs/).

## Where a Fountain case is heard

Fountain lies in El Paso County, and state-court civil matters arising here are generally filed in the District Court serving El Paso County. Federal claims proceed in the United States District Court for the District of Colorado, the state's federal forum, and the proper venue for any particular matter is confirmed during the conflict check and intake rather than assumed at the outset. That habit of confirming rather than presuming extends to admissibility groundwork: any venue-specific registration or qualification requirement is identified and resolved at the same intake stage, before opinions are formed.

## Earning capacity measured against the metro market

A Fountain evaluee's employment prospects are not assessed against the town alone; the realistic market is the wider metro area the evaluee can actually reach. Vocational findings therefore rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Colorado Springs metropolitan area and for Colorado statewide, supplemented by state workforce agency labor market information and by direct labor market research when the referral questions warrant it. The written opinion states what each source can support and stops there, because an earning-capacity figure stretched past its data is exactly what opposing counsel is trained to find. When lost earnings must be reduced to a damages calculation, the firm offers [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement structure.

## Meeting the evaluee where the case is

There is no staffed Purinton Analytics office in Fountain, and the firm declines to pretend otherwise; accuracy about presence is part of the same discipline that governs its reports. Most interviews and standardized testing are completed remotely with no loss of rigor. When the assignment calls for an in-person evaluation, it is scheduled in the evaluee's own community, and when depositions or trial dates require the expert in the courtroom, travel is arranged from the Kansas City home office and committed against the case calendar in writing.

## Life care planning alongside the vocational work

Catastrophic cases in Fountain often need both disciplines at once: a plan for future care and an opinion on residual earning capacity. Handling [medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/) and vocational analysis within a single coordinated engagement keeps the assumptions consistent between the two documents, which matters when both experts are deposed on the same facts.
