Most calls about a Monument case arrive with a date attached: an expert disclosure deadline, a discovery cutoff, a trial setting. Purinton Analytics builds its Monument engagements backward from those dates. Before accepting a referral, the firm maps the disclosure calendar against the time an honest [vocational evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) or [life care plan](/services/life-care-planning/) actually requires, and says so if the two cannot be reconciled. Counsel for plaintiffs and counsel for defendants get the same answer, because a rushed opinion is a liability to whichever side serves it. ## Venue for Monument matters

Monument sits in El Paso County. Civil actions under state law are generally brought in the District Court serving El Paso County, while claims arising under federal law belong in the United States District Court for the District of Colorado. Which forum controls, and any registration or qualification requirement particular to it, is settled during the conflict check and intake process; the firm does not draft toward an assumed venue and correct course later.

## Timeline of a defensible opinion

The sequence in a Monument engagement is fixed even when the schedule is tight. Conflict clearance comes first. A defined scope and a records request tailored to the claims come next, so the eventual report rests on a record both sides can examine. Evaluee interviews and standardized testing follow, then labor market analysis, then a written opinion that discloses its methods and sources. Compressing the calendar compresses none of these steps; it only compresses the waiting between them. When a case also needs an economic loss calculation, [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) bring in independent economist partners who perform and sign the economic analysis within the same engagement structure, keeping the vocational and economic timelines synchronized rather than sequential.

## Reading the labor market honestly

Monument evaluees are assessed against the employment realistically open to them, which for most residents means the wider metro market rather than the town itself. Findings draw on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Colorado Springs metropolitan area and for Colorado statewide, together with state workforce agency labor market information and direct labor market research where the referral questions justify the added step. Each source's limits are stated in the report, not discovered at deposition.

## Appointments, not an address

The firm maintains no office in Monument and publishes no local street address, a fact stated plainly because implied presence is a small dishonesty that undermines a large one: the credibility of the written work. Remote interview and testing protocols cover most assignments. Where in-person evaluation is warranted, it takes place in the evaluee's community; where testimony requires the expert at counsel table or on the stand, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office with dates confirmed against the disclosure calendar before retention.
