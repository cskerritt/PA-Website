## Venue mechanics, confirmed rather than assumed

State claims arising in Berthoud are generally heard in the District Court serving Larimer County. Where federal jurisdiction attaches, the United States District Court for the District of Colorado stands as the available federal forum; which court and division actually control a given dispute is a fact the firm confirms during the conflict check and intake, together with any venue-specific registration or qualification requirement. Treating venue as something to verify, not to presume, is a small habit with a large payoff: it keeps the expert file clean when the other side goes looking for shortcuts.

## Reading a small town against a metro market

Berthoud is a small community within the Fort Collins and Loveland metropolitan area, and honest vocational analysis reflects that scale. The relevant labor market for a Berthoud evaluee is rarely the town alone; it is the reachable employment across the metro, assessed qualitatively through Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the metropolitan area and for Colorado statewide, state workforce agency labor market information, and targeted labor market research where warranted. The opinion states what those sources show, notes what they cannot show, and stops there.
