Counsel retaining an expert for a Timnath case often want the mechanics answered before anything else: how does an evaluation of a client in a small Colorado town actually get done, on what timeline, and at what point does the expert need to be in the room? Purinton Analytics answers those questions in writing at intake, and the answers do not change with the letterhead. Whether the retaining lawyer represents the injured plaintiff or the defending party, a [vocational evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) or [life care plan](/services/life-care-planning/) for a Timnath evaluee proceeds mostly remotely, moves in person only when the assignment requires it, and is scheduled backward from the deadlines the court has already set.

## What the evaluation looks like in practice

Records come first: a request tailored to the claims, so the file is complete before any interview. The evaluee interview and most standardized vocational testing are then conducted remotely, which spares a Timnath client repeated trips and keeps costs proportionate to the questions asked. Where the referral requires hands-on assessment, the evaluation is carried out in the evaluee's own community. When testimony is needed, deposition and trial travel is arranged from the Kansas City home office and committed against the case calendar in advance. The firm has no Timnath office and publishes no local address, and it says so plainly, because honesty about logistics is inseparable from honesty in opinions.

## Larimer County venue, verified at intake

Timnath belongs to Larimer County, so state-court matters are generally heard in the District Court serving Larimer County. Federal claims have the United States District Court for the District of Colorado as their available forum; the controlling venue for any particular case, along with any registration or qualification requirement that forum imposes, is identified and confirmed during the conflict check and intake rather than guessed at. That verification habit protects both the expert's file and counsel's calendar, on whichever side of the caption the retention arises.

## A small suburb, measured against the whole metro

Timnath is a small suburb within the Fort Collins and Loveland metropolitan area, and its residents' employment prospects are properly measured against the metro as a whole. The firm's labor market findings rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the metropolitan area and for Colorado statewide, on state workforce agency labor market information, and on direct labor market research where the questions warrant it. The analysis is qualitative and sourced; it does not manufacture precision the data cannot carry, and it identifies the limits of each source so that cross-examination confirms the method instead of exposing it. An opinion built that way reads the same to plaintiff counsel and to defense counsel, which is the point.

## When damages need an economist

Vocational conclusions often feed an economic loss calculation, and the firm keeps those roles distinct. Through [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/), independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement structure. For future medical needs, a [medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/) ties every projected item to the treating record and a stated cost source.
