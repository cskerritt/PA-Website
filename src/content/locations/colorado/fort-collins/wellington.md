Defining the labor market is the first methodological decision in any vocational opinion, and in a Wellington case it is the decision most likely to be tested. The firm's position on that question is stated openly rather than buried in a footnote: a small town is not, for purposes of earning capacity analysis, its own labor market, and the defensible frame for a Wellington evaluee is the Fort Collins and Loveland metropolitan area considered as a whole. Purinton Analytics builds its [vocational assessments](/services/vocational-expert-witness/), [life care plans](/services/life-care-planning/), and [rebuttal reviews](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) for Wellington matters on that stated frame, and writes each one so it holds up whichever side of the caption ordered it.

## The market frame, measured honestly

For a Wellington evaluee, the practical labor market is the Fort Collins and Loveland metropolitan area, considered qualitatively and without exaggeration. The firm draws on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the metropolitan area and for Colorado statewide, supplements them with state workforce agency labor market information, and conducts direct labor market research when the referral questions justify the added step. Restrictions that limit driving or stamina can shrink the market a particular evaluee can actually reach, and an opinion that ignores that interaction is not worth defending; the reports here address it explicitly and say plainly where the data runs out.

## Courts serving Wellington matters

Wellington is part of Larimer County, and state-court claims arising here are generally litigated in the District Court serving Larimer County. On the federal side, the United States District Court for the District of Colorado is the available forum, with the controlling venue and any forum-specific qualification requirement pinned down during the conflict check and intake. Nothing about jurisdiction is left to assumption, because a misread forum can cost counsel a disclosure deadline.

## Appointments, not addresses

The firm maintains no Wellington office and lists no street address in the town; saying so directly is part of the practice's credibility. Engagements run by appointment. Interviews and vocational testing are usually completed remotely, and when an assignment calls for in-person evaluation it happens in the evaluee's own community rather than in a rented room chosen for appearances. Deposition and trial attendance is planned as travel from the Kansas City home office and is calendared against the litigation schedule before the retention letter is signed.
