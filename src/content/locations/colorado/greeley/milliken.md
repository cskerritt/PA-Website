What separates a vocational report that survives litigation from one that does not is rarely the conclusion; it is the record beneath it. For Milliken matters, Purinton Analytics builds that record deliberately: a documented conflict check, a scope defined in writing, records requests tailored to the claims, standardized instruments identified by name in the report, and sources a reader can pull and verify. That construction governs every [vocational evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/), [life care plan](/services/life-care-planning/), and [expert testimony engagement](/services/expert-testimony-litigation-consulting/) the firm accepts from Milliken counsel, plaintiff or defense.

## Anatomy of the file

A Milliken engagement file moves in a fixed order. Intake establishes conflicts, venue, and deadlines. The records review extracts the medical restrictions and work history that the analysis must honor. The evaluee interview and testing, ordinarily conducted remotely, supply the individualized evidence no database contains. The labor market analysis then situates that evidence in the regional economy, and the written report ties each conclusion to its foundations. Nothing in the sequence is improvised, which is why the file reads the same to the retaining attorney and to the one preparing the cross-examination.

## Venue facts for Milliken cases

Milliken is a Weld County town; its state-court matters generally proceed in the District Court serving Weld County. Claims within federal jurisdiction have the United States District Court for the District of Colorado as the available federal forum. The venue that controls a specific case is confirmed during the conflict check and intake, along with any venue-specific registration or qualification requirement, so the question is answered on the record before opinions are drafted.

## Labor market evidence, cited not assumed

The economic frame for a Milliken evaluee is the surrounding metropolitan market. The firm's analysis draws on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Greeley metropolitan area and for Colorado statewide, on state workforce agency labor market information, and on direct labor market research where the referral questions warrant it. Every source appears in the report, and the analysis stops where the data stops. When counsel needs the loss expressed in economic terms, [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) place that computation with independent economist partners, who perform and sign the economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement structure.
