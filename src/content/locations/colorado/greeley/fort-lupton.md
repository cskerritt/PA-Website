Ask which side Purinton Analytics works for in Fort Lupton and the accurate answer is: whichever side calls first and clears the conflict check. The practice is built so that the retaining party changes the client, never the opinion. A [vocational evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) of a Fort Lupton evaluee follows the same instruments, the same sources, and the same reporting discipline whether plaintiff's counsel commissioned it to establish a loss or defense counsel commissioned it to test one, and the same holds for the firm's [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) and [rebuttal work](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/).

## Why retention symmetry matters here

An expert whose conclusions drift with the engagement letter is a liability to everyone, including the lawyer holding the letter. The firm's Fort Lupton files are therefore assembled for the reader on the other side: methodology stated, instruments named, sources cited, and inferences kept inside the evidence. When the deposition comes, the testimony matches the report, because nothing in the report was written to please the client.

## Fort Lupton's courts

Fort Lupton lies in Weld County, and its state-court matters generally proceed in the District Court serving Weld County. For claims within federal jurisdiction, the United States District Court for the District of Colorado is the available federal forum. The firm does not assume which court controls a given dispute; the operative venue, and any venue-specific qualification or registration requirement attached to it, is confirmed during the conflict check and intake so that scoping decisions rest on verified facts.

## Measuring a Fort Lupton evaluee's market

Employment and earning capacity conclusions are grounded in the regional economy rather than the town boundary. The firm draws on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Greeley metropolitan area and for Colorado statewide, on state workforce agency labor market information, and on direct labor market research where the referral questions justify original inquiry. Each finding states its source, and the report identifies the limits of the data as readily as its implications. Where the engagement extends to economic loss, [coordinated damages assessments](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) bring independent economist partners who perform and sign the economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement structure.

## By-appointment service, stated plainly

The firm has no Fort Lupton office and no published street address here, and it treats that disclosure as part of its credibility rather than a marketing inconvenience. Evaluees are generally interviewed and tested remotely; in-person evaluation, where warranted, takes place in the evaluee's own community. Travel for assessments, depositions, and trial appearances is arranged from the Kansas City home office, on the same footing as the firm's engagements throughout the [Greeley metropolitan area](/locations/colorado/greeley/).
