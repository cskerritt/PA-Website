## Sizing the market honestly

Severance is a small community within a larger metropolitan economy, and the vocational question is how much of that economy remains open to this particular evaluee. Findings rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Greeley metropolitan area and for Colorado statewide, on state workforce agency labor market information, and on direct labor market research when the referral questions call for verification beyond published data. The report distinguishes carefully between what the regional statistics establish and what they cannot, because the gap between the data and the conclusion is where opposing counsel lives.
