Vocational findings for an Erie evaluee rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Greeley metropolitan area and for Colorado statewide, on state workforce agency labor market information, and on direct labor market research where the referral questions warrant the additional inquiry.
