## Where a Windsor case is heard

Windsor lies in Weld County, and state-court injury, employment, and domestic matters arising there are generally filed in the District Court serving Weld County. When a claim belongs in federal court, the United States District Court for the District of Colorado is the available federal forum, and the venue that actually controls a given case is confirmed during the conflict check and intake rather than assumed from a mailing address. Any venue-specific registration or qualification requirement is identified at that same stage, so counsel knows before retention whether anything about the forum affects the expert's participation.

## Reading Windsor through the metro labor market

A Windsor evaluee does not work in a town-sized labor market; the realistic unit of analysis is the surrounding metropolitan economy. Vocational findings therefore rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Greeley metropolitan area and for Colorado statewide, supplemented by state workforce agency labor market information and by direct employer research where the referral questions warrant it. The written report identifies each source and stays inside what the data supports, because an opinion that reaches past its evidence is precisely what opposing counsel is paid to find. When lost earnings must be quantified, counsel may add a [coordinated damages engagement](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement structure.
