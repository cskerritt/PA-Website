Every defensible vocational opinion declares its data geography, and for an Evans case that geography is the Greeley, CO metropolitan area, the statistical region that takes its name from its principal city. Evans belongs to that metro, so the published labor market estimates covering the metro are the ones an Evans analysis must use and must be prepared to defend. Purinton Analytics builds its Evans work, from [vocational evaluations](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) to [life care plans](/services/life-care-planning/) to [medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/), on data whose coverage is named outright in the report, and it performs that work for plaintiff and defense counsel on identical terms.

## The forums for an Evans dispute

Evans matters filed in state court are Weld County cases, generally proceeding in the District Court serving Weld County. Where a claim lies within federal jurisdiction, the United States District Court for the District of Colorado is the available federal forum. The firm confirms the controlling venue during the conflict check and intake, and it identifies any venue-specific qualification or registration requirement at the same stage, so counsel learns of any forum-driven constraint before retention rather than after disclosure deadlines are set.

## Data with a named coverage area

Vocational findings for an Evans evaluee draw on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Greeley metropolitan area and for Colorado statewide, on state workforce agency labor market information, and on direct labor market research where the referral questions justify it. Naming the coverage area is not a formality. An opinion that cannot say which market its estimates describe invites the cross-examination it deserves, so each Evans report identifies its sources, states what they establish, and concedes what they cannot. Where the engagement extends to economic loss, [coordinated damages assessments](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) bring independent economist partners who perform and sign the economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement structure.

## Appointments in Evans, candidly framed

The firm maintains no staffed office in Evans and publishes no street address for this location, and it prefers to say so directly, because accuracy about presence and accuracy about earnings are the same habit. Evaluee interviews and standardized testing are ordinarily conducted remotely. When an in-person assessment is appropriate, it is carried out in the evaluee's own community, and travel for evaluations, depositions, and trial testimony is coordinated from the Kansas City home office, alongside the firm's broader practice across the [Greeley metropolitan area](/locations/colorado/greeley/).

## Written for the unpersuaded reader

Each Evans opinion is drafted on the assumption that its most attentive reader will be the lawyer who did not commission it. That assumption disciplines source selection, the treatment of restrictions and transferable skills, and the wording of every conclusion, and it keeps the deposition testimony inside the corners of the written report. It also explains why the practice is retained on either side of the caption, including for [rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) of opposing experts' reports.
