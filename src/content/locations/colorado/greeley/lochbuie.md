Counsel calling about a Lochbuie matter tend to bring the same core questions. Can the evaluee return to work, and to what work? What earning capacity remains after the injury? What will future care require? Purinton Analytics is retained to answer those questions in writing, through [vocational evaluations](/services/vocational-expert-witness/), [life care plans](/services/life-care-planning/), and [reviews of opposing experts' opinions](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/), and it answers them the same way whichever side of the caption is asking, because an answer that shifts with the client is not evidence.

## The return-to-work question

Whether a Lochbuie evaluee can work, and at what, is answered from medically supported restrictions documented in the record, transferable skills established by the work history, and the regional labor market described by Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Greeley metropolitan area and for Colorado statewide, supplemented by state workforce agency labor market information and by direct labor market research where the referral questions warrant it. Each conclusion cites its evidence and stops where the evidence stops, because the gap between data and opinion is exactly where cross-examination begins.

## The earnings and care questions

When the case requires vocational findings carried into economic loss, the firm structures [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement structure. Questions about future care are answered through life care planning that ties every recommendation to its clinical foundation and every projected cost to an identified source, so the plan reads the same to the retaining attorney and to the lawyer preparing to challenge it.
