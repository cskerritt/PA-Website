Purinton Analytics is regularly retained at exactly that point: to [review and rebut an opposing expert's vocational or life care opinions](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/), testing the methodology, the sourcing, and the distance between the evidence and the conclusions. The same standard the firm applies to other experts' work governs its own affirmative engagements in Firestone, from [vocational evaluations](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) to [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), which is why both plaintiff and defense counsel bring it their cases.

## What a rebuttal engagement examines

A peer review of a Firestone report asks disciplined questions. Were the labor market sources appropriate for this evaluee and identified transparently? Do the stated restrictions come from the medical record or from assumption? Does the earning capacity conclusion follow from the data, or merely sit near it? Are care recommendations tied to clinical foundation and priced from defensible sources? The resulting critique is written with the same restraint as an affirmative report, because an overreaching rebuttal fails counsel just as surely as an overreaching original.

## Grounding opinions in the regional data

Affirmative vocational findings for a Firestone evaluee rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Greeley metropolitan area and for Colorado statewide, on state workforce agency labor market information, and on direct labor market research where the referral questions warrant the added step. The metropolitan frame matters: a Firestone worker's realistic options span the regional commuting economy, and an analysis confined to the town itself would misstate the market in either direction.
