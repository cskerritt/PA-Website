How does an injured Johnstown resident actually get evaluated by a national expert practice? In most engagements, without leaving home. Purinton Analytics conducts the majority of its evaluee interviews and standardized testing remotely, a format that works as well for a Johnstown claimant as for one in a major city, and it reserves in-person assessment for the cases that genuinely need it, conducted in the evaluee's own community. That logistics model, remote by default and local when warranted, is how the firm delivers [vocational evaluations](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) and [life care plans](/services/life-care-planning/) throughout the [Greeley metropolitan area](/locations/colorado/greeley/) for plaintiff and defense counsel alike.

## Getting the evaluation done

A typical Johnstown engagement moves from records to interview to testing to report on a schedule fixed at intake. Remote sessions eliminate travel burdens on evaluees managing medical care, and they compress timelines when a disclosure deadline is near. When the referral questions require observation that a screen cannot support, or when deposition and trial dates require presence, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office and committed against the case calendar before the work begins. The firm keeps no office in Johnstown and publishes no street address here; it would rather say so plainly than imply a storefront it does not have.

## Weld County and the federal forum

Johnstown matters filed in state court are Weld County cases, generally proceeding in the District Court serving Weld County. For claims within federal jurisdiction, the United States District Court for the District of Colorado is the available federal forum. Which court controls a given dispute, and whether any venue-specific registration or qualification requirement applies, is identified and confirmed during the conflict check and intake, so the answer arrives before retention rather than after.

## The market a Johnstown evaluee competes in

Employment prospects for a Johnstown evaluee are measured against the regional economy, not the town limits. The firm's findings draw on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Greeley metropolitan area and for Colorado statewide, on state workforce agency labor market information, and on direct labor market research where the questions presented justify it. Conclusions about employability, transferable skills, and earning capacity are stated only as far as the documented evidence carries them. Where the case needs the loss reduced to numbers, [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) assign that calculation to independent economist partners, who perform and sign the economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement structure.

## Written for the reader who was not in the room

Because remote work must survive the same scrutiny as any other, every Johnstown file documents its methods, instruments, and sources in enough detail that a reader who never attended the interview, most pointedly opposing counsel, can trace each conclusion to its foundation. The firm also applies that same lens to other experts' reports through [rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/).
