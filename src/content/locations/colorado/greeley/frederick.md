When a catastrophic injury reaches litigation, the future cost of care often becomes the largest and most contested element of damages, and a Frederick family's case is no exception. Purinton Analytics prepares [life care plans](/services/life-care-planning/) for Frederick matters that itemize future medical and support needs from clinical foundation, source each cost from defensible data, and hold up whether the plan is offered by the plaintiff or examined by the defense. Alongside that care-planning work, the firm provides [vocational evaluations](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) and [medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/), and it accepts retention from either side of the caption.

## Care needs, documented item by item

A Frederick life care plan starts with the medical record and, where appropriate, consultation with treating providers, then maps the evaluee's projected needs across categories of care, equipment, and support. Every recommendation carries its clinical basis, and every cost carries its source, because a plan assembled any other way invites the cross-examination it cannot survive. Where lost earning capacity accompanies the care claim, the vocational analysis proceeds in parallel, and [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) allow independent economist partners to perform and sign the economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning work continues under one engagement structure.

## The courts that will read the plan

Frederick sits in Weld County, so state-court matters arising there generally proceed in the District Court serving Weld County. Claims within federal jurisdiction have the United States District Court for the District of Colorado as the available federal forum. The firm confirms which court controls a particular case during the conflict check and intake, and it resolves any venue-specific qualification or registration requirement at that same stage rather than discovering it midstream.

## Earning capacity in a metro-wide frame

Vocational conclusions for a Frederick evaluee are anchored in the regional economy the evaluee can realistically reach. The firm relies on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Greeley metropolitan area and for Colorado statewide, on state workforce agency labor market information, and on direct labor market research when the referral questions require it. Restrictions come from the medical evidence, transferable skills from the documented work history, and the report declines to state more than those sources support.
