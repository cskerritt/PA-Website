## The courts that hear New Castle matters

New Castle is a Garfield County town, and civil disputes involving New Castle parties commonly proceed in the District Court serving Garfield County. Cases resting on federal claims or diverse parties may instead be heard in the United States District Court for the District of Colorado; the forum that governs a particular case, along with its disclosure schedule, is confirmed during the conflict check and intake. That schedule then becomes part of the engagement plan, because an opinion that arrives late is no opinion at all.

## A small town, a wider job market

New Castle is a small community, and a credible earning capacity analysis acknowledges that its residents work across a wider area than the town itself. Vocational findings rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Rifle area and for Colorado statewide, alongside state workforce agency labor market information and direct labor market research when the referral questions require it. The transferable skills analysis weighs what the evaluee can still do against the occupations genuinely open within a sustainable commute, and the report shows that reasoning instead of asserting its conclusion. When lost earnings must be brought to present value, the firm offers [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement structure.
