## Reading the labor market from Glenwood Springs

Vocational findings for a Glenwood Springs evaluee rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Rifle area and for Colorado statewide, supplemented by state workforce agency labor market information and by direct labor market research when the referral questions call for it. The transferable skills analysis defines the relevant labor market by what the evaluee can realistically reach rather than by the town line, and it says plainly which market each conclusion is drawn from. Where lost earnings must be reduced to present value, the firm offers [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement structure.

## How a Glenwood Springs evaluation proceeds

The firm keeps no office in Glenwood Springs and publishes no local address; candor about presence is part of the same discipline that governs the reports. Most evaluations are completed remotely, through structured interview and standardized testing scheduled around the evaluee's treatment and work obligations. When an in-person evaluation is warranted, it is arranged in a suitable professional setting in the evaluee's own community, with travel scheduled from the Kansas City home office. Deposition and trial appearances are planned the same way, against the court's calendar, so counsel is never left explaining an expert's availability to a judge.
