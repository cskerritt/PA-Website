## Venue for Snowmass Village matters

Snowmass Village lies in Pitkin County, and civil litigation involving Snowmass Village parties commonly proceeds in the District Court serving Pitkin County. ## The labor market beyond a small town

Snowmass Village is a small community, and its residents' realistic employment options extend well past its boundaries. Vocational findings for a Snowmass Village evaluee rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Rifle area and for Colorado statewide, together with state workforce agency labor market information and direct labor market research when the referral questions justify it. The transferable skills analysis names the occupations the evaluee's history and residual capacity support, then verifies where such work exists within a commute the evaluee can sustain, so the earning capacity figure handed to the economists reflects a market that actually exists.
