Every vocational report Purinton Analytics prepares for an Aspen matter is written on a single assumption: the most careful reader will be the lawyer on the other side. That assumption disciplines everything, from the scope memo to the final signature. Claims are stated only as strongly as the record supports, methods are disclosed rather than gestured at, and the file behind the opinion is organized so it can be produced without embarrassment. The firm brings that standard to [vocational expert](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) and [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) engagements for injury, employment, and disability matters involving Aspen parties, representing no side permanently and either side capably, as part of its [Rifle area practice](/locations/colorado/rifle/).

## Pitkin County venue and the federal alternative

Aspen sits in Pitkin County, and civil actions involving Aspen parties are commonly heard in the District Court serving Pitkin County. When federal jurisdiction attaches, the United States District Court for the District of Colorado stands as the available federal forum; the venue that actually controls is pinned down during the conflict check and intake, because guessing at jurisdiction is not a habit an expert should bring into a case. Whichever court hears the matter, the engagement follows the same sequence: documented conflict check, defined scope, tailored records request, written opinion.

## Earning capacity, link by link

An earning capacity opinion is only as reliable as the weakest step in its reasoning, so the analysis follows the evidence in the record rather than forcing it into a template. Vocational findings for an Aspen evaluee rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Rifle area and for Colorado statewide, on state workforce agency labor market information, and on direct labor market research where the referral questions warrant that added step. The report separates what the evaluee earned, what the evaluee can still do, and what the accessible labor market will pay for it, so each link in the chain can be examined on its own. Where present-value calculations are needed, the firm offers [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement structure.

## Logistics for an Aspen evaluee

The firm maintains no Aspen office and lists no local address, a matter of accuracy rather than modesty. Most evaluations are conducted remotely, through structured interview and standardized testing arranged around the evaluee's treatment and working schedule. When the questions in the referral require an in-person evaluation, it takes place in a professional setting in the evaluee's own community, with travel from the Kansas City home office planned well ahead of any disclosure deadline. Depositions and trial appearances are calendared the same way, driven by the court's schedule.

## Requesting a conflict check

An engagement opens with a conflict check rather than a fee agreement. Counsel should [request a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) with the caption and party names and identify any approaching disclosure, deposition, or trial dates. The firm accepts assignments from initial evaluation through [rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) of opposing experts' work, for plaintiff and defense alike.
