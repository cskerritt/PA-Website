## Where Carbondale disputes are heard

Carbondale lies in Garfield County, and civil matters involving Carbondale parties commonly proceed in the District Court serving Garfield County. Federal-question and diversity cases may instead belong in the United States District Court for the District of Colorado; which forum controls a given dispute is confirmed during the conflict check and intake, never presumed from geography. The opinion itself is venue-neutral in its construction: documented conflict check, defined scope, a records request tailored to the claims, and a written report whose every statement can be traced to the record by whichever lawyer is reading it.

## A labor market measured, not assumed

Vocational findings for Carbondale evaluees are grounded in Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Rifle area and for Colorado statewide, along with state workforce agency labor market information and, where the referral questions justify it, direct labor market research conducted for the specific case. The transferable skills analysis identifies the occupations the evaluee's history supports, then tests whether those occupations exist in meaningful numbers within a commute the evaluee can realistically sustain, rather than assuming the answer either way. When the damages theory requires that lost earnings be reduced to present value, the firm offers [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement structure.

There is no Purinton Analytics office in Carbondale, and the firm will not pretend otherwise with a rented address.
