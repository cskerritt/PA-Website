## The La Plata County venue

The Durango micropolitan area consists of La Plata County. The firm accepts
matters venued in the District Court in La Plata County and in the United
States District Court for the District of Colorado, together with
arbitrations and administrative claims arising locally. Because the same
methodology serves whichever party retains the firm, its written opinions
are drafted for the reader across the aisle: every conclusion traces to
records, testing, or published data that can be verified. Counsel with
matters elsewhere in the state can begin with the
[Colorado state page](/locations/colorado/), which describes the statewide
practice.

## Travel planned around the court's calendar

Durango meetings are by appointment. The firm maintains no staffed office
in Durango and publishes no street address for this location, a fact stated
plainly because accuracy about presence is part of the same discipline that
governs its reports. The working assumption is remote-first: records
review, structured interviews, and standardized testing under publisher
protocols that support remote administration. What changes for a Durango
matter is planning. When in-person evaluation is warranted, or when
deposition and trial dates require the expert in the courtroom, travel is
scheduled from the Kansas City home office with generous lead time, so the
litigation calendar, not travel logistics, drives the schedule. Counsel are
told early what dates the firm can hold.

## Local labor market evidence for Durango evaluees

Employability and earning capacity opinions for La Plata County evaluees
rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage
estimates for the relevant area and for Colorado statewide, state workforce
agency labor market information, and direct labor market research where the
referral questions warrant it. The analysis is anchored to the evaluee's
own commuting area: what occupations exist there, what they pay, and
whether the evaluee's residual capacities and transferable skills provide
realistic access to them. Life care plans and medical cost projections for
local residents are priced through provider-specific inquiries and
published fee data for the region, so that projected care costs reflect the
market where the care will actually be obtained.
