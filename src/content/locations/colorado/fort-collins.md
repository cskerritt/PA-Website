A referral from Fort Collins usually arrives with a deadline attached: a
disclosure date, a scheduled deposition, or a mediation that needs a damages
figure both sides can test. Purinton Analytics is built for that cadence,
accepting vocational and life care planning engagements from plaintiff and
defense counsel across the Fort Collins-Loveland area. The work most often
requested includes
[vocational expert evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) of
earning capacity and employability,
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) for catastrophic
injuries, [medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/) where
the future care question is narrower, and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under a single
engagement structure.

## The Larimer County venue

The Fort Collins-Loveland metropolitan area consists of Larimer County, and
the firm accepts matters venued in the District Court there as well as in
the United States District Court for the District of Colorado. Because the
practice is national, a Larimer County case receives the same intake
sequence used everywhere else: a documented conflict check, a defined scope,
and a records request tailored to the claims, so the written opinion rests
on a record either side of the caption can examine. Counsel handling matters
elsewhere in the state will find the broader picture on the
[Colorado state page](/locations/colorado/).

## Appointment-based logistics, stated plainly

The firm maintains no staffed office in Fort Collins or Loveland and
publishes no street address for this location; candor about presence is
part of the same discipline that shapes the reports. Meetings are by
appointment. Most engagements proceed remotely from records review through
structured interview and standardized testing, using publisher protocols
that support remote administration. Where the referral questions require an
in-person evaluation, or where testimony must be given live, travel is
scheduled from the Kansas City home office and coordinated with counsel
well ahead of the dates that matter. Retaining counsel work with a single
point of contact from intake through testimony.

## Grounding opinions in the local labor market

Vocational conclusions for Larimer County evaluees are grounded in Bureau of
Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Fort
Collins-Loveland metropolitan area and for Colorado statewide, together with
state workforce agency labor market information and direct employer contact
when the questions call for it. The analysis asks what work the evaluee can
actually obtain in the local commuting area given the residual capacities
the medical record supports, not what an abstract national worker might
earn. Care-related costs in life care plans and medical cost projections are
priced to the evaluee's own market through provider-specific inquiries and
published fee data for the region.

## How to begin

The most useful early step is also the simplest:
[request a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) with the case caption and party
names, and flag any disclosure, deposition, or trial dates already on the
calendar. Once conflicts clear, the firm can scope the engagement, estimate
the records needed, and set a realistic delivery schedule. For matters
outside Colorado, the firm's [national coverage](/locations/nationwide/)
page explains how the same report standard travels to any venue in the
country.
