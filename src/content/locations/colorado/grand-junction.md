Distance is a solvable problem in expert work, and the Grand Junction bar
should not have to choose between a nearby generalist and a disciplined
specialist who happens to sit elsewhere. Purinton Analytics runs a
remote-first national practice precisely so that Mesa County counsel, on
the plaintiff side or the defense side, can retain the same expertise
available in any major litigation market. Grand Junction engagements most
often involve
[vocational expert evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) of
earning capacity and employability,
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) for catastrophic
injuries, [rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) of
opposing experts' opinions, and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under a
single engagement structure.

## Serving the Mesa County venue

The Grand Junction metropolitan area consists of Mesa County. The firm
accepts matters venued in the District Court in Mesa County and in the
United States District Court for the District of Colorado, along with
arbitrations and administrative claims arising from the area. Every file
opens the same way regardless of venue: a documented conflict check, a
defined scope, and a records request tailored to the claims, so that the
resulting opinion rests on a record both sides of the caption can examine.
The [Colorado state page](/locations/colorado/) shows how the practice
covers the rest of the state under the same structure.

## How remote-first actually works

There is no staffed Purinton Analytics office in Grand Junction and no
published street address for this location; the firm states that plainly
because candor about presence is part of its credibility. Records review,
structured interviews, and standardized testing proceed remotely under
publisher protocols that support remote administration, which means most
engagements never require anyone to travel at all. When the referral
questions call for an in-person evaluation, or when deposition and trial
dates require the expert's presence in Mesa County, travel is scheduled
from the Kansas City home office with lead time agreed in advance. Counsel
receive the same report standard and the same point of contact either way.

## Labor market research on the local economy

Vocational conclusions for Grand Junction evaluees are grounded in Bureau
of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the
Grand Junction metropolitan area and for Colorado statewide, supplemented
by state workforce agency labor market information and direct labor market
research where the questions warrant it. The analysis stays local: the
occupations, wages, and hiring patterns that matter are the ones in the
evaluee's own commuting area. Life care plans and medical cost projections
for Mesa County residents are priced to the regional market through
provider-specific inquiries and published fee data, since care costs where
the evaluee lives, not national averages, determine what the plan will
actually cost to fund.

To explore availability for a Mesa County matter,
[request a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) with the case caption and party
names, and note any imminent disclosure, deposition, or trial dates. Once
conflicts clear, scope and schedule are confirmed in writing. Counsel
handling cases beyond Colorado will find the firm's
[national coverage](/locations/nationwide/) page useful for understanding
how the same practice serves any venue in the country.
