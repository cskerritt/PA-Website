Every opinion Purinton Analytics writes for a Boulder County matter is
drafted on the assumption that opposing counsel will read it closely, and
that a careful cross-examiner will test every step from records request to
conclusion. That publication standard, not any local address, is what the
firm offers Boulder litigators on both sides of the caption. Engagements
from the Boulder area typically involve
[vocational expert evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) of
earning capacity and employability,
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) in catastrophic injury
cases, [expert testimony and litigation consulting](/services/expert-testimony-litigation-consulting/)
at deposition and trial, and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under a single
engagement structure.

## The venue footprint

The Boulder metropolitan area consists of Boulder County. The firm accepts
matters venued in the District Court in Boulder County and in the United
States District Court for the District of Colorado, along with claims
resolved through arbitration or administrative proceedings. Each engagement
begins the way it does everywhere else in the practice: with a documented
conflict check, a defined scope, and a records request tailored to the
claims, so that the written opinion rests on a record both sides can
examine. The [Colorado state page](/locations/colorado/) describes how the
same practice covers the rest of the state's trial and federal courts.

## Presence, honestly described

Boulder meetings are by appointment. There is no staffed Purinton Analytics
office in Boulder and no published street address for this location,
because accuracy about presence belongs to the same discipline that governs
the firm's reports. In practice, most of the work does not require an
office at all: records review, structured interviews, and standardized
testing under publisher protocols that support remote administration all
proceed from wherever the evaluee is. When the referral questions require
an in-person evaluation, or when a deposition or trial date requires the
expert in the room, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office
and confirmed with counsel well before the date at issue.

## Labor market evidence for Boulder County evaluees

Vocational conclusions are grounded in Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates for the Boulder metropolitan
area and for Colorado statewide, supplemented by state workforce agency
labor market information and direct labor market research where warranted.
The question is always concrete: what occupations can this evaluee perform
and obtain in this commuting area, at what earnings, given the capacities
the medical record supports. Life care plans and medical cost projections
for Boulder County residents are priced to the evaluee's own market through
provider-specific inquiries and published fee data, so the cost figures
reflect where the care will actually be purchased.

## Beginning the engagement

Retention is most valuable early, before disclosure deadlines limit what a
thorough evaluation can include. To begin a Boulder County engagement,
[request a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) with the case caption and party
names, and identify any disclosure, deposition, or trial dates already
scheduled. Once conflicts clear, the firm defines scope and delivery dates
in writing. Counsel whose dockets extend beyond Colorado can consult the
firm's [national coverage](/locations/nationwide/) page for the same
services under the same standard in any United States venue.
