The District Court in El Paso County and its counterpart in Teller County
hear the injury, employment, and family matters that most often call for
vocational evidence, and Purinton Analytics serves plaintiff and defense
counsel alike in that work. Colorado Springs engagements typically involve
[earning capacity and employability evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/)
in personal injury and employment cases,
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) in catastrophic injury
matters, and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under a single
engagement structure.

## Courts and counties served

The Colorado Springs metropolitan area, as defined for statistical purposes,
comprises El Paso County and Teller County. The firm accepts matters venued
in the District Court in either county and in the United States District
Court for the District of Colorado, which hears the federal cases that arise
from the region. Counsel elsewhere in the state can begin with the
[Colorado state page](/locations/colorado/), which describes the same
practice statewide; a change of venue changes the labor market data and the
logistics, never the methodology. Reports are written to the same standard
whether the matter will be tried, arbitrated, or resolved at mediation, and
whether the retaining party sits at the plaintiff table or the defense
table.

## By appointment, without a local storefront

Meetings in Colorado Springs are by appointment. The firm keeps no staffed
office in the city and publishes no street address for this location,
because accuracy about presence is part of the same discipline that governs
its written opinions. Most engagements begin remotely: a documented conflict
check, a defined scope, a records request tailored to the claims, and then
structured interviews and standardized testing under publisher protocols
where remote administration is supported. When the referral questions call
for in-person evaluation, or when deposition and trial dates require
presence, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office and planned
around the court's calendar rather than the firm's convenience.

## Labor market research for Colorado Springs evaluees

Vocational findings for evaluees in El Paso County and Teller County rest on
Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for
the Colorado Springs metropolitan area and for Colorado statewide,
supplemented by state workforce agency labor market information and direct
labor market research where the referral questions warrant it. Employability
and placeability opinions are grounded in the occupations actually available
in the evaluee's own commuting area rather than in national averages. Life
care plans and medical cost projections for local residents are priced to
the regional market using provider-specific inquiries and published fee
data, so that the resulting figures can withstand examination by either
side.

## Starting a Colorado Springs engagement

Early retention protects the record: it allows the vocational interview,
testing, and labor market research to be completed before disclosure
deadlines compress the schedule, and it gives opposing counsel no timing
argument to make. Submit the caption or party names through the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and note any imminent disclosure,
deposition, or trial dates. Counsel with matters beyond Colorado can review
the firm's [national coverage](/locations/nationwide/) for the same services
delivered under the same report standard in any United States venue.
