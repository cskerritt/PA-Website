Earning capacity is a local question, and answering it for a Greeley evaluee
means starting from the Greeley labor market rather than a national
composite. That premise shapes every vocational and life care planning
engagement Purinton Analytics accepts from Weld County, for plaintiff and
defense counsel alike. The firm's Greeley work centers on
[earning capacity and employability evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/)
in injury and employment litigation,
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) in catastrophic injury
matters, [rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) of
opposing vocational and care-planning opinions, and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under a single
engagement structure.

## Where Greeley matters are heard

The Greeley metropolitan area consists of Weld County. Engagements are
accepted for matters venued in the District Court in Weld County and in the
United States District Court for the District of Colorado, and for claims
proceeding in arbitration or administrative forums under the same report
standard. Whether the retaining party is prosecuting or defending the claim
does not change the analysis; the methodology is designed so that the same
opinion would issue regardless of which side asked the question. Counsel
with matters spread across the state can orient themselves through the
[Colorado state page](/locations/colorado/).

## Meetings by appointment only

The firm keeps no staffed office in Greeley and publishes no street address
for this location; stating that plainly is part of the same discipline that
governs the reports themselves. Most engagements are performed remotely,
beginning with a documented conflict check, a defined scope, and a records
request tailored to the claims. Structured interviews and standardized
testing proceed under publisher protocols where remote administration is
supported. When an in-person evaluation is warranted, or when deposition
and trial dates require presence in Weld County, travel is scheduled from
the Kansas City home office with enough lead time to protect the litigation
calendar.

## Reading the Weld County labor market

Vocational findings for Greeley evaluees rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates for the Greeley metropolitan
area and for Colorado statewide, state workforce agency labor market
information, and direct labor market research, including employer contact,
where the referral questions warrant it. Transferable skills analysis
identifies the occupations the evaluee's work history and residual
capacities support, and the local data then shows whether those occupations
exist in the commuting area in meaningful supply. Life care plans and
medical cost projections for Weld County residents are priced to the
evaluee's own market using provider-specific inquiries and published fee
data for the region, not national averages applied by assumption.

## Opening a Weld County engagement

Counsel who retain early get the full benefit of the process: time to
gather complete records, time for a properly administered evaluation, and
time to resolve any gaps before disclosure deadlines arrive. To begin,
[request a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) with the case caption and
parties, and note any dates already set. Matters outside Colorado are
handled through the same national practice described on the firm's
[nationwide coverage](/locations/nationwide/) page, with the identical
methodology and report standard wherever the case is venued.
