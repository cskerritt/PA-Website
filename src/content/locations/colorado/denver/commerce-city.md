An earning capacity dispute usually turns on a deceptively short question: what is this particular worker's history worth now? For a Commerce City evaluee, answering it means taking the work record seriously, testing what actually transfers after an injury or job loss, and pricing the remainder against real published data rather than impressions. Purinton Analytics performs that analysis for plaintiff and defense counsel in Commerce City matters, pairing [vocational expert opinions](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) with [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) where the injuries demand it, as part of the firm's larger [Denver metro practice](/locations/colorado/denver/).

## Adams County and the federal forum

Commerce City lies in Adams County, and its civil disputes are commonly litigated in the District Court serving Adams County. Claims that arise under federal law or otherwise support federal jurisdiction may proceed in the United States District Court for the District of Colorado; the firm treats the choice of forum as an intake question, confirmed during the conflict check with the caption in front of it, never a website assumption. Either way, the written product is prepared as if the most skeptical reader in the case will study it, because that reader always does.

## Transferable skills against a metro market

The transferable skills analysis for a Commerce City worker is run against the regional market where residents actually compete for jobs, using Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Denver metropolitan area and for Colorado statewide, along with state workforce agency labor market information and direct employer research when the referral questions call for that depth. Where diminished capacity must become a damages figure, the firm structures [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement structure.
