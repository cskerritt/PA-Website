## The Adams County courthouse and the federal alternative

Thornton sits in Adams County, and state-court matters involving its residents and employers are commonly filed in the District Court serving Adams County. Colorado's federal forum, the United States District Court for the District of Colorado, is available where subject-matter jurisdiction supports it, and the question of which courthouse controls a given caption is resolved during the conflict check and intake rather than guessed at in advance. Whichever forum applies, the expert file is assembled the same way: a documented scope, sourced data, and an opinion that does not outrun the record.

## Where Thornton fits in the metro economy

Thornton is a large suburb whose working residents participate in the labor market of the whole region, so an earning capacity analysis anchored only to jobs inside the city limits would understate the evidence. Findings instead rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Denver metropolitan area and for Colorado statewide, augmented by state workforce agency labor market information and, where the referral warrants, direct labor market research. Wage evidence is treated qualitatively in the narrative and traceably in the appendices, so that opposing counsel can audit every input against its published source.

## Seeing the evaluee, remotely or in person

No Purinton Analytics office exists in Thornton, and the firm declines to imply otherwise, because a practice that testifies about facts should be factual about itself. Records review, structured interviews, and standardized testing are conducted remotely wherever publisher protocols allow, an approach that takes travel off the critical path of the engagement. If the assignment requires an in-person evaluation, it takes place in the evaluee's community at an appropriate professional setting, with travel scheduled from the Kansas City home office. Deposition and trial appearances are arranged the same way, keyed to the court's schedule.
