A city of Aurora's size generates employment across nearly every occupational category, yet its residents also work throughout the surrounding region, so vocational findings rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Denver metropolitan area and for Colorado statewide, supplemented by state workforce agency labor market information and direct employer research where the referral questions call for it.
