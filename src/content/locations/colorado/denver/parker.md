## Douglas County venue for Parker matters

Parker is a Douglas County town, and civil actions involving Parker parties are commonly heard in the District Court serving Douglas County. Where federal jurisdiction exists, the United States District Court for the District of Colorado is the available forum; the firm resolves the controlling-venue question during the conflict check and intake, once the caption, claims, and parties are actually known. A life care plan or vocational report headed for either courthouse is prepared to survive the scrutiny of whichever bench and jury will read it, and prepared to agree with its companion report on every fact the two share.

## Work, wages, and care costs in context

When earning capacity is contested for a Parker evaluee, the analysis draws on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Denver metropolitan area and for Colorado statewide, along with state workforce agency labor market information, because Parker residents work throughout the region rather than within town boundaries alone. Care costs are handled with parallel discipline: provider-level verification in the evaluee's market rather than borrowed national figures. Where the case requires the full damages chain, the firm arranges [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement structure.
