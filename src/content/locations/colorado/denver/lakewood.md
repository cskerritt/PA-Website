## Jefferson County venue for Lakewood matters

Lakewood is a Jefferson County city, and civil litigation involving Lakewood parties commonly proceeds in the District Court serving Jefferson County. Claims arising under federal law, or otherwise removable, may instead be heard in the United States District Court for the District of Colorado; the controlling forum for any given case is confirmed during the conflict check and intake rather than presumed here. Reports are drafted to the same standard for both systems, with the methodology laid out so that the opposing expert can retrace every step of it.

## Defining the labor market for a Lakewood evaluee

Lakewood is among the larger suburban cities in the metro, and its residents hold jobs across the entire region, which means the honest unit of analysis is rarely the city itself. Vocational conclusions therefore draw on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Denver metropolitan area and for Colorado statewide, together with state workforce agency labor market information and targeted employer contact where warranted.
