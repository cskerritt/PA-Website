A Westminster resident's career rarely stays inside Westminster. People who live in this large suburb build work histories across the whole metro, and any credible opinion about what an injured or displaced Westminster worker can still earn has to respect that commuting reality instead of pretending the city is its own sealed economy. That premise shapes how Purinton Analytics approaches [earning capacity and employability questions](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) for Westminster parties, whether the retaining attorney represents the plaintiff or the defense, and it carries through the firm's wider [Denver metro practice](/locations/colorado/denver/).

## Adams County courts serving Westminster

Westminster matters are commonly venued in Adams County, where the District Court serving Adams County hears the civil docket. For claims that qualify, the United States District Court for the District of Colorado stands as the federal forum; the firm never announces on a webpage which courthouse a case belongs in, because that determination is made during the conflict check and intake with the actual caption in hand. What counsel can rely on in either court is a report whose scope, sources, and reasoning are set out explicitly enough for the opposing side to test.

## Measuring a commuter's labor market

Because the relevant market for a Westminster evaluee is regional, vocational findings are built on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Denver metropolitan area and for Colorado statewide, together with state workforce agency labor market information and direct labor market research where the referral requires it. Transferable skills analysis asks what the evaluee's demonstrated work history is worth across that regional market after the injury or termination at issue, and the answer is stated qualitatively in the narrative with the underlying data appended for verification.

## Appointments without an office

Purinton Analytics has no Westminster location and does not manufacture the appearance of one. Engagements run remotely by default: records analysis, structured interviewing, and standardized testing under protocols the test publishers approve for remote administration. In-person evaluation, when the assignment calls for it, is convened in the evaluee's own community rather than requiring travel to a distant office, and courtroom or deposition appearances are supported by travel scheduled from the Kansas City home office. [Life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) engagements follow the same pattern, adding clinical record review and provider-level cost verification for the evaluee's actual market.

## Requesting a conflict check

The engagement path opens with clearance. [Submit a conflict check request](/refer-a-case/) listing the caption or parties, a sentence on the claims, and any dates the court has already set. Cleared matters receive a written scope, a tailored records request, and a delivery commitment counsel can hold the firm to. Where the damages case will need economic modeling, the firm arranges [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement structure.
