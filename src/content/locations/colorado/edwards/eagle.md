## The labor market question for Eagle evaluees

Eagle is a small community, and the vocational question in most Eagle cases is not what jobs exist inside the town but what employment the surrounding labor market realistically offers a person with this evaluee's skills, restrictions, and history. That question is answered with Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the area and for Colorado statewide, state workforce agency labor market information, and direct labor market research when published sources run thin, as they often do in smaller markets. The [vocational expert analysis](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) documents each source so that cross-examination tests the evidence rather than the expert's memory. Where the referral extends to lost earnings and their present value, the economic analysis is performed and signed by independent economist partners coordinated by the firm through its [forensic economic damages](/services/forensic-economic-damages/) engagements.
