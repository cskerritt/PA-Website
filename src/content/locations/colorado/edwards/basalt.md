## What a review actually examines

A review engagement proceeds in a fixed order: whether the report's methodology is one the field recognizes, whether the data sources support the numbers the report claims they do, and whether the conclusions follow from the stated facts of the evaluee. The findings are delivered in whatever form the case posture requires, from an oral consult that shapes a deposition outline to a signed rebuttal report served on the disclosure deadline. Original opinions, when Basalt counsel needs them, are built the standard way: documented conflict check, defined scope, tailored records request, written opinion.
