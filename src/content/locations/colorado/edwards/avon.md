An earning capacity opinion for an Avon evaluee stands or falls on how honestly it defines the labor market. Avon is a small town within the wider Edwards area economy, and a report that treats the town itself as the whole market will understate opportunity, while one that silently imports statewide averages will overstate it. Purinton Analytics draws the line deliberately and shows its work, which is why plaintiff and defense counsel alike retain the firm for [vocational expert](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) and [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) engagements involving Avon parties. The methodology is the one described across the firm's [Edwards area page](/locations/colorado/edwards/), applied to the facts of the individual evaluee.

## Sourcing the market evidence

Vocational findings for Avon evaluees are grounded in Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the surrounding area and for Colorado statewide, supplemented by state workforce agency labor market information. In a smaller market, published estimates can leave gaps, and where the referral questions warrant it the firm conducts direct labor market research, contacting employers and documenting the results, so that the transferable skills analysis reflects work that actually exists within the evaluee's reach rather than a statistical abstraction. Every source lands in the report's file, available to whichever side wants to test it.

## Eagle County venue and the federal alternative

Avon lies in Eagle County, and civil actions involving Avon parties are commonly heard in the District Court serving Eagle County. The United States District Court for the District of Colorado is the available federal forum when a claim arises under federal law or the parties are diverse. The firm does not presume which court controls; the venue for a particular matter, along with any registration or qualification requirement it carries for the expert, is identified during the conflict check and intake process. Reports are written so that the analysis holds up in either setting, from disclosure through cross-examination.

## From vocational findings to damages

Counsel often need the vocational and care-planning opinions to feed a damages number a jury can evaluate. For those matters the firm offers [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement structure. The division of labor is deliberate: the vocational expert opines on capacity and the labor market, the life care planner on future care needs and their costs through a [medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/), and the economists on valuation, each within their own qualifications.

The firm keeps no office in Avon and lists no local address, a fact stated plainly because candor about presence belongs to the same habit of accuracy that governs the reports.
