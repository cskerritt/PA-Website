## Earning capacity in a small-town setting

Gypsum is a small town, and an honest earning capacity analysis says so. Rather than treating the town line as the boundary of the labor market, vocational findings for a Gypsum evaluee rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the surrounding area and for Colorado statewide, along with state workforce agency labor market information and direct labor market research where the referral questions warrant it. The transferable skills analysis then addresses what work actually exists within a reasonable commuting range of the evaluee, not what a national table implies in the abstract. When lost earnings must be reduced to present value, the firm offers [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement structure.
