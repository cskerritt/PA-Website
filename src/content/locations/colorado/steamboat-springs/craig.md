## A small labor market examined honestly

A community of Craig's size does not offer the occupational breadth of a large metropolitan area, and a credible vocational opinion says so rather than papering over it. Findings for Craig evaluees rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Steamboat Springs area and for Colorado statewide, supplemented by state workforce agency labor market information and, where the referral questions warrant it, direct labor market research into the employers actually accessible to the evaluee.
