The Rifle micropolitan area comprises Garfield County and Pitkin County.

## Appointments, not an office

Purinton Analytics keeps no staffed office in Rifle or anywhere else in the
area and publishes no street address for this location. Meetings are by
appointment, and the firm says so directly because honesty about presence
is part of the discipline it brings to its written work. Most engagements
proceed remotely, with structured interviews and standardized testing
administered under publisher protocols that support remote use. When the
referral questions require in-person evaluation, or when a deposition or
trial date in Garfield County or Pitkin County requires presence, travel is
scheduled from the Kansas City home office with the litigation calendar in
mind.

## Small-market labor analysis done properly

Labor market analysis in a smaller market demands more care, not less,
because thin occupational data is easier to misuse. Vocational findings for
evaluees in Garfield County and Pitkin County rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the relevant area
and for Colorado statewide, state workforce agency labor market
information, and direct labor market research, including employer contact,
where published estimates alone cannot answer the referral questions. The
resulting opinions describe the work actually available in the evaluee's
commuting area. Life care plans and medical cost projections are priced to
the evaluee's own market through provider-specific inquiries and published
fee data, reflecting where care will really be purchased.
