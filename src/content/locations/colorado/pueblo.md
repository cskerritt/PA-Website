For a Pueblo evaluee, the evaluation comes to the evaluee, not the other
way around. Purinton Analytics structures its Pueblo County engagements so
that injured workers, patients, and claimants are interviewed and tested in
their own community or by remote protocol, rather than being asked to
travel to an expert's office in another city. Plaintiff and defense counsel
in the Pueblo area retain the firm for
[earning capacity and employability evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) in catastrophic injury
matters, [medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/) where
the dispute concerns future care costs alone, and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under a
single engagement structure.

## Pueblo County courts

The Pueblo metropolitan area consists of Pueblo County, and the firm
accepts matters venued in the District Court in Pueblo County as well as in
the United States District Court for the District of Colorado. The same
methodology serves claims in arbitration and administrative forums. Because
the firm works for either side of the caption, its reports are written to
survive scrutiny from the other: findings are tied to records, testing, and
published data that opposing counsel can check line by line. Counsel with
matters in other parts of the state can start from the
[Colorado state page](/locations/colorado/).

## Evaluation logistics built around the evaluee

The firm has no staffed office in Pueblo and publishes no street address
for this location; it says so plainly because honesty about presence is
part of the same discipline that governs its opinions. Meetings are by
appointment. A typical engagement opens with a documented conflict check, a
defined scope, and a tailored records request, followed by a structured
interview and standardized testing, administered remotely under publisher
protocols where supported or in person in the evaluee's community when the
referral questions require it. Travel for in-person evaluation, deposition,
or trial is scheduled from the Kansas City home office and planned against
the court's deadlines.

## Pueblo labor market data, applied honestly

Vocational findings for Pueblo County evaluees rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Pueblo
metropolitan area and for Colorado statewide, together with state workforce
agency labor market information and direct labor market research where
warranted. Local data matters because employability conclusions are only as
good as the market they describe: an occupation that exists in the
abstract but not in the evaluee's commuting area does not support an
earning capacity opinion. Life care plans and medical cost projections for
Pueblo residents are priced through provider-specific inquiries and
published fee data for the region, so future care costs reflect the market
where care will be delivered.

## Requesting a conflict check

Whether the case involves a disputed earning capacity, a contested life
care plan, or a full damages package, the path in is the same:
[request a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) with the case caption and party
names, noting any disclosure, deposition, or trial dates already set. The
firm responds with scope, records needs, and a delivery schedule. For
matters beyond Colorado, the [national coverage](/locations/nationwide/)
page describes the same practice operating in every United States venue.
