An evaluee in Palisade should not have to organize a case around an expert's travel schedule. That premise shapes how Purinton Analytics handles Palisade referrals: the assessment is designed around the person being evaluated, with remote evaluation as the default and in-person work brought to the evaluee's own community when the referral questions require it. ## The forum that will test the opinion

Palisade lies in Mesa County, and its state-court civil disputes are ordinarily heard in the District Court serving Mesa County. Matters that belong in federal court proceed in the United States District Court for the District of Colorado. Which forum controls a given engagement, and what qualification or registration that forum expects of a testifying expert, is confirmed during the conflict check and intake rather than assumed. The sequencing is deliberate: an opinion drafted before the forum's standards are settled invites avoidable cross-examination, and a report intended for deposition and trial should carry no assumption its author cannot defend.

## Earning capacity beyond the town line

Palisade is a small community, and a vocational opinion that treated the town boundary as the boundary of the labor market would be easy to dismantle. Analyses for Palisade evaluees therefore examine the employment the evaluee can realistically reach, anchored in the Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates covering the Grand Junction area and the state of Colorado, alongside state workforce agency labor market information and direct employer research where the questions call for it. When the assignment runs the other way, [rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) of a report the other side has already disclosed, the same question is turned outward: whether the opposing expert defined a market the Palisade evaluee can actually reach, and whether each conclusion about that market traces back to a published estimate or a documented inquiry rather than to assumption.

## The disclosure calendar drives the schedule

Expert work in litigation lives or dies by dates: the disclosure deadline, the deposition notice, the trial setting. Palisade engagements are scheduled backward from those dates at intake, so records requests go out early, the evaluation is completed with room for supplementation, and the written opinion is served when counsel needs it rather than when travel happens to permit. The firm keeps no staffed office in Palisade and publishes no street address for this location; presence is by appointment, and travel for depositions, trial testimony, and any in-person evaluation is arranged from the Kansas City home office. When the case also requires economic valuation, [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) allow independent economist partners to perform and sign the economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning work continues under a single engagement structure.
