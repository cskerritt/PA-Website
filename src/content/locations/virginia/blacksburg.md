Purinton Analytics is retained in the Blacksburg-Christiansburg-Radford
metro by whichever side of the caption needs the work: plaintiff counsel
proving what an injury took from a client's working life, and defense
counsel testing whether a claimed loss survives scrutiny. The methodology
does not change with the caption: the same records request, the same
interview structure, and the same documentation stand behind every opinion,
whether the engagement calls for
[vocational expert evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), or
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) of opposing
expert reports.

## The venue footprint

Engagements are accepted for matters in the Circuit Court of Floyd County,
Giles County, Montgomery County, and Pulaski County, and in the circuit
court serving the independent city of Radford. Federal matters from the
region proceed in the United States District Court for the Western District
of Virginia, and the firm also appears in the United States District Court
for the Eastern District of Virginia when a matter is venued there. For
cases elsewhere in the Commonwealth, the firm's
[Virginia](/locations/virginia/) page describes statewide coverage, and its
[national practice](/locations/nationwide/) extends the same engagement
terms across state lines.

## Meetings, evaluations, and travel

Meetings in the metro are by appointment. The firm maintains no staffed
office here and publishes no street address for this location; stating that
plainly is part of the same discipline that governs its reports. Most
engagements begin remotely, with records review, structured vocational
interviews, and standardized testing administered under publisher protocols
where remote administration is supported. When the referral questions call
for in-person evaluation, the evaluation is scheduled in the evaluee's own
community rather than requiring the evaluee to travel, and when deposition
or trial dates require presence, travel is arranged from the Kansas City
home office with the case calendar confirmed at intake. Counsel receive the
same report standard and the same point of contact regardless of format, and
every methodological choice is documented in the file so it can be defended
at deposition.

## Labor market data

Employability and earning capacity findings for evaluees in the region rest
on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for
the Blacksburg metropolitan area and for Virginia statewide, together with
state workforce agency labor market information and direct labor market
research where the referral questions warrant it. Conclusions about
placeability are anchored to the evaluee's actual commuting market, because
a transferable skills analysis is only useful if the jobs it identifies
exist where the evaluee lives. Life care plans and medical cost projections
are priced to the evaluee's own market for care, and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
are available in which independent economist partners perform and sign the
economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under
one engagement structure.

## Starting with a conflict check

The route into any engagement in the metro runs through a documented
conflict check and a defined written scope. To begin,
[request a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) with the case caption and party
names, and identify any imminent disclosure, deposition, or trial dates so
that scheduling and availability are settled at intake. Any venue-specific
registration or qualification requirement is identified and confirmed during
the conflict check and intake process.
