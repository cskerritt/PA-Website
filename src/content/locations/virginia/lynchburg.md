Most referrals from the Lynchburg metro arrive as a question rather than a
title: can this evaluee return to past work, what would replacement care
actually cost, does the opposing report hold up. Purinton Analytics is
retained by plaintiff and defense counsel in the region to answer those
questions in writing, through
[earning capacity and employability evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/),
[medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/), and
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) of opposing
expert work, each under a defined scope agreed before the work begins.

## Where engagements are accepted

The firm accepts matters venued in the Circuit Court of Amherst County,
Appomattox County, Bedford County, and Campbell County, and in the circuit
court serving the independent city of Lynchburg. Federal matters from the
region proceed in the United States District Court for the Western District
of Virginia, and the firm likewise appears in the United States District
Court for the Eastern District of Virginia when venue calls for it. Counsel
whose cases sit outside the metro can review the firm's
[Virginia](/locations/virginia/) coverage or its
[national practice](/locations/nationwide/); the engagement terms, report
standard, and intake process are the same everywhere.

## Appointments and travel

Lynchburg meetings are by appointment. The firm has no staffed office in the
metro and publishes no street address for this location, because being
precise about presence is part of the same discipline that governs its
opinions. Engagements typically begin remotely: records review, structured
vocational interviews, and standardized testing administered under publisher
protocols where remote administration is supported. When the referral
questions require an in-person evaluation, it is scheduled in the evaluee's
own community, and when depositions or trial dates require attendance,
travel is planned from the Kansas City home office and confirmed against
counsel's calendar during intake rather than left to chance. The file, the
testing record, and the report standard stay identical whether the work is
performed remotely or in person, so nothing about the logistics changes what
opposing counsel will find when they examine the opinion.

## Labor market foundations

Employability and earning capacity findings for evaluees in the region rest
on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for
the Lynchburg metropolitan area and for Virginia statewide, along with state
workforce agency labor market information and direct labor market research
where the referral questions warrant it. Care-related opinions are priced to
the evaluee's own market using provider-specific inquiries and published fee
data rather than national averages. When a matter needs a complete damages
picture, the firm offers
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure, so counsel manage a single engagement rather than
several.

## Opening a file

The first step in any Lynchburg metro matter is a documented conflict check.
[Request a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) with the case caption and party
names, describe the referral questions as you currently see them, and note
any imminent disclosure, deposition, or trial dates. Any venue-specific
registration or qualification requirement is identified and confirmed during
the conflict check and intake process, and a written scope issues before
substantive work begins.
