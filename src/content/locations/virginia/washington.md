Counsel in the Washington-Arlington-Alexandria metro often litigate across
jurisdictional lines, with a client in Virginia, a defendant in Maryland, and
a forum in the District of Columbia. Purinton Analytics is built for that
kind of practice: a single engagement structure, a single report standard,
and a single point of contact for plaintiff and defense counsel who need
[vocational expert evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), or
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## A venue footprint that crosses jurisdictional lines

On the [Virginia](/locations/virginia/) side of the metro, engagements are
accepted for matters in the Circuit Court of Arlington County, Fairfax
County, Loudoun County, Prince William County, Stafford County, Spotsylvania
County, Clarke County, Culpeper County, Fauquier County, Rappahannock
County, and Warren County, and in the circuit courts serving the independent
cities of Alexandria, Fairfax, Falls Church, Fredericksburg, Manassas, and
Manassas Park. Federal matters proceed in the United States District Court
for the Eastern District of Virginia or the United States District Court for
the Western District of Virginia, depending on venue. The metro also reaches
the District of Columbia, Montgomery County, Frederick County, Charles
County, and Prince George's County in Maryland, and Jefferson County in West
Virginia; matters venued there are handled under the same intake, scope, and
reporting discipline the firm applies to its
[national practice](/locations/nationwide/).

## Meetings by appointment

Washington-area meetings are by appointment. The firm maintains no staffed
office anywhere in the metro and publishes no street address for this
location, because accuracy about presence is part of the same discipline that
governs its written opinions. Most engagements begin remotely, with records
review, structured interviews, and standardized testing administered under
publisher protocols where remote administration is supported. When the
referral questions call for in-person evaluation, or when deposition and
trial calendars require presence, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City
home office and coordinated around counsel's dates rather than around any
local footprint.

## Labor market research for Washington metro evaluees

Vocational findings for evaluees in this metro rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Washington
metropolitan area and for the relevant statewide areas, supplemented by
state workforce agency labor market information and direct employer research
where the referral questions warrant it. Because the metro spans several
jurisdictions, the analysis is anchored to the evaluee's own commuting
labor market rather than to a generic regional average, and life care plans
and medical cost projections are priced to the evaluee's actual market for
care. The result is a record that either side of the caption can test
against published sources.

## Starting an engagement

The first step for any Washington metro matter is a documented conflict
check, followed by a defined scope and a records request tailored to the
claims. Submit the caption or party names through the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and note any imminent disclosure,
deposition, or trial dates so scheduling can be confirmed at intake, along
with any venue-specific registration or qualification requirement, which is
identified and confirmed during the conflict check and intake process.
