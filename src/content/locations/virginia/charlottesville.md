Every Charlottesville metro engagement begins the way engagements begin
everywhere in the practice: with a documented conflict check, a written
scope, and a records request tailored to the claims, so that the eventual
opinion rests on a record either side of the caption can examine. Purinton
Analytics brings that method to plaintiff and defense matters in the region
through [vocational expert evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[expert testimony and litigation consulting](/services/expert-testimony-litigation-consulting/),
with the same standards that govern the firm's work in any other venue.

## Venues served

Engagements are accepted for matters in the Circuit Court of Albemarle
County, Fluvanna County, Greene County, and Nelson County, and in the
circuit court serving the independent city of Charlottesville. Federal
matters from the region proceed in the United States District Court for the
Western District of Virginia, and the firm also accepts matters venued in
the United States District Court for the Eastern District of Virginia.
Cases arising elsewhere in the Commonwealth are covered through the firm's
[Virginia](/locations/virginia/) practice, and matters beyond the state are
handled through its [national coverage](/locations/nationwide/) under
identical engagement terms.

## By appointment only

The firm keeps no staffed office in the Charlottesville metro and publishes
no street address for this location; meetings are by appointment, and that
candor about presence is deliberate. Most engagements begin remotely, with
records review, structured vocational interviews, and standardized testing
administered under publisher protocols where remote administration is
supported. Where the referral questions call for an in-person evaluation,
it is conducted in the evaluee's own community; where deposition or trial
dates require attendance, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home
office and confirmed at intake so the case calendar drives the logistics
rather than the reverse. The working file, the testing protocols, and the
report standard do not vary with the meeting format, which means the mode of
evaluation never becomes a cross-examination issue of its own.

## Labor market and cost research

Vocational findings for evaluees in the metro rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the
Charlottesville metropolitan area and for Virginia statewide, supported by
state workforce agency labor market information and direct labor market
research where the referral questions warrant it. Employability and
placeability conclusions are tied to the evaluee's actual commuting market,
and care-related opinions are priced to the evaluee's own market using
provider-specific inquiries and published fee data. Where the damages
presentation requires economic analysis, the firm offers
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Conflict check first

Retention in a Charlottesville metro matter starts with a conflict check
rather than a consultation pitch. Submit the caption or party names through
the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/), and note any imminent
disclosure, deposition, or trial dates along with the referral questions as
counsel currently frames them. Any venue-specific registration or
qualification requirement is identified and confirmed during the conflict
check and intake process, and a written scope is agreed before substantive
work begins.
