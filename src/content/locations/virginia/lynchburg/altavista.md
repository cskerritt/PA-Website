An injured worker in Altavista does not job-hunt in Altavista alone. The
town sits in Campbell County within the Lynchburg metropolitan area, and
its residents' realistic employment options are spread across that wider
commuting market, with Lynchburg as its principal city. That basic fact
drives how Purinton Analytics approaches an Altavista referral: the
earning capacity question is framed around the market an evaluee can
actually reach, not around the town limits. Plaintiff and defense
engagements alike are supported through
[vocational expert work](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/).

## Courts serving Campbell County matters

Litigation involving an Altavista party typically lands in state court,
where Campbell County claims are heard in the Circuit Court. In federal
practice, the forums available across the Commonwealth are the United
States District Court for the Eastern District of Virginia and the United
States District Court for the Western District of Virginia. Which district
governs a specific matter is a question the firm resolves with counsel
during the conflict check and intake, never by assumption. Neighboring
venues and the regional picture are covered on the
[Lynchburg metro page](/locations/virginia/lynchburg/), while the
[Virginia overview](/locations/virginia/) addresses engagements anywhere
in the state.

## Labor market evidence that survives scrutiny

Because the commuting market defines an Altavista evaluee's options,
vocational conclusions are grounded in Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates covering the Lynchburg
metropolitan area and Virginia statewide, supplemented by state workforce
agency labor market information and, where the questions call for it,
direct labor market research. Sources are cited in the report itself, item
by item, so that either side can retrace the analysis.

Where the damages picture extends to present-value calculations, counsel
may add a
[coordinated damages engagement](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. Authorship is never blurred: each discipline's
opinions are signed by the professional who formed them.

## The disclosure calendar and the evaluation

Expert work in a small-town case fails most often on timing, not
substance, so scheduling is treated as part of the methodology. At intake
the firm maps the report against the operative disclosure deadlines and
works backward: records requests go out early, the structured vocational
interview and standardized testing are completed by video under publisher
protocols, and drafts are sequenced so the written opinion is ready before
the calendar tightens. An Altavista evaluee is not asked to travel for the
firm's convenience; when the referral genuinely requires an in-person
assessment, it takes place in the evaluee's own community. Depositions and
trial appearances are reached by travel from the Kansas City home office.
There is no staffed Altavista office and no local street address, and the
firm says so plainly because accurate statements about presence belong to
the same standard as accurate opinions. Any venue-specific registration or
qualification requirement is identified and confirmed during the conflict
check and intake process.
