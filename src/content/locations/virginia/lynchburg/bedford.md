## Bedford County venue, state and federal

For state-court litigation, the operative venue fact is simple: Bedford
County claims proceed in the Circuit Court, and report schedules are built
backward from that court's deadlines. Federal matters require a further
step. Virginia's federal trial forums are the United States District Court
for the Eastern District of Virginia and the United States District Court
for the Western District of Virginia, and the district that controls a
particular case is confirmed during the conflict check and intake rather
than assumed in advance. Counsel looking at the wider region will find the
surrounding venues discussed on the
[Lynchburg metro page](/locations/virginia/lynchburg/), and the
[Virginia overview](/locations/virginia/) describes how statewide
engagements are handled.

## Where a Bedford evaluee actually competes for work

Bedford is a small town, and a residual earning capacity opinion that
pretended its labor market ended at the town line would not survive
cross-examination. The relevant market is the Lynchburg metropolitan area,
where Lynchburg itself anchors the employment base a Bedford resident can
realistically reach. Vocational findings therefore rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Lynchburg
area and for Virginia statewide, together with state workforce agency labor
market information and direct labor market research where the referral
questions warrant it. Each conclusion in the written opinion cites its
published source, so opposing counsel can check the work without taking the
expert's word for anything.

## Meeting and evaluating in Bedford

The firm maintains no staffed office in Bedford and publishes no street
address for this location; candor about presence is part of the same
discipline that governs the substantive opinions. Most engagements proceed
remotely, with records review, structured interviews by video, and
standardized testing administered under publisher protocols. When the
referral questions call for an in-person evaluation, the assessment is
arranged in the evaluee's own community, and when a deposition or trial
date requires attendance, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home
office around the court's calendar. Any venue-specific registration or
qualification requirement is identified and confirmed during the conflict
check and intake process.
