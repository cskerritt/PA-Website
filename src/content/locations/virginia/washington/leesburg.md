A vocational opinion written for a Leesburg evaluee cannot stop at the town
line. Residents of this Loudoun County community participate in the
employment market of the wider Washington-Arlington-Alexandria metropolitan
area, and an earning capacity analysis that ignores that reach would not
survive a careful cross-examination. Purinton Analytics builds its Leesburg
work on that premise, accepting engagements from either side of the caption
for [vocational assessment](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) of opposing
expert reports.

## Loudoun County venue context

Leesburg matters filed in state court proceed in the Circuit Court of
Loudoun County, and the firm calibrates each report to that court's
disclosure and testimony practice. Federal claims are heard in the United
States District Court for the Eastern District of Virginia or in the United
States District Court for the Western District of Virginia; which forum
controls a given case is a question resolved during the conflict check and
intake, not a fact the firm presumes. Counsel with
matters spread across the region will find the
[Washington metro page](/locations/virginia/washington/) useful for the
metro-wide picture and the [Virginia overview](/locations/virginia/) for
statewide engagements.

## Grounding the labor market analysis

Because Leesburg workers commute throughout the metro, vocational findings
here draw on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage
estimates for the Washington-Arlington-Alexandria metropolitan area and for
Virginia statewide, supplemented by state workforce agency labor market
information and, where the referral questions warrant, direct contact with
employers. The written report distinguishes metro-level data from statewide
data and cites the published source behind every finding, a habit that
matters equally in plaintiff and defense engagements because both sides
depend on the opinion holding up under scrutiny.

## When economic damages are also in issue

Many Loudoun County cases pair the vocational and care-planning questions
with a lost earnings or lost household services calculation. For those
matters the firm offers
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. Counsel receive an integrated set of opinions without
the firm ever holding itself out as the economist.

## Appointments, not an office

There is no staffed Purinton Analytics office in Leesburg, and the firm
publishes no street address for this location. Meetings happen by
appointment. The bulk of a typical engagement is remote: records review,
video interviews under a structured protocol, and standardized testing where
publishers support remote administration. If the referral questions require
an in-person evaluation, the evaluee is evaluated in his or her own
community, and travel for depositions or trial in Loudoun County is arranged
from the Kansas City home office to fit the court's calendar. Any
venue-specific registration or qualification requirement is identified and
confirmed during the conflict check and intake process.
