Alexandria is an independent city under Virginia's court structure, which
means the venue analysis for an Alexandria case begins with the city itself
rather than with a surrounding county. Purinton Analytics writes its reports
with that structure in mind. Counsel for plaintiffs and counsel for
defendants retain the firm here for
[vocational expert evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under a single
engagement structure. Every opinion is prepared on the assumption that the
other side will read it line by line.

## Where an Alexandria case is heard

State-court matters arising in the city are heard in Alexandria's Circuit
Court, and the firm's reports are built to satisfy that court's disclosure
and testimony expectations. On the federal side, a claim proceeds
in the United States District Court for the Eastern District of Virginia or
the United States District Court for the Western District of Virginia, and
the controlling venue for any specific matter is identified during the
conflict check and intake rather than assumed in advance. For
engagements elsewhere in the region, the
[Washington metro page](/locations/virginia/washington/) describes the
broader practice, and the [Virginia overview](/locations/virginia/) covers
the state as a whole.

## Alexandria within a national-scale labor market

The city sits inside the Washington-Arlington-Alexandria metropolitan area,
one of the largest employment markets in the country, and an Alexandria
resident's realistic job base extends well beyond the city limits. Earning
capacity opinions for evaluees here rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates for that metropolitan area and
for Virginia statewide, along with state workforce agency labor market
information and direct labor market research when the referral questions
call for it. The report identifies which published source supports each
finding, so cross-examination can test the data rather than the expert's
memory.

## Meeting and evaluating by appointment

The firm maintains no staffed office in Alexandria and publishes no street
address for this location; candor about that is part of the same discipline
that governs the written work. Records review, structured vocational
interviews by video, and standardized testing under publisher protocols
proceed remotely in most engagements. When an in-person evaluation is
warranted, the evaluee is seen in his or her own community, and when
deposition or trial dates require presence in the city, travel is scheduled
from the Kansas City home office around the litigation calendar.

## Qualification, addressed at intake

Jason Purinton's certifications are issued by national credentialing bodies
rather than by any single state, and forensic economic analyses are always
performed and signed by independent economist partners coordinated by the
firm. Whether an expert may testify in a particular Virginia matter is a
question for the forum under its own rules, and any registration or
qualification requirement specific to the venue is confirmed during intake
before substantive work begins.
