## How the evaluation fits the deadline

Because the practice is remote-first, the schedule rarely waits on travel.
Records analysis, video-based vocational interviews, and standardized
testing under publisher protocols proceed as soon as conflicts clear and
records arrive. If an in-person evaluation is needed, it takes place in the
evaluee's own community rather than a distant office, and appearances for
deposition or trial are scheduled from the Kansas City home office around
the court's dates. The firm has no staffed Fredericksburg office and
publishes no local street address; saying so plainly is part of the candor
