The practical question counsel ask first about a Manassas engagement is
usually logistical: where does the evaluation actually happen? For most
referrals the answer is that it happens where the evaluee already is.
Purinton Analytics runs a remote-first practice, so records review,
structured vocational interviews by video, and standardized testing under
publisher protocols are completed without asking an injured person in
Manassas to travel anywhere. When the referral questions genuinely require
an in-person assessment, the firm comes to the evaluee's community. That
logistical design serves plaintiff and defense engagements alike across
[vocational expert work](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/).

## An independent city and its courts

Manassas is an independent city in Virginia's system, and its state-court
matters proceed in the city's Circuit Court, whose disclosure and testimony
expectations shape how each report is assembled. In federal litigation,
Virginia's available forums are the United States District Court for the
Eastern District of Virginia and the United States District Court for the
Western District of Virginia; the controlling venue for a particular case
is settled during the conflict check and intake. Regional context sits on
the [Washington metro page](/locations/virginia/washington/), and the
[Virginia overview](/locations/virginia/) explains statewide engagements.

## The labor market a Manassas evaluee actually faces

Manassas is a small city inside the Washington-Arlington-Alexandria
metropolitan area, and its residents' employment prospects are defined by
that larger market rather than by the city alone. Vocational findings
therefore rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and
wage estimates for the metropolitan area and for Virginia statewide,
together with state workforce agency labor market information and direct
labor market research where warranted. Each finding in the written opinion
is tied to its published source, so the analysis can be verified by either
side without depending on the expert's say-so.

## Coordinated damages under one engagement

Where lost earnings or the cost of future care must be reduced to present
value, counsel can retain the firm for
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. The division of labor is stated openly in the reports
themselves: the economic opinions belong to the economists who sign them.

## Presence without pretense

The firm keeps no staffed office in Manassas and lists no street address for
this page, because accuracy about presence is part of the same discipline
that governs the substantive work. Meetings with counsel are by appointment,
in person or by video. When a deposition or trial date requires attendance,
travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office around the court's
calendar, and any venue-specific registration or qualification requirement
is identified and confirmed during the conflict check and intake process
before substantive work begins.
