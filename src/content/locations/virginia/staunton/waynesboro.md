## Waynesboro City as a Venue

State-law matters from Waynesboro proceed in Virginia's Circuit Court system,
in the circuit that serves the city. Because Waynesboro city is its own
jurisdiction, venue questions here are usually straightforward at the state
level, and the written opinion is prepared for the bench and jury that will
actually hear it. For federal claims, Virginia is divided between the United
States District Court for the Eastern District of Virginia and the United
States District Court for the Western District of Virginia; both are
available forums for Virginia litigants, and the controlling venue for any
particular matter, along with any registration or qualification requirement
it imposes, is identified and confirmed during the conflict check and intake
process. Every opinion is written on the assumption that it will be read
first by the lawyer on the other side.
