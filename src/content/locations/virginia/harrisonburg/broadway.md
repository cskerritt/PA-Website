What can this person still earn? For a Broadway claimant, that question
cannot be answered by looking at Broadway alone. The town is a small
community inside the Harrisonburg metropolitan area, and the jobs its
residents realistically hold or seek are spread across that wider regional
market. Purinton Analytics answers the question with sourced labor market
evidence rather than impression, preparing
[vocational expert analyses](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care plans](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/) that
plaintiff and defense counsel alike can put in front of a factfinder.

## The courts that hear Broadway matters

Broadway sits in Rockingham County, and state-court litigation arising
there proceeds in the county's Circuit Court, whose scheduling orders and
disclosure expectations frame the reporting timeline from the day of
retention. For federal claims, Virginia offers the United States District
Court for the Eastern District of Virginia and the United States District
Court for the Western District of Virginia as available forums; the venue
that controls a particular Broadway case is identified during the conflict
check and intake. Counsel working across the region will find the broader
picture on the [Harrisonburg metro page](/locations/virginia/harrisonburg/)
and the statewide framework on the
[Virginia overview](/locations/virginia/).

## Reading a commuter's labor market honestly

Because Broadway workers participate in the Harrisonburg area economy, a
credible earning-capacity opinion has to be built on data for that
metropolitan market and for Virginia statewide, not on anecdote about a
single town. The firm relies on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational
employment and wage estimates for the Harrisonburg area, supplemented by
state workforce agency labor market information and by direct employer
research when the referral questions call for it. Every occupational
finding in the written report is tied to its published source. That
sourcing is what lets the same opinion serve either side of a case: it
does not depend on who retained the analyst, only on what the data show.

## Evaluation without a road trip

The practice is remote-first. Records arrive by secure transfer, the
vocational interview is conducted by video on a structured protocol, and
standardized instruments are administered under their publishers' rules,
so a Broadway evaluee is not asked to travel for the firm's convenience.
In-person assessment, when the case requires it, happens in the evaluee's
community by appointment, with travel arranged from the Kansas City home
office. There is no staffed Purinton Analytics office in Broadway and no
street address published for this page; stating that plainly is part of
the same accuracy the reports themselves are held to. When lost earnings
or future care costs must be reduced to present value, counsel may retain
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Starting with the conflict check

Retention begins with a conflict check rather than a pitch. Provide the
caption or party names through the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and identify any expert disclosure
deadlines, depositions, or trial settings already scheduled, so the
engagement calendar can be built backward from the court's. The firm's
[national coverage](/locations/nationwide/) describes how the identical
methodology is applied for counsel in courts far beyond Rockingham
County.
