## Deadline-proof method, not shortcuts

A compressed calendar never changes the method. The engagement still opens
with a documented conflict check and a defined scope; the records request
is still tailored to the claims; and every occupational finding still
rests on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage
estimates for the Harrisonburg metropolitan area, where Elkton residents
actually compete for work, and for Virginia statewide, supplemented by
state workforce agency labor market information and direct labor market
research where warranted. What compresses is the schedule, managed through
early record collection and interim status reports to counsel, never the
sourcing that lets the opinion survive cross-examination. If a deadline
cannot be met responsibly, counsel hears that at intake, not after
retention, because a candid no protects the case better than a rushed yes.
