Nothing about retaining a national expert should require an injured person
in Timberville to spend a day traveling. That premise shapes how Purinton
Analytics handles small-town referrals: the evaluation is designed around
the evaluee, not the other way around. Records review, a structured
vocational interview by video, and standardized testing under publisher
protocols all proceed wherever the evaluee already is, and the resulting
[vocational opinions](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care plans](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/) are
available to counsel on either side of a Timberville case.

## Where the resulting opinion will be tested

Timberville lies in Rockingham County, so state-court matters go to the
county's Circuit Court, and the report is drafted with that court's
disclosure and testimony expectations in view from the outset. Federal
claims from the area may proceed in the United States District Court for
the Eastern District of Virginia or the United States District Court for
the Western District of Virginia; the forum that actually controls is
pinned down during the conflict check and intake rather than presumed on a
map. Context for the surrounding region appears on the
[Harrisonburg metro page](/locations/virginia/harrisonburg/), and
statewide practice is described on the
[Virginia overview](/locations/virginia/).

## A small town priced against a metro economy

An earning-capacity conclusion for a Timberville evaluee cannot stop at
the edge of a small town. Residents work within the Harrisonburg
metropolitan economy, so the firm grounds its occupational findings in
Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for
the Harrisonburg area and for Virginia statewide, adds state workforce
agency labor market information, and performs direct labor market research
when the referral questions justify it. No figure or placement conclusion
enters the report without a published source behind it, which is what
allows opposing counsel to check the work instead of merely attacking it.
Transferable skills analysis proceeds the same way, matching the demands
of the evaluee's past work against documented functional limitations
before any occupation is offered as a realistic alternative.

## Appointments, travel, and candor about presence

When an in-person assessment genuinely serves the referral questions, it
is scheduled in the evaluee's community, and the firm travels from its
Kansas City home office; deposition and trial appearances are handled the
same way, around the court's calendar. Purinton Analytics maintains no
staffed location in Timberville and prints no street address here, a
policy of plain honesty that mirrors the standard applied to every
substantive opinion. For cases in which the damages must be reduced to
present value, counsel can pair the vocational and care-planning work with
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure, with authorship of each opinion stated openly.

## From referral to retention

The gate at the front of every matter is a conflict check. Counsel should
submit the caption or the names of the parties through the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/), together with any looming
disclosure, deposition, or trial dates, and the firm will confirm
availability, scope, and any venue-specific qualification requirement
before substantive work begins. How this same engagement model operates
across the country is set out under the firm's
[national coverage](/locations/nationwide/).
