## Rockingham County venue and the federal alternatives

State-court matters arising in Bridgewater proceed in the Circuit Court of
Rockingham County, and the written opinion is organized so that it can be
defended there: a documented conflict check, a defined scope, and findings
tied to sources both parties can inspect. Where a claim is federal,
Virginia's district courts are the United States District Court for the
Eastern District of Virginia and the United States District Court for the
Western District of Virginia; which of them controls a given Bridgewater
case is confirmed during the conflict check and intake, never assumed. The
[Harrisonburg metro page](/locations/virginia/harrisonburg/) describes the
regional practice, and the [Virginia overview](/locations/virginia/) covers
statewide engagements.

## Earning capacity measured against the regional market

Bridgewater is a small town whose residents' realistic employment options
extend across the Harrisonburg metropolitan area rather than stopping at
the town line. Vocational findings for a Bridgewater evaluee therefore
draw on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage
estimates for the Harrisonburg area and for Virginia statewide, together
with state workforce agency labor market information and direct labor
market research where the referral questions warrant it. Qualitative and
sourced throughout, the analysis identifies what work exists in the
regional economy for a person with the evaluee's specific profile, so the
conclusion can be checked rather than taken on faith.

## How a Bridgewater evaluee is actually seen

Most of the work arrives by secure records transfer and proceeds remotely:
structured vocational interviews by video and standardized testing
administered under publisher protocols. When the referral genuinely calls
for in-person assessment, the appointment is arranged in the evaluee's own
community, and travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office. The
firm keeps no staffed office in Bridgewater and publishes no local street
address, because candor about presence belongs to the same discipline that
governs the opinions themselves. Where damages must be reduced to present
value, counsel can add
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.
