Every report Purinton Analytics writes for a Grottoes matter is drafted on
the assumption that the most skeptical reader will be the lawyer who did
not hire the firm. That assumption, rather than any marketing claim, is
the working definition of independence here: the same
[vocational expert analysis](/services/vocational-expert-witness/), the
same [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and the same
[medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/) methodology
are offered to plaintiff and defense counsel, and a report should read
identically no matter which side commissioned it.

## Courts serving a Grottoes dispute

Grottoes sits in Rockingham County, whose Circuit Court hears the town's
state-court litigation and whose expectations about expert disclosure
shape the form of the written opinion. Federal matters from the area look
to Virginia's district courts, the United States District Court for the
Eastern District of Virginia and the United States District Court for the
Western District of Virginia; determining which of those forums controls a
specific Grottoes case is part of the conflict check and intake, not a

## Labor market evidence that survives the other side

Scrutiny-proof vocational findings for a Grottoes evaluee begin with the
recognition that this is a small town whose workers participate in the
Harrisonburg metropolitan economy. The firm therefore anchors its
occupational conclusions in Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational
employment and wage estimates for the Harrisonburg area and for Virginia
statewide, in state workforce agency labor market information, and in
direct labor market research when the questions presented require it.
Because each conclusion cites its published source, opposing counsel can
retrace the analysis step by step, and an opinion that can be retraced is
far harder to exclude than assert-and-trust testimony. The same sourcing
discipline applies whether the opinion helps or hurts the retaining
party's theory, and counsel are told so before the engagement begins.

## Logistics that respect the evaluee

Assessment is remote-first: secure records transfer, a structured
vocational interview conducted by video, and standardized instruments
administered under publisher protocols, none of which requires a Grottoes
resident to leave home. If the referral calls for in-person evaluation,
the firm comes to the evaluee's community, traveling from its Kansas City
home office, and the same travel arrangement covers depositions and trial
testimony. No staffed office exists in Grottoes and none is claimed; the
page carries no street address because the firm does not manufacture
presence it does not have. When damages require present-value analysis,
counsel can retain
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.
