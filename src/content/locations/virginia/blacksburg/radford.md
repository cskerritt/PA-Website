Radford is unusual among the towns of the Blacksburg-Christiansburg-Radford
metropolitan area in how it is organized: the brief jurisdictional fact that
matters to counsel is that Radford city stands as its own county-equivalent
jurisdiction, with a Circuit Court of its own. That detail shapes where a
case is filed, which clerk holds the file, and which docket sets the expert
deadlines, and it is exactly the kind of detail Purinton Analytics confirms
at intake before any opinion work begins. Plaintiff and defense counsel
alike retain the firm here for
[vocational evaluation and testimony](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) of opposing
expert work.

## A court of its own

State-court matters involving Radford parties are typically heard in the
Circuit Court serving Radford city, and the firm prepares its reports for
that forum the way it prepares them for any other: scope defined in writing,
records requested against the claims, methodology disclosed, and
conclusions limited to what the record supports. In federal practice,
Virginia offers the United States District Court for the Western District of
Virginia and the United States District Court for the Eastern District of
Virginia as its forums; the district that controls a particular Radford
matter is identified during the conflict check and intake rather than
presumed on a landing page. Registration or qualification requirements
specific to the venue are confirmed at the same stage.

## Scheduling an evaluation in Radford

The firm keeps no office in Radford and lists no street address for this
location; every meeting is by appointment, and saying so plainly is part of
the practice's credibility. In most engagements the work is remote from
start to finish, which keeps costs proportionate and timelines tight. Where
an in-person vocational evaluation serves the referral questions, or where
deposition and trial testimony require attendance, travel is arranged from
the Kansas City home office and the evaluation is conducted in the
evaluee's own community. The same by-appointment model applies across the
firm's [Virginia practice](/locations/virginia/), so counsel with related
matters elsewhere in the state work with a single, consistent process.
