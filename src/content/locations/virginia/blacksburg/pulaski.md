A vocational opinion is useful to a Pulaski case only if it arrives before
the deadlines that govern the case. Disclosure dates, deposition windows,
and trial settings are fixed by the court, not by the expert, so Purinton
Analytics treats the calendar as the first substantive question in any new
matter: whether a vocational evaluation, a
[life care plan](/services/life-care-planning/), or a
[rebuttal of an opposing expert's report](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/)
can be completed inside the disclosure schedule already entered in the case.
Intake captures the disclosure, deposition, and trial dates before scope is
set, and the firm declines work it cannot deliver on time rather than
promising and slipping.

## Pulaski County courts

Pulaski shares its name with Pulaski County, and matters arising in town are
most commonly filed in the Circuit Court there. Opinions prepared for that
forum follow the practice's uniform sequence: documented conflict check,
written scope, records request tailored to the claims, and a signed report
whose reasoning both sides can trace. For federal matters, Virginia's
available forums are the United States District Court for the Western
District of Virginia and the United States District Court for the Eastern
District of Virginia; the controlling district for any particular case, like
any venue-specific qualification requirement, is confirmed during the
conflict check and intake rather than asserted in advance.

## Labor market evidence for Pulaski claims

Earning-capacity disputes in Pulaski County turn on what employment actually
exists within reach of the evaluee, and the firm treats that as an
evidentiary question. Vocational findings rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates for the
Blacksburg-Christiansburg-Radford metropolitan area and for Virginia
statewide, on state workforce agency labor market information, and on direct
employer contact when the referral questions justify it. Transferable-skills
analysis connects the evaluee's work history to occupations the data shows
are present in the regional market. The same evidence base supports
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under a
unified engagement.
