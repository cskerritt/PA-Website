Residents of a town the size of Dublin typically work across the wider
Blacksburg-Christiansburg-Radford region rather than inside town limits,
and that commuting reality is where a sound vocational opinion begins. When
Purinton Analytics evaluates a Dublin evaluee, the relevant market is the
regional one the evaluee can actually reach, and the relevant question is
whether injury or disability has narrowed access to it. That framing runs
through the firm's [vocational expert work](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
its [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and its
[medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/), each
available to whichever side of the caption makes the referral.

## Venue for Dublin matters

Dublin is a Pulaski County town, and litigation involving Dublin parties in
state court ordinarily proceeds in the county's Circuit Court. In federal
court, Virginia's forums are the United States District Court for the
Western District of Virginia and the United States District Court for the
Eastern District of Virginia; the firm does not presume which district
controls a given matter, and confirms that venue question, together with
any registration or qualification requirement particular to the forum,
during the conflict check and intake. Every report headed for any of these
courts carries the same architecture: written scope, a records request
matched to the claims, disclosed methodology, and conclusions confined to
the evidence.

## A commuter's labor market

Because Dublin workers draw their livelihoods from across the metro,
earning-capacity analysis here leans heavily on regional data. The firm
uses Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates
for the Blacksburg-Christiansburg-Radford metropolitan area and for
Virginia statewide, state workforce agency labor market information, and
targeted employer research when the referral questions call for it. The
transferable-skills analysis asks what occupations the evaluee's history
supports, whether those occupations exist within realistic commuting range
of Pulaski County, and what the evidence shows about wages and physical
demands. Where damages require economic quantification, the firm offers
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis alongside the vocational and care-planning work.
