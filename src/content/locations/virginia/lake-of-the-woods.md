## By-appointment logistics, described accurately

The firm has no staffed office in Lake of the Woods or elsewhere in Orange
County, and it publishes no street address for this location. That candor
is intentional: a practice that claims offices it does not keep would be
asking counsel to trust reports built on the same habit. Meetings are by
appointment. Records conferences, intake discussions, and attorney
preparation proceed remotely on a predictable schedule, and when the
referral questions call for in-person evaluation, or when deposition and
trial dates require presence, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City
home office and planned against the case calendar rather than improvised
at the deadline.

## Labor market research for Orange County evaluees

Employability and earning capacity findings for evaluees in the Lake of
the Woods area rest on evidence about the market where the evaluee
actually lives and seeks work. The analysis draws on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the local area
and for Virginia statewide, state workforce agency labor market
information, and direct labor market research where the referral warrants
it. Each report states which commutable labor market frames the analysis
and why, because a conclusion that cannot show its geographic foundation
invites the cross-examination it deserves. The same sourcing discipline
applies whether the report supports a plaintiff's disclosure or a defense
rebuttal.
