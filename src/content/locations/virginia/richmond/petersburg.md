Petersburg occupies its own place in Virginia's court structure. As
Petersburg city, an independent jurisdiction rather than a part of any
surrounding county, it gives injury and employment cases a venue identity
distinct from the rest of the [Richmond metropolitan
area](/locations/virginia/richmond/). Purinton Analytics accepts
[vocational expert](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) and [life care
planning](/services/life-care-planning/) engagements for Petersburg matters
from counsel on either side of the caption, applying the same documented
conflict check, defined scope, and tailored records request that governs
the practice [nationwide](/locations/nationwide/).

## Venue in an independent city

Matters arising in Petersburg are heard in the Circuit Court serving
Petersburg city, and counsel litigating there often manage companion
questions in neighboring jurisdictions of the metro at the same time. In
federal practice, Virginia is divided between the United States District
Court for the Eastern District of Virginia and the United States District
Court for the Western District of Virginia; which district controls a
particular Petersburg case is confirmed during the conflict check and
intake rather than assumed. Because the written opinion is built to survive
cross-examination wherever it is disclosed, a report prepared for a
Petersburg city courtroom reads the same way it would in any other forum,
and rebuttal or [peer review work](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) is
available on the same terms.

## Earning capacity questions answered from the record

Where a Petersburg evaluee can realistically work is a question the case
record must answer, not an assumption an expert imports. The analysis
begins with the evaluee's documented skills, restrictions, and work
history, then tests them against the labor market the evaluee actually
participates in as part of the wider Richmond, VA metropolitan area.
Vocational findings rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational
employment and wage estimates for the Richmond metropolitan area and for
Virginia statewide, supplemented by state workforce agency labor market
information and direct labor market research where the referral questions
warrant it. The analysis describes the labor market the evaluee actually
faces, including the tradeoffs between local openings and a longer commute,
without resting on assumptions the record cannot support.

## Meeting and evaluating in Petersburg

The firm keeps no staffed office in Petersburg and publishes no street
address here; meetings are by appointment, and the practice says so
plainly. Most engagements proceed remotely: records review, structured
vocational interviews, and standardized testing under publisher protocols
where remote administration is supported. When the referral calls for
in-person evaluation, the evaluation is conducted in the evaluee's own
community, with travel scheduled from the Kansas City home office and
coordinated against counsel's deadlines. [Medical cost
projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/) and life care plans for
Petersburg residents are priced to the evaluee's own market rather than to
a national average, and coordinated damages engagements are available in
which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.
