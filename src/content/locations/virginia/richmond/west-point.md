Small communities are where a remote-first expert practice proves its
worth. West Point, a small King William County town within the Richmond,
VA metropolitan area, is the kind of place where counsel
might once have settled for whichever expert happened to be nearby.
Purinton Analytics is built so that a West Point evaluee receives the same
[vocational expert](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) and [life care
planning](/services/life-care-planning/) rigor the practice delivers in
the largest cities it serves, on behalf of either side of the caption.

## Distance without dilution

Nothing in the methodology changes because the evaluee lives in a small
town. Records review, structured interviews by secure video, and
standardized testing under publisher protocols where remote administration
is supported carry the bulk of a West Point engagement without anyone
traveling at all. When the referral questions require an in-person
evaluation or a home assessment, the appointment is held in the evaluee's
own community, and travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office,
planned around deposition and trial dates identified at intake. The firm
keeps no office in West Point, staffs no location anywhere in the
[Richmond metro](/locations/virginia/richmond/), and publishes no street
address for this page, stating that plainly because honest logistics and
honest opinions are the same habit.

## King William County and the courts beyond it

West Point lies in King William County, and state-law matters from the
town proceed in the Circuit Court serving King William County. When a
case is in federal court, Virginia offers the United States District
Court for the Eastern District of Virginia and the United States District
Court for the Western District of Virginia as its forums, and the one
controlling a particular matter is fixed during the conflict check and
intake. Reports are drafted so that the analysis reads identically well
in either setting, and [rebuttal or peer
review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) of an opposing expert's opinion
is accepted under the same engagement discipline.

## Candor about a small town's job market

Earning capacity analysis for a West Point resident has to be candid
about geography. The town's own employer base is limited, and a realistic
job search may reach toward Richmond and the metro's larger employment
centers. Vocational findings therefore rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates for the Richmond metropolitan
area and for Virginia statewide, on state workforce agency labor market
information, and on direct labor market research where warranted, and the
written opinion describes the commuting tradeoffs an evaluee actually
faces rather than assuming metro-wide access. [Medical cost
projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/) and life care plans are
priced to the evaluee's local market with provider-specific inquiries.
