## Measuring earning capacity in a commuter city

Vocational findings for Colonial Heights evaluees rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Richmond
metropolitan area and for Virginia statewide, together with state
workforce agency labor market information and direct labor market research
when the referral questions call for it. The analysis stays qualitative
about scale and specific about method: it identifies transferable skills,
tests the realistic geography of the evaluee's job search, and documents
each source, so that either side can trace every conclusion back to the
record. Where an opposing expert has already opined, the firm accepts
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) work under the
same standard it applies to its own affirmative reports.

## County and court context

city, an independent jurisdiction, and state-law claims arising there are

available forums are the United States District Court for the Eastern
District of Virginia and the United States District Court for the Western
District of Virginia; the controlling district for any specific matter is
established during the conflict check and intake, not guessed at in a
marketing page. Reports are written to a single standard so that the same
opinion holds up whether it is disclosed in the local Circuit Court or in
federal litigation.
