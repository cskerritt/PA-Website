Expert work in an Ashland case is governed less by the map than by the
calendar. Disclosure deadlines, deposition settings, and trial dates
determine when a vocational opinion must be complete and defensible, and
Purinton Analytics builds every Ashland engagement backward from those
dates. From the first conflict check, the practice asks counsel for the
scheduling order's key milestones so that records requests,
[vocational evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/), and report
drafting land with time to spare, for plaintiff and defense engagements
alike.

## Working the disclosure calendar

An engagement opened well before the disclosure deadline allows the full
sequence: a documented conflict check, a written scope, a records request
tailored to the claims, evaluation, and a report both sides can examine.
An engagement opened late is not refused out of hand, but the intake
conversation is frank about what a compressed schedule permits, and the
scope is written to match. The same calendar discipline applies to
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) assignments,
where the operative deadline is often the response date rather than the
original disclosure, and to supplemental work when new records arrive
after the report is served.

## Hanover County venue context

Ashland sits in Hanover County, and state-court matters arising in and
around the town are heard in the Circuit Court serving Hanover County.
Virginia's federal forums are the United States District Court for the
Eastern District of Virginia and the United States District Court for the
Western District of Virginia, and the district that controls any given
Ashland matter is confirmed during the conflict check and intake. Any
venue-specific registration or qualification requirement is identified at
the same stage, so no deadline is lost to a foreseeable formality.

## An Ashland evaluee in the Richmond market

For earning capacity purposes, Ashland is a small Hanover County town

Vocational findings rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational

residents are priced to the evaluee's own market through
