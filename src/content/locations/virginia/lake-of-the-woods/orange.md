## Vocational and Life Care Planning Work in Orange

Orange is a small Virginia town, and litigation arising there tends to put the
question of work at the center of the case: what an injured or displaced
person did for a living, what they can still do, and what it will cost to
care for them going forward. Purinton Analytics answers those questions for
counsel in Orange through [vocational expert](/services/vocational-expert-witness/)

## How the Local Labor Market Enters the Analysis

A vocational opinion about an Orange resident has to reflect the labor
market that person actually faces, and in a small town that market usually
extends well past the municipal boundary. The firm's earning capacity and
employability findings rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational
employment and wage estimates, drawn from the area coverage appropriate to
the evaluee and from Virginia statewide data, supplemented by state
workforce agency labor market information and direct employer research where
the referral questions warrant it. Every source is cited in the report so
that the methodology can be tested on cross-examination. When the damages
model requires an economist, the firm offers
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.
