## Grounding opinions in the local labor market

Vocational findings for evaluees in the Staunton area rest on Bureau of
Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the metro
area and for Virginia statewide, state workforce agency labor market
information, and direct labor market research where warranted. No wage or
employment figure enters a report without a cited source, and the report

evaluee's circumstances. That transparency is what allows the same report
to serve a plaintiff's disclosure obligations or a defense rebuttal with
equal integrity.
