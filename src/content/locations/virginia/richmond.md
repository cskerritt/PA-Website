What counsel in the Richmond metro receive from Purinton Analytics is
deliberately uniform: a documented conflict check, a written scope, a
records request tailored to the claims, and a signed report that discloses
its methods and sources so that either side of the caption can examine it.
That consistency, rather than local advertising, is the basis on which
plaintiff and defense attorneys in the region retain the firm for
[vocational expert evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) of opposing
expert work.

## Venue coverage in the Richmond metro

Engagements are accepted for matters in the circuit courts of the
independent cities of Richmond, Colonial Heights, Hopewell, and Petersburg,
and in the Circuit Court of Amelia County, Charles City County, Chesterfield
County, Dinwiddie County, Goochland County, Hanover County, Henrico County,
King and Queen County, King William County, New Kent County, Powhatan
County, Prince George County, and Sussex County. Federal matters proceed in
the United States District Court for the Eastern District of Virginia, and
the firm also appears in the United States District Court for the Western
District of Virginia when a matter is venued there. Counsel with cases
elsewhere in the Commonwealth can review the firm's
[Virginia](/locations/virginia/) coverage, and matters beyond the state line
are served through the [national practice](/locations/nationwide/).

## Working by appointment

Richmond meetings are by appointment. The firm keeps no staffed office in
the metro and publishes no street address for this location; candor about
presence is part of the same discipline that governs the reports themselves.
Most engagements begin remotely, with records review, structured interviews,
and standardized testing administered under publisher protocols where remote
administration is supported. When the referral questions call for in-person
evaluation, or when a deposition or trial requires attendance, travel is
scheduled from the Kansas City home office and confirmed against counsel's
calendar at intake, so scheduling risk is addressed before the engagement
letter is signed rather than after.

## Grounding findings in the Richmond labor market

Employability and earning capacity opinions for evaluees in the metro rest
on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for
the Richmond metropolitan area and for Virginia statewide, supplemented by
state workforce agency labor market information and direct employer contact
where warranted. Life care plans and medical cost projections are priced to
the evaluee's own market for care using provider-specific inquiries and
published fee data. When a matter requires a full damages presentation, the
firm offers
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Beginning an engagement

The path into any Richmond metro matter starts with a conflict check, not a
sales call. Submit the caption or party names through the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/), identify the referral questions as
you currently understand them, and flag any imminent disclosure, deposition,
or trial dates. Any venue-specific registration or qualification requirement
is identified and confirmed during the conflict check and intake process,
and counsel receive a written scope before any substantive work begins.
