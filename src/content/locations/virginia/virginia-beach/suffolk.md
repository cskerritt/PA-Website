A Suffolk resident's working life rarely stays inside Suffolk. The city
belongs to the commuting shed of the Virginia Beach and Chesapeake and
Norfolk metropolitan area, which means the jobs a Suffolk evaluee held
before an injury, and the jobs still open afterward, are spread across an
entire region. Any earning capacity opinion that ignores that reality is
vulnerable, and Purinton Analytics builds its
[vocational expert evaluations](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) and
[life care plans](/services/life-care-planning/) for Suffolk matters on the
regional market from the outset, for counsel on either side of the caption.

## The commuting market as the unit of analysis

Vocational findings for a Suffolk evaluee rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates for the metropolitan area and for
Virginia statewide, supplemented by state workforce agency labor market
information and, where the referral questions justify it, direct employer
research. The analysis never leans on generalizations about what a city like
Suffolk contains; it identifies the occupational categories the evaluee's
documented history supports and tests their availability across the market
the evaluee can actually reach. The
[metro practice page](/locations/virginia/virginia-beach/) sets out how the
firm treats the region as a whole.

## Venue for Suffolk litigation

Civil matters arising in Suffolk are generally filed in the Circuit Court
sitting in the city. Where a claim belongs in federal court, Virginia's
available forums are the United States District Court for the Eastern
District of Virginia and the United States District Court for the Western
District of Virginia, and the controlling venue is confirmed during the
conflict check and intake. The report does not change with the courthouse:
in either forum it discloses its methodology, cites sources opposing counsel
can pull, and draws no conclusion the record cannot bear. Statewide venue
context appears on the [Virginia practice page](/locations/virginia/).

## Evaluating in Suffolk, reporting from anywhere

The firm keeps no staffed office in Suffolk and prints no local address,
because a practice that verifies everything else should not be vague about
where it actually sits. Engagements typically open remotely, with records
review, a structured interview, and standardized testing administered under
publisher protocols that permit remote use. When the referral questions
require evaluating the person in their own community, or when deposition or
trial requires the expert in the room, travel is arranged from the Kansas
City home office and planned around counsel's schedule. The
[nationwide page](/locations/nationwide/) describes the remote-first
structure that makes this workable at any distance.

## Assembling the rest of the damages case

Serious injury litigation seldom ends with the vocational question. The firm
offers [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure, and it accepts
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) assignments
limited to methodology critique when an opposing expert's report is already
in hand.

## Referring a Suffolk matter

Conflict clearance comes first, and earlier is always better for the quality
of the evaluation. Submit the caption or party names through the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) with any imminent disclosure,
