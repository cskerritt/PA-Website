Start with the person to be evaluated. A Portsmouth evaluee recovering from
a serious injury may find travel difficult, may be mid-treatment, and may
never have sat for standardized testing before; how the evaluation is
arranged around those realities shapes the quality of everything that
follows. Purinton Analytics structures its
[vocational expert evaluations](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) and
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) for Portsmouth matters
around the evaluee's circumstances, while writing for an audience of
lawyers, on both sides, who will test every step.

## How the evaluation actually unfolds

Most Portsmouth engagements open remotely. Records arrive and are reviewed
in full before any interview, the structured interview itself is scheduled
at the evaluee's convenience, and standardized instruments are administered
under publisher protocols wherever remote administration is supported. When
the referral questions require an in-person evaluation, the firm comes to
the evaluee's community rather than requiring the evaluee to come to it,
with travel scheduled from the Kansas City home office. The same trip
planning covers deposition and trial appearances. There is no staffed
Purinton Analytics office in Portsmouth and no published address here; the
firm states that outright, since precision about small facts is the habit
that produces precision about large ones. The
[nationwide page](/locations/nationwide/) explains the remote-first model in
full.

## Courts hearing Portsmouth cases

Civil litigation arising in Portsmouth is generally filed in the Circuit
Court sitting in the city. For matters that belong in federal court,
Virginia's forums are the United States District Court for the Eastern
District of Virginia and the United States District Court for the Western
District of Virginia; which one controls a particular case is settled during
the conflict check and intake, never assumed. Reports carry the same
architecture into either forum: disclosed methodology, citable sources, and
conclusions that go no further than the record. The
[Virginia practice page](/locations/virginia/) describes the statewide
practice these engagements belong to.

## Portsmouth wages, regional data

Because Portsmouth sits inside the Virginia Beach and Chesapeake and Norfolk
metropolitan area, its residents' employment options are regional, and the
firm's labor market work reflects that. Vocational findings draw on Bureau
of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the metro
area and for Virginia statewide, on state workforce agency labor market
information, and on direct employer research where the questions warrant.
The [metro practice page](/locations/virginia/virginia-beach/) covers the

## Beyond the vocational opinion

Where a Portsmouth case requires the complete damages picture, the firm
arranges [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)

analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. It also takes
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) assignments
restricted to methodology critique of an opposing expert's report, on the
same conflict-checked footing as any other engagement.

## Bringing the firm a Portsmouth case

Begin with the conflict check, and begin it early enough that the evaluation
can be done properly. Submit the caption or party names through the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/), note any imminent disclosure,
deposition, or trial dates, and describe the evaluee's situation, including
whether travel is realistic or the work should come to them.
