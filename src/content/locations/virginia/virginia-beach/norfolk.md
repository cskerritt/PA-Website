Counsel on opposite sides of a Norfolk case need the same thing from a
vocational expert: an opinion that survives the other side's scrutiny.
A plaintiff's lawyer needs earning capacity loss documented well enough to
withstand cross-examination; a defense lawyer needs an independent check on
claims that may be overstated. Purinton Analytics serves both, providing
[vocational expert evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) and
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) for Norfolk matters with
reports written on the assumption that the most careful reader will be
opposing counsel.

## Norfolk venue and the available forums

Norfolk is one of the named principal cities of the Virginia Beach and
Chesapeake and Norfolk metropolitan area, and civil matters arising here are
generally filed in the Circuit Court sitting in the city. Where a claim
belongs in federal court, the state's forums are the United States District
Court for the Eastern District of Virginia and the United States District
Court for the Western District of Virginia; the controlling venue for a
given matter is confirmed during the conflict check and intake. Reports are
prepared identically for either forum, with methodology stated, sources
cited, and conclusions limited to what the record will carry. Statewide
context for these engagements appears on the
[Virginia practice page](/locations/virginia/).

## Reading the Norfolk labor market honestly

An urban center draws workers from across its region, so the earning
capacity of a Norfolk evaluee is measured against the metropolitan labor
market as a whole. The firm grounds vocational findings in Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the metro area
and for Virginia statewide, in state workforce agency labor market
information, and in direct labor market research where the referral
questions call for it. What the analysis never does is substitute
impressions of a city for data: transferable skills are traced from the
evaluee's actual documented work history, and placement opinions identify
the specific occupational categories the record supports.

## How a Norfolk evaluation proceeds

The firm keeps no staffed Norfolk office and lists no street address here;
meetings happen by appointment, and stating that plainly is part of the
discipline the reports themselves are built on. Records review, structured
interview, and standardized testing typically begin remotely, using
publisher-approved remote administration where the instruments allow it.
In-person evaluation of a Norfolk evaluee, and appearances for deposition or
trial, are scheduled as travel from the Kansas City home office. The
[metro page](/locations/virginia/virginia-beach/) sets out how the practice

## Damages coordination and opposing-expert review

When a Norfolk matter needs a full damages picture, the firm structures
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. Counsel holding an opposing expert's report can also
retain the firm for [rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/)
confined to methodology, an assignment the two-sided practice is built to
handle without advocacy creeping into the critique.

## Starting a Norfolk engagement

The strongest expert work is retained early, before disclosure deadlines
compress the evaluation. Submit the caption or party names through the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/), flag any imminent disclosure,
deposition, or trial dates, and indicate whether the evaluee should be seen
in the community or can participate remotely.
