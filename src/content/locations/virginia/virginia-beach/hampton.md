## Hampton's courts and the question of forum

Hampton sits within the Virginia Beach and Chesapeake and Norfolk
metropolitan area, and civil matters arising in the city are generally filed
in the Circuit Court sitting there. Federal claims proceed in one of
Virginia's two district courts, the United States District Court for the
Eastern District of Virginia or the United States District Court for the
Western District of Virginia, with the controlling venue for a given case
identified during the conflict check and intake. The forum changes the
procedural posture but never the report: methodology is disclosed, every
source is citable, and opinions stop where the record stops. The
[Virginia practice page](/locations/virginia/) covers the statewide
framework.

## What the disclosure calendar demands of the work

A defensible vocational opinion has stages that resist compression: complete
records must be gathered, the evaluee interviewed, standardized instruments
administered to protocol, and labor market research completed before writing
begins. Retained early, the firm sequences those stages around mediation and
disclosure dates; retained late, it will say candidly what can and cannot be
done well in the time remaining. That candor is the same trait counsel will
want in the report itself when the other side reads it.

## The labor market frame for a Hampton evaluee

Hampton residents participate in a regional employment market rather than a
purely local one, so earning capacity is assessed against the metropolitan
area, using Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage
estimates for the metro and for Virginia statewide, alongside state
workforce agency labor market information and direct employer contact where
warranted. The [metro practice page](/locations/virginia/virginia-beach/)
describes the regional market in fuller detail. Transferable skills analysis
works forward from the evaluee's documented history, not backward from a
desired conclusion.
