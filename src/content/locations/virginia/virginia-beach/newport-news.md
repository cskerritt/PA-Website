The question at the center of most Newport News referrals is deceptively
simple: what can this person still earn, and what will their care cost? The
answer determines settlement posture and trial strategy alike, and it cannot
be assumed; it has to be built from records, testing, and labor market data
that hold up under examination. Purinton Analytics answers that question for
Newport News matters through
[vocational expert evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) and
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), on referral from
plaintiff and defense counsel in equal measure.

## The courts serving Newport News matters

Newport News is a city of substantial size within the Virginia Beach and
Chesapeake and Norfolk metropolitan area, and civil litigation arising here
is generally filed in the Circuit Court sitting in the city. Virginia's
federal forums are the United States District Court for the Eastern District
of Virginia and the United States District Court for the Western District of
Virginia, and which forum controls a particular case is a question resolved
during the conflict check and intake rather than assumed in advance. Every
report is written so that it functions the same way in either court:
methodology disclosed, sources traceable, opinions bounded by the record.
The [Virginia practice page](/locations/virginia/) explains the statewide
approach.

## Measuring earning capacity across the metro

A Newport News evaluee's employment prospects extend well beyond city
limits, so the analysis uses the metropolitan labor market as its frame.
Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for
the metro area and for Virginia statewide anchor the vocational findings,
with state workforce agency labor market information and targeted employer
research added where the referral questions require it. The
[metro practice page](/locations/virginia/virginia-beach/) describes this
regional frame; what matters at the town level is that transferable skills
analysis starts from the evaluee's own documented work history and ends with
occupational categories the data actually supports.

## Life care planning built on the medical record

Where injuries are catastrophic, the care plan matters as much as the
earnings analysis. The firm's life care plans are grounded in the medical
record and treating-provider recommendations, with each item and its source
identified so that opposing counsel can test the plan line by line. When a
matter needs the full damages picture, the firm offers
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Seeing a Newport News evaluee

There is no staffed Purinton Analytics office in Newport News and no
published street address here; meetings are by appointment, and the firm
says so plainly because candor about presence is inseparable from candor in
reporting. Engagements usually open remotely with records review, interview,
and standardized testing where publisher protocols permit remote
administration. When the referral calls for in-person evaluation in the
evaluee's own community, or when deposition and trial dates demand
attendance, travel is arranged from the Kansas City home office. The
[nationwide page](/locations/nationwide/) sets out how this remote-first
model works across the country.
