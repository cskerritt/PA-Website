Distance is the first question counsel in the Roanoke metro tend to ask, and
the honest answer is that Purinton Analytics serves the region the way it
serves most of the country: remotely first, in person when the case requires
it, with travel scheduled from the Kansas City home office. That model was
built for exactly this kind of engagement, and it lets plaintiff and defense
counsel in the region retain the same
[vocational expert](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[expert testimony](/services/expert-testimony-litigation-consulting/)
support available anywhere in the firm's
[national practice](/locations/nationwide/), without depending on a local
storefront that does not exist.

## Circuit and federal venues

Engagements are accepted for matters in the circuit courts of the
independent cities of Roanoke and Salem and in the Circuit Court of
Botetourt County, Craig County, Franklin County, and Roanoke County. Federal
matters from the region proceed in the United States District Court for the
Western District of Virginia, and the firm also accepts matters venued in
the United States District Court for the Eastern District of Virginia when a
case moves east. Statewide coverage, including the Commonwealth's other
metros, is described on the firm's [Virginia](/locations/virginia/) page,
and venue is confirmed as part of intake rather than assumed.

## Distance, and how the firm manages it

There is no staffed Purinton Analytics office in the Roanoke metro and no
published street address here; meetings are by appointment. Most engagements
begin remotely, with records review, structured vocational interviews, and
standardized testing administered under publisher protocols where remote
administration is supported. When the referral questions call for an
in-person evaluation, the evaluation is scheduled in the evaluee's own
community, and when deposition or trial dates require presence, travel is
arranged from the Kansas City home office with enough lead time to protect
the case calendar. Counsel receive the same report standard and the same
point of contact regardless of where the work is performed, which is the
point of the model.

## Labor market research

Vocational findings for evaluees in the region rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Roanoke
metropolitan area and for Virginia statewide, together with state workforce
agency labor market information and direct labor market research where the
referral questions warrant it. The analysis is anchored to the evaluee's
actual commuting market rather than to statewide averages, because
employability and placeability conclusions are only as credible as the
market they describe. Life care plans and medical cost projections are
priced to the evaluee's own market for care, and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
are available in which independent economist partners perform and sign the
economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under
one engagement structure.
