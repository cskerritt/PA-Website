Bluefield, Virginia shares its name with the principal city of the
metropolitan area that surrounds it, and the state line running through that
metro does more than divide a map. It decides which state's courts hear a
claim, which body of damages law applies, and which venue's disclosure
schedule controls the expert's calendar. Purinton Analytics serves attorneys
litigating on the Virginia side of that line, providing
[vocational expert](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) opinions,
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. Plaintiff and defense referrals are handled with the
same methodology, because a report written for either side of the caption
must survive scrutiny from the other.

## Venue context in Tazewell County

Bluefield sits in Tazewell County, and state-court matters arising there
proceed in the Circuit Court. For federal claims, Virginia counsel may be
litigating in the United States District Court for the Western District of
Virginia or the United States District Court for the Eastern District of
Virginia; the controlling district and division for any particular matter
are confirmed during the conflict check and intake rather than assumed. The
firm's reports are structured for either forum: a defined scope, a records
request tailored to the claims, stated methodology, and opinions that can be
traced from the underlying documents to the conclusions, so that direct and
cross-examination proceed from the same written record.

## A labor market that crosses the state line

Earning-capacity questions for a Bluefield evaluee rarely stop at the town
boundary, because the commuting market itself does not. The analysis
considers the work realistically available across the Bluefield metropolitan
area, which spans West Virginia and Virginia, alongside conditions in the
wider region. Vocational findings rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates for the metro area and for
Virginia statewide, state workforce agency labor market information, and
direct labor market research where the referral questions warrant it. The
qualitative character of a smaller regional market, where suitable
alternative employment may be thinner than in a large city, is addressed
explicitly rather than papered over, since that character often drives the
mitigation analysis on both sides.

## Meetings and evaluations by appointment

The firm keeps no staffed office in Bluefield and publishes no street
address for this location; accuracy about presence is part of the same
discipline that governs the reports. Counsel conferences, records review,
and many evaluations proceed remotely. When the referral calls for in-person
vocational evaluation, it is scheduled in the evaluee's own community, and
when deposition or trial dates require presence, travel is arranged from the
Kansas City home office with the litigation calendar in mind. The

## Starting an engagement

Early retention protects the disclosure calendar, particularly where records
must be gathered from multiple providers before an evaluation can be
scheduled. To begin, [request a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) with the
case caption and party names, and note any imminent report deadline,
deposition, or trial setting so scheduling can be confirmed at intake.
