## Courts serving Tazewell County matters

Richlands lies in Tazewell County, where state-court claims are heard in
the Circuit Court. Federal matters involving Virginia parties may proceed
in the United States District Court for the Western District of Virginia
or the United States District Court for the Eastern District of Virginia,
and the district and division that control a given case are identified
during the conflict check and intake. Reports are written to a single
standard regardless of forum: methodology stated, sources cited, and every
opinion traceable to the record, so the document holds up under
cross-examination as well as it reads on direct.

## Transferable skills and the regional market

Richlands belongs to the Bluefield metropolitan area, a labor market that
spans West Virginia and Virginia, and the employment realistically open to
a Richlands evaluee must be assessed against that regional market rather
than against national averages alone. The firm grounds its vocational
findings in Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage
estimates for the metro area and for Virginia statewide, supplemented by
state workforce agency labor market information and direct employer
research where warranted. In a smaller regional market,
transferable-skills analysis carries particular weight, because the
difference between a theoretical occupation and one actually hiring within
commuting distance is frequently where the parties' damages positions
diverge. Where the claim requires a full economic loss calculation, the
firm offers
[coordinated damages assessments](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## How a Richlands evaluation proceeds

An engagement begins with a documented conflict check and a defined scope,
followed by a records request tailored to the claims. Once records are in
hand, the evaluation itself is scheduled around the evaluee: a remote
format where the referral questions permit it, or an in-person session
arranged locally when standardized testing or the nature of the impairment
requires one. Travel, when needed, is scheduled from the Kansas City home
office; the firm maintains no staffed office in Richlands and publishes no
street address here, because candor about presence belongs in the same
category as candor about methodology.
