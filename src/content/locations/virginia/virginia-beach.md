The Virginia Beach-Chesapeake-Norfolk metro is one of the markets in the
practice where a case footprint can cross a state line: the metropolitan
statistical area takes in independent cities and counties on the Virginia
side together with counties in North Carolina. That shape is why Purinton
Analytics confirms venue at intake, defines the scope in writing, and builds
each opinion to hold up wherever the matter sits. Counsel on either side of
the caption retain the firm for
[vocational expert evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/) work.

## Venue footprint across the metro

Engagements are accepted for Virginia matters venued in the Circuit Court,
whether the case arises in the independent cities of Chesapeake, Hampton,
Newport News, Norfolk, Poquoson, Portsmouth, Suffolk, Virginia Beach, and
Williamsburg or in Gloucester County, Isle of Wight County, James City
County, Mathews County, Surry County, and York County. Federal matters
proceed in the United States District Court for the Eastern District of
Virginia or, where venue requires, the United States District Court for the
Western District of Virginia. The metro also extends into Camden County,
Currituck County, and Gates County in North Carolina; those matters are
handled through the firm's [national practice](/locations/nationwide/) under
the same engagement terms. Statewide questions, including venues elsewhere
in the Commonwealth, are covered on the [Virginia](/locations/virginia/)
page.

## By-appointment presence, honestly described

There is no staffed Purinton Analytics office in this metro, and the firm
publishes no street address for it. Meetings are by appointment, and most
engagements begin remotely: records review, structured vocational
interviews, and standardized testing under publisher protocols where remote
administration is supported. When an in-person evaluation is warranted, or
when deposition and trial dates require attendance, travel is scheduled from
the Kansas City home office and planned around the case calendar. Counsel
receive the same report standard and the same point of contact whether the
work is performed remotely or on site.

## Labor market evidence for the metro

Vocational findings for evaluees in the region rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Virginia
Beach metropolitan area and for Virginia statewide, along with state
workforce agency labor market information and direct labor market research
where the referral questions call for it. For evaluees on the North
Carolina side of the metro, the analysis is grounded in the corresponding
North Carolina data so that employability and earning capacity conclusions
reflect the evaluee's actual commuting market. Life care plans and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/),
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure, are priced and documented to the same standard.

## Requesting a conflict check

Every engagement in the metro begins with a documented conflict check and a
defined scope. To begin,
[request a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) with the case caption and
parties, and note any disclosure deadlines, hearing dates, or evaluation
constraints. Any venue-specific registration or qualification requirement is
identified and confirmed during the conflict check and intake process.
