Counsel litigating injury, disability, and employment matters in the
Harrisonburg, VA metropolitan area retain Purinton Analytics for
[vocational evaluation and expert testimony](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. The practice accepts work from either side of the
caption, and every report is written to the same standard: a documented
conflict check, a defined scope, and a records request tailored to the
claims, so that the written opinion rests on a record both sides can
examine.

## Venue footprint in the Harrisonburg area

Engagements are accepted for matters arising in Rockingham County and
Harrisonburg city, including proceedings in the Circuit Court for each
jurisdiction. Federal matters proceed in the United States District Court
for the Western District of Virginia and the United States District Court
for the Eastern District of Virginia, and the firm's reports are prepared
with the disclosure and testimony expectations of federal practice in mind
regardless of where the case is filed. Counsel with related matters
elsewhere in the Commonwealth can review the firm's
[Virginia coverage](/locations/virginia/) for the statewide picture,
including any venue-specific registration or qualification requirement,
which is identified and confirmed during the conflict check and intake
process.

## Meetings by appointment

Harrisonburg meetings and evaluations are by appointment. The firm keeps no
staffed office in the metro and publishes no street address for this
location, because accuracy about presence is part of the same discipline
that governs its reports. Most engagement activity, from records
conferences to attorney preparation sessions, proceeds remotely on a
predictable schedule. When the referral questions call for in-person
evaluation, or when deposition and trial settings require presence in the
area, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office and planned
against the operative disclosure calendar rather than left open-ended.
Evaluations can be conducted in the evaluee's own community, at counsel's
offices, or remotely when the referral questions and testing protocols
permit it.

## Labor market research for Harrisonburg matters

Employability and earning capacity findings for evaluees in the
Harrisonburg area are grounded in data specific to the market where the
evaluee actually works and lives. The analysis draws on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Harrisonburg
metropolitan area and for Virginia statewide, state workforce agency labor
market information, and direct labor market research where the referral
warrants it. The report is explicit about which geographic market defines
the evaluee's commutable labor market and why, so that the foundation for
each opinion can be tested on cross-examination rather than taken on
faith.

## Starting an engagement

The firm serves Harrisonburg counsel as part of a
[national practice](/locations/nationwide/), which means local matters
receive the same methodology, the same reporting standards, and the same
scheduling discipline as engagements anywhere else in the country. The
first step is always the same: submit the caption or party names through
the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and note any imminent
disclosure, deposition, or trial dates. Once the conflict check clears,
counsel receive a records checklist tailored to the claims and a proposed
timeline set against the deadlines that actually govern the case.
