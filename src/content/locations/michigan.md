A vocational opinion earns its weight in cross-examination, and Purinton Analytics writes for that test in every Michigan engagement. Reports state their methodology, identify their sources, and separate findings from assumptions, so that plaintiff and defense counsel alike can put them in front of an opposing expert without surprise. The same discipline extends to damages: the firm offers [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement structure, keeping the roles of vocational expert and economist distinct on the record.

## Michigan courts and venues

Engagements are accepted for matters pending in the Circuit Court, in the United States District Court for the Eastern District of Michigan, and in the United States District Court for the Western District of Michigan. Forum does not change the work product; it changes only the disclosure and scheduling requirements the engagement is run against.

Dedicated pages cover the practice's work in Michigan's metropolitan areas: Detroit-Warren-Dearborn, MI, Grand Rapids-Wyoming-Kentwood, MI, Lansing-East Lansing, MI, Flint, MI, Ann Arbor, MI, Kalamazoo-Portage, MI, Saginaw, MI, Muskegon-Norton Shores, MI, Jackson, MI, Traverse City, MI, Monroe, MI, Niles, MI, Battle Creek, MI, Bay City, MI, and Midland, MI. A matter that arises outside those markets is handled the same way; the metro pages exist for counsel's convenience, not as a limit on where the practice accepts work.

## How the firm serves Michigan

Records review, transferable skills analysis, labor market research, and report drafting are remote-first, and conferences with counsel are ordinarily held by videoconference. Michigan meetings are by appointment. The firm maintains no staffed office in the state and publishes no Michigan street address; candor about presence is part of the same standard that governs the reports themselves. When an evaluation is better conducted in person, or when deposition and trial dates require attendance, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office and coordinated with the dates counsel supplies at intake.

Vocational findings for Michigan evaluees rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the relevant metropolitan area and for Michigan statewide, supplemented by state workforce agency labor market information and by direct labor market research where the referral questions call for it. Every source relied on is cited, so that a finding can be checked by either side rather than taken on faith.

## Qualification and disclosure

Because expert practice requirements vary by forum, qualification is treated as an intake task rather than an afterthought. Any venue-specific registration or qualification requirement is identified and confirmed during the conflict check and intake process, and disclosure deadlines are docketed against the schedule counsel provides before the substantive work begins.

## Starting a Michigan engagement

To begin, [request a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) with the case caption and parties, and flag any imminent disclosure, deposition, or trial dates so availability can be confirmed at the same time as clearance. Engagements are accepted from either side of the caption on identical terms. Counsel with matters beyond Michigan, or with a docket that crosses state lines, can review the [nationwide practice](/locations/nationwide/) for the full scope of services offered under the same engagement structure.
