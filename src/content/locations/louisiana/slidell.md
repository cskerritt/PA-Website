The Slidell-Mandeville-Covington metro lies wholly within St. Tammany
Parish, which makes the venue picture unusually clean: counsel here are
most often preparing a matter for the District Court serving St. Tammany
Parish. Purinton Analytics supports those cases, for plaintiff and defense
alike, with [vocational expert opinions](/services/vocational-expert-witness/)
on earning capacity and employability,
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) in catastrophic injury
litigation, and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## State and federal tracks from the same parish

Alongside St. Tammany Parish matters in state court, the firm accepts
engagements in Louisiana's federal courts: the United States District Court
for the Eastern District of Louisiana, the United States District Court for
the Middle District of Louisiana, and the United States District Court for
the Western District of Louisiana. A case that begins in the parish
courthouse and is later removed keeps the same expert, the same file, and
the same report standard. Counsel working matters beyond the metro can
start from the [Louisiana coverage page](/locations/louisiana/), which
applies the identical structure statewide.

## Appointments in Slidell, Mandeville, and Covington

The firm keeps no staffed office anywhere in the metro and publishes no
street address for this location; meetings in Slidell, Mandeville, and
Covington are scheduled by appointment. Saying that plainly is part of the
practice's credibility, because a report about a person's working life
should come from an expert who is equally precise about his own footprint.
Most engagements run remotely from the outset: electronic records review,
structured vocational interviews, and standardized testing under publisher
protocols where remote administration is supported. In-person evaluations,
depositions, and trial appearances are handled through travel scheduled
from the Kansas City home office, coordinated with the dates counsel flag
during intake, and the engagement letter records where each step of the
work will occur so there are no surprises when the schedule tightens.

## The labor market behind the findings

Vocational conclusions for evaluees in the metro rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates covering the local
area and Louisiana statewide, supplemented by state workforce agency labor
market information and direct labor market research where the referral
questions warrant it. Life care plans and
[medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/) are priced
to the evaluee's own market rather than a national composite, and the
report discloses each source so that either side of the caption can audit
the path from data to opinion. When counsel already hold an opposing
report, the same standards support
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/).

## Getting a matter started

Engagements open with a documented conflict check and a defined scope,
here as in the firm's [national practice](/locations/nationwide/). Provide
the caption or party names through the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and note any imminent disclosure,
deposition, or trial dates so scheduling can be settled at intake rather
than negotiated under deadline pressure. Any
venue-specific registration or qualification requirement is identified and
confirmed during the conflict check and intake process.
