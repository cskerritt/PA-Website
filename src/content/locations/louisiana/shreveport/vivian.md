Small towns do not keep resident forensic experts, and counsel with a
Vivian case have usually accepted that the right expert will come from
somewhere else. Purinton Analytics built its practice around that fact.
From its Kansas City home office, the firm delivers
[vocational expert opinions](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) and
[life care plans](/services/life-care-planning/) to communities the size of
Vivian on the same terms it offers major metros, and it accepts plaintiff
and defense referrals without preference between them.

## Caddo Parish courts and Louisiana's federal districts

Vivian lies in Caddo Parish, and its state-court matters are heard in the
District Court serving Caddo Parish. Louisiana's federal trial courts, the
United States District Court for the Western District of Louisiana, the
United States District Court for the Middle District of Louisiana, and the
United States District Court for the Eastern District of Louisiana, stand
as the available federal forums; intake and the conflict check identify
which governs a particular case and what qualification it demands of a
testifying expert. That sequencing keeps venue questions out of the opinion
itself, where they do not belong. Coverage across the rest of the state is
set out on the [Louisiana page](/locations/louisiana/).

## A small town inside a metro market

For labor market analysis, Vivian belongs to the Shreveport-Bossier City,
LA metropolitan area, and the classification matters less than what the
analysis does with it. Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment
and wage estimates for the metro and for Louisiana statewide anchor the
opinion; state workforce agency labor market information and direct labor
market inquiry refine it. Transferable skills are matched to occupations
before any placeability conclusion is offered. The report is explicit about
the difference between opportunity as a metro-level abstraction and
opportunity as it actually presents to a resident of a small Caddo Parish
town, because that gap is where cross-examination tends to begin. Writing
the limitation into the opinion, rather than waiting for opposing counsel
to find it, is the firm's standing habit.

## The evaluation comes to the evaluee

No part of the model requires the evaluee to go find the expert. Records
move electronically, conferences with counsel are remote, and when the
referral questions require an in-person evaluation, the evaluator travels
and the session is held in Vivian's own community, by appointment, at a
time that works around treatment. Life care planning home assessments
follow the same travel pattern when a plan requires them. The firm keeps no
office here and says so without hedging; deposition and trial appearances
are made from the Kansas City home office as the case demands. How the same
model operates across the area is described on the
[Shreveport metro page](/locations/louisiana/shreveport/).
