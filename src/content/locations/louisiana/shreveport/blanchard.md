The question counsel tend to open with on a Blanchard engagement is
practical: where does the evaluation actually happen? The answer is that it
happens where the evaluee is. Purinton Analytics runs a remote-first
practice in which records review, counsel conferences, and analysis proceed
at a distance, while
[vocational evaluations](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) that require
presence are conducted in the evaluee's own community by appointment. The
model is the same whether the referral comes from the plaintiff or the
defense side of the caption.

## Caddo Parish venue notes

Blanchard is a Caddo Parish town, and its state-court matters are tried in
the District Court serving Caddo Parish. Federal cases in Louisiana proceed
in the United States District Court for the Western District of Louisiana,
the United States District Court for the Middle District of Louisiana, or
the United States District Court for the Eastern District of Louisiana,
with the governing venue confirmed at intake alongside any court-specific
qualification requirement for a testifying expert. The report reads the
same in any of those forums: scope stated in writing, sources disclosed,
and conclusions confined to what the record supports. The wider state
picture is on the [Louisiana page](/locations/louisiana/).

## A small town, a metro employment base

For labor market purposes, Blanchard belongs to the Shreveport-Bossier
City, LA metropolitan area. Findings on employability and earning capacity
rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage
estimates for that metro and for Louisiana statewide, supplemented by state
workforce agency labor market information and, where the questions justify
it, direct employer contact. The distinction the analysis works hardest to
preserve is between the jobs a metro reports in the aggregate and the jobs
a Blanchard resident with documented restrictions can actually obtain,
perform, and keep.
[Medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/) and
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) address the parallel
question of future care when the injuries require it, on the same
records-based footing.

## Scheduling, records, and travel

There is no Purinton Analytics office in Blanchard and no published local
address, a fact stated plainly because accuracy about presence is part of
the product counsel are buying. Scheduling follows the case rather than the
firm's convenience: interviews and testing are set in the evaluee's
community on dates that respect treatment and work schedules, records
arrive and return electronically, and in-person appearances for deposition
or trial are staged from the Kansas City home office. Nothing in the
engagement requires the evaluee, or counsel, to travel to the expert.
Broader metro logistics appear on the
[Shreveport metro page](/locations/louisiana/shreveport/).

## Start with the conflict check

Every engagement, whether local or part of the
[national practice](/locations/nationwide/), starts identically: the
caption or party names through the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/), a short description of the claimed
damages, and the operative deadlines. Clearance comes back with a proposed
scope and a records list, so counsel can weigh the retention on paper
before committing to it. Questions about fit and scope are welcome before
any formal referral is made.
