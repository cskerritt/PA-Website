Some retentions are shaped mostly by the injury; a Mansfield retention is
often shaped just as much by the forum. Purinton Analytics prepares
[vocational expert](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) and
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) opinions for De Soto
Parish litigation with the courtroom in view from the outset, on the theory
that a report likely to be read aloud under oath should be written for that
reading. Plaintiff and defense engagements are accepted on the same terms
and to the same standard.

## De Soto Parish and the federal forums

Mansfield matters proceeding under state law are heard in the District
Court serving De Soto Parish. Where a case belongs in federal court,
Louisiana offers the United States District Court for the Western District
of Louisiana, the United States District Court for the Middle District of
Louisiana, and the United States District Court for the Eastern District of
Louisiana; the controlling venue, and any qualification requirement it
imposes on a testifying expert, is identified during the conflict check and
intake. Whatever the forum, the file is assembled the same way: a
documented conflict check, a defined scope, and a records request tailored
to the claims, so that the written opinion rests on a record either side
can examine. Other Louisiana venues are covered from the
[state page](/locations/louisiana/).

## The metro market seen from Mansfield

Mansfield belongs to the Shreveport-Bossier City, LA metropolitan area, and
vocational findings for a Mansfield evaluee draw on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for that metro and
for Louisiana statewide, along with state workforce agency labor market
information and direct labor market research where the questions call for
it. The analysis treats the market qualitatively: what matters is that a
small De Soto Parish community sits within reach of a regional employment
center, and that pre-injury and post-injury findings account for that
geography without exaggeration in either direction. When the claimed
damages require economic modeling,
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
are available, in which independent economist partners perform and sign the
economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds
under one engagement structure.

## Presence, candidly stated

The firm maintains no Mansfield office and lists no local address; meetings
in De Soto Parish are by appointment. Evaluations that require sitting with
the evaluee are conducted in the evaluee's own community, at times that
respect medical treatment and family obligations. The balance of the work,
from records analysis to counsel conferences, proceeds remotely, and when
deposition or trial requires the expert in the room, travel is made from
the Kansas City home office. Metro-wide logistics are described on the
[Shreveport metro page](/locations/louisiana/shreveport/).

if it is known, and identify the expert disclosure and trial dates. The
reply sets out scope, records, and schedule in the same format used across
the [national practice](/locations/nationwide/), so a De Soto Parish case
