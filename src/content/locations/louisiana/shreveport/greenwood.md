In most Greenwood retentions the binding constraint is not geography but
the calendar. Expert disclosure deadlines, deposition settings, and trial
dates arrive on their own schedule, and a vocational or care-planning
opinion is useful only if it is complete, disclosed, and defensible when
they do. Purinton Analytics takes Greenwood engagements with those dates as
the organizing frame, for plaintiff and defense counsel alike, and the
sections below explain how venue, labor market evidence, and logistics are
handled when the clock is already running.

## Where Greenwood cases are heard

Greenwood sits in Caddo Parish, and its state-court litigation proceeds in
the District Court serving Caddo Parish. On the federal side, Louisiana's
district courts are the United States District Court for the Western
District of Louisiana, the United States District Court for the Middle
District of Louisiana, and the United States District Court for the Eastern
District of Louisiana. Which of them controls a given matter, and what
qualification or registration it requires of an expert, is worked out
during the conflict check and intake rather than assumed in advance. Venue
never changes the report standard, only the caption on it. Statewide
coverage is collected on the [Louisiana page](/locations/louisiana/).

## Labor market evidence built to deadline

A compressed schedule does not thin the evidentiary base. Vocational
findings for a Greenwood evaluee rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates for the Shreveport-Bossier City
metropolitan area and for Louisiana statewide, on state workforce agency
labor market information, and on direct labor market research where
warranted, with each source cited so the opinion can be checked line by
line. The town's place in the metro is described qualitatively, and the
analysis separates what the market reports from what the specific evaluee
can do within it. Where the damages theory needs an economist as well,
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
allow independent economist partners to perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure, on a shared timetable.

## By appointment, on the record

The firm has no Greenwood office and claims none. The pieces of the work
that require presence, chiefly the in-person
[vocational evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) and testimony
itself, are set by appointment: the evaluation in the evaluee's own
community, appearances calendared from the Kansas City home office once
disclosure and trial dates are fixed. Everything else, from records
analysis to conference calls with counsel, proceeds remotely, which is
precisely what keeps a tight pretrial schedule workable. The metro's full
venue and logistics picture is on the
[Shreveport metro page](/locations/louisiana/shreveport/).

## Requesting a conflict check on a live schedule

When dates are already set, say so in the opening message. Submit the
caption or party names through the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) along with the disclosure,
deposition, and trial settings, and the response will state plainly whether
the work can be done well in the time available. Declining an impossible
schedule is part of the same discipline that governs the rest of the
[national practice](/locations/nationwide/), and counsel are told quickly
either way.
