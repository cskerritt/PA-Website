## Venue for Stonewall matters

Stonewall's state-court matters proceed in the District Court serving De
Soto Parish. Federal matters in Louisiana are heard in the United States
District Court for the Western District of Louisiana, the United States
District Court for the Middle District of Louisiana, and the United States
District Court for the Eastern District of Louisiana; the conflict check
and intake fix which forum controls a given case, along with any expert
qualification requirement specific to it. The opinions themselves are built
to be venue-portable, with methodology and sourcing documented in the
report so the work holds up through removal or transfer. Louisiana's
remaining venues are addressed on the [state page](/locations/louisiana/).

## Earning capacity beyond the town line

A Stonewall evaluee's employment prospects are analyzed against the
Shreveport-Bossier City, LA metropolitan area, of which the town is part.
The foundation is qualitative use of Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates for that metro and for Louisiana
statewide, joined to state workforce agency labor market information and
direct labor market research where the referral questions warrant it. The
analysis states what the evaluee could earn before injury, what remains
available afterward, and how it knows; gaps in the record are flagged as
gaps rather than papered over. Where the case also needs an economic model,
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
place independent economist partners, who perform and sign the economic
analysis, alongside the vocational and care-planning work under one
engagement structure.

## The conflict check

Retention starts with clearance. Provide the caption or party names through
the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/), describe the claimed vocational
or care-related damages, and identify the deadlines that will govern the
work. The response follows the standard applied across the
[national practice](/locations/nationwide/): conflicts resolved before
scope, scope and records defined in writing, and no conclusion promised
before the record supports it.
