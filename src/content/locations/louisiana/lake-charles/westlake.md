For an evaluee who lives in Westlake, the practical question counsel asks
first is rarely about methodology; it is whether the expert can actually
reach the person. Purinton Analytics is built for that problem. Records
review, structured interviews, and standardized testing proceed remotely
under publisher protocols where remote administration is supported, and
when the referral questions call for an in-person evaluation, the expert
comes to the evaluee's own community rather than requiring the evaluee to
travel. On that foundation the firm provides
[vocational expert opinions](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic

referrals alike.

## Venue: Calcasieu Parish and the federal system

Westlake is a Calcasieu Parish town, and suits arising from events here are
tried in the District Court serving Calcasieu Parish unless they proceed
federally. Louisiana's federal trial forums are the United States District
Court for the Western District of Louisiana, the United States District
Court for the Middle District of Louisiana, and the United States District
Court for the Eastern District of Louisiana; the controlling venue for a
specific matter, and any qualification or registration requirement that
venue carries, is confirmed with referring counsel during the conflict
check and intake. The written opinion is prepared to the same standard for
each forum, so a change of court does not mean a change of expert.

## A small town inside a regional employment center

Westlake is a small suburb, and no responsible earning capacity opinion can
be built from the town's boundaries alone. The firm grounds its vocational
findings in Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage
estimates for the Lake Charles metropolitan area and for Louisiana
statewide, adds state workforce agency labor market information, and
conducts direct labor market research when the occupations in dispute
require it. What the analysis then weighs is access: which of the metro's
occupations a Westlake resident with this work history and these
restrictions can realistically obtain and hold. That access reasoning is
laid out in the report rather than asserted.
