## Courts serving DeQuincy

DeQuincy is part of Calcasieu Parish, and its state-court litigation is
heard in the District Court serving Calcasieu Parish. When a matter is
federal, Louisiana's available forums are the United States District Court
for the Western District of Louisiana, the United States District Court
for the Middle District of Louisiana, and the United States District Court
for the Eastern District of Louisiana. The firm treats the question of
which district governs a specific DeQuincy filing as counsel's call to
confirm, and it verifies the controlling venue, plus any registration or
qualification requirement that venue imposes on a testifying expert,
during the conflict check and intake. A report built on a documented
record needs no adjustment when the forum changes.

## Labor market grounding

A DeQuincy evaluee's earning capacity cannot be measured against the town
alone, so the analysis reaches to the employment base of the Lake Charles,
LA metro and the state beyond it. The sources are Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Lake Charles
metropolitan area and for Louisiana statewide, state workforce agency
labor market information, and direct labor market research where the
occupations in dispute warrant original inquiry. The report then does the
work the sources cannot: it explains which of the region's occupations
this evaluee can actually access from DeQuincy, given restrictions,
transferable skills, and commuting realities, and it cites every source
so the analysis can be checked line by line.
