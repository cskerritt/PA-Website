Sulphur belongs to the commuting core of the Lake Charles, LA metro, and most
vocational questions that arise in Sulphur litigation are metro questions:
what work an injured resident can still perform, and what that work pays,
across an employment market that does not stop at the city limits. Purinton
Analytics answers those questions for plaintiff and defense counsel alike,
delivering [vocational expert opinions](/services/vocational-expert-witness/)
on employability and earning capacity,
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) in catastrophic injury
cases, and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. The practice across the wider metro is described on
the [Lake Charles page](/locations/louisiana/lake-charles/).

## Calcasieu Parish venue and the federal forums

Sulphur sits in Calcasieu Parish, and matters arising here are tried in the
District Court serving Calcasieu Parish. On the federal side, Louisiana
offers the United States District Court for the Western District of
Louisiana, the United States District Court for the Middle District of
Louisiana, and the United States District Court for the Eastern District of
Louisiana; which of them controls a particular filing is a venue question
for counsel, and the firm confirms the operative court, along with any
venue-specific registration or qualification requirement, during the
conflict check and intake. Methodology does not shift with the forum: the
same documented file supports a parish courtroom, a removal, and a federal
deposition without revision of the underlying analysis.

## Sulphur's place in the regional labor market

Because Sulphur's workforce participates in a labor market organized around
Lake Charles, earning capacity findings for a Sulphur evaluee rest on
Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for
the Lake Charles metropolitan area and for Louisiana statewide, supplemented
by state workforce agency labor market information and direct labor market
research where the referral questions warrant it. Transferable skills
analysis considers the occupations actually reachable from Sulphur across
the metro rather than any single employer, and every wage source relied on
in the opinion is cited in the report itself, so opposing counsel can trace
each finding back to its origin.

## How a Sulphur evaluation proceeds

The firm keeps no office in Sulphur and publishes no street address here.
Meetings are by appointment, and the firm states that plainly because
accuracy about presence is part of the discipline that governs its reports.
Most engagements proceed remotely: records review, a structured interview,
and standardized testing under publisher protocols where remote
administration is supported, with the administration conditions noted in
the file. When the referral questions call for an in-person evaluation, or
when a deposition or trial date requires the expert in the room, travel is
scheduled from the Kansas City home office and confirmed at intake. The
same standards apply to engagements far beyond Louisiana, as the
[nationwide page](/locations/nationwide/) explains.
