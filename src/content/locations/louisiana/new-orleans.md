Every opinion Purinton Analytics issues for a New Orleans matter is written to
be read by opposing counsel. That standard shapes the firm's work for
plaintiff and defense lawyers alike across the New Orleans-Metairie metro:
[vocational expert opinions](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) on earning
capacity and employability, [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/)
in catastrophic injury cases,
[medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/), and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Venues across the metro parishes

Engagements are accepted for matters venued in the District Courts serving
Orleans Parish, Jefferson Parish, Plaquemines Parish, St. Bernard Parish,
St. Charles Parish, St. James Parish, and St. John the Baptist Parish.
Federal matters are supported in the United States District Court for the
Eastern District of Louisiana, the United States District Court for the
Middle District of Louisiana, and the United States District Court for the
Western District of Louisiana, so a case that is removed, transferred, or
refiled outside the metro does not need a new expert. Counsel with matters
elsewhere in the state can begin from the firm's
[Louisiana overview](/locations/louisiana/), which describes the same
engagement structure applied statewide.

## Meetings by appointment, not a storefront

There is no staffed Purinton Analytics office in New Orleans or Metairie,
and the firm publishes no street address for this location, because accuracy
about offices belongs to the same discipline that governs its reports.
Meetings, evaluations, and testimony preparation sessions are scheduled by
appointment. Most engagements begin remotely, with records review,
structured interviews, and standardized testing administered under publisher
protocols where remote administration is supported. When the referral
questions call for in-person evaluation, or when deposition and trial dates
require presence in the metro, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home
office and confirmed against the case calendar, so counsel are never left
guessing about availability as disclosure deadlines approach.

## Labor market research for New Orleans evaluees

Vocational findings rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational
employment and wage estimates for the New Orleans-Metairie metropolitan area
and for Louisiana statewide, supplemented by state workforce agency labor
market information and direct labor market research where the referral
questions warrant it. Life care plans and medical cost projections are
priced to the evaluee's own market rather than to national averages, and the
sources behind every figure are disclosed in the report so that either side
of the caption can trace the analysis from record to conclusion. Where an
opposing expert has already opined, the same sourcing discipline supports
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) work.

## Starting a New Orleans engagement

New engagements begin the way they do everywhere in the practice: with a
documented conflict check and a defined scope, whether the matter sits in a
metro parish or anywhere else in the firm's
[national coverage](/locations/nationwide/). Submit the caption or party
names through the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and note any
imminent disclosure, deposition, or trial dates so scheduling can be
confirmed at intake. Any venue-specific registration or qualification
requirement is identified and confirmed during the conflict check and intake
process.
