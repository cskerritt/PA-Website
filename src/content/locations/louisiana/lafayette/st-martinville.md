An expert opinion in a St. Martinville case is only as strong as the
record beneath it. Purinton Analytics builds that record deliberately:
medical documentation, work and education history, standardized test
results, and cited labor market data are assembled before conclusions are
drawn, so the written opinion can be examined page by page by whichever
side did not retain the firm. This record-first habit governs every
[vocational expert witness](/services/vocational-expert-witness/)
engagement and every [life care plan](/services/life-care-planning/) the
firm prepares, for plaintiff and defense counsel in equal measure.

## The venue picture for St. Martin Parish

St. Martinville matters filed in state court are ordinarily heard in the
District Court serving St. Martin Parish. When a case belongs in federal
court, the available Louisiana forums are the United States District Court
for the Western District of Louisiana, the United States District Court
for the Middle District of Louisiana, and the United States District Court
for the Eastern District of Louisiana, with the controlling venue
identified during the conflict check and intake alongside any
registration or qualification requirement that court imposes. The record
supporting the opinion is assembled the same way for every forum, which is
precisely why it transfers between them.

## Sourcing the labor market conclusions

St. Martinville is a small community within the Lafayette metro, and its
evaluees are assessed against the regional market they genuinely
participate in. The firm relies on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational
employment and wage estimates for the Lafayette metropolitan area and for
Louisiana statewide, on state workforce agency labor market information,
and on direct labor market research when the referral questions require
it; each item enters the record with its citation attached. Future care
costs in a life care plan or
[medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/) are
documented to the same standard and priced to the evaluee's own market.

[Lafayette metro page](/locations/louisiana/lafayette/).

## Building the record with a local evaluee

The firm maintains no St. Martinville office and publishes no street
address for this location; accuracy about presence is part of the record
discipline this page describes. Most record-building happens remotely,
including interviews and standardized instruments administered under
publisher protocols that support remote use. Where an in-person evaluation
will produce a better-founded opinion, it is scheduled by appointment in
the evaluee's own community, with travel arranged from the Kansas City
home office. The same scheduling discipline covers depositions and trial
testimony, with dates confirmed against counsel's calendar during intake.
Every interview note, test protocol, and source document is retained in
the engagement file, so that what the opinion asserts, the file can
show.
