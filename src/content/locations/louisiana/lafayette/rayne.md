Ask which side retains Purinton Analytics in Rayne and the answer is:
either. The retaining party changes the engagement letter, never the
opinion. A defense referral gets the same transferable skills analysis,
the same labor market sourcing, and the same care-cost discipline a
plaintiff referral gets, because an expert whose conclusions bend toward
the client is useless to both. That independence is the product, delivered
through [vocational expert witness work](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) when what
counsel need is a rigorous, independent examination of an opinion already
in the record. In each format, the deliverable is a written report whose
assumptions are stated, whose sources are cited, and whose reasoning can
be followed by a reader who wants it to fail.

## Courts serving Rayne litigation

Rayne is an Acadia Parish town, and its state-court matters ordinarily
proceed in the District Court serving Acadia Parish. In federal practice,
Louisiana's district courts are all potential forums: the United States
District Court for the Western District of Louisiana, the United States
District Court for the Middle District of Louisiana, and the United States
District Court for the Eastern District of Louisiana. The forum that
actually controls, and any registration or qualification requirement
peculiar to it, is worked out during the conflict check and intake, not
guessed at on a landing page.

## Labor market analysis for a Rayne evaluee

Rayne is a small town whose residents realistically compete for work
across the Lafayette metro, so employability and earning capacity findings
draw on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage
estimates for the Lafayette metropolitan area and for Louisiana statewide,
alongside state workforce agency labor market information and direct
labor market research where the file warrants it. Both sides can trace
every figure to its cited source, which is the point. When economic loss
must be quantified as well, the firm coordinates
[damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) in which
independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis
while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement
structure. Metro-wide coverage is described on the
[Lafayette page](/locations/louisiana/lafayette/).

## No storefront, full service

There is no Purinton Analytics storefront in Rayne, no published street
address, and no suggestion of either, because a firm that shades the facts
about its own offices invites doubt about its opinions. Engagements are
conducted remote-first, with vocational interviews and standardized
instruments handled under publisher protocols where remote administration
is supported, and records work done off-site. In-person evaluation is
available by appointment in the evaluee's own community when the referral
questions call for it, and testimony obligations are met with travel
planned from the Kansas City home office against dates confirmed at
intake.
