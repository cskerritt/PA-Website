The damages side of most Abbeville injury and disability cases turns on
the same recurring questions: what can this person still earn, and what
will their future care cost. Neither question answers itself, and neither is well
served by advocacy dressed up as analysis. Purinton Analytics exists to
answer both with method: a
[vocational expert witness](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) opinion
built from work history, transferable skills, and documented labor market
evidence, and a [life care plan](/services/life-care-planning/) built from
the medical record and priced to the evaluee's own market. Either side of
the caption may retain the firm; the method does not change with the
client.

## Vermilion Parish venue and the federal alternatives

Abbeville matters filed in state court ordinarily belong to the District
Court serving Vermilion Parish. If a case sounds in federal jurisdiction,
Louisiana offers the United States District Court for the Western District
of Louisiana, the United States District Court for the Middle District of
Louisiana, and the United States District Court for the Eastern District
of Louisiana; determining which of them governs a particular dispute is a
task for the conflict check and intake, as is any venue-specific
registration or qualification requirement. Because the analysis is
venue-independent, counsel can retain before venue questions settle
without wasting the work.

## Grounding the numbers in the record

For an Abbeville evaluee, employability and earning capacity conclusions
rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage
estimates for the Lafayette metropolitan area and for Louisiana statewide,
together with state workforce agency labor market information and direct
labor market research where warranted, each source cited so that opposing
counsel can trace every step. Future care items in a life care plan or
[medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/) carry the
same sourcing discipline. Where the referral extends to economic loss, the
firm coordinates
[damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) in which
independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis
while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement
structure.
