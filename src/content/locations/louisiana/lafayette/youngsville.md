Youngsville is one of the larger suburban communities in the Lafayette
metro, and the people who live there tend to build their working lives
across the whole region rather than inside the town limits. When one of
those working lives is interrupted by injury or disability, the vocational
question is regional too: what work remains realistic for this person, in
this labor market, given this medical picture. Purinton Analytics answers
that question for plaintiff and defense counsel alike, through
[vocational expert witness work](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) and
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) built for
cross-examination.

## Lafayette Parish venue and the federal forums

Youngsville sits in Lafayette Parish, and matters arising there are
typically heard in the District Court serving Lafayette Parish. Louisiana's
federal forums are also available where a case belongs in federal court:
the United States District Court for the Western District of Louisiana, the
United States District Court for the Middle District of Louisiana, and the
United States District Court for the Eastern District of Louisiana. Which
of those courts controls a particular caption is confirmed during the
conflict check and intake, along with any venue-specific registration or
qualification requirement. The engagement itself does not change with the
forum; the same documented file supports the opinion wherever it is tested.

## A commuter's earning capacity, measured regionally

Lafayette metro, employability and earning capacity findings rest on
Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for

by state workforce agency labor market information and direct labor market
research where the referral questions warrant it. Every source is cited in

analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one

## Evaluating a Youngsville resident

There is no staffed Purinton Analytics office in Youngsville or anywhere
else in the metro, and the firm publishes no street address here, because
plain statements about presence belong in the same category as plain
statements about methodology. Most work proceeds remotely: records review,
vocational interviews, and standardized testing under publisher protocols
that support remote administration. When an in-person evaluation serves the
referral questions better, it is scheduled in the evaluee's own community,
with travel arranged from the Kansas City home office. Deposition and trial
appearances are handled the same way, planned against counsel's calendar
rather than improvised around it.
