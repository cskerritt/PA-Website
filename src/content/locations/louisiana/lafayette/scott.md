Every vocational file Purinton Analytics opens on a Scott case starts in
the same place: the evaluee's own work history. What this person has
done, what skills that work demonstrates, and which of those skills
transfer to other occupations after injury or disability form the spine
of the analysis; labor market data enters afterward, to test the
transferable options against real hiring conditions rather than
assumptions. Plaintiff and defense counsel retain the firm for that
method through
[vocational expert witness](/services/vocational-expert-witness/)
engagements, [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) of opinions
already served in the case.

## Venue for a Scott case

Scott lies within Lafayette Parish, and its state-court matters are
ordinarily heard in the District Court serving Lafayette Parish. Federal
litigation may belong to the United States District Court for the Western
District of Louisiana, the United States District Court for the Middle
District of Louisiana, or the United States District Court for the Eastern
District of Louisiana, depending on the case; the controlling forum, and
any registration or qualification requirement it carries, is confirmed
during the conflict check and intake. Counsel on either side receive an
opinion prepared for the scrutiny of the other.

## A regional market, documented from public sources

Once the transferable skills are established, the market evidence that
tests them is regional and public. Findings on employability and earning
capacity for a Scott evaluee
are grounded in Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and
wage estimates for the Lafayette metropolitan area and for Louisiana
statewide, in state workforce agency labor market information, and in
direct labor market research where the referral justifies it. Citations
accompany every source. Life care plans and
[medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/) price
future care to the market the evaluee actually lives in, not to a national
composite. The regional practice as a whole is described on the
[Lafayette metro page](/locations/louisiana/lafayette/).

## Appointments, not addresses

Purinton Analytics keeps no office in Scott and publishes no street
address for this location. The practice runs remote-first: records are
reviewed and analyzed off-site, vocational interviews proceed by
videoconference, and standardized instruments are administered under
publisher protocols wherever remote administration is supported. When an
evaluation should happen face to face, it is arranged by appointment in
the evaluee's community; when testimony requires presence, travel is
scheduled from the Kansas City home office and fixed to counsel's calendar
during intake. What the firm will not do is imply a local footprint it
does not have. Counsel weighing remote against in-person evaluation get a
recommendation grounded in the referral questions themselves, with the
tradeoffs explained before the choice is made rather than defended after
it.
