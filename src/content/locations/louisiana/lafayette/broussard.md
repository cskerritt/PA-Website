## Where a Broussard caption lands

Broussard lies in Lafayette Parish, so state-court matters ordinarily
proceed in the District Court serving Lafayette Parish. On the federal
side, each of Louisiana's district courts is a potential forum: the
United States District Court for the Western District of Louisiana, the
United States District Court for the Middle District of Louisiana, and the
United States District Court for the Eastern District of Louisiana. The
controlling venue for a given matter is identified during the conflict
check, together with any registration or qualification requirement that
venue imposes. Nothing about the analysis depends on the courthouse; the
methodology travels intact from parish court to federal court.

## Labor market evidence that survives scrutiny

Vocational findings for a Broussard evaluee draw on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates covering the
Lafayette metropolitan area and Louisiana as a whole, along with state
workforce agency labor market information and, where the questions call
for it, direct employer contact and labor market research. No figure enters
the report without a citation. When economic damages are part of the
referral, the firm coordinates
[damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) in which
independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis
while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement
structure, so counsel manage a single expert relationship instead of
several. The broader regional practice is described on the
[Lafayette metro page](/locations/louisiana/lafayette/).

## Scheduling the evaluation

The firm keeps no office in Broussard and lists no street address for this
location; candor about presence is part of the product. Remote work covers
most of an engagement, from records analysis to vocational interviews to
standardized instruments administered under publisher protocols approved
for remote use. An in-person evaluation, when warranted, is conducted in
the evaluee's community on an appointment basis, with travel scheduled from
the Kansas City home office. Testimony dates receive the same treatment:
confirmed early, calendared precisely, and honored.
