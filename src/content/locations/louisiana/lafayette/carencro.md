For the person at the center of a Carencro case, the expert evaluation is
not an abstraction; it is an interview, a set of standardized instruments,
and a stranger asking detailed questions about work history and daily
functioning. Purinton Analytics designs that experience around the evaluee.
Interviews and testing for
[vocational expert witness](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) and
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) engagements are
conducted remotely where publisher protocols permit, or in person in the
evaluee's own community when the referral questions call for it, so a
Carencro resident is never asked to organize their recovery around an
expert's convenience. Plaintiff and defense referrals are handled with the
same care.

## The courts behind a Carencro matter

Carencro is a Lafayette Parish community, and its state-court litigation
ordinarily belongs to the District Court serving Lafayette Parish. Federal
matters may proceed in any of Louisiana's district courts as the case
requires: the United States District Court for the Western District of
Louisiana, the United States District Court for the Middle District of
Louisiana, and the United States District Court for the Eastern District
of Louisiana. Confirming which forum controls, and whether that forum
imposes any registration or qualification requirement, is part of the
conflict check and intake rather than an afterthought. The report is
written to the same standard for every one of them.

## What the local labor market shows

An employability or earning capacity opinion for someone living in
Carencro has to reflect the labor market that person actually faces. The
firm grounds those findings in Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational
employment and wage estimates for the Lafayette metropolitan area and for
Louisiana statewide, in state workforce agency labor market information,
and in direct labor market research when the file justifies it, with every
source cited so either side can check the work. Care-related costs in a
[life care plan or medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/)
are priced to the evaluee's own market. The full metro practice is
described on the [Lafayette page](/locations/louisiana/lafayette/).

## Presence, stated plainly

Purinton Analytics maintains no office in Carencro and publishes no local
street address, because a location page should be as accurate as a report.
What counsel get instead is a remote-first practice with disciplined
travel: records review and analysis from the firm's base, evaluation
sessions arranged by appointment where the evaluee is, and deposition or
trial attendance planned from the Kansas City home office and locked to
counsel's calendar at intake. Distance is managed as scheduling, never as
an excuse, and the evaluee's own constraints, from medical appointments to
transportation, are treated as planning inputs rather than obstacles.

## From referral to defined scope

The path into an engagement is short. Counsel submit the caption or party
names through the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/), note any
disclosure, deposition, or trial dates already fixed, and receive
confirmation of availability once the conflict check clears. Scope is then
defined in writing before work begins. Matters elsewhere in the state are
covered on the [Louisiana page](/locations/louisiana/), and the firm's
[national practice](/locations/nationwide/) follows the identical intake
