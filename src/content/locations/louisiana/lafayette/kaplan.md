Purinton Analytics keeps no office in Kaplan and will not imply one, so
this page opens with what the firm actually is here: a national,
remote-first practice that reaches small Vermilion Parish communities as
reliably as it reaches major cities, with
[vocational expert witness](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) opinions
and [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) prepared for
plaintiff or defense counsel and written to survive the other side's
scrutiny.

## Vermilion Parish courts and the federal options

A Kaplan matter in state court ordinarily proceeds in the District Court
serving Vermilion Parish. Federal jurisdiction, where it exists, points to
the United States District Court for the Western District of Louisiana,
the United States District Court for the Middle District of Louisiana, or
the United States District Court for the Eastern District of Louisiana;
which court controls is settled during the conflict check, along with any
venue-specific registration or qualification requirement. Distance from
any particular courthouse never changes the analysis, only the travel
plan.

## A small town inside a regional market

An evaluee living in Kaplan is not confined to a Kaplan job market, and an
opinion that pretended otherwise would understate or overstate capacity
depending on who paid for it. The firm instead documents the regional
picture: Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage
estimates for the Lafayette metropolitan area and for Louisiana statewide,
state workforce agency labor market information, and direct labor market
research when the referral questions justify the added depth, all cited in
the report. Counsel needing the full damages package can add
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. The metro practice is described on the
[Lafayette page](/locations/louisiana/lafayette/).

## How remote-first serves a rural evaluee

For a Kaplan evaluee, remote-first is not a compromise; it is what makes
rigorous evaluation practical far from any expert's office. Records
analysis, vocational interviews, and standardized testing under publisher
protocols that permit remote administration all proceed without asking the
evaluee to travel. When the referral questions are better served in
person, the evaluation comes to the evaluee's own community by
appointment, with travel from the Kansas City home office, and the same
arrangements cover deposition and trial appearances, calendared at intake.
No street address is published for this location because none exists to
publish. The evaluation itself loses nothing in the exchange: the same
instruments, the same interview structure, and the same documentation
standards apply whether the session happens over a screen or across a
table.
