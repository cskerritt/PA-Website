## Data behind the opinion

Vocational conclusions for Covington evaluees are grounded in Bureau of
Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the local
area and for Louisiana statewide, in state workforce agency labor market
information, and in direct labor market research where the referral
questions warrant it. No figure is asserted without a named source, and the
report shows its methodology in enough detail for cross-examination to test
it. When the assignment is defensive rather than affirmative, the same
standards drive [rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/)
of an opposing expert's vocational or care-planning report, separating what
the underlying data support from what has been layered on top of them.

## Appointments, not an office

The firm maintains no staffed office in Covington and lists no street
address for this location; that candor is deliberate, because an expert who
overstates his own footprint invites doubt about everything else in the
report. Meetings are by appointment. Engagements typically run remotely
from the outset, with electronic records review, structured interviews, and
standardized testing under publisher protocols where remote administration
is supported. In-person evaluation, when the questions require it, takes
place in the evaluee's own community, and deposition or trial attendance is
scheduled through travel from the Kansas City home office. Life care plans
and [medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/) price
future care in the evaluee's actual market.
