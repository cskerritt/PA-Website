An earning capacity question in Mandeville is rarely answered by Mandeville
alone. The town is part of the Slidell-Mandeville-Covington metro, and the
jobs an injured worker held before the loss, and the jobs still open to that
worker afterward, tend to spread across the commuting range rather than stop
at the town line. Purinton Analytics writes
[vocational expert opinions](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) that treat
that commuting reality as the unit of analysis, for plaintiff and defense
engagements alike, alongside
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) in catastrophic injury
matters and coordinated damages work in which independent economist partners
perform and sign the economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning
opinions proceed under the same engagement.

## Where Mandeville cases are heard

Mandeville sits in St. Tammany Parish, and state-court matters proceed in
the District Court serving the parish. On the federal side, Louisiana's
available forums are the United States District Court for the Eastern
District of Louisiana, the United States District Court for the Middle
District of Louisiana, and the United States District Court for the Western
District of Louisiana. Which of them controls a particular Mandeville case
is a venue question resolved with counsel during the conflict check and
intake, not assumed from geography, and the report standard does not change
with the caption. A matter removed from the parish courthouse keeps the
same expert and the same file.

## Reading the commuting market honestly

Because the practical labor market reaches beyond the town, vocational
findings for a Mandeville evaluee rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates for the surrounding area and for
Louisiana statewide, supplemented by state workforce agency labor market
information and by direct labor market research when the referral questions
call for it. The analysis stays qualitative about place and specific about
method: what work exists within reach, what it requires, and how the
evaluee's residual capacity matches it. Every source is disclosed in the
report so that either side of the caption can retrace the path from data to
opinion, and the same sourcing discipline supports
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) when counsel
already hold an opposing report.

## Evaluations built around the evaluee

Most engagements begin remotely: records review, a structured vocational
interview, and standardized testing under publisher protocols where remote
administration is supported. When the referral calls for in-person
evaluation, the assessment is arranged in the evaluee's own community
rather than requiring travel to a distant office, and depositions or trial
appearances are handled through travel scheduled from the Kansas City home
office. The firm keeps no staffed office in Mandeville and publishes no
street address here; meetings are by appointment, and saying so plainly is
part of the same discipline that governs the reports. Life care plans and
[medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/) are priced
to the evaluee's own market rather than a national composite.
