Abita Springs is a small town, and small-town evaluees are the ones most
often shortchanged by expert logistics: asked to travel to a distant
office, assessed against a labor market they do not actually live in, or
skipped over for in-person work entirely. Purinton Analytics structures the
engagement the other way around. The evaluation comes to the evaluee, the
labor market analysis starts from where that person can realistically work,
and the resulting [vocational opinions](/services/vocational-expert-witness/)
and [life care plans](/services/life-care-planning/) are written to hold up
whether the firm has been retained by the plaintiff or by the defense.

## The parish and its courts

Abita Springs lies in St. Tammany Parish, and its state-court matters
proceed in the District Court serving the parish. For federal litigation, the
available Louisiana forums are the United States District Court for
the Eastern District of Louisiana, the United States District Court for the
Middle District of Louisiana, and the United States District Court for the
Western District of Louisiana. The forum that controls any particular case
is identified with counsel during the conflict check and intake, and the
answer changes scheduling, not substance: the same expert, the same file,
and the same report discipline carry through state court, federal court,
and removal in between.

## A small town inside a larger market

Scale is a methodological question, not a limitation. A vocational opinion
for an Abita Springs evaluee cannot honestly be confined to the town
itself, so the analysis looks at the broader commuting area anchored by the
Slidell-Mandeville-Covington metro, using Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates for the area and for Louisiana
statewide, state workforce agency labor market information, and direct
labor market research when the referral questions require it. The report
names its sources and keeps its geographic assumptions explicit, so that
either side of the caption can test whether the market described is the
market the evaluee actually faces. Counsel who want the metro-wide picture
will find it on the
[Slidell metro page](/locations/louisiana/slidell/).

## How the evaluation actually runs

There is no staffed Purinton Analytics office in Abita Springs and no
published street address for this location; meetings happen by appointment,
and the firm says so plainly rather than implying a storefront that does
not exist. Records review, structured vocational interviews, and
standardized testing under publisher protocols proceed remotely where
remote administration is supported, which spares a small-town evaluee
needless travel. When the referral requires an in-person assessment, it is
conducted in the evaluee's own community, with travel scheduled from the
Kansas City home office, and the same travel practice covers depositions
and trial dates. [Medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/)
and life care plans are priced against the evaluee's actual market, and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
pair that work with independent economist partners who perform and sign the
economic analysis.
