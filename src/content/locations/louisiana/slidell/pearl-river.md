## Venue for Pearl River matters

Pearl River is in St. Tammany Parish, and state-court litigation proceeds
in the District Court serving the parish. Louisiana's federal forums are
the United States District Court for the Eastern District of Louisiana, the
United States District Court for the Middle District of Louisiana, and the
United States District Court for the Western District of Louisiana; which
of those courts controls a given matter is settled with counsel during the
conflict check and intake. Because the report is written to an
unchanging standard, a case that moves between the parish courthouse and a federal
docket keeps its expert and its file intact, and the deadlines simply
transfer to the new scheduling order.

## Earning capacity in a town this size

Pearl River is a small community, so the honest labor market for a Pearl
River evaluee is the commuting area around it, reaching across the metro
anchored by Slidell. Vocational findings draw on Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates for the area and for Louisiana
statewide, on state workforce agency labor market information, and on
direct labor market research where the referral questions justify it. The
report states its geographic assumptions outright and cites every source,
because a conclusion that cannot be audited by opposing counsel is not
worth signing. Where the economic loss calculation is also needed,
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
bring in independent economist partners who perform and sign that analysis
while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds in parallel.
