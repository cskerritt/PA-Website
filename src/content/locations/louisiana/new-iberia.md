## Labor market and care-cost research

Findings about employability and earning capacity for New Iberia evaluees
rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage
estimates covering the area and Louisiana statewide, on state workforce
agency labor market information, and on direct labor market research where
warranted. In a smaller market, the analysis turns on what work is
realistically available to the evaluee, so local inquiry carries real
weight. Life care plans and medical cost projections are priced to the
evaluee's own community through provider-specific inquiries and published
fee data, and each figure in the report traces to a cited source.
