Distance is a solved problem in this practice. Purinton Analytics serves
Shreveport and Bossier City counsel from its Kansas City home office
through a remote-first engagement model, providing
[vocational expert opinions](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) on
employability and earning capacity,
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) in catastrophic injury
matters, and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. Plaintiff and defense referrals are accepted on the
same terms, and each report is written for the scrutiny of the other side.

## The metro's parishes and courts

Matters are accepted from the District Courts serving Caddo Parish,
Bossier Parish, and De Soto Parish, and from Louisiana's federal courts:
the United States District Court for the Western District of Louisiana,
the United States District Court for the Middle District of Louisiana, and
the United States District Court for the Eastern District of Louisiana.
The engagement file, methodology, and report standard are identical across
those venues, so removal or transfer does not disturb the work. Counsel
with cases elsewhere in the state will find the equivalent coverage
described on the [Louisiana page](/locations/louisiana/).

## What remote-first actually means here

There is no staffed Purinton Analytics office in the Shreveport-Bossier
City metro, and no street address is published for this location; meetings
happen by appointment, and the firm says so plainly rather than implying a
storefront that does not exist. The working sequence is built for that
reality. Records arrive and are reviewed electronically, vocational
interviews are held by scheduled video or telephone session, and
standardized testing is administered under publisher protocols where
remote administration is supported. When the referral questions call for
an in-person evaluation, or when a deposition or trial requires presence,
travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office against the dates
counsel provide, and testimony is delivered in person or by remote
appearance as the court allows.

## Reading the Shreveport labor market

Vocational findings for evaluees in the metro rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the
Shreveport-Bossier City metropolitan area and for Louisiana statewide,
along with state workforce agency labor market information and direct
labor market research where the questions warrant it. Life care plans and
[medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/) are priced
to the evaluee's own market, with sources disclosed line by line so
opposing counsel can trace each conclusion back to its record. That
sourcing discipline matters at deposition: when a report cites its data by
area and by program, its author can answer questions about any entry
without improvisation, because every figure carries a named source that
either side can check against the record.

## Beginning a Shreveport engagement

Every matter starts with a documented conflict check and a defined scope,
whether venued in the metro or anywhere in the firm's
[national coverage](/locations/nationwide/). Submit the caption or party
names through the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and identify any
imminent disclosure, deposition, or trial dates. Any venue-specific
registration or qualification requirement is identified and confirmed
during the conflict check and intake process.
