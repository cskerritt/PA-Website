## Labor Market Foundations

A Zachary evaluee's employability is analyzed against the labor market the evaluee can actually reach, which for this community means the wider Baton Rouge region. The firm relies on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Baton Rouge metropolitan area and for Louisiana statewide, together with state workforce agency labor market information. Where the case turns on a narrow occupational question, direct labor market research supplements the published estimates. No figure enters a report without a cited source, so the foundation of each opinion is open to examination by both sides. Where treating-provider restrictions and an independent examination diverge, the report analyzes employability under each set of restrictions separately, so the trier of fact can see exactly how the vocational conclusions shift with the medical assumptions.
