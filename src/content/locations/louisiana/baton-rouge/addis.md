Addis is a small West Baton Rouge Parish suburb, and no forensic vocational practice keeps an office there. Purinton Analytics does not pretend otherwise: it serves Addis counsel by appointment, bringing the same disciplined workup to a small-suburb case that it applies in the largest venues it covers.

## Methodology Travels Better Than Offices

A small suburb like Addis will never host a forensic vocational office, and it does not need one. What an Addis case needs is methodology that arrives intact: a transferable skills analysis run the same way it would be run for a case in any major city, a life care plan built on the same clinical foundations, and a report written to the same standard of documentation. Purinton Analytics delivers exactly that to West Baton Rouge Parish counsel, offering [vocational expert](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) opinions, [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and [expert testimony and litigation consulting](/services/expert-testimony-litigation-consulting/) to plaintiff and defense attorneys alike.

## Addis Workers, Regional Data

An Addis evaluee's vocational outlook is assessed against the full Baton Rouge metropolitan labor market, the market in which residents of a community this size actually find work. The firm grounds those findings in Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Baton Rouge metropolitan area and for Louisiana statewide, in state workforce agency labor market information, and in direct labor market research where the referral question outruns the published data. Sources are cited, methods are described, and conclusions follow from both, so the opinion can be examined line by line from either counsel table.

## Serving the Evaluee by Appointment

Remote work covers most of an engagement: record review, analysis, drafting, and conferences with counsel all happen without anyone boarding a plane. When the case calls for an in-person vocational or care-planning evaluation, the appointment is set in the evaluee's community, and when testimony requires the expert in the courtroom or at the deposition table, travel is arranged from the Kansas City home office. Video conferencing also carries counsel through the engagement itself, from the initial scope call to pre-deposition preparation, without adding travel expense to a modest case budget. The firm publishes no Addis address and claims no local staff, an accuracy policy that applies to every location the [nationwide practice](/locations/nationwide/) covers.

Every Addis engagement, whichever party initiates it, opens with a documented conflict check.
