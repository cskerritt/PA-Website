## The File Determines the Findings

A vocational or care-planning opinion is only as sound as the record beneath it, so a Baker engagement begins by assembling that record: the medical documentation that establishes functional capacity, the employment and earnings history that establishes the pre-injury baseline, and the education and training that establish which skills can transfer to other work. From that foundation, Purinton Analytics prepares [vocational expert](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) opinions and [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) for Baker matters, and offers [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement structure. Plaintiff and defense referrals are accepted on the same terms, and the record-first discipline is identical for both.

## Parish and Federal Courts

Baker lies within East Baton Rouge Parish, and state-court matters arising there are heard in the parish District Court. The federal forums available in Louisiana are the United States District Court for the Eastern District of Louisiana, the United States District Court for the Middle District of Louisiana, and the United States District Court for the Western District of Louisiana, each a possible venue depending on the parties and the claims. The controlling venue for any particular Baker matter, and any registration or qualification requirement that venue imposes, is identified during the conflict check and intake. Counsel can consult the [Baton Rouge metro page](/locations/louisiana/baton-rouge/) for the regional practice and the [Louisiana page](/locations/louisiana/) for statewide coverage.

## Data Behind the Opinions

Labor market findings for Baker evaluees draw on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Baton Rouge metropolitan area and for Louisiana statewide, alongside state workforce agency labor market information. When a case turns on whether a specific kind of work is genuinely available to a specific evaluee, direct labor market research supplements the published series. Every source is cited in the report, and every step from data to conclusion is documented, because an opinion that cannot show its work does not survive a capable cross-examination. Supplemental reports, when late-produced records change the foundation, follow the same documentation standard as the original disclosure.

## Logistics for Baker Engagements

The firm works remotely wherever the task permits: records analysis, transferable skills work, plan drafting, and counsel conferences all proceed without travel. In-person evaluations are arranged by appointment in the evaluee's community when the referral questions call for them, and testimony travel is planned from the Kansas City home office around the deposition or trial date. Purinton Analytics keeps no staffed office in Baker and publishes no street address for this location; that accuracy is deliberate, and it is the same accuracy counsel should expect inside the reports.
