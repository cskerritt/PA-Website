## Ascension Parish Venue, National Standards

Gonzales litigation belongs, in the first instance, to Ascension Parish: civil actions arising in the city are tried in the parish's District Court, and it is that courtroom, and the judge and jury in it, that the expert report must ultimately serve. Purinton Analytics writes for that room. Every [vocational expert](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) opinion and [life care plan](/services/life-care-planning/) prepared for a Gonzales matter follows the same national methodology the firm applies everywhere, documented so that direct examination can walk through it step by step and cross-examination can test each of those steps against the cited record. Retention is open to plaintiff and defense counsel on equal terms.

## Federal Forums and Venue Confirmation

When a Gonzales case proceeds in federal court, the available Louisiana forums are the United States District Court for the Eastern District of Louisiana, the United States District Court for the Middle District of Louisiana, and the United States District Court for the Western District of Louisiana. Rather than assume which district will control, the firm confirms the presiding court during the conflict check and intake, and identifies at the same step any venue-specific registration or qualification requirement that bears on the engagement. ## The Labor Market Around Gonzales

Gonzales evaluees compete for work across the Baton Rouge metropolitan region, and the vocational analysis reflects that scope. Findings rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Baton Rouge metropolitan area and for Louisiana statewide, on state workforce agency labor market information, and on direct labor market research when the referral question concerns a specific occupation or a placement issue the published estimates cannot resolve. The report identifies each source it relies on, in qualitative and cited terms, so that opposing counsel can test the foundation rather than guess at it. Draft opinions are never circulated for editing by the retaining side; counsel receives a finished report, and any later revision is documented and grounded in newly produced records rather than in advocacy.

## Meeting the Evaluee Where They Are

The firm's working model is remote-first. Record review, transferable skills analysis, and drafting proceed without travel, and counsel conferences are held by video or phone at the convenience of the case team. When an in-person evaluation is warranted, it takes place in the evaluee's own community by appointment; there is no Purinton Analytics office in Gonzales, no published local address, and no suggestion of one, because candor about presence is part of the credibility the reports depend on. Deposition and trial appearances are scheduled with travel from the Kansas City home office. The [nationwide page](/locations/nationwide/) sets out how the same approach covers other jurisdictions.
