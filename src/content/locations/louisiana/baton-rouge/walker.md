## The Written Opinion Is the Product

In a Walker case, what the firm ultimately delivers is a document: a written opinion that states its referral questions, lists the records reviewed, explains its methods, cites its data, and reasons its way to conclusions a reader can check. Everything else in the engagement exists to make that document sound. Purinton Analytics prepares [vocational expert](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) reports and [life care plans](/services/life-care-planning/) to that standard for Walker matters, and offers [rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) when counsel needs an opposing expert's report held to the same measure. Plaintiff and defense engagements are accepted on identical terms.

## Livingston Parish Venue

Walker matters filed in state court are tried in the District Court for Livingston Parish. Cases that proceed federally do so in the United States District Court for the Eastern District of Louisiana, the United States District Court for the Middle District of Louisiana, or the United States District Court for the Western District of Louisiana, each an available forum depending on the case. Determining which court controls a given matter, and whether that court imposes any registration or qualification requirement, is part of the conflict check and intake, not a guess made in advance. The [Baton Rouge metro page](/locations/louisiana/baton-rouge/) covers the surrounding regional practice; the [Louisiana page](/locations/louisiana/) covers the state.

## Labor Market Evidence for Walker Evaluees

A Walker evaluee's employment prospects are evaluated against the wider Baton Rouge metropolitan labor market rather than the town alone, because a sound earning-capacity analysis considers the full range of occupations the data sources describe for the region. The evidentiary base is the Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Baton Rouge metropolitan area and for Louisiana statewide, joined by state workforce agency labor market information and, when a disputed placement or occupation requires it, direct labor market research. Those sources appear in the report by name so the foundation can be examined, not merely accepted. Deposition testimony tracks the written disclosure exactly; the firm does not volunteer opinions at deposition that the report has not already stated and supported.

## Practical Logistics

Purinton Analytics serves Walker without a local office and says so plainly; no street address is published for this location because none exists. The working model is remote-first: records analysis, transferable skills work, drafting, and counsel conferences proceed from the home office, while in-person evaluations, when warranted by the referral questions, are held by appointment in the evaluee's own community. When deposition or trial testimony requires presence, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office against the litigation calendar. ## How to Retain the Firm

Retention for a Walker matter starts with a documented conflict check. Submit the caption or the party names through the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/), name the court where the case is pending, and list the disclosure, deposition, and trial dates that will bound the schedule. Once conflicts clear, the firm confirms scope and delivery in writing, requests the records the claims require, and begins building the document the case will stand on.
