## A Vocational Expert for Central Evaluees

When the injured party in a case lives in Central, the vocational and care-planning questions begin at home: what work the evaluee performed before the injury, what functional capacity remains, and what employment realistically exists for a resident of one of the larger suburban communities in East Baton Rouge Parish. Purinton Analytics provides [vocational expert](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) and [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) opinions for counsel litigating matters that arise in Central and throughout the [Baton Rouge metro](/locations/louisiana/baton-rouge/), retained by plaintiff and defense alike. The analysis is built for scrutiny, because every report is written on the assumption that opposing counsel will read it closely.

## Courts Serving East Baton Rouge Parish

Central matters proceed in the District Court for East Baton Rouge Parish and, where federal jurisdiction attaches, in Louisiana's federal forums: the United States District Court for the Eastern District of Louisiana, the United States District Court for the Middle District of Louisiana, and the United States District Court for the Western District of Louisiana. Which of those courts controls a particular case depends on the parties and the claims, and the answer is confirmed during the conflict check and intake rather than assumed. The same intake step identifies any venue-specific registration or qualification requirement, so the engagement letter reflects the forum before work begins. Engagements elsewhere in the state are described on the [Louisiana locations page](/locations/louisiana/).

## Central Within the Baton Rouge Labor Market

Employability and earning-capacity findings for a Central evaluee rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Baton Rouge metropolitan area and for Louisiana statewide, supplemented by state workforce agency labor market information and direct labor market research where a specific occupation or employer question warrants it. Because Central residents participate in the broader metro commuting market, the analysis considers the occupations available across the region, not only those inside city limits, and the report states its sources so both sides can trace every finding.

## Evaluation Logistics

Most engagement work proceeds remotely: records review, transferable skills analysis, labor market research, and report drafting do not require travel. When the referral question calls for an in-person vocational or care-planning evaluation, the evaluation is arranged in the evaluee's own community, and when deposition or trial testimony requires presence, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office. The firm maintains no staffed office in Central and publishes no local street address, because stating presence accurately is part of the same discipline that governs the opinions themselves. Counsel with national dockets can review the [nationwide practice](/locations/nationwide/) for how the same structure operates in other venues.

## Start with a Conflict Check

The first step in any Central engagement, for either side of the caption, is a documented conflict check. Submit the caption or party names through the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/), note the court where the matter is pending, and flag any imminent disclosure, deposition, or trial dates so scheduling can be confirmed at the outset. Once the check clears, the firm defines the scope, issues a records request tailored to the claims, and commits to a delivery date that respects the disclosure calendar.
