## Grounding Opinions in the Regional Market

Vocational conclusions about a Donaldsonville evaluee are anchored in the labor market the evaluee can realistically access, which extends across the Baton Rouge metropolitan region. The firm relies on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Baton Rouge metropolitan area and for Louisiana statewide, on state workforce agency labor market information, and on direct labor market research when a contested occupation requires more than the published series can show. Each data source appears in the report by name; nothing in the analysis asks the reader to take a foundation on faith. When the assignment is rebuttal, the review applies the same checklist the firm's own reports must pass: sourcing, method transparency, internal consistency, and the fit between the data and the conclusions drawn from it.
