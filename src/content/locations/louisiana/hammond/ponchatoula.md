An earning capacity opinion about someone who lives in Ponchatoula should
describe the work that person can realistically obtain, at the wages actually
paid where that person will look for work. Purinton Analytics builds its
vocational and care-planning opinions from that starting point, accepting
referrals from plaintiff and defense counsel alike for
[earning capacity and employability evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/). Where the
referral also requires an economic loss calculation, the firm offers
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Where Ponchatoula cases are heard

Ponchatoula sits in Tangipahoa Parish, and state court matters proceed in
the District Court serving the parish. Federal filings involving Ponchatoula
parties may proceed in any of Louisiana's federal forums: the United States
District Court for the Eastern District of Louisiana, the United States
District Court for the Middle District of Louisiana, or the United States
District Court for the Western District of Louisiana, with the controlling
venue identified during the conflict check and intake. The report itself
does not change with the courtroom. Every opinion begins with a documented
conflict check and a defined scope, followed by a records request shaped to
the claims actually pled, and every source is cited so opposing counsel can
trace each conclusion to its foundation. Counsel with matters elsewhere in
the state can start from the [Louisiana practice page](/locations/louisiana/).

## A small city in a commuting market

Ponchatoula is a small city within the Hammond metropolitan area, and its
workforce is not confined to the town itself. Residents find work locally,
in Hammond, and across the surrounding parish, so an employability analysis
limited to Ponchatoula's own employers would understate or misstate the
market. Vocational findings for Ponchatoula evaluees therefore rest on the
Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates
covering the Hammond area and the state as a whole, on labor market
information from the state workforce agency, and on direct labor market
research where the referral questions warrant it. The
[Hammond metro page](/locations/louisiana/hammond/) describes the firm's
coverage across the wider metropolitan area.

## Evaluating an evaluee who lives in Ponchatoula

Most engagements begin remotely: records review, a structured vocational
interview by video, and standardized testing administered under publisher
protocols that support remote use. When an in-person evaluation is the
better tool, it is conducted in the evaluee's own community rather than by
asking an injured person to travel, and life care planning costs are
researched with the providers who actually serve that community. The firm
keeps no staffed office in Ponchatoula and publishes no local street
address; meetings are by appointment, and when depositions or trial
testimony require presence, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home
office. The result is a process that treats a small-town evaluee with the
same rigor a large-city evaluee would receive, without inventing a local
presence the firm does not have.
