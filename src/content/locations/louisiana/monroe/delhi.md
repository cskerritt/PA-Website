## Richland Parish and the federal alternatives

Delhi sits in Richland Parish, and state court litigation from the town is
heard in the District Court serving that parish. Louisiana's federal
system offers the United States District Court for the Western District of
Louisiana, the United States District Court for the Middle District of
Louisiana, and the United States District Court for the Eastern District
of Louisiana as its available forums; the district with authority over a
particular Delhi case is confirmed at the conflict check and intake stage,
where any venue-specific expert qualification requirement is also
identified. The firm's reports are drafted so that forum questions never
force a rewrite, and testimony is prepared against the record itself
rather than against the peculiarities of any single courtroom.

## Rural evaluees and the metro wage base

Delhi is a small Richland Parish town within the
[Monroe metro area](/locations/louisiana/monroe/), and honest vocational
analysis has to weigh what small-town residence means for access to work.
The report never assumes which occupations are within practical reach of a
particular Delhi evaluee; access to any given kind of employment is a
finding to be supported, evaluee by evaluee, not a premise imported from
the metro's name. The wage and employment evidence comes from Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Monroe
metropolitan area and for Louisiana statewide, joined with state workforce
agency labor market information, all cited so that either side can verify
the basis of every conclusion.
