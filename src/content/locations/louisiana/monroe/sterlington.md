## The courts that serve Sterlington

Sterlington is an Ouachita Parish town, and its state court matters
proceed in the District Court serving the parish. For federal litigation,
Louisiana's available districts are the United States District Court for
the Western District of Louisiana, the United States District Court for
the Middle District of Louisiana, and the United States District Court for
the Eastern District of Louisiana; the controlling venue for any specific
matter is determined during the conflict check and intake, together with
any local qualification requirement. Nothing in the report depends on
which courthouse ultimately hears it: the sourcing, the reasoning, and the
signature standard are constant, so a transfer or removal changes
logistics without touching substance.

## Situating a Sterlington evaluee in the metro economy

Employability findings for a Sterlington worker are anchored in the
[Monroe metropolitan labor market](/locations/louisiana/monroe/), the
economy in which residents of the town realistically compete for work. The
firm relies on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage
estimates for the Monroe metro area and for Louisiana statewide, together
with state workforce agency labor market information, and it treats the
distinction between what exists in a small town and what exists within
commuting range as an analytical question to be answered, not glossed. No
figure appears in a report without a source behind it.

## By-appointment service, stated plainly

Purinton Analytics keeps a single home office in Kansas City and none in
Sterlington or elsewhere in Louisiana. Meetings here are by appointment.
Most engagement activity, from counsel conferences to evaluee interviews,
runs over secure video; when the referral questions justify an in-person
evaluation, the firm comes to the evaluee's community, and deposition or
trial presence in the parish is planned from the Kansas City home office
well ahead of the date. The
[nationwide practice page](/locations/nationwide/) explains how that model
holds together across the country. The practical effect for counsel is
predictability: scheduling commitments made at intake are commitments, not
aspirations, because the travel calendar is managed as carefully as the
analytical work.
