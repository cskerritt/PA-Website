For an evaluee living in Houma, Bayou Cane, or Thibodaux, the evaluation
should fit the person, not the expert's travel preferences. Purinton
Analytics builds its work in the Houma-Bayou Cane-Thibodaux metro around
that principle, offering remote evaluation where publisher protocols
support it and in-person evaluation in the evaluee's own community where
the referral questions require it. The services counsel draw on here are
[vocational evaluation of earning capacity and employability](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) in catastrophic injury
matters, and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure, all available to plaintiff and defense counsel on
identical terms.

## Courts serving Terrebonne and Lafourche

Engagements are accepted for matters in the District Courts serving
Terrebonne Parish and Lafourche Parish, and in Louisiana's federal courts:
the United States District Court for the Eastern District of Louisiana,
the United States District Court for the Middle District of Louisiana, and
the United States District Court for the Western District of Louisiana.
The same expert, methodology, and report standard follow a case through
removal, transfer, or refiling, and the firm's
[Louisiana page](/locations/louisiana/) describes how that structure
extends across the rest of the state.

## Appointments, candidly framed

There is no staffed Purinton Analytics office in the metro and no
published street address for this location. Meetings in Houma and
Thibodaux are by appointment, and the firm says so without hedging,
because candor about presence is part of the same discipline that governs
its written opinions. Records review, structured vocational interviews,
and standardized testing typically proceed remotely under publisher
protocols where remote administration is supported, which keeps the
schedule in counsel's hands rather than the airline's. When an in-person
evaluation is warranted, or when deposition and trial dates require the
expert in the room, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office
and locked to the dates counsel supply at intake.

## Pricing care and work to the local market

Life care plans and [medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/)
for evaluees in the metro are priced to the evaluee's own market through
provider-specific inquiries and published fee data, never to a national
composite. Vocational findings rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates covering the local area and
Louisiana statewide, together with state workforce agency labor market
information and direct labor market research where the referral questions
call for it. Each source is disclosed in the report so that either side of
the caption can follow the analysis from record to conclusion.

## Referring a Houma matter

Engagements here begin as they do throughout the firm's
[national practice](/locations/nationwide/): with a documented conflict
check and a defined scope. [Submit the conflict check](/refer-a-case/)
with the case caption and party names, and note any imminent disclosure,
deposition, or trial dates so scheduling is settled before substantive
work begins and the evaluation can be planned around the evaluee. Any venue-specific registration or qualification requirement
is identified and confirmed during the conflict check and intake process.
