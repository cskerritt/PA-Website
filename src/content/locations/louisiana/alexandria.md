Litigation venued in Rapides Parish and Grant Parish brings Purinton
Analytics to the Alexandria metropolitan area for vocational expert and life
care planning work on behalf of plaintiff and defense counsel alike.
Referrals from this market typically call for
[earning capacity and employability evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/)
in injury and employment disputes, [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/)
and [medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/) in
catastrophic injury cases, and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Courts served from the Alexandria metro

The firm accepts matters pending in the District Court serving Rapides
Parish and the District Court serving Grant Parish, and in Louisiana's
federal forums, including the United States District Court for the Western
District of Louisiana, the United States District Court for the Middle
District of Louisiana, and the United States District Court for the Eastern
District of Louisiana. Because the report standard does not change with the
caption, the same written opinion is prepared whether the engagement comes
from the party bringing the claim or the party defending it. Counsel with
matters elsewhere in the state will find the full venue picture on the
[Louisiana practice page](/locations/louisiana/).

## Meetings by appointment, not a storefront

Alexandria meetings are by appointment. The firm maintains no staffed
location in the metro and publishes no street address for it, because
accuracy about presence belongs to the same discipline that governs the
reports themselves. Most engagements begin remotely with a documented
conflict check, a defined scope, and a tailored records request, followed by
structured interviews and standardized testing under publisher protocols
where remote administration is supported. When an in-person evaluation is
warranted, or when deposition and trial dates require presence in Rapides
Parish or Grant Parish, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home
office and planned around the court's calendar rather than the expert's.

## Labor market research for Alexandria evaluees

Vocational findings for evaluees from the Alexandria area rest on Bureau of
Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates covering the
local area and Louisiana statewide, on state workforce agency labor market
information, and on direct employer contact where the referral questions
warrant it. Life care plans and medical cost projections are priced to the
evaluee's own community using provider-specific inquiries and published fee
data, so the resulting figures reflect what care actually costs where the
evaluee lives rather than a national abstraction. Each source is cited in
the report so that opposing counsel can trace every input to its origin.

## Starting an Alexandria engagement

Early referral protects the record: it allows the records request,
interview, testing, and labor market research to be completed before
disclosure deadlines compress the schedule. Any venue-specific registration
or qualification requirement is identified and confirmed during the
conflict check and intake process. To begin, submit the caption or party
names through the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and note any
imminent disclosure, deposition, or trial dates. Counsel weighing a matter
outside Louisiana can review the firm's
[national coverage](/locations/nationwide/), which follows the same
remote-first model that serves the Alexandria metro.
