## Franklinton in the Regional Labor Market

An earning capacity opinion for a Franklinton evaluee has to reflect the labor
market the evaluee actually faces, not a generic statewide abstraction. The
community sits within the Bogalusa metropolitan area, and the practical
employment picture for many residents spans Washington Parish and the
surrounding region rather than a single employer base. Vocational findings
therefore rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage
estimates for the relevant area and for Louisiana statewide, on state
workforce agency labor market information, and on direct labor market research
where the referral questions warrant it. The analysis is qualitative about
place and specific about method: transferable skills, functional capacity as
documented in the record, and the occupations genuinely open to this evaluee
in this market.
