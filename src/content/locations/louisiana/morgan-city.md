## Venues for Morgan City matters

Engagements are accepted for cases in the District Court serving St. Mary
Parish and in Louisiana's federal forums, including the United States
District Court for the Western District of Louisiana, the United States
District Court for the Middle District of Louisiana, and the United States
District Court for the Eastern District of Louisiana. Each engagement
opens with a documented conflict check, a defined scope, and a records
request tailored to the claims, so the resulting opinion rests on a record
either side can test. Counsel with matters in other parts of the state can
review the [Louisiana practice page](/locations/louisiana/) for the firm's
statewide footprint.

## No local storefront, and no pretense of one

The firm does not maintain a staffed location in Morgan City and publishes
no street address for the area. Meetings are by appointment, and that
arrangement is stated outright because a report's credibility starts with
the accuracy of everything around it. In practice, most of the engagement
proceeds remotely: records analysis, structured interviews, and
standardized testing under publisher protocols where remote administration
is supported. When in-person evaluation is warranted, or when deposition
and trial dates in St. Mary Parish require attendance, travel is scheduled
from the Kansas City home office, arranged at intake so that availability
for the courtroom is never in doubt.

## Labor market data that survives scrutiny

Employability and earning capacity findings for Morgan City evaluees rest
on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates
covering the area and Louisiana statewide, on state workforce agency labor
market information, and on direct labor market research where the referral
questions warrant it. Life care plans and medical cost projections are
priced to the evaluee's own community using provider-specific inquiries
and published fee data. Every figure carries a citation, because an
unsourced number is an invitation to cross-examination that no expert
should extend.
