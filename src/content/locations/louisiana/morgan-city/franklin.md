## St. Mary Parish venue context

Franklin sits in St. Mary Parish, and civil matters arising there are heard
in the District Court serving the parish. When a claim proceeds in federal
court, the available Louisiana forums are the United States District Court
for the Eastern District of Louisiana, the United States District Court for the
Middle District of Louisiana, and the United States District Court for the
Western District of Louisiana. The controlling venue for a particular
caption is identified during the conflict check and intake, and the report
is prepared with that court's disclosure expectations in mind. Counsel
handling related matters elsewhere in the state can start from the
[Louisiana practice page](/locations/louisiana/), which maps the firm's
statewide coverage.

## A small town read against a regional market

Franklin is a small town within the Morgan City micropolitan area, and its
residents' realistic employment options extend across that regional market
rather than stopping at the town line. Employability and earning capacity
findings therefore rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational
employment and wage estimates covering the area and Louisiana statewide, on
state workforce agency labor market information, and on direct employer
contact when the referral questions call for it. The wider context for the
surrounding market is described on the
[Morgan City metro page](/locations/louisiana/morgan-city/). Every source
is cited, because a labor market conclusion that cannot be traced to its
data does not survive a careful cross-examination.

## Evaluation in the evaluee's own community

Most of a Franklin engagement proceeds without anyone traveling: records
review, structured vocational interviews, and standardized testing under
publisher protocols where remote administration is supported. When the
referral questions require in-person evaluation, the assessment is
conducted with the evaluee's own community in view, since transferable
skills and job placement conclusions mean little if they ignore where the
person actually lives. The firm keeps no staffed office in Franklin and
publishes no street address for the area; meetings are by appointment, and
that fact is stated plainly because candor about logistics belongs to the
same discipline that governs the opinions themselves. Travel for
evaluations, depositions, and trial testimony is scheduled from the Kansas
City home office and confirmed at intake, so counsel can commit to dates
with confidence.

## Life care planning priced to place

Life care plans and medical cost projections for Franklin evaluees are
priced to the evaluee's actual community of care, using provider-specific
inquiries and published fee data rather than generic national assumptions.
Where a needed service is not available locally, the plan says so and
accounts for how the evaluee will reach it, because a plan that quietly
assumes away geography invites impeachment. Each recommendation ties to the
medical foundation in the record, and each cost carries a citation an
opposing expert can check.
