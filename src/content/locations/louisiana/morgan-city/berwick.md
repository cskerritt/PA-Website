for the lawyer who will attack it, not just the lawyer who commissioned it.
That standard governs the firm's
[earning capacity and employability evaluations](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),

sent it, because a conclusion that shifts with the client is not an expert
opinion at all.

## St. Mary Parish courts and federal forums

For a Berwick claim filed in state court, the forum is the District Court
serving St. Mary Parish, the parish in which the town sits. Claims that belong in federal
court may be heard in the United States District Court for the Eastern
District of Louisiana, the United States District Court for the Middle
District of Louisiana, or the United States District Court for the Western
District of Louisiana. The forum that controls a particular caption, and
any qualification requirement specific to that venue, is confirmed during
the conflict check and intake so the report is written to the right court's
expectations from the start. Coverage across the rest of the state is
collected on the [Louisiana practice page](/locations/louisiana/).

## Berwick's place in the regional labor market

Berwick is a small town within the Morgan City micropolitan area, and its
workers participate in a commuting market centered on Morgan City, the
area's principal city. An employability finding that stopped at the town
line would misstate an evaluee's real options, so transferable skills and
earning capacity conclusions are anchored to Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates for the area and for Louisiana
statewide, supplemented by state workforce agency labor market information
and direct employer research when the referral questions require it. Every
market assertion carries its citation, so an opposing expert can retrace
the analysis rather than take it on faith. How the surrounding market is
treated is set out on the
[Morgan City metro page](/locations/louisiana/morgan-city/).

## Evaluating a Berwick evaluee

The working model is remote-first. Records review, structured vocational
interviews, and standardized testing under publisher protocols are
completed without travel wherever remote administration is supported,
which keeps expert costs proportionate to the case. When the referral
questions call for in-person assessment, the evaluation is arranged in the
evaluee's own community, and travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home
office; deposition and trial dates are confirmed at intake so availability
is settled before scope is. The firm keeps no staffed office in Berwick
and publishes no local street address; meetings are by appointment. What
counsel gets instead of a storefront is a documented process: intake,
scheduling, and report delivery run on the same disciplined calendar
whether the venue is nearby or across the country.

## Future care costed where it will be delivered

Life care plans and medical cost projections for Berwick evaluees are
priced to the evaluee's actual community of care through provider-specific
inquiries and published fee data. Where a recommended service must be
obtained outside the immediate area, the plan documents that reality
instead of smoothing it over. Each element rests on the medical record,
and each figure carries a source an opposing expert can verify
independently.
