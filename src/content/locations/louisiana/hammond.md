When a Tangipahoa Parish case needs a vocational expert or a life care
planner, counsel usually need the opinion on a schedule set by disclosure
deadlines, not by an expert's convenience. Purinton Analytics serves the
Hammond metropolitan area with that constraint in mind, accepting referrals
from lawyers on either side of the caption for
[earning capacity and employability evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/), along with
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## The Hammond venue footprint

Engagements are accepted for matters in the District Court serving
Tangipahoa Parish and in the state's federal forums, including the United
States District Court for the Eastern District of Louisiana, the United
States District Court for the Middle District of Louisiana, and the United
States District Court for the Western District of Louisiana. The written
opinion is built the same way regardless of venue or retaining party: a
documented conflict check, a defined scope, and a records request tailored
to the claims, so that both sides can examine the record behind every
conclusion. The [Louisiana practice page](/locations/louisiana/) describes
the firm's statewide coverage for counsel whose matters sit outside the
Hammond metro.

## How the work reaches Hammond

There is no staffed Purinton Analytics location in Hammond, and the firm
does not publish a street address for the metro, because candor about
presence is part of the product. Engagements are by appointment and begin
remotely in most cases: records analysis, structured interviews, and
standardized testing under publisher protocols where remote administration
is supported. When the referral questions call for an in-person evaluation,
or when a deposition or trial date in Tangipahoa Parish requires presence,
travel is arranged from the Kansas City home office. Scheduling is built
around the docket, not the reverse, and counsel work with the same expert
and the same report standard from intake through testimony.

## Grounding opinions in the local labor market

Employability and earning capacity findings for Hammond-area evaluees are
grounded in Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage
estimates covering the area and Louisiana statewide, supplemented by state
workforce agency labor market information and direct labor market research
where warranted. Care-cost work follows the same logic: life care plans and
medical cost projections are priced through provider-specific inquiries and
published fee data for the evaluee's own community, and every source is
cited so the analysis can withstand cross-examination rather than merely
survive a first reading.

## Referring a Tangipahoa Parish matter

The most useful time to involve an expert is before the schedule tightens,
while records can still be gathered and the evaluation completed without
compressing anyone's calendar. Any venue-specific registration or
qualification requirement is identified and confirmed during the conflict
check and intake process. To explore availability,
[request a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) with the case caption and
parties, and flag any dates already on the docket. A summary of how the
same model serves courts beyond Louisiana is available on the
[national coverage page](/locations/nationwide/).
