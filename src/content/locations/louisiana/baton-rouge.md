Purinton Analytics supports Baton Rouge litigation with vocational expert
and life care planning services for plaintiff and defense counsel across
the metro's parishes. Engagements from the region typically involve
[earning capacity and employability evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/)
in injury and employment matters,
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) in catastrophic injury
cases, and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. Each report is built on a defined scope and disclosed
sources, so it holds up whichever side of the caption commissioned it.

## Courts and parishes served

The Baton Rouge metro reaches well beyond its principal city, and the firm
accepts matters venued in the District Courts serving East Baton Rouge
Parish, West Baton Rouge Parish, Ascension Parish, Livingston Parish,
Iberville Parish, Assumption Parish, Pointe Coupee Parish, St. Helena
Parish, East Feliciana Parish, and West Feliciana Parish. Federal
engagements proceed in the United States District Court for the Middle
District of Louisiana, and equally in the United States District Court for
the Eastern District of Louisiana and the United States District Court for
the Western District of Louisiana when a matter is filed or transferred
there. The [Louisiana page](/locations/louisiana/) describes coverage for
the rest of the state.

## How the work gets done at a distance

The firm's home office is in Kansas City, and it keeps no staffed office in
Baton Rouge; meetings in the metro are by appointment, and no street address
is published for this location. That candor is deliberate. Most engagements
proceed remotely from intake onward: records review, structured vocational
interviews, and standardized testing under publisher protocols where remote
administration is supported. When an in-person evaluation is warranted, or
when a deposition or trial requires presence, travel is scheduled from the
Kansas City home office around the dates counsel identify at intake.
Counsel receive the same report standard and the same point of contact
regardless of where any step of the work is performed.

## Grounding findings in the Baton Rouge labor market

Vocational opinions for Baton Rouge evaluees draw on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Baton Rouge
metropolitan area and for Louisiana statewide, along with state workforce
agency labor market information and direct labor market research where the
referral questions call for it. Life care plans and
[medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/) are priced
to the evaluee's own market, with each source identified in the report so
that opposing counsel can check the work rather than take it on faith. The
same documentation habit makes the underlying file straightforward to
produce when discovery requires it.

## Requesting a conflict check

To begin a Baton Rouge engagement, or a matter anywhere in the firm's
[nationwide practice](/locations/nationwide/),
[request a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) with the case caption and
parties, and flag any imminent disclosure, deposition, or trial dates. Any
venue-specific registration or qualification requirement is identified and
confirmed during the conflict check and intake process, before any
substantive work on the merits begins.
