When counsel calls about a Ferriday case, the immediate practical question
is often not methodology but access: how does a rigorous vocational
evaluation happen for a claimant who lives in a small Concordia Parish
town, without the evaluation itself becoming a burden on the case?
Purinton Analytics answers that question with a remote-first practice
designed to reach evaluees where they are. The firm delivers
[vocational expert opinions](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) on
employability and earning capacity,
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) in catastrophic injury
matters, and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis under a single engagement structure alongside the vocational and
care-planning work. Referrals come from both sides of the caption, and the
wider regional practice is set out on the
[Natchez page](/locations/mississippi/natchez/).

## Where Ferriday matters are heard

Ferriday sits in Concordia Parish, and state-court litigation arising here
proceeds in the District Court serving the parish. Federal practice in
Louisiana runs through the United States District Court for the Eastern
District of Louisiana, the United States District Court for the Middle
District of Louisiana, and the United States District Court for the
Western District of Louisiana; the controlling venue for any particular
Ferriday filing is worked out with counsel during the conflict check and
intake, together with any registration or qualification requirement
specific to that forum. The written opinion is built to hold up wherever
the case lands: records requested against the claims actually pleaded,
assumptions stated on the page, and conclusions that read the same way
under direct examination and under cross.

## Ferriday work in the Natchez area economy

Ferriday is a small community whose residents look to the broader Natchez
metropolitan area, which spans Mississippi and Louisiana, for much of
their employment. Vocational findings therefore start from the regional
picture: Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage
estimates for the Natchez area and for Louisiana statewide, state
workforce agency labor market information, and direct labor market
research where the referral questions call for it. For a Ferriday evaluee,
the transferable skills question is what work is realistically reachable
from this community given the claimant's residual capacity, education, and
work history, not what exists somewhere in the abstract, and the report
answers it with cited sources rather than assumption.

## How the evaluation reaches the evaluee

There is no Purinton Analytics office in Ferriday, and none is implied;
meetings are by appointment only, and the firm treats plain statements
about presence as part of its professional discipline. Records review,
structured interviews, and standardized testing under publisher protocols
proceed remotely where remote administration is supported, with the
administration conditions noted in the file, so a Ferriday evaluee is not
asked to travel for work that can be done credibly at a distance. Where
the referral requires an in-person evaluation in the evaluee's community,
or where a deposition or trial requires the expert present, travel is
arranged from the Kansas City home office. That same approach serves
counsel across the country, as the
[nationwide page](/locations/nationwide/) explains.

## Beginning an engagement

An engagement begins with a documented conflict check: submit the case
caption or party names through the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/), include any disclosure, deposition,
or trial dates already on the calendar, and describe the evaluee's
circumstances in general terms so the scope and the evaluation format can
be defined at intake rather than improvised later. Statewide coverage and
the practice's other venues in the state appear on the
[Louisiana page](/locations/louisiana/).
