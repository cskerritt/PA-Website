A Lake Charles engagement with Purinton Analytics begins the way every
engagement in the practice does: with a documented conflict check, a
defined scope, and a records request tailored to the claims, so the written
opinion rests on a record both sides can examine. On that foundation the
firm delivers [vocational expert opinions](/services/vocational-expert-witness/)
on employability and earning capacity,
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) in catastrophic injury
cases, and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. Referrals are welcome from plaintiff and defense
counsel alike.

## Parishes and courts of the Lake Charles metro

The firm accepts matters venued in the District Courts serving Calcasieu
Parish, Cameron Parish, and Jefferson Davis Parish, and in the federal
system through the United States District Court for the Western District of
Louisiana, the United States District Court for the Middle District of
Louisiana, and the United States District Court for the Eastern District of
Louisiana. Because methodology and report standards do not change with the
courthouse, counsel can bring the same expert to a state filing, a removal,
or a related federal action. Statewide coverage is laid out on the
[Louisiana page](/locations/louisiana/).

## Presence in the metro, honestly stated

Purinton Analytics keeps no staffed office in Lake Charles and publishes no
street address for this location. Meetings are by appointment, and the firm
states that directly because accuracy about offices is part of the same
discipline that governs its reports. The practical consequence for counsel
is a remote-first workflow: records review, structured interviews, and
standardized testing under publisher protocols where remote administration
is supported, all documented as carefully as if conducted across a
conference table, with the administration conditions noted in the file. When the referral questions require an in-person
evaluation, or when deposition and trial dates demand presence, travel is
scheduled from the Kansas City home office and confirmed during intake.

## Sourcing the Lake Charles labor market

Employability and earning capacity findings for evaluees in the metro are
grounded in Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage
estimates for the Lake Charles metropolitan area and for Louisiana
statewide, supplemented by state workforce agency labor market information
and direct labor market research where warranted. Life care plans and
[medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/) are priced
to the evaluee's own market, and every source appears in the report itself,
because an opinion that cannot be audited by opposing counsel is not worth
offering. That same standard drives the firm's
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) work when an
opposing expert's report needs a documented critique.

## From conflict check to opinion

Whether a matter is venued in the metro or anywhere in the firm's
[national footprint](/locations/nationwide/), the door in is the same:
[submit a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) with the case caption and
parties, and note any imminent disclosure, deposition, or trial dates so
availability is confirmed early. Any venue-specific registration or
qualification requirement is identified and confirmed during the conflict
check and intake process.
