When a Lafayette case turns on what an injured or displaced person can
still earn, or what a lifetime of care will actually require, counsel need
opinions that survive cross-examination. Purinton Analytics provides
[vocational expert witness work](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[expert testimony and litigation consulting](/services/expert-testimony-litigation-consulting/)
to plaintiff and defense counsel in the Lafayette metro, and coordinates
[damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) in which
independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis while
the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement
structure.

## Where engagements are venued

The firm accepts matters in the District Courts serving Lafayette Parish,
Acadia Parish, St. Martin Parish, and Vermilion Parish, and in Louisiana's
federal courts: the United States District Court for the Western District
of Louisiana, the United States District Court for the Middle District of
Louisiana, and the United States District Court for the Eastern District of
Louisiana. Because the engagement structure is the same in every venue, a
matter that moves between parishes, or between state and federal court,
keeps its expert and its file intact. Coverage for the rest of the state is
described on the [Louisiana page](/locations/louisiana/).

## By-appointment presence, remote-first work

Lafayette meetings are by appointment. The firm maintains no staffed office
in the metro and publishes no street address for this location; plain
statements about presence are part of the same discipline that governs the
reports themselves. In practice, most of the work happens remotely:
records are reviewed, vocational interviews are conducted, and standardized
instruments are administered under publisher protocols where remote
administration is supported. When the referral questions justify an
in-person evaluation, or when deposition and trial dates require an expert
in the room, travel is arranged from the Kansas City home office and
confirmed against counsel's calendar during intake. The result is a
practice that treats distance as a scheduling detail rather than a
limitation on the quality or defensibility of the work.

## Labor market evidence for Lafayette evaluees

Employability and earning capacity findings for evaluees in the Lafayette
metro rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage
estimates for the Lafayette metropolitan area and for Louisiana statewide,
together with state workforce agency labor market information and direct
labor market research where warranted. Life care plans and medical cost
projections are priced to the evaluee's own market rather than to a
national average, and every source is cited in the report so the analysis
can be tested by either side. The same sourcing discipline supports
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) of opposing
expert reports when counsel need an independent, documented look at the
methodology, sources, and conclusions already in the record.

## Opening a Lafayette engagement

Engagements begin with a documented conflict check and a defined scope, in
the Lafayette metro as in the firm's
[national practice](/locations/nationwide/). Send the caption or party
names through the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/), together with any
imminent disclosure, deposition, or trial dates, so availability can be
confirmed at intake. Any venue-specific registration or qualification
requirement is identified and confirmed during the conflict check and
intake process.
