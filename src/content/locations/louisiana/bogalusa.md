its opening lines because that is how the firm approaches every fact it
puts in writing. What Washington Parish counsel get instead of a local
storefront is a national practice that serves the Bogalusa micropolitan
area by appointment, accepting referrals from both plaintiff and defense
lawyers for
[earning capacity and employability evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/),
[medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/), and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Washington Parish venues

The firm accepts matters pending in the District Court serving Washington
Parish and in the federal courts of Louisiana, including the United States
District Court for the Eastern District of Louisiana, the United States
District Court for the Middle District of Louisiana, and the United States
District Court for the Western District of Louisiana. The side of the
caption does not change the method: every engagement starts with a
documented conflict check, a defined scope, and a records request tailored
to the claims, so the written opinion stands on a record both parties can
inspect. The [Louisiana practice page](/locations/louisiana/) collects the
firm's coverage across the rest of the state.

## Serving Bogalusa by appointment

Engagements typically begin remotely, which suits a smaller market well:
records review, structured interviews, and standardized testing proceed
under publisher protocols where remote administration is supported, and
the evaluee is not asked to travel to reach the expert. Where the referral
questions call for in-person evaluation, it is conducted in the evaluee's
own community. When deposition or trial dates in Washington Parish require
presence, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office. Counsel
deal with the same expert and the same report standard from the conflict
check through testimony.

## Vocational evidence for a smaller market

Opinions about employability and earning capacity for Bogalusa-area
evaluees are grounded in Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational
employment and wage estimates covering the local area and Louisiana
statewide, in state workforce agency labor market information, and in
direct employer inquiry where warranted. In a market of this size,
credible analysis means testing conclusions against the work actually
available within the evaluee's reach rather than borrowing conditions from
larger metros. Life care plans and medical cost projections are priced
through provider-specific inquiries and published fee data for the
evaluee's own community, and the report cites each source so that nothing
in it must be taken on faith.

## Requesting a conflict check

Bringing the firm in early keeps the records request, evaluation, and
labor market research ahead of disclosure deadlines instead of behind
them. Any venue-specific registration or qualification requirement is
identified and confirmed during the conflict check and intake process. To
begin, submit the caption or party names through the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and note any imminent disclosure,
deposition, or trial dates. For matters beyond the state, the firm's
[national coverage](/locations/nationwide/) page outlines the same
approach at national scale.
