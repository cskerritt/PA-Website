Counsel in the Monroe metro can retain Purinton Analytics for the full arc
of an expert engagement: intake and conflict check, records review,
evaluation, a written report built for scrutiny, and
[deposition and trial testimony](/services/expert-testimony-litigation-consulting/)
when the case requires it. The substantive work spans
[vocational evaluation of earning capacity and employability](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) in catastrophic injury
matters, and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. Plaintiff and defense referrals are handled under the
same methodology and the same report standard.

## Venues around Monroe

Engagements are accepted for matters in the District Courts serving
Ouachita Parish, Morehouse Parish, Richland Parish, and Union Parish, and
in Louisiana's federal courts: the United States District Court for the
Western District of Louisiana, the United States District Court for the
Middle District of Louisiana, and the United States District Court for the
Eastern District of Louisiana. A matter that moves between those forums
keeps its expert and its record. Counsel elsewhere in the state can review
the firm's [Louisiana coverage](/locations/louisiana/) for the same
engagement structure applied to other venues.

## Scheduling and presence

The firm's only home office is in Kansas City. There is no staffed Monroe
office and no published street address for this location; meetings in the
metro are by appointment, and the firm prefers to say so plainly rather
than imply otherwise. Most steps of an engagement proceed remotely, from
records review through structured interviews and standardized testing
administered under publisher protocols where remote administration is
supported. When an evaluation is better conducted in person, or when
deposition and trial dates call for presence in the courtroom, travel is
scheduled from the Kansas City home office against the deadlines counsel
identify at intake, so testimony dates are protected from the start.
Counsel work with the same expert and the same point of contact at every
stage, and the file is maintained so that any step of the analysis can be
produced, explained, and defended on the record when the engagement reaches
deposition or trial.

## Labor market research for Monroe evaluees

Vocational findings rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational
employment and wage estimates for the Monroe metropolitan area and for
Louisiana statewide, joined with state workforce agency labor market
information and direct labor market research where the referral questions
warrant it. Life care plans and
[medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/) are priced
to the evaluee's own market rather than to national averages. Every figure
in the report is traceable to a disclosed source, which is what allows the
same opinion to serve either side of the caption without adjustment.

## Starting the engagement

The path in is always the same:
[request a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) with the case caption and party
names, and flag any imminent disclosure, deposition, or trial dates.
Matters outside the metro proceed identically under the firm's
[national practice](/locations/nationwide/). Any venue-specific
registration or qualification requirement is identified and confirmed
during the conflict check and intake process.
