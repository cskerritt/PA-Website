Kenner is a substantial suburban city inside the New Orleans metro's
commuting market, and that position shapes how earning capacity should be
analyzed when a Kenner resident is the subject of litigation. The relevant
labor market is rarely the city limits; it is the web of employers an
evaluee can realistically reach across the region. Purinton Analytics
prepares [vocational expert opinions](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care plans](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/) for counsel
on either side of the caption in matters arising in Kenner and throughout
the [New Orleans metro](/locations/louisiana/new-orleans/).

## Venue in Jefferson Parish

Kenner sits in Jefferson Parish, and civil actions filed there proceed in
the District Court serving the parish. Federal practice adds additional
forums: Louisiana matters may be heard in the United States District Court
for the Eastern District of Louisiana, the United States District Court for
the Middle District of Louisiana, or the United States District Court for
the Western District of Louisiana, and the controlling venue for any
particular case is identified during the conflict check and intake. The
report does not change with the courthouse. Every opinion rests on a
documented methodology, disclosed sources, and conclusions confined to the
record, so it holds up whether the trier of fact sits in the parish or in
federal court.

## Kenner's place in the metro labor market

Vocational findings for Kenner evaluees rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates for the New Orleans metropolitan
area and for Louisiana statewide, supplemented by state workforce agency
labor market information and direct labor market research where the
referral questions warrant it. Because Kenner belongs to a broad regional
commuting market, transferable skills analysis considers occupations across
the metro rather than stopping at municipal boundaries, and any opinion
about employability accounts for what commuting patterns make practical for
the specific evaluee, given the restrictions documented in the record.

## Evaluations and meetings in Kenner

Meetings in Kenner are by appointment. The firm keeps no staffed office in
the city and publishes no street address for this location, because
accuracy about presence is part of the same discipline that governs its
reports. Records review, counsel conferences, and many evaluee interviews
proceed remotely. When the referral calls for in-person evaluation, the
assessment is conducted in the evaluee's own community, and when deposition
or trial testimony requires presence, travel is scheduled from the Kansas
City home office. Where a claim also requires economic quantification, the
firm offers
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.
