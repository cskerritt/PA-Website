Expert disclosure deadlines set the tempo of vocational and life care
planning work, and a Gretna case is no exception. By the time counsel
knows a case needs a vocational opinion or a life care plan, the disclosure
calendar is usually already set, and the question becomes whether a
defensible report can be built on the available record in the time that
remains. Purinton Analytics is structured around that reality: intake moves
quickly once the conflict check clears, the records request goes out with
the engagement letter, and the schedule for evaluation, drafting, and
review is mapped against the case's actual deadlines rather than a generic
timeline. The same pace applies whether the firm is retained by the party
asserting the loss or the party contesting it.

## Courts hearing Gretna disputes

Gretna lies in Jefferson Parish, so state-court matters proceed in the
District Court serving the parish. On the federal side, Louisiana offers
the United States District Court for the Eastern District of Louisiana, the
United States District Court for the Middle District of Louisiana, and the
United States District Court for the Western District of Louisiana as
available forums; which of them controls a given dispute is confirmed
during the conflict check and intake, along with any venue-specific
qualification requirement. Reports are written so that either forum's
scrutiny, and opposing counsel's, can be met without revision:
[testimony support](/services/expert-testimony-litigation-consulting/)
follows the same record the written opinion cites.

## The labor market behind a Gretna opinion

An earning capacity opinion for a Gretna evaluee draws on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the New Orleans
metropolitan area and for Louisiana as a whole, together with state
workforce agency labor market information. The town's economy is not
analyzed in isolation; Gretna is part of the wider metro employment market,
and the analysis asks what occupations exist across that market, which of
them the evaluee's skills transfer to, and what the medical restrictions in
the record permit. Where the questions call for it, direct labor market
research supplements the published estimates.

## Scheduling evaluation under deadline

There is no Gretna office; the firm serves the community by appointment and
does not publish a street address here. That does not slow the work.
Interviews and counsel conferences are routinely conducted remotely, which
often shortens the path to a completed
[vocational evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) or
[life care plan](/services/life-care-planning/). When an in-person
assessment is warranted, it takes place in the evaluee's community, and
travel for evaluation, deposition, or trial is arranged from the Kansas
City home office with the disclosure dates in view. If economic
quantification is also needed, independent economist partners perform and
sign that analysis under a coordinated engagement, keeping the damages
presentation consistent without the firm issuing economist opinions itself.
