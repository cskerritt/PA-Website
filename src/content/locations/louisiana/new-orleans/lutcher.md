St. James Parish is where a Lutcher case begins, and it is where Purinton
Analytics begins as well. The parish, not the town line, defines the state
venue, and Lutcher's place within the New Orleans metro frames the labor
market questions an expert must answer. The firm accepts
retentions from counsel prosecuting a claim and counsel defending it, and
delivers the same product to each:
[vocational expert opinions](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care plans](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/) grounded in
the record and written to withstand the scrutiny of the lawyer on the
other side.

## St. James Parish courts and the federal alternatives

Civil actions arising in Lutcher are heard in the District Court serving
St. James Parish. Where federal jurisdiction exists, Louisiana's district
courts are the United States District Court for the Eastern District of
Louisiana, the United States District Court for the Middle District of
Louisiana, and the United States District Court for the Western District
of Louisiana; the firm does not presume which of these governs a given
matter, and the controlling venue, together with any qualification
requirement it carries, is confirmed during the conflict check and intake.
Opinions are drafted to the same standard regardless of forum, so a change
of venue disturbs nothing in the analysis.

## A small town read against a metro market

Lutcher is a small community, and small-community evaluees present a
distinct analytical problem: the local job base is thin, so the honest
question is what work exists within the evaluee's realistic reach across
the wider region. The firm answers it with Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates for the New Orleans
metropolitan area and for Louisiana statewide, with state workforce agency
labor market information, and with direct labor market research when the
referral requires more granularity than published estimates provide. The
analysis never assumes mobility the record does not support, and it never
ignores mobility the record establishes.

## Serving a St. James Parish evaluee

The firm has no office in St. James Parish and publishes no street address
for Lutcher; every meeting here is by appointment, and saying so directly
is part of the discipline the reports themselves reflect. Distance is
managed, not minimized: records move electronically, interviews and
counsel conferences are held remotely when appropriate, and in-person
evaluation is conducted in the evaluee's own community when the referral
questions call for it. Travel for evaluations, depositions, and trial is
scheduled from the Kansas City home office. When the case also demands
economic quantification, the firm arranges
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.
