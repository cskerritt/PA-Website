When the person at the center of a case lives in Harahan, the evaluation
should come to them, not the other way around. Purinton Analytics builds
its work around the evaluee: the clinical interview, the functional and
vocational history, and any in-person assessment are arranged in the
evaluee's own community or conducted remotely, whichever the referral
questions and the individual's circumstances support. Counsel for the
injured party and counsel defending the claim retain the firm on identical
terms, and the resulting
[vocational opinion](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) or
[life care plan](/services/life-care-planning/) reads the same way to both.

## Where a Harahan case is tried

Harahan is a small Jefferson Parish community, and its state-court civil
matters are heard in the District Court serving Jefferson Parish. Federal
litigation involving Louisiana parties may proceed in the United States
District Court for the Eastern District of Louisiana, the United States
District Court for the Middle District of Louisiana, or the United States
District Court for the Western District of Louisiana; the forum that
actually governs, and any registration or qualification requirement that
comes with it, is identified and confirmed during the conflict check and
intake. Because the firm's reports are written for cross-examination from
the outset, nothing about them needs to change when the venue is settled.

## Measuring a Harahan work history against regional data

A resident of a small suburb almost never works only within it, so the
labor market analysis for a Harahan evaluee is regional by design. Opinions
rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage
estimates for the New Orleans metropolitan area and for Louisiana
statewide, on state workforce agency labor market information, and, where
warranted, on direct labor market research. The method runs from the
individual outward: the evaluee's education, skills, and documented
restrictions are established from the record, then tested against the
occupations the surrounding metro market actually contains. A
[medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/) or full life
care plan follows the same record-first sequence when future care is at
issue, with each recommendation tied to a documented clinical foundation
and priced from sources the report identifies.

## Logistics without a local office

Purinton Analytics does not maintain an office in Harahan and does not
publish a street address for this page; candor about presence is part of
the firm's credibility with courts and opposing counsel. What counsel gets
instead is a practice built for distance: secure remote interviews and
conferences, records exchanged electronically, and travel from the Kansas
City home office scheduled whenever an in-person evaluation, a deposition,
or trial testimony requires it. When damages must also be reduced to
economic terms, independent economist partners perform and sign the
economic analysis within a
[coordinated damages engagement](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/),
so the vocational, care-planning, and economic strands stay aligned.
