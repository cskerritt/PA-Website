A vocational or life care planning opinion earns its place in a Westwego
case only if it survives the other side's expert. That is the standard
Purinton Analytics writes to. Whether retained by the plaintiff or by the
defense, the firm produces reports whose methodology, sources, and
reasoning are laid out for inspection, and it applies the same rigor when
the assignment runs the other direction, in
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) of an opposing
expert's earning capacity opinion or life care plan. That symmetry is the
offer to Westwego counsel on either side of the caption: the same standard
governs the work whichever party issues the retention.

## Forums for Westwego litigation

Westwego is part of Jefferson Parish, and civil claims filed in state court
proceed in the District Court that serves the parish. When a matter belongs
in federal court, Louisiana's available forums are the United States
District Court for the Eastern District of Louisiana, the United States
District Court for the Middle District of Louisiana, and the United States
District Court for the Western District of Louisiana. Which court controls
a particular Westwego dispute, and whether that venue imposes any
registration or qualification requirement, is identified during the
conflict check and intake rather than assumed. The firm's working premise
is that every report may be read aloud in any of them.

## Earning capacity in a metro-wide market

Westwego is a small city whose workforce participates in the larger New
Orleans metro economy, so an employability analysis confined to the town
itself would understate or misstate the evaluee's real options. The firm's
[vocational assessments](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) instead rely
on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates
for the New Orleans metropolitan area and for Louisiana statewide, along
with state workforce agency labor market information and, when the
questions require it, direct contact with the labor market. Restrictions
come from the medical record; transferable skills come from the documented
work history; the market data shows where the two intersect.

## In-person and remote evaluation

Service in Westwego is by appointment, with no storefront and no published
street address, and the firm says so plainly because overstating presence
is the kind of shortcut that undermines an expert on the stand. Evaluee
interviews for a [life care plan](/services/life-care-planning/) or
vocational workup can be conducted remotely or arranged in the evaluee's
community, and when a deposition, trial date, or hands-on assessment
requires the expert in the room, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City
home office. Economic damages, where needed, are quantified by independent
economist partners who perform and sign that analysis under a coordinated
engagement, keeping the firm's own opinions strictly vocational and
care-planning in scope.
