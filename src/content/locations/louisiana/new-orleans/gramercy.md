The quality of a vocational opinion or a
[life care plan](/services/life-care-planning/) in a Gramercy matter
depends on the records, the interview, and the method, none of which
requires the expert to live nearby. Purinton Analytics is organized around
that premise, as a national remote-first practice, and it treats Gramercy
matters as full engagements: the same documented conflict check, the same
defined scope, and the same tailored records request that govern its work
everywhere, available to the lawyers bringing the claim and the lawyers
answering it alike.

## Venue for a Gramercy matter

Gramercy lies in St. James Parish, and its state-court civil litigation
proceeds in the District Court serving the parish. Louisiana's federal
forums are the United States District Court for the Eastern District of
Louisiana, the United States District Court for the Middle District of
Louisiana, and the United States District Court for the Western District
of Louisiana; rather than assert which controls, the firm confirms the
governing venue, and any registration or qualification requirement that
attaches to it, during the conflict check and intake. Counsel receive a
report built for whichever bench and whichever opposing expert the case
ultimately draws.

## Labor market analysis for Gramercy evaluees

Employability and earning capacity opinions for a Gramercy evaluee are
anchored in Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage
estimates for the New Orleans metropolitan area and for Louisiana
statewide, joined with state workforce agency labor market information and
direct labor market research where the referral questions justify it.
Gramercy's own employment base is modest, so the analysis weighs regional
opportunity honestly: what occupations the metro and state markets
contain, which of them fit the evaluee's skills and documented
restrictions, and what the record shows about the evaluee's actual access
to them. Numbers enter the report only with a cited source behind them.

## How the work reaches Gramercy

The practice maintains no local office in Gramercy and lists no street
address here; representing otherwise would be inconsistent with the candor
the firm's reports depend on. Instead, engagements run on secure remote
infrastructure: electronic records exchange, remote interviews and counsel
conferences, and drafts delivered on the agreed schedule. In-person
evaluation, when the referral calls for it, happens in the evaluee's
community, and appearances for deposition or trial are scheduled from the
Kansas City home office. If the damages case requires economic
quantification, independent economist partners perform and sign that
analysis through a
[coordinated damages engagement](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/),
with the vocational and care-planning opinions remaining the firm's own.
