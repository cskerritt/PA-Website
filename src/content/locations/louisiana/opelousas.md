## Reading the labor market around Opelousas

Vocational opinions for evaluees from the Opelousas area draw on Bureau of
Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates covering the
local area and Louisiana statewide, on state workforce agency labor market
information, and on direct employer contact where the questions warrant it.

within the evaluee's reach, not against opportunities available only in
distant metros. Life care plans and medical cost projections are priced
through provider-specific inquiries and published fee data for the
evaluee's own community, with every source cited in the report.
