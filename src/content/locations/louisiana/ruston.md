Most of the work behind a defensible vocational opinion happens in the
record: the medical file, the earnings history, the testing data, and the
labor market research that connects them. That is why Purinton Analytics
can serve the Ruston micropolitan area on a remote-first basis without
diluting the product counsel receive. Plaintiff and defense referrals from
Lincoln Parish cover
[earning capacity and employability evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/),
[medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/), and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Lincoln Parish and the federal districts

The firm accepts matters pending in the District Court serving Lincoln
Parish and in the federal courts of Louisiana, including the United States
District Court for the Western District of Louisiana, the United States
District Court for the Middle District of Louisiana, and the United States
District Court for the Eastern District of Louisiana. The engagement
structure does not vary with the retaining side: the same conflict check,
scope definition, and tailored records request precede every opinion, so
that the analysis reads the same to the lawyer who commissioned it and the
lawyer preparing to cross-examine it. Coverage for the rest of the state
is set out on the [Louisiana practice page](/locations/louisiana/).

## What remote-first means in practice

Ruston meetings are by appointment. The firm has no staffed location in
the area and publishes no street address for it; saying so plainly is part
of the discipline the reports are built on. Records review, structured
interviews, and standardized testing proceed remotely under publisher
protocols where remote administration is supported. Where the referral
questions require an in-person evaluation, the evaluation is conducted in
the evaluee's own community, and when deposition or trial dates in Lincoln
Parish call for presence, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home
office around the court's calendar, with dates confirmed at intake so the
schedule never becomes the story.

## Evidence behind the labor market findings

Vocational conclusions for Ruston-area evaluees are anchored in Bureau of
Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates covering the
local area and Louisiana statewide, in state workforce agency labor market
information, and in direct employer contact where the questions warrant
it. A smaller labor market is not a reason for looser analysis; it is a
reason for closer analysis, because placement prospects there depend on a
narrower band of actual openings. Life care plans and medical cost
projections are priced to the evaluee's community through
provider-specific inquiries and published fee data, with each input cited
in the report.

## How to refer a Ruston matter

The earlier the referral, the more room the records request, testing, and
labor market research have to finish before disclosure deadlines. Any
venue-specific registration or qualification requirement is identified and
confirmed during the conflict check and intake process. Submit the caption
or party names through the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and note
any imminent disclosure, deposition, or trial dates. For matters beyond
Louisiana, the firm's [national coverage](/locations/nationwide/) page
explains how the same standard travels.
