A practice built to serve counsel at a distance does not treat Washington as
an outlier. Purinton Analytics works with plaintiff and defense counsel
across Washington State from its Kansas City home office, pairing
remote-first case work with scheduled travel when a matter calls for
presence, so that a Superior Court case in Seattle or Spokane receives the
same vocational expert and life care planning support as a matter filed
close to home. The report standard does not change with the mileage, and
neither does the expectation that opposing counsel will read every page of
it.

## Remote-first work, by appointment in Washington

Most Washington engagements begin and often finish remotely: records review,
structured vocational interviews, and standardized testing under publisher
protocols where remote administration is supported. Meetings in Washington
are by appointment only. The firm maintains no staffed office anywhere in
the state and publishes no Washington street address, and it says so
plainly, because candor about presence is part of the same discipline that
governs its written opinions. When the referral questions call for an
in-person evaluation, or when deposition and trial calendars require it,
travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office and coordinated around
the deadlines counsel identify at intake.

Vocational findings for Washington evaluees rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the state's
metropolitan areas and for Washington statewide, together with state
workforce agency labor market information and direct labor market research
where warranted. Where a case also needs economic analysis, coordinated
damages engagements are available in which independent economist partners
perform and sign the economic analysis while the vocational and
care-planning work proceeds under the same engagement structure.

## Qualification for Washington matters

The forum decides who may testify in it, under its own rules of evidence,
and the firm prepares for that scrutiny in every Washington engagement:
disclosure materials are kept current and ready for production in the form
the venue expects. Any venue-specific registration or qualification
requirement is identified and confirmed during the conflict check and intake
process, so counsel know before substantive work begins that nothing about
qualification remains open.

## Beginning a Washington engagement

To begin a Washington engagement, [request a conflict check](/refer-a-case/)
with the case caption and parties, and flag any disclosure, deposition, or
trial deadlines that bear on scheduling. Referrals from either side of the
caption are reviewed the same way, and counsel receive a prompt answer on
availability once the conflict review is complete.
