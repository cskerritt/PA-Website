A vocational opinion is only as strong as its connection to the labor market where the evaluee actually lives and works, and in Idaho that may mean Boise City, Twin Falls, or a community well outside any metropolitan area. Purinton Analytics writes vocational expert and life care planning opinions for Idaho matters with that connection in mind: evaluees are assessed in their own communities, by secure video or in person, and the resulting reports are grounded in the markets those evaluees would actually enter. Engagements are accepted from either side of the caption on the same terms.

## Courts served across Idaho

The firm accepts engagements for matters in Idaho's District Courts and in the United States District Court for the District of Idaho, with testimony offered at deposition and at trial in both forums. Dedicated pages describe the firm's work in Boise City, Coeur d'Alene, Idaho Falls, Twin Falls, Pocatello, Rexburg, Lewiston, Sandpoint, Blackfoot, Burley, Moscow, Hailey, and Mountain Home. A matter arising outside those areas is handled the same way; the venue analysis is part of intake, not an afterthought.

## Remote-first service, statewide reach

The firm maintains no staffed office anywhere in Idaho and publishes no street address for the state, because accuracy about presence belongs to the same discipline that governs its written opinions. What Idaho counsel get instead is a remote-first structure that mirrors the firm's [nationwide practice](/locations/nationwide/): a documented conflict check, a defined scope, records requests tailored to the claims, and evaluations conducted by secure video or scheduled in the evaluee's community. When in-person evaluation is warranted, or when deposition and trial dates require presence, travel is arranged from the Kansas City home office and planned around the case calendar. Meetings anywhere in Idaho are by appointment.

The labor market foundation is the same in every Idaho report: Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for Idaho statewide and for its metropolitan areas, state workforce agency labor market information, and direct labor market research where the referral questions warrant it. When a matter also needs economic analysis, the firm offers [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under the same engagement structure.

## Registration and disclosure questions

Forum rules differ, and the firm treats them as intake questions rather than surprises. Any venue-specific registration or qualification requirement is identified and confirmed during the conflict check and intake process, and disclosure deadlines are confirmed at retention so that report drafts are scheduled against the dates that actually control the case.

## Beginning an Idaho engagement

The first step in every Idaho matter is the conflict check. Send the caption or the party names through the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/), identify the venue, and flag any disclosure, deposition, or trial dates already on the calendar. Once conflicts clear, the firm confirms scope, fee structure, and schedule in writing, and the records work begins, whether the case sits in a metropolitan District Court or in a rural county far from any of the areas listed above.
