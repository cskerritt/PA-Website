Much of West Virginia's litigation economy sits at the state's edges. The
Huntington-Ashland market reaches into Kentucky and Ohio, Wheeling and
Weirton adjoin Ohio, and Bluefield straddles the Virginia line, so counsel
here regularly handle matters whose parties, employers, and evaluees cross
state boundaries. That pattern suits a national practice. Purinton Analytics
provides vocational expert and life care planning services to plaintiff and
defense counsel throughout West Virginia, applying the same methodology on
whichever side of a border, and of a caption, the work arises.

## West Virginia's Circuit Courts and federal districts

Engagements are accepted for matters in the Circuit Courts, West Virginia's
trial courts, and in the United States District Court for the Northern
District of West Virginia and the United States District Court for the
Southern District of West Virginia. The firm maintains dedicated pages for
the state's metropolitan markets:

communities are accepted on the same terms; no West Virginia county is
outside the practice.

## Serving West Virginia by appointment

The firm has no staffed office in West Virginia, publishes no street address
here, and does not pretend otherwise; plain statements about presence belong
to the same discipline that governs its reports. What counsel get instead is
a remote-first engagement: records review, structured vocational interviews,
and standardized testing under publisher protocols that permit remote
administration, with in-person evaluation scheduled in the evaluee's own
community when the referral questions require it. Travel for evaluations,
depositions, and trial is arranged from the Kansas City home office and
planned around the deadlines counsel identify at intake, the same way the
firm handles its [nationwide practice](/locations/nationwide/).

Vocational findings for West Virginia evaluees rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the relevant
metropolitan areas and for West Virginia statewide, supplemented by state
workforce agency labor market information and direct employer contact where
warranted; for cross-border evaluees, the analysis is grounded in the labor
market where the person actually lives and works. When economic analysis is
also needed, coordinated damages engagements let independent economist
partners perform and sign the economic analysis while the vocational and
care-planning work moves forward within the same engagement structure.

## Qualification and disclosure for West Virginia venues

Whether a matter proceeds in a Circuit Court or in federal court,
qualification to testify is decided by the forum under its rules of
evidence, and the firm's disclosure materials are maintained for production
in whatever form the venue requires. Any venue-specific registration or
qualification requirement is identified and confirmed during the conflict
check and intake process, before substantive work begins.

## Referring a West Virginia case

Every West Virginia matter begins with a conflict check. Send the
caption or the party names through the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/), note the venue, and flag any
disclosure, deposition, or trial dates already on the calendar. Referrals
from plaintiff and defense counsel receive the same review, and availability
is confirmed as soon as the conflict check clears.
