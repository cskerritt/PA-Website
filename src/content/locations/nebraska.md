labor market that evaluee actually lives in, not about national averages, and
an opinion built on generic tables has answered the wrong question before the
first objection is raised. Purinton Analytics answers the right one with
documented local evidence for plaintiff and defense counsel alike across the
state, providing
[earning capacity and employability evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/)
in injury and employment litigation,
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) in catastrophic injury
cases, and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one

## How Nebraska engagements run

The method does not change with the venue. Every engagement opens with a conflict
check on the caption, a scope defined in the engagement letter, and a records
request built around the claims actually pleaded. From there, most of the
work proceeds remotely: records analysis, structured interviews by video,
and standardized testing under publisher protocols where remote
administration is supported. Meetings in Nebraska are by appointment. The
firm maintains no staffed Nebraska office and publishes no street address in
the state, and it says so plainly because candor about presence is part of
the same standard counsel are hiring for the report itself. When the
evaluation should occur in the evaluee's own community, or when the calendar
calls for courtroom presence, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home
office.

Vocational findings for Nebraska evaluees rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates for Nebraska statewide and for
its metropolitan areas, on state workforce agency labor market information,
and on direct labor market research where warranted. Life care plans and
medical cost projections are priced to the evaluee's own market, because the
cost of future care in a rural Nebraska community and in a metropolitan one
are separate factual questions.

## Qualification and disclosure in Nebraska

Professional credentials in vocational evaluation and life care planning are
issued by national certifying bodies, and each forum applies its own rules of
evidence to expert opinion. Any venue-specific registration or qualification
requirement is identified and confirmed during the conflict check and intake
process, so disclosure deadlines are met with the qualification question
already answered.
