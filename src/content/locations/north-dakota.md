A single federal district covers all of North Dakota, and several of the
state's population centers sit directly on the Minnesota line, so counsel
here routinely manage cases whose venue, evaluee, and witnesses are spread
across jurisdictions. Purinton Analytics is structured for exactly that
situation: a national vocational expert and life care planning practice
that works to one report standard wherever the caption is filed, available
to plaintiff and defense counsel alike, with coordinated damages
engagements in which independent economist partners perform and sign the
economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds
under one engagement structure.

## Where North Dakota cases are heard

Engagements are accepted for matters in the District Court, North Dakota's
trial court of general jurisdiction, and in the United States District
Court for the District of North Dakota. The practice profiles the state's
communities individually, including the cross-border metros of
Fargo,
Grand Forks, and
Wahpeton, along with
Bismarck,
Minot,
Williston,
Dickinson, and
Jamestown. A case venued outside
those areas changes nothing about the engagement: the conflict check, the
scope definition, and the records request are the same everywhere.

## Serving North Dakota from a distance, honestly

The firm has no staffed office in North Dakota and publishes no street
address for the state. Meetings are by appointment, and the practice says
so plainly because honesty about presence is part of the same discipline
that governs its written opinions. Most North Dakota engagements are
conducted remotely: records review, structured interviews, and
standardized testing under publisher protocols where remote administration
is supported. When the referral questions require evaluating someone in
person in their own community, or when deposition and trial dates call for
appearance, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office and
confirmed against counsel's deadlines at intake. Vocational findings for
North Dakota evaluees rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational
employment and wage estimates for North Dakota and its metropolitan areas,
state workforce agency labor market information, and direct labor market
research where warranted. For evaluees whose realistic labor market spans
the state line, the analysis describes the market they actually face
rather than stopping at a border the evaluee's commute does not respect.
Life care plans and medical cost projections for North Dakota residents are
priced to the evaluee's own market using provider-specific inquiries and
published fee data for the region.

## Qualification and disclosure

Certifications held within the practice are issued by national
credentialing bodies rather than by any single state. Whether an opinion
is admitted in a North Dakota matter is a question for the forum under its
own rules of evidence, and any venue-specific registration or
qualification requirement is identified and confirmed during the conflict
check and intake process. Disclosure materials, prior testimony lists, and
fee schedules are provided in the form the venue and the parties require.

## Starting a North Dakota engagement

Every matter begins with a documented conflict check and a defined scope.
Submit the caption or party names through the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/), and identify any imminent
disclosure, deposition, or trial dates so the schedule can be set
realistically from the start. A cleared conflict check is followed by a
written scope and fee agreement before any records change hands. For
matters reaching beyond the state, the
firm's [national coverage](/locations/nationwide/) page explains how the
same structure operates nationwide.
