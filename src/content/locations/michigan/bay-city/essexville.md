## A small community inside the Bay City labor market

Essexville is a small community within the Bay City metropolitan area, and
its residents work across that wider market rather than within town
boundaries alone. Vocational findings therefore rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Bay City
metropolitan area and for Michigan statewide, supplemented by state workforce
agency labor market information and direct labor market research where the
referral questions warrant it. Transferable skills analysis considers the
occupations realistically open to the evaluee given the commuting patterns of
the area, not a hypothetical national market. Where a claim requires economic
quantification, the firm offers
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Evaluating an Essexville evaluee

The practice is remote-first, and much of the record work, interviewing, and
labor market research proceeds without travel. When the referral calls for
in-person evaluation, the evaluee is seen in or near their own community, by
appointment, so that the assessment reflects their actual circumstances
rather than requiring them to travel to the expert. The firm keeps no staffed
office in Essexville or elsewhere in the Bay City area and publishes no local
street address, because accuracy about presence is part of the same
discipline that governs its reports. When deposition or trial testimony
requires attendance, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office and
planned around the court's calendar. The same structure supports
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) assignments,
where the work product is an examination of an opposing expert's methodology
rather than an independent evaluation.
