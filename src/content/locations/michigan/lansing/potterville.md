What does a Potterville attorney actually receive when a case is referred
to Purinton Analytics? Not a brochure promise, but a sequence: a conflict
check documented before substantive discussion, an engagement letter that
fixes scope in writing, a records request drafted against the actual
claims, an evaluation matched to the referral questions, and a signed
report in which every source is cited well enough for the other side to
check it. The sequence is identical whether the referring lawyer
represents the plaintiff or the defense, and it ends, when the case
requires it, with
[testimony](/services/expert-testimony-litigation-consulting/) that says
nothing the file cannot support.

## Venue for Potterville matters

Potterville sits in Eaton County, so its state-court cases proceed in the
Circuit Court of Eaton County. For federal claims, Michigan is served by
the United States District Court for the Western District of Michigan and
the United States District Court for the Eastern District of Michigan;
which district holds a particular caption is one of the things the
conflict check and intake resolve before the engagement letter issues.
Counsel with matters spread across the surrounding metro can see the
wider venue picture on the
[Lansing metro page](/locations/michigan/lansing/).

## Grounding opinions in the regional data

For a Potterville evaluee, employability and earning capacity findings
draw on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage
estimates for the Lansing-East Lansing metropolitan area and for Michigan
statewide, alongside state workforce agency labor market information and
direct employer contact where a specific occupation must be verified. A
small community's residents work across a much larger region, and the
analysis defines the evaluee's reasonable labor market explicitly rather
than leaving the boundary to argument. When future medical needs are in
issue, a [medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/) or
life care plan prices each item of care to the market the evaluee will
actually face, source by source.

## How the evaluation happens

No office exists in Potterville and none is implied; the practice runs by
appointment, and stating that openly is part of the same standard the
reports are held to. Most engagements move on records, remote interviews,
and standardized instruments administered under publisher protocols.
Referral questions that require an in-person evaluation are handled in the
evaluee's own community, and when depositions or trial dates call for
attendance, travel is planned from the Kansas City home office with the
scheduling order in hand. Counsel deal with one point of contact from
first call to final invoice, so responsibility for the file always rests
in an identifiable place.
