Purinton Analytics has no office in DeWitt. That admission opens this page
deliberately, because an expert practice that would shade the truth about
its own address should not be trusted with an earning capacity opinion.
What DeWitt counsel get instead of a storefront is a national practice run
by appointment: conflict check first, scope in writing, records request
tailored to the pleadings, and a single point of contact from intake
through testimony. Referrals are welcome from plaintiff and defense
counsel equally, for
[vocational evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) and life
care planning alike.

## DeWitt's place in the court system

DeWitt is a Clinton County community, and its state-court matters are
heard in the Circuit Court of Clinton County. Michigan's federal
districts, the United States District Court for the Eastern District of
Michigan and the United States District Court for the Western District of
Michigan, stand as the available federal forums; determining which one
governs a given caption is part of the conflict check and intake, not a
guess made from a map. The firm's files are assembled so that the same
report serves either courthouse without revision, because venue disputes
should never ripple into the expert file.

## Data before conclusions

Labor market findings for DeWitt evaluees are constructed from Bureau of
Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the
Lansing-East Lansing metropolitan area and for Michigan statewide,
supplemented by state workforce agency labor market information and direct
research with employers where a referral question turns on a specific
occupation. Conclusions follow the data rather than the retention letter:
if the record supports residual earning capacity, the report says so no
matter who signs the invoice. That indifference to which side retained the
firm is what makes the opinion durable in front of a jury. Transferable
skills analysis, wage sampling, and placeability findings are each tied to
their sources, so the path from record to conclusion can be audited by
anyone who cares to try.

## The mechanics of a DeWitt evaluation

Appointments replace the office. Records review, structured interviews,
and standardized testing proceed remotely under publisher protocols where
the instruments permit; when the questions require presence, the
evaluation is conducted in the evaluee's own community rather than
requiring travel to the expert. When a deposition or trial date demands
attendance, the trip is arranged from the Kansas City home office with
lead time built around the scheduling order.
[Rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) engagements
follow the same remote-first logic, since a records-based critique needs
no local address at all.
