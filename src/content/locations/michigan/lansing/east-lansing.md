East Lansing shares billing in the very name of the Lansing-East Lansing
metropolitan area, and litigation arising here deserves analysis that treats
the community as more than a suffix. Purinton Analytics accepts vocational
and life care planning referrals from East Lansing counsel on either side of
the caption, applying the same sequence to each:
a documented conflict check, a written scope, and a records request built
around the claims actually pleaded. Counsel who want the broader regional
picture first can start with the
[Lansing metro overview](/locations/michigan/lansing/); this page addresses
what an East Lansing engagement looks like in practice.

## Ingham County venue and the federal alternatives

East Lansing matters in state court proceed in the Circuit Court of Ingham
County, where the firm accepts both plaintiff and defense engagements.
Michigan's federal forums, the United States District Court for the
Eastern District of Michigan and the United States District Court for the
Western District of Michigan, are available as well, and the district
controlling a particular caption is confirmed during the conflict check
and intake rather than assumed. Whatever the forum, the deliverable is the same: a written opinion
whose sources are cited specifically enough that opposing counsel can
reproduce every step.

## Reading the East Lansing labor market

An earning capacity opinion for an East Lansing evaluee is anchored in
Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for
the Lansing-East Lansing metropolitan area, supplemented by Michigan
statewide estimates, state workforce agency labor market information, and
direct employer research where the referral questions warrant it. The
analysis stays qualitative until the data supports a figure, and every
figure in the report traces to a named source. Where a claim also requires
economic loss calculations, the firm offers
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## How an East Lansing evaluee is evaluated

There is no Purinton Analytics office in East Lansing, and the firm will
not pretend otherwise; meetings happen by appointment because saying so is
more honest than printing a borrowed address. Most evaluations proceed
remotely through records review, structured interviews, and standardized
instruments administered under publisher protocols. When the referral
questions call for an in-person assessment, the evaluation is conducted in
the evaluee's own community, and when deposition or trial attendance is
required, the trip is planned from the Kansas City home office far enough
ahead to protect the disclosure calendar.
[Life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) engagements follow the
same pattern, with care costs priced to the evaluee's actual market rather
than national averages.

## Opening an East Lansing engagement

The first step is always the same: submit the caption or party names
through the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/), and flag any disclosure,
deposition, or trial dates already on the docket. Intake also surfaces any
venue-specific registration or qualification requirement before the
engagement letter is signed, so there are no surprises at the disclosure
stage. Related matters elsewhere in the state can be reviewed against the
[Michigan overview](/locations/michigan/), and counsel with cases beyond
Michigan will find the firm's reach described on the
[national coverage](/locations/nationwide/) page. The practice was built to
serve courts far from its home office, and East Lansing sits comfortably
within that design.
