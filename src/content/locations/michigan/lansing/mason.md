An expert opinion in a Mason case earns nothing by being persuasive if it
cannot be checked. Purinton Analytics writes every Mason report against
that standard: each source named, each inference shown, the path from
record to conclusion laid out plainly enough that opposing counsel or an
opposing expert can retrace it step by step. A
[vocational expert](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) report built
this way needs no rehabilitation at deposition, because nothing in it is
asserted that the file cannot support. Plaintiff and defense referrals are
equally welcome, and the method does not change with the caption.

## State and federal forums for Mason litigation

Mason sits in Ingham County, and matters in Michigan's state system
proceed in the Circuit Court of Ingham County, where engagements are
accepted from either side of the caption. On the federal side, the state
is served by the United States District Court for the Eastern District of
Michigan and the United States District Court for the Western District of
Michigan; the district that actually controls a given case is confirmed
during the conflict check and intake rather than presumed from geography.
Counsel comparing venues across the region will find the fuller court
picture on the [Lansing metro page](/locations/michigan/lansing/).

## Mason workers in a regional market

A Mason evaluee's employability is not measured against the town line.
Findings rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and
wage estimates for the Lansing-East Lansing metropolitan area and for
Michigan statewide, joined by state workforce agency labor market
information and, where the occupation is specialized, direct inquiry with
employers. The report distinguishes carefully between what the regional
market offers in theory and what this evaluee, with these restrictions and
this work history, can actually access. That distinction is where
vocational opinions are won and lost under cross-examination, and it is
documented rather than asserted.

## Seeing the evaluee, not just the file

Records carry most of the analysis, but some referral questions require
sitting across from the person. The firm keeps no Mason office and lists no
local address; evaluations are by appointment, conducted remotely where the
instruments and the questions permit, and conducted in the evaluee's own
community when they do not. Standardized testing follows publisher
protocols in either setting. When an in-person evaluation, deposition, or
trial appearance is needed, travel originates at the Kansas City home
office and is booked around the court's calendar, not the other way
around. [Life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) referrals
follow the same logistics, with care costs priced to the market where the
evaluee actually lives.
