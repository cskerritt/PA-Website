For the injured person at the center of a Grand Ledge case, the evaluation
itself is often the most human part of the litigation: an interview about
work history, a battery of standardized tests, questions about what a day
now looks like. Purinton Analytics designs that experience to be rigorous
without being punishing. A Grand Ledge evaluee is interviewed on a schedule
that respects treatment and family obligations, tested under publisher
protocols whether the session is remote or face to face, and never asked to
travel far from home for the firm's convenience. Counsel for either party
receive the same product: an opinion built from records the other side can
check.

## The courts that serve Grand Ledge

Grand Ledge lies in Eaton County, and its state-court matters are heard in
the Circuit Court of Eaton County. Federal filings from the area may
proceed in the United States District Court for the Western District of
Michigan or the United States District Court for the Eastern District of
Michigan; the controlling district for any particular caption is
established during the conflict check and intake. The firm prepares every
report to withstand the scrutiny of whichever forum ultimately applies, and
the [Lansing metro page](/locations/michigan/lansing/) sets out the
metro's wider court landscape for counsel managing related matters.

## Placing Grand Ledge skills in the regional economy

Vocational conclusions about a Grand Ledge evaluee are grounded in Bureau
of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the
Lansing-East Lansing metropolitan area and for Michigan statewide, together
with state workforce agency labor market information and direct labor
market research where the occupation calls for it. The question is never
abstract employability; it is whether identified occupations exist within
this evaluee's reasonable labor market at wages the data will support.
Where lost earnings must be reduced to a damages figure, the firm offers
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Logistics, stated plainly

There is no local office in Grand Ledge, no storefront, and no street
address to publish, and the firm says so because accuracy about presence is
the same discipline as accuracy about data. Appointments are the mechanism:
remote sessions handle most records review, interviews, and testing, while
in-person evaluations are held in the evaluee's own community when the
referral questions require direct observation. Depositions and trial
appearances are scheduled with travel from the Kansas City home office,
booked early enough that the case calendar never bends around the expert.
A [life care plan](/services/life-care-planning/) prepared this way prices
future care to the market the evaluee will actually use.
