The disclosure calendar, not the courtroom, is where most expert problems
begin, and counsel in Charlotte know it. A vocational or life care planning
opinion that arrives late, or arrives thin, costs a case more than a
vigorous cross ever will. Purinton Analytics builds Charlotte engagements
backward from the dates that matter: the disclosure deadline, the
deposition window, the trial setting. Scope is confirmed in writing at
intake, the records request goes out early, and counsel on either side of a
caption receive interim status rather than silence. The
[expert testimony and litigation consulting](/services/expert-testimony-litigation-consulting/)
page describes how that support continues once the report is served.

## Where Charlotte cases are heard

Charlotte sits in Eaton County, and state-court matters from the town
proceed in the Circuit Court of Eaton County. Federal claims may be filed
in the United States District Court for the Western District of Michigan
or the United States District Court for the Eastern District of Michigan;
which district governs a given matter is a venue question resolved during
the conflict check and intake, not a fact this page will guess at. Engagements are accepted for either
forum, and the report format is built to satisfy
federal disclosure practice even when the caption is a state one, because a
file prepared to the stricter standard survives in either room.

## The labor market behind a Charlotte opinion

Employability and earning capacity findings for a Charlotte evaluee draw on
Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates
covering the Lansing-East Lansing metropolitan area and Michigan as a
whole, along with state workforce agency data and targeted labor market
research when the occupation at issue demands it. Charlotte workers
participate in an employment market considerably wider than the town
itself, and the analysis reflects that reality without overstating it:
placeability is assessed against jobs that actually exist within a
reasonable labor market, documented source by source.

## Evaluations on the evaluee's schedule

Purinton Analytics maintains no office in Charlotte and publishes no local
address, because claiming a presence it does not have would sit poorly with
the accuracy the rest of its work depends on. What counsel get instead is
flexibility: remote records review and interviews for most engagements,
standardized testing under publisher protocols, and in-person evaluation in
the evaluee's own community when the referral questions require it. Travel
for evaluations, depositions, and trial dates is arranged from the Kansas
City home office with the case calendar in view.
[Medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/) and life
care plans are priced against the evaluee's actual market, with each rate
tied to a cited source.
