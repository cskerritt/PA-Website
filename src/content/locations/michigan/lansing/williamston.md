A reader of a Purinton Analytics report in a Williamston case should not
be able to tell which side made the referral, and that is a design
constraint rather than an accident of tone. Vocational and life care
planning findings for a Williamston evaluee are stated the same way
whoever retained the firm: qualified where the record is thin, firm where
it is not, and silent where the evidence does not reach. Counsel get an
opinion that does not lean toward the engagement letter, which is
precisely what makes it useful in a courtroom where the other side's
version of events will be heard in full.

## Ingham County venue and the federal districts

Williamston sits in Ingham County, and matters proceeding under Michigan
law in state court are heard in the Circuit Court of Ingham County, where
engagements are accepted from either side of the caption. Federal claims
may proceed in the United States District Court for the Eastern District
of Michigan or the United States District Court for the Western District
of Michigan; the district that controls a particular caption is pinned
down during the conflict check and intake, and the disclosure practice of
that court then governs the schedule. The working file does not change
with the courthouse: a documented conflict check, a scope letter, a
records request matched to the claims, and an opinion whose every source
is cited. Counsel coordinating related cases across the metro will find
the wider court picture on the
[Lansing metro page](/locations/michigan/lansing/).

## What the findings rest on

Earning capacity and employability findings for Williamston evaluees are
built from Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage
estimates for the Lansing-East Lansing metropolitan area and for Michigan
statewide, together with state workforce agency labor market information
and direct labor market inquiry when a specific occupation requires
confirmation. The report walks from data to conclusion in visible steps,
because the standard the firm writes to is not persuasion but
reproducibility: an opposing expert given the same sources should be able
to retrace the path. Where economic loss must be quantified, the firm
arranges
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Meeting the evaluee where they are

The firm maintains no Williamston office and publishes no address here;
candor about presence is part of the same discipline that governs the
reports. Evaluations run by appointment, remotely for most records review,
interviews, and publisher-protocol testing, and in person in the evaluee's
own community when the referral questions require direct assessment.
Travel for evaluations and court dates originates at the Kansas City home
office and is scheduled to protect the disclosure calendar. A
[life care plan](/services/life-care-planning/) for a Williamston evaluee
prices care to the local market the evaluee will actually use, provider by
provider.
