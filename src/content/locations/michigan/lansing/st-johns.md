Not every St. Johns referral asks for a new opinion. Some ask whether an
opinion already served can withstand scrutiny, and Purinton Analytics
accepts both kinds of engagement from St. Johns counsel: original
vocational and life care planning analysis, and
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) of work
another expert has signed. The discipline runs in both directions. An
original report is written so its sources can be checked line by line; a
review holds someone else's report to exactly that standard, no harsher
and no softer. Plaintiff and defense counsel receive the same measured
product either way, because a critique that only ever cuts against one
side is advocacy, not analysis.

## Clinton County venue and the federal districts

St. Johns matters filed in Michigan's state system are heard in the
Circuit Court of Clinton County. On the federal side, the state is served
by the United States District Court for the Western District of Michigan
and the United States District Court for the Eastern District of Michigan,
and the district with authority over a specific caption is confirmed
during the conflict check and intake rather than announced here. The firm
accepts St. Johns engagements for either forum and prepares the file to
the same evidentiary standard regardless, because the caption can move
even when the facts do not.

## The data behind a St. Johns opinion

The data spine of a St. Johns opinion is the Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates for the Lansing-East Lansing
metropolitan area, read alongside Michigan statewide estimates, state
workforce agency labor market information, and direct employer research
where the referral questions justify it. Transferable skills analysis then
narrows the field from occupations that exist to occupations this evaluee
can perform and plausibly obtain, and the report states the limits of the
evaluee's reasonable labor market rather than leaving them implied. Each
step is cited, because a wage conclusion that cannot be traced is a wage
conclusion that will not survive. The same sourcing discipline governs
review work: when the firm critiques an opposing report, the critique
points to the data the original opinion skipped, not to rhetoric.

## Appointments in the evaluee's community

The firm has no office in St. Johns and does not manufacture one for
marketing purposes; every meeting is by appointment, and most of the work
travels over records rather than roads. Interviews and standardized
testing proceed remotely under publisher protocols where the instruments
support it. When the questions require an in-person evaluation, the
session is held where the evaluee lives, not where the expert would prefer,
and appearances for deposition or trial are planned with travel from the
Kansas City home office well ahead of the date. Care-cost work follows the
same principle:
[medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/) are priced
to the providers and rates the evaluee will actually encounter.

Send the caption or party names through the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) along with any scheduled disclosure,
deposition, or trial dates, and note whether the engagement involves
reviewing an opinion already served. Any venue-specific registration or
qualification requirement is identified and confirmed during intake,
before the engagement letter is signed. Counsel weighing related matters
elsewhere in the metro can consult the
[Lansing metro page](/locations/michigan/lansing/); the
[Michigan overview](/locations/michigan/) and the
[national coverage](/locations/nationwide/) page place the practice in its
statewide and national context. Whether the file needs a fresh opinion or
a careful reading of an existing one, the standard applied is the same.
