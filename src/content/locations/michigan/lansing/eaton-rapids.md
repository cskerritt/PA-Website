Small communities punish sloppy vocational work. In a town the size of
Eaton Rapids, an opinion that casually assumes a deep local bench of
alternative jobs will not match what the evaluee, the jury, or opposing
counsel knows about the place, and the mismatch discredits everything
around it. Purinton Analytics treats labor market scale as a finding to be
proven, not a backdrop to be assumed: the analysis states plainly what a
small employment base offers, what the surrounding region adds, and what
the difference means for this evaluee's earning capacity. Both sides of
the caption get that same candor.

## Eaton County courts and the federal system

State-court litigation from Eaton Rapids proceeds in the Circuit Court of
Eaton County, where the firm takes plaintiff and defense referrals alike.
Michigan's federal trial courts, the United States District Court for the
Western District of Michigan and the United States District Court for the
Eastern District of Michigan, are both available forums, and the one that
controls a particular matter is identified during the conflict check and
intake. Reports are drafted to disclosure standards strict enough for
either system, so a change of forum never means a rewrite.

## Honest arithmetic about a small market

Findings for an Eaton Rapids evaluee begin with Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates for the Lansing-East Lansing
metropolitan area and for Michigan statewide, refined by state workforce
agency labor market information and direct labor market research when a
specific occupation needs verification. The metro data describes the
region; the report then asks the harder question of what portion of that
region constitutes a reasonable labor market for a person living in Eaton
Rapids with these restrictions and this history. Where the answer feeds a
damages model, the firm structures
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## By appointment, without pretense

No Purinton Analytics office exists in Eaton Rapids, and none is claimed;
the firm considers a truthful account of its own footprint the first test
of its reliability on everything else. Engagements run by appointment,
with records review, interviews, and standardized testing handled remotely
under publisher protocols wherever the instruments allow. In-person
evaluations, when the referral questions require them, take place in the
evaluee's own community. Deposition and trial attendance is coordinated
with travel from the Kansas City home office, scheduled to serve the
court's calendar. [Life care plans](/services/life-care-planning/) built
for Eaton Rapids evaluees price future care to the market they will
actually purchase it in.
