## A small community inside a metro labor market

Earning capacity for an Albion resident is not assessed against the town
line alone. The report defines a commutable labor market and explains why
that definition follows from the evaluee's residence, restrictions,
transportation, and work history, which for a small community ordinarily
means looking across the wider Battle Creek, MI metropolitan area. The data
foundation is named in the report itself: Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates for the Battle Creek metropolitan
area and for Michigan statewide, state workforce agency labor market
information, and direct labor market research where the referral questions
warrant it. Each conclusion is tied to those sources, so the path from data
to opinion stays visible on the page and can be tested at deposition rather
than merely asserted.

## Evaluating an evaluee who lives in Albion

Interviews and testing are conducted remotely when the referral questions
and instruments permit, which spares an Albion evaluee the burden of travel
and keeps scheduling inside the case calendar. When the evaluation requires
an in-person component, it is arranged in the evaluee's own community rather
than at a distant office. The firm keeps no staffed office in Albion and
publishes no street address here; meetings with counsel are by appointment,
ordinarily by videoconference, and when a deposition or trial date requires
personal presence, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office.
That candor about presence is deliberate: a practice that asks the trier of
fact to credit its analysis should be equally exact about its own logistics.
