## Reading the commuting market honestly

Because Springfield sits within a metropolitan commuting market, the
vocational analysis begins by asking where this evaluee can actually work,
given residence, restrictions, transportation, and prior employment. The
answer is documented rather than assumed: Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates for the Battle Creek metropolitan
area and for Michigan statewide anchor the wage evidence, state workforce
agency labor market information supplies the local texture, and direct labor
market research is added when the referral questions require it. The report
then explains why the defined market fits this evaluee, so that the
market definition itself can be challenged, tested, and defended on the
record instead of standing as an unexamined premise.

## Working with a Springfield evaluee

Evaluation logistics are arranged around the evaluee, not the other way
around. Where the referral questions and testing protocols permit,
interviews and assessments are completed remotely; where hands-on evaluation
is required, it takes place in the evaluee's own community. The firm has no
staffed office in Springfield and lists no street address for this location,
because a practice built on verifiable statements does not imply offices it
does not keep. Counsel meetings are by appointment, most often by
videoconference, and when a deposition or trial in Calhoun County requires
the expert in the room, travel is planned from the Kansas City home office
with enough lead time to protect the court's schedule.
