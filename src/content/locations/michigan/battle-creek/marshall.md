## Venue and the Calhoun County record

Marshall lies in Calhoun County, and the Circuit Court is the state trial
forum for the civil dockets that most often produce vocational and life care
planning referrals, from personal injury and workers' compensation to
employment and domestic relations matters. On the federal side, Michigan
offers the United States District Court for the Western District of Michigan
and the United States District Court for the Eastern District of Michigan;
which district controls a given Marshall caption is a question resolved
during the conflict check and intake, not a fact the firm presumes. Reports
are written the same way for either system: methodology disclosed, sources
cited, assumptions stated, so the opinion can be examined on its merits at
deposition and trial. Venue-specific registration or qualification
requirements, where they exist, are confirmed during intake as well.

## Marshall wage earners and the metro market

A Marshall evaluee's earning capacity is measured against the labor market
actually available to that person, and for a small Calhoun County community
that market ordinarily reaches across the Battle Creek, MI metropolitan
area. The analysis defines the commutable market from the evaluee's
residence, restrictions, and work history, then documents it with Bureau of
Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Battle
Creek metropolitan area and for Michigan statewide, state workforce agency
labor market information, and direct labor market research when the referral
questions call for it. Because every conclusion cites its source, the
opposing expert can check the work, which is precisely the standard the firm
writes to.

## Appearing in Marshall by appointment

The firm maintains no staffed office in Marshall and publishes no local
street address, and it says so plainly because accuracy about presence
belongs to the same discipline that governs its written opinions. Counsel
conferences and pre-deposition preparation are typically held by
videoconference. Evaluations proceed remotely where the referral questions
and testing instruments allow, and when in-person assessment is warranted it
is conducted in the evaluee's own community rather than in a rented suite.
If a Calhoun County courtroom setting requires personal appearance, travel
is arranged from the Kansas City home office and coordinated with counsel
against the case schedule, so appearance dates are commitments rather than
aspirations.
