## The courts that hear Muskegon Heights cases

State-court engagements from Muskegon Heights proceed in the Muskegon
County Circuit Court. Where a matter belongs in federal court, Michigan's
districts, the United States District Court for the Western District of
Michigan and the United States District Court for the Eastern District of
Michigan, are the available forums, and the controlling venue for a given
caption is identified during the conflict check and intake, together with
any registration or qualification requirement that venue imposes. Whatever
the forum, opposing counsel receives a document built to be examined:
methods disclosed, sources cited, and no conclusion resting on the
identity of the retaining party.
