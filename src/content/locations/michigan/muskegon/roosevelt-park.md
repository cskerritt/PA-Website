## Where Roosevelt Park disputes are heard

Roosevelt Park lies within Muskegon County, and its state-court matters
are heard in the Circuit Court for that county. For federal litigation,
Michigan makes available the United States District Court for the Western
District of Michigan and the United States District Court for the Eastern
District of Michigan; which district controls a particular case is
settled during the conflict check and intake, as is any venue-specific
registration or qualification requirement. The firm's work product does
not vary by courtroom: disclosed methods, cited sources, and reasoning
that either party can test.

## Reading a small suburb against a regional market

A suburb of this size does not constitute its own labor market, and no
credible opinion would treat it as if it did. Employability and
earning-capacity findings for Roosevelt Park evaluees are therefore
grounded in Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage
estimates for the Muskegon-Norton Shores metropolitan area and for
Michigan statewide, refined with state workforce agency labor market
information and direct labor market research when the referral questions
justify it. Future care is costed the same way it will be purchased,
locally:
[medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/) rest on
provider-specific inquiries in the evaluee's market. Where the damages
model requires an economist, the firm arranges a
[coordinated damages engagement](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning opinions proceed in
parallel.

## The mechanics of a Roosevelt Park evaluation

Records review, structured interviews, and standardized testing under
publisher protocols are ordinarily completed remotely, which spares the
evaluee unnecessary disruption and keeps the schedule in counsel's
control. Purinton Analytics maintains no staffed office in Roosevelt Park
and publishes no local street address; stating that plainly is part of
the same habit of accuracy the reports themselves are built on. When the
referral requires in-person assessment, the appointment is set in the
evaluee's community, and testimony obligations are met through travel
planned from the Kansas City home office around the case calendar.
