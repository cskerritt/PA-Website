The real test of an expert report is not who commissioned it but how it
reads in the hands of the lawyer on the other side. That standard governs
every opinion Purinton Analytics writes for Muskegon-Norton Shores
litigation, whether the engagement comes from plaintiff or defense counsel
and whether it calls for
[vocational expert evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), or a
[coordinated damages engagement](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. A report written to withstand cross-examination needs
no rewriting when cross-examination arrives.

## Muskegon County courts and captions

The metro consists of Muskegon County, and state-court engagements proceed
in the Muskegon County Circuit Court. Federal matters from the region are
accepted in the United States District Court for the Western District of
Michigan, and the firm also takes cases in the United States District
Court for the Eastern District of Michigan when a caption is venued across
the state. In each forum the deliverable is the same document: a written
opinion whose methods are disclosed, whose sources are cited, and whose
conclusions follow from the record rather than from the identity of the
retaining party.

## Honest logistics for a Muskegon engagement

Purinton Analytics maintains no staffed office in Muskegon and publishes no
street address for this location. Meetings are by appointment, and the firm
says so plainly because accuracy about presence is the same professional
habit as accuracy about evidence. Most engagements open remotely: records review,
structured interviews, and standardized testing under publisher protocols
where remote administration is supported. When the referral questions call
for an in-person evaluation in the evaluee's own community, or when
deposition and trial dates require attendance, travel is scheduled from
the Kansas City home office and confirmed against counsel's calendar
before commitments are made.

## Muskegon labor market research

Vocational findings for Muskegon County evaluees are grounded in Bureau of
Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the
Muskegon metropolitan area and for Michigan statewide, supplemented by
state workforce agency labor market information and direct labor market
research where the referral questions warrant it. Life care plans and
[medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/) are priced
to the evaluee's own market through provider-specific inquiries and
published fee data, since future care will be purchased locally and the
report should reflect that reality. Nothing in the opinion asks to be
taken on trust; the sources are on the page, where either side of the
caption can weigh them.
