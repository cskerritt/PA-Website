## Calhoun County courts, state and federal

The Battle Creek metropolitan area consists of Calhoun County, and the firm
accepts engagements for matters in the Circuit Court for Calhoun County and
in Michigan's federal courts, the United States District Court for the
Western District of Michigan and the United States District Court for the
Eastern District of Michigan, according to where the matter is filed.
Referrals arrive from injury, workers' compensation, employment, and
domestic dockets, and the deliverable is consistent across all of them: an
opinion whose methodology and sources are disclosed fully enough for
cross-examination to engage the substance. Any venue-specific registration
or qualification requirement is identified and confirmed during the conflict
check and intake process.

## How the firm appears in Battle Creek

Battle Creek meetings are by appointment. The firm has no staffed office
here and publishes no street address for this location, a fact stated
plainly because accuracy about presence belongs to the same discipline that
governs the firm's written work. In practice, counsel conferences and
pre-deposition preparation are handled by videoconference, evaluations are
conducted remotely when the referral questions and testing protocols permit,
and in-person assessment is arranged in the evaluee's own community when the
evaluation requires it. When deposition or trial settings in Calhoun County
call for personal appearance, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home
office and coordinated with counsel against the case calendar.

## Battle Creek labor market foundations

Vocational findings for Battle Creek evaluees rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Battle Creek
metropolitan area and for Michigan statewide, state workforce agency labor
market information, and direct labor market research where warranted. The
report defines the evaluee's commutable labor market and explains why that
definition follows from the evaluee's residence, restrictions, and work
history. Earning capacity conclusions are then tied to the named data, so
the path from source to opinion is visible and the analysis can be tested
rather than merely asserted.
