Purinton Analytics accepts vocational expert and life care planning
engagements from either side of the caption throughout the
Detroit-Warren-Dearborn metro. Referrals from the region most often call for
[earning capacity and employability evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/)
in injury and employment disputes, [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/)
in catastrophic injury matters, and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. Plaintiff firms and defense firms receive the same
intake discipline, the same methodology, and the same report standard,
because a written opinion should read identically no matter who retained it.

## Venue footprint across the Detroit metro

The metro spans Wayne County, Oakland County, Macomb County, Livingston
County, Lapeer County, and St. Clair County, and the firm takes matters
venued in the circuit courts of each. State-court engagements typically
proceed in the Wayne County Circuit Court and its counterparts across the
surrounding counties. Federal matters proceed in the United States District
Court for the Eastern District of Michigan, and the firm also accepts cases
in the United States District Court for the Western District of Michigan
when venue lies there. Because the analysis is built on records, structured
interviews, and published data rather than on proximity to a courthouse, the
same work product supports state and federal captions alike, and the same
file discipline supports direct testimony and cross-examination equally.

## By appointment in Detroit

Detroit meetings are by appointment. The firm maintains no staffed office in
the metro and publishes no local street address, because accuracy about
presence belongs to the same discipline that governs its reports. Most
engagements begin remotely: records review, interviews, and standardized
testing under publisher protocols where remote administration is supported.
When the referral questions call for in-person evaluation, or when
deposition and trial dates require attendance, travel is scheduled from the
Kansas City home office and planned around counsel's calendar. Counsel keep
a single point of contact and one report standard regardless of where any
given step of the work is performed.

## Labor market research for Detroit-area evaluees

Vocational findings for evaluees in the metro rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Detroit
metropolitan area and for Michigan statewide, supplemented by state
workforce agency labor market information and by direct labor market
research where the referral questions warrant it. Life care plans and
[medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/) are priced to
the evaluee's own market rather than to national averages, and every source
is cited in the report so that opposing counsel can trace each conclusion
back to its origin. The goal is an opinion that survives scrutiny, not one
that merely states a result.

## Starting a Detroit engagement

The first step is a conflict check, not a consultation. Submit the caption
or party names through the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and note
any imminent disclosure, deposition, or trial dates so that scheduling can
be assessed honestly at the outset. Counsel weighing venues elsewhere in the
state can review the [Michigan overview](/locations/michigan/), and firms
with matters beyond Michigan will find the same engagement structure
described on the [national coverage](/locations/nationwide/) page.
