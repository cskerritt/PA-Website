## Where Monroe matters are heard

The Monroe, MI metropolitan area consists of Monroe County, and the firm
accepts engagements for matters in the Circuit Court for Monroe County and
in Michigan's federal courts, the United States District Court for the
Eastern District of Michigan and the United States District Court for the
Western District of Michigan, depending on where the case is filed. The
referral questions vary with the docket, from lost earning capacity in
injury litigation to mitigation in employment disputes and future care needs
in catastrophic cases, but the deliverable does not: a written opinion whose
methodology, sources, and reasoning are stated plainly enough for either
side to test them. Any venue-specific registration or qualification
requirement is identified and confirmed during the conflict check and intake
process.

## Evaluation logistics for Monroe evaluees

Meetings in Monroe are by appointment. The firm maintains no staffed office
here and publishes no street address for this location; saying so plainly is
part of the same discipline that governs its reports. What counsel get
instead is a practice built to serve the evaluee where the evaluee actually
is. Remote evaluation is available when the referral questions and testing
protocols permit it, and in-person evaluation is arranged in the evaluee's
own community when the assessment requires it. Records conferences and
pre-deposition preparation are handled by videoconference, and when
deposition or trial dates require presence, travel is scheduled from the
Kansas City home office against the operative litigation calendar.

## Labor market data behind the opinions

Employability and earning capacity conclusions for Monroe County evaluees
are grounded in Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage
estimates for the Monroe metropolitan area and for Michigan statewide,
supplemented by state workforce agency labor market information and direct
labor market research when the referral requires it. The report identifies
the evaluee's commutable labor market and explains the basis for that
definition, so the connection between the data and the conclusion is
explicit rather than implied, and cross-examination can proceed on what the
report actually says.
