An engagement in the Lansing-East Lansing metro starts with paperwork, not
promises: a documented conflict check, a scope confirmed in writing, and a
records request shaped by the actual claims. That is how Purinton Analytics
opens every matter, for plaintiff and defense counsel alike, whether the
referral calls for
[earning capacity and employability evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), or a
[coordinated damages engagement](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. The method is deliberately unexciting, because an
opinion built on process holds up better than an opinion built on
salesmanship.

## Where Lansing matters are venued

State-court engagements from the metro proceed in the circuit courts of
Ingham County, Eaton County, and Clinton County, and the firm accepts
matters in each. On the federal side, the firm takes cases in the United
States District Court for the Western District of Michigan and in the
United States District Court for the Eastern District of Michigan,
depending on where the matter is venued. The court holding the caption
changes the disclosure schedule; it does not change the analysis. The same
records-based file supports a bench conference, a deposition, and
cross-examination without adjustment.

## Appointments, not an office

The firm keeps no staffed office in Lansing and lists no street address for
this location. Saying so plainly is part of the brand: honesty about
presence is the same habit as honesty about data. Meetings with counsel and
evaluees are by appointment. Most engagements from the metro proceed
remotely, through records review, structured interviews, and standardized
testing administered under publisher protocols where remote administration
is supported by the instrument. When the questions require an in-person evaluation, or when a
deposition or trial date requires attendance, travel is scheduled from the
Kansas City home office with enough lead time to protect the case calendar,
and counsel keep a single point of contact throughout the engagement.

## Labor market evidence for the Lansing area

Vocational findings for evaluees in the metro rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Lansing-East
Lansing metropolitan area and for Michigan statewide, alongside state
workforce agency labor market information and direct labor market research
where warranted. Care-cost work follows the same logic:
[medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/) and life
care plans are priced to the evaluee's own market using provider-specific
inquiries and published fee data, never generic national figures. Each
source is cited in the report so that the opinion can be tested line by
line by whoever sits across the table.

## Beginning a Lansing engagement

Any venue-specific registration or qualification requirement is identified
and confirmed during the conflict check and intake process, so the place to
start is the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/): submit the caption or
party names and note any disclosure, deposition, or trial dates already
set. Counsel with related matters elsewhere in the state can consult the
[Michigan overview](/locations/michigan/), and the
[national coverage](/locations/nationwide/) page explains how the firm
serves courts well beyond it.
