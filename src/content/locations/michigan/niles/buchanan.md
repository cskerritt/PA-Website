An expert practice built around a big-city office asks the injured person to come to the expert. For a Buchanan evaluee, that model gets the geography backward. Purinton Analytics runs a remote-first national practice: records analysis and counsel conferences proceed by secure video, and when a matter calls for in-person vocational or care-planning assessment, the evaluation comes to the evaluee's own community. The firm serves Buchanan counsel on both plaintiff and defense engagements, with [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) and [medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/) work held to a publication standard suitable for the opposing side to read line by line.

## Courts Serving Buchanan

Buchanan lies in Berrien County. State-court matters proceed in the Circuit Court of Berrien County, and the firm calibrates each engagement to that court's scheduling orders and disclosure expectations before any opinion work begins. Michigan's federal forums are the United States District Court for the Eastern District of Michigan and the United States District Court for the Western District of Michigan; the district with venue over any particular Buchanan matter, along with any venue-specific registration or qualification requirement, is confirmed as part of the conflict check and intake process rather than presumed from the map. Whatever the forum, the deliverable is the same kind of document: an opinion whose reasoning is visible on its face.

## A Small Town Inside the Niles Metro Market

A vocational opinion about a Buchanan worker cannot treat the town as its own labor market, and it cannot dissolve the town into statewide averages either. Buchanan sits within the Niles, MI metropolitan area, whose principal city is Niles, and that metro's commuting market is the relevant frame for transferable-skills and placement analysis. The firm grounds its findings in Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the metro area and for Michigan as a whole, in state workforce agency labor market information, and in direct labor market research when the published estimates do not reach the occupation at issue. What the evaluee can earn is always answered relative to the market the evaluee can actually reach. The [Niles metro page](/locations/michigan/niles/) sets out the regional approach in more depth.
