St. Joseph matters in Michigan's state system proceed in the Circuit Court of Berrien County, whose scheduling orders establish the expert disclosure sequence around which the firm plans its work. In federal practice, Michigan is served by the United States District Court for the Western District of Michigan and the United States District Court for the Eastern District of Michigan; which district controls a particular St. Joseph filing, and what that court's disclosure practice requires of a testifying expert, is identified during the conflict check and intake rather than assumed. ## How Evaluations Are Scheduled

By-appointment service is the rule for every location outside Kansas City, and St. Joseph is no exception: the firm maintains no local office there and publishes no street address, and it says so because candor about presence belongs in the same category as candor about methodology. Interviews and records work proceed by secure video in most matters, which lets substantive analysis begin as soon as the records arrive. In-person evaluation, when the referral questions require it, takes place in the evaluee's community; appearances for deposition or trial are made by travel arranged from the Kansas City home office and planned against the court's calendar rather than the firm's convenience.
