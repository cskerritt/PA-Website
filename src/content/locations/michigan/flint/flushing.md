An evaluation should meet the evaluee where they actually live, and for a
Flushing resident that means the evaluation comes to Flushing, not the
other way around. Purinton Analytics designs its
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) and
[vocational evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) work around
the evaluee's circumstances: interviews scheduled around treatment and
work obligations, testing administered under conditions the publisher
permits, and care priced to the market where the evaluee will actually
receive it. Plaintiff and defense referrals are handled identically,
because the person being evaluated deserves the same care regardless of
which side sent the engagement letter.

## The county and its courts

Flushing lies in Genesee County, and civil matters filed in state court
proceed in the Circuit Court for Genesee County. Federal practice in
Michigan runs through the United States District Court for the Eastern
District of Michigan and the United States District Court for the Western
District of Michigan, and the district with venue over a specific dispute
is confirmed as part of the conflict check and intake. The firm writes to
a single standard for all of these forums: opinions documented thoroughly
enough that a stranger to the case, including a hostile one, can trace
every conclusion back to a source.

## What the surrounding market means for a Flushing evaluee

Flushing is a small community inside a county-wide employment market, so
vocational conclusions look outward to the work available across the Flint
metropolitan area. The analytical backbone is Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates for the Flint area and for
Michigan statewide, joined to state workforce agency labor market
information and, when the referral warrants, direct labor market research
with employers. Life care plans and
[medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/) are priced
against the evaluee's own care market rather than a national average, so
the numbers presented to the court reflect what treatment costs where this
evaluee lives. Metro-level venue and market context appears on the
[Flint metro page](/locations/michigan/flint/).

## Logistics, stated honestly

There is no staffed Purinton Analytics location in Flushing, and the firm
publishes no street address here because it will not imply an office that
does not exist. Engagements typically open remotely with records review
and video interviews, which most referrals never need to go beyond. When
in-person evaluation is warranted, or when a deposition or trial requires
the expert in the room, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home
office and coordinated with counsel against the case calendar. For matters
that need economic modeling alongside the vocational and care-planning
work, [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
are available, with independent economist partners performing and signing
the economic analysis inside one engagement structure.

during the conflict check and intake process. The
