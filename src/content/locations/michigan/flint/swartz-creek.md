The real test of an expert report in a Swartz Creek injury or employment
case is not whether retaining counsel likes it; it is whether the report
survives the reader who wants it excluded. Purinton Analytics writes to
that reader. Every
[vocational assessment](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) and
[life care plan](/services/life-care-planning/) prepared for a Swartz
Creek matter discloses its data, names its methods, and draws only the
conclusions the record supports, which is why the firm can accept work
from plaintiff and defense counsel without changing a thing about how the
work is done.

## Venue and courts for Swartz Creek matters

Swartz Creek belongs to Genesee County, so state-court litigation is heard
in the Circuit Court for Genesee County. Federal matters in Michigan fall
to the United States District Court for the Eastern District of Michigan
or the United States District Court for the Western District of Michigan,
with the controlling venue for any given case identified during the
conflict check and intake. Cross-examination practice differs between
those systems, but a report built on disclosed sources and reproducible
methods needs no separate version for each; the firm files the same
quality of document everywhere, and expects opposing counsel to hold it
to that standard at deposition.

## A small city inside a county labor market

Swartz Creek is a small city whose residents work throughout the Flint
metropolitan area, and vocational analysis treats the county market as the
relevant frame. Findings rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational
employment and wage estimates for the Flint area and for Michigan
statewide, on state workforce agency labor market information, and on
direct labor market research where the referral questions justify it.
When an opposing expert has already filed a market opinion, the firm's
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) service
examines it against the same published sources, with the same rigor
applied to the firm's own work. Metro-wide context is collected on the
[Flint metro page](/locations/michigan/flint/).

## Getting the evaluation done

No Purinton Analytics office exists in Swartz Creek, and the firm says so
rather than hinting otherwise; all meetings are by appointment. The
typical engagement opens remotely, with records review, structured
interviews, and standardized testing conducted under publisher protocols
that support remote administration. If the referral requires an in-person
evaluation, the firm travels to the evaluee's community, and deposition or
trial attendance is planned with travel from the Kansas City home office
so that dates set by the court are never at risk. Where the damages
picture calls for economic modeling, the firm arranges
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis alongside the vocational and care-planning work in a single
engagement structure.
