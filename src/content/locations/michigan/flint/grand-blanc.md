## Genesee County venue for Grand Blanc disputes

Grand Blanc is a Genesee County community, and state-court matters proceed
in the Circuit Court for Genesee County. In the federal system, Michigan
litigants appear in the United States District Court for the Eastern
District of Michigan or the United States District Court for the Western
District of Michigan, and the controlling district for any particular
matter is settled during the conflict check and intake rather than
presumed from geography. The firm's reporting practice does not bend to
the forum: sources disclosed, methods reproducible, and opinions bounded
by the record, whether the audience is a state trial judge or a federal
gatekeeper.
