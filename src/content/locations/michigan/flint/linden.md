Linden is a small town, and honest vocational analysis for a small town
begins by admitting what the local job base can and cannot absorb. An
opinion that treats a community of this size as though it offered the full
occupational spread of a major city would not survive cross-examination,
and it should not. Purinton Analytics writes
[vocational opinions](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) and
[life care plans](/services/life-care-planning/) for Linden evaluees that
name the actual market frame, distinguish local opportunity from
commuting-distance opportunity, and support every characterization with a
published source, whichever party issued the engagement letter.

## The Genesee County forums behind a Linden case

Linden's civil disputes in state court are heard in the Circuit Court for
Genesee County, the county in which the town sits. Federal litigation in
Michigan proceeds in the United States District Court for the Eastern
District of Michigan or the United States District Court for the Western
District of Michigan, and the venue that governs a specific matter is
determined during the conflict check and intake rather than announced in
advance. The firm accepts engagements in all of these forums from either
side of the caption, on identical terms.

## Small-town evaluee, metro-scale data

The data frame for a Linden evaluee is the Flint metropolitan area,
captured in Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage
estimates for that area and for Michigan statewide, alongside state
workforce agency labor market information. Where the referral turns on
finer questions, the firm performs direct labor market research and
documents it so the survey can be examined. Life care planning follows the
same discipline in the other direction:
[medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/) are priced
to the care market the evaluee actually uses, with provider-specific
inquiry where warranted, rather than to an averaged market the evaluee
will never see. The metro-wide analysis lives on the
[Flint metro page](/locations/michigan/flint/).

## By-appointment service, no local storefront

The firm keeps no office in Linden, publishes no address here, and treats
that plainness as part of its credibility; presence claims are held to the
same standard as market claims. Engagements ordinarily begin remotely
with records review, structured interviews, and standardized testing
under publisher protocols that allow remote administration. In-person
evaluation, when the referral needs it, is conducted in the evaluee's own
community, and travel for evaluations, depositions, or trial comes from
the Kansas City home office on a schedule agreed with counsel. For
matters requiring economic modeling, the firm structures
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work advances under one
engagement structure.
