## Forum context for a Mount Morris dispute

Mount Morris is part of Genesee County, and its state-court civil matters
are heard in the Circuit Court for Genesee County. At the federal level,
Michigan cases proceed in the United States District Court for the Eastern
District of Michigan or the United States District Court for the Western
District of Michigan; which of those districts controls a given matter is
one of the questions resolved during the conflict check and intake. The
firm prepares its reports to satisfy the most demanding of these forums,
on the view that an opinion fit for federal gatekeeping loses nothing in a
state courtroom.

## Where a Mount Morris evaluee finds work

Mount Morris is a small community whose labor market is, in practical
terms, the Flint metropolitan area. Vocational conclusions therefore rest
on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates
for the Flint area and for Michigan statewide, supported by state
workforce agency labor market information and by direct labor market
research when the referral questions require it. Placeability and earning
capacity are described qualitatively from those sources, and the report
distinguishes clearly between what the published data establishes and
what remains professional judgment. The county-wide market discussion is
carried on the [Flint metro page](/locations/michigan/flint/).

## Serving Mount Morris by appointment

The firm operates no office in Mount Morris and offers no local address,
stating that outright because candor about logistics belongs in the same
category as candor about data. Engagements usually begin and often finish
remotely: records analysis, structured interviews by video, and
standardized testing under publisher protocols where remote
administration is supported. When an in-person evaluation is called for,
the firm conducts it in the evaluee's own community, and appearances for
deposition or trial are met with travel from the Kansas City home office
planned around the dates the court has set. Matters needing economic
modeling can proceed as
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/),
with independent economist partners performing and signing the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work continues under a
single engagement structure.
