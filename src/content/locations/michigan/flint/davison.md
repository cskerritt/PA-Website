Sometimes the engagement begins with someone else's report. Counsel in a
Davison matter receive an opposing vocational assessment or life care plan
and need to know, quickly and candidly, whether its methods hold up.
Purinton Analytics offers
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) for exactly
that situation, applying to the opposing expert's work the same tests the
firm's own reports must pass, and it offers original
[vocational evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) and
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) when counsel need
affirmative opinions of their own. Both sides of the caption retain the
firm, and both get the same candor: if the opposing report is sound, the
review says so.

## Davison's courts, state and federal

Davison sits in Genesee County, which places state-court matters in the
Circuit Court for Genesee County. Michigan's federal trial courts are the
United States District Court for the Eastern District of Michigan and the
United States District Court for the Western District of Michigan; the
district that controls a particular Davison case is established during the
conflict check and intake. Whether the opinion is affirmative or rebuttal,
it is drafted for the forum's scrutiny, with every source cited and every
analytical step visible on the page, so that the judge deciding
admissibility sees the reasoning rather than a bare conclusion asking to
be trusted.

## Measuring work prospects from Davison

A Davison evaluee's vocational outlook is assessed against the employment
market of the Flint metropolitan area, the market a Genesee County
resident realistically draws on. The firm works from Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Flint area
and Michigan statewide, from state workforce agency labor market
information, and from direct labor market research when the referral
demands more resolution than published data offers. The same sources
anchor rebuttal work: an opposing report's market claims are checked
against what the public data actually shows, not against a competing
impression. County-level venue and market discussion continues on the
[Flint metro page](/locations/michigan/flint/).

## Practical arrangements for Davison referrals

Purinton Analytics has no office in Davison and publishes no local
address, because a firm that audits other experts' claims cannot be loose
with its own. Work proceeds by appointment and, in most engagements,
remotely: records analysis, video interviews, and standardized testing
under publisher protocols where remote administration is supported.
In-person evaluation happens in the evaluee's own community when the
referral requires it, and travel for depositions or trial comes from the
Kansas City home office, scheduled against counsel's dates. Where a full
damages presentation is needed, the firm provides
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while vocational and care-planning work runs under the same
engagement structure.
