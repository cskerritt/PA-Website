Most of the work Purinton Analytics performs for Ann Arbor matters happens
at a distance, and deliberately so. Records review, structured interviews,
and standardized testing under publisher protocols proceed remotely
wherever remote administration is supported, which means a Washtenaw County
engagement is usually underway within days of a cleared conflict check
rather than waiting on travel. Plaintiff and defense counsel in the metro
retain the firm for
[earning capacity and employability evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Venues for Washtenaw County matters

The Ann Arbor metro consists of Washtenaw County, and state-court
engagements proceed in the Washtenaw County Circuit Court. Federal matters
are accepted in the United States District Court for the Eastern District
of Michigan, and the firm also appears in the United States District Court
for the Western District of Michigan when a case is venued there. The
firm's role is identical in each forum: an independent examination of
employability, earning capacity, or future care needs, documented well
enough that either side of the caption can probe the reasoning, test the
assumptions, and find the sources exactly where the report says they are.

## When distance work is not enough

Remote-first does not mean remote-only. When the referral questions call
for an in-person evaluation, when an evaluee is better served face to
face, or when deposition and trial dates require presence in Ann Arbor,
travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office and confirmed against
the case calendar. What the firm does not do is pretend to be local: there
is no staffed Ann Arbor office, no published street address, and no
implication of one, because a practice that certifies the accuracy of its
reports should be equally accurate about where it keeps its desks.
Meetings in the metro are by appointment.

## Labor market and care-cost research

Vocational opinions for Washtenaw County evaluees rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Ann Arbor
metropolitan area and for Michigan statewide, together with state workforce
agency labor market information and direct labor market research where the
questions warrant it. On the care side,
[life care plans](/services/life-care-planning/) and
[medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/) are priced
to the evaluee's own market through provider-specific inquiries and
published fee data, because future care will be purchased where the evaluee
lives, not at a national average. Sources are cited in full so the opinion
can be audited, not merely read, by either side of the caption.

## Starting an Ann Arbor engagement

The engagement begins with a conflict check. Submit the caption or party
names through the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and identify any
disclosure, deposition, or trial dates already scheduled, so the firm can
say honestly whether the proposed timeline is workable as written. The
[Michigan overview](/locations/michigan/) describes coverage across the
state, and the [national coverage](/locations/nationwide/) page sets out
the same structure for matters anywhere else.
