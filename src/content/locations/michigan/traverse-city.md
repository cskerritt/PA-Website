Traverse City anchors a multi-county metropolitan area that takes in Benzie,
Grand Traverse, Kalkaska, and Leelanau counties, and litigation here can
originate anywhere across that footprint. Purinton Analytics serves counsel
throughout the Traverse City region on either side of the caption, providing
[vocational evaluation and expert testimony](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. The region is served as part of the firm's broader
[Michigan practice](/locations/michigan/), with intake, methodology, and
reporting standards that do not change with the size of the venue.

## Venue footprint across the region

The firm accepts engagements for matters in the Circuit Courts serving
Benzie County, Grand Traverse County, Kalkaska County, and Leelanau County,
and in Michigan's federal courts, the United States District Court for the
Western District of Michigan and the United States District Court for the
Eastern District of Michigan, according to where the matter is filed. Because
a single referral in this region may involve an evaluee who lives in one
county, worked in another, and is litigating in a third, the engagement is
scoped around the case as counsel frame it, and any venue-specific
registration or qualification requirement is identified and confirmed during
the conflict check and intake process.

## By-appointment service, honestly framed

There is no staffed Purinton Analytics office in Traverse City, and the firm
publishes no street address for this location. That candor is deliberate:
accuracy about presence is part of the same discipline that governs the
firm's written opinions. Counsel meetings, records conferences, and
deposition preparation proceed by videoconference on ordinary scheduling.
Evaluations are conducted remotely when the referral questions and testing
protocols permit, or arranged in the evaluee's own community when the
referral calls for in-person assessment. When deposition or trial dates
require presence in the venue, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home
office and confirmed against the litigation calendar well before the
appearance date.

## Grounding opinions in the local labor market

Employability and earning capacity findings for evaluees in the Traverse
City region rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and
wage estimates for the Traverse City area and for Michigan statewide, state
workforce agency labor market information, and direct labor market research
where warranted. In a region where an evaluee's realistic commuting range
may cross county lines, the report is explicit about which labor market
defines the evaluee's opportunities and why, so that the employability and
placeability conclusions can be tested on their stated foundation rather
than on assumptions left unwritten.

## Beginning an engagement

Every Traverse City engagement begins the way the firm's work begins
everywhere: a documented conflict check, a defined scope, and a records
request tailored to the claims. To start, submit the caption or party names
through the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/), and note any imminent
disclosure, deposition, mediation, or trial dates so the report timeline can
be built around them. Counsel in the region receive the same service the
firm delivers across its [national practice](/locations/nationwide/): a
written opinion that rests on a record both sides can examine.
