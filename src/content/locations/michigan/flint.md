The Flint metro is a single county, Genesee County, which keeps the venue
picture straightforward and lets the engagement conversation move quickly
to what actually matters: the claims, the records, and the questions the
expert is being asked to answer. Purinton Analytics serves plaintiff and
defense counsel in Genesee County with
[vocational expert evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. The firm also provides
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) when counsel
need an opposing report examined with the same rigor applied to the firm's
own work.

## Genesee County venues

State-court matters proceed in the Genesee County Circuit Court, and the
firm accepts engagements venued there from either side of the caption.
Federal matters are handled in the United States District Court for the
Eastern District of Michigan, and the firm likewise takes cases in the
United States District Court for the Western District of Michigan when a
related matter is venued across the state. In every forum the deliverable
is the same: a written opinion that discloses its sources and reasoning
fully enough for opposing counsel to test it, because that is the standard
a report should meet before anyone swears to it.

## Presence in Flint, stated plainly

Purinton Analytics keeps no staffed office in Flint and publishes no street
address for this location. Meetings are by appointment, and there is no
storefront to walk into, because claiming a local office that does not
exist would be inconsistent with the discipline the firm applies to its
reports. Engagements generally open remotely, with records review,
structured interviews, and standardized testing under publisher protocols
where remote administration is supported. When the referral requires an
in-person evaluation in the evaluee's own community, or when deposition and
trial dates require attendance, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City
home office and coordinated with counsel well in advance.

## Reading the Flint labor market honestly

Vocational conclusions for Genesee County evaluees are grounded in Bureau
of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the
Flint metropolitan area and for Michigan statewide, supported by state
workforce agency labor market information and by direct labor market
research where the referral questions call for it. Local conditions are
described from those published sources, never from impression or anecdote.
Life care plans and medical cost projections are priced to the evaluee's
own market, with provider-specific inquiries where warranted, so the
resulting figures reflect what care actually costs where the evaluee
lives rather than what it costs somewhere else.
