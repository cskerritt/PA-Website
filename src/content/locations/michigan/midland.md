## Labor market evidence for Midland evaluees

Vocational findings for evaluees in the Midland area rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Midland
metropolitan area and for Michigan statewide, state workforce agency labor
market information, and direct labor market research where the referral
requires it. The report defines the evaluee's commutable labor market
explicitly, explains why that definition follows from residence,
restrictions, and work history, and ties each employability and earning
capacity conclusion to the data behind it, so the analysis stands on stated
evidence rather than assumption.
