Ypsilanti workers have never treated the city line as the edge of their
labor market, and a defensible vocational opinion cannot treat it that way
either. Among the larger communities in the Ann Arbor metro, Ypsilanti
sends its residents to jobs across Washtenaw County and throughout the
surrounding region, so the question in an earning capacity dispute is
rarely what work exists in town; it is what work the evaluee can actually
reach, perform, and hold across the commuting market as a whole. Purinton
Analytics answers that question for plaintiff and defense counsel through
[earning capacity and employability evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Courts serving Washtenaw County matters

Ypsilanti sits in Washtenaw County, and state-court engagements arising
there proceed in the Washtenaw County Circuit Court. For federal matters,
Michigan offers the United States District Court for the Eastern District
of Michigan and the United States District Court for the Western District
of Michigan as available forums; the controlling venue for a particular
case is identified during the conflict check and intake, not assumed from
a map. The firm's product is the same in every forum: a written opinion
whose reasoning, assumptions, and sources are laid out plainly enough for
either side of the caption to test them.

## The commuting market, measured honestly

Because an Ypsilanti evaluee's realistic employment options span the
metro, vocational findings rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational
employment and wage estimates for the Ann Arbor metropolitan area and for
Michigan statewide, supplemented by state workforce agency labor market
information and direct employer research where the referral questions
warrant it. Transferable skills analysis considers the occupations the
regional market actually supports, not a generic national list. On the
care side, [medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/)
and life care plans are priced to the evaluee's own market, because future
care will be purchased where the evaluee lives.

## Meeting evaluees where they are

There is no staffed Purinton Analytics office in Ypsilanti, and the firm
does not publish a street address suggesting otherwise; candor about
presence is part of the same discipline that governs the reports. Records
review, structured interviews, and standardized testing proceed remotely
wherever publisher protocols support remote administration, which keeps
most engagements moving without travel. When an in-person evaluation
serves the evaluee better, or when deposition and trial dates require
presence, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office and meetings
in Ypsilanti are held by appointment.
