Counsel usually arrive at a page like this with a forum already in mind,
so venue is the right place to begin. Milan is briefed in this practice as
a Washtenaw County community, and state-court matters arising there
proceed in the Washtenaw County Circuit Court. On the federal side,
Michigan offers the United States District Court for the Eastern District
of Michigan and the United States District Court for the Western District
of Michigan; the firm does not guess which district governs a particular
Milan case, because the controlling venue is confirmed during the conflict
check and intake along with any registration or qualification requirement
the forum imposes. That confirmation happens before the engagement letter
is signed, not after the report is due. Within any of these courts, Purinton Analytics provides
[vocational expert testimony](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) for plaintiff
and defense counsel alike.

## What the forum receives

Whatever the court, the deliverable is the same: an independent, sourced
opinion on employability, earning capacity, or future care needs that
either side of the caption can audit line by line. For matters needing
economic loss calculations, the firm offers
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure, so the vocational foundation and the economic
superstructure are built to fit each other from the outset.

## Placing Milan in the metro labor market

Milan is a small town, and its residents' employment prospects are
properly measured against the regional market rather than the town
itself. Vocational conclusions therefore rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Ann Arbor
metropolitan area and for Michigan statewide, alongside state workforce
agency labor market information and, where the questions justify it,
direct contact with employers. No wage or employment figure enters an
opinion without a citation that opposing counsel can pull and check.
Future care costs are researched in the evaluee's own market, since care
is purchased where the evaluee lives, not at a national average.

## Evaluation logistics

The practice is remote-first by design. Records analysis, structured
interviews, and standardized testing proceed at a distance wherever the
test publishers support remote administration, which means a Milan
engagement is typically underway shortly after the conflict check clears.
When an in-person evaluation is warranted, it is conducted in or near the
evaluee's community; when deposition or trial requires presence, travel is
scheduled from the Kansas City home office. The firm maintains no staffed
office in Milan, publishes no local street address, and will not imply a
storefront it does not keep. Meetings are by appointment.
