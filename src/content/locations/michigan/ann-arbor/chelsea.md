Expert work in a Chelsea case is governed less by geography than by the
calendar. Disclosure deadlines, deposition notices, and trial settings
arrive on their own schedule, and the worst position an attorney can be in
is holding an expert report that was rushed to meet them. Purinton
Analytics is structured against that outcome: scope is defined at intake,
the records request goes out early, and the firm tells counsel honestly at
the conflict check stage whether the remaining runway supports a
defensible opinion. On that footing, plaintiff and defense counsel with
Chelsea matters retain the firm for
[earning capacity and employability evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[expert testimony and litigation consulting](/services/expert-testimony-litigation-consulting/).

## Where Chelsea cases are heard

Chelsea lies in Washtenaw County, so state-court engagements proceed in
the Washtenaw County Circuit Court. Federal matters in Michigan may be
venued in the United States District Court for the Eastern District of
Michigan or the United States District Court for the Western District of
Michigan; rather than assuming which district applies, the firm confirms
the controlling forum during the conflict check and intake, and calendars
the engagement to that court's disclosure and discovery schedule from the
start. Any registration or qualification requirement the chosen forum
imposes is identified in that same intake conversation.

## A small town inside a larger labor market

Chelsea is a small Washtenaw County town whose working residents
participate in the broader regional economy, and the vocational analysis
is built accordingly. Employability and earning capacity findings draw on
Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for
the Ann Arbor metropolitan area and for Michigan statewide, on state
workforce agency labor market information, and on direct labor market
research where the referral questions warrant it. Every source is cited so
the opinion can be audited rather than merely read. Where a case requires
economic loss figures, the firm offers
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Scheduling the evaluation

Most of the work happens at a distance and on schedule: records review,
structured interviews, and standardized testing proceed remotely wherever
publisher protocols permit, which protects the disclosure date from
travel delays. When the questions call for an in-person evaluation, it is
arranged in the evaluee's own community, and when deposition or trial
requires presence in Washtenaw County, travel is planned from the Kansas
City home office against the case calendar. The firm has no staffed
Chelsea office and publishes no street address here; a practice that
certifies the accuracy of its reports owes counsel the same accuracy about
where it actually sits. Meetings are by appointment.
