## County and courthouse context

Dexter is a small Washtenaw County town, and civil matters filed in state
court proceed in the Washtenaw County Circuit Court. Michigan's federal
forums are the United States District Court for the Eastern District of
Michigan and the United States District Court for the Western District of
Michigan; the district that controls any particular Dexter case, along
with any venue-specific qualification requirement, is identified and
confirmed during the conflict check and intake. The report is written for
whichever forum receives it, with reasoning and sources exposed for either
side of the caption to test.

## Dexter's place in the regional labor market

Measuring a Dexter resident's earning capacity against Dexter alone would
understate the market they actually work in, so vocational findings are
grounded in Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage
estimates for the Ann Arbor metropolitan area and for Michigan statewide,
supported by state workforce agency labor market information and direct
employer inquiry where the referral questions justify it. The analysis is
qualitative about place and rigorous about sourcing: no figure appears in
an opinion without a citation opposing counsel can retrieve. Future care
costs in [life care plans](/services/life-care-planning/) and
[medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/) are
researched in the evaluee's own market, where the care will actually be
purchased.

## In-person and remote evaluation options

Distance does not dilute the work. Records analysis, structured
interviews, and standardized testing are completed remotely wherever the
test publishers support remote administration, so most Dexter engagements
begin within days of a cleared conflict check. Where the referral calls
for face-to-face evaluation, the session is arranged in or near the
evaluee's community; where deposition or trial demands presence, travel is
scheduled from the Kansas City home office. The firm keeps no staffed
office in Dexter and publishes no local address, because honesty about
presence is part of the credibility it sells. Meetings are by appointment.
