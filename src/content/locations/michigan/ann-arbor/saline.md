For an injured or ill evaluee living in Saline, the logistics of the
evaluation matter almost as much as the evaluation itself. A person
recovering from a serious injury should not have to organize a claim
around repeated trips to an expert's distant office, and with Purinton
Analytics they do not: the practice is built so that a Saline evaluee can
complete records review, structured interview, and standardized testing
remotely wherever publisher protocols allow, with in-person evaluation
arranged in the evaluee's own community when the referral questions call
for it. Plaintiff and defense counsel alike retain the firm on this basis
for [vocational expert work](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),

## How a Saline evaluation proceeds

Once the conflict check clears and the scope is defined, the records
request goes out first, tailored to the claims rather than copied from a
template. Interviews and testing follow, remote where supported, and any
in-person session is scheduled around the evaluee's condition and calendar
rather than the expert's convenience. Counsel receive interim status
updates tied to the scope, so there are no surprises at the disclosure
deadline. When face-to-face evaluation,
deposition, or trial requires presence in Washtenaw County, travel is
arranged from the Kansas City home office. The firm keeps no staffed
office in Saline and publishes no street address there, because accuracy
about presence belongs in the same category as accuracy about everything
else in the file. Meetings are by appointment.

## Venue context for Saline cases

Saline is a small Washtenaw County city, and state-court matters arising
there are heard in the Washtenaw County Circuit Court. In federal
litigation, Michigan's forums are the United States District Court for the
Eastern District of Michigan and the United States District Court for the
Western District of Michigan; which district controls a given case is
confirmed during the conflict check and intake. The opinion delivered is
built for either side of the caption to examine, whichever forum the case
lands in.

## Labor market research beyond the town line

A small city does not confine its residents to a small labor market.
Vocational findings for Saline evaluees draw on Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates for the Ann Arbor metropolitan
area and for Michigan statewide, together with state workforce agency
labor market information and direct labor market research where warranted,
so that employability and earning capacity conclusions reflect the
regional market a Saline resident actually competes in. Care-related
opinions, including [medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/),
are priced to the evaluee's own market through provider-specific inquiry
and published fee data.
