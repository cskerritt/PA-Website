## Venue for Ionia matters

Ionia sits in Ionia County, and its state-court matters are venued in the
Ionia County Circuit Court. Federal cases from Michigan proceed in the
United States District Court for the Western District of Michigan or the
United States District Court for the Eastern District of Michigan; the
controlling district for a specific Ionia matter, and any registration or
qualification requirement that attaches to it, is confirmed during the
conflict check and intake rather than assumed in advance. Testimony
practice does not change with the forum. The deposition answers and the
written report have to match, so the report is written first and the
testimony follows it, never the reverse, and the engagement file
documents that sequence from the initial intake exchange onward.

## Labor market findings built to be examined

Ionia is a small city within the Grand Rapids-Wyoming-Kentwood
metropolitan area, and defining the labor market for an Ionia evaluee is
itself a finding the firm expects to defend at deposition. The analysis
rests on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage
estimates for the Grand Rapids metropolitan area and for Michigan
statewide, on state workforce agency labor market information, and on
direct labor market research where a referral question turns on present,
verifiable opportunity. The geographic frame chosen for the opinion is
stated in the report along with the reasons for choosing it, so opposing
counsel can attack the reasoning directly instead of suggesting it was
fitted to the retaining party. When the numbers behind a conclusion come
from a published source, the source is cited where the conclusion
appears, not gathered in an appendix no one reads.
