In many Holland cases, the hardest damages question is not what an evaluee
earned before the injury but what their future care will require, and what
that care will cost in the place where they actually live. Purinton
Analytics builds [life care plans](/services/life-care-planning/) and
[medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/) for Holland
matters with exactly that question in view, pricing future care to the
evaluee's own locality rather than to a generalized market, and pairs the
care-planning work with
[vocational expert opinions](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) whenever
capacity to work is contested as well. Plaintiff and defense counsel
receive the same product on the same terms: a signed written opinion whose
sources and methods are stated on its face.

## Care costs and work capacity, both in context

A life care plan is a local document by nature. Future care is researched
and priced for the community where the evaluee lives, each recommendation
is tied to the medical record counsel provide, and each cost carries a
cited source, so the plan can be examined item by item rather than taken
on faith. Vocational findings follow the same discipline. Holland is a
mid-sized city inside a regional economy, so earning capacity conclusions
rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage
estimates for the Grand Rapids metropolitan area and for Michigan
statewide, together with state workforce agency labor market information
and direct labor market research when a referral question requires
evidence of present openings. The report explains the geographic frame it
uses, because a market definition that cannot be defended weakens every
conclusion built on it.

## Appointments, candor, and travel

The firm's only staffed office is its Kansas City home office; there is no
Holland location, and no street address is published for this page.
Meetings are by appointment. Most stages of an engagement proceed
remotely, from records analysis through structured interviews and
standardized testing under publisher protocols that support remote
administration. When the referral is better served in person, the
evaluation takes place in the evaluee's own community, with travel
scheduled from Kansas City, and the same arrangements cover depositions
and trial appearances. Where economic loss must be modeled alongside the
vocational and care findings, the firm offers
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.
