Plaintiff and defense counsel retain vocational experts for the same
underlying reason: a dispute about what a person can earn, do, or will
need cannot be resolved by argument alone. Grandville cases are no
exception. Though the city is a small one within the Grand
Rapids-Wyoming-Kentwood metro, its dockets produce the full range of
disputes in which Purinton Analytics works, and the firm brings the same
posture to each: [vocational expert opinions](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care plans](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[expert testimony](/services/expert-testimony-litigation-consulting/)
prepared as if the primary reader were the lawyer on the other side,
because eventually that is exactly who reads them.

## Courts serving Grandville

Grandville's county is Kent County, and its state-court matters are heard
in the Kent County Circuit Court. When a matter proceeds federally,
Michigan offers its litigants the United States District Court for the
Western District of Michigan and the United States District Court for the
Eastern District of Michigan as forums; the firm does not guess at which
one controls a specific case, and instead confirms the operative venue,
and any qualification requirement that comes with it, during the conflict
check and intake. Reports are drafted to survive scrutiny in either
system, with methodology disclosed rather than implied.

## A small city inside a large market

Scale matters when defining an evaluee's labor market, and for Grandville
the honest frame is usually regional. A small suburb does not contain its
own residents' employment prospects, so the analysis reaches outward,
resting on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage
estimates for the Grand Rapids metropolitan area and for the state of
Michigan, checked against state workforce agency labor market information
and supplemented by direct labor market research when the referral turns
on the availability of specific work. The written opinion sets out that
geographic reasoning explicitly, treating the market definition as a
finding to be defended, not a default to be assumed.

## Meeting evaluees where they are

No Purinton Analytics office exists in Grandville, and the firm declines
to imply otherwise; there is no published street address because there is
no local storefront to attach it to. Engagements are handled by
appointment, and the model is remote-first: records review and structured
interviews conducted by video, standardized instruments administered under
publisher protocols where remote testing is supported. When the referral
questions justify an in-person evaluation, the evaluee is seen in their
own community, with travel scheduled from the Kansas City home office; the
same scheduling covers depositions and trial dates. Interim findings can
be discussed with retaining counsel before drafting begins, so scope
questions surface early instead of at the disclosure deadline. The
[Grand Rapids metro page](/locations/michigan/grand-rapids/) describes how
these logistics operate across the wider region.
