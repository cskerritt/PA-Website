## Ottawa County courts and the federal districts

Hudsonville's state-court venue is the Ottawa County Circuit Court.
Federal matters from Michigan proceed in the United States District Court
for the Western District of Michigan or the United States District Court
for the Eastern District of Michigan; rather than declare which district
governs a given Hudsonville case, the firm treats venue as an intake
fact, verified during the conflict check together with any
venue-specific registration or qualification requirement, confirmed in
writing as part of the engagement file. Once the forum
is settled, nothing else about the engagement bends to it: report
standards, disclosure practice, and methodology remain the same in every
courtroom the firm serves.

## Hudsonville and the metro labor market

Vocational questions for Hudsonville evaluees are answered against the
regional economy the town belongs to. The firm draws on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Grand
Rapids metropolitan area and for Michigan statewide, on state workforce
agency labor market information, and on direct labor market research
where a referral question turns on specific, present-day opportunity.
Sources are cited in the body of the report, so a reader can move from
any conclusion to the data behind it without a discovery fight. For
cases in which the vocational and care findings feed an economic loss
calculation, [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
are available; in that structure, independent economist partners perform
and sign the economic analysis, and the report chain makes clear which
professional stands behind which opinion.

## Appointments, not an office

The firm is direct about geography: its staffed office is the Kansas City
home office, there is no Hudsonville location, and no street address is
published for this page. Meetings are by appointment, and most of the
engagement happens remotely, from records analysis through structured
interviews and standardized testing under publisher protocols that
support remote administration. Where the referral is better served by
seeing the evaluee in person, the evaluation is scheduled in the
evaluee's own community with travel from Kansas City, and the same travel
practice covers depositions and trial appearances across the
[Grand Rapids metro](/locations/michigan/grand-rapids/).
