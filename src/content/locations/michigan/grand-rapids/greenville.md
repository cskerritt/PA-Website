A vocational opinion is only as strong as the record beneath it, so
Greenville referrals begin with the file. Restrictions are taken from the
medical records as treating and examining providers actually stated them,
work history is documented rather than remembered, and education and
training are verified before any conclusion about employability is
drafted. From that base, Purinton Analytics prepares
[vocational expert opinions](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care plans](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/) for
plaintiff and defense counsel alike, with every conclusion traceable to a
document the other side can also read.

## Montcalm County courts

Greenville sits in Montcalm County, and its state-court matters are heard
in the Montcalm County Circuit Court. When a matter proceeds federally,
Michigan's trial courts are the United States District Court for the
Western District of Michigan and the United States District Court for the
Eastern District of Michigan; the district controlling a specific
Greenville case is confirmed during the conflict check and intake,
together with any venue-specific registration or qualification
requirement. The record-first habit extends to venue itself: what the
firm asserts about a case, down to where it is pending and when its
deadlines fall, comes from counsel and the file, never from assumption.

## Labor market evidence for a small city

Greenville is a small city, and the firm treats the definition of its
evaluees' labor market as a conclusion that must be supported, not a
setting to be defaulted. The statistical foundation is the Bureau of
Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Grand
Rapids metropolitan area and for Michigan statewide. State workforce
agency labor market information is read against those estimates, and
where a referral question depends on what work is genuinely open to a
person with the restrictions the record documents, the firm performs
direct labor market research rather than extrapolating. The written
opinion identifies the geographic frame it used and the reasons for it,
so the frame can be tested at deposition on its stated grounds instead of
being discovered by inference.

## Evaluations built around the evaluee

There is no Greenville office and no published street address, because
the firm's only staffed location is its Kansas City home office and it
declines to suggest otherwise. Meetings are by appointment. The
remote-first model keeps most of the engagement, from records analysis
through video interviews and standardized testing under
publisher-supported remote protocols, from requiring the evaluee to
travel at all, which matters when the person at the center of the case is
injured or disabled. In-person evaluation is scheduled in the evaluee's
own community when the referral warrants it, with travel arranged from
Kansas City, and depositions and trial dates are covered by the same
scheduling discipline. The
[Grand Rapids metro page](/locations/michigan/grand-rapids/) describes
the wider regional practice these engagements belong to.
