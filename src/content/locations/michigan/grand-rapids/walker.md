For a Walker evaluee, the practical question is rarely whether an expert
can be retained; it is what the evaluation will ask of an injured or
disabled person and how much of it can happen without adding burden. That
is where Purinton Analytics starts. Records are gathered and analyzed
before the evaluee is ever scheduled, interviews are structured so nothing
is asked twice, and standardized testing is administered remotely under
publisher protocols wherever remote administration is supported. Counsel
on either side of a Walker caption can direct the referral toward a
[vocational assessment](/services/vocational-expert-witness/), a
[life care plan](/services/life-care-planning/), or both under a shared
scope.

## Where Walker cases are heard

Walker lies in Kent County, so its state-court disputes are venued in the
Kent County Circuit Court. Michigan's federal bench is split between the
United States District Court for the Western District of Michigan and the
United States District Court for the Eastern District of Michigan; the
district that governs a particular Walker matter is pinned down during the
conflict check and intake, together with any venue-specific qualification
requirement. The opinion delivered at the end does not vary by courtroom:
it is a signed written report whose sources, methods, and assumptions are
set out for the opposing side to probe.

## The commuting market around Walker

Walker is a mid-sized city inside a much larger regional economy, and its
residents' realistic employment options extend well beyond municipal
boundaries. Vocational conclusions therefore rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates covering the Grand
Rapids metropolitan area and Michigan statewide, alongside state workforce
agency labor market information and targeted employer research when a
referral question turns on actual openings rather than published
estimates. Published figures are treated as a starting point, not an
answer, and any departure from them is explained in the report itself. Where the loss analysis also requires economic modeling, the
firm offers [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work moves in parallel.

## What the evaluation itself looks like

The practice is candid about its footprint: the only staffed office is the
Kansas City home office, and no Walker address exists or is published.
Meetings are by appointment. Remote work covers most of an engagement, and
when in-person evaluation, deposition, or trial attendance is needed,
travel from Kansas City is scheduled around the dates that matter to the
case. Evaluees are seen in their own community whenever the referral calls
for it, because functional and vocational findings are more persuasive
when they reflect the setting where the person actually lives and seeks
work. Details of the regional practice are on the
[Grand Rapids metro page](/locations/michigan/grand-rapids/).
