Wyoming appears in the formal name of its own metropolitan area. The Grand
Rapids-Wyoming-Kentwood, MI metro carries the city in its title alongside
Grand Rapids itself, and the litigation that arises here reflects that
standing: injury, disability, and employment disputes involving people who
live and often work in Wyoming rather than merely passing through it.
Purinton Analytics provides [vocational expert](/services/vocational-expert-witness/)
and [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) opinions for those
cases on either side of the caption, under the same remote-first model
described on the [Grand Rapids metro page](/locations/michigan/grand-rapids/).
Every opinion is written to be examined by the other side: defined scope,
documented methods, and sources cited in the report itself.

## Venue context in Kent County

Wyoming sits in Kent County, and state-court matters from the city proceed
in the Kent County Circuit Court. Michigan's federal trial forums are the
United States District Court for the Western District of Michigan and the
United States District Court for the Eastern District of Michigan; which of
them controls any particular matter depends on the case, and that answer is
confirmed during the conflict check and intake rather than assumed in
advance. The report standard does not change with the forum. Whether the
caption is state or federal, counsel receive a written opinion that
discloses its data, explains its reasoning, and acknowledges the limits of
what the record supports.

## Wyoming's place in the metro labor market

A vocational opinion for a Wyoming evaluee cannot stop at the city line.
Wyoming is among the larger cities in the metro, and its residents compete
in a regional labor market, so transferable skills findings and placement
conclusions rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and
wage estimates for the Grand Rapids metropolitan area and for Michigan
statewide, together with state workforce agency labor market information
and direct labor market research where the referral questions call for it.
The report states why the chosen geography fits the particular evaluee, so
the market definition itself can withstand cross-examination instead of
becoming a soft spot in the opinion.

## How a Wyoming evaluation proceeds

There is no Purinton Analytics office in Wyoming, and the firm publishes no
street address here; meetings are by appointment, and saying that plainly
is part of the same discipline that governs the reports. Most engagements
begin remotely, with records review, a structured interview, and
standardized testing administered under publisher protocols where remote
administration is supported. When the referral calls for in-person
evaluation, or when deposition and trial dates require presence, travel is
scheduled from the Kansas City home office and arranged around the case
calendar. Where vocational and care-planning questions sit alongside
economic damages, [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
are available, with independent economist partners performing and signing
the economic analysis while the vocational and life care work proceeds
under a single engagement structure.
