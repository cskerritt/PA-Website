Damages questions rarely arrive one at a time. An East Grand Rapids case
may need a vocational opinion on capacity to work, a life care plan for
future needs, and an economic analysis of the resulting loss, and the
weakest version of that lineup is a set of experts who never reconcile
their assumptions. Purinton Analytics structures
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
to prevent exactly that failure: independent economist partners perform
and sign the economic analysis, while the
[vocational](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) and
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) work proceeds under
one engagement structure, with each professional responsible for the
opinions that carry their signature. Plaintiff and defense counsel engage
the firm on identical terms, because the structure only has value if it
does not bend to the retaining side.

## Kent County venue and the federal districts

East Grand Rapids is a Kent County city, and its state-court matters are
heard in the Kent County Circuit Court. Michigan's federal forums are the
United States District Court for the Western District of Michigan and the
United States District Court for the Eastern District of Michigan; which
district governs a particular matter, and what registration or
qualification requirements come with it, is settled during the conflict
check and intake rather than presumed. Coordination does not blur
accountability in either system: every report identifies its author, its
data, and its limits, whichever courtroom will eventually receive it, and
the engagement file shows who did what from intake forward.

## One market frame across the damages case

East Grand Rapids is a small city, and its evaluees' employment prospects
are measured against the wider regional market. Vocational findings draw
on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates
for the Grand Rapids metropolitan area and for Michigan statewide,
checked against state workforce agency labor market information and
extended by direct labor market research when a referral question turns
on actual, current openings. In a coordinated engagement, that same
market frame is carried into the economic analysis, so the economist
partners' loss calculations and the vocational conclusions describe the
same evaluee in the same economy instead of drifting apart under
cross-examination. The report states why the chosen geography fits the
evaluee, treating the market definition as a defended finding rather
than a default.

## How the work is scheduled

The firm's staffed office is the Kansas City home office; there is no
East Grand Rapids location, and no street address is published for this
page. Meetings are by appointment. Records review, structured interviews,
and standardized testing under publisher-supported remote protocols
proceed without travel, and in-person evaluation is added when the
referral questions justify it, conducted in the evaluee's own community
with travel scheduled from Kansas City. Depositions and trial appearances
are scheduled the same way, against the dates counsel identify at intake,
and when several opinions are moving in parallel the calendar is managed
so no report is finished at the expense of another. The

regional practice in fuller detail.
