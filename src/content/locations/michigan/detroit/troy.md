Venue shapes expert work before a report is ever drafted, and for Troy
that means Oakland County. Where a case is pending determines the
scheduling order, the disclosure expectations, and the audience the
written opinion must satisfy, so Purinton Analytics treats the courthouse
as a starting fact of every Troy engagement. From that footing the firm
delivers [vocational evaluation and
testimony](/services/vocational-expert-witness/), [life care
planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and [expert testimony and
litigation consulting](/services/expert-testimony-litigation-consulting/)
to counsel on either side of the dispute.

## Oakland County and the federal forums

Troy's state-court matters ordinarily proceed in the Circuit Court for
Oakland County. In the federal system, Michigan is served by the United
States District Court for the Eastern District of Michigan and the United
States District Court for the Western District of Michigan; the
controlling district for a given Troy case is identified during the
conflict check and intake, and the report is prepared to whichever
forum's disclosure practice is more demanding. Counsel who want the wider
map of courts and counties the practice covers in the region will find it
on the [Detroit metro page](/locations/michigan/detroit/).

## Labor market foundations

Every earning capacity conclusion for a Troy evaluee is anchored to
sources both sides can check: Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational
employment and wage estimates for the Detroit-Warren-Dearborn
metropolitan area and for Michigan statewide, state workforce agency
labor market information, and direct labor market research when a
referral question demands more than published data can give. Regional
description stays qualitative by design. The precision goes into the
evaluee analysis: documented restrictions, transferable skills, and the
occupations realistically open within commuting distance. When damages
require an economist, the firm offers [coordinated damages
engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) in which
independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis
while the vocational work proceeds under the same engagement structure.

## In-person and remote evaluation

Purinton Analytics has no staffed Troy office and publishes no street
address for this location; meetings happen by appointment, and the firm
prefers stating that plainly to implying a presence it does not keep.
Much of the work, from records review through deposition testimony, is
conducted remotely. When the referral is better served face to face, the
evaluation is scheduled in the community where the evaluee lives, with
travel arranged from the Kansas City home office. Delivery timelines are
agreed at intake and revisited only if the court moves the underlying
dates. The [nationwide
practice page](/locations/nationwide/) details how the firm keeps this
model reliable across the country.

## Engaging the firm

A Troy engagement opens with a documented conflict check. Counsel submit
the caption or party names through the [referral form](/refer-a-case/)
and note the dates the court has already fixed. Once conflicts clear,
intake settles scope, records, and schedule, and confirms any
venue-specific qualification requirement before substantive work begins.
Statewide coverage is summarized on the [Michigan locations
page](/locations/michigan/). Because the practice serves plaintiff and
defense referrals with identical methods, the opinion that reaches an
Oakland County courtroom reads the same way no matter which table
commissioned it, which is exactly what makes it useful there.
