## Data behind vocational findings

A report that expects cross-examination cannot lean on impressions.
Findings for Farmington Hills evaluees are grounded in Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the
Detroit-Warren-Dearborn metropolitan area and for Michigan statewide, in
state workforce agency labor market information, and in direct labor
market research where the questions require it. The market is
characterized qualitatively; numbers belong in the cited tables, not in
adjectives. The evaluee-specific analysis then does the real work,
matching documented functional capacity and transferable skills to
occupations that remain realistically available. The same sourcing
discipline governs rebuttal assignments: an opposing report is measured
against the published data it should have used, and the critique
identifies exactly where its method departs from that record.
