A Livonia resident's working life is rarely confined to Livonia. Suburban
evaluees commute across municipal and county lines as a matter of course,
which means an earning capacity opinion bounded by city limits would
understate or misstate the market that actually matters. Purinton
Analytics frames its [vocational expert
work](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) for Livonia cases around the
commuting market the evaluee genuinely faces, and pairs that analysis with
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) when future care needs
are part of the damages picture, for referring counsel in plaintiff and
defense practice alike.

## Venue for Livonia matters

Livonia lies within Wayne County, so its state-court litigation is
ordinarily filed in the Circuit Court for Wayne County. Michigan's federal
trial forums are the United States District Court for the Eastern District
of Michigan and the United States District Court for the Western District
of Michigan; which forum controls any particular dispute is a question the
conflict check and intake process answers case by case. The firm's
regional coverage, including the courts across the wider metro, is
described on the [Detroit metro page](/locations/michigan/detroit/), and
the statewide view is on the [Michigan locations
page](/locations/michigan/).

## Earning capacity in a metro-wide market

Because the relevant market is regional, the data must be regional too.
Opinions for Livonia evaluees draw on Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates for the Detroit-Warren-Dearborn
metropolitan area and for Michigan statewide, on labor market information
published by the state workforce agency, and on direct labor market
research when a specific occupation or employer question calls for it. The
report treats the region qualitatively, then narrows to the evaluee: what
skills transfer, what restrictions the medical record supports, and what
work remains within a reasonable commuting radius given those facts. Every
step is cited so the opinion can be examined rather than taken on faith.

## Logistics for counsel and evaluees

The firm operates by appointment and says so without embellishment; there
is no Livonia office and no published local address, because a practice
that trades on accuracy cannot be casual about its own footprint. Records
review, evaluee interviews, and testimony are routinely handled remotely.
Where the case is better served in person, evaluation is conducted in the
evaluee's own community, and any required travel originates from the
Kansas City home office. Counsel receive written confirmation of each
appointment and a working schedule that anticipates the discovery
calendar rather than reacting to it. The [nationwide practice
page](/locations/nationwide/) explains the remote-first model that makes
this coverage dependable.

## Opening the file

Work on a Livonia matter starts when counsel submit the caption or party
names through the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and identify any
looming disclosure, deposition, or trial dates. A documented conflict
check comes before any substantive discussion. Intake then sets the
referral questions, the records list, and the schedule, and confirms any
qualification requirement particular to the venue. From that point
forward, counsel can expect a defined scope and a written opinion that
measures a Livonia evaluee against the labor market they actually inhabit,
not an abstraction of it.
