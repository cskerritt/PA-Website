A damages case is an assembly problem. Vocational findings, a life care
plan, and an economic loss analysis must fit together without seams,
because a gap between experts is where cross-examination lives. For
Southfield litigation, Purinton Analytics addresses the assembly directly
through [coordinated damages
engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) in which
independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis
while the [vocational](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) and
[care-planning](/services/life-care-planning/) work proceeds under the
same engagement structure. Counsel for either party get a coherent damages
record instead of a stack of opinions that were never introduced to each
other.

## Southfield venues, state and federal

Southfield lies in Oakland County, and state-court proceedings involving
Southfield parties are generally brought in the Circuit Court for Oakland
County. Michigan's federal forums are the United States District Court for
the Eastern District of Michigan and the United States District Court for
the Western District of Michigan, and the district with authority over a
given case is determined through the conflict check and intake. Whatever
the forum, each component of a coordinated engagement is drafted to the
same disclosure standard, so no piece becomes the weak point. The wider
regional coverage appears on the [Detroit metro
page](/locations/michigan/detroit/).

## The labor market record

The vocational layer of a Southfield damages case is built from Bureau of
Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the
Detroit-Warren-Dearborn metropolitan area and for Michigan statewide, from
state workforce agency labor market information, and from direct labor
market research when the referral questions require it. Market conditions
are characterized qualitatively, and the evaluee analysis supplies the
particulars: documented restrictions, transferable skills, and the
occupations that remain accessible. Those findings then feed the
economist partners' calculations, which keeps the earning capacity
assumptions consistent from report to report and witness to witness. When
rebuttal is part of the assignment, opposing damages experts are evaluated
as a set, since inconsistencies among them can be as telling as errors
within any of them.

## Evaluation logistics

Purinton Analytics maintains no Southfield office and publishes no address
for this page, and it declines to pretend otherwise. Engagements are
handled by appointment: remotely for most stages, and in person when the
referral is better served by an evaluation conducted in the evaluee's own
community. Travel, when needed, is arranged from the Kansas City home
office. Working sessions among the experts and counsel are built into the
engagement plan rather than left to chance. The mechanics of the remote-first national practice are set out on
the [nationwide practice page](/locations/nationwide/), and the firm's

## Submitting a conflict check

Coordination begins before retention: the [conflict check
form](/refer-a-case/) asks for the caption or party names and any
disclosure, deposition, or trial dates already on the docket. Once
conflicts clear, intake maps the full engagement, including which
components counsel need, the records each requires, and any
venue-specific qualification requirement, which is identified and
confirmed at that stage. Southfield counsel who have watched a damages
case wobble because its experts diverged will recognize the value of an
engagement built to keep them aligned from the start.
