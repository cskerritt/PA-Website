At the center of every Dearborn engagement is a person: an evaluee whose
injury, illness, or employment dispute has put their working life in
question. Purinton Analytics starts there. Before the report, before the
deposition, the firm's job is a careful [vocational
evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) of that individual,
grounded in their medical records, work history, education, and the
functional limits the treating providers have documented. Where the
injuries are catastrophic, the same evaluee-centered discipline extends to
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) and [medical cost
projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/), for counsel on whichever
side of the caption makes the referral.

## Wayne County courts

Dearborn is a Wayne County city, and its state-court matters proceed in
the Circuit Court for Wayne County. Federal litigation in Michigan is
divided between the United States District Court for the Eastern District
of Michigan and the United States District Court for the Western District
of Michigan; the forum that governs an individual case is settled during
the conflict check and intake rather than presumed. Reports are prepared
to a disclosure standard that serves either forum, and the [Detroit metro
page](/locations/michigan/detroit/) sets out how the firm covers the
surrounding counties and courts.

## The labor market behind the opinion

An evaluee-centered opinion still has to answer a market question: which
occupations remain open to this person, in this region, at what general
earnings level. For Dearborn evaluees the firm relies on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the
Detroit-Warren-Dearborn metropolitan area and for Michigan statewide,
alongside state workforce agency labor market information and targeted
labor market research when the referral requires it. Descriptions of the
market remain qualitative; the specificity is reserved for the evaluee's
transferable skills and restrictions, where specificity belongs.

## Meeting the evaluee in Dearborn

Evaluations are scheduled around the evaluee, not the other way around.
Many can be completed remotely, which spares an injured person a difficult
trip. When an in-person meeting will produce a better record, the firm
arranges it in Dearborn or wherever the evaluee actually lives, traveling
from the Kansas City home office. Family members and treating providers
inform the picture where appropriate, since a working life rarely fits
inside a chart. Purinton Analytics maintains no local
office in Dearborn and lists no street address here; counsel get by-appointment
service and an honest account of how it works, described more fully on the
[nationwide practice page](/locations/nationwide/).

## Starting with a conflict check

Because the firm accepts referrals from plaintiff and defense counsel
alike, the conflict check is the true beginning of every matter. Send the
caption or the parties' names through the [referral
form](/refer-a-case/), along with any dates that already bind the case.
After conflicts clear, intake defines the referral questions and the
records needed to answer them, and identifies any venue-specific
qualification requirement so it is confirmed before the work begins, not
discovered afterward. Statewide coverage, including the other Michigan
metros the firm serves, is collected on the [Michigan locations
page](/locations/michigan/). What Dearborn counsel receive at the end is
an opinion about a specific human being, documented well enough that
either side could have commissioned it.
