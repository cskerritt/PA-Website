Strip away the procedure and most Novi referrals come down to a short list
of questions. Can this person return to their former work? If not, what
work can they do, and what would it pay relative to what they earned
before? What will their care cost over a lifetime? Purinton Analytics
exists to answer those questions in writing, under oath if needed, through
[vocational evaluation and
testimony](/services/vocational-expert-witness/), [life care
planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and [medical cost
projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/). The answers do not bend
to the referral source; plaintiff and defense counsel receive the same
analysis because the questions are the same.

## Courts that hear Novi matters

Novi is part of Oakland County, and its state-court litigation ordinarily
proceeds in the Circuit Court for Oakland County. In the federal system,
Michigan's forums are the United States District Court for the Eastern
District of Michigan and the United States District Court for the Western
District of Michigan; sorting out which governs a specific Novi case
happens during the conflict check and intake, where it belongs. Reports
are written to hold up under the disclosure practice of either forum, and
the [Detroit metro page](/locations/michigan/detroit/) covers the courts
and counties of the surrounding region in more depth.

## Answering the earning capacity question

The earnings questions are answered against evidence, not intuition. For a
Novi evaluee, the firm works from Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational
employment and wage estimates for the Detroit-Warren-Dearborn metropolitan
area and for Michigan statewide, from state workforce agency labor market
information, and from direct labor market research when a referral
question outruns the published sources. Regional conditions are described
qualitatively. The individual analysis carries the weight: the functional
capacity the medical record documents, the skills that transfer, and the
occupations realistically open within the evaluee's commuting orbit. Each
conclusion cites its source so the opinion can be tested line by line.

## Where and how evaluations occur

The care question and the work question both start with the evaluee, and
the firm meets evaluees where they are. Interviews and evaluations are
frequently remote; when the referral is better served in person, the
appointment is set in the evaluee's own community, with travel arranged
from the Kansas City home office. Purinton Analytics has no Novi office
and lists no address here, an accuracy the firm considers part of its
product. Counsel are kept informed at every stage, from records receipt
through report delivery, so the answers arrive when the case actually
needs them. The [nationwide practice page](/locations/nationwide/) explains
the by-appointment model in full.

already set. Intake then converts the case into defined referral
questions, a records list, and a schedule, and confirms any
venue-specific qualification requirement along the way. The firm's
statewide footprint is summarized on the [Michigan locations
page](/locations/michigan/). When the report arrives, Novi counsel should
find each question they asked answered plainly, sourced fully, and ready
