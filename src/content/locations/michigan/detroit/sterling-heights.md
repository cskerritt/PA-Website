## Labor market evidence

Earning capacity opinions for Sterling Heights evaluees draw on Bureau of
Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates covering the
Detroit-Warren-Dearborn metropolitan area and Michigan as a whole, on
state workforce agency labor market information, and on direct employer
research when a referral question requires it. The report describes the
market qualitatively and documents each source, so that the opinion can be
tested in deposition rather than merely asserted. Where an opposing expert
has already opined, the same sources support a methodical rebuttal that
addresses methodology instead of rhetoric.
