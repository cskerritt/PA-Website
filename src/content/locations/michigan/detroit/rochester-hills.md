## Grounding opinions in market data

Vocational conclusions about a Rochester Hills evaluee rest on Bureau of
Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the
Detroit-Warren-Dearborn metropolitan area and for Michigan statewide,
augmented by state workforce agency labor market information and by
direct labor market research where warranted. The regional economy is
addressed qualitatively; the evaluee's situation is addressed
specifically, through transferable skills analysis and the functional
restrictions the medical record actually supports. Sources are cited so
opposing counsel can retrace the path, which is a feature of the method,
not a courtesy. Where the record leaves a genuine gap, the report says
so, because an acknowledged limitation is more credible than a
papered-over conclusion.

## By-appointment service, stated plainly

firm conducts it in the evaluee's own community, and travel is scheduled

the [nationwide practice page](/locations/nationwide/) documents how the
