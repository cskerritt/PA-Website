When an injury is catastrophic, the damages case has a long horizon: a
lifetime of attendant care, equipment, therapies, and medical follow-up
that must be documented before it can be valued. For Westland matters of
that gravity, Purinton Analytics builds the foundation through [life care
planning](/services/life-care-planning/) and [medical cost
projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/), each item tied to the
medical record and to clinical recommendation rather than to advocacy. The
same engagement can carry the [vocational
analysis](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) of what work, if any,
remains open to the injured person, and the firm performs this work for
plaintiff and defense counsel without altering its method for either.

## County and courthouse

Westland is situated in Wayne County, and its state-court cases are
typically heard in the Circuit Court for Wayne County. Federal matters in
Michigan fall to the United States District Court for the Eastern District
of Michigan or the United States District Court for the Western District
of Michigan, and the district that applies to a specific case is
established during the conflict check and intake process. Reports and
plans are drafted to survive disclosure practice in either system. The
[Detroit metro page](/locations/michigan/detroit/) describes the firm's
coverage across the surrounding region.

## Vocational evidence and the local market

Where residual earning capacity is at issue, the analysis for a Westland
evaluee is grounded in Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment
and wage estimates for the Detroit-Warren-Dearborn metropolitan area and
for Michigan statewide, in state workforce agency labor market
information, and in direct labor market research when the referral
questions call for it. The regional market is described in qualitative
terms; the individual analysis carries the detail, connecting documented
restrictions and transferable skills to occupations genuinely within
reach. When the case also needs an economic loss calculation, independent
economist partners perform and sign that analysis through the firm's
[coordinated damages
assessment](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) structure.

## How a Westland evaluation happens

Purinton Analytics keeps no office in Westland and prints no address for
this location, a fact the firm states rather than obscures. Engagements
run by appointment: records review and interviews are commonly remote, and
for a severely injured evaluee a remote evaluation is often the more
humane choice. Where an in-person assessment will materially strengthen
the record, it is conducted in the evaluee's community, with travel
arranged from the Kansas City home office. Caregivers and family members
are interviewed where appropriate, because daily care routines rarely
appear in the medical chart. The full remote-first model is
described on the [nationwide practice page](/locations/nationwide/).

## Referring a case

The path in is the same for every referral: the [conflict check
form](/refer-a-case/), with the caption or party names and any dates the
court has set. After conflicts clear, intake defines the plan's scope, the
records required, and the reporting schedule, and confirms any
qualification requirement specific to the venue. The [Michigan locations
page](/locations/michigan/) shows the practice's statewide reach. Westland
counsel handling a life-altering injury need documentation that will still
stand years after the verdict, and that is the standard each care plan and
vocational opinion is written against.
