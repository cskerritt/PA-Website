Warren's name appears in the official title of the Detroit-Warren-Dearborn
metropolitan area, and the city is among the largest communities in the
region outside Detroit itself. Litigation of that scale produces a steady
need for vocational evidence, and Purinton Analytics serves it from either
side of the caption: [vocational evaluation and expert
testimony](/services/vocational-expert-witness/), [life care
planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and coordinated damages work for
counsel whose cases turn on what an injured person can still earn and what
their future care will require. The practice is national in scope, as the
[nationwide practice page](/locations/nationwide/) describes, but each
Warren engagement is built around a particular evaluee and a particular
venue.

## Macomb County venue and courts

Warren sits in Macomb County, and state-court matters involving Warren
parties are ordinarily litigated in the Circuit Court there. Michigan's
federal forums are the United States District Court for the Eastern
District of Michigan and the United States District Court for the Western
District of Michigan; the district that controls a given case is confirmed
during the conflict check and intake rather than assumed in advance. The
firm's written opinions are prepared to the same disclosure standard for
either setting, and the [Detroit metro page](/locations/michigan/detroit/)
maps the broader venue footprint across the region.

## Reading the Warren labor market

Vocational findings for a Warren evaluee rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the
Detroit-Warren-Dearborn metropolitan area and for Michigan statewide,
supplemented by state workforce agency labor market information and by
direct labor market research where the referral questions warrant it. The
analysis stays qualitative about the region and specific about the person:
transferable skills, residual functional capacity as documented by the
medical record, and the occupations that remain realistically available
within the evaluee's commuting range. Opposing counsel should be able to
trace every conclusion back to a cited source, which is the standard each
report is written to meet.

## Evaluations by appointment

The firm keeps no staffed office in Warren and publishes no local street
address, because candor about presence belongs in the same discipline that
governs the reports. Meetings and evaluations are scheduled by
appointment. Many engagements proceed remotely from records review through
testimony; when the referral calls for in-person evaluation, the
appointment is arranged in the evaluee's own community, and travel is
scheduled from the Kansas City home office. Counsel receive a defined
scope, a records request tailored to the claims, and a report designed to
withstand cross-examination.

## Requesting a conflict check

Engagements begin with a documented conflict check, never with a sales
conversation. Submit the caption or party names through the [conflict
check form](/refer-a-case/) and note any disclosure, deposition, or trial
dates already on the calendar. Once conflicts clear, intake defines the
referral questions, the records needed, and the schedule for evaluation
and reporting. Warren counsel can also consult the [Michigan locations
page](/locations/michigan/) for the statewide picture, including any
venue-specific qualification requirements, which are identified and
confirmed during intake. Plaintiff and defense referrals receive the same
methodology, the same sources, and the same written product, which is the
point of retaining an expert whose work is meant to hold up regardless of
who engaged it.
