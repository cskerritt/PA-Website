Vocational and life care planning referrals from the Saginaw metro usually
arrive with a date attached: a disclosure deadline, a scheduled deposition,
a trial term that is no longer hypothetical. Purinton Analytics builds the
engagement around that calendar from the first call, telling plaintiff and
defense counsel honestly whether the timeline is workable before anyone is
retained. Within that schedule the firm delivers
[earning capacity and employability evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Saginaw County venue picture

The metro consists of Saginaw County. State-court matters proceed in the
Saginaw County Circuit Court, where the firm accepts engagements from
either side of the caption. Federal matters are accepted in the United
States District Court for the Eastern District of Michigan, and the firm
likewise takes cases in the United States District Court for the Western
District of Michigan when venue places them there. Because every opinion is
prepared as though opposing counsel will read it closely, the transition
from written report to deposition to trial testimony requires no
repackaging at any stage: the file says what the testimony will say.

## Scheduling around distance, candidly

The firm keeps no staffed office in Saginaw and publishes no street address
for this location; meetings are by appointment. That candor is not a
limitation so much as a description of how the work actually flows. Records
review, structured interviews, and standardized testing under publisher
protocols proceed remotely where remote administration is supported, which
protects tight calendars from unnecessary travel delay. When the referral questions require an in-person
evaluation, or when a deposition or trial date requires presence, travel is
scheduled from the Kansas City home office with the case deadlines fixed
first and the logistics arranged to serve them.

## Data behind Saginaw-area opinions

Vocational conclusions for Saginaw County evaluees rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Saginaw
metropolitan area and for Michigan statewide, supplemented by state
workforce agency labor market information and by direct labor market
research where the questions call for it. Life care plans and
[medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/) are priced
to the evaluee's own market, drawing on provider-specific inquiries and
published fee data for the region, because the cost of future care is a
local fact and should be documented as one. Sources are cited in the
report itself, where counsel on both sides can inspect them and trace
every conclusion back to the record.
