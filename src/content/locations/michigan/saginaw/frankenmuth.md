Attorneys handling injury, employment, and disability litigation involving
Frankenmuth residents retain Purinton Analytics for
[vocational expert witness](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) analysis and
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) prepared to the same
standard the firm applies in major metropolitan venues. A small town produces
the same disputed questions a large city does: what work an injured person can
still perform, what that work pays, and what future care will be required. The
firm answers those questions for either side of the caption, in reports
written to withstand examination by opposing counsel.

## Saginaw County venue context

Frankenmuth sits in Saginaw County, and state-court matters arising there
proceed in the Circuit Court, where vocational and care-planning opinions are
disclosed and tested like any other expert evidence. Federal matters involving
Frankenmuth parties may be filed in Michigan's federal forums, the United
States District Court for the Eastern District of Michigan and the United
States District Court for the Western District of Michigan; the controlling
venue for a particular case is identified during the conflict check and
intake, along with any registration or qualification requirement the forum
imposes. The firm's disclosure practice is the same in every courtroom: a
documented conflict check before any records are reviewed, a defined scope,
and a written opinion that states its methods and sources so either party can
retrace them. Counsel working under a compressed disclosure calendar should
raise dates at intake, because scheduling around depositions and discovery
cutoffs is part of scope-setting rather than an afterthought.

## Frankenmuth in the Saginaw labor market

Earning-capacity opinions for a Frankenmuth evaluee are grounded in the labor
market where that person actually competes for work, which for most residents
means the Saginaw, MI metropolitan area anchored by the city of Saginaw.
Vocational findings rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment
and wage estimates for that metropolitan area and for Michigan statewide,
supplemented by state workforce agency labor market information and direct
employer research where the referral questions warrant it. Because Frankenmuth
is a small community, the analysis pays particular attention to commuting
range: the occupations realistically open to an evaluee include those in the
surrounding metro, and the report says so explicitly rather than treating the
town line as the market boundary. Transferable-skills analysis, labor market
surveys, and placement-oriented findings all follow from that defined market,
not from generic national tables.

There is no Purinton Analytics office in Frankenmuth, and the firm does not
publish a local address, because candor about presence belongs in the same
category as candor about methodology. Evaluations are conducted by secure
video where the referral questions permit, and in person in the evaluee's own
community when the case calls for it, with travel scheduled from the Kansas
City home office. In-person work in Saginaw County is arranged around
deposition and trial dates so a single trip can serve several purposes.
[Medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/) and life care

[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)

while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement
