## Berrien County venue footprint

The Niles metropolitan area consists of Berrien County, and engagements are
accepted for matters in the Circuit Court for Berrien County and in
Michigan's federal courts, the United States District Court for the Western
District of Michigan and the United States District Court for the Eastern
District of Michigan, as the filing venue dictates. State or federal, the
engagement follows the same sequence: a documented conflict check, a defined
scope, a records request tailored to the claims, and a written opinion that
rests on a record both sides can examine. Any venue-specific registration or
qualification requirement is identified and confirmed during the conflict
check and intake process rather than discovered late.

## Appointments, not an office

The firm keeps no staffed office in Niles and publishes no street address
for this location. That is stated plainly because honesty about presence is
part of the same discipline that governs the reports themselves. Niles
meetings are by appointment: counsel conferences, records reviews, and
pre-deposition preparation proceed by videoconference on ordinary
scheduling, and evaluations are conducted remotely when the referral
questions and testing protocols permit. When the referral calls for
in-person evaluation, the assessment is arranged in the evaluee's own
community, and when deposition or trial dates require presence, travel is
scheduled from the Kansas City home office with enough lead time to protect
the litigation calendar.

## Researching the Berrien County labor market

Employability and earning capacity findings for evaluees in the Niles area
are built on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage
estimates for the Niles metropolitan area and for Michigan statewide, state
workforce agency labor market information, and direct labor market research
where the referral warrants it. The report states which labor market defines
the evaluee's realistic opportunities and why, tying restrictions, work
history, and residence to the data relied upon. Nothing in the employability
analysis is left to inference: the sources are named, the reasoning is on
the page, and the conclusion can be tested against both.
