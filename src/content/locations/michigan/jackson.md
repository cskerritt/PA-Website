The Jackson, MI metropolitan area is a single-county venue, and that
concentration shapes how counsel litigate here: the same Circuit Court, the
same local bar, and the same jury pool matter in case after case. Purinton
Analytics serves plaintiff and defense counsel in Jackson with
[vocational evaluation and expert testimony](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. The practice is part of the firm's
[Michigan coverage](/locations/michigan/), and every report is written to the
same standard: suitable for the closest reading opposing counsel can give it.

## Courts serving Jackson County matters

Engagements are accepted for matters pending in the Circuit Court for Jackson
County and in Michigan's federal courts, the United States District Court for
the Eastern District of Michigan and the United States District Court for the
Western District of Michigan, with the proper district determined by where
the matter is filed. The work spans personal injury, workers' compensation,
employment, and family law damages questions, and the engagement follows the
case rather than the venue: the same methodology and the same disclosure
discipline apply whether the report is headed for a state courtroom or a
federal one. Where a venue-specific registration or qualification requirement
applies, it is identified and confirmed during the conflict check and intake
process.

## Meetings and evaluations by appointment

Jackson meetings are by appointment. The firm keeps no staffed office in
Jackson and publishes no street address for this location, because accuracy
about presence is part of the same discipline that governs its reports.
Counsel meetings, records conferences, and pre-deposition preparation are
routinely handled by videoconference, and evaluations can be conducted
remotely when the referral questions and testing protocols permit, or
scheduled in the evaluee's own community when in-person assessment is
warranted. When deposition or trial dates require presence, travel is
scheduled from the Kansas City home office and planned against the operative
litigation calendar rather than left open-ended.

## Labor market research for Jackson evaluees

Employability and earning capacity opinions for evaluees in the Jackson area
rest on data specific to this market, not on national generalities. The
analysis draws on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage
estimates for the Jackson metropolitan area and for Michigan statewide, state
workforce agency labor market information, and direct labor market research
where the referral requires it. The report defines the evaluee's commutable
labor market explicitly and states why that definition fits the evaluee's
residence, restrictions, and work history, so the foundation of every
employability conclusion is visible on the page and available for
cross-examination.

## Starting a Jackson engagement

Every engagement begins with a documented conflict check, a defined scope,
and a records request tailored to the claims. Submit the caption or party
names through the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and note any imminent
disclosure, deposition, or trial dates so the timeline can be set against
them from the outset. Once the conflict check clears, counsel receive a
records checklist and a scope confirmation before analysis begins. Jackson
counsel work with the same office that serves the firm's
[national practice](/locations/nationwide/): the same intake, the same
methodology, and the same reporting standards, applied to a local caption.
