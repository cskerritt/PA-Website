Referrals from the Grand Rapids-Wyoming-Kentwood metro tend to arrive as a
mix: an earning capacity dispute in a personal injury case, a life care plan
for a catastrophic injury, a rebuttal deadline that is closer than anyone
would like. Purinton Analytics handles that full range for plaintiff and
defense counsel alike, from
[vocational expert evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) and
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) to
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) of opposing
reports, along with
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Courts served across the metro

The firm accepts matters venued in the circuit courts of the counties that
make up the metro: Kent County, Ottawa County, Barry County, Ionia County,
and Montcalm County. Federal engagements from the region typically proceed
in the United States District Court for the Western District of Michigan,
and the firm also takes matters in the United States District Court for the
Eastern District of Michigan when a case is venued on that side of the
state. Whichever court holds the caption, the engagement follows the same
sequence: a documented conflict check, a defined scope, a records request
tailored to the claims, and a written opinion that both sides can examine.

## No storefront, by design

There is no staffed Purinton Analytics office in Grand Rapids, and the firm
publishes no street address for this location, because candor about
presence is part of the same discipline that governs its reports. Meetings
are by appointment. Most Grand Rapids engagements begin remotely with
records review, structured interviews, and standardized testing under
publisher protocols where remote administration is supported. When an
evaluation is better conducted in person, or when deposition or trial
attendance is required, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home
office, arranged around the case calendar rather than the other way
around. Counsel receive the same report standard whether a given step was
performed remotely or on site.

## Grounding opinions in Grand Rapids data

Vocational conclusions for evaluees in the region draw on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Grand Rapids
metropolitan area and for Michigan statewide, together with state workforce
agency labor market information and direct labor market research where the
questions call for it. Life care plans and
[medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/) are priced
to the evaluee's own community, since the cost of future care in the
Grand Rapids area is a local question, not a national one. Every source appears in
the report itself, so opposing counsel can check the work rather than take
it on faith.
