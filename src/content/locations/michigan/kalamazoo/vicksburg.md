A forensic opinion is only as strong as its fit to the person it describes.
When the evaluee lives in Vicksburg, a small village in Kalamazoo County,
that fit cannot be borrowed from generalities about the wider metro; it has
to be built from the evaluee's actual restrictions, skills, and community.
Plaintiff and defense counsel alike bring Purinton Analytics matters from
communities of this size precisely because the firm treats a village case
with the same documentary rigor as a big-city one, whether the referral
calls for a [vocational expert opinion](/services/vocational-expert-witness/)
or a [life care plan](/services/life-care-planning/).

## Courts that hear Vicksburg matters

Vicksburg cases in the state system proceed in the Circuit Court in
Kalamazoo County, where the firm accepts retention from either side of the
caption. In the federal system, Michigan's forums are the United States
District Court for the Western District of Michigan and the United States
District Court for the Eastern District of Michigan; the controlling venue
for a given caption is settled during the conflict check and intake rather
than assumed. Venue matters chiefly for scheduling, because disclosure
deadlines differ from court to court, and the firm builds its work plan
backward from those dates so a report is never rushed against them.

## Labor market evidence for a village evaluee

Residents of a small village rarely work only inside it, so the vocational
analysis situates a Vicksburg evaluee within the commuting market of the
Kalamazoo-Portage metropolitan area, anchored by Kalamazoo as its principal
city. The evidence base is the Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational
employment and wage estimates for that metropolitan area and for Michigan
statewide, together with state workforce agency labor market information
and direct employer research where warranted. Care-related costs are
handled the same way: a [medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/)
is priced to the providers a Vicksburg resident can realistically reach,
not to national averages. When the damages case also requires an economic
loss analysis, the firm offers
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## How the evaluation gets done

There is no Purinton Analytics office in Vicksburg, and none is implied;
the firm's home office is in Kansas City, and it publishes no street
address here because it keeps none. Most engagements start remotely, with
records review, structured interviews, and standardized testing under
publisher protocols that permit remote administration, which keeps early
costs proportionate for a smaller case. If the referral questions require
an in-person evaluation, it is conducted in the evaluee's own community, by
appointment, with travel scheduled from the Kansas City home office; the
same travel planning covers deposition and trial appearances.
