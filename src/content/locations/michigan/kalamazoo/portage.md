The metropolitan area that covers this part of Michigan carries the city's
name in its official designation: Kalamazoo-Portage, MI. That title is a
fair description of how litigation actually arises here. Portage is a
substantial city in its own right, and counsel on either side of the
caption retain Purinton Analytics for Portage matters directly, not as an
afterthought to work centered elsewhere in the metro. The firm provides
[vocational expert evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/) for injury,
employment, and disability disputes, and offers
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Venue for Portage cases in Kalamazoo County

Portage sits in Kalamazoo County, and state-court matters involving Portage
parties proceed in the Circuit Court there. On the federal side, Michigan
offers the United States District Court for the Western District of
Michigan and the United States District Court for the Eastern District of
Michigan as available forums; which district controls a particular Portage
caption is confirmed during the conflict check and intake. What changes
between forums is mostly the disclosure schedule. What does not change is
the underlying file: records, structured interviews, standardized testing,
and published data assembled so that opposing counsel can trace every
conclusion back to its source.

## Portage in the metro labor market

A vocational opinion about a Portage evaluee has to describe the market
that evaluee actually faces, which is a commuting market shared with
Kalamazoo, the metro's principal city, rather than a labor pool bounded by
municipal lines. Findings rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational
employment and wage estimates for the Kalamazoo-Portage metropolitan area
and for Michigan statewide, supplemented by state workforce agency labor
market information and direct labor market research where the referral
questions warrant it. The analysis stays qualitative about the region and
specific about the person: transferable skills, restrictions, and realistic
placement prospects are documented for the individual, not asserted from
averages.

## Evaluating an evaluee in Portage

The firm keeps no office in Portage and publishes no street address for
this location. Its only home office is in Kansas City, and every other
location, this one included, is served by appointment. That candor is
deliberate: a practice built on accurate reporting should be equally
accurate about its own footprint. Engagements typically open remotely, with
records review, interviews, and testing administered under publisher
protocols that support remote use. When the referral questions call for
in-person evaluation in the evaluee's own community, or when deposition and
trial dates require presence, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home
office and planned around counsel's calendar.
