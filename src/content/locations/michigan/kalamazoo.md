Some referrals from the Kalamazoo-Portage metro need more than a single
expert opinion. A catastrophic injury case may require a vocational
assessment, a life care plan, and an economic loss analysis that all fit
together without a stack of separate retention letters and mismatched
assumptions. For those matters,
Purinton Analytics offers
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. For narrower questions, plaintiff and defense counsel
in Kalamazoo County retain the firm directly for
[vocational expert evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/).

## Kalamazoo County courts

The metro consists of Kalamazoo County, and state-court matters proceed in
the Kalamazoo County Circuit Court, where the firm accepts engagements from
either side of the caption. Federal cases from the region are typically
venued in the United States District Court for the Western District of
Michigan, and the firm also takes matters in the United States District
Court for the Eastern District of Michigan when a caption sits there. The
forum shapes the disclosure calendar and little else: the underlying file,
built from records, interviews, testing, and published data, is prepared to
the same standard for every court.

## Local meetings without a local office

There is no Purinton Analytics storefront in Kalamazoo or Portage, and the
firm publishes no street address for this location. Stating that outright
is intentional, since a practice whose product is accuracy should not
imply offices it does not keep. Meetings are by appointment. Engagements
usually begin remotely, through records review, structured interviews, and
standardized testing under publisher protocols where remote administration
is supported, and move to in-person work only when the referral questions
require it. That sequencing keeps costs proportionate to the case and
keeps the schedule in counsel's hands rather than the airline's. Evaluations conducted in person, along with deposition and
trial appearances, are scheduled as travel from the Kansas City home
office.

## Evidence from the Kalamazoo labor market

Vocational findings for evaluees in the metro are grounded in Bureau of
Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the
Kalamazoo-Portage metropolitan area and for Michigan statewide, along with
state workforce agency labor market information and direct labor market
research where warranted. Life care plans and medical cost projections are
priced to the evaluee's own community using provider-specific inquiries and
published fee data rather than national averages. The report shows its
work: every source is identified so that opposing counsel can follow the
path from record to conclusion without guesswork.
