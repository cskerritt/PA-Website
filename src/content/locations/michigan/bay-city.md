calendar is usually the first thing counsel need an expert to respect.
Purinton Analytics builds every Bay City engagement backward from the
operative dates: disclosure, deposition, mediation, and trial settings are
gathered at intake, and the report timeline is committed against them rather
than left open-ended. Within that structure the firm serves plaintiff and
defense counsel in the Bay City, MI metropolitan area with
[vocational evaluation and expert testimony](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic

## Bay County venues

The Bay City metropolitan area consists of Bay County. The firm accepts
engagements for matters in the Circuit Court for Bay County and in
Michigan's federal courts, the United States District Court for the Eastern
District of Michigan and the United States District Court for the Western
District of Michigan, depending on the filing venue. The engagement
structure does not change between courthouses: a documented conflict check,
a defined scope, and a records request tailored to the claims come first,
and the written opinion that follows is built to be examined by both sides.
Any venue-specific registration or qualification requirement is identified
and confirmed during the conflict check and intake process.

## By-appointment presence in Bay City

The firm keeps no staffed office in Bay City and publishes no street address
for this location; candor about presence is part of the same discipline that
governs its reports. Bay City meetings are by appointment. Counsel
conferences, records reviews, and pre-deposition preparation are conducted
by videoconference, which keeps scheduling responsive even late in a case.
Evaluations proceed remotely when the referral questions and testing
protocols permit, and in-person evaluation is arranged in the evaluee's own
community when the assessment calls for it. When deposition or trial dates
require personal appearance, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home
office with the litigation calendar, not the expert's convenience, setting
the priority.

## Data behind Bay City opinions

Employability and earning capacity findings for Bay County evaluees are
grounded in Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage
estimates for the Bay City metropolitan area and for Michigan statewide,
state workforce agency labor market information, and direct labor market
research where the referral warrants it. The report is explicit about which
labor market defines the evaluee's realistic opportunities and why,
connecting residence, restrictions, and work history to the data relied
upon, so the earning capacity conclusion can be traced to its sources line
by line.

To retain the firm for a Bay City matter, submit the caption or party names

disclosure, deposition, or trial dates at the outset. Once the conflict
check clears, counsel receive a records checklist and a scope confirmation,
and work begins on a schedule aligned to the case. Bay City counsel engage
the same office and the same reporting standards that carry the firm's
[national practice](/locations/nationwide/), applied to a Bay County
caption.
