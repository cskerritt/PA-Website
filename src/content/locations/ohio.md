In Ohio, civil litigation of consequence runs through each county's Court
of Common Pleas, which means the courthouse that matters to a case may sit
in any corner of the state. Purinton Analytics serves that decentralized
landscape with a centralized standard: vocational expert and life care
planning work produced the same way for a Common Pleas matter as for a
federal one, for a plaintiff retention as for a defense retention. Counsel
who need economic loss quantified can pair the work with coordinated
damages engagements in which independent economist partners perform and
sign the economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning work
proceeds under one engagement structure.

## Ohio venues, state and federal

Engagements are accepted for matters in the Court of Common Pleas and in
both of Ohio's federal trial courts, the United States District Court for
the Northern District of Ohio and the United States District Court for the
Southern District of Ohio. The practice profiles Ohio's litigation centers
individually: Cincinnati,
Columbus,
Cleveland,
Dayton-Kettering-Beavercreek,
Akron,
Toledo,
Youngstown-Warren,
Canton-Massillon,
Springfield,
Mansfield,
Wooster,
Sandusky,
Lima,
Salem, and
New Philadelphia-Dover. A matter
venued in a county outside those metros is handled identically, because
the methodology travels with the case rather than with the courthouse.

## How the firm serves Ohio

There is no staffed Purinton Analytics office in Ohio and no published
street address in the state; meetings are by appointment, and the firm
states that plainly because accuracy about presence is part of the
discipline behind its reports. Most Ohio engagements begin remotely, with
records review, structured interviews, and standardized testing under
publisher protocols where remote administration is supported, and many are
completed that way. When in-person evaluation of an evaluee is warranted,
or when deposition and trial dates require appearance, travel is scheduled
from the Kansas City home office and coordinated with counsel's calendar.
Vocational findings for Ohio evaluees rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates for Ohio and for the relevant
metropolitan areas, state workforce agency labor market information, and
direct labor market research where warranted, so the employability and
earning capacity conclusions reflect the evaluee's actual market. Life
care plans for Ohio residents are priced to the evaluee's own market
using provider-specific inquiries and published fee data for the region.

## Qualification and disclosure in Ohio matters

Certifications held within the practice are issued by national
credentialing bodies rather than by any single state. Admissibility in an
Ohio matter is determined by the forum under its own rules of evidence,
and any venue-specific registration or qualification requirement is
identified and confirmed during the conflict check and intake process.
Disclosure materials and prior testimony lists are provided in the form
the venue and the parties require.

## Starting an Ohio engagement

To begin, [request a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) with the case caption
and parties, noting any imminent disclosure, deposition, or trial dates.
Engagements proceed only after the conflict check clears and a written
scope and fee agreement is in place, so the record of the engagement is as
orderly as the report it produces. For matters that extend beyond Ohio,
the firm's
[national coverage](/locations/nationwide/) page describes how the same
engagement structure and report standard apply across the country.
