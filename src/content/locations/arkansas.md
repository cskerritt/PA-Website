Every Arkansas engagement follows the same documented sequence: a conflict
check, a defined scope, a records request tailored to the claims, a written
report with stated methodology, and availability for deposition and trial.
Purinton Analytics offers that structure to plaintiff and defense counsel
alike, for vocational expert and life care planning questions arising
anywhere in the state, because a process that both sides can examine is the
foundation the opinions stand on.

## Arkansas venues, state and federal

Engagements are accepted for matters in the Circuit Courts that serve
Arkansas's counties and in both of the state's federal districts: the United
States District Court for the Eastern District of Arkansas and the United
States District Court for the Western District of Arkansas. Workers'
compensation and administrative matters proceed under the same engagement
structure as civil litigation.

## Serving Arkansas from the Kansas City home office

The firm keeps no staffed office in Arkansas and publishes no street address
for this location. Meetings are by appointment, and most engagements begin
remotely: records review, clinical interviews, and many standardized
vocational instruments can be administered by secure video under publisher
protocols, which keeps timelines short without compromising the record. When
the referral questions call for in-person evaluation, or when deposition and
trial dates require presence, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home
office. The written report identifies how each component of the evaluation
was conducted, remote or in person, so opposing counsel can test the process
itself.

Employability and earning capacity conclusions are tied to the labor market
where the evaluee actually lives and works. Vocational findings for Arkansas
evaluees rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage
estimates for Arkansas and its metropolitan areas, on state workforce agency
labor market information, and on direct labor market research where
warranted. When economic damages testimony is needed, the firm offers
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. The [nationwide coverage page](/locations/nationwide/)
describes how the national practice operates, from remote evaluation
protocols to travel and testimony logistics.

## Qualification and disclosure at intake

Disclosure practice and admissibility standards differ between Arkansas's
state and federal courts, so the forum shapes the engagement from its first
day: expert disclosure deadlines, deposition dates, and trial settings are
confirmed at intake and tracked through delivery. Any venue-specific
registration or qualification requirement is identified and confirmed during
the conflict check and intake process.

## Requesting a conflict check

The sequence described above starts with the conflict check. Send the case
caption or party names through the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and
identify any imminent disclosure, deposition, or trial dates. The firm
responds with availability, CVs, and fee information, and once the conflict
check clears, a records checklist tailored to the claims follows with the
engagement letter.
