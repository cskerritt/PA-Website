Every report Purinton Analytics writes for a Grant County case is drafted on
the assumption that the most careful reader will be opposing counsel. That
assumption, rather than any marketing claim, is the firm's offer to the
Silver City bar: opinions that disclose their methodology, cite their
sources, and stop where the record stops. Plaintiff and defense counsel in
southwestern New Mexico retain the firm for
[vocational expert witness](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) work,
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) in serious injury
matters, and [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Grant County courts and the federal district

Engagements are accepted for matters in the District Court serving Grant
County and in the United States District Court for the District of New
Mexico. Because the practice runs to both sides of the caption, each file
begins the same way regardless of who is calling: a documented conflict
check before substance is discussed, a written scope, and a records request
tailored to the pleadings. Deposition and trial support belongs to the
firm's [expert testimony and litigation consulting](/services/expert-testimony-litigation-consulting/)
practice and is scoped at intake when testimony is anticipated. Counsel with
matters in other parts of the state can start from the
[New Mexico page](/locations/new-mexico/).

## What by-appointment coverage means in Silver City

The firm has no staffed office in Silver City and publishes no street
address for this location; its home office, and its only staffed office, is
in Kansas City. Stating that plainly is part of the same discipline that
governs the reports, and it changes little about how a Grant County
engagement runs. Records review, intake, counsel conferences, and drafting
proceed remotely on a schedule set at the start of the engagement and kept
current as the litigation calendar moves.
Vocational interviews are conducted by secure video where appropriate or in
person where the referral questions require it, and travel for evaluations,
depositions, and trial is arranged from the Kansas City home office against
the court's calendar.

## Sourcing the labor market picture

Employability and earning capacity opinions for Silver City evaluees are
built on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage
estimates for the relevant area and for New Mexico statewide, on state
workforce agency labor market information, and on direct labor market
research where the file warrants it. The report distinguishes what the
published data shows from what direct research adds, so that a
cross-examining attorney can test each layer separately. Where future care
costs are in issue, [medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/)
work is sourced and disclosed with the same rigor.

## Initiating a conflict check

Grant County matters travel the same national intake path as the rest of
the practice, summarized on the
[nationwide services page](/locations/nationwide/). Any venue-specific
registration or qualification requirement is identified and confirmed
during the conflict check and intake process. To begin a local engagement,
[request a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) with the case caption and
parties, noting any disclosure deadlines, deposition settings, or trial
dates that bear on scheduling.
