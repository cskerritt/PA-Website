Purinton Analytics answers those questions for Portales counsel on either side of the caption through [vocational expert](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) and [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) engagements that begin the same way everywhere in the practice: a documented conflict check, a defined scope, and a records request tailored to the claims, so the written opinion rests on a record that plaintiff and defense counsel can each examine.

## Roosevelt County venue and the courts that serve Portales

Portales matters are Roosevelt County matters, and the reports the firm prepares are written for disclosure there. State-court claims proceed in the District Court, and the opinion is drafted to that court's expectations for disclosed expert work: methodology stated, sources cited, and conclusions confined to what the record supports. Any venue-specific registration or qualification requirement is identified at the same stage, before the engagement letter issues, so disclosure deadlines and scheduling are planned against settled ground rules rather than assumptions.

## Portales within the Clovis area labor market

Employability and earning-capacity findings for a Portales evaluee are grounded in the labor market that evaluee actually faces, which for many occupations includes the broader [Clovis, NM metro](/locations/new-mexico/clovis/) commuting area anchored by Clovis itself. The vocational analysis draws on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the relevant New Mexico area and for the state as a whole, on state workforce agency labor market information, and on direct labor market research where the published estimates are too coarse for the question presented. Each report names its geographic frame and its sources, so cross-examination can test the work instead of guessing at it.

## Evaluations for Portales evaluees

Portales work is by appointment. The firm keeps no staffed office in Portales and publishes no local street address, because accuracy about presence is part of the same discipline that governs its reports. Much of a vocational or care-planning engagement proceeds remotely: records review, structured interviews by video, and written analysis do not require travel. When the referral questions call for in-person evaluation, or when deposition or trial dates require presence, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office and planned around the case calendar. Evaluations can be conducted in the evaluee's own community, which matters for claimants whose functional limitations make long-distance travel itself a burden.
