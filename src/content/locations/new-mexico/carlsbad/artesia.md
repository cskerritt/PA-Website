## A two-town labor market in southeastern New Mexico

Earning capacity for an Artesia evaluee cannot be assessed against Artesia alone. The realistic commuting market runs between the town and Carlsbad, the metro's principal city, and a defensible opinion has to weigh what work actually exists across that corridor, at what wage levels, and whether the evaluee's residual capacity and transferable skills reach it. Vocational findings for Artesia matters therefore rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the region and for New Mexico statewide, supplemented by state workforce agency labor market information and by direct labor market research when the referral questions call for it, never on national averages presented as local fact. ## How an Artesia evaluee is evaluated

The firm maintains no office in Artesia and lists no local address, because candor about presence belongs to the same discipline that governs the opinions themselves. Records review, transferable skills analysis, [medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/), and labor market research are conducted from the Kansas City home office, and evaluee interviews proceed by video when the referral questions permit it. When the questions require an in-person evaluation, the appointment is held in the evaluee's own community, so that a rural or working evaluee is not asked to travel long distances to be assessed, and the trip is planned from Kansas City around the case's disclosure and deposition calendar.
