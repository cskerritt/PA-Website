A written opinion should survive the scrutiny of the lawyer who did not
retain it. That standard governs every report Purinton Analytics prepares
for matters in the Gallup, NM micropolitan area, whether the engagement is
[vocational expert evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), or a

in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. The firm works for plaintiff and defense counsel in
comparable measure, and each report states its methodology, cites its
sources, and documents its file so the analysis can be retraced from
either side of the caption.

## What by-appointment service means here

Purinton Analytics keeps no staffed office in Gallup and publishes no
street address for this location. Meetings are by appointment, and the firm
prefers to say so plainly: a practice that asks juries to trust its
sourcing should describe its own presence with the same precision. Gallup
engagements typically begin remotely, with records review, structured
interviews, and standardized testing under publisher protocols where remote
administration is supported. When the referral questions require in-person
evaluation, or when deposition or trial attendance is needed, travel is
scheduled from the Kansas City home office against the deadlines counsel
identifies at intake, so the record is complete before testimony is given.

## Labor market foundations for McKinley County opinions

Employability and earning capacity conclusions for evaluees in the Gallup
area are grounded in Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and
wage estimates covering the area and New Mexico statewide, in state
workforce agency labor market information, and in direct labor market
research where the referral questions warrant it. The analysis addresses
the evaluee's own commuting labor market, not an abstraction of the
national economy. Life care plans and medical cost projections for
McKinley County residents are priced through provider-specific inquiries
and published fee data for the region, and each cost item carries a cited
source, which is what allows the projection to be defended element by
element under cross-examination.
