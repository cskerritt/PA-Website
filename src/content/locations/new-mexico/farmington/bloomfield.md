fails on the calendar. Disclosure deadlines, expert designation dates, and
deposition settings arrive on their own schedule whether or not the
vocational analysis is ready, and a small-town docket offers no more slack
than a big-city one. Purinton Analytics builds each Bloomfield engagement
backward from those dates: the conflict check first, then a defined scope,
a records request tailored to the claims, and a written work plan that puts
the interview, the labor market research, and the draft opinion ahead of

## San Juan County venue and the federal forum

Bloomfield sits in San Juan County, and state-court civil matters from the
town proceed in the District Court. In the federal system, New Mexico's
forum is the United States District Court for the District of New Mexico,
and the controlling venue for any particular matter, along with any
venue-specific registration or qualification requirement, is identified and
confirmed during the conflict check and intake process. Because the same
report may be tested in either setting, every opinion is written to a
court-ready standard: methodology an opposing expert can retrace, sources
cited precisely enough to be pulled and verified, and conclusions that stay
within the disclosed scope. Surrounding venues are described on the
[Farmington metro page](/locations/new-mexico/farmington/) and the
statewide [New Mexico page](/locations/new-mexico/).

## Reading the labor market a Bloomfield worker actually faces

An earning-capacity opinion about a Bloomfield evaluee has to describe
employment as it exists within realistic reach of the evaluee's home, not
as a statewide average abstracts it. The firm grounds that description in
Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for
the Farmington, NM area and for New Mexico statewide, supplemented by state
workforce agency labor market information and by direct employer contact
when the referral questions call for it. Reports state which geography each
source covers and why it applies to this worker, so the analysis holds up
whether the challenge is that it reached too far or that it did not reach
far enough.

## By-appointment work, evaluated where the evaluee lives

The firm keeps no office in Bloomfield and publishes no local address;
plain accuracy about presence is part of the same discipline that governs
its reports. Engagements run remote-first: records review, counsel
conferences, and most vocational interviews proceed by secure video on the
case schedule. When the referral questions require in-person assessment, or
when a deposition or trial date requires presence, travel is scheduled from
the Kansas City home office and the evaluation is conducted in the
evaluee's own community, where daily routine, transportation, and actual
access to work can be observed rather than assumed.
