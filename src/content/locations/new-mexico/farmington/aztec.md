Distance is the quiet variable in an Aztec damages case. An evaluee in a
small San Juan County community does not choose among employers the way a
resident of a major city does, and a vocational opinion that ignores that
fact invites cross-examination it cannot survive. Purinton Analytics treats
geography as evidence: what work this particular person can still perform,
where that work genuinely exists within reach of Aztec, and what it pays in
the market the evaluee actually faces rather than the market a national
table assumes. Counsel on either side of the caption retain the firm on the

## Where an Aztec matter is heard

State-court civil litigation arising in Aztec proceeds in the District
Court serving San Juan County. New Mexico's federal trial forum is the
United States District Court for the District of New Mexico; which court
controls a given dispute, and any venue-specific registration or
qualification requirement that follows from it, is identified and confirmed
during the conflict check and intake process. The firm writes each report
so that the choice of forum changes nothing about its quality: a
methodology disclosed step by step, source material an opposing expert can
locate and check, and opinions that do not wander past the assignment. The
region's other venues are covered on the
[Farmington metro page](/locations/new-mexico/farmington/), and the state's

## Aztec within the Farmington employment area

Vocational findings for an Aztec evaluee rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates for the Farmington, NM area and
for New Mexico statewide, alongside state workforce agency labor market
information and direct labor market research where warranted. The report
explains the relationship between the local picture and the statewide one
in plain terms: which occupations are realistically available to this
worker given restrictions, education, and work history, and what those
occupations pay in this employment area. Scale is described qualitatively
and honestly; a smaller regional market is neither inflated into a
metropolis nor dismissed as empty, because either distortion would hand the
opposing side its opening question.

## Appointments, interviews, and travel

There is no Purinton Analytics office in Aztec, and the firm publishes no
local address anywhere it does not staff one. Most of an engagement's work
never requires a meeting room: records analysis, scheduling, counsel
conferences, and the majority of vocational interviews proceed by secure
video. When the assignment calls for an in-person evaluation, the firm
comes to the evaluee rather than the reverse, with travel arranged from the
Kansas City home office and the assessment conducted in the evaluee's own
community, where actual commuting burdens and daily function can be
observed firsthand. The same travel practice covers depositions and trial
testimony when the calendar requires the expert in the room.
