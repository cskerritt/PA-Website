Matters venued in Doña Ana County reach Purinton Analytics through the same
intake path as matters anywhere else in the country: a conflict check, a
defined scope, and a records request tailored to the claims. The firm
provides [vocational expert services](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure, and it accepts that work from plaintiff and defense
counsel alike in the Las Cruces, NM metropolitan area.

## Courts serving Las Cruces matters

The Las Cruces metropolitan area consists of Doña Ana County, and state-court
engagements from the region are typically venued in the District Court
sitting there. Federal matters proceed in the United States District Court
for the District of New Mexico. The firm prepares the same disclosure-ready
report for either forum, with methodology, sources, and file documentation
set out so that both retaining and opposing counsel can trace every finding.
Counsel with related matters elsewhere in the state will find the same
approach described on the firm's [New Mexico page](/locations/new-mexico/).

## Presence, stated plainly

There is no staffed Purinton Analytics office in Las Cruces, and the firm
publishes no street address for this location. Meetings are by appointment.
That candor is deliberate: a practice whose reports depend on verifiable
sourcing should not imply a local storefront it does not keep. What counsel
get instead is a remote-first process, records review, structured
interviews, and standardized testing under publisher protocols where remote
administration is supported, with in-person evaluation available when the
referral questions call for it. When presence is required for an evaluation,
a deposition, or trial, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office
and coordinated against the litigation calendar counsel provides.

## Labor market evidence for Doña Ana County evaluees

Employability and earning capacity opinions for evaluees in the Las Cruces
area rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage
estimates for the Las Cruces metropolitan area and for New Mexico statewide,
together with state workforce agency labor market information and direct
labor market research where warranted. The analysis is anchored to the
evaluee's own commuting labor market rather than to national averages that
may not describe local conditions. Life care plans and medical cost
projections for area residents are priced through provider-specific
inquiries and published fee data for the region, and each cost item in the
plan carries its source so the projection can be tested line by line in
deposition.

## Beginning an engagement

Engagements are accepted on either side of the caption, and early retention
protects the analysis: it allows records to be requested once, completely,
and it gives testing and interviews room on the calendar before disclosure
deadlines compress the schedule. Counsel can review the firm's
[nationwide practice](/locations/nationwide/) for a fuller description of
how out-of-town venues are served, then
[request a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) with the case caption and
parties. Include the court in which the matter is pending and any dates
already set for disclosure, deposition, or trial, and the firm will respond
with availability, a proposed scope, and the records request that would
begin the work.
