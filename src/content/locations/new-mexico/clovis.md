The Clovis, NM micropolitan area spans Curry County and Roosevelt County,
and Purinton Analytics serves counsel across both under a single engagement
structure. Whether a matter is pending on the Curry County side or the
Roosevelt County side, the work follows the same sequence: documented
conflict check, defined scope, tailored records request, and then the
analysis itself, whether that is
[earning capacity and employability evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), or a
[coordinated damages engagement](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. Plaintiff and defense referrals are accepted on the
same terms.

## Venues in Curry and Roosevelt counties

State-court matters from the region are typically venued in the District
Court sitting in Curry County or in Roosevelt County, depending on where
the case arises, and federal matters proceed in the United States District
Court for the District of New Mexico. The firm writes to a
disclosure-ready standard for every forum, with methodology stated and
sources cited so the opinion can be retraced by either side of the caption.
Counsel with matters elsewhere in the state can find the same standard
described on the firm's [New Mexico page](/locations/new-mexico/).

## Appointments, not an office

Purinton Analytics maintains no staffed office in Clovis and publishes no
street address for this location. Meetings throughout the area are by
appointment, and the firm says so directly because candor about presence
is part of the credibility it brings to the witness stand. Engagements
generally begin remotely: records review, structured interviews, and
standardized testing under publisher protocols where remote administration
is supported. When the referral questions require in-person evaluation, or
when deposition and trial settings require attendance, travel is scheduled
from the Kansas City home office and sequenced against the litigation
calendar counsel supplies at intake.

## Labor market research across the area

Employability and earning capacity opinions for evaluees in the Clovis
area rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage
estimates covering the area and New Mexico statewide, together with state
workforce agency labor market information and direct labor market research
where warranted. Because the area takes in both Curry County and Roosevelt
County, the analysis is anchored to the evaluee's own commuting labor
market rather than to a single citywide generalization. Life care plans
and medical cost projections for area residents are priced through
provider-specific inquiries and published fee data for the region, with
each cost element sourced in the report so the projection can be tested
item by item in deposition.

## Engaging the firm for a Clovis area matter

Early retention gives the analysis room: records arrive complete,
evaluation dates precede disclosure deadlines, and testimony preparation
builds on a settled report rather than a rushed one. Counsel can review
the firm's [nationwide coverage](/locations/nationwide/) for how it
serves venues at any distance, then
[request a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) with the case caption and
parties. Include the county and court in which the matter is pending and
any imminent disclosure, deposition, or trial dates, and the firm will
respond with availability, a proposed scope, and a records request
tailored to the claims.
