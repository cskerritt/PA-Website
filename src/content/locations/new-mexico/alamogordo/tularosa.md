## Measuring earning capacity beyond the village limits

A defensible opinion about a Tularosa evaluee begins with an honest definition of the labor market, which here means the commuting orbit around Alamogordo, the principal city of the surrounding metropolitan area. The practice asks what work exists across that orbit, whether the evaluee's residual functional capacity and transferable skills actually reach it, and what the commute itself costs a person with documented limitations. Vocational findings rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the area and for New Mexico statewide, on state workforce agency labor market information, and on direct labor market research when the referral questions call for it; national figures are never passed off as local fact. The regional employment picture is treated at length on the [Alamogordo metro page](/locations/new-mexico/alamogordo/).
