The Carlsbad-Artesia area pairs its principal communities within a single
county, and litigation arising anywhere in Eddy County reaches the same
courts and the same labor market questions. Purinton Analytics serves
plaintiff and defense counsel across the area with
[earning capacity and employability evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) in catastrophic injury
matters, and [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. Statewide coverage is described on the
[New Mexico page](/locations/new-mexico/).

## Courts serving Eddy County

Matters are accepted whether venued in the District Court serving Eddy
County or in the United States District Court for the District of New
Mexico. Retention by either side of the caption follows the same sequence:
a documented conflict check before any substantive discussion, a written
scope, and a records request tailored to the claims actually pleaded. That
sequence exists so the eventual report can be defended from the record
alone, and it does not change with the identity of the retaining party.
Rebuttal and peer review assignments, where the task is to test another
expert's opinion rather than issue an affirmative one, are handled through
the firm's [rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/)
service under the same rules.

## How by-appointment service works here

There is no staffed Purinton Analytics office in Carlsbad or Artesia, and
the firm publishes no street address for this location; accuracy about
offices is part of the same discipline that governs its opinions. The home
office is in Kansas City, and Eddy County engagements are served by
appointment from there. In practice most of the work is remote: records
review, vocational interviews by secure video where appropriate, report
drafting, and pre-deposition conferences with counsel. Scheduling
expectations are set in the engagement letter, so counsel knows at the
outset when drafts and final reports will arrive. When the referral
questions call for in-person evaluation, or when a deposition or trial date
requires presence, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office
against the dates counsel provides.

## Grounding opinions in the local labor market

Employability and earning capacity opinions for Carlsbad-Artesia evaluees
are grounded in Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage
estimates for the relevant area and for New Mexico statewide, together with
state workforce agency labor market information and, where warranted, direct
labor market research such as employer sampling. The report identifies which
sources carried the analysis so that opposing counsel can check the work
rather than take it on faith. Life care plans follow the same posture, with
costing sources disclosed and tied to the care actually recommended in the
medical record.

## Requesting a conflict check

Eddy County cases enter the practice through the same national intake that
serves every other venue, described on the
[nationwide services page](/locations/nationwide/). Any venue-specific
registration or qualification requirement is identified and confirmed during
the conflict check and intake process. To begin an engagement,
[request a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) with the case caption and
parties, and flag any disclosure deadlines or hearing dates that should
shape the schedule.
