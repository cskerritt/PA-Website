## The San Juan County venue

The Farmington metropolitan area consists of San Juan County, and
state-court matters from the region are typically venued in the District
Court sitting there. Federal matters proceed in the United States District
Court for the District of New Mexico. The firm prepares each report to a
single disclosure-ready standard for both forums, with methodology stated
and sources cited so that either side of the caption can retrace the
analysis. Counsel handling related matters in other parts of the state can
review the firm's [New Mexico practice](/locations/new-mexico/), which
operates under the same standard.

## Serving a distant venue candidly

The firm has no staffed office in Farmington and publishes no street address
for this location. Meetings are by appointment, and the firm says so plainly
rather than implying a local presence it does not keep. What counsel in San
Juan County receive instead is a process designed for distance: the analytic
work proceeds remotely on a complete record, and when the referral questions
require in-person evaluation, or when deposition and trial dates require
attendance, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office. Because
travel is planned rather than improvised, counsel are asked at intake for
the litigation calendar, so evaluation dates, disclosure deadlines, and
testimony can be sequenced without compressing the analysis.

## Grounding opinions in the local labor market

Distance never substitutes national generalities for local evidence.
Employability and earning capacity findings for Farmington-area evaluees
rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage
estimates for the Farmington metropolitan area and for New Mexico
statewide, together with state workforce agency labor market information
and direct labor market research where warranted. Conclusions are tied to
the evaluee's own commuting labor market. Life care plans and medical cost
projections for San Juan County residents are priced through
provider-specific inquiries and published fee data for the region, and
every figure in the plan carries a cited source that can be tested in
deposition.
