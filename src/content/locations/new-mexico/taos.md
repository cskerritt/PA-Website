For an injured evaluee in Taos County, the evaluation should come to the
evaluee, not the other way around. Purinton Analytics structures its Taos
engagements around that principle: vocational interviews and life care
planning assessments are conducted by secure video or, when the referral
questions require it, in the evaluee's own community, so that opinions about
employability and future care reflect the person's actual circumstances.
Plaintiff and defense counsel in the area retain the firm for
[vocational expert witness](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) work,
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Taos County venues

Engagements are accepted for matters in the District Court serving Taos
County and in the United States District Court for the District of New
Mexico. The firm's two-sided practice shapes how every file is handled:
because the next retention may come from across the aisle, reports are
written to a single standard, with methodology disclosed, sources cited,
and conclusions confined to what the record supports. Testimony support,
from deposition preparation through trial, is part of the
[expert testimony and litigation consulting](/services/expert-testimony-litigation-consulting/)
side of the practice and follows the same discipline. Coverage across the
rest of the state is described on the [New Mexico page](/locations/new-mexico/).

## No local office, and why that is stated

Purinton Analytics keeps no staffed office in Taos and publishes no street
address for this location. The firm's home office is in Kansas City, and
every other location in the practice, Taos included, is served by
appointment. That candor is deliberate: a practice whose reports depend on
verifiable statements should not imply a presence it does not have. Routine
engagement activity is remote, including intake, records review, counsel
conferences, and report drafting. When in-person evaluation is warranted or
a court date requires attendance, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City
home office and confirmed against the deadlines counsel identifies.

## Labor market evidence for Taos County files

Employability and earning capacity findings for Taos evaluees rest on
Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for
the relevant area and for New Mexico statewide, on state workforce agency
labor market information, and on direct labor market research where the
referral questions warrant it. The analysis is qualitative where the data
demands caution and specific where the record permits, and every source
relied upon is identified in the report so opposing counsel can retrace the
path from data to opinion. Life care plans are costed with the same
transparency, tied to care recommendations documented in the medical record.

## Beginning a Taos engagement

Taos County matters enter through the same national intake as the rest of
the practice, outlined on the [nationwide services page](/locations/nationwide/).
Any venue-specific registration or qualification requirement is identified
and confirmed during the conflict check and intake process. To begin,
[request a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) with the case caption and
parties, and include any disclosure, deposition, or trial dates that should
drive the schedule.
