## Where Santa Fe matters are heard

The Santa Fe metropolitan area consists of Santa Fe County. State-court
engagements from the region are typically venued in the District Court
sitting in Santa Fe County, and federal matters proceed in the United States
District Court for the District of New Mexico. The firm's reports are
prepared to the same standard for either forum: methodology stated,
sources cited, and the underlying file organized so that opposing counsel
can retrace the analysis. Matters arising elsewhere in the state are handled
through the firm's broader [New Mexico practice](/locations/new-mexico/)
under the identical report standard.

## Appointment-based service, honestly described

The firm keeps no staffed office in Santa Fe and publishes no street address
for this location; meetings here are by appointment. That is stated plainly
because honesty about presence belongs to the same discipline that governs
the reports themselves. In practice, most engagements begin remotely:
records review, structured interviews, and standardized testing under
publisher protocols where remote administration is supported. Where the
referral questions call for in-person evaluation, or where deposition and
trial dates require attendance, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City
home office and planned against the deadlines counsel identifies at intake.
The point of contact and the report format stay constant regardless of
where individual tasks are performed.

## Labor market and cost research for Santa Fe County evaluees

Vocational findings for evaluees in the Santa Fe area draw on Bureau of
Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Santa Fe
metropolitan area and for New Mexico statewide, alongside state workforce
agency labor market information and direct labor market research where the
questions warrant it. Employability and placeability conclusions are tied to
the evaluee's own commuting labor market. Life care plans and medical cost
projections for area residents are priced to that same local market through
provider-specific inquiries and published fee data, with every cost item
sourced in the report so the projection can be examined element by element.

## Opening a Santa Fe engagement

Retaining the firm early in the life of a case preserves options: records
can be requested once and completely, evaluation dates can be set before
disclosure deadlines narrow the calendar, and rebuttal or peer review work
can be scoped alongside the affirmative analysis. Counsel comparing venues
or coordinating multi-state litigation can consult the firm's
[national coverage](/locations/nationwide/) for how distant matters are
staffed. To begin, submit the caption or party names through the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and note any imminent disclosure,
deposition, or trial dates. The firm replies with availability, a proposed
scope, and a records request tailored to the claims.
