## Where Rio Arriba County matters are heard

The firm accepts engagements for matters venued in the District Court
serving Rio Arriba County and in the United States District Court for the
District of New Mexico. Because retention comes from either side of the
caption, opinions are written to be examined, not merely believed: the
report states its methodology, discloses its sources, and separates what the
record supports from what it does not. Counsel elsewhere in the state can
review the firm's full New Mexico coverage on the
[state page](/locations/new-mexico/).

## Presence, stated plainly

The firm maintains no office in Española and lists no street address for
this location. Its single home office is in Kansas City, and it treats
plain statements about presence as part of its credibility: counsel should
know exactly what they are retaining. What the Española bar gets instead of
a storefront is a by-appointment practice in which intake, records review,
attorney conferences, and report drafting proceed remotely on a predictable
schedule, and in which evaluations can be conducted by secure video or, when
the referral questions require it, in person in the evaluee's own
community. Travel for in-person
evaluations, depositions, and trial testimony is scheduled from the Kansas
City home office around the dates the court sets.

## Reading the labor market around Española

Vocational opinions for evaluees in the Española area draw on Bureau of
Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the relevant
area and for New Mexico statewide, on state workforce agency labor market
information, and on direct labor market research where the file warrants it.
The analysis addresses the employment actually available to the evaluee
given residual capacity, transferable skills, and the character of the
surrounding labor market, and it says so in terms qualitative enough to be
accurate and specific enough to be tested. Where the damages picture also
requires future medical costing, a
[medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/) can be scoped
within the same engagement.
