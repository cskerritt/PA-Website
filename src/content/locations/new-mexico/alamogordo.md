The evaluee, not the expert's office, is the natural center of a vocational
and life care planning engagement, and Purinton Analytics organizes its
Alamogordo work accordingly. For matters arising in the Alamogordo, NM
micropolitan area, evaluations are arranged around the person being
assessed: remote interviews and standardized testing where publisher
protocols support remote administration, and in-person assessment in the
evaluee's own community when the referral questions require it. On that
basis the firm provides
[earning capacity and employability evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure, for plaintiff and defense counsel alike.

## Otero County venue and courts

The Alamogordo micropolitan area consists of Otero County. State-court
matters from the region are typically venued in the District Court sitting
in Otero County, while federal matters proceed in the United States
District Court for the District of New Mexico. The firm prepares one
disclosure-ready report standard for both forums, with methodology stated
and sources cited so either side of the caption can retrace the analysis.
Related matters elsewhere in the state fall under the firm's
[New Mexico practice](/locations/new-mexico/) and the same intake process.

## By-appointment service, described accurately

There is no staffed Purinton Analytics office in Alamogordo, and the firm
publishes no street address for this location; meetings are by appointment.
That accuracy is intentional, because a report built on verifiable sourcing
should come from a firm that describes its own footprint just as
carefully. The working pattern is remote-first: conflict check, defined
scope, tailored records request, then interviews and testing. When
in-person evaluation is warranted, or when deposition and trial dates
require attendance, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office
and coordinated with the litigation calendar counsel provides at intake, so
neither the assessment nor the testimony is left to short-notice
improvisation.

## Reading the labor market where the evaluee lives

Employability and earning capacity findings for Alamogordo-area evaluees
rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage
estimates covering the area and New Mexico statewide, along with state
workforce agency labor market information and direct labor market research
where warranted. Opinions are tied to the evaluee's own commuting labor
market, because placeability in the community where a person actually lives
is the question the trier of fact must answer. Life care plans and medical
cost projections for Otero County residents are priced through
provider-specific inquiries and published fee data for the region, with
every cost item sourced so the projection withstands line-by-line
examination.
