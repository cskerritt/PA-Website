Counsel practicing in Roswell and the surrounding Chaves County region often
work at a distance from the state's larger expert markets, and Purinton
Analytics is built to close that distance. The firm provides
[vocational expert](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) and
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) services to plaintiff and
defense counsel throughout southeastern New Mexico, and it structures
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. Counsel with matters elsewhere in the state will find
the broader practice described on the [New Mexico page](/locations/new-mexico/).

## Venue footprint in Chaves County

Engagements are accepted for matters venued in the District Court serving
Chaves County and in the United States District Court for the District of
New Mexico. The work product is the same in either forum: a documented
conflict check, a defined scope, a records request tailored to the claims,
and a written opinion that rests on a record both sides of the caption can
examine. Because the firm appears for plaintiffs and defendants alike, every
report is drafted on the assumption that opposing counsel will read it
closely, and the methodology sections are written so that cross-examination
can proceed directly from the disclosed materials.

## Meetings by appointment, not a storefront

Purinton Analytics keeps no staffed office in Roswell and publishes no
street address for this location. Candor about presence is part of the same
discipline that governs the reports: the firm's single home office is in
Kansas City, and everything else is served by appointment. Most engagement
activity, from intake and records review through report drafting and
attorney conferences, is handled remotely. When an in-person evaluation is
warranted, or when deposition and trial calendars require presence in
southeastern New Mexico, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home
office and coordinated around the court's dates rather than the firm's
convenience.

## Labor market research for Roswell evaluees

Vocational findings for evaluees in the Roswell area rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates covering the relevant
area and New Mexico statewide, supplemented by state workforce agency labor
market information and direct labor market research where warranted.
Employability and earning capacity opinions are tied to the labor market the
evaluee actually faces, not to a generic national profile, and the sources
relied upon are cited in the report so that either side can retrace the
analysis. Where a file calls for future medical costing rather than
vocational opinion, [medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/)
work follows the same sourcing discipline.

## Starting a Chaves County engagement

The practice is national, and Roswell matters move through the same intake
as cases from any other venue; the scope of that coverage is described on
the [nationwide services page](/locations/nationwide/). Any venue-specific
registration or qualification requirement is identified and confirmed during
the conflict check and intake process. To begin, submit the caption or party
names through the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and note any
imminent disclosure, deposition, or trial dates so scheduling can be
confirmed against the court's calendar before the engagement letter issues.
