Purinton Analytics accepts referrals from attorneys litigating in the
Albuquerque, NM metropolitan area, working for plaintiff and defense counsel
in comparable measure. Engagements from the metro typically call for
[earning capacity and employability evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/)
in injury and employment matters, [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/)
in catastrophic injury cases, and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. Each matter begins with a documented conflict check and
a defined scope, so that the written opinion rests on a record either side of
the caption can examine.

## Venue footprint across the metro

The Albuquerque metropolitan area takes in Bernalillo County, Sandoval
County, Torrance County, and Valencia County, and the firm accepts matters
venued in the District Court sitting in each of those counties. Federal
matters proceed in the United States District Court for the District of New
Mexico. The report standard does not change with the forum: the same
methodology disclosure, source citation, and file documentation apply whether
the opinion is prepared for a state District Court jury or for federal
litigation, and the same standard travels to the rest of the state through
the firm's broader [New Mexico practice](/locations/new-mexico/).

## By appointment in Albuquerque

Meetings in Albuquerque are by appointment. The firm maintains no staffed
office in the metro and publishes no street address for this location,
because accuracy about presence is part of the same discipline that governs
its reports. Most engagements begin remotely with records review, structured
interviews, and standardized testing under publisher protocols where remote
administration is supported. When the referral questions require in-person
evaluation, or when deposition and trial calendars require attendance,
travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office and planned around the
deadlines counsel identifies at intake. Counsel receive a single point of
contact regardless of where any given task is performed.

## Labor market research for metro evaluees

Vocational findings for evaluees in the metro rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Albuquerque
metropolitan area and for New Mexico statewide, supplemented by state
workforce agency labor market information and by direct employer contact
where the referral questions warrant it. Transferable skills analysis and
employability opinions are grounded in the evaluee's own commuting labor
market rather than in national generalities. Life care plans and medical
cost projections for area residents are priced to the evaluee's actual
market using provider-specific inquiries and published fee data, and every
source is cited in the report so that opposing counsel can trace each figure
to its origin.

## Starting an Albuquerque engagement

The practice is built to serve this metro the way it serves venues across
the country: remote-first work, by-appointment meetings, and reports written
to withstand cross-examination. Counsel weighing an engagement can review the
firm's [national coverage](/locations/nationwide/) for a description of how
multi-state and out-of-state matters are handled under the same engagement
structure. To begin, submit the caption or party names through the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and note any imminent disclosure,
deposition, or trial dates, along with the county or federal venue in which
the matter is pending. The firm responds with availability, a proposed
scope, and a records request tailored to the claims before any substantive
work begins.
