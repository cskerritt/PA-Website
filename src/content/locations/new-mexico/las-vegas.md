The Las Vegas, New Mexico area spans two counties, Mora County and San
Miguel County, and Purinton Analytics accepts engagements arising in either.
That two-county footprint matters at intake: venue, the applicable labor
market, and the logistics of an evaluation are confirmed at the outset so
the scope letter reflects where the case actually sits. Plaintiff and
defense counsel across both counties retain the firm for
[earning capacity and employability evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Venues across Mora and San Miguel counties

The firm takes matters venued in the District Court serving Mora County and
San Miguel County and in the United States District Court for the District
of New Mexico. Whichever forum a case occupies, the file moves through the
same sequence: documented conflict check, defined scope, records request
tailored to the claims, and a written opinion built to be examined by both
sides of the caption. Where counsel needs an existing opinion tested rather
than a new one issued, the firm's
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) work applies the
same standards to the other expert's methodology. The full statewide
practice is described on the [New Mexico page](/locations/new-mexico/).

## Appointment-based service, honestly described

There is no Purinton Analytics office in Las Vegas, and the firm publishes
no street address for this location. Its one staffed office is the home
office in Kansas City; every other location is served by appointment, and
saying so plainly is part of the same discipline that governs the reports.
Day-to-day engagement work is remote: intake, records review, vocational
interviews by secure video where appropriate, counsel conferences, and
drafting, with deliverable dates fixed in the engagement letter. When the referral questions call for an in-person evaluation in
Mora County or San Miguel County, or when deposition or trial requires
presence, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office around the
dates the case imposes.

## Labor market analysis for a rural two-county area

Vocational opinions for evaluees in the Las Vegas area rest on Bureau of
Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the relevant
area and for New Mexico statewide, on state workforce agency labor market
information, and on direct labor market research where warranted. In a
rural labor market the distance between statewide data and an evaluee's
realistic options can be significant, so the analysis addresses the
employment actually reachable from the evaluee's community and says
explicitly which sources ground each finding. Life care plans and any
associated [medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/)
follow the same sourcing transparency.

Cases from Mora County and San Miguel County enter the same national intake
as every other venue, described on the
[nationwide services page](/locations/nationwide/). Any venue-specific
registration or qualification requirement is identified and confirmed during
the conflict check and intake process. To begin, submit the caption or
party names through the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and note any
imminent disclosure, deposition, or trial dates.
