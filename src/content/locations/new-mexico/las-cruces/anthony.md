An evaluee in Anthony should not have to organize an injured life around an
expert's travel preferences. Purinton Analytics runs its evaluation practice
the other way around: interviews, testing, and record review are scheduled by
secure remote connection or arranged in the evaluee's own community, so the
assessment reflects the person's actual circumstances rather than a
conference room far from home. That evaluee-first posture serves counsel on
either side of the caption equally, in
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/),
[vocational expert](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) work, and
[medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/) engagements
arising from Anthony.

## Where Anthony cases are heard

Anthony lies in Doña Ana County. State-court claims from the town proceed in
the District Court, and the United States District Court for the District of
New Mexico is the federal forum available to New Mexico litigants; which
court controls a given case is settled during the conflict check and intake,
not assumed. The firm writes for either forum with the same rigor, because a
report that survives scrutiny in front of a state trial judge should be no
different from one prepared for federal disclosure. Its statewide approach
to New Mexico venues, including registration and qualification questions
identified at intake, is described on the
[New Mexico page](/locations/new-mexico/).

## A small town, a metro-wide labor market

Anthony is a small community, and treating its town limits as the labor
market would understate what a resident can realistically pursue. The town
belongs to the Las Cruces, NM metropolitan area, so vocational conclusions
are grounded in Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage
estimates for that metropolitan area and for New Mexico statewide,
supplemented by state workforce agency labor market information and by
direct employer research when the referral questions call for it. The
analysis asks what work exists within a reasonable commute of Anthony, what
the evaluee's residual capacity and transferable skills permit, and what the
gap between pre-injury and post-injury earning capacity actually is. The
broader regional employment picture appears on the firm's
[Las Cruces page](/locations/new-mexico/las-cruces/).

## Meetings and appearances, by appointment

The firm maintains no office in Anthony and lists no street address here;
implying a local storefront would sit poorly beside reports that demand
verifiable sourcing from everyone else. Counsel meetings are held by
appointment, most often by video conference. In-person evaluations, site
visits, depositions, and trial testimony are supported through travel
scheduled from the Kansas City home office, planned against the litigation
calendar rather than improvised. This is the same structure the firm applies
across the country, as outlined on the
[nationwide page](/locations/nationwide/), and it means an Anthony case
receives the identical documentation standard a case in a major metropolitan
center would receive.

## Requesting a conflict check

Before any substantive conversation about the file, the firm clears
conflicts. Counsel can [request a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) with the
case caption and party names, along with a short description of the claims
and any dates already set for disclosure, deposition, or trial. Once the
matter clears, intake produces a written scope and a records request
tailored to the injuries and the damages theory, and the evaluation is
calendared around the evaluee in Anthony rather than the other way around.
