## Doña Ana County venue context

Sunland Park sits in Doña Ana County, and state-court matters from the town
are heard in the District Court, the same trial forum that serves the rest of
the Las Cruces, NM metropolitan area. Federal claims from New Mexico parties
may proceed in the United States District Court for the District of New
Mexico; the controlling venue for any particular matter is confirmed during
the conflict check and intake. The work product does not change with the
forum. Every opinion is set out with its methodology, sources, and file
documentation visible, so that retaining counsel can build on it and opposing
counsel can test it. Counsel with related matters in the principal city will
find the metro-level venue picture on the firm's
[Las Cruces page](/locations/new-mexico/las-cruces/), and matters elsewhere
in the state are covered on the [New Mexico page](/locations/new-mexico/).

## Earning capacity beyond the town line

Sunland Park residents commonly work outside the town itself, which is why a
credible earning-capacity opinion for a Sunland Park evaluee cannot stop at
the municipal boundary. The firm's vocational findings rest on Bureau of
Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Las
Cruces metropolitan area and for New Mexico statewide, on state workforce
agency labor market information, and on direct labor market research where
the referral questions warrant it. Transferable skills are analyzed against
the occupations actually available in the commuting market, not against a
generic national profile. When the damages theory requires economic
modeling, the firm offers
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Evaluations without a local storefront

Purinton Analytics keeps no staffed office in Sunland Park and publishes no
street address for this location, because accuracy about presence is part of
the same discipline that governs its reports. Most evaluations proceed by
secure remote technology, which shortens timelines and spares evaluees
unnecessary travel. When the referral questions call for in-person
assessment, the evaluation is arranged in the evaluee's own community, with
travel scheduled from the Kansas City home office. Deposition and trial
appearances are planned the same way and are calendared around the case
schedule, consistent with the national practice described on the
[nationwide page](/locations/nationwide/).

## Starting a Sunland Park engagement

The engagement path is deliberately short. Submit the caption or party names
through the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/), identify the claims and
any imminent disclosure, deposition, or trial dates, and the firm will
respond with a defined scope and a records request tailored to the matter.
From there the file proceeds on the calendar the court has set, whether the
case is venued in Doña Ana County or in federal court.
