## Lea County courts and the disclosure calendar

Eunice lies in Lea County, and its state court matters proceed in the District Court. The firm does not guess at the operative forum: the controlling venue for a given matter, and any registration or qualification requirement particular to it, is identified and confirmed during the conflict check and intake process. What matters more than the name of the courthouse is the calendar it imposes. The expert disclosure deadline governs how the engagement is sequenced: records requests go out at the outset, the evaluation is scheduled as soon as releases clear, and the report is calendared to leave counsel time to absorb it and to prepare its defense at deposition. A report that arrives late or thin is a problem no courtroom advocacy can fully cure, and the work plan is built so that neither happens.

## Reading the labor market around Eunice

Employability and earning capacity findings for a Eunice evaluee are anchored in the commuting market the evaluee can actually reach, which is the [Hobbs, NM metro area](/locations/new-mexico/hobbs/) with Hobbs as its principal city. The sourcing is disciplined: Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the applicable area and for New Mexico statewide, state workforce agency labor market information, and direct labor market research where the published estimates do not answer the referral questions. A smaller market raises real methodological issues, among them commuting scope, the depth of demand within the evaluee's residual capacity, and the feasibility of placement, and the report works through each of them from cited data rather than local impression. The method is disclosed on the face of the report so that either side can test it.

## How a Eunice evaluee is evaluated

Candor about presence belongs to the same discipline that governs the findings: the firm keeps no staffed office in Eunice or anywhere else in New Mexico, publishes no local address, and meets by appointment only. Secure video carries much of a typical engagement, from the structured vocational interview to conferences with counsel over the records, and that remote posture holds the expense of the work in proportion to what the case actually requires. An in-person vocational evaluation or life care planning assessment, where the referral requires one, takes place in the evaluee's own community; travel for it is scheduled from the Kansas City home office and coordinated with counsel around treatment and deposition calendars. Where economic loss calculations are also needed, the firm offers [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement structure.
