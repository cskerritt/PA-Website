Litigation deadlines, not geography, are what usually drive a Luna County
retention: a disclosure date approaching, a deposition set, a trial docket
firming up. Purinton Analytics builds Deming engagements backward from those
dates, scoping the [vocational expert](/services/vocational-expert-witness/)
or [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) work so the report is
complete, disclosed, and defensible when the calendar requires it. The firm
serves plaintiff and defense counsel alike, and it offers
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Luna County venue coverage

The firm accepts matters venued in the District Court serving Luna County
and in the United States District Court for the District of New Mexico. The
same intake discipline applies in both forums: a documented conflict check
before any substantive conversation, a defined written scope, and a records
request tailored to the claims, so the eventual opinion rests on a record
either side of the caption can examine. When the assignment is to evaluate
another expert's disclosed opinion rather than to issue one, that work runs
through the firm's [rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/)
service on the same terms. The statewide practice is described on the
[New Mexico page](/locations/new-mexico/).

## Served by appointment from Kansas City

Purinton Analytics maintains no office in Deming and publishes no street
address for this location. The firm's only staffed office is its home
office in Kansas City, and it says so plainly because accuracy about
presence belongs to the same discipline that governs its reports. A Deming
engagement runs mostly remotely: intake, records review, vocational
interviews by secure video where appropriate, conferences with counsel, and
report drafting, all sequenced in the engagement letter so the disclosure
date is protected. When an in-person evaluation is warranted or a Luna County
deposition or trial requires attendance, travel is scheduled from the Kansas
City home office and locked to the dates the court and counsel set.

## Labor market foundations for Deming opinions

Vocational findings for evaluees in the Deming area are grounded in Bureau
of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the
relevant area and for New Mexico statewide, in state workforce agency labor
market information, and in direct labor market research where the referral
questions warrant it. The opinion addresses the work realistically available
to the evaluee from their own community, describes scale and opportunity in
qualitative terms the data will support, and cites every source so the
analysis can be retraced. Life care plans and any supporting
[medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/) carry the same
disclosed sourcing.

## Getting a Deming case started

Luna County matters enter through the same national intake as the rest of
the practice, described on the [nationwide services page](/locations/nationwide/).
Any venue-specific registration or qualification requirement is identified
and confirmed during the conflict check and intake process. To begin,
submit the caption or party names through the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and identify the disclosure,
deposition, or trial dates the engagement must meet, since those dates set
the schedule from the first day.
