Before an expert is the right fit for a Peralta case, the referral question has to be named: is the dispute about what the claimant can still earn, what their future care will require, or both? The answer decides whether counsel needs a [vocational expert witness](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) opinion, a [life care plan](/services/life-care-planning/), or a combined engagement in which the workstreams inform each other. Purinton Analytics starts Peralta retentions by defining that question in writing, then answers it with the same disciplined methodology whether the engagement letter comes from plaintiff or defense counsel.

## Peralta's place in the court system

Peralta sits in Valencia County, and state-court matters arising in the town are heard in the District Court serving Valencia County, where the firm accepts work from both sides of the caption. For litigation in the federal system, the United States District Court for the District of New Mexico is the forum available within the state; the venue that governs any particular case, together with whatever registration or qualification requirement attaches to it, is nailed down during the conflict check and intake process. The firm's reach across New Mexico's other venues is presented on the [New Mexico locations page](/locations/new-mexico/).

## Grounding the market analysis

A Peralta evaluee's employment prospects are evaluated against the region the evaluee can actually work in, which may include Valencia County employers as well as the broader Albuquerque metropolitan market, with the reach of any individual evaluee established by evidence rather than assumption. The firm's vocational findings rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Albuquerque metropolitan area and for New Mexico statewide, supported by state workforce agency labor market information and by direct labor market research where the referral questions require it. Counsel can review the regional treatment on the [Albuquerque metro page](/locations/new-mexico/albuquerque/). When the matter also demands present-value economics, [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) assign that work to independent economist partners, who perform and sign the economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning pieces move under one engagement structure.

## Serving a Peralta evaluee by appointment

The firm holds no office in Peralta and advertises none; its staffed location is the Kansas City home office, and it says so because precision about small facts is how an expert earns trust on large ones. Documentary work, including records review, transferable skills analysis, and [medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/), proceeds from Kansas City, and video interviews are used where they suit the referral questions. In-person evaluations, when indicated, are conducted in the evaluee's own community, and travel for evaluations, depositions, and trial is planned from Kansas City around the dates the court has set. Counsel expecting a fight over expert opinions may add [rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) of the opposing disclosures to the same engagement.
