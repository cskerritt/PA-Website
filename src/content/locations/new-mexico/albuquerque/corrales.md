## Venue for Corrales disputes

Corrales lies in Sandoval County, and state-court matters arising in the village proceed in the District Court serving that county; the firm accepts engagements there for plaintiffs and defendants alike. In the federal system, the United States District Court for the District of New Mexico is the forum available for federal filings in the state, and the question of which court controls a given case, along with any venue-specific registration or qualification requirement, is resolved during the conflict check and intake process. Corrales is a small residential community whose working residents largely earn their livings in the surrounding metro, so a credible earning-capacity opinion must look past the village boundary. Vocational findings are grounded in Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Albuquerque metropolitan area and for New Mexico statewide, in state workforce agency labor market information, and in direct labor market research when the referral questions require employer-level detail. ## What the evaluee experiences

There is no Purinton Analytics office in Corrales and no local address to publish; the firm states its footprint plainly because overstated presence would sit poorly beside carefully stated opinions. For the claimant, the process is deliberately light: records review, transferable skills analysis, and [medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/) happen away from the evaluee entirely, at the Kansas City home office; a video interview often suffices; and when an in-person evaluation is warranted, it is scheduled in the village or wherever the evaluee can comfortably attend. Travel for depositions and trial is planned from Kansas City around the dates counsel must protect, and [expert testimony and litigation consulting](/services/expert-testimony-litigation-consulting/) can be scoped from the outset when a contested hearing is likely.
