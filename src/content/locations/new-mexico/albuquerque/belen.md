## Belen's county and its courts

Belen sits in Valencia County, and state-court litigation arising there proceeds in the District Court serving Valencia County, where the firm takes engagements from either side of the caption. For cases in the federal system, the United States District Court for the District of New Mexico is the available federal forum in the state; which venue governs the matter at hand, and any registration or qualification requirement attached to it, is established during the conflict check and intake process rather than assumed. Counsel handling New Mexico matters beyond this county will find the statewide picture on the [New Mexico locations page](/locations/new-mexico/).

## The labor market a Belen evaluee actually faces

Belen is a small city within a metro-scale labor market, and vocational conclusions must be honest about both parts of that description: local opportunities exist, and so does the wider Albuquerque metropolitan market, though whether a particular evaluee can realistically work in it is a question of evidence about that evaluee, never an assumption drawn from the map. Findings rely on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Albuquerque metropolitan area and for New Mexico statewide, on state workforce agency labor market information, and on direct employer research when the referral questions demand it; the metro-level analysis is described on the [Albuquerque metro page](/locations/new-mexico/albuquerque/). When lost earning capacity or future care costs must become a damages figure, [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) bring in independent economist partners who perform and sign the economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning workstreams stay within a single coordinated structure.

## Scheduling the evaluation around the case

The firm has no Belen office and claims none; every location outside the Kansas City home office is served by appointment, and saying so is part of the practice's credibility. Records analysis, transferable skills work, and [medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/) proceed from Kansas City on a timeline reverse-engineered from the report deadline. Interviews are conducted by video where suitable, and in-person evaluations, when the referral requires them, are held in the evaluee's own community and booked early enough that travel never crowds the disclosure date. Deposition and trial appearances are calendared from Kansas City at intake, and counsel can fold [expert testimony and litigation consulting](/services/expert-testimony-litigation-consulting/) into the engagement when examination of the opinions is expected.
