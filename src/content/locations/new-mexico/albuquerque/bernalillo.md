## Courthouse and county for Bernalillo matters

On the state side, cases arising in Bernalillo proceed in the District Court serving Sandoval County, and the firm accepts appointments for that court from either side of the caption. Federally, the United States District Court for the District of New Mexico is the forum available for matters filed in federal court within the state; the venue that actually governs a particular dispute, and any registration or qualification requirement that flows from it, is pinned down during the conflict check and intake process. ## A small town in a large commuting market

Bernalillo is a small community, but its residents work in a much larger market, and vocational conclusions have to reflect that gap between where an evaluee lives and where the jobs are. Findings rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Albuquerque metropolitan area and for New Mexico statewide, augmented by state workforce agency labor market information and, where the referral questions justify it, direct research with employers. Context for the surrounding market appears on the [Albuquerque metro page](/locations/new-mexico/albuquerque/). When the referral extends to economic loss, the firm structures [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) so that independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis while vocational and care-planning work continues under one engagement structure.

The firm keeps no storefront in Bernalillo and lists no street address for it, because a practice built on accurate reporting does not overstate its own footprint.
