An evaluee who lives in a small town like Westville should not have to
organize their claim around an expert's office location. Purinton
Analytics structures its work so the evaluation comes to the evaluee:
records are reviewed wherever they are produced, vocational interviews are
conducted by video when the questions allow it, and in-person assessment,
when the referral requires one, is arranged in the evaluee's own
community. Attorneys on both sides of the caption retain the firm on that
basis, and the resulting reports are written to be read by whichever
lawyer did not send the referral.

## Where Westville cases are heard

Westville sits in LaPorte County, and civil matters arising there are
generally venued in the Circuit and Superior Courts that serve the county.
Cases with a federal hook can proceed in either of the state's federal
forums, the United States District Court for the Northern District of
Indiana or the United States District Court for the Southern District of
Indiana, with the controlling venue confirmed during the conflict check
and intake process rather than presumed from geography. Whatever the
forum, the deliverable is the same: a signed report that states its
methodology, cites its sources, and separates what the record shows from
what it does not. Broader venue context for the surrounding area is
collected on the [Michigan City and La Porte metro
page](/locations/indiana/michigan-city/) and on the [Indiana state
page](/locations/indiana/).

## A small town inside a regional labor market

A Westville evaluee's earning capacity is not defined by Westville alone.
Residents of smaller communities routinely work across the surrounding
county and toward the principal city of Michigan City, so the vocational
analysis considers the commuting market as a whole. Findings rest on
Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for
the Michigan City and La Porte metropolitan area and for Indiana
statewide, together with state workforce agency labor market information
and, where warranted, direct contact with employers through labor market
research. The opinion describes qualitatively which occupations remain
open to the evaluee, how those occupations are represented in the regional
economy, and what the record supports about placeability, without resort
to unsupported local color.

## How an evaluation proceeds here

The firm keeps no office in Westville and lists no local address anywhere,
because claiming a presence it does not have would undercut the accuracy
it promises in its reports. What counsel gets instead is a defined
process. After the documented conflict check, intake fixes the scope and
the records request. [Vocational expert
witness](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) and [life care
planning](/services/life-care-planning/) work then proceed remote-first,
with any in-person component scheduled by appointment and travel arranged
from the Kansas City home office. Where the damages picture calls for
economic analysis, [coordinated damages
engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) are available, in
which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work continues under one
engagement structure. Counsel with dockets beyond Indiana can review the
[nationwide practice](/locations/nationwide/) for how the same model
serves other venues.
