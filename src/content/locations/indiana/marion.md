Purinton Analytics is retained in Grant County by attorneys on both sides
of the caption, and that two-sided practice shapes everything about how
Marion engagements are run. A report written to persuade only the
retaining party fails when the other side reads it; a report written to
withstand the other side serves whoever retained it. On that footing the
firm provides Marion counsel with
[vocational evaluation and expert testimony](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)

## Venue coverage for Marion matters

The Marion micropolitan area rests on Grant County. Engagements are
accepted for matters in the Grant County Circuit and Superior Courts and
in both federal forums serving the state, the United States District Court
for the Northern District of Indiana and the United States District Court
for the Southern District of Indiana. Whether a claim proceeds in the
county courthouse or in federal court, the report discloses its
methodology, cites its sources, and confines its conclusions to the
record. The [Indiana locations page](/locations/indiana/) sets out the
firm's coverage across the rest of the state.

## Presence in Marion, stated exactly

Meetings in Marion are by appointment. The firm keeps no staffed office
here and publishes no street address for this location; a practice that
holds evaluees' work histories to documentary proof should hold itself to
the same standard about offices. Counsel instead get a remote-first
engagement built for speed: electronic records intake, video conferences,
pre-deposition preparation by phone, and remote evaluation where the
referral questions and testing protocols permit. When in-person work is
required, whether an evaluation in the evaluee's community or testimony at
trial, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office against the
controlling case dates.

## The data behind Grant County opinions

Vocational findings for Grant County evaluees rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the area and
for Indiana statewide, state workforce agency labor market information,
and direct labor market research where the referral requires it. Every
employability and earning capacity opinion identifies the labor market it
treats as commutable for the evaluee and explains that choice, because an
opinion whose foundation cannot be examined is not evidence, it is
assertion, and the firm does not traffic in assertion. The same sourcing
discipline extends to the life care planning side of an engagement, where
each recommended item of care is tied to the medical record and to the
provider opinions that support it.

Begin with the conflict check, not a consultation. Submit the caption or
party names through the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and flag any
imminent disclosure, deposition, or trial dates so scheduling is resolved
at intake. Once conflicts clear, counsel receive a records checklist
tailored to the claims and a committed timeline. Marion referrals draw on
the same office and standards as the firm's
[national practice](/locations/nationwide/), applied here with the same
