## Venue context in St. Joseph County

Mishawaka lies in St. Joseph County, and state-law claims arising there
proceed in Indiana's Circuit and Superior Courts. Matters that belong in
federal court are heard in the United States District Court for the
Northern District of Indiana or the United States District Court for the
Southern District of Indiana; the controlling forum for a particular
caption, along with any venue-specific qualification or registration
requirement, is identified and confirmed during the conflict check and
intake process rather than assumed in advance. Opinions are prepared to
the same disclosure standard in either court system, so a report drafted
for a Circuit or Superior Court matter does not need rebuilding if the
case is removed. Coverage of the state's remaining venues is described on
the [Indiana locations page](/locations/indiana/).

## Earning capacity in a market that crosses a state line

The commuting economy around Mishawaka extends beyond the city and, as
the market's designation reflects, beyond Indiana itself into Michigan.
An earning capacity opinion for a Mishawaka evaluee therefore has to
define the labor market honestly: broad enough to capture the work the
evaluee can actually reach, narrow enough that no occupation is counted
twice across the state line. The firm grounds that definition in Bureau
of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the
metropolitan area and for Indiana statewide, in state workforce agency
labor market information, and in direct employer research where the
referral questions warrant it. Transferable skills analysis connects the
evaluee's documented work history to occupations that exist in this
market rather than in a national abstraction. When lost earnings must be
reduced to present value, the firm offers
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.
