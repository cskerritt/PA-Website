an Osceola case reaches for a vocational expert. Once a scheduling order
fixes disclosure and deposition dates, the time available for records
collection, evaluation, and a written opinion is already set, and a

scope, and records request move in a defined sequence, so counsel knows
early whether the calendar supports a full
[vocational assessment](/services/vocational-expert-witness/), a

## Where Osceola disputes are heard

Osceola sits in St. Joseph County. State-law claims proceed in Indiana's
Circuit and Superior Courts, while federal matters arising in the state
are heard in the United States District Court for the Northern District
of Indiana or the United States District Court for the Southern District
of Indiana. Which forum controls a given case, and any qualification or
registration requirement that venue imposes, is identified and confirmed
during the conflict check and intake process; nothing about venue is
presumed in advance of that review. How the firm covers Indiana's other
courts is set out on the [statewide page](/locations/indiana/).

## A small town read against its regional economy

Osceola is a small community, and the labor market question for a small
community is where the honest boundary of opportunity lies. Treating the
town limits as the whole market understates what an evaluee can reach;
substituting national averages invents opportunity that is not there.
The defensible middle is the regional commuting economy anchored by
South Bend, and the firm documents it qualitatively and specifically:
Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates
for the metropolitan area and for Indiana statewide, state workforce
agency labor market information, and direct contact with employers when
the referral questions call for it. Transferable skills analysis then
ties the evaluee's actual training and work history to occupations that
exist within reach of Osceola. When the matter also requires economic
loss calculations, the firm offers
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Evaluating an Osceola evaluee where they live

No staffed office exists in Osceola, and the firm publishes no street
address here; saying so plainly is part of the discipline counsel is
retaining. Most of the work never requires a meeting room: records
review, structured interviewing, and standardized testing under
publisher-supported remote protocols are all conducted at a distance.
When an in-person evaluation is warranted, the firm comes to the
evaluee's own community rather than asking an injured person to travel,
and appearances for deposition or trial are scheduled from the Kansas
City home office. The same by-appointment approach extends across the
surrounding [South Bend metro market](/locations/indiana/south-bend/)
and to any venue in the country through the
[nationwide practice](/locations/nationwide/).
