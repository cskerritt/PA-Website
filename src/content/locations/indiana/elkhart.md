Elkhart County is the whole of the Elkhart-Goshen metropolitan area, which
gives litigation here a compact quality: the parties, the evaluee's labor
market, and the courthouse usually share the same county. Purinton
Analytics serves that concentrated venue the same way it serves the largest
metros in its [national practice](/locations/nationwide/), providing
plaintiff and defense counsel with
[vocational expert witness](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) opinions
on employability and earning capacity,
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) and
[medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/) in serious
injury matters, and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Elkhart County venues

The firm accepts matters venued in the Circuit and Superior Courts of
Elkhart County, whether the case is captioned in Elkhart or Goshen. Federal
matters proceed in the United States District Court for the Northern
District of Indiana, with the United States District Court for the Southern
District of Indiana available when a related matter is venued elsewhere in
the state. Any venue-specific registration or qualification requirement is
identified and confirmed during the conflict check and intake process, and
every report is written to a standard suitable for examination by opposing
counsel in any of these forums.

## No storefront, and no pretense of one

The firm does not maintain a staffed office in Elkhart or Goshen and
publishes no street address for this location. That candor is intentional:
a practice whose opinions turn on verifiable sourcing should describe its
own presence with the same accuracy. Meetings and evaluations are by
appointment. Most engagements run remotely from intake forward, through
records review, structured interviews, and standardized testing under
publisher protocols where remote administration is supported. When
in-person evaluation is warranted, or when deposition and trial dates
require attendance in northern Indiana, travel is scheduled from the Kansas
City home office.

## Reading the Elkhart-Goshen labor market

Vocational findings for Elkhart County evaluees are grounded in Bureau of
Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the
Elkhart-Goshen metropolitan area and for Indiana statewide, together with
state workforce agency labor market information and direct labor market
research where the referral questions warrant it. Because the analysis is
tied to the evaluee's actual commuting range, conclusions about
employability and earning capacity reflect the occupations and wage
structure of this specific regional market rather than borrowed national
figures. Life care plans and cost projections follow the same principle,
priced through provider-specific inquiries and published fee data for the
evaluee's own community.

## Submitting a case for conflict check

An Elkhart engagement starts the way every engagement in the practice
starts: with a documented conflict check, a defined scope, and a records
request tailored to the claims, so the written opinion rests on a record
both sides can examine. Submit the caption or party names through the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and note any imminent disclosure,
deposition, or trial dates. The Elkhart-Goshen practice operates within the
firm's statewide [Indiana coverage](/locations/indiana/), and referrals are
welcome from either side of the caption.
