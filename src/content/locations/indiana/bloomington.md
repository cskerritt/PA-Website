A Bloomington engagement with Purinton Analytics follows a defined arc: a
documented conflict check, a scoped retention, a records request tailored
to the claims, an evaluation, and a written opinion that both sides of the
caption can examine, carried through deposition and trial if the case goes
that far. Along that arc, counsel in Monroe County and Owen County draw on the
firm's [vocational expert witness](/services/vocational-expert-witness/)
work on employability and earning capacity,
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) in catastrophic injury
matters, [medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/), and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. Plaintiff and defense referrals are handled to the
same standard.

## Courts serving Monroe and Owen counties

The Bloomington metropolitan area takes in Monroe and Owen counties, and
the firm accepts matters venued in the Circuit and Superior Courts of each.
Federal matters proceed in the United States District Court for the
Southern District of Indiana, with the United States District Court for the
Northern District of Indiana available when a related case is venued
elsewhere in the state. Any venue-specific registration or qualification
requirement is identified and confirmed during the conflict check and
intake process, before the scope is fixed and the work begins.

## Scheduling and travel

Bloomington meetings are by appointment. The firm keeps no staffed office
here and publishes no street address for this location, because describing
its presence accurately is part of the same discipline it applies to its
opinions. Most of the engagement arc runs remotely: records analysis,
structured interviews, and standardized testing under publisher protocols
where remote administration is supported. When the referral questions
require an in-person evaluation, or when deposition and trial dates require
attendance in southern Indiana, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City
home office and coordinated with counsel's dates, so logistics never drive
the substance of the work.

## Labor market evidence for Bloomington evaluees

Vocational findings for evaluees in the Bloomington area are grounded in
Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for
the Bloomington metropolitan area and for Indiana statewide, together with
state workforce agency labor market information and direct labor market
research where the questions warrant it. The analysis stays within the
evaluee's realistic commuting range, so conclusions about employability
reflect the occupations actually present in this regional market. Life care
plans and cost projections are priced to the evaluee's own community
through provider-specific inquiries and published fee data, keeping
projected care costs tied to the market where care will be delivered.
