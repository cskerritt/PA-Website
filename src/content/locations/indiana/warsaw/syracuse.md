## County and courthouse context

Syracuse sits in Kosciusko County, and civil matters arising there are heard in the county's Circuit and Superior Courts. Engagements are accepted for cases in those courts and in Indiana's federal forums, the United States District Court for the Northern District of Indiana and the United States District Court for the Southern District of Indiana, with the controlling venue for any particular matter confirmed during the conflict check and intake. Once venue is fixed, report format, disclosure timing, and deposition logistics follow that court's requirements, and testimony is prepared with the understanding that the opinion must hold up under cross-examination in that room.

## Reading the labor market from a small town

A Syracuse claimant's employment prospects are not confined to the town itself. The relevant market is the surrounding Warsaw, Indiana metropolitan area, whose principal city is Warsaw, and the analysis asks what work exists across that commuting market for a person with the evaluee's specific skills, restrictions, and history. The evidentiary base is the Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the applicable area and for Indiana as a whole, supplemented by state workforce agency labor market information and by direct employer research when a placement question turns on it. Findings are stated qualitatively and sourced, so that both sides can trace every conclusion to its data. When damages require an economic projection, the firm offers [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under the same engagement structure.

## How a Syracuse evaluation actually runs

Most work begins remotely. Medical, employment, and educational records are collected under a defined scope, the structured interview and standardized testing proceed by secure videoconference, and counsel sees drafts against the disclosure calendar rather than after it. Where the referral questions require observation that a screen cannot support, the in-person portion is conducted where the evaluee lives, with travel arranged from the firm's Kansas City home office. Deposition and trial appearances are scheduled the same way, against the dates the assigned court has actually set. There is no staffed Syracuse office and no published local address; appointments, not storefronts, are how the town is served, and saying so plainly is part of the firm's credibility.
