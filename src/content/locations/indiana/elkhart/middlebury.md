An injured person in Middlebury should not have to leave their community to
be properly evaluated, and with this practice they do not. Middlebury is a
small town in the Elkhart-Goshen, IN metropolitan area, and Purinton
Analytics structures its
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) and
[vocational expert witness](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) engagements
so that the evaluation comes to the evaluee: records move electronically,
interviews run over secure video, and any in-person assessment is held where
the person actually lives. Referrals are welcome from plaintiff and defense
counsel, and the resulting reports are written to withstand the scrutiny of
whichever side did not send them.

## Where a Middlebury opinion will be tested

The venue context for a Middlebury matter starts with Elkhart County, where
engagements are accepted in the Circuit and Superior Courts. Indiana's
federal forums are the United States District Court for the Northern
District of Indiana and the United States District Court for the Southern
District of Indiana, and the controlling district for any particular case,
along with any venue-specific registration or qualification requirement, is
pinned down during the conflict check and intake. Because the opinion may be
examined in any of these forums, the working file is assembled to a single
standard: a documented conflict check before engagement, a scope letter
before analysis, and records requested to match the claims. Broader regional
coverage is described on the
[Elkhart metro page](/locations/indiana/elkhart/), and statewide practice on
the [Indiana state page](/locations/indiana/).

## A small town measured against a metro market

Earning capacity for a Middlebury evaluee is not a function of what exists
inside the town limits; it is a function of the occupations reachable within
the surrounding Elkhart-Goshen labor market and consistent with the
evaluee's restrictions and skills. The firm grounds that inquiry in Bureau
of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the
Elkhart-Goshen metropolitan area and for Indiana statewide, in state
workforce agency labor market information, and in targeted employer research
when the referral requires it, and it presents the findings qualitatively,
with sources disclosed. Where a case calls for the damages to be reduced to
economic terms, [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
are available: independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis, and the firm coordinates it alongside the vocational and
care-planning work within a single engagement.

## Appointments, travel, and candor about presence

Meetings for Middlebury matters are by appointment. The firm operates from
its Kansas City home office, keeps no local office in Middlebury, and lists
no street address for this location, because a practice built on accurate
reporting has no business implying offices it does not have. Travel for
depositions, trial testimony, or in-person evaluations is scheduled from
Kansas City as the case requires, and the remote-first early phases keep
costs proportionate for counsel weighing whether a small-town case supports
expert work at all. The same engagement structure serves matters far beyond
this region, as the [nationwide practice](/locations/nationwide/) page
explains.

Counsel who retain before the records are complete get an expert who can

someone else's file. To begin,
[request a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) with the caption and parties,
