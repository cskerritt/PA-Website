## Measuring a small-city labor market honestly

The temptation in small-city cases is to treat the local market as either
empty or unlimited, depending on which side is writing. The firm does
neither. Vocational findings for Nappanee evaluees rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Elkhart-Goshen
metropolitan area and for Indiana statewide, on state workforce agency labor
market information, and on direct labor market research where the referral
questions call for it, with each source cited so opposing counsel can trace
every finding. When the case also needs present-value economics, the firm
offers [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which the economic analysis is performed and signed by independent
economist partners while the vocational and care-planning work continues
under the same engagement structure, coordinated by the firm.

There is no Purinton Analytics storefront in Nappanee, and no street address

because a firm that overstates its offices invites doubt about its opinions.
What counsel gets instead is a remote-first process: secure video interviews
and electronic records exchange for the early phases, an in-person
evaluation conducted in the evaluee's own community when the referral
questions require one, and travel from the Kansas City home office when
