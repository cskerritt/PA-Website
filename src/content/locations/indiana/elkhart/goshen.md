## How a Goshen evaluation proceeds

Most Goshen engagements open remotely, with records review and a structured
interview conducted by secure video, which keeps early costs proportionate to
the questions asked. When the referral calls for an in-person assessment, it
is scheduled in the evaluee's own community, and when a deposition or trial
date requires presence in the county, travel is arranged from the Kansas
City home office. The firm maintains no staffed office in Goshen and
publishes no street address here; meetings are by appointment, and saying so
plainly reflects the same accuracy the reports themselves are held to.
