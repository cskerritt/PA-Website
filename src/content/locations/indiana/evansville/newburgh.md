Purinton Analytics organizes every Newburgh engagement around the litigation calendar counsel is actually facing: the scoping call establishes the disclosure date, the deposition window, and the trial setting before the records request goes out, and the work plan runs backward from those dates. ## Timing the work to the case schedule

A well-timed engagement leaves room for the record to be complete: treating-provider records take time to gather, an evaluee interview must be scheduled, and a report that will support deposition testimony cannot be rushed through its final review. When counsel raises an imminent deadline during intake, the firm says plainly whether the timeline is workable rather than accepting an engagement it cannot serve properly. Supplemental disclosures and rebuttal assignments follow the same discipline, with scope defined in writing before analysis begins.
