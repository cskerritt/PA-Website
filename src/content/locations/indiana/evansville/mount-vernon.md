An injured worker or catastrophically hurt claimant in Mount Vernon should not have to travel far, or wait long, to be properly evaluated. Purinton Analytics structures its Mount Vernon engagements around the evaluee: records are gathered and reviewed remotely, interviews are scheduled at times that respect treatment calendars, and when an in-person vocational evaluation is warranted it takes place in the evaluee's own community. Plaintiff and defense referrals receive identical treatment, because the evaluation protocol does not change with the side of the caption that sends the retainer.

## How a Mount Vernon evaluation actually runs

An engagement begins with a defined scope and a records request tailored to the claims: medical records, employment and earnings history, education and training documentation, and any prior vocational or independent medical evaluations. The evaluee interview covers work history, transferable skills, physical and cognitive capacities as documented by treating providers, and vocational goals. Testing, where indicated, is selected for the referral questions rather than administered as a fixed battery. The resulting report states its methodology, its sources, and its limits, so that a reader preparing cross-examination can trace every conclusion back to the record.

## Reading the labor market from Posey County

Employment opportunity for a Mount Vernon evaluee is assessed against the regional market centered on Evansville as well as conditions in Posey County itself. The analysis draws on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates covering the Evansville metropolitan area and Indiana statewide, supplemented by state workforce agency labor market information and, where the case justifies it, direct employer contact and labor market survey work. No wage or employment figure enters an opinion without a citation a defense expert or plaintiff's expert could independently verify.

## Coordinated damages and the economist question

When lost earning capacity must be converted into an economic damages figure, the firm offers [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement structure. The vocational opinion and the economic opinion remain separately authored and separately defensible, which is how each survives scrutiny.
