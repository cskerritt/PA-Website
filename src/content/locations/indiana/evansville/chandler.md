Chandler is a small town whose working residents largely earn their livings across the wider Evansville region, and that commuting reality is where any credible vocational opinion about a Chandler evaluee has to start. A person's post-injury employability is defined by the jobs actually reachable from where they live, not by the boundaries of the town itself. Purinton Analytics builds its Chandler engagements on that regional frame, offering vocational expert and life care planning services to attorneys on the plaintiff side and the defense side under the same methodology.

## A commuter town's labor market, documented properly

Because Chandler workers move through the Evansville metropolitan labor market, opinions about employability, placeability, and residual earning capacity are grounded in Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Evansville metropolitan area and for Indiana statewide, together with state workforce agency labor market information. Where a referral question turns on whether specific work actually exists within a reasonable commute of Chandler, direct labor market research and employer contact supplement the published data. ## What a Chandler referral looks like in practice

Most of the work happens remotely: records analysis, transferable skills assessment, vocational testing review, and report drafting do not require anyone to sit in the same room. When the referral questions call for an in-person evaluation, it is scheduled in the evaluee's own community, which keeps the process accessible for a Chandler resident and keeps observed function connected to the environment where the person actually lives and works. The firm maintains no office in Chandler and does not pretend otherwise; when depositions or trial dates require presence, travel is arranged from the Kansas City home office around the deadlines counsel supplies.

## Life care planning and economic coordination

In catastrophic cases, the life care plan documents projected care needs from the medical record and treating-provider input, itemized so both sides can test each element. Where the damages model also requires economic analysis, counsel may elect [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement structure. The firm coordinates; the economists opine; the report says so.
