The Indianapolis-Carmel-Greenwood metropolitan area presents counsel with an
unusually broad venue footprint, with Marion County at its center and a ring
of surrounding counties that each maintain their own trial courts. Purinton
Analytics serves plaintiff and defense counsel across that entire footprint
with [vocational expert witness](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) work
on employability and earning capacity,
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) in catastrophic injury
matters, [medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/), and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. The metro practice operates within the firm's wider
[Indiana coverage](/locations/indiana/).

## Venue footprint across central Indiana

Engagements are accepted for matters in the Circuit and Superior Courts of
Marion County and of the surrounding counties that make up the metropolitan
area, including Boone County as well as Brown, Hamilton, Hancock, Hendricks, Johnson,
Madison, Morgan, Shelby, and Tipton counties. In federal court, the firm
appears in the United States District Court for the Southern District of
Indiana and, where a matter is venued there, the United States District
Court for the Northern District of Indiana. The report standard does not
change with the courthouse: every opinion rests on a documented record,
disclosed methodology, and sources that opposing counsel can check.

## Meetings by appointment

Indianapolis meetings are by appointment. The firm keeps no staffed office
in the metro and publishes no street address for this location, because
accuracy about presence is part of the same discipline that governs its
reports. Most engagements proceed remotely from intake onward: records
review, structured interviews, and standardized testing under publisher
protocols where remote administration is supported. When the referral
questions call for in-person evaluation, or when deposition and trial
calendars require attendance, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home
office and coordinated around counsel's dates. Counsel receive the same
report format and the same working relationship whether the work is
performed remotely or on site.

## Labor market research for Indianapolis evaluees

Vocational findings for evaluees in the metro rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Indianapolis
metropolitan area and for Indiana statewide, supplemented by state workforce
agency labor market information and direct employer research where the
referral questions warrant it. Because the metro functions as a major
regional employment center, transferable skills analysis and labor market
surveys are framed to the occupations and industries actually present in
the evaluee's commuting range rather than to national generalities. Life
care plans and medical cost projections are priced to the evaluee's own
market using provider-specific inquiries and published fee data, so the
resulting figures reflect central Indiana rather than a national average.

## Starting an Indianapolis engagement

Every engagement begins the same way regardless of which side of the
caption makes the referral: a documented conflict check, a defined scope,
and a records request tailored to the claims. Submit the caption or party
names through the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and note any
imminent disclosure, deposition, or trial dates so scheduling can be
confirmed at the outset. Counsel with matters beyond central Indiana can
review the firm's [national coverage](/locations/nationwide/) or reach out
about any venue where a vocational or life care planning question needs a
defensible answer.
