## Earning Capacity in and Beyond a Small Town

Spencer is a small town, and an honest earning capacity analysis says what that means for the evaluee in front of it. A worker who lives in Spencer may find suitable employment in town, in the principal city, or anywhere between, so the opinion defines the commuting area it relies on and defends that definition openly rather than leaving the market assumption implied. Vocational findings rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Bloomington, IN metropolitan area and for Indiana statewide, on state workforce agency labor market information, and on direct labor market research where the referral questions warrant it. The transferable skills analysis is recorded step by step, and the wage-loss foundation is laid out in the file so that a cross-examiner on either side can move from record to conclusion without guessing at the path.
