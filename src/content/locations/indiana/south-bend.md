The South Bend-Mishawaka metropolitan area crosses a state line, pairing
St. Joseph County in Indiana with Cass County, Michigan, and litigation in
the region reflects that geography: an evaluee may live on one side of the
line and work, treat, or litigate on the other. Purinton Analytics is built
for exactly that situation. As a remote-first national practice serving
plaintiff and defense counsel on equal terms, the firm provides
[vocational expert witness](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) services,
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/),
[medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/), and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure, without regard to which side of the line the caption
sits on.

## A venue footprint that crosses the state line

On the Indiana side, the firm accepts matters venued in the Circuit and
Superior Courts of St. Joseph County, and in federal court in the United
States District Court for the Northern District of Indiana, with the United
States District Court for the Southern District of Indiana available for
matters venued elsewhere in the state. Matters arising in Cass County,
Michigan are accepted under the same national engagement standards, with
any Michigan-specific qualification or registration requirement identified
and confirmed during the conflict check and intake process. The written
opinion follows the same standard in every forum.

## By-appointment presence, honestly described

The firm maintains no staffed office in South Bend or Mishawaka and
publishes no street address for this location. Meetings are by appointment,
and most engagements are handled remotely: records review, structured
interviews, and standardized testing under publisher protocols where remote
administration is supported. When the referral calls for in-person
evaluation, or when depositions and trial dates require presence in the
region, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office. Counsel work
with the same evaluator and receive the same report format from intake
through testimony, wherever the work is physically performed.

## Labor market data on both sides of the line

Because the metro spans Indiana and Michigan, vocational findings are grounded in
Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for
the South Bend-Mishawaka metropolitan area together with the statewide
estimates for Indiana and, where the evaluee's market requires it,
Michigan. State workforce agency labor market information and direct
employer research supplement the published estimates when the referral
questions warrant it. Life care plans and medical cost projections are
priced to the evaluee's own community through provider-specific inquiries
and published fee data, since the cost of care is a local fact rather than
a statewide one.

## Beginning an engagement

Whether the matter is venued in St. Joseph County, in Cass County,
Michigan, or in federal court, the engagement begins with a documented
conflict check, a defined scope, and a records request tailored to the
claims. Submit the caption or party names through the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and note any imminent disclosure,
deposition, or trial dates. The South Bend practice sits within the firm's
[Indiana coverage](/locations/indiana/) and its
[national practice](/locations/nationwide/), which is how cross-border
matters stay under a consistent standard.
