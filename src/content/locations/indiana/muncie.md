Delaware County gives the Muncie metropolitan area its footprint, and that
compact footprint gives counsel a clearly defined venue for the employment,
injury, and disability disputes in which vocational and care-planning
evidence matters. Purinton Analytics serves plaintiff and defense attorneys
in Muncie with [vocational evaluation and expert testimony](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. Each engagement begins with a documented conflict
check and a defined scope, so the written opinion rests on a record either
side can examine.

## Venue footprint

Engagements are accepted for matters in the Delaware County Circuit and
Superior Courts and in the federal courts that serve Indiana, the United
States District Court for the Northern District of Indiana and the United
States District Court for the Southern District of Indiana. Because the
firm's reports follow the same structure regardless of forum, an opinion
prepared for a state court matter in Muncie holds to the same disclosure
and documentation standards as an opinion prepared for federal litigation.
Counsel with matters elsewhere in the state will find the broader venue
picture on the [Indiana locations page](/locations/indiana/).

## Meetings and evaluations by appointment

Muncie work is handled by appointment. The firm maintains no staffed office
in Muncie and publishes no street address for this location, because
accuracy about presence is part of the same discipline that governs its
reports. Most referrals proceed remotely from intake through report
delivery: records review, counsel conferences, and, where the referral
questions and testing protocols permit, remote evaluation of the evaluee.
When an in-person evaluation is warranted, or when deposition and trial
dates require attendance, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home
office and planned against the operative litigation calendar rather than
left open-ended. Counsel are told at intake which parts of the engagement
will proceed remotely and which require presence, so the logistics never
surprise either the retaining firm or the court.

## Labor market research for Muncie matters

Employability and earning capacity findings for Delaware County evaluees
are grounded in data specific to this market. The analysis draws on Bureau
of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the
Muncie metropolitan area and for Indiana statewide, state workforce agency
labor market information, and direct labor market research where the
referral requires it. The report is explicit about which labor market
defines the evaluee's commutable range and why, so that the foundation for
each finding can be tested in deposition rather than taken on faith.

## Starting a Muncie engagement

The first step is a conflict check, not a consultation. Submit the caption
or party names through the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and note
any imminent disclosure, deposition, or trial dates so scheduling can be
addressed at intake. Once the check clears, counsel receive a records
checklist tailored to the claims and a report timeline set against the
disclosure calendar. Counsel comparing regional and national options can
review the firm's [nationwide coverage](/locations/nationwide/), which
applies the same methodology and reporting standards to every venue it
serves, Muncie included.
