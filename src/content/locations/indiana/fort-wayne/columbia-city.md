A Columbia City case is a Whitley County case before it is anything else.
The caption, the local rules, and the trial setting all come from the
county, even though the economic life of the town is tied to the larger
Fort Wayne region. Purinton Analytics keeps those two facts separate on
purpose: venue governs the procedure, the regional economy governs the
data, and neither is allowed to distort the other. On that footing the
firm serves counsel on either side of Whitley County disputes with
[vocational expert witness services](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) of the
opinions the other side has served.

## The Whitley County courts and Indiana's federal forums

State-court litigation arising in Columbia City proceeds in the Whitley
County Circuit and Superior Courts. In federal court, Indiana's available
forums are the United States District Court for the Northern District of
Indiana and the United States District Court for the Southern District of
Indiana; which of them controls a particular Columbia City matter, and
what registration or qualification requirements that forum places on a
testifying expert, are identified during the conflict check and intake
rather than announced in advance. The firm's engagements across the state
are summarized on the [Indiana locations page](/locations/indiana/).

## Regional data behind a county opinion

An earning capacity opinion for a Columbia City evaluee cannot stop at
the county line, because the market the evaluee competes in does not.
The firm grounds its findings in Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational
employment and wage estimates for the Fort Wayne metropolitan area and
for Indiana statewide, and it layers in state workforce agency labor
market information along with direct labor market research when the
referral questions warrant it. Every conclusion in the report names its
supporting geography, so the move from county venue to regional data is
documented rather than assumed, and either side can retrace the
analysis from the sources cited. The broader regional practice appears on
the [Fort Wayne metro page](/locations/indiana/fort-wayne/).

## Presence, stated accurately

The firm maintains no office in Columbia City and publishes no local
street address, and it says so plainly because overstating presence would
undercut the credibility its reports depend on. Engagements run
remote-first: records analysis, structured interviews, and most
standardized testing are handled at a distance under publisher protocols.
Where an in-person evaluation is warranted, it is scheduled in the
evaluee's own community, and travel for evaluations, depositions, and
trial appearances originates at the Kansas City home office, the hub of
the firm's [nationwide practice](/locations/nationwide/).

## From caption to engagement

Every Whitley County engagement starts the same way: with the caption or
party names run through a conflict check before any merits discussion.
Counsel should [submit the conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and note
imminent disclosure, deposition, or trial dates. Once the check clears,
the firm confirms scope in writing and tailors its records request to the
claims. If the case also needs economic testimony, the firm arranges
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.
