case can turn on whether an opposing vocational or life care opinion
holds up, and testing that opinion is work Purinton Analytics does as
methodically as it builds its own:
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) that examines
the data, the instruments, and the reasoning of the report on the other
side of the caption. The firm also provides affirmative
[vocational expert opinions](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) and
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) for Ossian matters,
on the same terms for plaintiff and defense counsel.

## Forums for a Wells County dispute

Ossian belongs to Wells County, and its state-court matters proceed in
the Wells County Circuit and Superior Courts. At the federal level,
Indiana litigants have the United States District Court for the Northern
District of Indiana and the United States District Court for the Southern
District of Indiana as available forums; which one controls a given
Ossian case, and what expert registration or qualification requirements
it carries, is a question resolved during the conflict check and intake
rather than assumed at the outset. The shape of the firm's practice
across Indiana is described on the
[state locations page](/locations/indiana/).

## The market an Ossian evaluee actually faces

Scrutiny of an opposing opinion often begins with its geography: did the
analysis use the market the evaluee really works in? For Ossian, that
market is the Fort Wayne metropolitan economy, and the firm's own
findings, affirmative and rebuttal alike, rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Fort Wayne
metropolitan area, Indiana statewide estimates, state workforce agency
labor market information, and direct labor market research where the
referral questions justify it. Because the report links each conclusion
to its supporting source, the same test the firm applies to opposing
work can be applied to its own. The regional context is laid out on the
[Fort Wayne metro page](/locations/indiana/fort-wayne/).

No Purinton Analytics office operates in Ossian, and no street address is

## How an Ossian engagement begins

Whether the assignment is an affirmative opinion or a review of the
other side's, it starts with a conflict check on the caption or party
names. [Start the conflict check](/refer-a-case/) and identify any
disclosure, deposition, or trial dates that bear on timing, since
rebuttal work in particular is calendar-sensitive. Once cleared, scope is
confirmed in writing and the records request follows. Cases that need an
economist as well are structured as
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.
