## Getting on the calendar

The engagement begins where the case does, with the caption.
[Request a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) with the party names, and
state the controlling dates up front so the work plan can be built
backward from disclosure. When a referral arrives close to a deadline,
the firm says so at intake and proposes a scope the remaining time can
actually support, rather than promising a report the calendar cannot
deliver. After the check clears, the firm confirms scope
in writing, issues its records request, and schedules the evaluation
against the deadlines counsel identified. When the damages model calls
for economic testimony as well, the firm arranges
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.
