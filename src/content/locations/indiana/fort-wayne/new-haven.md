opposing counsel has already marked it up. That is the standard Purinton
Analytics applies to every engagement it accepts here, for plaintiff and
defense alike: a documented conflict check, a scope confirmed in writing,
and conclusions that trace to cited sources rather than to advocacy. New
Haven counsel retain the firm for
[vocational expert witness work](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/),
[medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/), and

## Allen County venue and Indiana's federal forums

New Haven sits in Allen County, and a state-court matter arising in the
city proceeds in the Allen County Circuit and Superior Courts. Indiana's
federal forums are the United States District Court for the Northern
District of Indiana and the United States District Court for the Southern
District of Indiana. Both are available to Indiana litigants; the
district that controls a particular New Haven case, together with any
registration or qualification requirement that venue imposes on a
testifying expert, is confirmed during the conflict check and intake
rather than assumed from the map. The firm's statewide work is described
on the [Indiana locations page](/locations/indiana/).

## A labor market shared with the principal city

For earning capacity purposes, New Haven is not treated as a market of its
own. Its workers participate in the Fort Wayne metropolitan economy, and
an opinion that ignored that would understate or overstate the
opportunities a New Haven evaluee actually faces. Vocational findings
therefore rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and
wage estimates for the Fort Wayne metropolitan area and for Indiana
statewide, supplemented by state workforce agency labor market information
and by direct labor market research when the referral questions call for
it. Each finding in the written report identifies the geography that
supports it, so the analysis can be checked line by line rather than
taken on trust. The regional practice as a whole is described on the
[Fort Wayne metro page](/locations/indiana/fort-wayne/).

## How the firm appears in New Haven

There is no Purinton Analytics office in New Haven, and the firm publishes
no street address for this location, because accuracy about presence
belongs to the same discipline that governs its reports. Service is
by appointment. Records analysis, structured interviews, and most
standardized testing proceed remotely under publisher protocols; when the
referral questions warrant in-person evaluation, it is conducted in the
evaluee's own community rather than in a distant office. Deposition and
trial appearances are scheduled with travel from the Kansas City home
office, on the same logistics that support the firm's
[national practice](/locations/nationwide/).

## Starting with a conflict check

Engagements open with a conflict check, not a consultation. Counsel on
either side of the caption should
[request a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) with the case caption or party
names and flag any disclosure deadline, deposition setting, or trial date
already on the calendar. After clearance, the firm defines scope in
writing and issues a records request tailored to the claims. Where the
damages model also requires economic testimony, the firm arranges
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.
