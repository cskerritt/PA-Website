## The first step is a conflict check

Merits wait until the conflict check clears. Counsel should send the
caption or party names through the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and mention any dates that are
already fixed, especially expert disclosure deadlines, depositions, and
trial settings. The firm then confirms scope in writing and issues a
records request matched to the claims. Where economic testimony belongs
in the damages model, the firm arranges
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.
