For counsel in the Lafayette-West Lafayette area, the practical question is
often logistical: how does an evaluee in Tippecanoe, Carroll, Benton, or
Warren County get evaluated by a national expert without the case absorbing
unnecessary travel cost? Purinton Analytics answers with a remote-first
model. Records review, structured vocational interviews, and standardized
testing under publisher protocols proceed remotely where remote
administration is supported, and in-person evaluation is scheduled only
when the referral questions genuinely require it. On that foundation the
firm delivers
[vocational expert witness](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) opinions,
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure, for plaintiff and defense counsel alike.

## Venues for Lafayette and West Lafayette matters

The metropolitan area takes in Benton County along with Tippecanoe,
Carroll, and Warren counties, and the firm accepts matters venued in the Circuit and Superior
Courts of each. Federal engagements proceed in the United States District
Court for the Northern District of Indiana, with the United States District
Court for the Southern District of Indiana available for matters venued
elsewhere in the state. Reports are written to be read by opposing counsel:
methodology disclosed, sources cited, and conclusions confined to what the
record supports, whichever courthouse the matter calls home.

## Remote by default, in person when needed

There is no staffed Purinton Analytics office in Lafayette, and the firm
publishes no local street address, because honesty about presence is part
of the discipline counsel are hiring. Meetings are by appointment. When an
in-person evaluation is warranted, the firm can evaluate in the evaluee's
own community, and when deposition or trial attendance is required, travel
is scheduled from the Kansas City home office and coordinated with
counsel's dates. The report standard, the evaluator, and the point of
contact stay constant across remote and on-site work, so nothing about the
logistics shows up in the work product.

## Labor market research for Tippecanoe County evaluees

Earning capacity findings for evaluees in the metro rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the
Lafayette-West Lafayette metropolitan area and for Indiana statewide,
alongside state workforce agency labor market information and direct
employer research where the questions warrant it. The analysis reflects the
occupational mix actually present in the evaluee's commuting range rather
than statewide abstractions. Life care plans and medical cost projections
are priced through provider-specific inquiries and published fee data for
the evaluee's own market, since the cost of future care depends on where
that care will be purchased.
