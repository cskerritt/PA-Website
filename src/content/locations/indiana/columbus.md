Every report Purinton Analytics prepares for a Columbus matter is written
on the assumption that opposing counsel will read it line by line. That
standard, not proximity, is what the firm offers Bartholomew County
attorneys: [vocational evaluation and expert testimony](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. Plaintiff and defense firms alike retain the
practice, and the work product is the same regardless of who sends the
referral: documented methodology, cited sources, and conclusions that stay
inside the record.

## The Columbus venue

The Columbus metropolitan area is anchored on Bartholomew County, and
engagements are accepted for matters in the Bartholomew County Circuit and
Superior Courts. Federal matters proceed in the United States District
Court for the Southern District of Indiana or the United States District
Court for the Northern District of Indiana, depending on where the case is
filed, and the firm's disclosure practices are built to satisfy the more
demanding of the two forums so that no report needs rewriting when a case
is removed. Statewide coverage, including the state's other metro areas, is
described on the [Indiana locations page](/locations/indiana/).

## How engagements run without a local office

Columbus meetings are by appointment. The firm keeps no staffed office
here and publishes no street address for this location; overstating
presence would sit poorly with a practice whose credibility rests on
precision. In practice, most of the engagement is location-independent.
Records arrive electronically, conferences with counsel run by video, and
remote evaluation is available when the referral questions and testing
protocols permit. Where the case requires an in-person evaluation, or where
deposition and trial settings require attendance, travel is scheduled from
the Kansas City home office with enough lead time to protect the litigation
calendar.

## Labor market evidence for Bartholomew County evaluees

Employability and earning capacity opinions are grounded in Bureau of
Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the
Columbus metropolitan area and for Indiana statewide, together with state
workforce agency labor market information and direct labor market research
where the referral warrants it. The report identifies the evaluee's
commutable labor market and explains why that market, rather than a
broader or narrower one, is the right frame, so the vocational foundation
can be examined openly in deposition. Transferable skills analysis,
placeability, and wage findings each carry their own sourcing, and the
report distinguishes between what the data show and what the evaluator
concluded from them, so cross-examination can engage the reasoning
directly.

## Opening a file

The engagement sequence is deliberately unglamorous: conflict check first,
scope second, work third. Submit the caption or party names through the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and note any imminent disclosure,
deposition, or trial dates. Once conflicts clear, counsel receive a records
checklist tailored to the claims and a committed report timeline. Columbus
referrals are handled by the same office that runs the firm's
[national practice](/locations/nationwide/), under the same methodology and
the same review standards applied everywhere the firm works.
