Fairmount is a small town, but the labor market that decides a Fairmount
claimant's earning capacity is considerably wider than its town limits.
Residents here work across Grant County and toward Marion, the principal
city of the surrounding metropolitan area, so a credible vocational opinion
has to measure the commuting market a Fairmount worker actually reaches,
not the handful of employers inside the town itself. That is the analytical
starting point for Purinton Analytics in Fairmount matters, whether the
firm is retained for
[vocational expert witness](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) work,
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), or
[forensic economic damages](/services/forensic-economic-damages/) performed
and signed by independent economist partners whom the firm coordinates.

## Measuring the market a Fairmount worker reaches

The wage and employment evidence behind a Fairmount opinion comes from
Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for
the relevant area and for Indiana as a whole, from state workforce agency
labor market information, and from direct labor market research when the
referral questions call for it. Scale is treated honestly: this is a
regional market organized around Marion rather than a major metropolitan
economy, and transferable skills conclusions are limited to occupations
with a real presence in it. Both retaining plaintiffs' counsel and
retaining defense counsel receive the same method, because the opinion has
to survive cross-examination no matter who commissioned it. The broader
regional context is set out on the
[Marion metro page](/locations/indiana/marion/).

## Courts that hear a Fairmount case

A Fairmount dispute filed in state court is tried in Grant County's Circuit
and Superior Courts. Where federal jurisdiction exists, Indiana's two
federal forums are the United States District Court for the Northern
District of Indiana and the United States District Court for the Southern
District of Indiana; the district with venue over a specific Fairmount
filing, and any local qualification requirement that comes with it, is
identified during the conflict check and intake. Statewide venue notes for
counsel with matters beyond Grant County appear on the
[Indiana page](/locations/indiana/).

## The disclosure calendar comes first

In practice the controlling fact in most Fairmount referrals is not
geography but time: the expert disclosure deadline, the discovery cutoff,
and the deposition window already on the court's calendar. Intake therefore
begins by mapping the report against those dates. Records requests go out
early, the evaluee interview is scheduled by video or telephone without
waiting on travel, and any in-person evaluation is held in the evaluee's
own community. The firm has no staffed office in Fairmount and publishes no
local address; when testimony requires presence in the courtroom or at a
deposition, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office. The same
calendar-first approach governs
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) assignments,
where the response window is usually shortest. It is one habit of a
[national by-appointment practice](/locations/nationwide/) that treats
deadlines as part of the standard of care.
