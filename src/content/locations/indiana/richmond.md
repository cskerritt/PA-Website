Purinton Analytics keeps no office in Richmond, and this page will not
pretend otherwise. What Wayne County counsel get instead is a remote-first
national practice that treats distance as a logistics problem to be
solved, not a reason to decline the case. Plaintiff and defense firms
retain the practice for
[vocational evaluation and expert testimony](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. Every file opens with a documented conflict check
and a defined scope before any opinion work begins.

## Courts serving Wayne County

Engagements are accepted for matters in the Wayne County Circuit and
Superior Courts and in the United States District Court for the Southern
District of Indiana and the United States District Court for the Northern
District of Indiana. The Richmond micropolitan area rests entirely on
Wayne County, which keeps the venue analysis straightforward; the firm's
reports are prepared to the same disclosure standard in the county courts
as in federal litigation, so removal or remand does not change the work
product. Coverage across the rest of the state is described on the
[Indiana locations page](/locations/indiana/).

## Logistics from the Kansas City home office

Richmond meetings are by appointment, and no street address is published
for this location because none exists to publish. Most stages of an
engagement never require travel at all: records arrive electronically,
conferences with counsel proceed by video and phone, and remote
evaluation is available when the referral questions and testing
protocols permit it. When the matter calls for an in-person evaluation,
it can be conducted in the evaluee's own community; when deposition or
trial testimony requires presence, travel is scheduled from the Kansas
City home office and fixed to the case calendar early, so that the
expert's availability never becomes opposing counsel's argument. Remote
and in-person stages are identified for counsel at intake, and the
engagement letter records which is which, so expectations are set in
writing before the work begins.

## Researching the Richmond labor market

Employability and earning capacity findings for Wayne County evaluees are
grounded in Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage
estimates for the area and for Indiana statewide, state workforce agency
labor market information, and direct labor market research where the
referral warrants it. The report states which market it treats as the
evaluee's commutable labor market and why, and it keeps the qualitative
character of that market, its industries and its scale, tied to the data
sources cited rather than to impression.

## Starting with a conflict check

Richmond engagements begin the way every engagement in the practice
begins. Submit the caption or party names through the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/), note any imminent disclosure,
deposition, or trial dates, and expect a records checklist tailored to
the claims, together with a proposed report timeline, once conflicts
clear. The same methodology and review
standards that support the firm's
[national practice](/locations/nationwide/) stand behind every Wayne
County report it signs.
