## Where Evansville matters are heard

The Evansville metropolitan area comprises Posey County along with
Vanderburgh and Warrick counties, and the firm accepts matters venued in the Circuit and Superior
Courts of each. In federal court, engagements proceed in the United States
District Court for the Southern District of Indiana, and the United States
District Court for the Northern District of Indiana is available when a
related matter is venued there. Any venue-specific registration or
qualification requirement is identified and confirmed during the conflict
check and intake process, so counsel know before the engagement is scoped
whether anything about the forum affects the work.

## Appointment-based logistics from the Kansas City home office

The firm keeps no staffed office in Evansville and publishes no street
address for this location; meetings and evaluations are by appointment.
Stating that plainly is deliberate, because accuracy about presence is part
of the same discipline that governs the reports themselves. Most
engagements are conducted remotely, from records review through structured
interviews and standardized testing under publisher protocols where remote
administration is supported. When the referral questions require an
in-person evaluation, or when deposition and trial dates call for
attendance in southwestern Indiana, travel is scheduled from the Kansas
City home office around counsel's calendar.

## Evidence behind the vocational findings

Opinions about what an Evansville area evaluee can earn are grounded in
Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for
the Evansville metropolitan area and for Indiana statewide, supplemented by
state workforce agency labor market information and direct labor market
research where warranted. Transferable skills analysis is confined to
occupations realistically available within the evaluee's commuting range.
Life care plans and cost projections are priced to the evaluee's own market
through provider-specific inquiries and published fee data, so projected
care costs reflect the region where care will actually be delivered rather
than a national composite.
