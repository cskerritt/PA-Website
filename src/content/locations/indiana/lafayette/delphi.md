A Delphi case starts with a short caption and a wide market. The city sits in Carroll County, inside the Lafayette-West Lafayette, IN metropolitan area, which means the courtroom that will hear the claim and the labor market that will define its damages are not the same place, and a credible earning-capacity opinion has to be honest about that gap. Purinton Analytics serves plaintiff and defense counsel in Delphi matters with [vocational expert witness](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) opinions and [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), each written to withstand examination by whichever side did not retain the firm.

## Carroll County Venue and the Federal Districts

State claims arising in Delphi proceed in the Circuit and Superior Courts serving Carroll County. For federal matters, Indiana offers the United States District Court for the Northern District of Indiana and the United States District Court for the Southern District of Indiana as its available forums; the firm does not guess which district governs a given dispute, and the controlling venue, along with any registration or qualification requirement particular to it, is identified during the conflict check and intake. Because a small-community venue can put an opinion in front of jurors who know the local economy from their own working lives, reports for Delphi cases are written with particular care to show their work: every source named, every step from record to conclusion laid out. The broader Indiana venue landscape appears on the [Indiana state page](/locations/indiana/).

## Delphi Earners in the Metro Economy

Few small cities contain their own labor markets, and vocational findings for a Delphi evaluee are therefore grounded in Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Lafayette-West Lafayette area and for Indiana statewide, together with state workforce agency labor market information and direct labor market research where a specific occupation or employer practice is in dispute. The analysis asks what employment realistically exists within reach of a Delphi resident with the evaluee's residual functional capacity, education, and vocational history, and it documents how the answer was reached rather than asserting it. Lafayette, the metro's principal city, anchors much of that surrounding market, and the [Lafayette metro page](/locations/indiana/lafayette/) covers it in full.

## Evaluating Without a Local Office

The firm maintains no office in Delphi, publishes no street address there, and does not pretend otherwise; the accuracy that governs the reports governs the letterhead too. Records analysis and counsel conferences are handled remotely from the outset. When the referral questions call for an interview or standardized testing, the evaluation is conducted by secure remote session under conditions that protect test validity, or in the evaluee's own community when direct observation matters. In-person evaluation days, depositions, and trial testimony are planned as travel from the Kansas City home office, coordinated with the litigation calendar counsel supplies. Contested future medical and attendant care can be addressed through a [medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/) or a complete life care plan within the same engagement.
