## A Labor Market Shared with Lafayette

West Lafayette workers and Lafayette workers compete for the same jobs, which is why vocational findings for a West Lafayette evaluee begin with Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Lafayette-West Lafayette area and for Indiana statewide. Those estimates are supplemented by state workforce agency labor market information and by direct employer and labor market research when a disputed question turns on a specific occupation, hiring practice, or accommodation. The conclusions stay qualitative about geography and explicit about method: what employment exists within the evaluee's reach given residual functional capacity, education, and work history, and how each finding was derived, so that cross-examination confronts sources rather than assertions.
