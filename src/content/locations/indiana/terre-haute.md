Purinton Analytics works both sides of the caption in Terre Haute, and that
fact shapes everything about how the firm practices here. An expert who
knows the next report may be retained by the opposing bar writes
differently: methodology disclosed, sources verifiable, conclusions
confined to the record. On those terms the firm provides counsel across the
Terre Haute metropolitan area with
[vocational expert witness](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) opinions,
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) and
[medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/) in serious
injury matters, [rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/)
of opposing expert work, and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## The Terre Haute venue footprint

The metropolitan area comprises Clay County along with Vigo, Sullivan,
and Vermillion counties, and the firm accepts matters venued in the Circuit and Superior
Courts of each. Federal engagements proceed in the United States District
Court for the Southern District of Indiana, and the United States District
Court for the Northern District of Indiana is available when a related
matter is venued there. Any venue-specific registration or qualification
requirement is identified and confirmed during the conflict check and
intake process rather than discovered mid-engagement.

## Presence by appointment

The firm maintains no staffed office in Terre Haute and publishes no street
address for this location; meetings and evaluations are by appointment.
Saying so plainly is part of the same discipline that governs the reports.
The typical engagement is remote from the outset: records review,
structured vocational interviews, and standardized testing under publisher
protocols where remote administration is supported. Where the referral
questions call for in-person evaluation, the evaluee can be seen in the
evaluee's own community, and when depositions or trial require attendance
in western Indiana, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office
around counsel's calendar.

## Labor market foundations

Earning capacity and employability findings for Terre Haute area evaluees
rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage
estimates for the Terre Haute metropolitan area and for Indiana statewide,
supplemented by state workforce agency labor market information and direct
employer research where warranted. Transferable skills analysis is limited
to occupations realistically available within the evaluee's commuting
range, which keeps the opinion anchored to this regional market rather than
to a national abstraction. Life care plans and cost projections are priced
to the evaluee's own market through provider-specific inquiries and
published fee data, because the cost of future care is a local question.

## How engagements begin

Whether retained by plaintiff or defense, counsel start a Terre Haute
engagement the same way: a documented conflict check, a defined scope, and
a records request tailored to the claims. Submit the caption or party names
through the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and note any imminent
disclosure, deposition, or trial dates so scheduling is settled early. The
Terre Haute practice sits within the firm's
[Indiana coverage](/locations/indiana/) and its
[national practice](/locations/nationwide/), and the reporting standard
does not vary with the venue.
