A referral from the Michigan City-La Porte metropolitan area usually starts
with a question about a deadline: a disclosure date approaching, a
deposition set, a mediation that needs a damages picture counsel can
defend. Purinton Analytics is built to answer that call for attorneys on
either side of the caption, providing
[vocational evaluation and expert testimony](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. Intake begins with a documented conflict check and a
defined scope before any substantive work starts.

## Courts serving LaPorte County matters

The metro rests on LaPorte County, and engagements are accepted for matters
in the LaPorte County Circuit and Superior Courts as well as in the United
States District Court for the Northern District of Indiana and the United
States District Court for the Southern District of Indiana. The firm's
reports are written to the same standard in every forum: methodology
disclosed, sources cited, and conclusions confined to what the record
supports. Counsel handling matters across the rest of the state can start
from the [Indiana locations page](/locations/indiana/) for the statewide
picture.

## By-appointment presence, stated plainly

There is no staffed Purinton Analytics office in Michigan City or La Porte,
and the firm publishes no street address for this location. Candor about
presence is part of the same discipline that governs the reports
themselves. What counsel get instead is a remote-first practice that moves
quickly: records review, attorney conferences, and pre-deposition
preparation are conducted by video and phone, and remote evaluation is
available when the referral questions and testing protocols permit it.
When the matter calls for in-person evaluation or courtroom presence,
travel is arranged from the Kansas City home office and coordinated with
counsel around the controlling dates.

## Grounding opinions in the local labor market

Vocational findings for evaluees in this market rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Michigan
City-La Porte metropolitan area and for Indiana statewide, supplemented by
state workforce agency labor market information and by direct labor market
research where warranted. Each report states which labor market defines the
evaluee's commutable range and explains the basis for that choice, because
an earning capacity opinion is only as strong as the market data beneath
it, and both sides are entitled to see that foundation. Where published
estimates and the evaluee's actual occupation diverge, the report says so
and explains how the divergence was resolved rather than papering over it.

## Requesting a conflict check

Engagements open with a conflict check rather than a sales conversation.
Send the caption or party names through the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/), flag any imminent disclosure,
deposition, or trial dates, and expect a records checklist tailored to the
claims once the check clears. The same office that serves Michigan City
counsel administers the firm's [national practice](/locations/nationwide/),
so a local referral draws on the same methodology, templates, and quality
review applied to matters across the country.
