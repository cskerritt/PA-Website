## A small town inside the Muncie labor market

Yorktown is a small community, and its residents largely earn their
livings in the broader market anchored by Muncie, the metro's principal
city. For an earning capacity analysis, that distinction matters: the
relevant question is not what work exists inside the town limits but what
employment a Yorktown evaluee can realistically access across the metro.
