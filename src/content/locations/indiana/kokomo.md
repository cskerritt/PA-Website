Litigation calendars, not marketing calendars, drive vocational and
care-planning work in Kokomo. Disclosure deadlines, deposition settings,
and trial dates determine when an expert report is useful, and Purinton
Analytics schedules its Howard County engagements against those dates from
the first call. Counsel on either side of the caption retain the firm for
[vocational evaluation and expert testimony](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure, with the conflict check and scope defined before
substantive work begins.

## Where Kokomo matters are heard

The Kokomo metropolitan area rests on Howard County, and engagements are
accepted for matters in the Howard County Circuit and Superior Courts and
in the federal courts serving the state, the United States District Court
for the Northern District of Indiana and the United States District Court
for the Southern District of Indiana. Reports are prepared to a single
standard across forums, with methodology stated and sources cited, so a
change of venue does not mean a change of work product. The
[Indiana locations page](/locations/indiana/) describes how the firm
covers the rest of the state.

## Appointment-based service, described accurately

Purinton Analytics keeps no staffed office in Kokomo and publishes no
street address for this location. Saying so plainly is part of the same
discipline the firm applies to its opinions. The practical consequence for
counsel is a remote-first engagement: records review, attorney
conferences, and pre-deposition preparation proceed by video and phone,
and remote evaluation is available when the referral questions and testing
protocols permit it. When the referral calls for an in-person evaluation
in the evaluee's community, or when testimony requires presence, travel is
scheduled from the Kansas City home office and locked to the case calendar
early enough to avoid last-minute conflicts. Draft timelines are shared
with counsel in writing, and any change to the schedule is documented the
same way, so the file reflects how the engagement actually ran.

## Market data behind Kokomo opinions

Findings on employability and earning capacity for Howard County evaluees
draw on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage
estimates for the Kokomo metropolitan area and for Indiana statewide,
state workforce agency labor market information, and direct labor market
research where the referral questions require it. Because reasonable
minds can differ over what counts as an evaluee's commutable labor
market, each report says which market it uses and why, putting the
assumption on the table where both sides can address it.

## First step: the conflict check

New matters begin with the caption, not a pitch. Use the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) to submit the caption or party
names, and identify any imminent disclosure, deposition, or trial dates
so the timeline conversation happens at intake rather than after it. When
the check clears, counsel receive a records checklist tailored to the
claims. Kokomo engagements run through the same office and the same
methodology as the firm's [national practice](/locations/nationwide/), so
local counsel get the standards of a national practice on a local
schedule.
