The Indianapolis-Carmel-Greenwood, IN metropolitan area crosses county lines, and where a case is filed can matter as much as what it alleges. Greenwood belongs to Johnson County, which means a Greenwood matter often proceeds in a different courthouse than an otherwise similar case arising elsewhere in the metro. Purinton Analytics builds its [vocational expert](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) and [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) engagements around that venue reality, delivering opinions for plaintiff and defense counsel that are fitted to the forum actually hearing the case rather than to a generic metropolitan template.

## Johnson County Venue Questions

State court actions arising in Greenwood are heard in Johnson County's Circuit and Superior Courts. Federal matters may be filed in the United States District Court for the Northern District of Indiana or the United States District Court for the Southern District of Indiana, and the firm treats the question of which district controls as one to be answered, not assumed: the operative forum and any venue-specific qualification requirement are confirmed during the conflict check and intake. Once the forum is fixed, the disclosure calendar and report format follow from its rules, and the workplan is scheduled backward from those obligations. The [Indiana page](/locations/indiana/) sets out the firm's statewide venue coverage.

## Earning Capacity Beyond the County Line

A Greenwood evaluee's labor market does not stop at the county boundary. Residents work throughout the Indianapolis-Carmel-Greenwood region, and an earning-capacity opinion that ignored that commuting reality would be easy to discredit. The firm therefore rests its vocational findings on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Indianapolis metropolitan area and for Indiana statewide, on state workforce agency labor market information, and on direct labor market research when the referral questions demand it. Each employment or wage conclusion in the report is sourced, so that opposing counsel can check the data and find that it says what the report says it says. The metro-wide market is discussed further on the [Indianapolis metro page](/locations/indiana/indianapolis/).
