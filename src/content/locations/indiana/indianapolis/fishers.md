Civil actions arising in Fishers are heard in Hamilton County's state trial courts, the Circuit and Superior Courts. ## A Suburban Workforce Measured Honestly

An earning-capacity opinion for a Fishers evaluee has to reflect how suburban residents actually work: some within the town, many across the wider Indianapolis-Carmel-Greenwood labor market. Vocational findings therefore rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Indianapolis area and for Indiana statewide, supplemented by state workforce agency labor market information and by direct labor market research when a case turns on a particular occupation, employer practice, or accommodation question. Every figure in the report carries a cited source. Nothing rests on impressions of what suburban work looks like, because impressions do not survive deposition.

## Evaluation Logistics for Fishers Evaluees

The firm keeps no office in Fishers and publishes no local address, and it says so plainly, because accuracy about presence is part of the same discipline that governs its reports. Records review, the vocational interview, and standardized testing can proceed by secure remote administration under conditions that protect test validity. When the referral questions call for in-person assessment, the evaluation is conducted in the evaluee's own community, and travel for evaluations, depositions, and trial testimony is scheduled from the Kansas City home office against the case calendar. Where future medical needs are disputed, a [medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/) or full life care plan is developed inside the same engagement, so the care-planning opinion and the vocational opinion rest on a shared record.
