Most Fort Wayne referrals to Purinton Analytics turn on a familiar set of
questions: what work an injured person can still perform, what that work
pays in the region where the person actually lives, and what future care
will cost. The firm answers those questions for plaintiff and defense
counsel alike through
[earning capacity and employability evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. Where an opposing expert's report needs scrutiny,
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) is available
under the same methodology standards.

## Courts served in the Fort Wayne area

The Fort Wayne metropolitan area takes in Allen County along with Wells
and Whitley counties, and the firm accepts matters venued in the Circuit and Superior
Courts of each. Federal matters proceed in the United States District Court
for the Northern District of Indiana or, when venue lies elsewhere in the
state, the United States District Court for the Southern District of
Indiana. Because the same written opinion may be examined in any of these
forums, reports are prepared to a publication standard suitable for reading
by opposing counsel, with methodology and sources disclosed rather than
implied.

## How the firm appears in Fort Wayne

There is no staffed Purinton Analytics office in Fort Wayne, and the firm
publishes no local street address, because candor about presence belongs to
the same discipline that governs its opinions. Meetings and evaluations are
by appointment. In practice, most engagements begin and often finish
remotely: records analysis, structured vocational interviews, and
standardized testing under publisher protocols where remote administration
is supported. When an in-person evaluation is warranted, or when deposition
and trial dates require attendance in northeastern Indiana, travel is
scheduled from the Kansas City home office and planned around the case
calendar rather than the other way around.

## Grounding opinions in the regional labor market

Vocational conclusions for Fort Wayne area evaluees are grounded in Bureau
of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Fort
Wayne metropolitan area and for Indiana statewide, together with state
workforce agency labor market information and direct labor market research
where the referral questions call for it. The analysis is tied to the
evaluee's own commuting range and the occupations realistically available
there. Life care plans and medical cost projections follow the same logic,
priced through provider-specific inquiries and published fee data for the
region so that projected costs reflect the market in which care will
actually be purchased.

## Requesting a conflict check

Engagements in Allen, Wells, and Whitley counties begin the way they do
everywhere in the practice: with a documented conflict check, a defined
scope, and a records request tailored to the claims. To begin, submit the
caption or party names through the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and flag any disclosure deadlines,
depositions, or trial settings that bear on timing. The Fort Wayne practice
is part of the firm's statewide [Indiana coverage](/locations/indiana/) and
its broader [national practice](/locations/nationwide/), so counsel with
related matters in other venues can consolidate them under the same
reporting standard.
