Candor about presence is where this page begins. Purinton Analytics maintains no office in Wyoming, publishes no Wyoming street address, and does not describe itself as a local practice, because accuracy about offices belongs to the same discipline that governs the firm's written opinions. What Wyoming counsel engage instead is a national vocational expert and life care planning practice that serves plaintiff and defense matters across the state by appointment, with methodology documented carefully enough to withstand examination by the other side of the caption.

## Wyoming's venue landscape

Engagements are accepted for matters in the District Court, Wyoming's trial court, and in the United States District Court for the District of Wyoming, whose jurisdiction covers the entire state. Reports and disclosures are prepared to the standards of the forum holding the case, whether the matter is proceeding in state or federal court, and every written opinion is grounded in a record both sides can examine.

Because a vocational opinion is only as strong as its labor market foundation, coverage within Wyoming is organized around the communities where evaluees live and work. Dedicated pages describe the practice's work in Cheyenne, Casper, Gillette, Rock Springs, Riverton, Laramie, Jackson, Sheridan, Cody, and Evanston. A matter arising outside those areas is handled the same way, with labor market research scoped to the evaluee's own community rather than borrowed from somewhere else.

## How the firm serves Wyoming counsel

The practice is remote-first by design. Records review, vocational interviews, transferable skills analysis, and life care plan development proceed by secure document exchange and scheduled video conference, and many Wyoming engagements are completed without any travel at all. When the referral questions call for in-person evaluation, or when deposition and trial dates require presence, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office and coordinated with counsel around the court's calendar. Meetings anywhere in Wyoming are by appointment.

Vocational findings for Wyoming evaluees rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the relevant Wyoming areas and for Wyoming statewide, state workforce agency labor market information, and direct labor market research where warranted. When a matter also requires economic damages testimony, counsel may structure [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement structure. That approach is not particular to Wyoming; the [nationwide practice page](/locations/nationwide/) describes how the same model operates across the firm's full footprint.

## Qualification and disclosure in Wyoming courts

Each engagement opens with a documented conflict check, a defined scope, and a records request tailored to the claims, so that the eventual opinion rests on a file assembled deliberately rather than reconstructed under deadline pressure. Qualifications, prior testimony, and the bases for each opinion accompany the disclosure in whatever form the forum requires. Any venue-specific registration or qualification requirement is identified and confirmed during the conflict check and intake process, so admissibility groundwork is laid before substantive work begins.

## Starting a Wyoming engagement

A conflict check comes before any substantive discussion of the case. Submit the caption or party names through the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and flag any imminent disclosure, deposition, or trial dates so the schedule can be confirmed while the conflict search runs. Counsel on either side of the caption receive a defined scope and fee agreement before work begins, and the engagement is documented from that point forward.
