Montana counsel should not have to choose a vocational expert based on where
the expert happens to live. Purinton Analytics serves plaintiff and defense
counsel across Montana through a remote-first national practice, so a report
prepared for a Billings jury rests on the same records discipline as one
prepared for any other venue in the country. The firm's Montana work includes
[earning capacity and employability evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/)
in injury and employment matters, [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/)
in catastrophic cases, and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Montana courts and coverage

Engagements are accepted for matters in the District Court in venues across
Montana and in the United States District Court for the District of Montana.
Dedicated pages describe how the practice serves
Billings,
Missoula,
Bozeman,
Kalispell,
Helena,

Butte-Silver Bow. Counsel elsewhere in the state
are served on identical terms. The venue shapes scheduling and logistics; it
never changes the report standard, the methodology, or the willingness to
accept the matter.

## A remote-first practice, in person when it matters

Most Montana engagements begin, and many conclude, without travel: records
analysis, structured vocational interviews by video, and standardized testing
under publisher protocols where remote administration is supported. Meetings
in Montana are by appointment. The firm keeps no staffed office in the state
and publishes no Montana street address, because accuracy about presence
belongs to the same discipline that governs its written opinions. When a
referral calls for in-person evaluation in the evaluee's own community, or
when deposition and trial calendars require presence, travel is scheduled
from the Kansas City home office and planned around the court's dates rather
than the firm's convenience.

Vocational findings for Montana evaluees draw on Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates for Montana statewide and for its
metropolitan areas, on state workforce agency labor market information, and
on direct labor market research where the referral questions warrant it.
Life care plans and future medical cost projections are priced to the
evaluee's own market rather than to national averages, since the cost of
care in a Montana community is an evidentiary question, not an assumption.

## Qualification for Montana matters

Jason Purinton's professional credentials are issued by national certifying
bodies, and the admissibility of expert opinion in a Montana courtroom is
determined by the forum under its own rules of evidence. Any venue-specific
registration or qualification requirement is identified and confirmed during
the conflict check and intake process, before substantive work begins, so
that neither side of the caption encounters a qualification surprise on the
eve of disclosure.

## Starting a Montana engagement

The first step is the same for a state court case in Great Falls as for a
matter in federal court: submit the caption or party names through the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and note any imminent disclosure,
deposition, or trial dates. Once conflicts clear, counsel receive a defined
scope, a fee schedule, and a records request tailored to the claims actually
pleaded. For matters beyond Montana, the firm's
[national coverage](/locations/nationwide/) page describes the same process
applied across the country.
