Referrals from New Jersey counsel tend to cluster around a few recurring
questions: employability and mitigation in employment disputes, lost earning
capacity in personal injury litigation, and the scope and cost of lifelong
care after catastrophic injury. Purinton Analytics addresses each of them
for plaintiff and defense counsel through
[earning capacity and employability evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Remote-first work, by-appointment presence

New Jersey meetings are by appointment. The firm keeps no staffed office in
the state and publishes no New Jersey street address, and it says so
directly, because a report that will be picked apart by opposing counsel
should not sit on a website that blurs the facts about presence. Most
engagements begin remotely: records analysis, structured vocational
interviews by video, and standardized testing under publisher protocols
where remote administration is supported. When the referral questions call
for in-person evaluation in the evaluee's own community, or when deposition
and trial dates require the expert in the courtroom, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office and planned against
the court's calendar from the outset. The deliverable is the same either
way: a written opinion resting on a documented record that both sides can
examine.

Vocational findings for New Jersey evaluees rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for New Jersey
statewide and for its metropolitan areas, on state workforce agency labor
market information, and on direct labor market research where warranted.
Life care plans and future medical cost projections are priced to the
evaluee's own market through provider-specific inquiry and published fee
data, because care costs in New Jersey communities are an evidentiary
question rather than a national average.

## Qualification for New Jersey matters

Credentials in vocational evaluation and life care planning are issued by
national certifying bodies, and each New Jersey forum applies its own rules
of evidence in deciding what expert opinion reaches the finder of fact. Any
venue-specific registration or qualification requirement is identified and
confirmed during the conflict check and intake process, so the question is
settled before expert disclosures come due.

Submit the caption or party names through the
