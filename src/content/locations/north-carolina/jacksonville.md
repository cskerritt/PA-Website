Counsel litigating injury, employment, and disability claims in Jacksonville
usually need answers to a short list of questions: what work the evaluee can
still perform, what that work pays, and what future care will cost. Purinton
Analytics answers those questions for plaintiff and defense counsel in the
Jacksonville metro through
[vocational expert](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) evaluations,
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/), with
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
available when independent economist partners are needed to perform and sign
the economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds
under one engagement structure.

## The Onslow County venue picture

The Jacksonville metropolitan area consists of Onslow County, and engagements
are accepted for matters in the Superior Court there as well as in the
federal courts serving the state: the United States District Court for the
Eastern District of North Carolina, the United States District Court for the
Middle District of North Carolina, and the United States District Court for
the Western District of North Carolina. Referrals that reach beyond the metro
are covered through the [North Carolina page](/locations/north-carolina/).
Testimony is provided in person or by remote appearance where the court
permits, and reports are written to be read closely by the opposing side.

## How the firm appears in Jacksonville

There is no staffed Purinton Analytics office in Jacksonville, and the firm
publishes no street address for this location; meetings are by appointment
because saying so plainly is part of the same accuracy that governs the
reports. In practice, counsel schedule evaluations in the evaluee's own
community or by secure video under publisher protocols, and when deposition
or trial dates require presence, travel is arranged from the Kansas City home
office against the litigation calendar established at intake. Records
exchange, counsel conferences, and draft-stage questions are handled
remotely, which keeps the engagement moving between in-person dates.

## Wage and employment evidence for the metro

Vocational findings for Jacksonville evaluees are grounded in Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Jacksonville
metropolitan area and for North Carolina statewide, together with state
workforce agency labor market information and direct labor market research
where the referral questions call for evidence about actual hiring. The
report identifies each source so that a cross-examining attorney can check
every finding, and when the assignment is to test another expert's
conclusions rather than build an original opinion,
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) engagements apply
the same standard to that expert's data and method.
