An injured person in Ayden should not have to rebuild their week around an
expert's travel preferences. Purinton Analytics designs its Ayden
engagements so the evaluation comes to the evaluee: by secure video from
home, or in person in the evaluee's own community when the referral
questions require direct observation. Ayden is a small town in the
Greenville, NC metropolitan area, and the logistics of reaching people there
are treated as part of the engagement plan rather than an inconvenience to
be passed along. Counsel for plaintiffs and counsel for defendants retain
the firm on identical terms, and each
[vocational expert](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) opinion and
[life care plan](/services/life-care-planning/) is written to hold up under
the other side's questioning.

## The courts that hear Ayden matters

Ayden sits in Pitt County. State-court civil claims arising there are tried
in the Superior Court, and counsel weighing a federal filing look to the
United States District Court for the Eastern District of North Carolina, the
United States District Court for the Middle District of North Carolina, and
the United States District Court for the Western District of North Carolina
as the available forums. The controlling venue for any given caption, along
with whatever expert registration or qualification that venue expects, is
identified and confirmed during the conflict check and intake process.
Whatever the forum, the file is built the same way: conflicts documented in
writing, scope defined before work begins, and records requested to match
the claims actually pleaded.

## The labor market behind an Ayden opinion

Small-town evaluees compete in a regional market, so Ayden opinions define
opportunity in terms of the Greenville metropolitan area and the state as a
whole. The evidentiary base is the Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational
employment and wage estimates for the Greenville metropolitan area and for
North Carolina statewide, joined by state workforce agency labor market
information and targeted employer contact where warranted. Commuting range
is treated as part of the evaluee's genuine options, and the report says so
explicitly rather than leaving the market definition to be guessed at
during cross-examination. The
[Greenville metro page](/locations/north-carolina/greenville/) describes
that regional picture, and the
[North Carolina state page](/locations/north-carolina/) collects venue
information statewide.

## What an Ayden evaluation involves

A vocational evaluation for an Ayden evaluee typically pairs a structured
interview with standardized assessment of skills and functional capacities,
reviewed against the medical record. Where future care is at issue, the
care-planning work follows published methodology and can be paired with a
[medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/) when counsel
needs the narrower deliverable. Scheduling is driven by the litigation
calendar; counsel who flag a disclosure deadline at intake will see it
reflected in the written scope, and travel for in-person work is arranged
from the Kansas City home office. The firm maintains no Ayden office and
lists no address in the town, a fact stated plainly because overstated
presence would undercut reports that trade on precision. When economic loss
figures are required, the firm arranges
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while vocational and care-planning work continues under the same
engagement.
