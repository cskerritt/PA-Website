A Farmville case arrives with a calendar attached: discovery cutoffs, expert
disclosure deadlines, deposition windows, and a trial setting that may or
may not hold. Purinton Analytics builds its Farmville engagements backward
from those dates, so that the
[vocational expert](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) report or
[life care plan](/services/life-care-planning/) is complete, reviewed, and
served when the rules require it rather than when the expert gets around to
it. Farmville is a small town within the Greenville, NC metropolitan area,
and the firm serves it the way it serves every venue: available to whichever
side of the caption calls, and writing for the side that did not.

## Where Farmville disputes are decided

Civil claims arising in Farmville belong to Pitt County, where state-court
matters are heard in the Superior Court under North Carolina's rules of
civil procedure. On the federal side, North Carolina offers the United
States District Court for the Eastern District of North Carolina, the
United States District Court for the Middle District of North Carolina, and
the United States District Court for the Western District of North Carolina
as its forums; which of them governs a specific Farmville matter, and what
that court asks of a testifying expert by way of registration or
qualification, is identified and confirmed during the conflict check and
intake process. Court by court, the discipline holds constant: a documented
conflict check opens the file, a written scope bounds it, and a records
request keyed to the pleadings supplies the evidence the opinion will cite.
