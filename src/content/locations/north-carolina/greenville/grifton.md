Grifton is a small town, and small-town cases are sometimes handed to
experts who treat them as small work. Purinton Analytics rejects that
premise. A catastrophic injury in Grifton raises the same questions a
catastrophic injury raises anywhere: what work remains open to this person,
what will their care cost over a lifetime, and can the answers withstand a
skilled cross-examiner. The firm brings its full national methodology to
Grifton matters in the Greenville, NC metropolitan area, whether the
retaining lawyer represents the plaintiff or the defense, and delivers
[vocational expert witness](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) opinions
and [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) built to the
standard the opposing expert will be measured against.

## County and courthouse context

Grifton's civil disputes are Pitt County disputes, and in state court they
are heard in the Superior Court. Federal practice in North Carolina runs
through the United States District Court for the Eastern District of North
Carolina, the United States District Court for the Middle District of North
Carolina, and the United States District Court for the Western District of
North Carolina; the district that controls a given Grifton case, together
with any registration or qualification requirement that court places on a
testifying expert, is identified and confirmed during the conflict check
and intake process. None of this alters how the file is assembled. Conflicts
are checked and documented, the scope is put in writing, and the records
request is drawn to fit the claims, so the resulting opinion can be traced
to evidence either party may inspect.

## A small town inside a larger labor market

Employment opportunity for a Grifton evaluee is a regional question, not a
municipal question, and answering it takes regional data. Vocational
conclusions rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and
wage estimates for the Greenville metropolitan area and for North Carolina
statewide, together with state workforce agency labor market information
and direct inquiry with employers when the referral calls for it. The
analysis makes commuting patterns part of the opportunity assessment and
identifies its data sources on the face of the report, so cross-examination
finds the foundation already disclosed. A fuller account of the regional
economy appears on the
[Greenville metro page](/locations/north-carolina/greenville/).
