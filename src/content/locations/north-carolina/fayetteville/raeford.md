Raeford's litigation identity begins with its county. A Raeford injury or employment case is a Hoke County case, captioned and tried on Hoke County's docket, even though the evaluee at its center draws work and wages from the wider Fayetteville metropolitan area. An expert opinion has to keep both frames straight at once, and Purinton Analytics accepts Raeford referrals from counsel on either side with exactly that in mind, delivering [vocational expert witness](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) opinions, [expert testimony and litigation consulting](/services/expert-testimony-litigation-consulting/), and life care plans written to withstand examination by the lawyer who did not retain the firm.

## Hoke County Venue and the Federal Alternatives

State court matters arising in Raeford are ordinarily heard in the Superior Court serving Hoke County. In federal practice, the available North Carolina forums are the United States District Court for the Eastern District of North Carolina, the United States District Court for the Middle District of North Carolina, and the United States District Court for the Western District of North Carolina; the controlling venue for any specific matter, together with any qualification or registration requirement that venue imposes, is identified during the conflict check and intake process. Because Raeford sits within the broader Fayetteville metro for economic purposes, counsel may also find the regional overview on the [Fayetteville, NC metro page](/locations/north-carolina/fayetteville/) useful, along with the [North Carolina state page](/locations/north-carolina/) for venue structure elsewhere in the state.

## Reading the Regional Labor Market from Raeford

A Raeford evaluee competes for work in a market larger than Hoke County, and pretending otherwise would distort the opinion in either direction. Employability and earning capacity analyses therefore draw on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Fayetteville metropolitan area and for North Carolina statewide, on state workforce agency labor market information, and on direct labor market research when the referral questions call for it. The report distinguishes between what the regional data show and what is realistically reachable from Raeford given the evaluee's restrictions, transportation, and skills, and it cites every source so opposing counsel can retrace the path. Where lost earnings must be quantified, [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) let independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds in parallel.
