Spring Lake sits in Cumberland County, so state court matters involving its residents are generally filed in the Superior Court there. Which forum controls a given case, and whether that forum imposes any registration or qualification requirement on the expert, is confirmed during the conflict check and intake process rather than assumed. ## Labor Market Context for Vocational Findings

Before logistics come the findings those logistics serve. Spring Lake's workers draw their opportunities from the wider Fayetteville metropolitan area, and the analysis reflects that reality. Post-injury employability and earning capacity opinions are anchored in Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Fayetteville metro and for North Carolina statewide, alongside state workforce agency labor market information and, where warranted, direct employer sampling. No figure enters the report without a named source, and no occupation is treated as available to the evaluee without attention to what actually exists within reach of Spring Lake. When damages must be reduced to present value, the work is performed and signed by independent economist partners coordinated by the firm through [forensic economic damages](/services/forensic-economic-damages/) engagements, keeping the vocational and economic opinions distinct and each defensible on its own terms.

## Meeting the Evaluee Where They Live

An assessment gains nothing from forcing a Spring Lake evaluee to travel, and it can lose accuracy: functional limitations, household demands, and the realistic commuting radius all read differently in the evaluee's own setting. Interviews are therefore conducted by video where the record supports it, and in the evaluee's community when the referral questions call for in-person evaluation. The firm has no staffed office in Spring Lake or anywhere else in the metro, publishes no local street address, and does not pretend otherwise; travel for evaluations, depositions, and trial testimony is scheduled from the Kansas City home office and confirmed against counsel's dates at intake. That candor about presence is deliberate, because a report is only as credible as the practices behind it.
