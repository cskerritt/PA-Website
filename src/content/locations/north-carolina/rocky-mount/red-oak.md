## The county and courthouse behind a Red Oak claim

Red Oak lies in Nash County, so a state-court claim arising there is tried in
the Superior Court, with expert disclosure and deposition practice following
North Carolina's familiar course. Federal jurisdiction opens a separate
question of forum: North Carolina's district courts are the United States
District Court for the Eastern District of North Carolina, the United States
District Court for the Middle District of North Carolina, and the United
States District Court for the Western District of North Carolina, and the
controlling venue for any particular matter, together with whatever expert
registration or qualification that court requires, is identified during the
conflict check and intake. None of this alters the method. Every file opens
with a documented conflict check, a written scope, and a records request
tailored to the claims, so that both sides can trace each conclusion back to
the record.
