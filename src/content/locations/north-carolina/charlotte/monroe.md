Ask who retains a vocational expert in a Monroe case and the honest
answer is: both sides, for opposite reasons. Plaintiff counsel need a
defensible account of what an injury took from a client's working life;
defense counsel need a rigorous test of whether the claimed loss survives
scrutiny. Purinton Analytics is built to be retained by either, and that
double exposure disciplines the work. Every Monroe
[vocational opinion](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) and
[life care plan](/services/life-care-planning/) is prepared as though the
firm's next engagement will be on the other side of the same kind of
case, because it often is. The result is a report that neither inflates
nor minimizes, which is precisely what makes it useful at mediation and
durable at trial.

## Monroe's courts, from Union County to the federal bench

Monroe lies in Union County, and state-court matters involving Monroe
parties proceed in the Superior Court there. Federal captions are heard in
the United States District Court for the
Middle District of North Carolina, the United States District Court for
the Eastern District of North Carolina, or the United States District
Court for the Western District of North Carolina. The firm never assumes
which district controls; the governing forum, and any venue-specific
qualification requirement attached to it, is identified during the
conflict check and intake process. Regional coverage is described on the
[Charlotte metro page](/locations/north-carolina/charlotte/), and the
[North Carolina state page](/locations/north-carolina/) reaches the rest
of the state.

## An even-handed read of the Monroe labor market

Whichever side retains the firm, the labor market analysis is the same.
Monroe workers participate in the commuting economy of the metropolitan
area anchored by Charlotte, a market broad enough to cross the state line
into South Carolina, and an evaluee's residual earning capacity is
measured against that full market. Findings rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the
metropolitan area and for North Carolina statewide, on state workforce
agency labor market information, and on direct labor market research
where warranted. The report cites its quantities; the prose, like this
page, keeps the market's description qualitative.

## When one opinion is not enough

A Monroe damages case may call for several coordinated opinions: a
[medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/) to price
future care, and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. When the task is instead to answer an opposing
expert, [rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/)
subjects the served report to the same standards the firm applies to its
own work.
