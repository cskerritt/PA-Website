Consider the Kannapolis evaluee before anything else. Behind every
referral is a person whose working life has been interrupted, and the
quality of the eventual opinion depends on how carefully that person is
assessed: functional capacity, education and work history, transferable
skills, the realistic path back to employment or the documented reasons
there is none. Purinton Analytics structures its Kannapolis engagements
around that assessment. The
[vocational evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) or
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) appointment takes
place in the evaluee's own community, scheduled around treatment and
functional limits, while everything that does not require presence, the
records review, the counsel conferences, the drafting, proceeds remotely.
Plaintiff and defense counsel alike retain the firm on these terms.

## Measuring a Kannapolis career against the regional market

A vocational opinion is only as good as the labor market it is measured
against, and for Kannapolis that market is the commuting economy of the
metropolitan area anchored by Charlotte, which extends across the state
line into South Carolina. The firm draws on Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates for that metropolitan area and
for North Carolina statewide, on state workforce agency labor market
information, and on direct labor market research where the questions
warrant it, keeping every quantitative claim inside the sourced report
and describing the market qualitatively everywhere else.

## No local office, and why that is stated plainly

The firm has one staffed office, its home office in Kansas City, and it
publishes no Kannapolis address because it has none. That candor is
deliberate: an expert whose letterhead overstates his presence invites
the same skepticism toward his opinions. When a Kannapolis matter
requires deposition or trial attendance, travel is arranged from the
Kansas City home office against the dates the court has set, which is one
reason the firm asks for scheduling information at intake rather than
after the report is served. The engagement model is the same one that
carries the firm's [national practice](/locations/nationwide/).
