## Gastonia workers and the metro economy

Gastonia gives its name, alongside Charlotte and Concord, to the
metropolitan area it belongs to, and its workers participate in a labor
market that crosses the state line into South Carolina. An honest
vocational opinion about a Gastonia evaluee therefore looks at the
commuting economy as a whole: what occupations exist within reach, what
the evaluee's residual capacity permits, and what the transition from
pre-injury work realistically costs. The firm grounds those findings in
Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for
the metropolitan area and for North Carolina statewide, supplemented by
state workforce agency labor market information and direct labor market
research where warranted, with regional scale described only in
qualitative terms.

## What by-appointment service means here

There is no Gastonia office. The firm's single staffed office is its home
office in Kansas City, no local street address is published, and no
storefront is implied, because accuracy about presence belongs to the
same discipline as accuracy about damages. In practice this costs counsel
nothing: records analysis, scope conferences, and drafting are remote by
design, an in-person evaluation is scheduled in the evaluee's own
community when the referral questions call for one, and deposition or
trial appearances are planned as travel from the Kansas City home office
around the dates the court sets. Gastonia is served exactly as the rest
of the [national practice](/locations/nationwide/) is served.
