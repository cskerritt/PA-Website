## What the evaluee experiences

For the person being evaluated, the process is deliberately local. When
an in-person assessment is needed, it is scheduled in the evaluee's own
community, arranged around treatment and functional limitations, so the
burden of distance falls on the expert rather than the injured person.
The firm keeps no Salisbury office and publishes no local address; its
one staffed office is the home office in Kansas City, and deposition or
trial attendance is planned as travel from that home office against the
dates the court has fixed. Stating that plainly is part of the practice's
credibility, not a caveat to it.
