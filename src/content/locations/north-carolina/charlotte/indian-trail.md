Most Indian Trail damages cases turn on companion questions: what work
the injured person can still perform, and what the injury will cost in
care across a lifetime. A
[vocational assessment](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) answers
the first, a [life care plan](/services/life-care-planning/) answers the
second, and Purinton Analytics prepares both under the same discipline,
opening every engagement with a documented conflict check, a defined
scope, and a records request built for the specific claims. Referrals
reach the firm from both sides of the caption, and nothing in the method
shifts with the identity of the retaining party, because the reader
every opinion must finally satisfy sits at the opposing table.

## Union County courts for Indian Trail parties

Indian Trail lies in Union County, and state-court matters involving
Indian Trail parties are heard in the Superior Court there. On the
federal side, North Carolina's trial forums are the United States
District Court for the Eastern District of North Carolina, the United
States District Court for the Middle District of North Carolina, and the
United States District Court for the Western District of North Carolina.
The firm treats the controlling district as an intake question rather
than an assumption: it is confirmed during the conflict check and intake
process, together with any venue-specific qualification requirement.
Counsel whose dockets reach beyond the town will find the regional
practice described on the
[Charlotte metro page](/locations/north-carolina/charlotte/), while the
[North Carolina state page](/locations/north-carolina/) addresses
engagements statewide.

## The market an Indian Trail evaluee competes in

An Indian Trail evaluee does not compete for work inside town limits.
The relevant geography is the commuting economy of the
Charlotte-Concord-Gastonia metropolitan area, which spans North Carolina
and South Carolina, and conclusions about residual earning capacity are
drawn from that wider market. Findings rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the
metropolitan area and for North Carolina statewide, on state workforce
agency labor market information, and on direct labor market research
where the referral questions require it. Transferable skills are matched
against occupations the evaluee can realistically reach, quantities
appear only in the report with their sources cited, and prose
descriptions of the market remain qualitative.

## An address policy as strict as the opinions

The firm's letterhead lists no Indian Trail address, and the omission is
deliberate: the letterhead reports only what is true, exactly as the
opinions do. The sole staffed location is the home office in Kansas
City, and Indian Trail, like every other place the firm serves, is
served by appointment. Records analysis, conferences with counsel, and
drafting are handled remotely. When an assessment must happen in person,
the expert travels to the evaluee rather than the reverse, holding the
appointment where the evaluee lives and letting the demands of treatment
and physical tolerance set its timing. Deposition and trial appearances
are planned as travel from the Kansas City home office once the court's
dates are fixed, under the same model that carries the firm's
[national practice](/locations/nationwide/).

## Opinions that travel together

Because earning capacity and future care are companion questions, the
opinions that answer them often proceed together. A
[medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/) prices the
care a life care plan identifies, and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
bring in independent economist partners who perform and sign the
economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds
under one engagement structure. Counsel facing an adverse expert report
can instead refer it for
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) on the same
conflict-checked basis.

## Referring an Indian Trail matter

To begin, submit the caption or party names through the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/), summarize the claims, and flag
any disclosure, deposition, or trial dates already set. The firm
responds with a defined scope, a records request matched to the claims,
and a work plan keyed to the Union County or federal calendar, so both
of the case's central questions are answered on time and on the record.
