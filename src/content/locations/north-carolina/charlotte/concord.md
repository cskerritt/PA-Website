of the cities that gives the Charlotte, Concord and Gastonia region its
official designation, and that status says something useful about the
cases that arise here: Concord matters are not satellite matters. Counsel
litigating an injury, employment, or disability dispute with a Concord
party need a [vocational expert](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) or
[life care planner](/services/life-care-planning/) who treats the town as
a jurisdiction and an economy in its own right, not as a footnote to the
principal city. Purinton Analytics accepts referrals here from counsel on
either side of the caption, and every report is drafted on the assumption
that the lawyer who did not commission it will read it most closely.

## Cabarrus County courts and the North Carolina forums

Concord sits in Cabarrus County, and state-court matters involving
Concord parties proceed in the Superior Court there. North Carolina's
federal trial forums are the United States District Court for the Eastern
District of North Carolina, the United States District Court for the
Middle District of North Carolina, and the United States District Court
for the Western District of North Carolina; the district that controls a
particular caption, along with any venue-specific qualification
requirement, is identified and confirmed during the conflict check and
intake process. Counsel handling matters across the wider region can
consult the [Charlotte metro page](/locations/north-carolina/charlotte/),
and the [North Carolina state page](/locations/north-carolina/) covers
engagements statewide.

## How Concord fits the regional labor market

A Concord evaluee's earning capacity is assessed against the commuting
economy of the metropolitan area whose principal city is Charlotte, a
market that reaches across the state line into South Carolina, because
that is the geography in which the evaluee actually competes for work.
Vocational findings rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational
employment and wage estimates for the metropolitan area and for North
Carolina statewide, on state workforce agency labor market information,
and on direct labor market research where the referral questions warrant
it. Scale is always described qualitatively in the written opinion, and
transferable skills are analyzed against occupations that exist within
the evaluee's realistic commuting range rather than against a national
abstraction.

engagement structure. Counsel who already hold an opposing expert's

accepted on the same conflict-checked terms.
