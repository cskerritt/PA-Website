An expert opinion is not finished when it is served. Its real test comes
later, when opposing counsel reads it line by line, when a deposition
probes its sources, and when a motion challenges its method. Purinton
Analytics prepares every Huntersville
[vocational opinion](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) and
[life care plan](/services/life-care-planning/) for that afterlife. The
firm accepts referrals from plaintiff and defense counsel alike, and the
discipline is identical on either side of the caption: a documented
conflict check, a defined scope, a records request tailored to the
claims, and a written opinion in which every conclusion can be traced
back to its source when the harder questions arrive.

## Mecklenburg County venue and the federal districts

Huntersville sits in Mecklenburg County, and state-court matters
involving Huntersville parties proceed in the Superior Court there. For
federal captions, North Carolina's trial forums are the United States
District Court for the Western District of North Carolina, the United
States District Court for the Middle District of North Carolina, and the
United States District Court for the Eastern District of North Carolina.
The firm does not presume which forum controls a given case; the
governing district, along with any venue-specific qualification
requirement, is confirmed during the conflict check and intake process.
The [Charlotte metro page](/locations/north-carolina/charlotte/)
addresses the region as a whole, and the
[North Carolina state page](/locations/north-carolina/) covers matters
elsewhere in the state.

## Earning capacity measured against the metro economy

Huntersville belongs to the metropolitan area anchored by Charlotte, a
labor market that crosses the state line into South Carolina, and a
Huntersville evaluee's residual earning capacity is measured against
that market rather than the town alone. The firm identifies transferable
skills, tests them against occupations actually present within the
evaluee's realistic reach, and grounds its findings in Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the
metropolitan area and for North Carolina statewide, together with state
workforce agency labor market information and direct labor market
research where the referral questions warrant it. Quantities stay in the
report's cited tables; the prose keeps its description of the market
qualitative, because that restraint is part of what makes the report
durable.

## By-appointment service, stated plainly

The firm keeps no office in Huntersville and publishes no local street
address, because its only staffed office is the home office in Kansas
City and it does not pretend otherwise. That candor serves the same
purpose as the sourcing in the reports: an expert who is accurate about
small facts is easier to believe on large ones. Records review, counsel
conferences, and drafting proceed remotely; when the referral questions
call for an in-person evaluation, the appointment is arranged in the
evaluee's own community; and deposition or trial attendance is scheduled
as travel from the Kansas City home office against the dates the court
fixes, the same way the firm's
[nationwide engagements](/locations/nationwide/) are handled.
