## How a Clemmons Evaluation Proceeds

Most files open remotely. When the referral questions require an in-person assessment, the evaluation is conducted in or near the evaluee's own community, so functional observations reflect real conditions rather than a clinic far from home. Travel for those visits, and for depositions and trial testimony, is scheduled from the Kansas City home office.
