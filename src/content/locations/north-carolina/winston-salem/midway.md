When earning capacity is contested in a Midway case, the dispute is rarely about whether the evaluee was hurt; it is about what the injury means for work, and the parties usually hold opposite views. Resolving that dispute takes an opinion each side must take seriously, which is the standard Purinton Analytics writes to in its [vocational expert](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) and [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) engagements for this Davidson County community. The firm is retained by plaintiffs and by defendants, and the analysis does not change with the client: the record, the testing, and the labor market data lead, and the opinion follows.

## Testing the Other Side's Numbers

Because contested capacity is the usual posture, [rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) is a frequent assignment in matters like these. The review examines an opposing expert's report against the methodology the field publishes: whether the transferable skills analysis follows from the actual work history, whether the labor market sources are current and correctly applied, and whether the conclusions would survive the same scrutiny the reviewing expert's own reports are built for. Findings are reported in writing, whether they help or hurt the retaining side, because a review that flatters the client is worthless in a deposition.
