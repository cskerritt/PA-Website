When an injury is catastrophic, the largest damages question in the file is often not what the evaluee once earned but what their future care will require, and what it will cost to provide that care where the person actually lives. A [life care plan](/services/life-care-planning/) answers that question item by item, sourced and priced, and it frequently becomes the anchor document around which the vocational and economic analysis is built. Purinton Analytics prepares life care plans and [vocational expert](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) opinions for Walkertown matters on behalf of plaintiff and defense counsel, holding both to the same publication standard: suitable for the opposing side to read line by line.

## The Vocational Side of the File

Where earning capacity is disputed, the vocational analysis for a Walkertown evaluee rests on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Winston-Salem metropolitan area and for North Carolina statewide, supplemented by state workforce agency labor market information and direct labor market research where warranted. The town's workforce participates in the regional economy, and the opinion is framed against that market with the evaluee's actual access to it examined rather than assumed. ## Care Needs Priced for the Evaluee's Community

A life care plan is only useful if its recommendations can actually be obtained by a person living in Walkertown, in Forsyth County, within the Winston-Salem metropolitan area. The plan therefore works from the treating record and evaluee assessment outward to providers and services realistically available to the evaluee, with [medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/) methods applied where the referral is limited to discrete future-care items rather than a comprehensive plan. Every recommendation carries its clinical foundation, and every cost carries its source.
