What can this evaluee still do for pay? Nearly every earning capacity dispute reduces to that question, and for a King case the answer begins with the work history, not the town map. Purinton Analytics builds its [vocational expert](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) opinions for King matters on transferable skills analysis: the occupations the evaluee has actually held, the skills those occupations conferred, and the work that remains realistically open after injury. [Life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) proceeds under the same discipline when future care is at issue. Plaintiff and defense counsel receive the same analysis, because the method does not change with the identity of the retaining side.

## Stokes County Venue and the Federal Districts

King is a Stokes County town, and state civil matters arising here proceed in the Superior Court serving Stokes County. Federal filings go to one of North Carolina's district courts: the United States District Court for the Eastern District of North Carolina, the United States District Court for the Middle District of North Carolina, or the United States District Court for the Western District of North Carolina. The controlling district for a particular case, along with any registration or qualification requirement that forum imposes, is confirmed during the conflict check and intake, and the disclosure package is then assembled for that forum from the start. Statewide venue questions are treated on the [North Carolina page](/locations/north-carolina/).

## From Work History to Labor Market

Transferable skills analysis is only half of the opinion; the other half is the market those skills meet. King lies within the Winston-Salem metropolitan area, and vocational findings for its residents rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Winston-Salem metropolitan area and for North Carolina statewide, on state workforce agency labor market information, and on direct labor market research when a disputed occupation requires verification. The report connects each step, from documented work history to identified occupations to cited market data, so the reader can follow the chain without taking anything on faith. The regional practice this analysis sits within is described on the [Winston-Salem metro page](/locations/north-carolina/winston-salem/).

## Evaluating a King Resident

The firm keeps no office in King and claims none; service is by appointment. What stays fixed is the sequence, not the setting. A file opens on paper, with the records assembled and read before anyone is interviewed. Secure video then carries the structured vocational interview and the standardized testing, under protocols whose validity has been established for remote administration. Where the referral questions demand physical presence, the trip runs toward Stokes County, not away from it: the expert travels, and the evaluee stays put. Testimony dates, deposition or trial, go on the calendar at intake, with travel arranged from the Kansas City home office.
