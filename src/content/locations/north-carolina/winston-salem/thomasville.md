A scheduling order sets the disclosure dates, discovery narrows what is genuinely in dispute, and the time available for a vocational opinion or a life care plan that can survive scrutiny is defined by the court, not by the expert's convenience. Purinton Analytics structures its work for Thomasville matters around that reality: intake establishes the operative deadlines first, and the [vocational expert](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) or [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) engagement is scoped so the written report is complete, documented, and defensible when it is due, whether the client is plaintiff or defense.
