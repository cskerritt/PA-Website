Purinton Analytics is a [national practice](/locations/nationwide/), and a
consequence of that structure is that a case in Goldsboro receives the same
expert resources as a case in a major metropolitan market: the same
methodology, the same sourcing standards, and the same availability for
testimony. Plaintiff and defense counsel in the Goldsboro metro can retain
the firm for [vocational expert](/services/vocational-expert-witness/)
evaluations of employability and earning capacity,
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) in catastrophic injury
cases, and [medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/)
where the dispute centers on future care costs.

## The Wayne County venue footprint

The Goldsboro metropolitan area consists of Wayne County, and engagements
are accepted for matters in the Superior Court there and in the federal
courts that serve North Carolina: the United States District Court for the
Eastern District of North Carolina, the United States District Court for the
Middle District of North Carolina, and the United States District Court for
the Western District of North Carolina. Counsel whose dockets extend past
the metro can review statewide engagement terms on the
[North Carolina page](/locations/north-carolina/). Deposition and trial
testimony are provided in person or by remote appearance where the court
permits.

## What service by appointment looks like here

Goldsboro meetings are by appointment. There is no staffed Purinton
Analytics office in the metro and no published street address for this
location, because a practice that demands precision from its reports owes
the same precision in describing its own footprint. In its place counsel
receive a clear working arrangement: evaluations conducted in the evaluee's
own community or by secure video under publisher protocols, records and
conference work handled remotely, and travel from the Kansas City home
office scheduled when in-person evaluation, deposition, or trial dates make
presence necessary.

## Local wage data, nationally consistent method

Employability and earning capacity findings for Goldsboro evaluees draw on
Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for
the Goldsboro metropolitan area and for North Carolina statewide, alongside
state workforce agency labor market information and direct labor market
research, including employer contact and job posting analysis, when the
referral questions call for evidence of actual hiring. The method is the
same one applied across the national practice; only the data changes with
the venue, and every source is cited so opposing counsel can verify each
step. Related engagements are available where the case requires them:
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) of an opposing
expert's report, and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Engaging the firm for a Goldsboro case

Goldsboro referrals commonly arise in personal injury, medical malpractice,
wrongful death, employment, and disability matters, from either side of the
caption. Every engagement begins with a documented conflict check, a defined
scope, and a records request tailored to the claims, so the opinion is built
on a record both parties can examine.

To open a file, [request a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) with the caption
or party names and note any imminent disclosure, deposition, or trial dates.
CVs and fee information follow promptly, and scheduling is set against the
litigation calendar rather than promised loosely and revised later.
