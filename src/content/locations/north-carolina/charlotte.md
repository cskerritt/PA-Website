The Charlotte-Concord-Gastonia metro crosses a state line, and litigation
here follows the same path: a caption filed in North Carolina may involve an
evaluee who lives and works on the South Carolina side, or the reverse.
Purinton Analytics serves plaintiff and defense counsel throughout this
footprint with [vocational expert evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. The state line changes the venue analysis; it does not
change the methodology, the report standard, or the point of contact.

## Venue footprint on both sides of the line

On the North Carolina side, engagements are accepted for matters in the
Superior Court of Mecklenburg County and the Superior Courts of Anson
County and of Cabarrus, Gaston, Iredell, Lincoln, Rowan, and Union counties, and in the
state's federal forums, including the United States District Court for the
Western District of North Carolina, the United States District Court for the
Middle District of North Carolina, and the United States District Court for
the Eastern District of North Carolina. The metro's South Carolina counties,
York County, Lancaster County, and Chester County, are served through the
same intake, with any venue-specific registration or qualification
requirement identified and confirmed during the conflict check and intake
process. Counsel elsewhere in the state can start from the
[North Carolina practice page](/locations/north-carolina/).

## Meetings by appointment, not a storefront

Charlotte meetings are by appointment. The firm keeps no staffed office in
the metro and publishes no street address for this location, because
accuracy about presence is part of the same discipline that governs its
reports. Most engagements begin remotely: records review against a defined
scope, structured interviews, and standardized testing under publisher
protocols where remote administration is supported. When the referral
questions call for in-person evaluation, or when deposition and trial dates
require appearance, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office and
planned around the court's calendar rather than the firm's.

## Labor market research for a cross-state economy

Because Charlotte-area evaluees may reasonably seek work on either side of
the state line, vocational findings rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates for the Charlotte metropolitan
area and for each state the metro touches, supplemented by state workforce
agency labor market information and direct employer contact where the
referral questions warrant it. Life care plans and medical cost projections
are priced to the evaluee's own community and provider market, not to a
national average, and every source is cited in the written report so that
opposing counsel can trace each figure to its origin.

## Starting a Charlotte engagement

Whether the matter is venued in state or federal court, in North Carolina or
South Carolina, the engagement begins the same way: submit the caption or
party names through the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and note any
imminent disclosure, deposition, or trial dates. Conflicts are confirmed
before any substantive discussion of the case. Counsel with matters beyond
this metro, or with a docket that spans several venues, can review the
firm's [national coverage](/locations/nationwide/) for how the same
remote-first structure serves engagements across the country.
