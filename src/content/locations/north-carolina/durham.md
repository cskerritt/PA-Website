Most of the work in a Durham-Chapel Hill engagement happens long before
anyone meets in person. Records are requested against a defined scope and
reviewed remotely. Interviews are conducted by video where the referral
questions permit. Standardized testing follows publisher protocols,
including remote administration where the publisher supports it. Purinton
Analytics has built its practice around that sequence, which is how the firm
delivers [vocational expert evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
to plaintiff and defense counsel across the metro, with independent
economist partners performing and signing the economic analysis while the
vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement structure.

Engagements are accepted for matters venued in the Superior Court of Durham

in the state's federal forums, including the United States District Court
for the Middle District of North Carolina, the United States District Court

[North Carolina practice page](/locations/north-carolina/).

## When remote is not enough

The remote-first default is a starting point, not a limitation. When an
evaluation should be conducted in the evaluee's presence, when a treating
team conference warrants attendance, or when deposition and trial dates
require the expert in the room, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City
home office. What the firm does not do is pretend to be local: there is no
staffed Purinton Analytics office in Durham or Chapel Hill, no published
street address, and no suggestion otherwise. Meetings are by appointment, and
counsel are told exactly where the work will be performed and why.

## Labor market evidence for Durham-area evaluees

Employability and earning capacity opinions for evaluees in the metro rest
on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates
for the Durham-Chapel Hill metropolitan area and for North Carolina
statewide, together with state workforce agency labor market information
and direct labor market research where the referral questions warrant it.
Life care plans and medical cost projections are priced to the evaluee's
own community and provider market. Every data source appears in the written
report with enough specificity for opposing counsel to retrace it, because
an opinion that cannot be checked is an opinion that cannot be defended.
The same sourcing discipline applies whether the retention comes from the
plaintiff or the defense.

## From inquiry to engagement

The path into a Durham engagement runs through the conflict check. Submit
the caption or party names through the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/), identify any imminent disclosure,
deposition, or trial dates, and expect substantive case discussion only
after conflicts have cleared. The process is the same for plaintiff and
defense referrals, and the same again for counsel whose matters sit far
from this metro; the firm's [nationwide practice](/locations/nationwide/)
shows how the identical remote-first structure serves any venue in the
country.
