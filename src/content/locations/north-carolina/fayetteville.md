Disclosure deadlines, deposition notices, and trial settings drive damages
work more than anything else, and Fayetteville counsel usually call with a
date already on the calendar. Purinton Analytics structures its intake
around that reality: availability is assessed against the actual deadlines
before an engagement is accepted, never after. Within that frame, the firm
provides plaintiff and defense counsel in the Fayetteville metro with
[vocational expert evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## The metro's venues

Engagements are accepted for matters venued in the Superior Court of
Cumberland County and the Superior Court of Hoke County, and in the federal
courts of the state, including the United States District Court for the
Eastern District of North Carolina, the United States District Court for
the Middle District of North Carolina, and the United States District Court
for the Western District of North Carolina. Any venue-specific registration
or qualification requirement is identified and confirmed during the
conflict check and intake process, and the firm's coverage of the rest of
the state appears on the
[North Carolina practice page](/locations/north-carolina/).

## By appointment, with the schedule stated plainly

The firm keeps no staffed office in Fayetteville and publishes no street
address for this location; meetings happen by appointment, and counsel are
told at intake exactly how the work will be scheduled. The default sequence
is remote and therefore fast to start: records review against a defined
scope, structured interviews by video where appropriate, and standardized
testing under publisher protocols where remote administration is supported.
When the referral questions require an in-person evaluation, or when a
deposition or trial requires attendance, travel is arranged from the Kansas
City home office and committed to counsel in writing, so the expert's
calendar never becomes the case's surprise.

## Labor market research for Fayetteville-area evaluees

Employability and earning capacity opinions rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Fayetteville
metropolitan area and for North Carolina statewide, together with state
workforce agency labor market information and direct labor market research
where the referral questions warrant it. Life care plans and medical cost
projections are priced to the evaluee's own community and provider market
rather than to national averages. Every source is cited in the written
report, in the expectation that opposing counsel will check each of them,
as they should. A method that survives that checking is the only kind worth
offering under oath.

## Securing dates and clearing conflicts

Because scheduling is usually the pressing question, the fastest path is
also the required path: submit the caption or party names through the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and state any imminent disclosure,
deposition, or trial dates in the same message. Conflicts are cleared
before substantive discussion, and availability is confirmed against the
dates counsel provide. For matters outside this metro or outside North
Carolina, the firm's [nationwide practice](/locations/nationwide/) runs on
the same intake and the same calendar discipline.
