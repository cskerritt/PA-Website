An evaluee in the Asheville metro should be assessed against the labor
market where they actually live and the providers who will actually treat
them, not against an abstraction. Purinton Analytics anchors its
Asheville-area work in that principle, offering plaintiff and defense
counsel [vocational expert evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. The evaluee's own community sets the frame; the
firm's documentation standard does the rest.

## Venue coverage in the metro

Engagements are accepted for matters venued in the Superior Court of
Buncombe County and the Superior Courts of Henderson County and Madison
County, and in the state's federal forums, including the United States
District Court for the Western District of North Carolina, the United
States District Court for the Middle District of North Carolina, and the
United States District Court for the Eastern District of North Carolina.
Any venue-specific registration or qualification requirement is identified
and confirmed during the conflict check and intake process. The firm's
coverage across the rest of the state is collected on the
[North Carolina practice page](/locations/north-carolina/).

## Presence by appointment, distance by design

Purinton Analytics operates from a single home office in Kansas City and
keeps no staffed office in Asheville; no street address is published for
this location because none exists to publish. Meetings are by appointment.
In practice, most Asheville engagements begin and largely proceed remotely:
records review against a defined scope, structured interviews by video, and
standardized testing under publisher protocols where remote administration
is supported. When the referral questions require evaluating the evaluee in
person, in their own community, or when deposition and trial dates require
the expert's presence, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home
office and confirmed with counsel well ahead of the dates that matter.

## Labor market and cost research for Asheville-area evaluees

Employability and earning capacity findings rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Asheville
metropolitan area and for North Carolina statewide, supplemented by state
workforce agency labor market information and direct labor market research
where the referral questions warrant it. Life care plans and medical cost
projections are priced through provider-specific inquiry in the evaluee's
own market, since local care availability and cost can differ from
statewide patterns in either direction. The written report shows the
sources and the reasoning so that either side of the caption can test both,
step by step, from the raw data to the stated opinion.

## How engagements begin

The door into an Asheville engagement is the conflict check. Submit the
caption or party names through the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and identify any imminent disclosure,
deposition, or trial dates. Conflicts are resolved before the case is
discussed in substance, and the process is identical for plaintiff and
defense referrals. Counsel with matters beyond this metro can review the
firm's [national coverage](/locations/nationwide/) for the same structure
applied nationwide.
