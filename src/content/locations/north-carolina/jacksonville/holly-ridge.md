## Onslow County venue for Holly Ridge matters

Holly Ridge sits in Onslow County, and state-court claims of consequence
proceed in the Superior Court, North Carolina's trial court of general
jurisdiction. Matters filed federally may proceed in the United States
District Court for the Eastern District of North Carolina, the United
States District Court for the Middle District of North Carolina, or the
United States District Court for the Western District of North Carolina;
the district that controls a particular Holly Ridge case, along with any
venue-specific qualification or registration requirement, is identified
and confirmed during the conflict check and intake process. The written
opinion is built to the same standard for either forum, with methodology,
sources, and reasoning documented so that cross-examination has a full
record to test.

## The labor market around Holly Ridge

Vocational findings for a Holly Ridge evaluee rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the
Jacksonville, NC metro area and for North Carolina statewide, state
workforce agency labor market information, and direct labor market
research where the referral questions warrant it. Because Holly Ridge is
a small town, the analysis does not treat it as a closed market: the
relevant employment base is defined by the data for Onslow County and
the wider metro that includes Jacksonville, the principal city, rather
than by assumptions about where residents happen to work. The
transferable skills analysis is framed the same way: what work the
documented market actually offers a person with this evaluee's
education, history, and medically supported restrictions. Earning
capacity conclusions are stated qualitatively in the report's reasoning
and quantitatively only where the underlying estimates support them, and
every figure that does appear in a report is tied to a cited source.

## Evaluating a Holly Ridge resident

Most engagements begin remotely. Records are collected under a request
tailored to the claims, the clinical and work history is analyzed, and
interviews and standardized testing are administered under publisher
protocols that permit remote use, which spares an injured or ill evaluee
unnecessary travel. When the referral questions call for an in-person
evaluation in the evaluee's own community, or when deposition or trial
requires presence, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office.
The firm keeps no office in Holly Ridge and publishes no local street
address; saying so plainly is part of the same discipline that governs
the reports themselves. Counsel deal with one expert team and one
engagement structure from intake through testimony, including
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.
