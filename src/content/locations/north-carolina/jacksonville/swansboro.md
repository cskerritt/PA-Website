## Where a Swansboro case is heard

Swansboro lies in Onslow County. Civil claims of consequence in state
court proceed in the Superior Court, the trial court of general
jurisdiction, while federal matters in North Carolina may be heard in
the United States District Court for the Eastern District of North
Carolina, the United States District Court for the Middle District of
North Carolina, or the United States District Court for the Western
District of North Carolina. Which forum controls a given Swansboro
filing, and any qualification or registration requirement that forum
imposes on a testifying expert, is identified and confirmed during the
conflict check and intake process. The report itself does not change
with the courthouse: it is written once, to a standard that assumes the
opposing side will read every line.

## Reading the labor market from Swansboro

Swansboro is a small town within the Jacksonville, NC metro area, and
the labor market that matters to a Swansboro evaluee is defined by the
data rather than the town line. Vocational conclusions draw on Bureau of
Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the
Jacksonville, NC metro area, whose principal city is Jacksonville, and
for North Carolina statewide, along with state workforce agency labor
market information and, where warranted, direct contact with employers
about actual hiring requirements. The
question the analysis answers is concrete: given this evaluee's
education, work history, and medically supported restrictions, what
employment remains realistically available to them, and what does that
availability mean for earning capacity.

## Meeting the evaluee where they are

The firm has no office in Swansboro and lists no local street address;
every meeting is by appointment, and being candid about that is part of
the credibility the reports depend on. In practice, most of an
engagement proceeds remotely: records review, structured interviews,
and standardized testing administered under publisher protocols that
support remote use. When an in-person evaluation in the evaluee's own
community serves the referral questions better, or when a deposition or
trial date requires attendance, travel is arranged from the Kansas City
home office. Where a matter also needs economic damages quantified, the
firm offers
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure, so the pieces of the damages case fit together
rather than arriving from separate silos.
