Expert work in the Rocky Mount metro is usually retained against a calendar:
a disclosure deadline, a deposition setting, a mediation date. Purinton
Analytics builds its engagements around those dates from the outset, fixing
the evaluation schedule and report timeline at intake so counsel are never
guessing where the opinion stands. For plaintiff and defense counsel in
Edgecombe County and Nash County, the firm provides
[vocational expert](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) evaluations of
employability and earning capacity,
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[expert testimony and litigation consulting](/services/expert-testimony-litigation-consulting/)
through disclosure, deposition, and trial.

## Venues on both sides of the metro

Engagements are accepted for matters in the Superior Court in Edgecombe
County and Nash County and in the federal courts serving the state: the
United States District Court for the Eastern District of North Carolina, the
United States District Court for the Middle District of North Carolina, and
the United States District Court for the Western District of North Carolina.
Cases that reach beyond the Rocky Mount metro are handled on the same terms
described on the [North Carolina page](/locations/north-carolina/).
Testimony is provided in person or by remote appearance where the court
permits, and every report is written so that its reasoning holds up in
whichever forum hears the case.

## No storefront, and no pretense of one

The firm keeps no staffed office in Rocky Mount and publishes no street
address for this location. Meetings are by appointment, and that is said
plainly because accuracy about presence is part of the same discipline that
governs the reports themselves. Counsel instead get predictable logistics:
conflict checks answered promptly, evaluations held in the evaluee's own
community or by secure video under publisher protocols, and travel from the
Kansas City home office scheduled whenever in-person evaluation, deposition,
or trial attendance is required.

## Data behind the earning capacity opinion

Vocational conclusions for Rocky Mount evaluees are grounded in Bureau of
Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Rocky
Mount metropolitan area and for North Carolina statewide, together with
state workforce agency labor market information and direct labor market
research, including employer contact and job posting analysis, when the
referral questions require evidence of actual hiring. Each source is cited
in the report so opposing counsel can trace the analysis end to end. Where
the damages case also needs economic modeling,
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
bring in independent economist partners who perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure, and
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) assignments test
an opposing expert's figures against the same sourcing standard.
