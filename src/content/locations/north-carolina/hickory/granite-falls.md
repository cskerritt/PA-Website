The test of an expert report is not who retained it but whether it survives the reader who wants it to fail. Purinton Analytics writes every Granite Falls opinion for that reader. Retained by plaintiff or by defense in matters involving this Caldwell County town, the firm produces [vocational expert](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) and [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) reports whose assumptions are stated, whose sources are cited, and whose conclusions stop where the evidence stops, which is also why the firm is regularly asked to provide [rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) of opposing opinions.

## What that discipline looks like in practice

A Granite Falls engagement starts with a defined scope and a records request matched to the claims, so the opinion rests on documents both sides can examine. Interview notes, test selections, and labor market contacts are kept in a file built for production. Where the analysis depends on judgment, the report says so and explains the basis; where it depends on data, the data is identified down to the release. Counsel on either side gets the same product, because the firm's value to both is that it cannot be accused of writing for either.

## Grounding the labor market analysis

Granite Falls is a small town whose residents work across the Hickory metropolitan market that also serves Lenoir, Sawmills, Hudson, and the wider region. Employability and earning-capacity findings draw on occupational employment and wage estimates from the Bureau of Labor Statistics for the Hickory metropolitan area and for North Carolina, on state workforce agency labor market information, and on direct labor market research when the case turns on actual openings.
