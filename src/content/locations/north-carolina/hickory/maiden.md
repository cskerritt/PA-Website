Retention is a process, not a phone call. For counsel with a matter arising in Maiden, a small Catawba County town in the Hickory metropolitan area, Purinton Analytics runs that process the same way in every case: conflict check, written scope, records request, analysis, report, testimony. Each step is documented so that the file itself demonstrates the independence the opinion claims. The firm accepts [vocational expert](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) and [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) engagements from plaintiff and defense counsel in Maiden matters, and supports retained experts on the other side of the aisle through [rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/).

## Before anything else: the conflict check

Nothing substantive is discussed until the caption or party names have been screened and clearance documented. Counsel submits the parties through the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/); the firm responds with clearance status, a proposed scope, and a schedule keyed to the case's deadlines. This order of operations protects both the client and the opinion, and it is never skipped for a small case or a familiar referrer.

## Next, venue and scope

Maiden matters tried in state court proceed in the Superior Court for Catawba County. If the case is federal, North Carolina's available forums are the United States District Court for the Western District of North Carolina, the United States District Court for the Middle District of North Carolina, and the United States District Court for the Eastern District of North Carolina, and the district actually controlling the matter is identified during intake, along with any qualification requirement that follows from it. Scope is written to the venue's disclosure rules; the statewide picture is on the [North Carolina page](/locations/north-carolina/).

## The analysis a Maiden case requires

A Maiden evaluee's employment prospects are regional, shaped by the Hickory metropolitan labor market shared with Newton, Conover, and the neighboring communities. The firm's vocational findings are built on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for that metropolitan area and for the state, on state workforce agency labor market information, and, where warranted, on direct labor market research documented contact by contact. Care-planning opinions follow the same sourcing discipline. Every figure is attributed and market scale is described qualitatively and accurately.

## Evaluation and testimony logistics

The firm keeps no office in Maiden and says so; its staffed location is the Kansas City home office. Interviews and conferences run remotely over secure channels, in-person evaluations happen in the evaluee's own community, and deposition or trial appearances in Catawba County are met by scheduled travel with dates locked at intake. The [Hickory metro page](/locations/north-carolina/hickory/) shows how this works across the region, and the [nationwide page](/locations/nationwide/) shows the same system at national scale. Where the damages must be computed, independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis inside [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) coordinated by the firm.

The process starts whenever the parties' names arrive. Send them through the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) with the court, a short description of the claims, and the dates that matter; everything after that is confirmed in writing at intake.
