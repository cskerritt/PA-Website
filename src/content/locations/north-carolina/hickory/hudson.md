A referral out of Hudson can pose a single question or several. Some matters involving this small Caldwell County town in the Hickory metropolitan area turn on an opinion about earning capacity alone; others, typically catastrophic injury and long-term disability cases, require a life care plan, a projection of future medical costs, and an economic damages analysis built on both. Purinton Analytics scopes each Hudson engagement to the questions the case actually presents. The firm offers [vocational expert witness](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) analysis, [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and [medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/) to plaintiff and defense counsel alike, and it adds nothing to a file that the referral questions do not require.

## Scoping the engagement in writing

Scope is settled before analysis begins. At intake, the firm and retaining counsel define the questions the opinion will answer, and that written scope becomes part of the engagement record, so the report's boundaries are visible to the court and to the lawyer who will cross-examine it. When the damages picture requires economic analysis, the firm assembles [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement structure. A single scope, records pipeline, and calendar then serve the whole damages case, whichever side of the caption retained it.

## Caldwell County courts and the federal forums

State-court matters arising in Hudson are heard in the Superior Court for Caldwell County, and reports are prepared with that court's disclosure practice in view. Federal litigation in North Carolina proceeds in the United States District Court for the Western District of North Carolina, the United States District Court for the Middle District of North Carolina, or the United States District Court for the Eastern District of North Carolina; the controlling district for a given Hudson matter, along with any venue-specific registration or qualification requirement, is identified and confirmed during the conflict check and intake process. ## The labor market a Hudson evaluee actually faces

Hudson sits inside the Hickory metropolitan commuting economy, alongside Lenoir, Granite Falls, Sawmills, and the metro's other communities, and a Hudson worker's realistic employment options are defined by that regional market rather than by the town line. Vocational findings for a Hudson evaluee therefore rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Hickory metropolitan area and for North Carolina statewide, on state workforce agency labor market information, and on direct labor market research where the referral questions warrant it. Every figure in the report carries a citation, and the market is described as the data show it, neither inflated nor diminished.

## Logistics for counsel and evaluee

The firm keeps no office in Hudson and publishes no local address, because it has none; meetings are by appointment, and most of the engagement, records analysis, structured interviews, and counsel conferences included, is conducted remotely over secure channels. When the referral calls for an in-person evaluation, the evaluee is seen in his or her own community, and when a deposition or trial requires presence in Caldwell County, the expert appears in person, with travel scheduled from the Kansas City home office.
