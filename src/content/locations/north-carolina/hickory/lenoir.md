## Reading the labor market around Lenoir

A vocational opinion for a Lenoir evaluee has to reflect the employment realities of a small city inside a larger regional market. The firm grounds its findings in occupational employment and wage estimates published by the Bureau of Labor Statistics for the Hickory metropolitan area and for North Carolina statewide, supplemented by state workforce agency labor market information and, where the referral questions warrant it, direct employer research. The analysis addresses what work exists within a reasonable commuting range of Lenoir, not what exists somewhere in the state, and every source is cited so the opinion can be tested on cross-examination. When damages must be quantified, the firm offers [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement structure.
