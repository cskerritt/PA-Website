The referral questions that arrive from Raleigh counsel tend to be specific:
what could this evaluee have earned but for the injury, what can they earn
now, what will their care cost, and how do those opinions hold up under
cross-examination. Purinton Analytics answers those questions for plaintiff
and defense counsel alike through
[vocational expert evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. Every opinion is built to be examined by the other
side of the caption, because that is where every opinion eventually ends up.

## Courts served in the Raleigh-Cary metro

Engagements are accepted for matters venued in the Superior Court of Wake
County and the Superior Courts of Johnston County and Franklin County, and
in the federal courts of the state, including the United States District
Court for the Eastern District of North Carolina, the United States District
Court for the Middle District of North Carolina, and the United States
District Court for the Western District of North Carolina. Counsel whose
matters sit outside this metro will find the firm's full in-state coverage
described on the [North Carolina page](/locations/north-carolina/), and the
intake process is identical regardless of venue: conflicts are cleared,
scope is defined, and opinions issue only after the record supports them.

## How a by-appointment practice works here

There is no staffed Purinton Analytics office in Raleigh, and the firm
publishes no street address for this location. Meetings happen by
appointment, and most of the work happens before any meeting is needed:
records are reviewed remotely against a defined scope, interviews are
conducted by video where appropriate, and standardized testing follows
publisher protocols, including remote administration where the publisher
supports it. When the case calls for in-person evaluation or when deposition
and trial dates require presence, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City
home office. Counsel receive the same report standard and the same point of
contact whether the work is performed on site or at a distance.

## Grounding opinions in the local labor market

Vocational findings for Raleigh-area evaluees rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Raleigh-Cary
metropolitan area and for North Carolina statewide, together with state
workforce agency labor market information and direct labor market research
where the referral questions warrant it. Life care plans and medical cost
projections are priced to the evaluee's own provider market rather than to
national averages, and the written report cites every source so that both
sides can verify the path from data to conclusion.

## Beginning an engagement

The engagement starts with a conflict check, not a sales call. Submit the
caption or party names through the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/), note any imminent disclosure,
deposition, or trial dates, and expect a prompt answer on availability.
Substantive discussion of the case waits until conflicts are cleared. For
counsel managing dockets that reach beyond this metro or beyond the state,
the firm's [nationwide practice](/locations/nationwide/) applies this same
structure to engagements in any venue.
