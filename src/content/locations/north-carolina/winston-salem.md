Damages cases in Winston-Salem often need more than a vocational opinion
standing alone: the earning capacity analysis, the life care plan, and the
economic loss calculation have to fit together without contradicting each
other on the stand. That is the problem
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
are built to solve, with independent economist partners performing and
signing the economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning work
proceeds under one engagement structure. Purinton Analytics offers that
coordination, along with standalone
[vocational expert evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) and
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), to plaintiff and
defense counsel throughout the Winston-Salem metro.

## The metro's courts and counties

Engagements are accepted for matters venued in the Superior Court of
Forsyth County and the Superior Courts of Davidson County and of Davie,
Stokes, and Yadkin counties, and in the federal courts of the state, including the
United States District Court for the Middle District of North Carolina, the
United States District Court for the Western District of North Carolina,
and the United States District Court for the Eastern District of North
Carolina. Any venue-specific registration or qualification requirement is
identified and confirmed during the conflict check and intake process, so
that admissibility questions are surfaced at intake rather than discovered
at disclosure. The firm's broader in-state footprint, and the metro pages
that sit within it, are collected on the
[North Carolina practice page](/locations/north-carolina/).

## Appointment-based presence, honestly described

Purinton Analytics keeps no staffed office in Winston-Salem and publishes no
street address for this location. Saying so plainly is deliberate: the same
discipline that keeps invented facts out of a report keeps invented offices
off a website. What counsel get instead is a practice built for distance.
Records review, structured interviews, and standardized testing under
publisher protocols proceed remotely wherever remote administration is
supported, and when the referral questions require an in-person evaluation
or a courtroom appearance, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home
office and confirmed with counsel before deadlines harden. The report
standard and the point of contact stay constant either way, and counsel are
told at intake which parts of the work will be performed remotely and which
in person.

## Researching the local labor market

Vocational findings for evaluees in the metro rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Winston-Salem
metropolitan area and for North Carolina statewide, supplemented by state
workforce agency labor market information and direct labor market research
where warranted. Life care plans and medical cost projections are priced to
the evaluee's own provider market rather than to a national average. Because
the coordinated engagement structure keeps the economist partners working
from the same vocational and care-planning foundations, the damages
presentation reaches counsel internally consistent, with every source cited
for the other side to test.

## Opening a file

An engagement begins when conflicts clear, and conflicts clear fastest when
counsel provide the caption or party names up front. Use the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and note any imminent disclosure,
deposition, or trial dates so availability can be confirmed against the
real calendar. The same intake serves plaintiff and defense referrals
without distinction. Counsel with matters beyond this metro or outside
North Carolina can review the firm's
[national coverage](/locations/nationwide/) to see how the identical
structure travels.
