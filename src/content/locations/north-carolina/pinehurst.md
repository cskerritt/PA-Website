In a catastrophic injury case, the life care plan often becomes the document
the whole damages dispute turns on, and it deserves to be built accordingly.
Purinton Analytics serves counsel in the Pinehurst-Southern Pines metro with
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) grounded in the medical
record and priced from cited sources,
[medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/) where the
question is narrower, and
[vocational expert](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) evaluations of
employability and earning capacity, for plaintiff and defense engagements
alike. When the case also requires economic modeling,
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
bring in independent economist partners who perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Moore County courts and beyond

The Pinehurst-Southern Pines metropolitan area consists of Moore County, and

Middle District of North Carolina, the United States District Court for the

remote appearance where the court permits.

## An accurate account of local presence

Meetings in Pinehurst and Southern Pines are by appointment. The firm has no
staffed office in Moore County and publishes no street address for this
location; an expert practice that expects its opinions to be checked should
describe its own presence just as carefully. Counsel work instead with a
defined process: conflict check answered promptly, evaluation conducted in
the evaluee's own community or by secure video under publisher protocols,
and travel from the Kansas City home office scheduled when in-person
evaluation, deposition, or trial attendance is required. Between those
dates, records review and counsel conferences proceed remotely without loss
of momentum.

## Earning capacity evidence for this market

Vocational findings for evaluees in the metro rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the
Pinehurst-Southern Pines area and for North Carolina statewide, supported by
state workforce agency labor market information and direct labor market
research, including employer contact and job posting analysis, when the
referral questions require proof of actual hiring. Each source appears in
the report so that opposing counsel can trace every figure to its origin.
The same scrutiny is available in reverse:
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) engagements
evaluate whether an opposing expert's plan or earning capacity opinion is
supported by its own cited data.
