## Evaluation logistics for an Elon evaluee

Most engagements begin remotely. Records are gathered under a request
tailored to the claims, the clinical and employment history is analyzed,
and interviews and standardized testing proceed under publisher
protocols that support remote administration, which spares an injured
or ill evaluee unnecessary travel. When the referral questions are
better served by an in-person evaluation in the evaluee's own community,
that visit is scheduled from the Kansas City home office, as is
attendance at deposition or trial. The firm keeps no office in Elon and
publishes no local street address; stating that plainly is part of the
same discipline that keeps the reports accurate.

## The county and the courts behind the case

Elon is part of Alamance County, so state-court matters involving Elon
parties are heard in the Superior Court there. On the federal side,
North Carolina offers the United States District Court for the Eastern
District of North Carolina, the United States District Court for the
Middle District of North Carolina, and the United States District Court
for the Western District of North Carolina as possible forums, and the
one that governs a specific matter is confirmed during the conflict
check and intake. The evaluation protocol does not bend to the
courthouse: sources are cited so they can be pulled, methods are
described so they can be repeated, and conclusions are stated so they
can be tested on cross-examination.

## What the regional market contributes

An opinion about an Elon evaluee's employability draws on Bureau of
Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the
Burlington metropolitan area and for North Carolina statewide, along
with state workforce agency labor market information and targeted
employer research where warranted. The town's residents work across the
metro that Burlington anchors, and the analysis reflects that reach.
Regional data establishes the range of realistic opportunity; the
evaluee's transferable skills and restrictions establish where within
that range this particular person can function, and the report keeps
those findings distinct and traceable. When the damages theory calls
for economic modeling, the firm arranges
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.
