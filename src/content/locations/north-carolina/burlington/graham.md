## State venue in Alamance County

Graham lies in Alamance County, and civil matters involving Graham
parties proceed in the Superior Court there when they are filed in state
court. Federal actions may be brought in the United States District
Court for the Eastern District of North Carolina, the United States
District Court for the Middle District of North Carolina, or the United
States District Court for the Western District of North Carolina, and
the district that controls a given caption is settled during the
conflict check and intake, not presumed beforehand. Whatever the forum,
the file is built the same way: a documented conflict check, a defined
scope, and a records request tailored to the claims, so that both sides
of the caption can examine the record the opinion rests on.

## Graham wages in a Burlington-anchored market

A Graham resident's earning capacity is measured against the labor
market anchored by Burlington, the metro's principal city, because that
is where the realistic opportunities lie. The quantitative base is the
Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates
for the Burlington metropolitan area and for North Carolina statewide,
supplemented by state workforce agency labor market information and by
direct employer research when the referral questions require it. None
of that data decides the case by itself. The vocational analysis of
what this evaluee can still do, given education, work history, and
restrictions, is what converts regional statistics into an opinion
about a person, and the report shows each step of that conversion.
