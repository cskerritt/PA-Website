## Defining the market before measuring it

Reports for Mebane matters begin by stating what labor market the
opinion actually describes. The foundation is Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates for the Burlington
metropolitan area and for North Carolina statewide, read alongside state
workforce agency labor market information and, where the referral
questions warrant it, direct labor market research at the employer
level. Those sources describe what work exists within realistic
commuting reach of a Mebane resident; the individualized analysis of
transferable skills, medical restrictions, and placement prospects
determines what portion of that work remains open to the evaluee. The
report keeps those layers separate so that opposing counsel can trace
every conclusion back to either the data or the evaluee's own record.
