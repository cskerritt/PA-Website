Small towns raise a methodological question that opposing counsel will
ask sooner or later: what labor market does the opinion actually
describe? Green Level is a small community, and no credible earning
capacity analysis pretends its town limits contain a labor market of
their own. Purinton Analytics answers the question explicitly in every
Green Level report, for retaining counsel on the plaintiff side or the
defense, across
[vocational expert evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/).

## Why metro data governs a small-town opinion

Residents of a community the size of Green Level find their realistic
employment across the surrounding region, in the market that Burlington
anchors. The analysis therefore rests on Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates for the Burlington
metropolitan area and for North Carolina statewide, with state workforce
agency labor market information and direct employer research added when
the referral questions demand finer resolution. The report states this
choice of market openly, so the foundation is an argument the other side
can engage with rather than an assumption they can attack. On top of
that regional base sits the individual analysis: transferable skills,
restrictions, and the placement options genuinely available to this
evaluee, each finding tied to a cited source or a documented
observation.

## Alamance County venue and the federal alternatives

Green Level's cases arise in Alamance County, where state-court matters
proceed in the Superior Court. When a matter is federal, North
Carolina's available forums are the United States District Court for the
Eastern District of North Carolina, the United States District Court for
the Middle District of North Carolina, and the United States District
Court for the Western District of North Carolina; identifying the
controlling district is part of the conflict check and intake rather
than a guess made in advance. The same file discipline applies in every
forum, because a report that cannot survive the opposing expert's
scrutiny is not worth serving in any of them.

## Serving a Green Level evaluee

The firm has no office in Green Level, in Burlington, or anywhere else
in North Carolina, and it says so rather than implying otherwise; its
home office is in Kansas City, and everything else is by
appointment. In practice this costs a Green Level evaluee nothing.
Records review, structured interviews, and standardized testing under
publisher protocols proceed remotely in most engagements, and when the
questions justify an in-person evaluation in the evaluee's own
community, or when deposition or trial testimony requires attendance,
travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office to fit the case
calendar. Where economic modeling belongs in the damages picture, the
firm offers
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.
