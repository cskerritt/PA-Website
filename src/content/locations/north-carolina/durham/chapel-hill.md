## Orange County venue and the federal forums

Chapel Hill sits in Orange County, and state-court matters there proceed in the Superior Court, the trial forum where vocational and life care planning testimony most often appears in civil litigation. On the federal side, North Carolina is served by the United States District Court for the Eastern District of North Carolina, the United States District Court for the Middle District of North Carolina, and the United States District Court for the Western District of North Carolina. Which district controls a given Chapel Hill action turns on removal, party residence, and claim structure, so the controlling venue is confirmed during the conflict check and intake rather than assumed from geography. The written opinion is prepared the same way for either forum: defined scope, documented sources, and conclusions that survive cross-examination.

## The labor market behind a Chapel Hill opinion

Earning capacity is measured against the market in which an evaluee can realistically seek work, and for Chapel Hill residents that market extends across the surrounding metropolitan area rather than stopping at the town limits. Vocational findings rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Durham-Chapel Hill metropolitan area and for North Carolina statewide, supplemented by state workforce agency labor market information and direct labor market research when the referral questions call for it. Each source is cited in the report so opposing counsel can trace every conclusion to its origin, which is the standard the practice applies whether it is retained by the plaintiff or the defense.

## By-appointment meetings and evaluations

The firm keeps no staffed office in Chapel Hill and publishes no local street address, because it has none; candor on that point is part of the same discipline the reports themselves are built on.
