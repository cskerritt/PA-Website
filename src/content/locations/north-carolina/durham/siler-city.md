The most practical question counsel tend to ask about a Siler City referral is logistical: how does a national expert practice actually evaluate someone who lives in a small Chatham County town? The answer shapes this page. Purinton Analytics does not require an injured worker or a catastrophically injured evaluee to organize their life around the expert's location; the [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) and [medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/) work travels to the evaluee or proceeds remotely, whichever the referral questions and the record support. Siler City counsel on both the plaintiff and defense side use the practice through the same door, described more broadly on the [Durham metro page](/locations/north-carolina/durham/).

## Chatham County courts and federal forums

Siler City sits in Chatham County, and its state civil litigation proceeds in the Superior Court. Federal actions from this area may be venued in the United States District Court for the Eastern District of North Carolina, the United States District Court for the Middle District of North Carolina, or the United States District Court for the Western District of North Carolina; because district assignment follows the pleadings and parties rather than the town's location on a map, the practice treats the controlling forum as an intake question, confirmed alongside the conflict check before scope is set. Reports are then built to that court's disclosure expectations from the beginning instead of being retrofitted later.

## Labor market evidence for Siler City matters

A Siler City evaluee is analyzed within the labor market that person can genuinely reach, which for many residents includes employment across the surrounding Durham-Chapel Hill metropolitan area as well as work closer to home. The vocational opinion rests on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Durham-Chapel Hill metropolitan area and for North Carolina statewide, together with state workforce agency labor market information and targeted labor market research where the questions demand it. Scale is described qualitatively, sources are cited, and the same evidentiary standard applies whether the opinion helps or hurts the retaining party's theory, because that is what makes it useful at trial.

## Where the evaluation happens

There is no local office in Siler City and no address to publish, and the firm says so rather than implying otherwise. What counsel get instead is a deliberately flexible evaluation model: structured interviews and records-based work conducted by secure video; in-person vocational or life care planning evaluations conducted in the evaluee's own community when the referral calls for direct observation; and expert attendance at depositions and trial arranged through travel from the Kansas City home office. The evaluee's functioning is assessed in the context of their actual daily life, which is often better evidence than an office visit would produce.
