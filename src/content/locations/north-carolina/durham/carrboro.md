Few evaluees earn their living entirely inside the town where they sleep, and Carrboro is a clear example of why a credible vocational opinion has to be built at the scale of the commuting market rather than the municipality. A Carrboro resident's realistic employment options reach across the Durham-Chapel Hill metropolitan area, and an analysis that ignored that reach would understate or overstate capacity depending on who commissioned it. Purinton Analytics prepares [vocational expert](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) and [medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/) work for Carrboro matters with the same market-wide lens regardless of which party retains the firm, and the broader regional practice is set out on the [Durham metro page](/locations/north-carolina/durham/).

## Venue in Orange County and the federal districts

Carrboro is an Orange County town, and its state-court civil actions are heard in the Superior Court. Federal matters may proceed in any of the state's district courts as the case posture dictates: the United States District Court for the Eastern District of North Carolina, the United States District Court for the Middle District of North Carolina, or the United States District Court for the Western District of North Carolina. Rather than guessing at the forum from a map, the practice identifies the controlling venue during the conflict check and intake, then calibrates report format and disclosure timing to that court's expectations.

## Measuring the commuting market, not the town line

The methodological consequence of Carrboro's position is that labor market evidence must be drawn from the regional level. Vocational findings for Carrboro evaluees rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Durham-Chapel Hill metropolitan area and for North Carolina statewide, on state workforce agency labor market information, and on direct employer research when the questions presented warrant it. No figure enters the report without a citation, and no occupation is treated as available to an evaluee without considering the functional, educational, and transferable-skill evidence in the record. That restraint reads the same to a plaintiff attorney testing damages and a defense attorney testing mitigation.

## Evaluation logistics for Carrboro residents

There is no Purinton Analytics office in Carrboro, and the firm does not pretend otherwise; every local meeting is by appointment. In practice most of an engagement is conducted remotely: records exchange, structured interviews by secure video, and testimony preparation with counsel. Where an in-person evaluation of the evaluee is appropriate, it is conducted in the evaluee's own community, with travel arranged from the Kansas City home office. Deposition and trial appearances are scheduled the same way, planned far enough ahead that the court's calendar, not travel, sets the pace.
