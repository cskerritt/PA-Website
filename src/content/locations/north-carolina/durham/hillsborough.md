## The Superior Court and the federal alternatives

Hillsborough lies in Orange County, whose civil trial work in state court proceeds in the Superior Court. When a matter is federal, North Carolina offers its litigants the United States District Court for the Eastern District of North Carolina, the United States District Court for the Middle District of North Carolina, and the United States District Court for the Western District of North Carolina; which of those districts governs a particular Hillsborough case is a question the practice resolves during the conflict check and intake, not one it answers by assumption. Any venue-specific registration or qualification requirement is identified and confirmed in that same intake process, so counsel are never surprised at the disclosure stage.

## Hillsborough work in a regional labor market

A Hillsborough evaluee's employment prospects are assessed against the wider Durham-Chapel Hill metropolitan area, because that is the market a job search would actually cover. The vocational analysis draws on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Durham-Chapel Hill metropolitan area and for North Carolina statewide, on state workforce agency labor market information, and, where the referral questions justify it, on direct labor market research with identified sources. The report describes scale and opportunity qualitatively and ties every conclusion to cited data, a discipline intended to read identically whether the reader is retaining counsel or the cross-examining party.

## How meetings and site work are scheduled

The practice serves Hillsborough by appointment. It maintains no storefront or street address in the town, and it says so plainly rather than implying a local presence it does not have. Most engagement phases are remote by design: secure records exchange, video interviews, and conference calls with counsel keep the file moving between court dates. When an evaluation should occur face to face, or when deposition or trial testimony requires the expert in the room, travel is arranged from the Kansas City home office with enough lead time to protect the court's schedule.
