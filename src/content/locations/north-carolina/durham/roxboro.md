Roxboro's cases are Person County cases, and that county identity is where any honest engagement in this town has to begin. Although Roxboro belongs to the Durham-Chapel Hill metropolitan area for labor market purposes, its state-court litigation does not run through the courts of the counties around the principal city; it proceeds in Person County's own Superior Court. ## Person County venue

State civil actions arising in Roxboro are heard in the Superior Court sitting for Person County. Matters that belong in federal court may land in the United States District Court for the Eastern District of North Carolina, the United States District Court for the Middle District of North Carolina, or the United States District Court for the Western District of North Carolina, depending on how the case is pleaded and removed; the practice confirms the controlling district during the conflict check and intake so that report formatting, disclosure content, and scheduling assumptions match the forum from the outset. Where forensic economic damages are also needed, the firm coordinates engagements in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement structure.

## A Person County evaluee in a metro-scale market

The tension in a Roxboro file is often between where the evaluee lives and where the work is. Vocational conclusions therefore rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Durham-Chapel Hill metropolitan area and for North Carolina statewide, read alongside state workforce agency labor market information and, when warranted, direct labor market research. The analysis weighs what employment is realistically reachable for this evaluee, with this record, rather than assuming the whole metro is equally available to everyone in it. That weighing is documented, sourced, and written to withstand examination by whichever party did not retain the firm.
