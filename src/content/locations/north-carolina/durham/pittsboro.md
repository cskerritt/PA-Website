## Venue in Chatham County

Pittsboro is a Chatham County town, and its state-court civil matters proceed in the Superior Court. Where a case belongs in federal court instead, North Carolina's available forums are the United States District Court for the Eastern District of North Carolina, the United States District Court for the Middle District of North Carolina, and the United States District Court for the Western District of North Carolina. The practice does not guess which district a Pittsboro action will occupy; the controlling venue, and any registration or qualification requirement that attaches to it, is identified and confirmed during the conflict check and intake process, before scheduling commitments are made.

## Labor market foundations

Damages and mitigation questions for a Pittsboro evaluee are answered against the regional employment picture, since realistic job search extends into the wider Durham-Chapel Hill metropolitan area. Vocational conclusions draw on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Durham-Chapel Hill metropolitan area and for North Carolina statewide, on state workforce agency labor market information, and on direct labor market research when a referral question requires it. Where a claim also calls for economic loss calculations, the firm offers [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement structure, keeping a single timeline for counsel to manage.

## Scheduling around the disclosure calendar

Pittsboro is served by appointment; the firm has no office there and publishes none, a fact stated openly because accurate small print is part of expert credibility. Most work between deadlines is remote: secure records exchange, video interviews with the evaluee, and preparation sessions with counsel. When an in-person evaluation strengthens the record, it is conducted in the evaluee's community, and when a deposition or trial date requires the expert's presence, travel from the Kansas City home office is booked against the docket early enough that the calendar never becomes the opposing party's argument.
