A catastrophic injury changes what the rest of a life will cost, and
somebody has to put that future on paper in a form a court can weigh.
For Summerfield families and the counsel who represent them, and just as
often for the defense examining the other side's projections, Purinton
Analytics prepares [life care plans](/services/life-care-planning/) that
inventory future medical, therapeutic, and daily-living needs item by
item, each tied to the clinical record. A
[medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/) can serve
narrower disputes, and [rebuttal and peer
review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) is available when the question
is whether an existing plan holds up.

## Guilford County venue, federal alternatives

Summerfield lies in Guilford County, and state civil actions involving
Summerfield parties are generally tried in the Superior Court. Should a
matter proceed federally, North Carolina's districts are the United
States District Court for the Eastern District of North Carolina, the
United States District Court for the Middle District of North Carolina,
and the United States District Court for the Western District of North
Carolina, with the controlling venue identified during the conflict
check and intake. A life care plan is drafted to survive whichever
forum receives it: sourced pricing, stated methodology, and no line
item that cannot be traced to a treating recommendation or accepted
standard of care. Surrounding coverage appears on the
[Greensboro metro page](/locations/north-carolina/greensboro/).

## Care needs and earning capacity, analyzed together

Future care is only part of the damages picture; lost earning capacity is
the other, and the same file usually needs both. The
[vocational analysis](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) for a
Summerfield evaluee applies Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational
employment and wage estimates for the Greensboro and High Point
metropolitan area and for North Carolina statewide, together with state
workforce agency labor market information and direct labor market
research where warranted, always described qualitatively. When counsel
want the full damages case assembled at once,
[coordinated engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
bring in independent economist partners who perform and sign the
economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds
under one engagement structure, so the plan, the earning capacity
opinion, and the loss calculations agree with each other before anyone
is deposed.

## How the work reaches Summerfield

The firm maintains no Summerfield office and publishes no address for
this page; its only staffed office is the Kansas City home office, and
it says so. Engagements are by appointment, with most phases conducted
remotely. In-person needs assessments are performed in the evaluee's
own home and community when the referral calls for it, which is often
exactly where a life care plan should be built, and travel for
evaluations or testimony is arranged from Kansas City. The remote-first
model is described on the [nationwide page](/locations/nationwide/),
and other North Carolina venues are collected on the
[state page](/locations/north-carolina/).

## Beginning the engagement

Start with the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/): caption or party
names, the nature of the injuries, and any dates already on the
calendar. Once conflicts clear, intake determines whether the case
needs a life care plan, a vocational opinion, a coordinated damages
team, or review of the other side's work, and confirms any
venue-specific qualification requirement. Summerfield counsel receive a
future-care record built to be examined, not merely admired.
