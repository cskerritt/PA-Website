Damages evidence usually fails at the joints. A vocational opinion, a
life care plan, and an economic loss calculation each look sound alone,
yet disagree about the evaluee's work life, care needs, or growth
assumptions when read side by side, and opposing counsel reads them
side by side. Purinton Analytics offers Trinity counsel a structural
answer:
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the [vocational](/services/vocational-expert-witness/)
and [care-planning](/services/life-care-planning/) components proceed
under one engagement structure, with shared assumptions documented from
the outset for whichever side of the caption retains the firm.

## Randolph County and the federal map

Trinity lies in Randolph County, whose Superior Court is the ordinary
forum for state civil claims of consequence arising in the town. In
the federal system, North Carolina's trial courts are the United States
District Court for the Eastern District of North Carolina, the United
States District Court for the Middle District of North Carolina, and
the United States District Court for the Western District of North
Carolina; the conflict check and intake establish which district, if
any, controls the matter at hand. A coordinated engagement is drafted
to the receiving forum's disclosure requirements as a unit, so no
component becomes the late or noncompliant piece. The wider
region's coverage is presented on the
[Greensboro metro page](/locations/north-carolina/greensboro/).

## One labor market analysis, used by every expert

Coordination works because there is a common vocational foundation
under the case. For a Trinity evaluee, that foundation is built from
Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates
for the Greensboro and High Point metropolitan area and for North
Carolina statewide, from state workforce agency labor market
information, and from direct labor market research when the referral
requires it, with all market observations expressed qualitatively. The
resulting findings on restrictions, transferable skills, and accessible
occupations flow to the care planner and to the economist partners in
identical form, which is what keeps the eventual reports congruent.
Rebuttal assignments run on the same principle in reverse: opposing
experts are examined as a set, because their disagreements with each
other are evidence too.

## Assembling the team

The coordinated model starts working before retention does. Send the
caption or party names through the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/), sketch the components the case
may need, and note the operative dates. After conflicts clear, intake
maps the engagement, from records requests to any venue-specific
qualification requirement, and Trinity counsel receive a damages team
