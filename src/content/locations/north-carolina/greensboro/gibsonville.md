## What a review actually examines

A vocational or care-plan critique is only as strong as the data it
stands on. Review engagements for Gibsonville cases check an opposing
report's labor market claims against Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates for the Greensboro and High
Point metropolitan area and for North Carolina statewide, against state
workforce agency labor market information, and against direct labor
market research when the report's assertions demand it. The questions
are always the same: are the sources real, are they current, were they
applied as their publishers intend, and do the conclusions follow. When
affirmative opinions are also needed,
[coordinated engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
add independent economist partners who perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.
