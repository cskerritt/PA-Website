## The Rockingham County forum and its federal counterparts

Eden is a Rockingham County town, and state-court civil actions arising
there are ordinarily heard in the Superior Court. North Carolina's
federal trial forums are the United States District Court for the
Eastern District of North Carolina, the United States District Court for
the Middle District of North Carolina, and the United States District
Court for the Western District of North Carolina; the controlling
district for any particular matter is confirmed during the conflict
check and intake rather than assumed. Wherever the case lands, the
written opinion is prepared to a disclosure standard that does not vary
by courthouse. Regional context for the surrounding venues appears on
the [Greensboro metro page](/locations/north-carolina/greensboro/).

## Measuring an Eden evaluee's market

A small town does not confine its residents' earning capacity, and the
analysis should not pretend it does. Vocational findings for Eden
evaluees are grounded in Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational
employment and wage estimates for the Greensboro and High Point
metropolitan area and for North Carolina statewide, in state workforce
agency labor market information, and in direct labor market research
where the referral requires it. The evaluee's documented restrictions,
education, and work history determine which of the region's occupations
remain accessible; the data describes what those occupations pay and how
commonly they occur, always characterized qualitatively. In
[coordinated engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/),
independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis
while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure, so the earning capacity story stays consistent
across every expert who tells it.
