An expert opinion earns its keep in the deposition chair. The written
report opens the argument, but the testimony defends it, and Purinton
Analytics prepares every Oak Ridge engagement on the assumption that
each finding will be probed under oath by capable opposing counsel.
That is why the firm's
[expert testimony and litigation consulting](/services/expert-testimony-litigation-consulting/)
practice sits alongside its
[vocational](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) and
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) work rather than
apart from it: the analysis and its defense are designed together, for
retaining counsel on either side of the caption.

## Forums that hear Oak Ridge disputes

Oak Ridge is part of Guilford County, and civil litigation of
consequence originating there generally proceeds in the Superior Court.
In federal practice, North Carolina presents the United States District
Court for the Eastern District of North Carolina, the United States
District Court for the Middle District of North Carolina, and the
United States District Court for the Western District of North
Carolina; identifying the district that governs a specific Oak Ridge
case is a task for the conflict check and intake, not for assumption.
Testimony preparation is calibrated to the identified forum, since the
questions an expert faces vary with the rules of the court asking them.
Regional coverage beyond the town is mapped on the
[Greensboro metro page](/locations/north-carolina/greensboro/).

## Findings built to withstand cross-examination

Every substantive claim in an Oak Ridge vocational opinion is anchored
to a source the other side can pull: Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates for the Greensboro and High
Point metropolitan area and for North Carolina statewide, state
workforce agency labor market information, and direct labor market
research when the referral questions require it, with market conditions
characterized qualitatively throughout. The evaluee's restrictions and
transferable skills are documented before conclusions are drawn from
them, so the chain from record to opinion has no unexplained links.
Where the damages case is assembled as a whole,
[coordinated engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
engage independent economist partners to perform and sign the economic
analysis under the same engagement structure, which prevents the
cross-examiner's favorite discovery: experts who contradict each other.

## Presence when it counts, candor throughout

The firm has no Oak Ridge office and offers no local address, a fact
stated here because accuracy about small things is how credibility on
large ones is kept. Routine phases of an engagement are handled
remotely by appointment; evaluations occur in the evaluee's own
community when in-person assessment strengthens the referral; and when
a deposition, hearing, or trial in an Oak Ridge matter requires the
expert in the room, travel is arranged from the Kansas City home
office. The national practice behind this model is described on the
[nationwide page](/locations/nationwide/), and companion North Carolina
venues appear on the [state page](/locations/north-carolina/).
