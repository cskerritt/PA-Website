## Quantifying the commuting market

The analytical spine of an Archdale opinion is Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the
Greensboro and High Point metropolitan area, read alongside the
statewide North Carolina estimates, state workforce agency labor market

specific employers or job classes. Market observations stay
qualitative; the evaluee's restrictions, training, and work history
