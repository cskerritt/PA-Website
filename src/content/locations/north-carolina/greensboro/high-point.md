High Point is written into the name of its own labor market. The federal
statistical area that frames wage evidence for this part of North Carolina
is the Greensboro and High Point metropolitan area, which means a High
Point damages case draws on data defined, in part, around the city where
the evaluee lives and works. Purinton Analytics serves counsel on either
side of the caption here with [vocational expert
testimony](/services/vocational-expert-witness/), [life care
planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and [coordinated damages
engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) in which
independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis
while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement
structure.

## Where High Point cases are heard

High Point sits in Guilford County, and state-court matters involving
High Point parties are ordinarily tried in the Superior Court, the
state's forum for civil claims of consequence. On the federal side, North
Carolina offers the United States District Court for the Eastern District
of North Carolina, the United States District Court for the Middle
District of North Carolina, and the United States District Court for the
Western District of North Carolina; which of those districts controls a
particular case is confirmed during the conflict check and intake, along
with any venue-specific qualification requirement. Reports are drafted to
the same disclosure standard regardless of forum, because a written
opinion should not need translation when the case moves. The firm's wider
regional coverage is described on the [Greensboro metro
page](/locations/north-carolina/greensboro/).

## Wage evidence built on the named market

Vocational findings for a High Point evaluee rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Greensboro
and High Point metropolitan area and for North Carolina statewide,
supplemented by state workforce agency labor market information and by
direct labor market research when the referral questions call for it.
Market conditions are described qualitatively; the particulars come from
the evaluee's own record: documented restrictions, work history,
transferable skills, and the occupations that remain realistically open.
When the engagement is coordinated, those findings pass to the economist
partners in a form their loss calculations can absorb without
restatement, which keeps the damages record internally consistent from
report to deposition.

## Meetings by appointment, work without borders

There is no Purinton Analytics office in High Point, and no street
address is published for this page; candor about presence is part of the
same discipline that governs the reports. Most stages of an engagement
proceed remotely, from records review through draft opinion.
When an in-person evaluation better serves the referral, it is conducted
in the evaluee's own community, and when depositions or trial dates
require presence, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office.
The structure of the remote-first national practice is described on the
[nationwide page](/locations/nationwide/), and the firm's other North
Carolina coverage is collected on the [state
page](/locations/north-carolina/).

## Opening a High Point engagement

Every engagement begins with a documented conflict check. Submit the
caption or party names through the [conflict check
form](/refer-a-case/), and flag any disclosure, deposition, or trial
dates already set. Once conflicts clear, intake defines the scope, the
records request, and the components counsel actually need, whether that

High Point counsel get the same file either way: an opinion built on a
record both sides can examine.
