Distance from the courthouse does not change the data. Vocational
findings for Stokesdale evaluees are developed from Bureau of Labor

## What by-appointment service means in practice

Most of an engagement never requires anyone to travel: records
analysis, counsel conferences, and many evaluee interviews are
conducted remotely under secure arrangements. When an in-person
evaluation serves the referral, it is held in the evaluee's own
community, and when a Stokesdale matter reaches deposition or trial,
the expert appears, with travel scheduled from the Kansas City home
office. The same model supports engagements across the country, as
