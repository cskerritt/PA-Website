Counsel on either side of a Wilmington caption should be able to read the
same expert report and find nothing to distrust in its method. That is the
test Purinton Analytics applies to every opinion it issues for the metro,
whether the retention comes from plaintiff or defense. The firm serves
Wilmington-area litigation with
[vocational expert evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure, and it applies the same documentation standard to
each of them.

## What by-appointment service means here

There is no Purinton Analytics storefront in Wilmington and no published
street address for this location, and the firm says so directly rather than
implying otherwise. Meetings are by appointment. The practical effect for
counsel is that the work starts immediately rather than waiting on
logistics: records review begins remotely against a defined scope,
interviews proceed by video where appropriate, and standardized testing is
administered under publisher protocols, remotely where the publisher
supports it. When the referral questions call for an in-person evaluation,
or when a deposition or trial date requires presence, travel is scheduled
from the Kansas City home office with the court's calendar as the fixed
point. Counsel are told at intake which portions of the work will happen
remotely and which require presence, so scheduling holds no surprises.

## Evidence from the local labor market

Vocational findings for Wilmington-area evaluees rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Wilmington
metropolitan area and for North Carolina statewide, along with state
workforce agency labor market information and direct labor market research
where warranted. Life care plans and medical cost projections are priced to
the evaluee's own community and provider market rather than to national
averages. Sources are cited in full in the written report, because the
report's audience always includes the lawyer whose job is to take it apart.

## Requesting a conflict check

Every Wilmington engagement opens the same way: with a conflict check, not
a consultation. Send the caption or party names through the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and note any imminent disclosure,
deposition, or trial dates so that availability is assessed against real
deadlines. Substantive discussion follows clearance. Counsel whose dockets
extend beyond the metro or beyond the state can see the same structure
applied everywhere on the firm's
[national coverage page](/locations/nationwide/).
