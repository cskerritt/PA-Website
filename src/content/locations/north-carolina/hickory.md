The Hickory-Lenoir-Morganton metropolitan area takes in Alexander County,
Burke County, Caldwell County, and Catawba County, and a single case there can
easily involve an evaluee, treating providers, and party employers spread
across all of them. Purinton Analytics serves plaintiff and defense counsel
throughout the metro with [vocational expert](/services/vocational-expert-witness/)
evaluations of employability and earning capacity,
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) for catastrophic
injuries, and [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Venues across the metro's counties

Engagements are accepted for matters in the Superior Court in Alexander
County, Burke County, Caldwell County, and Catawba County, and in the federal
courts that serve the state: the United States District Court for the Western
District of North Carolina, the United States District Court for the Middle
District of North Carolina, and the United States District Court for the
Eastern District of North Carolina. When a referral arises beyond the metro,
the [North Carolina page](/locations/north-carolina/) describes statewide
coverage. Depositions and trial testimony are provided in person or by remote
appearance where the court permits, and the written report is prepared to the
same standard in every forum.

## By-appointment presence, stated plainly

Meetings in Hickory, Lenoir, and Morganton are by appointment. The firm keeps
no staffed office in the metro and publishes no street address for this
location, because candor about presence is part of the same discipline that
governs the opinions themselves. What counsel can rely on instead is a
defined process: a prompt conflict check, an evaluation date set against the
disclosure calendar at intake, and, when the referral questions call for
in-person evaluation or testimony, travel scheduled from the Kansas City home
office. Evaluations are held in the evaluee's own community, at counsel's
offices, or by secure video under publisher protocols when remote
administration better serves the case.

## Labor market research grounded in this area

Employability and earning capacity findings for evaluees in the metro rest on
Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the
Hickory-Lenoir-Morganton area and for North Carolina statewide, supplemented
by state workforce agency labor market information and direct labor market
research, including employer contact and job posting analysis, where the
referral questions warrant it. Because conditions in this metro differ from
those in the state's largest cities, the analysis draws on data for this area
rather than figures borrowed from elsewhere, and every source is cited in the
report so that opposing counsel can trace each finding to its origin. Where a
treating team or opposing expert has already produced a plan or report,
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) work applies the
same sourcing standard to someone else's methodology.

## Starting an engagement in the Hickory area

Referrals from the metro commonly involve personal injury, medical
malpractice, wrongful death, employment, and disability matters, and the firm
accepts work from either side of the caption. Every engagement begins with a
documented conflict check, a defined scope, and a records request tailored to
the claims, so that the resulting opinion rests on a record both sides can
examine.

To begin, [request a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) with the case caption or
party names, and note any imminent disclosure, deposition, or trial dates.
CVs and fee information follow promptly, and the
[national coverage page](/locations/nationwide/) explains remote evaluation
logistics, travel, and the wider reach of the practice.
