Southern Pines gives the Pinehurst-Southern Pines metro area half of its name, even though Pinehurst is the metro's designated principal city. For counsel weighing a referral, neither point on the map decides anything: what matters is whether the retained expert's opinions will survive scrutiny from the other side of the caption. Purinton Analytics provides [vocational expert witness](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) analysis and [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) for Southern Pines matters from a national, remote-first practice, accepting engagements from plaintiff and defense counsel alike and drafting every report on the assumption that opposing counsel will read it line by line.

## Venue context in Moore County

Civil matters arising in Southern Pines are tried in the Superior Court, the state trial court serving Moore County. On the federal side, North Carolina's district courts are the United States District Court for the Eastern District of North Carolina, the United States District Court for the Middle District of North Carolina, and the United States District Court for the Western District of North Carolina. The firm does not guess at venue: the controlling court for a particular matter, together with any venue-specific registration or qualification requirement, is identified and confirmed during the conflict check and intake process. Work in this town follows the same methodology described for the wider [Pinehurst metro area](/locations/north-carolina/pinehurst/) and for [North Carolina](/locations/north-carolina/) as a whole.

## How the local labor market is analyzed

Vocational findings for a Southern Pines evaluee rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the metro area and for North Carolina statewide, supplemented by state workforce agency labor market information and direct labor market research where the referral questions warrant it. The analysis stays concrete: which occupations exist within the evaluee's commuting range, which of them the evaluee can still perform given documented restrictions, and what those occupations actually pay in this market rather than in a generic national average. Where counsel needs lost earnings carried through to a damages figure, the firm offers [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement structure.

## Meeting a Southern Pines evaluee

Meetings are by appointment only. The firm keeps no staffed office in Southern Pines and publishes no street address here, because candor about presence belongs to the same discipline that governs the written opinions. Most phases of an engagement proceed remotely: records review, structured interviews by video, and report preparation do not depend on geography. When the referral questions call for in-person evaluation, the evaluee is seen in their own community, and travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office. Deposition and trial testimony are given in person or by video, as the court and the parties prefer.
