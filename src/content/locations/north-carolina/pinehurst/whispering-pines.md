## Where a Whispering Pines case is heard

State-court civil litigation touching Whispering Pines proceeds in the Superior Court that serves Moore County. In the federal system, North Carolina is divided among the United States District Court for the Eastern District of North Carolina, the United States District Court for the Middle District of North Carolina, and the United States District Court for the Western District of North Carolina, each an available forum depending on the case. The firm treats the controlling venue as a fact to be confirmed, not assumed: it is settled during the conflict check and intake, along with any registration or qualification requirement particular to that court. ## A village inside a regional labor market

Whispering Pines is a small residential village, and almost by definition its residents earn their livings across the broader Pinehurst-Southern Pines metro area. A defensible earning capacity opinion therefore starts with the regional market, not the village itself. The firm builds vocational findings on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates covering the metro area and North Carolina statewide, reinforced by state workforce agency labor market information and by direct labor market research when the referral questions call for it. Every figure that reaches the report is sourced, and every occupational conclusion is tied to the evaluee's documented restrictions, skills, and work history. If the engagement also requires economic loss calculations, the firm arranges [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement structure.

## Scheduling the evaluation

The disclosure date drives the schedule backward: evaluation, testing, and drafting are sequenced so the written opinion is complete with room to spare. Records review and interviews proceed remotely wherever the questions permit, which is often the fastest path for a Whispering Pines evaluee. If the referral requires an in-person assessment, it takes place in the evaluee's own community, with travel arranged from the Kansas City home office. There is no staffed Purinton Analytics office in Whispering Pines and no published local address; the firm states that outright rather than implying a presence it does not have.
