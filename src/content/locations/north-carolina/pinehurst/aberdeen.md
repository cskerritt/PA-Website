## Earning capacity in a small-town market

Aberdeen is a small town whose workers participate in a wider regional economy, and an earning capacity opinion has to reflect that reality. The firm grounds its findings in Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Pinehurst-Southern Pines metro area and for North Carolina statewide, along with state workforce agency labor market information and, where warranted, direct contact with employers in the commuting area. The question is never abstract employability; it is whether specific occupations, at documented wage levels, remain open to this evaluee with these restrictions. Where the damages model requires present-value calculations, counsel can add [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement structure.
