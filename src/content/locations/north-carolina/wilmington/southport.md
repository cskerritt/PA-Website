## Courts serving Southport matters

Southport's state-court civil litigation belongs to Brunswick County,
where trials are held in the Superior Court. On the federal side,
North Carolina's district courts are the United States District Court
for the Eastern District of North Carolina, the United States District
Court for the Middle District of North Carolina, and the United States
District Court for the Western District of North Carolina; the venue
that will actually govern is confirmed during the conflict check and
intake process. Because testimony obligations differ by forum, intake
also establishes early what the disclosure and deposition sequence is
likely to look like. Regional coverage is gathered on the [Wilmington
metro page](/locations/north-carolina/wilmington/).

## Opinions built to be cross-examined

Testimony holds only when the underlying data holds. Earning capacity
findings for Southport evaluees are drawn from Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the
Wilmington metropolitan area and for North Carolina statewide, from
state workforce agency labor market information, and from direct labor
market research where the referral questions require it, so that every
answer given at deposition traces to a source the questioner can
verify. When the damages presentation needs an economic loss analysis,
the firm's [coordinated damages
assessment](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) provides it
through independent economist partners, who perform and sign the
economic analysis within a single engagement structure.

## Appearing when the case requires it

The firm keeps no office in Southport and publishes no address for
this location, and it says so without hedging. Day-to-day work runs
remotely by appointment, which serves both the evaluee and the
schedule; in-person evaluation, when it will strengthen the record,
takes place in the evaluee's community. When deposition or trial dates
require physical presence, travel is arranged from the Kansas City
home office and planned against the court's calendar so appearances
are never left to chance. The practice model behind this is set out on
the [nationwide page](/locations/nationwide/).
