The Wilmington metro does not stop at a single county line, and neither
does the practice that serves it. Surf City belongs to Pender County,
which gives its litigation a different county venue from much of the
surrounding region even as its labor market questions remain metro-wide.
Purinton Analytics treats that distinction as an intake fact, not an
afterthought: the county determines the courthouse, while the
[vocational analysis](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) looks to the
broader regional economy. The firm brings the same approach to this
small town for plaintiff and defense referrals alike, alongside [life
care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) where the injuries demand
it.

## Pender County and the federal districts

Surf City's state-court civil cases proceed in Pender County, where the
trial forum is the Superior Court. Federal matters in North Carolina
are heard in the United States District Court for the Eastern District
of North Carolina, the United States District Court for the Middle
District of North Carolina, or the United States District Court for the
Western District of North Carolina, and the district with authority over
a particular Surf City case is confirmed during the conflict check and
intake process. Reports are written to hold up under the disclosure
practice of whichever court applies. The [Wilmington metro
page](/locations/north-carolina/wilmington/) explains how the firm
covers the region as a whole.

## One metro economy, one data foundation

Although the courthouse is a county question, earning capacity is a
regional one. Opinions for Surf City evaluees rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Wilmington
metropolitan area and for North Carolina statewide, on state workforce
agency labor market information, and on direct labor market research
where the referral questions justify it. The report characterizes the
market qualitatively and reserves quantification for the analysis
itself. When lost earnings must become a number, the firm's
[coordinated damages
assessment](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) engages
independent economist partners, who perform and sign the economic
analysis within the same engagement structure.

## By appointment in Surf City

The firm operates no office in Surf City and lists no street address
for this location; that candor is deliberate, because a practice built
on accurate reporting should be accurate about itself. Evaluations
default to remote formats when they serve the record, and in-person
assessment, where warranted, is conducted in the evaluee's community
with travel from the Kansas City home office. Caregiver and family
interviews are added when daily routines matter to the plan. The
remote-first framework behind all of this is documented on the
[nationwide practice page](/locations/nationwide/).
