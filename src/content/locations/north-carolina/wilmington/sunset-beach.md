A town as small as Sunset Beach produces few serious injury cases, but
the ones it produces carry the same stakes as any filed in a major city,
and they deserve the same expert infrastructure. Purinton Analytics
does not maintain a lighter version of its method for smaller venues:
a Sunset Beach [vocational evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/)
or [life care plan](/services/life-care-planning/) is built with the
identical rigor the firm applies in its largest metropolitan
engagements, for whichever side of the caption retains it. Scale
changes the logistics of an engagement; it never changes the standard.

## The Brunswick County forum

Sunset Beach lies in Brunswick County, and state-court civil claims
from the town are tried in the Superior Court. Federal jurisdiction in
North Carolina runs through the United States District Court for the
Eastern District of North Carolina, the United States District Court
for the Middle District of North Carolina, and the United States
District Court for the Western District of North Carolina; the
controlling venue for any given matter is identified during the
conflict check and intake process rather than assumed from a map.
Whether the destination is state or federal court, the written opinion
is prepared for adversarial reading. The firm's regional footprint is
described on the [Wilmington metro
page](/locations/north-carolina/wilmington/).

## A regional market for a small-town evaluee

No opinion about a Sunset Beach worker can be honest if it treats the
town as its own economy. Vocational findings are therefore grounded in
Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates
for the Wilmington metropolitan area and for North Carolina statewide,
in state workforce agency labor market information, and in direct labor
market research when the questions warrant it, with the market always
described in qualitative terms. Where the case requires an economic
loss figure, [coordinated damages
assessment](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) supplies the
structure: independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work continues under
the same engagement.

## Logistics without a local office

The firm will not imply a Sunset Beach office it does not have; there
is no staffed location here and no published address, and the practice
regards that plainness as part of its professional discipline. Service
is by appointment. Remote evaluation handles most record review,
testing, and interviews, and it is frequently the kinder option for an
injured evaluee; when in-person assessment will genuinely improve the
record, it is carried out in the evaluee's community with travel from
the Kansas City home office. The complete remote-first model appears on
the [nationwide practice page](/locations/nationwide/).

## Engaging the firm

The engagement begins when counsel [request a conflict
check](/refer-a-case/) with the case caption and parties, flagging any
disclosure, deposition, or trial dates already scheduled. Intake then
converts the referral into a defined scope, a records list, and a
reporting calendar, and confirms any qualification requirement the
venue imposes. Statewide coverage is mapped on the [North Carolina
locations page](/locations/north-carolina/). However small the town, a
Sunset Beach case gets the full weight of the practice behind it, from
the initial records request through the last question of
cross-examination.
