## New Hanover County and the federal forums

Carolina Beach cases filed in state court proceed in New Hanover County,
where civil trials are heard in the Superior Court. Federal litigation
in North Carolina is divided among the United States District Court for
the Eastern District of North Carolina, the United States District Court
for the Middle District of North Carolina, and the United States
District Court for the Western District of North Carolina, and the
district that will hear a particular matter is established during the
conflict check and intake process. Opinions are drafted to satisfy the
disclosure obligations of whichever forum applies. The [Wilmington
metro page](/locations/north-carolina/wilmington/) collects the firm's
regional coverage in one place.

## Reading the labor market honestly

Whether offered affirmatively or in rebuttal, every earning capacity
opinion for a Carolina Beach evaluee rests on the same sources: Bureau
of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the
Wilmington metropolitan area and for North Carolina statewide, state
workforce agency labor market information, and direct labor market
research where a disputed occupation deserves closer scrutiny. A
rebuttal that criticizes an opponent's data must survive the same
scrutiny itself, and the firm writes with that symmetry in mind. Where
damages must be quantified, [coordinated damages
assessment](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) brings in
independent economist partners who perform and sign the economic
analysis under a unified engagement.

## Appointments, not an address

There is no staffed Purinton Analytics location in Carolina Beach and no
published street address here, a fact stated outright because opposing
counsel will check. Evaluations run remotely wherever that serves the
evaluee and the record; in-person assessment, when warranted, happens in
the evaluee's community with travel from the Kansas City home office.
Deposition and trial appearances are scheduled the same way, against the
court's calendar. The remote-first structure of the whole practice is
laid out on the [nationwide page](/locations/nationwide/).
