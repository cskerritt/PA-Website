A catastrophic injury does not scale down because it happened in a small
town. When a Shallotte case involves lifelong care needs, the damages
record must capture decades of attendant care, equipment replacement,
therapies, and medical follow-up before any of it can be valued, and
that documentation burden falls on the expert work. Purinton Analytics
carries it through [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/)
and [medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/), with
every item anchored to the medical record and to clinical
recommendation. The vocational side of the same case, what work if any
remains available to the injured person, runs through the firm's
[vocational assessment](/services/vocational-expert-witness/), and both
sides of the caption retain the practice on identical terms.

## Where Shallotte cases are heard

Shallotte is a Brunswick County town, and its state-court civil claims
are tried in the Superior Court. North Carolina's federal trial courts
are the United States District Court for the Eastern District of North
Carolina, the United States District Court for the Middle District of
North Carolina, and the United States District Court for the Western
District of North Carolina; which of them controls a specific matter is
settled during the conflict check and intake process. A care plan
destined for federal disclosure is structured differently from one bound
for state practice, and intake establishes the format early. Coverage
of the surrounding region is collected on the [Wilmington metro
page](/locations/north-carolina/wilmington/).

## Valuing work capacity after severe injury

Even in catastrophic cases, residual earning capacity is a live
question, and it is answered with Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates for the Wilmington
metropolitan area and for North Carolina statewide, state workforce
agency labor market information, and direct labor market research when
the referral questions call for it. Where no work capacity remains, the
opinion documents why, in terms tied to the restrictions rather than to
sympathy. Translating the care plan and the earnings findings into a
damages figure is the province of [coordinated damages
assessment](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/), where
independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis
under one engagement structure.

## The evaluation, brought to the evaluee

For a severely injured person, travel is often the wrong ask. The firm
therefore keeps its Shallotte work by appointment and remote-first:
record review, interviews with the evaluee, and conversations with
caregivers and family members, whose knowledge of daily routines rarely
appears in any chart, can all be completed without a trip. When an
in-person evaluation will materially strengthen the plan, it happens in
the evaluee's community, with travel from the Kansas City home office.
No local address is published because none exists, and the firm prefers
stating that to implying otherwise. The full model is described on the
[nationwide practice page](/locations/nationwide/).

## Referring a catastrophic case

Begin with the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/): the caption or
party names, plus any disclosure or trial dates the court has set.
Intake then scopes the plan, lists the records to gather, and confirms
any venue-specific qualification requirement. The practice's reach
across the state appears on the [North Carolina locations
page](/locations/north-carolina/). Shallotte counsel handling a
life-altering injury need documentation built to outlast the verdict,
and that is the standard each plan is written to meet.
