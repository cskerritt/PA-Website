Expert engagements are won or lost on the calendar. A disclosure date,
a discovery cutoff, or a mediation set for the wrong week can turn a
strong Boiling Spring Lakes case into a scramble, which is why Purinton
Analytics builds every engagement backward from the dates the court has
already fixed. Intake for this small Brunswick County community starts
by mapping the schedule: when the report is due, when depositions are
likely, and how much record collection must happen first. Only then does
the substantive work begin, whether the assignment is a [vocational
evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) or a [life care
plan](/services/life-care-planning/), and the firm holds that same
calendar discipline for plaintiff and defense clients alike.

## The courts that set those deadlines

Boiling Spring Lakes sits in Brunswick County, and its state-court
civil matters are heard in the Superior Court. Federal cases in North
Carolina fall within the United States District Court for the Eastern
District of North Carolina, the United States District Court for the
Middle District of North Carolina, or the United States District Court
for the Western District of North Carolina; the venue that governs a
given matter, and the disclosure rules that come with it, are confirmed
during the conflict check and intake process. State and federal
practice impose different report obligations, and the engagement plan
reflects the difference from day one. Regional context is available on
the [Wilmington metro page](/locations/north-carolina/wilmington/).

## Labor market work on a litigation schedule

Vocational findings for a Boiling Spring Lakes evaluee are grounded in
Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates
for the Wilmington metropolitan area and for North Carolina statewide,
in state workforce agency labor market information, and in direct labor
market research where the questions justify it. Because that research
takes time, the schedule mapped at intake determines how early it must
begin. When the file also needs economic quantification, the firm's
[coordinated damages
assessment](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) structure has
independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis
in parallel, so one calendar governs the whole damages presentation.
