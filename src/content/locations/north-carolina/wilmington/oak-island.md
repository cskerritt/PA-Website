An evaluee in Oak Island should not have to travel far, or at all, for a
credible expert evaluation. That logistical premise shapes how Purinton
Analytics serves this small Brunswick County town: the file, not the
evaluee, does most of the moving. Records are reviewed remotely,
interviews are scheduled by video or telephone where clinically
appropriate, and when the referral questions call for an in-person
assessment, the evaluation comes to the evaluee's community rather than
requiring a trip to a distant office. The same logistics support both
[vocational assessment](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) and [life
care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), for counsel on either
side of the caption.

## Where an Oak Island case is tried

Oak Island lies in Brunswick County, whose state-court civil docket runs
through the Superior Court. When a matter is federal, North Carolina
offers the United States District Court for the Eastern District of North
Carolina, the United States District Court for the Middle District of
North Carolina, and the United States District Court for the Western
District of North Carolina as its forums; the district that governs a
particular Oak Island case is identified during the conflict check and
intake process rather than presumed from geography. Whatever the forum,
the written opinion is prepared to the same standard, on the assumption
that opposing counsel will read every line. Regional coverage is
described on the [Wilmington metro
page](/locations/north-carolina/wilmington/).

## The labor market behind the opinion

A small town does not have a self-contained labor market, and the
analysis does not pretend otherwise. Vocational findings for Oak Island
evaluees draw on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and
wage estimates for the Wilmington metropolitan area and for North
Carolina statewide, on state workforce agency labor market information,
and on direct labor market research when a specific occupation or
employer question needs testing. The market is characterized
qualitatively; quantification of lost earnings, where required, is
handled through [coordinated damages
assessment](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/), under which
independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis
within a single engagement structure.

## What by-appointment service means here

Purinton Analytics maintains no storefront on Oak Island and lists no
local address, and it says so plainly because candor about offices is of
a piece with candor in reports. Travel for in-person evaluations,
depositions, and trial testimony is arranged from the Kansas City home
office and planned around the court's calendar. For severely injured
evaluees, the firm often recommends the remote route first, since a
well-documented remote evaluation frequently serves the record better
than a burdensome trip. The complete service model is set out on the
[nationwide practice page](/locations/nationwide/).

## Opening an engagement

Every Oak Island referral starts the same way: [request a conflict
check](/refer-a-case/) with the case caption and parties, and flag any
imminent report deadline or hearing. Once conflicts clear, intake
settles scope, records, and schedule, and confirms any qualification
requirement particular to the venue. Statewide coverage, including the
other communities the practice serves in North Carolina, appears on the
[North Carolina locations page](/locations/north-carolina/). Counsel get
the same evaluation discipline here that the practice applies in its
largest metropolitan engagements.
