## Brunswick County venue and the federal forums

Leland sits in Brunswick County, where state-court civil matters are
tried in the Superior Court. Federal litigation in North Carolina
proceeds in the United States District Court for the Eastern District of
North Carolina, the United States District Court for the Middle District
of North Carolina, or the United States District Court for the Western
District of North Carolina; the district that will control a particular
Leland case is confirmed during the conflict check and intake process,
never assumed. The forum matters to the records plan as much as to the
caption: state and federal disclosure practice impose different
obligations on expert reports, and intake fixes the applicable format
before drafting begins. The [Wilmington metro

## Leland's place in the regional labor market

Vocational findings for a Leland evaluee are grounded in Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Wilmington
metropolitan area and for North Carolina statewide, supplemented by
state workforce agency labor market information and by direct labor
market research when the referral questions warrant it. The published
estimates describe the occupations the regional market actually
supports; the analysis supplies the connection to the individual,
matching documented restrictions and transferable skills to work
realistically within reach. When the file requires that lost earnings be
reduced to a damages figure, the firm offers [coordinated damages

while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.
