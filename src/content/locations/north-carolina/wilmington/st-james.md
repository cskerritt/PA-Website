There is no Purinton Analytics office in St. James, and this page will
not pretend there is one. What counsel handling a St. James matter
actually needs is not a local storefront but a defensible opinion: a
[vocational analysis](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) or [life care
plan](/services/life-care-planning/) built on documented restrictions,
tested against real labor market data, and written so that
cross-examination confirms rather than erodes it. The firm serves this
small Brunswick County community by appointment, for plaintiff and
defense engagements alike, and treats its honesty about presence as part
of the same discipline that governs its reports.

## Venue for St. James disputes

State-court claims arising in St. James belong to Brunswick County,
where the trial forum is the Superior Court. On the federal side, North
Carolina's district courts are the United States District Court for the
Eastern District of North Carolina, the United States District Court for
the Middle District of North Carolina, and the United States District
Court for the Western District of North Carolina; the controlling venue
for any specific case is confirmed during the conflict check and intake
process. Because report obligations differ between state and federal
practice, intake also fixes the disclosure format before drafting
begins. The firm's regional coverage is outlined on the [Wilmington
metro page](/locations/north-carolina/wilmington/).

## Grounding opinions in the regional market

Earning capacity questions for a St. James evaluee are answered with
Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates
covering the Wilmington metropolitan area and North Carolina statewide,
along with state workforce agency labor market information and, where
the questions demand it, direct contact with the labor market itself.
No wage figure or employment count appears until the signed report, and
the qualitative narrative never substitutes for individualized analysis
of transferable skills. If the case requires an economic loss
calculation, the firm's [coordinated damages
assessment](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) supplies it, with
independent economist partners performing and signing the economic
analysis inside the same engagement.

## Evaluating an evaluee where they live

By-appointment service means the engagement adapts to the evaluee. Most
record review and interviewing is completed remotely, which spares an
injured person unnecessary travel; when an in-person evaluation will
strengthen the record, it takes place in the evaluee's own community,
with the evaluator traveling from the Kansas City home office. Family
members and caregivers are interviewed when daily care routines bear on
the plan, because those routines rarely surface in medical charts. The
[nationwide practice page](/locations/nationwide/) describes this
remote-first model and the standards behind it.

## Starting the conflict check

Referrals begin with the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/): submit
the caption or party names and identify any looming disclosure,
deposition, or trial date. After clearance, intake defines scope,
records, schedule, and any venue-specific qualification requirement.
For a view of the practice's full footprint across the state, see the
[North Carolina locations page](/locations/north-carolina/). St. James
counsel receive the same methodology the firm brings to every
engagement, regardless of the size of the town the evaluee calls home,
and the opinion that results is written to be read by the lawyer on the
other side of the case as much as by the one who commissioned it.
