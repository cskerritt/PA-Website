Labor market evidence is where a vocational opinion is won or lost, and
Morrisville cases put that evidence to a real test. The town belongs to
a metropolitan labor market anchored by Raleigh, so the question is
rarely whether work exists within reach; it is whether this
particular evaluee, with these particular restrictions, can realistically
obtain and hold it. Purinton Analytics builds Morrisville opinions to
answer that harder question, serving plaintiff and defense counsel
through [vocational expert evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/),
[medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/), and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## The data stack for a Morrisville opinion

The foundation is the Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment
and wage estimates program, applied at the level of the Raleigh
metropolitan area and North Carolina statewide. On top of that sit state
workforce agency labor market information and, where a referral question
requires it, direct labor market research documenting what regional
employers actually demand. Each layer is cited so it can be pulled and
checked. The individual analysis then does the decisive work: the
evaluee's transferable skills, the restrictions the medical record
supports, and the occupations that remain realistically accessible are
established person by person, never inferred from regional averages.

## Courts serving Morrisville parties

Morrisville is a Wake County municipality, and its state-court matters
proceed in the Superior Court of Wake County. North Carolina's federal
forums are the United States District Court for the Eastern District of
North Carolina, the United States District Court for the Middle District
of North Carolina, and the United States District Court for the Western
District of North Carolina; the district that controls a given
Morrisville caption is determined during the conflict check and intake
rather than guessed at. Whichever court receives the report, it is
drafted for the reader most motivated to find its flaws, which is how
flaws get found before filing instead of after.
