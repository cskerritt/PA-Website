## Johnston County venue, statewide forums

State-court litigation involving Clayton parties is heard in the
Superior Court of Johnston County. On the federal side, North Carolina
offers the United States District Court for the Eastern District of
North Carolina, the United States District Court for the Middle District
of North Carolina, and the United States District Court for the Western
District of North Carolina; the district governing any particular
Clayton matter is confirmed during the conflict check and intake instead
of being assumed from geography. The report itself is indifferent to the
forum in the only way that matters: it is written so either courtroom's
most skeptical reader can trace every source and reproduce every step.

## A Johnston County evaluee, a metro-scale market

Although Clayton's courthouse is in Johnston County, its labor market is
not confined there. Residents work across the metropolitan area anchored
by Raleigh, so vocational findings are tested against Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Raleigh
metropolitan area and for North Carolina statewide, supported by state
workforce agency labor market information and by direct labor market
research when the referral questions require employer-level evidence.
The market is described in qualitative terms; the opinion's weight rests
on the individual record, where transferable skills, restrictions, and
realistic occupational access are documented against the standard of
cross-examination.
