side needs an opinion that will survive the other one. The same methods,
the same sources, and the same reluctance to overstate apply whether the
retaining lawyer represents the injured worker or the defendant, and that
symmetry is deliberate, because an expert whose conclusions depend on who
is paying is not an expert worth calling. Garner counsel retain the firm
for [vocational expert evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),

## Garner's courthouse geography

Garner lies within Wake County, and its state-court litigation is heard
in the Superior Court of Wake County. Federal matters find their forums
among the United States District Court for the Eastern District of North
Carolina, the United States District Court for the Middle District of
North Carolina, and the United States District Court for the Western
District of North Carolina, with the controlling district for a
particular caption identified during the conflict check and intake. The
adversarial standard travels to each of those rooms unchanged: cite
what can be pulled, describe what can be replicated, conclude only what
the record will hold.

## What the regional data can and cannot prove

A Garner evaluee competes in the labor market of the surrounding
metropolitan area, anchored by Raleigh, and the firm characterizes that
market with Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage
estimates for the Raleigh metropolitan area and for North Carolina
statewide, alongside state workforce agency labor market information and
direct labor market research where the questions require it. Published
estimates establish context; they do not by themselves prove what any
individual can earn. The report therefore keeps them apart, presenting
regional conditions qualitatively while grounding its conclusions in the
evaluee's documented skills, restrictions, and realistic access to work,
so that neither side can accuse the analysis of substituting averages for
evidence.

## By appointment, from Kansas City

Honesty about presence is part of the product. The firm keeps no Garner
office, publishes no local street address, and claims no storefront
anywhere in the region; Garner is served by appointment from the Kansas
City home office. Most engagements open remotely, through records
review, structured interviews, and standardized testing administered
under publisher protocols validated for remote use. When in-person
evaluation in the evaluee's community is warranted, or when deposition
and trial dates require the expert present, travel is scheduled from
Kansas City. Counsel needing help preparing for an opposing expert can
add [expert testimony and litigation consulting](/services/expert-testimony-litigation-consulting/)
to the engagement.
