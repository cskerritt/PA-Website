## Wake County venue, state and federal

Cary sits in Wake County, and state-court matters involving Cary parties
proceed in the Superior Court there. North Carolina's federal forums are
the United States District Court for the Eastern District of North
Carolina, the United States District Court for the Middle District of
North Carolina, and the United States District Court for the Western
District of North Carolina; which district controls a particular caption
is confirmed during the conflict check and intake rather than assumed in
advance. The work product does not change with the courthouse. Every
report is prepared so that its sources can be pulled, its methods can be
replicated, and its conclusions can be cross-examined without surprise.

## Reading Cary's place in the regional market

Earning capacity for a Cary evaluee is tested against the commuting
market that the town shares with Raleigh and the surrounding communities,
not against Cary's boundaries alone. The foundation is Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Raleigh
metropolitan area and for North Carolina statewide, supplemented by state
workforce agency labor market information and by direct labor market
research when the referral questions call for employer-level detail. The
regional data frames what is possible; the individual analysis of
transferable skills, restrictions, and realistic placement options
determines what is probable, and the report keeps those layers distinct
so opposing counsel can see exactly where each conclusion comes from.

## How engagements are staffed and met

The firm keeps no office in Cary and publishes no street address for this
location; candor about presence is part of the same discipline that
governs the reports. Work is directed from the Kansas City home office,
and most engagements open remotely with records review, structured
interviews, and standardized testing administered under publisher
protocols that support remote use. When an in-person evaluation in the
evaluee's own community is warranted, or when deposition or trial
testimony requires presence, travel is scheduled from Kansas City and
coordinated with counsel's deadlines. Where the damages theory requires
economic modeling, [forensic economic analyses](/services/forensic-economic-damages/)
are performed and signed by independent economist partners coordinated by
the firm.
