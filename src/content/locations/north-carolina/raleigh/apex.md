## The courts that hear Apex matters

Apex is a Wake County town, and state-court litigation involving Apex
parties is heard in the Superior Court of Wake County. In federal
practice, North Carolina offers the United States District Court for the
Eastern District of North Carolina, the United States District Court for
the Middle District of North Carolina, and the United States District
Court for the Western District of North Carolina as its trial forums; the
controlling district for any specific matter is settled during the
conflict check and intake, not presumed. Because either track ends with a
report the other side will study line by line, the file is assembled to
the same evidentiary standard regardless of where the caption lands.

## An Apex evaluee in a metro-wide market

Apex is a substantial suburb, but its residents work across a
commuting market anchored by Raleigh, and vocational opinions here are
tested against that wider market. The analysis rests on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Raleigh
metropolitan area and for North Carolina statewide, on state workforce
agency labor market information, and on direct labor market research when
a referral question turns on actual openings rather than published
estimates. Market data alone never carries an opinion: the evaluee's
transferable skills, medical restrictions, and realistic access to
specific occupations are documented individually, because that is the
level at which cross-examination happens.

## Scheduling the evaluation itself

There is no Apex office and no local street address; the firm says so
plainly because accuracy about its footprint belongs to the same
discipline as accuracy in its reports. Engagements are run from the
Kansas City home office, typically opening remotely with records review,
structured interviews, and standardized testing under publisher-approved
remote protocols. When the questions presented require meeting the
evaluee in person in the community where they live and work, or when
testimony requires presence, travel from Kansas City is arranged around
the same litigation calendar that shaped the engagement from the start.
When an opposing expert's report needs testing,
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) proceeds on
