metropolitan area that surrounds Raleigh, and many of its residents earn
their living somewhere other than the town where they sleep, which
means an honest earning-capacity opinion here cannot stop at the town
line. Purinton Analytics begins Wake Forest engagements with that
geography in view, offering plaintiff and defense counsel
[vocational expert evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/),

[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic

## Where a Wake Forest caption is heard

Wake Forest lies in Wake County, and its state-court disputes proceed in
the Superior Court of Wake County. At the federal level, North Carolina's
available forums are the United States District Court for the Eastern
District of North Carolina, the United States District Court for the
Middle District of North Carolina, and the United States District Court
for the Western District of North Carolina, with the venue that governs a
given case confirmed during the conflict check and intake. The firm's
posture toward all of them is identical: write the report so the judge
can follow the method and the opposing expert can find nothing hidden.

## Measuring the market a commuter actually faces

Because Wake Forest workers participate in a metro-wide labor market, the
vocational analysis draws on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational
employment and wage estimates for the Raleigh metropolitan area and for
North Carolina statewide, together with state workforce agency labor
market information. Where a referral question demands more granularity
than published estimates provide, direct labor market research documents
what employers in the region are actually seeking. The regional picture
is always characterized qualitatively; the load-bearing findings concern
the individual evaluee, whose transferable skills, restrictions, and
realistic placement options are analyzed and cited so that either side
can trace every step.
