## North Carolina's federal map

The state's federal trial forums are the United States District Court for
the Eastern District of North Carolina, the United States District Court
for the Middle District of North Carolina, and the United States District
Court for the Western District of North Carolina. Rather than assume
which of them governs a Fuquay-Varina matter, the firm confirms the
controlling venue during the conflict check and intake, then prepares the
file to the standard the toughest forum would demand. A report built for
cross-examination in the strictest forum needs no rewriting for any
other; sources
remain citable, methods remain replicable, and conclusions remain no
stronger than the record.

## The labor market behind the opinion

Fuquay-Varina is a suburban town whose residents participate in the
commuting economy centered on Raleigh, and vocational conclusions here
are tested against that regional market. The evidentiary base is Bureau
of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the
Raleigh metropolitan area and for North Carolina statewide, joined by
state workforce agency labor market information and, when the referral
questions warrant, direct labor market research with employers in the
region. Regional conditions are described qualitatively and
conservatively. The decisive analysis is individual: what this evaluee
can still do, what restrictions the medical record supports, and what
occupations remain realistically open, each documented so opposing
counsel can test it.
