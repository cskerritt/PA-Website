## Wendell in Wake County's court system

Wendell sits in Wake County, and its state-court disputes are heard in
the Superior Court of Wake County. Federal claims proceed in North
Carolina's district courts, which are the United States District Court
for the Eastern District of North Carolina, the United States District
Court for the Middle District of North Carolina, and the United States
District Court for the Western District of North Carolina; determining
which of them controls a given Wendell matter is a task for the conflict
check and intake, not for assumption. In each forum the file is held to
the standard of the reader who wants it to fail, because that reader is
guaranteed to exist.

## Why a small town gets metro-scale data

An evaluee living in Wendell does not job-search only in Wendell. The
town's workers draw on the labor market of the wider region anchored by
Raleigh, so the analysis uses Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational
employment and wage estimates for the Raleigh metropolitan area and for
North Carolina statewide, alongside state workforce agency labor market
information and direct labor market research when the referral questions
demand evidence beyond published estimates. That regional lens is
described qualitatively and kept in its place: the opinion's foundation
is the individual evaluee's documented skills, restrictions, and
realistic access to specific work, developed thoroughly enough to hold
under cross-examination.

## Distance without dilution

The firm's only home office is in Kansas City; there is no Wendell
