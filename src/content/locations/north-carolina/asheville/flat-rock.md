Flat Rock is a small village, but the damages questions that arise there
are not small. A catastrophic injury to a Flat Rock resident raises the
same analytical problems as the same injury anywhere: what work the person
could do before, what work remains realistically available now, what care
the medical record supports, and what all of that means over a working
life. Purinton Analytics prices none of its rigor by the size of the town.
The village's scale changes the logistics of an engagement, not its
standards, and counsel on either side of a Flat Rock caption receive the
same documented, source-cited analysis the firm produces for its largest
matters.

## A village evaluee, a regional labor market

Because Flat Rock itself is small, the labor market analysis looks
outward: a Flat Rock evaluee's employment prospects are assessed against
the commuting economy of the metropolitan area whose principal city is
Asheville. The firm draws on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational
employment and wage estimates for the relevant area and for North Carolina
statewide, on state workforce agency labor market information, and on
direct labor market research where the referral questions warrant it,
describing the region's scale in qualitative terms only. That outward
frame protects the opinion from the false precision of treating a village
as its own economy.
