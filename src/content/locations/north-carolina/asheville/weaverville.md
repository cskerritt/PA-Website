A national expert practice used to mean a practice concentrated in the
largest cities, with everywhere else served reluctantly or not at all.
Purinton Analytics was structured against that model. Its
[national practice](/locations/nationwide/) is remote-first by design,
which means a referral from Weaverville, a small Buncombe County town, is
handled with the same infrastructure, the same protocols, and the same
turnaround discipline as a referral from any major litigation center.
Records move electronically, conferences with counsel happen wherever
counsel are, and nothing about the analysis depends on the expert and the
evaluee sharing a zip code. Plaintiff and defense counsel engage the firm
on the same terms, and the work is identical either way.

## What remote-first means for a Weaverville referral

It means the conflict check, scope definition, and records request are
completed without a site visit; it means drafting and counsel conferences
proceed on the case's schedule rather than a travel schedule; and it means
in-person time is reserved for the step that genuinely benefits from it,
the evaluation itself. When the referral questions require an in-person
[vocational assessment](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) or
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) evaluation, the
appointment is arranged in the evaluee's own community. What remote-first
never means is a fictitious local presence: the firm's only staffed office
is its Kansas City home office, it publishes no Weaverville address, and
deposition or trial appearances are scheduled from Kansas City against the
court's dates.

## The economy a Weaverville opinion describes

Weaverville workers participate in the labor market of the metropolitan
area whose principal city is Asheville, and the firm's vocational findings
describe that regional market, not the town in isolation. The data
foundation is the Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and
wage estimates for the relevant area and for North Carolina statewide,
joined by state workforce agency labor market information and direct labor
market research where warranted, with scale characterized qualitatively.
Where future medical needs are in issue,
[medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/) proceeds in
the same engagement, and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
bring independent economist partners to perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.
