## Fitting the analysis to the docket

A [vocational assessment](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) or
[life care plan](/services/life-care-planning/) that is rushed to meet a
deadline invites cross-examination on its shortcuts, so the firm is candid
at intake about what can be completed well in the time available. Where
records are still being gathered, the engagement letter says what is
outstanding; where a supplemental report will be needed, that is flagged
early instead of discovered late. Counsel who share the disclosure order
and scheduling order at the outset get a work plan keyed to those dates.

Earning capacity for a Hendersonville evaluee is assessed against the
regional economy anchored by Asheville, the metro's principal city, not
against the town in isolation. Vocational findings rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the relevant
area and for North Carolina statewide, on state workforce agency labor
market information, and on direct labor market research where the referral
questions warrant it. Regional scale is described qualitatively, never
with figures invented for effect. When future care is at issue,

alongside the vocational work, and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
allow independent economist partners to perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.
