Where will a Mills River caption actually be tried? That question shapes
an expert engagement before any record is reviewed, because the venue sets
the disclosure rules, the qualification requirements, and the calendar the
opinion must meet. Mills River is a Henderson County town, so state-court
matters involving Mills River parties proceed in the Superior Court of
Henderson County. Federal matters go to the North Carolina district
courts: the United States District Court for the Eastern District of North
Carolina, the United States District Court for the Middle District of
North Carolina, or the United States District Court for the Western
District of North Carolina. Purinton Analytics does not guess which forum
controls; the governing venue, and whatever registration or qualification
requirement it imposes, is identified and confirmed during the conflict
check and intake process, for plaintiff and defense referrals alike.

## From venue to work plan

Once the forum and its calendar are known, the engagement is scoped
against them: a documented conflict check, a written scope, and a records
request tailored to the claims come before substantive analysis, so the
[vocational opinion](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) or
[life care plan](/services/life-care-planning/) is complete when the
disclosure rules require it rather than amended after. Testimony,
deposition preparation, and
[litigation consulting](/services/expert-testimony-litigation-consulting/)
are planned against the same docket, and where the damages theory needs an
economist, [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
let independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis
while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement
structure.

The firm keeps its only staffed office in Kansas City and runs a
remote-first [national practice](/locations/nationwide/) from it. No Mills
River address is published and none is implied. Records review, scope
