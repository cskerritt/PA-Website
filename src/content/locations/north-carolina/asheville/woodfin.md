Every report Purinton Analytics writes for a Woodfin matter is drafted for
its least sympathetic reader: the opposing counsel who will hunt for an
unsupported assumption, an undisclosed source, or a conclusion that
outruns the record. That is a deliberate publication standard, not a
slogan. The methodology is stated, the data sources are named, the
limitations are acknowledged in the text rather than conceded later
under questioning at deposition, and the same standard applies whether the
retaining party is plaintiff or defense. Counsel in Woodfin cases get the
benefit of that discipline in a report that reads the same way no matter
which table it lands on.

## What the discipline looks like in practice

The file begins with a documented conflict check, a scope defined in
writing, and a records request tailored to the claims. The
[vocational opinion](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) ties each
finding to identified records and identified data; the
[life care plan](/services/life-care-planning/) ties each recommended
service to the medical foundation for it. Where an opposing expert's
report is the assignment, the firm's
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) work applies
the identical standard in reverse, testing the other report's sources and
logic rather than its author. And in
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/),
independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis
while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement
structure, so no opinion is signed by someone outside their own
discipline.

## Buncombe County venue for Woodfin parties

Woodfin sits in Buncombe County, and state-court litigation involving
Woodfin parties proceeds in the Superior Court of Buncombe County. In the
federal system, North Carolina offers the United States District Court for
the Eastern District of North Carolina, the United States District Court
for the Middle District of North Carolina, and the United States District
Court for the Western District of North Carolina as trial forums; the
controlling venue for a particular caption, and any registration or
qualification requirement it carries, is confirmed during the conflict
check and intake process. The
[Asheville metro page](/locations/north-carolina/asheville/) and the
[North Carolina state page](/locations/north-carolina/) describe the
surrounding coverage.

## The market context behind a Woodfin opinion

Woodfin is a small Buncombe County town within the metropolitan area whose
principal city is Asheville, and its residents' employment prospects are
evaluated against that regional commuting market. Vocational findings draw
on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates
for the relevant area and for North Carolina statewide, on state workforce
agency labor market information, and on direct labor market research where
warranted, with regional scale described only in qualitative terms.

## Appointments, not an address

The firm's single staffed office is its home office in Kansas City. It
publishes no Woodfin or Asheville-area address, because claiming a local
office it does not keep would contradict the accuracy it promises in its
reports. Work runs remotely through the
[national practice](/locations/nationwide/); in-person evaluation happens
in the evaluee's own community when the referral requires it, and
courtroom or deposition presence is scheduled from Kansas City.
