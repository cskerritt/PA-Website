The person at the center of a Black Mountain case is an evaluee, not a
file, and the evaluation should meet that person where they live. Purinton
Analytics arranges in-person vocational and life care planning assessments
in the evaluee's own community, scheduled around treatment appointments
and functional limitations, so a Black Mountain resident is never asked to
absorb the cost of an expert's geography. Everything that does not require
presence, records review, scope negotiation, counsel conferences, and
drafting, is handled remotely through the firm's
[national practice](/locations/nationwide/). The firm is retained by
plaintiff and defense counsel alike, and it writes each report to
withstand the scrutiny of whichever side did not commission it.

## What an in-community assessment involves

An [in-person vocational evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/)
gathers work history, transferable skills, education, and functional
information directly from the evaluee, against the medical record counsel
has produced. A [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/)
assessment adds the treating providers' recommendations and the evaluee's
current care arrangements. Both feed a documented file that begins the
same way in Black Mountain as anywhere else: conflict check before
anything substantive, then a defined scope, then a records request
tailored to the claims. There is no
Black Mountain office and no published local address; the firm's only
staffed location is its Kansas City home office, and it says so plainly
because accuracy about offices belongs to the same discipline that governs
its opinions.

## Buncombe County courts and the statewide forums

Black Mountain lies in Buncombe County, and state-court matters involving
its residents proceed in the Superior Court of Buncombe County. In federal
litigation, North Carolina's trial forums are the United States District
Court for the Eastern District of North Carolina, the United States
District Court for the Middle District of North Carolina, and the United
States District Court for the Western District of North Carolina. The
controlling venue for a given caption, and any qualification requirement
that venue imposes, is confirmed during the conflict check and intake
process. The [Asheville metro page](/locations/north-carolina/asheville/)
covers the wider region, and the
[North Carolina state page](/locations/north-carolina/) describes
statewide coverage.

## Reading the labor market from Black Mountain

A Black Mountain evaluee's employment prospects are measured against the
commuting economy of the metropolitan area whose principal city is
Asheville. The firm grounds those findings in Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates for the relevant area and for
North Carolina statewide, in state workforce agency labor market
information, and in direct labor market research where warranted, and it
characterizes the region's scale in qualitative terms only. Where the
damages theory requires it,
[medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/) runs
alongside the vocational analysis, and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
place independent economist partners in charge of performing and signing
the economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds
under one engagement structure.

## Referring a Black Mountain matter

The engagement starts at the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/): provide the caption or party names,
a short description of the claims, and any disclosure, deposition, or
trial dates already on the docket. The firm replies with a proposed scope
and records list, and, where an in-community assessment is needed, with a
plan for getting it scheduled promptly in Black Mountain.
