the standard Purinton Analytics brings to Greensboro-High Point litigation.
Every engagement in the metro begins with a documented conflict check, a
defined scope, and a records request tailored to the claims, so that the
written opinion rests on evidence both sides of the caption can examine.

[vocational expert evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),

[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)

District Court for the Middle District of North Carolina, the United States
District Court for the Eastern District of North Carolina, and the United

[North Carolina practice page](/locations/north-carolina/).

## No storefront, and no pretense otherwise

The firm maintains its home office in Kansas City and keeps no staffed
office in Greensboro, High Point, or anywhere else in the metro. It
publishes no local street address, because a page that implied a storefront
would fail the same accuracy standard the firm applies to its reports.
Meetings are by appointment. The working default is remote: records review,
structured interviews by video, and standardized testing under publisher
protocols where remote administration is supported. When an evaluation
should happen in person, or when a deposition or trial requires attendance,
travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office around the deadlines
counsel actually face.

## Labor market data for Guilford County evaluees and their neighbors

Employability and earning capacity findings for evaluees in the metro rest
on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for
the Greensboro-High Point metropolitan area and for North Carolina
statewide, alongside state workforce agency labor market information and
direct labor market research where the referral questions call for it. Life
care plans and medical cost projections are priced to the evaluee's own
community and provider market. The report documents each source and each
step, because a transparent method is the only kind that survives
cross-examination, and it should not matter which party retained the author.
