A Louisiana referral to Purinton Analytics begins with process rather than promises: a conflict check run against the caption, a scope agreed in writing, and a records request tailored to the claims actually pleaded. That order of operations is deliberate. By the time a vocational assessment or life care plan is drafted for a Louisiana matter, the file already shows what was asked, what was reviewed, and what the opinion does and does not address, which is the posture an expert should be in before opposing counsel ever picks up the report.

## Louisiana courts and venues

Engagements are accepted for matters pending in the District Courts of Louisiana, the state's trial courts, and in the United States District Court for the Eastern District of Louisiana, the United States District Court for the Middle District of Louisiana, and the United States District Court for the Western District of Louisiana. Dedicated pages cover the New Orleans-Metairie, Baton Rouge, Lafayette, and Shreveport-Bossier City areas, along with Slidell-Mandeville-Covington, Lake Charles, Monroe, Houma-Bayou Cane-Thibodaux, Alexandria, Hammond, Opelousas, New Iberia, Ruston, Morgan City, and Bogalusa. Coverage is statewide; the metro pages describe local logistics rather than limits.

## How the practice serves Louisiana

The practice is remote-first, so distance rarely touches a Louisiana case schedule. Records review, video vocational interviews, report drafting, and pre-deposition preparation all proceed remotely, keyed to the litigation calendar rather than to travel. Louisiana meetings are by appointment: the firm maintains no staffed office in the state and publishes no Louisiana street address, and it states that plainly because honesty about presence is part of the discipline the reports themselves are built on. When in-person evaluation is warranted, or when deposition and trial dates require attendance, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office. Vocational findings for Louisiana evaluees rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the relevant metropolitan areas and for Louisiana statewide, state workforce agency labor market information, and direct labor market research where warranted. When the referral requires a full damages presentation, independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement structure. The firm takes plaintiff and defense engagements alike, and each report is written so either side of the caption can examine its basis line by line.

## Qualification and disclosure

Expert practice differs from venue to venue, and the burden of tracking those differences should not fall on referring counsel. Any venue-specific registration or qualification requirement is identified and confirmed during the conflict check and intake process, and disclosure materials, prior testimony lists, and fee schedules are furnished in the format the court expects. Intake also settles deadlines and the records that must be in hand before an opinion can responsibly issue.
