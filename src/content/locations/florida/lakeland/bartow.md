## Labor market evidence built to a deadline

Working against a scheduling order never justifies thinner evidence. Earning
capacity findings for a Bartow evaluee are grounded in Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the
Lakeland-Winter Haven metropolitan area and for Florida statewide, together
with state workforce agency labor market information; direct labor market
research is added when the transferable skills picture requires employer-level
answers. The report documents each source in a form both sides can check,
because an opinion assembled in a hurry that cannot show its work is worth
little at deposition. On compressed timelines the sequence tightens but
never truncates: the records request goes out the day scope is signed, the
interview and testing are calendared immediately, and drafting proceeds in
parallel so the disclosed report is a finished opinion rather than a
placeholder.
