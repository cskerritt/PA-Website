Often the Davenport file arrives with an expert report already in it: the
other side has disclosed a vocational assessment or life care plan, and
counsel need to know whether it will hold. Purinton Analytics performs
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) for exactly that
posture, testing an opposing expert's assumptions, sources, and arithmetic
against the record before anyone is deposed. The review proceeds
methodically: each conclusion is traced back to the data and reasoning
offered in its support, and the written product documents where the two part
ways, in terms a trier of fact can follow. The same rigor supports
affirmative work, from
[vocational evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) of
employability and earning capacity to
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and the firm is
retained by plaintiff and defense counsel on identical terms: the method
does not change with the client.

## Testing wage opinions against published data

Whether reviewing another expert's conclusions or producing original
findings, the benchmark is the same: Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational
employment and wage estimates for the Lakeland-Winter Haven metropolitan
area and for Florida statewide, read alongside state workforce agency labor
market information and, where a dispute turns on actual hiring practice,
direct labor market research. For a Davenport matter, those published
sources anchor the earning-capacity analysis, and an opposing opinion whose
wage conclusions cannot be traced to them, or to any disclosed methodology,
is precisely what the peer-review process is designed to surface. The
evaluee's own work history, transferable skills, and documented restrictions
are weighed against that same data, so affirmative and rebuttal opinions in
the same case rest on one consistent foundation rather than two competing
sets of assumptions.

## Meeting the evaluee where they are

There is no local Purinton Analytics office in Davenport; the firm publishes
no street address here and meets by appointment only, a fact stated plainly
because credibility is not divisible. Evaluations normally start remotely,
with records review, structured interviews, and standardized testing under
remote-approved publisher protocols, and a
[medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/) can be priced
to the evaluee's own care market within the same engagement. When presence is
required, whether for an in-person evaluation or for testimony, the trip is
scheduled from the Kansas City home office well ahead of the committed date.
