A case from a small town deserves the same expert discipline as a case from
a skyline. Dundee is a small Polk County community, and the injuries and
employment disputes that arise here carry stakes just as real as any
big-city docket, so Purinton Analytics applies an unvaried method to them:
documented conflict check, defined scope, records request matched to the
claims, and a written opinion each side can interrogate. Counsel for
plaintiffs and counsel for defendants retain the firm here on identical
terms for [vocational evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[coordinated damages work](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) in
which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis
under a single engagement structure.

## Venue for Dundee disputes

Dundee's state court matters are heard in the Circuit Court serving Polk
County. Should a claim proceed federally, Florida presents its district
courts as the available forums, the United States District Court for the
Middle District of Florida, the United States District Court for the
Northern District of Florida, and the United States District Court for the
Southern District of Florida among them; the controlling venue, and any
expert registration or qualification rule it applies, is confirmed in the
conflict check and intake. Broader regional practice is described on the
[Lakeland-Winter Haven metro page](/locations/florida/lakeland/), with
statewide coverage on the [Florida practice page](/locations/florida/).

## The market a Dundee worker actually faces

Earning capacity for a Dundee evaluee is not measured against the town
alone; residents work throughout the surrounding metro, and the opinion says
so with evidence. The foundation is Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational
employment and wage estimates for the Lakeland-Winter Haven metropolitan
area and for Florida statewide, joined by state workforce agency labor
market information and by direct labor market research whenever the
transferable skills analysis needs employer-level confirmation. Scale is
described qualitatively and sourced precisely, which is what lets a report
travel from a small town into a contested courtroom without apology. Where
restrictions narrow the occupational field, the analysis names the specific
capacities that remain and connects them to demand the published data can
verify, rather than gesturing at categories.

## What retention looks like from Dundee

The firm keeps no office in Dundee, claims none, and meets by appointment.
An engagement typically runs remotely from start to finish: records review,
structured interview, standardized testing under remote-capable publisher
protocols, then a report built for disclosure. If the referral requires
evaluating the evaluee in person, or if deposition or trial testimony is
scheduled, the expert travels from the Kansas City home office on dates
locked with counsel in advance. A
[medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/) priced to the
evaluee's own care market can be folded into the same retention.
