Consider what actually happens when Purinton Analytics is retained on an
Auburndale case. A records request tailored to the claims goes out after the
conflict check clears. The evaluee is interviewed, in most engagements
remotely, and tested under standardized instruments whose publishers permit
remote administration. Then the analysis is written up as a
[vocational expert opinion](/services/vocational-expert-witness/), a
[life care plan](/services/life-care-planning/), or both, in a form intended
to be read closely by the lawyer on the other side. That sequence is the
whole pitch: no marketing gloss, just a documented process that plaintiff and
defense counsel alike can rely on.

## Where the resulting opinion gets used

Auburndale belongs to Polk County, so state court matters are venued in the
Circuit Court serving the county. Federal matters from Florida may proceed in
the United States District Court for the Northern District of Florida, the
United States District Court for the Middle District of Florida, or the
United States District Court for the Southern District of Florida, and the
controlling forum for any particular claim is confirmed at intake along with
any venue-specific qualification requirement. How the same retention reaches
the rest of the region is covered on the
[Lakeland-Winter Haven metro page](/locations/florida/lakeland/) and across
the state on the [Florida practice page](/locations/florida/).

## Grounding wages in the market around Auburndale

Because Auburndale workers compete throughout the surrounding metro, the
earning-capacity analysis draws on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational
employment and wage estimates published for the Lakeland-Winter Haven
metropolitan area and for Florida statewide, checked against state workforce
agency labor market information. Where a placement question turns on what
actual employers require, direct labor market research supplements the
published data. Future care in a
[medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/) is priced the
same way, from provider-specific inquiry and published fee data for the
evaluee's own region rather than borrowed national figures.

## Logistics for the evaluee, candor for the court

No staffed Purinton Analytics office exists in Auburndale, and none is
claimed; every meeting is by appointment. Remote-first work keeps costs
proportionate and schedules flexible, and it is the default until the
referral questions justify more. When an in-person evaluation is warranted,
or when deposition and trial obligations require attendance, the expert
travels from the Kansas City home office on dates confirmed with counsel in
advance. The report discloses how each step was conducted, so the method
itself is never a surprise at cross-examination. Counsel receive the working
timeline at the outset, including when records must arrive for the schedule
to hold, so the evaluation and the litigation calendar move together instead
of colliding.
