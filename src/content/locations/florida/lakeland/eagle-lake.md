often not liability but the lifetime of care ahead, and that is where
Purinton Analytics is most frequently brought in. A
[life care plan](/services/life-care-planning/) built here itemizes future

companion [medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/)

[vocational evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) addresses
employability and earning capacity, and the whole package is prepared to the

## Earning capacity beyond the town line

Vocational conclusions for an Eagle Lake evaluee are anchored to the labor
market residents actually reach, which extends across the metro. The
evidence base is Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage
estimates for the Lakeland-Winter Haven metropolitan area and for Florida
statewide, supported by state workforce agency labor market information and
sharpened with direct labor market research when a placement or wage
question calls for employer contact. Care costs and wage findings alike are
tied to sources either party can verify, because a damages opinion is only
as strong as its weakest citation. Each recommended item of future care is
traced to the clinical record or to a treating recommendation, so the plan
reads as an extension of the medicine rather than a wish list appended to
it.
