## Polk County courts and the federal forums

Haines City sits in Polk County. Claims filed in state court proceed in the
Circuit Court serving the county, while federal claims from Florida may be
heard in the United States District Court for the Northern District of
Florida, the United States District Court for the Middle District of Florida,
or the United States District Court for the Southern District of Florida; the
district that governs a particular case is settled during intake, together
with any registration or qualification step a venue imposes. The
[metro practice page for Lakeland-Winter Haven](/locations/florida/lakeland/)
and the [Florida overview](/locations/florida/) describe how a retention here
extends to related matters elsewhere in the state.

## A commuter's earning capacity, measured honestly

When an evaluee commutes for work, the earning-capacity question becomes
where the person can realistically compete, at what wage, with which
residual capacities. Answering it starts with the Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates covering the Lakeland-Winter Haven
metropolitan area and Florida as a whole, layered with state workforce agency
labor market information and, when the referral warrants, direct labor market
research with employers who actually hire in the occupations at issue. The
resulting report states its assumptions openly so that opposing counsel can
test them, which is precisely the standard the firm holds itself to whether
retained by plaintiff or defense.
