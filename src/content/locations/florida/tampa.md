Every Tampa Bay engagement begins the same way the practice begins
everywhere: with a documented conflict check, a defined scope, and a records
request tailored to the claims. That discipline, rather than any local
address, is what Purinton Analytics offers counsel across the
Tampa-St. Petersburg-Clearwater metropolitan area, on both the plaintiff and
defense sides of the caption. The work most often requested from the region
is [vocational expert evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) of
earning capacity and employability, [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/)
for catastrophic injuries, and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## The Tampa Bay venue footprint

The metro spans Hillsborough County, Pinellas County, Pasco County, and
Hernando County, and the firm accepts matters venued in the Circuit Court
serving each of them. Federal matters proceed in Florida's district courts,
including the United States District Court for the Middle District of
Florida, the United States District Court for the Northern District of
Florida, and the United States District Court for the Southern District of
Florida. Counsel elsewhere in the state, from the Panhandle to the southeast
coast, are served through the firm's broader
[Florida practice](/locations/florida/) under the same report standard.

## How the firm appears in Tampa

There is no staffed Purinton Analytics office in Tampa or St. Petersburg,
and the firm publishes no street address here; meetings are by appointment.
That candor is deliberate. Engagements typically open remotely, with records
review, structured interviews, and standardized testing administered under
publisher protocols where remote administration is supported. When the
referral questions call for an in-person evaluation, or when deposition and
trial calendars require presence, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City
home office. Counsel work with a single point of contact from intake through
testimony, and the written product does not vary with the location of the
work.

## Labor market evidence for the region

Vocational opinions for Tampa Bay evaluees are grounded in Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the
Tampa-St. Petersburg-Clearwater metropolitan area and for Florida statewide,
along with state workforce agency labor market information and direct labor
market research where warranted. Care-related costs are handled with the
same locality discipline:
[medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/) and life care
plans for evaluees in the region are priced to the evaluee's own market
using provider-specific inquiries and published fee data, not national
averages. When the assignment is to test another expert's work, the firm's
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) applies the same
sources and methods it uses in first-chair engagements.

## Opening a conflict check

To begin a Tampa Bay engagement, submit the caption or party names through
the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and flag any imminent disclosure,
deposition, or trial dates. Any venue-specific registration or qualification
requirement is identified and confirmed during the conflict check and intake
process, and scheduling is confirmed as soon as clearance is complete. Firms
with dockets that extend beyond Florida can review the practice's
[national coverage](/locations/nationwide/), which runs on the same
remote-first model described here.
