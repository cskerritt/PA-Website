For a catastrophically injured evaluee in Marion County, the questions that
decide a damages case are practical ones: what care will be needed over a
lifetime, what it will cost, and what work remains realistic. Purinton
Analytics answers those questions for Ocala counsel through
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/),
[medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/), and
[vocational evaluation and expert testimony](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
in engagements accepted from both sides of the caption. Where the referral
also requires economic damages, the firm coordinates
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## The Marion County venue

The Ocala metro is a single-county venue, and state court engagements are
accepted for matters in the Circuit Court serving Marion County. Federal
engagements are accepted in Florida's district courts: the United States
District Court for the Northern District of Florida, the United States
District Court for the Middle District of Florida, and the United States
District Court for the Southern District of Florida. Each engagement
begins the way it does everywhere else in the practice, with a documented
conflict check, a defined scope, and a records request tailored to the
claims, so that the written opinion rests on a record both sides can
examine. The [Florida practice page](/locations/florida/) covers the
firm's work in the state's other venues.

## Appointments, evaluations, and travel

Ocala meetings are by appointment. The firm maintains no staffed office in
the metro and publishes no local address, and it will not imply otherwise,
because candor about presence is part of the discipline counsel are
retaining. Records review, scoping conferences, and many evaluation
protocols proceed remotely through secure video and document exchange.
When the referral calls for an in-person evaluation, the evaluation is
scheduled in the evaluee's community; when a deposition, mediation, or
trial setting requires attendance, travel is planned from the Kansas City
home office and locked to the case calendar well before the date arrives.
Attorney conferences and pre-deposition preparation are available by video
at whatever cadence the case requires.

## Reading the Ocala labor market honestly

A vocational opinion is only as strong as the labor market data beneath
it. Findings for Ocala evaluees rely on Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates for the Ocala metropolitan area
and for Florida statewide, together with state workforce agency labor
market information and direct labor market research where the referral
warrants it. The report identifies the evaluee's commutable labor market,
explains why that market was selected, and cites the estimates supporting
each employability and earning capacity conclusion, so the analysis can be
checked line by line.

## Beginning an engagement

Marion County referrals move through the same intake as every matter in
the [nationwide practice](/locations/nationwide/): conflicts before scope,
and scope before records. To begin,
[request a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) with the case caption and
parties, and identify any imminent disclosure, deposition, or trial dates
so the report timeline can be set against the deadlines that control the
case. Counsel receive a prompt, unambiguous answer on conflicts and
availability.
