What counsel in the Cape Coral-Fort Myers metropolitan area ultimately buy
from Purinton Analytics is a written opinion built to survive
cross-examination, whichever side of the caption retains it. Every report
for a Lee County matter starts from a documented conflict check, a defined
scope, and a records request tailored to the claims, so the opinion rests
on a record plaintiff and defense counsel can both examine. The services
behind those reports are
[vocational expert evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) of
employability and earning capacity,
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) for catastrophic
injuries, and [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Lee County venues

The metro consists of Lee County, and the firm accepts matters venued in
the Circuit Court serving it. Federal matters proceed in Florida's district
courts, including the United States District Court for the Middle District
of Florida, the United States District Court for the Northern District of
Florida, and the United States District Court for the Southern District of
Florida. When a Southwest Florida firm's docket reaches other parts of the
state, the same retention can extend through the firm's
[Florida practice](/locations/florida/) without any change in methodology
or report format.

## How meetings happen here

Cape Coral and Fort Myers meetings are by appointment. Purinton Analytics
keeps no staffed office in Southwest Florida and publishes no street
address for this location, because plain statements about presence belong
to the same discipline as plain statements in a report. Engagements
generally open remotely, with records review, structured interviews, and
standardized testing under publisher protocols where remote administration
is supported. When the referral questions call for in-person evaluation, or
when deposition and trial dates require presence, travel is scheduled from
the Kansas City home office, and counsel deal with a single point of
contact from intake through testimony, with methods and sources disclosed
in the report itself.

## Researching the Southwest Florida labor market

Vocational findings for Lee County evaluees rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the
Cape Coral-Fort Myers metropolitan area and for Florida statewide, together
with state workforce agency labor market information and direct labor
market research where the referral warrants it. Life care plans and
[medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/) are priced
to the evaluee's own market using provider-specific inquiries and published
fee data for the region rather than national averages. Counsel who need an
opposing expert's report examined can retain the firm for
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) grounded in the
same published sources.

The path into an engagement is short. Submit the caption or party names
through the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and note any imminent
disclosure, deposition, or trial dates, and intake begins as soon as
clearance is confirmed. Any venue-specific registration or qualification
requirement is identified and confirmed during the conflict check and
intake process. For matters outside Florida, the firm's
[national coverage](/locations/nationwide/) page describes the same
by-appointment, remote-first model at work across the country.
