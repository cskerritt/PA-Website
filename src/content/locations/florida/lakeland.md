Disclosure deadlines drive most calls Purinton Analytics receives from the
Lakeland-Winter Haven metropolitan area: counsel need a vocational or life
care planning expert who can clear conflicts quickly, define scope, and
deliver a defensible written opinion inside the calendar the court has set.
The firm serves plaintiff and defense counsel in Polk County matters on
exactly those terms, offering
[vocational expert evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) of
employability and earning capacity,
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) for catastrophic
injuries, and [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Polk County and its courts

The metro is coextensive with Polk County, and the firm accepts matters
venued in the Circuit Court serving it. Federal matters proceed in
Florida's district courts, including the United States District Court for
the Middle District of Florida, the United States District Court for the
Northern District of Florida, and the United States District Court for the
Southern District of Florida. Because many Polk County firms also litigate
across the wider region, related matters elsewhere in the state can be
handled through the same retention under the firm's
[Florida practice](/locations/florida/), with an identical methodology and
report format in every venue.

## Meetings by appointment only

Purinton Analytics keeps no staffed office in Lakeland or Winter Haven and
publishes no street address for this location; meetings are by appointment.
The firm says so directly because candor about presence is part of the same
discipline it applies to its opinions. Engagements typically begin
remotely, with records review, structured interviews, and standardized
testing under publisher protocols where remote administration is supported.
When the referral questions call for in-person evaluation, or when
deposition and trial dates require attendance, travel is scheduled from the
Kansas City home office and confirmed with counsel well before the date, so
that expedited timelines never come at the cost of a complete evaluation.

## Evidence from the local labor market

Employability and earning capacity findings for Polk County evaluees rest
on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates
for the Lakeland-Winter Haven metropolitan area and for Florida statewide,
supplemented by state workforce agency labor market information and direct
labor market research where warranted. Life care plans and
[medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/) are priced
to the evaluee's own market through provider-specific inquiries and
published fee data for the region. The same sources support
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) when counsel
need an opposing expert's assumptions and methodology tested before
deposition.

confirmed as soon as the conflict check clears. Any venue-specific
