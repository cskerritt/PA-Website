For an evaluee living in the North Port-Bradenton-Sarasota metropolitan
area, the practical question is how a thorough vocational or life care
planning evaluation actually happens, and Purinton Analytics answers it the
same way for plaintiff and defense referrals alike: most of the work comes
to the evaluee. Records review, structured interviews, and standardized
testing under publisher protocols proceed remotely where remote
administration is supported, and in-person evaluation is scheduled in the
evaluee's own community when the referral questions require it. The
underlying services are the firm's core practice:
[vocational expert evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Venues in Sarasota and Manatee counties

The metro comprises Sarasota County and Manatee County, and the firm
accepts matters venued in the Circuit Court serving each. Federal matters
proceed in Florida's district courts, including the United States District
Court for the Middle District of Florida, the United States District Court
for the Northern District of Florida, and the United States District Court
for the Southern District of Florida. Counsel with related matters
elsewhere in the state can consolidate expert retention through the firm's
[Florida practice](/locations/florida/), which applies an identical report
standard in every venue.

## Presence on the Gulf Coast

There is no staffed Purinton Analytics office in North Port, Bradenton, or
Sarasota, and no street address is published for this location; meetings
are by appointment. The firm states this plainly because accuracy about
offices is part of the same discipline that governs its written opinions.
When deposition or trial dates require attendance, or when an evaluation
must be conducted in person, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home
office and coordinated with counsel well ahead of the date. Counsel receive
a single report standard and a single point of contact regardless of where
any given step of the work is performed, and every opinion is written so
that its methods and sources can be examined by either side.
