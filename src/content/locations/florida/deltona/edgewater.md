Litigants in a smaller community like Edgewater have historically faced a
quiet disadvantage: the experts were somewhere else, and getting a thorough
vocational or care-planning evaluation meant either a long trip for an
injured evaluee or a thin file built from records alone. The remote-first
model exists to close that gap. Purinton Analytics delivers full
[vocational evaluations](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) and
[medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/) to Edgewater
cases with the same depth a big-city evaluee would receive, for counsel on
whichever side of the caption makes the referral.

## How the remote-first model reaches Edgewater

The sequence is deliberate. Records arrive through secure exchange under a
defined request list. The vocational interview is conducted by video at a
time that suits the evaluee, and standardized testing is administered under
publisher protocols wherever remote administration is supported. If the
referral questions require presence, the evaluation is brought to the
evaluee's own community by appointment instead of summoning the evaluee to a
distant office, with the expert traveling from the Kansas City home office.
Nothing about the town's size reduces the scope of the work; the same
protocol governs every file.

Middle District of Florida, and the United States District Court for the

## Candor about presence, and how to start

The firm will not claim an Edgewater office it does not have. There is no
staffed location here and no published address; every meeting is by
appointment, and that plain statement is part of the same discipline that
keeps fabricated facts out of reports. Counsel ready to proceed should
[request a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) with the case caption and
parties, describe the referral questions, and flag disclosure, deposition,
or trial dates already scheduled. Any venue-specific registration or
qualification requirement is identified and confirmed during the conflict
check and intake process, and the same small-venue commitment travels with
the firm's [national practice](/locations/nationwide/) wherever counsel need
it next.
