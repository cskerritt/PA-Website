## Why side-blind methodology matters here

A report written for one side's convenience tends to fail at deposition. The
firm's protection against that failure is procedural: a documented conflict
check before any substantive discussion, a written scope, a records request
tailored to the claims, and opinions that trace to sources both parties can
independently verify. When a defense referral asks whether a claimed
limitation is vocationally significant, or a plaintiff referral asks what
capacity survives an injury, the analytic steps are identical; only the
referral questions differ. That symmetry is the point, and it is why the same
expert can credibly appear for either side of the caption in successive
cases.
