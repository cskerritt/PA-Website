Here is what actually happens when counsel refer a South Daytona case to
Purinton Analytics, in order. The caption or party names go through a
documented conflict check before any substantive conversation. A written
scope follows, naming the referral questions and the disciplines engaged. A
records request tailored to the claims goes out. Only then does evaluation
begin. Counsel on either side of a dispute get the same sequence, because
the sequence is what makes the eventual
[vocational expert opinion](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) defensible
rather than merely confident.

## From intake to report in a South Daytona matter

After clearance and scoping, the evaluation proceeds remote-first: secure
records exchange, a structured interview by video, and standardized testing
under publisher protocols where remote administration is supported, with
in-person assessment arranged in the evaluee's community when the questions
require it. The written report states its methodology, cites its sources,
and separates findings from assumptions, so that both the retaining lawyer
and the opposing one can trace every conclusion. Where the case calls for
economics as well, the firm offers
[coordinated engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) in
which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis
while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement
structure.

## A small suburb, a metro-scale labor market

South Daytona is a small community, but its residents compete for work
across the Deltona-Daytona Beach-Ormond Beach metropolitan area, so that is
the market the vocational analysis measures. The firm draws on Bureau of
Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the
metropolitan area and for Florida statewide, on state workforce agency labor
market information, and on direct employer research where a referral
question turns on a particular occupation. Market scale is characterized
qualitatively in the report, and quantitative claims appear only with their
sources attached.
