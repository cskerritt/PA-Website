When the injury in an Orange City case is catastrophic, the hardest question
is usually not what the evaluee earned before; it is what care the evaluee
will need for the rest of life, and what that care will cost where the
evaluee actually lives. That is life care planning, and Purinton Analytics
accepts that work in Orange City from plaintiff and defense counsel alike.
A [life care plan](/services/life-care-planning/) prepared here itemizes
future medical, therapeutic, attendant, and equipment needs from the medical
record, and a companion
[medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/) prices each
item from sources either side of the caption can verify.

## Pricing care to the evaluee's own market

A care plan priced from national averages invites impeachment. The firm's
projections for an Orange City evaluee instead use provider-specific
inquiries and published fee data for the region, so that the cost of a
future surgery, a course of therapy, or an item of durable equipment
reflects what care actually costs in the evaluee's market. Each plan item is
tied to a stated medical foundation, and each price is tied to a stated
source, which is what allows the plan to survive the deposition it will
inevitably face, whichever party commissioned it.

## The vocational side of an Orange City file

Where earning capacity is also disputed, the firm's
[vocational expert practice](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) assesses
employability and placeability against the Deltona-Daytona Beach-Ormond
Beach metropolitan labor market, using Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates for the metro and for Florida
statewide together with state workforce agency labor market information and
direct labor market research when the referral questions call for it. The
vocational findings and the care plan are kept consistent with each other,
because a file that contradicts itself is a gift to the cross-examiner.
