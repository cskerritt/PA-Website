An Ormond Beach resident does not work in an Ormond Beach economy; they work
in the commuting market of the Deltona-Daytona Beach-Ormond Beach
metropolitan area, and a credible earning capacity opinion has to be scaled
to that market rather than to a single town. That framing is where every
Purinton Analytics engagement here begins. The firm's
[vocational expert work](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) asks what
occupations the evaluee can still perform, where those occupations exist
within the commuting range, and what the published record shows about them,
and it answers those questions the same way whether retained by the
plaintiff or the defense.

## The commuting market, measured properly

The analytical foundation is the Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational
employment and wage estimates for the Deltona-Daytona Beach-Ormond Beach
metropolitan area and for Florida statewide, read together with state
workforce agency labor market information. Where a referral turns on a
specific occupation or a specific employer's requirements, direct labor
market research supplements the published series. Because the metro market
includes communities from Palm Coast to New Smyrna Beach, the analysis
distinguishes carefully between what exists in the region and what is
realistically reachable for this particular evaluee given restrictions,
transportation, and work history.

## Volusia County and federal venue

Ormond Beach matters in state court are heard in the Circuit Court serving
Volusia County. Federal matters may proceed in any of Florida's district
courts, the United States District Court for the Northern District of
Florida, the United States District Court for the Middle District of
Florida, or the United States District Court for the Southern District of
Florida, and the district that actually controls is confirmed during the
conflict check and intake for each case. Statewide scope is described on the
[Florida page](/locations/florida/); the surrounding regional practice is
described on the [Deltona metro page](/locations/florida/deltona/).

## From vocational findings to damages

Lost earning capacity findings frequently feed a larger damages model. In
those cases the firm offers coordinated engagements in which independent
economist partners perform and sign the
[forensic economic analysis](/services/forensic-economic-damages/) while the
vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement structure,
so counsel manage a single retention instead of several. The vocational
opinion stays the vocational expert's; the economic opinion stays the
economists'; and the report record makes that division of authorship
explicit, which is precisely what a court and opposing counsel should expect
to see.
