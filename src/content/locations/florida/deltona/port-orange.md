Start with the evaluee. Somewhere in Port Orange is a person whose earning
capacity, care needs, or both are now the subject of litigation, and the
quality of the expert work depends on how carefully that person is evaluated,
not on where the expert's office happens to sit. Purinton Analytics structures
Port Orange engagements around the evaluee: a
[vocational evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) that documents
work history, capacities, and restrictions; a
[life care plan](/services/life-care-planning/) that maps future needs to a
sourced cost for each item; and a written report either side of the caption
can test line by line.

## What a Port Orange evaluation actually involves

Most engagements open remotely. Medical, employment, and educational records
are collected under a defined request list, the clinical and vocational
interview is conducted by secure video, and standardized instruments are
administered under publisher protocols where remote administration is
supported. When the referral questions require face-to-face assessment, the
evaluation is conducted in the evaluee's own community rather than forcing an
injured person to travel, with the expert's travel scheduled from the Kansas
City home office. Related [medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/)
are priced to the evaluee's actual market using provider-specific inquiry and
published fee data rather than national averages.

Port Orange matters in state court are heard in the Circuit Court serving
Volusia County. Federal engagements are accepted across Florida's trial
courts, namely the United States District Court for the Northern District of
Florida, the United States District Court for the Middle District of Florida,
and the United States District Court for the Southern District of Florida,
with the controlling district for any particular case identified during the
conflict check and intake. The firm's statewide scope is described on the
[Florida page](/locations/florida/), and its regional model for the
surrounding communities on the [Deltona metro page](/locations/florida/deltona/).

## The labor market a Port Orange evaluee competes in

Employability and placeability findings are framed against the
Deltona-Daytona Beach-Ormond Beach metropolitan area as a whole, because that
is the geography within which a Port Orange resident realistically seeks
work. The data foundation is the Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational
employment and wage estimates for the metropolitan area and for Florida
statewide, supplemented by state workforce agency labor market information
and, where warranted, direct contact with employers about specific positions.
Qualitative conclusions are stated as such; quantitative conclusions carry a
citation.

## Honest logistics, and the first step

The firm maintains no staffed location in Port Orange and lists no street
address for it. Meetings happen by appointment, and that is disclosed rather
than dressed up, because a report is only as credible as the practice behind
it. Retention for plaintiff or defense counsel begins with the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/): submit the caption or party names,
describe the referral questions, and note any disclosure, deposition, or
trial dates already set. Any venue-specific registration or qualification
requirement is identified and confirmed during the conflict check and intake
process, and counsel with evaluees outside Florida can extend the same
evaluee-centered model through the firm's
[nationwide practice](/locations/nationwide/).
