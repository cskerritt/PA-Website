A serious injury case out of DeBary rarely needs a single expert discipline.
Counsel typically need a vocational opinion on earning capacity, a life care
plan for future needs, and an economic analysis reducing both to present
value, and retaining each discipline separately means separate fee
agreements, separate schedules, and separate reports to reconcile. Purinton Analytics offers
DeBary counsel a different structure: a
[coordinated damages engagement](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the
[forensic economic analysis](/services/forensic-economic-damages/) while the
vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement structure,
available to plaintiff and defense referrals alike.

## What coordination changes, and what it does not

Coordination changes logistics, not authorship. Each opinion is signed by
the professional qualified to give it: the vocational findings by the
vocational expert, the economic loss calculations by the economist partners.
What counsel gain is consistency of inputs, the economists work from the
same earning capacity conclusions and care plan items that will be defended
at deposition, and a single point of contact from intake through trial. What
opposing counsel gain is a transparent record showing exactly who concluded
what, which is how a coordinated engagement should read.

DeBary is a Volusia County community, and state court cases are heard in the
Circuit Court serving Volusia County. In the federal system the firm takes
engagements in the United States District Court for the Northern District of
Florida, the United States District Court for the Middle District of
Florida, and the United States District Court for the Southern District of
Florida; the district that governs any specific matter is fixed during the
conflict check and intake rather than asserted in advance. Statewide

regional practice shared with Deltona, Orange City, and the rest of the
metro on the [Deltona metro page](/locations/florida/deltona/).

## Data behind the vocational and economic inputs

The vocational inputs to a DeBary damages model are grounded in Bureau of
Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the
Deltona-Daytona Beach-Ormond Beach metropolitan area and for Florida
statewide, supplemented by state workforce agency labor market information
and direct labor market research where the referral requires it. Care plan
items are priced from the evaluee's own market through provider-specific
inquiries and published fee data. Because the economists work from these
documented inputs, the final damages figures inherit their traceability.

The firm keeps no staffed office in DeBary and publishes no address for this
location; engagements run remote-first, meetings are by appointment, and
in-person evaluation or courtroom attendance is scheduled as travel from the
Kansas City home office. To open a coordinated engagement, or a
single-discipline retention where that is all the case needs, submit the
caption or party names through the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/)
and identify the disciplines required along with any disclosure or trial
dates. Any venue-specific registration or qualification requirement is
identified and confirmed during the conflict check and intake process, and
