Some DeLand referrals arrive before any expert has been retained; many arrive
after the other side's expert has already served a report. Purinton Analytics
handles both postures. When counsel need an affirmative opinion, the firm
provides vocational evaluation and life care planning under a documented
scope. When counsel need an opposing report examined, the firm's
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) work tests
whether the methodology, the sources, and the arithmetic of the other
expert's opinion actually support its conclusions, applying the same
standards to a plaintiff-retained report and a defense-retained one.

## Courts serving DeLand litigants

DeLand state court matters proceed in the Circuit Court serving Volusia
County. In federal court, engagements are accepted in the United States
District Court for the Northern District of Florida, the United States
District Court for the Middle District of Florida, and the United States
District Court for the Southern District of Florida; which district controls
a specific DeLand case is settled during the conflict check and intake, not
presumed on a landing page. For matters venued elsewhere in the state, the
[Florida practice](/locations/florida/) applies the same report standard, and
the [Deltona metro page](/locations/florida/deltona/) describes the regional
engagement structure shared with communities such as Daytona Beach and
Orange City.

## What review of an opposing report covers

A rebuttal engagement for a DeLand case is not a louder opinion; it is an
audit. The review traces each earning capacity conclusion to its data source,
checks whether the labor market cited is the one the evaluee actually
occupies, examines whether life care plan items are supported by the medical
record and priced from verifiable sources, and states plainly where the
opposing report is sound. Where the case requires an economic damages
critique, that analysis proceeds through
[coordinated engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) in which
independent economist partners perform and sign the
[forensic economic analysis](/services/forensic-economic-damages/) while the
vocational and care-planning review proceeds under one engagement structure.

## Grounding opinions in the regional labor market

Affirmative and rebuttal work alike rest on the Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates for the Deltona-Daytona
Beach-Ormond Beach metropolitan area and for Florida statewide, together with
state workforce agency labor market information and targeted employer
research where the questions demand it. A DeLand evaluee's employability is
assessed against the metro-wide market within reach, and every quantitative
statement in the report carries a source an opposing expert can pull.

## Presence, scheduling, and the conflict check

There is no staffed Purinton Analytics office in DeLand and no published
street address here; evaluations and meetings occur by appointment, most work
proceeds remotely, and in-person evaluation or testimony is scheduled as
travel from the Kansas City home office. To retain the firm in either
posture, affirmative or rebuttal, submit the caption or party names through
the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) with the report deadlines and any
deposition or trial dates. Any venue-specific registration or qualification
requirement is identified and confirmed during the conflict check and intake
process, and the same practice, in either posture, is available to counsel anywhere
through the firm's [national coverage](/locations/nationwide/).
