What Palm Coast counsel ultimately hand to a judge, a mediator, or an opposing
expert is not the evaluation itself but the document that comes out of it.
Purinton Analytics treats that document as the product. Every Palm Coast
engagement ends in a signed written report that states its scope, identifies
its sources, and shows its reasoning plainly enough for the lawyer who
commissioned it and the lawyer preparing to cross-examine it to test the same
pages. The firm accepts referrals here from plaintiff and defense counsel
alike, whether the question is
[vocational expert evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) of
earning capacity or a [life care plan](/services/life-care-planning/) for
future care needs.

## Where the report is filed: Flagler County and the federal courts

Palm Coast sits in Flagler County, and state court matters arising here are
heard in the Circuit Court that serves the county. Federal engagements are
accepted in the United States District Court for the Northern District of
Florida, the United States District Court for the Middle District of Florida,
and the United States District Court for the Southern District of Florida;
which district controls a given Palm Coast case is confirmed during the
conflict check and intake rather than assumed on this page. The report itself
does not change with the forum. The same scope discipline and sourcing
standard apply to a Flagler County filing and a federal one, and matters
filed elsewhere in the state are handled through the firm's wider
[Florida practice](/locations/florida/).

## Grounding the findings in published data

A vocational opinion for a Palm Coast evaluee is scaled to the labor market
the evaluee can realistically reach, which for most files means the
Deltona-Daytona Beach-Ormond Beach metropolitan area rather than the town
alone. The report's employability and placeability findings rest on Bureau of
Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for that
metropolitan area and for Florida statewide, on state workforce agency labor
market information, and on direct labor market research when the referral
questions call for it. Every source is cited in the report so that either
side of the caption can pull the same publications and check the work; the
regional engagement model is described on the
[Deltona area page](/locations/florida/deltona/).

## Evaluation logistics, stated plainly

The firm keeps no staffed office in Palm Coast and publishes no street
address for this location; that candor is part of the same discipline the
reports follow. Meetings are by appointment. Most engagements begin remotely,
with records review and a structured interview conducted by secure video, and
standardized instruments are administered under publisher protocols where
remote administration is supported. When the referral questions require an
in-person evaluation, the assessment takes place in the evaluee's own
community, and when deposition or trial attendance is set, travel is arranged
from the Kansas City home office and scheduled around the case calendar. None
of this is hidden from the report: how, where, and by what method the evaluee
was assessed appears on its face.

## Opening a Palm Coast engagement

Retention begins with clearance. Submit the caption or party names through
the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/), identify the referral questions,
and flag any disclosure, deposition, or trial dates already on the calendar,
since the report has to be finished before those dates arrive. Any
venue-specific registration or qualification requirement is identified and
confirmed during the conflict check and intake process. Because the practice
is remote-first by design, counsel with matters beyond Flagler County can
engage the same written report standard through the firm's
[nationwide coverage](/locations/nationwide/); a Palm Coast case receives the
same documented scope, the same records protocol, and the same signed report
a matter in any larger venue would receive.
