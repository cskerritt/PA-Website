Every employability opinion stands or falls on its labor market data, and
that is where Purinton Analytics starts for Gainesville referrals: with
the question of what work actually exists, at what wages, in the market a
particular evaluee can reach. On that foundation the firm builds
[vocational evaluations and expert testimony](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/) for
plaintiff and defense counsel across the Gainesville metro, and it
coordinates [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Venues across Alachua, Gilchrist, and Levy counties

The Gainesville metro covers Alachua County, Gilchrist County, and Levy
County, and state court engagements are accepted for matters in the
Circuit Court serving each. Federal engagements are accepted in Florida's
district courts: the United States District Court for the Northern
District of Florida, the United States District Court for the Middle
District of Florida, and the United States District Court for the Southern
District of Florida. Intake identifies the forum and the operative
disclosure calendar at the outset, because those facts control the shape and
timing of everything the firm produces. Counsel with matters elsewhere in
the state can review the [Florida practice page](/locations/florida/) for
the firm's statewide approach.

## Working with the firm by appointment

Gainesville meetings are by appointment. The firm has no staffed office in
the metro and publishes no local street address, a fact it states plainly
because accuracy about presence belongs to the same standard as accuracy
in a report. Most stages of an engagement, from records review through
attorney conferences and many evaluation protocols, proceed remotely by
secure video and document exchange. When the referral questions call for
in-person evaluation, the evaluation is set in the evaluee's own
community, and when depositions, mediations, or trial settings require
attendance, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office against
dates confirmed with counsel in advance. Once a conflict check clears,
counsel receive a records checklist tailored to the claims, and the report
timeline is set against the operative disclosure calendar rather than left
open-ended.

## The data behind Gainesville findings

Findings for evaluees in the metro draw on Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates for the Gainesville
metropolitan area and for Florida statewide, on state workforce agency
labor market information, and on direct labor market research where the
referral warrants it. Whether the evaluee's commutable market centers on
Alachua County or on the metro's more rural counties, the report says
which market was used and why, and it ties each employability and earning
capacity conclusion to the estimates behind it, so opposing counsel can
audit the foundation instead of guessing at it.

## Starting a Gainesville engagement

Referrals from the metro enter the same intake as every matter in the

check, then a defined scope, then a records checklist tailored to the
claims. To begin, submit the caption or party names through the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and note any imminent disclosure,
deposition, or trial dates. Counsel receive a clear answer on conflicts,
availability, and timeline before any records are sent.
