## Benchmarking a Seminole evaluee's market

A Seminole evaluee is a participant in the Tampa-St. Petersburg-Clearwater metropolitan labor market, not in a market bounded by a small city's limits, and the data follow that reality. Vocational conclusions rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the metropolitan area and for Florida statewide, on state workforce agency labor market information, and on direct labor market research where the referral questions call for occupation-specific evidence. When future care is disputed, [medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/) are assembled from provider-specific inquiries and published fee data in the evaluee's own market, so opposing counsel can trace every price to a source.
