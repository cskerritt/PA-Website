A working life in Largo rarely stops at the city line. People who live here hold jobs throughout the Tampa-St. Petersburg-Clearwater metropolitan area, and any honest opinion about what an injured Largo resident can still earn has to be measured against that commuting market rather than against a single town's postings. That measurement is the core of what Purinton Analytics does. The practice provides [vocational expert evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) of employability and earning capacity, [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) for serious injuries, and [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis alongside the vocational and care-planning work, all under one engagement. Retention comes from either side of the caption, and every report is written on the assumption that opposing counsel will read it closely.

## Where Largo cases are venued

Largo lies within Pinellas County, and civil actions filed in state court proceed in the Circuit Court serving Pinellas County. Federal actions belong to Florida's district courts, namely the United States District Court for the Northern District of Florida, the United States District Court for the Middle District of Florida, and the United States District Court for the Southern District of Florida. No landing page should tell counsel which district governs their case; the controlling venue, together with any venue-specific qualification requirement, is confirmed during the conflict check and intake. Engagements across the balance of the region run through the practice described on the [Tampa metro page](/locations/florida/tampa/).

## A commuting workforce and its earning capacity

Because the relevant market is metropolitan, the data are too. Vocational opinions for Largo evaluees are grounded in Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Tampa-St. Petersburg-Clearwater metropolitan area and for Florida statewide, checked against state workforce agency labor market information and against direct labor market research when a referral question calls for it. The same locality logic carries into care costs: [medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/) reflect provider-specific pricing where the evaluee actually receives care, and a [rebuttal or peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) of another expert's report applies these same sources before criticizing anyone else's.

## How a Largo evaluee is assessed

There is no Purinton Analytics office in Largo, no published local address, and no pretense of either; meetings occur by appointment. In most files the evaluation itself begins remotely, through records review, structured interviews, and standardized testing administered under publisher protocols where remote administration is supported. When an in-person evaluation better serves the referral questions, it is conducted in the evaluee's own community, with travel scheduled from the Kansas City home office. The same is true when deposition or trial attendance is required: presence is planned around the case calendar, and the written product does not change with the location of the work.
