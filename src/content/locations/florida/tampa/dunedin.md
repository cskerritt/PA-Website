## Pinellas County and the courts

Dunedin is a Pinellas County city, and civil matters filed in Florida's state system proceed in the Circuit Court serving Pinellas County. Federal matters belong to one of Florida's district courts: the United States District Court for the Middle District of Florida, the United States District Court for the Southern District of Florida, or the United States District Court for the Northern District of Florida. The practice does not announce from a landing page which district governs a given dispute; that determination is made during the conflict check and intake, where any venue-specific registration or qualification requirement is confirmed as well. Counsel whose caseload runs past Dunedin will find the regional footprint described on the [Tampa metro page](/locations/florida/tampa/).

## Sources the reader can open

A report written to be checked must draw on sources its reader can also consult. Vocational findings for Dunedin evaluees therefore rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Tampa-St. Petersburg-Clearwater metropolitan area and for Florida statewide, supplemented by state workforce agency labor market information and by direct labor market research where the occupations in dispute warrant it. Dunedin is a small city inside a large metropolitan labor market, and employability and earning capacity opinions are framed against that wider market rather than against anecdote. On the care side, [medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/) follow the same rule: pricing is grounded in provider-specific inquiry and published fee data from the evaluee's own market, cited so that opposing counsel can verify it.

## How the evaluation proceeds

Meetings in Dunedin are by appointment; the firm keeps no staffed office here and publishes no street address for the city. Records review and structured interviews are ordinarily conducted remotely, and standardized testing is administered under publisher protocols where remote administration is supported. When the referral questions call for in-person evaluation, it takes place in the evaluee's own community, and when deposition or trial attendance is required, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office around the court's dates. Whether the retaining side is plaintiff or defense, the sequence and the report standard are identical, and counsel work with a single point of contact from intake through testimony.
