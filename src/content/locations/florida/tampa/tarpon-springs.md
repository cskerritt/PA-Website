Purinton Analytics will not claim an office it does not keep, in Tarpon Springs or anywhere else. The firm's only staffed location is its Kansas City home office; everything offered to Tarpon Springs counsel is offered by appointment and delivered through a remote-first process. That admission leads the page deliberately, because an expert whose reports must withstand cross-examination should be equally exact about logistics. What the process delivers is [vocational expert evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) of employability and earning capacity, [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) for catastrophic injury, and [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning work advances under a common engagement, available to plaintiff and defense counsel on the same terms.

## County and courthouse questions

Tarpon Springs is part of Pinellas County, and its state-court matters are heard in the Circuit Court serving Pinellas County. Federal matters find their forums among Florida's district courts: the United States District Court for the Middle District of Florida, the United States District Court for the Northern District of Florida, and the United States District Court for the Southern District of Florida. The practice treats the identity of the controlling venue as an intake fact, established during the conflict check along with any venue-specific registration or qualification requirement, not as something to assert in advance. Counsel working across the broader region can consult the [Tampa metro page](/locations/florida/tampa/) for the full metropolitan footprint.

## The market a Tarpon Springs evaluee actually faces

Tarpon Springs is a small city, and its residents' employment prospects extend well beyond it into the Tampa-St. Petersburg-Clearwater metropolitan area. Vocational conclusions are therefore built on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for that metropolitan area and for Florida statewide, cross-checked with state workforce agency labor market information and supplemented by direct labor market research when the referral questions demand occupation-level evidence. For disputes over the price of future care, [medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/) are compiled from provider-specific inquiries and published fee data in the market where the evaluee is treated.

## Remote-first, with travel when the case needs it

Most Tarpon Springs engagements never require anyone to board a plane. Records review, structured interviews, and standardized testing, administered under publisher protocols where remote administration is supported, proceed remotely from clearance forward. The exceptions are principled ones: an in-person evaluation when the referral questions call for it, conducted in the evaluee's own community, and courtroom or deposition attendance when the calendar requires presence, with travel arranged from the Kansas City home office in each instance. Throughout, counsel work with a single point of contact, and the written opinion holds one standard regardless of where the work occurred.
