## The courts that hear Clearwater matters

Clearwater is a Pinellas County city, so state-court litigation from here proceeds in the Circuit Court serving Pinellas County. ## Reading the labor market around Clearwater

A Clearwater evaluee's realistic employment options are not bounded by city limits, so the analysis is not either. Opinions draw on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Tampa-St. Petersburg-Clearwater metropolitan area and for Florida as a whole, on state workforce agency labor market information, and on direct labor market research when a referral question turns on a specific occupation or employer type. Where future care is at issue, [medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/) are built from provider-specific pricing in the evaluee's own market, not from national fee averages.
