For an injured person facing a vocational evaluation, the practical questions come before the legal ones: where will it happen, how long will it take, and who will be in the room. Purinton Analytics builds its Pinellas Park engagements around those questions. Most of the assessment can be completed remotely, standardized testing is administered under publisher protocols where remote administration is supported, and when an in-person evaluation is warranted it takes place in the evaluee's own community rather than in a distant office. On that logistical foundation the firm delivers [vocational opinions](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) on employability and earning capacity, [life care plans](/services/life-care-planning/), and [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning components proceed together, for counsel on the plaintiff side and the defense side alike.

## County and court context

Pinellas Park cases filed in Florida's state system are heard in the Circuit Court serving Pinellas County, the county in which the city sits. Federal filings go to one of Florida's district courts, which are the United States District Court for the Middle District of Florida, the United States District Court for the Southern District of Florida, and the United States District Court for the Northern District of Florida. Rather than guess at the governing forum, the practice identifies the controlling venue during the conflict check and intake and confirms at the same time whether that venue imposes any registration or qualification requirement on the expert. Matters arising elsewhere in the metropolitan area are covered on the [Tampa metro page](/locations/florida/tampa/).

## Pinellas Park in the regional labor market

Pinellas Park is a mid-sized suburb whose residents draw their employment from across the Tampa-St. Petersburg-Clearwater metropolitan area, so vocational conclusions are benchmarked to that regional market. The sources are the Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the metropolitan area and for Florida statewide, state workforce agency labor market information, and direct labor market research where a specific occupation or employer question requires it. Future care is costed with matching locality discipline through [medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/) built on provider-specific inquiries and published fee data in the evaluee's own market.

## Logistics built around the evaluee

The firm does not keep a staffed office in Pinellas Park and does not publish a street address for the area; meetings are by appointment, and the practice says so plainly because overstating presence would sit poorly next to reports that depend on precision. What counsel gets instead of a storefront is a process: a single point of contact from intake through testimony, records review and interviews that begin as soon as clearance completes, and travel from the Kansas City home office whenever depositions, trial dates, or the referral questions themselves require someone in the room.
