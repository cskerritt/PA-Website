St. Petersburg is written into the name of its own metropolitan area, and the city's litigation docket reflects that standing. Counsel here are not referring cases out of a suburb; they are trying injury, employment, and disability disputes in a major city with a bar of its own. Purinton Analytics supports that docket as a national vocational expert and life care planning practice, accepting work from plaintiff and defense counsel alike. The assignments most often requested are [vocational evaluations](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) of employability and earning capacity, [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) after catastrophic injury, and [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning work moves forward under a single engagement structure.

## Venue context in Pinellas County

St. Petersburg sits in Pinellas County, and state-court matters arising in the city are heard in the Circuit Court serving Pinellas County. Federal claims have their own set of forums: Florida is served by the United States District Court for the Northern District of Florida, the United States District Court for the Middle District of Florida, and the United States District Court for the Southern District of Florida, and the district that controls a given case is confirmed during the conflict check and intake rather than assumed on a landing page. Intake also surfaces any venue-specific registration or qualification requirement before scheduling is set. Related matters elsewhere in the region are handled under the same report standard described on the [Tampa metro page](/locations/florida/tampa/).

## A major city inside a metropolitan labor market

Employability and earning capacity for a St. Petersburg evaluee are measured against the market the evaluee can actually reach, which extends across the Tampa-St. Petersburg-Clearwater metropolitan area. Vocational findings rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for that metropolitan area and for Florida statewide, supplemented by state workforce agency labor market information and direct employer research where the referral questions warrant it. Care-related costs follow the same locality discipline: [medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/) and life care plans are priced to the evaluee's own market using provider-specific inquiries and published fee data rather than national averages.

## Evaluation logistics for St. Petersburg evaluees

The firm keeps no staffed office in St. Petersburg and publishes no street address for the city; meetings are by appointment, and that candor is part of the same discipline that governs the written opinions. Most engagements open remotely, with records review, structured interviews, and standardized testing administered under publisher protocols where remote administration is supported. When the referral questions call for an in-person evaluation, or when deposition and trial calendars require presence, travel is arranged from the Kansas City home office. Counsel work with a single point of contact from intake through testimony, and the finished report reads the same whether the work was done remotely or in the room.
