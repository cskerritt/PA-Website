In catastrophic injury litigation, the life care plan often carries the
largest share of claimed damages and draws the closest scrutiny at
deposition. Coral Springs counsel on both sides of that dispute retain
Purinton Analytics to prepare
[life care plans](/services/life-care-planning/) grounded in the medical
record and treating-provider input, to develop
[medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/) where the
question is narrower, and to pair the care-planning work with
[vocational evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) of lost
earning capacity. Where a full damages presentation is needed, coordinated
engagements place the economic analysis with independent economist partners
who perform and sign it, keeping each discipline in its own lane under a
common engagement structure.

## Where Coral Springs cases are heard

Coral Springs is in Broward County, and its state court matters ordinarily
proceed in the Circuit Court for that county. Florida's federal forums are
the United States District Court for the Northern District of Florida, the
United States District Court for the Middle District of Florida, and the
United States District Court for the Southern District of Florida; the
venue that actually controls is confirmed at the conflict check and intake
stage. A plan or opinion prepared for a Broward County jury is prepared to
the same standard as one bound for federal court, because the standard was
never venue-specific to begin with.

## Labor market grounding for vocational findings

When the referral pairs care planning with earning capacity questions, the
vocational side rests on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment
and wage estimates for the Miami-Fort Lauderdale-West Palm Beach
metropolitan area and for Florida statewide, supplemented by state
workforce agency labor market information and, where warranted, direct
labor market research. The [metro page](/locations/florida/miami/) surveys
that regional market. For a Coral Springs evaluee the analysis turns on
fit rather than volume: which occupations remain consistent with residual
capacity, what those occupations demand, and what the published data show
about them across the metro.

## Working with a Coral Springs evaluee

There is no staffed Purinton Analytics office in Coral Springs and no
published street address, a fact stated plainly because plain statement is
the house style. Care-planning interviews with the evaluee and family,
records review, and collaboration with treating providers proceed remotely
in most engagements, with secure transfer of the medical file and video
conferencing throughout. In-person assessment, when the plan requires it,
takes place in the evaluee's own community, and courtroom or deposition
presence is scheduled with travel from the Kansas City home office, as with
the firm's engagements [across Florida](/locations/florida/). Families
navigating a catastrophic injury rarely need another trip added to their
calendar, and the remote-first structure is designed with that reality in
mind as much as with litigation efficiency.

## Requesting a conflict check

Life care plans reward early retention: treating-provider input takes time
to gather, and a plan finalized against a deadline reads that way. Submit
the caption or party names through the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) with any disclosure or trial dates
noted. For catastrophic matters venued elsewhere, the firm's
[nationwide practice](/locations/nationwide/) offers the same coordinated
vocational, care-planning, and economist-partner model.
