particular places. Bridging that gap honestly is the core of town-level
vocational work, and it is how Purinton Analytics approaches Miami Gardens
referrals from plaintiff and defense counsel alike. The firm provides
[vocational expert evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) of
employability and earning capacity,

matters, and coordinated damages work through
[structured engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) in
which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic

## Miami-Dade County courts serving Miami Gardens

Miami Gardens is a Miami-Dade County city, and its state court matters are
ordinarily venued in the Circuit Court there. Federal cases proceed in
whichever of Florida's district courts controls, among the United States
District Court for the Northern District of Florida, the United States
District Court for the Middle District of Florida, and the United States
District Court for the Southern District of Florida, a determination made
during the conflict check and intake. The forum affects deadlines and
disclosure formats; it does not affect the method, which stays constant so
that either side of the caption can examine the same reasoning.

## From metro data to an individual evaluee

The analysis begins wide and narrows deliberately. Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the
Miami-Fort Lauderdale-West Palm Beach metropolitan area and for Florida
statewide establish what the regional economy employs and pays in
qualitative terms; state workforce agency labor market information and
direct labor market research refine the picture where the referral
questions require it. The narrowing is the expert work: occupations
inconsistent with the evaluee's documented restrictions come out,
transferable skills are matched against actual job demands, and commuting
range is treated as a finding to be supported, not an assumption to be
waved at. Regional context sits on the
[metro page](/locations/florida/miami/).

## Evaluation logistics

Purinton Analytics maintains no staffed office in Miami Gardens and
publishes no street address for this location, stating so plainly because
overstated presence would undercut the credibility the reports depend on.
Engagements generally proceed remotely, with secure records transfer,
video-based vocational interviews, and standardized testing administered
under publisher protocols where remote administration is validated.
In-person evaluation is arranged in the evaluee's own community, and
travel for depositions and trial is scheduled from the Kansas City home
office, matching the firm's practice
[throughout Florida](/locations/florida/). Counsel meetings are likewise
by appointment, and the working file is organized from the outset so that
every document relied on can be produced with the report. That habit costs
little during the engagement and pays for itself the day the deposition
notice arrives.
