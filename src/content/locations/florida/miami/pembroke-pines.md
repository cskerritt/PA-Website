A Pembroke Pines referral usually arrives as a set of questions. Can the
evaluee return to past work, and if not, to what? What earning capacity
survives the restrictions the medical record actually supports? What will
future care require, and on what evidence does each element of the plan
rest? Purinton Analytics is retained by plaintiff and defense counsel alike
to answer those questions through
[vocational expert evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) and
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and to structure
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Broward County venue for Pembroke Pines matters

Pembroke Pines is a Broward County city, and its state court litigation
ordinarily proceeds in the Circuit Court serving that county. Federal
matters proceed in whichever of Florida's district courts controls: the
United States District Court for the Northern District of Florida, the
United States District Court for the Middle District of Florida, or the
United States District Court for the Southern District of Florida, with the
governing district identified during the conflict check and intake rather
than assumed. The referral questions, not the forum, shape the analysis.
The same records foundation and the same standardized methods produce the
report wherever it will be filed, and the report is written so that both
sides of the caption can trace each conclusion back to its source.

## Answering the earning capacity question with published data

The earning capacity question is answered against data, not impressions.
Vocational findings for a Pembroke Pines evaluee rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the
Miami-Fort Lauderdale-West Palm Beach metropolitan area and for Florida
statewide, supplemented by state workforce agency labor market information
and by direct labor market research when the referral questions call for
more than the published tables. The
[metro page](/locations/florida/miami/) describes the regional market those
estimates cover. At the town level the analysis narrows to the individual:
the restrictions the record supports, the work history actually documented,
and the occupations realistically open to this evaluee. The written opinion
keeps those layers separate, identifying which findings come straight from
the published sources and which required individual analysis, so the reader
can test each on its own terms.

## Where the work happens

Geography is never the reason a referral question goes unanswered. The firm
maintains no staffed office in Pembroke Pines and publishes no street
address for this location, and the engagement is built instead to reach the
evaluee wherever the evaluee already is. Records arrive by secure transfer,
the vocational interview is conducted by video, and standardized testing
proceeds under publisher protocols wherever remote administration is
validated. An in-person evaluation, when the referral demands one, is held
near the evaluee's home in Broward County. Presence before the court is a
separate commitment, met with travel from the Kansas City home office once
deposition or trial dates are fixed. The approach mirrors the firm's work
[across Florida](/locations/florida/), where distance is treated as a
logistics problem to solve rather than a limit on scope.
