## Miami-Dade County courts and the federal forums

Hialeah sits in Miami-Dade County, and state court matters involving Hialeah
parties are typically venued in the Circuit Court there. On the federal side,
Florida's trial courts are the United States District Court for the Northern
District of Florida, the United States District Court for the Middle District
of Florida, and the United States District Court for the Southern District of
Florida; the district that controls a particular matter is identified during
the conflict check and intake rather than assumed. The report itself does not
change with the forum. State or federal, it rests on records, standardized
instruments, and published data, and it is written so the lawyer who did not
commission it can trace every finding back to a source.

of available occupations treated as evidence rather than assumption.

## Evaluations without a local storefront

The firm keeps no staffed office in Hialeah and publishes no street address
for this location; accuracy about presence is part of the same discipline
that governs its reports. What stands in for the storefront is a process the
evaluee rarely has to travel for. Records move by secure transfer. The
vocational interview is held by video. Standardized testing is administered
under publisher protocols where remote administration is validated, and if
the referral requires an in-person session, the expert comes to the evaluee
rather than the reverse. Court dates put the travel where it belongs, on the
firm: deposition and trial presence is planned from the Kansas City home
office once the schedule is known, and nothing about the engagement asks a
Hialeah evaluee to absorb the logistics of the expert's location.
