Some Miramar referrals come from plaintiff firms building a damages record;
others come from defense counsel deciding whether that record holds.
Purinton Analytics does the same work in either posture, which is precisely
why both sides retain it. The firm provides
[vocational expert evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) of
employability and earning capacity,
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) in catastrophic injury
and medical negligence cases, and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. An opinion that changes with the client is not an
opinion; Miramar counsel get the analysis the record supports.

## Broward County venue and the federal question

Miramar's state court litigation is generally venued in the Circuit Court of
Broward County. Federal matters may proceed in any of Florida's district
courts, the United States District Court for the Northern District of
Florida, the United States District Court for the Middle District of
Florida, and the United States District Court for the Southern District of
Florida, with the controlling venue identified during the conflict check
and intake. Nothing in the firm's method depends on the answer: the same
records request, the same standardized instruments, and the same published
data support the opinion wherever the case is heard.

## Miramar within the metro's working economy

Employability findings for a Miramar evaluee are anchored in Bureau of
Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the
Miami-Fort Lauderdale-West Palm Beach metropolitan area and for Florida
statewide, with state workforce agency labor market information and direct
labor market research added where the referral questions warrant. The
[metro page](/locations/florida/miami/) treats the regional market at
length. What the town page adds is emphasis on fit: a large metro offers
many nominal occupations, and the analysis earns its credibility by showing
which of them remain genuinely available to an evaluee with specific
restrictions, a specific work history, and a commuting range a jury would
recognize as reasonable.

## Presence, honestly stated

The firm has no staffed office in Miramar and lists no street address for
this page; that honesty is deliberate and continuous with how its reports
are written. Engagements typically run remotely from intake through
disclosure, using secure records transfer, video interviews, and remotely
administered standardized testing under publisher protocols where
validated. In-person evaluations are held in the evaluee's own community by
appointment, and travel for depositions or trial is scheduled from the
Kansas City home office, as it is for engagements
[throughout Florida](/locations/florida/). Counsel who have retained the
firm from the opposite side of an earlier caption will recognize the
process, which is the point of keeping it uniform.

## Starting a Miramar engagement

The engagement begins with a documented conflict check, so submit the
caption or party names through the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) along with any imminent disclosure,
deposition, or trial dates. Counsel evaluating the firm for matters outside
Florida will find the [nationwide practice](/locations/nationwide/) built on
the identical model: remote-first work, by-appointment presence, and
opinions written to be examined by the other side.
