Expert disputes are often decided by the calendar before they are decided
on the merits. A vocational opinion commissioned late arrives thin, and a
life care plan assembled against a closing disclosure window shows it under
cross-examination. For Pompano Beach matters, Purinton Analytics encourages
counsel on either side to retain early, so that
[vocational evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and any
[coordinated damages work](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) in
which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis
can mature before the record closes rather than after it should have.

## Pompano Beach in Broward County

Pompano Beach litigation in the state system is ordinarily venued in the
Circuit Court of Broward County. Federal venue is a fact to be confirmed,
not assumed: Florida's district courts are the United States District Court
for the Northern District of Florida, the United States District Court for
the Middle District of Florida, and the United States District Court for
the Southern District of Florida, and the one that controls a given matter
is established during the conflict check and intake. The scheduling
implications differ by forum, which is another reason the intake
conversation asks for every date counsel already has.

## Logistics that protect the schedule

Remote-first practice is itself a scheduling advantage. Records move by
secure transfer the week the engagement opens, vocational interviews are
held by video without waiting on travel, and standardized testing proceeds
under publisher protocols where remote administration is validated. The
firm keeps no staffed office in Pompano Beach and publishes no street
address for this location; when in-person evaluation is warranted it is
held in the evaluee's own community, and deposition or trial presence is
scheduled with travel from the Kansas City home office, consistent with
the firm's work [across Florida](/locations/florida/). Because no stage of
the engagement waits on office availability or local staffing, the
timeline is governed by the record and the court's dates, which is exactly
where counsel need the leverage to be.
