When counsel refer an injured client in Hollywood for vocational evaluation,
the practical questions arrive before the analytical ones: where does the
evaluee have to go, who handles the records, how quickly can testing be
scheduled. Purinton Analytics is organized to make those answers simple.
Most work proceeds remotely, in-person evaluation happens in the evaluee's
own community when it is needed at all, and the firm's
[vocational expert](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) and
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) opinions follow the same
disciplined model whether the referral comes from the plaintiff or the
defense side of a Hollywood caption.

## Hollywood's county and courts

Hollywood is a Broward County city, and state court matters are ordinarily
venued in the Circuit Court serving that county. In the federal system,
Florida's forums are the United States District Court for the Northern
District of Florida, the United States District Court for the Middle
District of Florida, and the United States District Court for the Southern
District of Florida, and the district that applies to a particular case is
determined during the conflict check and intake rather than guessed at. The
opinion is drafted to the same evidentiary standard either way, since the
reader who matters most, opposing counsel, does not grade on venue.

## Reading the labor market from Hollywood

A Hollywood evaluee's realistic employment options extend well past the city
itself, so vocational findings are grounded in Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates for the Miami-Fort
Lauderdale-West Palm Beach metropolitan area and for Florida statewide,
along with state workforce agency labor market information and direct labor
market research where the referral questions justify it. Regional context
for those data appears on the [metro page](/locations/florida/miami/). The
town-level task is disciplined narrowing: from everything the metro employs
to what this particular evaluee, with these restrictions and this work
history, can actually obtain and hold.

## How the evaluation actually proceeds

Records arrive by secure transfer and are read before anyone is
interviewed. The vocational interview is conducted by video in most
engagements, and standardized testing is administered under publisher
protocols where remote administration is validated. If the referral
questions call for an in-person evaluation, it is scheduled in the evaluee's
community by appointment; the firm keeps no staffed office in Hollywood and
publishes no street address for this location, because claiming presence it
does not have would sit poorly beside reports that claim only what the
record supports. Depositions and trial appearances are planned with travel
from the Kansas City home office, consistent with the firm's approach
[across Florida](/locations/florida/). Counsel are kept informed at each
stage, and the file is maintained so that production requests can be met
without scramble.

## Referring a Hollywood matter

Begin with the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/): caption or party
names, the referral questions as counsel currently frame them, and any
disclosure, deposition, or trial dates already fixed. Early referral gives
the evaluation room to be thorough and the life care plan time to
incorporate treating-provider input. For matters beyond Florida, the
[nationwide practice](/locations/nationwide/) operates on the same
remote-first footing, with the same publication standard applied to every
signed opinion.
