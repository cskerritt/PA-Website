The most careful reader of an expert report is the lawyer preparing to take
it apart, and every opinion Purinton Analytics signs is written with that
reader in mind. Fort Lauderdale counsel on either side of the caption retain
the firm for [vocational expert work](/services/vocational-expert-witness/)
on employability and earning capacity,
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) in catastrophic injury
matters, and
[expert testimony and litigation consulting](/services/expert-testimony-litigation-consulting/)
when a case needs disciplined support short of a signed opinion. The economic
layer of a damages claim is handled through
[coordinated engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) in which
independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis; the
firm coordinates that work but does not author it.

## Venue context in Broward County

Fort Lauderdale matters in state court generally proceed in the Circuit
Court serving Broward County. Federal filings raise a venue question the
intake process answers case by case: Florida is divided among the United
States District Court for the Northern District of Florida, the United
States District Court for the Middle District of Florida, and the United
States District Court for the Southern District of Florida, and the
controlling district is confirmed during the conflict check rather than
presumed from geography. Because the methodology is identical in every
forum, counsel receive the same analysis whether the matter is headed for a
state jury or a federal bench.

## Where Fort Lauderdale evaluees actually work

Earning capacity is measured against a real market, and for a Fort
Lauderdale evaluee that market is the wider Miami-Fort Lauderdale-West Palm
Beach metropolitan area, among the largest labor markets in the country.
Findings rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage
estimates for the metro and for Florida statewide, on state workforce agency
labor market information, and on direct labor market research where the
referral questions call for it. The regional picture is described on the
[metro page](/locations/florida/miami/); the town-level work is matching
that data to a specific evaluee's restrictions, skills, and realistic
commuting range, so the written opinion describes options that exist rather
than categories that merely sound plausible.

## Meetings by appointment

There is no staffed Purinton Analytics office in Fort Lauderdale and no
published street address here, because candor about presence belongs to the
same discipline as candor about data. Engagements ordinarily run remotely
from intake through disclosure: secure records transfer, structured
interviews by video, and standardized testing under publisher protocols
where remote administration is validated. In-person evaluation, deposition
attendance, and trial testimony are scheduled from the Kansas City home
office and planned around the court's dates, the same model that serves the
firm's work [throughout Florida](/locations/florida/).
