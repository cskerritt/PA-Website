Not every Davie referral asks for an original opinion. Counsel often arrive
already holding the opposing expert's report and needing to know whether
its methods would survive their own logic. Purinton Analytics performs
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) on exactly that
question, applying to another expert's work the same standards it applies
to its own, and it provides original
[vocational expert evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) and
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) for plaintiff and
defense counsel alike. Where a matter needs economic modeling, the
analysis is performed and signed by independent economist partners under
the firm's coordination, never issued in the firm's own name.

## Davie's venue landscape

Davie lies in Broward County, and its state court litigation is ordinarily
venued in the Circuit Court for that county. On the federal side, the
candidates are the United States District Court for the Northern District
of Florida, the United States District Court for the Middle District of
Florida, and the United States District Court for the Southern District of
Florida; the controlling venue for any given case is fixed during the
conflict check and intake. Rebuttal work in particular benefits from that
early venue clarity, because response deadlines and disclosure rules frame
how much analysis the calendar will allow.

## The labor market behind any Davie opinion

Whether the assignment is an original evaluation or a critique of someone
else's, the benchmark is the same: Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational
employment and wage estimates for the Miami-Fort Lauderdale-West Palm
Beach metropolitan area and for Florida statewide, state workforce agency
labor market information, and direct labor market research where the
referral questions warrant it. An opposing report that cites the right
sources but applies them loosely is more common than one that cites the
wrong sources, and a careful review shows the difference. The
[metro page](/locations/florida/miami/) sets out the regional market that
any credible Davie opinion, from either side, has to respect.

## Logistics for Davie evaluees and counsel

The firm keeps no staffed office in Davie and publishes no street address
for this location; presence is claimed only where it exists. Original
evaluations proceed remotely in most engagements, through secure records
transfer, video vocational interviews, and standardized testing under
validated publisher protocols, with in-person evaluation available in the
evaluee's own community when the referral requires it. Review engagements
are naturally document-driven and fully remote. When depositions or trial
call for the expert in person, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City
home office, as it is for work [across Florida](/locations/florida/). A
review engagement can often be scoped and delivered on a shorter timeline
than an original evaluation, a difference worth raising at intake when
response deadlines are tight.

Whether the need is an original opinion or a response to one, begin by
submitting the caption or party names through the

and, for review work, noting when the opposing report was or will be
disclosed. Counsel with matters beyond Florida can engage the
[nationwide practice](/locations/nationwide/) under the same terms and the
same publication standard.
