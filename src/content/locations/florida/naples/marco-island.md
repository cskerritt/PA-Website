## Marco Island in the Naples Area Labor Market

Earning capacity opinions for a Marco Island evaluee turn on the work realistically available given residence, transferable skills, restrictions, and commuting geography, not on generic statewide averages. Vocational findings therefore rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Naples-Marco Island metropolitan area and for Florida statewide, supplemented by state workforce agency labor market information and direct labor market research where warranted. Because the metro is a smaller labor market than Florida's largest urban centers, the analysis addresses squarely what that scale means for the occupations open to the evaluee, and the written opinion shows the sources for each finding so that cross-examination can test them.

## Evaluations for Marco Island Evaluees

The practice is remote-first, and most Marco Island engagements begin with records review and a structured vocational or life care planning interview conducted by video. When the referral questions call for in-person evaluation, the assessment is scheduled in the evaluee's own community, on Marco Island or in Naples, rather than requiring an injured person to travel to the expert. Purinton Analytics keeps no staffed office on Marco Island or anywhere in Florida and publishes no street address for this location; meetings are by appointment, and travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office around deposition, mediation, and trial calendars. Counsel receive the same file discipline either way: a documented conflict check, a defined scope, and a records request tailored to the claims.
