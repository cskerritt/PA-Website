Catastrophic injury, employment disputes, and contested earning capacity
claims dominate the referrals Purinton Analytics receives from the
Orlando-Kissimmee-Sanford metropolitan area, and each calls for a different
piece of the practice. Counsel retaining the firm for plaintiff or defense
work draw on [vocational expert witness services](/services/vocational-expert-witness/)
when employability and earning capacity are disputed,
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) when future care needs
must be specified and priced, and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. Where testimony itself is the assignment,
[expert testimony and litigation consulting](/services/expert-testimony-litigation-consulting/)
support runs from disclosure through trial.

## Central Florida venues

The firm accepts matters venued in the Circuit Court for Orange County,
Seminole County, Osceola County, and Lake County, and in the federal trial
courts of the state, including the United States District Court for the
Middle District of Florida, the United States District Court for the
Northern District of Florida, and the United States District Court for the
Southern District of Florida. Central Florida counsel whose dockets reach
other parts of the state can route those matters through the firm's
[Florida practice](/locations/florida/), which applies the same methodology
and the same written report standard in every venue.

## Appointment-based presence, stated plainly

Purinton Analytics maintains no staffed office in Orlando, Kissimmee, or
Sanford, and it publishes no street address for this location. Meetings are
by appointment, and that plain statement of presence reflects the same
accuracy the firm applies to its opinions. Most engagements begin remotely:
records review, structured interviews, and standardized testing under
publisher protocols where remote administration is supported. When the
referral requires an in-person evaluation, or when deposition and trial
dates require attendance, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home
office and coordinated with counsel's calendar. Wherever a given step is
performed, the written product follows a single standard, with methods,
sources, and assumptions stated so that either side of the caption can
trace every conclusion back to the record.

## Grounding opinions in the Orlando labor market

Employability and earning capacity findings for Central Florida evaluees
rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage
estimates for the Orlando-Kissimmee-Sanford metropolitan area and for
Florida statewide, together with state workforce agency labor market
information and direct labor market research where the questions warrant it.
Life care plans and [medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/)
are priced to the evaluee's own market through provider-specific inquiries
and published fee data for the region. The same sources support
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) when counsel
need an opposing expert's report tested line by line.

## Beginning an Orlando engagement

The first step in any Central Florida matter is a conflict check. Submit the
caption or party names through the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/), note any imminent disclosure,
deposition, or trial dates, and intake follows immediately on clearance. Any
venue-specific registration or qualification requirement is identified and
confirmed during the conflict check and intake process. For matters outside
Florida, the firm's [national coverage](/locations/nationwide/) page
describes how the same remote-first, by-appointment model serves counsel
across the country.
