The Port St. Lucie metro spans Martin County and St. Lucie County, and
litigation here can sit in either county's Circuit Court or in federal
court, which is why Purinton Analytics frames every referral around the
venue before anything else. The firm serves plaintiff and defense counsel
across the metro with
[vocational evaluation and expert testimony](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/), and it
coordinates [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Courts serving the metro

State court engagements are accepted for matters in the Circuit Court
serving Martin County and St. Lucie County. Federal engagements are
accepted in Florida's district courts: the United States District Court for
the Northern District of Florida, the United States District Court for the
Middle District of Florida, and the United States District Court for the
Southern District of Florida. The forum shapes the disclosure calendar and
the form of the written opinion, so intake begins by confirming where the
case is pending and what the operative deadlines are. The
[Florida practice page](/locations/florida/) describes how the firm
approaches venues across the rest of the state.

## How Port St. Lucie engagements are staffed

There is no staffed Purinton Analytics office in Port St. Lucie, and the
firm does not publish a local street address, because it does not keep
one. Meetings are by appointment. The engagement runs remote-first:
records review, scoping calls, and attorney conferences proceed by secure
video and document exchange, and many evaluation protocols can be
administered the same way. When the referral questions require an
in-person evaluation, the evaluation can be scheduled in the evaluee's own
community, and when a deposition, mediation, or trial date requires
presence, travel is arranged from the Kansas City home office and
confirmed against counsel's calendar in advance. Counsel get candor about
logistics for the same reason they get candor in the report: the firm
treats both as part of its credibility.

## Grounding findings in the local labor market

Vocational findings for Port St. Lucie evaluees are grounded in data for
the market where the evaluee actually lives and works. The analysis relies
on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates
for the Port St. Lucie metropolitan area and for Florida statewide,
supplemented by state workforce agency labor market information and by
direct labor market research when the referral requires it. Every
employability and earning capacity conclusion identifies its data sources
so that opposing counsel can trace the finding to the estimate behind it.

## Requesting a conflict check

Port St. Lucie matters enter the same national intake used across the
[nationwide practice](/locations/nationwide/): conflicts are checked and
cleared before scope is set, and scope is set before records are
requested. To begin, send the caption or party names through the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and flag any imminent disclosure,
deposition, or trial settings. The firm responds with a clear answer on
conflicts, availability, and timeline before the engagement opens.
