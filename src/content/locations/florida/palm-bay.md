Attorneys on either side of a Brevard County caption retain Purinton
Analytics for the same reason counsel elsewhere do: a written opinion that
holds up when the opposing side reads it closely. The firm provides
[vocational evaluation and expert testimony](/services/vocational-expert-witness/)
and [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) for injury,
employment, and disability litigation across the Palm Bay-Melbourne-Titusville
metro, and it accepts
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. Plaintiff and defense engagements are handled with the
same methodology, the same file discipline, and the same expectation that
every conclusion will be tested.

## Venue footprint in Brevard County

The Palm Bay-Melbourne-Titusville metro is a single-county venue.
Engagements are accepted for state court matters in the Circuit Court
serving Brevard County and for federal matters in Florida's district
courts: the United States District Court for the Northern District of
Florida, the United States District Court for the Middle District of
Florida, and the United States District Court for the Southern District of
Florida. Whichever forum holds the case, the working product is the same: a
documented conflict check, a defined scope, a records request tailored to
the claims, and a report written so that both sides can examine the record
behind it. Counsel with matters elsewhere in the state will find the
broader venue picture on the [Florida practice page](/locations/florida/).

## Meetings and evaluations by appointment

Palm Bay meetings are by appointment. The firm keeps no staffed office in
the metro and publishes no local street address, because accuracy about
presence is part of the same discipline that governs its reports. Most
engagement stages proceed remotely: records review, attorney conferences,
and many evaluation protocols are handled by secure video and document
exchange. When the referral questions call for in-person evaluation, or
when a deposition or trial setting requires attendance, travel is scheduled
from the Kansas City home office and planned around the operative
disclosure calendar rather than left open-ended.

## Labor market research for Brevard County evaluees

Employability and earning capacity findings for evaluees in Palm Bay,
Melbourne, and Titusville rest on data specific to this market. The
analysis draws on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and
wage estimates for the Palm Bay-Melbourne-Titusville metropolitan area and
for Florida statewide, on state workforce agency labor market information,
and on direct labor market research where the referral warrants it. The
report is explicit about which labor market defines the evaluee's
commutable job base and why, so the foundation of every employability
conclusion is visible on the page rather than asserted.

## Starting a Palm Bay engagement

The practice is national, and Brevard County matters move through the same
intake as every other venue in the [nationwide practice](/locations/nationwide/):
a conflict check, then scope, then a records checklist keyed to the
claims. To begin, submit the caption or party names through the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and note any imminent disclosure,
deposition, mediation, or trial dates so scheduling can be confirmed
against the deadlines that matter. Counsel receive a direct answer on
conflicts and availability before any records change hands.
