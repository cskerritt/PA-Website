Damages cases from the Deltona-Daytona Beach-Ormond Beach metropolitan area
often need more than a single expert discipline, and that is the problem
Purinton Analytics is structured to solve. In a
[coordinated damages engagement](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/),
independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis while
the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement
structure, giving plaintiff or defense counsel in Volusia County and
Flagler County matters an integrated set of opinions without managing
separate retentions. Where a single discipline is all the case requires,
the firm is retained directly for
[vocational expert evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) or
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and questions of lost
earnings and household services fall to the
[forensic economic damages](/services/forensic-economic-damages/) work of
the economist partners it coordinates.

## Volusia and Flagler county venues

The firm accepts matters venued in the Circuit Court serving Volusia County
and Flagler County, and in Florida's federal trial courts, including the
United States District Court for the Middle District of Florida, the United
States District Court for the Northern District of Florida, and the United
States District Court for the Southern District of Florida. Matters venued
elsewhere in the state proceed under the same methodology and the same
written report standard through the firm's
[Florida practice](/locations/florida/).

## A by-appointment location, described honestly

Meetings in Deltona, Daytona Beach, or Ormond Beach are by appointment. The
firm maintains no staffed office on this stretch of the Atlantic coast and
publishes no street address for the location, because describing presence
accurately is part of the same discipline that produces accurate reports.
Most engagements open remotely, with records review, structured interviews,
and standardized testing under publisher protocols where remote
administration is supported. When in-person evaluation is required, or when
deposition and trial dates demand attendance, travel is scheduled from the
Kansas City home office, and counsel retain a single point of contact for
the life of the engagement, whether the retention covers a single
discipline or the full coordinated structure.

## Labor market and cost research for the metro

Vocational findings for evaluees in the region rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the
Deltona-Daytona Beach-Ormond Beach metropolitan area and for Florida
statewide, together with state workforce agency labor market information
and direct labor market research where the referral questions warrant it.
Life care plans and [medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/)
are priced to the evaluee's own market using provider-specific inquiries
and published fee data for the region rather than national averages, and
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) engagements
apply those same sources to an opposing expert's report.

## First step: the conflict check

Whether the referral is a full coordinated engagement or a single vocational
question, retention starts the same way. Submit the caption or party names
through the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and note any imminent
disclosure, deposition, or trial dates so scheduling can be confirmed with
clearance. Any venue-specific registration or qualification requirement is
identified and confirmed during the conflict check and intake process.
Counsel with matters beyond Florida will find the same remote-first model
described in the firm's [national coverage](/locations/nationwide/).
