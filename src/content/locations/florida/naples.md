Not every referral from the Naples-Marco Island metro asks for an
original opinion; many arrive as a request to test someone else's.
Purinton Analytics accepts both. Counsel in Collier County matters retain
the firm for [rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/)
of opposing vocational and life care planning opinions as often as for
original [vocational evaluation and expert testimony](/services/vocational-expert-witness/)
and [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and the same
standard governs both directions of the work: an opinion is sound only if
it survives the reading the firm itself would give an adversary's report.
Plaintiff and defense engagements are accepted on identical terms.

## Collier County and the federal courts

The metro is a single-county venue. State court engagements are accepted
for matters in the Circuit Court serving Collier County, and federal
engagements are accepted in Florida's district courts: the United States
District Court for the Northern District of Florida, the United States
District Court for the Middle District of Florida, and the United States
District Court for the Southern District of Florida. Intake fixes the
forum and its disclosure calendar before scope is set, because the
calendar determines when a rebuttal must land and how an original report
should be sequenced. Statewide coverage is described on the
[Florida practice page](/locations/florida/).

## Presence in Naples, stated accurately

The firm keeps no staffed office in Naples or Marco Island and publishes
no street address for this location. Meetings are by appointment, and most
of the engagement proceeds remotely: records review, attorney
conferences, scoping calls, and many evaluation protocols run through
secure video and document exchange. When the referral questions require
in-person evaluation, or when deposition and trial dates require presence,
travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office and confirmed against
counsel's deadlines in advance. Records conferences and pre-deposition
preparation are scheduled by video without regard to geography, so the
file keeps moving between appearances. The firm describes its footprint this way
because a practice built on scrutinizing other experts' claims cannot
afford loose claims of its own.

## Labor market foundations for Collier County findings

Employability and earning capacity conclusions for Naples-area evaluees
rest on market-specific data: Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational
employment and wage estimates for the Naples metropolitan area and for
Florida statewide, state workforce agency labor market information, and
direct labor market research where warranted. The same sources anchor
rebuttal work, where the question is frequently whether the opposing
expert's employability conclusions match the data for the market the
evaluee can actually reach. Either way, the report shows its sources so
the foundation can be verified rather than taken on faith.

## Retaining the firm for a Naples matter

Collier County referrals enter the same intake as the rest of the
[nationwide practice](/locations/nationwide/): a documented conflict
check, a defined scope, and a records checklist tailored to the claims,
in that order. Submit the caption or party names through the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and note any imminent disclosure,
deposition, or trial dates, including rebuttal deadlines, so availability
can be confirmed against the schedule that actually binds the case.
