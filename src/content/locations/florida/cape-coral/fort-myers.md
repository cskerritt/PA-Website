Expert disclosure deadlines rarely arrive at a convenient moment, and Fort
Myers counsel most often contact Purinton Analytics with a scheduling order
already in hand. The practice is organized for that reality. Intake opens
the moment the conflict check clears, the scope is confirmed in writing,
and the records request goes out tailored to the claims, so that a
[vocational expert evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) or a
[life care plan](/services/life-care-planning/) can be completed,
disclosed, and defended on the calendar the court has set. Plaintiff and
defense firms retain the practice on identical terms, and each report is
written so that either side of the caption could read it without finding
advocacy in it.

## Lee County and its forums

Fort Myers matters in state court proceed in Lee County, where the Circuit
Court hears the civil docket. Federal claims are filed among Florida's
district courts, which include the United States District Court for the
Middle District of Florida, the United States District Court for the
Northern District of Florida, and the United States District Court for the
Southern District of Florida; the controlling venue for a particular
caption is confirmed during the conflict check and intake rather than
assumed. When a Fort Myers firm's docket runs wider, the same retention
extends through the [Cape Coral-Fort Myers metro practice](/locations/florida/cape-coral/)
and the statewide [Florida practice](/locations/florida/) without any
change in methodology or report format.

## Reading the Fort Myers labor market

Fort Myers shares its name with the surrounding metropolitan area, and
earning-capacity opinions for its residents are grounded in the same
published record used everywhere in the practice: Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Cape
Coral-Fort Myers metropolitan area and for Florida statewide, state
workforce agency labor market information, and direct employer research
where the referral questions warrant it. Life care plans and
[medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/) are priced
to the evaluee's own regional market using provider-specific inquiries and
published fee data rather than national averages, and every source is
disclosed in the report where opposing counsel can test it.

## Evaluating an evaluee who lives here

Meetings in Fort Myers are by appointment. The firm maintains no staffed
office in Southwest Florida and publishes no local street address, because
candor about presence belongs to the same discipline that governs the
reports themselves. Most engagements open remotely, with records review,
structured interview, and standardized testing administered under
publisher protocols where remote administration is supported. When the
referral calls for in-person evaluation, or when a deposition or trial
date requires attendance, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home
office and planned around the court's dates, not the other way around.
Counsel work with a single point of contact from intake through testimony.

## Beating the scheduling order

The shortest path to a protected deadline is an early conflict check.
Submit the caption or party names through the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and flag any imminent disclosure,
deposition, or trial settings, and intake begins as soon as clearance is
confirmed. Any venue-specific registration or qualification requirement is
identified during that same intake. For matters beyond Florida, the firm's
[national coverage](/locations/nationwide/) page describes the same
remote-first, by-appointment model applied across the country, including
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.
