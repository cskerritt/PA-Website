Before anything else in a Fort Myers Beach case, there is a caption, and
the caption decides where the expert work will eventually be tested. A
state-law claim belongs to Lee County's Circuit Court; a federal claim
goes to whichever of Florida's district courts controls it, a question
Purinton Analytics confirms during conflict check and intake rather than
guessing from a map. The available federal forums are the United States
District Court for the Middle District of Florida, the United States
District Court for the Southern District of Florida, and the United
States District Court for the Northern District of Florida. Everything
the firm produces for that forum, whether a
[vocational expert evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) or a
[life care plan](/services/life-care-planning/), is written to withstand
cross-examination in front of it, regardless of which party issued the
retention.

## A shared county bench

Because Fort Myers Beach sits within Lee County, its civil docket shares
a bench with the rest of the county, and counsel here often litigate
alongside matters from across the region. That regional reality is why
the town page is only the entry point: the
[Cape Coral-Fort Myers metro practice](/locations/florida/cape-coral/)
describes the surrounding engagement footprint, and the
[Florida practice](/locations/florida/) page extends the same terms to
every corner of the state, with no shift in method or report format
between venues.

## A small town's workers, a regional market

Fort Myers Beach is a small community, and its workers participate in a
labor market far larger than the town itself. Vocational conclusions are
therefore anchored to Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment
and wage estimates for the Cape Coral-Fort Myers metropolitan area and
for Florida statewide, refined with state workforce agency labor market
information and, when a referral turns on a particular occupation or
employer class, direct labor market research. Future care is costed with
the same locality discipline:
[medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/) draw on
provider-specific inquiry and published regional fee data instead of
national averages, so the numbers in the report trace to the evaluee's
actual market.

## Serving an evaluee at the beach

The firm's presence here is by appointment only. There is no Purinton
Analytics office in Fort Myers Beach, no published street address, and no
suggestion of either, because a practice that trades on accuracy cannot
be casual about its own footprint. Engagements normally begin remotely,
combining records review, structured interview, and standardized testing
under publisher protocols approved for remote use. If an in-person
evaluation is warranted, it takes place in the evaluee's own community,
with travel arranged from the Kansas City home office; the same travel
planning covers depositions and trial testimony when the calendar
requires the expert in the room.
