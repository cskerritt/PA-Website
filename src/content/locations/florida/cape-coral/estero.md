The most practical question counsel ask about an Estero referral is
logistical: where does the evaluation actually happen? For most
engagements the honest answer is that it starts wherever the evaluee is.
Records review, a structured interview, and standardized testing under
publisher-approved remote protocols can all be completed without anyone
leaving Estero, and when the referral questions genuinely require sitting
across the table, the evaluation comes to the evaluee's community rather
than pulling an injured person to a distant office. That logistics-first
posture shapes how Purinton Analytics delivers
[vocational expert evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) and
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) for both plaintiff and
defense retentions here.

## The county behind the caption

Estero lies in Lee County, and its state-court matters are heard in the
Circuit Court serving that county. Florida's federal forums are the United
States District Court for the Northern District of Florida, the United
States District Court for the Middle District of Florida, and the United
States District Court for the Southern District of Florida; identifying
which district controls a specific Estero matter is part of the conflict
check and intake, and the report is drafted with that forum's disclosure
expectations in mind. Counsel handling related matters elsewhere in the
region will find the same methods described on the
[Cape Coral-Fort Myers metro page](/locations/florida/cape-coral/) and
across the [Florida practice](/locations/florida/).

## A smaller community in a metropolitan market

Estero is a smaller community within a much larger employment region, and
its residents' vocational prospects are properly measured against the
metropolitan market around them. The firm's opinions rest on Bureau of
Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Cape
Coral-Fort Myers metropolitan area and for Florida statewide, together
with state workforce agency labor market information and targeted labor
market research where a referral turns on particular occupations. Care
costs are handled the same way:
[medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/) and life
care plans price future care to the evaluee's own regional market from
provider-specific inquiries and published fee data, never from national
averages.

## What by-appointment service means in practice

Purinton Analytics keeps its home office in Kansas City and staffs no
location in Estero or anywhere in Southwest Florida; it publishes no local
address because it has none to publish. What counsel get instead is a
defined process: a documented conflict check, a written scope, a tailored
records request, and a report whose methods and sources are laid out for
the other side to examine. In-person evaluation days, depositions, and
trial appearances are scheduled through travel from the Kansas City home
office, coordinated against the case calendar so that testimony dates are
protected.

## Bringing an Estero referral in

Begin with the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/): the caption or party
names, a note about any looming disclosure or deposition dates, and
nothing confidential until clearance and engagement are confirmed. Any
venue-specific registration or qualification requirement surfaces during
that same intake. When economic loss calculations are needed alongside
the vocational and care-planning work, the firm structures
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis under one engagement structure, and the
[national coverage](/locations/nationwide/) page shows how that model
serves counsel far beyond Florida.
