A small community generates litigation less often than a large city, but
when a Sanibel case does arise, the damages questions inside it are no
smaller. Whether a resident can return to work, what that work would pay,
and what a lifetime of injury-related care will cost are questions that
demand the same rigor in a town of modest size as in the largest venue in
the state. Purinton Analytics applies an identical methodology either
way: the [vocational expert evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/)
and the [life care plan](/services/life-care-planning/) prepared for a
Sanibel matter follow the same documented process, on the same published
sources, that the practice uses nationwide, for whichever party retains
it.

## Lee County venue, statewide forums

Sanibel is part of Lee County, so its state-court cases proceed in the
Circuit Court serving the county. Federal litigation has Florida's
district courts available to it, namely the United States District Court
for the Middle District of Florida, the United States District Court for
the Northern District of Florida, and the United States District Court for
the Southern District of Florida; the district that actually governs a
Sanibel caption is pinned down during the conflict check and intake. The
surrounding regional practice is described on the
[Cape Coral-Fort Myers metro page](/locations/florida/cape-coral/), and
the [Florida practice](/locations/florida/) page covers the rest of the
state under the same retention terms.

## Small town, metropolitan evidence

However small the community, the evidentiary base is metropolitan.
Vocational opinions for Sanibel evaluees are built from Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates covering the Cape
Coral-Fort Myers metropolitan area and Florida statewide, alongside state
workforce agency labor market information and direct research into
specific occupations when the referral requires it. No figure in a report
is asserted without a source the other side can pull and verify. The same
sourcing discipline supports
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) when counsel
need an opposing expert's market conclusions tested against the published
record.

## How the firm reaches Sanibel

Candor about presence is part of the work product: the firm has no office
on Sanibel and no staffed location anywhere in Southwest Florida, and all
local meetings are by appointment. In most engagements the early phases
run remotely, through records review, structured interviews, and
standardized testing under publisher protocols that support remote
administration. Where the referral questions justify an in-person
evaluation, or where deposition and trial dates demand attendance, the
trip is scheduled from the Kansas City home office and built around the
court's calendar. From intake to the witness stand, counsel deal with the
same person who signed the report.
