Earning capacity is defined by the labor market a worker can actually
reach, not by the town line, and that principle does the heavy lifting in
Bonita Springs cases. Residents here work across the Cape Coral-Fort Myers
metropolitan area, so a credible opinion about what an injured Bonita
Springs worker can still earn has to survey the commuting market as a
whole rather than a single zip code. Purinton Analytics builds its
[vocational expert evaluations](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) on
that footing for plaintiff and defense counsel alike, pairing them with
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) when catastrophic
injury puts future care at issue.

## Where a Bonita Springs case is heard

Bonita Springs sits in Lee County, and state-court matters are heard in
the Circuit Court that serves the county. On the federal side, Florida
offers its district courts as available forums: the United States District
Court for the Middle District of Florida, the United States District Court
for the Southern District of Florida, and the United States District Court
for the Northern District of Florida. Which of them controls a given
matter is a question answered during the conflict check and intake, not
an assumption made from geography. Counsel with a mixed docket can extend the
same retention through the
[Cape Coral-Fort Myers metro practice](/locations/florida/cape-coral/) or
anywhere the [Florida practice](/locations/florida/) reaches.

## The commuting market, measured

Because the relevant market for a Bonita Springs evaluee is metropolitan,
the analysis draws on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment
and wage estimates for the Cape Coral-Fort Myers metropolitan area and for
Florida statewide, supplemented by state workforce agency labor market
information and direct labor market research when a referral question
turns on a specific occupation or employer type. The findings stay
qualitative until the record supports them, and each source is cited in
the report so the opinion can be checked line by line. Where an opposing
expert has already surveyed the same market, the firm offers
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) against the
same published data.

## Appointments, not an address

There is no Purinton Analytics office in Bonita Springs, and the firm does
not pretend otherwise; every local meeting happens by appointment.
Evaluations typically begin remotely with records review and structured
interview, and standardized testing follows publisher protocols wherever
remote administration is supported. If the referral requires sitting with
the evaluee in person, or a deposition or trial requires attendance,
travel is arranged from the Kansas City home office with enough lead time
to protect the litigation calendar. The evaluee is seen in or near their
own community whenever the in-person work is warranted.
