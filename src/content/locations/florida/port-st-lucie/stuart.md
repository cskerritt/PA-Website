is a calendar question: whether a defensible expert opinion can still be
built inside the disclosure window the court has already set. Intake is
organized around answering it honestly. The firm serves plaintiff and
defense counsel in Stuart with
[vocational evaluation and expert testimony](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),

## Martin County courts and the disclosure calendar

Stuart sits in Martin County, and state court engagements are accepted for
matters in the Circuit Court serving Martin County. Federal engagements
are accepted in Florida's district courts, which include the United States
District Court for the Northern District of Florida, the United States
District Court for the Middle District of Florida, and the United States
District Court for the Southern District of Florida; the controlling venue
for a given Stuart matter is identified during the conflict check and
intake. That identification is not a formality. The forum determines when
the written opinion is due, what it must contain, and how supplementation
works, so the engagement timeline is mapped to the operative deadlines
before any records are requested. Venue practice across the rest of the
state is described on the [Florida practice page](/locations/florida/).

## The labor market behind the findings

Stuart evaluees are analyzed against the market where they live and work,
not a statewide abstraction. Vocational findings rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Port St.
Lucie metropolitan area and for Florida statewide, along with state
workforce agency labor market information and direct labor market research
where the referral warrants it. Because every source is named in the
report, cross-examination on the data becomes a discussion of published
estimates rather than a dispute about where a figure came from, which
serves the retaining side regardless of which side that is.

## Logistics for Stuart evaluations

There is no staffed Purinton Analytics office in Stuart, and the firm
publishes no street address for this location; accuracy about offices is
part of the same discipline that governs its opinions. Meetings are by
appointment. Most of the engagement proceeds remotely, through secure
video conferences and document exchange, and many evaluation instruments
can be administered that way. Where the referral questions call for
in-person evaluation, it takes place in the evaluee's own community, and
when deposition or trial testimony requires presence in Martin County,
travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office against dates
confirmed with counsel in advance.
