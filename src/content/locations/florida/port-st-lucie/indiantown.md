Indiantown is a small community in Martin County, and Purinton Analytics
takes the position that the size of the town is irrelevant to the standard
its cases deserve. An earning capacity opinion or life care plan prepared
for an Indiantown evaluee is built with the same documented sourcing,
defined scope, and disciplined methodology the practice brings to the
largest venues it serves, because the opinion will face the same scrutiny.
Counsel for plaintiffs and counsel for defendants retain the firm for
[vocational evaluation and expert testimony](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/), and for
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Courts serving Martin County matters

An Indiantown case in state court proceeds in the Circuit Court serving
Martin County. If the claim is federal, Florida's district courts supply
the forum: the United States District Court for the Northern District of
Florida, the United States District Court for the Middle District of
Florida, and the United States District Court for the Southern District of
Florida. Which court governs a particular matter is settled during the
conflict check and intake, before any scope opens, because the forum sets
the disclosure deadlines and the required contents of the written opinion,
and an opinion scoped without knowing them invites avoidable rework. How
the firm approaches venues across the rest of the state is covered on the
[Florida practice page](/locations/florida/).

## A small town measured against its real market

An evaluee who lives in Indiantown does not look for work in an
abstraction, and the firm does not analyze one. Employability and earning
capacity findings describe the commuting market actually reachable from
the town, within the metro anchored by Port St. Lucie. The underlying data
are Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates
for the Port St. Lucie metropolitan area and for Florida statewide, state
workforce agency labor market information, and direct labor market
research when published estimates cannot carry the referral question
alone. The written opinion cites those sources as it goes, finding by
finding, so a reader who wants to test a conclusion can pull the same
published estimate and check it. Small-community cases receive that
transparency for the same reason big-city cases do: the report has to hold
up when the other side reads it.

## Meeting an Indiantown evaluee

There is no Purinton Analytics office in Indiantown, no street address is
published for this location, and the firm says so plainly rather than
implying a presence it does not have. Meetings happen by appointment. Most
of an engagement proceeds remotely: scoping conferences, records review,
and attorney communication run over secure video and document exchange,
and many assessment instruments can be administered the same way without
loss of rigor. When the referral requires in-person evaluation, the
appointment is set in the evaluee's own community, sparing an injured
person the burden of organizing a recovery around travel. When deposition,
hearing, or trial dates call for personal appearance, that travel is
scheduled from the Kansas City home office and coordinated with counsel's
calendar.
