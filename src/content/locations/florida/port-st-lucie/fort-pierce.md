A case that arises in Fort Pierce is, before anything else, a St. Lucie
County case, and Purinton Analytics treats that county grounding as the
starting point for every referral from the city. Counsel on either side of
the caption engage the firm for
[vocational evaluation and expert testimony](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/), and for
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. The work product is written to be examined by the
other side, because in Fort Pierce, as everywhere else, it will be.

## St. Lucie County venue and the federal alternative

State court engagements from Fort Pierce are accepted for matters in the
Circuit Court serving St. Lucie County. When a claim is filed federally,
Florida offers its district courts as forums: the United States District
Court for the Northern District of Florida, the United States District
Court for the Middle District of Florida, and the United States District
Court for the Southern District of Florida. Which court controls a
particular Fort Pierce filing is confirmed during the conflict check and
intake rather than assumed, because the forum dictates the disclosure
schedule, the form of the written opinion, and how the expert's file must
be maintained. The [Florida practice page](/locations/florida/) describes
how the firm handles venues across the rest of the state.

## Fort Pierce within the metro labor market

An employability or earning capacity opinion for a Fort Pierce evaluee has
to describe the market that evaluee actually faces, not a generic Florida
market. Findings rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational
employment and wage estimates for the Port St. Lucie metropolitan area and
for Florida statewide, supplemented by state workforce agency labor market
information and by direct labor market research when the referral calls
for it. Transferable skills analysis, labor market access, and placeability
conclusions each identify the data source behind them, so opposing counsel
can trace any finding back to the estimate it rests on instead of arguing
with an unsupported assertion.

## Evaluating a Fort Pierce evaluee

Purinton Analytics keeps no staffed office in Fort Pierce and publishes no
local street address, because it has none; candor about presence is part
of the same discipline that governs the reports. Meetings are by
appointment. Engagements run remote-first: scoping calls, records review,
and attorney conferences proceed by secure video and document exchange,
and many evaluation protocols can be administered remotely as well. When
the referral questions require an in-person evaluation, it is scheduled in
the evaluee's own community, and when a deposition, hearing, or trial date
requires presence, travel is arranged from the Kansas City home office and
confirmed against counsel's calendar well in advance.
