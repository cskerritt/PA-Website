## Where Williston Sits in the Regional Labor Market

Williston is a small town, and small-market honesty is the discipline its cases demand: the opinion must state what work actually exists within reach of a restricted worker living there, including employment accessed by commuting toward Gainesville, without inflating or shrinking the picture to fit a damages theory.
