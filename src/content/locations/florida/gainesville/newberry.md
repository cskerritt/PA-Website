## Commuting Geography and Earning Capacity

Newberry is a small community whose workers participate in the Gainesville, FL metropolitan labor market, and a credible earning capacity analysis has to take that commuting geography seriously in both directions: an opinion that assumes the whole metro is available to a restricted worker can overstate capacity, while an opinion confined to the town itself can understate it. The analysis therefore draws on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Gainesville metropolitan area and for Florida statewide, on state workforce agency labor market information, and on direct labor market research when the referral questions call for employer-level evidence. Every figure-bearing source is cited in the report itself, so the methodology is available to whichever side wants to probe it.
