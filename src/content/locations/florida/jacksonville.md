The Jacksonville metropolitan area reaches well beyond its principal city,
taking in Duval County, Clay County, St. Johns County, Nassau County, and
Baker County, and Purinton Analytics accepts vocational and life care
planning engagements from counsel throughout that footprint. Plaintiff and
defense firms alike retain the practice for
[earning capacity and employability evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) in catastrophic injury
matters, and [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Courts serving the Jacksonville metro

State court matters are accepted in the Circuit Court serving Duval County
and the surrounding counties of the metro, including Clay, St. Johns,
Nassau, and Baker counties. Federal matters proceed in Florida's district
courts, including the United States District Court for the Middle District
of Florida, the United States District Court for the Northern District of
Florida, and the United States District Court for the Southern District of
Florida. The report standard does not change with the forum: a documented
conflict check, a defined scope, and a records request tailored to the
claims, so that the written opinion rests on a record both sides can
examine. Matters elsewhere in the state route through the firm's
[Florida practice](/locations/florida/) under the same retention and the
same methodology.

## Northeast Florida logistics

Jacksonville meetings are by appointment. The firm has no staffed office in
Northeast Florida and publishes no street address for this location, a
statement it makes plainly because honesty about presence belongs to the
same discipline as honesty in a report. Engagements usually open remotely,
with records review, structured interviews, and standardized testing under
publisher protocols where remote administration is supported. When the
referral questions call for in-person evaluation, or when deposition and
trial dates require attendance, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City
home office, and counsel keep a single point of contact throughout the
engagement, from intake through report delivery, deposition preparation,
and testimony itself.

## Labor market research for Northeast Florida

Vocational findings for Jacksonville area evaluees are built on Bureau of
Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the
Jacksonville metropolitan area and for Florida statewide, along with state
workforce agency labor market information and direct labor market research
where warranted. [Medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/)
and life care plans for evaluees in the region are priced to the local
market through provider-specific inquiries and published fee data rather
than national averages. Where an opposing expert has already opined, the
firm offers [rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/)
against the same published sources, and
[forensic economic damages](/services/forensic-economic-damages/) questions
are referred to the independent economist partners the firm coordinates.

## Requesting a conflict check

To begin a Jacksonville engagement, submit the caption or party names
through the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and note any imminent
disclosure, deposition, or trial dates. Any venue-specific registration or
qualification requirement is identified and confirmed during the conflict
check and intake process. Counsel whose practices extend beyond Florida can
review the firm's [national coverage](/locations/nationwide/), which
operates on the same by-appointment, remote-first model described here.
