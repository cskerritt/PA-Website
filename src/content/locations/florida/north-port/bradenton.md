## Bradenton in the regional labor market

Earning capacity opinions for a Bradenton evaluee are grounded in the labor
market where that evaluee actually lives and would actually seek work.
Bradenton is a substantial city within the North Port-Bradenton-Sarasota
metropolitan area, and residents realistically commute throughout Manatee
County and the wider metro. Vocational findings therefore rest on Bureau of
Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the metro
area and for Florida statewide, supplemented by state workforce agency labor
market information and direct labor market research where the referral
questions warrant it. The analysis is qualitative and sourced, never
generic: transferable skills, residual functional capacity, and the jobs
genuinely available to this person in this market.

Early retention protects the disclosure calendar and gives the evaluation
