The Miami-Fort Lauderdale-West Palm Beach metropolitan area is among the
largest litigation markets Purinton Analytics serves, and the coastal
corridor from Palm Beach County down through Broward County to Miami-Dade
County produces a steady flow of matters in which vocational and
care-planning opinions decide the damages picture. Counsel on either side of
the caption retain the firm for
[earning capacity and employability evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/)
in injury and employment cases, [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/)
in catastrophic injury matters, and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Venues along the southeast coast

Engagements are accepted for matters in the Circuit Court for Miami-Dade
County, Broward County, and Palm Beach County, and in Florida's federal
trial courts, including the United States District Court for the Southern
District of Florida, the United States District Court for the Middle
District of Florida, and the United States District Court for the Northern
District of Florida. The same written opinion standard applies in every
forum: a documented conflict check, a defined scope, and a records request
tailored to the claims, so the report rests on a record both plaintiff and
defense counsel can examine. Matters venued elsewhere in the state are
handled through the firm's [Florida practice](/locations/florida/).

## By appointment, not by storefront

Miami meetings are by appointment. The firm keeps no staffed office in South
Florida and publishes no street address for this location, because accuracy
about presence is part of the same discipline that governs its reports. Most
engagements begin remotely, with records review, structured interviews, and
standardized testing under publisher protocols where remote administration
is supported. When an evaluation calls for in-person work, or when
deposition and trial dates require presence, travel is scheduled from the
Kansas City home office, and counsel receive a single point of contact and a
single report standard regardless of where the work is performed.

## South Florida labor market research

Vocational findings for evaluees in the metro rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the
Miami-Fort Lauderdale-West Palm Beach metropolitan area and for Florida
statewide, supplemented by state workforce agency labor market information
and direct employer research where the referral questions warrant it. Life
care plans and [medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/)
for South Florida residents are priced to the evaluee's own market rather
than to national averages, using provider-specific inquiries and published
fee data for the region. Where an opposing expert has already produced a
report, the firm also performs
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) against the same
published sources.

## Starting a Miami engagement

Whether the matter sits in state court in Miami-Dade County or in federal
court, the first step is the same. Submit the caption or party names through
the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and note any imminent disclosure,
deposition, or trial dates, so scheduling can be confirmed alongside
clearance. Any venue-specific registration or qualification requirement is
identified and confirmed during the conflict check and intake process.
Counsel with matters beyond Florida can review the firm's
[national coverage](/locations/nationwide/), which follows the same
remote-first, by-appointment model used here.
