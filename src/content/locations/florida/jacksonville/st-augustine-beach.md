How is earning capacity measured for somebody who lives in a small beach community? That question sits at the center of most St. Augustine Beach referrals Purinton Analytics receives, and the answer is methodological, not rhetorical. The firm's [vocational evaluations](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) define the relevant labor market on the record: what the evaluee can do given medical restrictions and transferable skills, where that work exists within realistic reach of a St. Johns County beach community, and what it pays according to published data. Plaintiff and defense counsel receive the same analysis, because the measurement does not change with the retention.

## Venue for a St. Johns County matter

St. Augustine Beach lies in St. Johns County, and state-court matters are heard in the Circuit Court. Federal matters fall to Florida's district courts, the United States District Court for the Northern District of Florida, the United States District Court for the Middle District of Florida, and the United States District Court for the Southern District of Florida, with the governing district confirmed at conflict check and intake rather than presumed from geography. The firm's regional coverage, including the counties surrounding the principal city, is collected on the [Jacksonville metro page](/locations/florida/jacksonville/).

## Defining the market before measuring it

The data behind the measurement comes from Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Jacksonville metropolitan area and for Florida statewide, from state workforce agency labor market information, and from direct labor market research where the referral questions warrant it. Market definition is stated, not assumed: a beach community is not treated as a sealed economy, and neither is an evaluee automatically credited with every opportunity Jacksonville offers. The report walks through the restrictions, the transferable skills, the commuting analysis, and the wage evidence in an order a cross-examiner can follow and test. Where vocational testing is administered, the instruments and their results appear alongside the labor market evidence, so the capacity side of the equation is as checkable as the market side.

## Where the evaluation takes place

The firm keeps no St. Augustine Beach office and publishes no address here; service is by appointment, and the pages say so because candor about presence belongs to the same standard as candor about data. Most of the work is remote, and scheduling is coordinated with counsel so the evaluation date, the report date, and the disclosure deadline line up without compression at the end. In-person evaluation is arranged in the evaluee's own community when the referral requires direct assessment, and testimony travel is organized from the Kansas City home office. For catastrophic injury matters, [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) and [medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/) proceed on the same record as the vocational work, each opinion citing sources the other can be checked against.
