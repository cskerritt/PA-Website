The product of an expert engagement is a written opinion that survives cross-examination, and that is the standard Purinton Analytics writes to for Atlantic Beach matters. Every conclusion in a [vocational evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) or [life care plan](/services/life-care-planning/) is tied to a source the opposing side can pull and check: medical records, vocational testing, published labor market data, documented care costs. Counsel on either side of an Atlantic Beach case get the same product, an opinion built on a record both sides can examine, because the firm accepts plaintiff and defense work in comparable measure and expects each opinion to be read by the other camp.

## Where Atlantic Beach cases are heard

Atlantic Beach is part of Duval County, and its state-court litigation proceeds in the Circuit Court. Federal cases go to Florida's district courts, meaning the United States District Court for the Northern District of Florida, the United States District Court for the Middle District of Florida, and the United States District Court for the Southern District of Florida, with the governing venue identified during the conflict check and intake rather than assumed. Venue detail for the surrounding counties is collected on the [Jacksonville metro page](/locations/florida/jacksonville/).

## Sourcing the labor market analysis

An earning capacity opinion for an Atlantic Beach evaluee cites Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Jacksonville metropolitan area and Florida statewide, state workforce agency labor market information, and, when the referral requires it, direct labor market research documented in the file. Atlantic Beach residents work across the metro, and the analysis reflects the region's employment rather than an artificially narrowed slice of it, while still accounting for the restrictions and commuting realities of the individual evaluee. Each data source appears in the report with enough specificity that a cross-examiner can locate it, which is precisely the point. Vocational test results and the medical restrictions they interact with are handled the same way: instrument named, administration documented, interpretation explained, so the chain from raw data to opinion is visible in the report itself rather than reconstructed under oath.
