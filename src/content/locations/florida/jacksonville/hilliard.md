In a catastrophic injury case, the life care plan often carries the heaviest share of the damages evidence, and that is as true for a Hilliard matter as for any other. Future care cannot be tried on impressions; it has to be documented, item by item, against the medical record, or it will not survive the examination that is coming. Purinton Analytics prepares [life care plans](/services/life-care-planning/) in which every recommended service, supply, and intervention traces to documented medical need, and it prepares them for either side of the caption, whether the assignment is to build the plan or to evaluate the one already disclosed. [Vocational evaluations](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) proceed under the same discipline when earning capacity is also in dispute, so the opinions rest on a common record rather than parallel guesses.

## From care needs to the labor market

A life care plan for a Hilliard evaluee starts in the medical record: diagnoses, restrictions, treating providers' documentation, and the care the record supports going forward. When the damages theory requires care costs projected over time, [medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/) work proceeds on the same record, so the plan and the projection can be examined against each other. Where the case also puts earning capacity at issue, vocational findings rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Jacksonville metropolitan area and for Florida statewide, supplemented by state workforce agency labor market information and direct labor market research where the referral questions warrant it. Geography enters the vocational analysis as a finding the record must support: before wage evidence is applied, the report sets out whether the restrictions documented by treating providers permit this evaluee to work across the wider metro or confine the job search closer to home, and it cites the portions of the record that carry that conclusion.

## Evaluations in the evaluee's community

The firm maintains no Hilliard office and publishes no local address; meetings are by appointment, and stating that plainly belongs to the same candor that governs the plans themselves. Records review, research, and drafting are performed remotely. When the referral calls for in-person assessment, the evaluation is conducted in the evaluee's own community, which matters most in precisely the kind of case a life care plan serves, where travel is hardest on the person being evaluated. Deposition and trial testimony is delivered with travel from the Kansas City home office, scheduled at intake against the court's calendar so the plan, the disclosure, and the testimony dates stay aligned.
