Nothing in a Macclenny engagement moves before the conflict check clears. Purinton Analytics will not take custody of records, discuss the facts of a claim, or hold dates for an expert whose independence has not been confirmed against the caption and parties, and that refusal is deliberate: an expert is only useful to the side that retained him if the other side cannot disqualify the retention. The file therefore opens with clearance and a written scope, and only then does the substantive work begin, whether the referral calls for a [vocational evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/), a [life care plan](/services/life-care-planning/), or both. Plaintiff and defense counsel referring Macclenny matters get the same sequence, in the same order, every time.

## Where a Baker County matter is heard

State-court litigation for Macclenny proceeds in the Circuit Court serving Baker County. When the case is federal, Florida's forums are the United States District Court for the Northern District of Florida, the United States District Court for the Middle District of Florida, and the United States District Court for the Southern District of Florida. The firm treats the controlling forum as an intake fact to be confirmed with counsel rather than a detail to be assumed, and any qualification or registration step particular to that venue is identified at the same stage. Counsel weighing venue questions across the wider region will find its counties and courts collected on the [Jacksonville metro page](/locations/florida/jacksonville/).

## Which market's data controls

An earning capacity opinion for a Macclenny evaluee has to commit, on the record, to a labor market. Baker County lies within the Jacksonville metropolitan area, so the published starting point is the Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates covering that metro. Whether the full metro is genuinely open to a particular evaluee is a separate finding: medical restrictions, transferable skills, and documented work history determine how far the market reaches for this person, and the report states that determination, with the record citations that carry it, before wage data enters the analysis. Statewide estimates for Florida, labor market information published by the state workforce agency, and the firm's own direct labor market research supplement the metro figures when the referral questions call for them. What never happens is a silent choice. The market definition is argued in writing, because it will be examined at deposition, and an opinion that cannot explain its own boundary invites the cross-examination it deserves.

## How the work is performed

Records analysis, research, and report drafting are performed remotely, which is how most of any engagement runs regardless of where the evaluee lives. Purinton Analytics keeps no office in Macclenny and publishes no local address; meetings happen by appointment, and the firm says so plainly rather than implying a storefront that does not exist. If the referral questions require an in-person evaluation, the appointment is set in the evaluee's own community. Testimony is a travel event planned in advance: deposition and trial appearances are scheduled from the Kansas City home office against the dates counsel supplies during intake, so preparation time is protected rather than improvised.
