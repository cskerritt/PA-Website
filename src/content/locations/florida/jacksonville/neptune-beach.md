## The analysis stage: building the labor market record

The analytical middle of the engagement grounds every employability and earning capacity finding in Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Jacksonville metropolitan area and for Florida statewide, supported by state workforce agency labor market information and direct labor market research where the referral questions justify it. A Neptune Beach evaluee is assessed against the employment reachable from the community, which for most occupations means the broader metro anchored by Jacksonville; the report states the market definition used and the reason for choosing it, so the choice itself can be tested on cross-examination. Each stage of the sequence leaves its own artifact in the file, from the clearance record and scope letter through the records index and the sourced report, and counsel can see at any moment what has been done and what remains.

Nothing in the sequence depends on a local office, and the firm does not have or claim any in Neptune Beach; no local address is published because none exists.
