By the time counsel in St. Augustine calls a vocational expert or life care planner, the expert disclosure deadline is usually already on the docket. Purinton Analytics builds engagements backward from that date: conflict check, then a defined scope and a records request tailored to the claims, then a written opinion delivered with enough runway for deposition preparation. ## St. Johns County and the available courts

St. Augustine sits in St. Johns County, where state-court matters proceed in the Circuit Court. Federal matters go to Florida's district courts: the United States District Court for the Northern District of Florida, the United States District Court for the Middle District of Florida, and the United States District Court for the Southern District of Florida. Which district governs a given case is a venue question settled during the conflict check and intake, not a fact the firm assumes, and any registration or qualification requirement particular to the venue is confirmed at the same time. For the wider region's counties and courts, see the [Jacksonville metro overview](/locations/florida/jacksonville/); St. Augustine matters are handled within that regional practice.

## Labor market evidence that holds up

Earning capacity opinions for a St. Augustine evaluee are grounded in Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates covering the Jacksonville metropolitan area and Florida statewide, together with state workforce agency labor market information and, where warranted, direct labor market research. The same public sources support a plaintiff-side loss analysis and a defense-side mitigation critique; the discipline lies in applying them identically no matter which side issued the retention letter. Where a catastrophic injury is involved, [medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/) work draws on documented care needs rather than assumptions, so the vocational and care-planning opinions can be examined against the same record.

## Scheduling the evaluation itself

There is no St. Augustine office and no published local address; the firm works by appointment and says so plainly. Most of an engagement, records analysis, research, and drafting among it, runs remotely on a schedule set by the disclosure date. In-person evaluation, when the referral questions require it, takes place in the evaluee's own community, and testimony travel originates at the Kansas City home office with dates locked well before the deadline they serve. Rebuttal timelines get the same treatment in reverse: the opposing report's disclosure date sets the clock, and the review is scoped at intake so the response lands inside the window the court has left open.
