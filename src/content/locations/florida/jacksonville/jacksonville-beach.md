The damages side of a serious Jacksonville Beach case often needs answers to more than the vocational question. What work an injured person can still perform, what care the medical record supports, and what the resulting losses are worth are separate opinions that must nonetheless fit together under cross-examination. Purinton Analytics structures engagements so they do. The firm performs [vocational evaluations](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) and prepares [life care plans](/services/life-care-planning/) directly, and in [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under the same engagement structure. Counsel for plaintiffs and for defendants retain the same coordination and receive the same documented product.

## Where a Jacksonville Beach matter is heard

Jacksonville Beach is a Duval County city, and state-court matters arising here proceed in the Circuit Court. Federal litigation belongs to Florida's district courts: the United States District Court for the Northern District of Florida, the United States District Court for the Middle District of Florida, and the United States District Court for the Southern District of Florida. The firm does not assume which district controls a given case; the governing venue, and any registration or qualification requirement particular to it, is identified during the conflict check and intake. The counties and courts of the surrounding region are set out on the [Jacksonville metro page](/locations/florida/jacksonville/), and in a coordinated engagement each expert's role is scoped against the confirmed venue from the start.

## Damages opinions that share a record

Coordination begins with the data. Vocational findings for a Jacksonville Beach evaluee rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Jacksonville metropolitan area and for Florida statewide, together with state workforce agency labor market information and direct labor market research where the referral questions warrant it. Because Jacksonville is the metro's principal city and anchors much of the region's employment, the analysis defines the evaluee's realistic labor market explicitly rather than assuming it, and the report states the definition used and the reason for it. The life care plan is developed against the same medical record the vocational opinion relies on, and when independent economist partners value the losses, they work from the vocational and care-planning conclusions as written. The point of the shared record is that the opinions can be examined against each other without gaps between them, by retaining counsel and opposing counsel alike.

## By-appointment service, stated plainly

The firm keeps no Jacksonville Beach office and publishes no local address, because it has none; candor about presence is part of the same discipline that governs its reports. Records review, research, and drafting proceed remotely. When the referral requires an in-person evaluation, the appointment is held in the evaluee's own community, and deposition or trial testimony is delivered with travel from the Kansas City home office, calendared against the court's dates at intake. Coordination does not change these logistics; it means the scheduling covers every workstream together instead of leaving counsel to sequence the experts separately and hope the calendars align.
