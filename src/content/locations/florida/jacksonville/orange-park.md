Vocational and life care planning questions run through a wider range of Orange Park litigation than counsel sometimes expect. A personal injury claim turns on residual earning capacity; an employment case may need testimony on job search adequacy or the realistic market for a terminated worker; a marital dissolution may require an earning capacity opinion for a spouse long out of the workforce; a catastrophic injury requires a [life care plan](/services/life-care-planning/) before damages can be tried. Purinton Analytics serves plaintiff and defense counsel across all of these matter types for Orange Park cases, with [vocational evaluation and testimony](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) at the core of the practice.

## Clay County courts and the federal alternatives

Orange Park lies in Clay County, and its state-court matters are heard in the Circuit Court. Federal litigation belongs to Florida's district courts, the United States District Court for the Northern District of Florida, the United States District Court for the Middle District of Florida, and the United States District Court for the Southern District of Florida; which of these controls a specific matter is identified during the conflict check and intake, together with any qualification requirement particular to the venue. ## The same methodology across matter types

Whatever the case type, the data foundation does not change: Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Jacksonville metropolitan area and for Florida statewide, state workforce agency labor market information, and direct labor market research where warranted. An Orange Park evaluee's employability is assessed against the metro's employment base, anchored by Jacksonville as the principal city, rather than against the suburb alone. In family matters those sources support imputed income analysis; in employment matters they frame mitigation; in injury matters they anchor loss of earning capacity. Consistency across matter types is deliberate, because it is what makes each opinion defensible in its own courtroom. The report also separates what the data shows from what the expert concludes, labeling each step of the reasoning, because opinions that blur that line invite exclusion motions and rarely help the counsel who commissioned them.

The firm keeps no Orange Park office and publishes no local address; every meeting is arranged in advance.
