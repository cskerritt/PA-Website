Purinton Analytics serves Tallahassee counsel the way it serves counsel
everywhere in its [national practice](/locations/nationwide/): remote-first,
by appointment, and without pretending to a local footprint it does not
have. What the metro's attorneys retain is the practice itself, its
[vocational evaluation and expert testimony](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[expert testimony and litigation consulting](/services/expert-testimony-litigation-consulting/),
delivered on the same terms to plaintiff and defense counsel, with every
report written on the assumption that opposing counsel will read it
looking for weaknesses.

## A litigation footprint across several counties

The Tallahassee metro takes in Gadsden County, Jefferson County, Leon
County, and Wakulla County, and state court engagements are accepted for
matters in the Circuit Court serving each of them. Federal engagements are
accepted in Florida's district courts: the United States District Court
for the Northern District of Florida, the United States District Court for
the Middle District of Florida, and the United States District Court for
the Southern District of Florida. Because the metro's cases spread across
several county courthouses, intake confirms the specific forum and its
disclosure schedule before scope is set, and the records request that
follows is tailored to the claims in that forum. The firm's approach to
the rest of the state is described on the
[Florida practice page](/locations/florida/).

## Appointment logistics across the metro

There is no staffed Purinton Analytics office in Tallahassee and no
published local address, because the firm does not maintain one, and
saying so is part of the discipline counsel are hiring. Meetings are by
appointment. Records review, scoping conferences, and many evaluation
protocols proceed through secure video and document exchange, which keeps
the engagement moving regardless of geography. When an in-person
evaluation is warranted, it is scheduled in the evaluee's community,
whether that community sits in Leon County or in the metro's smaller
counties; depositions, mediations, and trial appearances are reached by
travel from the Kansas City home office, planned against the case calendar
rather than improvised.

## Labor market evidence for Tallahassee evaluees

Vocational findings for evaluees across the metro rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Tallahassee
metropolitan area and for Florida statewide, on state workforce agency
labor market information, and on direct labor market research where the
referral requires it. The report identifies the evaluee's commutable labor
market, explains the selection, and ties each employability and earning
capacity conclusion to the estimates supporting it, so the analysis
withstands examination from either side of the caption. When the referral
also calls for economic loss calculations, the firm accepts
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## How to begin

Tallahassee referrals follow the same sequence as every engagement in the
practice: conflict check, scope, records. To begin,
[request a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) with the case caption and
parties, and identify any disclosure, deposition, mediation, or trial
dates already set, so the report timeline and any travel can be confirmed
against the deadlines that govern the case. Counsel receive a direct
answer on conflicts and availability before records are exchanged.
