## Seminole County venue context

Sanford anchors the Seminole County end of the Orlando-Kissimmee-Sanford
metropolitan area, and its state court matters are litigated in the Circuit
Court for Seminole County. Federal practice in Florida spans the United
States District Court for the Northern District of Florida, the United
States District Court for the Middle District of Florida, and the United
States District Court for the Southern District of Florida; rather than
guess at the controlling forum, the firm identifies the proper district and
division with counsel during the conflict check and intake, then conforms
the report and disclosure package to that court's requirements.

## Reading the labor market around Sanford

Sanford workers compete in a metropolitan labor market centered on Orlando,
and a defensible earning capacity opinion has to describe that market as it
actually functions for the individual evaluee: their occupation, their
restrictions, their realistic commuting range. The firm grounds those
findings in Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage
estimates for the Orlando-Kissimmee-Sanford metropolitan area and for
Florida as a whole, in state workforce agency labor market information, and,
when a placement dispute demands it, in direct contact with employers whose
responses are documented for the file. No wage or employment figure appears
in the opinion without a cited source.
