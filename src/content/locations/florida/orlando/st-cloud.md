For an injured worker or a catastrophically hurt claimant living in St.
Cloud, the evaluation itself is where a damages case becomes concrete. That
is why Purinton Analytics structures its St. Cloud work around the evaluee:
interviews conducted in the person's own community or by secure video,
functional and vocational histories taken directly rather than assumed, and
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) built from the treating
record. Attorneys for plaintiffs and for defendants bring the firm the same
questions in mirror image, and the answer in each case is a
[vocational assessment](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) that reads the
same no matter who paid for it.

## Where St. Cloud cases are heard

St. Cloud sits in Osceola County, and its state court litigation is tried in
the Circuit Court for Osceola County. On the federal side, Florida's trial
courts are organized into the United States District Court for the Northern
District of Florida, the United States District Court for the Middle
District of Florida, and the United States District Court for the Southern
District of Florida. Which of those forums, and which division, controls a
given St. Cloud matter is a venue question the firm confirms with counsel
during the conflict check and intake, and the report is then formatted to
that forum's disclosure requirements.

## Locating the labor market for a St. Cloud evaluee

St. Cloud is a small city, and the firm treats the size of the town as the
beginning of the labor market question rather than the end of it. An
earning capacity analysis for a St. Cloud evaluee is framed by the market
that individual can realistically reach given their restrictions, skills,
and circumstances, a determination the firm makes person by person instead
of assuming. Placeability and wage-earning conclusions draw on Bureau of
Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates covering the
Orlando-Kissimmee-Sanford metropolitan area, alongside Florida statewide
estimates and state workforce agency data. Where a residual capacity
question turns on what employers actually require, the firm performs direct
labor market research and documents each contact, so that opposing counsel
can trace every conclusion back to a source.

There is no Purinton Analytics office in St. Cloud, and the firm does not

description of how a national practice serves a Florida town. Most phases of
an engagement, from record review through report drafting and rebuttal work
such as [rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/), proceed
remotely. In-person evaluations, depositions, and trial testimony are
scheduled with travel from the Kansas City home office. Details on how the
practice covers the surrounding region appear on the
[Orlando metro page](/locations/florida/orlando/) and the
[Florida state page](/locations/florida/), and the firm's national
footprint is described on the
[nationwide page](/locations/nationwide/). When damages must be reduced to
economic terms, the firm coordinates
[damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) in which
independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis while
the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement
structure.

Every St. Cloud referral starts the same way: with clearance, not with
commitment. [Request a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) with the case caption
and the parties, flag any looming disclosure or deposition dates, and hold
