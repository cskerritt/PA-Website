An expert report earns nothing until the other side has finished attacking
it. Purinton Analytics writes its Ocoee opinions on that assumption: every
[vocational assessment](/services/vocational-expert-witness/), every
[life care plan](/services/life-care-planning/), and every
[rebuttal or peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) is prepared as if
the first careful reader will be opposing counsel, because in an Orange
County courtroom that is usually true. The methodology is the same whether
the retaining party is the plaintiff or the defendant, which is precisely
what lets the opinion survive the deposition designed to break it.

## Venue for Ocoee disputes

Ocoee's state court matters belong to the Circuit Court for Orange County.
When a dispute is federal, Florida offers its litigants the United States
District Court for the Northern District of Florida, the United States
District Court for the Middle District of Florida, and the United States
District Court for the Southern District of Florida as its trial forums;
which one, and which division within it, actually controls is settled with
retaining counsel during the conflict check and intake, never assumed from
geography. Once venue is fixed, the report is assembled to the disclosure
and testimony standards that forum applies to retained experts.

## Testing earning capacity against a metro economy

Cross-examiners in Orange County know the local economy, so a vocational
opinion about an Ocoee resident has to know it better. The evaluee's
realistic market is the Orlando-Kissimmee-Sanford metropolitan area, and the
firm's occupational and wage findings are anchored in Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for that metropolitan
area and for Florida statewide, cross-checked against state workforce agency
labor market information. Where the fight is over whether jobs truly exist
for a person with the evaluee's restrictions, the firm conducts and
documents direct employer research, so the answer is evidence rather than
impression.

## Presence, honestly stated

Purinton Analytics does not have an Ocoee office, and it will not imply one;
overstating presence would sit oddly in a practice whose product is
verifiable statements. What counsel get instead is a remote-first workflow
covering records analysis, conferencing, and drafting, plus in-person
evaluation held in the evaluee's own community whenever the referral
questions call for it. Court appearances and depositions are staffed by
travel from the Kansas City home office. The
[Orlando metro page](/locations/florida/orlando/) explains regional
coverage, the [Florida page](/locations/florida/) covers the state, and the
[nationwide overview](/locations/nationwide/) describes the same model
applied across the country. When economic quantification is needed, the
firm coordinates
[damages assessments](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) in which
independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis while
the vocational and care-planning components proceed under one engagement
structure.

## Opening an Ocoee file

Before strategy, before records, before scheduling, there is clearance.
Submit the caption or the party names through the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/), note the claims at issue, and
identify any disclosure or trial dates already set. If the firm can accept
the matter, counsel receive a written scope, a records request shaped to the
disputed issues, and a candid assessment of what the timeline allows.
