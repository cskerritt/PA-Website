Kissimmee lends its name to the Orlando-Kissimmee-Sanford metropolitan area,
and matters venued in Osceola County account for a steady share of the
vocational and life care planning referrals Purinton Analytics receives from
Central Florida. Counsel on either side of the caption retain the firm for
[vocational expert witness](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) opinions
when employability and earning capacity are contested, for
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) when future care must be
specified and priced, and for
[expert testimony and litigation consulting](/services/expert-testimony-litigation-consulting/)
that is built to withstand cross-examination. Every file begins with a
documented conflict check and a defined scope, so the written opinion rests
on records both sides can examine.

## Osceola County courts and federal forums

State court matters arising in Kissimmee proceed in the Circuit Court for
Osceola County, and the firm accepts appointments in personal injury,
employment, family, and probate dockets there. For federal matters, Florida
counsel practice across the United States District Court for the Northern
District of Florida, the United States District Court for the Middle
District of Florida, and the United States District Court for the Southern
District of Florida; the district and division that control a particular
case are confirmed during the conflict check and intake rather than assumed.
Reports are drafted to the disclosure standards of whichever forum will
receive them.

## Kissimmee's place in the Central Florida labor market

An earning capacity opinion for a Kissimmee evaluee has to reflect the labor
market that person can actually reach, which in Osceola County means the
broader Orlando commuting area rather than the town alone. Vocational
findings rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage
estimates for the Orlando-Kissimmee-Sanford metropolitan area and for
Florida statewide, supplemented by state workforce agency labor market
information and direct employer research where the referral questions
warrant it. The analysis stays qualitative about the market's character and
quantitative only inside the report, where every figure is sourced and
reproducible.

## Evaluations and meetings by appointment

Purinton Analytics keeps no office in Kissimmee and publishes no street
address for this location; candor about presence is part of the same
discipline that governs the firm's reports. Record review, counsel
conferences, and many evaluee interviews are handled remotely. When an
in-person evaluation is appropriate, it is scheduled in the evaluee's own
community, and when deposition or trial requires presence in an Osceola
County courtroom, travel is arranged from the Kansas City home office. The
[Orlando metro page](/locations/florida/orlando/) describes how the practice
covers the surrounding counties, and the
[Florida overview](/locations/florida/) outlines statewide coverage. Where a
matter also needs economic quantification,
[coordinated damages assessment](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
engagements bring in independent economist partners who perform and sign the
economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under
one engagement structure.

## Starting a Kissimmee engagement

The practice serves plaintiff and defense referrals nationwide, as described
on the [nationwide practice page](/locations/nationwide/), and treats a
Kissimmee case with the same intake rigor as any other. Submit the caption
or party names through the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and note
any imminent disclosure, deposition, or trial dates so scheduling can be
confirmed alongside clearance. Once conflicts clear, counsel receive a
defined scope, a records request tailored to the claims, and a realistic
timeline to a signed report.
