Earning capacity is not a statistic; it is a person's realistic options,
and for someone living in Oviedo those options are defined by training,
restrictions, and the jobs reachable from a Seminole County address. That
individualized question is the starting point of every Oviedo referral
Purinton Analytics accepts. The firm answers it through
[vocational expert witness](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) analysis,
pairs it with [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) when
future care needs are also in dispute, and extends it through
[forensic economic damages](/services/forensic-economic-damages/)
coordination when losses must be valued. Plaintiff and defense retention
alike proceed from the same file structure and the same tests.

## Where an Oviedo claim is decided

The Circuit Court for Seminole County hears Oviedo's state court cases.
Federal cases belong to one of Florida's district courts, which are the
United States District Court for the Northern District of Florida, the
United States District Court for the Middle District of Florida, and the
United States District Court for the Southern District of Florida. Because
venue assumptions are a common source of avoidable error, the firm resolves
the controlling district and division with retaining counsel during the
conflict check and intake, and only then finalizes the disclosure format
the report will follow.

## From individual evaluee to metropolitan data

Once the evaluee's residual capacity is established, the analysis widens
outward: which occupations fit, and where those occupations exist within
reach of Oviedo. The occupational and wage layer comes from Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the
Orlando-Kissimmee-Sanford metropolitan area and for Florida statewide,
checked against state workforce agency labor market information. Transferable
skills analysis does the narrowing; direct employer research, when the
referral requires it, does the verifying. The sequence matters, because it
lets the final opinion connect a specific person to a documented market
rather than gesturing at averages.

The firm's honesty policy covers geography: Purinton Analytics has no
Oviedo office and claims none, working instead from its Kansas City home
office with by-appointment presence wherever the case requires it.
Interviews and conferences default to secure video; in-person evaluations
are held in the evaluee's own community; deposition and courtroom
appearances are scheduled with travel planned against the docket. Counsel
who want the broader context can read the

analysis while vocational and care-planning work runs under one engagement
structure.

## A conflict check opens the file

Nothing substantive happens before clearance, and clearance is quick to
request. Provide the caption or party names through the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/), add a short statement of the claims,
and identify any imminent disclosure, deposition, or trial dates. If the
matter clears, the firm returns a defined scope, a records request focused
on the disputed issues, and dates counsel can hold the firm to.
