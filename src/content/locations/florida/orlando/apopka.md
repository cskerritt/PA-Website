A vocational opinion is only as strong as the record beneath it. That is
the premise Purinton Analytics brings to every Apopka referral: before any
conclusion about employability or earning capacity is drafted, the firm
assembles the treating records, the work history, the education and
training documentation, and the labor market data that will have to carry
the opinion under oath. Counsel for plaintiffs and counsel for defendants
retain the firm on identical terms, whether the assignment is
[vocational expert witness](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) analysis
where employability is disputed,
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) where future care is at
issue, or [forensic economic damages](/services/forensic-economic-damages/)
coordination where losses must be reduced to present value by an economist.

## Orange County and the federal districts

Apopka litigation in state court proceeds in the Circuit Court for Orange
County. Florida's federal system comprises the United States District Court
for the Northern District of Florida, the United States District Court for
the Middle District of Florida, and the United States District Court for
the Southern District of Florida. The firm does not presume which district
or division governs an Apopka filing; controlling venue is verified with
counsel at the conflict check and intake stage, and the deliverables are
then matched to that forum's disclosure conventions.

## Defining the labor market for an Apopka evaluee

Apopka lies within the Orlando-Kissimmee-Sanford metropolitan area, so the
published data that frames an Apopka opinion is metropolitan in scope.
Occupational findings rest on the Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational
employment and wage estimates for that metropolitan area and for Florida
statewide, enriched with state workforce agency information and, where
placement is genuinely contested, direct labor market survey work. The
market each evaluee can realistically reach is established individually,
from that person's restrictions, transportation circumstances, and work
history, never assumed from the address. Transferable skills analysis then
narrows the market to what this evaluee, with these restrictions and this
background, can perform, which is the question a jury actually needs
answered.

## What an Apopka engagement looks like in practice

The firm holds no office space in Apopka and does not advertise a local
address; every meeting is by appointment, and most of the engagement is
conducted remotely by design. Evaluations that should happen face to face
are held in the evaluee's community. Deposition and trial appearances are
scheduled with travel from the Kansas City home office. For a fuller picture
of regional coverage, see the
[Orlando metro page](/locations/florida/orlando/) and the
[Florida state overview](/locations/florida/); the firm's approach to
serving courts everywhere else is set out on the
[nationwide page](/locations/nationwide/). Where the case needs numbers a
vocational expert should not supply, the firm assembles
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while vocational and care-planning work continues under one
engagement structure.
