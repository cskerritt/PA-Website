Clermont belongs to Lake County, and it also belongs to the
Orlando-Kissimmee-Sanford metropolitan area. Purinton Analytics treats
those two facts as answers to two different questions: the county fixes
where a Clermont case is tried, and the metropolitan area fixes which
published labor market estimates an earning capacity opinion draws on.
Retaining attorneys on either side of a Clermont case use the firm for
[vocational expert witness](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) opinions,
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/), each
delivered as a signed report the opposing expert can be invited to test.

## Lake County courts, Florida's federal forums

State court claims arising in Clermont are tried in the Circuit Court for
Lake County. Federal claims fall somewhere within Florida's district court
system, made up of the United States District Court for the Northern
District of Florida, the United States District Court for the Middle
District of Florida, and the United States District Court for the Southern
District of Florida. The firm confirms the controlling district and
division with counsel as part of the conflict check and intake, then keys
its disclosure package to the identified forum. That verification habit is
small, but it is the same habit that keeps every factual statement in the
report checkable.

## A Lake County address, a metro-area data set

The labor market a Clermont evaluee can realistically reach is a question
the firm answers individually, from that evaluee's restrictions, skills,
and circumstances, rather than from assumptions about where a suburb's
residents happen to work. Findings for Clermont evaluees are grounded in
Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for
the Orlando-Kissimmee-Sanford metropolitan area and for Florida statewide,
with state workforce agency labor market information layered on. When
counsel dispute whether the identified occupations are genuinely open to
the evaluee, the firm runs direct labor market inquiries and preserves the
responses in the file, because a placeability conclusion should be
demonstrable, not asserted.
