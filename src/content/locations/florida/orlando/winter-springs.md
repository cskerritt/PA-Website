A Winter Springs engagement with Purinton Analytics begins as a question of
eligibility, not salesmanship: can the firm ethically take the case? The
[conflict check](/refer-a-case/) runs first, before records are exchanged
and before opinions are discussed, and everything that follows inherits
that order of operations. It is a procedural habit, but it signals how the
substantive work is done, whether the retention is for a
[vocational expert witness](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) opinion,
a [life care plan](/services/life-care-planning/), or a
[medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/), and whether
the retaining lawyer represents the claimant or the defense.

## Seminole County and the federal map

When a Winter Springs dispute is filed in state court, it is heard in the
Circuit Court for Seminole County. When it is federal, it sits within
Florida's district court system: the United States District Court for the
Northern District of Florida, the United States District Court for the
Middle District of Florida, and the United States District Court for the
Southern District of Florida. As part of the same intake conversation that
clears conflicts, the firm confirms with counsel which district and
division control the matter, then commits to that forum's expert
disclosure conventions in writing as part of the scope.

## Labor market evidence for a suburban evaluee

Winter Springs is a suburban community whose workforce is woven into the
Orlando-Kissimmee-Sanford metropolitan economy, so the labor market side of
a vocational opinion is metropolitan by necessity. The firm builds that
side from Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage
estimates for the metropolitan area and for Florida statewide, reads them
against state workforce agency labor market information, and, when
placeability is contested rather than conceded, verifies specific
opportunities through direct employer contact that is logged in the file.
Qualitative conclusions appear in the narrative; every quantitative claim
stays tied to a named source in the report's exhibits.

## Appointments, interviews, and travel

No Winter Springs office exists, and the firm's materials say so without
hedging; its staffed base is the Kansas City home office. In practice this
changes little for counsel: record exchange, working sessions, and most
interviews are remote, in-person evaluations are convened in the evaluee's
own community, and the firm travels for depositions and trial settings on
dates locked in at intake. The regional picture is drawn on the
[Orlando metro page](/locations/florida/orlando/), the statewide picture on
the [Florida page](/locations/florida/), and the national picture on the
[nationwide page](/locations/nationwide/). Cases needing valuation are
handled as [coordinated damages assessments](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/),
with independent economist partners performing and signing the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Submitting a Winter Springs referral

Send the caption or the party names through the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/), together with a brief description of
the claims and the dates that constrain expert disclosure. Hold any
confidential records until clearance is confirmed. The firm responds with
its determination, a proposed scope of work, and a schedule that reflects
the Seminole County or federal calendar the case actually faces.
