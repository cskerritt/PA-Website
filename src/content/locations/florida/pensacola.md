because honesty about presence belongs in the same category as honesty
about methodology. What counsel in the Pensacola-Ferry Pass-Brent metro
retain instead is a national practice that delivers
[vocational evaluation and expert testimony](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),

[expert testimony and litigation consulting](/services/expert-testimony-litigation-consulting/)

with every opinion written to withstand the scrutiny of the other side of
the caption.

## Where Pensacola cases are heard

The metro's litigation footprint covers Escambia County and Santa Rosa
County, and state court engagements are accepted for matters in the
Circuit Court serving each. Federal engagements are accepted in Florida's
district courts: the United States District Court for the Northern
District of Florida, the United States District Court for the Middle
District of Florida, and the United States District Court for the Southern
District of Florida. Intake confirms the forum and its disclosure schedule
at the outset, because the calendar governs everything that follows, from
the records request to the report deadline. Counsel working matters beyond
the metro can review the firm's statewide approach on the
[Florida practice page](/locations/florida/).

## What by-appointment service actually means

Every engagement stage that can proceed remotely does: conflict check,
scoping, records review, attorney conferences, and many evaluation
protocols are handled through secure video and document exchange. What
cannot be done remotely is scheduled deliberately. In-person evaluations
in the Pensacola area, along with depositions, mediations, and trial
appearances, are arranged through travel from the Kansas City home office
and confirmed against counsel's deadlines before commitments are made.
Nothing about this arrangement is improvised, and no local storefront is
implied, because the firm's position is that a court should be able to
rely on every representation it makes, including the ones about geography.

## Labor market data for Escambia and Santa Rosa County evaluees

Employability and earning capacity findings for Pensacola-area evaluees
are built on data for this specific market. The analysis draws on Bureau
of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the
Pensacola metropolitan area and for Florida statewide, on state workforce
agency labor market information, and on direct labor market research when
the referral questions call for it. The report states which market defines
the evaluee's commutable job base and shows the sources for each finding,
so cross-examination meets a documented foundation rather than an
assertion. Where the referral also requires economic loss calculations,
the firm accepts
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.
