An evaluee in Chattahoochee should not have to organize their life around an
expert's convenience. Purinton Analytics structures its
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) and
[vocational expert](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) work so that the
evaluation comes to the evaluee: by secure video for most interviews, and in
the evaluee's own community when the questions in the case require sitting
across from the person. That logistical commitment serves plaintiff and
defense retentions equally, because a credible opinion about a Chattahoochee
resident starts with an accurate picture of that resident's circumstances.

## Courts Serving Chattahoochee

Chattahoochee is part of Gadsden County, and its state-court litigation is
heard in the Circuit Court. Cases with a federal basis may be filed in the
United States District Court for the Northern District of Florida, the
United States District Court for the Middle District of Florida, or the
United States District Court for the Southern District of Florida; which of
those forums governs is settled during the conflict check and intake, not
presumed from geography. The written opinion is prepared for scrutiny in
either system, with every conclusion tied to identified records and
published data. A fuller account of Florida venues is on the
[Florida locations page](/locations/florida/).

## Labor Market Context

Chattahoochee is a small town within the Tallahassee, FL metropolitan
area, and its residents' employment prospects are shaped both
by local opportunities in Gadsden County and by the larger market centered
on Tallahassee. Earning-capacity opinions therefore draw on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Tallahassee
metropolitan area and for the state of Florida, together with state
workforce agency labor market information. Where published estimates leave
a genuine question open, the firm performs direct labor market research
rather than filling the gap with assumption. The metro-wide methodology is
described on the [Tallahassee metro page](/locations/florida/tallahassee/).

## Evaluation Logistics

The firm keeps no office in Chattahoochee and claims none. Remote
evaluation by secure video is the default because it is faster, less
burdensome for the evaluee, and fully adequate for most referral questions.
When in-person evaluation, a home assessment for a care plan, or testimony
is needed, travel is arranged from the Kansas City home office and set
against the case calendar so that report deadlines, depositions, and trial
settings are met without shortcuts. Where the damages picture calls for
economic analysis, the firm arranges
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/):
independent economist partners perform and sign the economic opinions while
the vocational and care-planning components proceed in parallel.
