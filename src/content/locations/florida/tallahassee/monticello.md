usually know their disclosure calendar before they know their expert.
Purinton Analytics takes those dates as the frame for the engagement:
retention, records, evaluation, and the written report are sequenced
backward from the disclosure, deposition, and trial settings the court has

counsel on the plaintiff side or the defense side, in the same form either

## Jefferson County and Its Courts

Monticello lies in Jefferson County, within the Tallahassee, FL
metropolitan area, and state-court cases from the town are heard in the
Circuit Court. Federal matters arising in Florida are distributed among the United
States District Court for the Northern District of Florida, the United
States District Court for the Middle District of Florida, and the United
States District Court for the Southern District of Florida, with the proper
district for any particular case confirmed at conflict check and intake.
Whatever the forum, the report is built to be examined: sources named,
methods stated, and limits acknowledged. Venue coverage across the rest of
the state is collected on the [Florida locations page](/locations/florida/).

## Position in the Regional Labor Market

Monticello is a small Jefferson County town within the Tallahassee, FL
metropolitan area, which means a realistic vocational assessment must weigh
both the character of local employment and the reach of the larger regional
market anchored by Tallahassee. The firm grounds those assessments in
Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for
the Tallahassee metropolitan area and for Florida statewide, in state
workforce agency labor market information, and, where a disputed question
justifies it, in direct research with employers. The methodology applied
across the metro is set out on the
[Tallahassee metro page](/locations/florida/tallahassee/).

## How Evaluations Proceed

Purinton Analytics has no Monticello office and says so plainly; the
practice is remote-first by design. Most interviews and evaluations are
completed by secure video, and when the referral questions require presence,
whether an in-person evaluation in the evaluee's community or testimony
before the trier of fact, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home
office with enough lead time to protect the analysis. When lost earnings or
future care costs must be reduced to economic terms, the firm offers
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis alongside the vocational and care-planning work.
