For a Midway resident, work and home are often in different places: the town
sits in Gadsden County while much of the surrounding employment is oriented
toward Tallahassee, the principal city of the metro. That commuting pattern
is the starting point for any honest earning-capacity analysis, and it is
where Purinton Analytics begins when counsel retain the firm for
[vocational expert](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) or
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) work in a Midway case,
whether the engagement comes from the plaintiff or the defense.

## County and Courthouse Context

Midway matters filed in state court proceed in the Circuit Court serving
Gadsden County. On the federal side, Florida is divided among the United
States District Court for the Northern District of Florida, the United
States District Court for the Middle District of Florida, and the United
States District Court for the Southern District of Florida, and the district
that controls a given filing is identified during the conflict check and
intake. Neither setting changes the work product: the report is written so
that its assumptions are visible, its data sources are cited, and its
conclusions follow from the record rather than from advocacy. Statewide
venue and service details appear on the
[Florida locations page](/locations/florida/).

## A Commuting Position in the Metro

Because Midway is a small town whose workforce participates in the wider
Tallahassee, FL labor market, the firm does not evaluate a Midway evaluee
against town-limits employment alone. Transferable skills analysis and labor
market research consider the occupations realistically available across the
metro, drawing on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and
wage estimates published for the Tallahassee metropolitan area and for
Florida as a whole, along with state workforce agency data. Where a specific
placement question matters, direct contact with employers supplements the
published estimates. How the practice approaches the metro overall is set
out on the [Tallahassee metro page](/locations/florida/tallahassee/).

## Meeting a Midway Evaluee

The firm maintains no office in Midway and publishes no local address,
because accuracy about presence is part of the same discipline that governs
its reports. Most evaluations proceed by secure video, which shortens
timelines and spares evaluees unnecessary travel. When an in-person
interview, a site visit, or courtroom testimony is required, the trip is
scheduled from the Kansas City home office and coordinated with counsel's
disclosure and deposition dates. For cases that also need economic
projections, [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
pair the vocational and care-planning work with independent economist
partners who perform and sign the economic analysis themselves.

## Requesting a Conflict Check

Engagements for Midway cases open the way they do everywhere in the
practice: with a documented conflict check. Send the caption or party names
through the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/), note the court and any
dates already set, and the firm will respond with clearance status and a
proposed scope. Counsel with matters outside Florida can review the
[nationwide practice page](/locations/nationwide/) for how the same
remote-first model serves cases in other states, from small towns like
Midway to major metropolitan venues.
