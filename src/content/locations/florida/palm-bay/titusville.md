## Venue for Titusville cases

State-court matters for Titusville parties are generally filed in the
Circuit Court serving Brevard County. Federal practice in Florida is divided
among the United States District Court for the Northern District of Florida,
the United States District Court for the Middle District of Florida, and the
United States District Court for the Southern District of Florida. The firm
does not guess at which of those forums governs a given dispute; the
controlling venue, and any registration or qualification requirement that
attaches to it, is identified and confirmed during the conflict check and
intake process. What counsel can rely on is that the opinion itself, its
methodology, its sources, and its file, will read the same way in any of
them.

## How an evaluation proceeds

There is no Purinton Analytics office in Titusville, and the firm will not
pretend otherwise; every meeting here is by appointment. Interviews and
testing can occur at counsel's offices, at a neutral location in the
evaluee's own community, or by remote administration when the protocols
allow it. In-person evaluation days, depositions, and trial appearances are
scheduled with travel from the Kansas City home office, and the firm builds
that travel around the operative deadlines rather than asking the case to
wait. The [Florida overview](/locations/florida/) and the
[nationwide page](/locations/nationwide/) describe the same by-appointment
model at the state and national level.

An engagement opens with a documented conflict check and a scope agreed in
writing, followed by a records checklist tailored to the claims. Counsel

disclosure, deposition, or trial dates so the schedule is realistic from
