There is a methodological trap in evaluating an evaluee from a small
community, and Indian Harbour Beach illustrates it well: treat the town
itself as the labor market and the earning capacity opinion collapses under
its first serious question. Indian Harbour Beach is a small city inside
the Palm Bay-Melbourne-Titusville, FL metropolitan area, and
its residents work across that metro. Purinton Analytics builds its
[vocational evaluations and testimony](/services/vocational-expert-witness/)
for local counsel, plaintiff-side and defense-side alike, on that wider
market from the outset.

## A small town in a metro market

Getting the market definition right is the analytical core of these
engagements. The firm draws on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational
employment and wage estimates for the Palm Bay-Melbourne-Titusville
metropolitan area, on statewide Florida estimates, and on state workforce
agency labor market information, adding direct employer research when a
contested occupation requires it. From that data, the report constructs the
evaluee's commutable labor market explicitly, factoring in medical
restrictions, transferable skills, and realistic travel, and it shows the
work. An opinion whose market boundaries are visible can be defended; one
whose boundaries are implicit cannot. The same transparency governs the
occupational side of the analysis: each occupation considered or excluded
is tied to the evaluee's documented restrictions and demonstrated skills,
so the path from record to conclusion can be audited step by step.

## Venue and courthouse context

Indian Harbour Beach is part of Brevard County, and state-court matters
involving its residents and businesses are usually heard in the Circuit
Court serving the county. Florida's federal judiciary is organized into the
United States District Court for the Northern District of Florida, the
United States District Court for the Middle District of Florida, and the
United States District Court for the Southern District of Florida; the firm
confirms during the conflict check and intake which of these forums
controls a given case and whether any venue-specific qualification
requirement follows from it. Regional and statewide venue detail appears on

## How evaluations are scheduled

The firm keeps no premises in Indian Harbour Beach and does not imply any.
Work proceeds by appointment: counsel conferences and records review are
remote, evaluations take place in the evaluee's own community or by remote
administration where the instruments allow, and depositions, trials, and
in-person evaluation days are met with travel scheduled from the Kansas
City home office. Deadlines drive the calendar, and the firm commits to
dates it can actually keep, a small discipline that spares counsel large
problems later.
