Damages in a West Melbourne injury or employment case are seldom the work
of an expert acting alone. Before an economist can project what a claim is
worth, someone must establish, defensibly, what the evaluee can still do,
what work remains realistically open, and what that work pays in the market
the evaluee actually faces. Purinton Analytics supplies that vocational
foundation for West Melbourne counsel on either side of the caption through
[vocational evaluation and expert testimony](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
and it offers
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Vocational findings built to carry weight downstream

The reason that structure matters is not administrative convenience. A
[forensic economic damages](/services/forensic-economic-damages/) model
takes the vocational conclusions as its inputs: residual earning capacity,
the occupations available to the evaluee, and the wages those occupations
command. If the vocational findings are vague, the economic projections
inherit the vagueness, and cross-examination of the economist becomes
cross-examination of the vocational file. The firm therefore writes its
West Melbourne findings so they can be handed to an economist partner, or
to opposing counsel, without translation: each conclusion tied to the
records, the testing, and the labor market data behind it, stated in the
order a careful reader would need them.

## Where Brevard County matters proceed

West Melbourne is a Brevard County municipality, and state-law claims
arising there are ordinarily venued in the Circuit Court serving Brevard
County. Florida's federal trial courts are the United States District Court
for the Northern District of Florida, the United States District Court for
the Middle District of Florida, and the United States District Court for
the Southern District of Florida. Which forum controls a given case, and
whether that forum imposes any registration or qualification requirement on
a testifying expert, is identified and confirmed during the conflict check
and intake process rather than assumed from a map. The report itself is
prepared to a standard intended to hold up in any of them.

## Labor market data behind the vocational layer

Employability and earning capacity findings for a West Melbourne evaluee
rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage
estimates for the Palm Bay-Melbourne-Titusville, FL metropolitan area and
for Florida statewide, supplemented by state workforce agency labor market
information and by direct labor market research when a contested occupation
warrants it. Because the same data ultimately feed the economist partner's
projections in a coordinated engagement, the report records where each
source was used and how it was applied, so the vocational opinion and the
economic model downstream can be audited against a common evidentiary base
instead of drifting apart under scrutiny.
