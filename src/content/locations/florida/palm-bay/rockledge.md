Expert deadlines drive expert engagements, and that is the frame Purinton
Analytics brings to Rockledge referrals. Whether the disclosure date is
months out or uncomfortably close, the first conversation with counsel maps
the report against the scheduling order: what records exist, what testing
is needed, and what can responsibly be delivered by when. Rockledge counsel
on both sides of the docket use the firm for
[expert testimony and litigation consulting](/services/expert-testimony-litigation-consulting/)
and for [rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) of
opposing vocational and life care planning opinions.

## Courts serving Rockledge

Rockledge lies in Brevard County, and its state-court litigation ordinarily
proceeds in the Circuit Court for that county. Federal matters arising in
Florida are heard in the United States District Court for the Northern
District of Florida, the United States District Court for the Middle
District of Florida, or the United States District Court for the Southern
District of Florida, and the district that will actually control a
particular Rockledge case is confirmed as part of the conflict check and
intake, never assumed from geography. Reports are drafted so that either a
state or a federal gatekeeping challenge finds a documented methodology
waiting for it, from data sources through reasoning to the stated
opinions, with the working file organized so production is painless.

## Scheduling around the disclosure calendar

Because there is no local storefront, and the firm says so plainly rather
than implying one, Rockledge work is organized around appointments set to
the case calendar. Records review and counsel conferences happen remotely
without any travel at all. Evaluations are held in the evaluee's community
or conducted by validated remote administration where the referral
questions and instruments permit. When a deposition, trial setting, or
in-person evaluation requires presence, the trip is planned from the Kansas
City home office with enough lead time to protect the deadline it serves.
The [Palm Bay metro page](/locations/florida/palm-bay/) covers the
surrounding communities under the same model.

## Labor market context

Earning capacity conclusions for a Rockledge evaluee are only as strong as
the market data beneath them. The firm relies on Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates for the
Palm Bay-Melbourne-Titusville metropolitan area, parallel estimates for
Florida statewide, and state workforce agency labor market information,
with targeted employer research when a specific occupation or restriction
demands it. Rockledge functions within a metro-wide employment market
rather than a self-contained one, and the report states how the evaluee's
commutable market was bounded so opposing counsel can interrogate the
choice.

## Submitting a conflict check

The engagement sequence is deliberately unexciting: conflict check, written
scope, records checklist, analysis, report. Counsel who also need the
economic side of damages quantified can pair the vocational and
care-planning work with independent economist partners who perform and sign
the economic analysis within one coordinated engagement. Start by
[submitting a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) with the caption or party
names, and include the current scheduling order if one has issued; the
[Florida page](/locations/florida/) and
[nationwide overview](/locations/nationwide/) explain how the practice
handles the rest of the state and the country.
