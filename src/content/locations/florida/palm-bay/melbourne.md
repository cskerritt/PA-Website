Melbourne is one of the larger cities in the Palm Bay-Melbourne-Titusville, FL
metropolitan area and one of the communities that gives the metro its name,
and the case mix that reaches Purinton Analytics from Melbourne counsel
reflects that breadth: personal injury, employment, workers' compensation,
and family law matters that each call for a defensible opinion on what an
evaluee can earn and what their future care will cost. The firm serves both
plaintiff and defense counsel here with
[vocational evaluation and expert testimony](/services/vocational-expert-witness/)
and [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), on the same
methodology it applies across the [Florida practice](/locations/florida/).

## Where Melbourne matters are heard

Melbourne sits in Brevard County, and state-court engagements from local
counsel are most often venued in the Circuit Court there. On the federal
side, Florida litigants may find themselves in the United States District
Court for the Northern District of Florida, the United States District Court
for the Middle District of Florida, or the United States District Court for
the Southern District of Florida; which district controls a particular case
is confirmed during the conflict check and intake rather than assumed. The
written report is prepared to the same standard regardless of forum, so that
it holds up whether the matter settles at mediation or proceeds to trial.

## The labor market behind the findings

Employability and earning capacity opinions for a Melbourne evaluee are
grounded in data for the market where that evaluee actually competes for
work. The analysis draws on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational
employment and wage estimates for the Palm Bay-Melbourne-Titusville
metropolitan area and for Florida statewide, along with state workforce
agency labor market information and direct employer research when the
referral questions require it. As one of the metro's principal employment
centers, Melbourne anchors a labor market that extends well beyond its city
limits, and the report is explicit about how the evaluee's commutable market
was defined.

## Appointments and evaluations

The firm maintains no staffed office in Melbourne and publishes no street
address for this location; candor about presence is part of the same
discipline that governs the reports themselves. Meetings and evaluations are
by appointment, scheduled at counsel's offices, in the evaluee's community,
or remotely when the referral questions and testing protocols permit. When
the engagement requires in-person evaluation, deposition testimony, or trial
appearance, travel is arranged from the Kansas City home office and planned
against the case schedule, not against the firm's convenience. Details on
metro-wide coverage are on the
[Palm Bay metro page](/locations/florida/palm-bay/), and the
[nationwide practice overview](/locations/nationwide/) describes how the
same structure operates in every state.

## Requesting a conflict check

Every Melbourne engagement begins with a documented conflict check, a
defined scope, and a records request tailored to the claims. Where the
damages picture calls for economic modeling, the firm offers
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. To begin, submit the caption or party names through
the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and note any disclosure deadlines,
deposition settings, or trial dates already on the calendar so the timeline
can be built around them.
