Sometimes the first document a Satellite Beach attorney sends is not a
records file but an opposing expert's report. Purinton Analytics is
regularly retained in exactly that posture: to conduct
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) of vocational
and life care planning opinions already served, testing their data sources,
their methodology, and the distance between them. The same standards
govern the firm's affirmative work, and Satellite Beach counsel for
plaintiffs and defendants alike also retain it for
[vocational evaluation and expert testimony](/services/vocational-expert-witness/)
from the outset of a case.

## Reviewing the other side's work

A useful rebuttal is specific. It checks whether the opposing report's
labor market data match the market the evaluee actually faces, whether the
stated restrictions were carried through to the occupational conclusions,
and whether the care plan's line items trace to the medical record. Because
Purinton Analytics writes its own affirmative reports for adversarial
reading, it reviews opposing reports the same way, and the resulting
critique is delivered in a form counsel can use in deposition outlines and
motion practice. Where the critique instead confirms the opposing work is
sound, counsel are told that plainly, because a review that always finds
fault is worth nothing to the lawyer deciding how to try the case.

## Grounding opinions in market data

Whether the task is rebuttal or an original opinion, the underlying data
discipline is identical. Satellite Beach is a small community
whose workers participate in the broader
Palm Bay-Melbourne-Titusville metropolitan labor market, and the firm's
analyses draw on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and
wage estimates for that metropolitan area and for Florida statewide,
supplemented by state workforce agency labor market information and direct
employer research where warranted. Each report, and each critique of an
opposing report, addresses how the commutable labor market was or should
have been defined.
