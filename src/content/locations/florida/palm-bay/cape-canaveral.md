When a Cape Canaveral case involves catastrophic injury, the damages
question turns on what future care the injured person will need and what
that care will cost over a lifetime. Purinton Analytics is built
around answering both questions defensibly. For Cape Canaveral counsel,
whether retained by the injured party or by the defense, the firm prepares
[life care plans](/services/life-care-planning/) tied line by line to the
medical record and [medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/)
priced from documented sources, alongside the vocational opinions that
establish what work, if any, remains open to the evaluee.

## Care planning built for scrutiny

A life care plan is a forecast, and forecasts attract cross-examination.
Each recommendation in the plan is anchored to the treating record or to a
consulting physician's stated opinion, each cost is traceable to its
source, and the narrative explains the bridge between diagnosis and
projected need. Where the record is silent on a future need, the plan says
so and identifies what opinion evidence would close the gap. When counsel also require the plan's costs reduced to
present value or integrated into a lost earnings model, independent
economist partners perform and sign that economic analysis within a
coordinated engagement, so the vocational, care-planning, and economic
strands stay consistent without the firm stepping outside its lane.

## County and district courts

Cape Canaveral sits in Brevard County, and its state-court injury
litigation is ordinarily venued in the Circuit Court serving the county.
Federal cases arising in Florida proceed in the United States District
Court for the Northern District of Florida, the United States District
Court for the Middle District of Florida, or the United States District
Court for the Southern District of Florida. Which district governs, and
what qualification or registration formalities apply there, are questions
the firm resolves during the conflict check and intake for each matter.
