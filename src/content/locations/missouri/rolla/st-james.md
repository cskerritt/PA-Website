St. James is a small Phelps County town, and cases that arise there tend to
reach counsel whose offices sit elsewhere: in Rolla, in the larger Missouri
cities, or out of state entirely. Purinton Analytics is built for exactly
that arrangement. The firm provides vocational expert and life care planning
services for St. James matters on the same remote-first, by-appointment
model it applies [across the country](/locations/nationwide/), for counsel
on either side of the caption, so the location of the injured person and the
location of the litigation never have to be the same place.

## Phelps County venue and the courts that hear St. James cases

State-court matters involving St. James parties are generally heard in the
Circuit Court serving Phelps County, and the firm prepares its vocational
and life care planning opinions to the standard that court's scheduling
orders demand: a defined scope, a documented records basis, and a written
report that survives cross-examination. Missouri's federal forums are the
United States District Court for the Eastern District of Missouri and the
United States District Court for the Western District of Missouri; which of
them controls a given federal filing is confirmed during the conflict check
and intake, along with any venue-specific qualification or disclosure
requirement. Broader Missouri venue questions, including matters filed
outside the county, are addressed on the [Missouri state
page](/locations/missouri/).

## A small town inside the Rolla labor market

Earning capacity for a St. James evaluee cannot be measured against the town
alone. Residents participate in the wider labor market anchored by Rolla,
the area's principal city, and a defensible vocational opinion has to
reflect that commuting reality rather than an artificially narrow local
snapshot. The firm grounds its findings in Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates for the Rolla area and for
Missouri statewide, supplemented by state workforce agency labor market
information and direct employer research where the referral questions
warrant it. Transferable skills analysis and labor market survey work are
scoped to the jobs actually reachable from St. James, and the reasoning is
documented so opposing counsel can trace every step. The metro-level venue
and methodology discussion appears on the [Rolla page](/locations/missouri/rolla/).

## Evaluating a St. James resident

The firm keeps no office in St. James and publishes no local address,
because accuracy about presence is part of the same discipline that governs
its reports. What counsel gets instead is flexible evaluation logistics
matched to the evaluee. Records review, counsel conferences, and most
interviews proceed remotely, which spares a rural evaluee the burden of
traveling to a distant office. When the referral calls for in-person
vocational evaluation, or when a [life care
plan](/services/life-care-planning/) requires direct assessment of the
evaluee's home and care environment, that work is scheduled in the evaluee's
own community, with travel arranged from the Kansas City home office.
Deposition and trial appearances are planned the same way, around the
court's calendar rather than the firm's.
