Purinton Analytics keeps no office in Columbia, and it says so plainly. What
counsel in the Columbia metro get instead is a national practice that treats
Columbia-area venues with the same rigor it brings anywhere else: a documented
conflict check, a defined scope, and a records request tailored to the
claims, followed by [vocational expert witness work](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. Plaintiff and defense referrals are accepted on equal
terms, and each written opinion is built to survive the other side's
scrutiny.

## The Columbia venue footprint

The Columbia, MO metropolitan statistical area comprises Boone County, Cooper
County, and Howard County. The firm supports matters in the Circuit Court
serving each of those counties, including injury and employment litigation
and dissolution matters in which earning capacity is disputed, and it brings
the same method to earning capacity questions that arise in a workers'
compensation or other administrative forum rather than in the trial courts.
When a Columbia-area case is filed federally, the engagement proceeds in the
United States District Court for the Western District of Missouri or the
United States District Court for the Eastern District of Missouri, depending
on where the action is pending. The evaluation method, the sourcing rules,
and the report format do not vary with the forum.

## How meetings and evaluations happen

All Columbia meetings are by appointment. Much of the work moves faster
remotely: records conferences, scoping calls, and pre-deposition preparation
are routinely handled by video, and remote vocational evaluation is available
when the referral questions and testing protocols allow it. Where the case
requires an in-person evaluation, or where a deposition, hearing, or trial
setting requires physical presence, travel is arranged from the Kansas City
home office. Scheduling is set against the operative disclosure calendar at
intake, so counsel know before the engagement begins how the report timeline
fits their deadlines rather than discovering it afterward.

## Grounding opinions in the local labor market

Vocational findings for Columbia-area evaluees rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Columbia
metropolitan area and for Missouri statewide, on state workforce agency labor
market information, and on direct labor market research where the referral
warrants it. The report states which labor market the evaluee can reasonably
reach and why, and it cites the sources behind every employability and
earning capacity conclusion. Care plans follow the same rule: services,
equipment, and supplies are priced in the community where the evaluee lives,
with each cost traceable to a named source that opposing counsel can check.

## Beginning a Columbia engagement

Start with a [conflict check](/refer-a-case/): submit the caption or party
names and flag any imminent disclosure, deposition, or trial dates. Once the
check clears, counsel receive a written scope and a records checklist
tailored to the claims before any analysis begins. Columbia engagements are
part of the firm's [Missouri practice](/locations/missouri/), and counsel
whose dockets extend beyond the state line can review the
[nationwide practice](/locations/nationwide/), which brings the same
engagement structure and the same documentation standards to state and
federal venues across the country.
