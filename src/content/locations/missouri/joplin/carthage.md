## Carthage evaluees and the regional labor market

Employability and earning capacity are questions about an actual market,
and for most Carthage evaluees that market is the Joplin metropolitan
area, with Joplin as its principal city and a commuting economy that
reaches across the state line into Kansas. Vocational findings rest on
Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for
the Joplin area and for Missouri statewide, on state workforce agency
labor market information, and on direct labor market research when a
referral question calls for it. The report pairs that regional picture
with an individualized account of the evaluee: which skills transfer,
which restrictions govern, and what work is realistically within reach
from Carthage, described qualitatively and tied to cited data.

## How evaluations are scheduled

The firm keeps no office in Carthage and publishes no street address for
this location; candor about presence is part of the same discipline that
governs the reports. Engagements typically begin remotely, with records
review, structured interviews, and testing administered under publisher
protocols approved for remote use, which keeps early phases moving
regardless of geography. When the referral questions require in-person
evaluation, it can be conducted in the evaluee's own community, and when
deposition or trial testimony requires presence, travel is arranged from
the Kansas City home office and planned around the court's schedule
rather than the firm's.
