By the time counsel in a Marshfield case reaches for a vocational expert or
life care planner, a disclosure deadline is often already fixed, and the
question is whether a defensible opinion can be built inside it. Purinton
Analytics structures its Marshfield engagements around that calendar:
[vocational evaluation and expert
testimony](/services/vocational-expert-witness/), [life care
planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and [rebuttal and peer
review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) of opposing expert work, delivered
on schedules that respect the docket. The practice accepts referrals from
both sides of the caption, in Marshfield as across the [national
practice](/locations/nationwide/).

## Webster County venue, state and federal

Marshfield sits in Webster County, and state-court matters involving
Marshfield parties are ordinarily litigated in the Circuit Court there.
Federal litigation in Missouri proceeds in the United States District Court
for the Western District of Missouri or the United States District Court for
the Eastern District of Missouri; the district that governs a specific case
is confirmed during the conflict check and intake, and the disclosure
package is then drafted to that court's requirements. Deadlines differ
between forums, which is another reason the venue question is settled at
intake rather than left open. Regional context for the surrounding counties
appears on the [Springfield metro page](/locations/missouri/springfield/).

## Labor market evidence that holds its date

A rushed labor market analysis is easy to attack, so the sequencing is
built in from the first day of the engagement. Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates for the Springfield metropolitan
area and for Missouri statewide anchor the vocational findings for a
Marshfield evaluee, with state workforce agency labor market information
and direct labor market research layered on when the referral requires it.
Marshfield is a smaller community whose workers reach employment across the
metro, and the analysis reflects that commuting reality while staying
strictly qualitative about the region and strictly specific about the
person being evaluated.

## Scheduling the evaluation itself

The firm keeps no staffed office in Marshfield and publishes no local
address; meetings happen by appointment, honestly described. Remote
handling covers many engagements completely, from records review through
deposition testimony. When the case needs an in-person evaluation, it is
set in the evaluee's own community on a date that protects the disclosure
deadline, with travel from the Kansas City home office arranged around the
court's schedule. Counsel sees the scope, the records request, and the
delivery date in writing before the analysis begins.

Because timelines drive these engagements, the sooner the conflict check
runs, the more schedule there is to work with. Submit the caption or party
names through the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and state plainly
which disclosure, deposition, or trial dates are already on the docket.
Intake confirms the referral questions and identifies any venue-specific
qualification requirement for confirmation during the same review. The
[Missouri locations page](/locations/missouri/) describes the statewide
practice for counsel with matters beyond Webster County. Plaintiff or
defense, the process and the product are identical.
