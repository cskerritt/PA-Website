Most of the work behind a credible vocational opinion happens in the
record, not in a conference room. Medical files, employment histories,
educational transcripts, and published labor market data do the heavy
lifting, which is why Purinton Analytics can serve a community like Willard
with full rigor despite having no office there. For Willard counsel the
practice provides [vocational evaluation and
testimony](/services/vocational-expert-witness/), [life care
planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and [forensic economic
damages](/services/forensic-economic-damages/) coordination in which
independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis. Both
plaintiff and defense referrals are accepted, here and throughout the
[nationwide practice](/locations/nationwide/).

## Where Willard cases are heard

Willard is a Greene County community, and its state-court matters
ordinarily proceed in the Circuit Court. On the federal side Missouri is
divided between the United States District Court for the Western District
of Missouri and the United States District Court for the Eastern District
of Missouri, and the practice confirms which district controls a given case
during the conflict check and intake instead of presuming an answer.
Because the written product is prepared to a disclosure-ready standard from
the outset, it moves between forums without rework. The [Springfield metro
page](/locations/missouri/springfield/) lays out the courts across the
region.

## From the record to the labor market

The analytical chain for a Willard evaluee runs from documents to data to
conclusions. The medical record establishes functional capacity; the work
history establishes transferable skills; and Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates for the Springfield metropolitan
area and for Missouri statewide establish the occupational landscape those
skills meet. State workforce agency labor market information and targeted
direct research refine the picture when the referral questions require it.
Willard is a small town whose residents work across the metro, so commuting
range is treated as part of the evidence, not an afterthought. Each link in
that chain is cited, because an opinion is only as strong as the weakest
citation behind it.

## Remote first, in person when it matters

There is no Purinton Analytics storefront in Willard and no published local
address, a fact stated plainly because honesty about presence belongs in
the same discipline as honesty about data. Records-based engagements often
proceed entirely remotely, including deposition and trial testimony where
the court permits. When an in-person evaluation adds real value, it is
scheduled by appointment in the evaluee's own community, with travel from
the Kansas City home office. Counsel receives a defined scope and a records
request tailored to the claims before analysis begins.

The path into an engagement is short: run the conflict check. Send the
caption or party names through the [conflict check
form](/refer-a-case/), flag any disclosure, deposition, or trial dates, and
intake will settle the referral questions and schedule. Any venue-specific
registration or qualification requirement is identified and confirmed
during that intake. The [Missouri locations page](/locations/missouri/)
shows the practice's full in-state reach. Whichever side refers the case,
the record drives the opinion, and the opinion says only what the record
supports.
