litigation in a principal city, and the parties deserve answers of the same
quality. That conviction is why Purinton Analytics accepts Buffalo
referrals on identical terms to its metropolitan work: [vocational
evaluation and expert testimony](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and [medical cost
projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/), available to plaintiff and
defense counsel alike. Buffalo is a small town, but a lost earning capacity
there is measured with the same care the firm brings to any matter in its
[national practice](/locations/nationwide/).

## Dallas County and Missouri's federal districts

A Buffalo caption is a Dallas County caption for venue purposes.
State-court matters involving Buffalo parties ordinarily proceed in
the Circuit Court, while a case with federal jurisdiction may be heard in
the United States District Court for the Western District of Missouri or
the United States District Court for the Eastern District of Missouri. The
practice does not announce a controlling district in advance; it confirms
the venue during the conflict check and intake and prepares its disclosure
materials for that court. How Dallas County relates to the neighboring
counties of the region is described on the [Springfield metro
page](/locations/missouri/springfield/).

## Small-town evaluees, metro-scale data

A Buffalo evaluee's employment options are not confined to the town
itself; residents draw on the wider labor market of the Springfield
metropolitan area, and the analysis measures that full reach. Bureau of
Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the
Springfield metropolitan area and for Missouri statewide form the published
base, supplemented by state workforce agency labor market information and
direct labor market research when the referral questions require it. The
report never generalizes about rural economies; it documents what this
person, with this history and these medically supported limitations, can
still do within a realistic commuting range, and it shows its sources.

## Bringing the evaluation to Buffalo

The firm has no office in Buffalo and publishes no address there, and it
says so rather than leaving the impression of local presence. What counsel
gets instead is flexibility: engagements that proceed remotely from records
review through testimony where the case permits, and in-person evaluations
scheduled by appointment in the evaluee's own community when the referral
warrants it, with travel arranged from the Kansas City home office. Scope,
records, and schedule are all fixed in writing before analysis starts.

## First step: the conflict check

A Buffalo engagement opens the same way every engagement opens, with a
documented conflict check. Submit the caption or party names through the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and note any disclosure, deposition,
or trial dates on the docket. Intake then defines the referral questions
and the records request, and any venue-specific qualification requirement
is identified and confirmed in the process. Counsel with matters across
the state can consult the [Missouri locations
page](/locations/missouri/). Whether the referral comes from the plaintiff
or the defense, the analysis follows the same method and the report says
the same thing.
