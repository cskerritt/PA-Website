## Christian County venue for a small town

Clever is a small Christian County town, and its state-court matters
ordinarily proceed in the Circuit Court. Where federal jurisdiction
exists, Missouri's forums are the United States District Court for the
Western District of Missouri and the United States District Court for the
Eastern District of Missouri; the district controlling a specific Clever
case is confirmed during the conflict check and intake rather than
presumed. Because the engagement is structured for disclosure from the
first day, the resulting report meets the expectations of state and
federal practice without revision. The [Springfield metro
page](/locations/missouri/springfield/) collects the regional venue
detail.

## Grounding the analysis in the metro market

Clever's residents work within the labor market of the Springfield
metropolitan area, and the vocational analysis is built to that scale
rather than to the town limits. Its published foundation is the Bureau of
Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the
Springfield metropolitan area and for Missouri statewide; state workforce
agency labor market information and, where the referral questions require
it, direct labor market research complete the picture. On that foundation
the report evaluates one person: transferable skills from the actual work
history, functional capacity as the medical record documents it, and the
occupations that remain within realistic commuting reach, each conclusion
carrying its citation. Nothing in the analysis depends on impressions of
the town; everything depends on the record and the published data behind
it.

## Logistics without pretense

No Purinton Analytics office exists in Clever, and the firm publishes no
address suggesting otherwise; stating that plainly is part of the same
discipline the reports are held to. Engagements are frequently completed
remotely, from records review through testimony where the court allows.
When the referral is better served by an in-person evaluation, the
appointment is made in the evaluee's own community, with travel scheduled
from the Kansas City home office and coordinated with the deadlines
already on the case.

## Opening a Clever engagement

The gate into the engagement is the conflict check. Counsel should send
the caption or party names through the [conflict check
form](/refer-a-case/), together with any disclosure, deposition, or trial
dates already scheduled. Intake follows immediately: referral questions
defined, records requested, schedule set, and any venue-specific
registration or qualification requirement identified and confirmed. The
[Missouri locations page](/locations/missouri/) describes the practice's
coverage across the rest of the state. Plaintiff and defense referrals
pass through the identical structure and receive the identical work
product, which is what an independent opinion means in practice.
