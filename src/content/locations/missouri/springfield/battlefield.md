## Venue for Battlefield parties

Battlefield lies within Greene County, and state-court matters involving
its residents and businesses ordinarily proceed in the Circuit Court.
Missouri's federal trial forums are the United States District Court for
the Western District of Missouri and the United States District Court for
the Eastern District of Missouri; which of them governs any particular
case is a question the practice answers during the conflict check and
intake, not one it assumes from a map. Reports are written so that the same
opinion, with the same support, satisfies the disclosure conventions of
either system. Counsel can review the surrounding regional venues on the
[Springfield metro page](/locations/missouri/springfield/).

## The employment picture for a Battlefield evaluee

Because Battlefield is a small suburb, its residents' realistic employment
options extend across the metro anchored by Springfield, and the vocational
analysis is scoped accordingly. Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational
employment and wage estimates for the Springfield metropolitan area and for
Missouri statewide supply the published base; state workforce agency labor
market information adds detail; and direct labor market research is used
where the referral questions justify it. What emerges is not a commentary
on the local economy but a specific, sourced account of what one person
with one documented set of limitations can still do, and where. When the
opposing side retains its own vocational expert, the same sourcing makes
the differences between opinions easy for the court to locate and weigh.

## Presence, stated accurately

Purinton Analytics staffs no office in Battlefield and lists no address for
the town. That is stated outright because a report's credibility begins
with the accuracy of everything around it, including the letterhead. Most
engagement phases can proceed remotely; where the referral calls for an
in-person evaluation or where testimony requires presence, appointments are
set in the evaluee's own community or at the courthouse, with travel
planned from the Kansas City home office around the litigation calendar.
