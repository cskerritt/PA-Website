Republic's economic life is bound up with the commuting market that
surrounds Springfield, and that connection is where a vocational analysis of
a Republic evaluee has to start. Whether a person who lives in Republic can
return to suitable work depends less on the town's own employer base than on
the occupations reachable across the metro, and Purinton Analytics builds
its opinions around that commuting-market reality. The firm offers
[vocational evaluation and testimony](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and [coordinated
damages assessment](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/), in which
independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis while
the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement
structure, for counsel on either side of the caption here and throughout the
[national practice](/locations/nationwide/).

## Greene County venue for Republic parties

Republic lies in Greene County, and state-court matters involving Republic
parties are ordinarily heard in the Circuit Court there. Federal claims may
be filed in the United States District Court for the Western District of
Missouri or the United States District Court for the Eastern District of
Missouri; the controlling district for any particular case is confirmed
during the conflict check and intake, never assumed from geography alone.
Because the firm writes every report to a disclosure-ready standard, the
same work product serves either court system. The [Springfield metro
page](/locations/missouri/springfield/) covers the regional venue footprint
in more detail.

## Measuring work capacity across the metro

The vocational method for a Republic case is deliberately conservative.
Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for
the Springfield metropolitan area and for Missouri statewide supply the
published foundation; state workforce agency labor market information and
direct employer research fill in where the referral questions demand more
resolution. Against that foundation the analysis places the individual:
work history, transferable skills, and the functional limitations the
medical record supports. The result is a set of occupational conclusions
that either side can probe, because each one carries its source with it.

## Where and how evaluations happen

Purinton Analytics keeps no staffed location in Republic and lists no street
address for the town, a policy of candor that extends to every location the
practice serves. A substantial share of engagements is completed remotely,
from records review through deposition. When the referral requires meeting
the evaluee in person, the evaluation is scheduled by appointment in the
evaluee's own community, with travel from the Kansas City home office
planned around the case calendar rather than the other way around.

## The conflict check comes first

No Republic engagement begins until a documented conflict check clears.
Counsel should submit the caption or party names through the [conflict check
form](/refer-a-case/) and identify any disclosure, deposition, or trial
dates already fixed. Intake then settles the referral questions, the records
request, and the schedule, and any venue-specific qualification requirement
is identified and confirmed in that same review. The [Missouri locations
page](/locations/missouri/) shows how Republic fits within the practice's
statewide coverage. Both plaintiff and defense referrals get the same
sources, the same method, and the same written product, which is the entire
point of retaining an independent expert.
