Venue shapes an expert engagement before any evaluation begins, and for
Ozark that venue is Christian County. State-court litigation involving Ozark
parties ordinarily runs through the Circuit Court there, so the written
work product is prepared with that courthouse context in view from the
outset. The firm supplies [vocational expert witness
work](/services/vocational-expert-witness/), [life care
planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/) to counsel on
both sides of the caption, in this community and across the [national
practice](/locations/nationwide/).

## From the Circuit Court to the federal districts

Beyond the Circuit Court, Missouri cases may proceed in the United States
District Court for the Eastern District of Missouri or the United States
District Court for the Western District of Missouri. The practice does not
guess at which forum controls a given Ozark matter; the answer belongs to
the conflict check and intake, where the caption, the parties, and the
posture of the case are reviewed together. Reports are drafted to satisfy
the disclosure expectations of state and federal practice alike, so a case
that removes or remands does not need its expert work redone. The
[Springfield metro page](/locations/missouri/springfield/) sets out how the
surrounding venues fit together.

## What the labor market analysis draws on

Ozark is a Christian County community whose workers participate in the
employment market anchored by Springfield, the metro's principal city. Vocational
findings for an Ozark evaluee are grounded in Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates for the Springfield metropolitan
area and for Missouri statewide, together with state workforce agency labor
market information. Where the referral questions require it, direct labor
market research supplements the published estimates. The analysis never
trades in generalities about the region; it addresses one person's
transferable skills, documented restrictions, and realistic occupational
options, each tied to a source opposing counsel can check.

## Meetings by appointment, not a storefront

There is no Purinton Analytics office in Ozark, and the firm will not
pretend otherwise by publishing an address it does not staff. Candor on that
point is deliberate: an expert whose marketing overstates presence invites
the same skepticism toward the opinions. Engagements frequently proceed
remotely end to end. If the case calls for an in-person evaluation or
courtroom testimony, the meeting is set in the evaluee's community or at the
courthouse, with travel arranged from the Kansas City home office and
scheduled around the deadlines counsel already faces.

## Opening the file

An Ozark engagement opens with a documented conflict check. Send the caption
or the party names through the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/), note
any imminent disclosure or deposition dates, and intake will define the
scope, the records request, and the reporting schedule from there. Any
registration or qualification requirement particular to the venue is
identified and confirmed during that intake review. Counsel weighing a
statewide strategy can consult the [Missouri locations
page](/locations/missouri/) for the practice's full in-state coverage.
Plaintiff and defense referrals move through the same process and receive
the same methodology, which is what makes the resulting opinion useful under
cross-examination.
