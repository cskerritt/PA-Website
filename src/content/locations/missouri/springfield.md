Counsel in the Springfield metro engage Purinton Analytics for
[vocational expert witness work](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)

while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement

every report is drafted on the assumption that opposing counsel will read it
closely. Springfield matters run through the firm's broader
[Missouri practice](/locations/missouri/), with the same intake discipline,
the same methodology, and the same documentation standards counsel see
statewide.

## Counties and courts served

The Springfield, MO metropolitan statistical area takes in Greene County,
Christian County, Webster County, Polk County, and Dallas County. The firm
accepts engagements for matters pending in the Circuit Court serving each of
those counties, where injury, employment, and workers' compensation disputes
raise questions of employability, earning capacity, and the cost of future
care. Federal matters are supported as well, whether filed in the United
States District Court for the Western District of Missouri or the United
States District Court for the Eastern District of Missouri. Venue does not
change the method: the same evaluation protocols, the same sourcing rules,
and the same reporting standards apply in every courtroom, so a report
prepared for a Greene County jury reads the way a report prepared for a
federal bench trial reads.

## By-appointment presence, stated plainly

Purinton Analytics keeps no staffed office in Springfield and publishes no
street address for this location, because accuracy about offices belongs to
the same discipline that governs the firm's written opinions. Meetings are by
appointment. Records conferences, attorney consultations, and pre-deposition
preparation ordinarily proceed by video or phone, and remote vocational
evaluation is available when the referral questions and the testing protocols
permit it. When the matter calls for in-person evaluation, or when deposition
and trial settings require presence, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City
home office and coordinated against the operative disclosure calendar, so
that court dates control the timeline rather than the other way around.

## Labor market research for Springfield evaluees

Employability and earning capacity findings for evaluees in this market rest
on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for
the Springfield metropolitan area and for Missouri statewide, on state
workforce agency labor market information, and on direct labor market
research where the referral warrants it. The report identifies the evaluee's
commutable labor market and explains why it is defined that way, so the
foundation for every vocational conclusion is visible on the page rather than
asserted. Life care planning follows the same practice, pricing services and
supplies in the community where the evaluee actually lives and documenting
each source so the plan can be tested line by line.

## Starting a Springfield engagement

Every engagement begins with a conflict check. Submit the caption or party
names through the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and note any imminent
disclosure, deposition, or trial dates so scheduling can be confirmed against
them. Once the check clears, counsel receive a defined scope, a fee
agreement, and a records request tailored to the claims. Counsel with related
matters outside Missouri can review the firm's
[nationwide practice](/locations/nationwide/), which serves state and federal
venues across the country under the same engagement structure and the same
standards of documentation.
