Circuit Court, which applies Missouri's established sequence of expert

of Missouri and the United States District Court for the Western District of
Missouri; which district governs a particular Waynesville matter, and what
that court asks of a testifying expert by way of registration or

assumed. Whatever the courtroom, the file is assembled the same way: a
