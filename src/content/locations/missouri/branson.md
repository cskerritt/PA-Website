Every report Purinton Analytics prepares for a Taney County matter is written
to be read by the opposing side. That standard, rather than any local
storefront, is what the firm offers Branson counsel: opinions on
employability, earning capacity, and the cost of future care whose methods
are stated, whose sources are cited, and whose conclusions can be traced line
by line. The work spans
[vocational evaluation and expert testimony](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. Referrals come from plaintiff and defense counsel, and
both are served on the same terms.

## Taney County and its courts

The Branson, MO micropolitan statistical area consists of Taney County. The
firm accepts engagements for matters pending in the Circuit Court serving
Taney County, where injury, employment, and workers' compensation litigation
turns on what an evaluee can still do and earn. Federal matters from the
area are supported in the United States District Court for the Western
District of Missouri and the United States District Court for the Eastern
District of Missouri. The forum sets the deadlines and the disclosure
requirements; it does not change the evaluation protocols or the way the
written opinion is built.

## Appointments in place of an office

Purinton Analytics maintains no staffed office in Branson and publishes no
street address for this location. Saying so directly is part of the same
habit of accuracy that governs the reports. Meetings are by appointment, and
the engagement is designed to run efficiently at a distance: scoping calls,
records conferences, and pre-deposition preparation proceed by video or
phone, and remote vocational evaluation is available when the referral
questions and testing protocols permit it. Where in-person evaluation is
warranted, it can take place in the evaluee's own community, with travel
scheduled from the Kansas City home office and set against the operative
disclosure calendar from the outset of the engagement.

## The labor market behind the opinion

Vocational conclusions for Branson-area evaluees rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the local area and
for Missouri statewide, on state workforce agency labor market information,
and on direct labor market research where the referral warrants it. The
written opinion identifies the evaluee's commutable labor market and explains
the basis for that definition, so the geographic reach of the employability
analysis is documented rather than assumed. Life care planning applies the
same sourcing discipline, pricing services, supplies, and equipment in the
community where the evaluee actually lives and recording where each figure
came from.

## Starting a Branson engagement

The engagement opens with a conflict check. Submit the caption or party
names through the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and identify any
imminent disclosure, deposition, or trial dates so scheduling can be
confirmed around them. Once the check clears, counsel receive a defined
scope and a records request tailored to the claims. Branson matters are
handled within the firm's [Missouri practice](/locations/missouri/), and the
[nationwide practice](/locations/nationwide/) offers the same structure to
counsel with cases in any other state or federal venue.
