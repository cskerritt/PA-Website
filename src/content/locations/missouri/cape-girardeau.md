Counsel with a matter pending in the Circuit Court serving Cape Girardeau
County or Bollinger County, or in a federal courtroom in Missouri, retain
Purinton Analytics when employability, earning capacity, or the cost of
future care is in dispute. The firm provides
[vocational evaluation and expert testimony](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. Plaintiff and defense referrals are taken on identical
terms, and every opinion is written for the reader most motivated to find
its flaws: opposing counsel.

## The venue map, including Illinois

The Cape Girardeau, MO-IL metropolitan statistical area comprises Cape
Girardeau County and Bollinger County in Missouri and Alexander County,
Illinois. Missouri matters proceed in the Circuit Court serving each county
or, when filed federally, in the United States District Court for the Eastern
District of Missouri or the United States District Court for the Western
District of Missouri. Engagements arising from Alexander County, Illinois are
accepted as well; the courts that serve that county, along with any
venue-specific registration or qualification requirement, are identified and
confirmed during the conflict check and intake process. The analysis itself
does not change with the forum.

## Local logistics without a local office

Purinton Analytics has no staffed office in Cape Girardeau and publishes no
street address here. That candor is deliberate: a firm whose reports depend
on verifiable sourcing should describe its own footprint just as accurately.
Meetings are by appointment. Scoping calls, records conferences, and
pre-deposition preparation typically run by video or phone, and remote
vocational evaluation is available when the referral questions and testing
protocols permit it. When in-person evaluation is warranted, or when
depositions and trial settings require attendance, travel is scheduled from
the Kansas City home office and planned against the disclosure deadlines
identified at intake.

## Data behind the vocational opinion

Vocational findings for evaluees in this metro rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Cape Girardeau
metropolitan area, on statewide estimates for Missouri and for Illinois where
the evaluee's market crosses the state line, on state workforce agency labor
market information, and on direct labor market research where warranted. The
written opinion defines the evaluee's commutable labor market and explains
the definition, so the geographic scope of the employability analysis is a
documented choice rather than an assumption. Life care planning follows the
same rule, pricing services and equipment in the community where the evaluee
actually lives.

Send the caption or party names through the

scope and a records checklist tailored to the claims. Cape Girardeau
engagements are part of the firm's [Missouri practice](/locations/missouri/),
and the [nationwide practice](/locations/nationwide/) extends the same
structure and standards to counsel litigating anywhere else in the country.
