letterhead. Depending on the questions presented, that work is a
[vocational evaluation with expert testimony](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
a [life care plan](/services/life-care-planning/) or
[medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/), a

expert's file, or a
[coordinated damages engagement](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. Purinton Analytics offers each of these to plaintiff
and defense counsel in the Rolla area on the same terms it applies across
[Missouri](/locations/missouri/) and its
[national practice](/locations/nationwide/).

## Phelps County venue context

Rolla area engagements are anchored in Phelps County. The firm accepts
matters pending in the Circuit Court of Phelps County and in the federal
courts, whether the case sits in the United States District Court for the
Eastern District of Missouri or the United States District Court for the
Western District of Missouri. Because state and federal forums carry
different disclosure formats and deadlines, the scope of work and the report
schedule are set against the operative rules and scheduling order at intake,
and any venue-specific registration or qualification requirement is
identified and confirmed during the conflict check and intake process.

## Meetings by appointment, not a local storefront

The firm does not keep a staffed office in Rolla and does not publish a
local street address, because a practice that asks courts to rely on the
accuracy of its reports should be just as accurate about its own footprint.
Rolla counsel work with the firm the way most of its national clients do:
secure records transfer, conferences by phone or video for intake and
pre-deposition preparation, and remote evaluation where the referral
questions and testing protocols permit. When the engagement calls for
in-person evaluation, or when depositions, hearings, and trial settings
require presence in Phelps County, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City
home office and planned well ahead of the dates that matter.

## Data behind the vocational findings

Opinions about employability and earning capacity for Rolla area evaluees
are grounded in sources the report names. The analysis draws on Bureau of
Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the relevant
area and for Missouri statewide, state workforce agency labor market
information, and direct labor market research where the referral warrants
it. The written opinion explains how the evaluee's commutable labor market
was defined and connects each finding to its source, so that
cross-examination can proceed on the record the report itself provides.
Life care planning and cost projection work follows the same rule: every
item sourced, every price documented, no figure resting on assertion alone.

## Opening a Rolla engagement

Substantive work begins only after a documented conflict check. To start,
[request a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) with the case caption and party
names, and note any imminent disclosure, deposition, mediation, or trial
dates so the timeline can be assessed honestly at intake. Once conflicts
clear, counsel receive a records checklist tailored to the claims, a defined
scope, and a committed report schedule. Whether the opinion is ultimately
examined in the Circuit Court of Phelps County or in federal court, it is
written for both sides of the caption to read.
