A micropolitan venue receives the same file the largest metros receive. That
is the premise of Purinton Analytics' work in the Farmington area: counsel in
St. Francois County get the identical intake discipline, evaluation
protocols, and reporting standards the firm brings to the biggest markets in
the country, not a lighter version scaled to the size of the town. The
practice covers
[vocational evaluation and expert testimony](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure, for plaintiff and defense counsel alike.

## St. Francois County venues

The Farmington, MO micropolitan statistical area consists of St. Francois
County. The firm accepts engagements for matters pending in the Circuit
Court serving St. Francois County, in injury, employment, workers'
compensation, and family law disputes where employability, earning capacity,
or the cost of future care is contested. Matters filed federally are
supported in the United States District Court for the Eastern District of
Missouri and the United States District Court for the Western District of
Missouri. A compact venue map does not simplify the work: the report is
sourced, structured, and written to the same standard regardless of the
courtroom it is headed for.

## Serving Farmington by appointment

The firm has no staffed office in Farmington and publishes no street address
for this location, because describing its own presence accurately is part of
the discipline counsel are engaging. Meetings are by appointment. Records
conferences, scoping discussions, and pre-deposition preparation are
typically handled by video or phone, and remote vocational evaluation is
available when the referral questions and testing protocols permit it. When
the matter warrants an in-person evaluation, the evaluation can be conducted
in the evaluee's own community, and travel is scheduled from the Kansas City
home office against the disclosure calendar established at intake, so
deadlines drive the plan from the outset.

## Labor market analysis for a smaller market

Assessing employability in a smaller labor market demands more care, not
less, and the sourcing shows it. Vocational findings for Farmington-area
evaluees are grounded in Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment
and wage estimates for the Farmington area and for Missouri statewide, in
state workforce agency labor market information, and in direct labor market
research where the referral warrants it. The written opinion defines the
evaluee's commutable labor market explicitly and explains why it is drawn
where it is, so a smaller market's realities are documented rather than
assumed away. Life care plans price care and equipment in the community where
the evaluee actually lives, with each figure tied to a named source.

tailored to the claims. Farmington engagements proceed within the firm's
[Missouri practice](/locations/missouri/), and counsel can review the
[nationwide practice](/locations/nationwide/) for matters beyond the state,
