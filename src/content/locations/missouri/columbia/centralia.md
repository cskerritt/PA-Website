time for counsel to digest it before it must be served. The firm works this
way for retaining plaintiffs' lawyers and defense lawyers alike, because a
report produced in a rush reads like one, and opposing counsel will say so.

## Where a Centralia case is heard

Centralia lies in Boone County, and civil claims arising in the town proceed
in state court through the Circuit Court, with Missouri's expert disclosure
and deposition practice setting the pretrial rhythm that the firm's
scheduling is built around. Federal claims have their own forums: the United
States District Court for the Eastern District of Missouri and the United
States District Court for the Western District of Missouri are Missouri's
district courts, and the venue that actually controls a given matter, along
with any expert registration or qualification requirement it imposes, is
confirmed during the conflict check and intake rather than assumed. Court by
court, the working method never changes: a documented conflict check, a
defined written scope, and a records request tailored to the claims come
before any opinion work begins.
