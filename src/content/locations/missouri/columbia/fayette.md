An honest earning-capacity opinion for a Fayette evaluee begins by admitting
what a small Howard County town can and cannot offer on its own. The local
job base is limited, the surrounding Columbia metropolitan economy is not,
and the analytical question is how far this particular evaluee can
realistically reach into that larger market given age, skills, restrictions,
and transportation. Purinton Analytics frames its Fayette
[vocational expert witness](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) work around
that question, and pairs it with
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) when future care needs
are in dispute. Engagements are taken from plaintiff counsel and defense
counsel on the same terms, with the same written product.

## Howard County courts and the federal alternatives

Before the market question is answered, the forum is settled. Fayette
matters filed in Missouri state court proceed in the Circuit Court serving
Howard County, under the state's expert disclosure and deposition practice.
When federal jurisdiction applies, Missouri offers its litigants the United
States District Court for the Western District of Missouri and the United
States District Court for the Eastern District of Missouri; the district
with venue over a specific Fayette case, and any registration or
qualification that court requires of a testifying expert, is identified and
confirmed during the conflict check and intake. The forum changes the
caption, not the craft. Every engagement starts from a documented conflict
check, a written scope, and a claims-tailored records request, so the final
opinion rests on materials both sides can test.

## Grounding the market analysis in evidence

With the forum fixed, the firm resolves the reach-of-market question with
data rather than assumption. Vocational findings for Fayette evaluees rest
on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for
the Columbia metropolitan area, where Columbia is the principal city, and
for Missouri statewide, supplemented by state workforce agency labor market
information and by direct labor market research when the referral questions
warrant it. The report states its market definition openly, treats commuting
range as a finding to be supported rather than a premise to be assumed, and
draws its transferable-skills conclusions from that documented regional
picture. The [Columbia metro page](/locations/missouri/columbia/) describes
the regional economy these town-level analyses sit within.
