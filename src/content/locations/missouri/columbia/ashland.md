For counsel with an injured client in Ashland, the practical questions come
first: where the evaluation will take place, whether the evaluee must travel
for it, and how quickly a signed report can be in hand. Purinton Analytics
builds its Ashland engagements around those logistics, offering
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) and
[vocational expert witness](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) evaluations
by secure video or in person in the evaluee's own community, on a schedule
set by the case rather than by the expert. Plaintiff and defense referrals
are accepted on identical terms, and every deliverable is written to be read
critically by the lawyer on the other side.

## Boone County courts and the forums beyond them

Ashland is a Boone County community, and state-court civil actions arising
there proceed in the Circuit Court under Missouri's expert disclosure and
deposition rules. For claims that belong in federal court, the available
Missouri forums are the United States District Court for the Western District
of Missouri and the United States District Court for the Eastern District of
Missouri; which district governs a particular Ashland matter, and what
qualification or registration that court requires of a testifying expert, is
identified and confirmed during the conflict check and intake. Whatever the
forum, the file is assembled the same way: documented conflict check, written
scope, and a records request matched to the claims, producing an opinion that
rests on evidence both sides can examine.

## A small town inside a larger employment market

Ashland itself is a small community, and its working residents typically
reach into the wider Columbia metropolitan economy, where Columbia serves as
the principal city. An earning-capacity analysis that ignored that commuting
reality would understate or misstate the evaluee's options, so the firm
defines the relevant market explicitly and supports it with Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Columbia
metropolitan area and for Missouri statewide, along with state workforce
agency labor market information and direct labor market research where
warranted. The [Columbia metro page](/locations/missouri/columbia/) gathers
the regional context that frames these town-level engagements.

## How evaluations actually get scheduled

There is no Purinton Analytics office in Ashland, and the firm publishes no
address there; candor about presence is part of the same discipline that
governs its reports. Remote evaluation by secure video is often the fastest
path to a completed report. When the referral questions require an in-person
evaluation, or when a deposition or trial date demands attendance, travel is
arranged from the Kansas City home office and coordinated with counsel's
calendar at intake. If economic loss calculations are needed alongside the
vocational and care-planning work, the firm arranges
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. Venue information for the rest of the state appears on
the [Missouri state page](/locations/missouri/), and the firm's national
footprint is described in the [nationwide overview](/locations/nationwide/).
