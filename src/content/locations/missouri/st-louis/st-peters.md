For an injured or displaced worker living in St. Peters, the evaluation
itself is often the most practical question counsel has to solve: where it
happens, how long it takes, and whether the evaluee must travel to be
seen. Purinton Analytics designs each St. Peters engagement around the
evaluee, pairing remote-capable assessment with in-community evaluation
when the referral questions require it. The firm serves plaintiff and
defense counsel with
[vocational expert work](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/),
[medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/), and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic

engagement structure.

## How a St. Peters evaluation proceeds

Most files open remotely: a documented conflict check, a defined scope,
and a records request tailored to the claims, followed by structured
interviews and standardized testing administered under publisher protocols
that support remote administration. When an in-person evaluation better
serves the questions presented, it is conducted in the evaluee's own
community, and travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office, the
firm's only office. There is no St. Peters location and no published local
address; every community outside Kansas City is served by appointment, and
the practice says so plainly because candor about logistics belongs to the
same discipline that governs its opinions.

## County and courts

St. Peters lies within St. Charles County, and state-court matters
involving St. Peters parties are heard in the Circuit Court of St. Charles
County. On the federal side, the United States District Court for the
Eastern District of Missouri and the United States District Court for the
Western District of Missouri are the forums available in the state, and
the district controlling a given caption is identified during the conflict
check and intake. Forum selection drives deadlines and disclosure format;
the substance of the file, from records through testing to published data,
is prepared to the same standard in either system so that opposing counsel
can examine every step.

## The market beyond the municipal line

An opinion about what a St. Peters evaluee can earn must look past the
city limits to the commuting market of the surrounding metropolitan area,
whose principal city is St. Louis. The firm grounds those findings in
Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for
the St. Louis metropolitan area and for Missouri statewide, in state
workforce agency labor market information, and in direct labor market
research where warranted. Regional conditions are described qualitatively;
the evaluee's transferable skills, functional restrictions, and realistic
placement prospects are documented individually, because that is the level
at which cross-examination happens.
