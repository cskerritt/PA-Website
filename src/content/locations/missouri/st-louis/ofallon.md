## The labor market an O'Fallon evaluee faces

A large suburb does not have a sealed labor market. An O'Fallon evaluee
competes across a commuting region anchored by St. Louis, the metro's
principal city, and a credible vocational opinion has to describe that
region as the evaluee actually experiences it. Findings rest on Bureau of
Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the
St. Louis metropolitan area and for Missouri statewide, supplemented by
state workforce agency labor market information and direct labor market
research where the referral questions call for it. The regional framing
stays qualitative; the individual analysis stays specific, documenting
transferable skills, restrictions, and realistic placement prospects for
the person rather than reciting averages.
