The economic question at the center of most Florissant referrals is a
labor market question: what work remains open to this person, in the
market this person actually inhabits. That market is the commuting
economy of the greater metropolitan area anchored by St. Louis, and
answering the question responsibly requires published data, individual
assessment, and a method that survives cross-examination. Purinton
Analytics supplies that answer for plaintiff and defense counsel through
[vocational evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[expert testimony and litigation consulting](/services/expert-testimony-litigation-consulting/),
and through
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Grounding the Florissant labor market analysis

Vocational findings for a Florissant evaluee rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the St. Louis
metropolitan area and for Missouri statewide, on state workforce agency
labor market information, and on direct labor market research when the
referral questions justify it. The regional picture is kept qualitative,
as it should be; the individual picture is kept specific. Transferable
skills are inventoried, restrictions are tied to the medical record, and
placement prospects are stated for occupations the evaluee can realistically
access, so that neither side has to argue with an average.

## Courts serving Florissant

Florissant is part of St. Louis County, and state-court actions involving
Florissant parties proceed in the Circuit Court of St. Louis County. In
the federal system, Missouri's available forums are the United States
District Court for the Eastern District of Missouri and the United States
District Court for the Western District of Missouri; which district
governs a particular matter is confirmed during the conflict check and
intake. The firm prepares the same traceable file for either system,
because the audience is the same in both: opposing counsel reading every
line for a weakness.
