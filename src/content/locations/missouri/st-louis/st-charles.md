## Venue for St. Charles parties

The city shares its name with its county: St. Charles sits in St. Charles
County, and state-court litigation involving its residents and employers
proceeds in the Circuit Court there. Federal matters may be heard in the
United States District Court for the Eastern District of Missouri or the
United States District Court for the Western District of Missouri, the
state's available federal forums, with the controlling district confirmed
during the conflict check and intake. Whatever the venue, the working
standard is identical: a written opinion that opposing counsel can probe
line by line, with every source identified and every method disclosed.

## Reading the market around St. Charles

Vocational conclusions about a St. Charles evaluee are grounded in the
commuting economy of the wider metropolitan area, in which St. Louis is
the principal city, not in the boundaries of any single municipality. The
firm relies on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage
estimates for the St. Louis metropolitan area and for Missouri statewide,
alongside state workforce agency labor market information, and it conducts
direct labor market research when the referral questions warrant it. The
resulting opinion pairs a qualitative account of the regional market with
an individualized account of the evaluee: what skills transfer, what
restrictions apply, and what work is realistically available.

## Appointments, not an address

There is no Purinton Analytics office in St. Charles and no published
street address for this location; the practice operates from a single home
office in Kansas City and serves every other community by appointment.
Stating that plainly is deliberate, because a firm that certifies the
accuracy of its reports should be just as exact about its own presence.
Most engagements begin remotely, with records analysis, structured
interviews, and testing administered under publisher protocols approved
for remote use. Where the questions presented require evaluation in the
evaluee's own community, or where deposition and trial appearances demand
it, travel is arranged from the Kansas City home office.
