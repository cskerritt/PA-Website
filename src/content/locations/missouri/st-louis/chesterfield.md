## Scheduling around the forum

The forum sets the calendar. Chesterfield sits in St. Louis County, so
state-court matters proceed in the Circuit Court of St. Louis County on
that court's schedule. Federal matters may be filed in the United States
District Court for the Eastern District of Missouri or the United States
District Court for the Western District of Missouri, Missouri's available
federal forums, each with its own disclosure regime; the controlling
district for a given caption is confirmed during the conflict check and
intake. Intake therefore begins by mapping the operative dates, so the
records request, evaluation, and report drafting land with time for
counsel to prepare, not at the deadline itself.

## What the report contains

Whatever the timeline, the content does not get thinner. A Chesterfield
opinion is built from the medical and employment record, structured
interviews, standardized testing, and published sources, with vocational
findings grounded in Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment
and wage estimates for the St. Louis metropolitan area, whose principal
city is St. Louis, and for Missouri statewide, along with state workforce
agency labor market information and direct labor market research where
warranted. The evaluee is analyzed as an individual moving through a
metro-wide commuting market: transferable skills, documented restrictions,
and realistic placement prospects, stated qualitatively about the region
and precisely about the person.
