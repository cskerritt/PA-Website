pretend otherwise. The practice operates from a single home office in
Kansas City, publishes no street address for any other location, and
serves Wildwood entirely by appointment. That candor is the right place to
begin, because it is the same standard applied to every opinion the firm
signs. For plaintiff and defense counsel with Wildwood matters, the
practice offers
[vocational expert evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/),
[expert testimony and litigation consulting](/services/expert-testimony-litigation-consulting/),
and [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic

## What by-appointment service actually means

In practice, most of a Wildwood engagement never required a local office.
Records are reviewed, scope is defined, and structured interviews and
standardized testing are administered remotely under publisher protocols
approved for remote use. The parts that do require presence get it: when
the referral questions call for an in-person evaluation, it takes place in
the evaluee's own community, and when depositions or trial require the
expert at counsel's side, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home
office. Counsel gets the substance of local service without the fiction of
a local address, and the engagement file records where and how each step
was performed so the logistics themselves can withstand questioning.

## Wildwood venue and county context

Wildwood lies in St. Louis County, and state-court cases involving
Wildwood parties proceed in the Circuit Court of St. Louis County. Federal
practice in the state runs through the United States District Court for
the Eastern District of Missouri and the United States District Court for
the Western District of Missouri, with the controlling district for any
particular caption confirmed during the conflict check and intake. In
either forum the deliverable is the same: a written opinion whose sources,
methods, and reasoning are laid out for opposing counsel to test.

## A Wildwood evaluee in a metro market

Employment prospects for a Wildwood evaluee are evaluated against the
metro-wide commuting market centered on St. Louis, the area's principal
city, rather than against any single suburb. The firm draws on Bureau of
Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the
St. Louis metropolitan area and for Missouri statewide, on state workforce
agency labor market information, and on direct labor market research where
the questions presented warrant it. Regional characterizations stay
qualitative; the analysis of the individual stays concrete, covering
transferable skills, medically supported restrictions, and the occupations
realistically open to this evaluee.
