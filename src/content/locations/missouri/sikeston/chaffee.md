The most practical question counsel ask about a Chaffee referral is
logistical: where does the evaluation actually happen? The answer is that
it happens where the evaluee is. Purinton Analytics runs a remote-first
[national practice](/locations/nationwide/), and for a small town like
Chaffee that structure is an advantage rather than a compromise. Records
review, counsel conferences, scope negotiation, and report drafting all
proceed without anyone traveling; when the referral questions require an
in-person [vocational evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) or
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) assessment, the
appointment is arranged in the evaluee's own community so the person being
evaluated is not asked to absorb the burden of the expert's geography.
Both plaintiff and defense counsel engage the firm on these terms, and the
resulting reports are written to be read by whichever side did not send
them.

## Getting the evaluation done in Chaffee

An in-community assessment is scheduled around the evaluee's treatment
calendar and functional limits, and it feeds the same documented file as
any big-city engagement: a conflict check completed before substantive
work, a defined scope agreed with counsel, and a records request tailored
to the claims. There is no Chaffee office and no published local address,
because the firm's only office is its home office in Kansas City and it
declines to pretend otherwise. Deposition and trial presence, when the
docket requires it, is planned from that home office against the dates the
court has fixed, which is why the firm asks for scheduling information at
intake rather than after the report is served.

## Scott County venue and the Missouri forums

Chaffee lies in Scott County, and state-court matters involving Chaffee
parties proceed in the Circuit Court of Scott County. Missouri's federal
trial forums are the United States District Court for the Eastern District
of Missouri and the United States District Court for the Western District
of Missouri; which of them governs a specific caption, and whether any
venue-specific qualification requirement applies, is confirmed during the
conflict check and intake process. Counsel with matters spread across the
region can consult the [Sikeston metro page](/locations/missouri/sikeston/)
for the wider area and the [Missouri state page](/locations/missouri/) for
statewide coverage.

## The labor market behind a Chaffee opinion

A Chaffee evaluee's employment prospects are measured against the
commuting economy of the metropolitan area whose principal city is
Sikeston, not against the town alone and not against a national abstraction.
The firm grounds vocational findings in Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates for the relevant area and for
Missouri statewide, in state workforce agency labor market information,
and in direct labor market research where warranted, describing regional
scale only in qualitative terms. Where the engagement includes future
medical needs, [medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/)
and life care planning proceed alongside the vocational work, and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
let independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis
while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement
structure.
