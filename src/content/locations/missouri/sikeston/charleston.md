## Forum as foundation in a Mississippi County case

Cross-examination of an expert often begins with foundations rather than
findings, and forum is a foundation. Charleston sits in Mississippi
County, so a state-court matter involving Charleston parties belongs to
the Circuit Court of Mississippi County. A claim that supports federal
jurisdiction looks instead to Missouri's federal trial forums, the United
States District Court for the Eastern District of Missouri or the United
States District Court for the Western District of Missouri. The firm does
not guess which forum controls a given caption. That determination, along
with any venue-specific registration or qualification requirement, is
made during the conflict check and intake process and recorded there, so
it sits in the file as a documented step rather than an assumption a
cross-examiner can pull loose. Coverage of the venues surrounding
Charleston is described on the
[Sikeston metro page](/locations/missouri/sikeston/), and coverage across
the state on the [Missouri state page](/locations/missouri/).

## A labor market definition stated before it is defended

An opposing expert reads the labor market section of a report looking for
convenience: a market drawn to fit the conclusion rather than the
evaluee. The firm forecloses that reading by stating its definition
before its findings. Charleston is a small community within the
metropolitan area centered on Sikeston, its principal city, and the
report says so in plain qualitative terms, then builds on sources rather
than impressions. Vocational findings rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates for the relevant area and for
Missouri statewide, and on state workforce agency labor market
information; where the published series do not speak to the referral
question, direct labor market research fills the gap and is documented
like everything else. The evaluee-specific work follows the same rule:
the transferable skills analysis, the restrictions anchored to the
medical record, and the occupations realistically open from where the
evaluee lives are each set out step by step, so the reader on the other
side can test them individually instead of gesturing at the whole.
