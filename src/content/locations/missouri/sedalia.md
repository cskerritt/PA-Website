this evaluee return to any work, what earning capacity remains, what will

## Where Sedalia cases are heard

Pettis County anchors the venue footprint for this area. Engagements are
accepted for matters pending in the Circuit Court of Pettis County and for
federal litigation, whether venued in the United States District Court for
the Western District of Missouri or the United States District Court for
the Eastern District of Missouri. The governing forum determines disclosure
obligations, report format, and deposition practice, so those requirements
are confirmed at intake and the engagement calendar is built around the
operative scheduling order. Any venue-specific registration or qualification
requirement is identified and confirmed during the conflict check and
intake process.

## An honest account of local presence

The firm has no staffed Sedalia office and publishes no street address for
this location. That is stated plainly because overstating presence would sit
poorly beside reports whose entire value lies in their accuracy. Sedalia
counsel work with the firm by appointment: secure records exchange, remote
conferences for intake, status, and pre-deposition preparation, and remote
evaluation where the referral questions and testing protocols permit it.
When in-person evaluation is warranted, it is scheduled in the evaluee's
community, and when depositions or trial settings require the expert
present, travel is arranged from the Kansas City home office against the
case calendar.

## Researching the Sedalia labor market

An earning capacity opinion for a Pettis County evaluee has to describe the
market that evaluee can actually reach. Vocational findings here rest on
Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for
the relevant area and for Missouri statewide, state workforce agency labor
market information, and direct labor market research when the referral
requires it. The report defines the evaluee's commutable labor market,
identifies every data source relied upon, and explains the reasoning that
connects the data to the conclusions, so that the opinion can be tested on
its stated record. Where the referral includes future care, a
[medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/) or full life
care plan is prepared under the same sourcing standard, and
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) assignments
receive the same rigor applied in reverse.

## From referral to report

Every Sedalia engagement opens with a documented conflict check and a
defined scope. To begin,
[request a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) with the case caption and
parties, noting any imminent disclosure deadlines, depositions, mediations,
or trial dates. Once conflicts clear, counsel receive a records checklist
tailored to the claims and a report timeline committed against the court's
schedule. The finished opinion, whether presented in the Circuit Court of
Pettis County or in federal court, is written so that either side of the
caption can examine the record beneath it.
