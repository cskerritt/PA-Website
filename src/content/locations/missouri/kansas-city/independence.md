An expert report prepared for an Independence case should be written with the
receiving courtroom in mind before anything else. Purinton Analytics prepares
[vocational expert witness](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) opinions and
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) analyses for Independence
matters with that venue in view from the outset, for counsel on either side of
the caption. Independence is among the larger communities on the Missouri side
of the [Kansas City metro](/locations/missouri/kansas-city/), and its injury,
employment, and disability cases raise the same contested questions the firm
addresses nationwide: residual work capacity, realistic earnings, and the cost
of future care.

## Jackson County venue and disclosure practice

Independence lies in Jackson County, and state-court claims arising there
proceed in the Circuit Court, where vocational and care-planning opinions are
disclosed, deposed, and cross-examined like any other expert evidence.
Missouri's federal forums, the United States District Court for the Western
District of Missouri and the United States District Court for the Eastern
District of Missouri, are the available district courts for federal filings;
the controlling venue for a particular Independence case, and any registration
or qualification requirement that forum imposes, is confirmed during the
conflict check and intake. The firm's home office is in Kansas City, so
Jackson County litigation sits at the center of its practice geography rather
than at the edge of it, but the disclosure discipline does not change with
distance: a documented conflict check precedes any records review, scope is
defined in writing, and the finished opinion states its methods and sources so
opposing counsel can retrace every step.

## The market where an Independence evaluee competes

Earning-capacity findings for an Independence resident are anchored in the
labor market that person can actually reach, which extends well beyond the
city limits into the broader metropolitan area on both the Missouri and
Kansas sides of the state line. Vocational analysis rests on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Kansas City
metropolitan area and for Missouri statewide, supplemented by state workforce
agency labor market information and direct employer contact when the referral
questions call for it. Transferable-skills analysis and labor market survey
work proceed from that defined regional market, so the resulting opinion
reflects the jobs and wages genuinely open to the evaluee rather than a
generic national table.

## Meetings and evaluations in Independence

The firm publishes no Independence street address and keeps no staffed office
there; meetings are by appointment, and saying so plainly is part of the same
candor that governs the reports themselves. In-person vocational and
care-planning evaluations can be conducted in the evaluee's own community,
and because the home office is within the same metro, local appearances for
evaluations, depositions, and trial testimony are straightforward to
schedule. Secure video evaluation remains available where the referral
questions permit. When a case requires economic damages alongside the
vocational and care-planning work, the firm offers
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. Counsel looking for wider context can consult the
[Missouri state page](/locations/missouri/) or the
[nationwide practice overview](/locations/nationwide/).

## Starting an Independence engagement

Plaintiff and defense counsel begin identically: send the case caption or
party names through the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/), and flag any
approaching disclosure, deposition, or trial dates. Once conflicts are
cleared, the firm defines scope in writing and issues a records request
tailored to the claims before opinion work begins.
