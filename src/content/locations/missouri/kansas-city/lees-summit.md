Most working residents of Lee's Summit earn their living somewhere in the
wider metropolitan economy, and that fact shapes how earning capacity should
be measured when one of them is injured or loses a career to disability.
Purinton Analytics builds its
[vocational expert witness](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) opinions for
Lee's Summit litigation around the commuting market the evaluee actually
occupies, not around the town line, and pairs that analysis with
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) when future care is in
dispute. The work is available to plaintiff and defense counsel alike, and
every report is drafted on the assumption that opposing counsel will read it
closely.

## Venue context in Jackson County

Lee's Summit sits in Jackson County, so state-court matters arising from its
residents and employers are tried in the Circuit Court, where expert opinions
face the ordinary sequence of disclosure, deposition, and cross-examination.
For federal claims, Missouri offers the United States District Court for the
Western District of Missouri and the United States District Court for the
Eastern District of Missouri as its district forums; identifying the venue
that controls a specific case, along with any qualification requirement that
court imposes, is part of the conflict check and intake rather than a
question the firm answers in the abstract. Whatever the courtroom, the
process is uniform: conflicts documented before records arrive, scope fixed
in writing, methods and sources stated in the report so either party can test
them.

## Earning capacity in a market that crosses a state line

A Lee's Summit evaluee competes for work across the
[Kansas City metropolitan area](/locations/missouri/kansas-city/), a labor
market that reaches from suburban Missouri communities into Kansas.
Vocational findings therefore rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational
employment and wage estimates for that metropolitan area and for Missouri
statewide, with state workforce agency labor market information and targeted
employer research added where the referral questions warrant. Because the
metro functions as an integrated commuting market, transferable-skills
analysis considers occupations throughout it, and the report explains that
market definition openly so the opinion's foundation is visible on its face.
Placement-oriented conclusions, wage-loss comparisons, and labor market
surveys all follow from the same defined market.

## How evaluations proceed for Lee's Summit evaluees

There is no Purinton Analytics office in Lee's Summit and no published local
address; accuracy about presence is treated as part of professional accuracy
generally. What counsel gets instead is flexible logistics: secure video
evaluation where appropriate, or an in-person evaluation conducted in the
evaluee's own community, arranged from the Kansas City home office located in
the same metro. That proximity also simplifies scheduling for depositions and
trial appearances in Jackson County courtrooms. Where the damages theory
requires an economist, the firm structures
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure, and [medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/)
is available when the dispute centers on future treatment costs. Broader
venue and practice context appears on the
[Missouri state page](/locations/missouri/) and the
[nationwide overview](/locations/nationwide/).
