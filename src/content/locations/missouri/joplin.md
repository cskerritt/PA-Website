The Joplin metro sits astride a state line, and litigation here reflects
that. A workforce that crosses between Missouri and Kansas daily means that
the question of where an evaluee can actually work is rarely answered by
either state's data alone, and Purinton Analytics builds its Joplin engagements
around that reality. The firm provides
[vocational evaluation and expert testimony](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure, and it accepts referrals from plaintiff and defense
counsel alike.

## Venues on both sides of the line

The Joplin, MO-KS metropolitan statistical area comprises Jasper County and
Newton County in Missouri and Cherokee County, Kansas. On the Missouri side,
the firm accepts engagements for matters in the Circuit Court serving Jasper
County and Newton County, and for federal matters in the United States
District Court for the Western District of Missouri and the United States
District Court for the Eastern District of Missouri. Engagements arising from
Cherokee County, Kansas are accepted on the same terms, with the courts that
serve that county identified and confirmed during intake. Whatever the forum,
the report is written to the same standard: methods stated, sources cited,
conclusions traceable.

## Appointments, not an office

There is no staffed Purinton Analytics office in Joplin, and the firm
publishes no street address here, because candor about presence is part of
the same discipline that governs its reports. Meetings happen by appointment.
Scoping calls, records conferences, and pre-deposition preparation are
ordinarily handled by video or phone, and remote vocational evaluation is
available when the referral questions and testing protocols permit. When the
case requires an in-person evaluation, or when a deposition or trial setting
requires presence, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office and
confirmed against the disclosure calendar counsel are working under.

## Labor market data for a cross-state workforce

Because Joplin-area evaluees may reasonably look for work in either state,
vocational findings here draw on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational
employment and wage estimates for the Joplin metropolitan area together with
statewide estimates for Missouri and for Kansas, supplemented by state
workforce agency labor market information and direct labor market research
where warranted. The written opinion is explicit about which market defines
the evaluee's commutable labor market and why, so the cross-state character
of the metro becomes a documented analytical choice rather than an unstated
assumption. Life care plans price services and supplies in the community
where the evaluee actually lives.

## Opening a Joplin engagement

Engagements begin with a documented conflict check. Send the caption or party
names through the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and note any imminent
disclosure, deposition, or trial dates. After clearance, counsel receive a
defined scope and a records request tailored to the claims. Joplin matters
are handled within the firm's [Missouri practice](/locations/missouri/), and
counsel with cases elsewhere can review the
[nationwide practice](/locations/nationwide/), which extends the same
engagement structure to state and federal venues across the country.
