For an injured or disabled evaluee in the Fort Leonard Wood area, the
evaluation should come to the evaluee, not the other way around. Purinton
Analytics structures its Pulaski County engagements around that principle:
remote evaluation where the referral questions and testing protocols permit
it, and in-person evaluation scheduled in the evaluee's own community when
the work requires it. The scope of service is the same offered across
[Missouri](/locations/missouri/) and the firm's
[nationwide practice](/locations/nationwide/):
[vocational expert evaluation and testimony](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. Referrals are welcome from either side of the caption.

## The venue footprint

Fort Leonard Wood area matters are anchored in Pulaski County. The firm
accepts engagements for cases pending in the Circuit Court of Pulaski County
and for federal matters, whether venued in the United States District Court
for the Western District of Missouri or the United States District Court for
the Eastern District of Missouri. State and federal forums impose different
disclosure and report obligations, and the engagement is scoped against the
governing rules and scheduling order from the outset, so that the written
opinion arrives when the calendar requires it and in the form the forum
expects.

## What by-appointment service means here

The firm keeps no staffed office in the Fort Leonard Wood area and publishes
no local street address. That candor is deliberate: a practice whose
credibility rests on documented, verifiable opinions should be equally
accurate about where it does and does not maintain offices. Counsel work
with the firm through secure records transfer and remote conferences for
intake, status, and pre-deposition preparation. When depositions, hearings,
or trial settings call for presence, or when the referral requires in-person
evaluation, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office and planned
against the litigation calendar rather than improvised around it.

## Grounding opinions in the local labor market

A transferable skills analysis or earning capacity opinion for a Pulaski
County evaluee cannot borrow its data from a distant metro. Vocational
findings here rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and
wage estimates for the relevant area and for Missouri statewide, state
workforce agency labor market information, and direct labor market research
where warranted. The report states how the commutable labor market was
defined, which sources were used, and why, so opposing counsel can test the
opinion on its own stated terms. When future medical and attendant care are
at issue, a [medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/) or
full life care plan is prepared under the same sourcing discipline, and
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) work is
available when the assignment is to evaluate another expert's file.

## Beginning an engagement

Engagements start with a documented conflict check and a defined scope, not
with a retainer form. To begin,
[request a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) with the case caption and
parties, and identify any imminent disclosure, deposition, or trial dates.
Once conflicts clear, counsel receive a records request tailored to the
claims, a defined scope of work, and a report timeline committed against the
operative schedule. The resulting opinion is written for the courtroom in
which it will be examined, whatever side of the caption issued the referral.
