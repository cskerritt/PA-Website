The Sikeston area spans Mississippi County and Scott County, and litigation
here moves through the circuit courts of each. Purinton Analytics accepts
vocational expert and life care planning referrals across that footprint,
serving plaintiff and defense counsel on identical terms. The work offered
locally is the same offered throughout [Missouri](/locations/missouri/) and
the firm's [national practice](/locations/nationwide/):
[vocational evaluation and expert testimony](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/),
[medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/), and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## State and federal forums

Engagements are accepted for matters pending in the Circuit Court of
Mississippi County, the Circuit Court of Scott County, and the federal
courts, whether the case is venued in the United States District Court for
the Eastern District of Missouri or the United States District Court for the
Western District of Missouri. Forum matters to methodology: disclosure
formats, report requirements, and deposition practice differ between the
circuit courts and the federal bench, and the engagement is scoped to the
governing rules from the first conference. Any venue-specific registration or
qualification requirement is identified and confirmed during the conflict
check and intake process.

## Appointment-based practice, stated plainly

There is no Purinton Analytics office in Sikeston, and the firm will not
imply otherwise by publishing a local address it does not staff. Accuracy
about presence belongs to the same discipline that governs every report the
firm signs. Sikeston area counsel work with the firm remotely for most of
the engagement: secure records exchange, scheduling and status conferences by
phone or video, and remote evaluation where the referral questions and
testing protocols allow. When the matter requires in-person evaluation in
the evaluee's community, or when deposition and trial dates require the
expert in the courtroom, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home
office and planned against the case calendar.

## Labor market data for a rural regional market

Employability and earning capacity opinions for Sikeston area evaluees must
reflect the labor market those evaluees actually face, not a metropolitan
market elsewhere in the state. The analysis draws on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the relevant area
and for Missouri statewide, state workforce agency labor market information,
and direct labor market research when the referral requires it. The written
opinion identifies how the commutable labor market was defined for the
evaluee and which sources support each finding, so the conclusions can be
examined line by line rather than taken on authority. The same documentation
standard applies when the assignment is a
[rebuttal or peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) of another
expert's report.

## How to refer a Sikeston area case

The first step is always the same: a documented conflict check before any
substantive discussion of the file. Submit the caption or party names
through the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and flag any imminent
disclosure deadlines, depositions, mediations, or trial settings. After
conflicts clear, counsel receive a records checklist tailored to the claims
and a defined scope of work, and the report timeline is committed against
the court's schedule. From intake through testimony in Mississippi County,
Scott County, or federal court, the file is built so that both sides can
examine the record behind every opinion.
