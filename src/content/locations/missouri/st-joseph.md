Every opinion Purinton Analytics issues is written to be read by opposing
counsel, and that standard does not change with the venue. Counsel in the
St. Joseph metro retain the firm for
[vocational evaluation and expert testimony](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. Referrals are welcome from either side of the caption,
and each engagement opens with a documented conflict check, a written scope,
and a records request tailored to the claims, so the record behind the
opinion is one both sides can examine.

## A metro that reaches into Kansas

The St. Joseph, MO-KS metropolitan statistical area comprises Buchanan
County, Andrew County, and DeKalb County in Missouri, together with Doniphan
County, Kansas. On the Missouri side, the firm accepts engagements for
matters in the Circuit Court serving each county and for federal matters in
the United States District Court for the Western District of Missouri and the
United States District Court for the Eastern District of Missouri. Matters
arising in Doniphan County, Kansas are accepted on the same terms, with the
appropriate courts and any venue-specific requirements identified and
confirmed during the conflict check and intake process. Whichever side of the
state line a matter sits on, the method, the sourcing rules, and the report
format remain the same.

## Meetings by appointment, honestly framed

The firm keeps no staffed office in St. Joseph and publishes no street
address for this location; candor about where the firm actually sits is part
of the discipline counsel are hiring. Meetings are by appointment. Records
conferences, strategy calls, and pre-deposition preparation ordinarily
proceed by video or phone, and remote vocational evaluation is available when
the referral questions and testing protocols permit. When the case calls for
in-person evaluation in the evaluee's own community, or when a hearing or
trial setting requires presence, travel from the Kansas City home office is
scheduled against the operative disclosure calendar so deadlines are met
without compressed, last-minute work.

## Reading the local labor market

Employability and earning capacity opinions for St. Joseph-area evaluees are
grounded in Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage
estimates for the St. Joseph metropolitan area, in statewide estimates for
Missouri and for Kansas, and in state workforce agency labor market
information from both states, supplemented by direct labor market research
when the referral requires it. Because the metro crosses a state line, the
written opinion states which market defines the evaluee's commutable labor
market and why, rather than leaving that judgment implicit. Life care plans
are priced in the community where the evaluee lives, with every cost tied to
a documented source.

## Getting started

Begin with a [conflict check](/refer-a-case/): provide the caption or party
names and flag any imminent disclosure, deposition, or trial dates. Once
cleared, counsel receive a defined scope and a records request tailored to
the claims before analysis begins. St. Joseph matters sit within the firm's
[Missouri practice](/locations/missouri/), and counsel whose cases reach
farther can review the [nationwide practice](/locations/nationwide/), which
carries the same engagement structure into state and federal venues across
the country.
