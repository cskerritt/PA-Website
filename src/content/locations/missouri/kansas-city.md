Kansas City is the home office of Purinton Analytics. The firm's principal,
Jason C. Purinton, has conducted vocational evaluations and testified across
Missouri and the greater Kansas City metro since the firm's founding in 2018,
and the national practice is administered from here. For local counsel that
means direct access to the firm's full scope of work:
[vocational evaluation and expert testimony](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and coordination of
forensic economic damages analyses that are performed and signed by independent
economist partners.

## A two-state metro

Kansas City litigation routinely crosses the state line, and the practice is
built for that. On the Missouri side, the firm serves counsel with matters in
the circuit courts of Jackson, Clay, Platte, and Cass counties and in the
United States District Court for the Western District of Missouri. On the
Kansas side, it serves counsel with matters in Johnson and Wyandotte county
district courts and in the United States District Court for the District of
Kansas. Jason Purinton is a Licensed Professional Counselor in Missouri and
holds workers' compensation rehabilitation credentials in both states:
Certified Rehabilitation Provider with the Missouri Division of Workers'
Compensation and Qualified Rehabilitation Professional with the Kansas
Division of Workers' Compensation.

## How local engagements proceed

Meetings are by appointment. Evaluations are scheduled at the firm's Kansas
City office, at counsel's offices, or in the evaluee's community, and remote
evaluation is available when the referral questions and testing protocols
permit it. Because the office is local, records conferences, attorney
meetings, and pre-deposition preparation can be scheduled quickly, and
last-minute needs around disclosure deadlines, depositions, mediations, and
trial settings in the metro are easier to accommodate in person. Once a
conflict check clears, counsel receive a records checklist tailored to the
claims, and the report timeline is set against the operative disclosure
calendar rather than left open ended.

## Labor market research for Kansas City matters

Employability and earning capacity findings for local evaluees are grounded in
data specific to this market. The analysis draws on Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates for the Kansas City metropolitan
area, statewide estimates for Missouri and for Kansas, and state workforce
agency labor market information from both states, supplemented by direct labor
market research when the referral requires it. Because the metro spans two
states, the report is explicit about which market defines the evaluee's
commutable labor market and why.
