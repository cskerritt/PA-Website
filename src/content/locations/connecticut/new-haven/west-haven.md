An earning capacity opinion is about a person before it is about a
case. When the evaluee lives in West Haven, Purinton Analytics plans the
evaluation around that person: the medical and employment records that
document what changed, a structured interview grounded in where the
evaluee actually lives and has worked, and standardized testing
administered under publisher protocols. From that foundation the
practice prepares
[vocational expert witness](/services/vocational-expert-witness/)
opinions and [life care plans](/services/life-care-planning/) for both
plaintiff and defense retentions, in state and federal matters alike.

## Forum and venue for West Haven matters

West Haven sits in the South Central Connecticut Planning Region, and
its civil disputes are tried in the Superior Court. Where a claim arises
under federal law or otherwise belongs in federal court, the United
States District Court for the District of Connecticut stands as the
available forum; the controlling venue for any given matter is
identified during the conflict check and intake rather than assumed.
Neither forum changes the substance of the work. The opinion is built to
the same standard either way, sourced so that cross-examination can be
aimed at the data and the reasoning rather than at an unexplained
conclusion. The [Connecticut overview](/locations/connecticut/) collects
the firm's statewide venue picture for counsel with a broader docket.

## Reading the labor market around West Haven

A West Haven worker participates in a labor market that is regional by
nature, and the analysis treats it that way. Vocational findings rest on
Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates
for the New Haven metropolitan area and for Connecticut statewide,
alongside state workforce agency labor market information. Where the
questions posed in the referral require it, the analysis adds direct
labor market research for the specific case, so that conclusions about
placeability reflect employers who actually hire and occupations that
actually exist within the evaluee's reach. How that research operates
across the wider region is described on the
[New Haven metro page](/locations/connecticut/new-haven/).

## How a West Haven evaluee is evaluated

Most of the work travels well without anyone traveling. Records analysis
and vocational interviews proceed remotely, and testing is administered
remotely wherever the test publisher supports remote administration,
which spares the evaluee unnecessary trips and spares the file
unnecessary delay. The firm keeps no staffed West Haven office and lists
no street address for this location, a fact stated plainly because
honesty about presence is part of the report standard itself. When the
referral requires an in-person evaluation, the appointment is set in the
evaluee's own community, and travel for evaluations, depositions, and
trial testimony is scheduled from the Kansas City home office.
