By the time counsel in Meriden reach out for a vocational expert, the
calendar is usually already speaking: an expert disclosure deadline has
been set, a deposition has been noticed, or a trial date has stopped
feeling distant. Purinton Analytics structures its work around those
dates. The practice provides
[vocational expert witness](/services/vocational-expert-witness/)
evaluation, [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. Retentions come from plaintiff and defense counsel
alike, and every report is written on the assumption that the other side
will read it carefully.

## The courts that hear Meriden cases

Meriden lies within the South Central Connecticut Planning Region, and
civil matters from the city proceed in the Superior Court, Connecticut's
trial court of general jurisdiction. For claims that belong in federal
court, the United States District Court for the District of Connecticut
is the available federal forum, with the controlling venue for a
particular case identified during the conflict check and intake. The
choice of forum shapes disclosure practice and scheduling far more than
it shapes the analysis: the same conflict clearance, the same defined
scope, and the same records request tailored to the claims open the
file, whichever courthouse the caption names. Counsel with matters
pending elsewhere in the state will find the wider picture on the firm's
[Connecticut overview](/locations/connecticut/).

## A commuting market, measured honestly

A Meriden evaluee's realistic employment prospects rarely stop at the
city line, and a credible earning capacity opinion has to account for
the regional market the evaluee can actually reach. Vocational findings
therefore rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and
wage estimates for the New Haven metropolitan area and for Connecticut
statewide, supplemented by state workforce agency labor market
information and, where the referral questions warrant it, direct labor
market research conducted for the specific case. Transferable skills are
tested against occupations that exist in this market rather than against
national abstractions, and every figure in the report carries its
source. The firm's [New Haven metro page](/locations/connecticut/new-haven/)
describes how the regional practice operates across the surrounding
communities.

## Scheduling the work to the deadlines

The firm keeps no staffed office in Meriden and publishes no local
street address, because accuracy about presence belongs to the same
discipline that governs the reports. What counsel get instead is a
working method built for deadlines. Records review, structured
interviews, and standardized testing under publisher protocols proceed
remotely where remote administration is supported, which keeps most
engagements moving without any travel at all. When the referral
questions call for an in-person evaluation, the appointment is conducted
in the evaluee's own community, and when deposition or trial dates
require presence, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office
with enough lead time to protect the court's calendar.

deposition, or trial dates already fixed, so availability can be

counsel receive a written scope, a records request, and a schedule keyed
to the dates that matter. For matters filed beyond Connecticut, the
