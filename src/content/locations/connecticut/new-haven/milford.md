## Milford's courthouse context

Milford belongs to the South Central Connecticut Planning Region, and
its civil litigation is tried in the Superior Court, Connecticut's trial
court of general jurisdiction. Claims suited to a federal forum may be
brought in the United States District Court for the District of
Connecticut; which venue controls a particular dispute is confirmed
during the conflict check and intake rather than presumed from
geography. In both settings the engagement follows the same sequence, a
cleared conflict check followed by a written scope and a records request
shaped to the claims actually pleaded, so the finished opinion rests on
a record that both sides can examine. The firm's
[Connecticut overview](/locations/connecticut/) maps the statewide venue
landscape for counsel whose practice extends past this corner of the
state.

## What the regional market means for a Milford opinion

Assessing what a Milford evaluee can earn requires looking at the market
the evaluee genuinely competes in, not a generic one. The practice
grounds its vocational findings in Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates for the New Haven
metropolitan area and for Connecticut statewide, adds state workforce
agency labor market information, and conducts direct labor market
research when the referral questions call for that level of specificity.
Life care plans are priced to the evaluee's own market through

## Serving Milford by appointment

There is no Purinton Analytics storefront in Milford, and the firm
publishes no street address here; saying so plainly is part of the same
candor that governs its written opinions. The practical consequence for
counsel is a remote-first engagement. Document review, interviews, and
publisher-sanctioned remote testing move forward without travel, and the
file progresses at the pace of the litigation rather than the pace of a
travel schedule. When an in-person evaluation is warranted, it takes
place in the community where the evaluee lives, and appearances for
deposition or trial are scheduled as travel from the Kansas City home
office with the court's dates protected.
