## When Counsel Retains for a Groton Evaluee

The most common Groton engagement starts with a person, not a courthouse: an injured worker, an accident plaintiff, or a claimant whose earning capacity a defendant needs tested. Purinton Analytics builds each of these files the same way for either side of the caption, beginning with a [vocational assessment](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) grounded in the evaluee's actual work history and, where the injuries require it, a [life care plan](/services/life-care-planning/) that prices future care from cited sources rather than assumption. Groton is a small town, and the practice treats that as an analytical fact: transferable skills and placement prospects are examined against the labor market the evaluee can realistically reach, not an abstraction borrowed from a larger city.

## Where a Groton Case Is Heard

Groton lies in the Southeastern Connecticut Planning Region and within the Norwich-New London-Willimantic metropolitan area, whose regional posture is described on the [metro page](/locations/connecticut/norwich/). State claims proceed in the Superior Court, and federal claims from Groton are filed in the United States District Court for the District of Connecticut. The controlling venue for a particular matter, together with any local qualification or disclosure rule, is identified during the conflict check and intake rather than assumed in advance, because a report drafted for the wrong forum helps neither side of the caption.

## Groton Work in a Regional Market

Because a Groton evaluee's realistic job search extends beyond town lines, vocational findings here are framed against Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Norwich-New London-Willimantic area and for Connecticut as a whole, alongside state workforce agency labor market information and targeted employer contact when a specific placement question is disputed. These pages stay deliberately free of figures; the numbers live in the report, each one sourced. Where lost earnings must be converted into an economic damages calculation, the firm coordinates with independent economist partners who perform and sign that analysis themselves, through a [coordinated damages engagement](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) that keeps the vocational and economic work aligned without blurring who authored what.

## Logistics Without a Local Office

Purinton Analytics keeps no office in Groton and lists no street address for this location; every meeting is by appointment, and saying so plainly is part of the firm's credibility with the court. In practice, most of the work never requires travel: records analysis, attorney conferences, and a large share of evaluee interviews are handled by secure video. An in-person evaluation, when warranted, is conducted in the evaluee's own community so that the findings reflect real conditions, and appearances for deposition or trial are scheduled as travel from the Kansas City home office. Counsel weighing a matter elsewhere in the state can review the [Connecticut overview](/locations/connecticut/), and the [nationwide page](/locations/nationwide/) sets out the practice's uniform standards.
