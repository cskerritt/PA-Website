Purinton Analytics supports plaintiff and defense counsel across the
Hartford-West Hartford-East Hartford metropolitan area with
[vocational expert witness](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) work,
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. The referral questions from the Hartford area follow
the patterns seen nationally: earning capacity after injury, employability
in disputed employment matters, and the future cost of care in catastrophic
cases.

## Venue footprint in the Hartford region

Engagements are accepted for matters venued in the Superior Court sitting
throughout the Capitol Planning Region and the Lower Connecticut River
Valley Planning Region, and in the United States District Court for the
District of Connecticut. The same report standard applies in either forum,
because the written opinion is prepared to be examined by the other side of
the caption regardless of where the case is filed. Counsel with matters
elsewhere in the state can begin from the firm's
[Connecticut overview](/locations/connecticut/), which describes coverage
beyond this metro.

## Meetings by appointment

Hartford meetings are by appointment. The firm keeps no staffed office in
the metro and publishes no street address for this location, because
accuracy about presence is part of the same discipline that governs its
reports. Most engagements from the region begin remotely: a documented
conflict check, a defined scope, a records request tailored to the claims,
structured interviews, and standardized testing under publisher protocols
where remote administration is supported. When the referral questions call
for in-person evaluation, or when deposition and trial dates require
presence, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office and planned
around the court's calendar rather than the firm's.

## Labor market research for Hartford evaluees

Vocational findings for evaluees in the region rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Hartford
metropolitan area and for Connecticut statewide, supplemented by state
workforce agency labor market information and direct labor market research
where the referral questions warrant it. Life care plans and medical cost
projections are priced to the evaluee's own market rather than to national
averages, using provider-specific inquiries and published fee data for the
region. The sources behind every figure are cited in the report itself, so
that opposing counsel can trace each finding to the data that supports it
rather than to the evaluator's say-so.

## Starting a Hartford engagement

An engagement from the Hartford area begins the way every engagement
begins: with a conflict check before any substantive discussion of the
case. Submit the caption or party names through the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and note any imminent disclosure,
deposition, or trial dates so scheduling can be confirmed at the outset.
Counsel weighing whether the practice fits a matter venued outside
Connecticut can review the firm's
[nationwide coverage](/locations/nationwide/), which describes how the same
remote-first methodology and travel model serve courts across the country.
Once clearance is confirmed, counsel receive a defined scope, a records
request, and a schedule keyed to the disclosure deadlines, and the work
proceeds under the same standard whether the retaining party sits at the
plaintiff table or the defense table.
