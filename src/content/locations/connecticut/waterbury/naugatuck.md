## Courts and the regional setting

Naugatuck sits within the Naugatuck Valley Planning Region, part of the
Waterbury-Shelton metropolitan area. State-court matters involving Naugatuck
parties are tried in the Connecticut Superior Court, and the firm prepares
its reports with that court's treatment of expert opinion in mind. Federal
claims may proceed in the United States District Court for the District of
Connecticut, and the forum that actually controls a given dispute is a
question the firm resolves with counsel during the conflict check and intake
rather than one it presumes from a map. The
[Waterbury metro page](/locations/connecticut/waterbury/) describes the
surrounding market in fuller detail, and the
[Connecticut state page](/locations/connecticut/) collects the firm's
statewide coverage.

## Earning capacity measured against the region

Naugatuck is a mid-size community, and an earning capacity opinion confined
to its borders would fail the first serious question at deposition. The
relevant market for a Naugatuck evaluee extends across the valley and toward
Waterbury, the principal city of the metro, and the analysis is built to
that scale. Transferable-skills and placeability findings rest on Bureau of
Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the
metropolitan area and for Connecticut statewide, on state workforce agency
labor market information, and on direct labor market research when the
referral questions require original inquiry. Opportunity and scale are
characterized qualitatively, and every characterization traces to a
published source that a cross-examiner can retrieve and check independently,
because an opinion that cannot be checked is an opinion that will not last.

## How a Naugatuck evaluee is served

The firm keeps no office in Naugatuck and publishes no local address; saying
so plainly costs nothing and protects credibility, which is the asset the
whole practice runs on. Service is by appointment. Records review,
structured interviews, and report drafting proceed remotely in most
engagements, which keeps expert costs proportionate to the case. When the
assignment warrants in-person evaluation, the firm conducts it in the
evaluee's own community, and when a deposition or trial date requires
presence, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office. Life care
planning for catastrophically injured Naugatuck evaluees follows the same
structure, and
[medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/) is available
when a narrower costing question is all the matter requires. Where the
damages picture also needs economic loss testimony, the firm offers
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.
