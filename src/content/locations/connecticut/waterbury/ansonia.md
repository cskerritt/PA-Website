## The labor market around Ansonia

Ansonia is a small city, and its residents' employment prospects are properly
measured against the regional market that surrounds it, reaching toward
Waterbury, the metro's principal city, and the broader valley economy.
Vocational conclusions for an Ansonia evaluee are grounded in Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the metropolitan
area and for Connecticut statewide, supported by state workforce agency labor
market information and, where warranted, direct labor market research. The
firm characterizes opportunity in qualitative terms tied to those published
sources; no figure appears in an opinion that cannot be traced to data
opposing counsel can pull for themselves. Where the treating records suggest

and the gap is flagged for counsel rather than papered over.
