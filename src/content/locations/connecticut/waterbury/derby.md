Derby is a small city, and no national expert practice keeps an office in it,
this firm included. What Derby counsel get instead is a working model built
for places exactly like it: remote-first analysis, evaluation in the
evaluee's own community when the case calls for it, and testimony delivered
where the court sits. Purinton Analytics provides
[vocational expert witness services](/services/vocational-expert-witness/)
and [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) here for plaintiff
and defense referrals alike, and treats that even-handedness as a
methodological commitment rather than a slogan.

## Venue for Derby parties

Derby lies in the Naugatuck Valley Planning Region, part of the
Waterbury-Shelton metropolitan area. State claims involving Derby parties are
tried in the Connecticut Superior Court. On the federal side, the United
States District Court for the District of Connecticut stands as the available
forum, and the venue that actually controls a matter is settled with counsel
during the conflict check and intake. Because the firm's written opinions are
drafted for scrutiny by whichever side did not retain it, the venue question
changes logistics but never the analysis. Broader regional coverage is
described on the [Waterbury metro page](/locations/connecticut/waterbury/)
and across the [Connecticut state page](/locations/connecticut/).

## A small city inside a regional market

The vocational question in a Derby case is rarely about Derby alone. A
resident's earning capacity is defined by the jobs reachable from Derby
across the valley and toward Waterbury, the principal city of the metro, so
the analysis must be regional even when the caption is local. The firm builds
that analysis on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage
estimates covering the metropolitan area and Connecticut statewide, on state
workforce agency labor market information, and on direct labor market
research where a placement or wage question requires original inquiry.
Findings about the market are expressed qualitatively and sourced, which is
what allows them to hold up when the record is contested.

## Remote-first evaluation logistics

Most of the work in a Derby engagement, records review, structured
interviewing, and report preparation, is performed remotely, which keeps the
cost of expert work proportionate to smaller-venue litigation. When an
in-person evaluation is warranted, it is scheduled by appointment in the
community where the evaluee lives. Deposition and trial appearances are
planned with travel from the Kansas City home office. For cases that need a
full damages picture,
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
bring in independent economist partners who perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work continues under one
engagement structure, and
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) is available when
the assignment is to test another expert's work rather than to build an
affirmative opinion.

A Derby referral opens the same way every engagement does: with a documented

names, and identify any dates already set for disclosure, deposition, or
