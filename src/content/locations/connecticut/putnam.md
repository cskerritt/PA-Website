Before Purinton Analytics will discuss the substance of any Putnam area
case, it runs a conflict check, and that ordering is the clearest
introduction to how the practice works. Process precedes substance
because the opinions have to survive scrutiny from both directions: the firm takes
plaintiff and defense retentions throughout the Putnam micropolitan area,
providing
[earning capacity and employability evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Venues for northeastern Connecticut matters

The firm accepts matters venued in the Superior Court sitting in the
Northeastern Connecticut Planning Region and in the United States
District Court for the District of Connecticut. Each engagement is
scoped in writing at the outset, with a records request tailored to the
claims actually pleaded, so that the eventual opinion rests on a record
both sides can examine rather than on materials selected to flatter a
conclusion. Counsel with cases in other Connecticut venues can consult
the firm's [statewide overview](/locations/connecticut/) for the same
approach applied across the state.

## Appointment-only, and said plainly

There is no staffed Purinton Analytics office in the Putnam area, and no
street address is published for this location. Meetings occur by
appointment, arranged for the particular engagement. The firm states
this outright because an expert who is careless about small facts
invites doubt about large ones. The working model is remote-first:
records review, structured interviews, and standardized testing under
publisher protocols where remote administration is supported, with
travel from the Kansas City home office scheduled when the referral
questions require an in-person evaluation or when deposition and trial
dates require the evaluator's presence. Where in-person work is needed,
it is conducted in the evaluee's own community.

## Evidence on work and earnings in the region

Vocational findings for evaluees in northeastern Connecticut rest on
Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates
for Connecticut and its published areas, on state workforce agency labor
market information, and on direct labor market research where the case
warrants it. In a smaller labor market, that direct research earns its
place, testing what published estimates suggest against what employers
in the evaluee's actual reach are doing. Life care plans and medical
cost projections are priced to the evaluee's own market using
provider-specific inquiries and published fee data for the region, and
every figure in the report is tied to a cited source that opposing
counsel can pull and check.

## Clearance before conversation

To explore an engagement, submit the caption or party names through the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and note any imminent disclosure,
deposition, or trial dates. No substantive case discussion occurs until
clearance is confirmed, which protects both the retaining firm and the
opposing party from an avoidable disqualification fight later. Once the
check clears, counsel receive a defined scope, a records request, and a
schedule built to the court's deadlines. The same sequence governs every
retention the firm accepts, in Connecticut and beyond, as described in
its [nationwide coverage](/locations/nationwide/); the Putnam area
simply gets the benefit of a process designed to work the same way
everywhere.
