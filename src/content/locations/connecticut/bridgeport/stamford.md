## Reading the Stamford labor market

Employability and earning capacity findings for Stamford evaluees rest on
Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for
the metropolitan area and for Connecticut statewide, supplemented by state
workforce agency labor market information and direct labor market research
when the referral questions call for it. Transferable skills analysis
considers the occupations realistically open to the evaluee across the
commuting region, and every data source is cited in the report so the
reasoning can be traced from record to conclusion. When damages require

## How a Stamford evaluation proceeds

The firm keeps no staffed office in Stamford and publishes no street
address for this location; accuracy about presence is part of the same
discipline that governs the reports. Most engagements run remotely from
intake through the written opinion: records review, structured interviews
by video, and standardized testing under publisher protocols where remote
administration is supported. When the referral questions call for in-person
evaluation, the assessment is arranged in the evaluee's own community, and
when a deposition or trial date requires presence, travel is scheduled from
the Kansas City home office. Counsel receive the same report standard and
the same point of contact either way.
