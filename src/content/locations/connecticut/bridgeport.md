Every opinion Purinton Analytics writes for the Bridgeport-Stamford-Danbury
metropolitan area is drafted on the assumption that opposing counsel will
read it closely, because in this practice they usually do. That standard,
rather than any local storefront, is what the firm offers counsel on either
side of the caption here:
[earning capacity and employability evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) in catastrophic injury
cases, and [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Courts served across the metro

The firm accepts matters venued in the Superior Court sitting in the
Greater Bridgeport Planning Region and the Western Connecticut Planning
Region, and in the United States District Court for the District of
Connecticut. Bridgeport, Stamford, and Danbury counsel often retain the
practice for cases pending in either forum, and the methodology does not
change with the courthouse: a documented conflict check, a defined scope,
and a records request tailored to the claims, so that the written opinion
rests on a record both sides can examine. Matters venued elsewhere in the
state are described on the firm's
[Connecticut page](/locations/connecticut/).

## Presence without a storefront

There is no staffed Purinton Analytics office in the metro, and the firm
publishes no street address for this location. Meetings are by appointment,
and most engagements proceed remotely from intake through report: records
review, structured interviews, and standardized testing under publisher
protocols where remote administration is supported. When an in-person
evaluation is warranted, or when a deposition or trial date requires
presence, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office. Counsel
receive the same report standard and the same point of contact whether the
work is performed remotely or in the evaluee's community.

## Grounding findings in the regional labor market

Vocational conclusions for evaluees in the region are grounded in Bureau of
Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the
metropolitan area and for Connecticut statewide, together with state
workforce agency labor market information and direct labor market research
where the referral questions call for it. Care-cost work follows the same
logic: life care plans and medical cost projections are priced to the
evaluee's own market using provider-specific inquiries and published fee
data for the region. Every source is cited in the report, and the
qualitative reasoning that connects data to opinion is set out step by
step, because a conclusion that cannot be traced is a conclusion that
cannot survive cross-examination.

## Requesting a conflict check

Engagements from the Bridgeport, Stamford, and Danbury area begin with a
conflict check, never with a substantive case discussion. To determine
availability, [request a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) with the case
caption and parties, and flag any disclosure, deposition, or trial dates
that are already set. If the matter reaches beyond Connecticut, the firm's
[national coverage](/locations/nationwide/) describes how the same
remote-first model serves courts in other states. After clearance, counsel
receive a written scope, a records request keyed to the claims, and a
schedule built backward from the court's deadlines, with the understanding
that the finished report will read the same way to the retaining party and
to the party across the aisle.
