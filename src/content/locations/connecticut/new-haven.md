The questions that bring New Haven counsel to Purinton Analytics are
usually versions of the same few: what an injured person can still earn,
whether a claimant can return to suitable work, and what a lifetime of
medically necessary care will cost. The practice answers them through
[vocational expert witness](/services/vocational-expert-witness/)
evaluation, [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure, for plaintiff and defense retentions alike.

## Where New Haven engagements are venued

Matters from the New Haven metropolitan area are accepted in the Superior
Court sitting in the South Central Connecticut Planning Region and in the
United States District Court for the District of Connecticut. The forum
shapes scheduling and disclosure practice, but not the analysis: the same
documented conflict check, defined scope, and claims-tailored records
request open every engagement, so that the resulting opinion rests on a
record either side can test. Counsel with cases pending in other parts of
the state will find the broader picture on the firm's
[Connecticut overview](/locations/connecticut/).

## Appointment-based logistics

New Haven meetings are by appointment. The firm maintains no staffed office
in the metro and publishes no street address here, a choice made
deliberately: candor about where the firm actually sits is part of the same
discipline that governs what its reports actually say. In practice, most of
the work travels well. Records review, structured interviews, and
standardized testing under publisher protocols proceed remotely where
remote administration is supported. When the referral questions require an
in-person evaluation, or when deposition and trial calendars require
presence, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office with enough
lead time to protect the court's dates.

## Labor market and cost research

Vocational findings for evaluees in the region rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the New Haven
metropolitan area and for Connecticut statewide, alongside state workforce
agency labor market information and, where warranted, direct labor market
research conducted for the specific case. Life care plans and medical cost
projections are priced to the evaluee's own market through
provider-specific inquiries and published fee data for the region, not
borrowed from national averages. Each figure in the report carries its
source, so cross-examination can be directed at the data and the reasoning
rather than at an unexplained bottom line.

## How to begin

A New Haven engagement starts with clearance, not with a case narrative.
Submit the caption or party names through the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and identify any disclosure,
deposition, or trial dates that are already on the calendar, so
availability can be confirmed honestly before the engagement opens. Once
the conflict check clears, counsel receive a written scope, a records
request, and a schedule keyed to the deadlines that matter. Firms weighing
the practice for matters beyond Connecticut can consult the description of
its [nationwide coverage](/locations/nationwide/), which explains how the
same remote-first methodology, the same travel model, and the same report
standard operate wherever the case happens to be filed.
