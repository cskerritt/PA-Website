For counsel in the Norwich-New London-Willimantic metropolitan area, the
practical question is usually venue: the case is pending in the Superior
Court or in the United States District Court for the District of
Connecticut, a disclosure deadline is approaching, and the file needs a
vocational or care-planning opinion that will hold up in that forum.
Purinton Analytics accepts those retentions from either side of the
caption, offering
[earning capacity and employability evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and

## The venue footprint in southeastern Connecticut

Matters are accepted throughout the Southeastern Connecticut Planning
Region, in the Superior Court and in the United States District Court for
the District of Connecticut. State and federal practice differ in their
disclosure mechanics, and the firm schedules to the forum's requirements
from the outset, but the analytical standard is constant: a documented
conflict check, a defined scope, and a records request tailored to the
claims, producing an opinion that rests on a record both sides can
examine. Counsel handling cases elsewhere in the state can start from the
firm's [Connecticut page](/locations/connecticut/).

## Serving the region by appointment

Norwich, New London, and Willimantic meetings are by appointment. The firm
keeps no staffed office in the region and publishes no street address for
this location, because honesty about presence belongs to the same
discipline that shapes its reports. Engagements typically begin remotely,
with records review, structured interviews, and standardized testing under
publisher protocols where remote administration is supported. Where the
referral questions call for an in-person evaluation, or where deposition
and trial dates require the evaluator's presence, travel is arranged from
the Kansas City home office with the court's calendar as the fixed point.

## Reading the local labor market

Vocational findings for evaluees in the region draw on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the metropolitan
area and for Connecticut statewide, together with state workforce agency
labor market information and direct labor market research where the case
warrants it. Life care plans and medical cost projections are priced to
the evaluee's own market, using provider-specific inquiries and published
fee data for the region rather than national averages. Because every
source is cited and every inference is written out, opposing counsel can
test the opinion against the data instead of against the expert's
reputation, which is precisely how the firm believes expert work should be
tested.

## Opening an engagement

An engagement from southeastern Connecticut opens with clearance. Submit
the caption or party names through the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/), and note any imminent disclosure,
deposition, or trial dates so the schedule can be set realistically from
the start. Once the conflict check clears, counsel receive a defined
scope, a claims-tailored records request, and a timeline built backward
from the forum's deadlines. For matters that extend beyond Connecticut, or
for firms comparing the practice across jurisdictions, the firm's
[nationwide coverage](/locations/nationwide/) explains how the same
methodology and report standard travel to every venue it serves.
