West Memphis occupies an unusual position in its own metro: it is an Arkansas
city in a metropolitan area named for a principal city that sits across the
state line in Tennessee. For counsel, that geography is not trivia. It shapes
which court hears the case, which state's procedural calendar governs
disclosure, and which labor market defines an injured worker's realistic
employment options. Purinton Analytics serves plaintiff and defense counsel
with West Memphis matters through
[vocational evaluation and expert testimony](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis
while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement
structure.

## Crittenden County venue and the Arkansas forums

West Memphis sits in Crittenden County, and state-court matters arising there
proceed in the Circuit Court. In federal practice, Arkansas counsel litigate
in the United States District Court for the Eastern District of Arkansas and
the United States District Court for the Western District of Arkansas; the
district and division controlling a particular matter, along with any
venue-specific registration or qualification requirement, is identified and
confirmed during the conflict check and intake process. Because the
surrounding metro reaches into Tennessee and Mississippi, some referrals that
begin with a West Memphis evaluee are ultimately venued outside Arkansas, and
the firm's [Memphis metro page](/locations/tennessee/memphis/) describes how
engagements are handled on the Tennessee side of the metro. Statewide coverage,
including the courts beyond this county, is outlined on the
[Arkansas page](/locations/arkansas/).

## The labor market question a state line creates

The central vocational dispute in a West Memphis case is often definitional:
is the evaluee's commutable labor market the Arkansas side alone, or the
broader Memphis metropolitan area that spans Tennessee, Mississippi, and
Arkansas? The answer changes the occupational base, and a report that never
addresses the question invites cross-examination. The firm's analyses draw on
Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the
Memphis metropolitan area and for Arkansas statewide, together with state
workforce agency labor market information and direct labor market research
where the referral questions warrant it. The written opinion states which
market was used and why, so that the foundation is visible to both sides
rather than implied.

## Evaluation logistics for a West Memphis evaluee

The firm keeps no office in West Memphis and publishes no street address for
this location; candor about presence is part of the same discipline that
governs its reports. Meetings are by appointment. Vocational evaluations can
be conducted remotely when the referral questions and testing protocols permit
it, or scheduled in the evaluee's own community when in-person administration
is preferable, which spares an injured worker unnecessary travel. When
deposition or trial testimony requires presence, travel is scheduled from the
Kansas City home office and planned against the case calendar rather than
improvised. The same engagement model supports counsel far beyond this metro,
as described on the [nationwide practice page](/locations/nationwide/).
