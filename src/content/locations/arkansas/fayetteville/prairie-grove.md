## From restrictions to the regional market

The analytical path in an employment or injury matter runs from the
evaluee's documented restrictions and demonstrated work history to the
occupations realistically available in the regional economy. Prairie
Grove belongs to the Fayetteville-Springdale-Rogers, AR metropolitan
area, so that regional economy is the relevant one, and the evidence
comes from Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage
estimates for the Fayetteville metropolitan area and for Arkansas
statewide, from state workforce agency labor market information, and from
direct labor market research when the referral questions require it.
Where an injury case also demands long-horizon care costs, the firm
prepares a [life care plan](/services/life-care-planning/) priced through
provider-specific inquiries and published regional fee data, and
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) is available
when the other side's expert has already opined.
