governs a specific Bentonville case is identified during the conflict check
and intake, together with any venue-specific registration or qualification
requirement. Counsel with matters spread across the state can consult the

## Testimony that survives the record

What distinguishes an opinion that survives a motion challenge from one
that does not is usually the record beneath it. Labor market conclusions
for a Bentonville evaluee are built on Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates for the Fayetteville
metropolitan area and for Arkansas statewide, on state workforce agency
labor market information, and on direct employer inquiries when the
referral questions call for them. Transferable skills findings are tied to
occupations that exist in the regional economy, and care-plan pricing rests
on provider-specific quotes and published regional fee data. The report
names its sources because a court should not have to take the expert's
word for anything.
