Catastrophic injury litigation asks a question that outlasts the lawsuit:
what will this person's care require, for how long, and at what cost in
the market where the person actually lives. For a Farmington resident,
Purinton Analytics answers that question with a
[life care plan](/services/life-care-planning/) built item by item from
the medical record, clinician recommendations, and provider-specific
pricing, paired where the case requires it with a
[medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/) or an
[employability and earning capacity evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/).
The plan is written to be examined, which is why counsel for plaintiffs
and counsel for defendants both retain the firm to prepare or to test one.

## Washington County and the available forums

Farmington lies in Washington County, and state-court matters proceed in
the Circuit Court there. Federal matters in Arkansas are heard in the
United States District Court for the Western District of Arkansas or the
United States District Court for the Eastern District of Arkansas, and
the district that controls a particular case is confirmed during the
conflict check and intake, along with any venue-specific registration or
qualification requirement. The firm's engagement across the rest of the
state is summarized on the [Arkansas page](/locations/arkansas/).

## Pricing care and work to the real market

A life care plan priced to a national average describes no market in
particular, so each Farmington plan is priced through provider-specific
inquiries and published regional fee data for the evaluee's own area. The
vocational side of the analysis follows the same principle. Farmington is
part of the Fayetteville-Springdale-Rogers, AR metropolitan area, and
earning capacity conclusions rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates for the Fayetteville
metropolitan area and for Arkansas statewide, on state workforce agency
labor market information, and on direct labor market research when the
referral questions justify it. Where lifetime costs and lost earnings
must become a present-value damages figure, the firm offers
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Evaluation logistics for a Farmington evaluee

The firm has no staffed office in Farmington and publishes no street
address for this location; that plain statement is part of the discipline
counsel is hiring. Care-planning interviews, records analysis, and
standardized testing proceed remotely wherever publisher protocols permit,
which spares a catastrophically injured evaluee unnecessary travel. When
assessment is better done in person, including in-home assessment for
care planning, the visit happens in the evaluee's own community, with
travel scheduled from the Kansas City home office. Deposition and trial
appearances are calendared the same way, as they are throughout the
firm's [national practice](/locations/nationwide/), and neighboring
communities are covered as described on the
[Fayetteville metro page](/locations/arkansas/fayetteville/).

## How counsel begins

Every Farmington engagement opens with a documented conflict check,
followed by a written scope and a records request tailored to the claims.
[Request a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) with the case caption and
parties, and note the evaluee's care situation and any imminent
disclosure or trial dates, so the plan of work can reflect both the
medicine and the docket from the outset.
