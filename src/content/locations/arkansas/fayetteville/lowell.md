Often the first call about a Lowell case comes after the other side's
expert report has already landed. Counsel wants to know whether the
opposing vocational or care-planning opinion is as solid as it looks, and
that is precisely the question Purinton Analytics is structured to answer.
In a [rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/)
engagement, the opposing opinion is tested against its own stated sources
and methods: whether the cited data says what the report claims, whether
the testing followed publisher protocols, whether the conclusions follow
from the findings. The review is the same exercise the firm applies to its
own drafts before they go out, which is why it works for plaintiff and
defense counsel alike.

Lowell is located in Benton County, and state-court proceedings are heard

## The affirmative side of the practice

Rebuttal work is only part of the practice. When counsel needs an affirmative
opinion, the firm provides
[employability and earning capacity evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/)
and [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) built on the same
sources a rebuttal would probe: Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational
employment and wage estimates for the Fayetteville metropolitan area and
for Arkansas statewide, state workforce agency labor market information,
and direct labor market research where the questions require it. Lowell
belongs to the Fayetteville-Springdale-Rogers, AR metropolitan area, so
the relevant market is the regional one, and the report says exactly which
data supports each conclusion. An opinion written that way needs no
defending later; it defends itself.

## Starting with a conflict check

Whether the need is a rebuttal on a tight response deadline or an
affirmative evaluation at the start of discovery, the entry point is
identical: [request a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) with the case
caption and the names of the parties. For rebuttal referrals, include the
date the response is due and, once the check clears, the opposing report
and its appendices, because the review begins with the sources the
opposing expert chose.
