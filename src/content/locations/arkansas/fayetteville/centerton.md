An earning capacity opinion is a chain of inferences, and every link in it
should hold when opposing counsel pulls. For a Centerton matter, Purinton
Analytics forges that chain the same way each time. The evaluee's
education, training, and demonstrated work history are established from
records and structured interview, never from assumption. Transferable
skills analysis converts that history into the occupations it genuinely
supports. Published data then shows what those occupations look like in
the relevant market. Because each step is documented and sourced, an
[employability and earning capacity evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/)
from the firm reads the same whether plaintiff or defense counsel
commissioned it, and either side can retrace it from conclusion back to
record.

## Benton County and the courts that hear Centerton cases

Centerton is a Benton County community, and matters filed in state court
proceed in the Circuit Court there. Arkansas federal litigation is heard
in the United States District Court for the Western District of Arkansas
and the United States District Court for the Eastern District of
Arkansas; the forum that controls a particular Centerton case, along with
any registration or qualification requirement that forum imposes, is
confirmed during the conflict check and intake rather than assumed at the
outset. The firm's work in venues across the rest of the state is
described on the [Arkansas locations page](/locations/arkansas/).

## Labor market evidence without guesswork

The data layer of a Centerton opinion is drawn from sources an opposing
expert can independently verify: Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational
employment and wage estimates for the Fayetteville-Springdale-Rogers, AR
metropolitan area and for Arkansas statewide, state workforce agency
labor market information, and, where the referral questions warrant it,
direct labor market research with employers. The same discipline governs
a [life care plan](/services/life-care-planning/): every recommended item
of future care carries a stated source and a price researched for the
market where the evaluee will actually obtain it. When lost earnings must
be carried through to a present-value damages figure, the firm structures
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## How the evaluation reaches Centerton

Purinton Analytics keeps no staffed office in Centerton and publishes no
street address for this location. Accuracy about presence is part of the
same discipline the firm applies to its opinions, so the arrangement is
stated plainly: most phases of an engagement proceed remotely, including
records review, structured interviews, and standardized testing under
publisher-supported remote protocols. When the referral questions call
for in-person evaluation, it is conducted in the evaluee's own community,
and appearances for deposition or trial are scheduled with travel from
the Kansas City home office. The surrounding communities of the metro are
served on the same model, described on the
[Fayetteville metro page](/locations/arkansas/fayetteville/), and the
practice extends to matters anywhere in the country through the firm's
[national engagement model](/locations/nationwide/).

## Starting with a conflict check

Every Centerton engagement begins the same way, whichever side of the
caption makes the call: a documented conflict check, then a written
scope, then a records request tailored to the claims. Counsel should
