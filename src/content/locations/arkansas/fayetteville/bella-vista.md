For an injured person living in Bella Vista, the practical questions about
a vocational evaluation come first: where it happens, who travels, and how
much of the process can be done without leaving home. Purinton Analytics
designs the engagement around the evaluee rather than the other way
around. Interviews and records work proceed remotely, standardized testing
is administered under remote publisher protocols where those protocols
allow it, and when the referral questions require an in-person
[vocational evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) or a home
assessment for a [life care plan](/services/life-care-planning/), the
evaluation comes to Bella Vista instead of summoning the evaluee to a
distant office. Plaintiff and defense referrals are handled identically;
the logistics serve the evidence, not the retaining party.

## What by-appointment service means here

Purinton Analytics keeps no staffed office in Bella Vista and lists no
street address for this location, and the firm says so rather than
implying otherwise, because honesty about presence is of a piece with
honesty in the reports. What counsel and evaluees get instead is a
practice engineered for distance: secure remote sessions for most phases,
in-community visits when the work is better done in person, and travel
from the Kansas City home office when deposition or trial testimony
requires presence. The [metro page](/locations/arkansas/fayetteville/)
covers the neighboring communities served under the same model, and the
[national practice](/locations/nationwide/) page explains how the model
scales to any venue in the country.

## First step for counsel

Before logistics, before scheduling, before any discussion of the merits,
there is a conflict check. It protects both sides and it is never skipped.
Counsel with a Bella Vista matter should
[request a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) with the caption and party
names, and mention early whether the evaluee has mobility, transportation,
or scheduling constraints, so the evaluation plan can be shaped around the
person it concerns. Where opposing opinions are already in the file,
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) can be scoped
in the same intake.
