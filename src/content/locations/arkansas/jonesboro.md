Counsel trying cases in the Circuit Courts of Craighead County and Poinsett
County, or in the United States District Court for the Eastern District of
Arkansas, retain Purinton Analytics for
[earning capacity and employability evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/), and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. The firm works for plaintiffs and for defendants in
the Jonesboro, AR metropolitan area, and the reports read the same way
regardless of which side sends the retention letter: scoped in writing,
grounded in records, and prepared for cross-examination.

## Where Jonesboro matters are venued

The metropolitan area consists of Craighead County and Poinsett County, and
state court engagements are accepted for matters pending in the Circuit
Court of either. In federal court, Jonesboro matters are supported
in the United States District Court for the Eastern District of Arkansas,
and the firm also appears in the United States District Court for the
Western District of Arkansas when a related matter is venued there. The
firm's approach to the rest of the state, from statewide labor market
questions to travel logistics, is set out on its
[Arkansas page](/locations/arkansas/).

## Serving Jonesboro without a local office

There is no staffed Purinton Analytics office in Jonesboro, and no street
address is published for this location. That is stated plainly because
honesty about presence is part of the product: a firm that would imply a
local storefront it does not keep should not be trusted to describe an
evaluee's limitations accurately either. Engagements proceed remotely where
the work supports it, through records review, structured interviews by
video, and standardized testing under publisher protocols where remote
administration is permitted. In-person evaluations, depositions, and trial
appearances are scheduled as travel from the Kansas City home office and
planned around the court's calendar.

## Reading the Jonesboro labor market

Vocational conclusions for evaluees in the region rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Jonesboro
metropolitan area and for Arkansas statewide, together with state workforce
agency labor market information and direct labor market research where the
referral questions warrant it. Because the employment base of a regional
market differs from that of the largest metros, transferable skills analysis
is run against the occupations that actually exist in the evaluee's labor
market, not against a national composite, and care costs are priced to the
evaluee's own region using provider-specific inquiries and published fee
data.

## Retaining the firm for a Jonesboro matter

The engagement process for a Jonesboro matter mirrors the firm's
[national practice](/locations/nationwide/): a conflict check before any
substantive case discussion, a written scope before any opinion work, and
disclosure materials assembled with deposition in mind. Any venue-specific
registration or qualification requirement is identified and confirmed during
the conflict check and intake process. To begin,
[request a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) with the case caption and
parties, and identify any disclosure, deposition, or trial dates that bear
on scheduling.
