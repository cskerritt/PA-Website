Most expert retentions arrive with a clock already running: a disclosure
deadline, a discovery cutoff, a trial setting. Purinton Analytics structures
its El Dorado engagements around that reality, sequencing the conflict
check, records request, evaluation, and report so that Union County counsel
know at intake what will be ready and when. The work itself spans
[vocational expert evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) of
employability and earning capacity,
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) in catastrophic injury
cases, and [rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) when
an opposing report needs a disciplined answer before the response deadline.
Plaintiff and defense engagements are handled on identical terms.

## Union County venues, state and federal

Engagements are accepted for matters in the Circuit Court of Union County
and, where a case proceeds in federal court, in the United States District
Court for the Eastern District of Arkansas or the United States District
Court for the Western District of Arkansas, depending on where the action is
filed. Federal settings bring their own disclosure rhythm, and reports are
drafted from the outset to meet it rather than retrofitted late. Counsel
whose practice covers other Arkansas venues will find the statewide summary
on the [Arkansas locations page](/locations/arkansas/).

## Scheduling around the case, not around an office

There is no staffed Purinton Analytics office in El Dorado, and no street
address is published for this location; meetings and evaluations are by
appointment. That arrangement is an asset for deadline management rather
than a limitation: because most of the engagement runs remotely, records
review, structured interviews, and standardized testing under publisher
protocols can begin as soon as the conflict check clears, without waiting on
travel. When the referral questions call for in-person evaluation, or when a
deposition or trial date requires presence, travel is scheduled from the
Kansas City home office and locked to the case calendar counsel provides.

## Labor market and cost research for Union County evaluees

Vocational findings rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational
employment and wage estimates for the El Dorado area and for Arkansas
statewide, on state workforce agency labor market information, and on direct
labor market research where warranted. Life care plans and
[medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/) are priced to
the evaluee's own community through provider-specific inquiries and
published fee data. Where the damages case requires an economic loss
calculation, the firm offers
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure, so a full damages package can be scheduled as a single
sequence instead of separate retentions. The identical structure is
available to counsel [nationwide](/locations/nationwide/).

## Starting before the deadline gets close

The engagement opens with a documented conflict check and a defined scope,
and earlier is always better: a report written against a comfortable
schedule is easier to defend than one written against the wire. Submit the
caption or party names through the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and
identify every disclosure, deposition, and trial date already set. Any
venue-specific registration or qualification requirement is identified and
confirmed during the conflict check and intake process.
