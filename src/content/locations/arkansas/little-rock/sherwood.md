The person at the center of a Sherwood case is usually not the lawyer but the evaluee: an injured worker, an accident victim, or a claimant whose future earning capacity and care needs the litigation must price. Purinton Analytics approaches Sherwood engagements from that starting point, structuring each [vocational evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) and [life care plan](/services/life-care-planning/) around the individual's records, capacities, and community, and delivering opinions that plaintiff and defense counsel can each put before a fact-finder with confidence.

## County and Court Context

Sherwood is a Pulaski County city, and disputes arising there ordinarily proceed in the Circuit Court when filed in state court. Arkansas maintains two federal trial forums, the United States District Court for the Eastern District of Arkansas and the United States District Court for the Western District of Arkansas; rather than assume which one applies, the firm confirms the controlling venue with counsel during the conflict check and intake, along with any qualification requirement the forum imposes. Because the report may be read by a judge, a jury, and an opposing expert, it is drafted with a stated methodology and a complete record citation trail from the first page. Broader venue discussion appears on the [Arkansas state page](/locations/arkansas/).

## Sherwood's Place in the Regional Economy

Sherwood functions as a residential city within a much larger metropolitan employment market, which means the relevant question is rarely what jobs exist inside Sherwood but what work the evaluee can secure across the region. Earning capacity findings therefore rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Little Rock metropolitan area and for Arkansas statewide, corroborated by state workforce agency labor market information and by targeted employer research when an opinion turns on actual hiring practice. The same sources support [rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) work when counsel needs an opposing report tested rather than a new one built.

## Interview and Testing Logistics

Sherwood evaluees are not asked to organize their recovery around the firm's convenience. Interviews and standardized vocational testing are offered remotely under protocols that preserve test integrity, and in-person evaluation is arranged in the evaluee's own community when the referral questions or counsel's strategy call for it. The firm has no office in Sherwood and publishes no local address; candor about presence is part of the same discipline that governs its written opinions. In-person evaluations, depositions, and trial appearances are handled as scheduled travel from the Kansas City home office, coordinated with the litigation calendar counsel provides.
