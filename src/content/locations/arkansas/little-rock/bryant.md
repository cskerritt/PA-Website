A vocational report earns its weight in the file, not the courtroom, and a Bryant engagement with Purinton Analytics begins there: with the medical records, the earnings history, the education and training documents, and a scope agreed with counsel before analysis starts. From that record the firm develops [vocational expert](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) opinions and [life care plans](/services/life-care-planning/) for Bryant matters on behalf of whichever side retains it, plaintiff or defense, applying one methodology to both.

## Courts Serving Bryant Matters

Bryant is part of Saline County, and its state court disputes are ordinarily resolved in the Circuit Court. Federal litigation proceeds in one of the state's two districts, the United States District Court for the Eastern District of Arkansas or the United States District Court for the Western District of Arkansas; the firm identifies the controlling district, and any venue-specific qualification requirement that comes with it, during the conflict check and intake process rather than asserting it in advance. A survey of Arkansas courts as a whole appears on the [state page](/locations/arkansas/), and the surrounding regional court context is addressed on the [Little Rock metro page](/locations/arkansas/little-rock/).

## Labor Market Foundations

The transferable skills analysis for a Bryant evaluee proceeds in ordered steps: residual functional capacities are taken from the medical record, the pre-injury occupation is profiled, and the remaining occupational base is matched against the regional market in which Bryant participates. Market evidence comes from Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Little Rock metropolitan area and for Arkansas statewide, from state workforce agency labor market information, and from direct labor market research where a disputed opinion needs employer-level verification. The report presents this qualitatively and cites each source, so that an opposing expert reviewing the file can locate every foundation, which is also the standard the firm applies when performing [rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) of others' work.

## Local Evaluation, National Practice

Bryant evaluees can complete interviews and standardized testing remotely, under administration conditions that preserve validity, or in person within their own community when counsel requests it. The firm operates without a Bryant office and without a published local address, a fact stated openly because the practice's credibility rests on precision about small things as much as large ones. In-person evaluations, depositions, and trial appearances are scheduled as travel from the Kansas City home office, the same way the firm serves venues throughout its [national practice](/locations/nationwide/). Report drafts are calendared against the disclosure deadlines counsel identifies at intake, so the written opinion is complete and reviewed before the date it is due, not finished on it.

## Submitting a Conflict Check

When counsel needs the economic loss quantified alongside the vocational and care findings, the firm arranges [coordinated damages assessments](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement structure. To retain the firm for a Bryant matter, [send the caption and party names through the conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and include any disclosure or trial dates already set. Conflicts are screened before substance is discussed, and engagement terms are confirmed in writing before the first record is opened.
