The test of a vocational opinion is not whether it persuades the lawyer
who commissioned it but whether it survives the lawyer who did not.
Purinton Analytics writes for that second reader. In Greenwood matters,
whether the firm is retained by plaintiff or defense counsel, the
[vocational evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) and the
[life care plan](/services/life-care-planning/) are built from a defined
scope, a tailored records request, and methods that are named in the
report itself, so cross-examination becomes a walk through documented
steps rather than a search for undisclosed assumptions. That standard does
not change with the size of the town or the size of the claim.

Greenwood sits in Sebastian County, and matters filed locally proceed in

Arkansas; which forum controls a given Greenwood case, and any
registration or qualification requirement that forum imposes, is confirmed

## A Greenwood evaluee in a regional market

Employability for a Greenwood resident is a question about the wider
labor market of the Fort Smith metropolitan area, not about Greenwood in
isolation. The firm grounds its findings in Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates covering that metropolitan
area and Arkansas statewide, in labor market information published by the
state workforce agency, and in direct employer and occupation research
where the referral questions require it. Numbers never appear without a
named source, transferable skills are analyzed against occupations that
actually exist in the regional data, and the resulting opinion states its
limits as plainly as its conclusions.

## Appointments, testing, and travel

There is no Purinton Analytics storefront in Greenwood, and the firm does
not pretend otherwise; every meeting is by appointment, and no street
address is published for this location. Most phases of an engagement run
remotely: records analysis, structured interviews, and standardized
testing wherever the test publisher's protocols permit remote
administration. If the case calls for an in-person evaluation, it is
conducted in the evaluee's community at a scheduled time, with travel
arranged from the Kansas City home office. Testimony follows the same
logistics model the firm applies to its
[national practice](/locations/nationwide/), so a Sebastian County trial
date is handled with the same reliability as one in the firm's home
market.

## Retaining the firm for a Greenwood matter

Retention begins with a conflict check, not a consultation, because
nothing substantive should be shared before clearance. Counsel can
[submit the caption or party names](/refer-a-case/) and note any
disclosure deadlines, noticed depositions, or trial settings already on
the docket. After clearance, the firm issues a written scope and begins
with the records, and where economic damages are also in issue it can add
[coordinated damages assessment](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/),
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.
