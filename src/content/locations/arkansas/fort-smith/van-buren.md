County. Federal practice in the state runs through the United States

identified during the conflict check and intake rather than assumed. The

## Reading the labor market around Van Buren

Earning capacity in Van Buren cannot be assessed from the town alone,
because its residents work across a regional market anchored by Fort
Smith. Employability and earning capacity opinions for a Van Buren evaluee
therefore rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and
wage estimates for the Fort Smith metropolitan area and for Arkansas
statewide, supplemented by state workforce agency labor market information
and by direct labor market research when a specific occupation or employer
question warrants it. Each source appears in the report by name, and each
analytical step is documented, so that opposing counsel can retrace the
path from data to conclusion. Findings that cannot be retraced are not
offered.
