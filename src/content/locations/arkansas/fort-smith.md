The Fort Smith, AR-OK metropolitan area crosses a state line, and the
litigation that arises there often does too. Purinton Analytics serves
plaintiff and defense counsel throughout the metro with
[earning capacity and employability evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) in catastrophic injury
matters, and [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. Because the firm's practice is national by design, a
case that spans the Arkansas and Oklahoma sides of the metro is handled under
a single methodology rather than split between local experts on each side of
the line.

## The venue footprint, on both sides of the line

On the Arkansas side, the metro takes in Crawford County and Sebastian
County, and the firm accepts matters pending in the Circuit Court of each,
as well as federal matters in the United States District Court for the
Western District of Arkansas and, where a case is venued there, the United
States District Court for the Eastern District of Arkansas. On the Oklahoma
side, the metro includes Sequoyah County, Oklahoma, and matters arising in
the state and federal courts serving it are supported through the same
national practice. Counsel with cases elsewhere in the state will find the
firm's broader [Arkansas coverage](/locations/arkansas/) described in the
same terms.

## Presence by appointment, candor about offices

The firm keeps no staffed office in Fort Smith and publishes no street
address for this location, because accuracy about presence belongs to the
same discipline that governs its written opinions. Engagements typically
begin remotely, with records review, structured interviews, and standardized
testing under publisher protocols where remote administration is supported.
When the referral questions call for an in-person evaluation, or when a
deposition or trial requires an appearance, travel is scheduled from the
Kansas City home office. Counsel are told at intake which phases of the
engagement will be remote and which in person, so the logistics live in the
written scope from the beginning. The report standard and the point of
contact are the same whether the work is performed remotely or in the
evaluee's community.

## Labor market evidence for a metro that crosses a state line

Vocational findings for evaluees in the region rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Fort Smith
metropolitan area, which cover the metro as a whole, together with statewide
estimates for Arkansas and for Oklahoma as the evaluee's residence and labor
market require. State workforce agency labor market information and direct
labor market research supplement the published estimates where warranted, and
life care plans are priced to the evaluee's own market using
provider-specific inquiries and published regional fee data.

## Beginning a Fort Smith engagement

Whether the caption sits in an Arkansas Circuit Court, an Oklahoma venue, or
federal court, the intake sequence matches the firm's
[national practice](/locations/nationwide/): conflicts first, scope second,
records third. Any venue-specific registration or qualification requirement
is identified and confirmed during the conflict check and intake process.
To begin, submit the caption or party names through the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and note any imminent disclosure,
deposition, or trial dates.
