The Harrison micropolitan area takes in Boone County and Newton County, and
Purinton Analytics serves counsel across both from a single engagement
structure. A case captioned in Harrison raises the same damages questions a
case in any larger venue does: what work an injured person can still
perform, what that work pays in the market where the person actually lives,
and what future care will cost. The firm answers those questions through
[vocational expert evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[expert testimony and litigation consulting](/services/expert-testimony-litigation-consulting/),
for retaining counsel on either side of the caption.

## Courts serving Boone and Newton counties

State court matters proceed in the Circuit Court of Boone County and the
Circuit Court of Newton County, and engagements are accepted for either
venue. Federal matters from the area are heard in the United States District
Court for the Eastern District of Arkansas or the United States District
Court for the Western District of Arkansas, depending on where the action is
filed, and the firm's reports and disclosures are prepared to the standard
federal practice expects. Counsel handling related matters in other parts of
the state can review coverage on the
[Arkansas locations page](/locations/arkansas/).

## Presence, stated plainly

There is no staffed Purinton Analytics office in Harrison, and the firm
publishes no street address for this location. Meetings and evaluations are
by appointment. That candor is deliberate: a practice that asks jurors and
judges to trust its market research should be equally precise about its own
footprint. In practice, most Harrison engagements proceed remotely, from the
initial records review through structured interviews and standardized
testing where publisher protocols support remote administration. When an
evaluation should occur in person, or when a deposition or trial requires
attendance, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office and
confirmed against the case calendar, the same arrangement the firm applies
to engagements [nationwide](/locations/nationwide/).

## Grounding opinions in the local labor market

Employability and earning capacity findings for evaluees in Boone County and
Newton County rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and
wage estimates for the area and for Arkansas statewide, along with state
workforce agency labor market information and direct labor market research
where warranted. Rural labor markets are treated on their own terms rather
than approximated from metropolitan data, because transferable skills
analysis is only as good as the market it is tested against. Care costs in
[life care plans](/services/life-care-planning/) are priced to the evaluee's
own community using provider-specific inquiries and published fee data, and
when the referral requires an economic loss calculation, independent
economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis while the
vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement structure
through a [coordinated damages engagement](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/).

## Opening a Harrison matter

Engagement begins with a documented conflict check and a defined scope, so
the eventual written opinion rests on a record both sides can examine. To
begin, [request a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) with the case caption and
parties, and identify any disclosure, deposition, or trial dates already
set. Any venue-specific registration or qualification requirement is
identified and confirmed during the conflict check and intake process.
