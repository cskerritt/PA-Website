## No local storefront, and why that is said out loud

The firm maintains no staffed office in Blytheville and publishes no street
address for this location. Meetings and evaluations are by appointment, and
most engagements begin remotely: records review, structured vocational
interviews, and standardized testing under publisher protocols where remote
administration is supported. When an evaluation should be conducted in
person, or when a deposition or trial requires presence, travel is scheduled
from the Kansas City home office. A firm that expects opposing counsel to
probe every line of its reports should not blur the facts of its own
footprint, so the by-appointment arrangement is stated plainly rather than
dressed up as a local office.

## Reading the Blytheville labor market honestly

Vocational findings for Mississippi County evaluees rest on Bureau of Labor

[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure, the same arrangement offered to counsel
[nationwide](/locations/nationwide/).
