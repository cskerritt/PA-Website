Purinton Analytics provides vocational expert and life care planning services
to plaintiff and defense counsel across the Little Rock-North Little
Rock-Conway, AR metropolitan area. Referrals from the metro most often
call for [earning capacity and employability evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/)
in personal injury and employment matters,
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) in catastrophic injury
cases, and [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. Each file begins with a documented conflict check, a
defined scope, and a records request tailored to the claims, so that the
resulting opinion can be examined on its record by either side of the
caption.

## Venue footprint across the metro counties

The metropolitan area takes in Faulkner County, Grant County, Lonoke County,
Perry County, Pulaski County, and Saline County, and engagements are accepted
for matters pending in the Circuit Court of each. Federal matters are
supported in the United States District Court for the Eastern District of
Arkansas and in the United States District Court for the Western District of
Arkansas, depending on where the case is venued. Counsel with matters
elsewhere in the state can review the firm's
[Arkansas coverage](/locations/arkansas/), which follows the same engagement
standards described here.

## Meetings by appointment, not a storefront

The firm maintains no staffed office in Little Rock and publishes no street
address for this location; candor about presence is part of the same
discipline that governs the reports themselves. Most engagements proceed
remotely from intake through disclosure: records review, structured
interviews by video, and standardized testing under publisher protocols where
remote administration is supported. When the referral questions require
in-person evaluation, or when a deposition or trial date requires presence,
travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office. Counsel receive the
same report standard and a single point of contact regardless of where the
work is performed.

## Labor market research for Little Rock evaluees

Vocational findings for evaluees in the metro rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Little Rock
metropolitan area and for Arkansas statewide, supplemented by state workforce
agency labor market information and direct labor market research where the
referral questions warrant it. Life care plans and
[medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/) are priced to
the evaluee's own market rather than to national averages, using
provider-specific inquiries and published fee data for the region, so the
cost conclusions describe the care environment the evaluee actually lives in.

## Starting a Little Rock engagement

The practice is national, and the procedures described on the
[nationwide coverage page](/locations/nationwide/) apply in Little Rock
without modification: the same intake sequence, the same disclosure
discipline, and the same willingness to have every conclusion tested at
deposition. Any venue-specific registration or qualification requirement is
identified and confirmed during the conflict check and intake process. To
begin, submit the caption or party names through the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and note any imminent disclosure,
deposition, or trial dates so scheduling can be addressed at intake rather
than discovered later.
