## Reading the labor market for a Southside evaluee

Employability and earning capacity opinions for a Southside evaluee begin with the labor market that person can realistically reach, which typically means employment within Independence County and the broader Batesville area rather than a distant urban center. The analysis rests on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates covering the Batesville area and Arkansas statewide, on state workforce agency labor market information, and on direct labor market research when the referral questions call for it. Because a smaller market offers a narrower range of occupations, the report says so plainly and documents its sources, rather than importing assumptions from larger metropolitan data that would not survive cross-examination.

## How an evaluation proceeds here

The firm keeps no staffed office in Southside and publishes no local street address, because candor about presence belongs to the same discipline that governs its opinions. Most work proceeds remotely: records analysis, structured vocational interviews conducted by video, and standardized testing administered under documented remote protocols. When the referral requires an in-person assessment, the evaluation is held in the evaluee's own community, with travel scheduled from the Kansas City home office; depositions and trial appearances are planned the same way, around the court's calendar. In catastrophic injury cases, [medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/) work can accompany the life care plan, and counsel who also need economic damages can use [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement structure.
