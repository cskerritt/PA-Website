An evaluee living in White County should be evaluated against the labor
market and care environment of White County, not against conditions imported
from a distant metro. That principle shapes how Purinton Analytics serves
counsel in the Searcy, AR micropolitan area, whether the referral calls for
[earning capacity and employability evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), or
[medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/). The firm
accepts these engagements from plaintiff and defense counsel on identical
terms, and the finished report is written so that either side of the caption
can trace every conclusion back to a record, a test result, or a cited data
source.

## The White County venue picture

The micropolitan area consists of White County, and state court engagements
are accepted for matters pending in its Circuit Court. Federal matters from
the region are supported in the United States District Court for the Eastern
District of Arkansas and in the United States District Court for the Western
District of Arkansas, wherever the case is venued. For counsel whose
caseload runs beyond White County, the firm's statewide engagement approach
is set out on the [Arkansas page](/locations/arkansas/), and it does not
change from venue to venue.

## Evaluation logistics for a Searcy area evaluee

The firm keeps no staffed office in Searcy and publishes no street address
for this location; meetings and evaluations happen by appointment. Much of
an engagement proceeds remotely, through records review, structured
interviews by video, and standardized testing under publisher protocols
where remote administration is supported. When the referral questions are
better answered in person, the evaluation is conducted in the evaluee's own
community rather than by requiring travel to the expert, with the trip
scheduled from the Kansas City home office. Depositions and trial
appearances are handled the same way, planned around the court's calendar
and confirmed at intake.

## Local labor market data, used honestly

Vocational findings for evaluees in the region rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates and on Arkansas state
workforce agency labor market information, applied at the geography that
best matches where the evaluee actually lives and works. Where published
estimates thin out for a smaller market, the report says so and supplements
them with direct labor market research rather than quietly substituting
statewide or national figures. Care costs in life care plans and cost
projections are priced through provider-specific inquiries and published fee
data for the evaluee's own region, since the availability of local providers
is itself a finding that belongs in the plan. The report cites each source
it relies on, and the path from record to conclusion is documented so that
either party can retrace it.

Retention follows the same sequence used across the firm's

substantive discussion, a written scope before analysis, and disclosure
prepared with cross-examination in mind. Any venue-specific registration or
qualification requirement is identified and confirmed during the conflict
check and intake process. To begin,
[request a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) with the case caption and
parties, and note any imminent disclosure, deposition, or trial dates.
