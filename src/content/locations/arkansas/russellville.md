A smaller venue deserves the same evidentiary rigor as the largest one, and
the Russellville, AR micropolitan area is served on exactly that premise.
Purinton Analytics provides
[earning capacity and employability evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) of opposing
expert work to plaintiff and defense counsel with matters in Pope County and
Yell County. Nothing about the methodology is scaled down for a regional
market: the conflict check is documented, the scope is written before the
opinion work begins, and the report is drafted on the assumption that the
lawyer across the caption will read every line of it.

## Venue coverage across the micropolitan area

The micropolitan area comprises Pope County and Yell County, and state court
engagements are accepted for matters pending in the Circuit Court of either
county. Federal matters are supported in the United States District Court
for the Western District of Arkansas and in the United States District Court
for the Eastern District of Arkansas, according to where the case is venued.
The firm's engagement standards for the rest of the state, including
statewide labor market research and travel logistics, are described on its
[Arkansas page](/locations/arkansas/).

## No local office, and why that is stated plainly

Purinton Analytics keeps no staffed office in Russellville and publishes no
street address for this location. Counsel are told this directly because the
firm treats accuracy about its own presence as a preview of how it treats
accuracy about an evaluee's work history. In practice, the absence of a
storefront changes little: records review, structured interviews, and
standardized testing under publisher protocols proceed remotely where remote
administration is supported, and when an in-person evaluation, deposition,
or trial appearance is needed, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home
office around the court's dates.

## Labor market findings sized to the actual market

Vocational conclusions for evaluees in the region rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates and on state workforce
agency labor market information for Arkansas, applied at the geography that
matches the evaluee's actual labor market. In a regional market, the
question of what work exists for a person with given restrictions is
narrower than in a major metro, and the analysis says so rather than
borrowing occupational density from somewhere else. Direct labor market
research supplements the published estimates where the referral questions
warrant it, and care-planning costs are priced to the evaluee's own region
through provider-specific inquiries and published fee data. Each source is
cited in the report, and the reasoning from data to conclusion is laid out
step by step so that it can be tested at deposition rather than defended by
assertion.
