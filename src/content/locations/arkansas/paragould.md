Purinton Analytics provides vocational expert and life care planning services
to attorneys litigating in Paragould and the surrounding Greene County area.
Referrals from this part of Arkansas typically involve
[earning capacity and employability evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/)
in injury and employment matters,
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) and
[medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/) in catastrophic
injury cases, and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. Plaintiff and defense counsel receive the same
methodology, the same disclosure practices, and the same report standard.

## Venue footprint in Greene County

Engagements are accepted for matters venued in the Circuit Court of Greene
County and, when a case proceeds in federal court, in the United States
District Court for the Eastern District of Arkansas or the United States
District Court for the Western District of Arkansas, according to where the
action is filed. The Paragould micropolitan area defines a labor market for
vocational purposes, but the practice is not confined to it: counsel with
matters elsewhere in the state will find the statewide picture on the
[Arkansas locations page](/locations/arkansas/), and the same standards
govern engagements accepted [nationwide](/locations/nationwide/).

## Meetings by appointment

The firm keeps no staffed office in Paragould and publishes no street
address for this location, because accuracy about presence belongs to the
same discipline that governs the reports. Meetings, evaluations, and
testimony are scheduled by appointment, and most engagements begin remotely
with records review, structured interviews, and standardized testing under
publisher protocols where remote administration is supported. When the
referral questions call for an in-person evaluation, or when deposition and
trial dates require presence, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home
office. Counsel are told plainly which parts of the work are performed
remotely, which are performed in person, and why the distinction does not
change the written product.

## Labor market research for Paragould evaluees

Vocational findings for evaluees in the Paragould area rest on Bureau of
Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates covering the
local area and Arkansas statewide, supplemented by state workforce agency
labor market information and direct employer contact where the referral
questions warrant it. Life care plans and medical cost projections for area
residents are priced to the evaluee's own market rather than to national
averages, using provider-specific inquiries and published fee data. Where an
opposing report is already on file,
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) follows the same
sourcing standards, so any criticism of another expert's market data is
grounded in the references the firm relies on affirmatively.

## Starting a Paragould engagement

Every engagement begins the way it does everywhere else in the practice:
with a documented conflict check, a defined scope, and a records request
tailored to the claims, so that the written opinion rests on a record either
side of the caption can examine. Submit the caption or party names through
the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and note any imminent disclosure,
deposition, or trial dates so scheduling can be confirmed at the outset. Any
venue-specific registration or qualification requirement is identified and
confirmed during the conflict check and intake process.
