[Vocational expert witness](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) opinions and [life care plans](/services/life-care-planning/) for Trumann cases follow the same methodology, the same sourcing discipline, and the same documentation the firm applies across [Arkansas](/locations/arkansas/) and beyond, and they are available to plaintiff and defense counsel on identical terms.

## Poinsett County venue for Trumann matters

Trumann sits in Poinsett County, and state court engagements arising here proceed in the Circuit Court. In federal practice, a case may be filed in or removed to the United States District Court for the Eastern District of Arkansas or the United States District Court for the Western District of Arkansas; the practice does not guess which district controls a particular dispute, because pleadings, parties, and removal can each change the answer. The controlling forum, together with any registration or qualification requirement it imposes on a testifying expert, is confirmed during the conflict check and intake.

## Labor market sources for a Trumann opinion

An employability or earning capacity finding is only as strong as its sources, so a Trumann opinion names them. Vocational findings draw on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the [Jonesboro, AR metropolitan area](/locations/arkansas/jonesboro/) and for Arkansas statewide, on state workforce agency labor market information, and on direct labor market research when a referral question requires contact with the market itself. Employment within Poinsett County and across the wider metro area is weighed against the evaluee's transferable skills and commuting tolerance, and every step from data to conclusion is set out in the written opinion so either side can retrace it.

## How the work reaches Trumann

The firm keeps no staffed office in Trumann and publishes no street address here; meetings are by appointment, and candor about presence is part of the same discipline that governs the reports. Most engagements proceed remotely, through records analysis, structured video interviews, and standardized instruments administered under documented remote protocols. When the referral questions call for in-person assessment, or when deposition or trial dates require presence, the evaluation or appearance is scheduled in the evaluee's community and travel proceeds from the Kansas City home office. For catastrophic injury cases, [medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/) work is available where the medical record supports it, and [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) allow independent economist partners to perform and sign the economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement structure.
