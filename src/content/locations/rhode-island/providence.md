The Providence-Warwick metropolitan area crosses a state line, and litigation practice here follows the geography: a single case may involve an evaluee who lives in Providence County, treats with providers in Bristol County, Massachusetts, and last worked for an employer in Kent County. Purinton Analytics serves counsel throughout this cross-state metro with [vocational expert witness](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) analysis, [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and [medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/) work, accepting engagements from plaintiff and defense counsel alike. The firm also provides [rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) of opposing vocational and life care plan opinions, along with [expert testimony and litigation consulting](/services/expert-testimony-litigation-consulting/) at deposition and trial. When a matter requires economic modeling of lost earnings or the cost of future care, the firm offers [coordinated damages assessments](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement structure.

## Venue footprint across the metro

The Providence-Warwick, RI-MA metropolitan statistical area takes in Providence County, Kent County, Washington County, Newport County, and Bristol County on the Rhode Island side, along with Bristol County, Massachusetts. Engagements are accepted for matters in the Superior Court, where Rhode Island injury and employment claims are tried, and in the United States District Court for the District of Rhode Island. For claims filed on the Massachusetts side of the metro, the same intake discipline applies, and any venue-specific registration or qualification requirement is identified and confirmed during the conflict check and intake process. Reports are written to the same standard regardless of forum: documented methodology, a defined scope, and opinions that rest on a record either side of the caption can examine.

## Meetings by appointment

Providence meetings are by appointment. The firm keeps no staffed office in Rhode Island and publishes no street address for this location, because accuracy about presence is part of the same discipline that governs the reports themselves. Most engagements proceed remotely from the first call through report delivery: records arrive electronically, evaluee interviews are conducted by secure video where appropriate, and counsel conferences are scheduled around deposition calendars. When the referral questions call for in-person evaluation, or when deposition and trial dates require presence, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office and planned around the court's calendar rather than the firm's.

## Labor market research for Rhode Island evaluees

Vocational findings for evaluees in this metro rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Providence-Warwick area and for Rhode Island statewide, supplemented by state workforce agency labor market information and direct labor market research where warranted. Because the metro reaches into Massachusetts, an evaluee's realistic labor market may straddle the state line; where that is true, the analysis draws on the estimates for the geography the evaluee actually faces rather than defaulting to a single state series. Transferable skills analysis, employability opinions, and labor market surveys are documented so that opposing counsel can trace each conclusion to its source.
