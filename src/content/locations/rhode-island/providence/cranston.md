retention, the real question is what the eventual forum will demand of the
opinion: a written report that discloses its methods, a witness who can
defend every source at deposition, and conclusions that stay inside the
referral questions. Purinton Analytics is organized around those demands.

[life care plans](/services/life-care-planning/) after catastrophic injury,
and [coordinated damages assessments](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one

in comparable measure, and every report is drafted so that either could
have commissioned it.

## Providence County venue for Cranston matters

Cranston sits in Providence County, and state-law claims arising there are
tried in the Superior Court. Where a case carries a federal footing, the
United States District Court for the District of Rhode Island is the
federal forum available within the state; the venue that will actually
control a given matter, together with any registration or qualification
requirement it imposes on a testifying expert, is identified and confirmed
during the conflict check and intake. Opinions are prepared to the same
standard for either bench: defined scope, reproducible methodology, and
sources cited so opposing counsel can retrace every step. Counsel with
matters spread across the surrounding region will find the broader
coverage described on the [Providence metro page](/locations/rhode-island/providence/)
and statewide practice on the [Rhode Island page](/locations/rhode-island/).

## Reading the Cranston labor market

Cranston is one of the larger cities in Rhode Island, and its workforce
competes across the Providence-Warwick, RI-MA metropolitan area rather
than within municipal lines. Vocational findings for a Cranston evaluee
therefore rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and
wage estimates for the Providence-Warwick area and for Rhode Island
statewide, supplemented by state workforce agency labor market information
and direct labor market research where the referral questions warrant it.
The report states which geography the analysis treats as genuinely open to
the evaluee and defends that framing with documented restrictions, work
history, and transportation rather than assumption, because an
unsupported market definition is the first target of a capable
cross-examination.

## Appointments, not an office

The firm keeps no staffed office in Cranston and publishes no street
address here; candor about presence is part of the same discipline that
governs the written opinions. Most engagements proceed remotely from
intake through report delivery, with records exchanged electronically and
interviews conducted by secure video where appropriate. When the referral
questions call for in-person evaluation, it takes place in the evaluee's
own community, where function and access to services can be observed
directly, and travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office.
Deposition and trial appearances are planned around the dates the court
has set, not the other way around.
