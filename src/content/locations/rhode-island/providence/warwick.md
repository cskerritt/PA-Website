The metropolitan area that organizes labor market data for this corner of
New England carries Warwick's name. The Providence-Warwick, RI-MA
designation reflects the city's weight in the regional economy, and it
matters to litigators because the wage and employment series experts rely
on are published for that named area. Purinton Analytics serves Warwick
counsel on both sides of the docket with
[vocational expert](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) analysis of
employability and earning capacity,
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) for serious injury,
and [rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) of
opposing expert work. When lost earnings or future care must be reduced
to present value, the firm offers
[coordinated damages assessments](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Kent County and the courts that serve it

Warwick lies in Kent County, and Rhode Island tort and employment claims
from the city proceed in the Superior Court. For matters with a federal
dimension, the United States District Court for the District of Rhode
Island is the district forum available in the state; which court controls
a particular case, and what qualification or registration it may require
of a testifying expert, is settled during the conflict check and intake
rather than assumed in advance. The wider venue picture for the
surrounding communities appears on the
[Providence metro page](/locations/rhode-island/providence/), and the
[Rhode Island page](/locations/rhode-island/) describes practice in the
rest of the state.

## Warwick's place in the regional labor market

An earning-capacity opinion for a Warwick evaluee begins from Bureau of
Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the
Providence-Warwick area and for Rhode Island statewide, with state
workforce agency labor market information and targeted employer research
layered on where the questions call for it. Because Warwick's workforce
moves throughout the metro, the analysis must say explicitly whether the
evaluee is treated as confined to work near home or as competing across
the broader area, and it must ground that choice in restrictions,
transferable skills, and documented history. A report that leaves the
market definition implicit invites the cross-examination it deserves,
whichever party offered it.

## Meeting counsel and evaluees by appointment

There is no Purinton Analytics office in Warwick, and the firm will not
pretend otherwise; stating presence accurately is part of the credibility
the reports depend on. Engagements ordinarily run remotely, with secure
video interviews, electronic records exchange, and counsel conferences
fitted to the deposition calendar. Where the referral questions justify
an in-person assessment, the evaluation is conducted in the evaluee's own
community so that daily function is observed rather than inferred, with
travel arranged from the Kansas City home office. Testimony dates are
planned around the court's schedule from the outset, so the opinion is
never the reason a deadline slips.
