## Measuring a cross-state market honestly

Because the metro spans Rhode Island and Massachusetts, a credible
vocational opinion for a Woonsocket evaluee cannot default to a single
state's data out of habit. Findings rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates for the Providence-Warwick
area and for Rhode Island statewide, and where the evaluee's realistic
market reaches across the line, the analysis draws on the estimates for
the geography that evaluee actually faces. State workforce agency labor
market information and direct employer research supplement the federal
series when the referral questions require it. The report then defends
its market definition with the evaluee's restrictions, transferable
skills, transportation, and work history, because the definition, not
the arithmetic, is where these opinions are won or lost.
