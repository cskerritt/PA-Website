Purinton Analytics provides vocational expert and life care planning services for litigation venued anywhere in Kansas. The practice treats every Kansas matter the same way regardless of where it sits: the earning capacity of an injured worker in a small Kansas town is evaluated under the same documented methodology as that of an evaluee in the state's largest metropolitan areas, because the standard is set by the record, not the venue. Plaintiff and defense engagements are accepted on the same terms, and every written opinion is prepared to withstand examination by the other side of the caption.

## Kansas venues and courts

Engagements are accepted for matters in the Kansas District Courts and, for federal litigation, in the United States District Court for the District of Kansas. Whatever the forum, the work product is the same: a documented conflict check, a defined scope, a records request tailored to the claims, and a written opinion that discloses its methods and sources so that either side can test them.

Within the state, the practice maintains dedicated pages for the metropolitan areas it serves: Wichita, Topeka, Manhattan, Lawrence, Hutchinson, Salina, Pittsburg, Garden City, Emporia, Arkansas City-Winfield, Dodge City, McPherson, Hays, Ottawa, and Great Bend. Those pages add local venue and labor market context; they mark depth of coverage, not its limits. A matter venued outside every one of them enters the same intake and receives the same analysis.

## How the firm serves Kansas

The practice is remote-first. Records review, vocational interviews, transferable skills analysis, and life care plan development proceed by secure document exchange and videoconference, which keeps schedules predictable for counsel and evaluees alike. Meetings in Kansas are by appointment. The firm keeps no staffed office in the state and publishes no street address for any Kansas location, because honesty about presence belongs in the same category as honesty about data. When the referral questions call for in-person evaluation, or when deposition and trial dates require presence, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office.

Vocational findings for Kansas evaluees rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for Kansas statewide and for its metropolitan areas, state workforce agency labor market information, and direct labor market research where warranted. Where a case also requires economic damages, the firm offers coordinated damages engagements in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement structure.

## Qualification and disclosure

Expert disclosure practice varies by forum, and the firm prepares its reports, file materials, and testimony history so that counsel can meet whichever standard applies. Any venue-specific registration or qualification requirement is identified and confirmed during the conflict check and intake process.
