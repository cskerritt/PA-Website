## Courts serving Fairmont and Marion County

The Fairmont micropolitan area consists of Marion County, and state-court
matters from the region are venued in the Circuit Court. Federal cases
proceed in the United States District Court for the Northern District of
West Virginia or the United States District Court for the Southern District
of West Virginia, according to where the action is filed. The firm prepares
disclosures, sits for depositions, and testifies at trial in state and
federal forums under the same methodology, so counsel do not receive a
different quality of work product depending on the courthouse. Matters
arising elsewhere in the state are described on the
[West Virginia locations page](/locations/west-virginia/), and the firm's
[nationwide practice](/locations/nationwide/) covers venues beyond it.

## How a Fairmont engagement actually runs

There is no staffed Purinton Analytics office in Fairmont, and the firm
publishes no street address here; meetings are by appointment only, and
saying so plainly is part of the practice's discipline. The working pattern
for most Marion County matters is remote-first: counsel transmit records
through a defined request list, vocational interviews are conducted by
scheduled video or telephone session, and standardized instruments are
administered under publisher protocols where remote testing is appropriate.
When the case requires an in-person evaluation, a site visit, or live
testimony, travel is arranged from the Kansas City home office and planned
against the litigation calendar so that disclosure and deposition deadlines
are met without compressed work.

## Grounding opinions in the local labor market

Employability and earning capacity opinions for Fairmont-area evaluees are
grounded in Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage
estimates covering the relevant area and West Virginia statewide, in state
workforce agency labor market information, and in direct labor market
research when the referral questions call for it. The analysis addresses the
labor market the evaluee can realistically access from Marion County, and
where commuting patterns or the evaluee's restrictions change that market,
the report explains the adjustment rather than assuming it. Every source is
cited so that opposing counsel can check the work, which is the standard the
firm applies to its own reports before either side ever sees them.

## Requesting a conflict check for a Fairmont matter

Engagements begin with a conflict check, never with a sales conversation.
Send the case caption or party names through the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/), flag any imminent disclosure,
deposition, or trial dates, and identify the referral questions as precisely
as the case allows. Any venue-specific registration or qualification
requirement is identified and confirmed during the conflict check and intake
process. Once conflicts clear and scope is defined, work on a Fairmont
matter can begin promptly, whether the need is a full evaluation, a
[medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/), or
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) of an opposing
expert's opinions.
