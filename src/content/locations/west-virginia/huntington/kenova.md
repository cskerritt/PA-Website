## Wayne County courts and the federal option

Kenova lies in Wayne County, and a state-law claim arising there proceeds
in the Circuit Court. Where a claim belongs in federal court, the forums
available in West Virginia are the United States District Court for the
Northern District of West Virginia and the United States District Court
for the Southern District of West Virginia; the district that controls a
particular case, and any registration or qualification requirement tied
to it, is established during the conflict check and intake. The firm
prepares each report to withstand review in whichever forum the case
lands, since the choice is often unresolved when the expert work begins.

## Small town, regional market

A credible employability opinion for a Kenova evaluee cannot stop at the
town line, because the working population here participates in the labor
market of the Huntington-Ashland, WV-KY-OH metropolitan area. The firm
frames its findings with Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational
employment and wage estimates for the Huntington-Ashland metropolitan
area and for West Virginia statewide, alongside state workforce agency
labor market information and direct labor market research when the
referral questions call for that added depth. The analysis then narrows
from market to person: which of the occupations that exist in this region
remain genuinely available to this evaluee, given the medical
restrictions, the education, and the work history in the record, with the
reasoning laid out step by step for the opposing expert to test.

## When the work must happen in person

Video does not replace everything, and the firm does not claim it does.
In-person vocational evaluation, when the referral requires it, is
conducted in the evaluee's own community, where functional capacity and
practical commuting limits can be seen rather than inferred; depositions
and trial appearances are attended wherever the court sits. All such
travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office, which is the
firm's only office. No Kenova address exists, and none is implied.
