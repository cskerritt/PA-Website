The Huntington-Ashland metropolitan area spans West Virginia, Kentucky,
and Ohio, and that is the defining fact for vocational analysis in
Milton: an evaluee's realistic job search can cross a state line without
ever leaving the commuting area. An opinion that ignores the market on
the far side of the border understates or overstates employability,
depending on which party it serves, and neither error survives careful
cross-examination. Purinton Analytics writes Milton opinions to the
regional reality, for retaining counsel on either side, across
[vocational expert opinions](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)

## Venue: local courts for a regional market

However far the labor market analysis reaches, venue stays grounded: a
state-law claim arising in Milton proceeds in the Circuit Court, Milton
being a Cabell County town. Claims within federal jurisdiction look to
the United States District Court for the Northern District of West
Virginia or the United States District Court for the Southern District of
West Virginia, and the district that governs a specific matter, along
with any registration or qualification requirement, is determined during
the conflict check and intake. Counsel comparing forums across the
region will find the broader discussion on the
[Huntington metro page](/locations/west-virginia/huntington/) and the
statewide practice on the [West Virginia page](/locations/west-virginia/).

## Measuring a cross-border labor market

Grounding a Milton opinion means using data drawn to the same boundaries
the workers actually cross. The firm relies on Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates for the Huntington-Ashland
metropolitan area, which is defined across the state lines the commuting
patterns ignore, and for West Virginia statewide, supplemented by state
workforce agency labor market information and by direct labor market
research when the referral questions warrant it. From that base, the
report reasons down to the individual: the occupations this evaluee can
still perform, the ones foreclosed by documented restrictions, and the
practical reach of each option from a Milton address, stated so an
opposing expert can test every step.

## Local evaluations, remote efficiency

Purinton Analytics keeps no office in Milton and claims none; the
practice here is by appointment. The engagement runs remote wherever
remote is sound, with secure video covering counsel conferences, records
work, and most vocational interviews, and it goes in person where the
evidence demands it, with evaluations conducted in the evaluee's own
community and testimony given wherever the case is heard. Travel for
those appearances originates at the Kansas City home office and is
planned against the case schedule, not the other way around. That split
between remote analysis and in-person observation is documented in the
report itself, so the record shows what was seen directly and what was
reviewed on paper.
