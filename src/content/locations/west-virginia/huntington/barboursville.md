Counsel with a Barboursville filing usually want to know where the
case will be tried and whether the answer changes the expert work. The
map itself is settled. Barboursville is part of Cabell County, so a

matter within federal jurisdiction belongs to the United States
District Court for the Southern District of West Virginia or to the
United States District Court for the Northern District of West
Virginia, with the controlling district confirmed during the conflict
check and intake along with any registration or qualification
requirement that venue imposes. Whether the forum changes the work is
a different matter: no in substance, yes in formality. The opinion's
method and candor stay constant from county courthouse to federal
bench, while disclosure timing and testimony format do shift, and
those are calendared at intake so the forum never surprises the
