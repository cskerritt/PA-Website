Clarksburg is a micropolitan rather than a metropolitan market, and that
distinction changes nothing about the work product. Counsel throughout the
area receive the same report architecture, the same sourcing standards, and
the same testimony preparation the firm brings to its largest venues.
Purinton Analytics serves plaintiff and defense engagements across the
Clarksburg micropolitan area with
[vocational evaluation and expert testimony](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Testimony and the venues that require it

The Clarksburg area takes in Harrison County, Doddridge County, and Taylor
County, and engagements are accepted for matters in the Circuit Court
serving each. Federal matters are served in the United States District Court
for the Northern District of West Virginia and the United States District
Court for the Southern District of West Virginia. Deposition and trial
testimony are treated as part of the engagement from the start, not an
afterthought: the report is structured so that direct examination can walk
through it cleanly, and the file is organized so that every exhibit,
source, and calculation referenced in testimony is already in the record
produced to the other side.

## Appointment logistics for the area

Clarksburg meetings are by appointment. The firm keeps no staffed office
here and publishes no street address for this location, because accuracy
about presence is part of the same discipline that governs its opinions.
Records review, counsel conferences, and most evaluee interviews are
conducted remotely, which keeps engagements on schedule for counsel in all
of the area's counties. When the referral questions call for in-person
evaluation, or when a deposition or trial date requires presence in a local
courtroom, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office and
coordinated with counsel well ahead of the date. The same structure carries
[engagements nationwide](/locations/nationwide/), so counsel who try cases
beyond the area can bring the same expert to each venue.

## Labor market foundations

Vocational findings for Clarksburg-area evaluees rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Clarksburg
area and for West Virginia statewide, state workforce agency labor market
information, and direct labor market research where warranted. Opinions
about earning capacity and placeability are limited to what that evidence
supports, and the report says so explicitly, which is what allows the
opinions to hold up when challenged. The same standards drive the firm's
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) practice, in
which an opposing expert's report is examined for the fit between its
methodology, its cited data, and its conclusions.
