Martinsburg is the West Virginia anchor of a metropolitan area that carries
its name alongside Hagerstown's, and litigation here has a distinctly
cross-border character: a Berkeley County case is tried under West Virginia
law while the evaluee's job prospects often extend into Maryland. Purinton
Analytics is built for that kind of file. The firm provides
[vocational expert witness services](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) to counsel on
either side of the caption, and it writes every opinion as though the
sharpest reader will be the lawyer across the aisle.

## Berkeley County and the West Virginia forums

State-court matters arising in Martinsburg proceed in the Circuit Court,
which serves Berkeley County and sets the disclosure and testimony
expectations the firm's reports are prepared to meet. On the federal side,
West Virginia litigants have the United States District Court for the
Northern District of West Virginia and the United States District Court for
the Southern District of West Virginia as available forums; the controlling
venue for a particular case, and any qualification or registration
requirement that comes with it, is identified during the conflict check and
intake. The firm's work across the rest of the state is described on the
[West Virginia locations page](/locations/west-virginia/).

## An employment base shared with Maryland

For a Martinsburg evaluee, the relevant labor market is the
Hagerstown-Martinsburg metropolitan area as a whole, not the West Virginia
portion alone, and vocational conclusions are drawn accordingly. The firm
grounds its employability and earning-capacity findings in Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the relevant area
and for West Virginia statewide, in state workforce agency labor market
information, and in direct labor market research when the referral
questions require testing what employers in the region would actually hire.
Sources are cited conclusion by conclusion, which lets a deposition test
the analysis on its merits. The Maryland side of the market, and the metro
as a whole, is covered on the
[Hagerstown metro page](/locations/maryland/hagerstown/).

## Logistics for counsel and for the evaluee

The firm maintains no staffed office in Martinsburg and publishes no local
street address, a matter of accuracy rather than modesty: the same
discipline that keeps invented facts out of the reports keeps invented
offices off the website. Engagements run remote-first, with records review,
transferable skills analysis, and counsel conferences conducted by secure
exchange and video on a schedule established at intake. In-person
vocational evaluations and life care planning assessments are arranged by
appointment in the evaluee's own community, keeping the burden of travel
off the injured party. When depositions or trial dates require the expert
in the courtroom, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office.
Cases that need an economic damages analysis can proceed as
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.
