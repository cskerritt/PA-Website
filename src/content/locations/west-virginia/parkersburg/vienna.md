## Reading the commuting market around Vienna

An earning-capacity opinion for a Vienna evaluee turns on a framing
decision that must be defended, not assumed: is the realistic labor
market the immediate community, the wider commuting market centered on
Parkersburg, or something broader still? Vocational findings rest on
Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for
the Parkersburg-Vienna, WV metropolitan area and for West Virginia
statewide, on state workforce agency labor market information, and on
direct employer contact where the referral questions require it. The
report states which market definition the analysis uses and grounds that
choice in the evaluee's restrictions, transportation, and documented work
history. That discipline protects whichever party retained the opinion,
because a market drawn for convenience rarely survives a capable
cross-examiner.

## Evaluation logistics for a Vienna evaluee

Purinton Analytics keeps no office in Vienna and publishes no local
address, because candor about presence belongs to the same discipline
that governs the reports. Meetings are by appointment. Most engagements
proceed remotely, with secure video carrying records conferences,
counsel discussions, and the majority of vocational interviews. When the
referral questions call for an in-person evaluation, it takes place in
the evaluee's own community, where daily function and actual access to
services can be observed rather than inferred, with travel scheduled
from the Kansas City home office. Deposition and trial appearances are
planned around the dates the court has already set, so scheduling never
becomes the weak point of an otherwise sound opinion.
