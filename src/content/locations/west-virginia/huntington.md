The Huntington-Ashland metropolitan area reaches across the state lines of
West Virginia, Kentucky, and Ohio, and litigation here often does the same:
an evaluee may live, treat, and face a caption in different states without
ever leaving the metro. Purinton Analytics serves plaintiff and
defense counsel throughout this cross-state market with
[vocational evaluation and expert testimony](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Venue footprint across the metro

On the West Virginia side, engagements are accepted for matters in the
Circuit Court serving Cabell County, Putnam County, and Wayne County, and in
the United States District Court for the Southern District of West Virginia
and the United States District Court for the Northern District of West
Virginia. The Kentucky portion of the metro takes in Boyd County, Carter
County, Greenup County, and Lawrence County, Kentucky, while the Ohio
portion takes in Lawrence County, Ohio; the firm accepts referrals for
matters in the state and federal courts that serve those counties as well.
Report format, disclosure timing, and testimony logistics are confirmed for
the specific venue during intake, so counsel on either side of the caption
receives work product prepared for the court that will actually hear it.

## Meetings by appointment, not a local storefront

Huntington meetings are by appointment. The firm keeps no staffed office in
the metro and publishes no street address for this location, because
accuracy about offices is part of the same discipline that governs its
reports. Records review, counsel conferences, and most evaluee interviews
proceed remotely, which keeps scheduling responsive across the metro's
communities. When the referral questions call for in-person evaluation, or
when deposition and trial dates require presence, travel is scheduled from
the Kansas City home office and planned around the court's calendar. The
same structure supports [engagements nationwide](/locations/nationwide/),
so a Huntington referral that grows into filings elsewhere stays with the
same expert.

## Labor market research for a cross-state economy

Vocational findings for Huntington-Ashland evaluees rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the
Huntington-Ashland metropolitan area and for each of the states the metro
touches, supplemented by state workforce agency labor market information and
direct labor market research where warranted. Because the commuting area
crosses state lines, transferable-skills analysis and placeability opinions
account for the labor market the evaluee can actually reach, not just the
state of filing. Each opinion is documented so that its sources and
reasoning can be examined by retaining and opposing counsel alike, and
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) engagements are
accepted when the assignment is to test another expert's methodology rather
than to author an original report.
