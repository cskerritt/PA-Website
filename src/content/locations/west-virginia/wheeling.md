Meetings in Wheeling are by appointment, and Purinton Analytics says so
plainly: the firm keeps no staffed office in the metro and publishes no
street address here, because honesty about presence is part of the same
discipline that governs its reports. What counsel across the Wheeling
metropolitan area retains instead is a national practice serving plaintiff
and defense engagements alike, offering
[vocational evaluation and expert testimony](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),

## The metro's courts on both sides of the state line

The Wheeling metropolitan area pairs Ohio County and Marshall County in West
Virginia with Belmont County in Ohio. On the West Virginia side, engagements
are accepted for matters in the Circuit Court serving Ohio County and
Marshall County, and in the United States District Court for the Northern
District of West Virginia and the United States District Court for the
Southern District of West Virginia. For Belmont County matters, referrals
are accepted for the state and federal courts that serve that county, with
report format and disclosure practice confirmed for the specific venue
during intake. Whichever side of the state line the caption sits on, the
opinion is written so that both sides of the case can examine the record it
rests on.

## How appointments and travel work

Records review, counsel conferences, and most evaluee interviews proceed
remotely, which keeps the engagement moving between hearing dates without
anyone leaving their own office. Scheduling is coordinated directly with counsel's
office, and intake confirms the dates that matter before the scope is
signed. When the referral questions call for in-person
evaluation, or when a deposition or trial date requires the expert present
in a Wheeling-area courtroom, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home
office and coordinated with counsel around the court's calendar. Because the
practice is structured for [engagements nationwide](/locations/nationwide/),
the same expert and the same file follow a matter if it moves to another
venue entirely.

## Evidence about the Wheeling labor market

Vocational findings for Wheeling-area evaluees rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Wheeling
metropolitan area and for the states on each side of the metro, state
workforce agency labor market information, and direct labor market research
where warranted. Because commuting patterns here cross the state line,
placeability and earning capacity opinions consider the labor market the
evaluee can actually reach rather than an arbitrary boundary. Each report
cites its data sources in full, and when the assignment is to evaluate
another expert's work instead,
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) engagements
apply the same sourcing standards to the report under review.
