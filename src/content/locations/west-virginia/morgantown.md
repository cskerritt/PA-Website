What Morgantown counsel receives from Purinton Analytics is a defined scope
of expert work rather than a local storefront: vocational evaluation and
earning capacity analysis, life care planning, medical cost projection, and
testimony to support each of them. The practice serves plaintiff and defense
counsel across the Morgantown metropolitan area through
[vocational expert witness engagements](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Where Morgantown matters are heard

The metro takes in Monongalia County and Preston County, and engagements are
accepted for matters in the Circuit Court serving each of them. Federal
matters are served in the United States District Court for the Northern
District of West Virginia and the United States District Court for the
Southern District of West Virginia. Before any analysis begins, the firm
documents its conflict check, defines the scope in writing, and requests the
records the claims put at issue, so the finished opinion rests on a file
both parties can inspect. Disclosure
deadlines and testimony dates are confirmed at intake and drive the work
plan from the outset of the engagement, so the report is complete and
produced before the court's schedule makes completeness expensive.

## Logistics for a by-appointment market

Morgantown meetings are by appointment. The firm keeps no staffed office in
the metro and publishes no street address for this location, because
accuracy about presence belongs in the same category as accuracy about
sources. Most of the work does not require a local office at all: records
review, counsel conferences, and many evaluee interviews proceed remotely on
a schedule the parties control. When the referral questions call for
in-person evaluation, or when deposition and trial dates require presence in
Monongalia County or Preston County, travel is scheduled from the Kansas
City home office. The same remote-first model supports
[engagements nationwide](/locations/nationwide/), which matters when a
Morgantown client's litigation touches courts outside the metro.

## Labor market evidence for Morgantown evaluees

Vocational findings for Morgantown-area evaluees rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Morgantown
metropolitan area and for West Virginia statewide, state workforce agency
labor market information, and direct labor market research where warranted.
Transferable-skills analysis and placeability opinions are grounded in the
occupations realistically available to the evaluee, and each report cites
its sources so that opposing counsel can trace every conclusion back to the
data behind it. Where counsel needs an existing report tested rather than a
new one written, [rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/)
engagements evaluate whether another expert's methodology supports the
opinions offered.
