Keyser occupies the West Virginia side of a metropolitan area whose
principal city, Cumberland, lies across the state line in Maryland. That
geography shapes the litigation as much as the commute: a Mineral County
case may be tried in a West Virginia courtroom while the evaluee's
realistic job market extends into another state. Purinton Analytics
prepares vocational and life care planning opinions that take that
two-state reality seriously, for plaintiff and defense engagements alike,
drawing on [vocational expert witness work](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) of reports
served by the other side.

## Venue context for Mineral County matters

State-court claims arising in Keyser proceed in the Circuit Court, and the
firm prepares its written opinions to satisfy that court's disclosure and
testimony expectations. In federal practice, West Virginia offers its
litigants the United States District Court for the Northern District of
West Virginia and the United States District Court for the Southern
District of West Virginia; which of those forums controls a given case,
and any venue-specific qualification or registration requirement that
follows from it, is identified during the conflict check and intake. The
firm's coverage across the state is set out on the
[West Virginia locations page](/locations/west-virginia/).

## Reading a cross-state labor market honestly

Because the Cumberland metropolitan area spans the Maryland and West
Virginia line, an earning capacity opinion for a Keyser evaluee cannot be
built from West Virginia data alone or from Maryland data alone; it has to
reflect where the work actually is. The firm grounds its employability and
earning capacity findings in Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational
employment and wage estimates for the relevant area and for West Virginia
statewide, alongside state workforce agency labor market information and
direct labor market research where the referral questions call for it. The
report names its sources at every step, which lets either side probe the
methodology on cross-examination rather than speculate about it. The
broader market is discussed on the
[Cumberland metro page](/locations/maryland/cumberland/).

## Seeing a Keyser evaluee

There is no Purinton Analytics storefront in Keyser and no local street
address to publish, and the firm says so plainly, because a report is only
as credible as the practice behind it. The engagement follows a defined
path instead. Document review, transferable skills analysis, and most
attorney conferences take place remotely on a timetable set at intake.
When an in-person vocational evaluation or life care planning assessment
is warranted, it is scheduled by appointment in or near the evaluee's own
community, so that an injured person is not asked to travel far and the
assessment stays anchored in the market that person will actually enter.
Travel for evaluations, depositions, and trial appearances is arranged
from the Kansas City home office. Where the damages model requires an
economist, the firm offers
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.
