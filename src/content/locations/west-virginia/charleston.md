Counsel litigating in the Charleston metropolitan area, whether the caption
sits in Kanawha County, Boone County, or Clay County, retain Purinton
Analytics for the same reason counsel elsewhere do: a written vocational or
life care planning opinion built to withstand examination by the other side.
The firm accepts plaintiff and defense engagements throughout the metro for
[vocational evaluation and expert testimony](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/),
[medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/), and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Courts served in the Charleston metro

Engagements are accepted for matters in the Circuit Court serving Kanawha
County, Boone County, and Clay County, and in the United States District
Court for the Southern District of West Virginia and the United States
District Court for the Northern District of West Virginia. Personal injury,
wrongful death, employment, and workers' compensation matters each carry
their own disclosure rhythms, and the engagement letter fixes scope and
deadlines against the schedule of the court where the matter is pending.
Because the same methodology supports the report whichever party retains the
firm, opinions are written so that either side of the caption could put the
underlying record in front of a jury.

## Appointment-based service, honestly described

There is no staffed Purinton Analytics office in Charleston, and the firm
publishes no street address here; candor about presence is part of the same
discipline that governs its reports. Counsel conferences, records review,
and most evaluee interviews are handled remotely, which shortens intake
timelines for the metro's counties. When an in-person evaluation is
warranted, or when a deposition or trial date requires the expert in the
courtroom, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office with
appearances planned around the court's calendar. The identical remote-first
structure carries [engagements nationwide](/locations/nationwide/), so a
matter that begins in Charleston and spreads to other venues does not need a
new expert in each of them.

## Grounding opinions in the local labor market

Vocational findings for Charleston-area evaluees rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Charleston
metropolitan area and for West Virginia statewide, state workforce agency
labor market information, and direct labor market research where warranted.
Earning capacity and placeability opinions are tied to the occupations the
evaluee can actually perform and the market where those occupations exist,
with every source cited in the report. When the assignment is defensive, the
firm's [rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) work
applies the same standards to another expert's report, identifying where
methodology and cited data do or do not support the stated conclusions.
