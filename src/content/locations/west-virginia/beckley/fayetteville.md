## Where a Fayetteville matter is heard

Fayetteville lies in Fayette County, and state-law civil claims from the
town proceed in the Circuit Court. Federal claims have two possible homes
within the state, the United States District Court for the Northern
District of West Virginia and the United States District Court for the
Southern District of West Virginia; which forum controls, and what it
asks of a testifying expert by way of qualification or registration, is
settled during the conflict check and intake rather than assumed. Every
report is prepared as if the toughest reader in the courtroom will study
it, because in a small-town case the expert's written work often carries
more weight than any local reputation could. Counsel handling matters
across the surrounding region can consult the
[Beckley metro page](/locations/west-virginia/beckley/); the
[West Virginia page](/locations/west-virginia/) describes the statewide
practice.

## Labor market evidence for a small-town evaluee

Fayetteville is a small town, and honest vocational work acknowledges
what that means: the employment realistically open to an injured resident
cannot be read off a national table. Opinions here draw on Bureau of
Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the
Beckley, WV metropolitan area and for West Virginia as a whole, on state
workforce agency labor market information, and on direct employer
research where the questions demand it. The analysis then addresses the
threshold issue squarely: whether this evaluee's restrictions,
transportation, and history confine the search to the town, extend it
toward Beckley, or leave the wider market open. Naming that assumption in
the report, and defending it, is what keeps the opinion standing when the
other side's examiner probes it.

## Evaluation logistics from a distance done honestly

There is no Purinton Analytics office in Fayetteville, and no address is
published for the town, because accuracy about presence belongs to the
same discipline as accuracy in the report. Most of the work proceeds
remotely: secure video for interviews, conferences, and records review
sessions with counsel. Where the referral requires observation that video
cannot supply, the in-person evaluation happens in the evaluee's own
community, with travel planned from the Kansas City home office and
timed against the litigation calendar the engagement was built around.
Testimony, whether by deposition or at trial, is scheduled the same way.
