Oak Hill is among the larger communities on the Fayette County side of the
Beckley, WV metro area, and its workforce moves in both directions: some
residents earn their living in town, others commute toward Beckley and the
wider region. That split matters in litigation, because an earning-capacity
opinion that ignores where an Oak Hill evaluee could actually work invites
cross-examination it cannot survive. Purinton Analytics is retained by
plaintiff counsel and defense counsel alike to get that question right,
providing [vocational expert opinions](/services/vocational-expert-witness/)
on employability and earning capacity,
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) after catastrophic
injury, and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Fayette County venue for Oak Hill matters

Civil actions arising in Oak Hill under West Virginia law are heard in the
Circuit Court serving Fayette County. Where a case carries federal
jurisdiction, West Virginia offers two forums, the United States District
Court for the Northern District of West Virginia and the United States
District Court for the Southern District of West Virginia; the district
that will actually control a given matter, along with any registration or
qualification requirement it imposes on a testifying expert, is identified
and confirmed during the conflict check and intake. Reports are written to
the same standard regardless of bench: methods a stranger could reproduce,
sources cited in full, and opinions held strictly to the disclosed scope.
Regional coverage is described on the
[Beckley metro page](/locations/west-virginia/beckley/), and statewide
practice on the [West Virginia page](/locations/west-virginia/).

## Situating an Oak Hill evaluee in the metro labor market

The vocational analysis begins with the labor market genuinely available
to this evaluee, not the market that makes either side's damages theory
convenient. Findings rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational
employment and wage estimates for the Beckley, WV metropolitan area and
for West Virginia statewide, supplemented by state workforce agency labor
market information and by direct labor market research when the referral
questions call for it. The written opinion states whether the evaluee is
treated as confined to Oak Hill itself, as able to reach work across the
metro commuting area, or as mobile beyond it, and it grounds that judgment
in documented restrictions, transportation, and work history so the
framing holds up under examination from either table.

## Meeting and evaluating in Oak Hill

The firm maintains no office in Oak Hill and will not pretend otherwise;
candor about presence is part of the discipline counsel is retaining. Work
is remote-first, with records review, conferences with counsel, and most
vocational interviews conducted over secure video. When an in-person
evaluation is warranted, it takes place in the evaluee's own community,
where function and access to care can be observed directly, with travel
arranged from the Kansas City home office. Deposition and trial
appearances are scheduled around the dates the court has set.
