Expert retention in a Nitro case tends to begin with a date rather than a
question: a disclosure deadline in the scheduling order, a deposition
already noticed, a trial term drawing close. Purinton Analytics organizes
its engagements around exactly those dates. Plaintiff and defense counsel
retain the firm for
[vocational expert opinions](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) on
employability and earning capacity,
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) for catastrophic
injury, and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure, and every engagement is scoped against the calendar
the court has already imposed.

## The Kanawha County forum and its federal counterparts

Nitro lies in Kanawha County, where the Circuit Court hears civil actions
under West Virginia law. When federal jurisdiction attaches, the state's
available forums are the United States District Court for the Northern
District of West Virginia and the United States District Court for the
Southern District of West Virginia; the controlling venue for any
particular caption, and the expert qualification steps it requires, are
identified and confirmed during the conflict check and intake, alongside
the operative scheduling deadlines. Coverage across the metro appears on
the [Charleston metro page](/locations/west-virginia/charleston/), and the
statewide practice is described on the
[West Virginia page](/locations/west-virginia/).

## Labor market findings for a Nitro evaluee

Vocational conclusions for a Nitro evaluee are anchored to Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Charleston,
WV metropolitan area and for West Virginia statewide, to state workforce
agency labor market information, and to direct labor market research where
the referral questions demand it. Because the evidence must be current
when it is disclosed, sources are cited as of the report date and the
methodology is stated so a reviewer on the other side can retrace it. What
the report will not do is stretch or shrink the evaluee's job search area
to fit a damages theory; the commuting picture it presents is the one the
case file actually supports, and the opinion is delivered early enough in
the schedule for either side to probe that picture before the deposition
rather than during it.

## Scheduling evaluations against the deadlines

Meetings in Nitro are by appointment; the firm maintains no office there
and says so plainly, because candor about presence is part of the same
discipline as candor about method. Remote work carries most of the
engagement, which protects the calendar: records conferences and
vocational interviews proceed by secure video without waiting on travel
windows. When in-person evaluation is warranted, it is performed in the
evaluee's own community and scheduled from the Kansas City home office
with the disclosure date in view. Deposition and trial appearances are
planned around the dates the court has fixed, so that testimony readiness
and report readiness arrive together.
