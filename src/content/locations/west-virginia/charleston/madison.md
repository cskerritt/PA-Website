## Boone County's Circuit Court and the federal forums

Civil actions under West Virginia law arising in Madison are heard in the
Circuit Court serving Boone County. Where a claim supports federal
jurisdiction, West Virginia offers the United States District Court for
the Northern District of West Virginia and the United States District
Court for the Southern District of West Virginia; the district that will
control a given case, and any registration or qualification requirement it
places on a testifying expert, is identified and confirmed during the
conflict check and intake. The firm's reports are prepared for whichever
forum receives them, with reasoning laid out for adversarial reading. For
matters elsewhere in the region, the
[Charleston metro page](/locations/west-virginia/charleston/) describes
the metro practice, and the [West Virginia page](/locations/west-virginia/)
covers the state as a whole.

## Measuring a Madison evaluee's market honestly

Madison is a small community, and honest vocational work neither shrinks
an evaluee's market to the town line by default nor assumes effortless
access to distant employment. The analysis rests on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Charleston,
WV metropolitan area and for West Virginia statewide, on state workforce
agency labor market information, and on direct employer contact where the
referral questions warrant it. How far this particular evaluee can
realistically travel for work is answered from the case file itself: the
functional limits the physicians have actually documented, the driving
and vehicle picture the record reflects, and the pattern of employment
the evaluee maintained before injury. The opinion then states plainly
what that evidence supports and why it supports no more, so the finding
can be probed on cross-examination without surprising anyone who read
the report.

## Evaluations where the evaluee lives

The firm has no office in Madison or anywhere in Boone County and does not
suggest otherwise; plainness about presence is part of the same discipline
that governs the written opinions. Engagements run remotely wherever the
referral questions allow, with secure video handling vocational
interviews, records conferences, and discussions with counsel, so a Boone
County case receives the same attention a metropolitan docket commands.
When in-person evaluation is justified, it takes place in the evaluee's
own community, where daily function, housing, and access to care can be
observed directly, and travel is arranged from the Kansas City home
office. Testimony appearances are scheduled around the dates the court
sets.
