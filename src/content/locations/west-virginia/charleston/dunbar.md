## Kanawha County courts and the federal alternatives

Dunbar sits in Kanawha County, and state-law civil actions are heard in
the Circuit Court. West Virginia's federal forums are the United States
District Court for the Northern District of West Virginia and the United
States District Court for the Southern District of West Virginia; the
venue that will control a particular case, along with any expert
qualification requirement that venue imposes, is identified and confirmed
during the conflict check and intake. Reports are drafted to survive
scrutiny in either system, with methods a reviewer can reproduce and
sources a reviewer can pull. Counsel can review the firm's regional
coverage on the
[Charleston metro page](/locations/west-virginia/charleston/) and its

## Dunbar workers in the metro labor market

A Dunbar evaluee's earning capacity is measured against the Charleston, WV
metropolitan labor market as the evaluee can genuinely access it.
Vocational conclusions rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational
employment and wage estimates for that metropolitan area and for West
Virginia statewide, together with state workforce agency labor market
information and direct employer contact where warranted. The analysis
proceeds in a stated order: the medical evidence establishes what the
evaluee can still do, the employment history establishes what the evaluee
has demonstrably done, and only then does the labor market data establish
where those abilities still command earnings. Each step cites the source
it relies on, so the reach the report attributes to the evaluee comes out
of that sequence, never going into it as a premise. Opposing counsel
should be able to follow every step from source to conclusion; that is
the test each report is written to pass.

## How a Dunbar evaluee is actually assessed

Most of the engagement runs remotely, and deliberately so. Secure video
carries the vocational interview, records conferences, and working
sessions with counsel, which keeps the schedule inside the court's
deadlines and the effort proportionate to the case. When the referral
questions require direct observation, the in-person evaluation is
conducted in the evaluee's own community, where housing, daily function,
and access to treatment and transportation can be seen rather than
inferred. The firm keeps no office in Dunbar, publishes no local address,
and treats that candor as part of the product; travel, when needed, is
scheduled from the Kansas City home office, including for deposition and
trial appearances.
