An earning-capacity dispute involving a South Charleston worker is rarely a
dispute about South Charleston alone. The town belongs to the Charleston, WV
metropolitan labor market, and the work an injured evaluee once performed,
along with the work that remains realistically available now, is spread
across that commuting area rather than confined to the town line. Purinton
Analytics is retained by plaintiff counsel and defense counsel to resolve
that question with method rather than impression, offering
[vocational expert opinions](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) on
employability and earning capacity,
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) after serious injury,
and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Kanawha County venue and the federal forums

South Charleston sits in Kanawha County, and civil actions under West
Virginia law are heard in the Circuit Court. Where a claim carries a
federal basis, the forums available within the state are the United States
District Court for the Northern District of West Virginia and the United
States District Court for the Southern District of West Virginia; the
district that will actually control a given caption, together with any
registration or qualification requirement it imposes on a testifying
expert, is identified and confirmed during the conflict check and intake.
The written product does not change with the bench: reproducible methods,
cited sources, and conclusions that stay inside the disclosed scope.

[Charleston metro page](/locations/west-virginia/charleston/), and

## Reading the commuting market

Because South Charleston workers and employers participate in the
metro-wide labor market, a defensible earning-capacity opinion has to
define which portion of that market is genuinely open to the evaluee.
Vocational findings rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational
employment and wage estimates for the Charleston, WV metropolitan area and
for West Virginia statewide, on state workforce agency labor market
information, and on direct employer contact where the referral questions
require it. The geographic boundary of the market that gets counted is
treated as a conclusion the report must justify, never as a setting
chosen at the outset. The opinion walks through what the medical evidence
permits, what the employment file shows the evaluee has actually done,
and how the evaluee has historically gotten to and from work, and it lets
those exhibits draw the line. A reader on either side of the caption can
trace that boundary back to the specific records that produced it, which
is the posture every report in this practice is written to hold.

## By-appointment presence in South Charleston

The firm keeps no office in South Charleston and publishes no street
address for the area, because accuracy about presence belongs to the same
discipline that governs the reports. Most engagements proceed remotely:
records conferences, discussions with counsel, and the majority of
vocational interviews are carried over secure video. When the referral
questions justify in-person evaluation, it takes place in the evaluee's own
community, where daily function and actual access to services can be
observed rather than inferred, with travel scheduled from the Kansas City
home office. Deposition and trial appearances are planned around the dates
the court has already set, so presence is never the obstacle to a timely
opinion.
