Every opinion Purinton Analytics writes for a St. Albans case is drafted on
the assumption that opposing counsel will read it line by line. That
standard, not geography, is what the firm offers Kanawha County litigants.
Plaintiff and defense attorneys alike retain the practice for
[vocational expert opinions](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) on
employability and earning capacity, for
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) that documents future
care needs from the medical record, and for
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. What follows is how that standard reaches a town like
St. Albans.

## Where a St. Albans case is heard

St. Albans lies in Kanawha County, where civil actions under West Virginia
law proceed in the Circuit Court. Claims resting on federal jurisdiction
may be filed in the United States District Court for the Northern District
of West Virginia or the United States District Court for the Southern
District of West Virginia; which of those districts controls a given
matter, and what qualification or registration a testifying expert must
complete there, is confirmed during the conflict check and intake rather
than assumed. Because the firm serves the full metro, counsel handling
related matters elsewhere in the region can consult the
[Charleston metro page](/locations/west-virginia/charleston/), and
statewide coverage is described on the
[West Virginia page](/locations/west-virginia/).

## Earning capacity in the Charleston labor market

A St. Albans evaluee's employment prospects are analyzed against the labor
market the evaluee can actually reach, not an abstraction. The vocational
findings draw on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and
wage estimates covering the Charleston, WV metropolitan area and West
Virginia as a whole, supplemented by state workforce agency labor market
information and by direct labor market research when the referral questions
call for it. Transferable-skills analysis proceeds from the documented work
history, and the resulting opinion explains its own reasoning so that
either side can test it: which occupations remain open, why the medical
restrictions do or do not close them, and what the record shows about
access, transportation, and realistic commuting range.

## Appointments, evaluations, and candor about presence

There is no Purinton Analytics office in St. Albans, and the firm will not
pretend otherwise; meetings are by appointment and most of the work is
remote by design. Secure video carries records review conferences,
strategy discussions with counsel, and the greater part of the vocational
interviewing. In-person evaluation, when the referral questions warrant it,
happens in the evaluee's own community, so that daily function is observed
where the evaluee actually lives instead of reconstructed from paper.
Travel for evaluations, depositions, and trial testimony is arranged from
the Kansas City home office and planned around the calendar the court has
imposed, which keeps the logistics from ever driving the substance.
