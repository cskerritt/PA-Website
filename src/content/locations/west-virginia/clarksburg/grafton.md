## A small town inside a regional market

Grafton is a small community, and its residents' realistic employment
options often extend toward Clarksburg and the wider metro area. A
credible earning-capacity analysis for a Grafton evaluee therefore begins
by defining the market honestly: what work exists within reach, what the
evaluee's restrictions and history permit, and whether the post-injury
market is genuinely narrower than the pre-injury one. Those findings rest
on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates
for the Clarksburg, WV metropolitan area and for West Virginia statewide,
on state workforce agency labor market information, and on direct
employer research when a referral question turns on it. Scale is never
treated as a shortcut; a rural market is documented with the same care as
an urban one, because either party's cross-examiner will know the local
facts better than any database does.

## Evaluations without a local office

There is no Purinton Analytics office in Grafton, and the firm does not
pretend otherwise. Engagements run remote-first: secure video handles
most interviews, records conferences, and counsel discussions, which
keeps costs proportional to the case. When the questions presented
require observation that a screen cannot deliver, the evaluation is
conducted in person in the evaluee's own community, and travel is
scheduled from the Kansas City home office. The same planning applies to
depositions and trial testimony, arranged around the court's existing
calendar rather than against it.
