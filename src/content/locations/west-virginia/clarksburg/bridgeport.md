person whose working life before and after injury must be described so
carefully that either side of the caption can test every line of the

## Harrison County venue

Bridgeport lies in Harrison County, and civil actions under West Virginia
law are heard there in the Circuit Court. A claim with a federal footing
may instead proceed in the United States District Court for the Northern
District of West Virginia or in the United States District Court for the
Southern District of West Virginia; which venue controls a particular
matter, and what qualification or registration it may require of a
testifying expert, is identified and confirmed during the conflict check
and intake. Reports are drafted for whichever bench will receive them,
with methods a reader can reproduce and conclusions held to the disclosed
scope. Regional coverage is described on the
[Clarksburg metro page](/locations/west-virginia/clarksburg/), and
statewide practice on the [West Virginia page](/locations/west-virginia/).

## Defining the labor market honestly

Bridgeport belongs to the Clarksburg, WV metropolitan area, and a
defensible earning-capacity opinion for a Bridgeport evaluee must state
plainly which labor market the analysis uses and why that market is the
right one for this person, given the restrictions, skills, and work
history the record supports. Vocational findings rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Clarksburg,
WV metropolitan area and for West Virginia statewide, supplemented by
state workforce agency labor market information and by direct labor
market research when the referral questions call for it. Every assumption
that shapes the market definition appears in the body of the report, not
in a footnote, because an assumption that surfaces for the first time on
cross-examination is a liability to whichever party served the opinion.

## Meeting a Bridgeport evaluee

The firm keeps no office in Bridgeport and never suggests otherwise; all
meetings are by appointment, and candor about presence is part of the
discipline counsel is hiring. Most of an engagement proceeds remotely,
with records review, conferences with counsel, and vocational interviews
carried over secure video. When an in-person evaluation is warranted, it
takes place in the evaluee's own community, where function, home
environment, and practical access to services can be observed directly,
with travel scheduled from the Kansas City home office. Deposition and
trial appearances are planned around the dates the court has set, so
testimony logistics never crowd the substance of the work.
