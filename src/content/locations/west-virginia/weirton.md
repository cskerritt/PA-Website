The metro joins Hancock County and Brooke County in West Virginia with
Jefferson County in Ohio. Engagements are accepted for West Virginia matters
in the Circuit Court serving Hancock County and Brooke County, and in the
United States District Court for the Northern District of West Virginia and
the United States District Court for the Southern District of West Virginia.

federal courts serving that county, with venue-specific disclosure and
report conventions confirmed during intake. The methodology does not change
with the retaining side or the state of filing; the same documented

## Presence, stated accurately

The firm maintains no staffed office in Weirton or anywhere else in the
metro and publishes no street address for this location. Stating that
plainly is deliberate: accuracy about offices reflects the same discipline
applied to sources and opinions. Day-to-day engagement work, including
records review, counsel conferences, and most evaluee interviews, is
conducted remotely. When in-person evaluation is warranted or a deposition
or trial requires the expert in the courtroom, travel is scheduled from the
Kansas City home office around the court's dates. That structure is the same
one that supports [engagements nationwide](/locations/nationwide/), so
regional counsel with matters outside the metro can keep the same expert
across venues.
