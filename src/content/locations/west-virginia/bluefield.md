Smaller markets deserve the same rigor as major metros, and the Bluefield
micropolitan area, which straddles the West Virginia and Virginia line, gets
exactly that from Purinton Analytics. Counsel in Mercer County, West
Virginia and Tazewell County, Virginia retain the firm for plaintiff and
defense engagements alike:
[vocational evaluation and expert testimony](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/),
[medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/), and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Courts on both sides of the state line

On the West Virginia side, engagements are accepted for matters in the
Circuit Court serving Mercer County, and in the United States District Court
for the Southern District of West Virginia and the United States District
Court for the Northern District of West Virginia. For Tazewell County,
Virginia matters, referrals are accepted for the state and federal courts
serving that county, with the venue's disclosure requirements and report
conventions confirmed during the conflict check and intake process. Because
the caption may sit in either state while the evaluee's life spans both,
the engagement letter states the venue explicitly and the report is prepared
for the court that will hear the matter.

## Working with counsel by appointment

Bluefield meetings are by appointment. The firm maintains no staffed office
in the micropolitan area and publishes no street address for this location;
accuracy about presence is part of the same discipline that produces its
reports. The bulk of the work, including records review, counsel
conferences, and most evaluee interviews, proceeds remotely, so distance
from the firm's home base adds no delay to the engagement. When the
referral questions call for in-person evaluation, or when deposition and
trial dates require presence in a Bluefield-area courtroom, travel is
scheduled from the Kansas City home office. The practice runs
[engagements nationwide](/locations/nationwide/) on this same model, and
regional counsel often use it to keep the same expert across matters in
different venues.

## Labor market research across state lines

Vocational findings for Bluefield-area evaluees rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Bluefield area
and for each state the micropolitan area touches, state workforce agency
labor market information, and direct labor market research where warranted.
Earning capacity and placeability opinions reflect the commutable labor
market the evaluee can actually reach across the state line rather than a
market defined by the caption alone, and every conclusion is tied in the
report to the data supporting it. When the assignment is to test rather
than to author, [rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/)
engagements evaluate whether an opposing expert's methodology and sources
support the opinions offered.
