vocational opinion and a vulnerable one is usually the data underneath it.

for Beckley-area evaluees in cited labor market evidence, and serves

## Venues served in Raleigh and Fayette counties

The metro comprises Raleigh County and Fayette County, and engagements are
accepted for matters in the Circuit Court serving each of them. Federal
matters proceed in the United States District Court for the Southern
District of West Virginia and the United States District Court for the
Northern District of West Virginia, and referrals are accepted for both.
Personal injury, wrongful death, employment, and workers' compensation
matters each shape the scope differently, so the engagement letter defines
the questions to be answered and ties the work plan to the court's
disclosure and trial schedule from the outset.

Beckley meetings are by appointment. The firm has no staffed office in the

presence accurately is part of the same discipline that governs its

## The data beneath a Beckley opinion

Vocational findings for evaluees in the Beckley metropolitan area rest on
Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for
the Beckley metropolitan area and for West Virginia statewide, state
workforce agency labor market information, and direct labor market research
where warranted. Transferable-skills analysis identifies the occupations an
evaluee can still perform after injury; the labor market research then tests
whether those occupations actually exist in the market the evaluee can
reach. Each step is documented in the report with its sources, so that
either side of the caption can trace the path from record to conclusion.
