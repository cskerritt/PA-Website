Purinton Analytics provides vocational expert and life care planning services
for litigation in the Parkersburg-Vienna metropolitan area, accepting
engagements from plaintiff and defense counsel on equal terms. Referrals from
the region most often call for
[earning capacity and employability evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/)
in personal injury and employment matters,
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) in catastrophic injury
cases, and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. Every file begins the same way regardless of which side
retains the firm: a documented conflict check, a defined scope, and a records
request tailored to the claims.

## Venue footprint in the Parkersburg-Vienna area

The metropolitan area comprises Wirt County and Wood County, and the firm
accepts matters venued in the Circuit Court of either county. Federal
matters from the region proceed in the United States District Court for the
Northern District of West Virginia or the United States District Court for
the Southern District of West Virginia, depending on where the case is
filed, and the firm's written opinions are prepared to the same disclosure
standard in either forum. Counsel with matters elsewhere in the state can
review the broader venue landscape on the
[West Virginia locations page](/locations/west-virginia/), and cases pending
outside the state entirely are handled through the firm's
[nationwide practice](/locations/nationwide/).

## By-appointment service, not a local storefront

Parkersburg meetings are by appointment. The firm maintains no staffed
office in the metro and publishes no street address for this location,
because accuracy about presence is part of the same discipline that governs
its reports. Most engagements from the region begin remotely: records
review, structured vocational interviews, and standardized testing under
publisher protocols where remote administration is appropriate. When the
referral questions require in-person evaluation, or when deposition and
trial dates require attendance, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City
home office and coordinated around the court's calendar. Counsel receive the
same written work product either way, prepared so that opposing counsel can
trace every conclusion to its source.

## Labor market research for Parkersburg-area evaluees

Vocational findings for evaluees in Wirt County and Wood County rest on
Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for
the Parkersburg-Vienna area and for West Virginia statewide, supplemented by
state workforce agency labor market information and direct labor market
research where the referral questions warrant it. Transferable skills
analysis, employability assessment, and placeability opinions are grounded
in the labor market the evaluee actually faces rather than in national
averages, and the sources behind each opinion are cited in the report so
that either side of the caption can verify them. Where an evaluee's
realistic job search extends beyond the metro, the analysis says so
expressly and documents the basis for the wider geography.

## Starting a Parkersburg engagement

The first step for any matter, on either side of the caption, is a conflict
check. Submit the case caption or party names through the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and note any imminent disclosure,
deposition, or trial dates so scheduling can be confirmed at intake. Any
venue-specific registration or qualification requirement is identified and
confirmed during the conflict check and intake process, and counsel receive
a defined scope and fee structure before any substantive work begins. Once
cleared, the firm can proceed on Parkersburg-Vienna matters at whatever
stage the case has reached, from early case assessment through
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) of an opposing
expert's disclosure.
