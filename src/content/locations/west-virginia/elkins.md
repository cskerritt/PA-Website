## Randolph County venue context

The Elkins micropolitan area consists of Randolph County, where state-court
matters are venued in the Circuit Court. Cases in federal court proceed in
the United States District Court for the Northern District of West Virginia
or the United States District Court for the Southern District of West
Virginia, depending on where the action is pending, and the firm's
disclosures are drafted to satisfy the stricter of the standards it works
under so that the same report holds up in either forum. Counsel handling
matters in other parts of the state will find the full venue picture on the
[West Virginia locations page](/locations/west-virginia/); the
[nationwide practice](/locations/nationwide/) covers everything beyond it.

## Evaluations without a local office

The firm keeps no staffed office in Elkins and publishes no street address
for this location; candor about presence is part of the same discipline that
governs its written opinions. What counsel get instead is a defined process.
Records arrive through a tailored request list, interviews are conducted by
scheduled video or telephone session, and testing is administered under
publisher protocols where remote administration is appropriate. When an
evaluation must happen in person, when a home assessment supports the life
care plan, or when deposition and trial dates require attendance, travel is
arranged from the Kansas City home office with enough lead time to protect
the litigation schedule. The by-appointment model is stated plainly in every
engagement letter, so there is never a gap between what the firm claims and
what it does.

## Labor market analysis for a rural venue

Opinions about employability and earning capacity for Elkins-area evaluees
draw on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage
estimates for the relevant area and for West Virginia statewide, on state
workforce agency labor market information, and on direct labor market
research where warranted. A rural labor market demands more care, not less:
the report addresses what work actually exists within the evaluee's
realistic commuting range from Randolph County, and where the accessible
market is thin, the analysis documents that thinness instead of papering
over it. Each source is cited so either side of the caption can retrace the
research, and the same rigor applies whether the opinion helps or hurts the
retaining party's theory.
