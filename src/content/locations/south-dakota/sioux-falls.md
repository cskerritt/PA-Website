The Sioux Falls metropolitan area crosses a state line, and that fact shapes
how Purinton Analytics serves it. A vocational or care-planning question that
arises in Minnehaha County may be litigated under South Dakota procedure
while a similar claim from Rock County, Minnesota proceeds under another
state's rules, yet counsel on either side of that line need the same things:
an [earning capacity and employability evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/)
that will hold up under cross-examination, a
[life care plan](/services/life-care-planning/) grounded in the evaluee's own
market, and a report written to be read by the opposing side. The firm
provides all of it to plaintiff and defense counsel alike, under a single
methodology that does not change at the border.

## Venue footprint across the metro

Engagements are accepted for matters venued in the Circuit Court serving
Minnehaha County, Lincoln County, McCook County, and Turner County, and in
the United States District Court for the District of South Dakota. For the
Minnesota portion of the metro, Rock County, matters are accepted under the
same national engagement structure, and any venue-specific registration or
qualification requirement on either side of the line is identified and
confirmed during the conflict check and intake process. The caption, not the
geography, drives the scope: the analysis is defined by the referral
questions and the record, wherever the courthouse sits.

## By appointment in Sioux Falls

The firm keeps no staffed office in Sioux Falls and publishes no street
address for this location, because accuracy about presence is part of the
same discipline that governs its reports. Meetings, evaluations, and
conferences are scheduled by appointment. Most engagements begin remotely,
with records review, structured interviews, and standardized testing under
publisher protocols where remote administration is supported. When the
referral questions call for in-person evaluation, or when deposition and
trial dates require presence, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home
office. Counsel receive the same report standard and the same point of
contact regardless of where the work is performed.

## Labor market research for the metro

Vocational findings for evaluees in the Sioux Falls area rest on Bureau of
Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Sioux
Falls metropolitan area and for South Dakota statewide, supplemented by state
workforce agency labor market information and direct employer research where
the referral questions warrant it. Because the metro spans states, the
analysis is anchored to the labor market the evaluee actually faces rather
than to a single state's aggregate data. Life care plans and
[medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/) are priced to
the evaluee's own market using provider-specific inquiries and published fee
data for the region. Where damages must be reduced to present value, the
firm offers [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.
