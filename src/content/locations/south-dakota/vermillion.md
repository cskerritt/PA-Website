An expert opinion is only as useful as the record behind it. That premise
shapes every engagement Purinton Analytics accepts from Vermillion counsel,
whether the assignment is an
[earning capacity and employability evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
a [life care plan](/services/life-care-planning/), a
[medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/), or a
[coordinated damages engagement](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. Plaintiff and defense referrals are handled under the
same intake, the same scoping, and the same publication standard, so the
finished opinion reads the same way no matter which side retained it.

## Clay County venue footprint

The Vermillion micropolitan area consists of Clay County. Engagements are
accepted for matters venued in the Circuit Court serving Clay County and in
the United States District Court for the District of South Dakota. Every
file is assembled so that opposing counsel can trace the path from records
request to conclusion: sources are cited, testing is identified by
instrument and protocol, and any departure from an evaluee's documented work
history is explained rather than assumed. Counsel with matters elsewhere in
the state will find the same structure described on the firm's
[South Dakota page](/locations/south-dakota/), which anchors the practice's
statewide coverage.

## Appointment-based presence, honestly described

Purinton Analytics keeps no staffed office in Vermillion and publishes no
street address for this location. Stating that plainly is deliberate: the
firm treats accuracy about its own footprint as inseparable from accuracy in
its opinions. Vermillion meetings are by appointment, most work begins
remotely with records review and structured interviews, and evaluations can
be conducted in the evaluee's own community when in-person administration is
indicated by the referral questions or by publisher testing protocols.
Travel for evaluations, depositions, and trial testimony is scheduled from
the Kansas City home office and coordinated with counsel's litigation
calendar so that appearances are planned rather than improvised.

## Labor market grounding for Clay County matters

Vocational conclusions for Clay County evaluees are grounded in Bureau of
Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for South
Dakota, supplemented by state workforce agency labor market information and
by direct labor market research where the questions warrant it. In a small
labor market, placeability turns on the jobs an evaluee can actually reach,
so the analysis weighs local availability and commuting patterns rather
than resting on statewide averages alone. Life care plan and cost projection
pricing follows the same logic, relying on provider-specific inquiries in
the evaluee's own market alongside published fee data, because the cost of
future care in a small community can differ from national benchmarks in
either direction.

## Starting a Vermillion engagement

Engagements begin with a conflict check rather than a sales conversation.
[Request a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) with the caption or party names
and note any imminent disclosure, deposition, or trial dates; scope, fees,
and any venue-specific qualification requirements are confirmed during the
intake process that follows. For a broader view of how the practice
operates, the firm's [nationwide coverage](/locations/nationwide/) page
describes the same engagement structure at the national level. Referrals
are welcome from either side of the caption, and a matter is declined
whenever a conflict would compromise the independence of the opinion.
