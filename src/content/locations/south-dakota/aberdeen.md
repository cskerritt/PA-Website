Referrals from the Aberdeen area tend to reach Purinton Analytics in the
same postures they arise everywhere else in the practice: counsel needs an
[earning capacity and employability evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/)
in an injury or employment matter, a
[life care plan](/services/life-care-planning/) in a catastrophic injury
case, or a [rebuttal review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) of an opposing
expert's report before a deposition. Plaintiff and defense firms receive the
same answer to each: a documented conflict check, a defined scope, and a
records request tailored to the claims, so that the written opinion rests on
a record both sides of the caption can examine. The size of the market never
changes the standard of the work.

## Venue footprint in the Aberdeen area

Engagements are accepted for matters venued in the Circuit Court serving
Brown County and Edmunds County, and in the United States District Court for
the District of South Dakota. Whether the question is the residual earning
capacity of an injured worker, the employability of a terminated employee,
or the lifetime care needs of a catastrophically injured evaluee, the
analysis is built around the referral questions counsel actually need
answered, and the resulting report is written to be read by opposing
counsel. Any venue-specific registration or qualification requirement is
identified and confirmed during the conflict check and intake process.

## How the firm appears in Aberdeen

There is no staffed Purinton Analytics office in Aberdeen, and the firm
publishes no street address for this location. Meetings and evaluations are
by appointment, and most engagements proceed remotely from start to finish:
records review, structured interviews by video, and standardized testing
under publisher protocols where remote administration is supported. When the
referral questions require an in-person evaluation, or when deposition and
trial dates require presence in the courtroom, travel is scheduled from the
Kansas City home office and coordinated with counsel's litigation calendar.
The engagement file records which work was performed remotely and which in
person, so the methodology can be defended step by step.

## Labor market research for the Aberdeen area

Vocational findings for Aberdeen area evaluees rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the relevant area
and for South Dakota statewide, alongside state workforce agency labor
market information and direct employer contact where the referral questions
warrant it. A regional employment center like Aberdeen presents a different
occupational mix than the state's larger metros, and the analysis is
anchored to the jobs realistically available to the evaluee rather than to
statewide averages. Life care plans and
[medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/) are priced to
the evaluee's own community. Where a full damages picture is needed,
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
let independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis
while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement
structure.
