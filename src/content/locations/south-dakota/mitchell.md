In a market the size of Mitchell, the same expert may be approached by
plaintiff's counsel in this matter and defense counsel in the next, and
Purinton Analytics is built for exactly that position. The methodology does
not bend to the retaining side: an
[earning capacity and employability evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/)
follows the same documented steps whoever pays for it, a
[life care plan](/services/life-care-planning/) cites the same classes of
sources, and a [rebuttal review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) applies
the same standards to an opposing report that the firm expects to be applied
to its own. That symmetry is not a slogan; it is what allows the work to
survive when the other side of the caption takes its turn.

## Venue footprint across the Mitchell area

Engagements are accepted for matters venued in the Circuit Court serving
Davison County, Hanson County, and Sanborn County, and in the United States
District Court for the District of South Dakota. Referral questions from
this area track the national practice: residual earning capacity and
placeability after injury, employability and mitigation in employment
disputes, and lifetime care needs in catastrophic injury cases. Every
engagement is scoped at intake around the questions counsel actually needs
answered, and any venue-specific registration or qualification requirement
is identified and confirmed during the conflict check and intake process.

## By-appointment service, no local storefront

The firm keeps no staffed office in Mitchell and publishes no street address
for this location; plain statements about presence are part of the same
discipline that governs the reports. Most engagements are conducted
remotely, through records review, structured video interviews, and
standardized testing under publisher protocols where remote administration
is supported, which keeps expert costs proportionate to the case. When the
referral questions call for in-person evaluation, or when deposition and
trial dates require presence, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home
office and coordinated with counsel's calendar. The report standard and the
point of contact stay the same throughout.

## Labor market foundations for Mitchell area opinions

Vocational findings for evaluees in Davison, Hanson, and Sanborn counties
rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage
estimates for the relevant area and for South Dakota statewide, together
with state workforce agency labor market information and direct employer
research where warranted. Because the Mitchell area carries a micropolitan
rather than a metropolitan designation, published estimates for it can run
thin, and the report says so where that is true rather than dressing sparse
data up as certainty; conclusions about the work available to the evaluee
are drawn only where the sources support them. Life care plans and
[medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/) are priced to
the evaluee's own community using provider-specific inquiries and published
fee data. Where the case calls for a full damages analysis,
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
allow independent economist partners to perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.
