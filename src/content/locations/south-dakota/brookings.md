Every Brookings engagement begins the way engagements begin everywhere in
Purinton Analytics' national practice: with a documented conflict check, a
defined scope, and a records request tailored to the claims. That procedural
discipline is the point. A
[vocational evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) or
[life care plan](/services/life-care-planning/) prepared for a Brookings
County matter will be examined by opposing counsel, and possibly by an
opposing expert, so the file is built from the outset to withstand that
examination. Plaintiff and defense counsel receive the same methodology, the
same report standard, and the same willingness to have every analytical step
tested from the other side of the caption.

## Where Brookings matters are venued

The firm accepts engagements for matters in the Circuit Court serving
Brookings County and in the United States District Court for the District of
South Dakota. The typical referral questions from this venue mirror the
national practice: residual earning capacity after injury, employability and
mitigation in employment disputes, and lifetime care needs in catastrophic
injury cases. Where an opposing expert has already opined, a
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) examines whether
that opinion's methods and sources actually support its conclusions. Any
venue-specific registration or qualification requirement is identified and
confirmed during the conflict check and intake process.

## Appointment-based presence, plainly disclosed

Purinton Analytics keeps no staffed office in Brookings and publishes no
street address for this location; accuracy about presence is part of the
same discipline that governs the reports. Most Brookings engagements are
performed remotely, through records review, structured video interviews, and
standardized testing under publisher protocols where remote administration
is supported, which keeps costs proportionate to the case. When the referral
questions call for in-person evaluation, or when deposition and trial dates
require presence, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office and
planned around counsel's calendar. Counsel receive the same report standard
and the same point of contact regardless of where each task is performed.

## Grounding opinions in the local labor market

Vocational findings for Brookings County evaluees rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the relevant area
and for South Dakota statewide, together with state workforce agency labor
market information and direct labor market research where warranted. The
analysis addresses the market the evaluee actually faces rather than
defaulting to statewide aggregates, because a smaller regional market
presents a different occupational mix than the state's largest metros. Life care plans and
[medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/) are priced to
the evaluee's own community through provider-specific inquiries and
published fee data. Where the case requires a complete damages analysis,
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
allow independent economist partners to perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.
