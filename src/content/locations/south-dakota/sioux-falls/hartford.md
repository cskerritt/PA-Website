The referral questions in a Hartford case tend to be the classic ones: what could this person earn before the injury, what can they earn now, and what will their care cost over a lifetime? Purinton Analytics answers those questions for Hartford matters the way it answers them everywhere, by starting from the documented record rather than from advocacy. Each engagement opens with a defined scope and a records request tailored to the claims, so the resulting [vocational assessment](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) or [life care plan](/services/life-care-planning/) rests on materials both sides can examine. Instructions are accepted from plaintiff and defense counsel, and the report reads the same either way.

## Minnehaha County context for Hartford matters

Hartford sits in Minnehaha County. Its state-court cases are heard in the Circuit Court, and the United States District Court for the District of South Dakota serves as the federal forum where jurisdiction lies; determining which court controls a specific matter is part of the conflict check and intake, together with any venue-specific registration or qualification requirement. Counsel working across the wider metro will find the fuller venue picture on the [Sioux Falls metro page](/locations/south-dakota/sioux-falls/), and engagements arising elsewhere in the state are covered on the [South Dakota page](/locations/south-dakota/).

## Small town, metro-scale earning questions

Hartford is a small community, but its residents participate in a labor market far larger than the town itself: the Sioux Falls metropolitan economy defines the realistic range of work available to a Hartford evaluee. The vocational analysis therefore measures pre-injury and post-injury capacity against Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Sioux Falls metropolitan area and for South Dakota statewide, informed by state workforce agency labor market information and by direct labor market research when a contested placement question needs primary evidence. The written opinion keeps market description qualitative and ties every quantified input to a cited source, because an earning capacity conclusion is only as strong as the data trail behind it.

## Serving a Hartford evaluee by appointment

Purinton Analytics has no Hartford office; the practice operates from its Kansas City home office and serves South Dakota by appointment, a fact stated here plainly because candor about presence is part of the product. Most of a Hartford engagement proceeds remotely, from secure records exchange through the video vocational interview and remotely proctored testing where the instruments are validated for it. When the questions require an in-person evaluation, it is conducted in the evaluee's own community, and appearances for deposition or trial are scheduled with travel from Kansas City. If the case also needs economic loss calculations, [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) provide them through independent economist partners who perform and sign the economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement structure. How this model scales across the country is described on the [nationwide practice page](/locations/nationwide/).

## The first step in a Hartford engagement

Substantive discussion waits until conflicts are cleared. Counsel should [request a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) with the case caption and parties, note any imminent disclosure, deposition, or trial dates, and withhold confidential records until clearance is confirmed. Intake then fixes the scope, the schedule, and the deliverables before analysis begins.
