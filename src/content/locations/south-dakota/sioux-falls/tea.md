## Measuring earning capacity from a Tea address

Tea is a small suburban community within the Sioux Falls, SD-MN metropolitan area, and that metro membership sets the benchmarks for the earning capacity analysis. Vocational findings for a Tea evaluee are measured against Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Sioux Falls metropolitan area and for South Dakota statewide, read alongside state workforce agency labor market information and direct labor market research where a disputed occupation requires it. What those benchmarks mean for a particular evaluee is a question the record answers: the analysis reads them against the documented work history, the transferable skills it supports, and the functional limitations established by the medical evidence, and it goes no further than that record allows. Scale is described qualitatively and every quantitative input in the report carries its citation, which keeps the opinion useful under cross-examination. Counsel needing the loss reduced to present value can pair this work with [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/), in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement structure.

## What an evaluation looks like for a Tea resident

An engagement typically opens with a records request tailored to the claims, followed by a structured vocational interview conducted by video. Standardized instruments are administered under remote protocols where validated for that use; where they are not, or where the medical picture calls for direct observation, an in-person session is scheduled locally. Life care planning work adds consultation with treating providers and, when appropriate, a home visit. Deposition and trial testimony are handled in person, with travel arranged from the firm's Kansas City home office, which is the only staffed office the practice maintains. There is no Tea office and no published local address, and the firm says so plainly, because accuracy about presence belongs in the same category as accuracy in the report.
