Litigation venued in and around Pierre often turns on questions of earning
capacity, employability, and future care that call for expert analysis
prepared to a defensible standard. Purinton Analytics serves plaintiff and
defense counsel in Pierre and the surrounding micropolitan area with
[vocational expert witness](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) evaluations,
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/),
[medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/), and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. Every matter begins with a documented conflict check
and a defined scope, so the written opinion rests on a record either side of
the caption can examine.

## Venue footprint in Hughes and Stanley counties

The Pierre micropolitan area spans Hughes County and Stanley County, and the
firm accepts engagements for matters venued in the Circuit Court serving
those counties as well as in the United States District Court for the
District of South Dakota. The same report standard applies regardless of
forum: opinions are written to withstand cross-examination, methodology is
disclosed rather than implied, and file materials are organized so that
opposing experts can trace every step from records request to conclusion.
Counsel with related matters elsewhere in the state are supported through the
firm's [South Dakota practice](/locations/south-dakota/), which follows the
same intake, scoping, and disclosure discipline in every venue.

## By appointment, without a local storefront

The firm maintains no staffed office in Pierre and publishes no street
address for this location, because accuracy about presence is part of the
same discipline that governs its reports. Meetings are by appointment. Most
engagements begin remotely, with records review, structured interviews, and
standardized testing administered under publisher protocols where remote
administration is supported. When the referral questions require in-person
evaluation, or when deposition and trial calendars require presence, travel
is scheduled from the Kansas City home office and planned around the
litigation calendar. Counsel work with a single point of contact and receive
a consistent report standard whether the work is performed remotely or on
site.

## Labor market research for the Pierre area

Vocational findings for Pierre-area evaluees rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for South Dakota, on
state workforce agency labor market information, and on direct labor market
research where the referral questions warrant it. Because a micropolitan
labor market can differ sharply from statewide patterns, transferable skills
analysis and placeability opinions are grounded in the occupations actually
available within the evaluee's reach rather than in national
generalizations. Life care plans and medical cost projections follow the
same logic: future care is priced to the evaluee's own market using
provider-specific inquiries and published fee data rather than national
averages alone.

## Starting a Pierre engagement

Availability is confirmed through a conflict check before any substantive
discussion of the case. Submit the caption or party names through the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and note any imminent disclosure,
deposition, or trial dates so that scheduling can be assessed at the same
time. Counsel weighing a retention can also review the firm's
[nationwide coverage](/locations/nationwide/) to see how the same engagement
structure operates in other venues. Referrals are accepted from either side
of the caption, and an engagement is declined whenever a conflict or a scope
limitation would compromise the independence of the opinion.
