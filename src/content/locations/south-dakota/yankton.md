A report prepared for a Yankton County case is held to the same standard as
a report prepared for the largest venues in Purinton Analytics' national
practice, because the standard belongs to the firm, not to the market. The
same intake discipline, the same source citation practice, and the same
expectation of scrutiny by opposing counsel apply to an
[earning capacity and employability evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/)
here as anywhere else, and the same is true of a
[life care plan](/services/life-care-planning/) for a catastrophically
injured evaluee. Counsel for plaintiffs and counsel for defendants retain
the firm on identical terms, and the work is written so that either side
could take it apart and find the methodology intact.

## Yankton County courts and venue

The firm accepts engagements for matters venued in the Circuit Court serving
Yankton County and in the United States District Court for the District of
South Dakota. Typical referrals raise earning capacity and employability
questions in injury and employment matters, lifetime care questions in
catastrophic injury cases, and methodology questions when an opposing
expert's report warrants a
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/). Scope is
defined at intake around the referral questions, and any venue-specific
registration or qualification requirement is identified and confirmed
during the conflict check and intake process.

## Serving Yankton by appointment

Purinton Analytics maintains no staffed office in Yankton and publishes no
street address for this location. That candor is deliberate: accuracy about
presence is part of the same discipline that governs the opinions
themselves. Engagements ordinarily begin remotely, with records review,
structured interviews conducted by video, and standardized testing under
publisher protocols where remote administration is supported. When the
referral questions require in-person evaluation, the assessment is scheduled
in the evaluee's community by appointment, and when deposition or trial
dates require presence, travel is arranged from the Kansas City home
office. Counsel work with a single point of contact from intake through
testimony.

## Labor market research behind Yankton opinions

Vocational findings for Yankton County evaluees rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the relevant area
and for South Dakota statewide, supplemented by state workforce agency labor
market information and direct labor market research where the referral
questions warrant it. Opinions are tied to the occupational mix the evaluee
actually faces in a smaller regional market rather than to statewide
abstractions, and every source is cited so the analysis can be traced. Life
care plans and [medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/)
are priced to the evaluee's own community using provider-specific inquiries
and published fee data. Where lost earnings must become economic damages,
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
allow independent economist partners to perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.
