The person at the center of a Watertown case, the injured worker, the
terminated employee, the catastrophically injured evaluee, lives and works
in Codington County, and Purinton Analytics builds its analysis around that
person's actual circumstances. An
[earning capacity evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) asks
what work this evaluee can realistically obtain and hold in the market they
face, not in a hypothetical national market. A
[life care plan](/services/life-care-planning/) prices future care in the
community where the evaluee will receive it. Plaintiff and defense counsel
alike retain the firm for that evaluee-centered discipline, and both sides
receive reports written to be examined by the other.

## Codington County venue and courts

Engagements are accepted for matters venued in the Circuit Court serving
Codington County and in the United States District Court for the District of
South Dakota. The referral questions define the scope: employability and
placeability after injury, mitigation of damages in employment litigation,
lifetime care needs and their costs, or a
[rebuttal review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) of an opposing expert's
methodology. Any venue-specific registration or qualification requirement is
identified and confirmed during the conflict check and intake process, so
that qualification questions are resolved before disclosure deadlines
arrive.

## Evaluations by appointment, remote or in person

The firm has no staffed office in Watertown and publishes no street address
for this location. What counsel gets instead is a practice built for
distance: records review, structured interviews conducted by video, and
standardized testing administered under publisher protocols where remote
administration is supported. When the referral questions require an
in-person evaluation, the assessment is scheduled in the evaluee's own
community by appointment, and when deposition or trial dates require
courtroom presence, travel is arranged from the Kansas City home office.
Either way the work product is identical: the same report standard, the same
documentation, the same point of contact.

## Labor market evidence for Watertown evaluees

Vocational findings rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational
employment and wage estimates for the relevant area and for South Dakota
statewide, supplemented by state workforce agency labor market information
and direct employer research where the case warrants it. Opinions about what
an evaluee can earn are tied to occupations that actually exist in the
market the evaluee faces, and the sources behind every figure in the report
are cited so opposing counsel can trace them. Life care plans and
[medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/) draw on
provider-specific inquiries and published fee data for the evaluee's
community. Where counsel needs lost earnings carried through to economic
damages, [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
allow independent economist partners to perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.
