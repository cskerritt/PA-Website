Rapid City is the principal city of a Metropolitan Statistical Area that
takes in Pennington County, Meade County, and Custer County, and Purinton
Analytics accepts engagements across that entire footprint. Plaintiff and
defense counsel retain the firm here for the same assignments it accepts
nationally: an
[earning capacity and employability evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/)
in an injury or employment dispute, a
[life care plan](/services/life-care-planning/) in a catastrophic injury
case, or a rebuttal review of an opposing expert's report ahead of
deposition. The label on the map matters less than the discipline behind
the work: every opinion is prepared to be read by opposing counsel,
whichever side of the caption made the referral.

## Courts serving the Rapid City area

The firm accepts engagements for matters venued in the Circuit Court
serving Pennington County, Meade County, and Custer County, and in the
United States District Court for the District of South Dakota. Personal
injury, employment, and catastrophic injury matters each raise their own
vocational and care-planning questions, and the scope of each engagement is
defined at intake around the specific referral questions counsel need
answered. Any venue-specific registration or qualification requirement is
identified and confirmed during the conflict check and intake process, so
disclosure deadlines arrive without procedural surprises.

## By appointment, honestly stated

Rapid City meetings are by appointment. The firm maintains no staffed
office in the area and publishes no street address for this location;
candor about presence is part of the same discipline that governs every
report it signs. Most engagements proceed remotely: records review,
structured interviews by video, and standardized testing under publisher
protocols where remote administration is supported. When the referral
questions call for in-person evaluation, when an evaluee cannot reasonably
be assessed remotely, or when deposition and trial dates require physical
presence, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office and planned
around the litigation calendar. Counsel are told at intake which parts of
the engagement will be performed remotely and which in person, and the file
documents both.

## Labor market data for the Rapid City metropolitan area

Vocational findings for evaluees in the Rapid City area rest on Bureau of
Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Rapid
City metropolitan area and for South Dakota statewide, together with state
workforce agency labor market information and direct labor market research
where warranted. Opinions about employability and earning capacity are
anchored to the market the evaluee actually faces, and the report documents
the path from source to conclusion so that either side can retrace it. Life
care plans and
[medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/) are priced
to the evaluee's own community using provider-specific inquiries and
published fee data. When lost earnings must be converted into economic
damages, [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
allow independent economist partners to perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Starting a Rapid City engagement

The starting point for any Rapid City area matter is a
[conflict check](/refer-a-case/): submit the caption or party names and note
any imminent disclosure, deposition, or trial dates. Once conflicts clear,
scope and fee structure are confirmed in writing before analysis begins.
Counsel weighing venue questions elsewhere in the state can review the
[South Dakota page](/locations/south-dakota/), and the
