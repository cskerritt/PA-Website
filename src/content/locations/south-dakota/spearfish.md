standardized testing under publisher protocols where remote administration

## Labor market research for Lawrence County evaluees

Vocational findings for Lawrence County evaluees rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the relevant area
and for South Dakota statewide, together with state workforce agency labor
market information and direct labor market research where warranted. The
opinion addresses the employment market the evaluee actually faces, and
every wage and occupational source cited in the report is identified so
that opposing counsel can check it. Life care plans and
[medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/) are priced to
the evaluee's own community through provider-specific inquiries and
published fee data. When the case requires complete economic damages,
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
let independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis
while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement
structure.
