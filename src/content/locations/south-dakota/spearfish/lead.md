## Lead's Place in the Spearfish Labor Market

Lead is a small town within the [Spearfish metro area](/locations/south-dakota/spearfish/), and its residents participate in a labor market that extends well beyond the town line, with Spearfish serving as the principal city. That reality shapes earning capacity analysis for a Lead evaluee: the relevant question is not what work exists inside the town itself, but what employment is realistically accessible across the surrounding area given the evaluee's restrictions, skills, and transferable work history. Vocational findings rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the relevant area and for South Dakota statewide, state workforce agency labor market information, and direct labor market research where the referral questions warrant it. Numbers enter the report only with their sources attached, and conclusions about placeability in a smaller market are stated with the caution a smaller market demands.
