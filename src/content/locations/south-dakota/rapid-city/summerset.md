For an evaluee living in Summerset, the practical question comes before the
legal one: how does a person recovering from a serious injury actually get
evaluated by a national expert practice? Purinton Analytics answers it by
bringing the process to the person. Records review, counsel conferences, and
report drafting are handled remotely, and when the referral calls for an
in-person [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) assessment or
[vocational evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/), the
appointment is set in the evaluee's own community, in appropriate
professional space, at a time worked out with counsel beforehand. Referrals
are welcome from plaintiff and defense counsel alike, and the process is
identical whichever side retains the firm.

## The county and courts behind a Summerset case

Summerset lies in Meade County, so state-court claims from the town

available to matters that belong there; which court ultimately controls a
particular caption is a venue question the firm leaves to the conflict check
and intake, where any venue-specific registration or qualification
requirement is also confirmed. Counsel looking at the wider region can

the [South Dakota state page](/locations/south-dakota/), or the
[nationwide practice page](/locations/nationwide/) for the methodology that
applies everywhere the firm works.

## A small town measured against a metro market

Summerset is a small community inside the Rapid City metro area, and that
context shapes the vocational analysis: the relevant market for a Summerset
evaluee is the one reachable from home, not an abstraction. Employability
and earning-capacity opinions draw on Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates for the Rapid City metropolitan
area and for South Dakota statewide, alongside state workforce agency labor
market information and direct labor market research where warranted. The
report characterizes opportunity qualitatively, discloses its assumptions,
and stops where the evidence stops. When the case needs a present-value loss
figure as well, the firm arranges
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
under which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work runs under one
engagement structure.

## What by-appointment service means here

Purinton Analytics keeps no office in Summerset and lists no street address
for this location; stating that plainly is part of the same candor the
reports practice. Secure electronic transfer carries the records, scheduled
calls carry the strategy discussions, and travel carries the rest: when
evaluation, deposition, or trial requires presence, it is scheduled from the
Kansas City home office. Disclosure deadlines, discovery cutoffs, and trial
settings are gathered at intake so the work is sequenced to the case
calendar from the first day of the engagement.
