## Earning Capacity in a Regional Context

An evaluee's residence in Waynesboro frames the labor market question but does not end it. Vocational conclusions rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Augusta metropolitan area and for Georgia statewide, together with state workforce agency labor market information and, where the referral questions justify it, direct contact with employers in the relevant market. The analysis asks what work is realistically available to this evaluee, with this education, this work history, and these restrictions, from this place; it does not assume mobility the record will not support, and it does not ignore opportunity the record shows is reachable. When the case also needs economic damages quantified, [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) pair the vocational and care-planning work with independent economist partners who perform and sign the economic analysis under one engagement structure.
