## Labor Market Evidence on a Deadline

A compressed schedule is never a reason to thin the evidence. Vocational findings for a Thomson evaluee draw on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Augusta metropolitan area and for Georgia statewide, supplemented by state workforce agency labor market information and by direct labor market research when the referral questions require it. Thomson belongs to a regional employment market centered on Augusta, so transferable skills analysis weighs the occupations reachable from McDuffie County, not a hypothetical market the evaluee could not actually access. ## Scheduling the Evaluation Itself

There is no Thomson office and no published street address here; that candor is deliberate, because accuracy about presence belongs to the same discipline as accuracy in a report. Meetings are by appointment. Records review and the vocational interview are ordinarily handled remotely by secure video, which is often the fastest path to a timely disclosure, and when the referral questions call for an in-person evaluation the appointment is set in the evaluee's own community, with travel scheduled from the Kansas City home office. Deposition and trial appearances are planned the same way. Counsel with matters far outside Georgia will find the identical process described for [national referrals](/locations/nationwide/).
