A small town does not lower the standard of proof. When a Harlem case turns on lost earning capacity or the cost of future care, the opinion offered in evidence faces the same cross-examination it would face in any large city, and it should be built accordingly. Purinton Analytics prepares vocational expert and life care planning opinions for Harlem matters on that premise, for plaintiff and defense counsel alike: a methodology that is documented, sources that are named, and conclusions that follow from the record rather than from the size of the community they concern.

## Where a Harlem Case Is Tried

Harlem lies in Columbia County, and its state-court civil matters are heard in the Superior Court of Columbia County. On the federal side, Georgia is served by the United States District Court for the Northern District of Georgia, the United States District Court for the Middle District of Georgia, and the United States District Court for the Southern District of Georgia; rather than declare here which of those forums governs, the firm confirms the controlling venue, and any registration or qualification requirement particular to it, during the conflict check and intake. Harlem is one of the communities covered by the regional practice on the [Augusta metro page](/locations/georgia/augusta/), and the [Georgia state page](/locations/georgia/) maps the practice across the rest of the state.

## The Labor Market Beyond the Town Line

For a Harlem evaluee, the town itself is rarely the whole employment picture, and pretending otherwise would distort the analysis in either direction. Findings are grounded in Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Augusta metropolitan area and for Georgia statewide, in state workforce agency labor market information, and in direct labor market research when the referral questions call for it. The report then connects that regional data to the individual: education, work history, transferable skills, and medically supported restrictions, so the trier of fact sees a specific person placed in a real market rather than an abstraction placed in a spreadsheet.

## Records, Appointments, and Travel

Candor about logistics is part of the methodology. There is no Harlem office, no local storefront, and no street address published for this page; every meeting is by appointment. Engagements ordinarily run remotely, with the records request tailored to the claims at intake and the vocational interview conducted over secure video. Where an in-person evaluation is warranted, it takes place in the evaluee's own community, and where deposition or trial testimony requires presence in the courtroom, travel is planned from the Kansas City home office with enough lead time to protect the dates. This is the same process the firm applies to [engagements accepted nationwide](/locations/nationwide/), which is why it holds up under scrutiny locally.
