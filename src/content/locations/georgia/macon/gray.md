Purinton Analytics is built to answer those questions plainly. The practice is remote-first, [vocational expert](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) and [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) work for Jones County matters is scoped at intake around the dates counsel already holds, and assessment of a Gray resident happens in that resident's own community or by video rather than in a distant office. Referrals are welcome from either side of the caption, and the workpapers are kept to a standard that assumes the other side will read them.

## Jones County Courts and Where a Gray Case Can Land

State-law claims arising in Gray proceed in the Superior Court, the trial court for Jones County matters. On the federal side, Georgia's district forums are the United States District Court for the Northern District of Georgia, the United States District Court for the Middle District of Georgia, and the United States District Court for the Southern District of Georgia. The firm does not guess at which district controls a given case; the governing venue, and any registration or qualification requirement that comes with it, is identified during the conflict check and intake. Opinions are prepared to the same disclosure standard for state and federal dockets alike, and the [Georgia practice page](/locations/georgia/) describes coverage across the state.

## Reading the Labor Market from Jones County

An earning-capacity opinion for a Gray claimant has to reflect where Jones County residents actually work, and for many that means the wider Macon area rather than the town alone. The firm grounds those opinions in the Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates covering the Macon metropolitan area and the state of Georgia, together with state workforce agency labor market information and, where warranted, direct labor market research with employers. The purpose of that sourcing is symmetry: the same documented data can support a plaintiff's loss claim or a defense rebuttal, and it is cited so that either party can trace every finding back to its source. Market context at the metro level appears on the [Macon metro page](/locations/georgia/macon/).

## How an Evaluation Actually Proceeds

There is no Purinton Analytics office in Gray, and the firm will not pretend otherwise; every meeting is by appointment. In a typical engagement the records come in before anything else, a structured vocational interview follows by secure video, and any in-person assessment takes place in the evaluee's community, with travel arranged from the Kansas City home office. Deposition testimony is given remotely where the court permits it and in person where it does not. When a referral needs economic loss calculations alongside the vocational and care-planning opinions, [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) allow independent economist partners to perform and sign the economic analysis while the rest of the work proceeds under one engagement structure. [Rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) of an opposing expert's report runs on the same calendar discipline, and the [nationwide page](/locations/nationwide/) explains how this model serves counsel across the country.
