## A commuting market wider than the city limits

An employability analysis confined to Centerville alone would understate the options open to its residents, who commute throughout the metropolitan area anchored by the principal city of Warner Robins. The firm therefore frames labor market questions at the metro level, drawing on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Warner Robins metropolitan area and for Georgia statewide, together with state workforce agency information and, where the referral demands it, direct contact with employers about actual openings and hiring practices. Every conclusion is anchored to a cited source, keeping the path from data to opinion open to inspection by either side.
