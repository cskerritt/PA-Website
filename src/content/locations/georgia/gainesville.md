The Gainesville metropolitan area is defined by Hall County alone, and that
compactness shapes how the practice serves it: a concentrated venue, a
clearly bounded labor market question, and an engagement model that does not
change with the size of the metro. Purinton Analytics provides plaintiff and
defense counsel in Gainesville with
[vocational evaluation and expert testimony](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. The same methodology that anchors the firm's
[Georgia practice](/locations/georgia/) statewide governs every Hall County
referral.

## Venue footprint centered on Hall County

Engagements are accepted for matters in the Superior Court of Hall County.
In federal court, the engagement follows the filing among the United States
District Court for the Northern District of Georgia, the United States
District Court for the Middle District of Georgia, and the United States
District Court for the Southern District of Georgia. A compact metro
does not simplify the expert's obligations: the written opinion is prepared
to the same standard as a filing in the largest venue the firm serves, with
a documented records basis, a defined scope, and conclusions framed so that
either side of the caption can test them.

## Meetings by appointment in Gainesville

The firm keeps no staffed office in Gainesville and publishes no street
address for this location, because describing presence accurately is part of
the discipline the reports themselves are held to. Counsel meet the practice
by appointment: records conferences, opinion discussions, and
pre-deposition preparation are held by video or telephone, and vocational
evaluations are conducted in the evaluee's own community when in-person
assessment is warranted, or remotely when the referral questions and testing
protocols permit it. When a deposition, mediation, or trial setting in Hall
County requires presence, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home
office against the operative court calendar.

## Defining the Gainesville labor market

Employability and earning capacity findings for Gainesville-area evaluees
rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage
estimates for the Gainesville metropolitan area and for Georgia statewide,
on state workforce agency labor market information, and on direct labor
market research where the referral questions warrant it. Because a
commutable labor market is defined by the evaluee's circumstances rather
than by metro boundaries, each report is explicit about whether the relevant
market is confined to the metro or extends beyond it, and it explains the
basis for that determination rather than assuming it. That transparency is what allows the wage and
employability conclusions to hold up under cross-examination.
