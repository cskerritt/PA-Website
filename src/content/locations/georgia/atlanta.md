Few venue footprints in the Southeast are as broad as the
Atlanta-Sandy Springs-Roswell metropolitan area, and litigation support here
has to be organized around that breadth. Purinton Analytics serves plaintiff
and defense counsel across the metro with
[vocational evaluation and expert testimony](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis
while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement
structure. The Atlanta practice sits within the firm's broader
[Georgia coverage](/locations/georgia/) and its
[national practice](/locations/nationwide/), and a matter filed in an outlying
county receives the same process as a matter filed in the metro's core.

## A venue footprint that spans the metro's superior courts

Engagements are accepted for matters in the Superior Court of Fulton County,
the Superior Court of DeKalb County, and the superior courts of the other
metro counties, including Barrow County, Cobb, Gwinnett, Clayton, Cherokee,
Forsyth, Henry, Fayette, Douglas, Rockdale, Paulding, Coweta, Newton, Walton, Bartow,
Carroll, Spalding, Dawson, Lumpkin, Pickens, Butts, Jasper, Morgan, Haralson,
Heard, Meriwether, and Pike. In federal court, the engagement follows the
filing: the United States District Court for the Northern District of Georgia,
the United States District Court for the Middle District of Georgia, or the
United States District Court for the Southern District of Georgia, depending
on where the matter sits. The written opinion does not change with the venue;
the same reporting standard applies in every court, so the file is prepared
from the outset for examination by either side of the caption.

## Meetings by appointment, offices honestly described

Atlanta meetings are by appointment. The firm keeps no staffed office in the
metro and publishes no street address for this location, because accuracy
about presence is part of the same discipline that governs the reports
themselves. Counsel schedule conferences by video or telephone for records
review, opinion discussion, and pre-deposition preparation, and vocational
evaluations are arranged in the evaluee's own community when in-person
assessment is warranted. When deposition or trial dates require presence,
travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office and planned against the
operative litigation calendar rather than left open-ended.

## Labor market research for Atlanta evaluees

Employability and earning capacity findings rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Atlanta
metropolitan area and for Georgia statewide, on state workforce agency labor
market information, and on direct labor market research where the referral
questions warrant it. Because a footprint this broad contains distinct local
labor markets, each report is explicit about how the evaluee's commutable
labor market was defined and why, so the wage and employability conclusions
can be tested on their own terms in deposition or at trial.

## Starting an Atlanta engagement

Engagements are accepted from plaintiff and defense counsel alike, and the
intake sequence is identical for either side: a documented conflict check, a
defined scope, and a records request tailored to the claims. Submit the
caption or party names through the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and
note any imminent disclosure, deposition, or trial dates so that report
scheduling can be set against them from the first conversation.
