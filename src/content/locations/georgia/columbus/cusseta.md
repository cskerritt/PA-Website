## Chattahoochee County venue context

Civil matters arising in Cusseta are heard in the Superior Court of
Chattahoochee County, and the firm prepares its opinions to the standard
that court's calendar demands: a defined scope, a records request tailored
to the claims, and a written report that either side of the caption can
test. When a Cusseta dispute is filed federally, Georgia offers the United
States District Court for the Northern District of Georgia, the United
States District Court for the Middle District of Georgia, and the United
States District Court for the Southern District of Georgia as its forums;
the controlling venue for a particular matter, along with any
venue-specific registration or qualification requirement, is identified
and confirmed during the conflict check and intake process. Counsel with
matters elsewhere in the surrounding counties will find the broader venue
footprint on the [Columbus metro page](/locations/georgia/columbus/).

## A small town in the Columbus labor market

Earning-capacity opinions for a Cusseta evaluee are not confined to the
town limits. The relevant labor market is the market the evaluee can
actually reach, and for Cusseta that means the Columbus metropolitan area,
which spans Georgia and Alabama. Vocational findings rest on Bureau of
Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the
Columbus metropolitan area and for Georgia statewide, on state workforce
agency labor market information, and on direct labor market research where
the referral questions warrant it. Because the analysis is qualitative
about place and rigorous about method, it holds up whether opposing
counsel argues the market is too small or too large for the occupations at
issue.

## Evaluating an evaluee who lives in Cusseta

The practice is remote-first, which matters most in small communities like
Cusseta. Records review, counsel conferences, and pre-deposition preparation
proceed by video and telephone, so the engagement moves at the speed of
the litigation rather than the speed of travel. When the referral
questions call for in-person assessment, the evaluee is seen in his or her
own community rather than summoned to a distant office. Purinton Analytics
keeps no staffed office in Cusseta or anywhere in the Columbus area and
publishes no local street address, because accuracy about presence is part
of the same discipline that governs its reports; when deposition or trial
dates require presence, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home
office. For matters that also need economic loss quantification, the firm
offers [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under a
single engagement structure.
