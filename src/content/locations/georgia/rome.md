## Floyd County and the Georgia federal courts

The Rome metropolitan area consists of Floyd County, and state-court
matters are heard in the Superior Court there. The firm accepts federal
engagements throughout Georgia, including the United States District Court
for the Northern District of Georgia, the United States District Court for
the Middle District of Georgia, and the United States District Court for
the Southern District of Georgia. Whether a matter proceeds in the Superior
Court of Floyd County or in federal court, the disclosure practices, the
methodology, and the report format remain identical, which lets counsel
plan discovery around a known quantity.

## What by-appointment service looks like in Rome

Rome meetings are by appointment. The firm keeps no staffed office in the
metro and publishes no street address for this location, a candor it
considers part of the same discipline that governs its written opinions.
Most engagements begin remotely: records analysis, structured interviews by
video, and standardized testing under publisher protocols where remote
administration is supported. When the referral questions call for an
in-person evaluation, or when a deposition or trial setting requires
attendance, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office and
confirmed against counsel's calendar. Seeing the evaluee in their own
community, when warranted, tends to yield a fuller picture of function,
transportation, and access to care than an office visit far from home.

## Rome area labor market foundations

Employability and earning capacity opinions for Rome area evaluees rest on
Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for
the Rome metropolitan area and for Georgia statewide, together with state
workforce agency labor market information and direct labor market research
where warranted. Grounding the analysis in the occupations of the actual
regional economy gives the opinion resilience on cross-examination. Life
care plans and medical cost projections for area residents are priced to
the evaluee's own market through provider-specific inquiries and published
fee data. The firm's [Georgia page](/locations/georgia/) shows how Rome
coverage fits the statewide practice.
