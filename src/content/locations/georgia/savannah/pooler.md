## Chatham County venue and the federal alternative

Pooler lies in Chatham County, and state-court matters involving Pooler parties proceed in the Superior Court of Chatham County. When a claim is filed federally, Georgia's available forums are the United States District Court for the Southern District of Georgia, the United States District Court for the Middle District of Georgia, and the United States District Court for the Northern District of Georgia; the district that controls a particular case, and the scheduling order that comes with it, is confirmed during the conflict check and intake rather than assumed. Venue determines the deadlines but never the method: the written opinion rests on the same documented record whichever courtroom hears the matter.

## Reading the labor market a Pooler worker actually faces

A Pooler resident's employment prospects are rarely confined to city limits. Vocational findings for Pooler evaluees therefore rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Savannah metropolitan area and for Georgia statewide, on state workforce agency labor market information, and on direct labor market research when a referral question calls for it. Every report states qualitatively what the data show and defines the evaluee's commutable market explicitly, so that an employability or earning capacity conclusion can be tested in deposition without ambiguity about which market it describes. No figure appears in an opinion unless the underlying source supports it.
