## An opinion that survives the market question

For a small Bryan County community, the opening challenge from opposing counsel is usually the labor market: too narrow and the opinion looks contrived, too broad and it looks careless. Pembroke opinions rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Savannah metropolitan area and for Georgia statewide, on state workforce agency labor market information, and on direct labor market research where warranted, with the commutable market defined explicitly and the reasons stated. Findings remain qualitative wherever the data are qualitative, which is precisely what keeps them standing under hostile questioning.
