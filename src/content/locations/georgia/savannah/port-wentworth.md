## The courts behind the calendar

Port Wentworth is a Chatham County community, and the Superior Court of Chatham County is the state trial forum for its civil matters. On the federal side, the available Georgia forums are the United States District Court for the Southern District of Georgia, the United States District Court for the Middle District of Georgia, and the United States District Court for the Northern District of Georgia. Which district's scheduling order actually governs is a question resolved during the conflict check and intake, not a fact assumed on this page. From that point forward, the disclosure sequence of the controlling court becomes the spine of the engagement plan.

## Scheduling the evaluation itself

By-appointment service is the honest description of the firm's presence here: no Port Wentworth office, no published street address, and no pretense of either. What counsel get instead is responsiveness. Records conferences and pre-deposition sessions run by video or telephone within days rather than weeks. When in-person assessment is warranted, the vocational evaluation takes place in the evaluee's own community; when the instruments allow, remote evaluation protects the timeline. Travel for depositions and trial testimony is arranged from the Kansas City home office and locked to the court's dates early, so presence is never the item that slips.
