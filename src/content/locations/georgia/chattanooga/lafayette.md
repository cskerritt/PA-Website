A LaFayette case arrives at Purinton Analytics as a set of dates before it
arrives as a set of facts: the disclosure deadline, the discovery cutoff,
the mediation setting, the trial calendar. The practice schedules Walker
County engagements backward from those dates, so that
[earning capacity and employability opinions](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care plans](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[rebuttal reviews](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) are disclosed complete
rather than supplemented under pressure. Plaintiff and defense firms retain
the practice on the same terms, and every report is written on the
assumption that opposing counsel will read it closely.

## Walker County courts and the federal alternative

For state-court matters, engagements are accepted in the Superior Court
serving Walker County. Where a LaFayette dispute is in federal court,
Georgia offers the United States District Court for the Northern District of
Georgia, the United States District Court for the Middle District of
Georgia, and the United States District Court for the Southern District of
Georgia as possible forums; which district controls, and whether the venue
imposes any registration or qualification requirement, is confirmed during
the conflict check and intake process rather than assumed. Report structure
does not change with the forum. The methodology counsel can review on the
firm's [Georgia page](/locations/georgia/) is the methodology applied in
Walker County.

## Where LaFayette sits in the regional economy

LaFayette is a small Georgia community within the Chattanooga metropolitan
area, and its residents' employment prospects are shaped by that larger
regional market as well as by local conditions in Walker County. Vocational
findings therefore draw on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational
employment and wage estimates for the Chattanooga metropolitan area and for
Georgia statewide, alongside state workforce agency labor market information
and, where the questions call for it, direct employer sampling. The
qualitative point matters in testimony: what an injured worker from
LaFayette can earn depends on the occupations realistically open within a
reasonable commute, and the analysis names its sources so either side can
test them. Context on the wider regional market appears on the
[Chattanooga metro page](/locations/tennessee/chattanooga/).

## Seeing the evaluee without moving the evaluee

Records review, vocational interviewing, and standardized testing are
handled remotely in most LaFayette engagements, which keeps costs
proportionate to the case. When an in-person evaluation is warranted, the
firm comes to the evaluee's community in Walker County instead of asking an
injured person to travel; that trip is planned from the Kansas City home
office and coordinated with counsel's calendar. There is no staffed
Purinton Analytics office in LaFayette and no local address to publish,
a fact the firm states plainly because candor about logistics is part of
the credibility it offers on the stand.

## Opening a Walker County engagement

Every engagement opens the same way: with a documented conflict check. Counsel
should [request a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) with the case caption and
parties, flagging any disclosure or trial date already on the calendar.
Cleared matters receive a defined scope, a fee schedule, and a tailored
records request. When damages require an economist, the firm offers
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. The same intake path serves matters far beyond
Georgia through the [nationwide practice](/locations/nationwide/).
