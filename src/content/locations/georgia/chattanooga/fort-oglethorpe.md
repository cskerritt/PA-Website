livings in a metropolitan economy centered across the state line in
Chattanooga. That geography matters in litigation: an earning capacity
opinion for a Fort Oglethorpe plaintiff or defendant that ignores where the
jobs actually are will not survive careful cross-examination. Purinton
Analytics accounts for that reality in
[vocational expert evaluations](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[rebuttal review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) prepared for counsel on
either side of the caption in Catoosa County matters.

## A commuting market, not a town market

Fort Oglethorpe is not a self-contained labor market. It functions as part
of the broader Chattanooga metropolitan area, and vocational findings for
evaluees here rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and
wage estimates for that metropolitan area and for Georgia statewide,
supplemented by state workforce agency labor market information and direct
labor market research where the referral questions warrant it. When
transferable skills analysis identifies occupations an evaluee can still
perform, the opinion addresses whether those occupations exist in reasonable
numbers within the commuting range a person in Fort Oglethorpe would
realistically face, rather than in an abstract national economy. The firm's
[Chattanooga metro page](/locations/tennessee/chattanooga/) describes how
the practice treats the market on both sides of the state line.

## How a Fort Oglethorpe evaluee is seen

Most engagements begin remotely: records review, structured vocational
interview by video, and standardized testing administered under protocols
designed for remote use. When the referral questions call for in-person
evaluation, the evaluation is scheduled in the evaluee's own community
rather than requiring travel to a distant office, and the firm's travel is
arranged from its Kansas City home office. Purinton Analytics keeps no
staffed office in Fort Oglethorpe and publishes no local street address,
because accuracy about presence is part of the same discipline that governs
its written opinions. Counsel receive a defined scope at intake, a records
request tailored to the claims, and a report built on sources both sides can
verify.

Deadlines drive expert work, and Catoosa County cases are scheduled around
disclosure dates, discovery cutoffs, and trial settings from the outset.
To begin, submit the caption or party names through the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and note any imminent disclosure,

scope, a fee schedule, and a records request, whether the matter is venued
in Catoosa County, elsewhere in Georgia, or in another state entirely
