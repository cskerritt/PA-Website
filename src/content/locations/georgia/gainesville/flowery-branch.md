## A suburb's labor market, defined on the record

Employability and earning capacity findings for a Flowery Branch evaluee
rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage
estimates covering the Gainesville metropolitan area and Georgia as a whole,
supplemented by state workforce agency labor market information and by
direct labor market research when the referral questions call for it.
Those [Gainesville metro](/locations/georgia/gainesville/) estimates
describe the market as the government surveys it, not as any one evaluee
experiences it, so each report states expressly whether the evaluee's
commutable market tracks that survey area, sits within Hall County, or
reaches beyond both, and it explains the evidence behind that judgment. A market definition that is
argued for, not assumed, is what keeps wage and employability conclusions
standing under cross-examination.
