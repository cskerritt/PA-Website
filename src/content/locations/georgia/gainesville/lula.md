## County and courthouse context for Lula matters

Lula sits in Hall County, and state-court matters proceed in the Superior
Court of Hall County. Federal filings in Georgia are heard in the United
States District Court for the Northern District of Georgia, the United
States District Court for the Middle District of Georgia, or the United
States District Court for the Southern District of Georgia, and the district
that governs a specific matter is confirmed as part of the conflict check
and intake. The venue never changes the product: a written opinion with a
documented records basis and a scope defined at the outset, prepared so that
cross-examining counsel can trace every conclusion to its source.

## The labor market question for a Lula evaluee

A small town poses the market-definition problem in its sharpest form,
because the jobs an evaluee can realistically hold are rarely all located
where the evaluee lives. The analysis therefore starts from residual
capacity, transferable skills, and commuting realities, and only then maps
those findings against Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment
and wage estimates for the
[Gainesville metropolitan area](/locations/georgia/gainesville/) and for
Georgia statewide, together with state workforce agency labor market
information and direct labor market research where the questions demand it.
The report says plainly whether the relevant market is the metro, something
narrower, or something wider, and defends that choice with evidence both
sides can examine.
