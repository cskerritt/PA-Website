A civil case arising in Oakwood carries a Hall County caption, and that is
where the vocational expert's obligations begin: the Superior Court of Hall
County expects opinions built on a documented record, and so does every
lawyer who will cross-examine them. Purinton Analytics writes to that
expectation for both sides of the bar, providing Oakwood counsel with
[vocational evaluation and expert testimony](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. Oakwood is a small suburb, but nothing about the
firm's standard scales down with the size of the town.

## Where Oakwood matters are heard

State-court engagements center on the Superior Court of Hall County. For
matters filed federally, Georgia offers the United States District Court for
the Northern District of Georgia, the United States District Court for the
Middle District of Georgia, and the United States District Court for the
Southern District of Georgia as its trial forums; the controlling district
for any given case is identified during the conflict check and intake
process, never presumed from the town's location. Reports are drafted so the
analysis reads identically whichever courtroom receives it: defined scope,
disclosed sources, and conclusions a jury can follow and an opposing expert
can attempt to pick apart.

## Oakwood within the Gainesville labor market

The [Gainesville metropolitan area](/locations/georgia/gainesville/) gives
the labor market question for an Oakwood evaluee a published statistical
frame, but the analysis never treats that frame as the answer. Findings rest
on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for
the Gainesville metro area, on the statewide Georgia series,
on state workforce agency labor market information, and on direct labor
market research where the referral warrants it. Each report addresses
whether the evaluee's realistic market lies within the county or extends
past it, and gives the reasoning. Rigor about market definition, rather than
borrowed assumptions, is what the firm offers counsel on either side of the
caption.

## Conducting the evaluation

There is no Purinton Analytics office in Oakwood, and the firm does not
pretend otherwise; meetings happen by appointment because that is the honest
description of how a national practice serves a small suburb. Most of the
work is remote by design: records analysis, counsel conferences, and
testimony preparation proceed by video or telephone. In-person vocational
assessment, when the referral requires it, takes place in the evaluee's own
community rather than imposing travel on an injured person. Court
appearances and depositions in Hall County are reached by travel scheduled
from the Kansas City home office, coordinated alongside the firm's
[nationwide docket](/locations/nationwide/) so that disclosure and hearing
dates hold.
