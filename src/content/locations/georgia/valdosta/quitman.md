injured person can still perform, what that work pays in the surrounding

## Brooks County courts and venue

State-court matters from Quitman are tried in the Superior Court, and the
firm prepares reports and testimony for Brooks County cases to the same
documentation standard it applies in any venue, large or small. Federal
engagements are accepted statewide. Georgia's federal trial courts are the
United States District Court for the Middle District of Georgia, the United
States District Court for the Northern District of Georgia, and the United
States District Court for the Southern District of Georgia; the controlling
venue for a particular matter is confirmed during the conflict check and
intake. Whichever forum the case occupies, counsel receive a written opinion
built to withstand cross-examination by the other side.

## A small market, read honestly

Brooks County is a small, rural labor market, and pretending otherwise is
the fastest way for a vocational opinion to fail under scrutiny. The firm's
analyses for Quitman evaluees describe the local employment base as it
actually is, then examine the wider commuting area centered on Valdosta,
the metro's principal city, where much of the region's hiring occurs.
Findings rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and
wage estimates for the Valdosta metropolitan area and for Georgia
statewide, supplemented by state workforce agency labor market information
and direct employer contact where the referral questions warrant it. The
resulting opinion distinguishes the jobs that exist in theory from the jobs
realistically attainable for this evaluee, in this place, with these
restrictions.

## Meeting Quitman evaluees where they are

There is no Purinton Analytics office in Quitman, and the firm does not
pretend otherwise; Brooks County work is served by appointment, and
accuracy about presence is part of the same discipline that governs the
reports. Most engagements proceed through remote interviews, structured
records review, and standardized vocational testing that requires no travel
from anyone. When an in-person evaluation is appropriate, it is arranged in
the evaluee's own community rather than forcing a trip, and when deposition
or trial dates require physical presence, travel is scheduled from the
Kansas City home office. Counsel weighing the model can review the
[Georgia practice page](/locations/georgia/) or the firm's
[nationwide practice](/locations/nationwide/) overview for the fuller
picture.

## Starting a Brooks County engagement

Early retention protects the disclosure calendar and the quality of the
record alike. Submit the caption or party names through the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/), note any pending deadlines, and
identify the referral questions; the firm responds with a defined scope and
a records request tailored to the claims. Plaintiff and defense engagements
are accepted on identical terms, and the same evaluee may be assessed with
the same instruments regardless of which side issued the retention. Where
economic damages are also in dispute, the firm offers
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under a
single engagement structure.
