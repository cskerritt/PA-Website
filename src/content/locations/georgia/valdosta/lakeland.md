## Lakeland inside the Valdosta commuting market

Lanier County's own employment base is modest, so a defensible vocational
opinion treats Lakeland as part of the commuting market anchored by
Valdosta, the metropolitan area's principal city, while stating candidly
what the immediate county does and does not offer. The firm's labor
market conclusions draw on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational
employment and wage estimates for the Valdosta metropolitan area and for

on direct contact with employers when a placement dispute makes it
necessary. Scale is described qualitatively and sourced precisely, which

methodology.
