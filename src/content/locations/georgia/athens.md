A vocational opinion is only as strong as the labor market data beneath it,
and for Athens-Clarke County matters that data question is where the
analysis starts. Purinton Analytics grounds employability and earning
capacity findings for Athens-area evaluees in market-specific evidence
before any conclusion is drawn, and it brings that same footing to
[vocational evaluation and expert testimony](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. The Athens metro is served as part of the firm's
[Georgia practice](/locations/georgia/), with the same standards applied in
every county of the footprint.

## Courts and counties in the Athens metro

Engagements are accepted for matters in the Superior Court of Clarke County
and the superior courts of Oconee County, Madison County, and Oglethorpe
County. Federal engagements follow the filing among the United States
District Court for the Middle District of Georgia, the United States
District Court for the Northern District of Georgia, and the United States
District Court for the Southern District of Georgia. Plaintiff and defense
referrals are handled identically in each of these venues: the same conflict
check, the same defined scope, and the same written opinion standard,
prepared from the outset for scrutiny by the other side.

## By-appointment logistics for Athens matters

The firm maintains no staffed office in Athens and publishes no street
address for this location. That is stated outright because accuracy about
presence belongs to the same discipline that governs the reports. Meetings
are by appointment: records conferences, opinion discussions, and
pre-deposition preparation proceed by video or telephone, and vocational
evaluations are conducted in the evaluee's own community when in-person
assessment is warranted or remotely when the referral questions and testing
protocols permit it. When deposition or trial dates call for presence,
travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office and set against the
court's calendar.

## Data before conclusions

Vocational findings for Athens-area evaluees rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Athens
metropolitan area and for Georgia statewide, on state workforce agency labor
market information, and on direct labor market research where a referral
question requires evidence the published estimates cannot supply. Each
report defines the evaluee's commutable labor market explicitly, explains
why that definition fits the evaluee's circumstances, and separates what the
data show from what the evaluator concludes, so that cross-examination can
engage the analysis on its actual terms rather than on assumptions. Where a
life care plan is part of the referral, the same sourcing discipline applied
to wage data governs the cost research behind each recommended item of care.

## Starting an Athens engagement

The practice accepts engagements from either side of the caption, in the
Athens metro and throughout its [national coverage](/locations/nationwide/).
To begin, submit the caption or party names through the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and note any imminent disclosure,
deposition, or trial dates. Once the conflict check clears, counsel receive
a records checklist tailored to the claims, and the report timeline is fixed
against the operative disclosure calendar so the opinion is ready when the
case demands it.
