The Valdosta metropolitan area reaches well beyond its principal city, and
Purinton Analytics serves the full footprint: Lowndes County, Brooks County,
Lanier County, and Echols County all fall within the firm's Valdosta
coverage. Counsel on either side of the caption retain the firm for
[vocational expert witness](/services/vocational-expert-witness/)
evaluations of employability and earning capacity,
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) in catastrophic injury
matters, and [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Courts serving the Valdosta area

State-court matters from the metro are heard in the Superior Court, and the
firm accepts engagements arising in the Superior Courts of Lowndes, Brooks,
Lanier, and Echols counties alike. Federal matters are accepted throughout
Georgia, whether venued in the United States District Court for the Southern
District of Georgia, the United States District Court for the Middle
District of Georgia, or the United States District Court for the Northern
District of Georgia. Because the firm's reports follow a single national
standard, the same written opinion holds up whether it is examined in a
state courtroom or a federal one, and counsel working companion cases across
venues receive consistent methodology in each.

## Meetings by appointment, not a local storefront

Valdosta meetings are by appointment. The firm keeps no staffed office in
the metro and publishes no street address for this location, because
accuracy about presence is part of the same discipline that governs its
reports. Most engagements begin remotely: records review, structured
interviews by video, and standardized testing under publisher protocols
where remote administration is supported. When an in-person evaluation is
warranted, or when deposition and trial calendars require attendance, travel
is scheduled from the Kansas City home office and planned around the court's
dates. Counsel receive a single point of contact and the same report
standard regardless of where the work is performed.

## Labor market research for south Georgia evaluees

Vocational findings for evaluees in the Valdosta area rest on Bureau of
Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Valdosta
metropolitan area and for Georgia statewide, supplemented by state workforce
agency labor market information and direct employer contact where the
referral questions warrant it. Transferable skills analysis and placeability
opinions are grounded in the occupations actually present in the regional
economy rather than in national generalities, and life care plans and
medical cost projections for area residents are priced to the evaluee's own
market using provider-specific inquiries and published fee data. The
[Georgia state page](/locations/georgia/) describes how this metro coverage
fits within the firm's statewide work.

## Starting a Valdosta engagement

Engagements begin with a documented conflict check, a defined scope, and a
records request tailored to the claims, so that the written opinion rests on
a record both sides can examine. Submit the caption or party names through
the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and note any imminent disclosure,
deposition, or trial dates so scheduling can be confirmed at the outset.
Counsel with matters outside south Georgia can review the firm's
[national coverage](/locations/nationwide/), which applies the same
methodology, the same disclosure practices, and the same reporting standard
to every venue the firm serves.
