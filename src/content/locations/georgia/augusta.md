Augusta litigation often carries a caption from either side of the Georgia
and South Carolina line, and the Augusta-Richmond County metropolitan area is
served with that reality in mind. Purinton Analytics supports plaintiff and
defense counsel throughout the metro with
[vocational evaluation and expert testimony](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. Whichever state the filing sits in, the engagement runs
on the same documented conflict check, defined scope, and tailored records
request that govern the firm's [Georgia practice](/locations/georgia/) as a
whole.

## Venue footprint on both sides of the state line

On the Georgia side, engagements are accepted for matters in the Superior
Court of Richmond County, the Superior Court of Columbia County, and the
superior courts of Burke County, McDuffie County, and Lincoln County. Federal
engagements follow the filing among Georgia's district courts: the United
States District Court for the Northern District of Georgia, the United States
District Court for the Middle District of Georgia, and the United States
District Court for the Southern District of Georgia. On the South Carolina
side of the metro, matters arising in Aiken County, South Carolina, and
Edgefield County, South Carolina, proceed under the same engagement
structure, and any venue-specific registration or qualification requirement
is identified and confirmed during the conflict check and intake process.

## By-appointment service, stated plainly

The firm maintains no staffed office in Augusta and publishes no street
address for this location. That is stated plainly because honesty about
presence is part of the same discipline that opposing counsel will test in
the report itself. What counsel receive instead is a practice built for
remote-first work: records conferences and pre-deposition preparation by
video or telephone, vocational evaluations conducted in the evaluee's own
community or remotely when the referral questions and testing protocols
permit it, and travel scheduled from the Kansas City home office when
deposition or trial dates require an in-person appearance.

## Labor market analysis for a cross-state metro

Employability and earning capacity opinions for Augusta-area evaluees are
grounded in Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage
estimates for the Augusta metropolitan area, together with statewide
estimates and state workforce agency labor market information from Georgia
and from South Carolina as the evaluee's circumstances require. Because the
metro spans states, each report states explicitly which labor market defines
the evaluee's commutable area and why, rather than leaving that judgment
implicit. Direct labor market research supplements the published estimates
where a referral question turns on a specific occupation or employer type.

## Starting an Augusta engagement

The practice accepts referrals from plaintiff and defense counsel on equal
terms, in the metro and across the firm's
[national coverage](/locations/nationwide/). To begin, submit the caption or
party names through the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/), identify the
court where the matter is pending, and note any imminent disclosure,
deposition, or trial dates. Once the conflict check clears, counsel receive a
records checklist tailored to the claims and a report timeline set against
the operative disclosure calendar.
