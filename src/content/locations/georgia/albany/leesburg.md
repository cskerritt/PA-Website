Leesburg sits inside the Albany, GA metropolitan area, and that fact carries
analytical weight: a Lee County evaluee's realistic employment options are
defined by the work within commuting reach of Albany, the metro's principal
city, not only by what exists inside the town limits. Purinton Analytics
builds its Leesburg opinions on that commuting-market reality, offering
counsel for plaintiffs and defendants alike a
[vocational evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) practice
grounded in labor market evidence and a
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) practice grounded in the
medical record.

## Lee County venue context

When a Leesburg matter is filed in state court, it proceeds in the Superior
Court, and every report is written on the assumption that opposing counsel
will read it closely. Georgia's federal trial forums are the United States
District Court for the Northern District of Georgia, the United States
District Court for the Middle District of Georgia, and the United States
District Court for the Southern District of Georgia; rather than assume
which one reaches a given dispute, the firm confirms the controlling venue
during the conflict check and intake, and resolves any qualification or
registration requirement that venue carries at the same time. The
[Georgia locations page](/locations/georgia/) collects the firm's statewide
venue coverage.

## Measuring earning capacity in a commuter county

Because many working residents of a county like Lee County look toward the
principal city for employment, an earning capacity opinion that stops at the
town line would misstate the market in either direction. The firm's
analyses instead use Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and
wage estimates for the Albany metropolitan area and for Georgia statewide,
state workforce agency labor market information, and, when the referral
questions require it, direct labor market research such as employer contact
and job posting review across the commutable region. The result is a
qualitative, sourced picture of what an evaluee can and cannot access after
injury, stated so that either side can test it.

## Meeting a Lee County evaluee where they are

The firm's only staffed office is its home office in Kansas City; Leesburg,
like every other location the practice serves, is handled by appointment,
and no local address is published because none exists. Evaluations proceed
by secure video under publisher protocols or in person in the evaluee's own
community or at counsel's offices, whichever the case requires. Deposition
and trial appearances are made in person or remotely where the court
permits, with travel from Kansas City scheduled against the deadlines
identified at intake. Where the case also demands an economic loss
calculation, [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
allow independent economist partners to perform and sign the economic
analysis while vocational and care-planning work advances under one
engagement structure.
