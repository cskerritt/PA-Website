The deliverable in a Warner Robins engagement is a written opinion built to
survive the other side's scrutiny, and everything else in the practice is
arranged to serve that document. For plaintiff and defense counsel in the
Warner Robins metropolitan area, Purinton Analytics provides
[vocational evaluation and expert testimony](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. Each report states its scope, documents its records
basis, and separates data from conclusions, consistent with the standards of
the firm's [Georgia practice](/locations/georgia/) as a whole.

## Venue footprint in Houston and Peach counties

Engagements are accepted for matters in the Superior Court of Houston
County and the Superior Court of Peach County. Federal engagements follow
the filing among the United States District Court for the Middle District of
Georgia, the United States District Court for the Northern District of
Georgia, and the United States District Court for the Southern District of
Georgia. The venue determines the calendar and the disclosure rules the
engagement must meet; it does not change the method, which is applied
identically whether the referral comes from the plaintiff's side or the
defense.

## Presence described accurately

The firm has no staffed office in Warner Robins and publishes no street
address for this location. That is said plainly, because a report that asks
a court to rely on its accuracy should come from a practice that describes
itself accurately. Meetings are by appointment: records conferences, opinion
discussions, and pre-deposition preparation are conducted by video or
telephone, and vocational evaluations take place in the evaluee's own
community when in-person assessment is warranted, or remotely when the
referral questions and testing protocols permit it. When deposition or trial
dates require an appearance, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home
office against the operative court calendar.

## Labor market evidence behind each report

Employability and earning capacity findings for Warner Robins-area evaluees
rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage
estimates for the Warner Robins metropolitan area and for Georgia statewide,
on state workforce agency labor market information, and on direct labor
market research where the referral questions warrant it. Each report defines
the evaluee's commutable labor market explicitly and explains why that
definition fits the facts of the evaluation, so opposing counsel can see the
foundation of every wage and employability conclusion before the deposition
starts. Where the published estimates leave a referral question open, the
report says so and describes the direct research that closed the gap.

## Starting a Warner Robins engagement

Referrals are welcome from either side of the caption, for metro matters and
for engagements anywhere in the firm's
[national coverage](/locations/nationwide/). Submit the caption or party
names through the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and note any
imminent disclosure, deposition, or trial dates. Once the conflict check
clears, counsel receive a records checklist tailored to the claims, and the
report timeline is set against the disclosure calendar so the written
opinion is complete when the case requires it.
