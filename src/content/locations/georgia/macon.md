Disclosure deadlines, deposition settings, and trial calendars are where
expert engagements succeed or fail, and the Macon practice is organized
around them. From the first intake conversation, Purinton Analytics sets the
report timeline for a Macon-Bibb County matter against the operative
disclosure calendar, so plaintiff and defense counsel alike know when the
[vocational evaluation and expert testimony](/services/vocational-expert-witness/)
work product, the [life care plan](/services/life-care-planning/), or a
[coordinated damages engagement](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
will be in hand. In coordinated engagements, independent economist partners
perform and sign the economic analysis while the vocational and
care-planning work proceeds under one engagement structure, a model applied
consistently across the firm's [Georgia practice](/locations/georgia/).

## Venue footprint in the Macon metro

Engagements are accepted for matters in the Superior Court of Bibb County
and the superior courts of the surrounding metro counties: Jones County,
Monroe County, Crawford County, and Twiggs County. In federal court, the
engagement follows the filing among the United States District Court for the
Middle District of Georgia, the United States District Court for the
Northern District of Georgia, and the United States District Court for the
Southern District of Georgia. Wherever the matter sits, the file is built to
the same standard: a defined scope, a documented records basis, and a
written opinion prepared for examination by either side of the caption.

## How by-appointment service works in Macon

The firm keeps no staffed office in Macon and publishes no street address
for this location; describing presence accurately is part of the same
discipline that governs the reports. Meetings are by appointment. Records
conferences, opinion discussions, and pre-deposition preparation are held by
video or telephone, which keeps scheduling responsive as deadlines approach.
Vocational evaluations are conducted in the evaluee's own community when
in-person assessment is warranted, or remotely when the referral questions
and testing protocols permit it. When testimony or trial support requires
presence, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office with the
court's calendar, not the firm's convenience, as the fixed point. The same
calendar discipline applies to
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) assignments when
an opposing report needs a timely response.

## Labor market foundations for Macon opinions

Employability and earning capacity findings for Macon-area evaluees rest on
Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for
the Macon metropolitan area and for Georgia statewide, on state workforce
agency labor market information, and on direct labor market research where
the referral questions warrant it. Each report states how the evaluee's
commutable labor market was defined and why, and it distinguishes plainly
between what the published estimates show and what direct research adds, so
that the foundation of every conclusion is visible to opposing counsel
before the deposition begins.

## Starting a Macon engagement

Referrals are accepted from plaintiff and defense counsel on identical
terms, for metro matters and for cases anywhere in the firm's
[national coverage](/locations/nationwide/). Submit the caption or party
names through the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and note any
imminent disclosure, deposition, or trial dates. Once the conflict check
clears, counsel receive a records checklist tailored to the claims, and the
engagement proceeds on the timeline the calendar requires.
