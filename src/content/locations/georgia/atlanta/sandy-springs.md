## Where a Sandy Springs case is heard

The city sits in Fulton County, so state-court filings run through the
Superior Court of Fulton County. Federal filings land in the state's district
courts: the United States District Court for the Northern District of
Georgia, the United States District Court for the Middle District of Georgia,
or the United States District Court for the Southern District of Georgia. The
controlling federal venue for a specific matter is identified during the
conflict check and intake, not presumed. Because the reporting standard never
varies by forum, a file opened for a Fulton County jury trial is prepared to
the same specification as one opened for a federal bench.

## A labor market larger than the city limits

A Sandy Springs evaluee competes for work in a labor market that extends
far past its municipal boundary. Vocational findings therefore draw on
Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for
the Atlanta metropolitan area and for Georgia statewide, on state workforce
agency labor market information, and on direct employer research when the
referral questions call for it. Each report defines the evaluee's commutable
labor market explicitly and explains the basis for that definition, so that
the employability and wage conclusions can be tested on their own terms at
deposition. Scale is described qualitatively and sourced; no figure appears
without a citation the other side can check, and deposition exhibits are
prepared from the same working papers as the report itself.

## By-appointment service, honestly framed

There is no Purinton Analytics office in Sandy Springs, and the firm does
not pretend otherwise with a rented address. Counsel conferences, records
review, and pre-deposition preparation proceed by video and telephone.
In-person vocational evaluation, when warranted, is scheduled in the
community where the evaluee actually lives, which keeps the assessment
grounded in the conditions that person faces. Depositions, hearings, and
trial appearances are supported by travel from the Kansas City home office,
arranged against the case calendar rather than left to chance, a practice
applied uniformly across the firm's [national work](/locations/nationwide/).
