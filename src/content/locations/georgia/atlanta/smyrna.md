Wage loss in a Smyrna case rarely stands alone. A serious injury claim
typically needs the vocational opinion, the care plan, and the economic
loss calculation to fit together without seams, because a gap between
experts is precisely what a cross-examiner hunts for. Purinton Analytics
addresses that risk for Smyrna counsel through
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure, supported by
[vocational evaluation and testimony](/services/vocational-expert-witness/)
and [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/). Smyrna sits inside
the firm's [Atlanta metro practice](/locations/georgia/atlanta/) and its
[Georgia coverage](/locations/georgia/).

## Shared record, separate signatures

Coordination does not mean shared conclusions; each discipline signs only
its own work. It means the [forensic economic damages](/services/forensic-economic-damages/)
analysis prepared and signed by an independent economist partner starts
from the same evaluee facts and the same records the vocational and
care-planning opinions rest on, so the defense of the damages model at
deposition is not undermined by inconsistent inputs. Plaintiff and defense
teams alike use the structure, sometimes to build a damages case and
sometimes to stress-test the one presented against them.

## Smyrna's courts and the venue question

State-court matters for this Cobb County city are heard in the Superior
Court of Cobb County. Federal matters go to Georgia's district courts: the
United States District Court for the Northern District of Georgia, the
United States District Court for the Middle District of Georgia, and the
United States District Court for the Southern District of Georgia. Which
district holds venue over a specific dispute is determined with counsel
during the conflict check and intake, and any registration or qualification
requirement particular to the forum is confirmed at the same stage.

## Labor market evidence for a Smyrna evaluee

Employability and earning capacity findings are built on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Atlanta
metropolitan area and for Georgia statewide, on state workforce agency
labor market information, and on direct labor market research where the
referral justifies it. The commutable labor market for a Smyrna evaluee is
defined explicitly in the report, occupation by occupation, with a cited
source behind every wage figure the economist partners later rely on, so
the whole damages chain can be audited from raw data to bottom line. The
economist partners receive those vocational findings with their sourcing
intact, so growth and discounting assumptions are applied to inputs that
have already been defended.

The firm keeps no office in Smyrna and lists no address there; meetings are

with in-person evaluation arranged in the evaluee's own community when
