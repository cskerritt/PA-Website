## Courts serving a Roswell caption

Roswell is a Fulton County city, and its state-court disputes are tried in
the Superior Court of Fulton County. Georgia's federal trial forums are the
United States District Court for the Northern District of Georgia, the
United States District Court for the Middle District of Georgia, and the
United States District Court for the Southern District of Georgia; the
district that governs any given filing is pinned down during the conflict
check and intake rather than announced in advance. What stays constant is
the file behind the opinion: a written scope, a records request drawn to the
claims, and a methodology section that discloses every source relied upon,
prepared on the assumption that opposing counsel will read it closely.

## Roswell workers in a metro-wide market

Because the metropolitan labor market surrounding Roswell is among the
largest in the Southeast, vocational conclusions cannot rest on impressions
about the local economy. They rest instead on Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates for the Atlanta metropolitan area
and for Georgia statewide, on state workforce agency labor market data, and
on targeted labor market research where the referral requires it. The report
explains which occupations were considered, how transferability of skills
was analyzed, and how the evaluee's commutable market was bounded, so that
each conclusion can be traced from evidence to opinion without a gap. Where
the record includes an earnings history, reported wages are reconciled with
the occupational data rather than replaced by a statistical average, so
capacity is measured for the person and not for a composite.

## How evaluation logistics work here

The practice is remote-first. Conferences with counsel, records analysis,
and preparation for testimony are conducted by video and telephone, and an
in-person vocational evaluation, when the questions call for it, takes place
in the evaluee's own community. The firm maintains no office in Roswell and
lists no local address, a fact stated plainly because overstating presence
would sit poorly next to reports that demand precision from everyone else.
Testimony travel, when depositions or trial require it, originates from the
Kansas City home office and is booked against the deadlines the court has
actually set. The same logistics govern the firm's
[nationwide practice](/locations/nationwide/).
