## Venue context for a Johns Creek dispute

Johns Creek sits in Fulton County, and state-court litigation involving its
residents and employers proceeds in the Superior Court of Fulton County.
Matters filed federally go to Georgia's district courts, which are the
United States District Court for the Northern District of Georgia, the
United States District Court for the Middle District of Georgia, and the
United States District Court for the Southern District of Georgia; venue for
a particular filing is a legal question resolved with counsel during the
conflict check and intake. Each court has its own scheduling rhythms, and
the engagement plan accounts for the forum's disclosure sequence from the
first conversation so the report is never racing the docket.

## Earning capacity measured against the metro market

A Johns Creek evaluee's employment prospects are assessed against the
metropolitan labor market, not against a guess about the neighborhood.
Findings draw on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage
estimates covering the Atlanta metropolitan area and Georgia statewide,
together with state workforce agency labor market information and direct
labor market inquiry where the referral questions demand it. The written
opinion identifies the occupations analyzed, the basis for the commutable
market boundary, and the source behind every wage observation, because a
conclusion that cannot be sourced is a conclusion the other side will take
apart. Draft opinions are calendared for counsel review with time to spare,
because a report finalized on the eve of disclosure invites the kind of
error a measured schedule would have caught.

## Scheduling evaluations without losing weeks

Remote work keeps the calendar intact. Records review, conferences with
counsel, and testimony preparation happen by video and telephone, so the
engagement progresses while depositions and motions proceed. If the
referral calls for an in-person vocational assessment, it is arranged in
the evaluee's own community at a time that protects the reporting deadline.
Purinton Analytics has no staffed office in Johns Creek and publishes no
address there; presence is described exactly as it is. When testimony
requires an appearance, travel is arranged from the Kansas City home office
with enough lead time to hold the dates the court has set, the same
discipline that governs the firm's [national engagements](/locations/nationwide/).
