## The venues behind a Brookhaven caption

Brookhaven lies in DeKalb County, and state-court matters are litigated in
the Superior Court of DeKalb County. Federal matters proceed in Georgia's
district courts, which are the United States District Court for the
Northern District of Georgia, the United States District Court for the
Middle District of Georgia, and the United States District Court for the
Southern District of Georgia. Which of them governs a specific filing, and
whether any venue-specific qualification requirement applies, is worked out
with counsel during the conflict check and intake rather than assumed in
advance. The candor promised above extends to the courtroom: reports carry
the same structure and sourcing discipline into every one of these forums,
prepared on the assumption that opposing counsel will read them closely.

## Grounding opinions in the regional labor market

For a Brookhaven evaluee, employability and earning capacity conclusions
are anchored in Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage
estimates for the Atlanta metropolitan area and for Georgia statewide,
augmented by state workforce agency labor market information and by direct
labor market research when the questions in the referral call for more. The
report sets out how the evaluee's commutable market was delineated, why
particular occupations were included or excluded, and where each wage
observation comes from, so that both retaining and opposing counsel can
audit the path from data to opinion. Interim findings reach retaining
counsel as the analysis matures, so strategy conferences are informed by
the developing evidence instead of surprised by the final draft, and the
opinion that emerges has already survived internal challenge.

## What by-appointment service actually looks like

Records arrive electronically and are reviewed against a defined scope.
Conferences with counsel, opinion discussions, and deposition preparation
run over video and telephone, which usually proves faster than coordinating
office visits. If the referral questions require an in-person vocational
evaluation, the firm arranges it in the evaluee's own community, meeting an
injured or displaced worker where daily life actually happens. And when a
deposition, hearing, or trial requires physical presence, travel is
scheduled from the Kansas City home office against the dates the court has
set. This is the model the firm runs everywhere in its
[national practice](/locations/nationwide/); Brookhaven is served no
differently, and no part of it depends on an office the firm does not have.

## Retaining the firm from Brookhaven

Engagements are open to plaintiff and defense counsel on identical terms,
and none begins until a conflict screen has cleared and the scope is
recorded in writing. The practical starting point is to
[request a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) with the case caption or the
parties' names, a brief note on the claims, and the litigation dates
already fixed, so the firm can confirm availability and propose a schedule
before confidential records move.
