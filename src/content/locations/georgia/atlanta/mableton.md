Mableton litigation is, for state-court purposes, Cobb County litigation:
the Superior Court of Cobb County is where its injury, employment, and
family law disputes are tried, and that courthouse context frames how
vocational and care-planning evidence should be prepared. Purinton Analytics
supports plaintiff and defense counsel in Mableton matters with
[vocational evaluation and expert testimony](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/), operating as
part of its [Atlanta metro practice](/locations/georgia/atlanta/) and
statewide [Georgia coverage](/locations/georgia/).

## From the county courthouse to the federal bench

While the Superior Court of Cobb County anchors state practice for
Mableton, federal matters proceed in whichever of Georgia's district courts
controls the filing: the United States District Court for the Northern
District of Georgia, the United States District Court for the Middle
District of Georgia, or the United States District Court for the Southern
District of Georgia, with the governing venue confirmed during the conflict
check and intake. The firm prepares the same disciplined file for either
setting. Scope is defined in writing, the records request follows the
claims, and the methodology is disclosed fully enough that cross-examining
counsel can retrace every step, which is precisely the standard a signed
opinion should invite.

## Cobb County workers, metro-scale data

Vocational conclusions about a Mableton evaluee are grounded in Bureau of
Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Atlanta
metropolitan area and for Georgia statewide, along with state workforce
agency labor market information and, when the referral calls for it, direct
labor market research. Living in Cobb County places an evaluee inside a
sprawling regional employment market, so the report is explicit about how
far that person can reasonably commute, which occupations were considered
and rejected, and what each wage conclusion is anchored to. When functional
restrictions narrow the occupational base, each restriction is tied back to
the medical record before it narrows anything, so the narrowing itself is
defensible. Qualitative scale, named sources, and no unsourced figures:
that is the standard the analysis is held to.

## Serving Mableton without a storefront

The firm has no office in Mableton and claims none. Engagement work runs
remotely by default, with counsel conferences, records analysis, and
pre-testimony preparation handled over video and telephone. Where the
referral questions require an in-person vocational evaluation, the firm
brings the assessment to the evaluee's community rather than summoning an
injured person across the metro. Appearances for deposition, hearing, or
trial are supported through travel from the Kansas City home office, booked
against the litigation calendar, exactly as the firm handles appearances
throughout its [national practice](/locations/nationwide/).

## Bringing a Mableton matter to the firm

Either side of the caption may retain the firm, and both move through an
identical intake: conflict screen, scope, records, then analysis. Counsel
can [submit a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) with the caption or the names
of the parties and a brief description of the claims. Listing any imminent
disclosure, deposition, mediation, or trial dates in that submission lets
scheduling be mapped before the engagement letter is signed, which protects
the deadlines that matter most.
