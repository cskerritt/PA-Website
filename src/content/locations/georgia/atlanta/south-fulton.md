## Fulton County venue, state and federal

South Fulton lies in Fulton County, and its state-court matters proceed in the
Superior Court of Fulton County. On the federal side, Georgia's available
forums are the United States District Court for the Northern District of
Georgia, the United States District Court for the Middle District of Georgia,
and the United States District Court for the Southern District of Georgia;
which district controls a particular case is confirmed during the conflict
check and intake rather than assumed from a map. The forum does not change the
work product. State or federal, every file begins with a documented conflict
screen, a scope agreed in writing, and a records request matched to the
claims, so the resulting opinion rests on a record that either side of the
caption can examine line by line.

## Reading the labor market from a South Fulton address

Employability and earning capacity conclusions for a South Fulton evaluee
rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates
for the Atlanta metropolitan area and for Georgia statewide, supplemented by
state workforce agency labor market information and by direct labor market
research when the referral questions warrant it. A metropolitan area this
large contains many distinct commuting patterns, so each report states
expressly how the evaluee's commutable labor market was defined and why,
rather than treating residence in a major metro as the end of the analysis.
No wage or employment figure enters the report without a named source that
opposing counsel can pull and verify.

## Evaluations where the evaluee lives

Most of the work in a vocational or care-planning engagement is remote by
design. Records review, counsel conferences, and pre-deposition preparation
are handled by video and telephone, which keeps the schedule moving without
waiting on travel. When the referral calls for in-person assessment, the
evaluation is arranged in the evaluee's own community instead of requiring a
trip to a distant office. The firm keeps no staffed office in South Fulton
and publishes no local street address; candor about presence is treated as
part of the credibility of the report itself. When deposition, hearing, or
trial dates require an appearance, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City
home office and planned against the operative litigation calendar.
