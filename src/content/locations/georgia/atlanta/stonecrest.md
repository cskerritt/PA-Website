After a catastrophic injury, the hardest questions in a Stonecrest case are
about the future: what care the injured person will need, for how long, and
what it will cost to provide it where they live. Purinton Analytics answers
those questions for plaintiff and defense counsel through
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) and
[medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/), paired with
[vocational evaluation and expert testimony](/services/vocational-expert-witness/)
when lost earning capacity is also in dispute. Stonecrest engagements run
through the firm's [Atlanta metro practice](/locations/georgia/atlanta/)
and its [Georgia coverage](/locations/georgia/).

## DeKalb County and the federal forums

Stonecrest is a DeKalb County city, and its state-court litigation proceeds
in the Superior Court of DeKalb County. Cases filed in federal court belong
to Georgia's district courts, namely the United States District Court for
the Northern District of Georgia, the United States District Court for the
Middle District of Georgia, and the United States District Court for the
Southern District of Georgia, with the controlling venue for each matter
established during the conflict check and intake. A life care plan headed
for any of these courts is prepared for adversarial reading: every
recommended service, supply, and intervention is tied to the medical record
or to a treating or consulting source, and its cost basis is documented so
the plan can be examined item by item. As the medical picture develops, the
plan is revised on the record, with each revision noting what changed and
why, so the version presented at trial carries its own audit trail.

## Vocational context from the metro labor market

Where earning capacity is contested, findings for a Stonecrest evaluee draw
on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates
for the Atlanta metropolitan area and for Georgia statewide, together with
state workforce agency labor market information and direct labor market
research when the referral requires it. The vocational analysis defines the
evaluee's commutable labor market on the record and keeps qualitative
descriptions of scale separate from sourced observations, so that
employability conclusions and care-planning conclusions can each stand on
their own evidentiary footing.

## Care planning in the evaluee's own setting

Life care planning is most credible when it reflects the setting where care
will actually be delivered. Assessments are arranged in the evaluee's own
community, with records review, family and provider interviews, and counsel
conferences handled by video and telephone wherever that serves the
schedule. The firm operates no office in Stonecrest and publishes no local
address; its presence is by appointment, described accurately, and backed
by travel from the Kansas City home office when depositions, hearings, or
trial require the expert in the room. That is the same service model the
firm applies to its [national engagements](/locations/nationwide/).

## Beginning a Stonecrest referral

Early referral matters most in catastrophic cases, where records are
voluminous and the care plan must mature alongside the medical picture.
[Submit a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) with the caption or party names
and an outline of the injuries and claims, and identify any dates already
set for expert disclosure or trial. Once the conflict screen clears, scope
and records requests follow in writing before analysis begins.
