The threshold question in any vocational opinion about an Alpharetta
evaluee is what labor market that person can actually reach, because the
answer controls everything that follows: which occupations count, which
wages are relevant, and how large the claimed loss really is. Purinton
Analytics treats that boundary-drawing as the analytical core of its
[vocational evaluation and expert testimony](/services/vocational-expert-witness/)
work for Alpharetta counsel, alongside
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) and
[medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/), within the
firm's [Atlanta metro practice](/locations/georgia/atlanta/) and its
[Georgia coverage](/locations/georgia/).

## The courts an Alpharetta matter may see

Alpharetta lies in Fulton County, so state-court litigation is heard in the
Superior Court of Fulton County. Federally, Georgia offers the United States
District Court for the Northern District of Georgia, the United States
District Court for the Middle District of Georgia, and the United States
District Court for the Southern District of Georgia; identifying the
district that controls a specific case is part of the conflict check and
intake, together with any venue-specific qualification requirements. The
reporting standard holds steady across all of these forums: opinions
written to be read by the side that did not commission them, with the
market boundary stated plainly enough that any courtroom can test it on
cross-examination.

## Defining the commutable market before measuring it

Alpharetta belongs to a metropolitan labor market of unusual breadth, and a
report that silently assumes the whole metro is available to every evaluee
will not survive a careful deposition. Each analysis therefore begins by
bounding the evaluee's commutable market on stated grounds, then measures
that market with Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage
estimates for the Atlanta metropolitan area and for Georgia statewide,
supplemented by state workforce agency labor market information and direct
employer research when the referral warrants. Transferable skills analysis
connects the evaluee's work history to the occupations examined, and every
wage observation in the report carries a source the opposing expert can
locate. Documented functional limitations are mapped to the specific
demands of the occupations considered, so any exclusion from the labor
market is explained rather than assumed.

## Working with a local evaluee, remotely and in person

Nearly all engagement stages proceed without travel. Counsel conferences,
records review, and testimony preparation are handled by video and
telephone. When an in-person vocational evaluation is warranted, it is
conducted in the evaluee's own community, arranged around that person's
medical and work circumstances. Purinton Analytics keeps no office in
Alpharetta and publishes no local address, a deliberate practice: the firm
describes its footprint with the same accuracy it demands of a damages
model. Deposition and trial appearances are met by travel from the Kansas
City home office, planned against the court's calendar, consistent with the
firm's [nationwide engagements](/locations/nationwide/).
