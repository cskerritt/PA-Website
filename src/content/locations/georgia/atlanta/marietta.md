## Reviewing and building opinions for Cobb County courts

Marietta sits in Cobb County, and its state-court matters are heard in the
Superior Court of Cobb County. In the federal system, Georgia's trial
courts are the United States District Court for the Northern District of
Georgia, the United States District Court for the Middle District of
Georgia, and the United States District Court for the Southern District of
Georgia; the district with authority over a given case is settled during
the conflict check and intake. A rebuttal engagement holds the opposing
report to the standard any of those courts would apply: are the sources
identified, is the methodology reproducible, do the conclusions follow from
the data cited. Review findings are reported neutrally: where the opposing
opinion is sound, the review says so, because credibility spent on weak
critiques is never recovered. An original opinion from the firm is built to
pass that same audit.

## Testing wage and employability claims against the data

Whether reviewing another expert's numbers or producing original findings,
the reference points are the same: Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational
employment and wage estimates for the Atlanta metropolitan area and for
Georgia statewide, state workforce agency labor market information, and
direct labor market research where the referral questions justify it. For a
Marietta evaluee, the analysis documents how the commutable labor market
was drawn and which occupations were evaluated, because a difference in
those choices is often where competing vocational opinions actually
diverge, and the trier of fact deserves to see the divergence explained
rather than buried.

## Appointment-based logistics for Marietta matters

The firm maintains no Marietta office and no local address, and it says so
without hedging. Day-to-day engagement work, from records review through
deposition preparation, is conducted by video and telephone. In-person
vocational evaluations, when needed, are arranged in the evaluee's own
community. For testimony, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home
office once dates firm up, the identical arrangement the firm uses across
its [national practice](/locations/nationwide/), where cases are staffed
the same way regardless of distance from the home office.
