Every Savannah engagement begins the same way: a documented conflict check, a
defined scope, and a records request tailored to the claims, so that the
written opinion rests on a record both sides can examine. That sequence is
the point of the practice. Purinton Analytics serves plaintiff and defense
counsel in the Savannah metropolitan area with
[vocational evaluation and expert testimony](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure, all within the firm's wider
[Georgia practice](/locations/georgia/).

## Courts serving the Savannah metro

Engagements are accepted for matters in the Superior Court of Chatham
County, the Superior Court of Bryan County, and the Superior Court of
Effingham County. In federal court, the engagement follows the filing among
the United States District Court for the Southern District of Georgia, the
United States District Court for the Middle District of Georgia, and the
United States District Court for the Northern District of Georgia. The venue
changes the calendar, not the method: the same written opinion standard, the
same disclosure discipline, and the same preparation for cross-examination
apply whether the matter sits in a superior court or a federal courtroom.

## What by-appointment service means here

The firm keeps no staffed office in Savannah and publishes no street address
for this location. Meetings are by appointment, and most of the work that
counsel need between filing and trial does not require a local storefront:
records conferences, opinion discussions, and pre-deposition preparation are
held by video or telephone on short scheduling cycles. Vocational
evaluations are conducted in the evaluee's own community when in-person
assessment is warranted, or remotely when the referral questions and testing
protocols permit it. When a deposition, mediation, or trial setting requires
presence, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office and planned
against the court's calendar rather than improvised.

## Grounding opinions in the local labor market

Vocational findings for Savannah-area evaluees rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Savannah
metropolitan area and for Georgia statewide, on state workforce agency labor
market information, and on direct labor market research where a referral
question calls for it. Each report defines the evaluee's commutable labor
market explicitly and explains the basis for that definition, so that
employability and earning capacity conclusions can be probed in deposition
without ambiguity about what market they describe. The same restraint
applies throughout: the report claims only what the data and the evaluation
record support.

## Starting a Savannah engagement

Referrals are accepted from plaintiff and defense counsel on identical
terms, for matters in the metro and throughout the firm's
[national coverage](/locations/nationwide/). Submit the caption or party
names through the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and note any
imminent disclosure, deposition, or trial dates. After the conflict check
clears, counsel receive a records checklist tailored to the claims, and the
report timeline is set against the operative disclosure calendar so that the
opinion arrives when the case needs it.
