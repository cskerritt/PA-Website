## By-appointment presence in the metro

The firm has no staffed office in Brunswick or on St. Simons and publishes
no street address for this location; meetings are by appointment. Stating
that plainly is part of the practice's credibility, since honesty about
presence and honesty in reporting come from the same habit. Engagements
typically open remotely, with records analysis, structured interviews by
video, and standardized testing under publisher protocols where remote
administration is supported. When an in-person evaluation is warranted, or
when deposition or trial attendance is required, travel is scheduled from
the Kansas City home office and planned around counsel's dates. Evaluees
can be seen in their own community, and counsel keep a single point of
contact from intake through testimony.
