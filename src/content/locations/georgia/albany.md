Referrals from the Albany metropolitan area most often present in a familiar
set of shapes: an injury case that turns on
[employability and earning capacity](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
a catastrophic injury matter that requires a
[life care plan](/services/life-care-planning/), an opposing expert report
that needs [rebuttal or peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/), or a
damages model that calls for
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. Purinton Analytics accepts each of these from
plaintiff and defense counsel alike, and applies the same methodology to
both.

## Venue footprint across southwest Georgia

The Albany metropolitan area takes in Dougherty County, Lee County, Terrell
County, and Worth County, and state-court engagements are accepted from the
Superior Court in each. In federal court, the firm serves matters in the
United States District Court for the Middle District of Georgia as well as
the United States District Court for the Northern District of Georgia and
the United States District Court for the Southern District of Georgia.
Because every report is written to be read by opposing counsel, the same
opinion travels intact between state and federal forums, and between
plaintiff and defense retention.

## Appointment-based logistics, stated plainly

The firm maintains no staffed office in Albany and publishes no street
address for this location; meetings occur by appointment. That candor is
deliberate, because accuracy about offices belongs to the same discipline
that governs the firm's opinions. In practice, most Albany engagements
begin remotely with records review, structured interviews by video, and
standardized testing under publisher protocols where remote administration
is supported. Where the referral questions require an in-person evaluation,
or where deposition and trial dates require presence, travel is scheduled
from the Kansas City home office and confirmed with counsel well before the
date. The evaluee can be seen in their own community, which often produces a
more accurate picture of function and access to services than requiring
travel to an expert's office ever would.

## Labor market evidence for Albany evaluees

Vocational opinions for evaluees in the Albany area rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Albany
metropolitan area and for Georgia statewide, together with state workforce
agency labor market information and direct labor market research where the
questions warrant it. Placeability conclusions reflect the occupations
actually hiring in the regional economy rather than national abstractions.
Life care plans and medical cost projections are priced to the evaluee's own
market with provider-specific inquiries and published fee data, and the
firm's broader [Georgia practice](/locations/georgia/) applies the same
research standards throughout the state.

## Requesting a conflict check

Every Albany engagement opens with a documented conflict check.
Submit the caption or party names through the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/), note any imminent disclosure,
deposition, or trial dates, and the firm will respond with availability and
a written scope before any substantive work begins. Counsel handling
matters beyond Georgia will find the same engagement structure described on
the [nationwide coverage](/locations/nationwide/) page, where a single
report standard governs every venue the firm serves.
