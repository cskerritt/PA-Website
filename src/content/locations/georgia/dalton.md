Distance from the firm's home office does not change the method. A Dalton
engagement follows the same sequence as an engagement anywhere else in the
country: a documented conflict check, a defined scope, a records request
tailored to the claims, and a written opinion that either side of the
caption can examine. Within that structure, Purinton Analytics offers
counsel in the Dalton metropolitan area
[vocational expert witness](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) work,
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/),
[medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/), and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Whitfield and Murray county venues

The Dalton metropolitan area comprises Whitfield County and Murray County,
and the firm accepts state-court matters venued in the Superior Court in
either county. Federal engagements are accepted across Georgia, including
matters in the United States District Court for the Northern District of
Georgia, the United States District Court for the Middle District of
Georgia, and the United States District Court for the Southern District of
Georgia. Opinions are prepared to the same disclosure standard in every
forum, so counsel can retain the firm before venue questions are resolved
without risking a report that fits only a single court's expectations.

## How a by-appointment practice serves Dalton

There is no staffed Purinton Analytics office in Dalton, and the firm
publishes no street address for this location. Meetings are by appointment,
and most of the work does not require them: records analysis, structured
interviews conducted by video, and standardized testing under publisher
protocols where remote administration is supported all proceed without
travel. When the referral questions call for an in-person evaluation, or
when a deposition or trial requires attendance, travel is arranged from the
Kansas City home office and coordinated with counsel's calendar. The result
is the same report standard and the same point of contact whether the work
is performed remotely or in the evaluee's own community.

## Grounding opinions in the Dalton labor market

Employability and earning capacity findings for Dalton area evaluees are
grounded in Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage
estimates for the Dalton metropolitan area and for Georgia statewide, along
with state workforce agency labor market information and direct labor
market research where warranted. That local grounding matters on
cross-examination: an opinion tied to the occupations and wages of the
evaluee's actual market is harder to dislodge than an opinion built on
national averages. Life care plans and medical cost projections for area
residents are priced to the regional market using provider-specific
inquiries and published fee data, and the firm's
[Georgia coverage](/locations/georgia/) page explains how metro-level
research fits the statewide practice.

To begin a Dalton area engagement,
[request a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) with the case caption and
parties, and identify any imminent disclosure deadlines, deposition
settings, or trial dates. Once conflicts clear, the firm confirms scope and
fees in writing before work begins. Counsel with related matters in other
states can consult the [nationwide practice](/locations/nationwide/) page,
which describes how the same methodology and reporting discipline apply in
every jurisdiction the firm serves.
