An earning capacity opinion about a Chatsworth evaluee should read as though
the evaluator understands where that person actually lives and works, not as
a template with a town name dropped in. Chatsworth is a small Murray County
community within the Dalton metropolitan area, and Purinton Analytics
accepts engagements arising there from counsel on either side of the
caption. The work offered is the same as everywhere else in the
practice: [vocational expert witness](/services/vocational-expert-witness/)
analysis, [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/),
[medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/), and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Murray County courts and Georgia's federal forums

State-court matters involving Chatsworth parties are typically venued in the
Superior Court for Murray County, and the firm prepares reports and
testimony to that court's disclosure expectations. For federal matters,
Georgia's district courts are all available forums for the firm's work: the
United States District Court for the Northern District of Georgia, the
United States District Court for the Middle District of Georgia, and the
United States District Court for the Southern District of Georgia. The
controlling venue for a particular case, and any venue-specific
qualification or registration requirement that comes with it, is identified
and confirmed during the conflict check and intake process, so counsel can
retain the firm before those questions are settled without commissioning a
report that fits only a single forum.

## Chatsworth's place in the Dalton labor market

A Chatsworth resident's employment prospects are shaped by the wider Dalton
area commuting market, not by the town line. Employability and earning
capacity findings for local evaluees therefore rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Dalton
metropolitan area and for Georgia statewide, supplemented by state workforce
agency labor market information and direct labor market research where the
referral questions warrant it. Describing the market an evaluee can actually
reach, rather than a national average, is what allows an opinion to hold up
under cross-examination. The [Dalton metro page](/locations/georgia/dalton/)
describes the regional research approach in more detail, and life care plans
and medical cost projections for Murray County residents are priced to the
regional provider market rather than to national schedules.

## Evaluating a Chatsworth resident

The firm keeps no office in Chatsworth and publishes no street address for
this location; meetings happen by appointment, and honesty about that is
part of the same discipline that governs the reports. Most engagements
proceed without any travel at all: records analysis, structured interviews
by video, and standardized testing under publisher protocols where remote
administration is supported. When an in-person evaluation serves the evaluee
better, or when a deposition or trial date requires attendance, travel is
scheduled from the Kansas City home office and the evaluation can be
conducted in the evaluee's own community rather than requiring a trip to a
distant office. Counsel receives the same report standard and the same
single point of contact either way.
