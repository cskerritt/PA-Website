For most Columbus matters, the vocational and life care planning work can
begin the week the conflict check clears, because the practice is built
remote-first: records review, opinion conferences, and pre-deposition
preparation proceed by video and telephone, and the evaluee is seen in his
or her own community when in-person assessment is warranted. Purinton
Analytics brings that model to plaintiff and defense counsel throughout the
Columbus metropolitan area, offering
[vocational evaluation and expert testimony](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure, as part of the firm's
[Georgia practice](/locations/georgia/).

## A metro that reaches into Alabama

The Columbus metropolitan area takes in Georgia and Alabama counties alike.
On the Georgia side, engagements are accepted for matters in the Superior
Court of Muscogee County and the superior courts of Harris County,
Chattahoochee County, Marion County, Stewart County, and Talbot County.
Federal engagements follow the filing among the United States District Court
for the Middle District of Georgia, the United States District Court for the
Northern District of Georgia, and the United States District Court for the
Southern District of Georgia. For the Alabama side of the metro, matters
arising in Russell County, Alabama, proceed under the same engagement
structure, and any venue-specific registration or qualification requirement
is identified and confirmed during the conflict check and intake process.

## Appointment-based presence, without pretense

There is no staffed Purinton Analytics office in Columbus, and the firm
publishes no street address for this location. That candor is deliberate:
a practice whose reports are written to withstand cross-examination should
describe its own footprint with the same accuracy. Counsel get scheduled
access instead of a storefront, and when a deposition, mediation, or trial
setting requires an in-person appearance, travel is arranged from the Kansas
City home office against the operative court calendar.

## Labor market research on both sides of the line

Employability and earning capacity findings for Columbus-area evaluees are
grounded in Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage
estimates for the Columbus metropolitan area, supplemented by statewide
estimates and state workforce agency labor market information for Georgia
and for Alabama as the evaluee's circumstances require. Because the metro
crosses a state line, each report is explicit about which market defines the
evaluee's commutable labor market and why, and direct labor market research
is added when a referral question turns on specific occupations or employer
types that the published estimates do not resolve.

## Starting a Columbus engagement

The practice serves plaintiff and defense counsel on the same terms, here
and across its [national coverage](/locations/nationwide/). To open a
matter, submit the caption or party names through the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/), identify the court, and flag any
imminent disclosure, deposition, or trial dates. Once the conflict check
clears, counsel receive a records checklist tailored to the claims and a
report timeline set against the disclosure calendar rather than left
open-ended. Records received at intake are logged against that checklist,
and gaps in the record are raised with counsel early, not discovered at
deposition.
