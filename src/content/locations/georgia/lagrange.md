The LaGrange micropolitan area sits astride a state line: Troup County on
the Georgia side and Chambers County, Alabama across it. A practice built
around remote-first work and by-appointment travel serves that geography
naturally, because the firm's methodology does not change at the border.
Purinton Analytics supports plaintiff and defense counsel throughout the
area with [vocational expert witness](/services/vocational-expert-witness/)
evaluations of employability and earning capacity,
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) in catastrophic injury
matters, and [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Venues on the Georgia side and across the line

Georgia state-court matters from the area are heard in the Superior Court,
and the firm accepts engagements venued in the Superior Court of Troup
County. Federal matters are accepted throughout Georgia, whether in the
United States District Court for the Northern District of Georgia, the
United States District Court for the Middle District of Georgia, or the
United States District Court for the Southern District of Georgia. Counsel
whose cases arise in Chambers County, Alabama are served through the same
national engagement structure the firm applies everywhere, with any
venue-specific registration or qualification requirement identified and
confirmed during the conflict check and intake process.

## Appointment-based service for a cross-state area

There is no staffed Purinton Analytics office in LaGrange, and the firm
publishes no street address for this location. Meetings happen by
appointment, and most of the engagement does not require one: records
review, structured interviews conducted by video, and standardized testing
under publisher protocols where remote administration is supported all
proceed without regard to which side of the state line the evaluee lives
on. When an in-person evaluation is warranted, or when deposition and trial
dates require presence, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home
office. A single trip can cover an evaluation in Troup County and a
conference across the line in Chambers County, Alabama, a practical
advantage of serving the area as a whole rather than as separate
jurisdictions.

## Labor market research that follows the evaluee

Because area residents may work in either state, vocational findings rest
on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates
for the LaGrange area and for Georgia statewide, with Alabama data
consulted when an evaluee's labor market crosses the line. State workforce
agency labor market information and direct labor market research supplement
the published estimates where the referral questions warrant it. Life care
plans and medical cost projections are priced to the evaluee's own market
using provider-specific inquiries and published fee data. The firm's
[Georgia coverage](/locations/georgia/) page describes the statewide
practice this area work belongs to.

Engagements start with a documented conflict check and a written scope.
Submit the caption or party names through the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and identify any imminent disclosure,
deposition, or trial dates. For counsel whose dockets reach beyond Georgia
and Alabama, the [nationwide page](/locations/nationwide/) explains how the
same report standard and the same disclosure discipline apply in every
venue the firm serves.
