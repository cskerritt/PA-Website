## Where a Hogansville case is heard

Hogansville lies in Troup County, whose civil docket runs through the
Superior Court, and every report prepared for that forum is written to
withstand the scrutiny of whichever party did not retain the firm. Federal
practice in the state offers several available forums: the United States
District Court for the Northern District of Georgia, the United States
District Court for the Middle District of Georgia, and the United States
District Court for the Southern District of Georgia. Which of them controls
a given filing is a venue question resolved by counsel; any
registration or qualification requirement particular to that forum is
identified and confirmed during the conflict check and intake process. The
firm's broader coverage of Georgia courts appears on the
[Georgia locations page](/locations/georgia/).

## Small-town evaluees, disciplined market research

A Hogansville claimant should not be measured against a labor market that
exists only in a database. Employability and earning-capacity opinions here
draw on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage
estimates for the surrounding metro area and for Georgia as a whole, on
state workforce agency labor market information, and, where the questions
demand it, on direct contact with employers who actually hire in the region
around LaGrange. The point of that layered approach is simple: a
transferable-skills conclusion about a Hogansville resident should survive
the question "would anyone in this labor market actually hire this person,"
asked under oath. Context on the wider regional market is collected on the
[LaGrange metro page](/locations/georgia/lagrange/).

## The evaluation itself

Meetings in Hogansville are by appointment only; the firm maintains no
staffed location in town and will not pretend otherwise. In practice, that
costs counsel nothing. Records review, structured interviews by video, and
report drafting all proceed remotely on secure channels, which is often the
fastest path when a disclosure date is close. Where the referral calls for
an in-person assessment, the evaluee is seen in their own community, and
travel is arranged from the Kansas City home office to fit deposition and
trial calendars. When the damages theory requires an economic loss
calculation, independent economist partners coordinated by the firm perform
and sign that analysis through
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/),
keeping vocational, care-planning, and economic workstreams aligned without
blurring authorship.

parties through the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/), state the venue
if known, and identify the controlling dates: disclosure, discovery close,
deposition, trial. A documented conflict determination follows, then a
defined scope and a tailored records request. Hogansville referrals receive
the same process the firm applies to matters across the country, described
