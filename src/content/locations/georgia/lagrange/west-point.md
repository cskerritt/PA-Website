## Troup County venue and the federal forums

West Point sits in Troup County, and civil actions filed locally proceed in
the Superior Court. Written opinions, deposition testimony, and trial
testimony are prepared on the assumption that opposing counsel will test
every source, so the file is organized for cross-examination before it is
ever produced. When a matter is filed federally, Georgia counsel may be
litigating in the United States District Court for the Northern District of
Georgia, the United States District Court for the Middle District of
Georgia, or the United States District Court for the Southern District of
Georgia; the district that controls a particular case, together with any
venue-specific qualification requirement, is identified during the conflict
check and confirmed at intake. Statewide court coverage is described on the
[Georgia locations page](/locations/georgia/).

## A labor market on both sides of the line

Vocational findings for a West Point evaluee rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the LaGrange area
and for Georgia statewide, supplemented by state workforce agency labor
market information and direct employer research where the referral questions
warrant it. Because the metro crosses into Alabama, transferable-skills
analysis and placeability opinions are assessed against the commuting
market a West Point resident actually faces, taking in nearby communities
such as Valley, Lanett, and LaGrange itself, rather than stopping at a
boundary on a map. Where lost earnings must be reduced to a damages figure,
the firm offers
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)

analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. The regional practice is described more fully on the
[LaGrange metro page](/locations/georgia/lagrange/).

## Evaluations without a local storefront

The firm keeps no office in West Point and publishes no local address,
because candor about presence belongs to the same discipline that governs
its reports. Most engagements begin remotely: records are transmitted
securely, interviews proceed by video, and counsel receives the written
opinion without anyone boarding a plane. When an in-person evaluation is
appropriate, it is conducted in the evaluee's own community, where daily
routines, driving tolerance, and access to work can be observed rather than
assumed. Travel for evaluations, depositions, and trial appearances is
scheduled from the Kansas City home office, and scheduling is discussed
candidly at intake so the case calendar drives the logistics rather than
the reverse.
