## Clinton County venue and the courts that hear DeWitt cases

DeWitt sits in Clinton County, and state-court matters arising there are
heard in the District Court. On the federal side, Iowa offers two forums:
the United States District Court for the Northern District of Iowa and the
United States District Court for the Southern District of Iowa. The firm
does not assume which of those districts controls a particular DeWitt
filing; the governing venue, along with any registration or qualification
requirement it imposes on a testifying expert, is identified and confirmed
during the conflict check and intake process. Whatever the forum, the
written product is the same: a documented conflict check, a defined scope,
a records request tailored to the claims, and an opinion whose reasoning
can be examined line by line. Engagements across the rest of the state are
described on the [Iowa locations page](/locations/iowa/).

## Reading DeWitt's place in the regional labor market

For vocational purposes, DeWitt is a small community whose workers
participate in a wider regional economy centered on Clinton, the metro's
principal city. An earning capacity analysis that treated the town as a
closed market would misstate what a DeWitt evaluee realistically faces, so
the firm grounds its findings in Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational
employment and wage estimates for the area and for Iowa statewide,
supplemented by state workforce agency labor market information and direct
labor market research where the referral questions warrant it. Every
report identifies which data source supports which conclusion, so
cross-examination meets documentation rather than assertion. When the
damages questions extend beyond vocational and care-planning issues, the
firm offers
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. The broader metro practice is described on the
[Clinton metro page](/locations/iowa/clinton/).

## How an evaluation proceeds for a DeWitt evaluee

Purinton Analytics keeps no staffed office in DeWitt and publishes no
local street address, because accuracy about presence is part of the same
discipline that governs its reports. Most stages of an engagement proceed
remotely: records review, counsel conferences, and many evaluee interviews
are handled through secure video and document exchange, which keeps
smaller-venue cases on the same footing as big-city ones. When the
referral questions call for an in-person evaluation, the firm schedules it
in the evaluee's own community at an appropriate professional setting, and
when deposition or trial testimony requires presence, travel is arranged
from the Kansas City home office. Counsel receive the same national
standard of practice described on the
[nationwide page](/locations/nationwide/), delivered by appointment rather
than through a storefront.
