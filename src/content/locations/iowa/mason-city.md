Counsel litigating injury, employment, and disability matters in the Mason
City area retain Purinton Analytics for
[earning capacity and employability evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) in catastrophic injury
cases, and [medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/)
where the dispute turns on future care needs rather than a full plan. When a
matter also requires economic damages, the firm offers
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. Plaintiff and defense referrals are accepted on the
same terms, as part of the firm's [statewide Iowa practice](/locations/iowa/).

## Venue footprint: Cerro Gordo and Worth counties

The Mason City, IA Micropolitan Statistical Area consists of Cerro Gordo
County and Worth County, and the firm accepts engagements for matters venued
in the District Court in either county. Federal matters are supported as
well, whether filed in the United States District Court for the Northern
District of Iowa or the United States District Court for the Southern
District of Iowa. The engagement process does not change with the
courthouse: a documented conflict check, a defined scope, and a records
request tailored to the claims come before any opinion work, so that the
written report rests on a record both sides can examine. Where an opposing
expert has already issued a report, the firm also accepts
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) assignments on
the same disciplined footing.

## By-appointment presence, without a storefront

There is no staffed Purinton Analytics office in Mason City, and the firm
publishes no street address for this location, because accuracy about
presence is part of the same discipline that governs its reports. Meetings
with counsel and evaluees are scheduled by appointment. Most engagements
begin remotely, with records review, structured interviews, and standardized
testing under publisher protocols where remote administration is supported.
When the referral questions call for in-person evaluation, or when
deposition and trial dates require attendance, travel is scheduled from the
Kansas City home office, the same base from which the firm serves counsel
[nationwide](/locations/nationwide/).

## Labor market research for Mason City evaluees

Vocational findings for evaluees in Cerro Gordo and Worth counties rest on
Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates
covering the Mason City area and Iowa statewide, on state workforce agency
labor market information, and on direct labor market research where the
referral questions warrant it. Employability and placeability conclusions
are grounded in the evaluee's own commutable labor market rather than in
national generalities, and life care plans and cost projections for local
residents are priced to the sources actually available to them. Where a
transferable skills analysis is disputed, the underlying occupational data
and methodology are disclosed so that either side of the caption can test
the work in deposition.

## Starting a Mason City engagement

New matters begin with conflicts, not with conclusions. Submit the caption
or party names through the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and note
any imminent disclosure, deposition, or trial dates. Once conflicts clear,
the firm confirms scope and fee terms in writing, issues a records request
tailored to the claims, and sets the evaluation and reporting schedule
around the court's deadlines. Counsel receive the same report standard and
the same point of contact whether the matter sits in Mason City or anywhere
else the practice serves.
