## Defining the labor market for a border-metro evaluee

Sergeant Bluff belongs to the Sioux City, IA-NE-SD metropolitan area, a
designation that names Iowa, Nebraska, and South Dakota in a single
title. For vocational analysis, that title is a caution rather than a
convenience: the market a Sergeant Bluff evaluee actually faces cannot
be assumed from a town line or a state line, and must instead be
