## What by-appointment service means here

The firm maintains no staffed office in Fort Dodge and publishes no street
address for this location. That is stated plainly because honesty about
presence is part of the same discipline that governs the firm's opinions.
In practice, most of the work does not depend on geography at all: records
review, structured interviews, and standardized testing under publisher
protocols proceed remotely where remote administration is supported. When
the referral questions require in-person evaluation, or when deposition and
trial dates require attendance, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City
home office, exactly as it is for the firm's engagements
[nationwide](/locations/nationwide/).

## Webster County labor market research

An earning capacity opinion for a Fort Dodge evaluee has to reflect the
labor market that evaluee can actually reach. Vocational findings rest on
Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates
covering the Fort Dodge area and Iowa statewide, on state workforce agency
labor market information, and on direct labor market research where the
referral questions warrant it. Scale is described qualitatively and sourced
transparently, so that conclusions about occupational demand in a smaller
regional market can be tested rather than taken on faith. Life care plans
and [medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/) for
Webster County residents are priced to providers and published fee data
relevant to the evaluee's own market.
