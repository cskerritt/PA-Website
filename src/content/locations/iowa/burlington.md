The Burlington, IA-IL Micropolitan Statistical Area sits astride a state
line: Des Moines County, Iowa on one side and Henderson County, Illinois on
the other. Purinton Analytics accepts vocational expert and life care
planning engagements from plaintiff and defense counsel on both sides of
that line, applying the same
[earning capacity and employability methodology](/services/vocational-expert-witness/)
and the same [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) standards
regardless of which state's law governs the claim. For counsel who need the
full damages picture, the firm offers
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Venues on the Iowa and Illinois sides

On the Iowa side, the firm accepts matters venued in the District Court for
Des Moines County, and federal matters in the United States District Court
for the Northern District of Iowa and the United States District Court for
the Southern District of Iowa. Matters arising in Henderson County,
Illinois are accepted under the same engagement terms, and any
venue-specific registration or qualification requirement is identified and
confirmed during the conflict check and intake process, before opinion work
begins. Cross-border retention is routine for the practice, which handles
Burlington matters as part of its [Iowa coverage](/locations/iowa/) and its
broader [national practice](/locations/nationwide/).

## How the firm appears in Burlington

Meetings in the Burlington area are by appointment. The firm maintains no
staffed office and publishes no street address for this location, because
being precise about presence is part of the same discipline that governs
its written opinions. Engagements generally open remotely: records review,
structured interviews with the evaluee, and standardized testing under
publisher protocols where remote administration is supported. When the
referral questions require an in-person evaluation, or when deposition and
trial calendars require the expert in the room, travel is scheduled from
the Kansas City home office and planned around the court's deadlines rather
than the firm's convenience.

## Labor market research across the metro

A Burlington area evaluee's employability cannot be assessed against a
single state's data when the commutable labor market crosses the state
line. Vocational
findings therefore draw on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational
employment and wage estimates covering the Burlington area, together with
statewide estimates and state workforce agency labor market information for
the relevant jurisdictions, and on direct labor market research where the
referral questions warrant it. Care-related opinions follow the same logic:
[medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/) and life
care plans are priced to the providers and published fee data actually
available to the evaluee, not to national averages. Every source is cited
in the report so that either side of the caption can verify it.

## Opening a Burlington engagement

Whether the caption reads Des Moines County or Henderson County, the path
in is the same: submit the parties through the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and flag any imminent disclosure,
deposition, or trial dates. Once conflicts clear, the firm confirms scope
and fee terms in writing, issues a records request tailored to the claims,
and commits to a reporting schedule that respects the deadlines already on
the docket. Counsel receive a single point of contact and a report written
to withstand examination from the other side of the state line as well as
the other side of the caption.
