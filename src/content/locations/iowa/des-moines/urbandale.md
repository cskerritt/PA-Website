Start with the evaluee. Somewhere in Urbandale is a person whose working
life a court will soon have to value, and the quality of that valuation
depends on how carefully the evaluation itself is run: what records are
gathered, what testing is administered, and whether the interview happens
in circumstances the evaluee can manage. Purinton Analytics builds its
Urbandale engagements around that reality, offering
[vocational evaluation and expert testimony](/services/vocational-expert-witness/)
and [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) to counsel on
either side of the caption, in Urbandale as across the firm's
[national footprint](/locations/nationwide/).

## Evaluation logistics for an Urbandale evaluee

The practice has no staffed office in Urbandale and lists no local street
address; candor about presence is part of the product. Engagements run by
appointment. Intake conferences, status calls, and pre-deposition
preparation take place remotely, records are exchanged through secure
channels, and where the referral questions and testing protocols permit,
the evaluation itself can be completed remotely as well. When the
assignment requires a face-to-face assessment, the appointment is set in
the evaluee's own community rather than forcing travel on an injured
person, and expert appearances for deposition or trial are scheduled from
the Kansas City home office against the litigation calendar.

## Where the resulting opinion will be tested

Urbandale is a Polk County community, and state-law matters are heard in
the Iowa District Court. Federal matters may proceed in the United States
District Court for the Northern District of Iowa or the United States
District Court for the Southern District of Iowa, the state's two federal
forums; the venue that actually controls, and any registration or
qualification requirement that comes with it, is pinned down during the
conflict check and intake. Report format and disclosure practice follow
the forum, so the firm confirms both before the scope is finalized.

## The market behind the numbers counsel will argue

An Urbandale evaluee's employment prospects extend across the
[Des Moines and West Des Moines metro area](/locations/iowa/des-moines/),
and the vocational analysis treats that commutable market as the unit of
study. Findings are grounded in Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational
employment and wage estimates for the metropolitan area and for
[Iowa](/locations/iowa/) as a whole, together with state workforce agency
labor market information and, where warranted, direct contact with the
labor market itself. Each report identifies its data sources and walks
through the reasoning from evidence to opinion, because an earning
capacity conclusion that cannot show its work should not survive
cross-examination, and the firm writes as though the examiner is already
reading.

## Care costs and the rest of the damages picture

Where future medical and support needs are in dispute, a
[medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/) or full life
care plan is prepared under the same sourcing discipline. When the damages
model requires economic analysis, the firm arranges
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.
