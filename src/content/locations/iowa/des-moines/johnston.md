Expert opinions are due when the scheduling order says they are due, and a
late one is worth little regardless of its quality. That is why Purinton
Analytics treats the disclosure calendar as the spine of every Johnston
engagement: the conflict check, the records request, the evaluation, and
the written report are each placed on a timeline that ends comfortably
before the deadline, not on it. Johnston counsel, whether representing the
injured party or the defense, can retain the firm for
[vocational expert evaluation and testimony](/services/vocational-expert-witness/)
and [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) with that
calendar-first discipline, applied here as it is
[nationally](/locations/nationwide/).

## The courts a Johnston matter answers to

Johnston is in Polk County, so its state-court litigation is heard in the
Iowa District Court. Federal claims have two possible Iowa homes, the
United States District Court for the Northern District of Iowa and the
United States District Court for the Southern District of Iowa, and the
firm does not guess which governs: controlling venue, and with it any
registration or qualification requirement for the testifying expert, is
determined during the conflict check and intake. State and federal
practice impose different disclosure obligations, and the report is
drafted to the standard of whichever forum holds the case.

## Vocational evidence for a metro commuter base

Johnston sits within the
[Des Moines and West Des Moines metro](/locations/iowa/des-moines/), and
its residents' employment prospects are properly measured at that
regional scale. The firm's earning capacity work draws on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the
metropolitan area and for the state of [Iowa](/locations/iowa/), on state
workforce agency labor market information, and on direct labor market
inquiry when a specific placement question needs first-hand evidence. No
figure enters the report without a named source; no conclusion is stated
without the reasoning that supports it. The purpose is an opinion that
withstands the deposition it will certainly face.

## Meeting the firm, and where the evaluation happens

Every Johnston engagement is by appointment, since the firm has no
staffed office there and publishes no local address, a fact stated
outright because overstating presence would undercut reports built on
accuracy. Secure records exchange and remote conferencing carry the
routine work of intake, status, and pre-deposition preparation. Remote
evaluation is available where the referral questions and testing
protocols support it; otherwise the evaluation is held in the evaluee's
own community. When testimony requires the expert in person, travel is
planned from the Kansas City home office as soon as the date is fixed.
