## Dallas County and the available forums

State-court matters for Waukee parties proceed in the Iowa District Court
serving Dallas County. For federal litigation, Iowa's forums are the
United States District Court for the Northern District of Iowa and the
United States District Court for the Southern District of Iowa; the
district that controls a specific Waukee matter is established during the
conflict check and intake, never presumed, and any venue-specific
registration or expert qualification requirement is confirmed at that
stage. Once the forum is fixed, the report schedule is built backward from
the operative scheduling order so that disclosure deadlines arrive with
margin rather than panic.
