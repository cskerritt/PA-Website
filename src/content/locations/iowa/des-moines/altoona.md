## Venue context: Polk County and beyond

Altoona's county is Polk County, and its state-court cases are heard in
the Iowa District Court. Where a matter belongs in federal court, Iowa
presents two districts, the United States District Court for the Northern
District of Iowa and the United States District Court for the Southern
District of Iowa; identifying which one governs, and whether that forum
imposes any expert registration or qualification requirement, is part of
the conflict check and intake rather than an afterthought. The forum's
disclosure rules then set the report's format and the pace of the
engagement.

## What scrutiny-proof labor market evidence looks like

For an Altoona evaluee, the relevant employment market is the surrounding
[Des Moines and West Des Moines metropolitan area](/locations/iowa/des-moines/),
and the analysis says so explicitly rather than leaving the market
definition to inference. The evidentiary base is disclosed in full: Bureau
of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the
metro area and for [Iowa](/locations/iowa/) statewide, state workforce
agency labor market information, and, when the referral requires it,
direct research into actual hiring conditions. Transferability of skills,
access to retraining, and the feasibility of a return to work are each
addressed with the underlying data identified, because an opinion whose
foundations are hidden invites the very attack it should be built to
survive.
