Ask who retains Purinton Analytics for Clive matters and the answer is
deliberately unhelpful: both sides. The firm's vocational and
care-planning opinions are produced the same way whether the retaining
party sits at plaintiff's table or defense table, and that indifference
to the caption is the point. Clive attorneys engage the practice for
[vocational expert evaluation and testimony](/services/vocational-expert-witness/)
and [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) precisely because
the analysis does not bend toward whoever is paying for it, in Clive as
in the firm's [work nationwide](/locations/nationwide/).

## Clive's place in the court system

Polk County is Clive's county, and the Iowa District Court hears its
state-law disputes. Federal matters find their forum in one of Iowa's two
districts, the United States District Court for the Northern District of
Iowa or the United States District Court for the Southern District of
Iowa; the firm confirms controlling venue during the conflict check and
intake, along with any registration or qualification requirement that
forum attaches to a testifying expert. Because the governing court
dictates disclosure form and deposition practice, no scope is finalized
until the venue question is settled.

## Measuring what a Clive evaluee can earn

A Clive resident's job market is the
[Des Moines and West Des Moines metro](/locations/iowa/des-moines/)
taken as a whole, and the firm's earning capacity analysis is framed at
that commutable scale. Its foundations are the Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates for the metropolitan area and
for [Iowa](/locations/iowa/) statewide, state workforce agency labor
market information, and direct labor market research where the referral
questions warrant it. The finished report defines the market it studied,
lists every source relied upon, and connects evidence to conclusion in
prose either party can follow, which is what makes the opinion usable by
whichever side happens to have commissioned it.

## The by-appointment model

Purinton Analytics keeps no office in Clive and offers no local street
address for this page; the firm would rather describe its presence
accurately than impressively. Clive engagements proceed by appointment:
records through secure exchange, intake and pre-deposition sessions by
remote conference, and evaluation conducted remotely where protocols
permit or in the evaluee's own community where they do not. Court
appearances and depositions requiring physical presence are handled as
scheduled travel from the Kansas City home office.
