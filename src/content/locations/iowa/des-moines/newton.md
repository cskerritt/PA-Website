Future care is often the largest and least examined element of a Newton
damages case: what treatment, equipment, therapy, and support an injured
person will need over a lifetime, and what it is reasonable to expect
that need to cost. Purinton Analytics approaches Newton engagements from
that care-planning question, pairing
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) with
[vocational expert evaluation and testimony](/services/vocational-expert-witness/)
so that lost earning capacity and future care are analyzed as parts of
the same injury, for plaintiff or defense counsel, in Newton as in the
firm's [nationwide work](/locations/nationwide/).

## Jasper County venue, state and federal

Newton lies in Jasper County, whose state-court matters proceed in the
Iowa District Court. A federal case will belong to one of Iowa's two
districts, the United States District Court for the Northern District of
Iowa or the United States District Court for the Southern District of
Iowa; rather than assume, the firm identifies the controlling venue
during the conflict check and intake, and confirms at the same time any
registration or qualification requirement the forum places on a
testifying expert. Disclosure obligations and report format then follow
the governing court, and the engagement calendar is drawn from its
scheduling order.

## Care needs priced honestly, earning capacity measured regionally

A Newton life care plan is built item by item from the medical record
and treating-provider input, with every projected need traced to its
clinical basis; where a full plan is more than the case requires, a
[medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/) addresses
the disputed categories alone. On the vocational side, a Newton
evaluee's employment prospects reach beyond Jasper County into the
[Des Moines and West Des Moines metropolitan area](/locations/iowa/des-moines/),
and the analysis is grounded in Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational
employment and wage estimates for that metro area and for
[Iowa](/locations/iowa/) statewide, along with state workforce agency
labor market information and direct market research where warranted.
Both opinions disclose their sources fully, because both will be read by
lawyers paid to find the gaps.

## Serving Newton by appointment

The firm's only home office is in Kansas City. It maintains no Newton
office and publishes no street address here, and that candor is
deliberate: a practice built on accurate reports should be accurate
about itself. Newton counsel work with the firm through secure records
exchange and remote conferences for intake, status, and pre-deposition
preparation. Evaluations are conducted remotely where the referral
questions and testing protocols permit, and otherwise in the community
where the evaluee lives. When a deposition or trial requires the expert
present, travel from the Kansas City home office is scheduled against
the case calendar.

## The economic layer and opposing experts

When counsel need care costs and earnings losses reduced to economic
terms, the firm offers

analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. Opposing life care plans and vocational reports

under the same standards.
