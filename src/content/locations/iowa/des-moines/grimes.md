Defining the labor market is the quiet decision that drives a vocational
opinion, and for a small suburb like Grimes it matters more than
anywhere: draw the boundary too tightly and the evaluee looks
unemployable, draw it too loosely and every lost job looks replaceable.
Purinton Analytics makes that boundary decision explicitly and defends
it in writing. Grimes counsel, plaintiff and defense alike, retain the
firm for
[vocational expert witness work](/services/vocational-expert-witness/)
and [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) built on that
kind of disclosed judgment, in Grimes as throughout the
[national practice](/locations/nationwide/).

## The commutable market around Grimes

A Grimes evaluee's realistic employment options lie across the
[Des Moines and West Des Moines metropolitan area](/locations/iowa/des-moines/),
and the firm's analysis studies that market rather than the town alone.
The sources are qualitative and named: Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates for the metro area and for
[Iowa](/locations/iowa/) statewide, state workforce agency labor market
information, and direct labor market research when a referral question
turns on present hiring conditions. The report states where the market
boundary was drawn and why, which occupations were considered and on
what evidence, and how the evaluee's restrictions interact with the work
that actually exists there.

## Polk County courts and the federal alternatives

Grimes belongs to Polk County, and its state-court matters proceed in
the Iowa District Court. Federal litigation is venued in one of the
state's two districts, the United States District Court for the Northern
District of Iowa or the United States District Court for the Southern
District of Iowa; which district controls a given Grimes case is
resolved during the conflict check and intake, together with any
venue-specific registration or qualification requirement. The engagement
then follows the governing forum's disclosure rules from records request
to final report.

## Working with a firm that is not down the street

The honest version: Purinton Analytics has no Grimes office and no local
address to publish, and says so rather than implying otherwise. What
Grimes counsel receive is a by-appointment practice that functions at
distance by design. Records travel by secure exchange; intake, status,
and deposition preparation happen over remote conference; evaluations
are performed remotely where testing protocols permit, and otherwise in
the community where the evaluee lives. When the case calendar demands
the expert in a deposition suite or courtroom, travel is arranged from
the Kansas City home office with the schedule set in advance.
