The Davenport-Moline-Rock Island metropolitan area sits astride a state line,
and litigation here reflects that: a single incident can generate filings on
the Iowa side, the Illinois side, or both. Purinton Analytics supports
plaintiff and defense counsel throughout this bi-state region with
[vocational expert evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. Because the metro spans jurisdictions, the conflict
check and intake process confirms the forum before any work begins, and any
venue-specific registration or qualification requirement is identified and
confirmed during that same process.

## Courts on both sides of the state line

On the Iowa side, the firm accepts matters venued in the District Court in
Scott County, Iowa. On the Illinois side, engagements are accepted for
matters arising in Rock Island County, Henry County, and Mercer County,
Illinois. Federal matters on the Iowa side are accepted in the United States
District Court for the Northern District of Iowa and the United States
District Court for the Southern District of Iowa alike, with the district
for a particular filing confirmed during the same intake that resolves the
forum question. The firm's broader Iowa
practice, including its other metro pages, is described on the
[Iowa locations page](/locations/iowa/).

## By-appointment service, honestly described

There is no staffed Purinton Analytics office in Davenport, Moline, or Rock
Island, and the firm publishes no street address for this location. Meetings
are by appointment because that is how the work is actually delivered, and
saying so plainly is part of the discipline counsel are hiring. Most
engagements proceed remotely from the outset: records review, structured
interviews, and standardized testing under publisher protocols where remote
administration is supported. When the referral calls for an in-person
evaluation, or when a deposition or trial date requires presence in the
region, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office with enough lead
time to protect counsel's calendar.

## Labor market data for a bi-state market

Evaluees in this region often live in one state and work in the other, so
vocational findings rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational
employment and wage estimates for the Davenport-Moline-Rock Island
metropolitan area as a whole, alongside statewide estimates for Iowa and
Illinois and the labor market information published by each state's
workforce agency. Direct labor market research is added where the referral
questions warrant it. Life care plans and
[medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/) are priced to
the evaluee's own community, on whichever side of the state line that
community sits, using provider-specific inquiries and published fee data.

## Starting an engagement in the region

Whether the caption will be filed in state court on the Iowa side, in state
court on the Illinois side, or in federal court, the starting point is the
same. Submit the
caption or party names through the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and
note the forum, the current schedule, and any imminent disclosure or
deposition dates. Counsel comparing venues, or coordinating related filings
in both states, can review the firm's
[national coverage](/locations/nationwide/) for a description of how one
report standard and one methodology travel across jurisdictional lines.
