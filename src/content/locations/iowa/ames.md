The measure of a national expert practice is that its standards do not vary
with the venue. Purinton Analytics serves Ames area counsel through the same
remote-first practice it applies everywhere else, which means an Ames
referral receives the identical intake, methodology, and report discipline
that a matter in any other market would receive. Plaintiff and defense
counsel in the area retain the firm for
[earning capacity and employability evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Venue coverage for the Ames area

The Ames metropolitan area consists of Story County and Boone County, and
the firm accepts matters venued in the District Court serving each. Federal
engagements are accepted in the United States District Court for the
Southern District of Iowa and the United States District Court for the
Northern District of Iowa alike, and the district in which a particular
matter belongs is confirmed with counsel as part of intake. Counsel whose cases sit outside these counties, or
who handle matters across several Iowa venues, can consult the
[Iowa locations page](/locations/iowa/) for the statewide practice
description; nothing about the firm's approach changes from venue to venue.

## Remote-first, by appointment when needed

The firm has no staffed office in Ames and publishes no street address for
this location. That candor is deliberate: a practice that documents every
opinion should describe its own presence just as accurately. Ames
engagements typically proceed remotely from start to finish, with records
review, structured interviews, and standardized testing administered under
publisher protocols where remote administration is supported. When an
in-person evaluation better serves the referral questions, or when a
deposition or trial in Story County or Boone County requires attendance,
travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office, and the schedule is
built around counsel's deadlines rather than the reverse.

## Labor market evidence for Ames evaluees

Vocational findings for Ames area evaluees rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Ames
metropolitan area and for Iowa statewide, together with state workforce
agency labor market information and direct labor market research where the
referral warrants it. Because a metro's occupational mix can differ from
statewide patterns, opinions are anchored to the evaluee's
own commuting labor market and each conclusion is cited to its source so it
can withstand cross-examination. Life care plans and
[medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/) for area
residents are priced through provider-specific inquiries and published fee
data for the evaluee's own market rather than through national averages.

Every engagement, local or national, opens with clearance. Submit the
caption or party names through the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and
note any imminent disclosure, deposition, or trial dates so that
availability is confirmed at the same time as the conflict review. Counsel
who want to see how the practice operates beyond Iowa can review its
[national coverage](/locations/nationwide/), which describes the single
report standard and testimony discipline that an Ames file shares with every
other matter the firm accepts.
