What is the relevant labor market for someone who lives in Robins? That
methodological question sits at the center of any earning capacity
dispute involving a resident of a small community, and how an expert
answers it often decides whether the opinion survives scrutiny. Define
the market as the town itself and capacity looks artificially bleak;
define it as the whole state and capacity looks artificially rosy.
Purinton Analytics defines it the way vocational science requires, by
the work actually reachable from where the evaluee lives, and offers
that discipline to plaintiff and defense counsel through
[vocational expert services](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/).

## Data sources for a Robins opinion

Robins lies within the Cedar Rapids metropolitan economy, so the firm's
analysis draws on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and
wage estimates for the Cedar Rapids metropolitan area and for Iowa
statewide, refined with state workforce agency labor market information
and, where the referral questions warrant, direct contact with employers
in the commuting region. The written opinion discloses which geography
and which source stand behind each finding, so that the definition of
the market is an argument opposing counsel can test rather than a
premise they must take on faith. The metro-level practice is described
on the [Cedar Rapids metro page](/locations/iowa/cedar-rapids/).

## The courts that hear a Robins case

Robins is a Linn County community, and state-court litigation proceeds
in the District Court for Linn County. Federal matters find their forum
in the United States District Court for the Northern District of Iowa or
the United States District Court for the Southern District of Iowa; both
are available across the state, and the controlling district for a given
case, with any expert registration or qualification requirement it
imposes, is identified during the conflict check and intake. The firm's
statewide engagements are collected on the
[Iowa locations page](/locations/iowa/).

## No local office, no lost capability

The firm will not manufacture a Robins presence it does not have: there
is no staffed office here and no published address, and meetings are by
appointment. The engagement is unaffected. Records analysis, structured
interviews, and most standardized testing are handled remotely under
publisher protocols, and where the questions require an in-person
evaluation it is conducted in the evaluee's own community. Travel for
evaluations, depositions, and trial testimony runs from the Kansas City
home office, as it does for every venue in the firm's
[national practice](/locations/nationwide/).

## Engaging the firm on a Robins matter

Counsel begins by
[submitting the caption or party names for a conflict check](/refer-a-case/),
noting any disclosure deadlines or trial settings already in place. Once
the check clears, the firm confirms scope and fee terms in writing and
issues a records request tailored to the claims at issue. If the case
also requires an economic damages opinion, the firm arranges
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure, so the definition of the labor market and the
economic model built on it stay consistent from report to testimony.
