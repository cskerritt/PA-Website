around that reality, pairing
[expert testimony and litigation consulting](/services/expert-testimony-litigation-consulting/)

## How by-appointment service works here

There is no staffed Purinton Analytics office in Iowa City and no published
street address for this location; stating that plainly is part of the same
accuracy that governs the firm's reports. Engagements typically begin
remotely with records review, structured interviews, and standardized
testing under publisher protocols where remote administration is supported.
When the referral questions call for an in-person evaluation, or when a
Johnson County deposition or trial setting requires the expert's presence,
travel is arranged from the Kansas City home office and coordinated with
counsel's schedule. Testimony preparation, exhibit review, and pre-hearing
conferences are handled by secure video unless counsel prefer otherwise.

## Labor market evidence for area evaluees

Vocational findings for Iowa City evaluees rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Iowa City
metropolitan area and for Iowa statewide, alongside state workforce agency
labor market information and direct labor market research where warranted.
Opinions on employability and earning capacity are anchored to the evaluee's
own commuting labor market and documented so that each conclusion can be
traced to its source under cross-examination. Life care plans and
[medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/) for area
residents are priced to the local market through provider-specific inquiries
and published fee data rather than through borrowed national figures.
