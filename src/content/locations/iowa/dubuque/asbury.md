Asbury alone. The town is a small suburb inside the Dubuque metropolitan
area, and the realistic labor market for an Asbury evaluee is the metro
market, not the boundaries of the municipality. Purinton Analytics builds
its opinions around that distinction from the first records request. The

[vocational expert services](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) of opposing

## Dubuque County venue for an Asbury matter

Asbury sits in Dubuque County, and state-court matters arising in the
town proceed in the District Court for Dubuque County. In federal
practice, Iowa offers the United States District Court for the Northern
District of Iowa and the United States District Court for the Southern
District of Iowa as available forums; the district that actually
controls a given Asbury case, along with any registration or
qualification requirement it imposes on a testifying expert, is
identified during the conflict check and intake rather than assumed from
a map. Reports are drafted to the same disclosure standard in either
system, so a change of forum changes the caption block, not the rigor.
The firm's statewide practice is described on the
[Iowa page](/locations/iowa/).

## A suburban evaluee, a metro labor market

Because Asbury is a residential community within the Dubuque metro, the
occupations, employers, and wages that matter to an Asbury earning
capacity analysis are distributed across the metropolitan area rather
than concentrated in the town. Vocational findings therefore rest on
Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates
for the Dubuque metropolitan area and for Iowa statewide, supplemented
by state workforce agency labor market information and direct labor
market research where the referral questions warrant it. Each finding in
the report identifies the geography that supports it, so opposing
counsel can trace every conclusion to a named source. The practice
across the metro, including work centered on the principal city of
Dubuque, is described on the
[Dubuque metro page](/locations/iowa/dubuque/).
