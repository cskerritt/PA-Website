Both sides of the caption bring Clinton County matters to Purinton Analytics
for the same reason: the reports are written to be read by opposing counsel.
Plaintiff firms retain the practice for
[earning capacity and employability evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/)
and [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/); defense firms
retain it for the same services and for
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) of opinions
already disclosed. Neither side receives a different methodology, because
the analysis is built to hold up regardless of who commissioned it. Clinton
engagements run through the firm's
[statewide Iowa practice](/locations/iowa/), which applies the identical
process in every county.

## Where Clinton matters are venued

The Clinton, IA Micropolitan Statistical Area comprises Clinton County, and
the firm accepts state court engagements venued in the District Court there.
Matters in federal court are supported in the United States District Court
for the Northern District of Iowa and the United States District Court for
the Southern District of Iowa alike. When economic damages must be
quantified alongside the vocational and care-planning questions, the firm
offers [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure, so counsel manage a single retention rather than
several.

## Appointment-based logistics, stated plainly

Purinton Analytics keeps no staffed office in Clinton and publishes no
street address for this location. Candor about presence is part of the
firm's credibility: what counsel get instead of a storefront is a
remote-first process that begins with records review, structured
interviews, and standardized testing under publisher protocols where remote
administration is supported. In-person evaluation, deposition attendance,
and trial testimony are scheduled by appointment, with travel arranged from
the Kansas City home office. That is the same model the firm uses for
matters [across the country](/locations/nationwide/), and it means a Clinton
case is staffed no differently than a case in a major metro.

## Clinton County labor market analysis

Opinions about what work a Clinton County evaluee can perform, and what it
pays, rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage
estimates covering the Clinton area and Iowa statewide, supplemented by
state workforce agency labor market information and by direct employer
contact when the referral questions call for it. The firm treats the
evaluee's commutable labor market as the unit of analysis, and it documents
sources so that a cross-examiner can trace every occupational conclusion
back to the data behind it. Life care plans and
[medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/) for local
residents are likewise priced to the providers and fee data relevant to the
evaluee rather than to national averages.

## Beginning an engagement in Clinton

To begin a Clinton County engagement,
[request a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) with the case caption and the
parties, and identify any disclosure, deposition, or trial dates already on
the calendar. After conflicts clear, scope and fees are confirmed in
writing before analysis begins, and the records request that follows is
tailored to the claims actually pleaded. Counsel on either side of the
caption can expect the same engagement letter, the same documentation
standard, and the same willingness to say what the record does not support.
