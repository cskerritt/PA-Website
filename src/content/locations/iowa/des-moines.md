Purinton Analytics serves plaintiff and defense counsel across the Des
Moines-West Des Moines metropolitan area with vocational expert and life care
planning services. Referrals from the metro most often call for
[earning capacity and employability evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/)
in personal injury and employment matters,
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) in catastrophic injury
cases, and [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis
while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement
structure. Every matter begins with a documented conflict check and a defined
scope, so the written opinion rests on a record either side of the caption can
examine.

## Venue footprint across the metro

The Des Moines-West Des Moines metropolitan area takes in Polk County, Dallas
County, Warren County, Madison County, Jasper County, and Guthrie County, and
the firm accepts matters venued in the District Court serving each of them.
Federal engagements are accepted in both of Iowa's federal districts, the
United States District Court for the Southern District of Iowa and the United
States District Court for the Northern District of Iowa, with the proper
district for a given filing confirmed with counsel at intake. Counsel with matters beyond
the metro will find the firm's statewide coverage described on the
[Iowa locations page](/locations/iowa/), which applies the same report
standard to every venue.

## Meetings by appointment, not a storefront

Des Moines meetings are by appointment. The firm keeps no staffed office in
the metro and publishes no street address for this location, because accuracy
about presence is part of the same discipline that governs its reports. Most
engagements begin remotely: records review, structured interviews with the
evaluee, and standardized testing administered under publisher protocols where
remote administration is supported. When the referral questions require an
in-person evaluation, or when deposition and trial calendars require the
expert in the room, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office and
planned around counsel's dates rather than the firm's.

## Labor market research grounded in the metro

Vocational findings for Des Moines area evaluees rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Des Moines-West
Des Moines metropolitan area and for Iowa statewide, supplemented by state
workforce agency labor market information and direct employer contact where
the referral questions warrant it. Employability and placeability opinions are
tied to the evaluee's own commuting labor market rather than to national
generalizations, and the sources behind each finding are cited in the report
so that opposing counsel can trace every conclusion to its data. Life care
plans and [medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/) for
Iowa residents are priced to the evaluee's own market using provider-specific
inquiries and published fee data rather than borrowed national averages.

## Starting a Des Moines engagement

Engagements are accepted from either side of the caption, and the intake
process is the same for a Polk County trial setting as for a matter venued
across the country. Submit the caption or party names through the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and note any imminent disclosure,
deposition, or trial dates so scheduling can be confirmed alongside
clearance. Counsel weighing whether a Des Moines matter fits the practice can
also review the firm's [national coverage](/locations/nationwide/), which
describes how the same methodology, file discipline, and testimony standards
apply in every jurisdiction where the firm appears.
