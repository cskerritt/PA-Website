Expert work in a Tiffin case is governed less by geography than by the
calendar. Disclosure deadlines, deposition notices, and trial settings
arrive on their own schedule regardless of where the parties live, and
an expert retained too late, or one who cannot produce a defensible
report inside the time remaining, costs counsel leverage that no
qualification can recover. Purinton Analytics structures every Tiffin
engagement backward from those dates, delivering
[vocational expert witness](/services/vocational-expert-witness/)
opinions, [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/) for
plaintiff and defense counsel on timelines set in writing at intake.

## Venue for a Johnson County dispute

Tiffin is a Johnson County community, and state-court matters arising
here are heard in the District Court for Johnson County. Federal
litigants in Iowa have the United States District Court for the Northern
District of Iowa and the United States District Court for the Southern
District of Iowa as their forums; determining which of the districts
controls a particular Tiffin case, and what registration or
qualification requirements it places on a testifying expert, is part of
the conflict check and intake rather than a guess made from geography.
The firm's practice across the state appears on the
[Iowa page](/locations/iowa/), and its regional work on the
[Iowa City metro page](/locations/iowa/iowa-city/).

## Employability findings tied to published data

Although Tiffin itself is a small community, its workers compete in the
labor market of the surrounding metro, and the firm's analysis reflects
that reality. Employability and earning capacity conclusions rest on
Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates
for the Iowa City metropolitan area and for Iowa statewide, joined by
state workforce agency labor market information and direct labor market
research where the referral questions require more granular evidence.
The report links each finding to its data source, which is what allows
it to hold up when the opposing side reads it closely. When economic
modeling is also needed, the firm arranges
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Meeting the calendar without a local office

The firm does not staff an office in Tiffin and does not publish a local
address, and its schedule discipline does not depend on having one. The
early and middle phases of an engagement, from records analysis through
structured interviews and most standardized testing, are completed
remotely under publisher protocols, which is often the fastest route to
a disclosed report. In-person evaluation, when the questions warrant it,
happens in the evaluee's own community, and travel for depositions and
trial testimony is scheduled from the Kansas City home office with the
lead time the court's calendar demands, exactly as it is throughout the
firm's [national practice](/locations/nationwide/).

## Getting dates on the table early

A Tiffin engagement opens with a documented conflict check on the
caption or party names. When counsel
[submit a conflict check request](/refer-a-case/), the most useful thing
they can add is the case schedule itself: every disclosure deadline,
noticed deposition, and trial date already fixed. Once conflicts clear,
scope and fee terms are confirmed in writing and a tailored records
request issues immediately, so the report is built on a documented
record with time left to defend it. Opposing opinions can be assessed on
the same footing through
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/).
