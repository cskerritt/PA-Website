Small towns have long been underserved by the expert witness market, on
the unstated theory that serious vocational and life care planning work
requires a big-city address on both ends of the engagement. Purinton
Analytics was built on the opposite premise. Its methodology is
remote-first by design, which means a Solon referral receives the same
depth of analysis as a matter venued in any major metropolitan area:
[vocational expert witness](/services/vocational-expert-witness/)
evaluation, [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[expert testimony](/services/expert-testimony-litigation-consulting/),
available to plaintiff and defense counsel on identical terms.

## Where a Solon case is heard

Solon lies in Johnson County, and state-court matters from the town
proceed in the District Court for Johnson County. Iowa's federal trial
forums, the United States District Court for the Northern District of
Iowa and the United States District Court for the Southern District of
Iowa, are both available; the controlling district for any given Solon
case, and whatever registration or qualification requirements it applies
to a testifying expert, are confirmed during the conflict check and
intake instead of being inferred from location. Statewide venue context
is collected on the [Iowa page](/locations/iowa/).

## Methodology that travels well

The reason a remote-first practice serves a small community without
compromise is that most of the work never needed to be local. Records
analysis, vocational diagnostic interviewing, and the bulk of
standardized testing proceed under publisher protocols from the firm's
Kansas City home office. What must be local is kept local: when the
referral questions call for an in-person evaluation, it is performed in
the evaluee's own community, and deposition or trial appearances are
scheduled with travel from Kansas City. Purinton Analytics has no Solon
office and claims none; the by-appointment model is stated openly
because the firm's credibility rests on saying only what is true, in
reports and in marketing alike. The same model supports engagements
across the firm's [national practice](/locations/nationwide/).

## The labor market a Solon evaluee actually faces

Employability and earning capacity findings for a Solon evaluee are
anchored in Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage
estimates for the Iowa City metropolitan area, the market in which the
town's workers realistically compete, and for Iowa statewide, with state
workforce agency labor market information and direct labor market
research added where warranted. Every conclusion is traceable to its
source, and the report states the geography behind each figure it
relies upon. In cases that also require economic modeling, the firm
arranges
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. Details of the firm's regional work appear on the
[Iowa City metro page](/locations/iowa/iowa-city/).

## A conflict check before anything else

No Solon engagement begins with a strategy call; it begins with a
documented conflict check run on the caption or party names. Counsel
can [request a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) and should include any
disclosure deadlines, deposition dates, or trial settings already
entered, so scheduling is realistic from the start. After clearance,
scope and fee terms are put in writing and a records request tailored to
the claims follows. Where the assignment is to evaluate work already
disclosed by an opposing expert,
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) is available
as a freestanding engagement.
