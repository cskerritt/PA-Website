## A Washington County town with metro-scale stakes

Kalona sits in Washington County, so state-court matters arising in town
are heard in the District Court for Washington County, apart from the
courts serving the metro's principal city, Iowa City. Federal options in
Iowa consist of the United States District Court for the Northern
District of Iowa and the United States District Court for the Southern
District of Iowa; which one governs a specific Kalona case, and what
registration or qualification requirements that forum imposes on a
testifying expert, are settled during the conflict check and intake, not
presumed. The firm's presence across the state is outlined on the
[Iowa page](/locations/iowa/).

## Data an opposing expert cannot dismiss

Cross-examination of a vocational opinion usually starts with its data,
so the data must be unimpeachable in provenance even where its
interpretation is contested. For Kalona evaluees, employability and
earning capacity findings are built on Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates for the Iowa City
metropolitan area and for Iowa statewide, with state workforce agency
labor market information and direct labor market research layered in
where the referral questions justify it. The written report discloses
which geography stands behind each finding. Cases needing a full damages
model are handled through
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure, and the report says so plainly. The firm's
regional footprint is described on the
[Iowa City metro page](/locations/iowa/iowa-city/).

The firm maintains no storefront in Kalona and publishes no address for

## From caption to cleared engagement

Nothing substantive is discussed until a documented conflict check has
been run on the caption or party names. Counsel with a Kalona matter
should [request a conflict check](/refer-a-case/), attaching the party
names and flagging imminent disclosure, deposition, or trial dates.
Once the check clears, the firm confirms scope and fee terms in writing
and issues a records request tailored to the claims, so that the
resulting opinion, whichever side retained it, stands on a record both
sides can examine.
