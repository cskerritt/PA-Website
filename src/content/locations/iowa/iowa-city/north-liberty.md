## How a North Liberty evaluation proceeds

Records analysis and the review of medical, employment, and educational
history begin remotely as soon as the file arrives. Structured
interviews and most standardized vocational testing are likewise
administered remotely under publisher protocols, which keeps the
schedule moving without waiting on travel. When the referral questions
warrant an in-person component, the evaluation is conducted in the
evaluee's own community, and travel is arranged from the Kansas City
home office. There is no Purinton Analytics office in North Liberty, and
none is implied anywhere on this page: meetings here are by appointment,
which is exactly what a candid expert practice should say.

## The courts that hear Johnson County cases

North Liberty sits in Johnson County, so state-court litigation arising
in town proceeds in the District Court for Johnson County. Iowa offers
litigants a choice of federal forums as well: the United States District
Court for the Northern District of Iowa and the United States District
Court for the Southern District of Iowa. Which district governs a
particular North Liberty matter, and what registration or qualification
requirements attach to a testifying expert there, are confirmed during
the conflict check and intake rather than presumed. Counsel weighing
venue questions elsewhere in the state will find the broader picture on
the [Iowa page](/locations/iowa/).

## Labor market evidence at the right scale

North Liberty workers participate in a labor market organized around the
metro rather than the town line, so employability and earning capacity
findings are grounded in Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational
employment and wage estimates for the Iowa City metropolitan area and
for Iowa statewide, along with state workforce agency labor market
information and direct employer research where warranted. Each finding
in the written report is tied to its source geography. When a case also
needs economic modeling, the firm offers
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure, a division of labor stated plainly in every
report.
