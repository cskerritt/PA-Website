An evaluee should not have to leave home to be evaluated well. That
principle organizes how Purinton Analytics handles Story City matters:
the evaluation comes to the evaluee, not the reverse. Records analysis,
structured vocational interviews, and most standardized testing are
conducted remotely under publisher protocols, and when the referral
questions call for in-person assessment, it happens in the evaluee's own
community rather than in a distant office. For counsel on either side of
a Story City case, that means
[vocational expert work](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/) proceed on
the case's schedule without imposing travel burdens on an injured or
disabled person.

## Evaluation logistics for a Story City evaluee

The sequence is the same whether the retaining party is plaintiff or
defense. Intake and a documented conflict check come before anything
substantive; a records request tailored to the claims follows; interview
and testing sessions are scheduled around the evaluee's medical
appointments and limitations. Where an in-home or in-community
assessment is warranted, travel is arranged from the Kansas City home
office, the same base that supports the firm's
[national practice](/locations/nationwide/). No staffed office exists in
Story City and no street address is published for this location, and the
firm says so plainly, because an accurate account of its own footprint
is the least a testifying expert owes the record.

## The venue picture for Story County matters

Story City litigation arises in Story County, and state-court cases
proceed in the District Court for Story County. On the federal side,
Iowa's forums are the United States District Court for the Northern
District of Iowa and the United States District Court for the Southern
District of Iowa. Both exist as available venues; the controlling
district for a given Story City case, and any qualification or
registration requirement it places on a testifying expert, is confirmed
during the conflict check and intake. The firm's work throughout the
state is collected on the [Iowa page](/locations/iowa/).

## Earning capacity in the Ames orbit

Story City is a small community whose workers participate in the wider
metro labor market anchored by Ames. Earning capacity and employability
findings therefore draw on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational
employment and wage estimates for the Ames metropolitan area and for
Iowa statewide, on state workforce agency labor market information, and
on direct labor market research when the referral questions require
occupation-level detail. The report discloses which geography supports
each conclusion so the analysis can be tested at deposition. Metro-level
engagements are described on the
[Ames metro page](/locations/iowa/ames/).

## Referring a Story City case

Counsel begin by
[submitting the caption or party names for a conflict check](/refer-a-case/),
noting any imminent disclosure, deposition, or trial dates. Once the
check clears, scope and fee terms are confirmed in writing before work
starts. When the damages presentation also requires economic analysis,
the firm arranges
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. The result, for a Story City evaluee and the
lawyers on both sides, is a damages record built without ever asking the
evaluee to chase the expert.
