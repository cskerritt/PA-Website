## Venue in Black Hawk County

State-court matters arising in Cedar Falls are tried in the District Court
for Black Hawk County. On the federal side, Iowa offers its litigants the
United States District Court for the Northern District of Iowa and the
United States District Court for the Southern District of Iowa; which of
those forums controls a given Cedar Falls case is a question the firm
answers during the conflict check and intake, along with any registration
or qualification requirement the venue imposes on a testifying expert.
Work throughout the rest of the state is described on the
[Iowa page](/locations/iowa/), and the wider regional practice on the
[Waterloo metro page](/locations/iowa/waterloo/).

## Measuring the market a co-anchor city creates

An earning capacity analysis for a Cedar Falls evaluee starts from the
recognition that the town is a substantial employment center in its own
right while remaining part of a single regional labor market shared with
Waterloo. Vocational findings therefore rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates for the Waterloo-Cedar Falls
metropolitan area and for Iowa statewide, supplemented by state workforce
agency labor market information and by direct labor market research when
the referral questions call for it. Every figure that enters the report is
cited to its source geography, so opposing counsel can trace each finding
to the data behind it rather than to the analyst's impression of the area.

## By-appointment service, honestly described

The firm keeps no staffed office in Cedar Falls and publishes no street
address here, because accuracy about presence belongs to the same
discipline that governs its reports. What counsel get instead is a
remote-first workflow that loses nothing to distance: records analysis,
structured interviews, and most standardized testing proceed under
publisher protocols from the firm's secure systems, and when an in-person
evaluation is warranted it takes place in the evaluee's own community.
Depositions and trial appearances are scheduled from the Kansas City home
office, on the same logistics that carry the firm's
[national practice](/locations/nationwide/).

## Opening a Cedar Falls engagement

Every engagement begins with a documented conflict check, run on the
caption or party names before any substantive discussion. Counsel should
[request a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) and flag any disclosure
deadline, deposition, or trial setting already on the calendar. Once the
check clears, scope and fee terms are confirmed in writing and a tailored
records request issues. Where the damages model also requires an
economist, the firm arranges
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.
