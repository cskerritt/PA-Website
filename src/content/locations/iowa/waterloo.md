The evaluee is the center of a vocational and life care planning engagement,
and for the Waterloo-Cedar Falls area that means meeting people where they
actually live and work rather than asking them to travel to a distant
office. Purinton Analytics evaluates Waterloo and Cedar Falls area evaluees
in their own community, by appointment or by secure remote session, for
plaintiff and defense counsel alike. The work spans
[earning capacity and employability evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Courts serving the metro

The Waterloo-Cedar Falls metropolitan area takes in Black Hawk County,
Bremer County, and Grundy County, and the firm accepts matters venued in the
District Court serving each of those counties. Federal engagements are
accepted in either of Iowa's federal forums, the United States District
Court for the Northern District of Iowa and the United States District Court
for the Southern District of Iowa, with the correct district for a given
matter identified during clearance. Counsel handling related cases in other Iowa venues can review the
[Iowa locations page](/locations/iowa/) for the statewide picture; the
intake, methodology, and report standard are identical in every county.

## Appointment-only presence, stated plainly

The firm keeps no staffed office in Waterloo or Cedar Falls and publishes no
street address for this location. That is a deliberate statement of fact,
not a gap in coverage: candor about presence belongs to the same discipline
that governs the firm's written opinions. Most engagements open remotely,
with records review, structured interviews, and standardized testing under
publisher protocols where remote administration is supported. When an
in-person evaluation serves the referral questions better, or when
deposition and trial dates require attendance in Black Hawk County, travel
is scheduled from the Kansas City home office around counsel's deadlines.

## Grounding opinions in the local labor market

An earning capacity opinion is only as strong as the labor market evidence
beneath it. Vocational findings for Waterloo-Cedar Falls evaluees rest on
Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for
the metropolitan area and for Iowa statewide, together with state workforce
agency labor market information and direct labor market research where the
referral warrants it. Conclusions about employability and placeability are
tied to the evaluee's own commuting area and cited to their sources so that
either side can test them. Life care plans and
[medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/) for area
residents are priced through provider-specific inquiries and published fee
data for the evaluee's own market.

## Beginning a Waterloo engagement

Retention starts with clearance. Submit the caption or party names through
the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/), identify the venue, and note any
imminent disclosure, deposition, or trial dates so scheduling is resolved
along with the conflict review. The firm accepts work from either side of
the caption, and its [national coverage](/locations/nationwide/) describes
how the same standards travel with the practice, so a Waterloo file receives
the scrutiny and documentation counsel would expect in any major venue in
the country.
