A Smithsburg resident's working life rarely begins and ends in Smithsburg.
The town is small, and the jobs its residents hold are scattered across a
commuting market that centers on Hagerstown and reaches over the state line
into West Virginia. That fact is where Purinton Analytics starts when
counsel asks what an injured Smithsburg evaluee can still earn: the
analysis follows the commute, not the town boundary. Attorneys on both
sides of the caption engage the firm for
[vocational expert witness opinions](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) of opposing
reports, each written for the scrutiny of opposing counsel.

## The courts that hear Smithsburg cases

Smithsburg lies in Washington County, and its state-court litigation is
heard in the Circuit Court, whose expert-disclosure and testimony practice
informs how the firm assembles and produces its reports. For matters with a
federal hook, the United States District Court for the District of Maryland
is the federal forum open to Maryland litigants; the controlling venue for
any particular case, together with any qualification or registration
requirement that venue imposes, is identified during the conflict check and
intake. Coverage of the state's other venues appears on the
[Maryland locations page](/locations/maryland/).

## Measuring earning capacity across a cross-border market

Because Smithsburg sits within the Hagerstown-Martinsburg metropolitan
area, an honest employability opinion has to weigh openings on both sides
of the Maryland and West Virginia line. The firm's findings rest on Bureau
of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the
relevant area and for Maryland statewide, supplemented by state workforce
agency labor market information and, where the referral questions warrant
it, by direct labor market research into the positions a specific evaluee
could actually hold. Every figure and placement conclusion in the report is
tied to a named source, so cross-examination can probe the method rather
than speculate about it. The broader regional market is treated on the
[Hagerstown metro page](/locations/maryland/hagerstown/).

## Seeing the evaluee where the evaluee lives

Candor about presence is part of the product. The firm keeps no staffed
office in Smithsburg and lists no street address for this location; what
counsel retains is a disciplined process rather than a storefront. The
analytical core of the engagement, records review, transferable skills
analysis, and conferences with counsel, is handled remotely on a schedule
set at intake. When an in-person vocational evaluation or life care
planning assessment is needed, it is held by appointment in the evaluee's
own community, which spares an injured person the burden of travel and
anchors the opinion in the labor market that person genuinely confronts.
Travel for evaluations, depositions, and trial testimony is scheduled from
the Kansas City home office. When the case requires economic damages
calculations, the firm offers
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.
