Most Boonsboro engagements arrive with a date already attached: an expert
disclosure deadline, a deposition notice, a trial setting that will not
move. Purinton Analytics scopes its work backward from that calendar. At
intake, counsel and the firm fix the dates that govern the case, and the
records request, the evaluation, and the written report are sequenced so
that the opinion is complete and producible before the deadline rather than
finished in the week it is due. Plaintiff and defense counsel alike retain
the firm for
[vocational expert witness work](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/), and every
report is written to withstand the reading it will get from the other side
of the caption.

## Washington County venue and the federal forum

Boonsboro is a Washington County town, and its state-court matters proceed
in the Circuit Court, where the pretrial schedule sets the rhythm the firm
plans around. Claims with a federal basis may belong in the United States
District Court for the District of Maryland, the federal forum available to
Maryland litigants; whether a given matter proceeds there, and any
venue-specific qualification or registration requirement that follows, is
confirmed during the conflict check and intake. The firm's coverage across
the rest of the state is set out on the
[Maryland locations page](/locations/maryland/).

## Reading a small town against a regional market

Boonsboro itself is a small community, and a vocational opinion that treats
the town line as the edge of the labor market would misstate the evaluee's
real options. The town belongs to the Hagerstown-Martinsburg metropolitan
area, a market anchored by Hagerstown that crosses the Maryland and West
Virginia line, and employability and earning-capacity findings for a
Boonsboro evaluee are framed against that wider geography. The data behind
those findings comes from Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational
employment and wage estimates for the relevant area and for Maryland
statewide, from state workforce agency labor market information, and from
direct labor market research where the referral questions call for it, with
each conclusion cited to its source. The regional market as a whole is
addressed on the [Hagerstown metro page](/locations/maryland/hagerstown/).

## Where and how the evaluation happens

There is no Purinton Analytics office in Boonsboro, and the firm publishes
no street address here, because overstating presence would sit poorly
beside reports that trade on precision. Instead, the engagement runs on a
remote-first structure: records analysis, transferable skills work, and
counsel conferences proceed by video and secure exchange on the schedule
fixed at intake. When the referral questions require an in-person
vocational evaluation or a life care planning assessment, the appointment
is arranged in or near the evaluee's own community, so an injured person is
not asked to travel to be examined. Travel for evaluations, depositions,
and trial appearances is scheduled from the Kansas City home office. Where
the damages model requires an economist, the firm offers
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.
