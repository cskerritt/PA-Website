Referral questions from the Lexington Park metro tend to turn on the same
issues that drive vocational disputes everywhere: what an injured or
displaced worker can still do, what that work pays in the worker's own
labor market, and what the difference is worth over a working life.
Purinton Analytics answers those questions for plaintiff and defense
counsel through
[earning capacity and employability evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) in catastrophic injury
matters, and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Venue footprint in the metro

Engagements are accepted for matters venued in the circuit courts of
Calvert County and St. Mary's County, and in the United States District
Court for the District of Maryland when the matter proceeds federally. The
firm's opinions are built to the same standard in either forum: a defined
scope, methods disclosed in the report, and sourcing that opposing counsel
can trace. Any venue-specific registration or qualification requirement is
identified and confirmed during the conflict check and intake process.
Counsel whose dockets reach beyond the metro will find the firm's other
venues on the [Maryland locations page](/locations/maryland/).

## Meetings by appointment only

The firm maintains no staffed office in Lexington Park and publishes no
street address for this location; candor about presence is part of the same
discipline that governs the reports themselves. Most engagements proceed
remotely from the outset: records review, structured vocational
interviews, and standardized testing under publisher protocols where
remote administration is supported. Where the referral questions call for
an in-person evaluation, or where deposition and trial dates require
presence, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office and planned
around the court's calendar rather than the firm's convenience.

## Labor market and cost research

Vocational findings for evaluees in Calvert County and St. Mary's County
rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage
estimates for the relevant area and for Maryland statewide, together with
state workforce agency labor market information and direct labor market
research where warranted. The report identifies which data series grounds
each finding, and why that series matches the evaluee's actual labor
market, so the analysis can be tested on cross rather than taken on
faith.
Life care plans and
[medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/) for area
residents are priced to the evaluee's own market using provider-specific
inquiries and published fee data for the region, since local care costs
can diverge from national averages in either direction.

## Starting a Lexington Park engagement

The most useful time to retain a vocational expert or life care planner is
before disclosure deadlines begin to compress the work, while records can
still be requested once and reviewed carefully. Submit the caption
or party names through the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and note
any imminent disclosure, deposition, or trial dates so scheduling can be
confirmed at intake. For matters venued outside Maryland, the firm's
[national coverage](/locations/nationwide/) describes how the same team,
methods, and report standard extend across the country.
