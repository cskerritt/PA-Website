The Hagerstown-Martinsburg metro sits astride the Maryland and West Virginia
line, and litigation here often does the same: an evaluee may live on either
side of the border while working, treating, and litigating on the other.
Purinton Analytics serves plaintiff and defense counsel throughout the metro
with [vocational expert evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. Because the analysis is anchored to the evaluee's own
labor market rather than to a single state's boundaries, a cross-border
commuting pattern is treated as a fact to be documented, not an obstacle.

## Courts on both sides of the line

Engagements are accepted for matters in the Circuit Court of Washington
County in Maryland and in the circuit courts of Berkeley County and Morgan
County in West Virginia. Federal matters arising on the Maryland side of
the metro proceed in the United States District Court for the District of
Maryland. Any registration or qualification requirement particular to a
given venue is identified and confirmed during the conflict check and
intake process, before the engagement letter issues. The
[Maryland locations page](/locations/maryland/) collects the firm's other
Maryland venues for counsel whose dockets extend beyond the metro.

## By-appointment service, honestly described

There is no staffed Purinton Analytics office in Hagerstown or Martinsburg,
and the firm publishes no street address for this location. Meetings are by
appointment, and most of the work is performed remotely: records review,
structured interviews, and standardized testing under publisher protocols
where remote administration is supported. When an in-person evaluation is
warranted, or when deposition and trial dates require presence, travel is
scheduled from the Kansas City home office. Counsel receive the same report
standard and the same point of contact regardless of which side of the
state line the courthouse sits on.

## Labor market research for a border economy

Vocational findings for evaluees in the metro rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the
Hagerstown-Martinsburg area, supplemented by statewide estimates for
Maryland and West Virginia, state workforce agency labor market
information, and direct labor market research where warranted. A border
labor market rewards care in defining the relevant geography: the report
states which area's data ground each finding and why, so that the choice of
market can be examined on cross rather than discovered there. Life care
plans and [medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/)
are priced to the evaluee's own community using provider-specific
inquiries and published fee data for the region.

## Starting an engagement in the metro

Whether the caption reads Washington County, Berkeley County, or Morgan
County, the path in is the same: submit the caption or party names
through the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and note any imminent
disclosure, deposition, or trial dates so scheduling and any forum-specific
requirements can be confirmed at intake. Counsel handling matters beyond
the metro can review the firm's
[national coverage](/locations/nationwide/) for the same services under the
same engagement structure, delivered by the same team.
