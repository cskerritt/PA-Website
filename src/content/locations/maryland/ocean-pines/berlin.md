A Berlin case begins, for expert purposes, with a question of forum:
where will this opinion be tested, and by whom? Berlin sits in Worcester
County, so a civil claim under Maryland law is tried in the Circuit
Court, while a matter with a federal basis may proceed in the United
States District Court for the District of Maryland, the federal forum
available within the state. Which bench controls a particular case, and
what qualification or registration duties that venue imposes on a
testifying expert, is a question Purinton Analytics resolves during the
conflict check and intake rather than by assumption. What does not vary
with the forum is the standard of the work: methods a rival expert can
reproduce, sources a paralegal can pull, and no conclusion offered beyond
the disclosed scope.

## Berlin evaluees and the surrounding labor market

For a Berlin resident, the honest vocational question is which market the
evaluee can actually reach. The firm grounds that answer in Bureau of
Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Ocean
Pines, MD metropolitan area and for Maryland as a whole, in state
workforce agency labor market information, and in targeted employer
research when the file calls for it. No figures are borrowed from
national averages when area data exist, and the report defends its choice
of geographic frame in writing: an evaluee's restrictions, work history,
and access to transportation determine whether the relevant market is the
immediate community or the wider region, and the opinion says so plainly
instead of leaving the framing to be exposed on cross-examination.

## Appointments, evaluations, and travel

The firm keeps no office in Berlin and publishes no local address,
because accuracy about presence belongs to the same discipline that
governs its reports. Engagements are remote-first: secure video handles
document conferences, counsel strategy calls, and a large share of
vocational interviews. In-person evaluation, when the referral questions
justify it, happens in the evaluee's own community so that actual daily
function informs the findings, and the visit is planned out of the Kansas
City home office. Deposition and trial appearances follow the court's
calendar and are built into the engagement timeline from the outset.
