Most of the firm's work for Salisbury area counsel is performed remotely,
and that is by design rather than by compromise. Records review, structured
vocational interviews, and standardized testing under publisher protocols
travel well; what matters is that the methodology, the sourcing, and the
report standard stay constant wherever the evaluee sits. Purinton Analytics
brings that remote-first model to plaintiff and defense engagements in the
Salisbury metro, offering
[vocational expert evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Venue footprint

Engagements are accepted for matters venued in the circuit courts of
Somerset County and Wicomico County, and in the United States District
Court for the District of Maryland when the case proceeds federally. State
and federal forums receive the same work product: a defined scope agreed
at intake, methods disclosed in the report, and citations that let
opposing counsel trace every finding to its source. Any venue-specific
registration or qualification requirement is identified and confirmed
during the conflict check and intake process. Counsel with matters
elsewhere in the state can survey the firm's other venues on the
[Maryland locations page](/locations/maryland/).

## Presence, stated plainly

There is no staffed Purinton Analytics office in Salisbury, and the firm
publishes no street address for this location. Meetings are by
appointment. When the referral questions call for in-person evaluation, an
evaluation can be conducted in the evaluee's own community, and when
deposition or trial dates require presence, travel is scheduled from the
Kansas City home office with dates confirmed against the court's calendar.
Counsel deal with a consistent point of contact from intake through
testimony, and the engagement letter states what will be done, by whom,
and on what schedule.

## Labor market research for the metro

Vocational findings for evaluees in Somerset County and Wicomico County
rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage
estimates for the Salisbury area and for Maryland statewide, together with
state workforce agency labor market information and direct labor market
research where the referral questions warrant it. When the published
series run thin for a given occupation, documented employer contact fills
the gap rather than looser inference. A regional market of
this kind rewards specificity: the report identifies the occupations
actually available to the evaluee in the relevant geography rather than
reasoning from national aggregates. Life care plans and
[medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/) are priced
to the evaluee's own market using provider-specific inquiries and
published fee data for the region.

## Starting a Salisbury engagement

Retaining the expert early keeps the schedule in counsel's hands: records
can be requested once, gaps identified before they become disputes, and
the report completed ahead of disclosure deadlines rather than against
them. Submit the caption or party names through the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and note any imminent disclosure,
deposition, or trial dates. For matters venued outside Maryland, the
firm's [national coverage](/locations/nationwide/) describes the same
services delivered under the same engagement structure nationwide.
