The written report is the product, and everything else about a Cambridge
engagement is organized to protect it. Purinton Analytics prepares
vocational and life care planning opinions for plaintiff and defense
counsel in the Cambridge metro on the assumption that opposing counsel
will read every line: scope is defined at intake, methods are disclosed on
the face of the report, and each finding is traceable to a cited source.
Engagements typically involve
[earning capacity and employability evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) in catastrophic injury
matters, and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Dorchester County venue footprint

Engagements are accepted for matters venued in the Circuit Court of
Dorchester County and in the United States District Court for the District
of Maryland when the case proceeds federally. The forum does not change
the method: state and federal matters receive the same interview
protocols, the same transferable skills analysis, and the same sourcing
standards. Any venue-specific registration or qualification requirement is
identified and confirmed during the conflict check and intake process.
Counsel with a statewide practice can survey the firm's other venues on
the [Maryland locations page](/locations/maryland/).

## Meetings by appointment

Purinton Analytics keeps no staffed office in Cambridge and publishes no
street address for this location; a firm that expects precision from every
record it cites owes the same precision about its own footprint. Meetings
are by appointment. Most engagements proceed remotely, through records
review, structured vocational interviews, and standardized testing under
publisher protocols where remote administration is supported. When the
referral questions call for an in-person evaluation, it can be conducted
in the evaluee's own community, and when deposition or trial dates require
presence, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office with dates
confirmed against the court's calendar at intake.

## Labor market and cost research for area evaluees

Vocational findings for Dorchester County evaluees rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the relevant
area and for Maryland statewide, together with state workforce agency
labor market information and direct labor market research where the
referral questions warrant it. In a smaller regional market, the analysis
names the geography it relies on and explains the choice, so the
definition of the evaluee's labor market is defended in the report rather
than improvised at deposition. Life care plans and
[medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/) are priced
to the evaluee's own market using provider-specific inquiries and
published fee data for the region.

## Starting a Cambridge engagement

Early retention gives the analysis room to be thorough: records requested
once, gaps identified before they harden into disputes, and a report
finished ahead of the disclosure deadline rather than against it. Submit
the caption or party names through the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and note any imminent disclosure,
deposition, or trial dates. For matters venued elsewhere, the firm's
[national coverage](/locations/nationwide/) describes the same services
delivered under the same engagement structure across the country.
