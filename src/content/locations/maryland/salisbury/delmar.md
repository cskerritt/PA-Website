## Venue context in Wicomico County

Delmar lies in Wicomico County, so a state-law claim arising in town is
heard in the Circuit Court. When a matter carries federal jurisdiction,
the United States District Court for the District of Maryland is the forum
available within the state, and the controlling venue for any given case,
together with whatever qualification or registration requirement attaches
to it, is confirmed during the conflict check and intake. Reports are
written to the stricter standard either forum might apply: a transparent
methodology, sources an opposing expert can locate and check, and
conclusions that stay inside what the record supports. Regional venue
detail is collected on the
[Salisbury metro page](/locations/maryland/salisbury/), with statewide

## The labor market question

Deadlines compress analysis; they must never replace it. Even on a short
calendar, a Delmar opinion has to answer the same question a leisurely one
would: given this evaluee's restrictions, training, and work history, what
employment remains realistically available from this small town within the
Salisbury metropolitan area? The firm grounds that answer in Bureau of
Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the
Salisbury metro area and for Maryland statewide, supplements them with
state workforce agency labor market information, and adds direct labor
market research when the referral requires it. Each report explains why
those sources apply to this person, which is the difference between an
opinion that survives deposition and one that merely fills a disclosure.

## How evaluations proceed

There is no Purinton Analytics office in Delmar, and the firm says so
plainly rather than implying a storefront that does not exist. A
remote-first structure is what makes tight calendars workable: records
review, counsel conferences, and most vocational interviews proceed by
secure video without waiting on travel. Where the referral questions
demand in-person evaluation, or where a deposition or trial appearance
requires presence, travel is arranged from the Kansas City home office and
the assessment takes place in the evaluee's own community, so observed
daily function informs the opinion rather than assumption.
