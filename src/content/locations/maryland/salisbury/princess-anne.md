Somerset County. For matters with federal jurisdiction, the United States
District Court for the District of Maryland sits as the available federal

identified during the conflict check and intake rather than presumed. The

## Labor market research

An evaluee from Princess Anne works, or seeks work, within the Salisbury
metropolitan area, and the vocational analysis has to reflect both halves
of that fact: the town is small, and the market that matters is regional.
The firm's findings rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational
employment and wage estimates for the Salisbury metro area and for
Maryland statewide, alongside state workforce agency labor market
information and, where the referral questions call for it, direct labor
market research. What reaches the courtroom is not a list of citations but
a reasoned path from those sources to this individual, because a jury in
Somerset County is entitled to understand why the numbers behind an
opinion apply to the person in front of them.

## Meeting the evaluee

Accuracy about presence is part of the discipline: the firm maintains no
office in Princess Anne and frames all local work honestly as
by-appointment. Records analysis, conferences with counsel, and the
majority of vocational interviews are conducted over secure video, which
serves plaintiff and defense budgets equally. When an in-person evaluation
is warranted, or when deposition or trial testimony requires the expert
present, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office and the
assessment is carried out in the evaluee's own community, where function
and circumstance can be seen firsthand.
