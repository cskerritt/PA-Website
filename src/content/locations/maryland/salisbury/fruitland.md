## Wicomico County courts and venue

A state-law claim arising in Fruitland proceeds in the Circuit Court for
Wicomico County. Where federal jurisdiction exists, the United States
District Court for the District of Maryland is the forum available within
the state; the controlling venue for a particular matter, along with any
registration or qualification requirement it carries, is identified during
the conflict check and intake. The firm drafts every report for both
settings at once, with a methodology section an opposing expert can retrace

## Reading the Salisbury labor market from Fruitland

The central question in a Fruitland file is rarely whether work exists
somewhere; it is what work is realistically open to this evaluee, with
these restrictions and this history, from this community. Answering that
question requires treating Fruitland as part of the Salisbury commuting
market without assuming every job in the metro is equally reachable.
Vocational findings rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational
employment and wage estimates for the Salisbury metropolitan area and for
Maryland statewide, on state workforce agency labor market information,
and on direct labor market research when the referral questions warrant
it. The written opinion shows the chain of reasoning from those sources to
this person, so cross-examination meets analysis rather than assertion.

## Evaluation logistics

Purinton Analytics keeps no office in Fruitland and does not suggest
otherwise; candor about presence is part of the same discipline that
governs the reports. Records review, attorney conferences, and most
vocational interviews are handled by secure video, which keeps timelines
short and costs proportionate for plaintiff and defense alike. When the
referral calls for in-person assessment, or when a deposition or trial
date requires the expert in the room, travel is scheduled from the Kansas
City home office and the evaluation is conducted in the evaluee's own
community, where daily function and actual commuting realities can be
observed instead of assumed.
