Getting a credible expert in front of a Crisfield evaluee is a logistics
problem before it is an analytical one. In a small community like this,
counsel too often chooses between an expert who never meets the evaluee at
all and one whose travel costs distort the economics of the case. Purinton
Analytics is structured to dissolve that tradeoff: a remote-first national
practice that still puts the evaluation in the evaluee's own community
when the questions require it. Plaintiff and defense counsel retain the
firm for
[vocational expert opinions](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) on
earning capacity and employability, for
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) in catastrophic injury
litigation, and for
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Venue in Somerset County and the federal forum

Crisfield sits in Somerset County, where state-law claims are heard in the
Circuit Court. If a matter supports federal jurisdiction, the United
States District Court for the District of Maryland provides the federal
forum within the state; the venue that actually controls, and any expert
qualification or registration requirement tied to it, gets pinned down
during the conflict check and intake. Because the destination courtroom is
not always known when work begins, each report is built to hold up under
the scrutiny of either forum, with methodology an opposing expert can walk
back through and citations exact enough to audit. The
[Salisbury metro page](/locations/maryland/salisbury/) covers the
surrounding region's venues, and the [Maryland page](/locations/maryland/)
covers the state.

## A small market inside a larger one

Crisfield belongs to the Salisbury metropolitan area for statistical
purposes, but a vocational opinion cannot stop at that label. The honest
analysis asks what employment a person with these restrictions and this
work history can actually reach from this community, and it says so when
the realistic answer is narrower than the metro-wide data alone would
suggest. The firm draws on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational
employment and wage estimates for the Salisbury metropolitan area and for
Maryland statewide, on state workforce agency labor market information,
and on direct labor market research when the referral calls for it, then
documents the reasoning that ties those sources to the individual. Both
sides of the caption get the same rigor, which is why the same report can
be useful to either.

## Evaluation logistics for Crisfield evaluees

The firm has no office in Crisfield and will not manufacture the
impression of one; that candor is of a piece with how the reports are
written. Secure video carries most of the work: records review,
conferences with retaining counsel, and the bulk of vocational
interviewing. When in-person assessment is warranted, or when a deposition
or trial appearance demands presence, travel is scheduled from the Kansas
City home office and the evaluation happens where the evaluee lives, so
the opinion reflects observed daily function and real access to work
rather than an armchair estimate.

## The conflict check

A Crisfield engagement starts the way every matter in the practice starts,
including the national work described on the
[nationwide page](/locations/nationwide/): with clearance. Counsel should
[request a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) listing the caption and the
parties, and note upcoming disclosure, deposition, mediation, or trial
dates so scheduling can be settled before the analysis begins.
