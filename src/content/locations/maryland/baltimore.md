Counsel on either side of the caption in the Baltimore-Columbia-Towson metro
retain Purinton Analytics for
[earning capacity and employability evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/)
in personal injury and employment matters, for
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) and
[medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/) in catastrophic
injury cases, and for
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. Every Baltimore matter starts the way the firm's work
starts everywhere: a documented conflict check, a defined scope, and a
records request tailored to the claims, so that the written opinion rests on
a record both sides can examine.

## Venue footprint across the metro

Engagements are accepted for matters venued in the Circuit Court sitting in
Baltimore city and in the circuit courts of the surrounding counties,
including Anne Arundel County, Baltimore County, Carroll County, Harford
County, Howard County, and Queen Anne's County, as well as in the United
States District Court for the District of Maryland. The methodology does not
change with the courthouse: the same interview protocols, the same
transferable skills analysis, and the same sourcing standards apply whether
the matter is pending in state or federal court. Counsel with matters
elsewhere in the state will find the broader venue picture on the
[Maryland locations page](/locations/maryland/).

## By appointment, without a storefront

Baltimore meetings are by appointment. The firm keeps no staffed office in
the metro and publishes no street address for this location, because
accuracy about offices is part of the same discipline that governs its
reports. Most engagements proceed remotely: records review, structured
vocational interviews, and standardized testing under publisher protocols
where remote administration is supported. When the referral questions call
for in-person evaluation, or when deposition and trial dates require
presence, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office and confirmed
against the case calendar well before disclosure deadlines.

## Labor market research for Maryland evaluees

Vocational findings for evaluees in the metro rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Baltimore
metropolitan area and for Maryland statewide, supplemented by state
workforce agency labor market information and by direct employer contact
where the referral questions warrant it. The metro is among the larger
labor markets in the mid-Atlantic, which makes occupation-level analysis,
rather than generalization from statewide aggregates, the appropriate
grounding for employability and placeability opinions. Life care plans and
medical cost projections are priced to the evaluee's own market rather than
to national averages, and every source is cited in the report so that
opposing counsel can trace each figure to its origin.

## Starting a Baltimore engagement

Early retention protects the record: it allows evaluation, labor market
research, and report drafting to proceed on a schedule set by the court
rather than by the calendar's remainder. Submit the caption or party names
through the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and note any imminent
disclosure, deposition, or trial dates. Counsel weighing a multi-venue
docket can review the firm's [national coverage](/locations/nationwide/) to
see how the same engagement structure extends to matters outside Maryland.
