By the time counsel in a Leonardtown case goes looking for a vocational
expert, the scheduling order usually exists already: disclosure deadlines
are docketed, depositions are being negotiated, and the question is whether
a defensible opinion can be built inside the time that remains. That is the
problem Purinton Analytics is structured to solve. The workplan is drawn
backward from the dates counsel supplies, records are requested against a
defined scope rather than gathered open-endedly, and drafts arrive with
enough runway for the disclosure to be reviewed before it is served. The
practice covers [vocational expert opinions](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure, for retaining counsel on either side of the caption.

## Where a Leonardtown matter is heard

State-law claims arising in Leonardtown proceed in the Circuit Court
serving St. Mary's County. When a caption carries federal jurisdiction, the
United States District Court for the District of Maryland stands as the
federal forum; which court controls a given case, and whether that venue
imposes an expert registration or qualification requirement, is confirmed
during the conflict check and intake rather than assumed in the report.
Opinions are written so that neither forum requires a different work
product, only a different caption block. The surrounding region is covered
on the [Lexington Park metro page](/locations/maryland/lexington-park/),
and the firm's statewide footprint on the
[Maryland page](/locations/maryland/).

## The labor market behind a Leonardtown opinion

Leonardtown is a small town, and its workers participate in an employment
market that runs through St. Mary's County and toward Lexington Park, the
metro area's principal city. An earning-capacity analysis has to describe
that market as it actually functions for a person with documented
restrictions, neither shrinking it to the town line nor stretching it past
what a sustainable commute allows. The foundation is qualitative and
verifiable: Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage
estimates for the relevant area and for Maryland statewide, state workforce
agency labor market information, and direct employer contact where the
referral questions warrant it. Nothing enters the report without a citation
opposing counsel can trace.

## Evaluation logistics for a St. Mary's County evaluee

There is no Purinton Analytics office in Leonardtown, and the firm says so
plainly; the home office is in Kansas City, and candor about presence is
part of the credibility the reports depend on. Records analysis,
transferable skills work, and counsel conferences are handled remotely, so
distance adds no delay to the file. Where the referral calls for an
in-person vocational interview or a life care planning assessment, the
meeting is set in the evaluee's own community, which keeps the findings
grounded in the circumstances the person actually lives in. When
depositions or trial testimony require presence, travel is scheduled from
the Kansas City home office. The same remote-first structure serves
referring counsel nationally, as described on the
[nationwide practice page](/locations/nationwide/).
