An evaluation conducted where the evaluee actually lives tells a different
story than one conducted across a desk in a distant office, and for a
Havre de Grace resident that difference can shape the entire damages case.
Purinton Analytics builds its
[vocational expert opinions](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) and
[life care plans](/services/life-care-planning/) around that principle:
assess the person in the setting where work, caregiving, and daily function
actually occur, then write the report so that either side of the caption
can test every step. Referrals from plaintiff and defense counsel receive
the same file, prepared the same way.

## Venue for Havre de Grace claims

Havre de Grace is a Harford County town, and a state-law injury,
employment, or disability matter arising there proceeds in the Circuit
Court. For claims carrying federal jurisdiction, the forum the state offers
is the United States District Court for the District of Maryland; the venue
controlling any specific caption, along with any registration or
qualification requirement it imposes on a testifying expert, is confirmed
during the conflict check and intake. Reports are drafted to hold up in
either courtroom, resting on a documented conflict check, a defined scope,
and records both parties can examine. Counsel can find the broader regional
court picture on the
[Baltimore metro page](/locations/maryland/baltimore/) and the statewide
summary on the [Maryland page](/locations/maryland/).

## Labor market analysis for a small city in a large metro

Havre de Grace is a small city whose workers draw on employment
opportunities across the wider region, and a credible earning-capacity
opinion must locate the evaluee within that reality rather than within a
generic metro average. The analysis is grounded in Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the
Baltimore-Columbia-Towson metropolitan area and for Maryland statewide, in
state workforce agency labor market information, and in direct labor market
research where the referral warrants contacting employers directly. Sources
are cited so opposing counsel can retrieve and verify them; nothing in the
report rests on unstated impressions of the local economy. Where economic
loss calculations are needed, the firm coordinates
[damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) in which
independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis while
the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement
structure.

## The evaluation itself

Once venue and market questions frame the assignment, the evaluation is
scheduled to fit the case rather than the other way around. Most stages of
a Havre de Grace engagement proceed by secure video, which keeps costs
proportionate and timelines short: records review, conferences with
counsel, and frequently the vocational interview. But when the referral
questions turn on functional capacity, household demands, or the practical
reach of a job search, the assessment is conducted in person, in the
evaluee's own community. Travel for those visits, and for depositions and
trial, is arranged from the Kansas City home office. The firm keeps no
office in Havre de Grace and publishes no local address; honesty about
presence is part of the same discipline that governs the opinions
themselves.
