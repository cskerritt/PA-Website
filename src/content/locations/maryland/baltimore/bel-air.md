Before retaining an expert for a Bel Air matter, it helps to be precise
about what the opinion must establish: whether the plaintiff can work at
all, what work is realistically available, what that work pays relative to
the pre-injury path, and, in catastrophic cases, what care the injury will
require over a lifetime. Purinton Analytics addresses each element in a
separate, documented analysis, delivering
[vocational expert opinions](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) and
[life care plans](/services/life-care-planning/) that plaintiff and defense
counsel in Harford County can put in front of an opposing expert with
confidence in the foundation.

## Grounding the earning-capacity analysis

Bel Air residents work throughout the surrounding region, so the analysis
neither stops at the town boundary nor sweeps in every job in the metro.
It begins with the evaluee's restrictions, training, and work history, then
tests those against the labor market using Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates for the Baltimore-Columbia-Towson
metropolitan area and for Maryland statewide, state workforce agency labor
market information, and, where the referral questions justify it, direct
contact with employers about the specific jobs the opinion depends on.
The result is qualitative, sourced, and reproducible: opposing counsel can
pull every citation and follow the reasoning from data to conclusion.
When the case also requires an economic loss calculation, the firm offers
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Logistics for Bel Air evaluees

The firm has no office in Bel Air and does not publish a local address;
accuracy about presence is treated as part of the work product. Records
review, counsel conferences, and most vocational interviews are handled by
secure video. Where in-person evaluation is warranted, and whenever
deposition or trial requires the expert's presence, travel is arranged from
the Kansas City home office, with the evaluation conducted in the evaluee's
own community so that functional capacity, household circumstances, and
practical commuting conditions are observed directly. Life care planning
follows the same rule, because a care plan should reflect the home where
care will actually be delivered.
