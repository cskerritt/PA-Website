Damages cases rarely need just a single expert discipline. A Mount Airy
matter may call for a vocational opinion, a life care plan, and an economic
loss calculation, and coordinating those retentions separately multiplies
cost, scheduling friction, and the risk of inconsistent assumptions.
Purinton Analytics addresses this with
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure, alongside standalone
[vocational expert opinions](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) and
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) when the case needs
a single discipline. Counsel for either party retain the same methodology,
which is the point.

## Carroll County venue and forum

Mount Airy sits in Carroll County, and state-law injury, employment, and
disability claims arising in town proceed in the Circuit Court. Where
federal jurisdiction is available, the forum in the state is the United
States District Court for the District of Maryland; the venue that actually
controls a caption, and any registration or qualification requirement it
imposes on a testifying expert, is confirmed during the conflict check and
intake rather than assumed in advance. The report is written so that
neither courtroom would find its foundation wanting. The
[Baltimore metro page](/locations/maryland/baltimore/) surveys the regional
court landscape, and the [Maryland page](/locations/maryland/) covers
practice statewide.

## A small town's place in a metropolitan labor market

Mount Airy is a small town whose working residents participate in a labor
market far larger than the town itself, and the earning-capacity analysis
must capture both facts at once. The evaluee's restrictions, education, and
transportation realities set the boundary of the market that is actually
reachable; within that boundary, the opinion documents what work exists
using Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates
for the Baltimore-Columbia-Towson metropolitan area and for Maryland
statewide, state workforce agency labor market information, and direct
employer research where a referral question turns on it. The treatment is
strictly qualitative and every source is cited for verification, because a
market description that cannot be checked is an invitation to
cross-examination.

## Seeing the evaluee where they live

No Purinton Analytics office exists in Mount Airy, and none is claimed;
the firm's statements about its own presence meet the same standard as its
expert opinions. Secure video carries most of the engagement, from records
review through counsel conferences to the vocational interview in many
cases. When the referral requires hands-on assessment, or when deposition
and trial dates require attendance, travel is planned from the Kansas City
home office and the evaluation occurs in the evaluee's own community,
where household demands, caregiving arrangements, and genuine commuting
conditions are matters of observation rather than inference. Interview
notes, testing results, and the records relied on are preserved in the
file, so that the basis for every conclusion is available to both parties
in discovery.
