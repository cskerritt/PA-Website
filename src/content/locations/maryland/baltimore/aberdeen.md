The cross-examination question that decides many Aberdeen damages cases is
simple: which jobs could this person actually get to? Aberdeen workers
participate in a labor market that extends well beyond the town, and the
value of a vocational opinion turns on whether the expert defined that
commuting range with evidence or with assumption. Purinton Analytics
answers the question with documentation, producing
[vocational expert opinions](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) and
[life care plans](/services/life-care-planning/) for Harford County counsel
on either side of the caption, under a methodology that does not change
with the retaining party.

## Defining the commuting market honestly

For an Aberdeen evaluee, the analysis starts with the person, not the
region: restrictions, education, work history, and the transportation
realities that determine how far employment can reasonably be pursued. Only
then does it turn to the data, using Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational
employment and wage estimates for the Baltimore-Columbia-Towson metropolitan
area and for Maryland statewide, state workforce agency labor market
information, and direct labor market research when a referral question
depends on whether specific employers actually hire under the conditions
the opinion assumes. Neither side is served by an opinion that treats every
job in the metro as available, or by one that pretends the town line is the
edge of the world; the report says which jobs are in reach and shows its
work.

## How an Aberdeen evaluation proceeds

Purinton Analytics maintains no Aberdeen office and lists no local street
address, a policy of accuracy that mirrors the standard applied to every
report. The bulk of an engagement moves by secure video: records review,
conferences with counsel, and in most cases the vocational interview
itself. When the referral requires in-person evaluation, or when deposition
and trial dates demand presence, travel is planned from the Kansas City
home office and the assessment takes place in the evaluee's own community,
where functional capacity and actual travel-to-work conditions are
observable facts rather than estimates. Where the case requires economic
loss quantification, the firm structures
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.
