Centreville proceed in the Circuit Court, and the engagement is built for

the state's federal forum is the United States District Court for the
District of Maryland; the venue controlling a particular caption, and any

## A county town in a metropolitan market

Centreville is a small county town whose residents may work locally or
reach employment across the larger region, and the earning-capacity
analysis must establish which of those describes the evaluee rather than
assuming either. The inquiry starts with restrictions, skills, and
sustainable commuting range, then documents the work available within
that range using Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and
wage estimates for the Baltimore-Columbia-Towson metropolitan area and
for Maryland statewide, state workforce agency labor market information,
and direct labor market research where the referral questions justify
contacting employers. Everything stays qualitative and every source is
cited so opposing counsel can retrieve it. When the damages model
requires economic quantification, the firm structures
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Evaluating in the evaluee's community

Purinton Analytics publishes no Centreville address and staffs no local
office; the firm describes its presence with the same care it puts into
its opinions. Records review, conferences with counsel, and many
vocational interviews are conducted by secure video, keeping cost and
schedule proportionate. When in-person evaluation is warranted, or when a
deposition or trial requires attendance, travel is arranged from the
Kansas City home office and the assessment is performed in the evaluee's
own community, where home circumstances, caregiving realities, and actual
commuting conditions can be observed directly rather than reconstructed
from paper.
