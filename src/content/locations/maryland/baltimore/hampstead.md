When a catastrophic injury reshapes a life in Hampstead, the central
damages question becomes what care that life will now require: attendant
services, therapies, equipment, medications, home modification, and the
replacement of household work the injured person once performed. Purinton
Analytics answers that question through
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) grounded in the medical
record and the evaluee's actual home circumstances, paired where needed
with [vocational opinions](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) on
whether and where the person can still work. Both plaintiff and defense
counsel receive the same disciplined file, because a care plan that flexes
with the retaining party is not evidence.

A state-law matter arising in Hampstead, which sits in Carroll County,

## Care needs first, then the labor market

In a Hampstead case involving residual work capacity, the vocational
analysis picks up where the medical restrictions leave off: what work
remains within the commuting range this evaluee can sustain from a small
Carroll County community whose workers reach jobs across the wider region.
That analysis draws on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment
and wage estimates for the Baltimore-Columbia-Towson metropolitan area and
for Maryland statewide, on state workforce agency labor market
information, and on direct labor market research when the referral turns
on particular employers or job requirements. The discussion remains
qualitative and every source is cited for retrieval. When lifetime cost
and earnings loss must be quantified, the firm structures
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Where the evaluation happens

Purinton Analytics keeps no office in Hampstead and invents no local
presence; what it brings instead is a process. Records review and counsel
conferences run by secure video, as do many vocational interviews. Life
care planning assessments, and vocational evaluations where the referral
requires them in person, are conducted in the evaluee's own home and
community, with travel arranged from the Kansas City home office, because
a plan for future care should be written by someone who has seen where
that care will be delivered. The same travel practice covers depositions
and trial.
