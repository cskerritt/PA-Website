Life care planning referrals from the Ocean Pines area often arrive with
the medical record already voluminous and the future care picture still
unwritten. Purinton Analytics builds that picture for plaintiff and
defense counsel through [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/)
and [medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/) grounded
in the treating record and priced to the evaluee's own market, alongside
[vocational expert evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) when
earning capacity is also in dispute. Where a full damages presentation is
needed, the firm offers
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Worcester County venue footprint

Engagements are accepted for matters venued in the Circuit Court of
Worcester County and in the United States District Court for the District
of Maryland when the case proceeds federally. The work product is the same
in either forum: a defined scope set at intake, methods disclosed in the
report, and every figure traceable to a cited source, so that the opinion
withstands the reading it will get from the other side of the caption.
Counsel whose dockets extend across the state can survey the firm's other
Maryland venues on the [Maryland locations page](/locations/maryland/).

## Appointment-based service, described accurately

The firm has no staffed office in Ocean Pines and publishes no street
address for this location. Meetings are by appointment, and most of the
work proceeds remotely: records review, structured interviews with the
evaluee and, where appropriate, family members and treating providers, and
standardized testing under publisher protocols where remote administration
is supported. When an in-person evaluation is warranted, it can be
conducted in the evaluee's own community, and when deposition or trial
dates require presence, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home
office with dates confirmed early against the court's calendar. Counsel
keep a consistent point of contact from intake through testimony.

## Labor market and care cost research

Vocational findings for Worcester County evaluees rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the relevant
area and for Maryland statewide, together with state workforce agency
labor market information and direct labor market research where the
referral questions warrant it. The report states which geography grounds
each finding and why it fits the evaluee's work history and commuting
pattern. Care costs receive the same locality
discipline: a life care plan for an Ocean Pines area resident is priced
through provider-specific inquiries and published fee data for the region,
not through national averages, because the plan's credibility depends on
whether its line items could actually be purchased where the evaluee
lives.

## Starting an Ocean Pines engagement

The earlier the referral, the more the record can be shaped rather than
salvaged: treating providers can be interviewed, care needs documented,
and the report completed ahead of disclosure deadlines. Submit the caption
or party names through the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and note
any imminent disclosure, deposition, or trial dates. For related matters
venued outside Maryland, the firm's
[national coverage](/locations/nationwide/) describes the same services
under the same engagement structure across the country.
