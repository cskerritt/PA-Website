Some Easton engagements begin with the opposing expert's report already on
file. For those matters, Purinton Analytics provides
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) that tests the
methodology, sourcing, and internal consistency of the existing opinion
rather than merely offering a competing number. For matters built from the
ground up, the firm serves plaintiff and defense counsel in the Easton
metro with
[earning capacity and employability evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Talbot County and the federal forum

Engagements are accepted for matters venued in the Circuit Court of Talbot
County and in the United States District Court for the District of
Maryland when the case proceeds federally. The same report standard
applies in both forums: a scope defined at intake, methods disclosed on
the face of the report, and findings traceable to cited sources. Any
venue-specific registration or qualification requirement is identified and
confirmed during the conflict check and intake process. Counsel practicing
across the state will find the firm's other venues collected on the
[Maryland locations page](/locations/maryland/).

## How by-appointment service works here

The firm maintains no staffed office in Easton and publishes no street
address for this location, because a practice that audits other experts'
claims must keep its own claims audit-proof. Meetings are by appointment.
Engagements ordinarily begin remotely with records review, structured
vocational interviews, and standardized testing under publisher protocols
where remote administration is supported; when the referral questions call
for in-person evaluation, or when deposition and trial dates require
presence, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office and locked
to the case calendar at intake, so distance never becomes the reason a
deadline slips.

## Labor market research for Talbot County evaluees

Vocational findings rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational
employment and wage estimates for the relevant area and for Maryland
statewide, supplemented by state workforce agency labor market information
and direct labor market research where warranted. In a compact regional
market, the report is explicit about which geography grounds each finding
and why that geography fits the evaluee's actual commuting and work
history, so the labor market definition can be examined in deposition
rather than left implicit. Where published series run thin for a
particular occupation, direct employer contact and documented survey work
fill the gap, and the report says so.
Life care plans and
[medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/) for area
residents are priced to the evaluee's own market through provider-specific
inquiries and published fee data for the region.

## Starting an Easton engagement

Whether the need is an affirmative opinion or a disciplined review of an
opinion already served, the engagement starts the same way: submit the caption
or party names through the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and note
any imminent disclosure, deposition, or trial dates. Counsel with matters
beyond Maryland can review the firm's
[national coverage](/locations/nationwide/) for the same services under
the same engagement structure nationwide.
