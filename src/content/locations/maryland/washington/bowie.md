A vocational file in Bowie starts with a person, not a venue. Before any
opinion about lost earning capacity or future care can hold up, the
evaluee's education, work history, medical restrictions, and day-to-day
functioning have to be documented well enough that a stranger to the case
could follow the reasoning from record to conclusion. That documentation
discipline is what Purinton Analytics brings to Bowie counsel on both sides
of the caption, whether the assignment is an
[employability and earning capacity evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
a [life care plan](/services/life-care-planning/) in a catastrophic injury
matter, or a
[coordinated damages engagement](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis alongside the vocational and care-planning work.

## Prince George's County courts

Bowie sits in Prince George's County, and state court matters proceed in the
Circuit Court for Prince George's County. Maryland's federal forum, the
United States District Court for the District of Maryland, is available for
cases within federal jurisdiction; the firm treats the identification of the
controlling venue, and of any qualification requirement that venue imposes,
as an intake task resolved during the conflict check rather than a fact to
be guessed at. The same standards govern the firm's work across
[Maryland](/locations/maryland/) and throughout the surrounding
[Washington metro region](/locations/virginia/washington/), so counsel with
companion cases in neighboring counties deal with a consistent methodology.

## The market around a Bowie evaluee

An earning capacity opinion is only as good as the labor market it points
to. For Bowie evaluees, that market is described through Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates covering the
Washington metropolitan area and Maryland statewide, along with state
workforce agency labor market information and, where a placement question is
genuinely contested, direct labor market research with employers. The
analysis is careful about reach: a suburban evaluee's realistic options
depend on restrictions, transferable skills, and commuting tolerance, and
the report states in plain terms which portion of the regional market each
conclusion assumes, so the assumption itself can be tested by either side.

## Scheduling the evaluation itself

Purinton Analytics keeps no staffed location in Bowie and publishes no local
address, because claiming presence the firm does not have would sit poorly
next to reports built on verification. Engagements are by appointment and
most proceed remotely: records arrive from counsel, the structured interview
is conducted by secure video, and standardized instruments are administered
under documented conditions. When the questions presented require seeing the
evaluee in person, or when hearings demand attendance, travel is planned
from the Kansas City home office and the appointment is set in the evaluee's
own community, which preserves an accurate picture of daily functioning.
