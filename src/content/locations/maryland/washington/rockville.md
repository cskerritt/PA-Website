## Labor market evidence on the record

When a Rockville case reaches the point of expert discovery, the labor
market evidence has to stand on published, checkable sources. Findings rest
on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for
the Washington metropolitan area and for Maryland statewide, on state
workforce agency labor market information, and on direct employer contact
when a contested occupation demands verification. Rockville sits within one
of the deepest employment regions in the country, and the analysis says
precisely which slice of that region an evaluee can access, given the
restrictions the medical record supports. Opinions are framed so that
cross-examination tests the data rather than exposing gaps in it.

## Scheduling the evaluation

The firm keeps no office and lists no address in Rockville; work here is by
appointment, stated plainly because overstated presence is the kind of
detail opposing counsel finds. In most files the entire engagement runs
remotely, from records review through a secure video interview to a signed
report, which suits compressed discovery periods. Where an in-person
evaluation is warranted, or where a deposition or trial appearance is set,
travel proceeds from the Kansas City home office and the evaluee is seen in
his or her own community, so functional observations reflect real
conditions rather than an unfamiliar setting.
