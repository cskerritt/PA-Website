The first document produced in a Frederick engagement is not the report; it
is the written scope. Before any records are reviewed, Purinton Analytics
and retaining counsel fix in writing which questions the expert will answer
and, just as deliberately, which questions fall outside the assignment. An
opinion that drifts past its scope is an opinion opposing counsel will
dismantle, so the boundary is set at retention and honored through
disclosure. Within that discipline, Frederick attorneys on either side of
the caption retain
[vocational evaluation and expert testimony](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Frederick County courts and the federal forum

Frederick sits in Frederick County, and its state court matters proceed in
the Circuit Court for Frederick County. Claims within federal jurisdiction
may proceed in the United States District Court for the District of
Maryland, the state's federal forum. The firm does not presume venue: the
controlling court for a particular matter, and any registration or
qualification requirement that venue imposes on a testifying expert, is
identified and confirmed during the conflict check and intake process.
Counsel with related matters elsewhere in
[Maryland](/locations/maryland/) or across the broader
[Washington metro](/locations/virginia/washington/) can proceed through the
same intake, with a single methodology governing every courthouse involved.

## Labor market evidence scoped to the evaluee

The same scope discipline governs the labor market work. Earning capacity
findings for Frederick evaluees rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates for the Washington metropolitan
area and for Maryland statewide, supplemented by state workforce agency
labor market information and direct employer research where the referral
questions warrant it. Regional data alone does not answer the question the
court cares about, so the analysis narrows from the published series to the
individual: what work a person with these documented restrictions, this
education, this work history, and this commuting capacity can realistically
obtain and hold. Each report states which portion of the data a conclusion
draws on and why, with sources cited at a level of detail that permits
opposing counsel to retrace the analysis.

## Evaluating a Frederick evaluee

There is no staffed Purinton Analytics office in Frederick and no published
street address here; meetings are by appointment, and the firm says so
plainly because accuracy about presence belongs to the same discipline that
governs its reports. Most engagements proceed remotely from start to
finish: records review, a structured vocational interview conducted by
secure video, standardized testing where appropriate, and written reporting
all move on the litigation calendar without travel. When the referral
questions call for in-person evaluation, or when a deposition or trial
setting requires presence, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home
office and the evaluation is arranged in the evaluee's own community, which
keeps the observed functioning consistent with the environment in which the
evaluee actually lives and works.

## Starting a Frederick engagement

The practice accepts referrals from either side of the caption, and the
[nationwide practice](/locations/nationwide/) runs on the same written
methodology in every venue, so a Frederick file is handled no differently
from a matter in the firm's home jurisdiction. Rebuttal and peer review
assignments are welcome on the same terms as affirmative evaluations. To
begin, [request a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) with the case caption and
party names, and note any disclosure deadline, deposition setting, or trial
date that should shape the schedule; scope and fee terms follow in writing
after the conflict check clears.
