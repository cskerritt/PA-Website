city line. Gaithersburg sits in Montgomery County within the Washington
metropolitan area, and its residents earn their livings across a commuting
market that is far larger than the city itself, which means that questions of
earning capacity after injury turn on what work actually exists across that
range for a person with specific restrictions and skills. Purinton Analytics
addresses those questions for plaintiff and defense counsel alike through
[earning capacity and employability evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/), with forensic
economic analyses performed and signed by independent economist partners
whom the firm coordinates.

## Montgomery County venue context

Gaithersburg matters in state court proceed in the Circuit Court for
Montgomery County; Maryland's federal district, the United States District
Court for the District of Maryland, is available for cases within federal
jurisdiction. Which court controls a given dispute is a question the firm
answers case by case, during the conflict check and intake, rather than by
assumption, and any venue-specific expert qualification requirement is
confirmed at the same stage. The practice covering
[Maryland](/locations/maryland/) statewide and the multistate
[Washington metro](/locations/virginia/washington/) operates from the same
intake pipeline, so a Gaithersburg referral can grow to reach related venues
without a change in methodology.

## Measuring the commuting market

Because a Gaithersburg worker's opportunities extend well past Gaithersburg,
labor market findings here lean on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational
employment and wage estimates for the Washington metropolitan area together
with Maryland statewide estimates, state workforce agency data, and targeted
labor market survey work when a disputed occupation calls for it. The
analysis names its geography explicitly: what an evaluee could earn within a
reasonable commute is a different question from what the metro pays at its
center, and a report that blurs the difference will not survive
cross-examination. Every figure-bearing exhibit is sourced so that opposing
counsel can pull the same series and check the work.

## Appointments, not an office

The firm maintains no storefront and publishes no street address in
Gaithersburg; every meeting is by appointment, and candor about that is part
of the practice's credibility. The default engagement is remote: counsel
sends records, the vocational interview is held over secure video, and the
report is delivered against the disclosure calendar. Where the assignment
requires observing the evaluee in person, or where testimony must be given
live, travel is arranged from the Kansas City home office and evaluations are
held in the evaluee's own community rather than in a borrowed conference room
chosen for the firm's convenience.
