Purinton Analytics keeps no office in Laurel, and it serves Laurel well
partly because it has never claimed to. The practice was built remote-first
for exactly this kind of community: a smaller suburb whose injury and
employment cases deserve the same caliber of expert work available in the
region's largest venues, without counsel having to accept either an inflated
local presence or a diluted product. What Laurel attorneys retain is a
national practice offering
[vocational expert evaluation and testimony](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/), each delivered
against the standard that opposing counsel will read every page.

## Laurel matters in county and federal court

Laurel's state court litigation proceeds in the Circuit Court for Prince
George's County, the trial court for its home county. Where a claim carries
federal jurisdiction, the United States District Court for the District of
Maryland stands as the state's federal forum; which court ultimately
controls a particular case is settled during the conflict check and intake,
together with any registration or qualification step that the chosen venue
requires of a testifying expert. The firm's engagement standards are uniform
whether the file sits here, elsewhere in [Maryland](/locations/maryland/),
or across the multistate
[Washington metropolitan area](/locations/virginia/washington/).

## A suburb inside a vast labor market

Laurel evaluees live in a modest-sized community embedded in an enormous
regional economy, and honest vocational analysis has to hold both facts at
the same time. Findings rely on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational
employment and wage estimates for the Washington metropolitan area and for
Maryland statewide, corroborated by state workforce agency labor market
information and by direct contact with employers when a disputed occupation
requires it. The report then narrows from the regional data to the evaluee:
what jobs a person with these restrictions, this work history, and this
commuting capacity can actually obtain and hold, stated with sources that
either side can pull and verify independently.

## Travel, remote work, and the evaluee's community

By-appointment service is not a limitation here; it is the design. Records
review, secure video interviews, standardized testing, and report drafting
proceed remotely on the court's schedule, which routinely proves faster than
waiting on a local expert's calendar. When the assignment genuinely calls
for in-person assessment, or when deposition or trial testimony must be
delivered live, travel originates from the Kansas City home office and the
evaluation is conducted where the evaluee actually lives, because functional
capacity observed in familiar surroundings makes for more defensible
findings than a clinical setting chosen for show. Forensic economic
analyses, when the damages theory needs them, are performed and signed by
independent economist partners the firm coordinates within a
[combined damages engagement](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/).

## Placing the conflict check

Plaintiff and defense inquiries receive the same intake and the same candor
about what the evidence will and will not support. The
[national footprint](/locations/nationwide/) means a Laurel referral is
routine rather than remote in any practical sense. To open a file,
[provide the caption or party names for a conflict check](/refer-a-case/),
state the questions the expert must answer, and list the deadlines already
entered so scope and scheduling can be committed to in writing from the
outset.
