## Venue context in Prince George's County

College Park lies in Prince George's County, and its state court litigation
proceeds in the Circuit Court for Prince George's County. Cases within
federal jurisdiction may proceed in the United States District Court for the
District of Maryland, the state's federal forum; rather than assuming where
a matter belongs, the firm confirms the controlling venue, and any
venue-specific expert registration or qualification requirement, during the
conflict check and intake. Counsel managing related matters across
[Maryland](/locations/maryland/) or elsewhere in the
[Washington metropolitan area](/locations/virginia/washington/) get
continuity of method across every courthouse involved.

## The data behind each opinion

Symmetry between plaintiff and defense work is possible only because
opinions are anchored to published data rather than advocacy. Labor market
findings for College Park evaluees draw on Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates for the Washington metropolitan
area and for Maryland statewide, on state workforce agency labor market
information, and on direct employer research where a specific occupation is
disputed. College Park is part of a regional labor market of unusual depth,
and the report specifies which segment of it a given evaluee can actually
reach in light of documented restrictions, so that the geographic assumption
is visible and testable rather than buried in the arithmetic performed by
the independent economist partners who sign any companion economic analysis.

## Logistics for College Park evaluations

The firm has no office in College Park, publishes no address here, and does
not pretend otherwise; local meetings happen by appointment because a
practice that testifies about facts should describe itself factually. The
working default is remote: counsel transmits records, the vocational
interview runs over secure video, and testing and reporting follow on the
case schedule. Where in-person evaluation is justified by the referral
questions, or where deposition or trial testimony is required, travel is
arranged from the Kansas City home office and evaluations are held in the
community where the evaluee lives, keeping observations tied to real
circumstances.
