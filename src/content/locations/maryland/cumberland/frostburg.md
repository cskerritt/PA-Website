A vocational evaluation should not require an injured Frostburg resident to
cross the state to be seen. Purinton Analytics builds its western Maryland
engagements around that premise: the analytical work proceeds remotely, and
when the referral questions call for an in-person assessment, the evaluee is
seen by appointment in or near Frostburg itself. Counsel on either side of
the caption retain the firm for
[vocational expert witness work](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) of opposing
reports, and every opinion is written on the assumption that opposing
counsel will read it closely.

## Allegany County courts and the Maryland forums

Frostburg sits in Allegany County, and state-court matters here proceed in
the Circuit Court, whose disclosure and testimony expectations shape how the
firm's reports are assembled and produced. For claims that belong in federal
court, the United States District Court for the District of Maryland is the
federal forum available to Maryland litigants; whether a particular case
proceeds there, along with any venue-specific qualification or registration
requirement, is confirmed during the conflict check and intake. The firm's
statewide coverage, including its other Maryland venues, is described on the
[Maryland locations page](/locations/maryland/).

## A labor market that reaches past the town line

Frostburg belongs to the Cumberland metropolitan area, a market that takes
its name from its principal city and reaches across the Maryland and West
Virginia line. An honest employability opinion about an Allegany County
evaluee has to account for that geography, because the realistic search for
work often runs toward Cumberland rather than stopping at the edge of town.
Findings on employability and earning capacity rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the relevant area
and for Maryland statewide, supplemented by state workforce agency labor
market information and by direct labor market research when the referral
questions warrant it. Each conclusion is tied to its source, so that
cross-examination can test the method instead of guessing at it. The
surrounding market is covered on the
[Cumberland metro page](/locations/maryland/cumberland/).

## How a Frostburg evaluation proceeds

The firm keeps no staffed office in Frostburg and publishes no street
address here; candor about presence is part of the same discipline that
governs the reports themselves. What counsel gets instead is a defined
sequence. Records review, transferable skills analysis, and most
conferences are handled remotely on a schedule fixed at intake. In-person
vocational evaluations and life care planning assessments are held by
appointment in the evaluee's own community, which keeps the travel burden
off an injured party and grounds the findings in the labor market that
person actually faces. Travel for evaluations, depositions, and trial
testimony is scheduled from the Kansas City home office. When damages
require an economist, the firm offers
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

Every engagement, plaintiff or defense, begins the same way. Submit the
