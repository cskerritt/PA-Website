Purinton Analytics serves Cook County and Chicagoland counsel with
employability, earning capacity, and life care planning evaluations for both
plaintiff and defense engagements. Chicago referrals frequently pair a
[vocational evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) with
[rebuttal or peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) of an opposing
expert's report, and catastrophic cases add
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) and economic damages
analysis, the latter performed and signed by independent economist partners
coordinated by the firm.

## The Chicagoland footprint

Engagements are accepted for matters in the Circuit Court of Cook County and
the circuit courts of the collar counties, including DuPage, Lake, Will,
Kane, and McHenry, and in the United States District Court for the Northern
District of Illinois. The same availability extends downstate when a referral
arises outside the metro. Depositions and trial testimony are provided in
person or by remote appearance where the court permits, and evaluation
sessions are held in the evaluee's community, at counsel's offices, or by
secure video under publisher protocols.

## What by-appointment means here

Chicago meetings are by appointment. The firm keeps no staffed storefront in
the Loop and publishes no street address for this location, because accuracy
about offices is part of the same discipline that governs its reports. What
counsel can rely on instead is responsiveness: conflict checks are answered
promptly, evaluation dates are set against the disclosure calendar at intake,
and travel from the centrally located Kansas City home office is
straightforward for depositions, trials, and in-person evaluations throughout
northern Illinois.

## Research in one of the country's largest labor markets

Chicago's labor market is deep and occupationally diverse, which cuts both
ways in litigation: more post-injury placement options for some evaluees,
sharper wage stratification for others. The analysis measures rather than
assumes. Employability and earning capacity findings rely on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Chicago
metropolitan area and for Illinois, state workforce agency labor market
information, and targeted labor market research, including employer contact
and job posting analysis, when the referral questions require evidence about
actual hiring in a specific corridor of the metro.
