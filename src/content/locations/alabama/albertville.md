## Labor market evidence for Marshall County evaluees

A credible earning capacity opinion in a smaller labor market demands care
about what work actually exists there. Vocational findings for Albertville
evaluees rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage
estimates for the relevant area and for Alabama statewide, on state
workforce agency labor market information, and on direct employer contact
when the referral questions require it. Life care plans and medical cost
projections are priced to the evaluee's own community, with each source
cited in the report. Opposing reports are reviewed against the same
standards in [rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/)
engagements.
