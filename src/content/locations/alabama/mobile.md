Methodology is the product Purinton Analytics delivers to Mobile counsel.
Whether a matter calls for a
[vocational evaluation of earning capacity](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
a [life care plan](/services/life-care-planning/), or a
[peer review of an opposing expert's report](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/),
the work follows a documented sequence: conflict check, defined scope,
records request tailored to the claims, evaluation under published
protocols, and a written opinion in which every source is cited. Because the
same sequence applies whether the retaining party sits at the plaintiff or
the defense table, the resulting report is built to withstand
cross-examination from either direction.

## The Mobile venue footprint

The Mobile, AL Metropolitan Statistical Area consists of Mobile County, and
engagements are accepted for matters venued in the Circuit Court of Mobile
County. In federal litigation, the firm accepts Alabama matters in the
United States District Court for the Southern District of Alabama, the
United States District Court for the Middle District of Alabama, and the
United States District Court for the Northern District of Alabama. Forum
choice affects disclosure timing and testimony logistics, which are
confirmed at intake, but it does not change the underlying analysis or the
report standard. Any venue-specific registration or qualification
requirement is identified and confirmed during the conflict check and intake
process.

## By appointment in Mobile

Purinton Analytics maintains no staffed office in Mobile and publishes no
street address for this location. The firm states that plainly because
accuracy about presence belongs to the same discipline that governs its
reports, and because counsel deserve to know exactly what they are engaging:
a remote-first national practice with a home office in Kansas City. Records
review, structured interviews, and standardized testing proceed remotely
where publisher protocols support remote administration. In-person
evaluation, depositions, and trial appearances are scheduled from the Kansas
City home office, coordinated with the deadlines counsel identify at intake.
Counsel receive the same report standard and the same point of contact
regardless of where any given step of the work is performed.

## Researching the Mobile labor market

Vocational findings for evaluees in Mobile County rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates from the OEWS program
for the Mobile area and for Alabama statewide, together with state workforce
agency labor market information and direct labor market research where the
referral questions warrant it. [Medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/)
and life care plans are priced to the evaluee's own market using
provider-specific inquiries and published fee data. When counsel also need
economic loss calculations, the firm offers
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

The [Alabama practice page](/locations/alabama/) covers the statewide venue
landscape, and the [nationwide page](/locations/nationwide/) explains how
the same standards travel to every state. To begin a Mobile engagement,
[request a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) with the case caption and
parties, and note any imminent disclosure, deposition, or trial dates.

counsel then receive a defined scope and a records request tailored to the
