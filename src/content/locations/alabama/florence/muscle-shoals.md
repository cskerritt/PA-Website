Muscle Shoals sits in Colbert County, and state-court matters arising in the

federal side, Alabama's trial forums are the United States District Court for

## A working town inside a compact labor market

A vocational opinion about a Muscle Shoals evaluee cannot treat the town as
an island. The realistic search for replacement work runs across the metro,
taking in Florence as the principal city along with the neighboring Colbert
County communities of Sheffield and Tuscumbia, and a defensible earning
capacity analysis has to reflect that commuting reality rather than a
hypothetical national market. Findings rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates for the Florence-Muscle Shoals
metropolitan area and for Alabama statewide, supplemented by state workforce
agency labor market information and by direct employer research where the
referral questions warrant it. The analysis stays qualitative about scale
and specific about method, so that either side of the caption can trace
every conclusion back to its source.

## How a Muscle Shoals engagement proceeds

The firm keeps no office in Muscle Shoals and publishes no local address,
because accuracy about presence belongs to the same discipline that governs
its written opinions. Meetings are by appointment. Records review, counsel
conferences, and many evaluee interviews proceed remotely, which keeps
schedules moving without waiting on travel. When the referral questions call
for in-person evaluation, the assessment is conducted in the evaluee's own
community, and when deposition or trial requires presence in an Alabama
courtroom, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office with enough
lead time to protect the case calendar. The same structure supports
[engagements nationwide](/locations/nationwide/), so a Muscle Shoals matter
with out-of-state parties or treatment providers stays under a coherent
scope.
