Colbert County. Matters that belong in federal court proceed in Alabama's

District of Alabama, the United States District Court for the Middle
District of Alabama, and the United States District Court for the Southern
District of Alabama; the controlling district for any specific case is
confirmed during the conflict check and intake instead of being assumed from
a map. Whatever the forum, the report is written so that the methodology,
sources, and reasoning are visible to the court and to opposing counsel
