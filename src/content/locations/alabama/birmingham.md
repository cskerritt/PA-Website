Counsel in the Birmingham metro refer vocational and care-planning questions
to Purinton Analytics in the same postures they arise everywhere else in the
practice: an [earning capacity and employability evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/)
in an injury or employment matter, a [life care plan](/services/life-care-planning/)
in a catastrophic injury case, or a [rebuttal review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/)
of an opposing expert's report. Plaintiff and defense firms alike receive the
same methodology, the same report standard, and the same willingness to have
every step of the analysis examined by the other side of the caption. The
Birmingham page is part of the firm's broader [Alabama practice](/locations/alabama/),
which in turn follows the standards described on the
[national coverage page](/locations/nationwide/).

## Venue footprint across the Birmingham metro

The Birmingham, AL Metropolitan Statistical Area takes in Jefferson County
and Shelby County along with Bibb County, Blount County, Chilton County,
St. Clair County, and Walker County. Engagements are accepted for matters in
the Circuit Court of each of those counties and in federal litigation in the
United States District Court for the Northern District of Alabama, the
United States District Court for the Middle District of Alabama, and the
United States District Court for the Southern District of Alabama. Venue
does not change the work product: the same written opinion, disclosure
practice, and testimony preparation apply in state and federal court alike.

## Meetings by appointment, not a storefront

Birmingham meetings are by appointment. The firm keeps no staffed office in
the metro and publishes no local street address, because accuracy about
presence is part of the same discipline that governs its reports. Most
engagements begin remotely with records review, structured interviews, and
standardized testing under publisher protocols where remote administration
is supported. When the referral questions call for in-person evaluation, or
when deposition and trial dates require attendance, travel is scheduled from
the Kansas City home office and planned around the court's calendar rather
than the firm's.

## Labor market research for Birmingham evaluees

Vocational findings for evaluees in the metro rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates from the OEWS program
for the Birmingham area and for Alabama statewide, supplemented by state
workforce agency labor market information and direct employer contact where
the referral questions warrant it. Life care plans and
[medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/) are priced to
the evaluee's own market rather than to national averages, and every source
is cited so opposing counsel can trace each figure in the report to its
origin. When a matter also requires economic loss calculations, the firm
offers [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Starting a Birmingham engagement

Any venue-specific registration or qualification requirement is identified
and confirmed during the conflict check and intake process, before
substantive work begins. To explore availability for a matter venued in the
Birmingham metro, submit the caption or party names through the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and note any imminent disclosure,
deposition, or trial dates so scheduling can be confirmed alongside
conflicts. Counsel receive a defined scope and a records request tailored to
the claims before the evaluation itself begins.
