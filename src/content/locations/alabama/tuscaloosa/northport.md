Counsel litigating a wage loss claim for a Northport plaintiff, or defending
against one, are in a real sense litigating the local labor market itself:
what work exists within reach of a Northport resident, what that work
demands physically and cognitively, and whether this particular claimant can
still obtain and hold it. Purinton Analytics addresses those questions with
[earning capacity and employability evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. The practice serves either side of the caption, and
every report is written to withstand the other side's scrutiny.

## Tuscaloosa County venue and courts

Northport sits in Tuscaloosa County, and civil matters arising there are
typically tried in the Circuit Court serving Tuscaloosa County. Federal
claims proceed in Alabama's federal forums: the United States District Court
for the Northern District of Alabama, the United States District Court for
the Middle District of Alabama, and the United States District Court for the
Southern District of Alabama. Which district controls a given Northport
matter is confirmed during the conflict check and intake, along with any
venue-specific registration or qualification requirement. Reports follow the
disclosure format the assigned court expects, and testimony is available for
deposition and trial in either the state or the federal setting.

## Northport's place in the metro labor market

As one of the larger communities in the Tuscaloosa metropolitan area,
Northport gives a vocational analysis a genuine employment base to work
with rather than a bedroom community's thin listings. Opinions about what a
Northport evaluee can earn rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational
employment and wage estimates for the Tuscaloosa metropolitan area and for
Alabama statewide, on state workforce agency labor market information, and
on direct labor market research where the referral questions warrant it.
The analysis is qualitative and sourced, never a recitation of national
averages, and the same method applies whether the report supports a claim
of diminished earning capacity or rebuts one. Broader context on the
statewide market appears on the [Alabama locations page](/locations/alabama/).

## Evaluating a Northport claimant

There is no staffed Purinton Analytics office in Northport, and the firm
publishes no local street address, because candor about presence belongs in
the same category as candor about opinions. Evaluations are conducted by
appointment: remotely by secure video where the referral questions permit,
or in person in the evaluee's own community when a hands-on vocational or
care-planning assessment is required. In-person work and testimony dates
are scheduled with travel from the Kansas City home office. The
[Tuscaloosa metro page](/locations/alabama/tuscaloosa/) describes how the
firm covers the surrounding area, and the
[nationwide practice overview](/locations/nationwide/) explains the
remote-first model that keeps a Northport engagement on the same footing as
one across the street from the home office.

## Starting an engagement

The starting point is always the same: submit the caption or party names
through the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and note any imminent
disclosure, deposition, or trial dates. Once the conflict check clears,
counsel receives a defined scope, a records request tailored to the claims,
and a schedule that works backward from the court's deadlines rather than
forward from the firm's convenience.
