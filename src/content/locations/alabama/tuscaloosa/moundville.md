Moundville belongs to the Tuscaloosa metropolitan area but not to its
principal county: the town sits in Hale County, and that distinction is
where a Moundville engagement properly starts. Venue shapes everything
downstream of it, from the disclosure rules the report must satisfy to the
jury pool that will eventually weigh the testimony. Purinton Analytics
provides [vocational expert](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) and
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) services for Hale
County litigation, along with
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. Referrals are welcome from plaintiff and defense
counsel alike.

## Venue in Hale County and the federal forums

State-court civil matters arising in Moundville are generally heard in the
Circuit Court serving Hale County. Where a claim belongs in federal court,
Alabama's district courts are the available forums: the United States
District Court for the Northern District of Alabama, the United States
District Court for the Middle District of Alabama, and the United States
District Court for the Southern District of Alabama. The firm does not
guess at venue; the controlling district for a specific matter, together
with any registration or qualification requirement particular to it, is
identified during the conflict check and intake. Whatever the forum, the
written opinion is prepared to the same standard: a documented conflict
check, a defined scope, and a records request tailored to the claims.

## Reading the labor market from Moundville

A small Hale County town does not generate its own labor statistics, and a
credible vocational opinion does not pretend otherwise. For a Moundville
evaluee, the relevant market is the wider region the metro area defines,
and findings rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment
and wage estimates for the Tuscaloosa metropolitan area and for Alabama
statewide, on state workforce agency labor market information, and on
direct labor market research when the case requires employer-level detail.
The same sources support work on either side: establishing what an injured
worker has lost, or testing whether a claimed loss survives contact with
the jobs actually available. The
[Tuscaloosa metro page](/locations/alabama/tuscaloosa/) describes the
regional market frame in more depth.

## Evaluation logistics

The firm maintains no office in Moundville and lists no street address for
this location, because accuracy about presence is part of the discipline
that governs its reports. What counsel gets instead is a by-appointment
practice: secure video evaluation where the referral questions allow it,
in-person assessment in the evaluee's own community when the vocational or
care-planning questions demand it, and deposition or trial appearances
scheduled with travel from the Kansas City home office. The
[Alabama locations page](/locations/alabama/) and the
[nationwide practice overview](/locations/nationwide/) explain how that
model serves rural and small-town venues without diluting the work.

## Beginning a Moundville engagement

Start with the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/): case caption or
party names, the referral questions in brief, and any dates already fixed
for disclosure, deposition, or trial. Clearance comes back promptly, and
with it a proposed scope and schedule keyed to the court's calendar. From
that point the engagement proceeds the way it would anywhere else in the
practice, with opinions grounded in records and stated only as far as the
evidence carries them.
