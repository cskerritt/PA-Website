Baldwin County is the Daphne-Fairhope-Foley, AL Metropolitan Statistical
Area, which makes its venue picture unusually simple: the county is the
metro, and the Circuit Court of Baldwin County is where its state-court
matters are heard. Purinton Analytics works with Baldwin County counsel on
either side of the caption, providing
[vocational expert evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) of
earning capacity and employability, [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/)
in catastrophic injury matters, and
[expert testimony and litigation consulting](/services/expert-testimony-litigation-consulting/)
through disclosure, deposition, and trial.

## Baldwin County venues, state and federal

Beyond the Circuit Court of Baldwin County, the firm accepts Alabama
matters in federal court, including the United States District Court for the
Southern District of Alabama, the United States District Court for the
Middle District of Alabama, and the United States District Court for the
Northern District of Alabama. The written opinion is prepared the same way
regardless of forum, with a documented conflict check, a defined scope, and
a records request tailored to the claims, so that the record underlying the
opinion is available for both sides to examine. Any venue-specific
registration or qualification requirement is identified and confirmed
during the conflict check and intake process.

## Serving Daphne, Fairhope, and Foley by appointment

Meetings in the Daphne, Fairhope, and Foley communities are by appointment.
The firm has no staffed office in Baldwin County and publishes no street
address for this location, and it says so directly because honesty about
presence is part of the discipline counsel are retaining. Engagements
ordinarily begin remotely: records analysis, structured interviews, and
standardized testing under publisher protocols where remote administration
is supported. When the referral questions call for evaluating someone in
person, or when deposition and trial dates require attendance, travel is
scheduled from the Kansas City home office around the deadlines counsel
identify at intake, so testing windows, report delivery, and testimony
preparation are sequenced to the court's calendar rather than the firm's
convenience.

## Labor market evidence for Baldwin County evaluees

Vocational opinions for evaluees in the metro are grounded in Bureau of
Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates from the OEWS
program covering the Daphne-Fairhope-Foley area and Alabama statewide,
together with state workforce agency labor market information and direct
labor market research where warranted. Life care plans and
[medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/) price care in
the evaluee's own market using provider-specific inquiries and published fee
data. When economic loss calculations are also needed, the firm offers
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.
