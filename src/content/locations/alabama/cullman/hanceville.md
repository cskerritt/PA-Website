## What the data supports for a Hanceville evaluee

A town is not a labor market, and the analysis does not pretend otherwise. Vocational findings for a Hanceville evaluee rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Cullman area and for Alabama statewide, on state workforce agency labor market information, and on direct labor market research when the referral questions justify the added step.
