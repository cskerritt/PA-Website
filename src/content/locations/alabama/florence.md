Litigation in the Florence-Muscle Shoals metropolitan area draws its parties,
witnesses, and jury pools from Colbert County and Lauderdale County, and
counsel on either side of the caption bring the firm the same core questions:
what an injured or displaced person can still do for work, what that work
pays, and what future care will cost. Purinton Analytics answers them through
[earning capacity and employability evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/). Where a full
damages picture is needed, the firm offers
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Courts serving Colbert and Lauderdale counties

Engagements are accepted for matters venued in the Circuit Court of Colbert
County and the Circuit Court of Lauderdale County, and in Alabama's federal
trial courts, including the United States District Court for the Northern
District of Alabama, the United States District Court for the Middle District
of Alabama, and the United States District Court for the Southern District of
Alabama. The opinion standard does not change with the forum: every
engagement begins with a documented conflict check, a defined scope, and a
records request tailored to the claims, so that the written opinion rests on
a record both sides can examine. Any venue-specific registration or
qualification requirement is identified and confirmed during the conflict
check and intake process.

## Meetings in Florence by appointment

Florence meetings are by appointment. The firm keeps no staffed office in the
metro and publishes no street address for this location, because accuracy
about presence is part of the same discipline that governs its reports. Most
engagements begin remotely, with records review, structured interviews, and
standardized testing under publisher protocols where remote administration is
supported. When the referral questions call for in-person evaluation, or when
deposition and trial dates require presence, travel is scheduled from the
Kansas City home office and planned around the court's calendar. Counsel
receive the same report format and the same point of contact whether the work
is performed remotely or on site.

## Labor market research for Florence-Muscle Shoals evaluees

Vocational findings for evaluees in the metro rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the
Florence-Muscle Shoals area and for Alabama statewide, supplemented by state
workforce agency labor market information and direct employer research where
the referral questions warrant it. Life care plans and medical cost
projections are priced to the evaluee's own market rather than to national
averages, and every source is cited in the report so that opposing counsel
can trace each input back to its origin. Opinions prepared for the other side
receive the same scrutiny through
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) work.
