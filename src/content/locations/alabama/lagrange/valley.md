## A labor market that crosses the state line

Because the metropolitan area around LaGrange is defined by commuting ties that run between Georgia and Alabama, a Valley evaluee's employability cannot be assessed responsibly from a single state's data. Vocational findings rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the LaGrange metropolitan area and for Alabama statewide, supplemented by state workforce agency labor market information and by direct labor market research when the referral questions warrant it. The report describes the market qualitatively and candidly, including where published estimates run out and fieldwork has to fill the gap, because an opinion that overstates its sources invites the cross-examination it should have anticipated.
