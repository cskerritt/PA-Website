## The Sylacauga labor market, analyzed honestly

Sylacauga is a small city, and a vocational opinion that treats it like a
major metropolitan job market will not survive review. Findings for a
Sylacauga evaluee rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational
employment and wage estimates for the relevant Alabama area and for the
state as a whole, supplemented by state workforce agency labor market
information and, where the referral questions warrant it, direct employer
research. Transferable skills are assessed against work that actually
exists within the evaluee's realistic reach, and every source is cited in
the report so that the other side's expert can retrace the analysis step
by step. An opinion either survives on its record or it should not be
offered.
