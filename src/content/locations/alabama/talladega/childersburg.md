## Courthouse and county for Childersburg claims

Childersburg is a Talladega County town, and its state court cases are
ordinarily tried in the Circuit Court of Talladega County. On the federal
side, Alabama litigants may find themselves in the United States District
Court for the Northern District of Alabama, the United States District
Court for the Middle District of Alabama, or the United States District
Court for the Southern District of Alabama; the controlling venue for any
particular Childersburg matter, and any qualification requirement that
venue carries, is identified during the conflict check and intake.
Scheduling orders differ from forum to forum, which is precisely why the
intake conversation fixes the operative dates before the scope of work is

## A small town's labor market, measured regionally

Childersburg is a small town, and honest vocational analysis there means
measuring an evaluee's prospects across the wider region they can actually
reach for work. Those findings are grounded in Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates for the relevant area and for
Alabama as a whole, in state workforce agency labor market information,
and in targeted employer research when the referral questions require more
granularity than published data provides. The report never asserts a labor
market conclusion it cannot document, because in a deadline-driven case
there is no time to repair an opinion that fails scrutiny.
