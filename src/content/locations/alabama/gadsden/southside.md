An evaluee who lives in Southside should not have to leave their own
community to be evaluated. That practical commitment is where Purinton
Analytics starts every Southside engagement: the injured person is met where
they live, the file is worked remotely wherever remote work is sound, and
counsel on both sides receive an opinion built on evidence rather than
convenience. From that starting point the firm delivers
[vocational expert witness](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) testimony,
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/),
[medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/), and
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) for matters
arising in Southside and throughout the surrounding
[Gadsden, AL metro](/locations/alabama/gadsden/). When the damages model
also needs an economic component, the firm coordinates
[damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) in which
independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis while
the vocational and care-planning work continues under the same retention.

## Etowah County courts and beyond

Southside sits in Etowah County, and state court matters from the town are
generally venued in the Circuit Court of Etowah County. Federal matters may
proceed in any of Alabama's district courts as the facts dictate: the United
States District Court for the Northern District of Alabama, the United
States District Court for the Middle District of Alabama, or the United
States District Court for the Southern District of Alabama. Which forum
controls a particular case, and any venue-specific registration or
qualification requirement that follows from it, is identified and confirmed
during the conflict check and intake process rather than assumed. Counsel
working across the state will find the broader venue landscape on the
[Alabama locations page](/locations/alabama/).

## Measuring work in the Gadsden market

A residual earning capacity opinion for a Southside evaluee is only as
strong as the labor market data beneath it. The firm grounds its findings in
Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for
the Gadsden metropolitan area, where Gadsden itself is the principal city,
and for Alabama statewide, alongside state workforce agency labor market
information. Where a case turns on whether specific jobs truly exist for a
person with specific restrictions, direct labor market research supplements
the published estimates, and every source appears in the report's citations
so that the analysis can be tested rather than taken on faith.

## Records, interview, and report

The sequence is deliberate. Records are requested against a defined scope,
reviewed, and summarized. The vocational diagnostic interview is conducted
by secure video or, when the referral questions require it, in person in the
evaluee's own community. Findings are then written into a report that states
its methods, its data, and its reasoning, because the firm assumes the
reader is opposing counsel. There is no staffed Southside office and no
published local address; by-appointment honesty is part of the product.
Travel, when needed for evaluations or testimony, is scheduled from the
Kansas City home office, and the same model serves referring counsel
[across the country](/locations/nationwide/).

## Starting a Southside engagement

Whether the referral comes from the plaintiff's side or the defense, the
path in is identical: [request a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) with the
case caption and parties, and flag any disclosure or trial deadlines already
on the calendar. A written scope and a tailored records request follow, and
the engagement proceeds on a schedule counsel can rely on.
