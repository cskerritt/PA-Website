Rainbow City is one of the larger suburban communities in the
[Gadsden, AL metropolitan area](/locations/alabama/gadsden/), and that
position shapes how a damages question there should be analyzed: an injured
worker who lives in Rainbow City competes for work across the whole metro,
so an opinion confined to the town's own boundaries would understate the
market and invite cross-examination. Purinton Analytics writes for that
wider frame, providing counsel on either side of the caption with
[vocational expert witness](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) opinions,
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/),
[medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/), and
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/). Where a
present-value figure is also required, the firm arranges
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under a single
engagement structure.

## Venue for Rainbow City matters

State court cases arising in Rainbow City are generally heard in the Circuit
Court of Etowah County, and reports prepared for that docket follow the same
methodology the firm applies in federal litigation. On the federal side,
Alabama offers the United States District Court for the Northern District of
Alabama, the United States District Court for the Middle District of
Alabama, and the United States District Court for the Southern District of
Alabama; the district that controls a given Rainbow City case, along with
any venue-specific registration or qualification requirement, is identified
and confirmed during the conflict check and intake process. Statewide
context for counsel appears on the [Alabama page](/locations/alabama/).

## A suburb read against the metro market

Because Rainbow City functions as part of a commuting market centered on
Gadsden, vocational findings for its residents rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Gadsden
metropolitan area and for Alabama statewide, supplemented by state workforce
agency labor market information and by direct labor market research when a
referral question turns on what actual employers require. Transferable
skills are matched to occupations that exist in this market rather than to a
national average, and each source is cited so that opposing counsel can
trace every conclusion back to the data behind it.

## How a Rainbow City evaluation proceeds

Most of the work happens at a distance by design: records review, vocational
diagnostic interviewing by secure video, transferable skills analysis, and
report drafting do not require anyone to travel. When the referral questions
call for an in-person evaluation, the firm meets the evaluee in or near
Rainbow City rather than asking an injured person to come to the expert.
Meetings with counsel are by appointment; the firm keeps no staffed office
in Etowah County and publishes no local street address, because accuracy
about presence is part of the same discipline that governs its reports.
Travel for evaluations, depositions, and trial testimony is scheduled from
the Kansas City home office, and the practice serves counsel
[nationwide](/locations/nationwide/) on the same terms.

## Begin with a conflict check

Engagements are accepted from plaintiff and defense counsel alike, and the
first step is always the same: submit the caption or party names through the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and note any imminent disclosure,
deposition, or trial dates. Once conflicts clear, the firm defines the scope
in writing and issues a records request tailored to the claims, so the
Rainbow City engagement starts on a record both sides can examine.
