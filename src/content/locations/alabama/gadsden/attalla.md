does not guess at which district governs an Attalla filing; the controlling
forum, together with any venue-specific registration or qualification

## Attalla work in a metro context

A small town does not have a labor market of its own, and pretending
otherwise is how vocational opinions get excluded. Attalla belongs to the
[Gadsden, AL metropolitan area](/locations/alabama/gadsden/), so earning
capacity findings for Attalla evaluees are anchored in Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for that metro and for
Alabama statewide, with state workforce agency labor market information
filling in the picture. When a referral question demands to know what real
employers in the region actually hire for, direct labor market research is
added, and the report cites each source so the route from data to conclusion
stays visible to both sides.

## Evaluation logistics for Attalla evaluees

The firm's model separates what needs presence from what does not. Records
analysis, transferable skills work, and report writing are performed
remotely; the vocational diagnostic interview runs over secure video or, if
the questions warrant it, in person in Attalla or nearby, so the evaluee is
seen in the community where they actually live and work. Counsel meetings
are by appointment only. There is no staffed local office and no published
street address for this location, a deliberate accuracy that mirrors the
discipline of the reports themselves. When depositions or trial dates
require the expert in the courtroom, travel is arranged from the Kansas City
home office, the same base from which the firm serves counsel
[nationwide](/locations/nationwide/).
