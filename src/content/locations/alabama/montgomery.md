Purinton Analytics serves Montgomery counsel as part of a remote-first
national practice, which means most engagements from the Montgomery, AL
Metropolitan Statistical Area never require the expert to be local at all.
Records analysis, structured interviews, and standardized testing under
publisher protocols proceed remotely where remote administration is
supported, and the deliverable is the same whether the evaluee sits in
Montgomery or anywhere else the firm works: a cited, source-transparent
report supporting [vocational expert testimony](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), or
[forensic economic damages](/services/forensic-economic-damages/) performed
and signed by independent economist partners whom the firm coordinates.
Plaintiff and defense referrals are welcome on the same terms.

## Counties and courts of the Montgomery metro

The metro takes in Montgomery County, Autauga County, Elmore County, and
Lowndes County, and the firm accepts matters venued in the Circuit Court of
each. Federal engagements are accepted across Alabama, including the United
States District Court for the Middle District of Alabama, the United States
District Court for the Northern District of Alabama, and the United States
District Court for the Southern District of Alabama. State or federal, the
opinion rests on the same documented record, and any venue-specific
registration or qualification requirement is identified and confirmed
during the conflict check and intake process rather than assumed.

## What by-appointment means in Montgomery

There is no staffed Purinton Analytics office in Montgomery, and no street
address is published for this location. Meetings, evaluations, and testimony
happen by appointment. When the referral questions call for in-person
evaluation of an evaluee in the metro, or when deposition and trial dates
require presence, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office and
planned around the dates counsel identify at intake. The firm treats this
candor about presence as part of its report discipline: an expert who is
precise about offices is easier to trust about methodology.

## Labor market and cost research for Montgomery evaluees

Vocational opinions for evaluees in the metro rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates from the OEWS program
covering the Montgomery area and Alabama statewide, along with state
workforce agency labor market information and direct labor market research
where warranted. Life care plans and
[medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/) are priced to
the evaluee's own market through provider-specific inquiries and published
fee data, never to an undifferentiated national average. In matters that
pair earning capacity questions with economic loss calculations, the firm
offers [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Opening an engagement from Montgomery

Statewide context lives on the [Alabama practice page](/locations/alabama/),
and the firm's [national coverage page](/locations/nationwide/) describes
the standards that apply in every venue. To begin a Montgomery matter,
[request a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) with the case caption and
