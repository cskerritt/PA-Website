## Appointment-based presence, stated plainly

The firm keeps no staffed office in Tuscaloosa and publishes no street
address for this location; meetings are by appointment. This is stated
plainly because accuracy about presence is part of the same discipline that
governs the reports themselves. When in-person evaluation is warranted, or
when deposition and trial dates require attendance, travel is scheduled from
the Kansas City home office. Counsel are asked to flag disclosure deadlines
and trial settings at intake so that travel, testing windows, and report
delivery can be sequenced around the case calendar rather than retrofitted
to it.

Vocational findings for evaluees in the metro rest on Bureau of Labor

calculations, the firm offers
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)

analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.
