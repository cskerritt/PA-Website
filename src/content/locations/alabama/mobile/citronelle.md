Measuring earning capacity for a resident of a small community like
Citronelle raises a methodological question that larger cities never pose:
the published data are not compiled at the town level, so the expert must
say, openly and defensibly, which labor market the evaluee actually
occupies. Purinton Analytics answers that question with sources rather than
assertion. Citronelle sits within the Mobile, AL metropolitan area, and the
firm's [vocational evaluations](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) locate
a Citronelle evaluee within the employment that area and the state actually
offer, documenting each step so that counsel on the opposite side of the
caption can retrace it. Retentions are accepted from plaintiff and defense
counsel under identical methodology.

## Sourcing a small-community labor market opinion

Vocational findings for Citronelle evaluees are grounded in Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates from the OEWS program
for the Mobile area and for Alabama statewide, layered with state workforce
agency labor market information and, where the referral questions warrant
it, direct contact with employers in the market the evaluee can reach. The
report states what the data cover, what they do not, and how the gap was
bridged, because an opinion that hides its inference is an opinion that
fails in deposition. [Rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/)
engagements apply the same scrutiny to opposing experts who treat a
small-town evaluee as if the metro's full wage structure were automatically
available.

## Mobile County venue, state and federal

Citronelle's state court matters proceed in the Circuit Court of Mobile
County. In federal practice, Alabama's district courts are the available
forums: the United States District Court for the Middle District of Alabama,
the United States District Court for the Southern District of Alabama, and
the United States District Court for the Northern District of Alabama. The
venue controlling a specific Citronelle case, together with any
venue-specific registration or qualification requirement, is identified and
confirmed during the conflict check and intake process. The methodology and
the report standard remain constant across forums; only the calendar and
the logistics adjust.

## Serving an evaluee where the evaluee lives

There is no Purinton Analytics office in Citronelle, and none is claimed;
the firm publishes no street address for this location because accuracy
about presence is inseparable from accuracy in its reports. For a Citronelle
evaluee, that honesty translates into practical logistics: records review,
interviews, and standardized testing are handled remotely where publisher
protocols allow, and when an in-person evaluation is warranted, it is
conducted in the evaluee's own community on a visit scheduled from the
Kansas City home office. [Life care plans](/services/life-care-planning/)
and [medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/) price
care to the providers realistically available to the household. When
economic loss opinions are needed as well, the firm arranges
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.
