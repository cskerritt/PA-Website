Cases with Prichard parties are, for venue purposes, Mobile County cases, and
that is where the engagement analysis starts. Purinton Analytics accepts
retentions from counsel on either side of the caption in injury, employment,
and disability matters involving Prichard residents and employers, providing
[vocational evaluation of earning capacity](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[rebuttal review of opposing expert reports](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/)
under the same written standard the firm applies nationally. The report is
drafted on the assumption that opposing counsel will read every line of it,
which is exactly how a credible expert report should be written.

## The courts that hear Prichard matters

State court claims arising in Prichard proceed in the Circuit Court of Mobile
County, and the firm's reports and testimony are prepared to that court's
disclosure practice as counsel describe it at intake. In federal litigation,
Alabama offers its district courts as available forums: the United States
District Court for the Southern District of Alabama, the United States
District Court for the Middle District of Alabama, and the United States
District Court for the Northern District of Alabama. Which forum controls a
given Prichard case is a question for counsel, confirmed during the conflict
check and intake process along with any venue-specific registration or
qualification requirement. Forum choice changes deadlines and logistics; it
does not change the methodology or the citation standard of the written
opinion.

## Prichard within the Mobile labor market

Prichard is a small city inside the Mobile, AL metropolitan area, and a
vocational opinion about a Prichard evaluee has to reflect the labor market
the evaluee can actually reach rather than the town boundary. Findings rest
on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates from
the OEWS program for the Mobile area and for Alabama statewide, supplemented
by state workforce agency labor market information and direct employer
contact when the referral questions warrant it. Transferable skills analysis,
placeability, and wage-earning capacity are documented source by source, so
that the opinion can be tested in deposition rather than taken on faith.

## Evaluating an evaluee who lives in Prichard

The firm keeps no staffed office in Prichard or anywhere else in the Mobile
area, and it publishes no street address for this location; candor about
presence is part of the same discipline that governs the reports. Records
review, structured interviews, and standardized testing proceed remotely
where publisher protocols support remote administration, which keeps costs
proportionate and schedules predictable. When the referral calls for
in-person evaluation, or when deposition and trial dates require presence,
travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office around the deadlines
counsel identify. [Medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/)
and life care plans are priced to the evaluee's own community using
provider-specific inquiries and published fee data, and when counsel also
need economic loss calculations, the firm offers
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.
