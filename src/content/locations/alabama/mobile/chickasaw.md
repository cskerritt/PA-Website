Expert work in a Chickasaw case is governed less by geography than by the
scheduling order, and Purinton Analytics takes the dates counsel must meet
as the starting point of the engagement. Disclosure deadlines, deposition windows,
and trial settings are collected at intake, and the sequence of the work,
from records request to signed report, is planned against them from the

alike: whichever side of a Chickasaw matter makes the referral, the
[written opinion](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) is complete,
sourced, and served when the order says it must be, not explained afterward.

## The disclosure calendar and the work plan

A vocational evaluation or [life care plan](/services/life-care-planning/)
has stages that cannot be compressed honestly: conflict check, defined
scope, records collection, evaluation under published protocols, analysis,
and a report in which every source is cited. When a Chickasaw referral
arrives close to a deadline, the firm says so candidly and scopes what can
be done well in the time that remains, including
[rebuttal and peer review work](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) responding
to an opposing expert's disclosure. Counsel get a realistic schedule at
intake rather than an optimistic promise on the eve of the deadline.

## Where Chickasaw cases are heard

Chickasaw is a Mobile County municipality, and its state court litigation
proceeds in the Circuit Court of Mobile County, whose practice on expert
disclosure frames most local scheduling orders. Federal matters may proceed
in Alabama's district courts as available forums, namely the United States
District Court for the Southern District of Alabama, the United States
District Court for the Northern District of Alabama, and the United States
District Court for the Middle District of Alabama; the controlling venue for
any particular case, and any venue-specific registration or qualification
requirement, is identified and confirmed during the conflict check and
intake process. Deadlines differ by forum, which is precisely why the firm
anchors its work plan to the operative order rather than to habit.

## The labor market behind the deadlines

Speed never substitutes for sourcing. Vocational findings for a Chickasaw
evaluee, a resident of a small town inside the Mobile, AL metropolitan area,
rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage
estimates from the OEWS program for the Mobile area and for Alabama
statewide, supported by state workforce agency labor market information and
direct labor market research where the questions warrant it. Care costs in a
[medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/) are priced to
the evaluee's own community. When economic loss must be quantified alongside
the vocational and care-planning opinions, the firm offers
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure, keeping the whole expert package on the same calendar.
