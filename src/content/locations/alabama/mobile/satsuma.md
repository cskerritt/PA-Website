For an injured person living in Satsuma, the practical question counsel
raise at the outset is where the evaluation will happen. Purinton Analytics answers it the
same way for every small town it serves: the work comes to the evaluee.
Records review, structured vocational interviews, and standardized testing
proceed remotely wherever the test publishers' protocols support remote
administration, so a Satsuma evaluee is not asked to organize an expert
evaluation around long travel. When the referral questions require in-person
assessment, that visit is scheduled from the Kansas City home office and
conducted in the evaluee's own community. Plaintiff and defense counsel
receive identical treatment, because the
[evaluation methodology](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) does not know
which side retained it.

## What remote-first means in practice

The firm maintains no staffed office in Satsuma and publishes no street
address for this location; it says so plainly because honesty about presence
belongs to the same discipline that produces its reports. What counsel
engage is a national practice with documented protocols: a conflict check
before substance, a defined scope, a records request tailored to the claims,
and a written opinion in which every source is cited. Remote administration
keeps the schedule inside the case calendar and the cost proportionate to a
smaller matter, without discounting the standard of the work.

## County and courthouse for Satsuma parties

Satsuma lies in Mobile County, and its state court matters are heard in the
Circuit Court of Mobile County. For federal claims, Alabama's district
courts serve as the available forums: the United States District Court for
the Middle District of Alabama, the United States District Court for the
Northern District of Alabama, and the United States District Court for the
Southern District of Alabama. Counsel's choice of forum, along with any
venue-specific registration or qualification requirement, is identified and
confirmed during the conflict check and intake process, and disclosure
practice in the controlling court shapes the working schedule from the very
start of the engagement.

## Placing a Satsuma evaluee in the labor market

A small town does not have its own published wage survey, so the analysis
reaches to the data that exist and says so openly. Vocational findings for
Satsuma evaluees draw on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment
and wage estimates from the OEWS program for the Mobile area and for Alabama
statewide, together with state workforce agency labor market information and
direct employer research where warranted. [Life care plans](/services/life-care-planning/)
and [medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/) are
priced to the providers a Satsuma resident would realistically use, through
provider-specific inquiries and published fee data. Where counsel need the
economic loss calculated as well, the firm arranges
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.
