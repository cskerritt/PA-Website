For a Tuskegee evaluee, the practical question is often not who will write
the report but where the evaluation will actually happen. Purinton
Analytics answers that question plainly: the evaluation comes to the
evaluee. Interviews and testing are conducted remotely where the referral
questions allow it, and in the evaluee's own community when they do not, so
that a Tuskegee resident is never asked to organize an injured life around
an expert's office hours in another city. That evaluee-first posture
applies equally to
[vocational evaluation and expert testimony](/services/vocational-expert-witness/)
and to [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), whichever side
of the caption makes the referral.

## Macon County and the courts that hear its cases

Tuskegee sits in Macon County, which gives the town a venue identity of
its own within the wider Auburn-Opelika area. State-court
matters proceed in the Circuit Court serving Macon County, and the report
is written from the outset to withstand cross-examination there. In
federal litigation, the available forums are the United States District
Court for the Northern District of Alabama, the United States District
Court for the Middle District of Alabama, and the United States District
Court for the Southern District of Alabama; the district that controls a
particular Tuskegee matter, like any registration or qualification
requirement particular to the venue, is confirmed during the conflict check
and intake. Statewide venue context appears on the
[Alabama page](/locations/alabama/).

## Reading a labor market beyond the metro core

An honest earning-capacity analysis for a Tuskegee worker cannot simply
borrow conclusions built for Auburn, the metro's principal city. The firm
grounds its opinions in Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment
and wage estimates for the Auburn-Opelika metropolitan area and for
Alabama statewide, then tests those estimates against state workforce
agency labor market information and, where warranted, direct employer
contact concerning the kinds of work realistically open to the evaluee.
The [metro overview](/locations/alabama/auburn/) describes the regional
market as a whole; the Tuskegee report addresses what that market means
for a specific person with specific restrictions.

## How the engagement runs

The firm maintains no office in Tuskegee and claims none. Every
engagement is administered from the Kansas City home office, from which
the [nationwide practice](/locations/nationwide/) also operates, and
travel for in-person evaluation, deposition, or trial is scheduled from
there. Records move through a defined request list agreed at intake, and
counsel on either side receives the same disciplined product: a written
opinion resting on a record that opposing counsel can examine line by
line. Where the damages picture requires economic modeling, the firm
offers
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under the
same engagement structure.

## Requesting a conflict check

Every Tuskegee referral begins with the conflict check. Send the
case caption or the party names through the
[referral form](/refer-a-case/), identify the claims and the evaluee's
county, and flag any disclosure, deposition, or trial dates already on the
calendar. Counsel receives a prompt answer on conflicts, followed by a
proposed scope and schedule sized to the case rather than to a template.
