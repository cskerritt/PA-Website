Opelika is written into the name of its own metropolitan area, and the
litigation that arises there tends to stay close to home: Lee County juries,
a local Circuit Court docket, and disclosure deadlines that do not wait for
an expert who treats the market as an afterthought. Purinton Analytics
serves Opelika counsel on either side of the caption with
[vocational expert](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) work,
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under a
unified engagement structure.

## The Circuit Court and the federal forums

State-court matters for Opelika parties are heard in the Circuit Court
serving Lee County, and the firm prepares its reports for that venue the
same way it prepares them anywhere in [Alabama](/locations/alabama/): a
documented conflict check, a defined scope, and a records request tailored
to the claims. For federal matters, Alabama's district courts, the United
States District Court for the Northern District of Alabama, the United
States District Court for the Middle District of Alabama, and the United
States District Court for the Southern District of Alabama, are the
available forums; which district controls a given Opelika case is confirmed
during the conflict check and intake rather than assumed. Any
venue-specific registration or qualification requirement is identified in
that same intake conversation.

## A labor market shared with Auburn

Opelika sits inside a compact regional economy it shares with Auburn, the
metro's principal city, and an earning-capacity opinion written for an
Opelika plaintiff or defendant has to reflect that shared market rather
than an abstraction. Vocational findings rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates for the Auburn-Opelika
metropolitan area and for Alabama statewide, supplemented by state
workforce agency labor market information and direct labor market research
where the referral questions warrant it. The analysis names its sources so
that opposing counsel can trace every figure in the report to a published
estimate or a documented inquiry, and the broader metro context is laid out
on the [Auburn-Opelika metro page](/locations/alabama/auburn/).

## Evaluations in Opelika, by appointment

The firm keeps no staffed office in Opelika and publishes no local street
address, because accuracy about presence is part of the same discipline
that governs its reports. Record review, counsel conferences, and many
evaluations proceed remotely. When the referral questions call for
in-person evaluation, the evaluation is scheduled in the evaluee's own
community, and travel is arranged from the Kansas City home office, from which the
[national practice](/locations/nationwide/) is administered. Deposition and trial appearances are calendared the same
way, with dates confirmed at intake so that the disclosure schedule drives
the work plan rather than the reverse.
