Cases arising in Alabaster answer first to Shelby County. The town is among the larger municipalities on the Shelby County side of the Birmingham metropolitan area, and counsel litigating there need an expert whose opinions are built for that venue: a Circuit Court bench and juries drawn from the county, with the possibility that a removal or federal claim moves the fight into a different courthouse entirely. Purinton Analytics prepares [vocational expert](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) opinions and [life care plans](/services/life-care-planning/) for Alabaster matters on that footing, retained for plaintiffs in some matters and for defendants in others, as part of its wider [Birmingham metro coverage](/locations/alabama/birmingham/).

## From Shelby County's Circuit Court to the federal districts

State-court litigation involving Alabaster parties typically proceeds in the Circuit Court serving Shelby County. Federal matters may be heard in the United States District Court for the Northern District of Alabama, the United States District Court for the Middle District of Alabama, or the United States District Court for the Southern District of Alabama; rather than guessing which district a given claim belongs to, the firm identifies the controlling venue during the conflict check and intake and confirms any venue-specific qualification requirement at the same time. Whatever the forum, the report is written so that both the retaining and the opposing side can trace each conclusion to a documented source.

## A Shelby County address, a metro-wide market

An Alabaster evaluee's employment prospects are not confined to Shelby County, and the analysis does not pretend they are. Vocational findings rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Birmingham metropolitan area and for Alabama statewide, on state workforce agency labor market information, and on direct labor market research when the referral questions call for it. The result is a transferable-skills and earning-capacity analysis that reflects the commuting market the evaluee actually participates in. When the case needs the loss reduced to present value, [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) bring in independent economist partners who perform and sign the economic analysis under the same engagement structure.

## Seeing the evaluee without a storefront

The firm keeps no office in Alabaster and lists no street address for it; that plain statement is deliberate, because a practice that will not exaggerate its footprint is less likely to exaggerate its findings. Interviews and standardized testing are usually completed remotely. Where in-person evaluation is warranted, it is conducted in the evaluee's own community, and expert travel, including deposition and trial appearances, is scheduled from the Kansas City home office around the court's calendar.
