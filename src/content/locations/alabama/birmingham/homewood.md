Every scheduling order in a Homewood case quietly fixes the expert's calendar before the expert is ever retained. The disclosure date sets the report date, and the report date sets everything upstream of it: records requests, evaluation windows, and the drafting time an opinion needs if it is to survive a motion to exclude. Purinton Analytics therefore builds Homewood engagements backward from those dates as a matter of policy, offering [vocational expert](/services/vocational-expert-witness/), [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and [testimony support](/services/expert-testimony-litigation-consulting/) to attorneys representing plaintiffs and attorneys defending against them, within its broader [Birmingham metro practice](/locations/alabama/birmingham/).

## Where Homewood cases are heard

Homewood is a Jefferson County community, and its civil disputes are most often filed in the Circuit Court serving Jefferson County. Alabama's federal forums, the United States District Court for the Northern District of Alabama, the United States District Court for the Middle District of Alabama, and the United States District Court for the Southern District of Alabama, remain available where federal jurisdiction exists; the venue that actually controls, along with any registration or qualification requirement it imposes, is pinned down during the conflict check and intake. The methodology does not change with the courthouse, which is precisely why the reports travel well between them.

## Calibrating opinions to the Birmingham labor market

A Homewood evaluee competes for work across the metropolitan area, so the earning-capacity analysis is anchored in Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Birmingham metropolitan area and for Alabama statewide, augmented by state workforce agency labor market information and targeted labor market research where the assignment requires it. Sources are cited, assumptions are stated, and the narrative is written so opposing counsel can test it line by line. If the damages theory requires economic modeling, the firm arranges [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) under which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning components proceed in parallel.

## Meeting the evaluee, not maintaining a storefront

Purinton Analytics has no Homewood office and does not print a local address, a candor policy that mirrors the standards applied to its findings. Evaluations ordinarily proceed by remote interview and standardized testing. When the questions presented require an in-person assessment, it is scheduled in the evaluee's own community, and the evaluator travels from the Kansas City home office. Depositions and trial testimony are calendared the same way, with availability confirmed against the scheduling order before the engagement is accepted.

## Getting ahead of the deadline

Because the scheduling order is the immovable object in any expert engagement, counsel are encouraged to [request a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) as early as the caption is known, listing the parties and the operative disclosure, deposition, and trial dates. Once conflicts clear, scope and delivery dates are confirmed in writing, and the records request follows immediately so that no part of the schedule waits on the expert. Counsel weighing venue questions elsewhere in the state can consult the [Alabama page](/locations/alabama/), and the [nationwide practice description](/locations/nationwide/) covers engagements outside it.
