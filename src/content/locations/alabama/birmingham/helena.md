Where an evaluee lives and where a case gets tried are different questions, and for a smaller suburban community like Helena the gap between them is routine: the person is here, but the accident, the employer, or the defendant may be anywhere in the region. Purinton Analytics is built for that gap. The evaluation follows the person, the report follows the claim, and the [vocational expert](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) and [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) work is accepted from counsel prosecuting claims and counsel defending them, as part of the firm's [Birmingham metropolitan coverage](/locations/alabama/birmingham/).

## Helena's county and the courts in play

Helena is in Shelby County, and matters filed in state court there ordinarily proceed in the Circuit Court serving Shelby County. Federal claims involving Helena parties may be venued in the United States District Court for the Northern District of Alabama, the United States District Court for the Middle District of Alabama, or the United States District Court for the Southern District of Alabama. The firm does not guess which forum applies; the controlling court, and any qualification or registration requirement particular to it, is confirmed as part of the conflict check and intake before scope is set.

## The labor market behind the opinion

Helena residents work throughout the Birmingham metropolitan area, so earning-capacity and employability findings are grounded in Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for that metropolitan area and for Alabama statewide, together with state workforce agency labor market information and direct labor market research where warranted. The report distinguishes carefully between what the data establish and what remains judgment, because that distinction is where cross-examination lives. If the damages model calls for economic valuation, the firm arranges [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning opinions proceed under the same engagement.

## Serving a Helena evaluee without a local office

The firm will state directly what some practices obscure: it has no office in Helena, no storefront anywhere in the metro, and no street address to publish for this page. What it offers instead is a process that does not need one. Records are requested and reviewed remotely; the structured interview and standardized testing that anchor a vocational evaluation are typically completed from the evaluee's home; and when the referral questions require an in-person assessment, the appointment is set in the evaluee's own community. Travel for evaluations, depositions, and trial testimony is scheduled from the Kansas City home office against the case calendar.
