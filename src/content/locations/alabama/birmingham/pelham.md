An earning-capacity opinion is, at bottom, a forecast: a statement about jobs not yet sought, wages not yet offered, and a medical recovery not yet complete. Forecasts are easy to demand and hard to defend, and the difference lies entirely in method. For Pelham matters, Purinton Analytics builds each opinion the way a careful forecaster would, from documented inputs, stated assumptions, and reasoning that either party can rerun for itself. Plaintiff firms and defense firms retain the practice on identical terms for [vocational expert](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) opinions, [life care plans](/services/life-care-planning/), and critiques of projections already served, all within its [Birmingham metro practice](/locations/alabama/birmingham/).

## The courtroom that will judge the forecast

A litigation forecast answers to a specific bench. Pelham belongs to Shelby County, so a civil dispute rooted in the town ordinarily proceeds in the Circuit Court serving Shelby County. Claims with a federal basis may instead be heard in the United States District Court for the Northern District of Alabama, the United States District Court for the Middle District of Alabama, or the United States District Court for the Southern District of Alabama. Before any projection is drafted, the firm settles with counsel which forum controls, and what registration or qualification requirements attach there, as part of the documented conflict check and intake that opens every file.

## Inputs before conclusions

Credible forecasting starts from published baselines rather than impressions of the town. Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates exist at the level that matters for a Pelham evaluee, the Birmingham metropolitan area, and again for Alabama statewide; both anchor the employability and earning-capacity findings. State workforce agency labor market information sharpens the occupational detail. When a referral question turns on evidence no published table holds, such as how a particular class of employer actually hires, direct labor market research fills the gap. The finished report then draws a visible line between what the data establish and what remains professional judgment, because a forecast that buries its assumptions is the first casualty of cross-examination. Economic valuation is a separate discipline and stays in separate hands: through [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/), independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under a single engagement structure.

## Meeting the person being forecast

No responsible forecast of a working life skips the worker. Each Pelham evaluee completes a structured interview, with standardized testing where the referral questions require it, usually by secure remote session from home. If the questions call for sitting with the person, the appointment is held in the evaluee's own community. Purinton Analytics keeps no office in Pelham and publishes no street address for this page, a fact stated plainly because candor about presence and candor about projections are the same habit.
