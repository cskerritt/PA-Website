## What the labor market data can and cannot say

For a Trussville evaluee, the honest unit of analysis is the metropolitan labor market. Findings draw on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Birmingham metropolitan area and for Alabama statewide, on state workforce agency labor market information, and on direct labor market research where the referral questions justify it. Just as important is what the report refuses to do: it does not stretch the data past what it supports, and it flags the limits of each source, because an opinion that overstates its evidence serves neither side. Where economic loss calculations are needed, [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) place that work with independent economist partners, who perform and sign the economic analysis within a single engagement structure.
