Circuit Court of Baldwin County. Federal filings in Alabama may be heard in
the United States District Court for the Southern District of Alabama, the
United States District Court for the Middle District of Alabama, or the
United States District Court for the Northern District of Alabama; which of
these controls a given case, together with any registration or
qualification requirement particular to the venue, is identified during the
conflict check and intake. The firm's approach to Alabama venues generally

## Reading the labor market around Fairhope

Employability conclusions for a Fairhope resident are grounded in the labor
market the resident can actually reach, which spans the metropolitan area
rather than any single town. The firm relies on Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates for the metropolitan area and for
Alabama as a whole, on state workforce agency labor market information, and
on direct employer research when a referral question calls for it. Findings
are stated qualitatively, matched to the evaluee's residual functional
capacity, education, and vocational history, and cited so that opposing
counsel can trace every source.
