## Testing labor market opinions about Robertsdale workers

A rebuttal assignment often turns on whether the opposing expert used the
right labor market. For a Robertsdale evaluee, the relevant market is the
metropolitan area the town belongs to, and the proper foundations are
Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for
that metropolitan area and for Alabama statewide, state workforce agency
labor market information, and direct labor market research where the
questions demand it. The firm holds opposing reports, and its own, to that
standard: occupations matched to documented residual capacity, market
conditions described qualitatively, and no figure or finding left without a
source.
