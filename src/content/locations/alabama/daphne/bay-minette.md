## State court, federal court, and the intake question

Alongside the Circuit Court of Baldwin County, Bay Minette litigation can
reach the federal bench. Alabama offers the United States District Court
for the Southern District of Alabama, the United States District Court for
the Northern District of Alabama, and the United States District Court for
the Middle District of Alabama as its federal forums; rather than assert
from a distance which district a given filing belongs to, the firm confirms
the controlling venue, and any registration or qualification requirement it
imposes, during the conflict check and intake. Every opinion, in every
forum, stands on documented conflict clearance, a defined scope, and a
records request fitted to the claims. Alabama-wide coverage is described on

## The labor market a Bay Minette worker actually faces

## Appointments, records, and travel

Bay Minette engagements are handled by appointment; the firm operates no
staffed office in the city and prints no street address for it, a plain
statement of fact offered in the same spirit as every other statement in
its reports. The working pattern is remote-first: electronic records
exchange, secure video interviews where appropriate, and written
deliverables that do not depend on geography. In-person evaluation of a Bay
Minette evaluee, and appearances for deposition or trial, are scheduled

## Beginning the engagement

The gateway for a Bay Minette matter is identical to the gateway for the
firm's [nationwide practice](/locations/nationwide/): a
[conflict check request](/refer-a-case/) carrying the caption or party
names and any dates already fixed for disclosure, deposition, or trial.
Clearance comes first, then a written scope, then the records request, in

reaches the Circuit Court of Baldwin County rests on a record assembled
deliberately rather than reconstructed at the deadline.
