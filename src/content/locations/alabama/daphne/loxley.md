A small town produces the same catastrophic injuries, the same contested
employability questions, and the same need for disciplined expert opinions
as a major city; what it rarely has is a resident expert. Purinton
Analytics closes that gap for Loxley counsel with a remote-first practice
that delivers [vocational expert evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[forensic economic damages](/services/forensic-economic-damages/) work, the
economic analyses performed and signed by independent economist partners
coordinated by the firm, to plaintiff and defense lawyers alike. Loxley
belongs to the service area described on the
[Daphne metro page](/locations/alabama/daphne/).

## Venue picture for a Loxley matter

Loxley's state-court matters proceed in the Circuit Court of Baldwin
County. If a case is in, or headed to, federal court, Alabama's forums are
the United States District Court for the Northern District of Alabama, the
United States District Court for the Southern District of Alabama, and the
United States District Court for the Middle District of Alabama; the
conflict check and intake process determines which district controls the
matter at hand and surfaces any venue-specific registration or
qualification requirement. Forum never changes the method: documented
conflict clearance, a defined scope, and a claims-tailored records request
come before any opinion is written. The firm's full Alabama footprint is
described on the [Alabama page](/locations/alabama/).

## Measuring opportunity beyond the town line

An honest employability analysis for a Loxley evaluee cannot stop at the
edge of a small town's economy; it must reach the metropolitan labor market
Loxley residents actually work in. The firm builds that analysis on Bureau
of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the
metropolitan area and for Alabama statewide, on state workforce agency
labor market information, and on direct labor market research when a
referral question warrants the extra step. Conclusions are qualitative,
matched to the evaluee's residual capacity, education, and work history,
and documented so either side of the caption can retrace them.

## Serving Loxley without pretending to be there

The firm keeps no office in Loxley, staffs no storefront, and publishes no
street address for the town, and it discloses that as readily as it
discloses its methods. What Loxley counsel gets instead is a practice
engineered for distance: records that move electronically, interviews by
secure video when appropriate, evaluation in the evaluee's own community
when the referral requires presence, and deposition or trial appearances
supported by travel from the Kansas City home office. The identical
machinery serves the firm's [nationwide practice](/locations/nationwide/),
so a Loxley case receives the same handling as one from a major metro,
including access to
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
that keep vocational, care-planning, and economic workstreams under a
single engagement structure.

## The conflict check comes first

Every Loxley engagement starts with clearance. Send the caption or party
names through the [referral form](/refer-a-case/), identify any disclosure,
deposition, or trial dates already set, and the firm will respond after the
conflict check with a written scope and fee terms, before substantive work
begins and regardless of which side of the caption is calling. Early
referral gives the records request time to run and keeps the evaluation
ahead of the disclosure calendar.
