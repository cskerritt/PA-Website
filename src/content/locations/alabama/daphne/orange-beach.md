report Purinton Analytics writes for an Orange Beach matter is drafted on
the premise that opposing counsel will hunt for that assumption. Sources are

[vocational expert evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), or

governs a particular Orange Beach case, and any registration or

## Grounding the labor market analysis

For an Orange Beach evaluee, employability and earning capacity findings
are tied to Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage
estimates covering the metropolitan area and Alabama statewide, to state
workforce agency labor market information, and to direct labor market
research when the referral questions justify it. The opinion considers the
occupations reachable from the evaluee's community across the metro area,
weighs them against documented residual capacity and vocational history,
and characterizes the market qualitatively, because a defensible report
never dresses estimates up as certainties.

## Conducting the evaluation

Orange Beach meetings are by appointment. There is no staffed local office
and no street address to publish, and the firm treats that disclosure as
part of its credibility rather than a drawback: the same insistence on
literal accuracy runs through every page of a report. In practice, most of
the engagement is remote, with records exchanged electronically and
interviews held by secure video when suitable; in-person evaluation of an
Orange Beach resident, or attendance at deposition or trial, is served by
travel from the Kansas City home office. When a case needs economic loss
modeling, the firm arranges
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure, an arrangement available throughout the
[nationwide practice](/locations/nationwide/).

## Requesting a conflict check

Scrutiny-ready opinions take time to build, so the invitation is simple:
[submit a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) with the caption or party names
as early as the case allows, noting any disclosure, deposition, or trial
settings. Once conflicts clear, counsel receives a written scope, and the
work begins with the records request rather than with conclusions, which is
the order an opinion must follow if it is going to survive the assumption
hunt.
