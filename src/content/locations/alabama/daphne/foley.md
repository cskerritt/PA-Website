## Venue context for Foley cases

State-court matters arising in Foley are heard in the Circuit Court of
Baldwin County. When a claim proceeds federally, the United States District
Court for the Southern District of Alabama, the United States District Court
for the Middle District of Alabama, and the United States District Court for
the Northern District of Alabama are the forums the state offers, and the
controlling venue for a particular matter is confirmed during the conflict
check and intake, along with any venue-specific registration or
qualification requirement. The report itself does not change with the forum:
a documented conflict check, a defined scope, and a records request tailored
to the claims precede every written opinion. Statewide engagements are
described on the [Alabama page](/locations/alabama/).

## Foley within the metro labor market

A Foley evaluee's realistic employment options are not confined to the city
limits; they extend across the wider metropolitan area, and the analysis
reflects that. Vocational findings rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates for the metropolitan area and for
Alabama statewide, supplemented by state workforce agency labor market
information and by direct labor market research where the referral questions
warrant it. Transferable skills analysis considers the work actually
available to the evaluee given residual capacity, education, and work
history, described qualitatively and tied to sources an opposing expert can
check.

## Evaluation logistics in Foley

Meetings are by appointment. The firm maintains no staffed office in Foley
and publishes no street address here, because accuracy about presence is
part of the same discipline that governs the reports. Most engagements
proceed remotely from records review through report delivery, with
interviews conducted by secure video where appropriate. When the referral
calls for in-person evaluation of a Foley resident, or when a deposition or
trial date requires presence, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home
office. The same standards apply throughout the firm's
[nationwide practice](/locations/nationwide/), so counsel whose dockets
reach beyond Baldwin County work within a familiar structure. Where the
damages case requires economic modeling, the firm offers
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Starting a Foley engagement

Early retention protects the quality of the record: it leaves room for a
complete records request, an unhurried evaluation, and a report that is
ready before disclosure rather than assembled against a deadline. To begin,
[request a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) with the case caption and party
names, and note any imminent disclosure, deposition, or trial settings so
scheduling can be confirmed alongside clearance. Counsel receive a defined
scope and fee structure before any substantive work starts, whichever side
of the caption they represent.
