## A commuter's labor market, measured properly

Because Spanish Fort workers participate in the metro economy rather than a
purely local one, vocational findings are anchored to Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the metropolitan
area and for Alabama statewide, augmented by state workforce agency labor
market information and, where warranted, direct contact with employers in
the relevant occupations. The transferable skills analysis maps the
evaluee's residual capacity, training, and work history onto occupations
genuinely present in that commuting market, and expresses the results
qualitatively, without manufactured precision.
