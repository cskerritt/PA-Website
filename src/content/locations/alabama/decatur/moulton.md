## Reading Moulton through the metro's employment data

For earning capacity purposes, the relevant market for a Moulton evaluee is
regional. The analysis draws on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational
employment and wage estimates for the Decatur metropolitan area and for

where the referral questions call for it, on direct labor market research
into whether the occupations identified in the transferable skills analysis
are realistically open to a worker living in Moulton. The finished report
then shows its work. The path from medical restrictions through transferable
skills to a wage conclusion is laid out in sequence, with the underlying
data identified at each step, and cross-examination becomes a review of the
record rather than a contest of impressions.
