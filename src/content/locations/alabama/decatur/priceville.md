Priceville is a small suburb whose working residents largely earn their
livings elsewhere in the Decatur metro area, and that commuting pattern is the
right starting point for any opinion about what an injured Priceville resident
can still earn. Purinton Analytics measures
[earning capacity and employability](/services/vocational-expert-witness/)
against the regional market a Priceville evaluee actually participates in,
not against the job openings inside town limits, and it offers that analysis
to plaintiff and defense counsel alike in injury, employment, and family law
litigation.

## Where a Priceville case is heard

Priceville lies in Morgan County, so state court matters involving Priceville
parties typically proceed in the Circuit Court of Morgan County. On the
federal side, Alabama's available forums are the United States District Court
for the Northern District of Alabama, the United States District Court for
the Middle District of Alabama, and the United States District Court for the
Southern District of Alabama; which district governs a given case is settled
as part of the conflict check and intake rather than assumed from geography.
Counsel with matters spread across the region will find the firm's
[Decatur metro page](/locations/alabama/decatur/) and its statewide
[Alabama page](/locations/alabama/) useful companions to this one.

## Testing capacity against the commuting market

Because a suburb this size supplies workers to the wider metro, the labor
market analysis for a Priceville evaluee draws on Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates for the Decatur metropolitan area
and for Alabama statewide, alongside state workforce agency labor market
information. Where the referral questions justify it, the firm conducts direct
labor market research with regional employers to confirm that the occupations
identified in the transferable skills analysis are actually hiring at the
wages the published estimates suggest. The result is an opinion opposing
counsel can probe line by line, because every occupational and wage assertion
traces to a cited source.

## Evaluation and care planning close to home

The firm maintains no office in Priceville and publishes no local street
address, a fact stated plainly because overstating presence would undercut
the credibility the reports depend on. What counsel gets instead is a
remote-first process: records analysis, interviews, and standardized testing
administered under publisher protocols, with in-person evaluation arranged in
the evaluee's own community when the assessment requires it. Travel for
evaluations, depositions, and trial testimony is scheduled from the Kansas
City home office. For catastrophic injuries,
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) prices future care to the
evaluee's own market, and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
add independent economist partners who perform and sign the economic analysis
while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement
structure.
