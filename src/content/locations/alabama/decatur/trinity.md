An injured person in a small town like Trinity should not have to organize
their recovery around an expert's travel preferences. Purinton Analytics
structures its evaluations around the evaluee: records work and interviews
happen remotely wherever the methodology supports it, and when a hands-on
assessment is needed, the assessment comes to Trinity. That evaluee-centered
design serves both sides of the caption equally, whether counsel needs an
[earning capacity opinion](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) for a
plaintiff or an independent review of one offered against a defendant.

## Where Trinity cases are heard

Trinity is a Morgan County town, and its state court litigation is generally
venued in the Circuit Court of Morgan County. Federal claims may proceed in
any of Alabama's district courts, which are the United States District Court
for the Northern District of Alabama, the United States District Court for
the Middle District of Alabama, and the United States District Court for the
Southern District of Alabama; the district with authority over a specific
Trinity matter is pinned down during the conflict check and intake, never
presumed. Counsel working a docket that spans the region can consult the
[Decatur metro page](/locations/alabama/decatur/) for the wider market and
the [Alabama state page](/locations/alabama/) for statewide coverage.

## Trinity's place in the regional economy

A town of Trinity's modest size does not constitute its own labor market, so
the vocational analysis situates a Trinity evaluee within the Decatur
metropolitan area's employment base. The data foundation is the Bureau of
Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Decatur
metropolitan area and for Alabama statewide, read together with state
workforce agency labor market information and, when the referral requires
it, direct labor market research. Qualitative context matters here as much
as data: the opinion must reflect what work is reachable from Trinity in
practice, and the report documents that reasoning so opposing counsel can
test it rather than guess at it.

## Remote by default, present when it counts

There is no staffed Purinton Analytics location in Trinity or anywhere else
in the metro; every office in the practice outside the Kansas City home
office is by appointment only, and the firm says so rather than implying a
storefront that does not exist. In practice this means an engagement moves
quickly: conflict check, scope, records request, then remote analysis. When
a deposition, trial date, or in-person evaluation requires presence, travel
is arranged from the Kansas City home office. Catastrophic injury cases can
add [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) priced to the
evaluee's own care market, and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work runs under one
engagement structure.

## Opening an engagement

Availability for a Trinity matter is confirmed through a
[conflict check](/refer-a-case/): send the caption or party names, the
venue, and any dates already set for disclosure, deposition, or trial. The
firm replies with a defined scope and a records request tailored to the
claims, the same opening it uses everywhere. How that consistency works at
scale is described on the [nationwide practice page](/locations/nationwide/),
which covers the full geography the Trinity engagement would join.
