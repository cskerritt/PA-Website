## A small city inside the Decatur labor market

For earning capacity purposes, Hartselle is analyzed as part of the Decatur
metropolitan labor market rather than as an isolated town. Vocational findings
for Hartselle evaluees rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational
employment and wage estimates for the Decatur metropolitan area and for
Alabama statewide, supplemented by state workforce agency labor market
information and direct employer research where the referral questions warrant
it. That framing matters in both directions: a plaintiff's residual capacity
is tested against jobs that actually exist within a reasonable commute of
Hartselle, and a defense critique of a claimed loss is grounded in the same
verifiable data rather than in impression.
