## The Auburn-Opelika venue footprint

The metro comprises Lee County and Macon County, and engagements are
accepted for matters venued in the Circuit Court of either county. In
federal litigation the firm accepts Alabama matters in the United States
District Court for the Middle District of Alabama, the United States
District Court for the Northern District of Alabama, and the United States
District Court for the Southern District of Alabama. Every opinion is
prepared to the same standard regardless of venue, beginning with a
documented conflict check, a defined scope, and a records request tailored
to the claims, so the record behind the report is open for either side to
examine. Any venue-specific registration or qualification requirement is
identified and confirmed during the conflict check and intake process.

## Appointment-based service in Auburn and Opelika

Purinton Analytics keeps no staffed office in Auburn or Opelika and
publishes no street address for this location. Meetings are by appointment,
and most engagements are conducted remotely from start to finish: records
review, structured interviews, and standardized testing under publisher
protocols where remote administration is supported. When the referral
questions call for in-person evaluation, or when deposition or trial
requires attendance, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office
and sequenced around the deadlines counsel identify at intake. The firm
regards this candor about presence as part of the same discipline that
governs its written work.

## Labor market research for metro evaluees

Vocational findings for evaluees in the Auburn-Opelika area rest on Bureau
of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates from the
OEWS program for the metro and for Alabama statewide, supplemented by state
workforce agency labor market information and direct labor market research
where the referral questions warrant it, such as when transferable skills
point toward occupations that published estimates describe thinly. Life
care plans and
[medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/) are priced in
the evaluee's own market using provider-specific inquiries and published
fee data, with every source cited in the report so that each figure can be
traced to its origin during cross-examination.
