## How the firm appears in Dothan

There is no staffed Purinton Analytics office in Dothan, and the firm
publishes no street address for this location; meetings are by appointment
only. That candor is deliberate, because a practice built on documented,
verifiable opinions should describe its own footprint with the same
precision. The working model is remote-first: records are reviewed,
interviews conducted, and standardized instruments administered under
publisher protocols that support remote administration. Where an evaluation
is better performed in person, or where a deposition or trial requires
attendance, travel is arranged from the Kansas City home office and
coordinated with counsel's schedule well before the date arrives.

## Grounding opinions in the Dothan labor market

Employability and earning capacity opinions for Dothan area evaluees are
grounded in Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage
estimates for the Dothan metropolitan area and for Alabama statewide, along
with state workforce agency labor market information and targeted employer
research where the referral questions call for it. Care-related costs in
life care plans and medical cost projections are researched in the evaluee's
own market, not imported from national tables, and each source is cited so
the opposing expert can check the work. The same standards are applied in
reverse when counsel retain the firm to review an opposing report.
