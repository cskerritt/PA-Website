Every Huntsville engagement is written to be read by opposing counsel. That
is the working assumption behind each report Purinton Analytics produces for
matters in the Huntsville, AL Metropolitan Statistical Area, whether the
firm is retained by the plaintiff or the defense: a documented conflict
check, a defined scope, a records request tailored to the claims, and a
written opinion that rests on a record both sides can examine. The services
counsel most often request here are
[vocational expert evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) of
earning capacity and employability, [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/)
in catastrophic injury matters, and
[expert testimony support](/services/expert-testimony-litigation-consulting/)
through deposition and trial.

## Courts serving Madison County and Limestone County

The Huntsville metro comprises Madison County and Limestone County, and the
firm accepts matters venued in the Circuit Court of either county. Federal
matters are accepted throughout the state, in the United States District
Court for the Northern District of Alabama, the United States District Court
for the Middle District of Alabama, and the United States District Court for
the Southern District of Alabama. The report standard does not vary with the
forum: the same disclosure discipline and the same documented methodology
support testimony in state and federal court, and any venue-specific
registration or qualification requirement is identified and confirmed during
the conflict check and intake process.

## How by-appointment service works here

There is no staffed Purinton Analytics office in Huntsville, and the firm
publishes no street address for this location. Saying so plainly matters,
because honesty about presence is part of the credibility that a testifying
expert brings to the stand. What counsel get instead is a remote-first
engagement structure: records analysis, structured interviews, and
standardized testing conducted under publisher protocols where remote
administration is supported, with in-person evaluation and courtroom
attendance scheduled from the Kansas City home office when the case requires
presence. Scheduling is coordinated around disclosure deadlines and trial
settings identified at intake.

## Labor market data for the Huntsville area

Vocational opinions for evaluees in the metro are grounded in Bureau of
Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates from the OEWS
program covering the Huntsville area and Alabama statewide, together with
state workforce agency labor market information and direct labor market
research where the referral questions warrant it. Care-related costs in a
[life care plan or medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/)
are researched in the evaluee's own market. Where economic loss calculations
are also needed, [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
are available, in which independent economist partners perform and sign the
economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under
one engagement structure, so counsel manage a coordinated retention rather than
separately supervised experts.
