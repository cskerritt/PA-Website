disclosure deadline arrives before the vocational question has been framed,
a mediation date needs a damages picture that does not yet exist, or a
report served by the other side demands a rebuttal on short time. Purinton
Analytics structures its Wetumpka engagements around those pressures from
the outset: scope is defined against the scheduling order, records are
requested immediately after clearance, and drafts move on dates counsel can
hold. The full practice is available here, from

[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) of opposing

## Where Wetumpka cases are heard

Wetumpka is in Elmore County, and its state-court civil matters are
ordinarily filed in the Circuit Court of Elmore County. Alabama's federal
trial forums are the United States District Court for the Northern District
of Alabama, the United States District Court for the Middle District of
Alabama, and the United States District Court for the Southern District of
Alabama; which of them controls a given case is a venue question the firm
confirms during the conflict check and intake rather than asserting in
advance. The reporting standard does not bend to the forum. Every opinion
is written to survive examination by the lawyer who did not retain the
firm.

## Wetumpka workers in the metro economy

Earning-capacity analysis for a Wetumpka evaluee looks outward to the
Montgomery metropolitan labor market, because that is where the realistic
employment alternatives lie. The firm grounds its findings in Bureau of
Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the
Montgomery metro area and for Alabama statewide, supplemented by state
workforce agency labor market information and direct labor market research
when the referral questions justify it. No figures are borrowed from
national averages when regional data answers the question, and the report
explains why the chosen data source fits the evaluee's actual commuting
range.

## Evaluation without a local storefront

The firm has no office in Wetumpka and does not print a local address,
because overstating presence would sit poorly beside reports that claim
precision. Interviews and testing are conducted remotely where the
methodology permits, which frequently serves rural and small-city evaluees
best; when the case requires an in-person evaluation or courtroom
attendance, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office against the
same calendar the engagement was scoped to. Catastrophic injury matters can
add [coordinated damages assessment](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/),
where independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis
while vocational and care-planning work continues under the same engagement.
Statewide practice details appear on the [Alabama page](/locations/alabama/),
and the firm's approach travels nationally, as described on the
[nationwide page](/locations/nationwide/).

## Submitting a conflict check

Plaintiff and defense counsel start at the same door:
[submit a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) with the caption or party names,
and state the disclosure, deposition, and trial dates that govern the case.
Clearance, scope, and a records request follow promptly, so the calendar
that prompted the call becomes the calendar the work is built around.
