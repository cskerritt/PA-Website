## Elmore County venue context

Millbrook lies in Elmore County, and state-court matters involving Millbrook
parties are generally filed in the Circuit Court of Elmore County. On the
federal side, Alabama's district courts are the United States District Court
for the Northern District of Alabama, the United States District Court for
the Middle District of Alabama, and the United States District Court for the
Southern District of Alabama; the controlling federal venue for any
particular matter is identified during the conflict check and intake, never
assumed from geography. Reports are prepared to the same disclosure standard
regardless of forum, and testimony is given where the case sits.

## The labor market a Millbrook worker actually faces

Vocational opinions for Millbrook evaluees are anchored in the Montgomery
regional economy rather than in a single town's job listings. The analysis
draws on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage
estimates covering the Montgomery metropolitan area and Alabama statewide,
along with state workforce agency labor market information and, where the
questions require it, direct contact with employers. The report addresses
what work the evaluee can perform, what that work pays in this regional
market, and how the injury or event at issue changed those answers. Scale is
described honestly: a suburban community's residents compete in the metro
labor market, and the analysis says so rather than pretending the town is
its own economy.

## How a Millbrook evaluation proceeds

The firm keeps no office in Millbrook and publishes no local address.

remotely by secure video, which keeps costs proportionate and schedules

the evaluee's own community, or where deposition and trial calendars demand

[coordinated damages assessment](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/),

Questions that reach beyond the metro, including statewide registration or
qualification requirements, are covered on the
[Alabama page](/locations/alabama/) and resolved at intake, consistent with
the firm's [nationwide practice standards](/locations/nationwide/).
