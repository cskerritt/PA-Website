Every report Purinton Analytics prepares for a Pike Road case is written for
its least sympathetic reader: the lawyer on the other side of the caption,
whose job is to take the opinion apart at deposition. That assumption
disciplines the work from the first records request, because an opinion that
cannot name its sources or defend its method is a liability to whichever
side serves it. For Pike Road counsel, plaintiff and defense alike, the firm
provides [expert testimony and litigation consulting](/services/expert-testimony-litigation-consulting/),
[vocational evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/), and
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) through its
[Montgomery metro practice](/locations/alabama/montgomery/).

## Montgomery County venue, confirmed rather than assumed

Pike Road is in Montgomery County, and state-court civil matters involving
Pike Road parties are generally heard in the Circuit Court of Montgomery
County. Federal claims may proceed in any of Alabama's district courts
depending on the parties and the events: the United States District Court
for the Northern District of Alabama, the United States District Court for
the Middle District of Alabama, or the United States District Court for the
Southern District of Alabama. The firm does not guess at venue; the
controlling forum, along with any registration or qualification requirement
it imposes, is confirmed during the conflict check and intake. What does not
change with the forum is the file: a documented methodology, sources cited
in full, and opinions confined to what the record supports.

## Grounding earning capacity in the metro labor market

Employability and earning-capacity findings for a Pike Road evaluee are
grounded in data for the Montgomery metropolitan area as a whole, because
that is the market in which realistic employment alternatives are found.
The firm relies on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and
wage estimates for the Montgomery metro and for Alabama statewide, together
with state workforce agency labor market information and targeted employer
research where warranted. That framework supports the questions Pike Road
cases actually present: whether a worker can return to past occupations,
what alternative occupations remain within residual capacity, and what the
difference is worth in the market the evaluee genuinely competes in.

## Meeting a Pike Road evaluee

The firm maintains no office in Pike Road and lists no local address;
accuracy about presence is part of the discipline the reports themselves
follow. Most work proceeds remotely, from records review through structured
interview and reporting. When in-person evaluation is appropriate, the
appointment is set in or near the evaluee's community, and travel comes from
the Kansas City home office, coordinated with the litigation calendar. Where
damages require an economist, the firm offers
[coordinated damages assessment](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning components proceed under a
single engagement. Broader Alabama venue questions are addressed on the
[state page](/locations/alabama/), and national coverage is described on the
[nationwide page](/locations/nationwide/).

Whether the referral comes from the plaintiff or the defense side, the
opening step is identical: [request a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) with the
case caption and parties, and identify the operative disclosure, deposition,
and trial dates. The firm replies with clearance status, a proposed scope of
work, and the records request that will anchor the analysis.
