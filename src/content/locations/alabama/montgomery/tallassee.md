Tallassee is a small town, and Purinton Analytics writes that fact into its
reports rather than around it. Honest treatment of scale is a credibility
asset in expert work: an earning-capacity opinion that inflates a small
community's job market does not survive cross-examination, and a report
that names the true unit of analysis earns trust from the reader who was
looking for reasons to doubt it. Tallassee counsel on either side of the

[forensic economic damages coordination](/services/forensic-economic-damages/),

[Montgomery metro practice](/locations/alabama/montgomery/).

## Court context for Tallassee matters

Tallassee lies in Elmore County, so state civil actions involving Tallassee
parties typically proceed in the Circuit Court of Elmore County. Cases with
federal claims may be venued in the United States District Court for the
Northern District of Alabama, the United States District Court for the
Middle District of Alabama, or the United States District Court for the
Southern District of Alabama, and the district that actually governs is
pinned down during the conflict check and intake. Because the firm testifies
where the case is, not where the expert is, the choice of forum changes the
travel plan and nothing else about the work.

## What the labor market analysis covers

For a Tallassee evaluee, the honest unit of analysis is the surrounding
regional economy. Vocational findings rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates for the Montgomery metropolitan
area and for Alabama statewide, alongside state workforce agency labor
market information and, where the questions demand it, direct research with
employers. The report is explicit about what a smaller community means for
job search realities: which occupations are reachable within a reasonable
commuting range, which require relocation or retraining, and how those
constraints bear on the earning-capacity opinion. Qualitative candor about
scale is part of the method, applied the same way whether it favors the
retaining side or cuts against it.

## Reaching a Tallassee evaluee

Purinton Analytics keeps no office in Tallassee and claims none. Records
review, clinical-style vocational interviewing, and standardized testing are
conducted by secure video whenever the methodology allows, so the evaluee is
not asked to travel for routine steps and expert costs stay in proportion to
the case. When an evaluation should happen in person, it is arranged in the
evaluee's own community, with travel from the Kansas City home office;
depositions and trial appearances are scheduled the same way. In
catastrophic matters, care needs and costs can be developed through
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) while independent
economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis within a
coordinated engagement, so counsel manages a unified file rather than a
scatter of experts. Alabama-wide qualification and registration questions
are handled at intake and summarized on the
[state page](/locations/alabama/), and the standards that govern this work
everywhere are described on the [nationwide page](/locations/nationwide/).

## How engagements begin

The door into the practice is the conflict check. Counsel on either side of
the caption can [request a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) by sending the
caption or party names along with any looming disclosure, deposition, or
trial settings. The firm answers with clearance status, a defined scope,
and a records request, and the engagement proceeds on a record both sides
can test.
