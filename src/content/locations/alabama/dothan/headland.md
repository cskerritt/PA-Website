## Reading a small town against a regional market

Headland is a small town, and a defensible vocational opinion says so rather than pretending otherwise. An evaluee who lives there competes for work across the wider regional economy anchored by Dothan, the metro's principal city, and employability and earning capacity are measured against that reachable market, not against the town in isolation and not against a market the evaluee cannot practically enter. Findings rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Dothan metropolitan area and for Alabama statewide, on state workforce agency labor market information, and on direct labor market research when the referral questions warrant the added step. Each source is cited for what it actually shows, because an inference stretched past its data is the first thing a capable cross-examiner finds.
