line. People who live in this small Calhoun County community earn their
livings across the wider Anniston-Oxford area, and an earning capacity
opinion that ignored that commuting reality would understate or overstate
the loss depending on who commissioned it. Purinton Analytics starts from
the market as it actually functions, offering counsel on both sides of

[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and

## Courts serving a Weaver case

Because Weaver is a Calhoun County municipality, state law claims arising
there are ordinarily heard in the Circuit Court of Calhoun County. On the
federal side, Alabama's available district forums are the United States
District Court for the Northern District of Alabama, the United States
District Court for the Middle District of Alabama, and the United States
District Court for the Southern District of Alabama. Which court controls
a given Weaver matter, and what that forum asks of a testifying expert by
way of registration or qualification, is settled during the conflict check
and intake so counsel is never surprised at the disclosure stage.

## Measuring the market a Weaver worker actually faces

The vocational analysis defines the evaluee's market by where employment is
realistically reachable, then documents it with Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates for the Anniston-Oxford
metropolitan area and for Alabama statewide, alongside state workforce
agency labor market information. Where the referral turns on a narrower
question, direct labor market research supplies the answer at the employer
level. Restrictions from the medical record, documented skills and work
history, and access to the surrounding metro all shape which occupations
remain open, and the report shows that reasoning step by step rather than
asserting a conclusion.

## Logistics for a Weaver evaluee

The engagement is structured so that living in a small town imposes no
burden on the evaluee and no unnecessary cost on the file. Records analysis
and structured interviews are handled remotely, standardized testing
follows publisher protocols with remote administration wherever the
instrument permits, and any in-person evaluation is brought to the
evaluee's own community. Depositions and trial testimony are covered
through travel planned from the Kansas City home office. The firm has no
staffed location in Weaver and publishes no address for one; every meeting
is by appointment, a fact stated here as plainly as the findings are stated
in the reports.
