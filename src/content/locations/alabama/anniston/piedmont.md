## Venue for a Piedmont matter

Piedmont lies in Calhoun County, so state court litigation from the town is
typically venued in the Circuit Court of Calhoun County. Federal claims may
proceed in the United States District Court for the Northern District of
Alabama, the United States District Court for the Middle District of
Alabama, or the United States District Court for the Southern District of
Alabama. The controlling venue for any given case,
together with whatever registration or qualification that forum requires of
a testifying expert, is identified and confirmed during the conflict check
and intake process before the engagement is accepted.

## Labor market context for a Piedmont evaluee

Residual earning capacity for someone living in Piedmont cannot be assessed
against the town alone; the relevant market is the broader Anniston-Oxford
area within which Piedmont sits, reached through Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates for that metropolitan area and
for Alabama statewide. State workforce agency labor market information
fills in where federal data are thin, and direct labor market research
answers employer-specific questions when a referral requires that level of
detail. Commuting realities, the evaluee's documented skills, and any
medically supported restrictions all enter the analysis explicitly, with
sources cited so the opinion can be tested rather than taken on faith.

## How the evaluation reaches Piedmont

Records review and vocational interviews proceed remotely as the default,
and standardized instruments are administered under publisher protocols,
remotely where the publisher supports it. When the referral questions call
for in-person assessment, the evaluation is scheduled in the evaluee's own
community, and testimony obligations are met through travel arranged from
the Kansas City home office. There is no Purinton Analytics office in
Piedmont, no published local address, and no claim of one: meetings happen
by appointment, and the firm says so directly because overstating presence
would sit poorly next to reports that depend on precision.
