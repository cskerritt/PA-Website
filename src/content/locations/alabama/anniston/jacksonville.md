## Calhoun County venue and the federal alternatives

Jacksonville sits in Calhoun County, and state court claims from the town
are ordinarily filed in the Circuit Court of Calhoun County. Where a matter
belongs in federal court instead, Alabama's district courts are the United
States District Court for the Northern District of Alabama, the United
States District Court for the Middle District of Alabama, and the United
States District Court for the Southern District of Alabama; the district
that governs a specific Jacksonville filing is identified during the
conflict check and intake rather than assumed, and any registration or
