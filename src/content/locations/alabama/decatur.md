Distance is a logistics question, not a quality question, and the Decatur,
AL Metropolitan Statistical Area illustrates how Purinton Analytics answers
it. The firm's home office is in Kansas City; its Decatur engagements are
handled through a deliberate division of labor between remote work and
scheduled travel. Records review, structured interviews, and standardized
testing under publisher protocols proceed remotely where remote
administration is supported, while in-person evaluation, depositions, and
trial attendance are scheduled travel, planned around the case calendar.
Counsel on either side of the caption retain the same
[vocational expert services](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) available
throughout the national practice.

## Morgan County, Lawrence County, and the courts that serve them

The Decatur metro comprises Morgan County and Lawrence County, and
engagements are accepted for matters venued in the Circuit Court of either
county. Federal matters are accepted across Alabama in the United States
District Court for the Northern District of Alabama, the United States
District Court for the Middle District of Alabama, and the United States
District Court for the Southern District of Alabama. The analysis and the
report standard do not change with the forum; what changes is scheduling,
which is why disclosure deadlines and trial settings are gathered at intake
and treated as fixed points that the work plan is built around.

## No local office, and why that is stated plainly

The firm keeps no staffed office in Decatur and publishes no street address
for this location. Meetings happen by appointment, in person when the case
requires it and remotely when it does not. Purinton Analytics states this
plainly because an expert's credibility rests on precision, and precision
about offices is the cheapest kind to demonstrate: a report that is candid
about where the firm is located is written by the same hand that is candid
about methodology, sources, and the limits of the data.

## Labor market foundations for Decatur evaluees

Vocational opinions for evaluees in the metro rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates from the OEWS program
covering the Decatur area and Alabama statewide, together with state
workforce agency labor market information and direct labor market research
where the referral questions warrant it. Findings are reported with their
sources cited so either side can verify each figure against the underlying
data. Life care plans and
[medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/) research care
costs in the evaluee's own market through provider-specific inquiries and
published fee data. Where economic loss calculations are also required, the
firm offers [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.
