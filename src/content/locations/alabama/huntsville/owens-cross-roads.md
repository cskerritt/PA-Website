## Referring on a fixed disclosure calendar

Every engagement for this small Madison County town starts with the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/), where counsel are asked to note any
imminent disclosure, deposition, or trial dates along with the caption or
party names. Once conflicts clear, the defined scope and fee agreement fix
what will be delivered and when, and the records request goes out tailored
to the claims so that the file is complete before opinions are formed.
Rebuttal timelines get the same treatment: a
[rebuttal or peer review engagement](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) is
scoped against the date the responsive disclosure is due, and
[testimony support](/services/expert-testimony-litigation-consulting/)
carries the opinion through deposition and trial. When the calendar is
tight, refer early; the deadline shapes the schedule, never the standard.
