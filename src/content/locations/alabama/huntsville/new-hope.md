A report does not get an easier cross-examination because the case comes
from a small town. Opinions written for New Hope matters face the same
scrutiny as opinions written for cases in the largest cities the firm
serves, and Purinton Analytics prepares them accordingly: a documented
methodology, sources cited so either side of the caption can check them,
and conclusions that stop where the record stops. That standard governs
[vocational expert work](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) alike, whether
the retention comes from the plaintiff or the defense.

## What the report standard means in practice

Each New Hope engagement opens with a documented conflict check, a defined
scope, and a records request tailored to the claims, so the written opinion
rests on a file both sides can examine. Standardized instruments are
administered under their publishers' protocols, findings are tied to
identifiable data rather than impressions, and the same report supports
[deposition and trial testimony](/services/expert-testimony-litigation-consulting/)
without restatement. When the damages theory also requires economic
modeling, independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis through a
[coordinated damages engagement](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/),
so the vocational and care-planning opinions and the economic loss
calculations proceed under one engagement structure without blurring who
authored what.

## Courts that hear New Hope cases

New Hope is a small Madison County community, and its state court matters
are accepted in the Circuit Court of Madison County. Alabama's federal
trial forums are the United States District Court for the Northern District
of Alabama, the United States District Court for the Middle District of
Alabama, and the United States District Court for the Southern District of
Alabama; federal engagements are accepted statewide, and the venue that
controls a given case, along with any registration or qualification
requirement particular to it, is identified during the conflict check and
intake. Statewide court coverage is described on the
[Alabama page](/locations/alabama/).

## Reading the labor market from New Hope

Vocational conclusions about a New Hope evaluee are anchored in the
Huntsville, AL metropolitan area labor market, the market in which
residents of the town actually compete for work. The firm relies on Bureau
of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates from the
OEWS program for the Huntsville area and for Alabama statewide, on state
workforce agency labor market information, and on direct employer research
when the referral questions justify it; the
[Huntsville metro page](/locations/alabama/huntsville/) details that metro
practice. Care costs in a
[medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/) are priced in
the market where the evaluee will receive treatment, not in an abstract
national one.

## Logistics and how an engagement begins

New Hope is served by appointment. The firm has no staffed presence in the
town, and it does not print a street address for this location, because an
expert's credibility starts with not claiming offices that do not exist.
Interviews, testing, and records analysis proceed remotely where publisher
protocols allow; in-person evaluation happens in the evaluee's own
community when the case calls for it; and travel for deposition or trial is
arranged from the Kansas City home office. Engagement standards that apply
nationally are collected on the [nationwide page](/locations/nationwide/).
To begin, [request a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) with the caption or
party names and note any disclosure, deposition, or trial dates already
set; scope, fee agreement, and the records request follow once conflicts
clear.
