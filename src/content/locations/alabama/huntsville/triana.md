An injured worker in a small town should not have to travel to a distant
office to be evaluated, and the evaluation should lose nothing for staying
close to home. That is the logistical premise behind how Purinton Analytics
serves Triana, a small Madison County community within the Huntsville, AL
metropolitan area. The interview, the testing, and the records work that
support a [vocational evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) or a
[life care plan](/services/life-care-planning/) are structured so that a
Triana evaluee participates from Triana, while the resulting report meets
the standard applied to every engagement in the practice: written to be
read, and challenged, by opposing counsel on either side of the caption.

## Madison County venue for Triana matters

Triana matters in state court are accepted in the Circuit Court of Madison
County. In federal practice, Alabama's forums are the United States
District Court for the Northern District of Alabama, the United States
District Court for the Middle District of Alabama, and the United States
District Court for the Southern District of Alabama; the firm takes federal
engagements statewide, and the venue controlling a particular case,
together with any forum-specific qualification requirement, is confirmed
during the conflict check and intake. The
[Alabama page](/locations/alabama/) sets out the statewide court landscape
in fuller detail.

## The labor market a Triana evaluee faces

Employability is never assessed against a town this size alone. A Triana
resident's realistic job market is the surrounding Huntsville metro, so
vocational findings rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational
employment and wage estimates from the OEWS program for the Huntsville area
and for Alabama statewide, alongside state workforce agency labor market
information and targeted employer research where warranted. The
[Huntsville metro page](/locations/alabama/huntsville/) describes that
market coverage. Care costs in a
[medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/) are researched
where the evaluee will obtain care, and when economic loss calculations are
needed, [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
are available in which independent economist partners perform and sign the
economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning work continues
under one engagement structure.

## How a Triana evaluation actually proceeds

Most components run remotely. Medical, employment, and educational records
are analyzed wherever they originate; structured interviews are conducted
by video or telephone; and standardized instruments are administered under
publisher protocols where remote administration is supported. When the
referral questions require an in-person component, the evaluation is
brought to the evaluee's own community rather than the evaluee to an
office. Purinton Analytics maintains no staffed location in Triana and
lists no street address here, and it says so on this page because counsel
should learn how the firm actually operates from its own materials, not
discover something different at deposition. When deposition or trial
attendance becomes necessary, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home
office around the dates counsel identify.
