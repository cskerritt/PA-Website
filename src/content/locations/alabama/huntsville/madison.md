## How Madison fits the metro labor market

Vocational findings for Madison evaluees rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates from the OEWS program covering
the Huntsville area and Alabama statewide, supplemented by state workforce
agency labor market information and direct employer research where the
referral questions warrant it. Because Madison workers routinely hold jobs
elsewhere in the metro, transferable skills analysis and job placement
feasibility are framed against the wider Huntsville market, not against a
notional town-only economy that no evaluee actually inhabits. When a case
also requires economic loss calculations, the firm offers
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Evaluation logistics for a Madison evaluee

Purinton Analytics keeps no staffed office in Madison and publishes no
street address for this location; accuracy about presence is part of the
same discipline that governs the reports. Most engagement work proceeds
remotely, through records analysis, structured interviews, and standardized
testing administered under publisher protocols that support remote use.
When an in-person evaluation is appropriate, it is conducted in the
evaluee's own community, and when a deposition or trial setting requires
attendance, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office. Costs in
a [life care plan or medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/)
are researched in the evaluee's own market rather than transplanted from a
national average, because the plan has to price the care where the evaluee
will actually receive it.
