Counsel who litigate in Pennsylvania work across a court system with deep
institutional traditions, from the Court of Common Pleas that anchors civil
practice in every county to federal districts that span the commonwealth
from Philadelphia to Pittsburgh. Purinton Analytics serves that landscape as
a vocational expert and life care planning resource for plaintiff and
defense engagements alike, bringing the same documented methodology to an
employment matter in a large urban venue and a personal injury case tried
before a small-county jury.

## How the Firm Serves Pennsylvania

The firm works from a single home office in Kansas City and maintains no
staffed office in Pennsylvania; every meeting in the commonwealth is by
appointment, and no Pennsylvania street address is published, because
candor about presence belongs in the same category as candor in a report.
The engagement model is remote-first: records review, transferable skills
analysis, labor market research, and report drafting proceed without travel,
while evaluee interviews are conducted by secure video conference or in
person in the evaluee's own community. When referral questions call for
in-person evaluation, or when deposition and trial calendars require the
expert in the room, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office.

Vocational findings for Pennsylvania evaluees rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the relevant
Pennsylvania metropolitan areas and for the commonwealth as a whole, along
with state workforce agency labor market information and direct labor
market research where warranted. Where lost earnings or the cost of future
care must be reduced to present economic terms, the firm offers coordinated
damages engagements in which independent economist partners perform and
sign the economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning work
proceeds under one engagement structure.

## Qualification and Disclosure

Expert practice in Pennsylvania differs meaningfully between the Court of
Common Pleas and the federal districts, particularly in the form and timing
of disclosures, so counsel are asked to provide the operative case
management or scheduling order at intake. Any venue-specific registration or
qualification requirement is identified and confirmed during the conflict
check and intake process.

## Starting a Pennsylvania Engagement

Every engagement here begins the way it does everywhere else in the
practice: with a documented conflict check, a defined scope, and a records
request tailored to the claims. Submit the caption or party names through
the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and note any imminent disclosure,
deposition, or trial dates. For matters beyond the commonwealth, the
[nationwide practice page](/locations/nationwide/) outlines the full
geographic reach of the firm's work.
