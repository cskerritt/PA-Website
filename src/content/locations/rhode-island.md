Every report Purinton Analytics writes is prepared on the assumption that
opposing counsel will read it closely, and Rhode Island engagements are no
exception. The firm provides vocational expert and life care planning
services throughout the state and accepts referrals from plaintiff and
defense counsel alike, so the same attorneys may encounter its reports from
either side of the caption over time. Discipline in method, not familiarity
with a courthouse, is what the firm asks to be judged on.

## Rhode Island Venues Served

Engagements are accepted for matters in the Superior Court and in the
United States District Court for the District of Rhode Island. The firm
maintains a dedicated page for the
Providence and Warwick area, which
describes the local engagement logistics in more detail. Wherever in the
state a matter arises, the analysis follows the same sequence: a documented
conflict check, a defined scope, a records request tailored to the claims,
and a written opinion that rests on a record both sides can examine.

## How the Firm Serves Rhode Island

The firm's home office is in Kansas City, and it keeps no staffed office in
Rhode Island; meetings in the state are by appointment, and no Rhode Island
street address is published, because accuracy about offices is part of the
same discipline that governs the firm's reports. In practice, most of the
work never requires travel. Records review, transferable skills analysis,
labor market research, and report drafting are performed remotely, and
evaluee interviews are conducted by secure video conference or scheduled in
person in the evaluee's own community. When the referral questions call for
in-person evaluation, or when deposition and trial dates require presence,
travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office and coordinated with
counsel's calendar.

Vocational findings for Rhode Island evaluees rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the relevant
metropolitan area and for Rhode Island statewide, together with state
workforce agency labor market information and direct labor market research
where the referral questions warrant it. When a matter also requires the
economic projection of lost earnings or future care costs, the firm offers
coordinated damages engagements in which independent economist partners
perform and sign the economic analysis while the vocational and
care-planning work proceeds under one engagement structure.

## Qualification and Disclosure

Disclosure expectations differ between the Superior Court and the federal
district, and individual scheduling orders control the details, so counsel
are asked to share the operative order at intake. Deadlines for reports and
disclosures are calendared before the substantive work begins, not
reconstructed afterward. Any venue-specific registration or qualification
requirement is identified and confirmed during the conflict check and
intake process.

## Starting a Rhode Island Engagement

To begin a Rhode Island engagement,
[request a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) with the case caption and
parties, and note any imminent disclosure, deposition, or trial dates. The
firm responds with a defined scope and a records request rather than a
sales pitch, and the engagement proceeds on the timetable the case demands.
Counsel whose matters extend beyond Rhode Island can consult the
[nationwide practice overview](/locations/nationwide/) for the broader
geographic scope of the firm's work.
