The Columbia metro area spreads litigation across a wide ring of counties,
and Purinton Analytics structures its work so that a case in Saluda County
receives the same vocational and life care planning rigor as one filed in
Richland County. The firm accepts matters venued in the Circuit Court (Court
of Common Pleas) sitting in Richland County, Lexington County, Kershaw
County, Fairfield County, Calhoun County, and Saluda County, and in the
United States District Court for the District of South Carolina. Plaintiff
and defense counsel throughout the metro use the practice for
[earning capacity and employability evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/).

## How engagements from the Columbia metro are scoped

Every engagement begins with a documented conflict check, a defined scope,
and a records request tailored to the claims, so that the written opinion
rests on a record either side of the caption can examine. Where the damages
picture requires economic modeling,
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
are available, in which independent economist partners perform and sign the
economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under
one engagement structure. Counsel whose matters sit outside the metro can
orient from the [South Carolina overview](/locations/south-carolina/), which
describes coverage for the state's other venues under the same intake.

## By-appointment presence, stated plainly

Columbia meetings are by appointment. The firm maintains no staffed office
in the metro and publishes no street address for this location; candor about
where the firm actually sits is part of the same discipline that shapes its
reports. Records review, structured interviews, and standardized testing
under publisher protocols proceed remotely for most referrals. When an
in-person evaluation is warranted, it can be conducted in the evaluee's own
community, and when depositions or trial dates require presence, travel is
scheduled from the Kansas City home office with the litigation calendar, not
office logistics, setting the pace. Counsel work with the same point of
contact throughout, and the report reads the same whether the underlying
work was performed remotely or on site.

## Grounding opinions in the Columbia labor market

Vocational conclusions for metro evaluees rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates for the Columbia area and for
South Carolina statewide, along with state workforce agency labor market
information and direct labor market research where the referral questions
call for it. The analysis asks what work actually exists within the
evaluee's commutable area, at what wage, and under what physical and
cognitive demands, rather than assuming a national profile applies. Life
care plans and cost projections are priced to the evaluee's own market
through provider-specific inquiries and published fee data, so a Kershaw
County plan is not silently priced to another region's costs.

## Beginning a Columbia engagement

The practice serves the metro as part of a national footprint described on
the [nationwide practice](/locations/nationwide/) page, and referrals from
Columbia follow the same sequence as referrals from any other venue. Send
the caption or party names through the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/), flag any disclosure deadlines,
depositions, or trial settings, and identify the referral questions as
precisely as the pleadings allow. Clearance, scope, and scheduling are
confirmed together, and both plaintiff and defense inquiries receive the
same review and the same willingness to decline when the record cannot
support the requested opinion. Early referrals leave the most room for
records collection and evaluation, but the intake works the same when the
calendar is short.
