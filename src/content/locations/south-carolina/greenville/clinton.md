Not every Clinton case needs an affirmative expert; some need a careful
second set of eyes on the expert work already in the file. Purinton
Analytics serves Laurens County counsel in both postures. The firm prepares
affirmative [vocational opinions](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) and
[life care plans](/services/life-care-planning/), and it performs
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) of opposing
reports, testing their methods, sources, and arithmetic against the
standards the discipline publishes. Because the firm works for plaintiffs
and defendants in roughly equal spirit, it critiques opposing work the same
way it expects its own to be critiqued.

## Clinton's courts

Clinton sits in Laurens County, and state civil claims from the town are
heard in the Circuit Court, sitting as the Court of Common Pleas. For
matters within federal jurisdiction, the United States District Court for
the District of South Carolina is the available federal forum; the venue
that governs a particular case, and the expert requirements that come with
it, are established during the conflict check and intake process. Counsel
with matters spread across the region can orient themselves with the
[metro overview](/locations/south-carolina/greenville/) and the
[statewide venue page](/locations/south-carolina/).

## What review work looks like

A rebuttal engagement from Clinton follows the same discipline as an
affirmative one. The opposing report is read against its own cited sources;
occupational and wage claims are checked against the Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the metropolitan
area and for South Carolina; care plan items are compared with the medical
record and with published pricing for the evaluee's market. The product is
a written critique that distinguishes methodological disagreement from
error, which is the distinction a court needs when experts collide.

## Affirmative work for Clinton evaluees

When the firm carries the affirmative burden, a Clinton evaluee is assessed
through the remote-first sequence used across the
[national practice](/locations/nationwide/): tailored records requests,
structured video interviews, and standardized testing under
publisher-supported remote protocols, with in-person evaluation arranged in
the evaluee's own community when the questions require it. The town's
position within a metropolitan commuting economy shapes the employability
analysis, which weighs local opportunity against the realistic reach of the
evaluee's restrictions and transportation. Future care needs are addressed
through [medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/)
priced to the market where the evaluee actually receives care.

## Presence, plainly stated

There is no Purinton Analytics office in Clinton. Meetings are by
appointment, no street address is published for this location, and travel
for evaluations, depositions, and trial is scheduled from the Kansas City
home office. A practice that earns part of its keep checking other experts'
claims cannot afford an unsupported claim of its own, so this listing is
limited to what is true: appointments and travel, not an address.

## Retain or rebut: same first step

Whether the need is an affirmative opinion or a review of someone else's,
the engagement opens with a
[conflict check](/refer-a-case/). Provide the caption or parties, identify
the reports already served if review is the assignment, and note the
deadlines in play. Scope and fee terms follow in writing before the work
begins.
