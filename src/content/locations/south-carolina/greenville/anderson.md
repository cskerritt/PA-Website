Anderson carries its own name in the title of the Greenville-Anderson-Greer
metropolitan area, and it anchors its own county with its own civil docket.
That distinction matters for litigation: an Anderson case is tried before an
Anderson County jury, and damages testimony should be prepared with that
courtroom in mind rather than treated as an afterthought to the metro's
principal city. Purinton Analytics accepts Anderson County engagements from
both sides of the bar, offering
[earning capacity and employability evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) of opposing
expert work.

## The Anderson County docket

Civil actions arising in Anderson proceed in the Circuit Court, sitting as
the Court of Common Pleas, for Anderson County. Where a claim belongs in
federal court, the United States District Court for the District of South
Carolina is the available forum; which venue controls a given case is
confirmed during the conflict check and intake, along with any registration
or qualification requirement particular to it. The firm testifies for
plaintiffs and defendants alike, and every report is written so that the
side that did not retain the firm can still follow its reasoning.

## A county economy inside a metro economy

Anderson is a small city whose workers move between local employers and the
larger regional market centered on Greenville. Vocational analysis for an
Anderson evaluee therefore looks at both layers: the occupations realistically
available near home and those reachable within the commuting range the
evaluee actually has. The data foundation is the Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage survey for the metropolitan area and for
South Carolina, read alongside state workforce agency information and, where
warranted, direct labor market contact with employers. The
[metro page](/locations/south-carolina/greenville/) describes the regional
market in more detail, and the [state page](/locations/south-carolina/)
covers venues beyond it.

## Appointments, not an office

The practice maintains no staffed location in Anderson and does not publish a
street address here, because implying a local office that does not exist
would sit poorly with the accuracy the reports themselves demand. What
counsel get instead is a remote-first workflow: records analysis, video
interviews, and standardized testing under publisher-approved remote
protocols, with in-person evaluation arranged in the evaluee's own community
when the referral requires it. Travel for evaluations, depositions, and trial
testimony is scheduled from the Kansas City home office, and the
[national scope of the practice](/locations/nationwide/) means the same
procedures have been tested in venues far from home.

## Life care planning for Anderson residents

Catastrophic injury cases from Anderson County frequently pair vocational
questions with future care needs. Life care plans and
[medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/) for local
evaluees are priced to the evaluee's own market through provider-specific
inquiries and published fee data, not to a national average that no Anderson
family actually pays. When a full damages picture is needed, coordinated
engagements are available in which independent economist partners perform and
sign the economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning work
proceeds under one engagement structure.
