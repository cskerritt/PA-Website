## Where Simpsonville cases are heard

Simpsonville lies in Greenville County. State civil claims proceed in the
Circuit Court, sitting as the Court of Common Pleas, and federal claims may
be brought in the United States District Court for the District of South
Carolina. Which forum controls, and what expert disclosure obligations attach
in it, is pinned down during the conflict check and intake process rather
than guessed at. The firm's engagement mix here, as everywhere, runs both
directions: retained by plaintiffs in some captions and by defendants in
others, with the same report standard in each.

## Sequencing the opinion against the order

A credible damages opinion has dependencies. Records must arrive before the
interview makes sense; testing precedes the transferable skills analysis;
labor market research comes after the restrictions are understood. The firm
maps those dependencies onto the scheduling order at engagement, so that a
rebuttal deadline or an early mediation date changes the plan before work
begins, not after. When a case needs the full damages chain, coordinated
engagements bring in independent economist partners who perform and sign the
economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under
one engagement structure, keeping even multi-expert timelines on a single
calendar.
