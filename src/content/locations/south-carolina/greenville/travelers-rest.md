Here is what actually happens after a Travelers Rest lawyer sends a case to
Purinton Analytics. The caption goes through a documented conflict check.
A written scope and fee agreement follow. A records request tailored to the
claims goes out, an interview is scheduled, testing is arranged, and a
report is drafted that either side of the caption could hold up to the
light. Walking through that pipeline is the clearest way to explain what
the firm offers this Greenville County town, because the pipeline itself,
not proximity or salesmanship, is the product behind every
[vocational expert](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) and
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) engagement.

## Step one: forum and requirements

Travelers Rest civil matters in state court are heard in the Circuit Court,
sitting as the Court of Common Pleas, for Greenville County. Federal claims
may proceed in the United States District Court for the District of South
Carolina. The intake process confirms which venue controls the specific
case and whether it imposes any registration, qualification, or disclosure
requirement, so the engagement is compliant before any substantive work
begins. Venue context for the surrounding region lives on the
[metro page](/locations/south-carolina/greenville/), and for the rest of
the state on the [South Carolina overview](/locations/south-carolina/).

## Step two: records, interview, testing

The substantive work follows a fixed order. Medical, employment,
educational, and earnings records come first, because an interview without
records invites speculation. The structured interview is typically
conducted by video, and standardized instruments are administered remotely
under the protocols their publishers authorize. If the referral requires
an in-person evaluation, it takes place in the evaluee's own community
near Travelers Rest, with the expert traveling from the Kansas City home
office. There is no staffed office in the town and no street address to
publish; appointments and honesty about that fact are the local presence.

## Step three: the labor market analysis

With restrictions and skills established, the analysis turns to the
metropolitan economy in which a Travelers Rest evaluee actually competes.
The foundation is the Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment
and wage estimates for the metro area and for South Carolina statewide,
read with state workforce agency labor market information and extended by
direct employer research when the referral questions require it. Every
source is named in the report. The same standard governs the firm's
[national work](/locations/nationwide/), which is why its reports look
alike from one venue to the next.

## Step four: the opinion under pressure

The pipeline ends where litigation begins: deposition and trial. Reports
are written in anticipation of cross-examination, opinions are limited to
what the record supports, and appearances are calendared with travel
planned from Kansas City so that court dates hold. Where a case needs
economic modeling, independent economist partners perform and sign the
economic analysis in a coordinated engagement while the vocational and
care-planning work proceeds under one engagement structure.

## Put a case into the pipeline

The entry point is the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/). Send the
caption or the party names, describe the claims briefly, and flag any
imminent dates. Everything after that follows the steps above, in order,
on the record.
