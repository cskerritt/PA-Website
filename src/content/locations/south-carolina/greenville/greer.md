Greer is one of the larger suburban communities in the Greenville-Anderson-Greer
metropolitan area, and the working lives of its residents rarely stop at the
municipal line. People who live in Greer commute across the metro for work, and
people who work in Greer commute in from elsewhere. When an injury, a
termination, or a catastrophic medical event interrupts one of those working
lives, counsel on either side of the caption need an expert who treats the
whole commuting market as the relevant labor market rather than stopping at
the town boundary. Purinton Analytics provides
[vocational expert witness](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) and
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) services for Greer matters
as part of a [national practice](/locations/nationwide/) that applies one
methodology and one report standard everywhere it works.

## Greenville County venue and courts

Greer matters proceed in Greenville County, where civil claims are heard in
the Circuit Court, sitting as the Court of Common Pleas. The firm accepts
plaintiff and defense engagements in that court and in the other trial courts
of the [broader metro](/locations/south-carolina/greenville/). Federal claims
may be filed in the United States District Court for the District of South
Carolina; the controlling venue for any particular case, along with any
qualification or disclosure requirement it carries, is identified and
confirmed during the conflict check and intake process. Statewide context for
counsel practicing beyond the metro appears on the
[South Carolina page](/locations/south-carolina/).

## Earning capacity in a commuter market

Because Greer workers participate in a metro-wide labor market, earning
capacity questions here are framed at the metro level. Vocational findings
rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates
for the metropolitan area and for South Carolina statewide, supplemented by
state workforce agency labor market information and direct employer research
where the referral questions warrant it. The estimates are used qualitatively
and transparently, with sources disclosed in the report, so that opposing
counsel can trace every finding back to the data behind it. No figure appears
in a report that cannot be tied to a published source or a documented inquiry.

## How a Greer evaluee is evaluated

Most engagements begin remotely: a structured records review, a vocational
interview by video, and standardized testing under publisher protocols where
remote administration is supported. When the referral calls for in-person
work, the evaluation is scheduled in the evaluee's own community, at a
location convenient to Greer, with travel arranged from the Kansas City home
office. The firm keeps no staffed office in Greer and publishes no street
address for this location; meetings are by appointment, and that candor about
presence is part of the same discipline that governs the written opinions.
Deposition and trial appearances are scheduled the same way, and counsel work
with a single point of contact from intake through testimony.

Referrals from Greer counsel, and from out-of-state counsel with matters
