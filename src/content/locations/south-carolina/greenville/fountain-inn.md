What is a small town's labor market worth in court? That analytic question
sits at the center of most Fountain Inn referrals. An evaluee from a smaller
community inside a large metropolitan area is neither limited to the jobs
within town limits nor automatically able to reach every job in the region,
and an honest earning capacity opinion has to locate the truth between those
poles. Purinton Analytics approaches Fountain Inn engagements with that
question stated openly, offering
[vocational expert analysis](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) and
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) to plaintiff and defense
counsel on identical terms.

## Framing the market honestly

The starting data are the Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment
and wage estimates for the surrounding metropolitan area and for South
Carolina statewide, along with state workforce agency labor market
information. But data alone do not answer the question; the report also
accounts for the evaluee's documented restrictions, transferable skills,
education, and realistic commuting range before opining on what work exists
for this person from this town. Where the answer is genuinely uncertain,
direct labor market research fills the gap, and the report says which
employers were contacted and what was asked. The
[regional overview](/locations/south-carolina/greenville/) describes the
wider market this analysis draws from.

## Courts serving Fountain Inn

Fountain Inn lies in Greenville County, and state civil claims proceed in
the Circuit Court, sitting as the Court of Common Pleas. Claims within
federal jurisdiction may be filed in the United States District Court for
the District of South Carolina; the venue controlling any specific case, and
the expert disclosure rules that follow from it, are identified during the
conflict check and intake process. Counsel across the rest of the state will
find venue notes on the [South Carolina page](/locations/south-carolina/).

## Serving a small town from a national practice

The firm's [practice is national](/locations/nationwide/), and Fountain Inn
is served the way every community is served: by appointment, without a local
storefront, and without a published street address that would suggest
otherwise. Remote methods carry the bulk of the work, from records review
through video interviews to standardized testing under publisher-approved
remote protocols. In-person evaluation, when the referral requires it,
happens in the evaluee's own community at a location that spares an injured
person unnecessary travel, and the expert's own travel is scheduled from
the Kansas City home office. Small-town evaluees get the same instruments,
the same report structure, and the same preparation for cross-examination
as evaluees from the largest cities the firm serves.
