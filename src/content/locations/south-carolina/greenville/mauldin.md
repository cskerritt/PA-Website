## What the evaluation looks like

The sequence is deliberate. First comes a documented conflict check and a
written scope. Then a records request tailored to the claims, followed by a
structured interview conducted by video, and standardized testing
administered under publisher protocols wherever remote administration is
supported. If the referral questions require face-to-face assessment, an
in-person session is arranged near Mauldin at a mutually convenient
location, with the expert traveling from the Kansas City home office. Nothing
about this workflow is improvised for South Carolina; it is the same sequence
the firm follows in every state, which is why the resulting reports read
consistently no matter where the evaluee lives.

## County and courthouse context

Mauldin sits in Greenville County, and civil claims arising here are heard in
the Circuit Court, sitting as the Court of Common Pleas. Federal filings
proceed in the United States District Court for the District of South
Carolina, with the controlling venue for any specific matter identified
during intake rather than assumed. The firm appears for plaintiffs and for
defendants, and its role is identical in either chair: an opinion grounded in
records, testing, and published data. Counsel with matters elsewhere in the
region will find the [metro overview](/locations/south-carolina/greenville/)
and the [statewide page](/locations/south-carolina/) useful for venue

## Reading the labor market from Mauldin

A Mauldin resident's employment prospects are shaped by the surrounding
metropolitan economy, so earning capacity opinions are anchored in
occupational employment and wage estimates published by the Bureau of Labor
Statistics for the metro area and for South Carolina as a whole, together
with state workforce agency labor market information. Those sources are
treated qualitatively and cited openly. Where a case turns on whether
specific employers would actually hire a person with the evaluee's
restrictions, the analysis extends to direct labor market research, and the
methodology for that research is documented so opposing counsel can probe it.
