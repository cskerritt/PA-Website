For an evaluee living in Andrews, earning capacity is not an abstraction pulled from a national table; it is the set of jobs a person with particular skills and particular restrictions can realistically hold in and around a small Georgetown County town. That distinction is where damages cases are won and lost, and it is where Purinton Analytics concentrates its work. The firm provides [vocational expert](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) and [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) services for Andrews matters on behalf of plaintiff and defense counsel alike, and every report is drafted on the assumption that opposing counsel will read it more carefully than anyone else.

## Where an Andrews case is heard

Andrews lies in Georgetown County, and state-court damages actions involving Andrews parties proceed in the Circuit Court, whose civil docket is the Court of Common Pleas. For claims that belong in federal court, the United States District Court for the District of South Carolina serves as the available federal forum; which court controls a given matter, and any venue-specific registration or qualification requirement that follows from it, is identified and confirmed during the conflict check and intake process. ## Measuring work within reach of Andrews

Andrews sits within the Murrells Inlet metropolitan area, whose principal city is Murrells Inlet, and the honest starting point for any vocational opinion here is the labor market an Andrews resident can practically access. The firm grounds its findings in Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the metropolitan area and for South Carolina statewide, adds state workforce agency labor market information, and performs direct labor market research where the referral questions warrant that step. Rather than assuming a small-town evaluee can absorb the commute a larger market would demand, the analysis documents which occupations are genuinely available, states the transferable skills reasoning behind each, and sources every figure so the conclusions survive cross-examination. ## Bringing the evaluation to the evaluee

An injured person in a small town should not have to build a recovery around long drives to an expert's office. The practice is structured so the file travels instead: secure remote channels handle records exchange, interviews, and counsel conferences whenever remote work serves the case, and in-person evaluation is scheduled in the evaluee's own community when the referral questions require hands-on assessment. The firm keeps no staffed office in Andrews and lists no local street address, and it says so directly, because accuracy about presence is part of the credibility the reports depend on. When testimony or evaluation requires attendance in South Carolina, travel is arranged from the Kansas City home office. If the damages theory calls for economic modeling, [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) are available, with independent economist partners performing and signing the economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning work continues under a single engagement structure. Counsel elsewhere in the country are served on identical terms through the [nationwide practice](/locations/nationwide/).

## Opening the file for an Andrews matter

Begin with clearance, not records. Send the caption or the party names through the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/), flag any imminent disclosure, deposition, or trial date, and withhold confidential materials until intake confirms scope, venue, and turnaround. That sequence protects privilege, keeps the engagement clean, and lets counsel plan the disclosure calendar around a defined delivery date.
