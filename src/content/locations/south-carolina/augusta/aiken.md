Aiken is the anchor of the South Carolina side of a metropolitan area
that takes its name from a Georgia city, and that geography shapes
litigation here in a particular way: the injured person may live and
treat in Aiken while the employer, the insurer, or the defendant
operates across the state line, and the caption can land in either
state's courts. Purinton Analytics serves counsel on either side of that
caption with [vocational expert opinions](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. Plaintiff and defense referrals are accepted on
identical terms, and every report is written to be examined by the
lawyer who did not commission it.

## Aiken County courts and the state-line question

Aiken sits in Aiken County, where state-court matters proceed in the
Circuit Court (Court of Common Pleas). Federal matters filed on the
South Carolina side are heard in the United States District Court for
the District of South Carolina; which forum controls a particular
dispute, and any registration or qualification requirement that forum
imposes, is identified and confirmed during the conflict check and
intake process. Because a metro that straddles a state line can send
related claims into different systems, reports are drafted to the
standard of the strictest likely reader, so the same written opinion
holds up whether it is tested in a state courthouse or a federal one.

## An employment center inside a larger market

Aiken is a substantial community with an employment base of its own, not
simply a bedroom suburb of the principal city, and a sound earning
capacity opinion has to respect that dual character. Some evaluees work
in Aiken itself; others commute toward Augusta. Vocational findings
therefore draw on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and
wage estimates for the Augusta metropolitan area and for South Carolina
statewide, supplemented by state workforce agency labor market
information and by direct employer contact when a disputed occupation
requires confirmation at the source. The regional context is set out on
the [Augusta metro page](/locations/georgia/augusta/); the individual
analysis, restrictions, transferable skills, and realistic placement
prospects, is always specific to the evaluee in front of the firm.

## By-appointment service, honestly described

The firm keeps no office in Aiken and publishes no local address,
because accuracy about presence is part of the same discipline that
governs its opinions. Counsel conferences are held by video or scheduled
in person as the case requires. Records review, structured interviews,
and standardized testing proceed remotely where publisher protocols
permit; when the referral questions call for an in-person evaluation, it
is conducted in the evaluee's own community rather than requiring an
injured person to travel. Depositions and trial appearances are
scheduled from the Kansas City home office with dates confirmed at
intake, and the same model supports the
[national practice](/locations/nationwide/).
