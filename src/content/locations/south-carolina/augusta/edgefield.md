Edgefield gives its name to its county, and litigation arising here
tends to be tried close to where the parties actually live, before
judges and juries who know the community. That local character is worth
taking seriously in expert work: an opinion that treats a small
Edgefield County case as an afterthought to big-city practice will read
that way on cross-examination. Purinton Analytics prepares
[vocational expert opinions](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care plans](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[rebuttal and peer review analyses](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/)
for Edgefield matters with the same documented method used in the
largest venues, and it takes referrals from whichever side of the
caption calls first, on terms that do not change with the caller.

## Courts serving Edgefield County

State-court matters from Edgefield proceed in the Circuit Court (Court
of Common Pleas), and federal matters in South Carolina are heard in the
United States District Court for the District of South Carolina. The
firm does not guess at venue: the controlling forum for a given case,
and any registration or qualification requirement that forum carries, is
pinned down during the conflict check and intake before any analysis
starts. Where economic loss must be quantified alongside the vocational
findings, the firm offers
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## A small town at the edge of a metro economy

Edgefield is a small town, and its residents' realistic employment
options extend well past the town itself into the wider Augusta
metropolitan area, a market that crosses the Georgia and South Carolina
line. Assessing what an injured Edgefield resident can still earn means
starting from Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and
wage estimates for that metro and for South Carolina statewide, layered
with state workforce agency labor market information and, where a
disputed occupation justifies it, direct contact with employers. The
metro-wide context lives on the
[Augusta metro page](/locations/georgia/augusta/); what this page adds
is the commitment that scale never substitutes for the individual
analysis of restrictions, transferable skills, and access to actual
openings.

## Bringing the evaluation to the evaluee

Small-town evaluees should not bear the cost of the expert's geography.
Records review and structured interviews are conducted remotely, and
standardized testing is administered remotely wherever the publisher's
protocol allows it. If the referral questions require an in-person
evaluation, the firm schedules it in or near the evaluee's own
community, with travel arranged from the Kansas City home office. The
same trip planning covers depositions and trial testimony, with dates
committed at intake so the disclosure calendar drives the schedule
rather than the other way around. There is no Edgefield office and no
published local address; the practice model behind that choice is
explained on the [nationwide page](/locations/nationwide/).
