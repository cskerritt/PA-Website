More expert retentions go wrong on the calendar than on the merits, and
counsel with a Burnettown case usually discovers the problem late: the
disclosure deadline is set, the treating records are still arriving, and
the expert has not yet met the evaluee. Purinton Analytics structures
every Burnettown engagement against that calendar from the first call.
Scope, evaluation dates, and report delivery are committed in writing at
intake, so [vocational expert opinions](/services/vocational-expert-witness/)
and [life care plans](/services/life-care-planning/) arrive when the
docket needs them, not when the expert gets around to them. Referrals
are welcome from plaintiff and defense counsel equally, and the file is
built from the start on the assumption that the other side will read
every page of it.

## Where a Burnettown case is heard

Burnettown lies in Aiken County. Its state-court disputes proceed in the
Circuit Court (Court of Common Pleas), while South Carolina's federal
forum, the United States District Court for the District of South
Carolina, hears the federal matters; the venue that actually governs,
and whatever qualification or registration that venue requires of a
testifying expert, is confirmed during the conflict check and intake.
That early confirmation matters here precisely because of the calendar:
scheduling orders differ across forums, and the engagement plan has to
match the right one.

## A small community priced against the metro

Burnettown itself is a small community, so the labor market that
determines an evaluee's earning capacity is the surrounding Augusta
metropolitan area, which spans the Georgia and South Carolina sides of
the region. The firm grounds its market findings in Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for that metro and
for South Carolina statewide, in state workforce agency labor market
information, and in targeted employer research when a specific
occupation is genuinely in dispute. Metro-level context is collected on
the [Augusta metro page](/locations/georgia/augusta/). The report then
narrows to the person: what this evaluee's documented restrictions
permit, which skills transfer, and which openings are realistically
within reach, each finding sourced so it can be verified.

## Getting the evaluation done on time

Because the calendar governs, evaluation logistics are designed for
speed without shortcuts. Records review begins as soon as the scope is
signed. Interviews and standardized testing run remotely under publisher
protocols where remote administration is supported, which often saves
weeks; when the referral questions demand an in-person evaluation, it is
scheduled in the evaluee's own community, with the examiner traveling
from the Kansas City home office rather than the injured person
traveling to an office. The firm maintains no Burnettown location and
claims none. Deposition and trial dates are reserved when the engagement
opens, a practice applied across the
[national practice](/locations/nationwide/). Where the damages model
needs an economist, the firm arranges
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.
