## Walhalla's place in the Seneca area labor market

A vocational opinion about a Walhalla evaluee has to reflect where that person can realistically work. Employability and earning capacity findings rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the applicable South Carolina area, state workforce agency labor market information, and direct labor market research where the referral questions warrant it. The analysis asks what occupations exist within a reasonable commuting range, what transferable skills the evaluee brings to them, and what the documented restrictions permit, rather than importing assumptions built for a much larger market.
