Expert deadlines, not office locations, are what usually bring Florence
counsel to Purinton Analytics: a disclosure date approaching, a deposition
calendared, or an opposing report that needs a considered response. The firm
schedules its work backward from those dates, offering
[earning capacity and employability evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/),
[medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/), and
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) to plaintiff and
defense counsel across the Florence metro area, with the calendar confirmed
at intake so no one discovers a conflict between the report and the
scheduling order later.

## Florence and Darlington county venues

Engagements are accepted for matters venued in the Circuit Court (Court of
Common Pleas) sitting in Florence County and Darlington County, and in the
United States District Court for the District of South Carolina. Every
matter opens with a documented conflict check, a defined scope, and a
records request tailored to the claims, so that the written opinion rests on
a record both sides can examine. When the damages case requires economic
modeling as well, the firm offers
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. Counsel with matters in other parts of the state can
start from the [South Carolina overview](/locations/south-carolina/).

## Appointment-based presence in the Florence metro

Florence meetings are by appointment. The firm keeps no staffed office in
the metro and publishes no street address for this location, a plain
statement the firm prefers over the implication of a local storefront it
does not have. Most referrals proceed remotely: records review, structured
vocational interviews, and standardized testing administered under publisher
protocols where remote administration is supported. When the referral
questions call for in-person evaluation, it is arranged in the evaluee's own
community, and travel for depositions and trial testimony is scheduled from
the Kansas City home office against the dates counsel identified at intake.
Wherever the work is performed, the report documents the method step by
step, so it reads the same to the retaining attorney and to opposing
counsel.

## Labor market research for Florence evaluees

Vocational findings for evaluees in the metro rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Florence area
and for South Carolina statewide, supplemented by state workforce agency
labor market information and direct labor market research where the referral
questions warrant it. The analysis addresses the occupations actually
present in the evaluee's commutable labor market and their genuine wage
levels rather than a national abstraction. Life care plans and cost
projections for Florence County and Darlington County residents are priced
through provider-specific inquiries and published fee data for the evaluee's
own market, so the numbers in the report can be traced to sources opposing
counsel can check.

## Starting a Florence engagement

Referrals from the metro use the national intake described on the
[nationwide practice](/locations/nationwide/) page. Submit the caption or
party names through the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/), state the
referral questions, and list every date that matters: disclosure deadlines,
depositions, mediation, and trial. Clearance and scheduling are confirmed
together, and plaintiff and defense inquiries are handled under the same
review and the same report standard.
