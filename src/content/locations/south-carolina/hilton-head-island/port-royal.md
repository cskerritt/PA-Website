## Where and how a Port Royal evaluee is evaluated

Most files proceed remotely from start to finish. Records move through
secure transfer, the vocational interview is conducted by video on a
schedule the evaluee can keep, and standardized instruments are
administered under publisher protocols wherever remote administration is
validated. When a referral question requires observation in person, the
evaluation is conducted in the evaluee's own community, with travel
scheduled from the Kansas City home office. The firm maintains no staffed
office in Port Royal and publishes no local address, and it says so
plainly, because a practice that stretched the truth about its footprint
would invite the same doubt about its findings.

## Beaufort County courts and the federal forum

Civil litigation arising in Port Royal proceeds in the Circuit Court, with
civil claims heard in the Court of Common Pleas for Beaufort County. For
federal matters, South Carolina's forum is the United States District Court
for the District of South Carolina, and the venue controlling a specific
case is confirmed during the conflict check and intake, together with any
qualification requirement particular to that forum. Reports are written to
the same standard for either court and either party, on the assumption
that the closest reader will always be the lawyer who did not commission
the work.
