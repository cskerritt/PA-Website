## Working backward from the disclosure date

A credible opinion has fixed prerequisites: the records must be gathered
and reviewed, the vocational interview conducted, testing administered and
scored, and the labor market research completed before anything is
written. When retention comes early, each step gets its full measure; when
it comes late, the firm says so candidly at intake and defines a scope
that can actually be met rather than promising a report the timeline
cannot support. Draft schedules are set against the scheduling order at
the outset, so counsel in a Hardeeville matter knows before signing the
engagement letter what will be ready and when.

## Jasper County venue context

Hardeeville sits in Jasper County, and state court civil matters proceed
in the Circuit Court, with civil claims heard in the Court of Common
Pleas. South Carolina's federal forum is the United States District Court
for the District of South Carolina; the venue that controls a particular
case, and any expert qualification requirement that attaches to it, is
identified during the conflict check and intake. The report itself is
venue-neutral by design, resting on documented methodology and published
data so that it reads the same way to a state court jury, a federal judge,
and the opposing lawyer who studies it line by line.
