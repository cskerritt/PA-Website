Beaufort shares its name with Beaufort County, and for litigation counsel
the town is inseparable from the courts that serve that county. A
vocational or life care planning opinion disclosed here will be measured
against the record by whichever lawyer did not retain its author, and every
report Purinton Analytics signs is written with that reader in mind. The
firm accepts Beaufort engagements from plaintiff and defense counsel alike:
[vocational expert evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) of
employability and earning capacity,
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) for catastrophic
injuries, [rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) of
opposing expert work, and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## The courts that serve Beaufort

State court matters proceed in the Circuit Court, where the Court of
Common Pleas hears the civil docket for Beaufort County. When a case
belongs in federal court, South Carolina's district is the United States
District Court for the District of South Carolina; which forum controls a
given dispute, and whether that forum imposes any registration or
qualification requirement on a testifying expert, is identified and
confirmed during the conflict check and intake process. The firm's reports
are structured so that the same document supports a state jury trial or a
federal bench proceeding without rewriting, because the methodology, not
the venue, carries the opinion.

Beaufort belongs to the metropolitan area whose principal city is Hilton
Head Island, and vocational findings for evaluees living in the town rest
on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates
for that metro and for South Carolina statewide, together with state
workforce agency labor market information and direct labor market research
where warranted. Scale and opportunity are described qualitatively, never
with invented figures, so that every characterization of the market an
evaluee faces can be traced to a published source. The broader regional

## Appointments, not an office

There is no Purinton Analytics storefront in Beaufort, and the firm
publishes no street address for the town, a fact stated here as plainly as
any finding in one of its reports. Engagements ordinarily run remotely:
secure records transfer, video vocational interviews, and standardized
testing under publisher protocols where remote administration is
validated. In-person evaluation in the evaluee's community, and courtroom
or deposition appearances, are handled through travel scheduled from the
Kansas City home office and built around the court's calendar rather than
the firm's.
