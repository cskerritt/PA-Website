Some Ridgeland files arrive at Purinton Analytics after the other side has
already disclosed its expert. Counsel is holding a vocational report or a
life care plan written by someone else and needs to know, quickly and
honestly, whether its methods survive scrutiny. That reviewing posture
informs everything the firm writes under its own signature, because a
report drafted by someone who spends part of the practice dissecting other
experts' work tends to anticipate the dissection of its own. Plaintiff and
defense counsel in Ridgeland matters retain the firm for
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) of opposing
opinions, [vocational expert evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/)
of employability and earning capacity,
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) in catastrophic injury
cases, and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Venue: Jasper County and the federal district

A Ridgeland matter filed in state court proceeds in the Circuit Court,
where the Court of Common Pleas hears civil claims for Jasper County.
Federal litigation in the state belongs to the United States District
Court for the District of South Carolina. Which forum controls a given
case, and any expert registration or qualification requirement that forum
imposes, is identified and confirmed during the conflict check and intake
process. Beyond that threshold question, venue changes little about the
work: an opinion whose methods are stated openly and whose sources are
cited can be checked from the record wherever the case is heard, and that
checkability, not the courthouse, is what the firm builds toward.

## The labor market question, done carefully

The most common defect the firm encounters when reviewing opposing
vocational work is a labor market drawn to fit the conclusion, either
confined to the town itself or stretched past anything the evaluee could
plausibly reach. Ridgeland belongs to the metropolitan area whose
principal city is Hilton Head Island, and the firm's own findings for
Ridgeland evaluees rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational
employment and wage estimates for that metro and for South Carolina
statewide, alongside state workforce agency labor market information and
direct labor market research where the referral questions justify it.
Market scale and opportunity are characterized qualitatively and sourced,
never asserted. How that regional analysis fits together is described on
the [metro overview](/locations/south-carolina/hilton-head-island/), and
the statewide practice appears on the
[South Carolina page](/locations/south-carolina/).

## What the engagement asks of a Ridgeland evaluee

Not much, by design. The vocational interview happens by video.
Standardized testing follows publisher protocols, administered remotely
where the publisher has validated remote administration and in person
where it has not. Records travel only by secure transfer. If a referral
question demands in-person work, the firm comes to the evaluee rather
than the reverse, with travel arranged from the Kansas City home office,
and the same is true when a deposition or trial date requires presence in
court. Purinton Analytics keeps no office in Ridgeland and publishes no
local address; candor about that footprint is part of the same discipline
counsel should expect in the reports.

## Requesting a conflict check

Whether the need is an affirmative opinion or a review of one already
disclosed, the first step is the same: submit the caption or party names
through the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/), attach nothing
confidential, and identify any disclosure, deposition, or trial dates
already set. Counsel receive a direct answer on conflicts, scope, and
timing. Matters venued outside this metro proceed through the firm's
[nationwide practice](/locations/nationwide/) on the same terms.
