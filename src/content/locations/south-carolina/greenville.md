Attorneys on either side of the caption in the Greenville-Anderson-Greer
metro area retain Purinton Analytics for vocational expert and life care
planning opinions in injury, employment, and catastrophic loss litigation.
The work most often requested from the region includes
[earning capacity and employability evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/)
in personal injury and employment matters,
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) and
[medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/) in serious
injury cases, and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Venues served across the Greenville metro

Engagements are accepted for matters venued in the Circuit Court (Court of
Common Pleas) sitting in Greenville County, Anderson County, Pickens County,
and Laurens County, and in the United States District Court for the District
of South Carolina. The same intake applies whether the matter is a state
court injury claim, a federal employment case, or an arbitration: a
documented conflict check, a defined scope, and a records request tailored
to the claims, so that the written opinion rests on a record both sides can
examine. Counsel with matters elsewhere in the state can begin from the
firm's [South Carolina overview](/locations/south-carolina/), which maps the
same services to the rest of the state's venues.

## Meetings by appointment, work without a storefront

Greenville meetings are by appointment. The firm keeps no staffed office in
the metro and publishes no local street address, because accuracy about
presence is part of the same discipline that governs its reports. Most
engagements from the region proceed remotely: records review, structured
vocational interviews, and standardized testing administered under publisher
protocols where remote administration is supported. When the referral
questions call for in-person evaluation, or when depositions and trial
testimony require attendance, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home
office and planned around the litigation calendar rather than around office
hours. Counsel receive the same report standard and the same point of
contact regardless of where the work is performed, and remote work is
documented with the same care as in-person work so the method can be
explained plainly at deposition.

## Labor market research for the Greenville area

Vocational findings for evaluees in the metro rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Greenville
area and for South Carolina statewide, supplemented by state workforce
agency labor market information and direct labor market research where the
referral questions warrant it. Transferable skills analysis considers the
occupations actually present in the evaluee's commutable labor market, not a
generic national profile. Life care plans and medical cost projections are
priced to the evaluee's own community using provider-specific inquiries and
published fee data, so a plan for a Laurens County resident reflects that
resident's actual market rather than a national average.

## Starting a Greenville engagement

Referrals from Greenville, Anderson, and the surrounding counties follow the
same path as referrals from anywhere in the country, described on the
[nationwide practice](/locations/nationwide/) page. Submit the caption or
party names through the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and note any
imminent disclosure, deposition, or trial dates, so that scheduling can be
confirmed alongside clearance. Plaintiff and defense inquiries receive the
same review, the same methodology, and the same candid declination when the
record would not support the opinion requested. That evenhandedness is not a
slogan; it is what keeps the work useful to whichever side retains it.
