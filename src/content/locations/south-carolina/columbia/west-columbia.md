A municipal boundary is a poor description of a working life. People who live in West Columbia routinely hold jobs throughout the Columbia, SC metropolitan area, including in the principal city of Columbia itself, and a vocational opinion that stopped at the town line would misstate what the evaluee could actually earn before the injury and what remains reachable after it. Purinton Analytics writes [vocational expert](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) opinions and [life care plans](/services/life-care-planning/) for West Columbia matters with that commuting reality at the center of the analysis, for counsel on whichever side of the caption makes the referral.

## Venue: Lexington County and the federal court

West Columbia sits in Lexington County, so state-court litigation involving its residents and employers is heard in the Circuit Court (Court of Common Pleas). The federal forum available in the state is the United States District Court for the District of South Carolina, and the venue that actually governs a given matter, together with any qualification requirement it carries, is identified and confirmed during the conflict check and intake process. Statewide coverage is described on the [South Carolina locations page](/locations/south-carolina/); the [Columbia metro page](/locations/south-carolina/columbia/) addresses the metropolitan practice as a whole.

## A metro-wide earning capacity analysis

Because a West Columbia worker's realistic options run across the metro, the earning capacity analysis is built at that scale. The wage and employment evidence comes from Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates covering the Columbia, SC metropolitan area, read alongside statewide South Carolina estimates, state workforce agency labor market information, and targeted labor market research where the referral questions warrant it. The task is not to describe the regional economy in the abstract but to connect this evaluee's restrictions, education, and work history to occupations that genuinely exist within commuting reach, and to say plainly when an occupation does not.

## Meetings, evaluations, and travel

There is no Purinton Analytics office in West Columbia. The firm works from its Kansas City home office, publishes no street address here, and says so directly, because candor about presence is a fair preview of candor in the report. Most phases of an engagement are remote: secure records exchange, counsel conferences by video, and vocational or care-planning interviews by secure video when that format serves the evaluee. Where the questions demand in-person evaluation, or where deposition and trial dates require presence, travel is arranged from Kansas City and the assessment takes place in the evaluee's own community. When counsel also needs the loss quantified, the matter can proceed as a [coordinated damages engagement](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement structure. The same model serves referrals far beyond South Carolina through the [nationwide practice](/locations/nationwide/).
