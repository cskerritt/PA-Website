## Fairfield County venue

Winnsboro's state-court civil litigation proceeds in the Circuit Court (Court of Common Pleas) serving Fairfield County. In federal matters, South Carolina's forum is the United States District Court for the District of South Carolina; the court that will actually govern a given case, and any registration or qualification requirement it imposes on a testifying expert, is identified and confirmed during the conflict check and intake process rather than assumed from the town's location. ## Labor market analysis beyond the county line

For many Winnsboro evaluees, meaningful employment options extend past Fairfield County toward the principal city of Columbia and the metropolitan market around it, and the vocational analysis follows the options wherever they lead. Its foundations are Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Columbia, SC metropolitan area and for South Carolina as a whole, state workforce agency labor market information, and direct labor market research in the occupations the evaluee could plausibly hold. Where the realistic market is narrower than the metro, the report says so and explains why; where it is broader, the same rule applies. The market is described as the evidence shows it, not as either side might prefer it.
