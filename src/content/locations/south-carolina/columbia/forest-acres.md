## Where Forest Acres cases are heard

Forest Acres is a Richland County community, and its state-court civil matters are tried in the Circuit Court (Court of Common Pleas). Federal litigants in the state look to the United States District Court for the District of South Carolina; the venue controlling a particular dispute, and any expert qualification requirement that flows from it, is identified and confirmed during the conflict check and intake process rather than announced in advance. Broader regional coverage is described on the [Columbia metro page](/locations/south-carolina/columbia/), and the [South Carolina locations page](/locations/south-carolina/) covers the state as a whole.

## Earning capacity in the metro market

Forest Acres workers draw their opportunities largely from the Columbia, SC metropolitan labor market and its principal city of Columbia, so that market frames the earning capacity analysis. The evidentiary base is the Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Columbia metropolitan area together with the South Carolina statewide series, augmented by state workforce agency labor market information and, when a referral question turns on it, by direct research among employers in the relevant occupations. Qualitative conclusions about the market's breadth or an occupation's presence are stated carefully and sourced, and quantitative claims are reserved for the report itself, where their citations can sit beside them.

## Remote work, local evaluation

The practice runs from a Kansas City home office. It keeps no staffed office in Forest Acres, publishes no street address for this page, and treats that candor as part of its method: a report writer who is precise about small facts earns trust on the large ones. Day to day, the engagement is remote, with secure records exchange and video conferences; when an in-person evaluation is warranted, it happens in the evaluee's own community, and deposition or trial appearances are met with travel scheduled from Kansas City. If the damages theory also requires economic quantification, the matter can be structured as a [coordinated damages engagement](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement structure. The [nationwide practice](/locations/nationwide/) extends identical service to matters beyond South Carolina.
