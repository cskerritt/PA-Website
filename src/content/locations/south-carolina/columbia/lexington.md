## Lexington County venue and the federal forum

Lexington is a Lexington County town, and state-court matters involving its residents and employers proceed in the Circuit Court (Court of Common Pleas). South Carolina's federal trial forum is the United States District Court for the District of South Carolina; which court controls a particular case, and any expert registration or qualification requirement that follows from it, is confirmed during the conflict check and intake rather than assumed in advance. Counsel working across the wider region can start from the [Columbia metro page](/locations/south-carolina/columbia/), and the [South Carolina locations page](/locations/south-carolina/) describes the statewide practice.

## The labor market a Lexington evaluee can reach

Lexington is among the larger suburban communities of the Columbia, SC metropolitan area, and most of its workers participate in an employment market that extends across the metro and into the principal city of Columbia. Vocational findings for a Lexington evaluee therefore rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Columbia area and for South Carolina statewide, supplemented by state workforce agency labor market information and by direct employer research when the referral questions call for it. Wage levels and occupational availability are cited to those sources, never estimated from impression, so that cross-examination finds a trail of citations rather than a shrug.

## How the work is staged

The firm's home office is in Kansas City, and it keeps no staffed office in Lexington and publishes no local street address, because there is none to publish and pretending otherwise would be a poor way to open a relationship built on accuracy. Records exchange and counsel conferences run through secure remote channels, interviews proceed by secure video when video serves the evaluee well, and when the questions require in-person assessment, the evaluation is conducted in the evaluee's own community with travel scheduled from the Kansas City home office. Matters that pair lost earning capacity with economic modeling can proceed as [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement structure. Counsel with matters beyond this metro are served through the firm's [nationwide practice](/locations/nationwide/) on the same terms.
