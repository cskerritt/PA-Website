Purinton Analytics accepts [vocational expert](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) and [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) engagements from counsel on either side of the caption in Rock Hill matters, and treats that split between forum and market as the starting point of the analysis rather than an afterthought.

## York County venue and the courts that hear Rock Hill claims

Rock Hill sits in York County, and civil claims arising here proceed in the Circuit Court (Court of Common Pleas), South Carolina's trial bench for personal injury, wrongful death, and employment disputes. On the federal side, the United States District Court for the District of South Carolina is among the forums available for matters with federal jurisdiction; the controlling venue for any particular claim is identified during the conflict check and intake, along with any venue-specific registration or qualification requirement. Reports are written to the same standard regardless of forum: a documented methodology, disclosed sources, and opinions that hold up under cross-examination in either courthouse.

## Reading a Rock Hill evaluee against the Charlotte market

Rock Hill is a substantial city in its own right, but its workforce participates in the broader Charlotte-Concord-Gastonia labor market, and an earning-capacity opinion that ignores that reality understates or overstates what work is genuinely available. Vocational findings for Rock Hill evaluees rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Charlotte metropolitan area and for South Carolina statewide, supplemented by state workforce agency labor market information and direct labor market research where the referral questions warrant it. The analysis is qualitative about geography and explicit about sources, so opposing counsel can trace every conclusion to a published dataset or a documented inquiry.
