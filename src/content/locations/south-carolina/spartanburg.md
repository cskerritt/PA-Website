## Presence in Spartanburg, described accurately

Spartanburg meetings are by appointment. The firm maintains no staffed
office in the metro and publishes no street address for this location. That
candor is deliberate: an expert whose marketing overstates an office
footprint invites the same skepticism toward the rest of the file. Most
referrals proceed remotely through records review, structured vocational
interviews, and standardized testing administered under publisher protocols
where remote administration is supported. In-person evaluation, when
warranted, is arranged in the evaluee's community, and travel for
depositions or trial is scheduled from the Kansas City home office to match
the court's calendar. Every step is documented as it is performed, so the
report can state plainly what was done, where, and under which protocol.

## Spartanburg labor market research

Vocational conclusions for evaluees in the metro rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Spartanburg
area and for South Carolina statewide, alongside state workforce agency
labor market information and direct labor market research where the referral
questions warrant it. Transferable skills analysis is grounded in the
occupations actually present in the evaluee's commutable labor market and
their real wage structure, not in a national composite. Life care plans and
medical cost projections for Spartanburg County and Union County residents
are priced through provider-specific inquiries and published fee data for
the evaluee's own market, so the projection reflects what care actually
costs where the evaluee lives.

## Opening a Spartanburg engagement

Spartanburg referrals travel the same intake path as matters from anywhere
in the country, described on the [nationwide practice](/locations/nationwide/)
page. Send the caption or party names through the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and note any imminent disclosure,
deposition, or trial dates so that clearance and scheduling can be resolved
together. Plaintiff and defense inquiries receive identical treatment, and
when the record cannot support the opinion a caller hopes for, the firm says
so before the engagement begins rather than after the report is due. Early
referrals give records collection the most room, though the same intake
serves counsel facing a short calendar.
