Disclosure deadlines, deposition settings, and trial dates drive expert work
in Gaffney as much as the substance does, so Purinton Analytics builds every
Cherokee County engagement backward from the case calendar. Scope is defined
at intake, the records request follows the claims, and the report timeline is
set against the operative disclosure date rather than left open-ended. Within
that structure, counsel retain the firm for
[vocational evaluation and expert testimony](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis
while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement
structure. Referrals are accepted from either side of the caption, and the
methodology does not shift with the retention.

## Cherokee County courts, state and federal

Engagements are accepted for matters in the Circuit Court (Court of Common
Pleas) for Cherokee County and in the United States District Court for the
District of South Carolina. In each forum the firm produces written opinions
prepared for disclosure, provides deposition and trial testimony, and offers
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) when counsel
needs an opposing expert's work tested against its own claimed methods.
Matters filed in other South Carolina venues are covered on the firm's
[South Carolina page](/locations/south-carolina/), under the same engagement
structure described here.

## By-appointment logistics for Gaffney matters

The firm keeps no staffed office in Gaffney and publishes no street address
for this location; stating that plainly is part of the same discipline that
governs the reports. Gaffney meetings are by appointment, at counsel's
offices, at a location convenient to the evaluee, or by videoconference, and
the bulk of the work, from records review through drafting, proceeds
remotely. When the referral questions call for in-person evaluation, or when
a deposition or trial setting requires presence, travel is scheduled from the
Kansas City home office and planned early enough that logistics never crowd a
deadline. Counsel are told at intake what the schedule can and cannot
absorb, before the engagement is accepted.

## The labor market behind Gaffney opinions

Employability and earning capacity findings for Cherokee County evaluees are
grounded in Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage
estimates for the surrounding area and for South Carolina statewide, in state
workforce agency labor market information, and in direct labor market
research where the referral warrants it. Each report defines the evaluee's
commutable labor market explicitly and connects the transferable skills
analysis to occupations actually present in that market, because a vocational
conclusion detached from the local market invites exactly the
cross-examination it cannot survive. Where local conditions and statewide
estimates diverge, the report identifies the divergence and its basis.

## Starting a Gaffney engagement

Every engagement opens with a documented conflict check, a defined scope, and
a records request tailored to the claims, so the written opinion rests on a
record both sides can examine. The same practice serves counsel far beyond
this venue through the firm's [national coverage](/locations/nationwide/). To
begin, submit the caption or party names through the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and note any imminent disclosure,
deposition, or trial dates so they can anchor the schedule from the first
conversation.
