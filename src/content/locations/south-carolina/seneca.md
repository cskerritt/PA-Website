An expert engagement in the Seneca area rarely turns on where the expert
sits. Records arrive electronically, interviews and conferences can be held by
videoconference, and the evaluation itself can be scheduled in the evaluee's
own community when in-person testing is warranted. Purinton Analytics built
its practice around that reality, which is how Oconee County counsel obtain
[vocational evaluation and expert testimony](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis
while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement
structure. Plaintiff and defense referrals proceed under the same methodology
and the same reporting standards.

## The Oconee County venue picture

Engagements are accepted for matters in the Circuit Court (Court of Common
Pleas) for Oconee County and in the United States District Court for the
District of South Carolina. Whichever forum the case sits in, the deliverable
is a written opinion prepared for disclosure and built to be defended at
deposition and trial, and counsel can also retain the firm for
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) of an opposing
expert's report. Matters filed elsewhere in the state fall under the practice
described on the firm's [South Carolina page](/locations/south-carolina/),
which operates identically.

## Appointment-based presence, stated plainly

The firm maintains no staffed office in Seneca and publishes no street
address for this location. That candor is deliberate: a practice whose
credibility rests on documented, verifiable opinions should describe its own
footprint with the same precision. Seneca meetings are by appointment, at
counsel's offices, at a location convenient to the evaluee, or online. When
the referral questions call for in-person evaluation, or when deposition and
trial dates require presence, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home
office and coordinated against the case calendar so that no disclosure
deadline depends on last-minute logistics.

## Labor market data behind Seneca opinions

Findings about employability and earning capacity for Oconee County evaluees
are grounded in the labor market those evaluees can actually reach. The
analysis draws on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage
estimates for the surrounding area and for South Carolina statewide, state
workforce agency labor market information, and direct labor market research
where warranted by the referral. Because conditions in a smaller market can
depart from statewide patterns, each report defines the evaluee's commutable
labor market explicitly, ties the transferable skills analysis to occupations
present in that market, and explains the reasoning so that the opinion can be
tested on cross-examination rather than merely asserted.
