## Horry County and Its Courts

Conway matters proceed in South Carolina's Circuit Court, where civil claims are heard in the Court of Common Pleas, and Horry County supplies the venue for most disputes arising in the town. Cases with a federal basis may instead be filed in the United States District Court for the District of South Carolina; the controlling forum for any particular matter is identified during the conflict check and intake rather than assumed from geography. The firm's reports are prepared to the same standard for either court, and testimony is scheduled around the deadlines the court actually sets. Engagements across the rest of the state are described on the [South Carolina locations page](/locations/south-carolina/).

## Conway in the Metro Labor Market

Conway belongs to the metropolitan area that also includes Myrtle Beach and North Myrtle Beach, and a Conway evaluee's realistic employment prospects are measured against that wider market rather than the town alone. Vocational findings rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the metropolitan area and for South Carolina statewide, supplemented by state workforce agency labor market information and direct employer research where the referral questions warrant it. What matters analytically is not local color but the occupations a specific evaluee can perform, the transferability of the skills documented in the record, and the wages those occupations command in this market. Engagements framed at the metro level are described on the [Myrtle Beach metro page](/locations/south-carolina/myrtle-beach/).

## Evaluating a Conway Resident

The firm serves Conway by appointment. There is no staffed office in the town and no published street address here, because accuracy about presence belongs to the same discipline that governs the reports themselves. Most engagements begin remotely: records review, a structured vocational interview by video, and standardized testing administered under protocols designed for remote use. When the referral questions call for an in-person evaluation, or when a deposition or trial date requires attendance, travel is arranged from the Kansas City home office and the evaluation is conducted in the evaluee's own community rather than in a distant clinic.
