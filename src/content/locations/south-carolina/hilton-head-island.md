Purinton Analytics keeps no office on Hilton Head Island, in Bluffton, or in
Port Royal, and says so before saying anything else, because a location page
that implies a storefront the firm does not have would contradict the
discipline its reports are built on. What counsel in the metro actually get
is a national practice that serves the area by appointment:
[earning capacity and employability evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/) for plaintiff
and defense counsel, delivered remotely where the work supports it and in
person where the referral questions require it.

## Venues in Beaufort and Jasper counties

Engagements are accepted for matters venued in the Circuit Court (Court of
Common Pleas) sitting in Beaufort County and Jasper County, and in the
United States District Court for the District of South Carolina. Each
engagement opens with a documented conflict check, a defined scope, and a
records request tailored to the claims, so the written opinion rests on a
record either side of the caption can test. Where the case also calls for
economic modeling, the firm offers
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. The [South Carolina overview](/locations/south-carolina/)
describes the same services for venues elsewhere in the state.

## How by-appointment service works here

Meetings in the metro are by appointment, arranged around counsel's schedule
and the evaluee's circumstances rather than around a local office. Records
review, structured vocational interviews, and standardized testing under
publisher protocols proceed remotely for most referrals. When an in-person
evaluation is warranted, it is conducted in the evaluee's own community,
which for injured or medically fragile evaluees is often the difference
between a complete evaluation and an incomplete one. Travel for evaluations,
depositions, and trial testimony is scheduled from the Kansas City home
office and planned against the litigation calendar, with dates confirmed at
intake whenever counsel can identify them. The report states where and how
each element of the work was performed, so logistics never become a
credibility question later.

## Labor market analysis for a coastal metro

Vocational findings for evaluees in the metro rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Hilton Head
Island area and for South Carolina statewide, together with state workforce
agency labor market information and direct labor market research where
warranted. The analysis examines the occupations genuinely available within
the evaluee's commutable area and their actual wages and demands, rather
than importing assumptions from larger markets. Life care plans and cost
projections for Beaufort County and Jasper County residents are priced to
the evaluee's own market through provider-specific inquiries and published
fee data, since local care availability is a research question, never an
assumption.

## Requesting a conflict check

Referrals from the metro enter the same national intake described on the
[nationwide practice](/locations/nationwide/) page. Submit the caption or
party names through the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and flag any
imminent disclosure, deposition, or trial dates so scheduling is settled
alongside clearance. Inquiries from either side of the caption receive the
same review and the same report standard, and when the record will not
support the opinion sought, counsel hear that assessment at intake, before
fees or deadlines are committed.
