## Venue: The Court of Common Pleas and the Federal Forum

Darlington shares its name with Darlington County, and state-court civil actions arising in town proceed in the Circuit Court (Court of Common Pleas). Federal question and diversity matters have the United States District Court for the District of South Carolina available as a forum; which court actually controls a particular caption is confirmed during the conflict check and intake, not presumed from geography. The firm's reports are built to serve either setting, with methodology stated plainly enough for cross-examination and conclusions traceable to the underlying records.

## Reading the Labor Market from Darlington

For a Darlington evaluee, the relevant labor market is regional rather than strictly local. The vocational analysis draws on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Florence metropolitan area, of which Darlington County is a part, together with South Carolina statewide estimates and state workforce agency labor market information. Direct labor market research, including employer contact where appropriate, supplements the published data when a referral question turns on the availability of specific work.
