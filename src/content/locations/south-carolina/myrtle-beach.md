For an evaluee living in the Myrtle Beach-Conway-North Myrtle Beach area,
the practical question is often where the evaluation itself will happen.
Purinton Analytics answers it in the evaluee's favor: most work proceeds
remotely, and when an in-person evaluation is warranted it is conducted in
the evaluee's own community rather than requiring travel to a distant
office. On that foundation the firm provides
[earning capacity and employability evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/) to plaintiff
and defense counsel throughout the metro.

## Horry County venues and the federal district

Engagements are accepted for matters venued in the Circuit Court (Court of
Common Pleas) sitting in Horry County and in the United States District
Court for the District of South Carolina. The intake is the same in either
forum: a documented conflict check, a defined scope, and a records request
tailored to the claims, so that the written opinion rests on a record both
sides of the caption can examine. Where the damages case also needs economic
modeling, [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
are available, in which independent economist partners perform and sign the
economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under
one engagement structure. Matters filed elsewhere in the state are covered
through the [South Carolina overview](/locations/south-carolina/).

## By appointment on the coast

Myrtle Beach meetings are by appointment. The firm has no staffed office in
the metro and publishes no street address here; plain statements about
presence are part of the same discipline the firm applies to its opinions.
Records review, structured interviews, and standardized testing under
publisher protocols are handled remotely for most referrals. When
depositions or trial dates in Horry County require personal attendance,
travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office and coordinated with
counsel around the court's calendar, so testimony dates drive the schedule
rather than the other way around. The report documents where and how each
part of the work was performed, so the logistics themselves never become a
cross-examination problem.

## Reading the Myrtle Beach labor market honestly

Vocational findings for evaluees in the metro rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Myrtle Beach
area and for South Carolina statewide, together with state workforce agency
labor market information and direct labor market research where the referral
questions warrant it. The analysis asks what occupations actually exist
within the evaluee's commutable area and what they genuinely pay, rather
than assuming the profile of a larger market applies. Life care plans and
cost projections for Horry County residents are priced to the local market
through provider-specific inquiries and published fee data, because care
availability and cost in a coastal community are questions to be researched,
not assumed.

## Starting a Myrtle Beach engagement

Referrals from the metro follow the national intake described on the
[nationwide practice](/locations/nationwide/) page. Submit the caption or
party names through the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/), identify the
referral questions, and note any imminent disclosure, deposition, or trial
dates so scheduling can be confirmed alongside clearance. The same review
applies whether the inquiry comes from the plaintiff's side or the defense,
and the firm says so plainly because opposing counsel will read the report
either way. When the record cannot support the opinion a caller is seeking,
that conclusion is delivered at intake rather than discovered at deposition.
