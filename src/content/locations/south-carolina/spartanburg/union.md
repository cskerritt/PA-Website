Union stands apart from the other communities in the Spartanburg metro area in a way that matters to litigators: it lies in Union County, its own county with its own courthouse, rather than in the county that surrounds the principal city. Counsel handling an injury, employment, or wrongful death matter arising in Union therefore work a local docket while drawing on the wider [Spartanburg area](/locations/south-carolina/spartanburg/) economy for the vocational evidence. Purinton Analytics provides [vocational expert](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) and [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) opinions for those matters, for plaintiff and defense engagements alike.

## Venue in Union County

State-court matters arising in Union proceed in the Circuit Court (Court of Common Pleas) sitting for Union County, and reports prepared for that venue are written to the same standard the firm applies in any courtroom: a documented conflict check, a defined scope, and opinions that trace to identifiable records and published data. Federal claims may be filed in the United States District Court for the District of South Carolina; the controlling venue and division for any particular caption are confirmed during the conflict check and intake rather than assumed. Because the practice appears for either side of the caption, the working file is assembled so that opposing counsel can retrace every step, whichever party made the referral.

## A Small City Within a Larger Labor Market

For earning capacity questions, Union cannot be analyzed as if it were sealed off at the county line. Residents participate in a labor market that reaches across the metro area toward Spartanburg and its surrounding employment centers, and a transferable skills analysis has to reflect that commuting reality. Vocational findings rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the relevant area and for South Carolina statewide, supplemented by state workforce agency labor market information and direct employer research where the referral questions warrant it. Scale is treated honestly: Union is a small city, and the opinion says so rather than importing big-city assumptions.

## Evaluating an Evaluee in Union

There is no Purinton Analytics office in Union or anywhere else in South Carolina, and the firm does not pretend otherwise. Work is remote-first: records review, vocational interviews by videoconference, and written reports proceed without travel. When the referral calls for an in-person evaluation, the evaluation is scheduled in the evaluee's own community, and when deposition or trial testimony requires presence, travel is arranged from the Kansas City home office and planned around the court's calendar. Any South Carolina registration or qualification requirement is identified and confirmed during the conflict check and intake process.
