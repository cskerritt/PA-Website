## Building a Care Plan for a Woodruff Resident

A life care plan for a Woodruff evaluee begins in the records: the medical file, the treating providers' recommendations, and the functional picture they establish. Each projected need is tied to a clinical foundation, and each cost entry to a published or verifiable source, so the plan can be defended item by item at deposition rather than as a lump. Where the referral questions call for it, a home assessment is conducted in the evaluee's own residence, because a plan describing daily needs should rest on observation of the setting where those needs arise. Counsel who face an opposing plan rather than needing their own can commission [rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) held to the same standard.
