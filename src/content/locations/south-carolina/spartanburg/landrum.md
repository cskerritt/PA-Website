Preparation for testimony begins at intake, not the week before deposition. ## The Venues That Hear Landrum Cases

Landrum lies in Spartanburg County, so its state-court matters proceed in the Circuit Court (Court of Common Pleas) for Spartanburg County. Federal claims have the United States District Court for the District of South Carolina available as a forum, with the controlling venue and division confirmed during the conflict check and intake. The firm appears for plaintiff and defense engagements in both systems and writes to the standard the stricter forum would demand.

## A Small Town at the Edge of a Metro Market

Landrum is a small town within the [Spartanburg metro area](/locations/south-carolina/spartanburg/), and a Landrum evaluee's realistic employment options are defined by the commuting market around it rather than by the town alone. Vocational conclusions rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the relevant area and for South Carolina statewide, on state workforce agency labor market information, and on direct labor market inquiry where the referral questions turn on specific occupations. The analysis names the market's character honestly instead of assuming the density of a large city.
