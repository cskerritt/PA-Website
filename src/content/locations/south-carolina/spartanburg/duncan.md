Expert retentions for Duncan cases tend to arrive with a calendar attached: a scheduling order already entered, a disclosure deadline visible on the horizon, a mediation date that would benefit from a finished report. Purinton Analytics builds its Duncan engagements backward from those dates. Scope is fixed at intake, the records request goes out immediately after clearance, and the [vocational expert](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) opinion or [life care plan](/services/life-care-planning/) is drafted so that supplementation, deposition, and trial testimony follow from the same disclosed methodology rather than requiring repairs later. Plaintiff and defense referrals are handled on the same footing.

## Working to the Disclosure Calendar

An opinion that is rushed reads as rushed, and opposing counsel will say so. The firm's answer is sequencing, not speed for its own sake: the conflict check and engagement scope come before anything else, the records review and vocational interview are scheduled against the report deadline, and draft findings are complete early enough that counsel can evaluate them before disclosure rather than after. If a Duncan matter later needs a supplemental report, the original's documented method makes the supplement an extension rather than a rewrite.

## Spartanburg County and the Federal Forum

Duncan sits in Spartanburg County, so state-court matters proceed in the Circuit Court (Court of Common Pleas) for that county. On the federal side, South Carolina's forum is the United States District Court for the District of South Carolina; the venue and division that actually control a given caption are confirmed during the conflict check and intake rather than presumed from geography. Reports are drafted for cross-examination in either system, with every conclusion tied to identifiable records and published data that both sides of the caption can inspect.

## The Labor Market a Duncan Evaluee Faces

Duncan is a small town within the broader [Spartanburg metro economy](/locations/south-carolina/spartanburg/), and its residents compete in a commuting market considerably wider than the town itself. Vocational findings therefore rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the relevant area and for South Carolina statewide, along with state workforce agency labor market information and direct employer research where a referral question turns on a particular occupation. Qualitative honesty about scale, rather than borrowed metropolitan assumptions, is part of what makes the opinion defensible.

## Appointments, Travel, and Qualification

The firm has no office in Duncan and does not suggest otherwise. Records analysis and interviews are handled remotely in most engagements; in-person evaluations, when warranted, take place in the evaluee's own community by appointment. Testimony travel is arranged from the Kansas City home office and slotted against the court's dates. Any registration or qualification requirement particular to a South Carolina venue is identified and confirmed during the conflict check and intake process.
