The referral questions that arrive from the Charleston-North Charleston
metro tend to cluster around a familiar set of disputes: what an injured
person can still earn, what care a catastrophic injury will require over a
lifetime, and how those findings hold up under cross-examination. Purinton
Analytics answers those questions for plaintiff and defense counsel alike
through [earning capacity and employability evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/),
[medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/), and
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) of opposing
expert work, each delivered under the same written methodology regardless of
which side issues the retention.

## Charleston area venues and courts

Engagements are accepted for matters venued in the Circuit Court (Court of
Common Pleas) sitting in Charleston County, Berkeley County, and Dorchester
County, and in the United States District Court for the District of South
Carolina. Where a damages case requires economic modeling alongside the
vocational and care-planning opinions, the firm offers
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. Counsel with matters elsewhere in the state will find
the same services mapped to other venues on the
[South Carolina overview](/locations/south-carolina/).

## Appointment-based service, no local storefront

Charleston meetings are by appointment. The firm keeps no staffed office in
the metro and publishes no street address for this location, because
accuracy about presence belongs to the same discipline that governs its
reports. Most engagements begin remotely with records review, structured
vocational interviews, and standardized testing administered under publisher
protocols where remote administration is supported. When in-person
evaluation is warranted, it is arranged in the evaluee's own community, and
when deposition or trial testimony requires attendance, travel is scheduled
from the Kansas City home office around the court's calendar. The report
standard and the point of contact stay constant wherever the work is
performed, and the file documents how each step was conducted so the method
itself can be examined at deposition without surprises.

## Labor market evidence for Charleston evaluees

Vocational findings for evaluees in the metro rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Charleston
area and for South Carolina statewide, supplemented by state workforce
agency labor market information and direct labor market research where
warranted. The analysis is anchored to the evaluee's commutable labor
market: the occupations genuinely available there, their wages, and their
demands, weighed against the evaluee's residual capacities. Life care plans
and cost projections for residents of Berkeley County, Charleston County, or
Dorchester County are priced through provider-specific inquiries and
published fee data for the evaluee's own market rather than borrowed
national averages.

## Opening a Charleston engagement

Charleston referrals enter through the same national intake described on the
[nationwide practice](/locations/nationwide/) page. Submit the caption or
party names through the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and note any
imminent disclosure, deposition, or trial dates so scheduling can be
confirmed as soon as clearance completes. Plaintiff and defense inquiries
are reviewed identically, and the firm declines work when the record would
not support the opinion sought, a practice that protects the retaining
attorney as much as the opposing one. Referrals made early in discovery
leave the most room for complete records collection, though the same intake
serves counsel working against a compressed calendar.
