concentrate, and that is precisely where methodology matters most: counsel
should not have to accept thinner analysis because the case sits in a
micropolitan area. Purinton Analytics brings Newberry County matters the same

## Newberry County venues

Engagements are accepted for matters in the Circuit Court (Court of Common
Pleas) for Newberry County and in the United States District Court for the
District of South Carolina. The firm's deliverables do not vary by forum: a
written opinion prepared for disclosure, deposition and trial testimony when
the case calls for it, and
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) of opposing
expert reports when counsel needs a documented critique rather than an
impression. For matters filed elsewhere in the state, the firm's
[South Carolina page](/locations/south-carolina/) describes the statewide
practice built on the same terms.

## An honest account of local presence

There is no staffed Purinton Analytics office in Newberry, and the firm
publishes no street address for this location. That plain statement is
intentional: a firm whose reports depend on verifiable accuracy should
describe its own footprint the same way. Newberry meetings are by
appointment, at counsel's offices, at a location convenient to the evaluee,
or by videoconference. Records review, analysis, and drafting proceed
remotely on a defined schedule, and when the referral questions call for
in-person evaluation, or when deposition and trial dates require presence,
travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office against the case
calendar.

## Reading the Newberry labor market correctly

A vocational opinion in a smaller market succeeds or fails on how carefully
the market is defined. Findings for Newberry County evaluees draw on Bureau
of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the
surrounding area and for South Carolina statewide, state workforce agency
labor market information, and direct labor market research where the referral
warrants it. Each report states which commutable labor market frames the
evaluee's realistic options, ties the transferable skills analysis to
occupations present in that market, and explains any divergence between local
conditions and the statewide estimates, so the conclusion can withstand
cross-examination on its own record.
