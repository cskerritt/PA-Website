The Greenwood micropolitan area takes in both Abbeville County and Greenwood
County, and litigation in either county draws on the same expert disciplines
the firm provides nationally. Purinton Analytics serves Greenwood counsel with
[vocational evaluation and expert testimony](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis
while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement
structure. Referrals come from both sides of the caption, and the work product
is written so that it reads the same way whichever side retained it.

## Venue footprint across Abbeville and Greenwood counties

Engagements are accepted for matters in the Circuit Court (Court of Common
Pleas) for Greenwood County and for Abbeville County, and in the United States
District Court for the District of South Carolina. The deliverables are the
same in each venue: a written opinion prepared for disclosure, deposition and
trial testimony when the case requires it, and
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) of opposing expert
work when counsel needs a second, documented look at someone else's
methodology. For matters filed elsewhere in the state, the firm's
[South Carolina page](/locations/south-carolina/) describes the statewide
practice, which runs on the identical engagement structure.

## Meetings by appointment, not a local storefront

The firm keeps no staffed office in Greenwood and publishes no street address
for this location, because accuracy about presence is part of the same
discipline that governs its reports. Meetings are by appointment: at counsel's
offices, at a location convenient to the evaluee, or by videoconference.
Records review, conferencing, and report drafting proceed remotely on a
defined schedule, and when the referral questions call for in-person
evaluation, or when deposition and trial dates require presence, travel is
scheduled from the Kansas City home office and planned around the operative
disclosure calendar rather than left open-ended.

## Labor market research for Greenwood evaluees

Employability and earning capacity findings for evaluees in Abbeville and
Greenwood counties are grounded in data specific to their commutable labor
market rather than in national generalities. The analysis draws on Bureau of
Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the
surrounding area and for South Carolina statewide, state workforce agency
labor market information, and direct labor market research where the referral
warrants it. Because a smaller labor market can differ sharply from the
statewide picture, the report is explicit about which market defines the
evaluee's realistic employment options and why, and the transferable skills
analysis is tied to occupations that actually exist within reach of the
evaluee's community.

## Starting a Greenwood engagement

Every engagement begins the same way regardless of venue: a documented
conflict check, a defined scope, and a records request tailored to the claims,
so that the written opinion rests on a record both sides can examine. Counsel
with matters beyond South Carolina can review the firm's
[national coverage](/locations/nationwide/), which operates under the same
methodology and reporting standards. To begin, submit the caption or party
names through the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and note any imminent
disclosure, deposition, or trial dates so scheduling can be confirmed against
them from the outset.
