Counsel in Orangeburg typically bring the firm a familiar set of questions:
whether an injured person can return to work, what that person can now earn,
what future care will be required, and what the answers are worth when an
independent economist reduces them to present value. Purinton Analytics
addresses each of those questions for Orangeburg matters through
[vocational evaluation and expert testimony](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis
while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement
structure. The practice accepts work from either side of the caption, and the
same methodology applies no matter who retained the firm.

## Courts serving Orangeburg County

Engagements are accepted for matters in the Circuit Court (Court of Common
Pleas) for Orangeburg County and in the United States District Court for the
District of South Carolina. In either forum the firm delivers a written
opinion prepared for disclosure, supports it at deposition and trial, and
provides [rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) when
counsel needs an opposing expert's report examined against the published
methodology it claims to follow. Counsel handling cases across the state will
find the broader practice described on the firm's
[South Carolina page](/locations/south-carolina/).

## How the firm appears in Orangeburg

There is no staffed Purinton Analytics office in Orangeburg, and the firm
publishes no street address for this location. Saying so plainly matters: the
same insistence on accuracy governs every opinion the firm writes. What
counsel gets instead is a by-appointment practice built to travel. Meetings
take place at counsel's offices, in the evaluee's community, or by
videoconference, and most of the engagement, from records review through
report drafting, proceeds remotely on a defined schedule. When an in-person
evaluation is warranted, or when a deposition or trial setting requires
presence, travel is arranged from the Kansas City home office with enough lead
time to protect the disclosure calendar.

## Grounding opinions in the Orangeburg labor market

Employability and earning capacity conclusions for Orangeburg County evaluees
rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates
for the surrounding area and for South Carolina statewide, on state workforce
agency labor market information, and on direct labor market research where the
referral questions warrant it. A vocational opinion is only as strong as the
labor market it assumes, so each report identifies the evaluee's commutable
labor market explicitly and explains why that market, and not a statewide or
national abstraction, frames the transferable skills analysis and the
placement outlook. Where local conditions diverge from the statewide picture,
the report says so and shows the basis.
