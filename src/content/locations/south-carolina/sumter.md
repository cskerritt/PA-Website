A national practice should not treat a Sumter case differently from a case
in a much larger metro, and Purinton Analytics does not. The same written
methodology, the same testing protocols, and the same report standard the
firm applies across the country, described on its
[nationwide practice](/locations/nationwide/) page, govern every Sumter
engagement. Plaintiff and defense counsel in the metro retain the firm for
[earning capacity and employability evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/) in injury,
employment, and catastrophic damages litigation.

## Sumter County venue and the federal district

Engagements are accepted for matters venued in the Circuit Court (Court of
Common Pleas) sitting in Sumter County and in the United States District
Court for the District of South Carolina. Each engagement begins with a
documented conflict check, a defined scope, and a records request tailored
to the claims, so the written opinion rests on a record either side of the
caption can examine. Where a case also requires economic modeling, the firm
offers [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. Counsel with matters beyond Sumter County can orient
from the [South Carolina overview](/locations/south-carolina/), which maps
the same services across the state.

## Serving Sumter by appointment

Sumter meetings are by appointment. The firm has no staffed office in the
metro and publishes no street address here; describing its presence
accurately is part of the same discipline that opposing counsel will find in
its reports. Most referrals proceed remotely through records review,
structured vocational interviews, and standardized testing administered
under publisher protocols where remote administration is supported. When the
referral questions call for an in-person evaluation, it is conducted in the
evaluee's own community, sparing an injured evaluee unnecessary travel, and
attendance at depositions or trial is scheduled from the Kansas City home
office around the dates the court has set.

## The Sumter labor market on its own terms

Vocational findings for Sumter evaluees rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates for the Sumter area and for South
Carolina statewide, together with state workforce agency labor market
information and direct labor market research where warranted. A smaller
labor market deserves particular care: the analysis asks which occupations
actually exist within the evaluee's commutable area and what they truly pay,
because importing the occupational profile of a larger metro would overstate
or understate earning capacity depending on the case. Life care plans and
cost projections for Sumter County residents are priced to the local market
through provider-specific inquiries and published fee data, with care
availability treated as a question to research rather than assume.

## Beginning a Sumter engagement

To open a matter, submit the caption or party names through the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and note any imminent disclosure,
deposition, or trial dates so scheduling can be confirmed alongside
clearance. Inquiries from the plaintiff's side and the defense receive the
same review, the same methodology, and the same candor when the record will
not support the opinion requested. Referrals made early in the case leave
the most room for records collection and evaluation, but the intake
accommodates a compressed calendar when the court has already set one.
