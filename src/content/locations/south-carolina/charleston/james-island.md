A damages opinion is only as strong as the record beneath it, and for a James Island case that record is assembled deliberately: a records request tailored to the claims, treatment and employment documentation gathered before conclusions are drawn, and an interview protocol matched to the referral questions. Purinton Analytics builds its [vocational expert](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) and [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) work on that foundation for plaintiff and defense counsel alike, on the theory that a file both sides can examine is the only kind worth signing.

## County and courthouse context

James Island lies within Charleston County, so state-court damages litigation involving its residents proceeds in the Circuit Court, where civil claims are heard in the Court of Common Pleas. When a matter belongs in federal court, the United States District Court for the District of South Carolina serves as the available forum; confirming which venue controls, and whether it imposes any registration or qualification requirement on a testifying expert, happens during the conflict check and intake process rather than by assumption. The firm's engagement across the surrounding communities is described on the [Charleston metro page](/locations/south-carolina/charleston/), and its statewide reach on the [South Carolina locations page](/locations/south-carolina/).

## Earning capacity across the metro labor market

James Island residents work throughout the metro area, and the vocational question in most cases is what that regional market still offers a particular evaluee after injury or job loss. The firm addresses it with Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Charleston metropolitan area and for South Carolina statewide, supplemented by state workforce agency labor market information and by direct labor market research where the referral questions make it necessary. Findings are stated occupation by occupation, each tied to the evaluee's documented skills and limitations, and each figure in the report carries its source, so the opinion invites verification instead of resisting it. If the record leaves a question genuinely open, the report says so and explains what additional documentation would close it, a candor that costs little and earns credibility at deposition.

## How the work reaches the island

Counsel and evaluees are served by appointment: secure remote channels for records, interviews, and conferences; in-person evaluation in the evaluee's own community when the referral questions require it; and travel from the Kansas City home office when deposition or trial testimony calls for presence.
