## Reaching work from a rural town

For an evaluee in Ravenel, the labor market question is one of practical reach: what employment across the metro area is realistically attainable given the evaluee's location, skills, and restrictions. The firm answers with evidence rather than impression, using Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Charleston metropolitan area and for South Carolina statewide, state workforce agency labor market information, and direct labor market research when a referral question hinges on the availability of specific work. Each occupational finding is paired with its transferable skills rationale and its data source, so the opinion can be audited from the first page to the last. Where commuting distance itself limits the practical labor market, that limitation is analyzed openly and weighed in the conclusions rather than buried in a footnote.
