## Where Mount Pleasant cases are heard

Mount Pleasant lies in Charleston County, so state-court damages actions involving its residents proceed in the Circuit Court, with civil claims heard in the Court of Common Pleas. When a matter belongs in federal court, the United States District Court for the District of South Carolina stands available as the federal forum; which venue controls a given case, and what registration or qualification requirements follow from it, is worked out and confirmed during the conflict check and intake process. Counsel looking at the broader region will find the firm's footprint described on the [Charleston metro page](/locations/south-carolina/charleston/) and its statewide coverage on the [South Carolina locations page](/locations/south-carolina/).

## Measuring work within reach of an evaluee

A Mount Pleasant evaluee's employment prospects are not confined to the town itself; they extend across the surrounding metro labor market, and a credible earning capacity opinion has to reflect that commuting reality without exaggerating it. The firm anchors its vocational findings in Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Charleston metropolitan area and for South Carolina as a whole, adds state workforce agency labor market information, and performs direct labor market research when a referral question turns on the availability of specific work. The resulting report identifies the occupations at issue, explains the transferable skills reasoning, and sources every figure it uses.

## Appointments, not an office

There is no Purinton Analytics office in Mount Pleasant, and the firm says so plainly rather than implying a storefront it does not keep; its home office is in Kansas City, and every other location is served by appointment. Much of the work, from records analysis to structured interviews, is handled through secure remote channels. When the referral questions call for in-person evaluation, it is conducted in the evaluee's own community, and when depositions or trial testimony require presence, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office. If the damages theory requires economic analysis, [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) place that work with independent economist partners, who perform and sign the economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement structure. The same terms extend to counsel [nationwide](/locations/nationwide/).
