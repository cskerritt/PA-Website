## A small city inside a large labor market

Hanahan itself is a small city, but its workers move daily through the employment of the broader metro, and a defensible earning capacity opinion has to measure that wider market rather than the town limits. Vocational findings rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Charleston metropolitan area and for South Carolina statewide, on labor market information from the state workforce agency, and on direct labor market research where a referral question demands evidence about particular occupations. Every figure in the report carries a citation, and every occupational conclusion is tied to a documented transferable skills analysis.
