## Earning capacity when the town is not the market

Isle of Palms is a small coastal town, and few of its residents' vocational prospects begin and end at the town line; the relevant market is the metro economy around it. An earning capacity opinion for an Isle of Palms evaluee therefore draws on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Charleston metropolitan area and for South Carolina statewide, on state workforce agency labor market information, and on direct labor market research where the referral questions require proof about particular occupations. The report sets out which jobs were considered, why the evaluee's skills do or do not transfer to them, and where every figure came from. When seasonal or service-sector work histories complicate the earnings baseline, the report addresses that complication directly instead of smoothing it over, because an unexplained assumption is the first thing a capable cross-examiner will find.
