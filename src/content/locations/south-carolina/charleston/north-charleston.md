The metro carries a hyphenated name, Charleston-North Charleston, and the second half of that name is a large city in its own right. Purinton Analytics approaches North Charleston matters accordingly: its [vocational expert](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) and [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) opinions treat the city as its own community of evaluees rather than as an annex of its neighbor, and they are prepared for plaintiff and defense counsel on identical terms, written so that the lawyer who did not commission the report can probe every line of it without finding advocacy.

## Venue for North Charleston matters

North Charleston sits in Charleston County, and state-court damages litigation involving its residents and employers proceeds in the Circuit Court, where civil claims are heard in the Court of Common Pleas. For cases that belong in federal court, the United States District Court for the District of South Carolina is the available federal forum; the controlling venue for any particular matter, along with any registration or qualification requirement that venue imposes, is identified and confirmed during the conflict check and intake process. The firm's coverage of the surrounding region is described on the [Charleston metro page](/locations/south-carolina/charleston/), and the [South Carolina locations page](/locations/south-carolina/) collects its work across the state.

## Earning capacity for a big-city evaluee

A large city can tempt an analysis into arguing from abundance: surely, somewhere, there is work for this evaluee. A defensible opinion never adopts that assumption; it demonstrates what remains open, occupation by occupation. Vocational findings for North Charleston evaluees rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Charleston metropolitan area and for South Carolina statewide, supplemented by state workforce agency labor market information and by direct labor market research when the referral questions warrant it. Each opinion names the occupations considered, connects them to the evaluee's documented skills and restrictions through a transferable skills analysis, and cites a source for every figure, so the conclusion about residual earning capacity is shown rather than presumed, and its weight comes from the record it stands on.

## How evaluations are handled

The firm's home office is in Kansas City, and it keeps no staffed office in North Charleston and publishes no local street address; candor about presence belongs in an expert's file just as much as candor about method. Records review and interviews proceed through secure remote channels where that serves the evaluee, and in-person evaluation is arranged in the evaluee's own community when the referral questions require it. Deposition and trial appearances are scheduled from the Kansas City home office around counsel's calendar. Where the damages theory calls for economic modeling, [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) are available, with independent economist partners performing and signing the economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement structure. Counsel with matters beyond South Carolina can engage the firm [nationwide](/locations/nationwide/) on the same footing.

## Requesting a conflict check

Every engagement begins with clearance, not with records. Submit the caption or the party names through the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/), note any disclosure, deposition, or trial date already set, and hold confidential materials until intake confirms scope, venue, and turnaround. Counsel then knows before the file moves what questions the opinion will answer and how the analysis will be built.
