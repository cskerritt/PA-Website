## A suburb tied to a regional labor market

Summerville is a sizable suburb whose workers largely participate in the metro-wide labor market rather than a self-contained local one, and vocational analysis has to be honest about that. Earning capacity findings for a Summerville evaluee are grounded in Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Charleston metropolitan area and for South Carolina statewide, in state workforce agency labor market information, and, where the referral questions require it, in direct labor market research into the specific occupations at issue. The report shows its work: the occupations considered, the transferable skills analysis that connects the evaluee to them, and a cited source behind every figure.
