An injured or disabled person in Hollywood should not have to organize a recovery around trips to an expert's office. Purinton Analytics turns that logistics problem around: the evaluation comes to the case. Records and interviews move through secure remote channels where that serves the evaluee, and when the referral questions require face-to-face assessment, the [vocational expert](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) or [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) evaluation is arranged in the evaluee's own community. Plaintiff and defense counsel receive the same accommodation and the same standard of written work.

## Where a Hollywood matter is litigated

Hollywood is part of Charleston County, and state-court damages actions involving the town's residents proceed in the Circuit Court, whose civil docket is heard in the Court of Common Pleas. The United States District Court for the District of South Carolina is the available federal forum for cases that belong there; the venue that actually governs, and any expert registration or qualification requirement that comes with it, is identified and confirmed during the conflict check and intake process. The firm's broader regional practice is set out on the [Charleston metro page](/locations/south-carolina/charleston/), and the [South Carolina locations page](/locations/south-carolina/) shows its coverage of the rest of the state.

## Small-town evaluees, regional data

Hollywood is a small town, and the vocational analysis for a small-town evaluee has to be honest in both directions: it cannot pretend the local job base is larger than it is, and it cannot ignore the employment within realistic commuting reach across the metro. The firm holds that line with Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Charleston metropolitan area and for South Carolina statewide, with state workforce agency labor market information, and with direct labor market research when a referral question turns on whether specific work is actually attainable. Conclusions are documented occupation by occupation, with the transferable skills reasoning and the data source stated for each. Where a life care plan is in play, future care needs are itemized against the treating record and priced from cited sources, so the plan reads as evidence rather than as a wish list.

## What presence means here

Purinton Analytics keeps its home office in Kansas City and no office in Hollywood; it publishes no local street address because it has none to publish, and the firm treats that candor as part of its method. Meetings are by appointment. Depositions, trial testimony, and in-person evaluations that require the expert on the ground are handled through travel scheduled from the Kansas City home office. When the damages theory calls for economic quantification, [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) add independent economist partners who perform and sign the economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement structure. Counsel outside the region can engage the firm [nationwide](/locations/nationwide/) on the same terms.
