## Vocational evidence for a Berkeley County jury

A damages opinion read aloud in a Berkeley County courtroom should describe work a local jury would recognize. The firm therefore builds earning capacity findings for Goose Creek evaluees on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Charleston metropolitan area and for South Carolina statewide, on state workforce agency labor market information, and on direct labor market research when a referral question turns on whether particular jobs actually exist within reach. The written opinion names its occupations, explains its transferable skills reasoning, and attaches a source to every figure, which is what lets it survive cross-examination intact. Where restrictions are disputed, the analysis presents its conclusions against the medical record as counsel has developed it, so the trier of fact can see exactly how each functional assumption changes the vocational outcome.
