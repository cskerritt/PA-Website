The true audience for an expert report in a Moncks Corner case is not the lawyer who commissioned it; it is the lawyer preparing to take it apart. Purinton Analytics writes to that reader. Its [vocational expert](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) and [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) opinions are drafted as if every assumption will be read back at deposition, because in a well-tried case each of them will be. The firm takes retention from either side of the caption, and the report looks the same either way: defined scope, documented record, sourced figures.

## Venue in and around Berkeley County

Moncks Corner sits in Berkeley County, and state-court damages claims arising there are litigated in the Circuit Court, with civil matters heard in the Court of Common Pleas. For disputes that belong in federal court, the United States District Court for the District of South Carolina is the available forum, and the venue that controls a particular case, along with any expert qualification or registration requirement it brings, is identified and confirmed during the conflict check and intake process. The [Charleston metro page](/locations/south-carolina/charleston/) sets out the firm's regional coverage, and the [South Carolina locations page](/locations/south-carolina/) does the same for the state.

## The geographic unit behind the labor data

Labor market data is published for defined geographies, and a careful report is explicit about which one each of its sources actually describes. For Moncks Corner evaluees the firm relies on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates, published for the Charleston metropolitan area and for South Carolina statewide, on labor market information from the state workforce agency, and, when a referral question requires evidence finer-grained than published estimates provide, on direct labor market research into the specific occupations at issue. The written opinion states the geographic level of every source it uses, sets out the occupations considered and the transferable skills reasoning that connects the evaluee to them, and attaches a citation to every figure, so no conclusion rests on an unstated assumption about where the data comes from or what it covers.

## Presence stated accurately

Purinton Analytics is candid that its home office is in Kansas City and that it staffs no office in Moncks Corner and publishes no local address. Engagements are structured accordingly. Secure remote channels carry the records work, interviews, and case conferences; when the referral questions require an in-person evaluation, it is arranged in the evaluee's own community; and when a deposition or trial date demands the expert in the room, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office. Cases needing economic loss calculations proceed as [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/), in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement structure. Counsel anywhere in the country can retain the firm [nationwide](/locations/nationwide/) on identical terms.
