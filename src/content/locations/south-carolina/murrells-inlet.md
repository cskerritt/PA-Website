Every report Purinton Analytics prepares for a Murrells Inlet matter is
written on the assumption that opposing counsel will read it line by line.
That standard shapes the whole practice: opinions are documented, methodology
is disclosed, and conclusions follow from a record both sides can examine.
Counsel in Georgetown County retain the firm on that basis for
[vocational evaluation and expert testimony](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)

while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement
structure. Because the same discipline serves plaintiff and defense
engagements alike, the report does not change depending on who retained it.

## Where Murrells Inlet cases are heard

Engagements are accepted for matters in the Circuit Court (Court of Common
Pleas) for Georgetown County and in the United States District Court for the
District of South Carolina. In both forums the firm delivers written opinions
prepared for disclosure, testimony at deposition and trial, and
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) when an opposing
expert's report needs to be measured against the methodology it invokes.
Counsel with matters elsewhere in the state can consult the firm's
[South Carolina page](/locations/south-carolina/) for the statewide practice,
which follows the same structure.

## Presence in Murrells Inlet, described accurately

Accuracy about offices belongs to the same discipline that governs the
reports, so the firm states its footprint plainly: there is no staffed
Purinton Analytics office in Murrells Inlet, and no street address is
published for this location. Meetings are by appointment, held at counsel's
offices, at a location convenient to the evaluee, or by videoconference.
Records review, analysis, and drafting proceed remotely on a defined
schedule. When the referral questions call for in-person evaluation, or when
deposition and trial settings require presence, travel is scheduled from the
Kansas City home office with lead time built around the disclosure calendar.

## Labor market research for Georgetown County evaluees

Employability and earning capacity findings for evaluees in the Murrells
Inlet area rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and
wage estimates for the surrounding area and for South Carolina statewide,
state workforce agency labor market information, and direct labor market
research where the referral warrants it. The report identifies the evaluee's
commutable labor market and explains why that market, rather than a statewide
or national composite, controls the transferable skills analysis and the
placement outlook. Where local employment conditions differ from the
statewide picture, the report says so and documents the basis, because an
unexplained assumption is exactly what a careful cross-examiner looks for.
