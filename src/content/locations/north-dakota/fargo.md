The Fargo, ND-MN metropolitan area sits astride a state line, and the
litigation it produces reflects that geography: a caption may land in a North
Dakota courtroom or a Minnesota one while the evaluee's working life spans
both. Purinton Analytics serves plaintiff and defense counsel throughout the
metro with [vocational expert evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. The approach is the same discipline the firm applies
[across North Dakota](/locations/north-dakota/) and in every other venue it
serves.

## A venue footprint on both sides of the state line

The metro takes in Cass County, North Dakota, and Clay County, Minnesota,
and the firm accepts matters venued in the District Court in each. Federal
matters arising on the North Dakota side proceed in the United States
District Court for the District of North Dakota. Because the written opinion
rests on records, standardized testing, and published data rather than on
local presence, the same report serves either state's courts, and counsel on
either side of the caption receive the same analysis from the same record.
Any registration or qualification requirement particular to a venue is
identified and confirmed during the conflict check and intake process.

## Meetings by appointment, not a local office

There is no staffed Purinton Analytics office in Fargo, and the firm
publishes no street address for this location, because accuracy about
presence belongs to the same discipline that governs its reports. Most
engagements proceed remotely from intake through disclosure: secure records
transfer, structured vocational interviews by video, and standardized
testing administered under publisher protocols where remote administration
is validated. When the referral calls for in-person evaluation of an
evaluee, or when deposition and trial calendars require presence in a Fargo
courtroom, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office and planned
around the court's dates.

## Labor market research for a cross-border economy

Employability and earning capacity findings for evaluees in the metro rest
on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for
the Fargo metropolitan area and for North Dakota and Minnesota statewide,
supplemented by state workforce agency labor market information and direct
labor market research where the referral questions warrant it. That grounding
matters here because an evaluee who lives in Clay County may realistically
seek work in Cass County and the reverse; the analysis treats the metro as
the labor market it actually is rather than stopping at the state line, and
it presents placement prospects in terms opposing counsel can trace back to
published sources.

## Starting a Fargo engagement

Early retention protects the record on either side of the caption: it allows
the vocational interview, testing, and labor market research to be completed
before disclosure deadlines compress the schedule, and it gives a life care
plan time to reflect treating-provider input rather than assumption. To
begin, submit the caption or party names through the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and note any imminent disclosure,
deposition, or trial dates. Counsel with matters beyond the metro can review
the firm's [nationwide practice](/locations/nationwide/), which operates on
the same remote-first model with travel scheduled wherever the case requires
it.
