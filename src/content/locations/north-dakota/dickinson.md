Disclosure deadlines shape damages work more than geography does. The
difference between a defensible earning capacity opinion and a rushed one in
a Dickinson case is usually the date the expert was retained, not the
distance the expert traveled. Purinton Analytics offers plaintiff and
defense counsel in the Dickinson micropolitan area
[vocational expert evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure, on the same terms the firm applies
[across North Dakota](/locations/north-dakota/).

## Courts and counties around Dickinson

The Dickinson micropolitan area takes in Stark County, Billings County, and
Dunn County, and the firm accepts matters venued in the District Court in
each of them. Federal matters proceed in the United States District Court
for the District of North Dakota. Retention by either side of the caption
produces the same report from the same record, and any venue-specific
registration or qualification requirement is identified and confirmed during
the conflict check and intake process, before the schedule starts to run.

## Presence by appointment, scheduled to the case

The firm keeps no staffed office in Dickinson and publishes no street
address for this location; stating that plainly is part of the same
discipline that governs the reports. The working model separates what
travels from what does not. Records review, structured vocational
interviews by video, and standardized testing under publisher protocols
where remote administration is validated all proceed without regard to
distance. In-person evaluation of an evaluee, depositions, and trial
appearances are scheduled as travel from the Kansas City home office,
planned against the court's calendar so that presence arrives when the case
needs it rather than when it is convenient. Counsel work with the same point
of contact and receive the same report standard at every stage, regardless
of where any individual step of the engagement is performed.

## Labor market data behind the opinions

Employability and earning capacity findings for evaluees in the Dickinson
area rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage
estimates covering the Dickinson area and North Dakota statewide, together
with state workforce agency labor market information and direct labor market
research where the referral questions warrant it. Life care plans and
medical cost projections are priced to the evaluee's own market through
provider-specific inquiries and published fee data for the region, so the
projected cost of care reflects the community where the evaluee will
actually receive it.

## Getting a Dickinson matter started on time

Because timing drives quality, the intake process is deliberately fast to
initiate: submit the caption or party names through the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/), identify the venue, and note any
disclosure, deposition, or trial dates already set. Conflicts, scope, and
any qualification questions are resolved in writing before substantive work
begins. Counsel whose matters sit outside the Dickinson area can review the
firm's [nationwide practice](/locations/nationwide/), which brings the same
remote-first model and the same scheduling discipline to every venue it
serves.
