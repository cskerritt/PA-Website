Most of the work in a vocational or life care planning engagement happens
away from any courthouse: in medical and employment records, in structured
interviews, in standardized testing, and in published labor market data.
That is why distance from Grand Forks does not thin the analysis. Purinton
Analytics serves plaintiff and defense counsel across the Grand Forks, ND-MN
metropolitan area with
[earning capacity and employability evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure, applying the same methodology used
[across North Dakota](/locations/north-dakota/) and nationally.

## Where Grand Forks matters are venued

The metro pairs Grand Forks County, North Dakota, with Polk County,
Minnesota, and the firm accepts matters venued in the District Court in
each. Federal matters arising on the North Dakota side proceed in the United
States District Court for the District of North Dakota. Any venue-specific
registration or qualification requirement, in either state, is identified
and confirmed during the conflict check and intake process, so counsel know
before retention that the expert can serve the forum the caption actually
sits in.

## Remote-first, with honest logistics

The firm maintains no staffed office in Grand Forks and publishes no street
address for this location. Engagements typically begin and often finish
remotely: secure records transfer at intake, vocational interviews conducted
by video, and testing administered under publisher protocols where remote
administration is validated. What does not travel by video is handled in
person; when a referral requires face-to-face evaluation of an evaluee, or
when a deposition or trial requires presence, travel is scheduled from the
Kansas City home office and built around the court's dates. Counsel receive
the same report standard and the same point of contact regardless of where
any given step of the work is performed.

## Reading the Grand Forks labor market

Employability and earning capacity findings for evaluees in the metro rest
on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for
the Grand Forks metropolitan area and for North Dakota and Minnesota
statewide, supplemented by state workforce agency labor market information
and direct labor market research where warranted. Because the metro spans
the state line, the analysis considers the labor market an evaluee actually
faces rather than the portion that shares the evaluee's mailing address, and
every placement conclusion is written so opposing counsel can trace it to a
published source. Where the referral questions require it, direct employer
contact supplements the published estimates with documented, current
openings and wage information.

## Retaining the firm for a Grand Forks case

The strongest expert files are the ones opened early, while there is still
time for the interview, testing, and labor market research to inform the
report rather than merely decorate it. To explore availability, submit the
caption or party names through the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and note any imminent disclosure,
deposition, or trial dates. Matters beyond the metro are served through the
firm's [nationwide practice](/locations/nationwide/) on the same
remote-first terms.
