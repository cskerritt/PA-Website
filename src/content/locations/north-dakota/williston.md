## Venue context in Williams County

The Williston micropolitan area consists of Williams County, and the firm
accepts matters venued in the District Court there as well as federal
matters in the United States District Court for the District of North
Dakota. Nothing about the report changes with the size of the venue: the
methodology, the sourcing, and the willingness to show the path from record
to conclusion are identical whether the opinion will be read by a jury in
Williams County or by a federal judge, and identical whichever side of the
caption issued the retention.

## How the firm serves a distant venue

There is no staffed Purinton Analytics office in Williston, and the firm
publishes no street address for this location; plain honesty about presence
is part of the discipline counsel are retaining. Distance is managed by
design rather than improvisation. Records move by secure transfer at
intake, vocational interviews are conducted by video, and standardized
testing is administered under publisher protocols where remote
administration is validated. When the referral questions require in-person
evaluation of an evaluee, or when deposition and trial dates require
presence in a Williston courtroom, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City
home office and planned around the court's dates.

## Labor market evidence for Williston evaluees

Employability and earning capacity findings for evaluees in the area rest on
Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates
covering the Williston area and North Dakota statewide, supplemented by
state workforce agency labor market information and direct labor market
research where the referral questions warrant it. In a regional economy
whose realistic opportunities may sit inside Williams County or beyond it,
that direct research matters: the written opinion describes the work an
evaluee can actually obtain, at wages the published data will support, in
terms opposing counsel can verify rather than merely dispute.
