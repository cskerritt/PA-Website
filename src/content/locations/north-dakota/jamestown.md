the evaluee's own community. For a Jamestown evaluee, that means costing
future care to what treatment, equipment, and support services actually cost
where the evaluee lives, not to a national average that no local provider
charges. Purinton Analytics brings that grounding to

## Stutsman County venue context

The Jamestown micropolitan area consists of Stutsman County, and the firm
accepts matters venued in the District Court there as well as federal
matters in the United States District Court for the District of North
Dakota. The report that reaches either forum is built to the same standard:
methodology stated, sources cited, and the path from record to conclusion
left visible, so that the analysis holds up whether it was commissioned by
plaintiff or defense counsel. Any venue-specific registration or
qualification requirement is identified and confirmed during the conflict
check and intake process.

## Appointments, not an office

The firm maintains no staffed office in Jamestown and publishes no street
address for this location; describing its presence accurately is part of the
same discipline that governs its opinions. Most engagement steps proceed
remotely: secure records transfer at intake, structured vocational
interviews by video, and standardized testing administered under publisher
protocols where remote administration is validated. Where the referral
requires it, the work comes to the evaluee and to the courtroom: in-person
evaluations, depositions, and trial appearances are scheduled as travel from
the Kansas City home office, planned around the court's dates.
