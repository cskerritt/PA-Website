In the Wahpeton micropolitan area, the labor market does not stop at the
state line even though jurisdiction does. An evaluee may live in Richland
County, North Dakota, and hold a job in Wilkin County, Minnesota, or the
reverse, and a credible damages analysis has to account for that reality
rather than truncate it. Purinton Analytics brings that cross-border
perspective to
[vocational expert evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
for plaintiff and defense counsel throughout the area, with independent
economist partners performing and signing the economic analysis while the
vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement structure,
consistent with the firm's practice
[across North Dakota](/locations/north-dakota/).

## The venue question is settled at intake

The area comprises Richland County, North Dakota, and Wilkin County,
Minnesota, and the firm accepts matters venued in the District Court in
each. Federal matters arising on the North Dakota side proceed in the United
States District Court for the District of North Dakota. Which state's court
the caption sits in is settled long before the report is written: any
venue-specific registration or qualification requirement, in either state,
is identified and confirmed during the conflict check and intake process, so
retention never outruns eligibility to testify.

## Served by appointment from the Kansas City home office

The firm has no staffed office in Wahpeton and publishes no street address
for this location, and it says so plainly because honesty about presence is
part of the product counsel are buying. The working model is remote-first:
secure records transfer, structured vocational interviews by video, and
standardized testing under publisher protocols where remote administration
is validated. When the referral questions require evaluating an evaluee in
person, or when deposition and trial dates require an appearance, travel is
scheduled from the Kansas City home office and planned around the court's
calendar.

## Analyzing a labor market that straddles a state line

Employability and earning capacity findings for Wahpeton area evaluees rest
on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates
covering the Wahpeton area and both North Dakota and Minnesota statewide,
supplemented by state workforce agency labor market information from each
state and direct labor market research where warranted. Treating the area as
a single working economy, rather than as separate half-markets divided by a
boundary an employer never sees, produces placement and wage conclusions
that match how residents actually work. Every such conclusion is written so
opposing counsel can trace it to a published source, and where the published
estimates leave a gap, direct employer contact documents what the data
cannot.
