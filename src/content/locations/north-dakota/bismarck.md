A Bismarck engagement opens the way engagements open everywhere in this
practice: with a documented conflict check, a written scope, and a records
request shaped by the claims, so that the eventual opinion rests on a record
both sides can examine. From that foundation, Purinton Analytics provides
plaintiff and defense counsel in the Bismarck metro with
[vocational expert evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) in
injury and employment matters,
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) in catastrophic injury
cases, and [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. The same standards govern the firm's work
[throughout North Dakota](/locations/north-dakota/).

## Courts served in the Bismarck metro

The Bismarck metropolitan area comprises Burleigh County, Morton County, and
Oliver County, and the firm accepts matters venued in the District Court in
each of them. Federal matters proceed in the United States District Court
for the District of North Dakota. The report format does not change with the
forum: a state court jury and a federal bench receive the same methodology,
the same sourcing, and the same willingness to show how each conclusion was
reached, which is what allows the identical analysis to serve retention by
either side of the caption.

## Appointment-based presence, stated plainly

The firm keeps no staffed office in Bismarck and publishes no street address
for this location; candor about presence is part of the same discipline that
governs the reports themselves. In practice, most of the work travels well:
records review, structured interviews conducted by video, and standardized
vocational testing under publisher protocols where remote administration is
validated. When a referral requires in-person evaluation, or when deposition
and trial dates call for a seat in a Bismarck courtroom, travel is arranged
from the Kansas City home office and scheduled to the court's calendar
rather than the firm's convenience.

## Grounding opinions in the Bismarck metro labor market

Vocational findings for evaluees in the metro rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Bismarck
metropolitan area and for North Dakota statewide, together with state
workforce agency labor market information and direct labor market research
where the referral questions warrant it. Life care plans and medical cost
projections for area residents are priced to the evaluee's own market using
provider-specific inquiries and published fee data for the region, so that
future care costs reflect what care actually costs where the evaluee lives
rather than a national abstraction.

The conflict check that begins every engagement also protects it: scope,
parties, and deadlines are confirmed in writing before any substantive work
starts, and any venue-specific registration or qualification requirement is
identified during that intake process. To open a Bismarck matter,
[request a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) with the case caption and party
names, and flag any disclosure, deposition, or trial dates already on the
calendar. Counsel handling cases outside the metro can consult the firm's
[national coverage](/locations/nationwide/), which runs on the same
remote-first model with travel wherever the case requires.
