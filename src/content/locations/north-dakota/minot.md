A forensic report earns its keep at deposition. Every opinion Purinton
Analytics writes for a Minot matter is drafted on the assumption that
opposing counsel will read it line by line, check its sources, and
cross-examine its author, which is why the firm's
[vocational expert evaluations](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care plans](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
are built from records, standardized methodology, and published data rather
than impression. In coordinated engagements, independent economist partners
perform and sign the economic analysis while the vocational and
care-planning work proceeds under one engagement structure. Plaintiff and
defense counsel alike retain the firm here on the same terms it offers
[across North Dakota](/locations/north-dakota/).

## The Minot area's courts

The Minot metropolitan area takes in Ward County, McHenry County, and
Renville County, and the firm accepts matters venued in the District Court
in each of them. Federal matters proceed in the United States District Court
for the District of North Dakota. Because the same disciplined report serves
either forum, a change of venue does not change the analysis, and any
venue-specific registration or qualification requirement is identified and
confirmed during the conflict check and intake process before substantive
work begins.

## No storefront, and no pretense of one

The firm keeps no staffed office in Minot and publishes no street address
for this location. That candor is deliberate: an expert whose report will be
tested under oath should not imply a presence that does not exist. What
counsel get instead is a practice built to serve the venue from a distance
without losing anything material: secure records transfer, structured
vocational interviews by video, standardized testing under publisher
protocols where remote administration is validated, and travel from the
Kansas City home office whenever an in-person evaluation, deposition, or
trial appearance requires it, scheduled around the court's calendar.

## Sourcing the labor market analysis

Employability and earning capacity findings for Minot area evaluees rest on
Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates
covering the Minot area and North Dakota statewide, supplemented by state
workforce agency labor market information and direct labor market research
where the referral questions warrant it. Life care plans and medical cost
projections are priced to the evaluee's own market using provider-specific
inquiries and published fee data for the region. The point of that sourcing
is durability: a conclusion that survives cross-examination is one the
examiner can follow from the report back to the data, and the reports are
organized so that the path is short and plainly marked.

[request a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) with the case caption and party
names, and note any imminent disclosure, deposition, or trial dates. Counsel
with matters elsewhere can review the firm's
