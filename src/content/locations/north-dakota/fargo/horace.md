## Cass County courts and the federal forum

Horace lies in Cass County, and civil matters under North Dakota law are
heard there in the District Court. Where a claim raises a federal question
or otherwise carries a federal footing, the United States District Court
for the District of North Dakota is the district available within the
state. The firm does not presume which bench will ultimately control a
particular case; the governing venue, and any expert registration or
qualification requirement that venue imposes, is identified and confirmed
during the conflict check and intake. Every report is written to hold up in
whichever forum receives it, with reproducible methods, cited sources, and
conclusions that stay inside the disclosed scope. Coverage across the
surrounding communities appears on the
[Fargo metro page](/locations/north-dakota/fargo/), and the statewide

## Reading the labor market from a small town

The Bureau of Labor Statistics publishes its occupational employment and
wage estimates for metropolitan areas and for states, not for individual
small towns, and Horace is a small town. That gap is where a careless
opinion goes wrong. The published estimates for the metropolitan area that
includes Fargo and for North Dakota statewide describe the economy an
evaluee in Horace might reach; whether this particular evaluee can actually
reach it is a separate question, and it is answered from the record rather
than from the map. The vocational interview, the documented work history,
and the medical restrictions in the file establish what commute, schedule,
and physical demands the person can sustain, and the transferable-skills
analysis is then run against the market those findings genuinely support,
not the largest market available on paper. State workforce agency labor
market information and direct employer contact supplement the federal
estimates when the referral questions require that depth. The finished
opinion names the market it used and explains why, in terms a reader on
either side of the caption can test for themselves.

## Logistics settled at intake, not discovered later

Whether an engagement needs an in-person component is decided at intake,
as part of the same calendar the whole engagement is built on. Many
referral questions resolve fully within the remote-first model: records
arrive electronically, review conferences and attorney discussions run over
secure video, and the vocational interview itself is often conducted the
same way. Some questions cannot be answered at a distance, and the intake
conversation says so plainly. A life care planning referral that turns on
how a person manages a household, or an evaluation in which daily function
has to be seen firsthand, is scheduled in the evaluee's own community, with
travel from the Kansas City home office planned into the engagement
calendar from the start rather than bolted on at the end. The firm keeps no
office in Horace and does not suggest otherwise; the candor that governs
the reports governs the description of presence too. Deposition and trial
testimony follow the dates the court has set, in whatever form the court
requires.
