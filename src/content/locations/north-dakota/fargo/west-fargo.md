When a claim turns on what a West Fargo worker can still earn, the labor
market in question does not stop at the city line. West Fargo is a large
suburb inside a commuting market that reaches across the North Dakota and
Minnesota sides of the metro, and an earning-capacity opinion that ignores
how freely workers move within it will not survive a careful
cross-examination. Purinton Analytics is retained by plaintiff counsel and
defense counsel for
[vocational expert opinions](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) on
employability and earning capacity,
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) after catastrophic
injury, and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Venue for West Fargo matters

West Fargo sits in Cass County, where civil actions under North Dakota law
are heard in the District Court. For claims with a federal footing, the
United States District Court for the District of North Dakota is the forum
available within the state; because the metro itself crosses a state line,
which court will actually control a given dispute is a question the firm
never assumes, and the controlling venue, along with any registration or
qualification requirement it imposes on a testifying expert, is confirmed
during the conflict check and intake. Regional coverage is described on the
[Fargo metro page](/locations/north-dakota/fargo/), and statewide practice
on the [North Dakota page](/locations/north-dakota/).

## A suburb that is also a job market

The analytical mistake to avoid in a West Fargo case is treating the town
as a bedroom community defined entirely by the principal city. Opinions
here begin from Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage
estimates for the Fargo, ND-MN metropolitan area and for North Dakota
statewide, supplemented by state workforce agency labor market information
and by direct employer research where the referral questions warrant it.
The transferable-skills analysis then states, and defends, the geographic
frame it applies: whether the evaluee's restrictions, transportation, and
documented work history support access to the full metro market or confine
the search more narrowly. Both sides of the caption benefit from that
discipline, because a geographic frame adopted for convenience is precisely
where an opposing expert will press.

## How the work reaches West Fargo

The firm maintains no office in West Fargo and says so plainly; candor
about presence is part of the same standard that governs the written
opinions. Engagements are by appointment and most proceed remotely, with
secure video carrying records conferences, counsel discussions, and the
bulk of vocational interviewing. Where an in-person evaluation is justified
by the referral questions, it takes place in the evaluee's own community,
so that daily function and actual access to services are observed rather
than assumed, with travel scheduled from the Kansas City home office. The
same planning applies to deposition and trial appearances, which are built
around the dates the court has set rather than the expert's convenience.
