When the injured party lives in a small community like Lincoln, the first
practical question counsel asks is logistical: does retaining a national
expert mean dragging the evaluee to a distant office, or does the expert
come to the evaluee? Purinton Analytics built its practice around the
second answer. The work is remote-first, evaluations happen where the
evaluee actually lives, and the standard of the written product does not
vary with the size of the town on the caption. Plaintiff and defense
counsel alike retain the firm for
[vocational expert opinions](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) on
employability and earning capacity, for
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) after serious injury,
and for
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work runs under a single
engagement structure.

## Where a Lincoln case is heard

Lincoln sits in Burleigh County, and civil actions under North Dakota law
are heard there in the District Court. For claims with a federal footing,
the United States District Court for the District of North Dakota is the
forum available within the state; the venue that will actually govern a
particular case, along with any expert registration or qualification
requirement it carries, is identified and confirmed during the conflict
check and intake rather than assumed at the outset. Whatever the bench,
the report is built to be examined: transparent methodology, complete
source citations, and conclusions that do not wander past the questions
counsel disclosed. Regional coverage is described on the
[Bismarck metro page](/locations/north-dakota/bismarck/), and the
statewide practice on the [North Dakota page](/locations/north-dakota/).

## A small town inside a metro labor market

Few Lincoln residents work only in Lincoln. The community functions as
part of the commuting market anchored by Bismarck, the metro's principal
city, and an honest earning-capacity analysis begins by saying so. The
report defines the market open to this particular evaluee, then supports
that definition with restrictions, transportation, and work history
rather than assumption, because the market question is where opposing
counsel will press first. Vocational findings rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Bismarck,
ND metropolitan area and for North Dakota statewide, supplemented by
state workforce agency labor market information and by direct employer
research when the referral questions require it. Numbers never substitute
for judgment: the analysis explains why a given occupation is or is not
realistically available, not merely that it exists in a table.

## Evaluation without the travel burden

There is no Purinton Analytics office in Lincoln, and the firm does not
pretend otherwise; every meeting is by appointment, and candor about
presence is part of the credibility the reports depend on. Most
engagements proceed entirely by secure video, from records conferences
through vocational interviews, which spares a Lincoln evaluee any travel
at all. Where an in-person evaluation is justified by the referral
questions, the evaluator comes to the evaluee's community and observes
daily function, the home environment, and actual access to services
firsthand, with travel arranged from the Kansas City home office.
Deposition and trial testimony are scheduled around the court's own
calendar so the opinion is ready when the case needs it.
