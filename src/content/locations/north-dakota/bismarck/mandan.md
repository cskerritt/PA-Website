A case that arises in Mandan is, for venue purposes, a Morton County case,
and that distinction matters even though the city belongs to the Bismarck,
ND metropolitan area. The caption, the jury pool, and the courthouse
rhythm belong to Morton County, not to the principal city across the metro,
and an expert report that treats the two as interchangeable invites
avoidable cross-examination. Purinton Analytics is retained by plaintiff
and defense counsel in Mandan matters for
[vocational expert opinions](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) on
employability and earning capacity,
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) after catastrophic
injury, and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Morton County venue, state and federal

Civil actions under North Dakota law arising in Mandan are heard in the
District Court serving Morton County. Where a claim carries diversity or
federal-question footing, the United States District Court for the
District of North Dakota is the forum available within the state; which
bench actually controls a given matter, and any registration or
qualification requirement that venue imposes on a testifying expert, is
identified and confirmed during the conflict check and intake. The written
work is prepared to the same standard either way: methods another expert
can reproduce, sources cited in full, and opinions that stay inside the
disclosed scope. Counsel handling matters across the wider region will
find that coverage described on the
[Bismarck metro page](/locations/north-dakota/bismarck/), and the statewide
practice on the [North Dakota page](/locations/north-dakota/).

## Earning capacity on the Morton County side of the metro

Mandan is a city of meaningful size in its own right, yet its workers
participate in a labor market organized around the metro as a whole, with
Bismarck as its principal city. An earning-capacity opinion for a Mandan
evaluee therefore has to state which market the analysis assumes: work
within Mandan itself, the broader commuting market that includes Bismarck,
or mobility beyond the region. Vocational findings rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Bismarck, ND
metropolitan area and for North Dakota statewide, on state workforce
agency labor market information, and on direct labor market research where
the referral questions warrant it. The chosen framing is then defended
with the evaluee's documented restrictions, transportation, and work
history, because a market definition adopted for convenience is the
softest target in the report.

## Meeting a Mandan evaluee

The firm keeps no office in Mandan and publishes no local address, because
accuracy about presence belongs to the same discipline that governs the
opinions themselves. Meetings are by appointment. Records review,
conferences with counsel, and most vocational interviews proceed over
secure video, which keeps the schedule in counsel's control. When the
referral questions call for an in-person evaluation, it takes place in the
evaluee's own community, where home layout, daily function, and real
access to care and services can be observed directly, with travel
scheduled from the Kansas City home office. Deposition and trial
appearances are planned around the dates the court has set, in Morton
County or wherever the matter is pending.
