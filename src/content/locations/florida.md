Florida's federal trial work alone is divided among Northern, Middle, and
Southern districts, and its state-court damages docket reaches from
Pensacola to Miami, so counsel here often need an expert whose work holds
up across more than a home venue. Purinton Analytics accepts
vocational expert and life care planning engagements from plaintiff and
defense counsel throughout Florida, and it structures each engagement so
that the opinion holds up wherever the case is heard: the same records
discipline, the same testing protocols, and the same publication standard
from one end of the state to the other.

District of Florida, the United States District Court for the Middle
District of Florida, and the United States District Court for the

## How Florida matters are staffed and worked

The firm's home office is in Kansas City, and it maintains no staffed
office and publishes no street address anywhere in Florida. Florida
engagements begin remotely, as most of the firm's work does: records
review, structured vocational interviews, and standardized testing under
publisher protocols where remote administration is supported. When the
referral questions call for in-person evaluation of a Florida evaluee, or
when deposition and trial dates require presence, travel is scheduled
from the Kansas City home office around the court's calendar. Vocational
findings rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and
wage estimates for Florida and its metropolitan areas, state workforce
agency labor market information, and direct labor market research where
warranted. Where the damages picture also requires forensic economics,
the firm offers coordinated damages engagements in which independent
economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis while the
vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement
structure. Every Florida file runs on the intake, conflict, and quality
standards of the firm's [nationwide practice](/locations/nationwide/).

## Qualification and disclosure in Florida forums

An expert opinion is only as useful as its admissibility, and the firm
addresses that before the engagement begins rather than after the
opinion is served. Any venue-specific registration or qualification
requirement is identified and confirmed during the conflict check and
intake process. Disclosure materials, including the expert's
qualifications, prior testimony, and compensation terms, are prepared to
the standard the presiding court and the scheduling order require,
whether the matter sits in a Circuit Court or in any of Florida's
federal districts.

## Starting a Florida engagement

For plaintiff and defense counsel alike, a Florida engagement opens with
a conflict check. Submit the caption or party names through the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and note any imminent disclosure,
deposition, or trial dates, along with the circuit or district where the
case is pending. The firm responds with a defined scope, a records
request tailored to the claims, and a realistic schedule for evaluation,
reporting, and testimony.
