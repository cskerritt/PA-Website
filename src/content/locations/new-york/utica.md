## Presence by appointment, described accurately

Purinton Analytics keeps no staffed office in Utica or Rome and publishes
no street address for this location, because precision about presence is
part of the same standard that governs its reports. Meetings with counsel
are arranged by appointment at counsel's offices or by secure video, and
evaluation sessions are held in the evaluee's community or remotely under
publisher protocols. When an engagement calls for in-person evaluation,

Kansas City home office and planned against the disclosure calendar

## Reading the Utica-Rome labor market honestly

Employability and earning capacity findings rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Utica-Rome
metropolitan area and for New York statewide, on state workforce agency
labor market information, and on direct labor market research, including
employer contact and job posting analysis, when the referral questions
require evidence about actual hiring. In a market this size, the
difference between published estimates and real openings can decide the
credibility of an opinion, so the analysis documents where each figure in
the underlying data comes from and what additional research was done to
test it. That transparency serves plaintiff and defense counsel equally,
because it lets either side verify the work.
