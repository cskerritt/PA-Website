## Sourcing the labor market opinion

Vocational findings for a New Paltz evaluee rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Kingston metropolitan area and for New York statewide, on state workforce agency labor market information, and on direct labor market research when the referral questions call for it. The sources alone are not the whole discipline; the rest is the fit between source and person: the restrictions the medical record actually supports, the skills a specific work history transfers, and the employment those sources show is realistically available to someone with that profile. Every citation in the report is specific enough for opposing counsel to retrieve the underlying data and check the reading, which is precisely the test the firm expects the opinion to face.
