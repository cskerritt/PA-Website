## Tompkins County venue for Cayuga Heights matters

Cayuga Heights sits in Tompkins County, and the Supreme Court, New York's trial court of general jurisdiction, hears state-court civil claims arising there. Federal practice presents a wider map. A federal claim may proceed in the United States District Court for the Northern District of New York, the United States District Court for the Southern District of New York, the United States District Court for the Eastern District of New York, or the United States District Court for the Western District of New York, depending on where venue properly lies. Sorting that out is a question of law, and it belongs to counsel. What the expert owes counsel is a report that fits its destination, so intake starts by asking which court will hear the matter. The records request, the disclosure format, and the testimony plan then follow from that answer instead of from a template.

## Grounding the analysis in the Ithaca area labor market

Vocational findings for a Cayuga Heights evaluee rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Ithaca area, where Ithaca is the principal city of the metro, and for New York statewide, supplemented by state workforce agency labor market information and direct labor market research where the referral questions warrant it. The published data frames the market; the opinion turns on the person. Education, work history, transferable skills, and the restrictions documented by treating and examining physicians determine which occupations remain realistically available, and the report says so in terms both sides can test. When lost earning capacity must be reduced to a damages figure, the firm offers [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement structure.
