Lansing sits within the commuting orbit of Ithaca, and that geography shapes how earning capacity should be analyzed when a Lansing resident is injured. An evaluee here does not compete in a single-town job market; the realistic labor market is the broader [Ithaca metro](/locations/new-york/ithaca/), and a credible vocational opinion has to be built on that footing rather than on assumptions imported from a larger city. Purinton Analytics brings that discipline to plaintiff and defense engagements alike, pairing [vocational expert witness](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) analysis with [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) and [expert testimony and litigation consulting](/services/expert-testimony-litigation-consulting/) as the referral requires.

## Where Lansing cases are heard

Lansing lies in Tompkins County, and state-court civil actions arising there are ordinarily filed in the Supreme Court, New York's trial court of general jurisdiction, sitting in the county. On the federal side, New York offers several district courts as potential forums: the United States District Court for the Northern District of New York, along with the United States District Court for the Southern District of New York, the United States District Court for the Eastern District of New York, and the United States District Court for the Western District of New York. Which district holds controlling venue in a given case is a legal question resolved by counsel, and it is identified and confirmed during the conflict check and intake so the report is framed for the forum that will actually receive it. Any venue-specific registration or qualification requirement is addressed at the same stage.

## Reading a commuter labor market honestly

Because Lansing workers draw their employment from across the metro, the vocational analysis begins with Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Ithaca metropolitan area and for New York statewide, then narrows to the evaluee: education, work history, transferable skills, and the functional restrictions the medical record actually supports. State workforce agency labor market information and direct labor market research fill the gaps where published estimates run out. The written opinion distinguishes what the market offers from what this particular person can access, because that distinction is where cross-examination lives. When damages must be quantified, the firm structures [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement structure.

## Logistics for a Lansing evaluee

There is no Purinton Analytics office in Lansing, and the firm does not pretend otherwise; every location outside the Kansas City home office is served by appointment. In practice, most of the work is remote from the start: secure delivery of records, a structured vocational interview by video, and a written report produced against counsel's disclosure deadlines. When the questions presented require an in-person functional or vocational assessment, the evaluation is conducted in the evaluee's own community, and travel for depositions or trial is scheduled from the Kansas City home office.
