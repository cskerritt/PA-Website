## Grounding opinions in the regional labor market

Employability and earning capacity findings for Glens Falls area evaluees are grounded in Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Glens Falls metropolitan area and for New York statewide, together with state workforce agency labor market information and, where the questions require it, direct contact with employers in the relevant occupations. The analysis names its sources so that a cross-examining attorney can follow every step from data to conclusion, which is the standard the practice applies whether the client is plaintiff or defense.
