Purinton Analytics accepts vocational expert and life care planning referrals
across the New York-Newark-Jersey City metropolitan area, a market whose
captions routinely cross the state line between New York and New Jersey.
Counsel throughout the metro retain the firm for
[vocational evaluations](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) addressing
employability and earning capacity, for
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) in catastrophic injury
matters, and for
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. Plaintiff and defense referrals are accepted on
identical terms, and every opinion is written so that either side of the
caption can examine the record beneath it.

## A venue footprint that crosses a state line

On the New York side of the metro, the Supreme Court is the trial court, and
engagements are accepted for matters in Bronx County, Kings County, New York
County, Queens County, and Richmond County, as well as in the surrounding
counties of Nassau, Suffolk, Westchester, Rockland, and Putnam. On the New
Jersey side, referrals arise from Middlesex County and from Bergen, Hudson,
Essex, Union, Passaic, Morris, Sussex, Somerset, Monmouth, Ocean, and
Hunterdon counties,
and any venue-specific qualification requirement is identified and confirmed
during the conflict check and intake process. Federal engagements proceed in
the United States District Court for the Southern District of New York and
the United States District Court for the Eastern District of New York, and
the firm also appears in the United States District Court for the Northern
District of New York and the United States District Court for the Western
District of New York when a matter arises elsewhere in the state. Coverage
beyond the metro is described on the
[New York state page](/locations/new-york/).

## Meetings by appointment, stated plainly

The firm maintains no staffed office anywhere in the metro and publishes no
street address for this location, because accuracy about presence belongs to
the same discipline that governs its reports. Meetings with counsel are held
by appointment at counsel's offices or by secure video, and evaluation
sessions take place in the evaluee's community or remotely under publisher
protocols. When deposition, trial, or in-person evaluation dates require
presence, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office and planned
against the disclosure calendar established at intake, so that testimony
dates are protected rather than improvised.

## Labor market research at metropolitan scale

The metro is one of the largest labor markets in the country, and that scale
demands measurement rather than assumption. Employability and earning
capacity findings rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment
and wage estimates for the New York metropolitan area and for New York and
New Jersey statewide, on state workforce agency labor market information,
and on direct labor market research, including employer contact and job
posting analysis, when the referral questions turn on actual hiring in a
particular corridor of the metro. Wage stratification across the region can
cut for either party, which is precisely why the analysis documents its
sources rather than asserting conclusions.

## Starting an engagement in the metro

Referrals from the metro most often arise in personal injury, medical
malpractice, wrongful death, employment, and disability matters. Each begins
with a documented conflict check and a defined scope, followed by a records
request tailored to the claims. Submit the caption or party names through
the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and note any imminent disclosure,
deposition, or trial dates. CVs and fee information follow promptly, and the
[national coverage page](/locations/nationwide/) describes remote evaluation
logistics, travel, and the full geographic reach of the practice.
