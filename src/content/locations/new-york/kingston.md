Attorneys litigating injury, employment, and disability matters in the Kingston, NY metropolitan area retain Purinton Analytics for [vocational expert witness](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) opinions, [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and [medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/). The practice serves plaintiff and defense counsel on the same terms, and every Kingston engagement begins with a documented conflict check, a defined scope, and a records request tailored to the claims, so the written opinion rests on a record either side of the caption can examine.

## Venue footprint in Ulster County

Engagements are accepted for matters pending in the New York Supreme Court sitting in Ulster County, the trial court where most personal injury, medical malpractice, and employment claims in the Kingston area are filed. Federal matters are accepted throughout the state, whether venued in the United States District Court for the Northern District of New York, the United States District Court for the Southern District of New York, the United States District Court for the Eastern District of New York, or the United States District Court for the Western District of New York. Counsel with related matters elsewhere in the state will find the full venue discussion on the [New York locations page](/locations/new-york/).

## By-appointment service, honestly described

Purinton Analytics keeps no staffed office in Kingston and publishes no street address for this location. Accuracy about presence is part of the same discipline that governs the firm's reports: counsel retain a practice that works remotely by default, with secure records transfer, video conferencing for interviews and case conferences, and written opinions produced to a disclosure-ready standard. When the referral calls for in-person evaluation of an evaluee in Ulster County, or when deposition and trial dates require attendance, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office and planned around the court's calendar. The [nationwide practice page](/locations/nationwide/) describes how the same engagement structure operates across the country.

## Labor market research for Kingston evaluees

Vocational findings for evaluees in the Kingston area rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Kingston metropolitan area and for New York statewide, supplemented by state workforce agency labor market information and direct labor market research where the referral questions warrant it. Employability and earning capacity opinions address the labor market the evaluee actually faces, not a generic national abstraction, and the sources behind every finding are cited in the report so opposing counsel can trace each conclusion to its data.

## Coordinated damages and testimony

Where lost earnings must be reduced to present value, counsel may elect a [coordinated damages assessment](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement structure. The firm also accepts [rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) referrals limited to critiquing an opposing expert's methodology, and provides [expert testimony](/services/expert-testimony-litigation-consulting/) at deposition and trial when the schedule requires it. Any venue-specific registration or qualification requirement is identified and confirmed during the conflict check and intake process.

## Starting a Kingston engagement

To retain the firm for a matter in Ulster County or elsewhere in New York, submit the caption or party names through the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and note any imminent disclosure, deposition, or trial dates. Scope, fee terms, and the records request follow in writing once the conflict check clears, so the engagement is documented from its first step.
