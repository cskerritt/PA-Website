## Chautauqua County venue and the federal forums

Dunkirk is a Chautauqua County community, and a state-court damages case
arising here proceeds in the Supreme Court, New York's trial court. The
report is written for that courtroom: opinions stated to a reasonable
degree of professional certainty, sources cited so opposing counsel can
retrace every step, and methodology consistent with what the firm files in
other New York venues described on the
[New York state page](/locations/new-york/).

Federal matters are accepted as well. New York's federal forums are the
United States District Court for the Northern District of New York, the
United States District Court for the Southern District of New York, the
United States District Court for the Eastern District of New York, and the
United States District Court for the Western District of New York. Which
district controls a given Dunkirk case is confirmed during the conflict
check and intake, and the report is formatted to the disclosure practice
of the forum that will actually hear it.

## Where Dunkirk sits in the metro labor market

A vocational opinion about a Dunkirk evaluee cannot stop at the city
limits. Earning capacity is measured against the labor market an evaluee
can realistically reach, and for Dunkirk that means the wider
Jamestown-Dunkirk area and, where the record supports it, Chautauqua
County and regional employers beyond it. The analysis rests on Bureau of
Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the
relevant area and for New York statewide, state workforce agency labor
market information, and direct employer research when the referral
questions call for it. Every source is cited in the report, and every
conclusion about placeability is tied to occupations that actually exist
in the data rather than to generic job titles.

## Evaluation logistics for a Dunkirk evaluee

The practice is remote-first by design. Records review, transferable
skills analysis, and vocational interviews proceed by secure video and
telephone, which keeps schedules predictable for evaluees and counsel
alike. When the referral questions require in-person evaluation, or when
deposition or trial testimony requires presence, the evaluation is
arranged in the evaluee's own community and travel is scheduled from the
Kansas City home office. The firm keeps no staffed office in Dunkirk and
publishes no local street address, because accuracy about presence is
part of the same discipline that governs the reports themselves. Counsel
whose matters arise elsewhere in the country will find the same structure
described on the [nationwide practice page](/locations/nationwide/).

When a matter pairs vocational loss with economic damages, the firm offers
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under a
single engagement structure.
