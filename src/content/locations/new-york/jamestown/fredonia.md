An expert opinion is only as reliable as the evaluation behind it, and for
counsel with a Fredonia client or claimant the practical question is often
logistical: how does a national practice actually evaluate someone who
lives here? Purinton Analytics answers that question before it answers any
other. The firm structures each
[vocational evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) and each
[life care plan](/services/life-care-planning/) around the evaluee's
circumstances, not the expert's convenience, and it does the same work for
plaintiff and defense counsel on identical terms.

## County and court context

Fredonia sits in Chautauqua County, and its state-court injury,
employment, and disability matters proceed in the Supreme Court, the trial
court of New York. Reports are drafted so they hold up in that setting:
methodology disclosed in full, every data source cited, and conclusions
framed so that cross-examination tests the analysis rather than uncovers
it. In federal litigation, New York offers the United States District
Court for the Northern District of New York, the United States District
Court for the Southern District of New York, the United States District
Court for the Eastern District of New York, and the United States District
Court for the Western District of New York; the district that controls a
particular Fredonia matter is identified during the conflict check and
intake, and the work product follows that forum's disclosure conventions.
The [New York state page](/locations/new-york/) collects the firm's
statewide venue practice.

## Reading the labor market around Fredonia

Earning capacity for a Fredonia evaluee is assessed against the
Jamestown-Dunkirk area labor market rather than against the town alone,
because that is the market a resident here can actually reach. The firm
grounds its findings in Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment
and wage estimates for the relevant area and for New York statewide,
supplemented by state workforce agency labor market information and, where
the referral questions warrant, direct labor market research with
identifiable employers. The
[Jamestown-Dunkirk metro page](/locations/new-york/jamestown/) explains
how this regional frame applies across the surrounding communities. No
figure enters a report without a citation, and no occupation is named
unless the data show it exists within the evaluee's reachable market.

## Meeting the evaluee where they live

Most of the work proceeds without anyone traveling. Records analysis,
diagnostic interviews, and transferable skills assessment are conducted by
secure video and telephone, which lets a Fredonia evaluee participate from
home and keeps litigation deadlines insulated from scheduling friction.
When an in-person evaluation is warranted, it is conducted in the
evaluee's own community; when testimony requires presence, travel is
scheduled from the Kansas City home office. The firm maintains no staffed
office in Fredonia and lists no local address, a fact stated plainly
because candor about presence belongs in the same category as candor about
data. Counsel weighing a matter outside the region can review the
[nationwide practice page](/locations/nationwide/) for the same
remote-first structure applied across the country.

Where economic damages accompany the vocational questions, the firm
coordinates engagements in which independent economist partners perform
and sign the economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning work
proceeds alongside it, through the
[coordinated damages assessment](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
service.
