## The courts that hear Westfield cases

Westfield is part of Chautauqua County, and its state-court damages
litigation proceeds in the Supreme Court, the trial court of New York.
Deadlines and disclosure practice differ between that forum and the
federal system, which in New York consists of the United States District
Court for the Northern District of New York, the United States District
Court for the Southern District of New York, the United States District
Court for the Eastern District of New York, and the United States District
Court for the Western District of New York. Which of those forums controls
a given Westfield case, and what its schedule demands of the expert, is
pinned down during the conflict check and intake so the engagement is
planned backward from the operative dates. Statewide venue practice is
summarized on the [New York page](/locations/new-york/).

## Labor market evidence for a Westfield evaluee

The opinions themselves rest on data, not familiarity. For a Westfield
evaluee, earning capacity is measured against the regional market that
includes Jamestown and the wider Jamestown-Dunkirk area, using Bureau of
Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the
relevant area and for New York statewide, state workforce agency labor
market information, and direct labor market research where the referral
questions warrant it. The
[metro page](/locations/new-york/jamestown/) describes that regional
frame in detail. Because every source is cited, opposing counsel can
reproduce the analysis; because the same method is used regardless of
retaining party, the analysis does not move with the caption.

## Scheduling the evaluation

Remote-first logistics are what make tight calendars workable. Records
review, vocational interviewing, and transferable skills analysis proceed
by secure video and telephone, so a Westfield evaluee participates
without leaving home and the work advances while depositions and motion
practice continue elsewhere in the case. If the referral questions
require an in-person evaluation, it is conducted in the evaluee's own
community; if testimony requires presence, travel is scheduled from the
Kansas City home office. The firm has no staffed office in Westfield and
publishes no local address, and it states that plainly rather than imply
otherwise, since the same accuracy that governs its data governs its
description of itself. The
[nationwide practice page](/locations/nationwide/) shows this structure
applied across the country.

When the damages theory also requires an economic loss calculation, the
firm arranges
[coordinated damages assessments](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning components proceed in
parallel, keeping the expert calendar unified rather than fragmented.
