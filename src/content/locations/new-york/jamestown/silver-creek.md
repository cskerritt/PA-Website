The most useful test of an expert report is to imagine the other side
reading it. That is the standard Purinton Analytics applies to every
Silver Creek engagement: a
[vocational opinion](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) or
[life care plan](/services/life-care-planning/) is written so that
opposing counsel, working through it line by line, finds cited sources,
disclosed methodology, and conclusions that follow from the record.
Because the firm is retained by plaintiff and defense counsel on the same
terms, that discipline is not a posture; it is the product.

## State and federal venues for Silver Creek matters

Silver Creek belongs to Chautauqua County, and a state-court case arising
here is tried in the Supreme Court, New York's trial court. On the
federal side, New York's forums are the United States District Court for
the Northern District of New York, the United States District Court for
the Southern District of New York, the United States District Court for
the Eastern District of New York, and the United States District Court
for the Western District of New York. The district with controlling venue
over a particular Silver Creek matter, along with any forum-specific
qualification requirement, is identified during the conflict check and
intake, and the report is built to that forum's disclosure expectations
from the start. The firm's wider practice across the state is described
on the [New York page](/locations/new-york/).

## The labor market question in a small community

Silver Creek is a small community, and small communities raise a
recurring vocational question: what market should earning capacity be
measured against? The firm's answer is the market the evaluee can
realistically reach, which for Silver Creek means the surrounding
Jamestown-Dunkirk area, anchored by Jamestown, rather than the town in
isolation. That determination is grounded in Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates for the relevant area and for
New York statewide, in state workforce agency labor market information,
and in direct labor market research where the referral questions call for
employer-level confirmation. The
[Jamestown-Dunkirk metro page](/locations/new-york/jamestown/) sets out
how this regional analysis applies across the area's communities, and the
report shows its work at every step so the market definition itself can
be examined and challenged.

## Working with a Silver Creek evaluee

Evaluation logistics follow the evaluee. Interviews, records review, and
transferable skills analysis are conducted by secure video and telephone,
so participation from Silver Creek requires no travel at all. Where the
referral questions make an in-person evaluation appropriate, it is
arranged in the evaluee's own community; where deposition or trial
testimony requires the expert's presence, travel is scheduled from the
Kansas City home office. Purinton Analytics keeps no staffed office in
Silver Creek and publishes no local street address, and it says so
without hedging, because a report that demands precision from its data
sources should describe its author's footprint with the same precision.
Counsel with matters beyond Chautauqua County can consult the
[nationwide page](/locations/nationwide/) for the identical structure
nationally.

Where a case requires economic damages alongside the vocational and
care-planning opinions, the firm arranges
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis within the same coordinated engagement.
