A town line is not a labor market boundary. Lakewood is a small
Chautauqua County community within the Jamestown-Dunkirk, NY area, and the
people who live here work across that wider region, which is precisely how
Purinton Analytics frames earning capacity when a Lakewood damages case
calls for a
[vocational expert opinion](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) or a
[life care plan](/services/life-care-planning/). Retention terms and
report standards are identical whether the engagement letter comes from
plaintiff or defense counsel.

## Venue: Chautauqua County and the federal districts

State-court matters arising in Lakewood are heard in the Supreme Court,
New York's trial court, sitting for Chautauqua County. The firm writes for
that venue with the assumption that opposing counsel will read every
sentence: a documented conflict check, a defined scope, a records request
tailored to the claims, and opinions that can be traced from cited source
to stated conclusion. For federal matters, New York's available forums are
the United States District Court for the Northern District of New York,
the United States District Court for the Southern District of New York,
the United States District Court for the Eastern District of New York, and
the United States District Court for the Western District of New York.
The controlling district for a specific Lakewood case is confirmed during
the conflict check and intake, alongside any venue-specific qualification
requirement. The firm's broader New York work is described on the
[state page](/locations/new-york/).

## A small community inside a regional employment market

Because Lakewood itself is small, the honest unit of analysis is the
commuting market that surrounds it, anchored by Jamestown and reaching
across the metro area. The firm builds that analysis from Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the relevant
area and for New York statewide, from state workforce agency labor market
information, and from direct labor market research when the referral
questions require employer-level verification. This regional approach,
applied consistently across the communities covered on the
[Jamestown-Dunkirk metro page](/locations/new-york/jamestown/), prevents
the twin errors that undermine vocational testimony: overstating
opportunity by importing distant markets, and understating it by treating
a small town as if its residents could not commute.

## Evaluation without unnecessary travel

Lakewood evaluees are not asked to travel for the sake of appearances.
Interviews, records review, and transferable skills analysis proceed by
secure video and telephone; when the referral questions require an
in-person evaluation, it takes place in the evaluee's own community, and
when a deposition or trial date requires the expert in the room, travel is
scheduled from the Kansas City home office. There is no staffed Purinton
Analytics office in Lakewood and no published local address, and the firm
says so directly, because a practice that will not shade its presence will
not shade its findings. The
[nationwide page](/locations/nationwide/) sets out how this same model
serves counsel in every state.

For cases that pair vocational loss with a damages calculation, the firm
offers [coordinated engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under a
single engagement structure.
