## Albany County courts and the federal forums

Cohoes lies in Albany County, and its state court matters proceed in the
Supreme Court there. On the federal side, the available forums are New
York's district courts, namely the United States District Court for the
Northern District of New York, the United States District Court for the
Southern District of New York, the United States District Court for the
Eastern District of New York, and the United States District Court for the
Western District of New York. Which district governs a particular filing,
and what qualification or registration steps that venue requires of a
testifying expert, is settled during the conflict check and intake so that
scheduling commitments rest on the actual forum's rules. The
[Albany-Schenectady-Troy metro page](/locations/new-york/albany/) covers
the wider region, and the [New York page](/locations/new-york/) describes
practice across the state.

## Labor market evidence that survives the rush

A compressed schedule is never a license for thinner evidence. Vocational
conclusions for a Cohoes evaluee are anchored in Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates for the Albany-Schenectady-Troy
metropolitan area and for New York statewide, joined by state workforce
agency labor market information and, where the transferable skills
question demands it, direct labor market research into actual hiring
conditions. Cohoes workers participate in an employment market far larger
than the city itself, and the analysis reflects that full market. Each
source appears in the report with enough specificity that opposing counsel
can pull it and check it, which is precisely the point.

## Logistics for a small city

Purinton Analytics keeps no staffed office in Cohoes and lists no street
address there; the firm describes its presence exactly as it is. Remote
evaluation under publisher-approved testing protocols usually lets the

when the disclosure date is close. If the referral questions require an
in-person evaluation, it is conducted in the evaluee's community, and

analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.
