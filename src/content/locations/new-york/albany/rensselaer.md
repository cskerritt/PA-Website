The city of Rensselaer shares its name with Rensselaer County, and for
litigators the county is where the analysis starts: it fixes the state
court forum, shapes the jury pool, and frames the docket a scheduling
order will come from. Purinton Analytics approaches every Rensselaer
retention with that venue picture in view, supplying
[vocational expert opinions](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care plans](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[forensic economic damages coordination](/services/forensic-economic-damages/)
for counsel on either side of the caption, in matters from workplace
injury to medical negligence to marital dissolution.

## The county courthouse and the federal map

State court claims arising in the city proceed in the Supreme Court in
Rensselaer County. Federal claims have New York's district courts as their
available forums: the United States District Court for the Northern
District of New York, the United States District Court for the Southern
District of New York, the United States District Court for the Eastern
District of New York, and the United States District Court for the
Western District of New York. Rather than announce which district a
Rensselaer case belongs in, the firm confirms the controlling venue during
the conflict check and intake, together with any expert registration or
qualification requirement that venue applies. The
[Albany-Schenectady-Troy metro page](/locations/new-york/albany/) describes
the regional practice, and statewide coverage is detailed on the
[New York page](/locations/new-york/).

## A small city inside a metropolitan market

Rensselaer is a small city whose residents work across a much larger
metropolitan employment market, and earning capacity analysis has to be
scaled accordingly. The firm's vocational findings rely on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the
Albany-Schenectady-Troy metropolitan area and for New York statewide,
together with state workforce agency labor market information; direct
labor market research is added when published data cannot answer the
transferable skills question on its own. The result is an opinion about
real occupations within the evaluee's actual reach, documented so that
both sides can audit the path from source to conclusion.

## Evaluation and testimony logistics

There is no Purinton Analytics office in Rensselaer, staffed or otherwise,
and the firm publishes no local address; presence is stated exactly as it
exists because credibility is not divisible. Engagements typically open
remotely, with records review, structured interviews, and standardized
testing under publisher-approved remote protocols. In-person evaluation,
when the referral questions require it, is arranged in the evaluee's
community, and travel for depositions or trial is scheduled from the
Kansas City home office against the dates the court has set. When the
damages case also needs a signed economic analysis, the firm offers
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.
