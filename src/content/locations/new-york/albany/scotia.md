In a village the size of Scotia, the local bar is small enough that an
expert's reputation for evenhandedness travels fast, and Purinton
Analytics treats that as a feature of the work rather than a constraint.
The firm is retained by plaintiff counsel and defense counsel in
comparable measure, and a Scotia engagement produces the same opinion
regardless of who signs the retainer: a
[vocational evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) that follows
the evidence on employability and earning capacity, a
[life care plan](/services/life-care-planning/) priced to documented
needs, or a [rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/)
that critiques an opposing report only where the methodology actually
fails.

## Schenectady County venue, federal options preserved

Scotia sits in Schenectady County, and its state court matters proceed in
the Supreme Court there. Claims with a federal basis may be brought in New
York's district courts, the available forums being the United States
District Court for the Northern District of New York, the United States
District Court for the Southern District of New York, the United States
District Court for the Eastern District of New York, and the United States
District Court for the Western District of New York. The firm leaves the
question of controlling venue where it belongs, in the conflict check and
intake, where it is confirmed together with any registration or
qualification requirement the forum imposes on experts. The wider region
is addressed on the
[Albany-Schenectady-Troy metro page](/locations/new-york/albany/), and the
[New York page](/locations/new-york/) covers practice statewide.

## Village residence, metropolitan opportunity

A vocational opinion about a Scotia evaluee would mislead if it treated
the village as the labor market; the relevant market is the metropolitan
economy the village belongs to. The firm's findings rest on Bureau of
Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the
Albany-Schenectady-Troy metropolitan area and for New York statewide,
alongside state workforce agency labor market information, with direct
labor market research deployed when the analysis needs to test published
data against actual employer behavior. Sources are cited with the
specificity a cross-examiner would demand, because the firm assumes every
report will eventually be read by someone paid to find its weaknesses.

## What retention looks like in practice

The firm keeps no staffed office in Scotia and lists no address there;
overstating presence would sit poorly with a practice whose product is
verified fact. Work ordinarily begins remotely, through records review,
structured interviews, and standardized testing under publisher-approved
remote protocols. If an in-person evaluation serves the referral
questions better, it is held in the evaluee's community, and testimony
obligations are met through travel from the Kansas City home office booked
around the court's schedule. Where economic damages need to be quantified
and signed, the firm offers
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Requesting a conflict check

The engagement begins when counsel submits the caption or party names
through the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/), ideally with a note on
any disclosure, deposition, or trial dates already entered. Conflicts are
run first, scope follows in writing, and the records request goes out
immediately after. Counsel juggling related matters in Schenectady or
elsewhere in the region can bundle them at intake, and the
[nationwide overview](/locations/nationwide/) explains the process for
venues beyond New York.
