An expert report earns its keep in Troy the same way it does anywhere else:
under cross-examination. Purinton Analytics writes every Troy opinion on
the assumption that opposing counsel will read it line by line, check each
source, and probe each inference at deposition. That standard shapes the
work whether the assignment is a
[vocational assessment](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) of
employability and earning capacity, a
[life care plan](/services/life-care-planning/) for catastrophic injury, or
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) testing whether
an opposing expert's report can meet the same scrutiny. Retentions are
accepted from plaintiff and defense counsel alike, and the methodology does
not change with the client.

## Courts serving Rensselaer County

Troy matters filed in state court proceed in the Supreme Court in

courts, which include the United States District Court for the Northern
District of New York, the United States District Court for the Southern
District of New York, the United States District Court for the Eastern
District of New York, and the United States District Court for the Western
District of New York. The firm does not guess at the controlling forum;
venue, along with any registration or qualification requirement it
carries, is pinned down during the conflict check and intake. Regional
practice across the Albany-Schenectady-Troy, NY area is described on the
[metro page](/locations/new-york/albany/), and statewide coverage on the
[New York page](/locations/new-york/).

## Earning capacity in a cross-county labor market

Troy belongs to a metropolitan labor market whose opportunities do not
respect the county line, and a credible earning capacity opinion has to
account for that. Vocational findings for a
Troy evaluee draw on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and
wage estimates for the Albany-Schenectady-Troy metropolitan area and for
New York statewide, on state workforce agency labor market information, and
on direct labor market research where the transferable skills profile
requires contact with actual hiring conditions. No figure enters the
report without a cited source, and the sources are chosen so that both
sides can verify them independently.

## Where the work happens

There is no staffed Purinton Analytics office in Troy, and the firm says so
plainly rather than implying a storefront it does not keep. Records review,
structured interviews, and standardized testing typically proceed remotely
under publisher-approved protocols, which keeps the schedule insulated from
travel friction. When the questions presented require an in-person
evaluation, it takes place in the evaluee's community; when testimony
requires presence in the courtroom or the deposition suite, travel is
booked from the Kansas City home office against the dates counsel has
committed to the court. For damages theories that also need economic
modeling, the firm offers
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.
