## Grounding the numbers before they exist

The labor market analysis follows the same discipline as the intake.
Vocational findings for a Mechanicville evaluee are built on Bureau of
Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the
Albany-Schenectady-Troy metropolitan area and for New York statewide,
with state workforce agency labor market information layered in and
direct labor market research commissioned when the transferable skills
question turns on what employers are actually doing. Mechanicville is a
small city whose residents draw their opportunities from the broader
metropolitan economy, and the analysis is framed at that regional scale. Every
source lands in the report with a citation an opposing expert can follow,
because an opinion that fears verification is not worth disclosing.

## Appointments, evaluations, and travel

The firm has no staffed Mechanicville office and publishes no address in
the city; it describes its footprint with the same precision it expects
of its sources. Engagements generally open remotely, with records review,
structured interviews, and standardized testing under publisher-approved
remote protocols. When the referral questions justify an in-person
evaluation, it is scheduled in the evaluee's own community, and when
depositions or trial require the expert present, travel is arranged from
the Kansas City home office with the committed dates held firm. Cases
that pair vocational and care-planning opinions with economic
quantification proceed as
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.
