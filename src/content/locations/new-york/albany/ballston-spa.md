When a catastrophic injury case involves a Ballston Spa resident, the
central damages question is often not what was earned but what care will
cost over a lifetime, and that is where Purinton Analytics is most often
retained here. A [life care plan](/services/life-care-planning/) built for
litigation itemizes future medical, therapeutic, and support needs from
the treating record and clinical foundation, and a
[medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/) prices a
defined subset of that care when the fuller plan is not required. Where
lost earning capacity is also in dispute, the firm's
[vocational expert work](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) completes
the picture, and all of it is available to plaintiff and defense counsel
on the same terms.

## Earning capacity for a village evaluee

Vocational loss for a Ballston Spa evaluee is measured against the
metropolitan labor market the village participates in, not against the
village alone. The firm anchors those findings in Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the
Albany-Schenectady-Troy metropolitan area and for New York statewide, in
state workforce agency labor market information, and in direct labor
market research where the opinion requires employer-level confirmation of
what the published data suggest. The care-planning and vocational analyses
are kept methodologically distinct but factually consistent, so that
neither opinion undercuts the other at deposition.

## Care planning logistics, honestly framed

Purinton Analytics has no staffed office in Ballston Spa and publishes no
local address, a fact stated openly because the firm's usefulness rests on
saying only what it can prove. Records review and structured interviews
proceed remotely, and standardized testing is administered under
publisher-approved remote protocols; when the referral questions warrant
an in-person evaluation or a home assessment, the visit is scheduled in
the evaluee's own community. Deposition and trial obligations are met by
travel from the Kansas City home office planned around the court's dates.
When the damages model also needs signed economic analysis, counsel can
elect
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.
