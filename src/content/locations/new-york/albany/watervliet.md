## Measuring earning capacity beyond the city line

No opinion about a Watervliet evaluee's earning capacity can stop at the
municipal boundary, because the jobs available to that evaluee do not.
Vocational findings draw on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational
employment and wage estimates for the Albany-Schenectady-Troy metropolitan
area and for New York statewide, on state workforce agency labor market
information, and on direct labor market research when employer-level
detail is needed to close the gap between published data and the evaluee's
actual prospects. The report states each source plainly so that the
analysis can be replicated, challenged, or confirmed by the other side's
expert, which is the test a serious opinion should invite.

## Remote-first, in person when it counts

The firm has no staffed office in Watervliet and publishes no address
there, and it declines to pretend otherwise; accurate statements about
presence are part of the same habit of accuracy the reports depend on.
Structured interviews and standardized testing are administered remotely
under publisher-approved protocols, which shortens timelines and spares
evaluees unnecessary travel. Where an in-person evaluation is warranted,
it is held in the evaluee's own community; where deposition or trial
testimony requires the expert in the room, travel is planned from the
Kansas City home office around the court's dates. Cases that pair
vocational loss with economic quantification can proceed as
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.
