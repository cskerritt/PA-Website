State court cases arising in the city proceed in the Supreme Court in
Schenectady County. When a claim sounds in federal law or diversity, New
York's district courts are the available forums: the United States District
Court for the Northern District of New York, the United States District
Court for the Southern District of New York, the United States District
Court for the Eastern District of New York, and the United States District
Court for the Western District of New York. Which of them controls a given
caption is confirmed during the conflict check and intake, along with any
venue-specific qualification or registration requirement, so no assumption
about forum ever reaches the report. Coverage across the surrounding region

## Reading the labor market from Schenectady

Earning capacity opinions for a Schenectady evaluee rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the
Albany-Schenectady-Troy metropolitan area and for New York statewide,
supplemented by state workforce agency labor market information and by
direct labor market research when the transferable skills analysis turns on
employer-level answers. The city sits inside a labor market that crosses
county lines in every direction, so the analysis considers the occupations
realistically open to the evaluee across that commuting footprint rather
than stopping at the municipal boundary. Every source is cited in a form
opposing counsel can trace, because a wage conclusion that cannot show its
provenance does not survive a careful deposition.

## How evaluations are conducted here

The firm keeps no staffed office in Schenectady and publishes no local
street address; candor about presence is part of the same discipline that
governs the opinions themselves. Most engagements begin remotely, with
records review, structured interviews, and standardized testing
administered under publisher-approved remote protocols. When the referral
questions call for an in-person evaluation, the assessment is scheduled in
the evaluee's own community, and when deposition or trial testimony
requires the expert at counsel table, travel is arranged from the Kansas
City home office with enough lead time to protect the committed dates.
Where the damages theory also requires economic analysis, counsel can use
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.
