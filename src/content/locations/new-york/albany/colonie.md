routinely earn their livings across the Albany-Schenectady-Troy, NY region
rather than inside any single municipality, and that fact should sit at
the center of any earning capacity dispute involving a Colonie resident.
Purinton Analytics builds its Colonie opinions around that regional
reality, offering
[vocational expert evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/) to plaintiff
and defense counsel on identical terms, with a methodology designed to be
read closely by the lawyer on the other side.

## Venue considerations for Colonie litigation

Matters arising in Colonie proceed in state court in the Supreme Court in
Albany County. Where a claim belongs in federal court, New York's district
courts stand as the available forums, specifically the United States
District Court for the Northern District of New York, the United States
District Court for the Southern District of New York, the United States
District Court for the Eastern District of New York, and the United States
District Court for the Western District of New York. The firm confirms the
controlling venue for each caption during the conflict check and intake,
along with any qualification or registration requirement that venue
places on a testifying expert, so no filing assumption is baked into the
engagement. Regional context is collected on the
[Albany-Schenectady-Troy metro page](/locations/new-york/albany/), and the
[New York page](/locations/new-york/) frames the statewide practice.

## Quantifying loss in a commuter's labor market

Because a Colonie evaluee's realistic job search spans the metropolitan
area, the vocational analysis does too. Findings are grounded in Bureau of
Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the
Albany-Schenectady-Troy metropolitan area and for New York statewide,
augmented by state workforce agency labor market information and by direct
labor market research when the case requires evidence about actual
openings and hiring practices rather than published aggregates. The
transferable skills analysis matches the evaluee's demonstrated abilities
and medical restrictions against occupations that exist within a
practical commute, and every step is documented in a form either side can
retrace.

records analysis, structured interviews, and standardized testing under
publisher-approved remote protocols, with in-person evaluation arranged in
the evaluee's own community when the referral questions call for it.

analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.
