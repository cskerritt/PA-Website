Every Binghamton engagement follows the same sequence, and counsel know it
before they sign: a documented conflict check, a defined scope, a records
request tailored to the claims, an evaluation matched to the referral
questions, and a written opinion that either side of the caption can
examine line by line. Within that sequence Purinton Analytics provides
[vocational evaluations](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) addressing
employability and earning capacity,
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) and
[medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/) in injury
and malpractice matters, and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Where Binghamton referrals are heard

Matters from the metro arise in the Supreme Court, New York's trial court,
sitting in Broome County and Tioga County. Federal engagements are
accepted as well, including in the United States District Court for the
Northern District of New York, and the firm follows a caption into the
state's other federal districts when a referral leads there. Depositions
and trial testimony are provided in person or by remote appearance where
the court permits, and plaintiff and defense retentions proceed on
identical terms. Coverage for matters arising elsewhere in the state is
described on the [New York state page](/locations/new-york/).

## An honest account of local presence

The firm has no staffed office in Binghamton and publishes no street
address for this location. Meetings happen by appointment, at counsel's
offices, in the evaluee's community, or by secure video under publisher
protocols. That accuracy about presence is intentional, because a practice
that overstates its offices invites doubt about everything else it states.
In place of a storefront, counsel get predictable logistics: conflict
checks answered promptly, evaluation dates reserved against the disclosure
calendar at intake, and travel from the Kansas City home office scheduled
whenever depositions, trials, or in-person evaluations require attendance
in the metro. Remote sessions are conducted so that test integrity is
preserved wherever the evaluee happens to be, and the report notes the
setting of each session so the record stays complete.

## Labor market analysis for Binghamton evaluees

Employability and earning capacity findings rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Binghamton
metropolitan area and for New York statewide, on state workforce agency
labor market information, and on direct labor market research, including
employer contact and job posting analysis, when the referral questions
demand evidence of actual hiring rather than published averages. The
report identifies every source relied upon and explains how each figure in
the underlying data was tested, so that the opinion invites scrutiny
instead of resisting it, whichever party retained the firm.
