Counsel in Erie County and Niagara County come to Purinton Analytics from
both directions of the caption: plaintiff firms seeking a documented
assessment of employability and lost earning capacity, and defense firms
testing whether an opposing expert's conclusions hold up under scrutiny. The
Buffalo-Cheektowaga practice answers both needs with
[vocational evaluations](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) of opposing
reports, and [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) in
catastrophic matters. Where economic damages are in issue, the firm offers
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## Where Buffalo engagements are venued

The Supreme Court is New York's trial court, and Buffalo referrals arise
from its terms in Erie County and Niagara County. Federal matters are
accepted as well, including in the United States District Court for the
Western District of New York, and the firm appears in the state's other
federal districts when a referral takes it there. Depositions and trial
testimony are given in person or by remote appearance where the court
permits, and evaluation sessions are held in the evaluee's community, at
counsel's offices, or by secure video under publisher protocols. When a
matter originates outside the metro, the
[New York state page](/locations/new-york/) describes coverage across the
rest of the state.

## No storefront, and why that matters

There is no staffed Purinton Analytics office in Buffalo, and the firm
publishes no street address for this location. Meetings are by appointment,
held at counsel's offices, in the evaluee's community, or by secure video.
That candor about presence is deliberate: an expert whose marketing
overstates an office footprint invites the same skepticism toward the rest
of the file. What counsel can rely on instead is process. Conflict checks
are answered promptly, evaluation dates are set against the disclosure
calendar at intake, and travel from the Kansas City home office is
scheduled whenever depositions, trials, or in-person evaluations require
presence in the metro.

## Grounding opinions in the Buffalo labor market

Vocational findings for Buffalo-area evaluees rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Buffalo
metropolitan area and for New York statewide, on state workforce agency
labor market information, and on direct labor market research, including
employer contact and job posting analysis, when the referral questions
require evidence about actual hiring rather than published averages. Local
conditions can favor either party's theory of the case, which is exactly
why the analysis measures them instead of assuming them, and why every
source is named in the report so that opposing counsel can check the work.

## Beginning a Buffalo referral

Buffalo engagements most often arise in personal injury, medical
malpractice, wrongful death, employment, and disability litigation, and the
firm accepts work from either side of the caption on the same terms. Each
engagement begins with a documented conflict check, a defined scope, and a
records request tailored to the claims, so the written opinion rests on a
record both sides can examine. To begin,
[request a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) with the case caption and
parties, and flag any imminent disclosure, deposition, or trial dates so
scheduling can be confirmed at intake. CVs and fee schedules follow
promptly, and the [national coverage page](/locations/nationwide/) explains
remote evaluation logistics and the practice's reach across the country.
