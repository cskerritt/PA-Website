## What by-appointment service means in practice

The firm's home office is in Kansas City, and no street address is
published for Walden because none exists to publish. Engagements are
built remote-first: secure records exchange, video interviews with the
evaluee, and scheduled conferences with counsel. When standardized
testing or an in-person evaluation is required by the methodology, the
