realistic employment prospects are defined by the occupations, employers,
and wages of the surrounding commuting market, and a vocational opinion
that pretends otherwise invites impeachment. Purinton Analytics analyzes
Woodbury evaluees against that wider market through
[vocational evaluation and expert testimony](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. Both plaintiff and defense counsel retain the
practice here, as they do across [New York](/locations/new-york/).

## The commuting market, measured honestly

The data behind a Woodbury opinion comes from the Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the
Kiryas Joel-Poughkeepsie-Newburgh metropolitan area and for New York
statewide, together with state workforce agency labor market information
and, when the referral questions call for it, direct contact with
employers hiring in the occupations at issue. Scale is described
qualitatively and sources are cited in full, so that the report's account
of what work exists within a reasonable commute of Woodbury can be checked
line by line. The [metro page](/locations/new-york/kiryas-joel/) describes
this regional data foundation and how it supports every town-level
engagement in the market.

## Orange County venue for Woodbury claims

Woodbury's state-law matters are heard in the Supreme Court in Orange
County. Federal actions may proceed in the United States District Court
for the Northern District of New York, the United States District Court
for the Southern District of New York, the United States District Court
for the Eastern District of New York, or the United States District Court
for the Western District of New York; which district governs a specific
Woodbury case is determined during the conflict check and intake, and the
answer shapes report format, disclosure timing, and deposition logistics
from the outset. Report drafts are completed against the disclosure
deadline with time reserved for counsel to check the factual record,
because a correction made before service costs nothing and a correction
extracted at deposition costs credibility. Any venue-specific registration
or qualification requirement is identified and confirmed during the
conflict check and intake process.

## Logistics for counsel and evaluee alike

Purinton Analytics has no staffed office in Woodbury and does not publish
a street address for this location, a fact stated plainly because the
firm's credibility is spent or preserved in small disclosures like this
one. Engagements proceed remote-first: secure records exchange, video
interviews, and scheduled counsel conferences. In-person evaluation, when
warranted, is arranged in the evaluee's own community rather than forcing
travel on an injured person, and appearances for deposition or trial are
scheduled from the Kansas City home office with the court's calendar
controlling. The identical model serves the firm's
