of the damages case than the wage claim does. For Warwick matters,
Purinton Analytics prepares
[life care plans](/services/life-care-planning/) and
[medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/) that
itemize future care from the treating record forward, alongside
[vocational evaluation and expert testimony](/services/vocational-expert-witness/)
and [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. Because the practice accepts work from plaintiff
and defense firms alike, a Warwick care plan is drafted to the same
standard whichever side commissions it: every recommendation tied to the
medical record and priced from cited sources.

## Care planning for a Warwick evaluee

A life care plan for a Warwick resident is grounded in the evaluee's own
treating providers and community. Record review and clinical interviews
proceed remotely in most engagements, and when an in-home or in-person
assessment is warranted, it is conducted where the evaluee actually
lives, so the plan reflects real conditions rather than assumptions. Each
recommended item of care is linked to a treating opinion or a documented
clinical need, which keeps the plan defensible when opposing counsel walks
through it entry by entry. The
firm maintains no staffed office in Warwick and publishes no local street
address; in-person attendance for evaluation, deposition, or trial is
scheduled as travel from the Kansas City home office. That is the same
by-appointment structure the firm applies across
[New York](/locations/new-york/) and its
[national practice](/locations/nationwide/).

## The vocational side: work and earnings after injury

Where the referral includes employability and earning capacity, findings
draw on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage
estimates for the Kiryas Joel-Poughkeepsie-Newburgh metropolitan area and
for New York statewide, supported by state workforce agency labor market
information and direct labor market research where warranted. The
[metro page](/locations/new-york/kiryas-joel/) describes that data
foundation in full. In a Warwick report the regional data is filtered
through this evaluee's restrictions, education, and work history, and
scale is characterized qualitatively so that no figure appears without a
source behind it.

## Orange County venue and the federal alternatives

Warwick's state-law claims proceed in the Supreme Court in Orange County.

United States District Court for the Northern District of New York, the
United States District Court for the Southern District of New York, the
United States District Court for the Eastern District of New York, and
the United States District Court for the Western District of New York;
the venue controlling a specific case is identified during the conflict
check and intake, and the applicable disclosure rules follow from that
determination. Any venue-specific registration or qualification
requirement is identified and confirmed during the conflict check and
intake process.
