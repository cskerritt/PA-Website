## Courts serving Port Jervis litigation

Port Jervis matters brought under state law are heard in the Supreme
Court in Orange County. Federal claims fall to New York's district
courts, namely the United States District Court for the Northern District
of New York, the United States District Court for the Southern District of
New York, the United States District Court for the Eastern District of New
York, and the United States District Court for the Western District of New
York. The controlling forum for a given Port Jervis case is identified
during the conflict check and intake, and its scheduling order becomes the
spine of the engagement. Any venue-specific registration or qualification
requirement is identified and confirmed during the conflict check and
intake process.

## Labor market research for a small city's evaluees

For a Port Jervis evaluee, the transferable skills analysis asks what the
evaluee can still do; the labor market research asks where that capacity
meets actual demand. Both questions are answered from Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the
Kiryas Joel-Poughkeepsie-Newburgh metropolitan area and for New York
statewide, supplemented by state workforce agency labor market information
and by direct employer contact when a referral question turns on real
hiring practices. The regional data foundation is described on the
[metro page](/locations/new-york/kiryas-joel/); the town-level report
applies it to the occupations this evaluee could realistically hold, and
it explains the reasoning step by step, because a conclusion that cannot
show its work is an invitation to a motion to exclude.

## Distance is not a barrier to rigor

A remote-first practice serves Port Jervis without discounting it.
Records analysis, interviews, and counsel conferences proceed by secure
exchange and video regardless of where in the metro the evaluee lives,
and in-person testing, when the methodology demands it, is brought to the
evaluee's own community. The firm has no staffed office in Port Jervis
and publishes no street address for this location; presence at
deposition or trial is provided by scheduled travel from the Kansas City
home office. Counsel elsewhere in [New York](/locations/new-york/) and
across the [national practice](/locations/nationwide/) are served on the
same terms.
