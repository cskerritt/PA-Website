Every Beacon engagement begins with a person, not a file number: an
injured worker, a catastrophically hurt child, a professional whose career
ended in an instant. What that person can still do, and what their care
will require, are questions Purinton Analytics answers through
[vocational evaluation and expert testimony](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/),
[medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/), and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure. Counsel for either side of the caption can retain
the practice for a Beacon matter, as throughout
[New York](/locations/new-york/).

## Meeting the evaluee where they live

The evaluation itself is designed around the evaluee. Interviews and
history-taking are typically conducted by video, which many evaluees find
less taxing than travel and which keeps the engagement moving. When the
methodology calls for standardized testing or an in-person functional
interview, the session is arranged in accessible professional space in or
near the evaluee's own community, so a Beacon resident is assessed in the
setting where they actually live and would actually seek work. The firm
keeps no staffed office in Beacon and lists no street address here; when
counsel need the expert physically present for deposition or trial,
travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office.

## Dutchess County and the courts that hear Beacon cases

Beacon sits in Dutchess County, and its state-law matters proceed in the
Supreme Court there. Federal claims may be venued in the United States
District Court for the Northern District of New York, the United States
District Court for the Southern District of New York, the United States
District Court for the Eastern District of New York, or the United States
District Court for the Western District of New York, and the district that
controls a particular case is settled during the conflict check and
intake. Any venue-specific registration or qualification requirement is
identified and confirmed during the conflict check and intake process,
before the scope is signed rather than after a problem surfaces.

## Placing a Beacon evaluee in the regional labor market

A small city's residents work in a much larger market, and the analysis
reflects that. Employability and earning-capacity opinions for Beacon
evaluees are grounded in Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational
employment and wage estimates for the Kiryas Joel-Poughkeepsie-Newburgh
metropolitan area and for New York statewide, with state workforce agency
labor market information and targeted employer research added where the
referral questions require it. The
[metro page](/locations/new-york/kiryas-joel/) explains the regional data
approach; the Beacon report ties it to this evaluee's restrictions,
transferable skills, and realistic commuting range, stated qualitatively
and sourced throughout.

## The first step is a conflict check

Before any records change hands, the practice clears conflicts. Use the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) to submit the caption or party
names, and identify any disclosure, deposition, or trial dates already on
the calendar. From there the engagement follows the same sequence the firm
applies [nationwide](/locations/nationwide/): defined scope, tailored
records request, and a written opinion built to withstand
cross-examination.
