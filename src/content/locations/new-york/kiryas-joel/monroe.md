## By-appointment service, stated plainly

Monroe engagements are served by appointment. The firm operates from its
Kansas City home office, maintains no staffed location in Monroe, and
publishes no street address here, because a practice that testifies about
facts for a living should not blur its own. Remote records review, video
interviews, and secure document exchange carry most engagements from
intake to report. Where in-person assessment is methodologically
necessary, it is conducted in the evaluee's own community, and travel for
depositions or trial is scheduled from Kansas City around the court's
dates. Raw test data, interview notes, and source lists are preserved in
the working file, so the complete basis for the opinion can be produced
whenever the governing rules require it, without scramble or surprise.
