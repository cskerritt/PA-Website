A vocational opinion is only as strong as the record beneath it. That
conviction shapes how Purinton Analytics serves Rochester counsel: every
engagement starts with a records request tailored to the claims, every
finding is tied to a named source, and every report is written to be read
by the opposing side. On that foundation the firm provides
[vocational evaluations](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) addressing
employability and earning capacity,
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) and
[medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/) in injury
matters, and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## The Rochester venue footprint

Referrals from the metro arise in the Supreme Court, New York's trial
court, sitting in Monroe County, in Livingston County, and in the
surrounding counties of Ontario, Orleans, and Wayne. Federal engagements are accepted as well,
including in the United States District Court for the Western District of
New York, and the firm follows a matter into any of the state's federal
districts when the caption requires it. Testimony is provided in person or
by remote appearance where the court permits. For referrals that originate
elsewhere in the state, the [New York state page](/locations/new-york/)
outlines the firm's statewide coverage.

## By-appointment service, described accurately

Purinton Analytics keeps no staffed office in Rochester and publishes no
street address for this location. Accuracy about presence is part of the
same discipline that governs the reports themselves. Meetings with counsel
are scheduled by appointment at counsel's offices or by secure video, and
evaluation sessions are held in the evaluee's community or remotely under
publisher protocols, whichever the referral questions and the evaluee's
circumstances support. When in-person evaluation, deposition, or trial
attendance is required, travel is arranged from the Kansas City home
office and planned around the disclosure deadlines confirmed at intake.

## Measuring the Rochester labor market

Employability and earning capacity findings for Rochester-area evaluees
rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage
estimates for the Rochester metropolitan area and for New York statewide,
supplemented by state workforce agency labor market information and by
direct labor market research, including employer contact and job posting
analysis, when the questions presented call for evidence about actual
hiring. Published estimates and local reality can diverge, and the
methodology is designed to surface that divergence rather than paper over
it, whichever party the answer favors. The same sourcing standard applies
whether the engagement is a full evaluation or a review of another
expert's conclusions.

## Opening a Rochester engagement

Rochester referrals commonly involve personal injury, medical malpractice,
wrongful death, employment, and disability claims, and the firm accepts
engagements from either side of the caption. Work begins with a documented
conflict check and a defined scope, so that expectations about
deliverables and timing are settled before any records are reviewed.
Submit the caption or party names through the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and identify any approaching
disclosure, deposition, or trial dates. CVs and fee information follow
promptly, and the [national coverage page](/locations/nationwide/)
describes remote evaluation logistics, travel practices, and the rest of
the firm's geographic reach.
