Much of expert practice now happens over a screen, and Purinton Analytics
built its Syracuse coverage for that reality. Case conferences, evaluee
interviews, and, where the court permits, deposition and trial testimony
can all proceed by remote appearance, with in-person work reserved for the
moments that genuinely require it. Syracuse counsel use that flexibility
across the firm's services:
[vocational evaluations](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) addressing
employability and earning capacity,
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) of opposing
expert reports, [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) in
catastrophic matters, and
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

## The Syracuse metro venue footprint

Referrals arise in the Supreme Court, New York's trial court, sitting in
Onondaga County, Madison County, and Oswego County. Federal matters are
accepted as well, including in the United States District Court for the
Northern District of New York, and the firm follows a caption into the
state's other federal districts when a referral requires it. Plaintiff and
defense engagements are taken on the same terms, and the report is written
so either side can trace every conclusion to its source. Matters
originating beyond the metro are addressed on the
[New York state page](/locations/new-york/).

## By appointment in Syracuse, honestly described

There is no staffed Purinton Analytics office in Syracuse, and no street
address is published for this location. The firm treats that plain
statement as part of its credibility: an expert careful about how it
describes its offices is more likely to be careful about how it describes
an evaluee's earning capacity. Meetings are held by appointment at
counsel's offices, in the evaluee's community, or by secure video under
publisher protocols. When in-person evaluation, deposition, or trial dates
require presence, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office and
coordinated with the disclosure calendar confirmed at intake, so remote
efficiency never comes at the cost of a missed appearance.

## Evidence from the Syracuse labor market

Vocational findings for Syracuse-area evaluees rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Syracuse
metropolitan area and for New York statewide, on state workforce agency
labor market information, and on direct labor market research, including
employer contact and job posting analysis, when the referral questions
call for evidence of actual hiring. The analysis distinguishes what the
published data show from what additional research establishes, and it
presents both so that the opinion can be tested rather than merely
accepted.

## Starting a Syracuse engagement

Syracuse referrals most often involve personal injury, medical
malpractice, wrongful death, employment, and disability litigation. Each
engagement begins with a documented conflict check, a defined scope, and a
records request tailored to the claims. To begin,
[request a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) with the case caption and
parties, and identify any imminent disclosure, deposition, or trial dates
so scheduling can be settled at intake. CVs and fee information follow
promptly, and the [national coverage page](/locations/nationwide/)
explains remote evaluation logistics, travel, and the firm's reach beyond
New York.
