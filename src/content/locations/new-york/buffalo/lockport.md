Begin with the courthouse. A Lockport damages case in state court will be
tried in the Supreme Court sitting in Niagara County, and everything Purinton
Analytics produces for such a case is built for that room: a
[vocational opinion](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) or
[life care plan](/services/life-care-planning/) whose methodology can be
walked through on direct, defended on cross, and checked against its cited
sources by the judge's clerk. The firm works for plaintiff and defense
counsel in equal measure, and the report reads the same way regardless of
which side signed the retention letter.

## Niagara County venue, and the federal alternatives

Alongside Supreme Court practice in Niagara County, Lockport counsel may be
litigating in federal court. New York's district courts are all available
forums for the firm's work: the United States District Court for the
Northern District of New York, the United States District Court for the
Southern District of New York, the United States District Court for the
Eastern District of New York, and the United States District Court for the
Western District of New York. The district that actually controls a given
Lockport matter, and any venue-specific registration or qualification
requirement that comes with it, is identified and confirmed during the
conflict check and intake. How this practice covers the rest of the state is
set out on the [New York page](/locations/new-york/).

## Lockport workers in a metro-wide market

Lockport belongs to the Buffalo-Cheektowaga metropolitan area, and its
residents compete for work across that geography, with Buffalo serving as
the principal city. When the question is what an injured Lockport worker can
still earn, the analysis draws on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational
employment and wage estimates for the metropolitan area and for New York
statewide, on state workforce agency labor market information, and on direct
employer inquiry when a disputed occupation calls for it. Scale is described
honestly: the opinion characterizes the market qualitatively and attaches a
citation to every quantitative claim it makes.

## Getting the evaluation done

Nothing about the process requires the evaluee to leave Lockport. Records
are requested against a defined list at intake, interviews run over secure
video, and standardized instruments are administered remotely wherever the
publisher's protocol supports it. If the referral questions require
face-to-face assessment, the evaluation happens in the evaluee's own
community, with the expert traveling from the Kansas City home office; the
firm keeps no staffed office in Lockport and publishes no local address,
and it states that fact rather than papering over it. Where future medical
needs are at issue, each recommendation in the plan carries a sourced price
through a [medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/)
grounded in the evaluee's actual market.
