Some injuries change a working life; others change every part of a life.
When a Lancaster case involves the latter kind, the central expert document
is often the [life care plan](/services/life-care-planning/): a
recommendation-by-recommendation map of future medical care, therapies,
equipment, and supports, each item tied to the medical record and each cost
sourced through a
[medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/) grounded in
the evaluee's own market. Purinton Analytics prepares these plans, and the
[vocational evaluations](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) that usually
accompany them, for retaining counsel on either side of a Lancaster
caption.

## Care needs and earning capacity, analyzed together

A catastrophic claim rarely separates cleanly into care and work. The
firm's practice keeps both analyses consistent with each other: the
same records, the same restrictions, and the same evaluee interview inform
both the plan and the vocational opinion, so cross-examination cannot pry
the documents apart. When the damages model also requires present-value
economics,
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
place that work with independent economist partners, who perform and sign
the economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning components
proceed under the same engagement structure.

Lancaster is part of Erie County, and its state-court cases are heard in
the Supreme Court, New York's trial court. In the federal system, the
firm's New York work spans the state's district courts, specifically the
United States District Court for the Northern District of New York, the
United States District Court for the Southern District of New York, the
United States District Court for the Eastern District of New York, and the
United States District Court for the Western District of New York. The
district controlling any particular matter, along with any venue-specific
registration or qualification requirement, is identified and confirmed
during the conflict check and intake. Statewide practice is described on
the [New York page](/locations/new-york/), and the regional model on the

## The employment picture for a Lancaster evaluee

Vocational conclusions for Lancaster residents are referenced to the
Buffalo-Cheektowaga metropolitan area, the labor market in which they
realistically compete, with Buffalo as the principal city. The evidentiary
base is the Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage
estimates for that metropolitan area and for New York statewide, extended
by state workforce agency labor market information and direct labor market
research where the referral questions justify it. Quantities belong in the
report with citations; this page describes the market only in qualitative
terms, which is itself a statement of method.

## Practical arrangements and the way in

Lancaster engagements are remote-first: defined records requests, secure
video interviews, and publisher-sanctioned remote testing. In-person
evaluation, when warranted, is conducted in the evaluee's community, with
travel from the Kansas City home office; the firm has no staffed Lancaster
location and lists no address here, and says so as a matter of policy. To
retain the practice, [request a conflict check](/refer-a-case/) with the
caption and parties and note every date already set; once conflicts
clear, the plan's scope and delivery schedule are confirmed in writing. The same service
structure is available to counsel nationwide through the firm's
[national practice](/locations/nationwide/).
