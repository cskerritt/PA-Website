## How Niagara Falls earning capacity is analyzed

A Niagara Falls resident does not search for work inside city limits alone;
the relevant market is the Buffalo-Cheektowaga metropolitan area, with
Buffalo as its principal employment center. Employability and placeability
findings therefore rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment
and wage estimates for that metropolitan area and for New York statewide,
supplemented by state workforce agency labor market information and, where
the referral questions warrant it, direct employer contact about specific
positions. Conclusions about transferable skills and residual capacity are
tied to the record, and qualitative judgments are labeled as such rather than
dressed up as data.

## Evaluations where the evaluee lives

Most engagements begin remotely: records are gathered under a defined request
list, interviews proceed by secure video, and standardized instruments are
administered under publisher protocols where remote use is supported. When
the questions call for in-person assessment, the evaluation is conducted in
the evaluee's own community in or near Niagara Falls, with travel scheduled
from the Kansas City home office. The firm keeps no staffed office here and
publishes no local street address, because accuracy about presence belongs to
the same discipline that governs its reports. Where a claim also requires
future medical pricing, a
[medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/) prices each
item to the evaluee's actual market rather than to a national average.
