Sometimes the assignment is to build the opinion; sometimes it is to test
someone else's. Purinton Analytics takes Hamburg referrals of both kinds.
Retaining counsel may need an original
[vocational evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) or
[life care plan](/services/life-care-planning/) for an injured Hamburg
resident, or may need an existing expert report examined through
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/), checking its
methodology, sources, and arithmetic against professional standards. The
same analytical rules govern either assignment, and the firm accepts both
from plaintiff and defense counsel, which keeps the criticism honest in
both directions.

## What a rebuttal engagement looks for

A useful review does not quarrel with conclusions; it audits the path to
them. Were the records complete, and were gaps acknowledged? Do the cited
labor market sources say what the report claims they say? Are care
recommendations traceable to the medical evidence, and are the costs
sourced or asserted? The product is a written critique that counsel can
use in deposition preparation or in
[expert testimony and litigation consulting](/services/expert-testimony-litigation-consulting/),
and it is drafted knowing the other side will read every word of it.

## Grounding opinions in the regional market

Whether building or reviewing an opinion, the reference market for a
Hamburg evaluee is the Buffalo-Cheektowaga metropolitan area, with Buffalo
as its principal city. The firm relies on Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates for the metropolitan area and
for New York statewide, together with state workforce agency labor market
information and direct employer contact where a contested occupation
demands it. A rebuttal that faults an opposing expert's data discipline
must model that discipline itself, so every quantitative statement in the
firm's reports carries its citation.
