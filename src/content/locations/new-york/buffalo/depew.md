The labor market, not the village line, defines what a Depew evaluee has
lost. A resident of Depew looks for work across the Buffalo-Cheektowaga
metropolitan area, with Buffalo as the principal city, and any credible
earning capacity opinion has to be anchored in that geography. Purinton
Analytics starts there: Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment
and wage estimates for the metropolitan area and for New York statewide,
state workforce agency labor market information, and direct employer
research when a disputed occupation requires it, all feeding a
[vocational evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) that either
party to the litigation can probe.

## From market data to a defensible opinion

Data alone do not make an opinion. The firm's method connects the
evaluee's documented work history, capacities, and restrictions to the
occupations the market actually contains, states which conclusions follow
from published estimates and which reflect professional judgment, and
cites every quantitative claim in the report itself. Where permanent
injury creates future care needs, a
[life care plan](/services/life-care-planning/) maps each recommendation
to the medical record, and a
[medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/) prices it in
the evaluee's own market. The prose you are reading stays qualitative on
purpose; the numbers live in the report, next to their sources.

## Erie County venue and New York's federal districts

Depew sits in Erie County, where state-court damages actions proceed in
the Supreme Court, New York's trial court. Federal work is accepted in the
state's district courts across the board: the United States District Court
for the Northern District of New York, the United States District Court
for the Southern District of New York, the United States District Court
for the Eastern District of New York, and the United States District Court
for the Western District of New York. Which forum governs a given Depew
caption, and whether that venue imposes any registration or qualification
requirement, is settled during the conflict check and intake rather than
guessed at here. The surrounding communities are addressed on the
[Buffalo metro page](/locations/new-york/buffalo/), and the full state on
the [New York page](/locations/new-york/).

## Logistics for the Depew evaluee

Evaluations are structured so the injured person is inconvenienced as
little as possible. Records collection, interviews, and most standardized
testing are handled remotely under secure video and publisher-approved
protocols; when the referral questions call for in-person assessment, it
takes place in the evaluee's community, with the expert traveling from the
Kansas City home office. The firm keeps no staffed location in Depew and
publishes no street address for it, and it would rather say that plainly
than imply otherwise, since the credibility of a report begins with the
accuracy of the practice that produced it.
