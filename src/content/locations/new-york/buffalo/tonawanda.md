Here is how a Tonawanda engagement actually runs. Retention opens with a
conflict check and a written scope. A records request tailored to the claims
goes out next, covering medical, employment, educational, and earnings
documentation. The clinical and vocational interview follows by secure
video, standardized instruments are administered under publisher protocols
where remote use is approved, and the resulting
[vocational evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) or
[life care plan](/services/life-care-planning/) is delivered as a written
report that either side of the caption can test line by line. Purinton
Analytics runs the sequence the same way for plaintiff counsel and defense
counsel, which is precisely the point.

## The Erie County and federal court context

Tonawanda sits in Erie County, and its state-court litigation proceeds in
the Supreme Court, New York's trial court. In federal practice the firm
appears across New York's district courts, meaning the United States
District Court for the Northern District of New York, the United States
District Court for the Southern District of New York, the United States
District Court for the Eastern District of New York, and the United States
District Court for the Western District of New York. Rather than assuming
which of them will control a Tonawanda case, the firm confirms the
governing forum, and any registration or qualification requirement specific
to it, during the conflict check and intake. The wider regional picture is
drawn on the [Buffalo metro page](/locations/new-york/buffalo/).

## Where a Tonawanda evaluee actually competes

The town's job seekers operate in the Buffalo-Cheektowaga metropolitan
labor market, with Buffalo as its principal city. Employability and
placeability conclusions are accordingly referenced to Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the metropolitan
area and for New York statewide, supported by state workforce agency labor
market information and, when a disputed position warrants it, direct
inquiry with employers. The report keeps its categories clean: published
data are cited, professional judgment is identified as judgment, and
neither is passed off as the other.

## In-person work without a local office

When the referral questions require face-to-face assessment, the evaluator
travels to the evaluee's community in or near Tonawanda, with the trip
scheduled from the Kansas City home office; injured people are not asked to
commute to their own evaluation. The firm maintains no staffed office in
Tonawanda, publishes no street address for it, and considers saying so a
feature of the practice rather than an admission. For cases that also need
economic loss quantified,
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
bring in independent economist partners who perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work continues under the
same engagement structure.
