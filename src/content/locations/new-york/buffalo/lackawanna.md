What can this person still do, and what will it pay? That pair of questions
sits at the center of most Lackawanna damages cases that reach a vocational
expert, and answering them honestly takes more than a resume review.
Purinton Analytics answers them with a documented method: a
[vocational evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) that tests
residual capacity against the actual demands of actual occupations, and,
when injuries create lifelong needs, a
[life care plan](/services/life-care-planning/) that translates the medical
record into itemized future care. Plaintiff and defense firms retain the
practice on the same terms and receive the same report.

## Erie County courts and the federal forums

Lackawanna is an Erie County city, and its state-court cases are heard in
the Supreme Court, the trial court of New York. Federal matters are accepted
throughout the state's district courts, which are the United States District
Court for the Northern District of New York, the United States District
Court for the Southern District of New York, the United States District
Court for the Eastern District of New York, and the United States District
Court for the Western District of New York; the forum that governs a
specific Lackawanna caption is confirmed during the conflict check and
intake, never presumed from geography. That intake step also surfaces any
venue-specific registration or qualification requirement. Statewide coverage
is described on the [New York page](/locations/new-york/).

## The market behind the opinion

An earning capacity opinion is only as good as the labor market it
references. For a Lackawanna evaluee that market is the Buffalo-Cheektowaga
metropolitan area, where Buffalo functions as the principal city and the
center of gravity for hiring. The firm grounds its findings in Bureau of
Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the
metropolitan area and for New York statewide, in state workforce agency
labor market information, and in direct labor market research when the
referral questions turn on a particular occupation or employer type.
Numbers appear in the report with citations attached; the prose here stays
qualitative by design.

## Remote-first, with travel when it matters

Engagements typically open without anyone booking a flight: the records
request goes out against a defined list, the vocational interview is
conducted by secure video, and testing follows publisher protocols that
support remote administration. When the questions demand an in-person
evaluation, it takes place in the evaluee's own community rather than in a
distant office, with travel scheduled from the Kansas City home office.
There is no staffed Purinton Analytics location in Lackawanna and no local
street address to publish, a fact the firm states outright because candor
about logistics is part of what makes its reports believable. Opposing
opinions can also be examined on request through
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) work.
