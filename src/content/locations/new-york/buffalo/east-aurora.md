In a village the size of East Aurora, the nearest expert of any specialty
is usually somewhere else, and litigation deserves better than whichever
expert happens to be closest. Purinton Analytics is built on that premise:
the practice brings the
[vocational evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) or
[life care plan](/services/life-care-planning/) to the evaluee, not the
other way around, and it does so for plaintiff and defense referrals on
identical terms. A small community's cases get the same records-driven,
citation-backed workup a case in a major city would get.

## The evaluation comes to the village

The working default is remote. Intake produces a defined records request;
the clinical and vocational interview is conducted over secure video; and
standardized instruments are administered under publisher protocols
wherever remote administration is supported, which keeps an injured East
Aurora resident from spending evaluation day in a car. When the referral
questions genuinely require face-to-face assessment, the evaluator travels
to the evaluee's own community, with the trip scheduled from the Kansas
City home office. The firm has no staffed office in East Aurora, publishes
no address for it, and treats that disclosure as part of its professional
obligations rather than a marketing problem.

## County and court context for East Aurora cases

East Aurora lies in Erie County, and state-court actions are heard in the
Supreme Court, which is the trial court of New York. Federal matters may
proceed in any of the state's district courts, namely the United States
District Court for the Northern District of New York, the United States
District Court for the Southern District of New York, the United States
District Court for the Eastern District of New York, and the United States
District Court for the Western District of New York; identifying the
district that actually governs, and any venue-specific registration or
qualification requirement, is part of the conflict check and intake. How
the firm covers the rest of the state appears on the
[New York page](/locations/new-york/).

## A small town measured against a metropolitan market

Earning capacity for an East Aurora evaluee is not measured against
village employment alone. Residents participate in the labor market of the
Buffalo-Cheektowaga metropolitan area, whose principal city is Buffalo,
and the firm's findings are referenced accordingly: Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the metropolitan
area and for New York statewide, state workforce agency labor market
information, and direct employer inquiry when a specific occupation is in
dispute. Where lost earnings must be reduced to an economic damages
figure, [forensic economic analyses](/services/forensic-economic-damages/)
are performed and signed by independent economist partners coordinated by
the firm.

## Referring an East Aurora matter

Retention begins with the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/): the
caption or party names, the referral questions, and the dates the
scheduling order has already fixed. When conflicts clear, the engagement
is confirmed in writing with a defined scope and delivery schedule. Sibling communities across the region
are served under the model described on the
[Buffalo metro page](/locations/new-york/buffalo/), and counsel whose
cases arise outside New York can engage the same evaluee-centered practice
through the [nationwide practice](/locations/nationwide/).
