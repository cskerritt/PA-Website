Expert retention usually arrives with a calendar attached. By the time
counsel in a North Tonawanda matter reaches for a vocational expert or life
care planner, disclosure deadlines are often set and deposition dates are
being negotiated, so Purinton Analytics builds every engagement backward from
those dates. Intake fixes the scope and the records list at retention,
the [vocational evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) or
[life care plan](/services/life-care-planning/) proceeds on a schedule agreed
in writing, and draft-to-final timing is planned so the report lands before
the disclosure date, not on it.

## Where North Tonawanda cases are heard

North Tonawanda lies in Niagara County. State-court claims are tried in the
Supreme Court, which is New York's trial-level court, and the county's
caseload is part of the broader court footprint described on the
[Buffalo metro page](/locations/new-york/buffalo/). On the federal side, the
firm accepts matters throughout New York's district courts, namely the
United States District Court for the Northern District of New York, the
United States District Court for the Southern District of New York, the
United States District Court for the Eastern District of New York, and the
United States District Court for the Western District of New York. No page
of this site guesses at the controlling forum; that determination, along
with any registration or qualification requirement particular to the venue,
is made during the conflict check and intake.

## Reading the labor market from North Tonawanda

For a North Tonawanda evaluee, the realistic job search spans the
Buffalo-Cheektowaga metropolitan area, anchored by Buffalo as the principal
city. Vocational findings are therefore framed against Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for that metropolitan
area and for New York as a whole, alongside state workforce agency labor
market information. Where a specific occupation is in dispute, direct labor
market research supplements the published estimates. The write-up
distinguishes between what the data show and what the evaluator infers,
because a report that blurs that line invites the cross-examination it
deserves.

## Meeting the evaluee, not the map

The firm has no staffed office in North Tonawanda and lists no street
address for it; meetings are by appointment, and the practice says so
plainly. Records review, interviews, and most testing proceed remotely under
secure video and publisher-approved protocols. When in-person assessment is
warranted, the evaluator comes to the evaluee's community, with travel
arranged from the Kansas City home office. For claims that pair earning
capacity loss with future care needs,
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
allow independent economist partners to perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds within the
same engagement structure.
