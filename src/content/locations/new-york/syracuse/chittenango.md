Chittenango is a small village, and honest vocational analysis begins by
admitting what that means: the local job base is limited, and the
employment prospects of a Chittenango evaluee depend on the regional market
around it. An expert who treats a small village as a self-contained economy
produces an opinion that overstates or understates loss, depending on which
side wrote the check. Purinton Analytics avoids that error in both
directions, offering
[vocational expert analysis](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) and
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) to plaintiff and
defense counsel on the same terms.

## Madison County and the federal forums

Southern District of New York, the United States District Court for the
Eastern District of New York, the United States District Court for the

confirmed during the conflict check and intake process.

## A small village in a regional market

The scale problem named at the top of this page is solved with data, not
assumption. Employability and earning capacity findings for a Chittenango
evaluee rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage
estimates for the Syracuse metropolitan area, of which Chittenango is a
part, and for New York statewide, supplemented by state workforce agency
labor market information and direct employer research where warranted. The
report explains how far the commutable market reasonably extends for this
evaluee, given the restrictions and circumstances in the record, so that
the definition itself can be tested at deposition. The
[Syracuse metro page](/locations/new-york/syracuse/) addresses the regional
market in fuller detail.

## Evaluation in the evaluee's community

Meetings in Chittenango are by appointment; there is no local office, and
the firm does not imply one. What the village's size makes practical is
evaluation close to home: interviews and testing at a neutral location in
the evaluee's own community, at counsel's offices, or by remote
administration when the instruments allow it. For in-person evaluation days
and for deposition or trial appearances, travel is arranged from the Kansas
City home office and slotted against the court's deadlines. The
[New York overview](/locations/new-york/) and the
