## County and courthouse context

Fayetteville is a village in Onondaga County, and its state-court civil
matters proceed in the Supreme Court serving Onondaga County. New York's
federal trial forums are the United States District Court for the Western
District of New York, the United States District Court for the Southern
District of New York, the United States District Court for the Northern
District of New York, and the United States District Court for the Eastern
District of New York. The firm presents these as the available forums
without predicting which one a Fayetteville dispute will land in; the
controlling venue and any registration or qualification requirement are
identified and confirmed during the conflict check and intake process.

## The labor market question

Vocational opinions for a Fayetteville evaluee are anchored in the
Syracuse, NY metropolitan labor market of which the village is a part.
The evidentiary base is Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment
and wage estimates for the Syracuse metropolitan area and for New York
statewide, augmented by state workforce agency labor market information and
by direct employer research when a referral's questions require it. The
report walks through the commutable market definition, the transferable
skills analysis, and the resulting occupational base in an order a reader
can follow and challenge, with each source identified well enough for the
other side to obtain and examine it. For the market discussion at regional
scale, see the [Syracuse metro page](/locations/new-york/syracuse/).

## Remote and in-person evaluation

The by-appointment model works because evaluation does not depend on a
local suite. Instruments validated for remote administration can be given
remotely; interviews can be held at counsel's offices or at a neutral
location in the evaluee's own community; records review happens wherever
the records are. When the referral questions call for in-person
evaluation, or when deposition and trial dates require attendance in
Fayetteville or elsewhere in the region, travel is scheduled from the
Kansas City home office and planned around the operative deadlines. The
[New York overview](/locations/new-york/) and the
[nationwide page](/locations/nationwide/) describe how this model serves
matters far beyond any single village.
