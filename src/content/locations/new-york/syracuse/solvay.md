A vocational report filed in a Solvay case should be written for its most
hostile reader: the cross-examining attorney who wants the methodology to
crack. Purinton Analytics drafts every opinion to that standard, which is
why the same report serves whichever side commissioned it. For Solvay
counsel that discipline covers
[vocational expert opinions](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care plans](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/), each
resting on sources the other side can pull and check.

## Courts serving Solvay parties

Solvay is a village in Onondaga County, and its state-court matters are
tried in the Supreme Court serving Onondaga County. In the federal system,
New York's forums are the United States District Court for the Northern
District of New York, the United States District Court for the Eastern
District of New York, the United States District Court for the Western
District of New York, and the United States District Court for the Southern
District of New York. This page does not tell counsel which of those courts
their dispute belongs in, because that answer depends on the dispute; the
controlling venue and any qualification requirement it imposes are
identified and confirmed during the conflict check and intake process.

## Grounding opinions in market data

For a Solvay evaluee, the employability question is answered against the
Syracuse, NY metropolitan labor market, not against a village boundary.
The firm's findings rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational
employment and wage estimates for the Syracuse metropolitan area and for
New York statewide, on state workforce agency labor market information, and
on direct labor market research when the referral questions call for it.
Every inference from restriction to occupation to earning capacity is laid
out in sequence in the written report, so that the hostile reader described
above finds a chain of reasoning rather than a leap. Regional market
context is developed further on the
[Syracuse metro page](/locations/new-york/syracuse/).

## Appointments, not offices

The firm's presence in Solvay is a calendar entry, not a building. Meetings
are by appointment; no local address exists to publish, and none is
invented. Evaluations proceed at counsel's offices, at a neutral location
in the evaluee's own community, or by remote administration where the
instruments are validated for it. When in-person evaluation, deposition, or
trial attendance is required, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home
office around the litigation calendar, with deadlines confirmed against
counsel before any date is set. The
[New York state overview](/locations/new-york/) and the
[nationwide page](/locations/nationwide/) explain the by-appointment model
in the same plain terms, because it operates identically everywhere the
practice serves.
