The center of a Fulton referral is a person: an injured worker, a claimant
whose medical restrictions have changed what jobs remain open, a family
weighing what future care will require. The legal questions follow from that
person's circumstances, and so does the expert work. Purinton Analytics
serves counsel on either side of the caption in Fulton matters, pairing
[vocational assessment](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) with
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) so that function, work
capacity, and future care needs are analyzed on a shared factual record.

## Where a Fulton case is tried

Fulton is an Oswego County city, and its state-court litigation proceeds in
the Supreme Court serving Oswego County. Federal matters are another
question entirely. New York is served by the United States District Court
for the Northern District of New York and the United States District Court
for the Southern District of New York, together with the United States
District Court for the Eastern District of New York and the United States
District Court for the Western District of New York. Which of those forums
governs a particular dispute is not something the firm guesses at; the
controlling venue and any qualification requirement it carries are
identified and confirmed during the conflict check and intake process,
before any opinion work begins.

## Employability beyond the city line

A Fulton evaluee does not compete for work only inside Fulton. The relevant
market is the commutable one, and for most Fulton residents that reaches
into the broader Syracuse, NY metropolitan area. Vocational findings
therefore draw on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and
wage estimates for the Syracuse metropolitan area and for New York as a
whole, on state workforce agency labor market information, and on direct
employer research where a referral warrants it. Each report states how the
commutable market was defined for the individual evaluee and why, a
methodological choice the [metro page for Syracuse](/locations/new-york/syracuse/)
discusses at the regional level.

## How the evaluation is scheduled

Fulton meetings are by appointment. The firm maintains no staffed location
in the city and does not print a local address it does not have. What
counsel gets instead is flexible logistics: interviews at the referring
firm's offices, evaluation at a neutral site in the evaluee's own
community, or remote administration of instruments that are validated for
it. Travel for in-person evaluation days, depositions, and trial testimony
is arranged from the Kansas City home office and is built around the
operative deadlines rather than the other way around. The
[New York state page](/locations/new-york/) and the
[nationwide overview](/locations/nationwide/) explain that model in full.

## The first step is a conflict check

Retention begins with a documented conflict check, followed by a written
scope and a records checklist tailored to the claims actually pleaded. When
the damages picture calls for economic modeling, counsel can add a
[coordinated damages assessment](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/),
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning opinions proceed under a
single engagement structure. Send the caption or the party names through the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/), and flag any disclosure, deposition,
or trial dates already on the calendar so scheduling is honest from the
start.
