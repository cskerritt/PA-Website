In a catastrophic injury case arising in Manlius, the question of future
care often arrives before the question of future work: what treatment,
equipment, therapies, and attendant support the medical record actually
supports, over what horizon, and at what documented source of cost. That is
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and it is frequently
the anchor of a Manlius engagement, with the
[vocational opinion](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) and any
[medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/) built on the
same underlying record. Purinton Analytics performs that work for plaintiff
and defense counsel alike, and the plan reads the same whichever side asks
for it.

## Earning capacity in the Syracuse market

When the vocational question does arise, it is answered against the
Syracuse, NY metropolitan labor market that Manlius belongs to. Findings
rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage
estimates for the Syracuse metropolitan area and for New York statewide,
with state workforce agency labor market information and direct labor
market research added where the referral requires. The report ties each
conclusion about residual capacity to specific occupations and to the
restrictions in the medical record, so that the path from evidence to
opinion is visible to both sides. The
[Syracuse metro page](/locations/new-york/syracuse/) discusses the
surrounding market at length.

## How and where evaluations occur

Manlius meetings are by appointment. No office exists here, and the firm
states that rather than obscuring it. Care-planning evaluations in
particular often work best in the evaluee's own community, where daily
routines, home layout, and support needs can be understood in context;
interviews and testing can also proceed at counsel's offices or by remote
administration where the instruments permit. Travel for in-person work,
depositions, and trial testimony is scheduled from the Kansas City home
office against the case calendar. The
[New York overview](/locations/new-york/) and the
[nationwide page](/locations/nationwide/) lay out the same by-appointment
approach.
