What actually happens after a Canastota attorney sends over a caption? In
this practice, a fixed sequence: the conflict check runs and is documented,
the scope is agreed in writing, a records request tailored to the claims
goes out, and only then does analysis begin. That sequence is the same for
a plaintiff retention and a defense retention, which is precisely why it
earns trust from either chair. It applies to
[vocational expert work](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[rebuttal or peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) of an opposing
expert's report in Canastota matters.

## Madison County courts and federal options

Canastota is a Madison County village. Its state-court litigation proceeds
in the Supreme Court serving Madison County, while federal matters may be
heard in the United States District Court for the Eastern District of New
York, the United States District Court for the Western District of New
York, the United States District Court for the Northern District of New
York, or the United States District Court for the Southern District of New
York, depending on the case. The firm makes no claim about which forum
controls a given dispute; that determination, and any registration or
qualification requirement flowing from it, is identified and confirmed
during the conflict check and intake process described above.

## Reading the labor market from Canastota

A Canastota evaluee's realistic employment options extend into the
Syracuse, NY metropolitan area, and the analysis reflects that. Opinions on
employability and earning capacity rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates for the Syracuse metropolitan
area and for New York statewide, joined by state workforce agency labor
market information and, when the referral warrants, direct research with
employers. The written report shows how the commutable market was bounded
for this individual and which occupations survived the transferable skills
analysis, so opposing counsel can interrogate the method rather than the
adjectives. The [Syracuse metro page](/locations/new-york/syracuse/)
carries the regional discussion.

## Evaluation and travel logistics

Canastota meetings occur by appointment only; the firm operates no office
in the village and will not suggest otherwise. Interviews and standardized
testing can take place at the referring firm's offices, at a neutral
location in the evaluee's own community, or through remote administration
where the instruments are validated for it. Records review proceeds
wherever the records are, and findings are reported to counsel as the
analysis develops rather than held for a single reveal. When the engagement
requires in-person evaluation or courtroom presence, travel is planned from
the Kansas City home office and fitted to the docket. The
[New York state page](/locations/new-york/) and the
[nationwide overview](/locations/nationwide/) present the same
by-appointment structure across the practice.

## Sending the caption

The sequence at the top of this page starts when counsel submits the
caption or party names through the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/). Note any disclosure, deposition, or
trial dates already scheduled, and indicate whether the damages case will
need economic modeling: where it does, the firm arranges
[coordinated damages assessments](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning opinions proceed under a
single engagement structure.
