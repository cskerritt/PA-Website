Some North Syracuse referrals come from plaintiff firms proving what an
injury took away; others come from defense counsel testing whether a
claimed loss is real. Purinton Analytics accepts both, and the file looks
identical either way: the same instruments, the same sources, the same
willingness to report findings that disappoint the retaining party. That
symmetry is the point of retaining an independent expert for
[vocational assessment](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), or
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) in a North
Syracuse matter.

## Onondaga County venue

North Syracuse is an Onondaga County village, and its state-court civil
litigation is heard in the Supreme Court serving Onondaga County. Federal
practice across New York runs through the United States District Court for
the Eastern District of New York, the United States District Court for the
Southern District of New York, the United States District Court for the
Northern District of New York, and the United States District Court for the
Western District of New York. Those are the available forums; which one
controls a specific dispute, and what registration or qualification
requirements attach there, is identified and confirmed during the conflict
check and intake process rather than assumed from geography.

## Match, not mileage

The labor market question for a North Syracuse evaluee is not answered by
pointing at a map. North Syracuse belongs to the Syracuse, NY metropolitan
area, so the operative question is one of match: which occupations in that
metro market remain open to this evaluee given the documented restrictions,
education, and work history, and which are foreclosed. Answers rest on
Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for
the Syracuse metropolitan area and for New York statewide, supported by
state workforce agency labor market information and direct employer contact
when the referral justifies it. Transferable skills analysis is shown step
by step in the report, not asserted, and every source relied upon is cited
so that the opposing expert can retrace the work from the same materials.
The broader regional picture is covered on the
[Syracuse metro page](/locations/new-york/syracuse/).

## Evaluation logistics

Meetings in North Syracuse are by appointment; the firm has no office in
the village and does not manufacture the appearance of one. An evaluation
can occur at the referring firm's offices, at a neutral site in the
evaluee's own community, or through remote administration where the
protocols are validated for it. For in-person evaluation days and for
depositions and trial appearances, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City
home office and coordinated against the case deadlines, so counsel can
plan disclosure and testimony dates around confirmed availability rather
than estimates. The [New York page](/locations/new-york/) and the
[nationwide overview](/locations/nationwide/) set out the same
by-appointment practice statewide and nationally.

## Conflict check first

Because the practice serves both sides of the caption, the conflict check
is not a formality; it is the gate every engagement passes through before
substantive work begins. After clearance comes a written scope and a
records request tailored to the pleadings, so that the eventual opinion
rests on a record both sides can examine. Where economic modeling belongs
in the same engagement, the firm arranges
[coordinated damages assessments](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis alongside the vocational and care-planning work. Submit the
caption or party names through the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/), noting any imminent disclosure,
deposition, or trial dates.
