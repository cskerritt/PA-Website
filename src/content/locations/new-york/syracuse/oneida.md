Expert retention in an Oneida case is usually driven by a date: a disclosure
deadline approaching, a deposition already noticed, a trial term assigned.
Purinton Analytics builds its Oneida engagements around that calendar from

## Venue in Madison County and beyond

Oneida is a Madison County city, and its state-court matters are heard in
the Supreme Court serving Madison County. Federal litigation involving
Oneida parties may proceed in any of New York's district courts depending
on the dispute: the United States District Court for the Northern District
of New York, the United States District Court for the Eastern District of
New York, the United States District Court for the Southern District of New
York, or the United States District Court for the Western District of New
York. The firm names those forums without presuming which one applies; the
controlling venue, and any registration or qualification requirement tied
to it, is identified and confirmed during the conflict check and intake
process. A report written for the Supreme Court reads no differently than
a report written for a federal courtroom.

## Labor market evidence for an Oneida evaluee

Because Oneida sits within the Syracuse, NY metropolitan area, the
employability analysis for an Oneida evaluee considers the regional market
an actual job seeker would face. That analysis rests on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Syracuse
metropolitan area and for New York statewide, on state workforce agency
labor market information, and, where the questions justify it, on direct
labor market research with employers. The report sets out the reasoning
behind the commutable market it uses, because a definition that cannot be
examined is not evidence. Regional context appears on the
[Syracuse metro page](/locations/new-york/syracuse/).

Work begins only after a documented conflict check clears, a scope is set
in writing, and a records request tailored to the pleadings goes out. If

care-planning work, the firm coordinates
