Oswego gives its name to the county whose courts hear most of its disputes,
and that is where this page begins. A caption filed for an Oswego party will
usually name Oswego County, and the expert work behind it has to hold up in
front of a judge, a jury, and the lawyer on the other side of the aisle.
Purinton Analytics prepares
[vocational expert opinions](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) and
[life care plans](/services/life-care-planning/) for plaintiff and defense
counsel alike, written to the same standard whichever party signs the
retention letter.

## The courts that hear Oswego cases

State-court matters for Oswego litigants proceed in the Supreme Court, the
trial court that serves Oswego County. On the federal side, New York's
practice is divided among the United States District Court for the Northern
District of New York, the United States District Court for the Western
District of New York, the United States District Court for the Eastern
District of New York, and the United States District Court for the Southern
District of New York. The firm treats those districts as available forums
rather than assuming which of them controls a given complaint; the operative
venue, along with any registration or qualification requirement that
attaches to it, is identified and confirmed during the conflict check and
intake process. The opinion itself does not change with the forum.

## A labor market wider than the city

Oswego sits within the Syracuse, NY metropolitan area, and an Oswego
evaluee's employability is measured against that wider market rather than
against the city limits alone. Findings on employability and earning
capacity rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage
estimates for the Syracuse metropolitan area and for New York statewide,
supplemented by state workforce agency labor market information and by
direct labor market research when a referral calls for it. The report
explains how the commutable market was drawn for this particular evaluee,
so opposing counsel can test the definition instead of arguing with a
conclusion. The [Syracuse metro page](/locations/new-york/syracuse/)
describes the regional market in more depth.

## Evaluating in Oswego

Meetings in Oswego are by appointment only. The firm keeps no office here
and publishes no local address, because accuracy about presence is part of
the same discipline that governs its reports. Interviews and standardized
testing can take place at counsel's offices, at a neutral site in the
evaluee's own community, or remotely where the instruments permit. When a
referral requires in-person evaluation, or when deposition and trial dates
demand attendance, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home office and
planned around the case calendar. The [New York overview](/locations/new-york/)
and the [nationwide page](/locations/nationwide/) describe how the same
by-appointment model operates across the state and the country.

Every engagement opens with a documented conflict check, a scope defined in

opinions, the firm offers
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)

engagement structure. To begin, submit the caption or party names through
the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/) and note any imminent disclosure,
