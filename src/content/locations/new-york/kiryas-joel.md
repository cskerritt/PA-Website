## What by-appointment coverage means here

The firm maintains no staffed office in Kiryas Joel, Poughkeepsie, or
Newburgh and publishes no street address for this location. That is stated
directly because accuracy about presence belongs to the same discipline
that governs the firm's written opinions. Meetings with counsel are
arranged by appointment, and when an engagement calls for in-person
evaluation, deposition attendance, or trial testimony, travel is scheduled
from the Kansas City home office against the disclosure calendar
established at intake. Between appearances, secure video keeps case
conferences, evaluee interviews, and follow-up sessions moving without
waiting on travel.

## Labor market research for metro evaluees

Employability and earning capacity findings rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the metro area
and for New York statewide, on state workforce agency labor market
information, and on direct labor market research, including employer
contact and job posting analysis, when the referral questions turn on
actual hiring conditions rather than published averages. Where an
evaluee's realistic labor market spans more than the immediate area, the
analysis says so and documents why, because a transparent geographic
assumption is easier to defend than a convenient one.
