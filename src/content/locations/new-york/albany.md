## Appointment-based presence, without embellishment

The firm keeps no staffed office in Albany, Schenectady, or Troy and
publishes no street address for this location. Meetings happen by
appointment, at counsel's offices, in the evaluee's community, or by secure
video, and the firm says so plainly because honesty about presence is part
of the credibility it asks courts to extend to its reports. What counsel
gets in place of a storefront is dependable logistics: prompt conflict
checks, evaluation dates reserved early against the disclosure calendar,
and travel from the Kansas City home office scheduled whenever a
deposition, trial, or in-person evaluation calls for attendance in the
metro.

## Labor market evidence for Albany-area evaluees

Vocational findings rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational
employment and wage estimates for the Albany metropolitan area and for New
York statewide, on state workforce agency labor market information, and on
direct labor market research, including employer contact and job posting
analysis, when the referral questions require proof of actual hiring
rather than published averages. Every source is cited in the report, so
that cross-examination becomes an opportunity to explain the method rather
than a threat to it, and so that the same analysis can serve plaintiff or
defense counsel with equal integrity.
