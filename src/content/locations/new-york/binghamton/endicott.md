Earning capacity questions in Endicott cases are answered by a labor market that extends well past the village boundary. An Endicott evaluee competes for work across the Binghamton metropolitan area, with the principal city of Binghamton anchoring a substantial share of the region's employment, and a credible vocational opinion has to define that commuting market explicitly before it says anything about lost earnings. Purinton Analytics takes that definitional step first in every Endicott engagement, whether the referral arrives from plaintiff or defense counsel, and documents it so the opposing expert can test it rather than merely dispute it.

## Data before conclusions

Vocational findings for Endicott evaluees are built on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Binghamton metropolitan area and for New York statewide, on state workforce agency labor market information, and on direct labor market research when the referral questions call for employer-level detail. No figure enters a report without a citation, and no characterization of the market's size or direction appears without a public source behind it. Where a case pairs the vocational loss with an economic damages calculation, the firm offers [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement structure.

## Where an Endicott case is tried

Endicott lies in Broome County, and state-court matters proceed in the Supreme Court, New York's trial court of general jurisdiction, sitting there. Federal matters have New York's district courts available as forums: the United States District Court for the Northern District of New York, the United States District Court for the Southern District of New York, the United States District Court for the Eastern District of New York, and the United States District Court for the Western District of New York. Which of those courts controls a given caption, and whether any venue-specific registration or qualification requirement applies, is identified and confirmed during the conflict check and intake process. The [Binghamton metro page](/locations/new-york/binghamton/) sets out how the firm covers the wider region, and the [New York locations page](/locations/new-york/) covers the state as a whole.

## Evaluations that come to the evaluee

Records review, research, and report drafting are performed remotely, which keeps costs proportional to the size of the case. The in-person components, the [vocational evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) interview and any life care planning assessment, are conducted in the evaluee's own community, by appointment. The firm maintains no Endicott office and lists no local address; that candor is deliberate, because a report is only as trustworthy as the expert's plainest factual statements. When testimony requires physical presence at a deposition or trial, travel is arranged from the Kansas City home office and planned against the court's calendar.
