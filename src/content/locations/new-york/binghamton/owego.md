## When the other side already has an expert

When retention follows the opposing side's expert disclosure, the assignment is responsive: reviewing an opposing vocational or life care planning report against its own stated methodology. The firm's [rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) engagements test whether the opposing opinion's sources say what the report claims, whether its market definitions match the evaluee's actual circumstances, and whether its conclusions follow from its data. The critique is written to the same standard as an affirmative opinion, since it may be examined just as closely, and the same compressed-calendar discipline applies, because a rebuttal deadline is rarely generous.

## Grounding opinions in the regional market

Affirmative employability and earning capacity findings for an Owego evaluee draw on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Binghamton metropolitan area and for New York statewide, along with state workforce agency labor market information and targeted labor market research where warranted. The analysis names the evaluee's realistic commuting range, within which Binghamton serves as the principal employment center, and characterizes the market qualitatively with a source behind every characterization. Life care planning referrals proceed on the same evidentiary footing, with care needs tied to the medical record before any costing begins.
