Plaintiff and defense counsel tend to bring the same Herkimer case to an
expert with opposite hopes. Purinton Analytics answers both with the same
document: an opinion written so that nothing in it reveals which side
made the call. Whether the assignment is a
[vocational evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) of what an
injured Herkimer resident can still earn, a
[life care plan](/services/life-care-planning/) setting out future care
after catastrophic injury, or
[testimony and litigation consulting](/services/expert-testimony-litigation-consulting/)
through deposition and trial, the method does not shift with the
retention, and that consistency is what lets the opinion hold when the
other side's turn comes.

## Venue for Herkimer matters

Herkimer sits in Herkimer County, and state court engagements proceed in
the Supreme Court, New York's trial court, sitting for that county. On
the federal side, the practice accepts referrals across New York's
district courts as available forums: the United States District Court for
the Northern District of New York, the United States District Court for
the Southern District of New York, the United States District Court for
the Eastern District of New York, and the United States District Court
for the Western District of New York. The firm does not announce from a
webpage which district reaches a particular Herkimer dispute; controlling
venue is confirmed during the conflict check and intake, together with
any registration or qualification requirement the venue imposes. The
firm's coverage across the rest of the state is collected on the
[New York page](/locations/new-york/).

## One labor market, whichever side asks

The symmetry extends to the market analysis. An earning capacity opinion
for a Herkimer evaluee is framed against the Utica-Rome metropolitan
area, the commuting market anchored by Utica, because that is the
geography in which such an evaluee realistically competes for work, and
the frame stays the same whether the retaining party wants the number
high or low. The evidentiary base is the Bureau of Labor Statistics
occupational employment and wage estimates for the Utica-Rome metro and
for New York statewide, read with state workforce agency labor market
information and with direct employer research when a referral question
turns on specific positions. The regional model behind this framing is
laid out on the [Utica metro page](/locations/new-york/utica/), and each
finding names the geography it assumes.

## Running the evaluation

Symmetry governs the logistics as well. Interviews are held by secure
video. Standardized testing follows the publisher's protocols, including
the publisher's own limits on what may be administered remotely, and
anything outside those limits waits for an in-person session. The records
that anchor both are requested against a list drafted from the claims in
the case rather than from boilerplate. When the referral questions call
for face-to-face assessment, the evaluee is seen in his or her own
community, with the expert traveling from the Kansas City home office.
There is no staffed Purinton Analytics office in Herkimer and no street
address to publish for one; counsel hear that at the outset, in the same
plain terms the reports themselves use, because a retention should begin
the way the opinion will read. Where the damages model requires signed
economic analysis, the firm arranges
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.

Because the practice serves plaintiff and defense alike, the door in is
the same for each: the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/), carrying
the caption or party names, a description of the referral questions, and
any disclosure, deposition, or trial dates already set. Scope and
schedule are confirmed in writing once the check clears. And because the
practice is national, a Herkimer matter with parties, companion cases, or
witnesses in other states can keep the same expert team and the same
method throughout, through the firm's
[nationwide practice](/locations/nationwide/).
