Rome is not a suburb of anyone. Its name sits in the title of the
Utica-Rome metro because it is a principal city in its own right, with its
own employers, its own workforce, and its own docket of injury,
employment, and disability disputes. Purinton Analytics serves Rome
counsel on that footing: a
[vocational evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) of
employability and earning capacity, a
[life care plan](/services/life-care-planning/) in catastrophic injury
matters, and [testimony and litigation consulting](/services/expert-testimony-litigation-consulting/)
prepared to the same standard whether the retaining party sits at the
plaintiff table or the defense table.

## Oneida County venue for Rome matters

State court engagements arising in Rome proceed in the Supreme Court, New
York's trial court, sitting for Oneida County. Federal referrals are
accepted throughout New York's district courts: the United States District
Court for the Northern District of New York, the United States District
Court for the Southern District of New York, the United States District
Court for the Eastern District of New York, and the United States District
Court for the Western District of New York. Which district controls a
given Rome case is a question the firm does not guess at; it is confirmed
during the conflict check and intake, along with any registration or
qualification requirement particular to the venue. The firm's broader
coverage of the state is set out on the
[New York page](/locations/new-york/).

## Reading Rome's labor market honestly

An earning capacity opinion for a Rome evaluee has to describe the work
that person can actually reach, which means the analysis is framed
against the Utica-Rome metropolitan area rather than against national
composites. The foundation is the Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational
employment and wage estimates for the metro and for New York statewide,
read alongside state workforce agency labor market information and, where
the referral questions warrant it, direct employer contact about specific
positions. The regional model behind that framing is described on the
[Utica metro page](/locations/new-york/utica/), and qualitative judgments
are labeled as such rather than dressed up as data.

## How a Rome engagement runs

Most work proceeds remotely. Records arrive under a defined request list,
interviews are conducted by secure video, and standardized instruments
are administered under publisher protocols where remote use is supported.
When the questions call for in-person assessment, the evaluation is
scheduled in Rome so the evaluee is seen in the community where he or she
lives and would seek work, with travel arranged from the firm's Kansas
City home office. The firm keeps no staffed office in Rome and publishes
no local street address, because candor about logistics belongs in the
same category as candor about findings. Where damages require economic
modeling, the firm offers
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one
engagement structure.
