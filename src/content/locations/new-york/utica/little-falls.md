An injured person in Little Falls should not have to organize his or her
recovery around an expert's convenience. Purinton Analytics builds its
Little Falls engagements the other way around: the evaluation comes to
the evaluee. Records review, a structured
[vocational assessment](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) of
capacities and restrictions, and the groundwork for a
[life care plan](/services/life-care-planning/) all begin remotely, and
when the referral questions require an in-person meeting, it is held in
the evaluee's own community rather than in a distant office. Plaintiff
and defense retentions receive the identical protocol, because the
protocol, not the client, is what makes the opinion stand.

## What remote-first means in practice

Records are requested against a defined list tied to the claims.
Interviews run over secure video. Standardized instruments are
administered under publisher protocols wherever remote administration is
supported, and anything that cannot be done properly at a distance is
done in person in Little Falls, with the expert's travel scheduled from
the Kansas City home office. The firm has no staffed office in Little
Falls and publishes no street address for it; by-appointment service is
stated outright, since exaggerated logistics would sit poorly next to a
report that demands precision from everyone else. Where future medical
needs are in dispute, a
[medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/) prices care
to the evaluee's actual market rather than to a national table.

## Herkimer County courts and the federal bench

Little Falls litigation in state court proceeds in the Supreme Court, New
York's trial court, for Herkimer County. In federal practice the firm
accepts appointments across the United States District Court for the
Northern District of New York, the United States District Court for the
Southern District of New York, the United States District Court for the
Eastern District of New York, and the United States District Court for
the Western District of New York. The firm does not announce which
district reaches a given Little Falls dispute; controlling venue, and any
qualification requirement that comes with it, is settled during the
conflict check and intake. The [New York page](/locations/new-york/)
describes the statewide picture.

## Placing a Little Falls evaluee in the regional market

Employability findings are only as good as the geography they assume. For
a Little Falls evaluee, the relevant market is the Utica-Rome
metropolitan area, and the firm frames its conclusions with the Bureau of
Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for that
metro and for New York statewide, alongside state workforce agency labor
market information and targeted employer inquiry where warranted. The
[Utica metro page](/locations/new-york/utica/) explains how the practice
treats the region as a single commuting market instead of a collection of
town-sized fragments.

## Beginning the engagement

Counsel on either side start with the
[conflict check form](/refer-a-case/): the caption or party names, the
referral questions, and any dates already fixed for disclosure,
deposition, or trial. Intake confirms scope, fee structure, and venue
requirements before work begins, so the engagement letter and the
evaluation plan are settled while the evaluee's schedule is still easy to
accommodate. For matters outside New York, the same
evaluee-first model travels through the firm's
[nationwide practice](/locations/nationwide/).
