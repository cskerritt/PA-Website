There is no Purinton Analytics office in Yorkville. That admission opens
this page deliberately, because plenty of expert websites imply a local
storefront that does not exist, and an expert willing to shade the truth
about an address invites the jury to wonder what else has been shaded.
What Yorkville counsel get instead is a by-appointment practice that
delivers [vocational evaluations](/services/vocational-expert-witness/),
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[expert testimony and litigation consulting](/services/expert-testimony-litigation-consulting/)
to the village on the strength of its process rather than its signage,
for retaining counsel on either side of the dispute.

## What by-appointment service looks like

The working default is remote. Records come in under a defined request
list keyed to the claims. The vocational and clinical interviews run by
secure video, and standardized instruments are administered under
publisher protocols wherever remote administration is supported. When the
referral questions require presence, whether for an in-person evaluation
of a Yorkville evaluee or for deposition and trial testimony, travel is
scheduled from the firm's Kansas City home office and planned around the
case calendar. Nothing about that arrangement is hidden, and nothing
about it limits the depth of the file; the record request, the interview
notes, and the instrument results are documented the same way they would
be anywhere else in the practice.

for the Southern District of New York, the United States District Court
for the Eastern District of New York, and the United States District
Court for the Western District of New York. The firm identifies the
controlling district for a specific Yorkville case during the conflict
check and intake, the same stage at which any venue-tied registration or
qualification requirement is confirmed. The broader map of the practice
in the state sits on the [New York page](/locations/new-york/).

The path in is short: plaintiff or defense counsel submits the caption or
party names through the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/), states the
referral questions, and lists any disclosure, deposition, or trial dates
already fixed. After clearance, scope and fees are confirmed in writing
before work begins. The same office-honest, by-appointment model extends
to matters anywhere in the country through the firm's
