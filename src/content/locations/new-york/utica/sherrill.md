Sometimes the retention is not to write the first opinion but to test one
already served. Counsel in Sherrill matters bring Purinton Analytics both
kinds of work: original
[vocational evaluations](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) and
[life care plans](/services/life-care-planning/), and
[rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) of an
opposing expert's report, checking its sources, its methodology, and
whether its conclusions about a Sherrill evaluee survive contact with the
underlying data. The firm applies the same scrutiny to its own work that
it applies to others', and it accepts that assignment from plaintiff or
defense counsel without altering the standard.

## Courts serving Sherrill

Sherrill is an Oneida County community, and its state court matters are
heard in the Supreme Court, New York's trial court, for that county.
Federal work is accepted in the state's district courts as available
forums: the United States District Court for the Northern District of New
York, the United States District Court for the Southern District of New
York, the United States District Court for the Eastern District of New
York, and the United States District Court for the Western District of
New York. Determining which of them controls a given case, and whether
the venue imposes any registration or qualification requirement, is part
of the conflict check and intake, not a claim made on a webpage. The
firm's presence throughout the state is described on the
[New York page](/locations/new-york/).

## Scale honesty in a small city's market

A community of Sherrill's size cannot be analyzed as if it were its own
labor market, and pretending otherwise is one of the errors a rebuttal
review most often finds. Employability and placeability for a Sherrill
evaluee are assessed against the Utica-Rome metropolitan area, using the
Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for
the metro and for New York statewide, state workforce agency labor market
information, and direct employer inquiry where the questions call for it.
The [Utica metro page](/locations/new-york/utica/) sets out that regional
frame in full, and a Sherrill report states plainly which geography each
finding relies on.

## Conducting the evaluation

Engagements open remotely as a rule: a tailored records request,
secure-video interviews, and standardized instruments administered under
publisher protocols where remote administration is supported. Where the
referral requires it, the evaluee is seen in person in the Sherrill area,
with the expert's travel arranged from the Kansas City home office; the
firm keeps no staffed office in Sherrill, publishes no local address, and
regards saying so as part of the job. When damages require signed
economic analysis, the firm arranges
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
work while the vocational and care-planning components proceed under one
engagement structure.

## First step for counsel

Whether the assignment is an original opinion or a review of someone
else's, it starts with the [conflict check form](/refer-a-case/): the
caption or party names, the referral questions, and the operative
disclosure, deposition, or trial dates. Counsel with related matters
outside New York can engage the same review standards anywhere through
the firm's [nationwide practice](/locations/nationwide/).
