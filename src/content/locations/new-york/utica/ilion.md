By the time an Ilion matter needs a vocational expert or a life care
planner, disclosure deadlines are usually already set, and the value of
the retention depends
on whether a defensible report can be built inside them. Purinton
Analytics scopes every Ilion engagement against that calendar from the
first call: what the
[vocational evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) must cover,
what records the [life care plan](/services/life-care-planning/) requires,
and what can be completed, documented, and signed before the dates that
matter. Retentions are taken from plaintiff and defense counsel on the
same terms.

## Herkimer County and the federal forums

Ilion sits in Herkimer County, and state court matters proceed in the
Supreme Court, New York's trial court, for that county. On the federal
side, the practice appears across the state's district courts, namely the
United States District Court for the Northern District of New York, the
United States District Court for the Southern District of New York, the
United States District Court for the Eastern District of New York, and
the United States District Court for the Western District of New York;
the district that governs a particular Ilion case, like any
venue-specific qualification requirement, is pinned down during the
conflict check and intake rather than assumed. Statewide coverage is
described on the [New York page](/locations/new-york/).

## The market an Ilion evaluee actually faces

A village-scale community does not supply its own complete labor market,
so employability and placeability conclusions for an Ilion evaluee are
tested against the Utica-Rome metropolitan area, the geography within
which such an evaluee realistically competes for work. The data
foundation is the Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and
wage estimates for that metro and for New York statewide, supplemented by
state workforce agency labor market information and direct labor market
research where the questions demand it. The firm's approach to the wider
region appears on the [Utica metro page](/locations/new-york/utica/), and
every market conclusion in an Ilion report identifies the geography it
assumes so the assumption itself can be examined.

## Fitting the evaluation to the schedule

Remote-first structure is what makes tight calendars workable. Record
collection, secure-video interviewing, and standardized testing under
publisher protocols proceed without waiting on travel, and a
[medical cost projection](/services/medical-cost-projection/) can be
priced to the evaluee's own market in parallel. When face-to-face
assessment is required, it happens in the evaluee's community, with the
expert traveling from the Kansas City home office; the firm maintains no
staffed office in Ilion and lists no street address there, and says so
plainly. Where the damages model needs an economist, the firm coordinates
[damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) in which
independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis
while the vocational and care-planning work runs under the same
engagement.
