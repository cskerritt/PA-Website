Ask where a Whitesboro resident works, and the answer is rarely just
Whitesboro. The village belongs to a commuting market that spans the
Utica-Rome metro, and any credible opinion about what an injured
Whitesboro evaluee can still earn has to be built on that wider
geography. Purinton Analytics starts there: a
[vocational evaluation](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) that maps
the evaluee's transferable skills onto the occupations the regional
market actually contains, paired where needed with a
[life care plan](/services/life-care-planning/) and with
[forensic economic damages](/services/forensic-economic-damages/)
coordination. The same analysis is available to whichever side of the
caption retains it first.

## Grounding the market analysis

The data behind a Whitesboro opinion comes from the Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Utica-Rome
metropolitan area and for New York statewide, read with state workforce
agency labor market information and, when a referral question turns on
particular jobs, direct contact with employers. Conclusions that are
qualitative are identified as qualitative; conclusions that rest on data
carry their citations. How this regional frame fits the metro's other
communities is described on the
[Utica metro page](/locations/new-york/utica/).

Whitesboro case, together with any venue-linked registration or

## Seeing the evaluee, not just the file

Data alone does not finish the job. Whitesboro engagements typically open
remotely, with records collected under a defined request list and
interviews conducted by secure video, and standardized instruments are
administered under publisher protocols where remote administration is
supported. When the questions require it, the evaluee is assessed in
person in his or her own community, with expert travel scheduled from the
Kansas City home office and coordinated with counsel so the visit serves
the deposition and disclosure schedule rather than disrupting it. The firm operates no staffed location in
Whitesboro and publishes no address there; it says so directly, because a
practice that inflates its logistics invites doubt about its findings.
For matters needing signed economic analysis, independent economist
partners perform and sign that work within
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
under one engagement structure with the vocational and care-planning
components.
