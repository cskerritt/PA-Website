Before Purinton Analytics reads a single record in an Albion case, the conflict check runs. That ordering is deliberate: an expert whose independence can be questioned is an expert whose opinions can be discounted, so every Albion engagement, vocational or life care planning, begins with documented clearance against prior and current matters, then a defined scope, then a records request tailored to the claims. Only after those steps does analysis start. Counsel for plaintiffs and counsel for defendants go through the identical sequence, which is exactly the point; the [expert testimony and litigation consulting](/services/expert-testimony-litigation-consulting/) practice depends on it.

## Neither closed nor wide open: defining the market

Opposing shortcuts distort earning capacity opinions written for small communities. One confines the evaluee to Albion itself and calls the market closed; the other hands the evaluee the whole of the Rochester, NY metro and calls it open. Each substitutes assumption for evidence, and the firm's [vocational opinions](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) commit to neither in advance. What the record supports decides where between those poles a given Albion evaluee actually sits, and only then does the opinion measure the opportunity inside that boundary, drawing on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Rochester metropolitan area and for New York statewide, state workforce agency labor market information, and direct labor market research when the referral questions require it. Whether the resulting characterization favors the retaining party is not the report's concern; whether either side can verify it is.

## Service model, disclosed up front

Just as clearance precedes analysis, candor precedes marketing: the firm has no Albion office, publishes no local street address, and describes its presence here as what it is, by appointment. The engagement's core, records analysis, research, and drafting, is performed remotely. In-person [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) and vocational evaluations are held in the evaluee's own community, and courtroom obligations are met by travel from the Kansas City home office, planned around deposition and trial dates. Where a case needs economic loss opinions, [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) assign that work to independent economist partners, who perform and sign the economic analysis while the vocational and care-planning tracks proceed under one engagement structure.
