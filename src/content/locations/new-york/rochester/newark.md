The written record decides more cases than the witness stand does. For Newark, New York litigation, Purinton Analytics builds vocational and life care planning opinions from documents first: medical records, employment files, earnings histories, and educational records, requested under a scope defined at intake and cited throughout the report. What the record does not support, the opinion does not say. That documentary discipline is the same whether the retaining party sits at the plaintiff table or the defense table, and it is why the firm's [rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) work and its affirmative [vocational expert](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) work follow one methodology.
