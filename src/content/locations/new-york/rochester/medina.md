## The questions an earning capacity opinion must answer

The labor market analysis for a Medina evaluee turns on separate questions: what work the person can still perform, where that work actually exists within reach of this community, and how its pay compares with the earnings history in the record. None of them is answered by pointing at the metro. Medina's own economy is that of a small town, the principal city of Rochester anchors the wider Rochester, NY market, and how much of the ground between the two a particular evaluee can cover is a finding the report must make and defend, not a premise it gets to borrow. Each answer is built from public sources: Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Rochester metropolitan area and for New York statewide, state workforce agency labor market information, and direct labor market research where warranted. Findings are stated qualitatively, in terms opposing counsel can check against the cited series rather than take on faith.
