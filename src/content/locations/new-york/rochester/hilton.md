Most of a vocational expert's work never happens in a conference room. For Hilton, New York referrals, the substance of the engagement, reviewing medical and employment records, running transferable skills analysis, researching the labor market, and drafting the opinion, is performed remotely, and Purinton Analytics has built its practice around doing that remote work well: defined scopes, documented sources, and drafts treated as future testimony. The in-person moments that matter, the evaluee assessment, the deposition, the trial, are then scheduled deliberately rather than improvised. Counsel on either side of a Hilton case get the same structure through the firm's [vocational expert witness](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) and [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) services.

## Courts serving a Hilton dispute

Hilton lies in Monroe County, and civil actions in New York state court proceed in the Supreme Court. Should the matter be federal, the available trial-level forums statewide are the United States District Court for the Northern District of New York, the United States District Court for the Southern District of New York, the United States District Court for the Eastern District of New York, and the United States District Court for the Western District of New York. The controlling venue for any particular caption is settled with counsel during the conflict check and intake, which is also where any venue-specific registration or qualification requirement gets identified and confirmed, well before deadlines compress.

## Grounding opinions in the regional market

A Hilton evaluee's employment prospects are analyzed against the Rochester, NY regional market, whose principal city is Rochester. The evidence base is consistent from case to case: Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Rochester metropolitan area and for New York statewide, state workforce agency labor market information, and direct labor market research where the referral questions justify it. The opinion describes the evaluee's realistic reach into that market in qualitative terms, tied to documented restrictions and skills, and cites each source so the characterizations can be audited by whichever side chooses to try. When placement questions are contested, the analysis also documents the search method itself, so the path from data to conclusion is reproducible.

## Presence, stated accurately

The firm keeps no office in Hilton, publishes no address for this location, and will not manufacture the appearance of one; a practice that polices accuracy in its reports owes the same accuracy on its website. Meetings are by appointment, remote or in person. Evaluations are conducted in the evaluee's community. Deposition and trial appearances are reached by travel from the Kansas City home office, coordinated with the court's dates. Where economic damages complete the picture, [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) place that analysis with independent economist partners, who perform and sign the economic work while the vocational and care-planning tracks proceed under one engagement structure.
