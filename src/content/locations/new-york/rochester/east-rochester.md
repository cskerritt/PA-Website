Sometimes the most valuable expert work in a case is a careful reading of the other expert's report. When an East Rochester case already contains a vocational opinion, the question for counsel is often not what a new expert would conclude but whether the existing one holds up, and Purinton Analytics offers [rebuttal and peer review](/services/rebuttal-peer-review/) engagements built for exactly that question: whether the opinion's sources support its conclusions, whether its transferable skills analysis follows accepted method, and whether its earning capacity figures rest on the record or on assumption. The review applies the same standards the firm's own affirmative reports must meet, which is what makes it useful to plaintiff and defense counsel alike.

## Testing and building labor market opinions

Whether reviewing an opposing report or authoring its own, the firm holds labor market work to identical sourcing: Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the Rochester metropolitan area and for New York statewide, state workforce agency labor market information, and direct labor market research when the questions call for it. An East Rochester evaluee works within the Rochester, NY regional market, anchored by its principal city of Rochester, and any opinion about employability must define the evaluee's realistic access to that market qualitatively and cite where each characterization comes from. A report that cannot show its sources is a report waiting to be impeached.

## Practical arrangements

There is no local Purinton Analytics office in East Rochester and no published street address, and the firm declines to imply otherwise. Engagements run remote-first: records analysis, research, and drafting happen off-site under a defined scope, with counsel meetings by appointment. When affirmative work is needed, in-person [vocational evaluations](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) and [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) assessments are held in the evaluee's own community, and testimony travel originates at the Kansas City home office, timed to the court's calendar. Cases requiring economic loss opinions proceed as [coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/), in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis while vocational and care-planning work continues under one engagement structure.
