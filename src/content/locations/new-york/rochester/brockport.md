## What by-appointment service means here

No office, no storefront, no published address in Brockport: the firm says so plainly because overstating presence would be a strange habit for a practice built on accurate statements. Counsel instead get remote-first records analysis and drafting, meetings by appointment, and in-person [vocational evaluations](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) and [life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) assessments held in the evaluee's community.
