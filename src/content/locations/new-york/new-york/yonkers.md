Yonkers generates the kind of civil docket a major city produces: personal
injury and medical negligence claims, employment and disability disputes,
matrimonial matters that turn on earning capacity, and workers' compensation
files that need a defensible return-to-work analysis. Purinton Analytics
serves counsel handling those cases as a
[vocational expert witness](/services/vocational-expert-witness/) and
[life care planning](/services/life-care-planning/) practice, accepting work
from either side of the caption and writing every report to be read closely
by the opposing side. Where economic quantification is also required, the
firm offers
[coordinated damages engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/)
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under a single
engagement structure.

## The Westchester County venue picture

Yonkers sits in Westchester County, and state-court civil actions arising
there are ordinarily heard in the Supreme Court, New York's trial court of
general jurisdiction. Cases with a federal basis may proceed in one of New
York's federal trial courts: the United States District Court for the
Southern District of New York, the United States District Court for the
Eastern District of New York, the United States District Court for the
Northern District of New York, or the United States District Court for the
Western District of New York. Which forum controls a given Yonkers matter,
and any registration or qualification requirement particular to it, is
confirmed during the conflict check and intake. The firm's statewide
practice is described on the [New York locations page](/locations/new-york/).

## A city inside one of the country's largest labor markets

Yonkers belongs to the New York-Newark-Jersey City, NY-NJ metropolitan area,
and its residents participate in a labor market that extends well beyond the
city limits, including into New York itself. A transferable skills analysis
for a Yonkers evaluee therefore has to reflect commuting reality rather than
a single municipal job base. Vocational findings rest on Bureau of Labor
Statistics occupational employment and wage estimates for the metropolitan
area and for New York statewide, supplemented by state workforce agency
labor market information and direct labor market research where the referral
questions warrant it. The broader regional practice, including the New
Jersey side of the metro, is described on the
[New York metro page](/locations/new-york/new-york/).

## Evaluation logistics in Yonkers

The firm keeps no staffed office in Yonkers and publishes no street address
for this location; meetings are by appointment, and that candor about
presence is part of the same discipline that governs the reports themselves.
Most engagements begin remotely: records review against a defined scope, a
structured vocational interview conducted by video, and standardized testing
administered under protocols appropriate for remote use. When the referral
questions call for an in-person evaluation, it is conducted in the evaluee's
own community, with travel scheduled from the Kansas City home office.
Deposition and trial testimony are planned the same way, with travel from
Kansas City arranged around the court's calendar in Westchester County.
