## County and courts for New Rochelle matters

New Rochelle is a Westchester County city, and its civil litigation is
ordinarily filed in the Supreme Court, the state's trial court of general
jurisdiction. Federal-question and diversity matters may be heard in the
United States District Court for the Northern District of New York, the
United States District Court for the Southern District of New York, the
United States District Court for the Eastern District of New York, or the
United States District Court for the Western District of New York; the
controlling venue for a specific case, along with any qualification

process. Counsel who practice across the state will find the wider venue

## How the evaluation actually happens

Most of the work arrives and proceeds remotely: medical, employment, and
educational records are reviewed against a defined scope; the vocational
interview is conducted by video; testing is administered under protocols
suited to remote administration. When an in-person evaluation is warranted,
it takes place in the evaluee's own community rather than requiring travel
to a distant office, and the firm's travel is scheduled from its Kansas City
home office. There is no staffed New Rochelle office and no published street
address here; meetings are by appointment, because accuracy about presence
belongs in the same category as accuracy about data. Where a case also
requires future medical costing or economic quantification, the firm
prepares [medical cost projections](/services/medical-cost-projection/) and
offers
[coordinated damages assessment](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/),
in which independent economist partners perform and sign the economic
analysis while the vocational and care-planning work proceeds under a single
engagement structure.
