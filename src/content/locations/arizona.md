The most careful reader of an expert report is usually the lawyer who did not
commission it. Purinton Analytics writes vocational expert and life care
planning opinions for Arizona litigation with that reader in mind:
methodology stated, sources cited, and conclusions built to hold their shape
under cross-examination. Because the same standard applies whichever side
issues the engagement letter, the firm accepts referrals from plaintiff and
defense counsel alike, anywhere in the state.

## Arizona courts and metropolitan areas

Engagements are accepted for matters in the Superior Court, Arizona's trial
court, and in the United States District Court for the District of Arizona,
along with workers' compensation and administrative proceedings. Metro pages

Show Low,

## Remote-first service, by-appointment presence

What Arizona counsel gets is a defined process rather than a nearby
storefront. The firm keeps no staffed office in Arizona and publishes no
street address for this location; meetings are by appointment. Most
engagements begin remotely, because records review, clinical interviews, and
many standardized vocational instruments can be administered by secure video
under publisher protocols, which shortens timelines and reduces cost. When
the referral questions call for in-person evaluation, or when deposition and
trial dates require presence, travel is scheduled from the Kansas City home
office, and the written report identifies how each component of the
evaluation was conducted so the record is complete.

Labor market conclusions stay local to the evaluee. Vocational findings for
Arizona evaluees rest on Bureau of Labor Statistics occupational employment
and wage estimates for Arizona and its metropolitan areas, on state workforce
agency labor market information, and on direct labor market research where
warranted. Where the claims call for economic damages testimony, the firm
offers [coordinated damages
engagements](/services/coordinated-damages-assessment/) in which independent
economist partners perform and sign the economic analysis while the
vocational and care-planning work proceeds under one engagement structure.
The full national practice, including evaluation protocols and travel
logistics, is described on the [nationwide coverage
page](/locations/nationwide/).

## Qualification in Arizona venues

Admissibility of expert testimony is governed by the forum's rules of
evidence, and disclosure practice differs between Arizona's state and federal
courts, so each engagement is scoped to its forum at the start. Expert
disclosure deadlines, deposition dates, and trial settings are confirmed at
intake and tracked through delivery. Any venue-specific registration or
qualification requirement is identified and confirmed during the conflict
check and intake process.

## Beginning an Arizona engagement

An Arizona referral starts with a documented conflict check, not a sales
call. Submit the case caption or party names through the [conflict check
form](/refer-a-case/), noting any imminent disclosure, deposition, or trial
dates, and the firm responds with availability, CVs, and fee information. A
records checklist tailored to the claims follows once the conflict check
clears, and the engagement letter defines scope and deliverables before the
work begins.
