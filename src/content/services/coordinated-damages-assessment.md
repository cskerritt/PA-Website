## The problem a coordinated assessment solves

A catastrophic damages case runs on three expert analyses that must agree
without merging. The vocational evaluation establishes what work and earnings
remain. The life care plan establishes what care the injuries require and
what it costs today. The economic analysis converts both streams into present
value. Retained separately, those three experts often meet for the first time
in the case's exhibit list, and the seams show: the economist prices an
earning capacity the vocational expert never found, household services are
claimed twice or not at all, the life care plan's costs reach the economist
after the disclosure deadline has spent the time the analysis needed.

Opposing counsel reads a damages package looking for exactly those seams,
because an inconsistency between two friendly experts impeaches both. The
coordinated damages assessment exists to remove the seams without removing
the independence: one engagement, one sequence, one set of documented
assumptions, and three separately signed opinions.

## Three disciplines, one sequence

The assessment runs in the order the dependencies dictate. The vocational
evaluation comes first, because post-injury earning capacity is an input the
economist cannot responsibly assume. The life care plan proceeds in parallel
once the medical record supports it, producing categorized future care needs
with current costs and cited medical foundation. The independent economist
partner works last, adopting the vocational findings and the life care
plan's cost stream as expressly stated inputs, adding the economic layer,
worklife expectancy, growth, discounting, household services, and signing a
present value opinion built on documented rather than assumed foundations.

Each handoff is recorded. The economist's report identifies the vocational
and life care planning findings it adopts and cites their sources; the
vocational report and life care plan stand on their own methodologies without
reaching into economics. When one analysis changes, the downstream reports
are updated and re-dated, so the package never contains two versions of the
same assumption.

The sequence is also a scheduling instrument. Because the dependencies are
known at intake, the firm works backward from the expert disclosure deadline
to set delivery dates for each report, with the economist's window protected
at the end where compressed schedules usually fail. Counsel sees one
timeline covering all three analyses, and slippage in any component is
flagged when it can still be absorbed rather than after it has consumed the
economist's runway.

## Separation of opinions, by design

Coordination is a logistics arrangement, not an authorship arrangement. Every
discipline in the assessment issues its own report under its own signature:
the vocational evaluation and life care plan signed by Jason C. Purinton
within his certified disciplines, and the economic analysis performed and
signed by an independent economist partner selected for the venue and the
case. No opinion is ghost-written across disciplines, no report is issued for
another expert's signature, and each expert maintains their own file and
testifies to their own work.

That separation is what makes the package durable in discovery. Each expert
is deposed on opinions they are qualified to hold and personally formed.
Cross-examination that pushes an expert toward a neighboring discipline meets
a stated boundary rather than an improvised answer. And because the adopted
inputs are cited expressly, counsel can trace every number in the economic
conclusion back through the life care plan and vocational evaluation to the
records and published data underneath.

## The four-step engagement process

The coordinated assessment is the fullest expression of the firm's standard
engagement sequence. Step one, conflict check and scope: counsel submits the
caption or party names, conflicts are cleared across all three disciplines,
including the economist partner, and the referral questions are defined. Step
two, expert selection and records request: the economist partner is confirmed
for the venue, and a single consolidated records request issues, covering
medical, vocational, wage, tax, benefits, and household documentation in one
pass. Step three, evaluation, analysis, and reports: the vocational
evaluation, life care plan, and economic analysis proceed in dependency
order, tracked against the disclosure calendar, and are delivered as three
separately signed reports. Step four, testimony: each expert is available for
deposition, mediation support, and trial, in person or by remote appearance,
with scheduling coordinated through one point of contact.

## When the coordinated model fits, and when it does not

The assessment earns its structure in cases where damages have both an
earnings dimension and a care dimension: catastrophic personal injury,
medical malpractice with lifelong sequelae, birth injury where earning
capacity must be evaluated without a work history and care needs span
decades, and wrongful death matters combining survivor economic loss with
household services. It is equally at home in the defense posture, reviewing
a served damages package for internal consistency, double counting, and
role-boundary violations across all three reports.

It is the wrong instrument for narrow questions. A single employability
dispute, a stand-alone medical cost projection, or a rebuttal confined to
one report calls for a single-discipline engagement, which is faster and
costs less. The firm scopes that honestly at the conflict check, because a
three-expert structure sold into a one-question case is its own kind of
overreach.

## Role boundaries across the team

The boundary map is stated at intake and repeated in every report. Physicians
own diagnosis, prognosis, causation, and life expectancy; every discipline in
the assessment adopts medical opinions rather than creating them. The
vocational expert owns employability, placeability, earning capacity, and
labor market access. The life care planner owns future care needs,
frequencies, durations, and current costs, on documented medical foundation.
The independent economist partner owns worklife application, growth,
discounting, household services valuation, and present value, and signs the
economic opinion. Nobody owns the legal conclusions; recoverability and
admissibility belong to counsel and the court.

## Plaintiff and defense engagements

The coordinated assessment is side-neutral by construction. For plaintiff
counsel it produces an affirmative damages package whose parts were built to
withstand separate depositions. For defense counsel it produces a coordinated
critique of the package on the other side of the caption, discipline by
discipline, under the same standards. The methodology, the sequence, and the
role boundaries are identical in both configurations, which is what allows
the firm to stand behind the work regardless of who retained it.
